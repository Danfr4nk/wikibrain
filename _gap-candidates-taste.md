  Brockhampton, Flatbush Zombies, Joey Bada$$, Night Lovell, Young Dolph) +

  outlier (Tove Lo "Habits (Stay High)"); liked but not boxed (zero crate

- **Sources:** `canonical_liked_songs.csv`, `soundiiz_playlist_5321_tracks.txt`,

## 3. Phonetic name-mishearing (Filter 0 on metadata)

- **Proposed path:** `wiki/mind/concepts/phonetic-name-mishearing.md`
- **Why a page:** two attested instances (2026-09-12: "Eat Me Better" →
  "eat you better"; "i'm loam" → "its loam") suggest the lyric qualifier runs
  on *metadata*, not just audio — track/artist names arrive as phonetic
  approximations. The synthesis entry filed the corollary with its operational
  consequence (verify names against Spotify before acting — already a standing
  wiki rule) and two testable predictions: mishearing correlates with
  unfamiliar names, and clusters at skip-test-boundary tracks. A dedicated page
  can own the corpus pass (searching his chats for more instances) that the
  synthesis entry scoped but didn't run.
- **Key facts:** 2 instances, both 2026-09-12, both title/artist names;
  same explicit-over-inferred mechanism as the lyric qualifier;
  convergence with the autism page's filing of the qualifier.
- **Sources:** operator testimony 2026-09-12; taste-as-filter-stack
  (Name-Mishearing Corollary section).
- **Connections:** taste-as-filter-stack; lyrics-as-timbre; autism
  (explicit-over-inferred processing); the standing verification rule.
