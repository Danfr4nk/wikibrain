**The conflict:** `wiki/people/john-paci.md` dates the $10,000 settlement letter 2025-03-05 10:42 ET; `wiki/places/307-e-76th-st.md` line 127 agrees ("two weeks after the move"). But `wiki/people/annie-ulmer.md` ("What the money did") and 307-e-76th-st.md line 69 say **2026-03-05**.
**Why it matters:** the letter is inversion 2's closing document — the $10,000-held-flat finding depends on its date. A 2026 letter would mean the debt stayed live a full year past move-out with zero correspondence in between, which changes the post-move financial history.
**What would close it:** a primary re-check of the letter itself (the RAW-DUMP row), not a vote between pages. The synthesis entry adopts 2025 as better-supported (timestamped; arithmetically coherent) but flags the conflict.
**Suggested shape:** a dated audit note on `wiki/people/john-paci.md` (or a kb evidence node) recording which rows support which year, then a one-line correction to the losing page.
