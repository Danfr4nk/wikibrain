## Why `lapsed` is counted separately

`ended` means the state stopped and something dates the stopping.
`lapsed` means the *record* stopped and the state's end is unknown. A
corpus going quiet is a fact about the corpus, so a lapsed claim is
reported as unsettled rather than as false — the same refusal
`bin/wiki-traits` makes when an instrument finds nothing.
