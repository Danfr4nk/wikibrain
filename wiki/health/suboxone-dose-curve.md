# The Dose Curve That Isn't: Suboxone, 2010–2026

Sixteen-plus years of daily Suboxone dosing, and the corpus holds exactly one
dosage figure: **2mg/day, self-reported on 2013-07-10** **[ATTESTED, dat:0055]**.
Not one figure per year. Not one per decade. One figure, in one overnight
Facebook exchange, after which the record never names a dose again — through
thirteen more years of the same regimen, two states, a pandemic, the [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]]
decade, an inheritance window, a job loss, and a supply failure that cost him
employment.

This page treats "one point + sixteen years of silence" as its own object: what
a dose curve would require, what the record actually holds around the point,
why the silence exists as a structural matter rather than an accident, and what
the single point can and cannot support. The framing — engineering vs recovery,
the vigilance axiom, the audit of what the system does — is carried on
[[wiki/health/chemical-architecture]] and
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/chemical-architecture]] and is not repeated here. This is
the evidence ledger.

The headline finding is not the dose. It is the shape of the absence. A
sixteen-year daily pharmacological run with exactly one dosage data point is
not a curve at all; it is a datum and a silence, and the silence is not
uniform — it has its own datable edges, its own channels, its own partial
verifications. That is what is inventoried below.

## The single point, stated exactly

The figure arrives inside a Facebook exchange on **2013-07-10** between Dan and
[[wiki/people/elizabeth-eleanor|Elizabeth Eleanor]], a fellow recovering addict.
The wiki's people page describes the entire relationship as one overnight
exchange, months after his return to Uniontown from NYC **[ATTESTED, dat:0061]**.
What he writes, outbound, in that thread **[ATTESTED, dat:0055]**:

- *"i've been on maintainence for 3.5 years"*
- *"i was on the strips when i had a doctor in nyc"* (past tense)
- *"and i'm down to 2mg/day"*
- *"yes still doing subs"*
- and, to the same counterparty, *"if you're ever unloading or come in contact call me"*

The surrounding exchange, dated into the overnight of 2013-07-10 to
2013-07-11, also carries the confessional edge that gives the point its weight:
*"i'm still on 2mg of suboxone a day, it's been almost 5 years since i touched
an opiate and i'm still too terrified to get off"* **[ATTESTED,
wiki/health/chemical-architecture]**. She is pushing NA/AA and the Big Book; he
declines on explicitly cognitive grounds — *"i think my brain works a little
differently than most and as an INTP and heavily introverted, logic-based
dude... i can't see myself flourishing in AA/NA"* — and she closes: *"I respect
your views... I hope you can prove me wrong though."* The two never spoke again
on record **[ATTESTED, dat:0061]**.

**Why the character of the exchange matters for the figure's weight.** This is
not a public post. It is not a tweet, not a status, not a thread. It is a
one-off overnight message exchange with a stranger-acquaintance who is herself
in recovery — the context in which a number gets stated confessional rather
than performative. There is no audience to impress and no identity to defend; if
anything, the incentive runs the other way (a recovering addict telling a fellow
recovering addict he is "still" on maintenance at a low dose is not a status
move). The figure therefore arrives with less performance pressure than almost
anything else in the corpus — which raises its credibility as a contemporaneous
self-report, without raising it to the level of a record. [INFERRED: the
confessional context is a credibility-positive feature, not a proof; seams
stated.]

**What "down to" does in the sentence.** *"down to 2mg/day"* presupposes a
prior higher dose — it is a taper-completed or taper-in-progress claim, not a
steady-state-from-induction claim [INFERRED from the sentence's own semantics;
the inference is grammatical, not clinical]. What the prior dose was, how many
steps the taper had, when it started, whether it was clinician-directed — none
of that is in the record. The sentence implies a history the record does not
supply. That implication is itself a bearing: the 2013 point is not an
induction dose. Something preceded it at a higher level, and the corpus has no
word on what it was.

## What a dose curve would need

Before inventorying what exists, state the requirement honestly, so the gap is
measurable rather than vibes. A dose curve for a sixteen-year maintenance
regimen would need, at minimum:

1. **An induction point.** The dose at which daily dosing began (Feb 2010, or
   thereabouts). Absent. The corpus names the start date of the run; it never
   names the start dose.
2. **At least one intermediate dated point.** The corpus has exactly this: the
   2013 point, 3.4 years into the run. One intermediate point makes a segment,
   not a curve — and a segment with an unknown start.
3. **A current or late-interval point.** Absent. Nothing after 2013 names a
   dose. The 2025 logistics messages name a prescription, a pharmacy, a doctor
   — never an amount.
4. **Channel evidence of dose changes (taper announcements, escalations,
   switches between formulations).** Absent, except for the 2013 implication
   of "down to." The record contains zero taper discussions in sixteen years
   [ATTESTED by absence across the iMessage corpus, the Facebook archive, and
   the Twitter archive].
5. **Clinical corroboration.** Absent entirely. No prescriber notes, no
   pharmacy records, no lab values, no appointment summaries appear anywhere in
   the corpus.

One out of five. The corpus holds one intermediate point and the start date of
the run. That is the entire curve: a start with no value, a value with no
neighbor, and thirteen years of silence after the value.

To put the interval in units: from 2010-02-17 to 2026-09-13 is roughly 6,050
calendar days **[DERIVED: date arithmetic; approximate, not a corpus claim]**.
One dosage figure across ~6,050 days of dosing is one observation per ~6,050
executions — a sampling density that does not support interpolation, slope
estimation, or any claim about the shape of the run between the point and
either end. The page will now show exactly what surrounds that one point,
because the surroundings are the only thing that can give it meaning.

## The inventory: dated bearings around the point

What follows is every dated bearing the corpus holds that touches the dosing
run, in chronological order. Each is scored for what it establishes about the
dose specifically — not about the regimen's existence, which is the easier
claim.

### 2010-02-17 — the run starts. Not a dose point.

The day-zero date: February 17, 2010 **[ATTESTED, wiki/self/twitter/2010]**.
Its epistemic history is fully documented on
[[wiki/health/chemical-architecture]] — originally computed (not recalled) as
"the day before a train," the derivation broken by the Twitter record showing
the train running the other way, the date surviving on a contemporaneous
15:07 ET tweet (*"this is the most stressful day/decision of my life. nothing
is making it easier."*) plus a 2013 *"3.5 years"* back-calculation.

**Dose content of this bearing: zero.** The date tells you when daily dosing
began, not at what amount, not by whose direction, not on what formulation.
The corpus holds no induction record — no first prescription, no titration
log, no account of what the first week's doses were. The run's most-cited date
is its least informative bearing on the dose itself. State that plainly,
because it is easy to let the certainty of the date launder certainty onto the
dose. The date is CONFIRMED (within weeks, three channels). The start dose is
[UNKNOWN] and the record does not even imply it.

### 2010-08-14 — six months pill-free. Not a dose point.

Six months into the run, Dan tells Suz: *"btw tomorrow is 6 months since
either of us have done anything in pill form. by far the longest that I've
been able to make it last"* **[ATTESTED, dat:1879]**.

What this establishes: a back-calculation pinning last pill use to
~2010-02-15 — within days of the 2010-02-17 day-zero this page carries. Two
independent derivations (the tweet-bearing day-zero and this six-month
anniversary) converge on the same mid-February window, which is the
strongest corroboration the date has gained since the 2013 *"3.5 years"*
back-calculation. Note the scope: "pill form" covers the pre-Suboxone pill
use, not the Suboxone regimen itself — this is an abstinence-from-pills
anniversary, not a dosing record. The same day's exchange also fixes a
January floor: on 2010-01-31 Dan asked Suz to bring him 5 Vicodin for his
back **[dat:1879]** — pill use was live two weeks before day-zero and gone
within weeks after, consistent with a February induction.

**Dose content of this bearing: zero.** Like day-zero, it dates the
regimen's start, not its dose.

### 2011-08-04 — the appointment with screening. Not a dose point.

A Facebook message, outbound, dated 2011-08-04 **[ATTESTED, dat:0033]**:

*"I was supposed to go to my Suboxone appointment today, so I haven't gotten
anything since Saturday night. I got one of those At-Home test kits just to
make sure I'd pass the test that they give me there, and apparently I did just
a liiittttle bit too much"* — followed by a request to the recipient for clean
urine.

What this establishes **[ATTESTED, dat:0033]**:

- In 2011 the regimen involved **scheduled appointments at a place** — not a
  pharmacy handoff, not a dealer.
- A **drug test was administered there**. That is monitored treatment, a
  substantive clinical relationship, which the later "logistics only"
  characterization does not cover.
- He was dry since Saturday night before a Monday/Tuesday appointment — the
  hand-to-mouth sourcing texture of the run even in good years.

**Dose content of this bearing: zero, again.** The appointment existed; the
amount discussed at it did not survive into the message. The message is about
passing the screening, not about what the screening was screening for or what
was prescribed. It proves the run was clinically supervised in 2011 — which
means a dose was *being set* by someone, somewhere — without recording the
dose. A supervised regime with an unrecorded setting is the pattern this whole
page documents.

The node carrying it is explicit about scope: this does **not** establish that
monitoring persisted. Fourteen years separate it from the 2025 material, and a
treatment relationship that begins with monitoring and decays into
script-moving is "an entirely ordinary trajectory" **[ATTESTED, dat:0033]**.
The honest form is temporal: monitored in 2011, logistics-shaped by 2025, the
transition undated.

  brackets, not pinpoints.
- *"i was on the strips when i had a doctor in nyc"* — the prescriber in the

- *"yes still doing subs"* — present-tense continuity, July 2013

- *"if you're ever unloading or come in contact call me"* — the informal

### May 2026 — the withdrawal. A functional bearing, not a number.

Mid-May 2026: the standing supply line ([[wiki/people/tom|Tom]]) no-shows,
and Dan goes roughly 72+ hours into withdrawal while working a shift at BFS
Foods **[ATTESTED, wiki/work/bfs-foods]**. Across four days he is burned by
three independent suppliers while trying to stay ahead of the Suboxone gap —
"three burns in four days." The same night carries a $35 personal-cash
Suboxone handoff to a customer, worked from inside the withdrawal.

**This is the only point in the sixteen-year interval where the corpus
observes the dosing behavior functionally rather than through self-report.**
The logic: a 72-hour withdrawal cascade following a single supplier's no-show
is evidence that daily dosing was still live in May 2026 — an intermittent or
lapsed regimen would not produce that failure mode [INFERRED: the inference
runs on the failure's shape, not on clinical mechanism; seams stated]. It is
not a dose figure. It is a frequency proof — the run was on, at daily
cadence, sixteen years and three months in. That is all it proves, and it is
worth exactly that: the interval's far edge has a live behavioral bearing,
just as the near edge has a live appointment bearing, and the middle has the
single figure.

It also establishes, in passing, what the inventory's other bearings cannot:
the run's redundancy was a person. The 2011 appointment shows institutional
contact; the 2025 messages show script-moving; the May 2026 cascade shows the
failover was neither. The dose held because people held it, until a person
didn't.

## What the single point can support

The 2mg figure is thin, but it is not nothing. State exactly what it
establishes, with the confidence each piece earns:

**1. By July 2013 — 3.4 years into the run — the dose was already low and
already stable in the speaker's own presentation.** *"down to 2mg/day"* reads
as arrived-at, not passing-through. Whether it stayed there is a separate
question the record cannot answer, but the point fixes a floor-level
maintenance dose at year three. [ATTESTED as self-report; INFERRED as
"arrived-at" from the sentence's completion grammar.]

**2. The dose in 2013 was at or near what the speaker presented as a minimum
viable level.** "Too terrified to get off" is not the language of someone
planning the next taper step; it is the language of someone who has reached a
level he does not intend to leave. The fear names the exit cost, and it names
it at 2mg. That reads as a resting point, not a waypoint. [INFERRED; seams
stated. It is a reading of tone, and tone is the weakest evidence in this
inventory.]

**3. The figure corroborates the "almost 5 years since i touched an opiate"
duration in the same thread.** The two claims are co-located in one exchange
and mutually consistent as self-report — a man three years into maintenance
reporting a low dose and several years opiate-free is a coherent bundle. What
it does not do is independently verify either claim. Two self-reports in one
message corroborate each other's *coherence*, not their truth. [ATTESTED that
both appear; INFERRED as to coherence-only.]

**4. The point anchors the earliest possible claim about dose stability: the
regimen was not escalating by 2013.** Whatever the induction dose was — and
the record never says — by year three it had come *down to* 2mg. Escalation
between 2010 and 2013 is inconsistent with the sentence's grammar. That is the
one directional claim the point supports about the unknown 2010–2013 segment:
the slope, if any, ran downward. [INFERRED from "down to"; the induction value
itself remains UNKNOWN.]

**5. The point fixes the formulation era: strips in the NYC window, past
tense by July 2013.** *"i was on the strips when i had a doctor in nyc"* —
the 2013 dose rides a formulation history the record otherwise never names.
This is the corpus's only formulation reference for his own dosing. (The
corpus's other mg figures — 8mg/$15, 12mg/$20 strips — are supply-graph
pricing on [[wiki/people/johnny-dealer]]'s page, 2018-era, and are not his
dose. They are included here so the next reader does not mistake them for
one.) [ATTESTED for both.]

Five supports. Note their character: every one is about the point's own
window (2010–2013) or the point's own grammar. None of them reaches forward.

## What the single point cannot support

**1. No interpolation to 2010–2013.** "Down to" implies a prior higher dose
but names nothing — not the induction amount, not the number of steps, not
the taper's duration, not whether it was clinician-directed. The 2010–2013
segment is a downward slope with one known endpoint and no known start.

**2. No extrapolation to 2013–2026.** Thirteen years, zero dose mentions.
The temptation is to read the silence as "it stayed at 2mg" — and the
temptation should be refused. A maintenance dose held for thirteen years and
a maintenance dose adjusted thirteen times with no record look identical in
this corpus. The silence is not evidence of stability; it is evidence of
non-observation. [INFERRED as the correct epistemic handling; the distinction
is the page's load-bearing methodological point.]

**3. No current dose.** The most recent anything is May 2026's functional
proof that *a* daily dose was still being taken. The amount is [UNKNOWN] and
has been since the night of 2013-07-10.

**4. No taper claim.** There is no taper plan, no taper discussion, and no
taper anywhere in the sixteen-year record — and the 2013 point does not
supply one retroactively. "Down to 2mg" describes an arrival, not a program.
[ATTESTED by absence.]

**5. No dose–outcome linkage.** The corpus cannot connect any amount to any
effect — not to the withdrawal severity in May 2026 (no dose recorded that
week), not to the hedonic-tension question on [[wiki/health/hyperreflexivity]]
(dat:0020), not to anything. A dose curve would let you ask whether outcomes
moved with the amount. This record cannot ask that question.

## The "almost five years" arithmetic — a preserved tension

The point carries a duration claim that does not line up, and the page keeps
the mismatch rather than sanding it:

- *"it's been almost 5 years since i touched an opiate"*, written 2013-07-10.
- Taken literally, "almost 5 years" back-dates the last opiate use to roughly
  **late 2008 / early 2009**.
- The dated record holds a named, specific episode *after* that window: the
  Thanksgiving 2009 [[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis]] episode — thirty
  Roxicet pills, a Winter Park–to–Ocala supply run, a Christmas trip home —
  resolving seven weeks before day-zero **[ATTESTED,
  wiki/health/chemical-architecture]**.

Three readings, none established:

1. **Loose rounding in casual text.** "Almost 5 years" for 3.4 years is a
   stretch, but confessional overnight text is not audited arithmetic.
2. **He is not counting the maintenance start.** If "touched an opiate" means
   *unprescribed* opiate use, the count might run from the last pre-day-zero

3. **Compression.** The dark-era window (2007–2008) is where the dependence

## Why the silence exists

The sixteen-year silence is not one fact. It is four overlapping conditions,
each independently sufficient to produce part of it:

**1. No clinical channel exists in the corpus.** There are no prescriber
notes, no pharmacy records, no appointment summaries, no lab values — for the
Suboxone row or any other. The corpus is messages, tweets, Facebook threads,
and exports of the same. A dose lives in clinical records; the corpus holds
none, so the dose is structurally invisible except when the subject volunteers
it. He volunteered it once, to one person, in 2013. [ATTESTED: the absence is
across all held sources.]

**2. The prescriber relationship, as recorded, is logistics-only.** The
2026-08-19 correction on [[wiki/health/the-configured-body]] established that
a prescribing doctor exists and that every recorded exchange about that doctor
concerns moving a script between pharmacies — never a dose, a taper, or a
review **[ATTESTED, dat:0025, moderate confidence]**. A doctor whose recorded
role is routing scripts is a doctor who generates no dose content for the
record. The logistics-only characterization is itself partly unverified
(dat:0028), but the verified portion is enough for the structural point: even
the quotes that survive carry no amount.

**3. A daily constant does not get narrated.** This is the reporting-bias
half. Messages document *changes* — appointments, shortages, moves, scares.
A dose taken identically every morning for years is the least narratable fact
in a life: there is no news in it. The corpus is dense on the two things that
vary (the supply topology, which breaks; the surrounding life, which churns)
and silent on the one thing that doesn't. The silence is therefore not
evidence of inattention — it is what a stable daily act looks like in a
message corpus. Steady states are boring, and boring does not get typed.
[INFERRED as a reporting-bias mechanism; seams stated. It explains the
silence without justifying any inference *from* the silence.]

**4. The corpus's channels do not cover the interval evenly.** The Facebook
archive — the channel that caught both the 2011 appointment and the 2013
figure — has fourteen `suboxone` occurrences total, all with one
counterparty, and the archive's coverage ends in the 2022–2023 window
**[ATTESTED, dat:0055; dat:0028 notes the superseded dump missing 2022 and
2026]**. The authoritative iMessage corpus holds almost nothing for 2011 and
has holes (June 2025: 35 messages all month; zero on the two quoted days).
Twitter never names a dose. Each channel covers some years and misses others,
and the one channel that ever named the dose stops covering the period just
when the logistics questions get interesting. The silence has a coverage
component, not just a behavioral one. [ATTESTED.]

There is a fifth condition worth stating because it disciplines the reading
of all four: **the nicotine record is the control case for what a public
channel can hide.** The eighteen-year tweet sequence on
[[wiki/health/chemical-architecture]] looks continuous, but the 2011 counter
restart shows a relapse visible only in the arithmetic — "the relapse is
nowhere in the archive: it is visible only because a counter restarted."
Applied here: the Suboxone record has no counter, no public feed, no
arithmetic to do. If the dose moved in 2015, 2019, 2023 — adjusted, doubled
for a week, cut for a month — there is no mechanism in the record that would
show it. The nicotine case proves that continuity in the archive is
compatible with unrecorded discontinuity in the behavior. The Suboxone row
has less evidence of continuity than the nicotine row and is cited with more
confidence. That asymmetry is worth sitting with. [INFERRED as a
methodological caution, built on the ATTESTED nicotine finding.]

**The silence is evidence of non-observation, full stop.** It does not
support "the dose stayed at 2mg" (non-observation is not stability),
"the dose changed" (non-observation is not change), or "the dose was
reviewed" (non-observation is not review). Every positive claim about the
2013–2026 dose trajectory is unsupported — including the stability claim,
which is the one most often smuggled in. [INFERRED; methodological.]

**The 2011 appointment is evidence that a dose was being set clinically in
2011, by someone, at some amount.** It does not support any claim about
what the amount was, whether the setting persisted, or when it stopped.
[ATTESTED for the appointment; INFERRED for "being set" — an appointment

## Falsifiability: what would change this page

A page built on a silence should state exactly what would fill it. In order
of evidentiary value:

1. **A second dosage figure, any date, any channel.** One more dated point
   turns the object from a datum into a segment and immediately raises the
   questions this page cannot currently ask (direction of change, rate,
   stability). The most likely source: the Facebook archive's later threads
   (coverage ends ~2022–2023), or a re-pull that closes the June 2025 hole.
2. **The 2025 quotes verified or refuted.** dat:0028 leaves three quotes in
   `never_observed` status. A corpus re-pull covering June 2025 would promote
   or retire them, which would settle whether the logistics-only
   characterization rests on corpus data or shelved-extract data.
3. **Any prescriber-side record.** The page assumes none exists in the
   corpus; the assumption is ATTESTED by absence across all held sources,
   but the corpus is message-shaped and a clinical record would not be in
   it by construction. Absence here is weak.
4. **A taper or adjustment mention anywhere.** Would immediately falsify the
   "no taper discussion in sixteen years" absence claim — which is precisely
   why the absence claim is stated as falsifiable rather than as a finding.
5. **Formulation history.** The strips reference (NYC era) is the only one.
   Anything on what came after — tablets, films, generics — would give the
   point a material context it currently lacks.

What would *not* change the page: another logistics quote without an amount
(adds a bearing, not a point), another "daily" self-description (the cadence
is not in dispute), or another attitude statement about staying on (attitude
is not dose).
