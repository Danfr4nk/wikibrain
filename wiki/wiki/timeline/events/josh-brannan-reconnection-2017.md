# Josh Brannan Reconnection (February 27, 2017)

> **Incidental entry** — A single warm Facebook reconnection thread; complete at this size because one thread and one later verified artefact are the whole record.

[[wiki/people/josh-brannan|Josh Brannan]] — Uniontown-era friend in Dan's only self-enumerated core friend group (February 23, 2012) — reconnected with Dan in a Facebook exchange on **February 27, 2017**, the sole warm reconnection in the record (kb/events/2017-josh-brannan-reconnection.md). The full friendship record — the 2009–2012 tweets, the cohort list, and the running joke — is on [[wiki/people/josh-brannan|his person page]]. This page is the event itself and what can actually be verified about it.

The exchange is notable in the source account for what Josh said about his own life — *"My job is fucking my girlfriend on webcam... Slash golf caddy"* — alongside grandmother and ski-trip nostalgia (kb/events/2017-josh-brannan-reconnection.md). Dan's side of the thread, per his person page, opened with *"I miss the fuck out of u. Call me when u get the chance sometime"* and carried his own unprompted account of the camming work and caddying.

## What happened

After years of distance since the 2009–2012 tweet record, Dan initiated contact and the two held a single rapid, joking catch-up thread — 41 messages per the person page — covering mutual friends, Josh's grandmother's stroke, and ski trips neither had taken in years (wiki/people/josh-brannan.md; kb/events/2017-josh-brannan-reconnection.md).

## The record in order

- **2009–2012** — ten tweets: the tweet record of the friendship. The tweet corpus is not held (dat:0678).
- **February 23, 2012** — Dan's cohort list (*"People who need to come visit me"*), the only self-enumeration of his core friend group in the corpus; November 6, 2012 — the Latrobe message. Both per person-page testimony (kb/events/2017-josh-brannan-reconnection.md).
- **February 27, 2017** — the Facebook reconnection thread (41 messages, per the person page). The thread itself is unheld: raw/facebook-threads/ carries only MANIFEST.json, so the camming-job admission and the nostalgia survive as page testimony only (dat:0678).
- **May 30, 2025, 04:09:17 UTC** — Dan sends *"josh brannan is innocent.wav"* (audio/x-wav, 162,608 bytes), twelve minutes after praising Google AI Studio's text-to-speech tool in the same thread. The wav row is corpus-verified (dat:0678).

## Conflicts in the record

There is no contradiction to carry here; there is a verification asymmetry, stated plainly. The 2017 reconnection — the actual event — rests entirely on an unheld Facebook export (dat:0678). Nothing in the held corpus contradicts it, and the one checkable piece, the 2025 wav, is consistent with the friendship surviving warm and confirms the person page's account of the joke's mechanics exactly (dat:0678).

Two attributions stay at testimony grade: the claim that the wav was traded with [[wiki/people/jerad-friedline|Jerad Friedline]] rests on operating_manual.md, not held attribution — the wav's thread has an empty display name in the corpus pull (dat:0678). The thread-volume figure (40,500 held vs. 40,514 claimed for 2018) corroborates scale only (kb/events/2017-josh-brannan-reconnection.md).
