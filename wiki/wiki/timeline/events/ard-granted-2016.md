# ARD Granted (February 17, 2016)

> **Incidental entry** — The single hearing that diverted the 2015 possession charges; complete at this size because the arrest case itself lives on its own page.

On **February 17, 2016**, a hearing before Judge Wagner approved Accelerated Rehabilitative Disposition (ARD) — Pennsylvania's first-offender diversion track — for the February 2015 possession charges, and the remaining fines were paid (kb/events/2016-02-ard-granted.md). ARD is why Dan's only real arrest left no criminal record. The arrest, the charges, and the full case timeline are at [[wiki/legal/2015-possession-arrest|The 2015 Possession Arrest]]; this page is the hearing itself.

The approval was not routine on the record as held. The Fayette County District Attorney of the period is said to have held a categorical policy refusing ARD to anyone charged with a cocaine or heroin misdemeanour, residue or not, which made Dan ineligible on the face of it; admission was nevertheless obtained through private counsel, Fayette County lawyer Jack Connor, at a cost Dan put at several thousand dollars (dat:0015).

## What happened

Dan had been charged in February 2015 with possession of a controlled substance (residue in an empty cocaine bag), possession of a small amount of marijuana, and possession of drug paraphernalia. After a December 2015 ARD application and a January 2016 status check, the case reached its hearing on February 17, 2016, where ARD was approved (wiki/legal/2015-possession-arrest.md).

## The record in order

- **February 17–19, 2015** — three possession charges filed before Magisterial District Judge Michael Metros (kb/events/2016-02-ard-granted.md, citing the arrest case timeline).
- **January–February 2016** — fines referenced in messages; whether they belong to this case, the separate Combos retail-theft matter, or both running concurrently is not determinable from the message record (kb/events/2016-02-ard-granted.md).
- **February 17, 2016** — hearing before Judge Wagner: ARD approved; remaining fines paid. The same day's *"My combos are paid off"* records a fine being cleared, though the phrasing suggests the money may have been the theft fine (kb/events/2016-02-ard-granted.md).
- **Morning of the hearing** — [[wiki/people/rick-frank|Rick Frank]] talked himself out of attending: *"You're obviously more comfortable with your mom"* (kb/events/2016-02-ard-granted.md).

The contemporaneous message record corroborates that something unusual happened without naming the bar: *"Jack had to do some wheeling and dealing"* (dat:0015).

## Conflicts in the record

- **The policy bar is single-source testimony.** The prior wiki names no source for the DA policy and does not name the DA, listing that identity under its own gaps. The claim is therefore carried at moderate confidence: trustworthy as testimony, weak as evidence (dat:0015). The "wheeling and dealing" message corroborates unusual effort, not the policy itself.
- **Three weeks of misattribution (corrected August 3, 2026).** From July 13 to August 3, 2026 the wiki carried the lawyer, the ARD application, and the Judge Wagner hearing under the Combos retail-theft page. Both matters are real and weeks apart; the machinery belongs to the possession case (dat:0016).
- **The February 17 triple (open).** February 17 recurs three times — Suboxone day-zero (2010), the blotter window opening (2015), and this hearing (2016). Whether that is docket-scheduling coincidence or an artefact propagated through earlier passes is unresolved (dat:0071, as cited in kb/events/2016-02-ard-granted.md).

Open questions carried from the source: the docket number (district MDJ-14-1-01 is known, the number is not); whether expungement was completed (unknown anywhere on disk); and the DA's identity behind the policy (kb/events/2016-02-ard-granted.md).
