# Annie Closure, June 1, 2026 (Group-Chat Night)

> **Incidental entry** — The kb-sourced record of the June 1, 2026 closure night; complete at this size because the full narrative already lives on the group-chat-closure page.

On **June 1, 2026**, a three-party group chat closed the eleven-year Dan–[[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie Ulmer]] relationship — for fifty-two days (kb/events/2026-06-01-annie-closure.md). The night's held-corpus volume was 666 messages across May 31–June 1 (601 Dan / 65 Annie) (dat:0125). The full narrative — the third-party confirmation, the defamation-campaign detail, and the data record — is at [[wiki/timeline/events/group-chat-closure|Group Chat Closure (June 1 2026)]]. This page is the compact event record: the closing run, its verified authorship, and what happened next.

The closure run itself, rows 223934–223939 in the held corpus, is an unbroken Dan outbound sequence from 04:21:47 to 04:27:49 UTC (00:21:47–00:27:49 EDT), closing with *"Goodbye forever. This was not how it should have ended but. sic semper lupanis"* (dat:1343). Every row in the window is sender "Me" (Dan); Annie's number says nothing after 04:11:29 UTC ("Understood") (dat:1343).

## What happened

The earlier closure-night exchange (rows 223817–223933) was a three-party group chat including Dan, the Annie handle, and Jerel Coles's handle (dat:1343). Dan then closed alone, in six minutes, while Annie was silent. Annie's only recorded contribution afterwards is the June 5 apology (dat:1342).

## The record in order

- **May 31 – June 1, 2026** — 666 held messages (601 Dan / 65 Annie) (dat:0125).
- **June 1, 00:08:19 EDT** — *"too bad so sad you just couldn't give me any decency and now there's a poison pill baked in"* — Dan, verbatim (kb/events/2026-06-01-annie-closure.md).
- **June 1, 00:22:46 EDT** — the "unring this bell" message — Dan (kb/events/2026-06-01-annie-closure.md; dat:0125).
- **June 1, 00:27:49 EDT** — the *"sic semper lupanis"* sign-off — Dan (dat:1343).
- **June 5, 2026** — Annie's apology, her only closure contribution (dat:1342).
- **June 15, 2026** — Coles's defection pivot in the epilogue thread (*"You can have her back ? … She's a compulsive liar with a drug addiction"*), corpus-attested (dat:0125).
- **July 23, 2026, 18:54 UTC** — recontact opens (*"I should not have responded to that email"*); the fifty-two-day severance ends (dat:0358; dat:0449). The recontact itself is at [[wiki/timeline/events/july-2026-recontact|July 2026 Recontact]].

## Conflicts in the record

- **The sign-off was Dan's, not Annie's (corrected).** The exit line was long attributed to Annie. Row-by-row reading of the held corpus confirms Dan as sender, in a run where Annie had already gone silent; the main Annie page corrected this on August 13, 2026, while the end-fight event page still carried the stale version — a propagation failure (dat:1343; dat:0125). Any reading of the night's agency that treats the line as Annie's is reasoning from a superseded attribution.
- **"Closure" overstates the outcome (revised July 26, 2026).** June 1 severed contact for fifty-two days — by a wide margin the most durable severance in the relationship — and then failed. The closure page's own revision stands: *"Everything described on this page about what happened that night stands; what does not stand is treating it as an ending"* (dat:0449).
- **Superseded counts (do not quote alone).** The 127-exit / 110-re-engagement framing is the stale pre-resolution pair; the August 23, 2026 resolution moved the figures to 129 episodes with 100% resumption. Neither pair may be quoted without the other (dat:0622, as cited in kb/events/2026-06-01-annie-closure.md). The July 23–26 recontact window also counts 624 messages on the page vs. 430 in the held corpus — an open discrepancy, not a refutation (dat:0358).

The June 5 apology's content and provenance are filed only as "Annie's only closure contribution"; a propagation pass over all event pages quoting the June 1 closure to correct the exit-line attribution remains open (kb/events/2026-06-01-annie-closure.md).
