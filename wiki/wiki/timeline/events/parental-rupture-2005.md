# Parental Rupture (November 2005)

> **Incidental entry** — The kb-sourced factual record of the November 2005 rupture; complete at this size because the full event entry already exists and this page's job is the compact record, not a second audit.

In **November 2005**, when Dan was seventeen, his father [[wiki/people/rick-frank|Rick Frank]] disclosed his cocaine addiction and entered rehabilitation, and his mother [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suzanne Frank]] was revealed to have been having an affair with the family's own cocaine dealer during his treatment. The marriage ended (kb/events/2005-11-parental-rupture.md). Dan names this event the hinge of his life — the point the rest of the psychological record organises itself around.

This page substantially duplicates the existing full entry at [[wiki/timeline/events/november-2005-rupture|The November 2005 Rupture]], which carries the family frame, the narrator map, and the limits at full strength. What follows is the kb-sourced factual record only: what the kb event asserts, what supports it, and what it cannot support. The audit of the axioms built on the rupture is at [[wiki/mind/synthesis/november-2005-hinge|the November 2005 hinge]] and is not repeated here.

## What happened

Rick entered cocaine rehab; during his treatment, Suz's affair with the man who supplied both parents came to light; the twenty-year marriage ended. Cocaine is the thread connecting both halves — the father's addiction and the mother's affair with its supplier — to a single source (kb/events/2005-11-parental-rupture.md). No day-level date exists anywhere on record; the month is consistent across every retelling.

## The record in order

- **2001, age 13** — cocaine first enters Dan's awareness as a known household concept through discovery of his parents' use (dat:1393, as cited in kb/events/2005-11-parental-rupture.md).
- **2004–2006** — Dan's first cocaine use at 17–18, in the Seven Springs ski-crew context; a later self-audit frames it partly as instrumental weight control tied to adolescent bulimia. The window overlaps the rupture rather than following it (kb/events/2005-11-parental-rupture.md; dat:1393).
- **November 2005** — Rick enters cocaine rehab; Suz's affair with the family dealer surfaces; the marriage ends. Retrospective testimony, consistent across the canonical record (kb/events/2005-11-parental-rupture.md; dat:1027).
- **2007–2008** — opiate onset (Roxicet/oxycodone), five months to addiction; the "dark era" that traces back to this event in Dan's telling. The period record is at [[wiki/timeline/periods/dark-era-2007-2008|the dark era]].

## Conflicts in the record

- **No contemporaneous record exists.** The held message corpus opens years later; the wiki's own gap list states that Suz's account of the 2005 rupture is "still entirely Dan-side" and that "the contemporaneous record begins in 2015, ten years after" (kb/events/2005-11-parental-rupture.md). Rick and Suz have no on-record account anywhere in the corpus, and the dealer's identity is not named in any held source.
- **The dating was checked for consistency, not re-derived.** dat:1027 is explicit that the November 2005 dating was checked against MEMORY.md for consistency with the canonical record, not re-derived from a dated primary record (dat:1027).
- **The origin story does not survive as a clean causal line.** Cocaine was a known household behaviour from 2001, four years before the rupture, and Dan's first use dates to 2004–2006, overlapping the rupture. A "the divorce caused the drug use" reading does not survive the timeline: the substance was already inside the house and inside him (kb/events/2005-11-parental-rupture.md; dat:1393).
- **Dossier synthesis is labelled as such.** The axiom reading — "intimacy is a precursor to abandonment, loyalty is conditional, emotional security is an illusion" — is Developmental Origins dossier synthesis, filed as page-attributed synthesis, not as Dan's own causal claim (dat:1027). It is described here, not carried as load-bearing.

Open questions carried from the source: the exact day in November 2005; the order of disclosure; when the divorce was finalised; Suz's version; the dealer's identity; and any rehab facility or records (kb/events/2005-11-parental-rupture.md).
