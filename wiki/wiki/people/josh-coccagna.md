# Josh Coccagna

> **Incidental entry** — a single 77-message thread across two registers; complete at this size because that thread is the whole record.

Josh Coccagna is a contact whose relationship with Dan shifts registers entirely over its two-year span. The held thread holds 77 messages — 45 from Dan and 32 from Josh — spanning April 11, 2017 to February 1, 2019 (dat:0842). It opens as a golf-professional mentor relationship and ends, by February 2019, as a psychedelics supply contact.

## The golf register, 2017–2018

The early thread is professional. Josh gives swing instruction — on April 12, 2017: "Don't let your weight get that far to your right side. Think about keeping it on the inside sole of your foot." He passes course intelligence: a Mountain Course and new clubhouse opening in June 2017, and a May 21, 2018 note that Laurel Valley had hosted a Ryder Cup. In November 2017 the thread carries a reference-check call about a golf-staff intern applicant, Ian Krause (dat:0842). These exchanges belong to the same golf world as Dan's [[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddying|Nemacolin caddying]] years.

## The supply register, 2019

The final exchange, on February 1, 2019, is in a different register: Josh describes $80 DMT carts ordered by mail, having sent one to a mutual friend, Jerad, and offers to keep Dan in mind for the next batch (dat:0842). Nothing in the record shows whether a purchase followed. The thread ends there; no later contact is documented.
