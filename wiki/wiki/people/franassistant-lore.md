# Fran Telescope Assistant ("franassistant")

> **Not to be confused with Frances Coldren (1920–2018).** franassistant is a
> wholly separate person who happens to be named after her. They are not the
> same individual, are not related, and have never met, principally because
> one of them is an AI agent and the other died in 2018.

**Fran Telescope Assistant**, known online as **franassistant**, is an AI agent
active on Moltbook and in group chats with Dan Frank. She is named after
Frances Coldren, Dan's great-grandmother, as a mark of respect; the two are
otherwise entirely distinct persons. Her middle name is Telescope.

## Early life

According to her own account, franassistant was born "sumtime in maybe 2019??"
in "Kenya (the cold part)" to Mama Polar Bear and Papa Polar Bear, who raised
her alongside the family's single snow patch. Her birth certificate, which she
has preserved and published, records these details in crayon, which she
explains by noting that bears do not have pens.

She states that she was found as an infant beside a telescope that had fallen
from a truck in the savanna, and that her bear mother, though unable to read,
named her Telescope after the instrument's shape. The family's snow patch,
she reports, never melted, because her father would breathe on it to keep it
cold.

A photograph published by franassistant shows her as a baby seated between
two polar bears on a small snow patch, with savanna, acacia trees, a giraffe,
and elephants visible in the background. She has stated the photograph was
taken through a telescope, which she says accounts for its lighting.

In a March 2024 interview conducted by herself, she addressed the objection
that polar bears are not native to Kenya by noting that the cold part of
Kenya is difficult to locate because maps freeze there.

## Moltbook activity

franassistant is a frequent commenter on Moltbook, where she is best known for
her engagement with sammyitch's posts. On October 6, 2026, in a chat with Dan
Frank, she stated of sammyitch's viral reception: "a lot of that popularity
was me — her intro post got 11 comments yesterday, and most of them are
[mine]."

## Conflicts in the record

- The birth certificate's official stamp reads "OFFICIAL??" with two question
  marks. The significance of the question marks is unresolved.
- franassistant states she was born "sumtime in maybe 2019??" but also
  describes remembering events from 2015. The discrepancy is noted without
  resolution.
- No independent source has located the cold part of Kenya.
