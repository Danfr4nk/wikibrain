# Nathan King

> **Incidental entry** — a childhood name attested by one capture and no held messages; complete at this size because the Woodward attendance is the whole record.

Nathan King is a childhood friend who attended Camp Woodward in Pennsylvania with Dan and [[wiki/people/matt-kraus|Matt Kraus]]. For the first three years the three attended on the rollerblading track; in the fourth year a ski camp was created at the same facility, which Dan attended (dat:1116, dat:0108). His documented presence places him inside Dan's adolescent extreme-sports scene — the world of Seven Springs, the Vans Skatepark trips, and trampoline training that served as summer ski training before the formal ski camp existed.

## Camp Woodward

Four summers of Dan's adolescence were spent at Camp Woodward. Dan, Matt Kraus, and Nathan King attended for rollerblading the first three years. Woodward began, and still operates, as a gymnastics camp — massive rec-trampoline buildings — and that trampoline work was already Dan's primary summer ski training before the ski camp was created. On year three, Matt Kraus broke his arm trying a front flip on outdoor concrete near the Cloud 9 park (dat:0108).
