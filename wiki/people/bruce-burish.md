# Bruce Burish

Bruce Burish is a Laurel Highlands high-school friend — one of the few
people Dan told, in real time, that he and Annie were starting a webcam
show — and, a decade earlier, the person who got Dan busted for the biggest
party he ever threw. That is the whole shape of him in the record: one
five-day, 348-message burst of explicit talk across iMessage and Facebook
in October 2018, and then six years of sparse bro-maintenance pings that
Dan almost never answered, ending with a 2024 Instagram flashback in which
Bruce confirmed, in his own words, the party bust Dan had been blaming on
him for twenty years.

What makes the thread worth more than its gossip value is its timing and
its audience. October 2018 is the launch window of the camming business —
Dan and Annie are just starting the webcam show — and Bruce has no stake in
it. He is not a partner, not a collaborator, not competition: he is a
hometown friend who reappeared out of nowhere ("Yo man! It's Bruce"), and
Dan spent two days teaching him the mechanics of the operation anyway —
pricing, tipping, tip-activated vibrators, solo-show strategy, the rule
about never revealing the current account name — while flirting, in the
same breaths, with the idea of having Bruce on cam with them in person
when he came home in mid-November. Then, the same week, Dan turned around
and spent an afternoon texting a different friend that Bruce was a
"shitdick loser bucket head rube" who had mistaken a sales pitch for a
come-on. Both registers are in the corpus. Neither is the "real" feeling;
both are the record of how Dan performed the friendship per audience.

## Identity and origin

Bruce Burish is a Laurel Highlands friend from high school — not a
caddying-era friend, as this page previously claimed (see Conflicts in the
record). He is a Fayette County contact in the 724 area code, confirmed via
Google Contacts — and corroborated independently by a "Bruce Burish" entry in
Instagram's synced-contacts export carrying the same 724 number as the
iMessage handle. The high-school origin is Dan's own correction, given
2026-09-24: "a friend from high school and not involved in caddying." It is
corroborated from inside the corpus by Dan's own line to Bruce in October
2018: *"fuck everyone we graduated with."*

The two were the same age cohort: turning 30 in the same days — Bruce's
birthday had just passed when the thread opened (Dan: "nov 1"; Bruce: "I
just had mine / Nice! Close!"). By 2018 Bruce had moved east. In the
Facebook thread he says *"I'm in Sag Harbor"* (October 2018) and, in
January 2021, *"What's good my friend! Let's meet up! I'm in the
Hamptons."* His Snapchat handle was `bburish9`; he reactivated it during
the October 2018 conversation specifically to receive Dan's previews
("I added you on snap"). In 2018 Dan gave him his number with the line
*"7242083475 give me a shout"* — the string the infobox has always carried
verbatim.

The friendship's only recorded real-world consequence predates the corpus
by roughly a decade. Everything else is talk.

## The Sunridge K2 party

The family condo was Unit K2 in The Villages at Sunridge, Seven Springs —
a ski-in, ski-out unit about 45 minutes from Uniontown (see
[[wiki/places/seven-springs]]). At some point in the high-school years, Dan
threw a party there: roughly 30 people from Laurel Highlands drinking. It
was, per Dan's words, *"the only party where my Uniontown friends and my
7springs friends collided"* — and it was the only party he ever threw at
the condo. One was enough.

Dan told the story himself on September 24, 2026, in his own words:

*"He also was the one who got me busted for my biggest party and the only
party where my Uniontown friends and my 7sprinys friends collided, the only
party I threw at our condo in the villages at 7 springs (sunridge k2)…
7sprinfs is 45 mins away from Uniontown and I had about 30 people from
laurel highlands at my condo drinking. Bruce had his mom arranged to come
pick him up. He was sloshed and if that wasn't obvious at first, im sure
45 mins in the car with him would have made that fact all too apparent
lmao… He was giving a ride home to Ryan Basescu whose parents are friends
with Rick."*

The cascade, link by link:

1. Bruce gets himself busted — picked up drunk by his mom, forty-five
   minutes of drunken kid in the backseat, no hiding it.
2. The kid he was taking home gets busted too — Ryan Basescu, collateral
   of the same car ride.
3. Basescu's parents are friends with Rick — so the second busted kid's
   parents know Dan's father socially.
4. Basescu's parents tell Rick about the party → Dan busted.

The account is no longer single-source. On September 14, 2024, Dan had a
flashback and messaged Bruce about it on Instagram — *"I had a flashback
today / Do you remember / The 7springs party lol"* — and Bruce answered in
his own words: *"Jesus / When I got picked up / I remember it well / I got
in so much trouble."* Twenty years on, the pickup and the trouble are
confirmed by the guy who lived them. The Basescu family, for its part, is
still in orbit: on November 8, 2022, Suz told Dan that "Joni Basescu
asked about the two of you" — Suz's report, so hearsay, but the parental
friendship network outlasted the bust either way.

The party is the one thing Bruce ever did *to* Dan, and Dan has never let
the ledger go: it is the first thing he thinks of when asked to describe
the friendship, twenty years later, and the last thing he ever said to
Bruce.

## October 2018: five days, two channels

The October 2018 thread runs October 23 through October 27 across two
channels at once — iMessage on the +17249848911 handle (348 messages in the
complete dump: 167 from Dan, 181 from Bruce) and a parallel Facebook
Messenger conversation carrying the same subject matter in a different
register. The two run side by side: Bruce opens on iMessage with "Yo man!
It's Bruce" at 14:31 on the 23rd; the same day on Facebook he opens with
"How is everything bro?" and the conversation runs through Hamptons talk,
number exchange, dabs, birthdays, and then — late that night — the full
webcam explainer, mirrored across both channels.

### Where Dan was in October 2018

The thread is a time capsule of a very specific Dan. He is 29, turning 30
on November 1. He is living in Uniontown on an interim — *"Yeah I'm here
until like May"* on Facebook, *"I’m moving back to nyc in a few months"*
on the other channel, *"i can't wait to get back to nyc."* He does not
drink at all — *"Right on. I can justify getting stupid baked all day
because I don't drink at all 😎"* — and smokes dabs constantly, including,
per his words, all day at work (*"And I can smoke dabs all day at work"*).
He is getting a sleeve tattoo so he can, in his words, tell everyone "to
fuck off and that i'm not doing this normal buckethead 9 to 5 shit that
everyone seems to be doing." He has, per his own telling, enough family
money to support himself while he does "whatevz" — *"lol i mean i'm super
lucky that i have enough family money to support me while i do
whatevz"* — and he spends the money's freedom on contempt for the hometown
cohort: *"like our whole class is fat and old and fucking shitty… they
have no taste in art or culture… how many people in our class could
identify a warhol painting? a rolling stones song? a tarantino film?"*
All of this is Dan performing the successful-escapee for a high-school
friend who also escaped — Bruce to the Hamptons, Dan (aspirationally) back
to New York.

Bruce's opener is nostalgia: *"Well I miss Nemacolin"* — the line that
this page once misread as proof the friendship started in the caddying
era. It established nothing of the kind; it was two Fayette County kids
being nostalgic about the hill (see Conflicts in the record).

## The Facebook and Instagram afterlife (2019–2024)

The friendship's long tail is all Bruce. After October 2018, Dan never
starts a conversation with him again — on Facebook he replies to nothing
except a single name-only message (*"Bruce Burish,"* July 14, 2022, likely
a contact share); on Instagram his only replies are the October 2019
podcast note and the September 2024 flashback. Everything else is Bruce
keeping a channel open that Dan leaves unanswered.

The Facebook register is bro-warm maintenance: *"Hey kid," "Sup kiddo,"
"How's my dude," "Miss you brother," "Brother!"* — pings every few months
from 2020 through September 2022, plus a missed video chat in March 2021.
Two details carry weight. First, the camming stayed a throughline — Bruce
is the one outsider who knew about it from the launch conversation and
kept checking in: *"Have any only fans?"* (January 2021), *"Only Fans
yet?"* (February 2022), and in August 2022, *"Onlyfans? / You would crush
it bro / Just saying / Secret is safe with me bro"* — the last line an
echo, four years later, of *"Your secret is safe with me man."* Second,
the "Are you still in nyc?" check-ins (2021, 2024) track Bruce's stable
mental model of where Dan belongs: New York, the escape he was
performing back in 2018.

Instagram (`bruceburish_557593328970813`) runs September 2019 to September
2024 in the same register across 22 messages: *"Suppppp"* (2019), a
*"What is this?"* in October 2019 that Dan answers — *"it's a live
podcast"* — to Bruce's *"Nice,"* *"I miss you man!"* (2021), *"How's it
hanging brother! / Only Fans yet?"* (February 2022), the repeated
*"Are you still in NYC?"* (April 2022, July 2024), *"Brother!"* (November
2022), a *"Yo"* (September 3, 2024) — and then, eleven days later, Dan's
flashback, the last contact on record.

## The 2024 flashback and last contact

On September 14, 2024 — six years after the thread, twenty years after the
party — Dan messaged Bruce on Instagram out of nowhere: *"I had a flashback
today / Do you remember / The 7springs party lol."* Bruce's reply came
within minutes: *"Jesus / When I got picked up / I remember it well / I
got in so much trouble."*

That exchange does two things for the record. It corroborates the K2
cascade from the other end — the pickup, the trouble, both confirmed by
the person who lived them. And it is the shape of the whole friendship in
miniature: Dan reaching back across years to poke at the one shared memory
that matters, Bruce answering warmly, and then nothing. No further contact
is on record after September 14, 2024.

## Assessment

Bruce Burish's value to the wiki is not the friendship — there barely is
one, by the end. It is the thread. October 2018 is the launch window of
the camming business, and this is one of the very few places Dan explains
the operation to someone outside it, in full, while it is brand new: the
rationale (Annie's solo history, the blackmail framing, the couple's
cover), the mechanics (tipping, tip-activated vibrators, tip-level
stripping, the studio room, the three-shows-a-week cadence), the opsec
("rule number 1," the old-account previews, the current screen name never
given out), and the unit economics (solo shows at a hundredth of the
couple's take). Bruce got the unredacted pitch because he asked
enthusiastically and Dan was in evangelist mode — and because, per Dan's
own later telling, Dan believed Bruce was hitting on him and enjoyed
performing for the attention.

That is the second thing the page establishes: the audience-split
register, documented in the same week. To Bruce, Dan is the warm
collaborator — "Let me run it by the queen," "i think she'll want to do
it," patient answers to every question, previews sent, a November threesome
penciled in. To the third-party friend, Bruce is a "shitdick loser bucket
head rube" whose thirst is a comedy routine and whose "dab it up" is a
set piece. The wiki reads this at the attention level, not the interior
one: it is evidence of how Dan tailors the same material per listener, not
a ruling on which feeling was genuine. The performance is the data.

Third, the contact shape. The old page read Bruce as the pure
dense-cluster tie — five days, then nothing. The full record is richer: a
five-day burst *plus* a six-year maintenance tail in which Bruce keeps the
channel open almost entirely alone. Dan never meets him, never takes the
Hamptons meetup offers, never answers the "miss you brother" pings — but
he answers the 2024 flashback within the hour, because the K2 party is the
one thing they actually share. The tail is unreciprocated, but it is not
nothing: "Secret is safe with me bro" is a loyalty claim Bruce restated
for four years, and he is the only friend in the corpus who carried the
2018 disclosure forward as a standing identity for Dan — the cam guy, the
OnlyFans question asked three times across three years.

Fourth, the party. The K2 bust is the only documented instance of a
teenage friend causing Dan a real-world consequence, and the only record
of the party itself, which appears nowhere else in the corpus. Its
four-link cascade — sloshed kid, mom's car, Basescu's parents, Rick — is
also a small sociology of Uniontown: a drunk car ride becomes parental
intelligence becomes punishment, and the parental network is still asking
about Dan and Annie four years after the corpus ends. Bruce confirming it
in 2024, in four short messages, closes the loop on a story Dan had been
telling for twenty years.

What is not known: whether the November 2018 visit ever happened (no
evidence it did), what Bruce does for work, whether the threesome Dan
said Annie would "want to do" was ever seriously proposed to her, and who
the third-party debrief contact was. The friendship, such as it was, ends
where the record ends: September 14, 2024, two old high-school friends
laughing about getting in trouble.
