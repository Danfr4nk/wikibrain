> **Incidental entry** — Marianne Williamson as Dan covered her in May 2023: the progressive long shot he openly rooted for. Complete at this size.

Marianne Williamson — "Houston's most famous and controversial crystal mommy" — was the candidate Dan actually agreed with and gave zero chance: "Will she win? Uh, no. But I like her and I'm always happy to see the party move left."

Key facts:
- The platform: Medicare for All, climate action, student loan forgiveness, even slavery reparations — "a much better ideological match for progressives" than Biden.
- The bind: backing a long shot risked committee positions and a role in a Biden administration, so even Justice Democrats wouldn't touch her.
- Dan declined to scrutinize her — "healthy electoral competition is a good thing" — the one candidate in the piece he treated with open affection rather than forensics.
- Dropped out February 2024.

Why incidental: a brief, fond portrait inside a single post. Promote to major only if she — or Dan's relationship to long-shot progressivism — becomes a subject again.
