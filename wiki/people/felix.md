# Felix

> **Incidental entry** — A 463 Morgantown painter verified by a single verbatim message, with one open identification question; complete at this size.

Felix is a painter in the [[wiki/legal/463-morgantown|463 Morgantown]] contractor network. The page records his role as "Family Friend" and his employment under [[wiki/people/john-carney|John Carney]]; both claims rest on sources not held in this repo (kb/data/0445). What the held corpus verifies directly is narrower and firmer.

## The verified record

A [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]] iMessage on 2026-02-10 reads in full: "Great! It's mushy. Felix showed up but Arnu didn't. He's bringing Elijah over to do his homework until basketball practice and Felix is going to paint more. Do you want to come over?" (kb/data/0445, verified verbatim). That places Felix painting at 463 in February 2026, during the move-in period before the June 2026 sale of 337, alongside an [[wiki/people/arnu|Arnu]] no-show. Elijah is Felix's son (Dan, 2026-09-25).

## The open identification

A separate page, [[wiki/people/john-felix|John Felix]], covers a 156-row thread (2016-02-12 to 2018-01-14) under that name, with matching domestic details — "feeding Elijah," painting references (kb/data/0587). The name "Felix" recurs in Dan's orbit across a decade, but whether the 2016 thread's owner and the 2026 painter are the same man is not resolved in the held record, and the later page revision's specific 2018 quotes were not located in the corpus (kb/data/0587). This page does not merge them. A "supply-adjacent" note in the page's unheld sources is likewise uncorroborated by the held messages.
