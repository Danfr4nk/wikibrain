# Trinity St. Clair

Trinity St. Clair — the adult film performer of that name, per the contact card (booking email `booktrinitystclair@gmail.com` alongside a personal one) — was a paid companion Dan and Annie booked days after Dan's March 2019 move back to New York, in the same broad March–June 2019 window as the [[wiki/timeline/events/shelbie-annie-threesome-april-2019|Shelbie threesome]] where the couple's non-monogamous arrangement was running with women rather than the more commonly documented male friends. The direct thread with Trinity (`+18186510883`, 20 messages) shows two rounds of cancellation and a comedy-of-errors delay — the "tryst or lack thereof" the thread itself leaves genuinely ambiguous — but a separate, independently dated message to a friend confirms flatly that it happened: paid, completed, and framed as one of the first things Dan did on returning to the city.

The booking was supposed to be his entry into the non-monogamy ledger — Annie had been with several guys he arranged, his side "conspicuously empty." It didn't work out that way. He couldn't get hard, and spent the hour watching the two women together: "I watched her and Annie for an hour lol." The hour's afterlife outlasted the hour itself: for months afterward Trinity was the couple's reference point — the booking Dan cited to vet future companions, the performer he named when asked for a recommendation, the night he kept measuring against. And in Dan's 2026 telling, the night carries one more distinction: it was Annie's first time eating pussy, step two in what he frames as her five-encounter girl history.

## The booking: "the first thing I did"

The origin is dated precisely and given real context. On March 12, 2019, minutes into catching an old friend up on his life, Dan announces: "Dude you're gonna love this. I moved back to nyc. And the first thing I did was booked an hour with trinity st clair for an hour with my girlfriend and I. So on March 20 I get to fuck trinity st clair for $1500." The framing to his friend is part celebration, part financial justification — "you're gonna pay 1000 for any high class call girl here, so for an extra 500" — and part general largesse: in the same conversation he offers to fly the friend and his girlfriend out to visit, unprompted ("I will book you a flight if I have to... I'm rich now I can do that stuff"). The booking reads less as a considered relationship decision and more as the inaugural extravagance of a flush, freshly-returned New York chapter — the same energy that funded it shows up nowhere else as clearly as in this one conversation.

The identity of the woman was apparently not even fully settled four days later. On March 16, texting Annie, Dan jokes: "I don't know if we call her trinity or Brittany lol" — a casual wobble between the performer's stage name and whatever name she actually answered to, preserved only in this one line. It's a small detail, but it cuts against the idea that he booked her out of deep fan familiarity: he was buying the name, the billing, and by his own admission four days before the visit he wasn't sure what to call her to her face.

## The thread itself: sick, groggy, and twice rescheduled

The direct messages from Trinity, starting March 21, are almost entirely apologetic. She opens warmly ("Hey luv... it's trin") but within hours is postponing, citing a sore throat she jokes came from "the insane deep throat scene I just did" and promising to make it work "maybe this weekend." They land on Sunday the 24th at 7 PM. That afternoon she asks Dan to send an Uber; a message mid-afternoon — "Moving slowly ask ur girl if i should bring this" [image not preserved] — confirms Annie was directly involved in the planning, not a passive third party. Then, at 7:55 PM, the visit collapses a second time: "Omg.... i'm so sorry I totally just doze off... I feel groggy as fuck like I took half a Xanax LOL." The thread's last message in this cluster is a bare "Hey there" the next afternoon (March 25) — and then nothing. Read in isolation, the thread ends on a cliffhanger: booked, twice postponed, last contact a non-committal greeting with no confirmed follow-through.

## What actually happened

It happened — just not inside this thread. Two terse, undated-in-context solo texts bracket the likely date: "Trinity st clair," sent March 20 (the original scheduled date, before the reschedules) and again March 26 (the day after the thread goes quiet) — plausibly a brag or a note-to-self on each end of the actual event. The firm confirmation comes from a completely separate conversation: on April 8, 2019, catching up with a friend, Dan writes, unprompted, "ps i paid $1500 last week to have pornstar trinity st clair come hang out with my girlfriend and i for exactly one hour last week soooooooo things are shaping up pretty well here in nyc too lololol" — past tense, specific, and dating the actual visit to roughly March 28 – April 1, a few days after the direct thread's last "Hey there." The $1500 figure matches the March 12 price quote exactly. So the record shows real, well-documented friction (sickness, oversleeping, a canceled Sunday) resolving into a completed booking that simply isn't captured in the surviving Trinity-handle export — probably closed out by phone call or a message thread not preserved in this corpus.

## The hour itself: his account (2026-09-18)

Seven years later, asked to tell the story, Dan's account of the hour is disarmingly plain. The booking had been meant as his entry into the arrangement's ledger — Annie had been with several guys he arranged; his side was "conspicuously empty," and Trinity was supposed to change that. It didn't. He couldn't get hard, and spent the hour watching the two women together: "I watched her and Annie for an hour lol."

His own account of the mechanism is specific and long-standing: in a bad headspace, or with extreme excitement, erection becomes "literally IMPOSSIBLE" for him — and the more he tries, the less possible it becomes. The hour that was supposed to balance the books became an hour of watching instead. Told without embarrassment, as a joke on himself as much as a fact about the night.

Three and a half months later he turned the booking into a standup bit for Annie (July 20, 2019) — the air-mattress era ("before i had a fucking bed in my apartment... one embarrassing conversation about whether or not she'd be willing to fuck on an air mattress"), the fifth-floor walkup ("she showed up at my apartment and was only REASONABLY annoyed about the surprise cardio workout"), and the house rule he claimed to learn: "when a hooker asks to borrow your credit card to make a line of the cocaine you're giving her...make sure you give her one that is already expired." Read against the page's other claims, the sequence also explains something the March thread can't: why Dan kept coming back to this night as the reference point — it was, in his telling, the first time the arrangement produced something genuinely new for Annie, not just for him.

## The long afterlife: vouching, redos, and regret

Trinity's role in the record didn't end with the visit. On July 9, 2019 — three and a half months later, in the middle of an unrelated conversation about one of Annie's own paid client bookings — Dan mentions he had recently emailed a prospective companion quoting $1700, and that he'd used the March booking as a credibility anchor in that outreach: "in my email i said (if you happen to know trinity st clair she can vouch for us)." He follows up moments later with an observation that the two performers were personally connected — "i think the $1700 girl that i emailed is staying with trinity... they're together at the very least, she was in her snap story." Trinity had become, by mid-2019, less a one-off transaction than a reference point: proof of a successful, no-drama prior booking that Dan actively invoked to vet and reassure future ones.

By August 2019 the booking had become a reference point Dan used more than once. Beyond the [[wiki/people/jaredtricia|Jared/Tricia]] email citation already noted above, a separately recovered thread shows the same couple asking directly for a provider recommendation and Dan (again writing in Annie's "Hazel" voice — see [[wiki/people/jaredtricia]] for the full context of that impersonation) naming Trinity as the only provider he had "personal experience with," quoting a price of "1400" — a minor $100 discrepancy against the $1,500 documented above from two independent contemporaneous sources, plausibly just loose recall in a casual text rather than a real second rate.

The private afterlife — the Dan-to-Annie texts — is less polished than the outreach use. On May 15, 2019, seven weeks after the visit, Dan is still working through the etiquette of it with Annie: "it's not bad manner to do that. i think trinity had me do that before her time was up." The surrounding context is missing — what "do that" meant is not recoverable from the surviving side — but the shape is a post-mortem argument about manners, the couple still negotiating the rules of something that was already over. Nine days later, on May 24, he floats a redo and talks himself out of it in the same message: "i'd really like to fuck trinity after the debacle last time but if i'm spending that much there are other porn girls that i'm more into. i missed the ad until today but one of my fav girls uma jolie was in town for 2 days and had an ad up :(" The word "debacle" is doing heavy lifting — this is the first time in the record the night gets a negative label, from Dan himself — and the economics are the same ones he ran on March 12: the name-brand premium only makes sense if it's the name you actually want. By August 22 the tone is retrospective regret about logistics: "i literally hate myself for not thinking to do that before trinity came here" — "do that" again unclear, but the direction is clear: the night was imperfect in ways he kept revisiting for months.

The night's residue kept surfacing well past 2019. A late-night browser session — captured in a ChatGPT-export archive — shows a downloaded clip titled "trinity st. clair - jerkoffinstructions - jerk it for me step daddy.mp4" alongside dozens of other performers' content; whether this predates the booking (prior familiarity with her work that led to the choice) or postdates it (unrelated later viewing) is not established. The booking stayed lodged in his head as a fixed point: the night that was supposed to balance his ledger and instead became the story he told about himself.

Standing: carried as retrospective testimony at moderate confidence; the contemporaneous record establishes only that the hour was completed.

## Assessment

Trinity St. Clair is the arrangement's most explicitly commercial data point in the March–June 2019 window: a contracted third party at an agreed fee, where the usual courtship mechanics were replaced by scheduling, cancellation logistics, and a price. The 2026 psychosexual-study framing — "The Hour ($1,500, one hour, no subtext; the extra $500 over the high-class baseline bought the name)" — is consistent with Dan's own March 12 arithmetic, and it reads as the booking's cleanest epitaph: the premium was for the billing, not for anything that happened in the room.

What gives the page its staying power in the corpus is the inversion. The booking was commissioned as Dan's ledger entry — Annie's side of the non-monogamy books was full; his was "conspicuously empty" — and it became an hour of watching instead, which Dan then spent seven years converting into story capital: the vouch-for-us citation, the standup bit, the regret texts, the 2026 testimony. The night failed at its stated purpose and succeeded as material, and that failure-to-material conversion is the pattern the rest of the arrangement-history pages keep instantiating.

One note of honest scope: the act-level claims about the hour — both women going down on each other, Annie's first time eating pussy — rest entirely on one retrospective telling. The corpus establishes the booking, the price, the friction, and the completion; everything about what happened inside the hour beyond Dan's watching is his word, given seven years later, uncontested and uncorroborated.
