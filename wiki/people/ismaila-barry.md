# Ismaila Barry ("DJ")

> **Incidental entry** — Ismaila "DJ" Barry, Au Za'atar busser-turned-server and one of Dan's best friends: two held-record anchors plus storytime testimony. Complete at this size; flagged for major promotion (see report).

Ismaila Barry — universally "DJ" — is the Senegalese busser-turned-server of [[wiki/work/au-zaatar|Au Za'atar]] Midtown whom Dan counts among his best friends: "smart and curious and funny," his "first impression of the very unusual group of people from the country known as Senegal." Brought in by [[wiki/people/dimitri|Dimitri]], DJ made the same busser-to-server climb as Dan — his first white-shirt day is preserved in the photo record — but where Dan ran the 9 p.m. "night class" escape from closing duty, DJ did the setup and stayed to close, absorbing the wreckage the alibi left behind. He is the other constant of the Triad, Dan's co-conspirator in the basement culture (the "outside bread" interrogation video is the two of them), and his backer through the Sergio war. The storytime record also preserves a three-shot of Dan, DJ, and Dimitri together. This page resolves a long-standing context-core gap: the "Ismaila — named anchor, no detail" entry in the social graph is DJ (`ent:ismaila-barry`).

The friendship had one documented fault line: religion. DJ is a serious Muslim, and Dan — by his own storytime account — deliberately antagonised him over Islam, up to and including taunts about Aisha's age, framing himself as "doing my part to represent the western world of free speech." His own summary: "We always got past it though." The friction never broke the bond, and it is the corpus's clearest record of Dan running his anti-deference register against a friend's sincere faith rather than an institution. That texture — the climb, the night-class wreckage, the fault line — is storytime testimony from the dansynth sessions, carried here as attributed self-narration; the dansynth sources are not held in this repository, and the limits below separate what is held from what is told.

Two anchors are held-record, verified verbatim (`dat:0629`). On May 11, 2024 (01:56 and 01:57 UTC), Dan sent two r/Senegal Reddit links into a thread the earlier page had flagged only as a candidate for DJ. The held corpus's only other Senegal mention is Dan's own July 18, 2024 message ("Muslims from Bangladesh, senegal, Syria etc"), so the Reddit-link thread is in fact the only Senegal-flagged personal thread in the held record — the candidate is confirmed as strongly as the corpus allows. On August 21, 2024, at 9:55 p.m. EDT, thirteen days after his own Au Za'atar exit and thirteen days after the shed shutdown, Dan wrote to [[wiki/people/tarik-fallous|Tarik]]: "But I do know that DJ is not going to reclaim his job when he gets back from vacation so," followed ten minutes later by the crew's running joke in its exit-week form: "I know I'm not *official* CIA agent anymore but I did hear some intel today that might interest you… You didn't hear it from me" (`dat:0629`; `dat:0452`).

## Conflicts in the record

**The vacation-exit quotation (page paraphrase vs held message; corrected 2026-09-09).** This page previously paraphrased Dan's August 2024 tip as DJ doing "what MD did" and not "returning" from vacation. The held message says no such thing: its wording is the "reclaim his job" sentence quoted above (`dat:0629`). Current standing: corrected to the verbatim text; cite the held wording, not the paraphrase. The message establishes only that Dan believed, on August 21, 2024, that DJ would not reclaim the job on return. It does not establish when or whether DJ left, and his own exit timing and current contact status post-2024 remain open gaps.

**Testimony vs held record (standing limit).** Everything beyond the two anchors — the Triad's basement culture, the Sergio-war backing, the religion fault line, the photo record — rests on the unheld storytime sessions and is carried as Dan's self-narration, not as verified fact (`ent:ismaila-barry`). No direct message thread under either of DJ's names has been confirmed beyond the Senegal-link thread identified above; his phone handle is withheld under the corpus redaction convention.
