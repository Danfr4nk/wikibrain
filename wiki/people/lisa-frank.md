# Lisa Frank

> **Incidental entry** — Lisa Frank, Rick's partner since December 2015: a decade of presence on five held messages. Complete at this size; flagged for major promotion only if held evidence grows (see report).

Lisa is [[wiki/people/rick-frank|Rick Frank]]'s partner, present in the record since December 2015 — a decade of documented presence that had never been written into the wiki until the August 2026 correspondence-review pass, discovered only because that pass also found the much larger correction to Rick's own page. Everything known about her arrives mediated through Rick's messages and other people's mentions. No direct message from Lisa herself appears anywhere in the corpus.

The held iMessage corpus contains exactly five rows mentioning "Lisa" (`dat:0847`). The earliest is December 2, 2015, Dan to [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]]: "We could go eat with Rick and Lisa." The remaining held hits show a persistent, low-frequency presence without texture: a March 29, 2020 mention of "lisa and syd" in the [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]] thread, a June 18, 2024 birthday-wish context, and a 2026 list naming her among family incidents. On that evidence Lisa is Rick's household co-anchor across the decade — consistently the other half of "Rick and Lisa" in invitations and family logistics — and nothing more specific can honestly be said from held sources.

The fuller portrait this page previously carried rests on exports this repository does not hold, and it is preserved below with its grade stated rather than deleted. From those unheld sources: Lisa is a teacher, scheduling around her work ("Lisa was thinking 5–6"), unwell at points in the record ("Lisa is really under the weather"), and teaching online during the March 2020 COVID lockdown while Rick did yard and house work. She is consistently invited alongside Rick to family and social events across the record ("do u and Lisa have any interest in going," 2018 and again 2024), and she lives with someone named Syd — host of a June 2018 birthday cookout ("ribs corn & sides") and still present in an October 2024 message ("Were Lisa and Syd with us?"). Syd's relationship to Lisa — daughter, most likely on the birthday-party framing — and to Rick is not stated anywhere in the corpus. "Frank" as Lisa's surname is a working assumption from household usage, not a confirmed legal name.

## Conflicts in the record

**First-mention date (page: December 27, 2015; held corpus: December 2, 2015).** This page previously dated Lisa's presence from December 27, 2015, measured against the MASTER_MESSAGES_DB_DUMP export. The held corpus's earliest mention is December 2, 2015 — twenty-five days earlier, in the Dan-to-Annie message quoted above (`dat:0847`). Current standing: the held date governs; the frontmatter range above is corrected to December 2, 2015. The discrepancy is export-dependent, not substantive, but the earlier date is the one a reader can check.

**Occupation, household detail, and the September 2023 wedding (unheld; unverified).** The teacher occupation, the Syd household detail, and a possible September 2023 wedding — raised by a Rick message asking "Other than the wedding & reception will I see you?" for the following weekend, which could be Rick and Lisa's own wedding eight years into the relationship or someone else's entirely — all rest on a dox-scan dump and a message CSV neither of which is held here. The held corpus contains no "teaching" hit near Lisa, no September 2023 wedding message, and no direct Rick thread (`dat:0847`). Current standing: carried as unheld-source attribution, flagged and not asserted. The decade-long continuity itself may be true; it is simply asserted from sources no worker here can audit, and marital status — partner or wife — remains unconfirmed. No independent contact record (Facebook, Google Contacts) has been checked for her yet. If the evidence stays this thin, the page should stay this short.
