> **Incidental entry** — RFK Jr. as Dan covered him in May 2023: the weird second-place Democrat running on vibes and conspiracy adjacency. Complete at this size.

Robert F. Kennedy Jr. held second place in early Democratic primary polling in May 2023 — polling Dan treated as a symptom of a broken field rather than a real candidacy. His verdict: "very fucking weird."

Key facts:
- The Kennedy package per Dan: "sailing trips in Hyannis Port, serial marital infidelity and a big boost in support during your inevitable political endeavors."
- Children's Health Defense and the long-debunked vaccine-autism link made him "the marquee advocate in vaccine skeptic communities" — kept off the official platform but present in every stump speech.
- The interesting part: a government-reform platform (reining in intelligence agencies, the "deep state," the revolving door) with Trump-like appeal, plus open talk of CIA involvement in the JFK assassination and an appearance in Oliver Stone's documentary.
- The incoherence: "both sides" moral-equivalency rhetoric stapled to Targeted Community Repair racial-healing policy — "a campaign that lacks cohesion in policy and rhetoric," hoping to "pull an inside straight" if Biden dropped out late.
- Later arc (public record): independent run from October 2023, dropped out August 2024, endorsed Trump, became HHS Secretary.

Why incidental: a single-post portrait plus coda. Promote to major if Dan ever revisits him as HHS Secretary.
