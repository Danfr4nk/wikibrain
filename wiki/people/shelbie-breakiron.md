# Shelbie Breakiron

Shelbie Breakiron — "Shelbie Rose" in Dan's own 2026 frame-describe
example — was a Fayette County stripper and aspiring cam model
(MyFreeCams, Snapchat premium) who carried on a May–August 2019
sugar arrangement with Dan that ran **openly alongside his
relationship with Annie**: the two women knew about each other,
joked about the arrangement directly, and at least once coordinated
a three-way visit together over text. The record is the wiki's most
textually complete side-relationship — the held corpus carries 639
rows on her handle (306 inbound, 333 outbound, 2019-05-15 to
2019-08-11); the page's earlier export reports 685, a different
extract of the same thread. He funded her camming setup — a
refurbished MacBook (Amazon, $793.94, April 18, 2019), a ring light,
a DJ interface — and called himself her "sugar dad" while telling
her in the same breath that he lived with his girlfriend in New
York.

The relationship ended July 16, 2019 in a single explosive exchange —
her refusal to return the equipment, a legal threat ("my aunts a
lawyer"), and a tirade of escalating personal insults — answered by Dan in the flattest register in the corpus ("lol,"
"thanks!," "i am thanks!," "oh i'm so hurtttttt"). He followed up twice more in
August with small-claims and state-trooper threats, then seven years
of silence. She resurfaced in September 2026 only as an image — an
avatar likeness, a shared album, a Goon Club roster name.

## Who she was when the corpus opens

The held thread starts mid-story on May 15, 2019 — with a police
scare, not romance: "Babyboy," then "State cops called here looking
for me for something and idk why / I'm scared lol." Dan's
reassurance is instant ("they don't call if you're in trouble lol"),
closed with a joke at a friend's expense ("maybe something with
your lovely jailbird friend") that she takes in stride ("TRUE").
The May 15 rows read like two people already familiar.

The May texture fills in the person: the strip club, plus a salon
job she quit on May 31 ("Sorry for the late reply I quit my job
today bc stress") — her prognosis, "I'll just be a hella good
stripper than." Her circle enters in passing: roommate Chy ("She
knows ur my bae"), a friend named John (the source for coke), and
dogs — Buddha, who "will probably eat" Dan's puppy Milo, and Diva.
Her real last name and any life after August 2019 are genuinely
undocumented, not merely unmined.

## The arrangement, May 2019

The relationship opens as an explicit exchange and Dan never hides
it — from her. On May 21, mid a logistics exchange about his train
into Greensburg, he writes the sentence that defines the arrangement's
emotional economy: "because believe it or not i'm the kind of idiot
who thinks it matters to you that i'm not fucking anyone else for
real." A few messages later the candor goes structural: "also i have
Annie in my ear gloating that i look stupid for trying so hard with
you."

That same day comes the first crisis of definition. Dan asks
plainly whether she even wants him to come; her answer names the
problem from her side: "I mean I kinda feel like a bigger idiot for
trying to like a dude that lives with his woman in New York." He
counters by pointing at the ledger — "you have to admit that i've
been willing to put myself out there for you financially and
emotionally" — and by his established candor. Her verdict: "I say
just stay w Annie because obviously she's right for ya." His,
resigned and fond: "shelby at the end of the day you know i've
tried to be good to you. i like you a lot." The thread continues the
next day, over a Boosted board he wants picked up and a spare set of
keys.

The money plumbing is a running subplot through the whole month.
Her Cash App repeatedly fails, Dan's fails to send to her
specifically ("i just tried again to send you money and it still
won't let me. I can send money to everyone else tho wtf"), he
reports being "kicked off venmo lol," and the pair cycle through
PayPal and Facebook Pay. The practical effect is a relationship
where the transfers happen but never smoothly — the friction itself
becomes part of the texture, "spoilings" always pending.

Drug talk runs casual and conflict-free through the whole spring:
on June 2 he reports "lots of acid lol... 3 since this morning,"
and his standing offer — "anyways i'm gonna give you all my money /
in exchange for you smothering me" — draws her clearest
self-description: "Yes babe because I need you first money second /
So yes X 10." On June 7 he asks whether she knows anyone who wants
to buy "this diesel fuel coke from nyc," and the next day he's
offering her lines directly as part of logistics.

## Funding the camming setup

The arrangement's material shape is equipment. The camming project
is built across the thread in installments: "My cam room is almost
done btw" (June 2); "Myfreecams is almost doneee / Something wrong
w my id" (June 3); "My cams is approved I just gotta paint this
room" (June 4). Dan responds as a producer — on June 3 he sends two
Amazon links for motorized thrusting machines, which she answers
with "Babe I want one / Ugh youuuu don't understand how complete my
life would be." "R U ALIVE IM BOURED" follows an hour later — the
thread's most economical portrait of the dynamic.

The capital items accumulate: the refurbished MacBook Retina, bought
on Amazon April 18, 2019 for $793.94 (Dan confirmed in September
2026: "The MacBook went to Shelbie lol," "I guess it was a refurb"),
a ring light, and a DJ interface. The breakup exchange prices the
total: "just return my shit and enjoy the$5,000 and laptop i gave
you." She disputes it — "You gave it to me so you're not getting it back
sorry," and "Lmao you mean. The $300 refurbished one you got me
because don't think I didn't look it up." The $5,000 is Dan's
figure, not receipt-corroborated; the MacBook price is
receipt-verified and both sides agree it was refurbished. The ring light, the DJ interface, and
a pair of camo adidas NMD become the three items Dan demands back on
July 16 — and the grievance she weaponizes in response is a car key:
"that's getting sold for the money for my key that you never got me"
(see the origin testimony; her version holds that the key was never
replaced).

## Annie, in the middle of it

Annie is not concealed from Shelbie or vice versa; she is a
participant. Dan's May 21 "Annie in my ear gloating"; her "How
Annie 😒😂"; his June 8 check — "you sure you're cool with her
getting involved" — answered with "U ad Annie duh"; and his
same-night negotiation: "an extra 500 would convince you to forget
you hate her for a few hours." Her reply — "Come over and hang out
w her for 500 lmao" — is the arrangement's honesty in one line: the
antagonism is real, and so is the price of suspending it.

The April material was reconstructed at minute-level from Annie's
side (Shelbie's number doesn't begin until May 15): on 2019-04-14,
Annie's "She can stay as long as she pleases I don't care. I just
wanna play a little too" (22:14 EDT), "just say fucking no next
time" (22:38), "She here. Everything is cool 😎 we hugged" (23:34),
and the line that names the terms going forward, "if you date her i
get her once a week" (01:56). The sequence holds an unresolved
contradiction: Dan's account says Annie "contacted her independently
without me knowing and invited her back over," but Dan himself sent
Annie the *Shelbie Breakiron.vcf* contact card at 22:56 — twenty
minutes after Shelbie walked out. The standing read: his memory
compresses a night in which he supplied the means without expecting
or authorizing the use.

Seven years later, Dan's own September 2026 telling starts the story
earlier and stranger (retrospective testimony, consistent with the
corpus on material points). Forty-five days after the February 2019
move to New York, back in Pennsylvania for a weekend, he decided on
the train to reach out to Shelbie — "who didn't know me at all.
Hadn't even heard of me" — and offered her $700 to fuck him. She
showed up; Annie caused a ruckus; he couldn't cum, got so mad at

"Dan falls in love with." Days later he dragged Annie on the
ninety-minutes-each-way drive to pick Shelbie up from the strip club
she was dancing in and made Annie stay an extra three days for
another threesome; he went home "completely absorbed by Shelbie."
A month later he went back alone with "like $4000 of every exotic
drug imaginable to seem cool" — and on the last night accidentally
took her only set of car keys home in his pocket, though he says he
paid to have a new key made that day. "It was completely shot after

history: "two threesomes on a trip home that extended into a 5-day

Both tellings are his; no contemporaneous record corroborates the

## June 8: the 180-message day

June 8, 2019 is the thread's densest day — 180 messages, nearly a third of the corpus: morning logistics, the $500
negotiation in the afternoon, the three-way evening ending in
friction at night.
It opens in the "daddy" register ("I need money haha / Baby needs
spoiled" / "i got u girllllll"), and Dan sends her his new online
crush — instagram.com/bbblud — with the instruction "she's a lesbian
and i want you to date her for me." The held corpus independently
pins the bbblud introduction to this thread and this night; whether
Shelbie ever acted on it is unrecorded.

The day's spine is logistics. Dan is driving in overnight from New
York with the dogs and Annie, due at 5:12am, and needs her to leave
Fran's-house keys outside so he can get the dogs to Fran's. Shelbie
invites the whole party over — "You guys can come in an chill lol...
She knows ur my bae" — and after checking twice that she's fine with
Annie being involved, he lays out the plan: he'll get the key, give
it to Annie, hang with Shelbie, while Annie drops the dogs at
Fran's and goes to get more drugs. Everybody knows the shape of the
night and nobody is pretending otherwise.

That night the plan bends. Shelbie gets her period and revises the
terms downward — "We can hang for sure I'm fed not fucking her
today" — then, for Dan's benefit, "Definitely not fucking Annie lol
just comin to see u but I'll play along n b nice." The evening
closes with a small rupture: Dan writes "i guess i'm just having a
hard time not feeling a bit insulted if i'm / being honest," and the
last message of the night is hers: "Ok then goodnightttty."

## The breakup (July 16, 2019)

After June 9 the thread goes completely dark — not a single message
between June 10 and July 15 (554 messages across May 15–June 9, then
a blank month, then 84 on July 16), and July 16 is not a
continuation of the June conversation — it is a restart with a new
grievance and a month of silence behind it. Dan's restart comes at 20:28 EDT, and it is administrative:
"i'm coming back for a wedding on august 3rd. please get these three
things to johnny before then: ring light, dj interface, camo adidas
NMD. / no need to respond." Fifteen minutes later comes the
detonation. Her position
is property law: "Sorry the ring light is mine also that's your
responsibility not mine sorry / You gave it to me so you're not
getting it back sorry." The DJ interface is being sold "for the
money for my key that you never got me." When Dan answers with a
lawyer threat, she raises him — "No need, my aunts a lawyer / Don't
waste your time" — and adds the counterclaim: "I'll show them the
messages you saying I can have the ring light, also where you said
your gonna buy my key so please try me." (The "aunts a lawyer" line
was not separately located in the held merge; the surrounding
exchange is verbatim there.)

From there it becomes a tirade — hers — and a study in register —
his: "Lowlife coke head trash ass limp dick fuck," "Enjoy living
off your dead grandmothers money" (Fran's death, just over a year
prior), "While your girlfriend gets gamgbanged by ten black dudes
that fuck better than you." His replies are short and dry — "lol,"
"thanks!," "i am thanks!," "yeah you might want to try another
insult.," "oh i'm so hurtttttt," "yeah that all sounds terrible
you're right" — correcting one factual point (the laptop had been
paid for; she disputes it on refurb grounds) without ever matching
her volume. It ends with mutual "block me" dares and her final
"Loser," answered with "yeah that all sounds terrible you're right /
lol." No resolution.

## The aftermath (August 2019)

Nearly a month later come three more messages, all equipment-
recovery, all one-sided. On August 9: "i'll be in town next week to

then "nevermind i guess i can get it started online. if johnny

16 — and the May 15 police scare she opened the thread with
bookends, four months later, with him threatening to bring state
troopers to her. Whether either threat was ever acted on is
unrecorded.

## Assessment

As a candidate for modeling Dan's relational qualities, this page
does not currently support that: it establishes the shape of a
negotiated arrangement and how it ended — an outcome, not qualities
either person brought to it. Almost everything here is **Dan's
account of her** — her own messages exist in the thread but are
thinly excerpted relative to his. Where her own words appear ("How
Annie 😒😂," "U ad Annie duh," "I need you first money second,"
"BLOCK ME BITCH") they are the material a future pass should
prioritize; where they do not, the page thinly attests the person
and only moderately better attests the arrangement.

What the record does support is structural. This is the wiki's
clearest case of an arrangement run *with* Annie rather than behind
her back — the three-way logistics are conducted over text with all
parties named, the money-for-Annie's-inclusion is negotiated
explicitly, and Annie's own terms are recorded in her own hand ("if
you date her i get her once a week"). The breakup is legible as the
arrangement's accounting: the equipment was the relationship's
material form, and when it ended the fight was about who owned the
cameras. Her insults aim at his softest targets — the inheritance,
the addiction, the sexual adequacy — and his refusal to defend any
of them is the answer to the scene: "yeah that all sounds terrible
you're right."
