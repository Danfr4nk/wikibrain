# Dakota

> **Incidental entry** — a single-role witness in the BFS Foods record, complete at this size because his documented function is one disclosure and its later corroboration.

Dakota is a Little Caesars employee at the BFS Foods building in Uniontown and a member of [[wiki/people/timmy|Timmy]]'s friend group. He enters Dan's record in spring 2026 in one specific role: he was the first person to tell Dan, informally and "on the low," that [[wiki/people/anita|Anita]] had pivoted blame for Timmy's firing onto Dan (dat:0353).

That role matters because of its place in the sequence. Dakota's disclosure came first, as hearsay with no preserved message thread. Weeks later, on May 21, 2026, [[wiki/people/maddox|Maddox]] gave a fuller, unprompted, first-person account of the same sequence in a sidewalk recording. A later independent corroboration of an earlier informal disclosure is a stronger structure than two simultaneous claims, and Dakota's page exists to record which leg of that structure is his.

## The disclosure

The sequence Dakota disclosed runs as follows. Timmy, an underage Little Caesars employee, was banned for playing the store's slot machines on the clock. When manager [[wiki/people/kim|Kim]] pulled camera footage to document the gambling, it incidentally caught Anita selling Timmy a vape while he was underage — a licensing liability that nearly got her fired (dat:0249). Anita first told Timmy that Kim had reported him; when Timmy confronted Kim and she denied it, Anita switched the story to "Daniel," a coworker with zero involvement in the firing.

Dakota relayed that pivot to Dan before anyone else did. No direct quotes from Dakota are preserved in the corpus — the disclosure was relayed informally rather than through a documented message thread — so his exact wording is not known. What is documented is the direction of travel: Dakota first, Maddox's corroboration later, with Maddox's verdict on Anita recorded as "she's always making everything so crazy" (dat:0353, dat:0249).

The full narrative — the gambling-on-shift firing, the footage, the vape sale, and the pivot — is told in full on [[wiki/work/bfs-foods]]; this page records Dakota's specific role as the originating witness rather than re-narrating the chain.

Limits: how Dan and Dakota knew each other beyond the shared building is undocumented, and Dakota appears in the record only in this witness role. No other contact, before or after the spring 2026 episode, is documented.
