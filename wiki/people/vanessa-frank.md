# Vanessa C. Frank

Vanessa was born on 16 January 1994 in Greensburg, Pennsylvania, five years
and two and a half months after her brother, to [[wiki/people/rick-frank|Rick
Frank]] and [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suzanne Frank]]
([[wiki/people/suzanne-frank]], family-record table; [[wiki/self/ancestry]]
GEDCOM). She is the fourth member of a nuclear family that stopped being one
when she was eleven.

What the record holds of her is a particular kind of relationship, and it is
worth naming up front because it is rare in this corpus: **Dan and his sister
have never had a fight that survived into the archive.** Over a decade and a
third of continuous messaging — 1,467 messages between 1 December 2015 and
22 April 2026, on the only thread in the held corpus that opens on that date
(`corpus/derived/threads.csv`, thread `aea4ba0615641748`) — there is no
rupture, no exit, no reconciliation, no cycle. There is politics, there is
family logistics, and there is teasing. In a wiki organised largely around
ruptures, she is the one long relationship with none.

She is also the person he tells things to while they are happening. That is
the through-line, and it shows up in the very first thing the record has of
her.

## Before the record opens

The corpus has nothing of Vanessa's childhood. Nothing of the two of them as
small children, nothing of the house at
[[wiki/places/337-saratoga-drive|337 Saratoga Drive]] that the family built
in 1996 when she was two ([[wiki/people/suzanne-frank]], timeline), nothing of
her side of November 2005.

That last absence is the loudest one. In November 2005 Rick disclosed in a car
that he was entering rehab for cocaine the next morning; while he was in
treatment, Suz slept with their shared dealer; the marriage ended; that same
winter Rick was diagnosed with stage-4 throat cancer and survived
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/november-2005-hinge]]). Dan was seventeen. **Vanessa was
eleven.** Everything the wiki knows about that winter is Dan's retrospective
testimony, carried by later dossiers — the hinge page states plainly that no
contemporaneous record of any of it exists and that no record from either
parent's side exists at all. To that list add a third absence: no record from
Vanessa's side either. The single most load-bearing event in her brother's
psychological record happened to an eleven-year-old in the same house, and the
archive does not contain one word from her about it, then or since.

## The first channel: nine tweets to @veefrank

The earliest held evidence of the relationship is not messages. It is Twitter,
and it starts four and a half years before the message thread does.

`raw/twitter/archive.jsonl` — 2,741 posts spanning 24 September 2008 to 2026 —
is held in this repository, and a search across it for her handles and her name
returns fourteen posts. Nine go to **@veefrank** between June 2011 and March
2012, two to **@VanessaFrankzz** in April 2013, and three name her outright in
August and September 2013. The complete log is printed below; what it reads
like is worth saying first.

It reads like an older brother. On 24 June 2011 (23:53 Eastern) he is jealous
of something she has: *"@veefrank awesome!!! Super jelly :|"*. On 15 July,
*"@veefrank 'bout time"*. On 28 July, *"@veefrank good luck tonight!! :)"* —
the record does not say what for. In November he pays her the specific
compliment of comparing her to a comic actor: *"I just watched an interview
with Aubrey Plaza and realized that you guys are the same person."* In December
he uses the sibling channel for exactly what sibling channels are for —
complaining about the family channel: *"wtf just got a text from mom about this
I hate you all"* (6 December 2011, 20:54 EST).

In February 2012 he teases her about school, twice in forty-two minutes, while
she is finishing high school:

> *"@veefrank get it together! I set the bar oh so high for high school
> performance, I'm sure it's hard to live up to :)"*
> — 22 February 2012, 23:23 EST

> *"@veefrank aww that's okay/ you didn't have a chance, given my flawless HS
> career."*
> — 23 February 2012, 00:05 EST

Both jokes are funnier against
[[wiki/timeline/periods/dark-era-2007-2008|what his own high school years
actually were]], and he is plainly aware of it — the second post exists only to
extend the bit.

One post in the log has never been discussed on any page: on 24 March 2012 he
addresses her alongside **@Woodguts**, one of the four handles from his only
self-enumerated friend group ([[wiki/people/josh-brannan]], cohort tweet),
with *"~APPLICATION APPROVED~"*. What was being applied for is not recorded.
It is the single trace in the archive of Vanessa standing inside her brother's
friend circle rather than beside it.

## Troy Davis, 21 September 2011

The best-dated thing in the pre-2015 record is one evening, and it is the
evening that explains the whole relationship.

Georgia executed Troy Davis on 21 September 2011. Dan spent the night on it.
From `raw/twitter/archive.jsonl`, in Eastern time:

| Eastern | Post |
|---|---|
| 21 Sep, 18:41 | *"10 reasons why executing Troy Davis is a bad idea: http://t.co/kDumSR6"* |
| 21 Sep, 22:36 | *"@veefrank this is awful to watch. first-world country? yeah right."* |
| 21 Sep, 23:14 | *"I have no idea whether Troy Davis was guilty or not, but he deserved the chance to live another day to fight in court."* |
| 22 Sep, 00:07 | *"See the Penn & Teller: Bullshit on capital punishment. Think this is a much overlooked issue and one we need to have a serious debate about."* |

Dan was 22. Vanessa was 17, a high-school senior. He posts the case at 18:41,
goes quiet for nearly four hours, and the next thing he writes is to his
sister — *this is awful to watch* — in the present tense, while it is
happening. Thirty-eight minutes later he posts the line that concedes the
hardest part of the argument in public: he does not know whether Davis was
guilty, and says so, and argues for the man anyway. An hour after that he is
recommending a documentary and asking for a debate.

She is who he says it to first. Not a friend, not a follower, not the crowd —
the seventeen-year-old. That is the same shape the 2016 and 2020 message
material shows, five years before the messages start.

**One correction to this page's earlier account.** A previous version of this
section said the message to Vanessa came *"roughly half an hour before"* the
execution and the follow-up *"six minutes after it happened."* Those two
statements are consistent with each other — they place the execution at about
23:08 Eastern — but **the repository holds no record of the execution time.**
It is external history, not corpus. What the archive itself establishes is the
sequence and the intervals: 3 hours 55 minutes from the case to the sister,
38 minutes from the sister to the concession. Those are the numbers this page
now stands on.

## The handle change, and the public teasing of 2013

Between March 2012 and April 2013 she changes handles, from **@veefrank** to
**@VanessaFrankzz**. Nothing in the corpus explains why, and nothing dates it
more tightly than those two posts — a gap of thirteen months. It remains open.

On 21 April 2013 (21:40 EDT) he is listening to her broadcast something live:
*"@VanessaFrankzz you still on? just loaded stream"*, then thirteen minutes
later, *"i listened until i heard 'swag james brown swag swag'"*. What she was
streaming is not recorded, and it is a genuine loss — it is the only evidence
anywhere in the archive of Vanessa making something.

Then, in August and September 2013, three posts that name her in the clear
rather than addressing her handle, and all three are jokes at her expense
delivered to his own followers:

> *"Hair Alert! Vanessa Frank"* — 14 August 2013, 17:10 EDT
>
> *"Kill yourself, make the world a better place Vanessa Frank"* — 14 August
> 2013, 18:49 EDT
>
> *"Vanessa Frank has geriatric comedy on lock"* + Vine — 3 September 2013,
> 18:40 EDT

The middle one is a sibling insult of the standard 2013 internet variety, and
it is the harshest thing either of them says to the other anywhere in the
record. Two things are worth holding at once: it is public, and it is the
register the message thread — 1,467 messages across the next decade — never
shows. Whatever the 2013 mode was, it did not survive into the channel that
replaced it.

## December 2015: the thread opens

The iMessage thread begins on **1 December 2015** and runs to **22 April
2026**: 1,467 messages, 842 from Dan and 625 from Vanessa, across 3,795 days.
It carries 1,391 messages with text and 152 with attachments
(`corpus/derived/threads.csv`).

What that volume means, in context: of 577 threads in the held corpus, hers
ranks **16th**. The [[wiki/self/context-core|behavioural spine]] lists her as
*"Vail, ski school; minimal presence"* and rates her contact load **Low**. On
the measured record she sits inside the top 3% of threads by volume, with a
span longer than all but a handful. *Minimal* is not what the corpus says.

What it also means: **Dan writes more of it than she does** — 842 to 625, a
1.35:1 ratio. This is the opposite of the shape the wiki's other thread
corrections found, where pages had counted only the received half and called
it the total ([[kb/interpretations/received-half-count-pattern]]). Here the
page's older figure of 709 messages, taken from the unheld
`MASTER_MESSAGES_DB_DUMP.csv`, is neither the total (1,467) nor the received
half (625). It is its own undercount, and it is corrected rather than
explained.

## Political kinship, 2016–2020

The thread's defining content is politics, and the striking thing is that she
is not following him into it. She arrives from her own direction and gets
there first on her own subjects.

In 2016 she is an undergraduate activist. She circulates a Carnegie Mellon
petition for *"zero tolerance for sexual assault,"* and when a friend's rape
claim is met with jokes she makes the structural argument herself — that it is
*"in the university's financial favor to keep the rapists at school … losing
tuition, risking lawsuits."* That is an institutional-incentives analysis
delivered by a college student to her older brother, four years before the
wiki dates his own conversion ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/2020-left-turn]]).

By 2020 she is making the climate-and-capital case — *"we can't stall climate
change w capitalism"* — and checking his homework: *"you gonna vote for daddy
joe?"* The humour is of a piece with it: *"I'm a corporate schill,"* *"the
locomotive to transphobia pipeline is real."* It is his register, arrived at
independently, which is why she is one of the very few family contacts the
record never shows him running forensic analysis on.

**What she did not do.** A 2026-07-14 revision on this page corrected an
earlier attribution: the Bernie Sanders New Hampshire canvassing trip and the
pushback on Suz's *"boomer mentality… modest anodyne centrist"* framing were
**both Dan's own outbound messages** — *"i have been relentlessly appealing to
mom to open her mind… i'm going to NH on monday to canvass"* — not hers. Sent
rows in the master dump carry no recipient handle, so even the claim that he
was saying it *to her* is plausible rather than confirmed; a same-week outbound
message in an identical style went to an unrelated New York contact. What her
own side independently confirms is climate politics and a shared
anti-capitalist frame, and that is all this page claims
([`dat:1300`](kb/data/1300-vanessa-frank-political-kinship-and-sender-correction.md)).

## The back channel on the parents

Her second function in the record is quieter and more useful: she is the
family's other reader.

She keeps a job change from their father in 2016 — *"dad does not know about my
new job … please don't say anything"* — and her few direct lines about him
carry the household's documented strain without elaborating on it: *"being with
dad at that time was fucking excruciating,"* *"lol dad is zero concerned."*
When something is off with their mother she comes to Dan to triangulate:
*"I got a weird message from mom today. Just making sure there isn't some
emergency?"*

That question is the whole role in one sentence. She is not managing Suz and
she is not being managed by her; she is checking a reading against the only
other person with the same vantage. It places her beside Dan as an observer of
[[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]]'s oscillations rather than inside them —
which, given that the mother channel is the corpus's second-largest by volume
and its record *"alternates rescue with an itemised bill"*
([[wiki/mind/concepts/contact-gini]]), is not a small thing to be.

**A gap worth naming here.** [[wiki/people/rick-frank]] does not mention
Vanessa at all. Her father's page carries no reference to his daughter in any
section. Whatever their relationship is, this wiki has never written it down.

## 15 September 2025: the best-documented seven minutes

The single most legible scene in the whole sibling record lasts about eight
minutes on a Monday evening, and it is the one time in the corpus Dan names his
own operating system out loud and then apologises for it.

The thread had opened four days earlier, on 11 September, with Vanessa asking
*"Are you living for this news cycle"* — five days after the 10 September
assassination of Charlie Kirk. On the 15th, at 17:41:44 Eastern, Dan gives her
a life update: he has ended things with [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]], a
decade-long relationship, and is seeing someone new — described as smart,
political, sober, vegan, a pharmacy technician (the description matches
[[wiki/people/kristin|Kristin]], August–November 2025).

Her reception is cool: *"Well thanks for the update I guess. I'll reserve my
opinions but just know that I have them."*

At 17:47:15 he concedes: *"i fucking hate how many times i have to tell you
that you were right"* — she had been right about the Annie dynamics, and he
says so.

Then he explains why he had not told her sooner, using a fairness schema of his
own construction: *"i still haven't talked to dad so i have to ice you out also
to keep things fair."*

At 17:48:58 Vanessa answers it in eight words:

> *"First of all what does dad have to do with me"*

Seven seconds later, at 17:49:05:

> *"SHUT UP I'M AUTISTIC"*

Then, six seconds after that, *"IT MAKES SENSE"*. Four seconds after that,
*"sorry lol"*. Thirty-eight seconds after that, *"don't you dare go
mecha-vanessa on me right now"*. Ten seconds after that, *"i'm grieving the
death of charlie kirk"*
([`dat:1554`](kb/data/1554-autism-declaration-s-conversational-context-vanessa-s-pushba.md),
timestamps UTC in the source, Eastern here).

That is the whole scene: his convoluted reasoning, her four-word demolition of
it, his comic attribution of the reasoning to autism, his ten-second retraction
of the attribution, and a joke name for her — *mecha-vanessa* — for the mode
she goes into when she is being correct at him.

It matters beyond the sibling record. This is the only standalone all-caps
autism declaration in 192,140 held messages, and for a period the wiki read it
as an asserted identity statement ([`dat:0939`](kb/data/0939-autism-dated-self-identification.md)).
The full-thread review moved it into the banter register — and **the thing that
moved it was Vanessa's line.** A reading of her brother's self-understanding
now rests on eight words she wrote without any idea they would be read this
way. What survives the register correction is that the label was a live
explanatory frame he reached for under conversational pressure and then
withdrew from inside ten seconds ([[wiki/mind/concepts/autism]]).

## The ATM card

One episode in the sibling record does not come from the thread. It comes from
Dan, and he offers it against himself.

At some point while Vanessa was away at college, her bank card was at Suz's
house, and Dan used it — in his own telling, *"'accidentally' … for a little
while,"* having *"got them mixed up."* The scare quotes are his. He supplies it
as his chosen illustration of a pattern in which a live pursuit meets a
resource within reach and consumes it with no consequence-modelling attached
([[wiki/mind/concepts/acquisition-drive]]).

Nothing about it is corroborated. There is no date, no amount, no duration, no
resolution, and no indication whether she knew, whether she was repaid, or
whether it was ever discussed. Her own 1,467-message thread, which opens in
December 2015, never mentions it — which places the episode either before that
date or outside the record entirely. Given that the thread's defining property
is how conflict-free it is, the silence is worth noting rather than explaining
away.

## What the record does not show

- **Her voice before December 2015.** Nine of the eleven pre-thread posts are
  Dan writing *to* her; not one of her replies is held. The whole first decade
  of the relationship survives only as one half of it.
- **November 2005, from her side.** Nothing. See above.
- **Where she lives.** The behavioural spine says Vail and ski school; the
  page's own infobox says NYC. **These have never been reconciled and neither is
  corpus-confirmed** — the thread is about politics and family, not her address
  or her work.
- **Her father.** [[wiki/people/rick-frank]] contains no mention of her.
- **The handle change**, undated inside a thirteen-month window.
- **What she was streaming** in April 2013.
- **Whether contact continued past 22 April 2026.** The held corpus runs to
  7 September 2026, so the thread is quiet for its final 138 days. That is an
  absence in the archive, not a documented break — and the corpus policy is
  explicit that a fragment cannot support a claim about what is not there
  ([`CORPUS_POLICY.md`](CORPUS_POLICY.md)).
- **Any photograph.** No image in `media/registry.json` is identified as her.
- **The ATM episode**, in every particular except that Dan says it happened.

## Appendix — forensic notes

Compact, and deliberately at the bottom.

**A1. The tweet archive is held; three pages and two kb nodes say it is not.**
`ent:josh-brannan` and [`dat:0678`](kb/data/0678-josh-brannan-wav-artifact-verified.md)
both state that *"no tweet corpus is held"* in this repository and file all
tweet material as unheld old-wiki testimony. `raw/twitter/archive.jsonl`
(940,553 bytes, 2,741 posts, 2008-09-24 → 2026) is present, alongside
`tweet-archive.csv` and `reposts.jsonl`. Every tweet quoted on this page was
read from it directly. The claim should be struck wherever it appears.

**A2. Eleven versus fourteen.** The page's earlier *"eleven tweets across two
handles"* is correct as stated — eleven posts address @veefrank or
@VanessaFrankzz — and the fourteenth figure counts the three 2013 posts that
name her without addressing her. Both numbers are right about different sets;
this page now prints the set rather than the count. The related claim that
*"eight of the eleven open with her name"* undercounts: eight open with
@veefrank, and two more open with @VanessaFrankzz, for ten of eleven.

**A3. The execution time is not in the corpus.** See the Troy Davis section.
The prior *"half an hour before"* / *"six minutes after"* framing imported an
external fact; the intervals between posts are what the archive supports.

**A4. The 709 figure.** Not the received half (625), not the total (1,467).
Unlike the fourteen threads catalogued in
[[kb/interpretations/received-half-count-pattern]], this one is not an
export-direction artifact, and should not be cited as an instance of that
pattern.

**A5. 1,446 versus 1,467.** [`dat:1554`](kb/data/1554-autism-declaration-s-conversational-context-vanessa-s-pushba.md)
records 1,446 rows for the handle; the held derivative records 1,467 messages
and 1,391 carrying text. The 21-row difference is unexplained and is flagged
rather than resolved — the row-level corpus is gitignored and cannot be
re-derived here ([`CORPUS_POLICY.md`](CORPUS_POLICY.md)).

**A6. Thread identification.** `corpus/derived/threads.csv` is anonymised: it
carries no handles or names. The identification of `aea4ba0615641748` as
Vanessa's thread rests on three joins — it is the **only** thread in all 577
whose first message falls on 2015-12-01, the date this page has independently
carried as the thread's opening since 2026-06-23; its volume band matches; and
its end date runs past the master dump's cutoff in the same direction and
manner documented for the Jerad thread
([`dat:0585`](kb/data/0585-jerad-friedline-thread-held-corpus-corrections.md)).
That is a strong join, not a certainty, and it is recorded as an inference.

**A7. Handle redaction.** This page previously printed her mobile number in
full. It is reduced here to `+1724****111`, matching the convention used for
every other non-principal handle in the wiki. Nothing downstream depends on the
digits.
