# Tarik Fallous

Tarik Fallous — owner-operator (with his brother Ghassan) of [[wiki/work/au-zaatar|Au Za'atar]], the Lebanese restaurant near 58th and 1st in Midtown Manhattan where Dan worked as a busser-turned-server from roughly March 2021 to August 2024. Tarik hired Dan on the spot despite zero food-service experience, and his defining structural decision was an equal front-of-house tip split — bussers paid the same as waiters — which Dan's Gemini reconstruction repeatedly calls "a glitch in the matrix," the economic condition that made the whole tenure viable.

Tarik is the tenure's anchor, not its beloved boss: Dan reserves the "only boss he ever loved" title for [[wiki/people/dimitri|Dimitri]], the Greek manager — Dimitri "appreciated me in the same way tarik and ghassan did but dimitri was like...a real person." Tarik is the employer case where Dan's vertical distrust never fired: hired on trust, treated fairly, and still warm twenty months after the involuntary termination, in a direct 80-message thread (August 2023 – April 2026) that runs from grocery errands on Tarik's Barclays card to a 2026 message thanking him for the Lebanon the job taught him about. The brothers rented an apartment above the restaurant to watch the floor — Dan's "acerbic surveillance" panopticon — and ran the house on Lebanese humor "incapable of not making fun of everyone and their flaws."

## The hiring and the terms

What made the job worth keeping was the equal FOH tip split — bussers paid the same as waiters, collapsing the busser/waiter hierarchy at a stroke; Gemini's synthesis notes there was "zero financial incentive" for the later busser-to-waiter promotion, so the split, not the title, was the real compensation. Dan adds that as the only white FOH employee he benefited from the owners' own prejudices.

The economics: Dan confirmed on 2026-07-14 a $15/hour base plus the tip pool, weekly Sunday checks typically $800–1,000 for a six-day week (kb/syntheses/nyc-years-2010-2025.md).

## The working relationship, 2021–2024

Tarik reads, in Dan's analysis, as high-visible-investment against high-human-friction: he built elaborate outdoor dining sheds (which ran for three winters because the promised interior renovation never happened) while presiding over turnover that let Dan become the self-styled "High Priest of Protocol" — keeper of the opener routine (sleep till 1, build 2–5, cut by 9–10, never close). Dan was the one who stayed long enough to become infrastructure: "all of the regulars would GUSH to tarik about how much they loved me." In Dan's testimony the brothers bought his "smart white guy" cover wholesale — "they probably thought you were studying law or engineering; in reality you were" leveraging the "night class" alibi of unspecified nature to keep a 10 PM cut time while the rest of the crew stayed to close. That alibi ran 18+ months under Tarik's roof: "tarik doesn't seem to think it's a big deal and i am kinda happy about it because this is what keeps the 'weird' qualities that i thrive in afloat."

Two episodes show the mediation in practice. The "Nonsense Meeting" — Tarik and Ghassan presiding over cultural analogies about tooth loss and professional "lanes" — is Tarik's mediation style in Dan's rendering. In the Sergio sit-down, it was Ghassan who mediated, Tarik joining toward the end (revised 2026-07-14 from a Dimitri attribution; see Conflicts in the record). When Dimitri left after six months, Gemini's synthesis reads it as "the STRUCTURAL COLLAPSE of the Au Za'atar hierarchy — he was the buffer between your 'sonofabitch' nature and the micromanaging Lebanese brothers."

## Direct Correspondence (Aug 2023 – Apr 2026)

The 80-message thread with `+19178259183` (59 Dan-sent, 21 inbound) splits into an employment-logistics phase (Aug 2023 – Aug 2024) and a post-termination phase (Aug 2024 onward). Read directly for this expansion (raw/imessage/messages-part2-2019-2026.csv); corrections to earlier thread claims sit in Conflicts in the record.

**Employment logistics.** The texts show Dan's scope ran well past serving tables. He ran grocery errands for Tarik with photo approval — "That's the only non-baby spinach here. Will that work?" / "Yes"; snow peas for Romano beans: "Picture plz" / "Okay get those"; "No Swiss chard at Morton Williams. I ran to Whole Foods to try there real quick" (Dec 27 2023) — while holding Tarik's own Barclays card ("When I got home I realized I forgot to hand you back your Barclays card after I went to Whole Foods... am I okay to just bring it with me tomorrow?" / Tarik: "No. Tomorrow is fine thanks"). He tracked bar inventory directly: two stock counts survive verbatim (Aug 11 2023, all zero; Aug 12 2023, restocked — Whispering Angel, Chateau La Coste Rose, La Luca Prosecco, Fidora Prosecco, Charles de Ferre Blanc de Brut, ViNO CasaSmith, Langewalter Riesling, Valdemonxes, Chateau la Croix Lartigue, Fattoria Casa Sola, Barolo), a responsibility not documented anywhere else in the corpus and a concrete data point for the "de facto manager" role [[wiki/work/au-zaatar]] ascribes to him. He coordinated hiring — "Just fyi I have 2 people outside for interviews. One of them is from the agency" (Sep 27 2023), "First Interview is here" / Tarik's "Speak to him and write his info plz" (Nov 3 2023) — and ran an AV-cable errand (low-voltage speaker wire, wrong "AC" vs. "MC" gauge, Oct 2023) plus two print-shop errands ("I'm bringing back one copy for you to inspect and if it's good he will do the rest for us today," Dec 1 2023; "I'm at the print shop. He doesn't have an email from you yet, did you send it?" Jan 24 2024 — Tarik-side email the hold-up, Dan chasing him up), suggesting a signage or menu project stalled on the owner's inbox.

The tone is as telling as the tasks. Dan's on-call availability reads as genuine flexibility — "Do you want me to come in tomorrow?" / Tarik's terse "No is okay. Thank you" (Aug 17 2023); "I'm on my way home from the vet. Do you still want me to come in" / "Yes that will be good thanks" (Oct 29 2023). He also used the channel as a media feed: two YouTube links — "This is the one you should check out though. It's only 20 mins" (Nov 23 2023) — and an Apple News link, "Thought you'd be interested in this" (May 27 2024), which drew Tarik's only in-thread read-reaction, a "Loved." A December 13, 2023 payroll dispute — "My last paycheck was missing one day (Saturday 12/2/23). I'm assuming this was my fault and somehow, in an act of staggering stupidity, I forgot both punch's. I know the tips have already been distributed but am I still able to submit a form to get the hourly? Or am I shit out of luck?" — corroborates the tip-pool opacity already documented on au-zaatar.md; Tarik's whole answer was "I'll find out," and Dan closed it with "Okay thanks. Sorry to bother you about it." Five sick-day call-outs are logged between Nov 2023 and Apr 2024, some opening "Hey it's dan" or similar. A previously unknown name, **"Modi,"** surfaces once (Apr 20 2024) — and the direction matters: it was Dan relaying to Tarik, not the reverse ("Garbage cans downstairs?" then "Also Modi came in to see you. He said he'll be around the neighborhood later tonight if you're around") — unidentified, no other corpus reference. On June 14, 2024, Dan sent an unprompted Arabic message, "مبروك للعرسان!" ("Congratulations to the newlyweds!") — the wedding's occasion is unidentified — answered with a simple "Thank you" the next day. The final logistics exchange, August 11, 2024, is the termination made concrete: Dan arranging to "grab my check," told by Tarik "I would have loved to see you. But I'm not there now. You can take from Patricia" — introducing **Patricia**, evidently the bookkeeper/cashier who handled payroll, otherwise undocumented. The held thread preserves Tarik's mid-edit: the first version read "I would loved to see you," then a corrected "I would have loved to see you" twelve seconds later — a small, telling care with English from a man texting his just-terminated employee.

**Post-termination.** Thirteen days after filing for unemployment (per [[wiki/work/au-zaatar]]'s Aug 8, 2024 date), Dan texted Tarik unprompted, Aug 21 2024, in full: "I know I'm not an *official* CIA agent anymore but I did hear some intel today that might interest you…don't get too excited though, it's not very important or surprising. You didn't hear it from me but, based on my conversation with him — I wouldn't be surprised if [[wiki/people/ismaila-barry|DJ]] does what MD did and doesn't 'return' when he gets back from his vacation." Then a second message: "I asked him why and he just said that it seemed 'different' now so i didn't get the impression he was upset or anything. Anyway — just thought I would pass along the tip. I really appreciate all that you guys did for me and I'll always get your back whenever I have the opportunity to help out. Send my best to Ghassan!" Tarik: "Thank you for the tip. I will send your regards to Ghassan." "DJ" is [[wiki/people/ismaila-barry|Ismaila Barry]], one of Dan's closest friends from the restaurant; "MD" is an unresolved initials-only reference to some earlier staff departure, not otherwise documented. The frame is the point: after an involuntary termination, Dan positioned himself as a voluntary informal source for the owner, and Tarik received it as gratitude, not suspicion. On Sep 28, 2024, during that autumn's escalation of the Israel–Lebanon conflict, Dan initiated: "Was thinking about you guys with everything going on in Lebanon. Hope your family over there is safe and well." Tarik: "Yes pretty bad." The relationship's most recent contact, April 12, 2026 — a year and eight months after termination — is the thread's longest message, Dan-sent:

"Hey wanted to give you a shout to tell you that with everything happening recently, I've realized how thankful i am for the time i spent at au za'atar for giving me such a great perspectives of Lebanon — how beautiful and unique the cultural tradition is, how nice and funny and diverse the people are. It's made seeing the evil, genocidal attacks that are happening even more horrifying and I am just disgusted that my country has enabled and facilitated it. hope your family and friends in the country are safe and healthy and doing as well as they could be, and I hope that everything is good with you, Ghassan and **Khalid** and **Hani**." Khalid and Hani are evidently Tarik's family or close friends in Lebanon, otherwise unidentified. Tarik's reply, about an hour later: "Hey Dan, everything is well here thank you for asking. Scary things happening all around. Hope you are well too."

## The exit

The loss was ruled involuntary (dossier Amendment 2, ACCEPTED): hours cut June–August 2024, resumes out from July 9, unemployment filed August 8 — though the record keeps an honest caveat that a held-corpus message from Tarik or Dimitri describing a quit would reopen the voluntary question. Dan's contemporaneous first-person testimony, 2024-08-21, is warm: "By the way they officially let me go from AZ"; "Tarik was really really nice about it and everything though. We parted on good terms"; "And I am already getting unemployment so it didn't cause me too many problems financially." The final paycheck was collected via Patricia on August 11; the shed shutdown closed the room ten days later. Manuel's survival through the post-Dan purge into 2026 is the tenure's clearest continuity marker (kb/entities/manuel.md).

## Role in the work power dynamic

Tarik is the pivot of the whole [[wiki/mind/synthesis/vertical-authority-skepticism]] analysis: the equal tip split removed the usual busser/waiter hierarchy and enabled Dan's "Ambassador of the Shed and its most effective saboteur" self-description — the fictitious "night class" alibi that let him leave at 9 PM for 18+ months, the pocketed cash tips framed as a "decentralized audit" of a digital pool the owners were themselves siphoning. The kb interpretation keeps the honest boundary: "the anchor is real as a psychological fact (he organizes around it) and unverified as a historical one (the interior is memoir)" — the first two and a half years of the employment relationship have no held message record, and the thread holds the texts of the April 2026 contact, not the reason for them.

The recurring contrast Dan draws: [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]], "the avatar of reliability," was fired from her own hosting job with zero notice, while Dan survived 2.5 years under Tarik — his gloss, "the universe rewards the sonofabitch over the proper."

## Conflicts in the record

- **[2026-07-20]:** the prior version of this page stated flatly that "Tarik is a peripheral work figure with no independent message corpus" and that everything here came from the Gemini "STORYTIME" reconstruction. That was wrong — a direct iMessage thread with Tarik (`+19178259183`, 80 messages, August 2023 – April 2026) exists in the corpus and had simply not been filed or mined. It shows a real, sustained, and warm relationship that ran well past the August 2024 termination: Dan continued passing Tarik informal workplace intel three weeks after filing for unemployment, and the two were still in affectionate, politically candid contact as recently as April 2026 — nearly two years after the job ended. Standing: corrected; the thread is this page's primary evidence.
- **[2026-07-14]:** the Sergio sit-down mediation was revised from a Dimitri attribution to Ghassan as mediator, with Tarik joining toward the end; the Tunisian manager was uninvolved. Standing: the revision holds across the kb (kb/entities/au-zaatar.md) and this page.
- **[2026-09-10 / flagged 2026-10-05]:** the kb verification node kb/data/1258-tarik-fallous-thread.md contains three errors against the held thread itself. (1) It describes the April 12, 2026 exchange as Dan-sent "hope all is well with you and the family my friend. Ramadam mubarak my brother" with inbound "Thank you my friend... See you soon" — but "mubarak," "ramadan," and "eid" occur zero times anywhere in the 80-row held thread (raw/imessage/messages-part2-2019-2026.csv). The actual April 12, 2026 messages are the extended Lebanon solidarity text and Tarik's "Hey Dan, everything is well here thank you for asking," quoted above. (2) It labels the "Modi came in to see you" row (2024-04-20 20:50:35 UTC) and the "CIA agent anymore" rows (2024-08-22) as inbound — all three rows are `is_from_me=1`, sender "Me": Dan-sent. Standing: the kb node is uncorrected; this page's thread claims rest on the direct 2026-10-05 thread read, not on the node.

## Assessment

The 80-message thread proves the relationship was sustained, voluntary on both sides, and warm past the job's end; it does not prove why. Whether the April 2026 contact was friendship, network, or something transactional stays open. What is not open: the involuntary-termination ruling stands, the thread's anchors are held-corpus-verified, and the warm exit — "Tarik was really really nice about it and everything though. We parted on good terms" — is Dan's contemporaneous testimony.
