# Jason Bermejo

Jason Bermejo is one of the two people alive whose musical taste Dan
genuinely defers to (the other is a stranger: Anthony Fantano) — the
Full Sail–era friend who built Dan's musical identity circa 2009–2011
and, seventeen years later, is still doing it. A DJ and producer in
Texas (`+18172693422`, ~270 messages, 2017–2026), born April 1987 —
about a year and a half older than Dan — he is the era's most durable
friendship and the friendship's shape never changed: beat talk,
all-night recommendation threads, production shop-talk, and a shared,
matter-of-fact chemical culture, resumed after every gap without a
single message of repair. Of all the formative-era friendships in the
corpus, this is the one that never needed fixing to restart.

## Relationship & Role in Dan's Life

**Phase 1: Formative era (2009–2015)**

Jason was a formative influence during Dan's most musically curious
period — the blog era, the skinny-jeans era, the era when a kid from
Uniontown was figuring out what kind of listener he was going to be.
The influence ran in a specific direction and both of them remember it
that way. In the Feb 2026 all-nighter, drunk and reflective, the
thread states it plainly:

*"You put me on kid cudi"*
*"You put me on skinny jeans haha"*
*"Haha yeah I was on that hip hop era but we clicked on music and of course getting g high"*
*"Blog era"*

Kid Cudi was the breakthrough — the gateway to a different style of
music that, in Dan's own accounting, still resonates today. Cool Kids,
the LCD Soundsystem Williamsburg moment, recording basics, the closet
as a studio, the early DIY ethos: Jason was the older head (by
eighteen months) who had the map. Dan describes him as a gateway, and
the respect calcified into something permanent — Jason is one of only
two people, the other being Anthony Fantano, whose music taste Dan
genuinely defers to. Not consults. Defers.

**Phase 2: The thread (2017–2026)**

The message record opens on 2017-01-20 already mid-conversation —
*"Good! Making breakfast right now. Still fuxking around these Beats.
You still djing?"* — and the chemical candor is there from the first
day: *"I was so coked out it was bad!! Hahahah!"* Nobody in this
thread has ever pretended otherwise about anything.

What follows across nine years is not a narrative arc but a
temperature: long silences (nothing at all between April 2019 and
September 2020, nothing between September 2020 and March 2025), then
sudden dense bursts where the two of them pick up exactly where they
left off. The March 2025 burst is 25 messages of photo-sharing and
cohort roll-call. The September 2025 burst is 31 messages of pure
recommendation-thread energy. The February 2026 burst is 105 messages
across four days — the thread's all-time peak, landing in the raw
aftermath of the Uniontown return — and it is the emotional center of
the whole friendship.

## The February 2026 all-nighter

Four days, 105 messages, both of them up late and being honest in the
way only old friends get to be. Jason is drunk (*"I'm a lil drunk
haha"*, *"And had sum mid yay"*, *"Yay vodka brews"*, *"Man I'm fucjd
up"*), Dan is in Uniontown with everything raw, and the conversation
keeps circling the same question Jason finally asks outright:

*"One question honest answer serious… what one thing back in 09-011 you wish we shared"*
*"Haha no homo like friendship business or chill chit"*

What comes out around that question is the page's most important
passage, because it revises the whole premise. The influence did not
run one way:

*"We were in diff type shit and linked up because of real shit. I looked upt to you even though you were younger in some new wave of music"*
*"You put me on game and same but still same similar interest"*
*"I fuxkin love music."*

Jason — the older head, the gateway — looked up to Dan. The kid from
Uniontown who got put on to Kid Cudi was, in the same years, putting
the older head on to the new wave. They were each other's map.

And then the dream, stated without self-pity:

*"Haha the best thing for me was to get of fl. I haven't done anything musically besides made some beats but mannnn it's still a dream that I've been working on"*
*"I know. I a lot of output stuff but haven't been heavy making music the past years. Used to be on top of shit"*

Getting out of Florida was the best thing he ever did; the music
dream is still a dream. Both things are true at once. The thread
holds them without resolving them.

The shop-talk runs through all of it — *"I'm a big saw wave guy"*,
*"I know modulation haha"*, *"Haha all types of kits"* — and the
taste philosophy that has organized Jason's listening for seventeen
years, finally stated as doctrine:

*"It's all bout talent and ideas put into original music bro"*

The personal stuff is all there too, unguarded: the girlfriend
(*"Haha my girl is kinda like fuxk sex so I get when's she drunk now
haha the spark is still there but I'm a horny guy so fuxk haha"*,
*"Haha well we broke up but we are friends"*, *"We been having more
sex lately then the past"*), the self-awareness (*"She is you young
get but she is a workaholic. And she puts up with my old ass"*), and
the single funniest line in the corpus — *"My girl said you have nice
cock haha"* — delivered the way everything in this friendship is
delivered: as a joke between people who don't need to be careful.

## "Bermejo-coded"

Dan has turned Jason's taste into an adjective. On 2025-09-20,
hearing something Jason never introduced him to: *"How the FUCK did
you never introduce me to this? It's so fucking bermejo-coded."* On
2026-02-07, the definition sharpens into a recipe: *"OH FUCK J THIS IS
YOUR SHIT DUDE. Literally if someone asked me what you get if you put
Imogen heap and a$ap rocky on a track I would say… if we can get chino
Moreno involved somehow that's the recipe to make a jason b"* — and
the taxonomy gets its subspecies: *"a diet jason bermejo is like Bun B
and Youth Lagoon lol."*

This is what "defers to his taste" looks like in practice: Jason is
not a person Dan gets recommendations from; Jason is a genre Dan
measures things against. The February all-nighter even has Dan doing
fieldwork — *"I just added like 40 jason bermejos on snapchat"* —
hunting the archetype in the wild.

## The Mohler exchange (March 2025)

The previous version of this page cited this exchange with fabricated
quotes. The real one is better. On 2025-03-17, Dan is doing cohort
roll-call with Jason — old photos, *"Fuckin Kayden in the back of the
sub photo"*, *"Do you have any more pics of us haha I'm hanging with
my girlfriend and I'm talking about my experience and how you and I
were bros"* — and he tells Jason about Mohler's transition:

*"well, maybe mohler has changed just a little bit more than you but"*
*"did anyone tell you about mohler yet"*

Jason: *"Jim?"*

Then, a beat later: *"Hahahahahha well that is how it is these days."*

That is the entire reaction. No follow-up questions, no awkwardness,
no performance of acceptance — just the casual, unremarkable
absorption of the news, and the conversation moves on (*"Haha mannn
that's crazy"* is about something else entirely). It is the primary
corpus record of the Full Sail cohort absorbing Mohler's transition,
and its very casualness is the point. Six months later Dan's own
rundown names the cohort as it stands: *"i love jester and Dunn and
ms. Jamie Mohler and the other shitcunts."*

## Musical Taste & Production Practice

**Jason's aesthetic**, as documented across the thread: DJ/producer
sensibility spanning UK garage and EDM (Odd Mob, NXSTY trap packs via
Splice, Westwood loops), hip-hop (Clipse — *"Hell yeah that new clipse
is fire"*, *"Malice got a bad ass verse on j.i.d. - the community"*;
Freddie Gibbs, A$AP Rocky, Papoose, early-2000s Bronx rap), R&B and
neo-soul (SiR — *"If you like rnb sir is cool"*, Thundercat,
Crosses), post-hardcore and alternative (Deftones, Bring Me The
Horizon — *"It was probably one of the best concerts I've ever been
too. Oli killed it"*, Limp Bizkit, Billy Idol, Mars Volta — *"sucked
because they only played new music that no one cared about haha"*),
and the deep underground (Molchat Doma, Nujabes, J Dilla). The
September 2025 burst is essentially a link-dump of all of it at once
— YouTube links, *"Board goes kinda hard. Signed with stones throw
hahaha"*, *"Haha you know I love music haha."*

**Production style**: the thread shows the shop-talk, not the rig.
*"Still fuxking around these Beats. You still djing?"* opens the whole
record in 2017; the Feb 2026 all-nighter has *"I'm a big saw wave
guy"*, *"I know modulation haha"*, *"Haha all types of kits"*,
*"Haha I don't know Mann my comp out of commission"* — a producer
talking shop on a struggling machine, sending SoundCloud links
(*"Check out some beats which he was trying to do"*) and asking Dan
for additions (*"You think you could add to this / Ideas"*). The
previous page named specific tools (Bitwig, Serum, Splice packs, a
sample-direct CPU workaround, a standing two-week flip competition);
none of those appear in the message thread — they came from the
original mine's unresolved sources and are not repeated here as
corpus fact. What the thread evidences is the dynamic: two producers
trading work and critique, Jason's ear trusted (*"Haha I can't
remember but I usually in those keys haha great ear"* — one of them
complimenting the other's).

**The philosophy**, again, because it organizes everything: *"It's all
bout talent and ideas put into original music bro."* He and Dan bond
over rejecting poseur culture — the early-2010s EDM kids at Full Sail
dismissed as fuccbois, Ross Lara remembered as "the world's first
fuccboi."

## Substance Use & Lifestyle Context

The thread's drug talk is notable for what it isn't: it is never
confessional, never crisis-shaped, never moralized. It is logistics
and weather — the substrate the music conversation happens on.

**Cocaine**: normalized from the first day of the corpus (*"I was so
coked out it was bad!!"*). *"Fuxk I've done a lot of coke since
then haha"* (2025-03-17). Night-long discovery sessions run on it;
nobody comments on it.

**Cannabis & tobacco**: long-term vaping; Black and Milds — Dan's
May 2026 reply is just *"Haha black and milds. Nice."*

**Ketamine**: Dan's frame — *"the best music drug I've ever tried"*,
45 minutes, dissociative without the long comedown; K-holed while
discovering new music.

**Xanax**: *"I've been on xans on for 3 days lately haha"*
(2019-04-24) — the "lately haha" doing a lot of work.

**Lean**: the fake-lean incident — *"Don't trust white drug dealers
haha"* / *"Haha well lean wise"* (2025-03-17). Jason got sold
non-lean product; Dan's warning is racially specific and clearly a
running joke between them.

**The heroin story**: the Orlando-era failed buy — Dan and Alexis
trying to score, the deal going bad, Jason and Spetch having to pick
them up at The Social in the middle of the night — **does not appear
in the message thread**. No "heroin," no "the social," no "Alexis" in
~270 messages. The story comes from the operator's account of the era,
not from Jason's messages, and this page previously presented it as
thread evidence. It stays as operator testimony, labeled as such.

**The DWI**: Christmas 2018 — *"I just got a dwi on Christmas. I'm in
and out of court right now"* (2019-03-13). It pinned him in Texas for
over a year: *"I def need to go visit. I just can't leave Texas until
my dwi case blows over"* (2019-04-21), *"I just got out of a
relationship as well but I can't leave the state"* (2019-04-24).

## Conversation Style & Humor

The thread's register is unmistakable: "haha" as punctuation, "fuxk"
as intensifier, ethnic and cultural humor delivered without a flinch,
self-deprecation as the default (*"I'm a cokehead"* energy throughout;
*"Haha mannn I can't hate on drake because he is a icon legend but
mannn he bitch haha"*). Music-nerd passion that turns into 3am
link-dumps. Nostalgia as a recurring key (*"can you believe that was
15 fucking years ago"* energy; *"Hahaha throwback"*). Generosity with
time — sample packs, production critique, endless song links — and a
dark playful streak (deepfake porn jokes, jailbreaking Snapchat). The
dominant move, though, is the late-night honest question — *"what one
thing back in 09-011 you wish we shared"* — asked drunk, meant
sober.

## Significance in Dan's Life

**Earned status**: Jason ranks in Dan's top-tier music influences —
equivalent to Anthony Fantano in critical weight. But the Feb 2026
exchange revises the hierarchy into something mutual: the kid who got
put on to Kid Cudi was putting the older head on to the new wave in
the same years. Deference, it turns out, was the wrong word. It was
an exchange.

**"Bermejo-coded"**: the ultimate compliment in Dan's lexicon is not
deference but taxonomy — Jason's taste became a genre Dan measures
other music against. *"If someone asked me what you get if you put
Imogen heap and a$ap rocky on a track… that's the recipe to make a
jason b."*

**Anchoring function**: the "formative friend" archetype — someone
from the high-creativity, high-risk Full Sail years who stayed
connected across Texas-sized distance and every life-stage change,
resuming without repair every time. The Feb 2026 renewal, landing in
the raw aftermath of the Uniontown return, reads as Dan reaching back
for the person who knew him when the map was being drawn — and finding
that the person had been holding his end of the map too.
