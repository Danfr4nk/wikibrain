# Danny Matthews

Danny Matthews plays two roles in Dan's life that the wiki long treated as unrelated and which turn out to converge on a single house: he's the local realtor who handled the sale of [[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Fran]]'s house at [[wiki/places/117-belmont-circle|117 Belmont Circle]] after her death, and — running the whole time alongside that professional thread — he's one half of a swinger couple (with his girlfriend Nicole) that Dan and [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] played with periodically from February 2018 through at least 2024. The two threads are interleaved in the same 349-message conversation with no apparent awkwardness on either side; Danny moves from confirming a real-estate closing to explicit sexual logistics in consecutive texts.

He is also, on the evidence of his own thread, **the first person outside the relationship that Dan and Annie's non-monogamy arrangement was ever executed with** — inside 117 Belmont Circle, on the night Fran fell and lost the house ([[wiki/timeline/events/the-fall-of-fran]]). Fifteen months later he was the agent who listed it.

**A note on the data:** the CSV export's direction field marks every row "Received," which is not literally true — read this as a two-way conversation.

## Before the thread: the social layer

The documented thread opens February 16, 2018, but the relationship is older. Dan's own account — operator testimony, not corpus — is that Danny was *"a grade above me at school, known as a dork then."* The corpus's earliest sighting is social, not transactional: on **July 3, 2017**, someone texts that they're *"getting ready to go play nine holes out Duck Hollow with Ted Anderson, Danny Matthews, and Shelley Johns.. grabbing dinner after"* — a casual summer round with dinner after, the ordinary texture of the Uniontown male friendships. By **June 19, 2018** Dan writes, *"You hadn't really met Danny Matthews either lol"* — the kind of line that implies a person being folded into a social circle in real time.

And the caddie yard knew him before the corpus's main thread did. On **May 16, 2018**: *"One of the caddies we were training said 'you guys know Danny Matthews? That dude can hit the ball 400 Fucking yards'"* — Danny circulating in [[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddying|the yard's]] oral tradition as a physical specimen. The golf layer of the relationship predates the February 2018 thread; the realtor layer, as the next section shows, was already active by then too. The February 16 messages are not an introduction. They are two people who already knew each other, in at least two other registers, opening a third.

## The realtor thread

Danny works as a residential realtor, based near Uniontown, and mentions Dan's mother Suz as an existing client relationship of his own (*"your mom even mentioned it to me"*). He handled the listing on Fran's house after her April 2018 death: an April 7, 2018 message shows him stopping by the property the same week and running into Suz there (*"I told her I stopped by to extend my condolences"*), and by July 11, 2019 he reports the house "finally went pending," with a follow-up crack that "second time's a charm" — implying an earlier listing attempt had fallen through sometime in the intervening year. This resolves part of the open disposition question on [[wiki/places/117-belmont-circle]]: the house was actively re-listed and went to a pending sale in mid-2019, roughly 15 months after Fran's death. That pending status held — the sale closed in **summer 2019 for $250,000** (operator testimony, 2026-08-27; uncorroborated in the message record, which never returns to the subject after Danny's July 11 line). The buyer is still unknown, so whether Danny's own commission or a colleague's closed it is undocumented.

The realtor thread is not confined to Fran's house. On **April 26, 2019**, Suz texts: *"Danny Matthews got a listing from me. Grrrr. 234 Michael dr. Sellers are liars too."* The "Grrrr" is doing work — this is not a warm handoff between colleagues, it is a competitor taking business, and Suz is annoyed about it. The line establishes two things the rest of the page needs: Danny and Suz operate in the same small market as rivals as well as collaborators, and the professional relationship has its own friction entirely separate from the personal one. The same man who stopped by Fran's house to extend condolences is, a year later, poaching — or outcompeting for — Suz's listings. Both things are true in the same thread, weeks apart from the pending-sale news, with no sign that either party finds the combination strange.

That is the page's central observation, stated once more because it keeps not landing: **the professional and the personal are not in tension in this relationship.** They are not even in separate compartments. The 349-message conversation moves between real-estate logistics and sexual logistics the way any other long thread moves between topics — no code-switching, no awkwardness, no apparent awareness that an outside reader would find the combination remarkable.

## The arrangement thread

From the first messages (Feb 16, 2018) the tone is established: Danny and his girlfriend **Nicole Semans** are an experienced but casual swinging couple (*"we're not pros... but it's always been a good time for everyone"*), and the four of them — plus, at points, other couples from what Danny describes as an established local circle — negotiate hangouts around golf, drinking, and sex over nearly seven years of intermittent contact.

### The opening evening (February 16, 2018)

The entire arrangement is opened, negotiated and disclosed inside a single twenty-minute exchange, and it is worth reading closely because it fixes a date the rest of the wiki depended on. The evening starts socially — *"We're going to the Palace Theatre in Greensburg Sunday... I have no idea what time it starts or ends though. Lol"* — and Dan introduces the frame: *"danny matthews (who is a real estate agent that knows my mom haha) and nicole semans."* Dan proposes a Sunday; Danny counters with the Palace Theatre and then discloses the asymmetry of experience (*"you guys gotta be gentle though... we've had our own experiences before we got together, and a few since"*); Dan answers with the line that settles the arrangement's chronology — **"you guys would be the first couple we were with"** — and follows it, four messages later, by volunteering the camming operation unprompted (*"also we do a cam show... we make like $150 an hour... the key is just keep the frame filled with the girl haha. the more i show my face the less money we make"*). Two strangers negotiating a first encounter, and within twenty minutes Dan has handed over the household's income structure and its production technique.

That single sentence has consequences beyond this page. It establishes that the arrangement had not been executed with anyone before, which makes the encounter three weeks later — documented at [[wiki/timeline/events/the-fall-of-fran]] — the first, and moves [[wiki/mind/psychosexual/arrangement-history]]'s earliest entry eight months earlier than it stood. It also dates the operator's story: a plan Dan remembers as running in March 2017 was in fact negotiated in February 2018, because in March 2017 this thread did not exist.

### The long middle

Recurring elements across the seven years:

- **Logistics friction.** Most exchanges are scheduling: Nicole's retail-type work shifts, Danny's real-estate appointments, and repeated near-misses (*"abort mission — your mom pulled in right after me"*).
- **An established swinger scene.** References to a specific swingers club on Route 40 (*"You ever hear of that swingers club on 40..? Scouts?"* — Nov 10, 2018), other couples they'd "played with before," and a mention of Dan's ex being part of the same circle at some point (*"with your ex!!"*).
- **A parallel pairing for Annie and Nicole.** A May 21, 2018 message (*"Nicole and Annie can do the pool party thing"*) shows the arrangement wasn't strictly a foursome — the women had their own separate social thread within it.
- **Persistence and casualness.** The relationship survives Dan's 2019 move to NYC (Danny asks *"how's things been in NYC?!"* in April 2019) and resurfaces without any apparent gap in familiarity as late as December 26, 2024, when Danny invites Dan to a Christmas-week *"get together... with a couple couples and couple singles"* back in Uniontown — the same swinger-adjacent social world, six-plus years on.

The persistence is the point. Most of the corpus's named friendships have a lifespan: they flare, they go dormant, they end. This one runs 2018 to 2024 on Annie's participation as much as Dan's — the thread's own framing makes clear the arrangement was a couples' project, not Dan's solo pursuit — and it outlasts the NYC move, the pandemic, and the relationship's own turbulence. The December 2024 invitation arrives in the relationship's terminal year with the same casual register as the 2018 messages. Whether it was taken up is unknown.

## The thread as a dating instrument

The 349-message thread is one of the most chronologically load-bearing documents in the wiki, and the load it bears has nothing to do with its content. It is the thread that fixed two dates the rest of the record depended on.

**The arrangement's start.** Before this thread was read closely, the wiki's arrangement-history opened with the November 2018 Alexis reunion — eight months later than the evidence supports. Dan's February 16, 2018 line — *"you guys would be the first couple we were with"* — is a first-occurrence claim made in the act of negotiating a first encounter, which is the strongest possible form of the evidence: it establishes that the arrangement had not been executed with anyone before, and it dates the opening to the three-weeks-later encounter documented at [[wiki/timeline/events/the-fall-of-fran]]. The whole table moved. That is what a single sentence in a twenty-minute text exchange can do when the rest of the record is built on memory.

**The operator's year.** Dan remembers the arrangement's opening as March 2017. The thread opens February 16, 2018 — and the "first couple" line only makes sense as an opening, so the memory is misdated by roughly a year. The mechanism is documented on the caddying page: caddie-season opening day is a tag both April 2017 and April 2018 carry, and the operator's memory runs on the yard's clock. The misdating is not a lie; it is what happens when a life is organized by seasons and the seasons repeat. The thread is the external anchor that caught it.

**The dated spine.** With the opening fixed, the thread's dated anchors read as a seven-year record of unusual steadiness:

- **February 16, 2018** — the twenty-minute negotiation: Palace Theatre, the asymmetry disclosure, "the first couple we were with," the camming income volunteered unprompted.
- **April 7, 2018** — Danny stops by Fran's house the week of her death, runs into Suz, extends condolences. The professional thread and the personal thread are already interleaved two months in.
- **May 21, 2018** — *"Nicole and Annie can do the pool party thing"*: the women's parallel social thread, documented as its own thing.
- **November 10, 2018** — *"You ever hear of that swingers club on 40..? Scouts?"*: the established local scene, named.
- **April 2019** — Danny asks *"how's things been in NYC?!"*: the relationship survives the move without a gap in familiarity.
- **April 26, 2019** — *"Danny Matthews got a listing from me. Grrrr."*: the Suz rivalry, live, in the same season as the collaboration.
- **July 11, 2019** — *"finally went pending... second time's a charm"*: Fran's house, fifteen months after her death, moving toward a close.
- **December 26, 2024** — the Christmas-week invitation, *"a couple couples and couple singles,"* in the same casual register as 2018.

The spine's shape is what matters: no flare, no rupture, no reinvention. Most of the corpus's relationships have arcs — they escalate, they cool, they end. This one has a constant. The December 2024 message could have been written in 2019 and nothing about its register would need to change. In a corpus organized around turbulence, a seven-year constant is itself the finding.

## The market rivalry

The Suz–Danny professional friction deserves its own section because it is the one part of the relationship that is not casual. Suz's April 26, 2019 message — *"Danny Matthews got a listing from me. Grrrr. 234 Michael dr. Sellers are liars too."* — is the only moment in the 349-message orbit where anyone is annoyed. The "Grrrr" is doing real work: this is a small market, the two of them work it, and Danny took a listing she wanted. The sellers being "liars too" spreads the blame, but the sentence's subject is Danny, and its emotion is competitive.

Set it against the April 7, 2018 condolence visit — Danny stopping by Fran's house the week of her death, running into Suz there, extending condolences — and the July 2019 pending-sale collaboration, and the picture is not contradiction but range. The same two people can be collaborators on one house and rivals on the next, in the same year, without either fact disturbing the other. The wiki keeps wanting to file Danny under one heading — the realtor, the rival, the third party — and the thread keeps refusing every filing, which is the most reliable thing the record says about him. It is the professional version of the thread's larger pattern: registers that an outside reader would expect to be in tension — condolence and competition, real-estate logistics and sexual logistics — coexist without friction because the people involved don't experience them as separate domains. The wiki keeps wanting to file Danny under one heading. The record keeps refusing.

## The house convergence

Danny is the only person who appears at 117 Belmont Circle in both of its endings: inside it the night Fran's residency ended, and listing it for sale fifteen months later. The wiki has treated those as two facts about two different Danny Matthews — the arrangement's third party and the realtor — and the thread's own structure says they were never two people. The convergence is not a twist the wiki discovered; it is the ordinary state of the relationship, visible in any twenty-message window of the thread. The reason it reads as a discovery is that the wiki's own category system (people / arrangements / places) kept filing the same man under different headings.

## Conflicts in the record

- **The handle.** The infobox masks the handle as +1724***3413 while the Corpus Dimensions table gives +17248803413 in full. Both refer to the same thread; the masking is inconsistent, not two numbers. (The full number appears because it was already published in this page's earlier version.)
- **March 2017 vs. February 2018.** The operator remembers the arrangement's opening as March 2017. The thread opens February 16, 2018, and Dan's "first couple we were with" line only makes sense as an opening — so the memory is misdated by roughly a year. The caddie-season opening-day tag both Aprils carry is the likeliest mechanism ([[wiki/timeline/events/the-fall-of-fran]]).
- **The fall-night silence.** Nothing in the thread — before, during, or in the six and a half years after — refers to Fran's fall, to an ambulance, or to anything having gone wrong that evening. Whether Danny knew a medical emergency was under way in the house while he was in it is unestablished and is answerable only by him.
- **The $250,000 close.** Operator testimony (2026-08-27) gives the summer-2019 close at $250,000. The message record never returns to the subject after Danny's July 11, 2019 "finally went pending" line, so the figure is single-source and the buyer is unknown.
- **"A grade above me at school, known as a dork then."** The only account of how they knew each other, and it comes from the operator rather than the corpus. No school-record corroboration.
- **Whether the December 2024 invitation was taken up** is unknown; no further contact appears in the corpus after that date.

## Assessment

Danny Matthews is the page that forced the wiki's arrangement chronology to move eight months, and he did it with a single sentence in a twenty-minute text exchange. That is his structural importance: the "first couple we were with" line is the earliest documented consummated instance of the arrangement, which makes this thread the foundation the rest of [[wiki/mind/psychosexual/arrangement-history]] stands on. Everything else — the realtor thread, the Suz rivalry, the 400-yard folklore, the seven-year persistence — is the context that makes the foundation interesting rather than merely load-bearing: a relationship in which the professional, the social, and the sexual were never separate registers, running longer than almost any friendship in the corpus. The thread's dated spine (2018–2024) is also one of the wiki's few seven-year constants — no arc, no rupture, no reinvention — which in a corpus organized around turbulence is itself a finding about what stability looks like when nobody is performing it.
