# Steve Kezmarsky

Steve Kezmarsky met Dan in 2004 in a Dairy Queen parking lot, where, by
Dan's later account, the first thing Steve said to him was *"you look like
Pete Wentz"* (corpus, Steve thread, 2018-01-31 22:30 ET). What followed was
a fourteen-year Uniontown friendship that survives on paper as a single
year of text messages: 162 of them, January 2017 to February 2018, plus a
handful of mentions in other people's threads and one unanswered Instagram
request in 2022. In that year Steve was about to become a father for the second time,
lost his golf clubs to a garage thief, asked Dan to get him a caddying job,
called himself "a very sick person," watched his father get arrested, and
got a doctor to prescribe him Klonopin. By December 2018, Dan reported, he
was sober.

The friendship was never simple. Steve had dated
[[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis]] before Dan did, and the two men spent
the next decade orbiting that fact — joking about it, resenting it, and in
one January 2018 night turning it into the strangest exchange in the
thread. This page tells that story in order, then prints the complete
record at the bottom.

## The Dairy Queen cohort

Steve belongs to the same Uniontown high-school-age cohort as
[[wiki/people/new-jim-shaffer|Jim Shaffer]],
[[wiki/people/rob-orange|Rob Orange]], [[wiki/people/tom|Tom Maison]] and
[[wiki/people/zach-clingan|Zach Clingan]]. The 2004 meeting date comes only
from Dan's January 2018 retelling; no 2004 record survives in the archive,
and the held iMessage corpus begins in 2011
(`CORPUS_POLICY.md`). The next-earliest trace is a pair of 2013 tweets
Dan addressed to Steve's Twitter handle. One reads *"@skezmarsky I remembr
a 2005 conversation wherein we determined Kanye to be the most racist
person/rapper on earth"* (2013-08-25; [[wiki/self/twitter/2013]]). The
2005 conversation is Dan's memory, not a record. It does place Steve inside
Dan's teenage world a year after the Dairy Queen.

What the cohort shared, on the record, was golf and drugs, often in the
same sentence. In April 2014 Dan told Tom Maison that he and someone else
had *"examined like how steve kezmarsky and zach clingan are 'drug
people' and they're assholes,"* then exempted himself and Tom from the
second half: *"we're assholes and drug people, but not shitty people"*
(`raw/messenger-drive-2026-09-12/messenger_drive_canonical.jsonl`, Tom
Maison thread, 2014-04-21). The same exchange records that Steve, like
Clingan, already had a child — *"so does kezmarsky btw."* The
[[wiki/people/zach-clingan]] page reads that taxonomy closely; what matters
here is that Steve was one of the fixed points Dan used to measure himself.

## Alexis, before and after

The single most important fact about the friendship is one the text thread
never states outright: Steve was Alexis's boyfriend before Dan was. Dan's
2026-09-24 reconstruction of the Alexis origin describes a pre-relationship
argument with her over *"her ex Steve Kezmarsky ('longtime boyfriend who
got her on opiates')"*
(`kb/data/1975-alexis-origin-reconstructed-20260924`). The
[[wiki/timeline/periods/full-sail-2008-2010]] page places that argument in
the fourteen months between first contact in September 2008 and
Thanksgiving 2009, when Alexis went back to Steve for a stretch. The
argument itself rests on Dan's word alone.

The 2014 Tom Maison thread carries Dan's harder account of what that
earlier relationship had been. The appendix records it with its sourcing.
The rest of the Alexis thread through Steve's life is lighter in tone and
better documented:

- **May 2016.** Dan asked Annie, *"Did I ever tell you about the time
  Alexis accidentally sent me pictures of her fucking Steve Kezmarsky?
  Lol"* (corpus, Annie thread, 2016-05-04 21:33 ET;
  `kb/data/1087-steve-kezmarsky-thread-162-rows`).
- **January 2017.** Dan opened the surviving Steve thread by telling him
  that "Lex" had come over the night before, and that he and Annie had
  moved into *"scerba's old place on Virginia Ave"* (Steve thread,
  2017-01-20 21:34 ET). *"Before u ask the answer to your question is
  yes."*
- **May 2017.** Dan passed along that Alexis had *"had to fall on the
  sword for her codefendants"* and taken a plea for house arrest. Steve
  already knew. His read was local and structural: she *"couldn't let
  them start digging and find out where everything was ending up. People
  are too powerful in this town"* (Steve thread, 2017-05-29 14:29–14:30
  ET).
- **January 2018.** Dan told Steve that Alexis was in jail. He had looked
  up her case and seen *"they picked her up around November 1 and she got
  6 months"* (Steve thread, 2018-01-31 23:39 ET). Steve had not heard.
  Dan's follow-up was *"do you care? Oh cool me either."*

So the woman who links the two men appears in the thread as news both of
them consume from a distance. By 2017 neither was with her. What remained
was a running joke, and Dan named it on the night it came due: *"the
classic Steve Kezmarsky opportunity move of fucking/stealing my girl"*
(Steve thread, 2018-01-31 22:03 ET).

## January 2017: "a boy and a girl to carry out my legacy"

The thread opens on 2017-01-20 with Steve trying to sell his TaylorMade M2
driver for $220. Dan answered four hours later: *"Speak of the devil. We
were just talking about our fav father to be stevey Kay kay"* (Steve
thread, 2017-01-20 17:12–21:32 ET). The next half hour is the most candid
stretch of the whole record, and it is worth following closely because it
is the only place Steve describes himself at length.

Steve announced the second child plainly. *"I'll officially have a boy and
a girl to carry out my legacy so I only have 18 years til I can go out in
the ball of fury that everyone always knew that I would"* (2017-01-20
21:40 ET). Dan's first reply was not a joke: *"Hey you should be proud u
have real people responsibilities."* Around that line the register stayed
the friendship's usual one. A minute earlier Steve had said Annie was *"the only one that has a
shot in the dark"* among Dan's circle, and only because *"she's never had
an serious and extensive relationship with me."* Put her around him *"for
a few months"* and she would be *"just as fucked up as the rest of us"* —
*"a drug induced comatose state for 5-10 years"* (21:40–21:43 ET).

Dan pushed him to be proud of being *"evil enough to ruin girls even
without substance."* Steve declined the word. *"Proud? I don't know if
proud is the right word. I'm a very sick person, I do know that. A dark and
tormented soul to say the least"* (21:44–21:45 ET). Dan answered that he
doubted he would want anything to do with Steve if he weren't. Steve's
reply is the thread's one statement about what the friendship was for:

> *"I've always respected the fact that our friendship is based on my*
> *sickness."* (Steve thread, 2017-01-20 21:47 ET)

Dan closed by promising *"our first round this year...you'll be graced
with a good story."* Steve begged to hear it now. Dan held it back. The
record does not show what the story was.

Two things in that exchange are easy to miss. The self-diagnosis is not
performed for sympathy. It arrives inside banter and leaves inside banter.
And the second child is announced in the same breath as a planned
self-destruction at eighteen years' remove. The thread gives no further
detail about the children. It never names them, gives their ages, or
mentions their mother, except for one line in 2018 about "Becky" being on
all of Steve's social media.

## Golf as the currency

Almost every exchange that is not about Alexis or damage is about golf.
It is the reason the friendship has a record at all. The
[[wiki/interests/golf]] page treats this thread as its exhibit of golf as
the connective tissue of Dan's Uniontown male friendships, and the detail
supports it:

- **The M2.** Steve sold his TaylorMade M2 in January 2017, then bought
  the new model by May. *"It's the only club I've ever been able to hit
  straight,"* he said, and Dan agreed he *"didn't hit a bad ball with that
  thing"* (Steve thread, 2017-05-29 14:25–14:26 ET).
- **The theft.** Someone stole Steve's irons and his Scotty Cameron putter
  out of his garage in May 2017 (2017-05-29 14:24–14:25 ET). Dan's
  sympathy came as a matching story: his own Ghost Monte Carlo putter had
  been *"jacked because I had a Scotty cover on it."*
- **The discount.** Dan had just started at Mystic Rock. He offered
  friend rounds at $50 once a month from July 12, and 40% off clubs at
  *"the golf academy up there"* (2017-04-04 00:47 ET; 2017-05-29 14:32
  ET). Steve asked him to price last year's TaylorMade PSi irons (14:35
  ET). The thread never shows the price coming back.
- **The longing.** *"Dude I wanna play golf so bad"* (2017-05-29 16:01
  ET), four minutes after Dan pulled in from a round. *"So jealous."*

Dan also sent him a photo of a CD he had found, by *"marc motherfuckin
umbel"* (2017-05-29 15:59 ET), and in July name-dropped a hockey player he
had caddied for and was about to play with. In April 2017 he proposed a
*"monthly luncheon so we can get on the same page about who we
collectively hate and how much we hate them"* (2017-04-04 00:48 ET). None
of these plans shows up again. The record holds no round that the two of
them actually played in 2017.

## July 2017: "get me in"

On 2017-07-12 Steve texted *"Yo I wanna start caddying get me in!"* Dan
answered four days later with the Nemacolin application link. He told Steve
to list him as a reference and said he would tell his boss to get him in.
The pitch was money: *"i haven't made less than $250 in tips any time i've
worked the new course"* (Steve thread, 2017-07-16 13:34–13:35 ET).

Half an hour later, Steve asked whether Dan knew *"anyone that ever has any
vikes or anything small."* Dan's answer, three hours later, was one word:
*"nah"* (2017-07-16 14:04, 17:12 ET). A week on, Dan checked in:
*"Did you still want to try looping. Or was that just the icebreaker on a
hydrocodone recovery mission"* (2017-07-23 15:16 ET). Steve said he still
wanted to and hadn't got to a computer to upload his resume. Dan urged him
to apply within the week, before the August training class, and promised to
*"talk to Brad"* ([[wiki/people/brad-hubeaut]]). Steve's last word on it
was *"Ok cool."* The resume never appears. The
[[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddying]] page files Steve as one of the era's
might-have-beens.

The "vikes" line places Steve on the buying edge of the Uniontown informal
supply world described in [[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]]. Nothing
in the record makes him a supplier, and Dan declined him.

## January 2018: the week the family broke

Six months of silence follow. Then, on 2018-01-22, Annie texted Dan from
work:

> *"They picked up mr Kezmarsky today"* / *"He's in jail"* / *"84 counts*
> *of felonies"* / *"Over some dead bodies 😂"* (corpus, Annie thread,
> 2018-01-22 11:48–11:49 ET)

Dan asked what she meant. *"The money from the funeral home lol,"* she
wrote, and went into work (11:53 ET). That is the whole of the record's
account of the charges: a secondhand text, typed in a hurry, with a
laughing emoji. It does not name Steve's father, the funeral home, the
statute or the outcome. `kb/data/1089-elder-kezmarsky-fall-jan-apr-2018`
verifies the arrest message and the later Jim Shaffer message. It files
the offense as unnamed, which is right for what Annie's text can support.

Eight days later Dan wrote *"Yo brother."* Steve answered *"Who's this.
Sorry I lost a bunch of numbers"* (Steve thread, 2018-01-30 11:33–11:41
ET). The next night Dan sent a photo, and Steve still didn't know who it
was from. When Dan identified himself, Steve wrote, *"Lol I need some
interest in my life. It's been a rough week"* (2018-01-31 21:16 ET). Dan
said he'd heard and asked how everyone was holding up. *"Eh we're holdin
bro."*

What Dan sent next was, in his framing, *"a goodwill gesture"*: intimate
images and video involving Annie and Alexis, delivered with rules attached.
Steve was to keep it to himself and not start messaging Annie. Dan was
explicit that Steve's week was the reason. *"That was honestly a
consideration in our discussion of 'who should we confuse with noodz?'"*
(2018-01-31 22:01 ET). The page records the exchange at that level and no
further. Its detail concerns two women who are not parties to this
thread. Its relevance here is what it shows about the two men.

Three things are in it. First, Steve's actual state, stated between Dan's
messages: *"All I know is that it took this happening for a dr to finally
prescribe me kolonopin"* (22:04 ET), and a few minutes later, *"Yeah dude
my life is in shambles"* (22:13 ET). Second, the friendship's origin, which
survives only because Dan used it as a flourish: *"Since the day we met in
2004 in a Dairy Queen parking lot and you said 'you look like Pete Wentz'
both of our lives have led to this moment"* (22:30 ET). Third, a sincere
line about Annie, sent to the one friend with a history of taking Dan's
girlfriends: *"Because Real talk I'm marrying this one. She's equal parts
honest and dirty."* Steve: *"Yes you would be an idiot if you don't"*
(22:24–22:25 ET).

Steve's last message in the thread is *"Hahaha my man"* (2018-01-31 23:42
ET). Dan sent five more over the next three days, and Steve answered none
of them. The last, at 3:33 in the morning on 2018-02-04, reads *"You should
really shoot me a call when you see this."* After that the thread is
empty. Everything later about Steve comes secondhand.

## Inside Dan and Annie's house

The same fortnight shows Steve as a subject between Dan and Annie. On
2018-01-19, three days before the arrest, Dan wrote Annie a clarification.
He was serious about letting her be with other people, he said, but *"the
Steve stuff is just fun dirty talk on my end"* because *"there is
sooooo000oOooo much bad blood and history there."* He then listed the
exceptions: *"Ex boyfriends / Zach Clingan/Steve Kezmarsky / Coworkers /
Drug dealers"* (corpus, Annie thread, 2018-01-19 00:47–00:55 ET;
`kb/data/1213-zach-clingan-caddie-yard-supply-inversion`).

Within two weeks the list had moved. Dan's messages to Annie between
2018-02-01 and 2018-02-11 return to Steve repeatedly. They report what he
told Steve on the 31st and weigh whether the rule should bend. On February
11 Dan wrote that it was *"kind of disconcerting to just have you snap back
to before as soon as I stop bringing Steve up"* (Annie thread, 2018-02-11
00:13 ET). The record carries only Dan's side of these particular lines.
It never shows Steve re-entering the thread. Whatever the negotiation was
about, it went on without him.

The page records this because it is the only evidence of how much space
Steve took up in Dan's head in early 2018. The two were not close that
winter. Steve had lost Dan's number. Yet Steve was the named limit case in
Dan's own house rules. "Bad blood and history" is Dan's phrase, and it
points back to Alexis.

## April and December 2018: told to Jim

The rest of Steve's 2018 is told to Jim Shaffer. On 2018-04-22 Dan asked
Jim, *"did you hear about mr.kezmarsky,"* added *"he made quite the legal
oopsie goof,"* and attached an image. Jim: *"Yes. He's fucked."* Dan: *"he
deserves a malignant tumor too"* and *"i wish he got smashed by a drunk
driver on new year's eve"* (corpus, Jim Shaffer thread, 2018-04-22
20:56–20:58 ET). The attachment is not stored, so the record cannot show
what it was. Dan's hostility toward the elder Kezmarsky is plain in the
wording. Its origin is not in the record.

Jim's next messages describe a legal ordeal of his own: an investment he
says he was owed, a *Herald-Standard* article he calls a smear, a $55,000
attorney, and lawsuits to come (Jim Shaffer thread, 2018-04-22 20:59–21:09
ET). Dan had opened that stretch by saying he was glad Jim was *"gonna come
out okay."* Whether Jim's case had anything to do with the Kezmarsky
matter, the record does not say. The [[wiki/people/new-jim-shaffer]] page
covers Jim's side.

Eight months later, on 2018-12-09, the two old friends spent a Sunday
morning on the cohort's history. They remembered drinking cough syrup until
they vomited red dye, driving at triple the speed limit to Tom Petty
concerts they had no tickets for, and Rob Orange. *"rob is the only one
that I get sad about,"* Dan wrote. *"Half of our friends died lol. And it's
just like here we are"* (Jim Shaffer thread, 2018-12-09 11:52–11:55 ET).
Then Jim asked the question that brought Steve back into the record:

> **Jim:** *"How did Steve Kezmarski make the cut?"*
> **Dan:** *"That, my good friend, is simply a gift from the lord for you*
> *and I"* / *"'Let him live...but he'll be sober and wait till you see what*
> *is gonna happen to his family'"*
> **Jim:** *"Wait... he's dead?"*
> **Dan:** *"No no I mean"* / *"He's alive but living the least enviable*
> *life EVER"* / *"two kids"* / *"Sober"* / *"Dad going to jail for the*
> *rest of his life"* / *"Family in ruins"*
> (Jim Shaffer thread, 2018-12-09 11:57–11:59 ET)

"Make the cut" meant survival. Steve made the list of the cohort's
survivors, not its dead. The earlier version of this page misread the
exchange the way Jim briefly did. The retained correction is in the
appendix, and `kb/data/1088-steve-kezmarsky-alive-correction-2018-12-09`
verifies it. Dan's report is also the only evidence for three facts: Steve
got sober, his father was expected to spend life in prison, and the family
was "in ruins." All three are Dan's secondhand summary, delivered as a
punchline. The record does not say how Dan knew.

It is still the thread's most human outcome, stated in the least humane
register available. The man who in January 2017 called himself sick, and
the friendship a function of it, was by December 2018 sober and raising two
children through the worst year his family had. Dan framed it as divine
punishment. The facts inside the joke are the ones any friend would want
to know.

## After 2018: two platforms and one question

Steve does not appear in the iMessage corpus again after December 2018. He
turns up twice on other platforms:

- **October 2021.** Dan's Facebook address-book export lists "Steve
  Kezmarsky" with the same phone number, synced 2021-10-16. An earlier
  2016-01-05 entry lists a second number and a Gmail address
  (`raw/facebook/export-20260908-a/gdocs/1zwYgKnGJX_LB8O8SRZgvoBrxL4lIQdvWk-N1nfNocoQ.from-gdoc.txt`).
  That is a contact card, not contact.
- **January 2022.** Steve sent Dan an Instagram message request:
  *"Yooooo do you still live in NY?"* (2022-01-19 18:04 ET;
  `raw/instagram/export-20250824/.../stevekezmarsky_761211931949464/message_1.json.from-cli-json.txt`).
  The export marks the request `is_pending: true`. It was still sitting
  unaccepted in August 2025, more than three and a half years later. The
  Messenger ledger carries the same single message and no reply
  (`raw/messenger-drive-2026-09-12/messenger_drive_canonical.jsonl`).

That is where the record ends. Steve reached out once, knowing Dan had
moved to New York in 2019, and asked a friendly question. The archive shows
no answer. It cannot show whether Dan ever saw the request, only that the
platform recorded it as unaccepted. Four years after Dan's last unanswered
message to Steve, the silence ran the other way.

## What the record shows and does not show

**Documented.** One continuous iMessage thread of 162 rows, 2017-01-20 to
2018-02-04, with 66 messages from Steve and 96 from Dan
(`kb/data/1087-steve-kezmarsky-thread-162-rows`). Also: third-party
mentions in the Annie thread (2016, 2018), the Jim Shaffer thread (2018),
and the Tom Maison Messenger thread (2014). Two 2013 tweets. One Facebook
contact entry. One Instagram message request.

**Secondhand.** Every fact about Steve after February 2018 comes from Dan
telling Jim: the sobriety, the two children as a present fact, the life
sentence and the family's ruin. So does every fact about the father's
charges, which comes from Annie's text. The 2004 Dairy Queen meeting comes
from Dan alone, fourteen years after the fact.

**Not in the record.** Steve's age, occupation, and where he lived. The
children's names, and the identity of "Becky." His father's first name,
offense, case number and sentence. Whether Steve and Dan ever golfed
together after 2016. What the "good story" was. Whether Dan saw the 2022
Instagram request. No photograph of Steve is held in `media/`. The
iMessage attachments Dan sent in January 2018 are referenced by the corpus
but not stored (`CORPUS_POLICY.md`).

**Inferred.** That Steve's "it took this happening" means his father's
arrest. The timing is eight days, and Dan's "I heard brother" points that
way, but Steve never names it. That the "Lex" of 2017 is Alexis Armel.
Context and the full-sail period page make that near-certain. That the
2022 Instagram account is the same Steve. The display name is exact and the
question fits, but no second identifier links them.
