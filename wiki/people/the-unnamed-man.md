At 00:25 on July 26, 2026, Dan's phone lit up with a message from Annie's
number that was not from Annie. It said: "She's a slut man lmfao." The
held-corpus verification of the July window confirms that timestamp to the
minute, and the exchange that followed over the next morning is the only
direct contact between the two men in the July record — about twenty minutes
of it, in a register Dan would still be quoting five weeks later. The man on
the other end of Annie's phone is the person she left Dan for, the person she
says raped her on July 25, the person whose continued presence organises
every hostile hour of the eight days that follow — and the person Dan decided,
on the record, twice, not to learn the name of. That decision held for
thirteen days, under extreme provocation, and it is the most interesting
thing on this page: the one node in Dan's life he has decided on the record
not to identify, inside a corpus whose operator's defining behaviour is to
identify everything. The identification eventually came anyway — a FOREWARN
lookup at 19:01:22 on August 8, confirmed the next day to be the same man as
Tuquick, the May/June interloper — and the canonical entity page is now
[[wiki/people/jerel-coles]]. This page is retained because the
refusal-to-investigate finding is attached to this path, and the finding
survived the identification: it now carries a termination date rather than a
retraction.

The page is written from a single vantage: Annie's statements about him, and
one direct exchange in which he wrote to Dan from her phone. Nothing here is
independently corroborated. He should be read as a documented *presence* with
documented *effects* — the effects are heavily evidenced — rather than as a
person the corpus has established facts about.

## The night he entered the record: July 25–26

Everything the record holds about this man postdates the 00:25 message by
hours. The held-corpus verification (kb/data/0358) checks the opener
verbatim — "She's a slut man lmfao" — against contemporaneous platform
timestamps, and it is the strongest verification tier this wiki gets for a
night otherwise sourced from exports that are no longer held.

The exchange that follows the next morning runs about twenty minutes and is
the only direct contact in the July corpus. His register is short,
contemptuous, and aimed with precision at Dan's specific vulnerabilities:
unemployment ("Time to get a job buddy"), sexual humiliation ("The cuck never
gives up," "Can't get anyone else"), and Annie's escort history ("Had her
fuck old men for drugs"). He then makes the accusation that structures the
following week: "I have video proof how you fingered her for hours while she
was unconscious."

On Dan's account, the harassment that night ran for hours, and Annie was
present for it and endorsed it in real time: "you literally told him you were
choosing him in the middle of it all happening." That sentence is load-bearing
for everything that follows in the eight-day window — it is the moment Dan
reads her as having taken the man's side while he watched.

The same morning, he took the transcript. Dan published the full text log at
`caakehorn.github.io/leviathan/transcript.html` — published at 05:36 on July
26 and sent to Annie — and the man read it from her phone and forwarded it to
himself. This is what caused the publication to be retracted within five
minutes of Annie's request: the only documented instance of the forensic
apparatus being turned against its author. See
[[wiki/mind/concepts/forensic-method]].

Two things about that morning deserve to be stated together. First, the man's
twenty minutes on the phone is the *only* unmediated trace of him in the July
record — everything else about him in the window arrives through Annie's
account, which means everything about him is somebody's account of him.
Second, he used those twenty minutes not to hide but to do damage with
information he could only have gotten by going through her phone: the
escort-history detail, the "video proof" claim, and the transcript
exfiltration all require him to have read Annie's private material and
weaponised it in real time. That is a behaviour, not a claim about
character — and it is the same behaviour, documented on tape, that appears
three weeks later on the August 16 call, where he admits on a recording that
he went through her phone while she slept.

## What Annie says he did to her

Recorded as her claims. They are consistent across eight days and she never
retracts them, but the corpus holds no corroboration and this page adjudicates
nothing.

That he broke her finger, two months before July 2026, in an injury the
[[wiki/timeline/events/july-2026-recontact|re-contact]] page also records. That
he raped her on July 25 after reading her texts with Dan. That he came to her
uncle's bedroom window while she was sleeping on the night of July 25–26,
woke her, poured water over her, threw her belongings around the room, and
filmed himself in her face asking "did I rape you." That he took her phone and
ran, which is the call the police actually attended. That he later "admitted
he did what he did." That she would press charges "if I felt it was necessary
AT ANY POINT" — and that as of August 2 she had made no report and taken no
rape kit, which Dan raised with her inside the seventy-two-hour window and did
not get an answer to.

Annie's parents, on her account, know what he has done and do not want her
near him. She nonetheless spoke to him again on July 27 — the day after
promising Dan she never would — and her explanation each time is contact
initiated by him: "HE CONTACTED ME," "He came as I was sleeping / And knocked
on my bedroom window."

The consistency of the claims across eight days is itself a datum: she never
varies the account while under pressure from both men in opposite directions.
But consistency is not corroboration, and this page does not treat it as such.
What the July record establishes about these claims is their *function*: they
are the reason Dan keeps re-engaging (the rape allegation, the broken finger,
the filmed window visit), and they are the currency Annie spends when she
needs him to understand her as endangered. Whether they are true is not
something the July record can settle, and the August record does not settle it
either — see [[wiki/people/jerel-coles]] on the four positions the corpus now
holds on the same claim, none of them scored.

## He telephoned Suz

This is the act with the longest consequences. The man telephoned
[[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]] to abuse her about a relationship she had no
part in, and Dan's assessment of it — "the call to my mom was enough of a red
flag alone. Annie NOBODY would do that, it's literally crazy and noone did
anything to this dude. Noone knew he existed" — is the closest the record
comes to a characterological read. Dan's own account of the asymmetry of the
campaign is precise: "the actual insults themselves weren't really effective.
With her, it's all I heard about for months."

It is the injury that Annie eventually walked to the house on August 2 to
apologise for — in person, to Suz directly, the first repair either party
made to anyone outside the relationship in the entire terminal record. That
apology is the terminal fact of the re-entanglement window, and it is worth
holding the scale of it: the window began with an accusation of attempted
assault and ended with a young woman walking to her ex-partner's mother's
house to apologise for the harassment her current partner had directed at
that mother. The man's call is what converted a private catastrophe into a
family matter, and the apology is what converted it back.

Note the merge consequence, because this page predates the merge and the
record now reads differently than it did in August: the three-hour
harassment call to Dan's mother attributed to Tuquick roughly five days
before the June 1 End Fight, and the July-window call attributed to this
page's subject, are now the same man operating the same instrument twice —
telephone harassment aimed at the mother, outside the relationship, as a way
to wound the son. Before 2026-08-09 they were two data points about two men;
after the confirmation they are one campaign. See
[[wiki/people/jerel-coles]] for the canonical account, including his written
comment on the matter — the closest thing to an admission the record holds.

## The switch in the eight days: July 26 – August 2

The [[wiki/timeline/events/july-august-2026-reentanglement|re-entanglement]]
page establishes the man's structural role in the window with a claim this
page carries as its headline: **every hostile phase across the eight days is
triggered by evidence of his continued presence in Annie's life, and every
de-escalation follows Annie's assertion that he is gone.** He is the switch
the window runs on.

The window is the densest contact period of 2026: 1,880 messages in eight
days, six in-person meetings — five of them drug handoffs, the sixth the
apology visit to Suz. Both parties know the arrangement is procurement and say
so. Dan states the terms three separate times ("stop contacting me if you want
me as your friend or dealer or anything other than wanting to be together")
and then supplies anyway, every time he is asked, within hours. And through
all of it, the temperature of the exchange tracks one variable: whether he is
present or gone.

The trace of that variable is dated. On July 27, the day after promising Dan
she would never speak to the man again, Annie spoke to him again — and her
explanation, "HE CONTACTED ME," is the form the switch takes all week: she
reports contact initiated by him, Dan escalates, she asserts he is gone, Dan
de-escalates. The pattern is not subtle once seen. The man's continued
presence is not merely an irritant inside the window; it is the organising
axis of the window's hostility, the thing every fight is about, and the thing
every reconciliation requires her to claim is over.

The last reference to him in the window is an ambiguous incident on the
evening of August 1, which Annie attributes to an accidental call from her
recent-calls list. Whether the harassment campaign against Dan and Suz
continued past August 1 is unknown; that ambiguous incident is the last trace,
and ambiguity is all it is. What is known is that the window ends the next day
with the apology to Suz — the man's longest-consequence act, repaired in
person by the woman he committed it on behalf of, while the man himself is
absent from the repair.

## The afterlife of a phrase

Five weeks later, the July 26 insult register was still in the record. On
August 23, Annie's handle produced "She's a slut man lmfao" again — the exact
phrase from the 00:25 opener, returned to the held corpus as a fresh row. Two
days later, on August 25 at 18:46, Dan answered it in his own voice: "Also is
this motherfucker THAT stupid and uncreative that 'she's a slut man lmfao'
is the only thing he can come up with because I've heard that like 200 times
and he's pretty much insulting himself more than me at this point."

That exchange is worth carrying on this page for two reasons. First, it is
Dan's own testimony that the man's July register had become a fixture — "I've
heard that like 200 times" — which says something about the volume and
repetition of the campaign across the summer that the July window alone does
not show. Second, it demonstrates the handle ambiguity that shadows every
attribution on this page: the August 23 row arrived from Annie's number, and
the corpus by then had documented the man operating her handle three times —
July 26, August 16, and August 18, always during a crisis, always producing
content of exactly that register (see [[wiki/people/jerel-coles]] §the seizure
pattern). Who wrote the August 23 row is not established by the held corpus;
that it is unattributable without context is itself the finding. The twenty
minutes of July 26 remain the only direct contact with the man in the July
record precisely because every other trace of him arrives through a channel he
is documented to operate.

## The refusal to know

Twice in six days Dan states that he is deliberately not finding out who this
man is.

> "I don't even want to know their fucking name and have made a point to not
> even find out." (July 26, 18:09)

> "you do understand that the reason I won't even learn who this is, is
> because I do not want to live my life with the HATRED that I would feel
> towards one person… I want to fuck this person's life up so badly and I
> haven't because I'm not going to do something stupid and illegal to
> accomplish it." (July 28, 23:45–23:46)

This is worth stating as a finding rather than a curiosity. The operator's
defining behaviour, across every domain this wiki documents, is total
instrumentation: 181,585 archived messages, a browser history mined for
relational traces, a reconstructed discography, dashboards built from his own
text logs, and this repository. [[wiki/mind/concepts/forensic-method]] is
described elsewhere as a general disposition — the thing he does to any
question that will hold still.

Here it does not fire, and the reason given is not that the answer is
unobtainable. Uniontown is small, Annie's family knows the man, and Dan had
her phone contents published on a website he controlled. The reason is that
**the knowledge is judged to carry a cost he has decided not to pay** — a
hatred with a face, and the risk of acting on it. The method has an off
switch, it is under deliberate control, and its criterion is emotional
consequence rather than difficulty or relevance.

That is a real limit on how the corpus should describe him. Statements like
"Dan documents everything" and "the forensic engine runs on whatever is put
in front of it" are now known to be false in at least one case, and the
exception was chosen, announced, and held for at least eight days under
extreme provocation.

It also has a practical consequence for this repository: the most
consequential live antagonist in the corpus is likely to stay unnamed, because
the person who would ordinarily identify him is the person who has decided not
to.

### Why the refusal is credible

The strongest evidence that the refusal was real rather than performed is
that the knowledge was *available the whole time*. Dan had the man's
messages on Annie's number, he had Annie herself as a source, he had
published her phone contents to a website under his own control — any of
those would have yielded a name in an afternoon. A man who announces he is
not investigating while holding the materials that would close the
investigation is not protecting an information gap; he is protecting himself
from information he already has access to. That is what makes the off-switch
a deliberate instrument rather than a limitation: the method could have fired
at any point and did not, because the operator held the switch open.

### The thirteen days

The refusal was declared on July 26 at 18:09 — less than eighteen hours after
the man's first message — restated on July 28 at 23:45, held thirteen days,
and ended on August 8 at 19:01:22 with the FOREWARN lookup. The provocation
it was held under is worth enumerating, because "extreme provocation" needs
receipts: the hours of July 25–26 harassment with Annie's real-time
endorsement; the transcript exfiltration and the retracted publication; the
July 27 contact the day after her promise never to speak to him again; the
eight days in which every fight tracked his presence; the ambiguous August 1
incident; and, on the same day the refusal ended, the collapse of the
re-entanglement window itself. He held the line through all of it and then
broke it thirteen days later — not under greater provocation, but under a
different kind of load. The working hypothesis on the canonical page is that
the threat model shifted from *historical antagonist* to *believed present
tonight*: the off-switch held while the man was a past-tense injury and
failed when he became a present-tense threat. That hypothesis is not yet
tested against the August 2–8 logs, and it is filed as open.

## The termination: August 8–9

At **19:01:22 on August 8, 2026**, Dan ran the FOREWARN background lookup. The
capture's own frontmatter is explicit about its target: `target:
wiki/people/tuquick` — the May/June interloper handle, identified by the exact
match between the lookup's mobile number and the phone number in the Tuquick
page's own title. The lookup identified Tuquick as Jerel Wayne Coles, 36, of
Uniontown. **That closed Tuquick's identity. It did not by itself identify
this page's subject** — no phone number independently attributed to the July
2026 man appears in the capture, and none of the lookup's numbers appears in
the July message exports. The capture aimed at one handle and hit it.

Thirty-seven minutes later, at 19:38:34, the first contact of the unmasking
night arrived: "hi you." The five hours that followed are documented at
[[wiki/timeline/events/august-2026-unmasking]]: the inference of the third
party from silence ("your big romantic Saturday night plans," 21:22:17),
the assertion ("if you even maintained contact with the person trying to
fuck my life up," 22:40:49), and then the name entering the message record
at 00:32:25 — "keep the stupidest lies for Jerel" — five and a half hours
after the lookup. The stated cost of knowing had been "a hatred with a face."
The original revision block on this page instructs that the five hours be
read as the resolution of that forecast rather than a discovered failure of
it — and the record bears that out: the name, once known, is used the same
night as a weapon in a confrontation ("HE'LL rape you again," 23:10:38),
exactly the instrumentalisation the refusal had been holding off.

On **August 9**, the operator confirmed directly: "They are the same person
— tuquick and unnamed." The corpus had carried them as two separate people
from August 2 until that correction — seven days of double-booked
antagonism — and the confirmation collapsed them. The next day the canonical
entity page was established at [[wiki/people/jerel-coles]], and this page was
retained for the refusal finding rather than merged out of existence.

## The merge: why "two people" became one

> **SUPERSEDED [2026-08-09].** The two-person model — Tuquick, the May/June
> interloper, and this page's subject, the July 2026 antagonist, as separate
> men — was superseded by direct operator confirmation on 2026-08-09. It was
> held from 2026-08-02 to 2026-08-09 and was never more than the absence of a
> closing identification; no positive evidence ever separated them. Nothing
> written under the two-person model is deleted by this pointer.

What was on file before the confirmation was circumstantial: both men
occupying the "who Annie left/was with" role six weeks apart in the same
small town, and the same "cuck" framing — unemployment, sexual humiliation,
escort history — across the Tuquick End Fight material of June 1 and the
unnamed man's July 26 messages from Annie's phone. The insult vocabulary
overlaps at a level that is difficult to attribute to coincidence: the same
three attack surfaces, in the same order of contempt, six weeks apart. That
was suggestive on its own, not identifying — no phone number, photo, or
third-party statement closed it independently the way the FOREWARN mobile
number closed Tuquick=Coles.

What closes it is Dan's own statement, which this wiki treats as
authoritative on facts about his own life that the message corpus cannot
independently verify — the same standing given the 2026-07-13 Tuquick
correction ("he is who Annie left Dan for," correcting the earlier
"stranger inserted into the situation" framing) and the 2026-08-02
twenty-four-hour-switch correction. The circumstantial case is not
superseded by that; it becomes corroboration, and it is worth keeping on the
page because it explains *why* the merge is unsurprising rather than only
*that* it happened.

This page keeps the two evidentiary bases visible rather than collapsing
them, because they are not the same kind of proof. Tuquick=Coles closes on an
exact identifier match — a phone number in a lookup against a phone number
in a page title. Tuquick=unnamed-man closes on the operator's word about his
own life. The second is held to the wiki's standard for such statements, but
it is not the same thing as an independent identifier, and the page does not
pretend it is. Anyone reading this merge should know exactly which part of
it rests on a number and which part rests on a sentence.

### What the identification revealed

The FOREWARN capture returned 64 records, collapsed by the wiki's
de-duplication to nine incident clusters spanning 2008–2025: traffic matters,
a 2008 disorderly-conduct/fighting guilty plea, a 2009 harassment conviction
for subjecting another person to physical contact (**guilty**), two DUIs
(2019, 2024), and a 2025 criminal-mischief/property-damage charge. The
capture is a commercial background-check aggregation, **not independently
docket-verified** — every claim carries that qualifier until a PA UJS portal
search upgrades it.

What the record corroborates: an adjudicated willingness to put hands on
another person, a willingness to fight in public, escalating alcohol
involvement, and — closest in time and kind to what Annie describes in July
2026 — property destruction in April 2025, the behavioural neighbour of
throwing her belongings around a room. What it does *not* corroborate: no
assault charge, no sexual offence, no violent felony, no PFA, nothing
supporting the rape allegation, nothing supporting the broken finger. That
absence is weak evidence in both directions — most intimate-partner conduct
of that class never reaches a docket — and the page says so. The
identification resolved *who he is*; it did not resolve the truth of
contested claims about *what he did*. Full record:
[[wiki/people/jerel-coles]].

## Assessment

This page is now a closed episode with a surviving finding. The episode:
an antagonist entered the record at 00:25 on July 26, 2026, through someone
else's phone; organised eight days of hostility as the single variable the
window tracked; was refused identification for thirteen days by an operator
who identifies everything; was identified anyway on August 8 and confirmed
the next day to be the May/June interloper, Jerel Wayne Coles; and acquired
a primary record on tape and in writing between August 16 and 19 that
corroborated his conduct while leaving the July claims exactly as
corroborated as they were. The canonical entity page for the man is
[[wiki/people/jerel-coles]]; this page is the record of the refusal, its
thirteen-day life, its termination date, and what the confirmation did and
did not change.

The finding survives the identification. The forensic method has a deliberate
off switch, it is under the operator's control, and its criterion is
emotional consequence — a hatred with a face — rather than difficulty or
relevance. The exception that [[wiki/mind/concepts/forensic-method]] carried
is now closed: the switch existed, was held for thirteen days under
provocation, and failed under a specific, not-yet-identified load. That is a
narrower and more interesting statement than the one this page opened with,
and it is the statement the record now supports.

What remains genuinely open is proportionate to what was never knowable from
this vantage: the relationship's history, the truth of the contested claims,
the evidentiary grade of a merge that rests on a sentence rather than a
number, and the one question the August record made newly askable — whether
the "video proof" accusation that structured the last week of July originated
with him or with her. The name arrived. The answers did not all arrive with
it.
