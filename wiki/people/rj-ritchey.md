# RJ Ritchey

[[wiki/people/index|people]] · [[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddying]] · [[wiki/interests/golf]]

RJ Ritchey was a Fayette County golfer and caddie who spent fifteen months in
Dan and [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie's]] orbit, came to New York once, and
talked to Dan about golf, work, drugs and politics until the thread stopped in
May 2020. He knew the same people Dan knew — [[wiki/people/teddy|Teddy]],
[[wiki/people/jack-rusko|Jack Rusko]] — followed the caddying world, and was
the kind of friend who is close enough to be invited into something and far
enough away to say no to it.

He said no to something in February 2019, and the friendship did not end. That
is the shape of the relationship and it is unusual enough in this corpus to
state first: most of the ruptures documented here resume in under a minute or
never resolve at all. This one produced a real boundary, was absorbed, and ran
on for another fifteen months.

He is also, on the evidence, the person Dan talked to when he wanted to say
something sincere about politics — which is not what the previous version of
this page said, and not what anyone reading the February 2019 exchange would
predict.

## February 2019: the boundary

The iMessage thread opens **2019-02-12**, and it opens in the middle of
something. Dan and Annie's non-monogamy arrangement had been an active
practice since February 2018 ([[wiki/mind/psychosexual/arrangement-history]]),
and RJ was being invited into it.

What the record shows is a man saying yes in the abstract and no in the
particular. He said repeatedly that he did not want to betray Dan, that he did
not want to offend either of them, and that he had discovered he was not
comfortable with the situation once it stopped being hypothetical. He was
particularly alarmed by the possibility that he had been recorded, and asked
Dan not to put any video online. The messages do not establish a complete
account of the encounter. They establish that he was curious, that he turned
up, and that he then withdrew rather than perform an enthusiasm he did not
have.

**On 2019-02-19 the arrangement was formally closed, and the person who closed
it was Annie.** Two *"It's over"* messages sit on this thread, both outbound
from Dan's handle, and both were **typed by Annie from Dan's phone**: the
exchange opens *"Hey it's Annie"*, and RJ asks, reasonably, *"why are you
texting me from Dan's phone"*
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/severance-language-atlas]]). The fuller of the two
reads: *"It's over, I wouldn't have even brought it up if I weren't concerned
that you were mad or thought I was mad."*

That is the corrected frame, and it matters for how the whole first month
reads. The previous version of this page described a withdrawal by RJ. The
severance atlas, working from the held corpus in September 2026, shows the
closing statement coming from the other direction — and shows it being a
reassurance rather than a rejection. Somebody was worried somebody else was
angry. The arrangement was ended so that nobody would have to be.

The atlas files this thread as its clearest *handle-is-not-a-person* case: a
declaration counted in Dan's outbound column that Dan did not write. For a
page about RJ the point is smaller and more human. He was the third party in
an arrangement between two people, he got a text from one of them on the
other's phone, and he asked why.

## The months between: talk, distance, and the plan to visit

The withdrawal did not end the friendship, and the record of the following
months is mostly a man describing the couple's life from outside it.

He talked about coming to the city. He asked for pictures. He talked about
golf and about work. He reacted to the couple's paid-companion plans with
concern about the cost and the pressure of them — interested, sometimes
teasing, and consistently more risk-averse than the people living it. The
register is that of someone watching a life he is partly attracted to and
partly alarmed by, and saying both things.

The trip took real logistical work, and the thread is unusually clear about
it because RJ had no money and said so. He had reduced hours at **the
Maywood** while waiting to hear back from **Nemacolin HR**. He needed to
coordinate a ride to Pittsburgh. He asked Dan to **book** the bus ticket
rather than reimburse him for it — a request that only makes sense from
someone who could not float the cost. [[wiki/people/teddy|Teddy]] became a
point of contact around the travel and supplies.

Teddy's role here is the one place the supply network and the friendship
network visibly touch. In July 2019 Teddy vouched for Dan to an anxious third
party — telling RJ that Dan would not strand him with a one-way ticket and
that Dan had the money to follow through ([[wiki/people/teddy]]). Teddy's own
page draws the right inference from it: Teddy knew Dan as someone who had left
Uniontown and could sponsor a trip, not only as a buyer.

RJ worried about the ticket being one-way, became combative about it, and
apologised afterwards. The held corpus preserves the moment the anxiety turned
into a plan. On **2019-06-28**, inbound:

> *"Lol good shit dude, I can't imagine what that might be. I'm going to look into bus tickets/figure out how to get up there and I trust you have full co[verage]"*

([`dat:0983`](../../kb/data/0983-rj-ritchey-thread-dimensions.md), quoted
verbatim from `corpus/messages.csv`.) *I trust you* is the operative clause. A
week later he got on the bus.

## July 2019: the visit

RJ boarded from Pittsburgh on **2019-07-05** and sent live updates as the bus
came into Manhattan. He described seeing the skyline through clouds. He was
confused by the bus route. He wanted video from a night of skating to show his
mother. He left for Fayette County on **2019-07-08**.

Three days, and the record of them is almost entirely about first sight. This
was not a symbolic reference to New York — it was a man who had never been
there, being shown it by someone who had moved there four months earlier. The
timing is worth noticing: Dan's own overnight-ping record puts the physical
move at **3–5 March 2019** ([[wiki/self/location-history]]), and the
apartment at [[wiki/places/307-e-76th-st|307 E 76th Street]] had been leased
on 25 February. RJ arrived four months into a six-year tenancy, at the point
where the new life was new enough to be worth demonstrating.

His messages frame Dan as the person who could show him a place he had never
seen. They frame Dan and Annie as a couple whose plans and resources he had to
trust from a distance — which he said, in those words, a week before he
travelled.

## Golf, the Maywood, and the caddie world

RJ's baseline world was Fayette County golf and contingent work, and it is the
subject the relationship keeps returning to across both platforms and all
fifteen months.

He discussed his father. He discussed country-club rounds and the footage he
took of them. He discussed the possibility of returning to caddying, and
reported that a Nemacolin manager had been fired amid a narcotics rumour. He
described limited work hours and the importance of keeping them while he
waited for caddying to resume.

This is the same world [[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddying]] documents from Dan's
side: a resort caddie operation with a visible status ladder, off-property
day trips to prestige courses as the marker of having made it, and a cohort of
loopers who each have a thread in the corpus. Dan worked there April 2016 –
November 2019; his first off-property call-up was **21 May 2018** to Laurel
Valley Golf Club, leaving Uniontown at 4:30 AM. RJ's later ask — to be got in
the door at Laurel Valley — is a request to enter the ladder at the rung Dan
had already reached.

The Facebook thread carries the same relationship forward past the point
where iMessage stops, into **2021–22**: country-club talk, *"I just showed my
dad this"*, *"Lmao I found all of our slow motion footage"*, and the ask
itself — *"I'm thinking about coming back for a few months and caddying for
the summer. Would you ask your dad if he could get me in the door at Laurel
Valley?"* ([[wiki/self/facebook/messages]]). Whether the ask went anywhere is
not recorded. Note that it is addressed to *Dan's* father, which is the one
place the record shows RJ treating the friendship as a route to something.

*(Evidence note: Facebook thread bodies are gitignored and not held in this
repository. Every FB quotation on this page is **page-attributed** — the prior
wiki's transcription of an export whose contents cannot be re-read here. See
[`dat:1030`](../../kb/data/1030-facebook-messages-archive-extent.md), which
states the limit explicitly.)*

The golf strand also places RJ in a documented cohort rather than as an
isolated contact. [[wiki/people/marc-charles]] occupies the same slot from the
same years — an 85-message Facebook thread, golf equipment and scores, a drone
flown over the country club — and [[wiki/people/jack-rusko]] the caddie-yard
version of it, 202 messages across the 2017–18 season. Read together they
describe a real but low-volume periphery: people Dan liked, saw at a course,
and messaged in the hundreds rather than the tens of thousands.

## February 2020: the politics, and the one sincere sentence

This is the part of the relationship the page previously reduced to a clause,
and it is the part with the best evidence on it.

The previous version said RJ *"expressed conspiratorial and socially
conservative views that often diverged from Dan's"* and that *"the
conversation survived the disagreement because both treated media skepticism
and speculative discussion as a shared pastime."* Both halves are consistent
with the record. What the record adds is that **Dan was not merely
disagreeing. He was recruiting.**

Over 7–8 February 2020, on this thread, verified row-by-row against the held
corpus ([`dat:1542`](../../kb/data/1542-chapo-evangelism-raw-rows-20260205.md)):

| Timestamp | Message | Service |
|---|---|---|
| 2020-02-07 21:55:19 | *"there's a podcast called 'chapo trap house'"* | SMS, sent |
| 2020-02-08 01:28:22 | r/ChapoTrapHouse shared, followed by *"that will keep you up to speed"* | SMS, sent |
| 2020-02-08 10:17:21 | *"i just spent 7 hours listening to the chapo trap house audiobook"*, followed by *"we should do a podcast."* | SMS, sent |

The audiobook is *The Chapo Guide to Revolution*
([[wiki/interests/chapo-trap-house]]). The same thread carries Buttigieg jokes
and *"did you know he is a cia asset?"*

And on the same two days, at **2020-02-08 02:17:46 UTC — 2020-02-07 21:17
EST** — on this thread, sender *Me*:

> *"the doctor thing is a really big thing for me...like, there's no reason that 50,000 people die a year because they don't have insurance"*

[`dat:1080`](../../kb/data/1080-healthcare-grievance-2020-02-08.md) files that
sentence as **the one place in the political record where Dan names a
grievance as his own rather than as analysis** — and the substring matches
exactly one row in the entire 192,140-message corpus.

Three things follow, and they change the weight of this page.

**First, the chronology.** February 2020 is **six months before** the
2020-08-22 conversion narration in which Dan names *"hasan piker, chapo trap
house, thoughtslime, etc."* as the causes of his turn
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/2020-left-turn]]). He was already far enough inside to
recruit before he had the story about how he got inside. The evangelism
predates the conversion narrative.

**Second, the target selection.** Three distinct recruitment targets are
documented across the two February pitches and a March follow-up, and RJ is
one of them — the 7–8 February SMS thread. He was not a bystander to Dan's
politics. He was a mark, chosen from a periphery of several hundred handles,
and chosen specifically because he disagreed.

**Third, and this is the human part.** The conspiratorial, socially
conservative friend from the golf circle is the person to whom Dan made his
only first-person political statement in fifteen years of messages. Not to
Annie. Not to the primary male ally. To the guy from Fayette County who
thought Buttigieg jokes were funny and mainstream politics was a con. Whatever
else the relationship was, it was a channel Dan used when he wanted to be
believed rather than agreed with.

The record does not show whether RJ listened to the podcast.

## May 2020: the last months

The thread's final stretch runs back through the material it began with. The
conversation ranged widely across its whole life — videos, bodybuilding,
gaming, comedy, conspiracy material, the 2020 election, and drug access.

RJ asked Dan for dealer contacts and spoke openly about wanting pills or other
"party supplies" for the New York trip. In **May 2020** he worried that
[[wiki/people/teddy|Teddy's]] absence would leave him without access. These
are contextual messages; they do not establish that any named substance was
obtained or used.

The Teddy detail is the thread's quiet structural fact. Teddy is the person
who vouched for Dan to RJ in July 2019 and the person whose absence worries RJ
in May 2020 — a supply node doing social work at one end of the friendship and
missed as infrastructure at the other. Teddy's own thread runs 2018-02-20 to
2020-02-11 and ends with him cleaning up his contacts, asking whether both
Dan's and Annie's numbers were still current ([[wiki/people/teddy]]). RJ's
worry three months later is downstream of a channel that had already closed.

**The iMessage thread ends 2020-05-19.** The corpus does not show why.

## After the thread: what the other platforms hold

The relationship did not end with the iMessage record, which is exactly the
failure mode [`CORPUS_POLICY.md`](../../CORPUS_POLICY.md) exists to prevent —
a single-channel extract showing a friendship stopping dead where it only
changed platform.

**Facebook Messenger: 426 messages.** RJ is the **sixth-largest Facebook
Messenger correspondent** in the Drive social-DM archive, ahead of
[[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis Armel]] (317) and
[[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suzanne Frank]] (290)
([`dat:1443`](../../kb/data/1443-messenger-drive-correspondents.md)). For
comparison, [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] has 133 Facebook messages
against 97,768 iMessages — the platform split is decisive, not a gap.

**The FB thread is alive in 2021–22**, more than a year after iMessage stops:
the country-club talk, the slow-motion footage, the Laurel Valley ask, a
*"Didn't know you were doing TikTok"* and a missed call in April 2021, and the
political exchange in which RJ writes *"I feel like I'm far from falling for
that or any other mainstream political bullshit... I'd rather know the truth
and find a higher meaning in life than to be ignorant and a willing pawn"* and
Dan answers *"yeah dude i just think it's such an important time to make sure
people don't fall for this right wing bullshit"*
([[wiki/self/facebook/messages]], page-attributed).

Read against the February 2020 SMS thread, that exchange is the same
conversation continuing on a different app, two years later, with both
positions intact. Neither converted the other. Neither stopped talking.

**Messenger, 2022–23: eight unread.** A screen recording of Dan's Messenger
thread list made on 2026-09-12 shows roughly 38 threads spanning October 2022
to the present, and among the 2022–23 entries: **"RJ Ritchey 8 new msgs"**
([`dat:1440`](../../kb/data/1440-messenger-pull-incomplete.md)). Eight messages
sent and not opened. The content is not held — Messenger history after
2022-09 is an uncovered gap requiring a fresh Facebook DYI export — so the
date, the count and the unread state are the whole of what can be said.

It is nonetheless the last thing the record holds about this friendship, and
it is one-directional.

## What the record does not show

- **Who he is now.** Current location, employment and status are undocumented.
  The last dated trace is a 2022–23 Messenger thread nobody opened.
- **Whether contact continued past 2022.** Facebook Messenger coverage ends
  2022-09-04 and Instagram DM coverage ends 2025-08-09; the window from 2022-09
  to the present is an acknowledged gap requiring a fresh Facebook export
  ([`dat:1440`](../../kb/data/1440-messenger-pull-incomplete.md)). Eight unread
  messages are evidence that *he* kept writing, and nothing about what
  happened next.
- **The full name.** The Facebook friends-list sample carries both *"RJ
  Ritchey"* and *"Robert Ritchey"* ([[wiki/self/facebook/friends]]). "RJ" is a
  conventional shortening of Robert, and the two entries are plausibly one
  person listed twice — but the export is a sample list, no reconciliation has
  been done, and this page does not assert it. Recorded as an open identity
  question rather than a resolved one.
- **How they met.** The golf/caddie circle is the setting; the introduction is
  undated and unrecorded.
- **The February 2019 encounter itself.** The messages establish approach,
  discomfort, withdrawal and a recording worry. They do not establish what
  happened.
- **Whether he listened to the podcast.** The February 2020 pitch is fully
  documented on Dan's side. No response is recorded in any held source.
- **Whether the Laurel Valley ask went anywhere.** Dan's own transfer request
  to Laurel Valley, made to caddie master Carl in May 2018, also never
  happened ([[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddying]]).
- **The Facebook thread body.** Every FB quotation here is page-attributed;
  thread contents are gitignored and cannot be re-read in this repository
  ([`dat:1030`](../../kb/data/1030-facebook-messages-archive-extent.md)).
- **Inferred vs documented.** Documented: all dated rows in the log above, both
  thread totals, and the February 2020 timestamps. Inferred: that RJ was
  *selected* as a recruitment target rather than merely being the person
  available that evening — the target-selection reading is a reading, and

## Appendix: the count, and how it was wrong

*Kept short and kept at the bottom, because it is a fact about the wiki rather
than a fact about RJ.*

This page originally described a **"518-row iMessage thread"** and carried a
caveat that the master export's `direction` field was unreliable. Both halves
of that were true. The conclusion drawn from them was not.

The held corpus holds the thread at **1,103 messages — 585 sent, 518 received**
— across exactly the date range this page already stated
([`dat:0983`](../../kb/data/0983-rj-ritchey-thread-dimensions.md)). **The 518
figure is byte-equal to the received count.** The page had read precisely one
side of the conversation and reported it as the whole of it.

This is not an isolated slip. It is the **fourth instance filed in a single
September 2026 batch** and one of **fourteen** corrections with an identical
shape — Mike Hinkle 21 → 85, Mohammed bin Salman 281 → 643, Jim Shaffer 894 →
1,845, Vicki 234 → 512, Jay Lauer 116 → 212, Jim Vrabel 313 → 612, and others
([`int:received-half-count-pattern`](../../kb/interpretations/received-half-count-pattern.md)).
The mechanism is the same every time: the MASTER-dump exports marked nearly
everything *Received*, pages noticed the field looked wrong, annotated it
*"unreliable — read as two-way"*, and then kept the received-only count anyway.

The interpretation node states the cost precisely, and it applies here:

> *"The page's number is almost always right as the received half — that is what makes it dangerous. The count verifies; the shape claim smuggles… A count error changes a table; a shape error changes what questions are considered answered."*

**What that means for this page specifically.** Every reading on it before
2026-09-09 was built from RJ's 518 messages without Dan's 585. The February
2019 section is the most affected: it characterised a boundary-setting episode
from one participant's side, and it missed that the closing declarations came
from Annie on Dan's handle — which is in the other half. That correction is
now in the body, where it belongs, rather than here.

One further correction, made in this pass: the infobox `location` field read
`nyc`. RJ's documented world is Fayette County — the Maywood, Nemacolin, the
country club, Fairchance-adjacent logistics — and New York is a place he
visited for three days in July 2019. Corrected to `uniontown`. The
`relationship_to_dan` field read `unknown` and is now stated at the strength
the record supports.
