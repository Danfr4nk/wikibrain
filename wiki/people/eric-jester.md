# Eric Jester

Eric Jester sat in the same Full Sail rooms as Dan in 2008 and 2009, left
Orlando on Halloween 2009, and spent the next sixteen years tending bar in
Texas. In that time he and Dan were never in the same place for long, and
they never stopped talking. The friendship moved platforms three times —
Twitter, then Facebook, then iMessage — and each move has, at one time or
another, been mistaken by this wiki for the friendship thinning out. It
did not thin out so much as change address. What it carried across all
three was the same thing: two people who could say the crudest thing
available to them and then, in the next message, the most sincere, and
have both land.

The two moments that define it are seven years apart. On the night of
**4 April 2018**, hours after [[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Fran]] died with
Dan at her bedside, Dan called Eric — *"i called you last night because i
think you'll appreciate this story (particularly coming from me)"* — and
the next evening wrote out what had happened in her last minutes. On
**30 October 2023**, the day Dan had his cat Gabe put down, Dan opened with
two words, *"Remember gabe?"*, and Eric answered with *"Duh"*, and then,
twenty minutes later, *"He was the cat that made me love cats!"*
(`raw/imessage/messages-part1-2011-2019.csv`,
`raw/imessage/messages-part2-2019-2026.csv`; times below converted from the
corpus's UTC to Eastern, per [`CORPUS_POLICY.md`](../../CORPUS_POLICY.md)).

## Orlando, 2008–2009

Facebook records Dan and Eric as friends from **1 November 2008**
(`…/1jrswKcBIyE7ojxBRsR-NqjJPWnkSpwEL3eqWqoGQwu4.from-gdoc.txt`), ten weeks
after Dan arrived in Orlando for Full Sail
([`ent:full-sail`](../../kb/entities/full-sail.md)). The first traces of
Eric are group texture rather than one-to-one exchange. A Facebook group
chat titled *"Where There's Gold..."*, running November 2008 to April 2009,
carries *"It's too cold to fountain hop, jester."* (23 November 2008) and,
from a night at the House of Blues with We the Kings and Fall Out Boy on the
bill, *"Skull in jesters beer. He dumped the whole thing."* (13 December
2008) (`…/147RxpduHfVNHnFJDiFVBbSacOXEvTAXnykEsT9WEBtk.from-gdoc.txt`; the
export prints the group's name in place of each author, so who wrote which
line is not preserved).

The earliest image of the two of them is a webcam frame dated **8 January
2009**: two figures in near-darkness, keyed against the Apollo *Earthrise*
photograph — the lunar surface in the lower half, the Earth hanging over
it — with an inset window in the corner showing a third party on the other
end of the call, long fringe, red T-shirt, an aquarium backdrop. Dan
identified the subjects as himself and Eric, on the call from the house
across the street from Full Sail that the cohort used as a base
([[wiki/timeline/periods/full-sail-2008-2010]], caption dated 2026-09-24).
The identification does not say which of the three visible people is which,
and the frame is too dark to settle it; this page does not guess.

Three weeks later, the first direct message in the Facebook thread is
Eric's: *"Here is a formal thank you for letting me come by and smoke all
your weed last night. i would have written on your wall but somehow i
didn't feel it was appropriate"* (28 January 2009,
`…/1slr_P_WSkx2TfcWYMTbWUMuog88vZPf0cH89w2hJtFM.from-gdoc.txt`, hereafter
*the Facebook thread*).

The second is the one that says the most about him. On **26 February 2009**
Eric pasted Dan a whole exchange he had just had with a classmate — the
classmate is not named — who had joked about his grandmother. Eric had
written to him: *"I don't tell everyone about the fact that I found her
after she had a stroke the day of my birthday party. I don't tell people
that because it is a painful memory, so when I hear you making light of the
situation … it makes me extremely angry."* The classmate apologized; Eric
closed it with a joke — *"Things won't be awkward unless I decide to come
into class without any pants on. See you tomorrow."* He had handled it
cleanly: said the thing, accepted the apology, made
the exit easy. He sent it to Dan, apparently, to show him. Nine years
later, when Dan wrote to him about sitting with Fran as she died, Eric
answered with his own grandparents.

The spring of 2009 shows the four-person core in motion. A tweet of
27 February: *"stuuuudyin with @ericjester @mattdunnisatool @jfreshest"*.
31 March: *"ESA lecture. putting up with @ericjester 's shit for another 30
days at least."* 30 April: *"Jester thinks I have swine flu and won't share
a spliff."* (`raw/twitter/archive.jsonl`). On 7 May Eric wrote to a
Facebook group of Dan, [[wiki/people/matt-dunn|Matt Dunn]], Lucas Tyler
Konrardy and one deactivated account, selling seats his uncle had given him
for *"the game on sunday"* at *"67 dollaroos"*, noting that *"J has lab on
sunday from 5-9"*
(`…/1njyFlAGAqY56jxZLz8ZdopqgBc2BEHBUsAjA3vKx52k.from-gdoc.txt`). The Sunday
after 7 May 2009 is 10 May, and the Full Sail period page carries a photo
from an Orlando Magic game on exactly that date
([[wiki/timeline/periods/full-sail-2008-2010]]). That the photo was taken
from Eric's uncle's seats is an inference from the dates; the record does not
say so.

[![Eric Jester, Dan, and Jason Bermejo at Backbooth, Orlando, 21 July 2009](wiki/media/jason-bermejo-2009-backbooth.jpg)](wiki/media/jason-bermejo-2009-backbooth.jpg)

The best photograph of him in the record is from **21 July 2009**, at
Backbooth in Orlando: Eric on the left in a blue T-shirt, grinning; Dan in
the middle in a red beanie, throwing a peace sign with an X marked on the
back of his hand; [[wiki/people/jason-bermejo|Jason Bermejo]] on the right
in a red fitted cap, holding a can. It carries the bar's logo and a
nightlife photographer's watermark (Dan's identification of the three,
2026-09-24, on [[wiki/people/jason-bermejo]]). Dan was twenty; the X is
consistent with a venue's under-21 mark, and the record does not say more
than that. The bar matters later: in November 2018 Eric texted Dan out of
nowhere to ask *"What was the name of that bar in Orlando that did Crush
Mondays? / Back booth??"* — nine years on, trying to name the room in this
picture.

Dan's Facebook comments on Eric's photos across that year are the ordinary
noise of the friendship — *"~blows smoke into face."* (13 December 2008),
*"I LIKE YOU...IT WORKED!!!"* (29 July 2009), *"no! he's turning up an FX
master duh"* (9 August 2009)
(`…/1gPaw1Du0svxtOEaO44WdGefNxzlh1EQAS1xV_QcvSbk.from-gdoc.txt`). On
8 July 2009 Eric sent Dan a Craigslist listing for an apartment in Los
Angeles's San Fernando Valley, without comment. Where he went first after
Full Sail is not recorded; where he ended up is.

The Orlando chapter closes on a date. **31 October 2009**, Dan's birthday
eve: *"birthday night x halloween x jesters last night in the o. hoooooly
inebriated."* Every Eric tweet after that is addressed to someone who has
left — *"@EricJester alice doesn't like when you trash talk teh stillerz"*
(22 November), *"@ericjester I'm in the control24 rooms right now. PT1
bitch"* (24 November), sent from the Pro Tools lab where Dan was still
finishing his certification work
([`ent:full-sail`](../../kb/entities/full-sail.md)).

## The Twitter and Facebook years, 2010–2016

Eric is the second-most-addressed person in Dan's entire tweet archive: 22
of the 2,741 tweets open with his handle, behind only
[[wiki/people/alexis-armel|@alexisarmel]]'s 56, and 26 carry it somewhere
(`raw/twitter/archive.jsonl`, counted for this revision). The 2010 run is
the densest: *"@EricJester dude I once heard a story about you in Montreal.
yeah...when will YOU start drinking like a man"* (31 January), the *Office*
idiom neither of them ever explains — *"remember when he stole 'shut up
Oscar!' from us in s5 of the office? do sumthan bout it"* (12 March;
[[wiki/interests/the-office]]) — *"slapa da bass mon!"* (27 April), *"8 year
old Eric Jester loves drinking in church...."* (19 August), a potato-famine
joke abandoned because *"i wouldn't be able to stand the grim reply i'd get
from @ericjester"* (31 August), and *"got a girlfriend and forgot how 2
twitter."* (30 November). The complete tweet log is below.

On Facebook the same months carry the one serious argument in the record.
On **3 September 2010** Dan, *"stoned obv"* by his own account, put a
predestination problem to Eric as the Catholic in the room: if God is
omniscient and salvation predetermined, *"God is intentionally sending
people to the Hell that he himself created?"* Eric's answer is the longest
message from him in the held record: *"The church is full of liars and snakes … No
religion has any idea what's actually out there and no one ever will. I've
come to firmly believe that what happens to you in life and after you die is
based on karma and the energy you put out in the universe … If you approach
life and people with a positive attitude then positive things will happen
to you. Peace, love, and pussy juice my friend."* Dan answered in kind:
religion as *"more 'fable' than 'reality'"*, *"i hold pretty firmly in my
belief of science over any sort of deity, but claiming to know the truth on
either side of the argument is simply irrational."* Eight years later, when
Dan described Fran's last minutes as something he could not explain, he
prefaced it with *"you know how little credit i give supernatural or
religious ideas"*, and Eric replied *"I believe in that stuff."* Both men
were being consistent with 2010.

By 2011 Eric was in Texas. On **7 September 2011** he told Dan he had
dreamed of visiting him in New York with his girlfriend, that *"thats one
of the (few) perks about bartending. don't have to get up till about 4:00
everyday"*, and — *"i miss you fuckers"* — proposed a reunion: *"me and j
will drive to west virginia and meet you and mohler … it will be just like
old times."* Dan warned him off the Texas wildfires and blamed *"your
wonderful Governor for cutting the state level firefighters budget"*, and
Eric mentioned *"austin is getting a little toasty"*.

**2 October 2011** is the busiest day on any channel in the record: 101
Facebook messages. Dan opened with Occupy Wall Street — *"you should be here
for everything thats happening on wall st"* — and explained the human
microphone, the general assemblies he was catching after work, and his
unease at being *"in marketing"* while doing it. Eric, who said plainly
*"i don't think i fully understand what exactly is going on"*, told him he
was free to visit because he had been suspended: a *"yuppie punk customer"*
tried to trade back a half-drunk beer for an Allagash White, the argument
escalated, and *"i eventually told him 'fuck this' and threw a lemon at
him … almost got physical but my manager stepped in between us."* Dan
relayed the story to [[wiki/people/jamie-mohler|Jamie Mohler]] the next
day — *"jester got suspended from his bartending job for getting in a fight
with some punker"* (`…/1EcP2STPjAL4DhVAm9dqVVmjl76_7pQssMdGlhf-yv00.from-gdoc.txt`,
3 October 2011; that thread's counterparty is identified as Jamie by
inference, per [[wiki/people/jamie-mohler]]).

The 2012–2014 messages are shorter and constant: the 2012 Mohler bit (*"i'm
ethan kray bitch"*, 14 March 2012 — the same persona Dan was still
describing to Eric in 2018); Dan asking Eric for the Full Sail email format
and Eric answering from memory with his own login, still working (3 May
2012); Dan forwarding the Jonny Craig scandal because Eric had once ridden a
train from Boston to San Francisco with him (17 June 2012) and Eric's reply,
*"Even on the train he was stealing beers and smoking weed. Just a
downright HOODLUM."*; a roller-coaster photo from Sea World that Eric
explained by *"Amanda's obsession with sea world"* (26 January 2013); and
the February 2013 blizzard, to which Eric replied *"I'M WEARING SHORTS AND A
TEE SHIRT"*.

On **15 April 2013**, the afternoon of the Boston Marathon bombing, Dan
wrote: *"Thinking of you with this shit going on. I know the Jester-house is
far from the carnage...but in my book, there exists no Mass without mom and
pops jester."* Eric answered after midnight: *"Thanks man I really
appreciate hearing from you. It's been a tough day. Kendel was 2 blocks
away from it when it happened. She's fine but I'd been freaking out all
day."* The message confirms what the phone number's 413 area code implies:
Eric's family was in Massachusetts. (Dan's line the day before, *"dudeman
i'm moving back to the big O. come visit."*, is not otherwise explained; the
record does not show a move to Orlando in 2013.)

In July 2013 Dan asked, *"Can you talk for a minute? Nothing heavy just
wanted to run something by you"*; Eric called. Four days later Dan wrote
that he had been punched in the face by [[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis]]
that day, and — in the same burst — *"i have a lot of things to tell you
that you're gonna be real proud of me for whenever you're free."* Neither
the call nor what he meant to be proud of is in the record. It is the only
place in the Facebook thread where Dan treats Eric as the person to report
to, and it is the pattern the iMessage years repeat.

Then the channel goes quiet in the ordinary way. *"Hey bud. Remember
'09!?!?!?!"* (18 February 2014, with a YouTube link). *"Mawfuckin J.
Haven't hung out with him in a few years."* (28 February 2016, about Jason).
A last Facebook burst on **23 September 2020** — fifteen messages in seven
minutes, several of Dan's garbled past reading (*"you dont bhsfkd will mkkm
dna forlaknf this sejmsjkis"*), Eric answering *"Lmao"* and *"Oh gawd"* —
has no surviving context and is recorded here only as a dated contact.

Two lines from other people's threads fix how Dan talked about Eric in
Eric's absence. To Jamie, 13 December 2017: *"Also why do I feel like
jester def voted trump"*. To Jamie again, **5 April 2018**, the evening
after Fran died: *"for real though we will link up. it would be a shame for
everyone to just turn into eric jester and disappear into a smoky barkeep
lifestyle somewhere in the deep south"* — and Jamie: *"It would be a shame.
I am no eric Jester sir."* Eric's condolence had landed in Dan's phone
roughly four hours earlier. Dan was mocking Eric's life to one friend the
same evening he was confiding in him on another channel. Neither message
cancels the other; that is how this cohort talks.

## The message thread, 2017–2025

The iMessage thread holds **237 messages — 134 from Dan, 103 from Eric —
in eleven sessions**, 8 May 2017 to 15 August 2025
([`dat:0408`](../../kb/data/0408-eric-jester-thread-count-correction.md);
recounted for this revision from the three held CSVs, identical). Dan
opened five of the nine distinct conversations and Eric four. The complete
session log is below; the conversations themselves are these.

**May 2017 — the lost bounce.** Just after midnight on 8 May 2017 Dan asked,
*"Do you still have the final bounce of our studio project"*. Eric: *"Doubt
it. I'll have to go through my mac and see. / What was it called?"* Dan never
answered. The same night Dan put the same question to David Keller, another
Full Sail classmate
([[wiki/timeline/events/valentines-day-switch-2009]], dated log). It was a
canvass, and Eric was on the short list of people who might have the file.

**January 2018 — the tee shirt.** *"In deep East Texas right now at a
Walmart buying beer. There's a kid walking around with a Blood on the
Dancefloor tee shirt. / Naturally, you were the first person I thought of."*
(10 January 2018, 23:22 EST). Blood on the Dance Floor had shared a
Jacksonville bill with the band Dan was working for in April 2009
([[wiki/timeline/events/valentines-day-switch-2009]]); Eric was remembering
Dan's 2009 scene nine years later, from a Walmart. No reply is recorded.

**April 2018 — Fran.** The longest conversation in the thread: 149 messages
over two evenings. It opens with Eric at 19:42 on 5 April 2018: *"Hey
brother. I saw on Facebook about your great grandmother. I'm sorry to hear
it man. She was an awesome lady."* Dan: *"ah it's cool buddy. i got to spend
a bunch of time with her in the past year and i got super good closure. but
i called you last night because i think you'll appreciate this story."* Then
the story, in four messages. Hospice had stopped feeding her; four days
passed; sitting with her and his girlfriend, half-asleep, he tried *"to
communicate with her in my mind … i said to myself 'if you can hear me give
me some kind of sign'"*; his girlfriend shook him awake to say Fran had
blinked for the first time in a week; *"i told her i loved her and she
smiled. and then she just slowly passed away."* Then: *"you know how little
credit i give supernatural or religious ideas. i'm the last person who would
be pushing a bullshit story about something spiritual happening."* Eric:
*"That's incredible man. I believe in that stuff."* Dan: *"oh and one more
thing....i fucking got the whole thing on camera."* Eric, gently: *"That's
great man. It will be really nice to have that to hold on to."* And then
the detail no other page has — Eric had met her: *"She was always super
sweet the times that I met her. I'll never forget you driving off with me
standing in her drive way and she asked if I was going to stay with her a
while longer lol."*

The next evening Dan filled in the rest — *"i spent the past 6 months
looking after her full time ($15 an hour to play video games with gram is a
good deal) … finally did something in my life that wasn't completely
selfish haha / and she got a poetic ended"* — and, in the same breath, the
estate: *"now that i'm not saddled here with gram anymore....and that
inheritance money coming...ya boy is getting out of PA"*
([[wiki/timeline/events/fran-death-vigil]]). Eric answered with his own
record: he had lived with his grandparents when he first moved to Texas;
*"My grandpa passed away a few years ago but I really felt like I got to
know him better than I ever would have."*

Asked how he was, Eric gave the plainest account of his own life anywhere
in the record: *"I have a house with a back yard, two dogs and a cat a
girlfriend a car that I finally paid off. I can't really complain too much.
I just hate my line of work and I feel like I'm stuck in it."* He was paying
Full Sail $800 a month, a loan his mother had co-signed, projected to run to
2025 and lengthening with deferrals; he had wanted to freelance in film but
could not stay available enough; *"I'm just losing my fucking hearing from
working in such a loud environment. I have ringing in both my ears."* Dan:
*"that is just mind blowing to me and makes me feel like such a spoiled
brat rich kid."* Then Dan's version of consolation — Full Sail is *"a trap
trap"*, and a named classmate *"will totally be filing chapter 11 because of
full sail"* — and the conversation turned back into the cohort: Gabe (*"I
miss gabe. I don't miss him running away though."*), Eric's new cat Felicia,
the rented house by the lake, *"is she THE ONE / Yes grandma I think so"*,
and a long duet of Jason impressions (*"his life is a cartoon / that i miss
being in"*).

Later that night Dan described his relationship with
[[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] to Eric in the most explicit terms
available, and Eric, who had just described his own as a relationship built
on a first conversation about trust — *"I trust her and she trusts me so
there's no use in getting jealous"* — answered with the line the thread is
remembered for: *"That's the most Dan Frank thing I've ever heard."* Dan:
*"it sort of is. leaves the option for self sabotage open for sure."* The
evening ended on drinking. Dan: *"i haven't had a sip of any alcohol in over
10 years"*, and *"i live in heroin city usa and i want to avoid dying
here"*. Eric: *"Damn I'm jealous. I'm definitely a hard alcoholic … As a
bartender having to be the soberest person in a full on rager you see the
worst in absolutely everyone. And it's funny because doing that makes you
want to drink even more … Most of the shitty things I have done in my life
stem from being drunk / But in my line of work I'd stick out like a sore
thumb if I didn't drink."* It is the only conversation in which either of
them says anything about what the other's life costs.

**November 2018 — Crush Mondays.** *"What was the name of that bar in
Orlando that did Crush Mondays? / Back booth??"* (16 November 2018, 21:15
EST). Dan: *"Dude I literally, not 30 seconds ago, was talking about
CRUSH / swear"*. Eric's reason: *"Dunn is down in that neighborhood right
now … He was snapping me videos of full sail earlier"* — the only pre-2025
sighting of [[wiki/people/matt-dunn|Dunn]] as a present person rather than a

## What the friendship actually looked like

**It never went dormant; it changed channel.** The prior version of this
page read the tweet counts (dense in 2009–10, a trickle by 2013) as a
friendship that "started dense and decayed" before the message thread
opened in 2017. The Facebook thread overturns the shape. Its busiest year is
**2011** — 140 messages, after the tweets had already fallen off — and it
carries contact in 2012, 2013, 2014 and 2016 before iMessage takes over.
Laid across all three channels, the busiest single years are 2011 and 2018,
and the longest silence in the combined record is the 2019–2023 gap in the
iMessage thread — which is exactly the kind of silence a single-channel
export manufactures ([`CORPUS_POLICY.md`](../../CORPUS_POLICY.md): *"A
single-handle extract shows a relationship stopping dead where it only
changed channel."*).

**It is reciprocal, and it is not maintenance.** Eric opens as often as
Dan. What opens a conversation is never scheduling: it is a dream, a tee
shirt in a Walmart, a bar name, a dead great-grandmother, a dead cat, a box
of recovered photos. Each of those is one of them noticing the other's
existence unprompted, and each is answered inside minutes. The claim on the
Full Sail period page that this friendship "never needed active maintenance
to survive" is right about the absence of logistics and wrong if read as an
absence of effort; the effort is the unprompted noticing.

**Eric is the friend Dan reports to.** In July 2013 (*"things to tell you
that you're gonna be real proud of me for"*), in April 2018 (the phone call
the night Fran died), in April 2019 (the Trinity disclosure, offered as
proof that *"things are shaping up pretty well here in nyc too"*), in
October 2023 (Gabe), and in March 2025 (*"you are the one who has actually
changed"*), Dan uses Eric as the measure — the member of the cohort who grew
up, and therefore the one whose opinion of how Dan is doing counts. That
reading is this page's; neither man says it.

**He is also a witness.** Three events elsewhere in the wiki are fixed or
corroborated by his thread: Fran's death and its aftermath, in Dan's words
the next day; the Trinity St. Clair booking's price and week; and Gabe's
death date. He was not asked to be any of those things.

## Coverage limits

- **What the record never shows is Eric's side of his own life beyond what
  he told Dan.** His partner is never named in the iMessage thread; the
  women named in the Facebook thread (Amanda, 2013; Kendel, 2013) are not
  identified further, and the record does not show whether either is the
  woman he married. His employer, city in Texas after 2011, and wedding
  date are not recorded.
- **Where he went between Orlando and Texas** is not recorded. The July
  2009 Los Angeles listing, the Boston-to-San-Francisco train ride, and the
  2011 Austin reference are the only waypoints, and none is a residence
  record.
- **Calls are invisible.** The record shows at least two phone calls (July
  2013; the night of 4 April 2018) and says nothing of what was said on
  either.
- **The Facebook counts are a parse of a Google-Docs conversion** of the
  2022 export, not the native JSON; message boundaries were inferred from
  author and timestamp lines. The export ends September 2022.
- **The 2020 Facebook burst and the 2025-08-15 screenshot** have no
  recoverable context; what Eric's *"morbid self-awareness"* referred to is
  not in the record.
- **Inference, labeled as such on this page:** that the 10 May 2009 Magic
  photo came from Eric's uncle's tickets; that the X on Dan's hand in the
  Backbooth photo is a venue mark; that Dan uses Eric as a measure of his
  own progress.
- **Not re-verified:** the prior page's contact-card note (*"sandwich eater.
  dream killer."*) — no contacts export is held — and the Jamie
  identification of the Facebook "Facebook user" thread, which is
  [[wiki/people/jamie-mohler]]'s inference.

## Appendix — corrections to the prior page

**A1. Message count.** *"| Messages | 103 |"* was Eric's inbound count
relabeled as the total. The thread is 237 (134 Dan / 103 Eric)
([`dat:0408`](../../kb/data/0408-eric-jester-thread-count-correction.md)).

**A2. Direction.** *"Unreliable in this export (marked 'Received'
throughout) — read as one-way"* described a retired extract. The held
corpus's `is_from_me` column is populated and the thread is two-way.

**A3. Gabe.** The prior page said Eric *"loses a beloved cat, Gabe, on
October 30, 2023"*. Gabe was Dan's cat; Dan wrote *"I had to put him down
today"* and Eric replied *"He was the cat that made me love cats!"* Eric's
own cat, in April 2018, was named Felicia. The prior claim that this date
"resolves the ambiguous 'Gabe' death reference on
[[wiki/people/jason-bermejo]] as a pet" is right about the pet and wrong
about whose.

**A4. Tweet counts.** *"22 tweets, 20 of them opening with his name"*, by
year 7 / 9 / 2 / 2 / 1, does not match the held archive: 28 tweets name
him, 26 carry the handle, 22 open with it; by year 13 / 11 / 1 / 2 / 1. The
second-most-addressed ranking stands (22 lead addresses, behind
@alexisarmel's 56). [`dat:0409`](../../kb/data/0409-eric-jester-verified-beats.md)
recorded the raw archive as absent; it is now held at
`raw/twitter/archive.jsonl`.

**A5. Role.** *"Film/TV industry crew"* and *"bartender-adjacent crew
role"* — Eric describes himself as a bartender from 2011 (*"one of the
(few) perks about bartending"*) through 2018 (*"As a bartender having to
be the soberest person…"*). He had wanted film work and said he could not
sustain it. The prior page's quoted *"sex is just thrown at you day after
day"* refers to bar work.

**A6. First contact.** The infobox's 2009-10-24 was the date of a tweet.
The Facebook friendship is dated 2008-11-01; the first held direct message
is 2009-01-28.

**A7. The decay thesis.** See *What the friendship actually looked like*:
the tweet-only curve was a channel artifact.
