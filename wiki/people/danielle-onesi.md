# Danielle Onesi ("Dee")

She picked out the cat.

That is the sentence the whole record keeps returning to, and it is worth
starting there rather than with the relationship, because it is the part that
turned out to be permanent. In August 2008, in Orlando, a young woman from
Uniontown, Pennsylvania went to a shelter with her boyfriend, who had arrived
the day before to start music-production school. She chose a solid-black
long-haired kitten because he was pawing at the cats in the neighbouring cages,
and because his paperwork said he got along well with rabbits, *"which the two
of them found endearing"*
([[wiki/people/gabe]]; [`dat:0534`](../../kb/data/0534-gabe-adoption-naming-relocation-new-detail.md)).
Fifteen years and five relocations later, on 2023-10-30, that cat was put down
and she paid for it, because Dan could not
([[wiki/people/gabe]]; [`dat:0292`](../../kb/data/0292-gabe-death-2023-10-30-corrects-memory.md)).

Between those two afternoons sits everything else: a relationship that began
around 2005 and ended badly in 2009, a breakup Dan ran cruelly and by his own
admission, sixteen years of silence about it, and a friendship that quietly
relocated itself into his family and stayed there. Danielle Onesi — "Dee" — is
the ex who became a fixture. She is, as of the record's close, Dan's mother
[[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suzanne]]'s closest friend, a presence at Frank
family Christmas, and the person Dan protected in 2025 by declining to tell her
something about her own boyfriend
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/dormancy-not-exit]]; [`dat:0487`](../../kb/data/0487-danielle-onesi-first-girlfriend-gabe-james.md)).

This page is the whole of what the wiki holds on her, which is less than the
length of the relationship implies and more than any single page previously
carried.

## Uniontown, and the year the house came apart

Danielle is from Uniontown — the same Fayette County, Rust Belt cohort that
produces nearly everyone else in the early record. The relationship is dated
`~2005–2009` throughout: on Dan's own HTML bio it is labelled, flatly, **"The
Origin"** — *"Danielle (05-09): The Origin"* — and it is the first serious
relationship of his life ([[wiki/self/context-core]];
[`dat:0487`](../../kb/data/0487-danielle-onesi-first-girlfriend-gabe-james.md)).
On his Facebook profile the relationship field lists, under **Previous
relationships**, *"Danielle Onesi, Alexis Armel"*, in that order
([[wiki/self/facebook]]).

The dating matters for a reason that has nothing to do with romance. November
2005 is the month Dan's parents' marriage ended — his father disclosing, in the
car on the way home from the learner's-permit appointment, that he was entering
rehab, with his mother's affair with the family's cocaine dealer behind it
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/november-2005-hinge]]). It is the single most-cited event
in the wiki's psychology layer. Dan was seventeen, a junior at Laurel Highlands,
living at 337 Saratoga Drive — and, at the same time, inside the first romantic
relationship he had ever had.

[[wiki/mind/synthesis/november-2005-hinge|The hinge page]] is unusually candid
about what that means and about what nobody ever did with it:

> "A seventeen-year-old's parents' marriage ends over cocaine and infidelity in
> November; the seventeen-year-old is simultaneously inside the first romantic
> relationship of his life. The wiki's developmental-origins cluster treats the
> rupture as a psychosexual origin point and has never asked what the
> contemporaneous first relationship was doing in the same months — whether the
> hinge deformed it, whether it buffered the hinge, whether the two are even
> separable."

The record's answer is nothing. It does not hold one line about what Danielle
saw of the Frank household in 2005 or 2006. The hinge page's conclusion is
structural and worth keeping in front of anyone reading this profile: the
hinge's *narrator* count is one, and its *witness* count is at least three —
Dan, his sister [[wiki/people/vanessa-frank|Vanessa]] (eleven at the time), and
Danielle. She has been inside this family's orbit for twenty years and has never
been asked about it on the record. Whether she has been asked off it is
**[UNKNOWN]** ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/november-2005-hinge]]).

Even the ordering is open. The hinge page states it precisely: if the
relationship predated November 2005, the rupture landed inside an existing bond;
if it began after, the rupture's aftermath is the relationship's entire
environment. The wiki dates neither precisely enough to sort them, and *"a single
dated photograph, message, or school record from 2005 could settle it"*
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/november-2005-hinge]]).

One earlier correction belongs here, because this page carried the error for
months. Danielle is **not** the first girlfriend. A 2026 operator capture places
a relationship with **Britney Bolen** in seventh grade, roughly 2000–01 — the
Seven Springs night-ski trip and the year-long "Bald Eagle" nickname that
followed it ([[wiki/timeline/events/bald-eagle-cummings]];
[`dat:0209`](../../kb/data/0209-bald-eagle-cummings.md)). Britney predates
Danielle by three to four years. What Danielle is, precisely, is the first
*serious* and first *adult-length* relationship, and the one every later bond in
the record is measured against.

## Orlando, 2008: the shelter

In September 2008 Dan left for Full Sail University in Winter Park, Florida —
an Associate's in Recording Arts, the ceremony dated 2009-10-02, Pro Tools HD 7
certification in February 2010 ([[wiki/self/context-core]];
[[wiki/timeline/periods/full-sail-2008-2010]]). The relationship went
long-distance. It did not immediately suffer for it; Danielle was there within a
day of his arrival.

The shelter trip is the most human thing in the entire Danielle file, and it
survives in real detail because Dan volunteered it directly on 2026-07-14. He
was Dan's first solid-black cat and his first long-haired cat. The kitten was
named **Gabe**, for Gabe Saporta — and the naming logic, in Dan's own words from
an unheld AI session, is characteristically sideways: *"my cat gabe was named
for douchebag cobra starship singer and fucking rad midtown singer Gabe
Saporta"*, which Dan says he chose **for the pivot itself** rather than for
either version of the man ([[wiki/people/gabe]];
[`dat:0534`](../../kb/data/0534-gabe-adoption-naming-relocation-new-detail.md)).
Danielle is not recorded as having an opinion about the name. She is recorded as
having chosen the animal.

Gabe went on to outlast the relationship by fourteen years and to move with Dan
through every subsequent address — **Orlando → NYC → PA → PA again → NYC again**,
five legs ([[wiki/people/gabe]]). Danielle is at the first leg and the last.

## Valentine's Day, 2009

The relationship ended because Dan ended it, by cheating, and the record has two
versions of how.

The **older telling**, carried in the CATO bootloader and in context-core, is
short: Dan cheated in Orlando toward the end of Full Sail with *a girl from
Baltimore he met through Jack from All Time Low*, and the bootloader records his
regret explicitly ([[wiki/self/context-core]];
`raw/self/dox-md/CATO_BOOTLOADER_DANFRANK.md`).

The **2025 telling** is longer, more specific, and considerably less flattering
to its author. The other woman is named — **Kelly Mulroy** — and the connection
is restated as knowing **Alex Gaskarth** rather than Jack, *"a credential that
carried real weight in 2009."* The date is given as **Valentine's Day**, five
months into the Orlando move. And Dan characterises his own execution without
hedging: he broke up with Danielle *"in a real cruel shitty way"* and left with
Kelly for a show in Jacksonville
([`dat:0487`](../../kb/data/0487-danielle-onesi-first-girlfriend-gabe-james.md);
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/vacancy-rule]]).

What happened next is the detail that makes the story smaller rather than
larger. The affair went nowhere. Kelly is, in Dan's account, *"the only girl
ever"* who required a condom, *"and the encounter ended there"*
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/vacancy-rule]]). One drive to Jacksonville, one night, and
a four-year relationship traded for it.

[[wiki/mind/synthesis/vacancy-rule|The vacancy rule]] reads the episode as its
oldest and least-documented transition, and reaches a conclusion that is not
softening:

> "He did not leave Danielle for nothing; he left her for Kelly Mulroy, and the
> substitution misfired. The nine-month vacancy is what a failed sourcing
> produces: the exit mechanism ran (substitution), the successor did not take,
> and the slot sat empty until Alexis was identified later in 2009."

That nine months — roughly February to November 2009 — is the longest adult
vacancy anywhere in the ledger, longer than any gap the rule's documented era
contains ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/vacancy-rule]], Transition 1). It is also the one
that ends when Dan comes home to Pennsylvania for **Thanksgiving 2009**, a few
months after the August graduation, meets [[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis
Armel]], spends two nights with her, and buys her a one-way ticket to Orlando a
week or two later — *"unsure himself whether it was meant to be permanent"*
(`raw/people/captures/2026-08-27_222932_gap-alexis-armel.md`, via
[[wiki/people/alexis-armel]]).

There is a third woman in that window who complicates the tidy line. **Stephanie
Nalbone** carries a six-week, high-intensity long-distance romance running
2009-09-25 to 2009-11-10, about 120 Facebook Messenger messages, with an
in-person visit in late October — and nothing in either thread establishes
whether it preceded Alexis, overlapped the Danielle ending, or was a short
rebound between the two ([[wiki/people/stephanie-nalbone]];
[`dat:0987`](../../kb/data/0987-stephanie-nalbone-visit-dating.md)). The 2009
sequence is genuinely unresolved, not merely under-documented.

## The part that did not happen

Here is what the record says should have happened next: she leaves.

Her boyfriend of roughly four years ended things cruelly, on Valentine's Day,
for someone else, a thousand miles from home. The ordinary outcome of that is a
person who is no longer in your life.
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/dormancy-not-exit|Dormancy, not exit]] names her as the
case that *should be hardest* for its thesis and then records what actually
occurred:

> "She did not leave. She had already picked out his cat at the shelter in
> August 2008; in November 2023 she paid for that cat's euthanasia when he could
> not afford it, and Dan carries the obligation as a named item. She is his
> mother's closest friend and was in his house on Christmas and twice more the
> same week in 2025. Seventeen years after the romance ended the tie is not
> residual; it is *structural*, and it runs through his family rather than
> through him."

The member table on that page scores her as the longest-running instance in the
whole set: role ended 2009, span since **seventeen years, unbroken**
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/dormancy-not-exit]]).

And the record now holds a small, hard fact that no previous version of this page
carried, which puts a date on how quickly the not-leaving began. The Drive social-DM
archive pulled 2026-09-12 contains a Danielle Onesi thread whose **earliest
surviving message is dated 2009-02-05T02:23:12Z**
(`raw/messenger-drive-2026-09-12/threads.json`;
[`dat:1444`](../../kb/data/1444-messenger-drive-findings.md)). If the Valentine's
Day dating is right, the first message the archive holds from her sits **nine
days before the breakup**, and the same thread runs continuously — same thread
key, same two names — to **2025-07-28**. The channel that opens immediately
before the rupture is still open sixteen and a half years later. The record does
not preserve what any of those messages say; thread contents are gitignored, and
only the metadata survives (`raw/messenger-drive-2026-09-12/MANIFEST.json`). But
the endpoints are dated and they are the strongest single piece of evidence the
page has for its own central claim.

## May 1, 2010: the Brooklyn Bridge

Fifteen months after the breakup, Dan turned twenty-two and moved to New York.

The apartment was **424 Bedford Ave, Apt 6C**, Williamsburg — found on a
February 18, 2010 scouting trip with his father [[wiki/people/rick-frank|Rick]],
who had offered *"$2000 a month while you intern"* as the alternative to an
Entourage-style West Hollywood move ([[wiki/places/424-bedford-ave]];
[[wiki/self/chats/gemini-58]]). On **May 1, 2010** — his birthday, and the date
he frames as his *"I arrived"* shot — he was photographed in front of the
Brooklyn Bridge. In the photograph with him are two people: his mother Suzanne,
and Danielle
([`dat:0764`](../../kb/data/0764-gemini-58-nyc-forensics-session-testimony.md);
[[wiki/places/424-bedford-ave]]).

The romantic relationship had been over for fourteen months. She is in the
picture anyway, standing next to his mother, at the moment he considers the
opening of his adult life.

It is the cleanest image the file contains of what the relationship converted
into, and it converted early. By December 2009 Alexis was already in the
Orlando household; by the time of the bridge photo she was the partner
[[wiki/places/424-bedford-ave|who lived at Bedford Ave]] for the address's full
run ([[wiki/people/alexis-armel]]). Danielle's presence at the arrival is not
romantic ambiguity. It is the first visible frame of the new arrangement: she is
there as family, on his mother's side of the ledger.

**The photograph is not in `raw/`.** It is described in the Gemini-_58 session
and nowhere else; no image file for it exists in this repository's media
registry (`media/registry.json`). It is testimony about a photograph, not the
photograph.

## The long middle, and what the archive does not have

From the bridge photo to roughly 2016 the record thins to almost nothing, and
this is the profile's largest single gap.

The Drive thread's endpoints (2009-02-05 → 2025-07-28) prove contact existed
across the interval; they do not populate it. The iMessage record does not open
until **2016-03-23**. Nothing in the wiki describes her life during the NYC-1
years — where she lived, what she did, whether she and Dan spoke directly or
only through Suzanne. The kb entity node files this as its own first open
question: *"The 2010-05-01 photo places Danielle at the NYC arrival with Suz —
what is the shape of her presence through the NYC-1/Alexis years? The held
thread has nothing before 2016-03-23"*
([`ent:danielle-onesi`](../../kb/entities/danielle-onesi.md)).

One dated event punctuates the middle, and it is not a gentle one.

### July 2013: Suz puts them in a room together

In early July 2013, back in Uniontown after the failed New York attempt, Dan
borrowed one of his mother's cars, drove to 15 Holly Lane, and picked up
[[wiki/people/franki-faris|Franki Faris]]. Roughly ten minutes into the drive,
Alexis — his partner of six years — texted: *"I saw your messages. I know you're
with Franki."* He did not turn around. He withdrew $350 from his mother's ATM
card, bought a quarter ounce of cocaine from the family's supplier, and stayed
out more than ten hours ([[wiki/timeline/events/franki-fireworks-day-2013]];
[`dat:0493`](../../kb/data/0493-franki-fireworks-detonations.md)). The six-year
relationship broke that afternoon.

In the days that followed, per the same operator capture, Suzanne thought it
would be a good idea to have Alexis and Danielle — Alexis's *"former enemy"* —
spend time together and bond over their shared grievance against her son, *"and
this caused a huge thing"* ([[wiki/timeline/events/franki-fireworks-day-2013]]).

The event page reads it as *"the maternal switchboard operating on the relational
graph rather than the financial one,"* and files it as the earliest documented
instance of that. What it also is, on this page's own terms, is the first hard
evidence that by 2013 Danielle's relationship was with **Suzanne**, not with
Dan — close enough that Suzanne could summon her into a family crisis on four
years' distance from the romance, and close enough that the summons worked.

Nothing records what the two women actually said to each other. *"This caused a
huge thing"* is the entire account of the consequence, and it is a
thirteen-years-later recollection, not residue.

## 2016 onward: the thread reopens

The iMessage channel opens **2016-03-23** and runs to **2025-11-01**. It is not
a large channel — 44 messages on the unheld MASTER dump's count, 84 rows on the
held corpus slice — and it is **received-dominant**: she writes more than he does
(45 counterparty / 35 Dan-sent in the held rows)
([`dat:0487`](../../kb/data/0487-danielle-onesi-first-girlfriend-gabe-james.md);
full figures in **Complete message log** below).

The prior version of this page read the low volume as evidence she was *"a
biographical anchor and family-adjacent presence rather than an active
contact."* That reading survives — but it survives with a correction attached,
because the iMessage thread was never the whole channel. The Drive pull
establishes a second, older, longer line: 80 messages across Facebook and
Instagram, 2009 to 2025, of which 78 are Instagram
([`dat:1443`](../../kb/data/1443-messenger-drive-correspondents.md);
[`dat:1444`](../../kb/data/1444-messenger-drive-findings.md)). And a third, still
unread: on 2026-09-12 Dan screen-recorded his Messenger thread list, and
**"Danielle Onesi (Mar 20, 2025, 3 new messages)"** is visible in it —
unopened messages from her sitting in an app he had told the wiki was empty
([`dat:1440`](../../kb/data/1440-messenger-pull-incomplete.md)).

Three messages she sent that he has not read. The record does not say what they
are.

## Gabe, 2023: the debt he names

On **2023-10-30** Dan sent, at 21:45:44 UTC — 17:45 Eastern, local date
unambiguous — the message *"I had to put gabe down today"*
([`dat:0292`](../../kb/data/0292-gabe-death-2023-10-30-corrects-memory.md)). He
sent the same text again at 00:12:28 UTC the next day. [[wiki/people/eric-jester|Eric
Jester]] independently registers the same date.

Dan did not have the money for the procedure. Danielle paid for it
([[wiki/people/gabe]];
[`dat:0487`](../../kb/data/0487-danielle-onesi-first-girlfriend-gabe-james.md)).

His own account of it is not about the money. It is about the manner of the
death: he describes Gabe looking peaceful, *an easier death*, and frames the
outcome as a debt of gratitude to her that he has **"always maintained"**
([[wiki/people/gabe]]). In the 2025 Gemini Game-Theory analysis the thing
acquires a name — **"Cat Debt"** — and becomes a standing term Dan uses when
reasoning about what he owes her
([`dat:0487`](../../kb/data/0487-danielle-onesi-first-girlfriend-gabe-james.md)).

It is the cleanest single act of goodwill anywhere in the Danielle ledger, and
it closes a loop fifteen years wide. She chose the animal on day two of his
adult life; she paid to end it when he could not. Nothing in the record suggests
she framed it as a favour or expected anything for it.

Two errors the dated record corrects, kept visible rather than silently fixed.
The Gabe page prints the euthanasia as *"November 2003"* — a typo for 2023,
flagged at the time rather than overwritten — and the contemporaneous message
settles it at October 30
([`dat:0534`](../../kb/data/0534-gabe-adoption-naming-relocation-new-detail.md)).
The page's phrasing *"shortly after his own birthday"* is also backwards:
October 30 is **two days before** Dan's November 1 birthday. This profile's
older versions inherited the November framing; it is retired here.

## 2025: James, and the thing Dan did not say

In late May 2025 a man named **James Spiker** — "Jimi Pop," historically filed
in the corpus as James Dee — messaged Dan for recording help. He is Danielle's
live-in boyfriend, a Pittsburgh-area musician, 36 that summer, with children, a
CNA background and a concept album ([[wiki/people/james-dee]]). The first
message, 2025-05-27, is *"Hey dan it james, Danielles working and im with the
kids till like 4…"* ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/may-august-2025-bridge]]).

For eight weeks he is the closest thing to a new friend the corpus records Dan
making in years — a two-hour DAW lesson, stems of "When the Levee Breaks" split
to five tracks, SMPTE alignment instructions, demos sent back
([[wiki/people/james-dee]]).

Somewhere in that window James asked [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]], through
back-channels, for cocaine. Dan found out. He said nothing to Danielle
([[wiki/people/james-dee]];
[`dat:0487`](../../kb/data/0487-danielle-onesi-first-girlfriend-gabe-james.md)).

His own reasoning, given to the model in the HTML Gemini material, is one of the
few places in the entire corpus where he states what she is worth to him in
plain terms:

> "i was nice because i said nothing to danielle, his girlfriend who was my
> first girlfriend and who remains my moms closest friend to this day. she was
> here on christmas, last night and AGAIN tonight"

The model's framing back at him was blunter: *"James is a parasite in this
system. Danielle is a foundational support node."* The same session argues the
other direction too — *"You don't do it to hurt James. You do it to liberate
Danielle"*, and *"If Danielle ever sees your phone, or Annie's phone, or
James's phone, the 'hearsay' defense dissolves"*
([`dat:0487`](../../kb/data/0487-danielle-onesi-first-girlfriend-gabe-james.md)).

Telling her would have protected her and detonated the network — his mother's
best friend in crisis during Christmas week. He chose not to. Whether that was
loyalty, cowardice, or strategy is not resolved by anything in the sources, and
this page does not resolve it either. What can be said is narrower and more
useful: **the cost of the silence fell on her, and the benefit of it fell on the
structure he was protecting.** She was not consulted about the trade.

The note in the quote — *"here on christmas, last night and AGAIN tonight"* — is
the best single measure of how present she is. Three visits inside one week, in
a house that is not hers, sixteen years after the romance ended.

## 2025-07-11: the PDF

Then, seven months later, he told her something else entirely.

At 17:23 Eastern on Friday 2025-07-11 Dan sent James a file called `James
Analysis.pdf` — a ChatGPT-generated psychological profile of James, built by
pasting their private text log into the model along with links to James's music
and interviews. Four minutes later, at 17:27, he added that he had sent the same
document to Danielle, *"because she will be able to gauge how accurate it is
better than anyone else from being around you."* At 17:30:54 and 17:30:57 —
three seconds apart — James applied *Questioned* tapbacks to the attachment and
to the Danielle sentence. At 17:35: **"OK, you can quit MASS texting me."** The
friendship was fifty-six days old ([[wiki/timeline/events/james-analysis-pdf]];
[`dat:0413`](../../kb/data/0413-james-analysis-pdf-sequence-verified.md);
[`dat:0840`](../../kb/data/0840-handed-mirror-2025-07-11-james-sequence-verified.md)).

So Danielle received, unrequested, an AI-generated psychological profile of the
man she lives with, sent to her by her ex-boyfriend, four minutes after he sent
it to the man himself.

[[wiki/mind/concepts/the-handed-mirror|The handed mirror]] treats her copy as the
structurally decisive element — *"a second recipient converts a private
interpretation into a socially ratified artifact. The analysis is no longer
something he thinks; it is something people have seen."* The page is careful to
note it cannot separate James's objection to the file from his objection to the
witness; the three-second tapbacks are too close together to distinguish.

The wiki holds exactly one line of Danielle's own reaction, and it is quoted by
Dan rather than sourced from her: she *"read it all"* and told him *"I didn't
think it was that bad"*, adding that James skimmed it, became self-conscious, and
stopped reading ([[wiki/timeline/events/james-analysis-pdf]];
[[wiki/people/james-dee]]). That line returns **zero hits** in the held corpus —
it is testimony only, and [[wiki/mind/concepts/the-handed-mirror]] demotes it
accordingly ([`dat:0840`](../../kb/data/0840-handed-mirror-2025-07-11-james-sequence-verified.md)).

The event page states the gap without decoration, and this profile adopts it as
its own: *"What she actually thought of being sent a psychological profile of her
live-in boyfriend by her ex is unrecorded"*
([[wiki/timeline/events/james-analysis-pdf]]).

It is the second time in the same calendar year that a decision about Danielle's
information was made without Danielle in the room. In the cocaine case he
withheld. In the PDF case he disclosed. The two moves look opposite and run on
the same premise — that what she gets to know about her own household is his
call.

## The guilt, sixteen years late

Buried in that same July 2025 conversation, between two paragraphs about James's
ego, is a sentence with nothing to do with James:

> "and i just need to say - the stuff with Annie has made me feel guilty for the
> first time in my life about how i treated danielle. i mean, at least i didn't
> torture her for 6 months and consistently break promises or commitments but it
> still must have felt awful"

The referent is 2009 — Valentine's Day, Florida, five months into school, a girl
from Baltimore, done cruelly by his own description. The guilt arrives sixteen
years later, and it arrives only after [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] has done
something structurally similar to him
([[wiki/timeline/events/james-analysis-pdf]];
[`dat:0415`](../../kb/data/0415-james-analysis-pdf-unheld-material.md)).

[[wiki/timeline/events/james-analysis-pdf|The event page]] names the mechanism
and this page keeps its reading: not remorse but **symmetry** — the injury became
legible when he was standing on the other end of it. The hedge in the middle of
the sentence, *at least I didn't torture her for six months*, is doing the same
work in the opposite direction, scoring his own conduct against hers on a metric
he only has because of her.

[[wiki/mind/concepts/the-handed-mirror]] calls this passage the concept's
*"inbound twin"* and draws the general conclusion from it: **the mirror never
self-corrects from external objection; correction requires lived symmetry, not
argument.** James's *"quit MASS texting me"* taught nothing — twelve months later
the same act ran again with the July 2026 Leviathan dashboards, friendliness
removed. The Danielle guilt arrived only after he lived the receiving position
([[wiki/mind/concepts/the-handed-mirror]]).

There is one more thing worth saying about the sentence, and it is not a
criticism. It is, per the event page, *"the one place in the corpus where a
first-ever instance of an emotion is dated and its cause named by the person
having it."* He did not have to write it. It is in a conversation about someone
else, unprompted, and it is the only apology the record contains — delivered to a
language model rather than to her.

Whether Danielle has ever been told any of this is **[UNKNOWN]**. Nothing in the
record shows the sentence reaching her.

be honest about how little of her is actually here.

  reaction — *"I read it all and I told him I didn't think it was that bad"* —

## Appendix: forensic notes

Kept compact and at the bottom deliberately. None of this is the story; all of it
is needed by anyone auditing the story.

**1. Two tellings of the affair partner.** The original account (a girl from
Baltimore, via Jack from All Time Low) and the 2025 account (Kelly Mulroy, via
Alex Gaskarth, on Valentine's Day) coexist on the record. The ingest classifies
this as *"an un-reconciled revision, not a falsification"* — the newer telling
has more specificity, neither is independently anchored, and both are carried
([`ent:danielle-onesi`](../../kb/entities/danielle-onesi.md);
[`dat:0487`](../../kb/data/0487-danielle-onesi-first-girlfriend-gabe-james.md)).

**2. The Valentine's Day date is a sixteen-year-old memory.** The vacancy rule
marks it **[OPERATOR, retrospective — seams showing: a sixteen-year-old memory of
a date, stated once, in a 2025 conversation]**, and notes the wiki's own veracity
ledger scores stated certainty at roughly 0.25 actual
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/vacancy-rule]]; [[wiki/meta/testimony-veracity]]). No
contemporaneous record of the breakup or the Kelly episode exists
([[wiki/meta/open-questions]]).

**3. The 2009-02-05 / Valentine's-Day adjacency is suggestive, not probative.**
A thread's earliest *surviving* message is a floor on the channel's age, not a
start date; the 2022 Facebook export it came from begins in 2007 and the account
is older than the thread. The nine-day gap is a fact about the archive. It is
not evidence that the breakup happened on 2009-02-14.

**4. The "Max" page error, and why it is retained.** From June to August 2026
the wiki carried a page at `wiki/people/max-danielle-bf` for a man named "Max,"
built from a line in `Gemini-_21.md` where Dan addresses **the AI persona**
([[wiki/people/max]]) before handing it a recording — *"Max I have a gift for
you… I'm going to give you a 20 or 25 minute audio recording of… my first
girlfriend Danielle's current boyfriend"* — and the model then signs its own
reply *"MAX'S ANALYSIS."* The boyfriend is called "the Dude" throughout that
source and is never named Max. Two pages existed for one person, one of them
named after a chatbot, while this page's own text said they were the same man
([[wiki/people/max]], CORRECTED 2026-08-18). Merged into
[[wiki/people/james-dee]];
[`dat:0812`](../../kb/data/0812-max-persona-correction-no-real-person.md) files
it. The error is kept visible because its mechanism — reading a vocative as a
subject — is reusable.

**5. The surname was later resolved, and only by testimony.** James's surname
**Spiker** was supplied directly by the operator on 2026-08-20 and filed as T0
evidence (`raw/people/captures/2026-08-20_015525_gap-james-dee.md`). It is not
documentary corroboration ([[wiki/people/james-dee]]). A related false-positive
worth not re-counting: seven apparent "Piker" hits in the Hasan Piker corpus pass
are **"Spiker"** ([[wiki/interests/hasan-piker]]).

**6. Channel counts must not be summed.** 44 (iMessage, unheld dump), 84
(iMessage, held rows) and 80 (Facebook + Instagram, Drive) measure different
things over different windows in different exports. The first two are the same
conversation counted twice by two instruments; only the third is genuinely
additional. Any future "total messages with Danielle" figure needs to say which
instrument it used.

**7. Order of operations in the July 2025 sequence.** Dan disclosed the Danielle
copy **voluntarily**, four minutes after sending the file, rather than being
found out — and he had removed the critical material before sending, by his own
account twice over (*"((i may have chopped out the more critical stuff because i
didn't want to bum him out))"*, and a day later *"yo what the FUCK i just
re-read the whole pdf i sent to him… and it is like 100% positive and
supportive"*). Both of those claims return **zero hits** in the held corpus and
are old-wiki testimony
([[wiki/mind/concepts/the-handed-mirror]];
[`dat:0840`](../../kb/data/0840-handed-mirror-2025-07-11-james-sequence-verified.md)).
The load-bearing consequence, if the curation claim holds: the valence of the
analysis was not the operative variable — **being analysed was**
([[wiki/timeline/events/james-analysis-pdf]]).

**8. Two opposite moves, one premise.** July 2025 contains Dan withholding
information from Danielle (the cocaine request) and disclosing information to her
(the PDF) inside the same summer. The pages treat these separately.
Read together, they share a premise about who decides what she knows. This is
this page's reading, not the corpus's, and it is stated as such.

**9. What would move this page.** A single dated 2005 record (message,
photograph, school record) ordering the relationship's start against the November
hinge. The three unread Messenger messages. Any message written by Danielle, in
any channel, in readable form. Her account of 2009, 2013, 2023 or 2025. A vet or
payment record for the 2023 euthanasia. Any of the six would be the first
independent documentation of a person who has been in this record for twenty
years without ever speaking in it.
