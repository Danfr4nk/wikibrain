# Ellen Ulmer

Ellen Ulmer is [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]]'s mother — and, in the record's own phrase, "the relationship that outlasted the one it came from": the most durable tie Dan holds to [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]]'s family, still sending unprompted birthday notes in November 2025, eight and a half years after the thread opened.

The thread has a documented origin, and it is not the one anybody would guess. On **April 11, 2017**, Dan wrote Ellen a condolence: [[wiki/people/jay-lauer|Jay Lauer]] — engaged to Ellen's niece [[wiki/people/betherin-mechling|Betherin Mechling]] — had died of a heroin overdose the day before, and Ellen herself had called Dan the previous night to tell him. Dan answered offering the one expertise he had: he had tried to help people like Jay, and gone through it a lot.

The thread runs **100 messages, two-sided** — 37 from Dan, 63 from Ellen — to **July 26, 2026**, when Dan texted her at 07:06 EDT to warn that messages were being written from [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]]'s phone by someone who was not [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]]. In between: the unfailing November birthday notes, Christmas gifts, a church flower arrangement during [[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Fran]]'s final decline, two worried-mother pings routed through Dan — and a quieter peer register of golf-course favors, a recommended book, tooth-extraction banter.

She matters for two reasons: the clearest instantiation of the dormancy-not-exit rule for a relationship Dan never chose — an in-law tie that survived the severance of the relationship it depended on — and the named instrument of the August 2026 Morgantown call: the work email Dan threatened, for three days, to send the recording to.

## How it began

Message one, at 19:09 on **April 11, 2017** (23:09 UTC), is the condolence:

> "Hi Mrs. Ulmer, hope everyone is okay. It's still so hard to believe and only seems more tragic after reflecting. If I can help out in any way please don't hesitate to let me know. I don't know if I have anything particularly profound to offer, but (as someone who tried to help, and has gone through this a lot) I wanted to offer."

The death was [[wiki/people/jay-lauer|Jay Lauer's]] — a heroin overdose dated to April 10 or 11, 2017, the network's only documented fatality — and Jay had been engaged to Ellen's niece [[wiki/people/betherin-mechling|Betherin Mechling]]. Ellen had called Dan herself the previous night. Her reply names the person she was carrying it for: "I appreciate the offer and will keep it in mind as things progress — especially with B. E." Dan answered within the hour: "She didn't deserve to be put through this. It's just awful."

The most durable tie Dan has to [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]]'s family — still running unprompted in 2025 — opened because a mutual acquaintance died and Dan wrote a note as the person who had "gone through this a lot." His parenthetical ("as someone who tried to help") maps onto the intervention account he told [[wiki/people/rick-frank|Rick]] that night — unheld in the corpus, but the Ellen thread's opening is the held half of it.

## The thread, year by year

The 2026-09-09 ingest corrected this page's count from 63 to **100 messages, two-sided: 37 sent by Dan, 63 inbound from Ellen**, 2017-04-11 → 2026-07-26 UTC — the old figure was the inbound count relabeled as the total. (The same defect hit six people pages that wave.) The old export marked every row "Received"; the held corpus reads direction correctly.

**2017.** The condolence exchange, then a boyfriend being folded into the family. November: Dan's tooth extraction — Ellen talks him through it and sends a milkshake ("Whatever it takes..."). November 26: Ellen checks in on [[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Fran]] — "Is Fran ok?" — and Dan, "moonlighting as a grandma-sitter on the weekends," reports in full; the answer is a dinner invitation and "Bill is making birthday brownies!"

**2018.** March 11: "I'm at Jim and Sugie's" — Ellen coordinating a church flower arrangement to cheer [[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Fran]] during her final decline. It confirms **Jim and Sugie are [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]]'s grandparents**, resolving an open identification on [[wiki/places/117-belmont-circle]]. (A 2026-08-10 pass dated **Jim's death to October 2, 2019** — after which Sugie was the sole surviving grandparent, the structural origin of the "Sugie needs care" alibi on [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]]'s page.) April 5: Ellen's condolence after [[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Fran]]'s death — "there's going to be a whole in your lives," corrected to "Hole..." May 20: "Good to see you last night. Thanks for coming!" — Dan had filled in for a bummed-out [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]], and learned Bill and Ellen met at the titlow. June 20: Dan texts Ellen at 09:27, frightened — [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] never came home and is answering intermittently; Ellen talks to her before work and promises to talk to her again. November 1: the birthday note, on rhythm.

**2019–2022.** January 8, 2019: "Are you awake? Can't get Annie to answer." May 2019: key-and-mailbox logistics. November 1, 2019: the birthday note; Dan thanks her. February 16, 2020: "Haven't heard from Annie. All ok there?" — the second worried-mother ping routed through Dan. The held rows then go quiet through 2022; whether those are genuinely sparse years or an export gap is unresolved.

**2023–2024.** The thread reopens in the same warm register. October 2023: Ellen sends Dan shirts ("Glad they fit!"). November 2023 and November 2024: the birthday notes. December 2023 and 2024: Christmas gifts — a shirt [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] "insisted on opening early," then a book that threads Dan's needle ("both right up my alley and is (surprisingly) one I've never even heard of. Great find!"). August 2024: a pure peer favor — Ellen at Shepherd's Rock asks Dan to identify her holes and whether the pond between them has a name ("it appears that the pond does not have a name").

**2025.** One held row: **November 1, 2025** — "Happy birthday!" The annual note still landing unprompted, years past the relationship's terminal precursors. The last warm message in the thread.

## Two registers: maternal check-ins and the peer underneath

The surface register is maternal: birthday notes every November, Christmas gifts, Fran check-ins, worried-mother pings. But the 37 Dan-sent messages show a peer register underneath — caddy questions (Shepherd's Rock, the unnamed pond), current-events chat, a book recommendation, tooth-extraction commiseration; Dan reporting [[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Fran]]'s medical scares like a stakeholder.

By late 2017 he is invited to the birthday-brownie dinner, consulted on the grandmother's care, and treated as the reliable channel when [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] goes unreachable — never kept at boyfriend distance. A de-facto in-law relationship became an independent tie — which is why it survived the severance of the relationship it came from. The paternal pole tells the story by contrast: [[wiki/people/bill-ulmer|Bill Ulmer]]'s thread is 58 messages, April 2017 to June 2020, and then it stops. Ellen's runs six more years. The asymmetry is itself a fact: the maternal tie is the one that outlasted, not the paternal one.

## July 26, 2026 — the one time Dan initiated, under duress

Every other message in this thread is Ellen's. The exception is the last documented contact: Dan's text at **07:06 EDT on July 26, 2026**, during the crisis recorded at [[wiki/timeline/events/july-august-2026-reentanglement]].

The operator's account of why (2026-08-17, T0):

> *"Dan was forced to contact her during the conflict that Annie created in late July where she claimed to be raped and held hostage by Jerel Coles. She made these claims minutes before Coles began texting Dan threats from Annie's phone, leading Dan to believe Annie was in danger."*

**Corroborated, not merely asserted.** The 2026-09-09 ingest settled every open question: the thread's final row, sender "Me," **2026-07-26 11:06:12 UTC = 07:06 EDT** — an iMessage text, not an email. The operator's "Alright I'm contacting Ellen" at 06:22 was the intent-announcement; the message went out 44 minutes later. The motive, per the operator's account: not a leverage threat executed, but a belief that [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] was in physical danger. The message, in full:

> "I am so incredibly sorry to bother you and I really, really hope that this is an overreaction and completely unnecessary but I started receiving messages from Annie's phone that were being written by someone other than Annie, the same person who she had filed a police report for sexual assault against yesterday (you were involved in contact police, according to what she told me) and while I'm very confused and have absolutely no idea what has happened or is going on at the moment I just needed to make sure that I told you, just in case. I had not spoken to her in several months before yesterday so I'm fully without context but she was clear about what happened and I just didn't know what to make of the bizarre messages from her phone at this hour. Again I am so sorry for bothering you and I hope that I was just misled and you don't have any idea what I'm talking about but I couldn't live with myself if something happened to her and I didn't say anything when I had the chance."

"You were involved in contact police" means Ellen was not hearing it cold: Dan assumed she knew about the July 25 police report. "I had not spoken to her in several months" dates the pre-July-25 silence in the thread's own words. The tie is one Dan never used and never leveraged; the single time he initiated, he believed her daughter's life was at risk — and against the six unexecuted "I'm telling your mom" threats, the channel's function holds even at the moment it looks most like it broke.

## August 2026 — named as the delivery address, by both men

Ellen does not appear in the August 16–19 record as a participant. She appears as **the instrument** — the only thing in it that both Dan and [[wiki/people/jerel-coles|Coles]] reach for.

On the night of the call, Coles: *"I'm FaceTiming Dan, you, and the mom"* / *"I'm going to call your dad. Fuck your mom."* [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]], on tape, twice: *"My mom doesn't need this in her life."* And Dan, at 13:30 while the call is still running, aims the recording at her in the moment: *"I'm going to send this whole thing to Ellen because it sounds really fucking bad and I haven't said a word this entire time."* Aimed first at Coles, re-aimed toward [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] afterward — leverage from inception, not repurposed later.

Over the next three days Dan names her email six times, narrating his reluctance:

> *"If I don't hear from you by noon, im sending to your moms work email… that's the only one I could find"* (Aug 17 04:21)
>
> *"should I send to your moms HHS.net email or the Verizon.net email"* / *"i'm really really really trying to avoid sending to mcmillen because as much as I want to make you face up to the truth that would not be good to do on a work email"* (Aug 18 19:43)

The demand was never money or contact but a statement — [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] saying in writing she was done with Coles — and the event record counts **28 maternal-disclosure threats in 67 hours**. [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]]'s stated reason for begging him not to is Ellen's state rather than her own exposure: *"This will literally kill my mom"* (Aug 19 01:49); *"She's at her absolute breaking point / She tried to find a therapist today for herself and broke down crying / Like I am hurting her"* (Aug 17 20:25).

**Whether anything was sent is unresolved**: he claims execution three times on August 19 and denies it twice the same day, and he is documented faking exactly this claim on August 18 — *"i did"* at 19:35, admitted at 19:38 *"it wasn't actually sent… I knew you would suddenly come back to life."* After the probe, Dan's assertions of execution can no longer serve as evidence of execution. See [[wiki/timeline/events/august-2026-morgantown-call]] §The false send. No email, reply, or reaction from Ellen appears anywhere in the held record.

The July 26 disclosure — a life-safety act — has a second case beside it: the same channel proposed as leverage, held for three days, possibly never used. The two should not be scored together.

## Assessment

Nothing about the tie was Dan's initiative — the origin was a death, the register was maternal, the annual rhythm was hers — and yet it is the Ulmer-family tie that survived: the November 2025 birthday notes, the July 2026 disclosure channel, the name both men reached for in August. The paternal thread went quiet in 2020; hers did not.

The channel's function held at every stress point: the one time Dan initiated, he believed [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]]'s life was at risk and apologized twice; the August campaign used her address, not her person — three days of naming the work email, with Dan's own reluctance on the record, and no evidence anything was ever sent.

The cleanest live prediction this page carries is the November 2026 birthday note — whether the annual rhythm survives the collapse and the July disclosure. The held record cannot answer it, nor the questions beside it: did the disclosure damage the tie (no row follows 07:06 EDT that day); is the 2021–2022 thin stretch real quiet or an export gap; did Ellen ever see the August recording. The event page names Dan's sent-mail folder as the cheapest high-value verification; until it is checked, the August case stays open.
