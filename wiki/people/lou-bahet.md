# Lou Bahet

Lou Bahet was an Au Za'atar hostess in the late summer of 2023 with whom
Dan exchanged **1,602 Instagram messages in 21 days** [RAW-CSV]
(`raw/messenger-drive-2026-09-12/messenger_drive_canonical.jsonl`, tkey
`"dan frank|lou bahet"`, 2023-08-31T02:10:13Z → 2023-09-20T23:52:41Z) —
the third-largest thread in the entire Messenger/Instagram Drive pull,
behind only Tom Maison (5,734) and Valeria Iglesias (4,879). She then
vanishes from the record completely. When the thread surfaced on
2026-09-12, Dan's response was "WHO?" — he had no memory of her at all.

  September 1 she tells "the story of how I became a hostess" — she was

No corpus-extract entry mentions her. No iMessage record of her has

## What the messages show

The register is flirtatious-intellectual coworker banter between two
people performing wit at each other. Night one runs on Weimar Republic
jokes, *Das Kapital* (his unread 2,500-page copy), and her comp-lit
background — "This is not how I expected your Insta banter to be yet here
we are." The September 5 marathon is looser: Fortnite map-building,
deepfakes of Gio (Solco, the Au Za'atar coworker), cat photos. It reads
like a crush conducted entirely in jokes — warm, high-energy, and notably
*unconsummated on the page*: no date is ever firmly made, no explicit
romance is declared. The flirtation stays in the banter layer.

Two structural beats matter:

1. **She quits.** On September 11 she's "at a bar with Dogan," "doing
   semi terrible lol," and: "I quit today." Dan's read to Gio afterward —
   "usually when someone says they're up in the air about quitting…
   they've already decided" — suggests the decision predated the
   announcement. She asks, "Wanna hang out this week?" He says "Of
   course 🙃" / "Whenever you want to hang, I'll make sure I'm free
   lmao." No hangout is confirmed anywhere in the thread.
2. **The drinking debate.** September 12–14, Dan delivers his standard
   monologue — a decade basically sober, "I just literally can't wrap my
   mind around the appeal" of drinking — and she pushes back: "I think it
   strongly depends on the company. Know plenty of people who can have a
   good time without drinking." It's the thread's only real disagreement,
   and it's affectionate.

## The end

The thread's last message is hers, September 20: **"U alive?"** There is
no reply from Dan in the record. Whether they ever hung out after she
quit, whether the correspondence continued on another channel, and why a
707-message-a-night rapport evaporated in six days are all undocumented —
confidence: unknown, and the record gives no purchase. The Instagram
export (2025-08-24) contains no later Lou Bahet thread, and she appears
nowhere else in the corpus.

## The forgetting

On 2026-09-12, when the Drive ingest ranked her the #3 correspondent in
the social-DM archives, Dan said "I forgot about that Lou girl even
existed i said WHO?" — verbatim, in chat
(`src:sammy-chat-transcript-20260912-0740`, 2026-09-12T07:22:46Z;
`dat:1447-lou-forgotten-despite-1602-messages`). Three years after 1,602
messages in 21 days, including the single most message-dense day of any
thread in the pull, she was gone from his memory entirely. The page exists
partly to keep that fact queryable: the intensity of a correspondence
predicts nothing about its retention. See
`int:volume-salience-contrast` for the bounded reading — volume measures
channel-local intensity, not durable relationship salience — with the
Annie (133 FB messages, eleven years) and Valeria (4,879 IG messages, one
iMessage) contrast cases and the falsifiers.
