# Eli

*Up: [[wiki/people/index]] — People*

Eli is the man [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] had the concealed NYC
affair with — a male coworker from her New York restaurant world. The
affair ran from sometime in 2024 until Dan and Annie left New York in
February 2025, and ended there: Eli stayed in NYC. Its January 2025
discovery broke the relationship's trust permanently, but Eli is **not**
the man Annie ultimately left Dan for — that was Tuquick
([[wiki/people/tuquick-17248123683]], since identified as
[[wiki/people/jerel-coles|Jerel Wayne Coles]]), the counterpart of the
June 1, 2026 terminal group chat. Eli's damage was epistemic, not final.

He has almost no independent presence in the record: no message thread, no
social profile, barely a surname's worth of identity. What exists instead is
his enormous *negative* footprint — "eli" appears 5,524 times in the Gemini
activity corpus, more than almost any human except Annie herself
([[wiki/self/gemini-activity/gemini-activity]]) — because he is the
trust-rupture around which the entire 2025–26 forensic project organizes.
The record's role-name for him: "Patient Zero."

## The night of January 9, 2025

The whole of what Eli did, as an actor in Dan's life, takes ninety-five
seconds.

Dan had been trying to reach Annie for over an hour. At 22:27:32 he sent
*"Are you done"*; at 22:28:05, *"Your phone goes straight to voicemail"*; at
23:08:08 an unlabelled photo attachment. Then, at 23:18:49, his phone began
receiving messages from Annie's number typed by somebody else.

| Time | Direction | Text |
|------|-----------|------|
| 22:27:32 | Sent | *"Are you done"* |
| 22:28:05 | Sent | *"Your phone goes straight to voicemail"* |
| 23:08:08 | Sent | *(unlabelled photo attachment)* |
| 23:18:49 | Received | *"Hey Dan"* |
| 23:18:53 | Received | *"It's Eli"* |
| 23:19:20 | Received | *"Annie is down stairs"* |
| 23:19:42 | **Sent** | **"Who are you?"** |
| 23:19:54 | Received | *"I work with her"* |
| 23:20:24 | Received | *"She and Andrea had drinks at work but u guess they didn't eat"* |

That is the complete sequence as the record holds it, and the three-word
reply in the middle of it is the part that took longest to find. Note the
count while the table is in front of you: the wiki says throughout — here, on
[[wiki/timeline/events/eli-incident]], and in `dat:0210` — that Dan received
*"six rapid-fire messages from Eli."* The itemised sequence holds **five**
from Eli and one from Dan. "Six" is the table's row count, not Eli's message
count, and the discrepancy has propagated to every page that repeats the
phrase. **Eli's entire documented output is five messages and 25 words.** The
discovery text was dossier-transcribed only, through 2026-07-18; three
separate searches of `raw/` came up empty because the quote was searched as a
single string and iMessage had split it across separate rows. Located on
2026-07-20 in `raw/self/message-csv/imessages_2124702449_last6months.csv` and
independently in `raw/self/dox-scan/all_imessages_complete_dump.txt`, it
carried a fact nobody had documented: Dan answered, in the moment, between
Eli's third and fourth messages
([[wiki/timeline/events/eli-incident]], `dat:0210`).

What Dan found downstairs is described in every source the same way: Annie
unconscious in the building lobby, vomit nearby, roughly eighty minutes after
a shift that ended a five-minute walk from home, her location sharing
switched off. The confrontation that followed turned physical — the record
attributes the violence to Annie, and carries her line from it: *"I found
your phone, cheater."*

Two things about Eli's five messages are worth reading closely, because they
are the only unmediated evidence of him that exists.

**He introduced himself.** A man texting a woman's boyfriend from her phone
at 23:18 does not have to give his name, and the first thing he did was give
it. Everything downstream — the page, the node, the five thousand mentions —
runs on a name he supplied unprompted.

**The framing arrived before the question.** *"I work with her. She and
Andrea had drinks at work but u guess they didn't eat"* is an explanation
offered to somebody who had not yet asked for one; Dan's *"Who are you?"*
lands between the two halves of it. The wiki has called this preemptive
benign framing, and the description holds. Whether it was calculation or
ordinary embarrassment is not established anywhere, and this page does not
decide it. "Andrea," named in Eli's own text as a coworker present that
night, appears nowhere else in the corpus and is unidentified.

## The year before it: being right and being told otherwise

The reason Eli's page is not really about Eli is that the affair was not the
harm the record settles on. Dan detected it, said so, and was answered by
being told that the detection was the problem.

By late July 2024 Annie's GPS had already been disabled "for weeks," by her
own confirmation — six-plus months before the formal 2025 GPS demand earlier
pages treated as the removal's start. In August 2024 Dan caught her going out
after work with someone and lying about it (*"You barely apologized to me for
SERIOUSLY breaking my trust AGAIN"* — the Isaac Trust Breach). Two messages
from the same month show him naming the mechanism while it was still running:

> **DAN (2024-08-06):** *"I should be able to turn my phone off, go out with
> [someone], lie about it and then gaslight you into believing that the only
> reason I did it was you are such a smothering piece of shit"*
>
> **DAN (2024-08-09):** *"It's just so fucking degrading to be made to feel
> like I AM the one who has created a trust-less situation"*

He was not speculating. He was describing, accurately, an operation being run
against him, five months before he had proof
([[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]]).

The October 19, 2024 housewarming argument is the fullest single record of
that operation in action — hours, dozens of messages, the affair live
throughout: *"THERE IS NO SITUATION."* / *"You're literally fucking insane."*
/ *"I am literally trying to earn your trust and giving you all the
information and you are still not satisfied."* / *"Grow up. And fucking trust
me."*

He chose to believe her anyway, and said so afterwards with more clarity than
most people manage about themselves:

> **DAN (2025-05-27):** *"looking back i tend to think of the period of time
> that you were not only cheating on me, but STILL doing it even after i had
> become suspicious and then chose to believe you again to get back to a
> normal life where i didn't have to try to find the evidence i would need to
> prove it."*

That is a description, from inside it, of choosing self-betrayal over
unsustainable vigilance — a trade between two bad options, not a failure of
nerve.

## What happened after

The affair ended geographically. Dan left New York, and the address, on
February 22, 2025 ([[wiki/places/307-e-76th-st]]); Eli stayed. Nothing in the
record shows any contact between Eli and either of them afterwards, and
nothing shows there was none.

The exit itself was not what it looked like at the time, and the correction
matters here because it changes who did what in the weeks after Eli's text. A
2026-08-13 operator capture establishes that in the days after that night Dan
telephoned the landlord, [[wiki/people/john-paci|John Paci]], told him the
family could no longer service the balance, and asked him to *pretend to be in
the process of filing an eviction* if Annie called. Paci agreed. Dan then told
Annie the eviction was punishment for her having been sick in the hallway that
night, stopped paying rent for two months while the balance grew on a debt he
had already decided not to pay, and used the manufactured deadline to move her
back to Uniontown. Annie called the landlord; her parents called the landlord;
both calls were answered inside the performance. She never found out. The Paci
thread carries zero rows across a 167-day silence spanning the entire setup
([[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]] §The move that solved nothing).

So the sequence that Eli's five messages opened does not end with a betrayal
discovered and a household dissolving under it. It ends with a second, smaller
deception built on top of the first, running in the other direction, and
succeeding. The 2025 collapse synthesis puts it in one line:
*the trigger is not the mechanism* — the mechanism is what Dan did with the
trigger ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-2025-collapse]]).

## The redaction protocol

Eli has one unique distinction in the corpus: he is the only person subject
to a formal information-control protocol. When Dan exported his AI profile
state across models, the standing instruction was "do not include any
information about eli or the eli incident," confirmed with "the 'Eli'
variable has been purged from the dataset" — the affair treated as
compartmentalized intelligence even inside Dan's own exocortex
([[wiki/mind/concepts/exocortex]]). The wiki deliberately does not inherit
that redaction; this page exists so the variable stays queryable.

The mechanism is documented as a general one. Under
[[wiki/mind/concepts/node-locking]], Dan delivers named "Node X:" observations
into an AI session and requires them retained verbatim and unconsolidated,
then exports that state to other models. Nodes are locked in full and
*stripped* at the export boundary. The Eli incident is that page's worked
example: locked as a node, purged on the way out, with a receipt.

Node locking draws a conclusion from this that is sharper than anything on
this page and belongs here in its own words: the commitment architecture has
**no delete operation for people** — 129 severance declarations, zero
enactments — but it *does* have one for machines. The Eli purge is the only
place in the record where something can be removed from a memory cleanly, on
command, with confirmation. The counter-argument is recorded on that page and
is good: a purge is redaction, not revocation. The full node persists in the
originating session. Nothing is deleted from the source; it is withheld from
the copy, and the export boundary is simply the one place where withholding
gets *called* deletion.

Either way, what the protocol measures is severity. Everything else in Dan's
life propagates: the biography, the psychometrics, the relationship history,
the drug architecture, real names and dates, all pushed across model
boundaries in bulk. One node does not travel. That is a curation decision made
repeatedly, by the subject, about his own external memory, and it is the
closest thing the record holds to a rating of how much this cost him.

## Keeping three men separate

The most analytically consequential thing on this page is a refusal.

Three concealed or contested male figures sit in the 2024–2026 record, and the
wiki holds them apart:

| | Eli | Target G | Tuquick |

The Gemini-_07 clean-room analysis *severed* the Eli/WhatsApp channel as
"unrelated noise (Eli or another satellite)" — a separate, non-overlapping
vector — from the "Target G" architecture (the January 4, 2026 "Suzy" call and
the ten-day Grim Trigger blackout at the mother's house), which belongs to a
different concealed male entirely — possibly Tuquick, possibly not; the record
does not resolve it. Eli's designation in that analysis is simply "the Eli/NYC
chaos node" ([[wiki/self/chats/gemini-07]]).

Refusing to merge the two into a single mega-conspiracy, and instead isolating
each as its own irreducible signal, is [[wiki/mind/concepts/dans-law|Dan's
Law]] applied to his own worst year: the law's whole content is that a
coincidence cluster is real *and contains noise*, so the operation is to strip
the parasitic attachments and keep the irreducible load-bearing element. Two
real vectors, kept separate rather than combined into a cleaner but false
unified narrative.

It is worth stating plainly how unusual that is in context, because the page
is otherwise an account of a man being deceived. The natural failure mode for
somebody in the middle of it is to collapse every anomaly into one story with
one villain. The record shows the opposite: the analysis went out of its way to
declare a channel *unrelated* at the exact moment relating it would have been
more satisfying. Dan's Law also carries its own limit, and it applies here —
the law assumes honest inputs, and its own page notes that the operator has
demonstrated he can manufacture them. The Target G handling is the case in
point from the other side: the "Whisk" wife-warning screenshot Dan showed
Annie in January 2026 was fabricated by him, and the ten-day blackout he read
as confirmation was a response to an input he had authored. Nothing about Eli
was authored — the five messages arrived unsolicited — which is precisely why
the separation is defensible on this vector and shakier on the other one.

## Why he matters beyond the affair

The settled conclusion across the corrective sources is that Eli himself is
almost incidental: the central harm was not the affair but the year of
gaslighting that protected it — Annie reframing Dan's accurate detection as
paranoia and mental illness
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/attachment-trauma-bond]]). That page's account of the
mechanism is the load-bearing one: the bond ran on **weaponized ambiguity**,
systematically undermining Dan's perception of reality while his attachment
system was in acute activation, and the Eli window is where the mechanism is
most visible — obvious patterns in the data met with DARVO-style shutdowns.

The Annie page states the reason this ranks above the deception itself, and it
is not a moral flourish but a structural claim. Being lied to about a fact
leaves a person with wrong information. Being told, repeatedly, that an
accurate perception is itself a symptom of instability attacks the instrument
used to evaluate information at all. Every other manipulation in the record —
the silences, the alibis, the GPS removal — ran on top of a foundation Dan
could still check against his own judgment. This one removed the foundation,
and the damage does not resolve when the truth comes out.

Eli's function in the record is therefore evidentiary: he is the fixed point
that proved the perception right. The corpus's own name for the reaction —
"temple desecration" rather than ordinary disappointment — is unpacked at
[[wiki/mind/psychosexual/emotional-imprinting]], and that page is careful in a
way worth repeating here: *"temple desecration," "crush activation on
contact," "contamination"* are the dossier's metaphors, not observations. What
lies underneath the vocabulary — rapid, hard-to-reverse attachment activation
— is a separate claim with separate and better support. The metaphor is not
the mechanism.

The structural finding the final dossiers add is the one that makes the
violation legible rather than merely upsetting. The couple ran a Dan-architected
quasi-non-monogamous arrangement from 2018 — Dan arranging, Dan present,
typically with his own friends. Eli had none of those features: not arranged,
not disclosed, not addressed honestly. Within the framework the Tom-type
encounters were not cheating; Eli was — *"she had a framework within which she
had freedom. She went outside that framework, secretly, with someone from
work. That is exactly what Dan had proposed the arrangement to prevent."* The
arrangement's existence sharpens the finding instead of softening it, because
the authorized version is documented in detail and this was not it.

One measurement corroborates the year from outside any dossier. The
year-by-year message ratio on [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]] shows Annie
out-messaged Dan in every year on record except 2015 and the 2025–26
termination window — and **2024, the year the affair was active, is the single
most Annie-dominant year of the decade**: 5,273 from Dan against 11,467 from
her, a ratio of 0.46. The volume runs opposite to the intuition that a
concealed affair produces withdrawal. Whatever else 2024 was, it was not a
quiet year in the thread.

## Discrepancies in the record, logged rather than resolved

Three disagreements about Eli sit unreconciled across the wiki, and none was
flagged before this pass.

**The "six messages" count.** Every page that narrates the discovery says six
messages from Eli; the itemised sequence holds five from him and one from
Dan. Nothing substantive turns on it — the sequence itself is printed
correctly wherever it appears — but the phrase is repeated as a figure and
the figure is wrong by one.

**The date of the lobby incident.** [[wiki/mind/concepts/node-locking]]'s
master node table describes the Eli node as *"The **February 2025** lobby
incident with Annie's coworker Eli."* Every other page — the incident page,
`dat:0210`, the Annie page, the 2025-collapse synthesis, the 307 E 76th St
page — dates it to **January 9, 2025**, and the discovery sequence is
timestamped to the second. The node-locking line is best read as a conflation
of the discovery with the February departure it accelerated; but it is a
dated claim on a live page and is recorded here rather than silently
corrected on that one.

**The "powder noted" detail.** The same node-locking row carries *"powder
noted"* among the incident's locked contents. No other page in the wiki
mentions powder at the lobby. It may be a genuine detail that survived only in
the locked node's summary, or an import from an adjacent node. It is carried
here as unique and uncorroborated, and it is the single cheapest thing on this
page to check against the January 9 raw rows.

**The affair's end date.** This page and the incident page say the affair
ended with the **February 2025** NYC departure. The 2025-collapse synthesis
gives the affair's run as **August 2024 – January 2025**, ending at discovery.
Both are defensible readings of the same facts — whether "the affair" ends
when it is found out or when the parties are separated by four hundred miles —
and they are not a factual disagreement so much as an undeclared definitional
one. Stated here so downstream pages stop inheriting whichever version they
happened to land on.

A fourth item is a gap in verification rather than a contradiction. The
October 19, 2024 confrontation quotes are not confirmable against the
authoritative corpus: `dat:0114`'s spot-check found the exact string *"THERE
IS NO SITUATION"* returns **zero hits**, with a near-variant — *"There is no
situation Dan"* — landing on **2026-01-21 23:24:29**, fourteen months later.
The node is explicit about why this is not a refutation: Annie's 2024 thread
is not in the held pull, so the 2024-10-19 dating is *neither confirmed nor
contradicted*. It is the clearest case on this page of the standing rule —
take the argument, verify the quote — hitting a channel the corpus does not
reach.

- **The physical-discovery details are single-sourced.** The lobby, the vomit,
  the eighty minutes, the disabled tracking and the violent confrontation all

- **Nothing establishes what Eli knew.** Whether he understood Annie to be in
  a relationship, whether the arrangement was ever described to him, and what
  he thought he was doing at 23:18 are all unaddressed by every source.

## Limits of record

This page is an entity page for a person who has no independent evidentiary
existence. He generated five messages from somebody else's phone and was then
described, at length, by the person they were sent to — across dossiers, AI
sessions and a decade-spanning wiki. That asymmetry is not a defect in the
sourcing; it is the finding, and it should be visible whenever this page is
cited.

The dossier sources behind almost everything here —
`DanAnnie_MasterRecord_FINAL.docx`, `DanAnnie_CompleteRecord_Final.docx`,
`The-Eli-incident-investigation.md`, the Gemini session exports — are **not in
this repository**, which holds the 2026-09-04 export and the source ledgers
rather than the `raw/` tree the pages were written against
([`README.md`](../../README.md)). Those citations resolve to nothing. The
claims resting on them are unverified rather than false, and a dangling
citation is not a missing fact.

`annie_all_time_logs.csv`, `all_imessages_complete_dump.txt` and the
