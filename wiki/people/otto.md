# Otto

Otto is [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]]'s nephew — the younger child of
[[wiki/people/claire-ulmer|Claire]] and [[wiki/people/garrett]], and the *"unnamed
nephew"* that Claire's page had recorded as a gap since it was written. He
appears 31 times between October 2023 and June 2025 in the main corpus, roughly
half as often as his sister [[wiki/people/alice]] — and then again,
unmistakably, in September 2026.

The record is thin and domestic, and its value is that it dates and orders the
household. His birthday falls in **late October** — on 2023-10-29 Annie reports
going to *"ottos party"* after visiting her grandmother's new apartment, Claire
having cancelled the children's half of the guest list because *"Otto isn't
feeling well. So it's just family now."* He is collected from school (2024-02-23)
and has shoes bought for him the same afternoon. In March 2025 Annie sets out
the afternoon in a single line — *"Nap time is at 1:00 until 3:00 for Otto..
while Alice has quiet time"* (2025-03-07) — a toddler's sleep schedule against a
school-age sister's supervised rest. In July 2024, holding one child and standing
next to the other: *"She wouldn't let me put her down and Otto didn't want up
lol win win."*

By 2025 the two are handled as a pair: soccer practice together (2025-03-19),
Sweet Frog (2025-05-08), a sleepover with pedicures *"from aunt Annie"*
(2025-05-17), church the next morning at their own request. On 2025-06-16 Annie
is watching both at 1:00 while Claire is at a doctor's appointment — one of
several instances where the childcare is scheduled around Claire's needs rather
than Annie's availability.

**The pattern outlived the relationship.** On **2026-09-17** Annie texts:
*"Got a call to please pick up Otto from school lol."* The recovery memo flagged
this Otto as a new unidentified name — it isn't. Same aunt, same school-pickup
pattern, school-age by 2026 exactly as the ~2021–2023 birth inference predicts.
The nap-schedule toddler of March 2025 is the school pickup of September 2026,
and Annie is still the one getting the call.

**A third child, Waylon, appears exactly twice**, both on 2024-07-11 and both
Thursday: *"Otto Alice and waylon"*, and Dan's reply, *"Waylon Jennings?"* Two
mentions in the corpus is not enough to establish whether Waylon is a third
sibling, a cousin or a friend, so no page is created and the fact is recorded
here and on [[wiki/people/claire-ulmer]] rather than lost.

## Gaps

1. **Surname**, as for Alice — Claire's married name is not in the corpus.
2. **Birth year.** Late-October birthday established; the year is not. The nap
   schedule in March 2025 suggested roughly 2–4 years old, placing birth around
   2021–2023 — and the September 2026 school pickup is consistent with that, but
   it remains inference.
3. **Who Waylon is.** Two mentions, one of them a joke about a country singer.
   Answerable by the operator in a word.
