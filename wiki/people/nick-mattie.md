# Nick Mattie

For fourteen days in the spring of 2017, Nick Mattie was the person Dan
Frank talked to most. They had known each other before — the thread opens
with no introductions — and what ran between them was not a customer
relationship in either direction. Each man sourced for the other, fronted to
the other, borrowed from the other, and carried the running balance in a
book Nick kept by hand. *"Yeah man I try to keep a pretty decent book,"* he
wrote at one in the morning on 11 May, after correcting Dan's arithmetic in
his own favour and being believed.

He was warm on the page in a way almost nobody else in the corpus is —
*"brotha man"*, *"duder"*, *"Ma fuckin dude"*, *"You have no idea lady"*
followed instantly by *"Hahaha that wasn't for you bro"* — and
constitutionally unable to hold a grudge about money. He had a nephew who
stayed over and *"got me for over nine bucks worth of candy."* He paid
toward a friend's baby shower and then used the shower as his delivery
window. He started a new job on 8 May and said so the night before, in the
middle of arranging a run. When Dan slept through fifteen hours of messages
and apologised, Nick's answer was *"Alright. Do you have anything left?"*

Then Dan stopped writing, on 12 May, and never started again. Nick sent ten
more messages across the following two months — *"Yo"*, *"Wanna do me favor*
*for something in return?"*, *"What up"*, *"Can you locate"*, and last, on
12 July, *"The hell are you doing duder"* — and the record closes there.

Nine years later, on 13 May 2026, working a gas-station register during the
month his life came apart, Dan texted Tom: *"Guess who I had the misfortune*
*of seeing / One Nick Mattie."*

## The reciprocal machine

Neither man was consistently the dealer, and the thread establishes that in
its first five messages. On 20 April 2017 Nick opens with *"What's up*
*dude"*, and ninety seconds later asks **Dan** where *"to grab anything*
*with the quickness"* before work. Dan's reply — *"Hmm possibly. How much u*
*thinking?"*, then *"I'll put some in with you if you have a link"* — is the
whole architecture in one line: not a sale, a pooled buy.

Nine days later the direction reverses. *"Yo can u get anything,"* Dan
writes on 29 April at 21:31; *"Yessir. I'm on my way home from work now."*
From there the flow runs both ways in the same denominations — twenties,
forties, fifties, hundreds, product never named once in 324 messages — with
cigarettes riding along (*"Good on the squares"*) and weed as the social
layer rather than the commodity: *"He'll just wait in the car and I'll run*
*in and blow one down with you,"* Nick says of his friend Paul Howell, and
Dan answers *"It's okay he can come in / I haven't seen him in forever / I*
*want to say hi."*

The fronts were bilateral and so was the forbearance. Nick, 10 May: *"Well*
*keep me posted. I can't really put much out on a limb this time but you*
*know I'll work with you."* Dan, forty minutes later: *"If I get u 25*
*tonight could u do a 50? I work in the morning so I can get u the rest*
*tomorrow afternoon."* Nick: *"Yeah that's straight."* Dan: *"You're a*
*gentleman and a scholar."*

And Nick ran his own upstream on Dan's behalf, at his own inconvenience and
without markup he ever mentions: *"He wants me to cop alone. But it only*
*takes me ten minutes from my place. You can just wait at my place til I*
*get back. I won't be long at all brotha."*

## The book, 11 May 2017, 01:00–01:15

The page has always carried Nick's itemisation. With Dan's half restored,
the exchange is a different thing — not a dealer asserting a balance, but
two men reconciling books at one in the morning and one of them conceding.

> **DAN, 01:00** — *"IOU 40"*
>
> **NICK, 01:01** — *"25 from the start plus 50 I gave you tonight. Minus*
> *15 you gave me"*
>
> **DAN, 01:06** — *"Oh I thought we were down to 5 after the 20 you picked*
> *up yesterday"*
>
> **DAN, 01:07** — *"But it's all good I trust your number."*
>
> **NICK, 01:12** — *"Yeah man I try to keep a pretty decent book"*
>
> **DAN, 01:13** — *"I trust you fam, plus I appreciate the favor so IOU*
> *60"*
>
> **NICK, 01:15** — *"Ma man. I'm sure it'll all iron out"*

Nick's arithmetic is $25 + $50 − $15 = **$60**. Dan's opening figure was $40
and his own recollection pointed lower still ($5 after a $20 pickup he
believed Nick had collected). He does not argue, does not ask for the book,
and does not split the difference: he adopts Nick's number whole and adds
twenty dollars to his own stated debt inside thirteen minutes. It is a clean
instance of the lateral-trust posture
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/vertical-authority-skepticism]] describes. Five years
earlier the same man had fought a $104 payroll charge for six weeks and
taken it to the IRS and the NYS Department of Labor before conceding it to
close the matter ([[wiki/work/creative-license]]); here he concedes $20 more
than he thinks he owes, to a peer, on the peer's word, unaudited, in
thirteen minutes. The axis, not the amount, is what differs.

The thread's last dated balance is therefore **$60 owed by Dan to Nick**,
and nothing in the record retires it.

## The other job: a caddie's fortnight

The restored half is also, incidentally, the most granular week of
caddie-season texture anywhere in the corpus, and none of it was visible
before. Dan was working the golf season — the
[[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddying|Nemacolin caddying job]] is the only golf
employment documented in this window, though the thread never names a course
— and it runs directly into the trading.

On 30 April at 06:03, after a night that had run to 03:13 and started again
at 06:00:

> *"Holy shit I can't believe I'm about to go run this course again."*
>
> *"If you can, hold onto like 50 for me and I'll see u after I finish up"*
>
> *"I've got an 8:12 tee time which means I'm home by 3-4pm"*
>
> *"Put it like this: would you run 10ish miles for ~$200 right now?"*

Nick's reply — *"In my state.. no fuckin way"* — is the honest one. By 14:20
Dan is back and asking for a hundred; by 15:12, *"I'm home."*

On 6 May the job produces its own small indignity: *"I had to come into*
*work at 830 because the assholes that were SUPPOSED to golf today aren't*
*calling to cancel, they're just not showing up. There's a 130 tee time,*
*and I can leave after that. There's literally zero chance anyone shows up*
*it's ridiculous."* On 11 May at 13:21, a pocket dial from the course —
*"Sorry I'm on the course and accidentally dialed."*

Ten miles for two hundred dollars, stated by the man doing it, is a rate
figure the caddying page does not otherwise have.

## Annie, and what the missing half had reversed

The thread's structural contribution to [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]]'s
record is the 2 May handoff, which this page has cited for months in a
version that was backwards.

The sequence, both sides, 2 May 2017:

- **13:20, Nick** — *"Am I meeting Annie before I leave?"*
- **14:04, Dan** — *"Did you leave yet"*
- **14:05, Dan** — *"Alright I'll see if she has any cash on her / I know
  I left some money there. How soon are y oh takin off"*
- **14:06, Nick** — *"If not she can grab it and I'll get the cash after
  work"*
- **14:08, Nick** — *"I don't have much on me. I have a buck on me that I
  was taking to work that I can drop off now. I won't have enough time to
  run back home then over again"*
- **14:08, Dan** — *"Okay for sure just take it to my place and stick it in
  the golf bag that's sitting outside / I'll throw u an extra 10 for the
  favor"*
- **14:13, Dan** — *"Is that cool? Annie is still asleep"*
- **14:17, Dan** — *"Nice just take a pic of where u leave it"*
- **14:18, Nick** — *"She just messaged me on FB. Cool to leave it with
  her?"*
- **14:19, Dan** — *"Yeah"*
- **14:27, Nick** — *"It's there"*

**The "buck" is Nick's and the work shift is Nick's.** This page previously
read it as *"Dan carrying 'a buck' ($100) to work as debt repayment"* —
which is what the sentence looks like when you cannot see that Dan was
already at work, that the drop was to *Dan's* house, and that the golf bag
left outside the door was the intended dead drop until Annie woke up and
intervened over Facebook.

What survives, and is strengthened: Annie took the handoff. The supply
network page documents her as logistics facilitator in the 2018–19 era; this
dates the role to **May 2017**, a full year earlier, and adds that she
initiated it — she messaged Nick, not the reverse.

## The drums, and a question this page had open

The old version of this page listed *"And did you get your drums?"* (6 May)
under texture, as *"a stray marker of gear acquisition."* Dan's half answers
it, and the answer is the opposite of acquisition.

At 12:36 on 6 May, bored, with Annie in Columbus: *"If you really want to*
*throw a fucking stick in the spokes I could go get my drum set."* Four
hours later Nick asks whether he did, and Dan replies at 16:39–16:43:

> *"I've got like 20 here but I have something else too. Might go see if I*
> *can get some quick cash from my grandma"*
>
> *"No drums. I still haven't talked to suz haha"*
>
> *"I do have a bass here and and acoustic and drum machines so all is not*
> *lost"*

The kit is at his mother's — [[wiki/places/337-saratoga-drive|337 Saratoga]]
— and retrieving it requires talking to [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]],
which he has not done. The plan is floated and abandoned inside four hours.
Alongside it, *"quick cash from my grandma"* is
[[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Fran]], named as a liquidity source in the middle
of the poverty floor, ten months before the terminal vigil.

And the night before, the same room produced the other thing:

> *"I've been working on music and haven't left the closet since 8 haha"* —
> *"#trappedinthecloset"* (7 May, 02:04–02:05)

Eight at night to two in the morning, in a closet, at
[[wiki/places/155-virginia-ave|155 Virginia Avenue]] — the production
practice running concurrently with everything above, at the same address
where the 2015 grow had been photographed eighteen months earlier. Which
alias the May 2017 sessions ran under is not documented:
[[wiki/interests/music/aliases/mogzart|MOGZART]] is dated ~2014–2016 and
[[wiki/interests/music/aliases/gripnotic|GRIPNOTIC]] is much later, so this
sits in a gap between them.

## Texture of a Uniontown spring

Small-town life, whole, and none of it commercial. Nick's place is *"the*
*old Howell house"*; he runs past Tobacco World; his nephew cleans him out
at the candy counter and Dan's response is a bit that goes three exchanges
deep — *"Fuck dude you're a mark / Next he's gonna start taking your money*
*in shell games or in a subway scam / Watch ur back."* There is Andy's baby
shower, which Nick both paid toward and used as a delivery window. There is
a new job starting on the morning of 8 May. There is a paycheck that went
wrong, and the exchange is the warmest in the thread:

> **NICK, 01:47** — *"What happened with your check? If you don't mind me*
> *asking"*
>
> **DAN, 01:50** — *"I put it in Annie's account and it hadn't cleared when*
> *she left"*
>
> **NICK, 01:51** — *"Talk about unfortunate.."*
>
> **DAN, 01:52** — *"I'm choosing to look at it as an annuity. Like I have*
> *the money to my name but am forcibly delayed from touching it yet lol"*

There is a piece of gear with a story — *"It's some BNI equipment with*
*custom art from Tom Maison hahaha"* — and Nick's *"Is that dude still*
*alive?"*, answered with *"Yeah I saw him last week. He's doing well*
*actually. Much better."* There is a stranger in a car at 22:29 on 7 May
whom Dan reports as looking *"like he was gonna beat me to a pulp"*, and
Nick's explanation, which also closes a gap this page had listed as
*"uninterpretable"*: *"Yeah. Brian brink. Hr wanted to see Tom"* — *he*
wanted to see [[wiki/people/tom|Tom]], not an HR department. One typed
letter had made a job thread out of a man looking for somebody.

And Dan's own boredom, twice, in his own words: *"Sooooooo bored / Thought*
*about going to get some food but I don't even have it in me"*; *"I mean I*
*have a car, but nowhere to go."*

The comic peak is Nick's, timestamped 03:55 on the thread's last live night:
*"How about chick tried adding me on Facebook."* Nobody answers it.

## How it ended

Dan's last message is **12 May 2017, 02:09** — *"About to leave"* — on his
way to collect Nick after a haircut at his mother's. Nick then sends five
more that night (02:10, 02:11, 02:16, 02:50, 03:55), and five more across
the next two months. Ten consecutive unanswered messages.

One caution against reading that as a clean cut: at 02:50 Nick writes *"One*
*sec brotha"*, which is not something you say into silence. The pair had
used voice throughout — *"Just give me a call"* alone appears five times and
fifteen messages mention calling — so contact on the night of the 12th may
well have continued off the text record, and what the corpus shows stopping
is the texting.

The ending needs no drama to explain it, and the restored half makes the
most obvious explanation harder to dismiss rather than easier. **Dan's**
**last stated balance is $60 owed, adopted against his own lower estimate,**
**thirty-six hours before he stopped writing.** That is a motive and it is
not a finding: nothing in the thread or anywhere else in the corpus says the
debt ended the contact, and the ordinary churn of the
[[wiki/timeline/periods/2017-poverty-floor|2017 floor]] — where most
relationships run hot and vanish — remains equally sufficient. Both readings
are recorded; neither is adopted.

## After the thread: the complete later record

Nick's name appears twice more in the entire 192,140-row corpus. Both are in
[[wiki/people/tom|Tom]]'s threads, and neither is Nick speaking.

| When (local) | Thread | Message |
|---|---|---|
| 2025-08-16 02:39 | Tom → Dan (`+17249987341`) | *"I'm gonna have to text Nick Mattie."* |
| 2026-05-13 05:54 | Dan → Tom (`phloxenheim@gmail.com`) | *"Oh so / Guess who I had the misfortune of seeing / One Nick Mattie"* |

**The 2025 line is a supply line.** It falls inside an argument in which Dan
has just told Tom he no longer even calls his own contact — *"tom i was not*
*joking i do not even call the guy myself / i go through suz / she*
*literally makes the call for me every time"* — and Tom, refused, names Nick
as the fallback. Eight years on, Nick Mattie is still a live node in the
Uniontown network, in somebody else's mouth.

**The 2026 line is not.** It arrives forty minutes after Dan has told Tom
*"I get my first paycheck on Saturday / Could you hold me something? Even*
*just one"* and that the family house is a month from closing and his mother
has *"absolutely zero money until her bankruptcy settles."* It is the same
week the [[wiki/work/bfs-foods|BFS Foods]] job fails and two days before
Tom's string-along begins. Whatever ended the thread in 2017, the register
nine years later is *misfortune* — the only evaluative word either party
ever attaches to the other anywhere in the record, and it is Dan's, about
seeing him.

On that evidence this page's `status` moves from `closed` to `active` and
its `date_range_end` from 2017-07-12 to 2026-05-13. The thread is closed;
the person is not.

## Appendix: the count, and what it cost

This page said **170 messages, all marked Received, his side only.** The
authoritative corpus says **324: 170 received, 154 sent, direction field**
**clean, `is_from_me` populated correctly on every row, single-party**
**iMessage thread, `chat_identifier` `+17242526986` throughout.**

The 170 was never a wrong count. It was the received half of a two-sided
thread, read off an export that marks nearly everything Received, and
presented as the shape of a relationship. That is the exact failure
[`int:received-half-count-pattern`](kb/interpretations/received-half-count-pattern.md)
documents across **fourteen** prior threads — mike-hinkle 21→85, jim-shaffer
894→1,845, vicki 234→512, jim-vrabel 313→612, and ten others. **Nick**
**Mattie is the fifteenth**: 154 messages, 47.5% of the thread, filed as
absent.

[[wiki/people/vaughn]] flagged the risk on this page by name in its own
provenance section — *"a uniform direction field across four unrelated*
*threads is a property of the dump, not of four unrelated correspondents"* —
and named nick-mattie as one of the four. That warning was right and this
page did not act on it. It is acted on now.

What the missing half actually cost, itemised:

| The page said | The whole thread says |
|---|---|
| *"The record is his side only"* | 154 of 324 messages are Dan's |
| *"Dan carrying 'a buck' ($100) to work as debt repayment"* | Nick was carrying the buck to *his* work; Dan was already at work |
| *"the 'Brian Brink'/HR job thread of May 7 (one fragment, uninterpretable)"* | *"Hr wanted to see Tom"* is a typo for *"He"*; Brian Brink came looking for Tom |
| *"'And did you get your drums?' … a stray marker of gear acquisition"* | The drums were never retrieved; they are at Suz's and he had not asked her |
| Ledger quoted as Nick's assertion | Dan disputed it, then adopted it, then raised his own debt by $20 |
| *"an unresolved balance is a plausible but undocumented cause"* | The balance is documented: $60, by Dan's own last word on it |
| Nothing about the golf season | An 8:12 tee time, a 10-mile loop, a $200 figure, no-show golfers |
| Nothing after 2017-07-12 | Two later corpus appearances, 2025 and 2026 |

The general lesson is the one
[`int:received-half-count-pattern`](kb/interpretations/received-half-count-pattern.md)
already states and this page is a fresh instance of: *a count error changes*
*a table; a shape error changes what questions are considered answered.*
Five of the seven rows above were things this page had filed as settled or
as gaps, and all five were sitting in the half it had counted as empty.

`CORPUS_POLICY.md` requires the outcome of a re-verification to be recorded
as confirmed, corrected or withdrawn. This is **corrected**; `RETRACTED.md`
should carry the count and the frame error, and the shelved
`MASTER_MESSAGES_DB_DUMP.csv` reference is retained in this page's `sources`
under the one permitted use — to establish what was previously believed and
why it was wrong — not as evidence about 2017.

## Limits

- **The product is never named.** 324 messages of trading and not one
  noun for the thing traded; only cash denominations ($20/$40/$50/$100) and
  the euphemisms *"anything"*, *"something"*, *"one"*.
- **Nothing here is attested by Nick.** Every characterisation on this
  page of a living man with a nephew and a job comes from one archive, kept
  by the other party to the trades.
- **The ending's cause is undetermined.** The $60 is documented; that it
  ended the contact is not. Voice calls, which the thread shows were
  routine, are invisible to the corpus entirely.
- **Nick's upstream source is undocumented** — *"He wants me to cop alone"*
  is the only reference to him and he is never named.
- **The attachments are gone.** Three files referenced, none stored.
- **The 2026 sighting is a single line** with no location, no account of
  the encounter and no follow-up anywhere in the corpus; "misfortune" is the
  whole of the characterisation.
- **Inferred vs documented.** Documented: every count, date, timestamp
  and quotation above, and both later appearances. Inferred: that the golf
  work in this window is the Nemacolin job (the thread names no course);
  that *"Hr"* is a typo for *"He"*; that the 02:50 message implies the night
  continued by voice; that the vCard attachments are dropped pins. Each is
  labelled where it appears.
- **Identity is unverified.** The handle resolves to the name through the
  contact card only, and 342 of the corpus's 498 counterparty handles never
  resolve to a name at all; nothing independently confirms that
  `+17242526986` belonged to the Nick Mattie named in Tom's 2025 message and
  Dan's 2026 one. The continuity across nine years is assumed on the name,
  and it is the least-supported claim on this page.
