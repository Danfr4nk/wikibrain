# "New Jim Shaffer" (Jim Shaffer)

Jim Shaffer is the friend who came back. For most of 2017 he was the subject
of other people's messages — a news clipping Dan pasted into two chats, a man
said to be out of reach, a punchline about how everyone from high school ends
up dead or in jail. Then, on the night of **22 April 2018**, eighteen days
after Dan's great-grandmother [[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Fran]] died, the two
of them opened a text thread that ran for eighteen months and 1,845 messages,
and in its first four hours Jim called Dan *"my longest standing friend on the
planet."* What followed was mostly golf, some grief, a good deal of sex talk,
one real piece of advice, and an open invitation to New York that the record
never shows being taken up.

The thread is the fullest adult record of any of Dan's childhood friends. It
is also, unusually for this wiki, a relationship with no fight in it.

## Before the thread: 2016 and the charges

The record of Jim before April 2018 is thin and entirely second-hand except
for sixteen messages.

- **3 January 2016, about 2:20 a.m.** Dan, texting Annie from his mother's
  house: *"jimmy shaffer was here....hanging out with my mom. i think he's on
  molly and he's acting so. fucking. weird."* (corpus `2016-01-03 7:20:26`
  UTC, [[wiki/timeline/annie-record]]). Eleven minutes later Dan sends a
  contact card to a second, older 724-area handle that the 2021 Facebook
  address-book upload stores under the plain name **"Jim Shaffer"**
  (`raw/facebook/export-20260908-b/gdocs/1l3W5TFb….from-gdoc.txt`).
- **8–9 August 2016.** On that older handle, Dan: *"dude you REALLY need to
  play golf with me"*; the other side answers that he still hasn't picked up
  his clubs, *"We'll be golfing by this weekend."* Sixteen rows in all on that
  handle, all 2016 (`raw/imessage/messages-part1-2011-2019.csv`).
- **11 March 2017.** Dan pastes a local newspaper article into two Messenger
  threads — to [[wiki/people/tom|Tom Maison]] (*"Jimmy Shaffer / Arrested on
  37 felonies / Embezzled $190,000"*) and to a friend named Alexandra Good
  (*"My dad sent me this a little bit ago from uniontown paper"*) — and the
  next day tells a Facebook contact that *"Jimmy Shaffer got arrested on 37
  felony counts for embezzling $190,000 from some investors"*
  (`raw/messenger-drive-2026-09-12/messenger_drive_canonical.jsonl`;
  `raw/facebook/export-20260908-b/gdocs/1zKl-Gcs….from-gdoc.txt`).

The 2017 reactions are the part worth keeping. Tom's first question is
whether it is *"his dad or good ol Jim"*; told it is Jim, he predicts *"20
plus"* and asks *"how many old friends do you have left that aren't
dead/in jail?"* Dan: *"It's just you and me bud … We should have been dead by
2006."* To Alexandra Good he adds two things he knew first-hand — that Jim
*"had my mom listing like $5M in apartments he 'owned' and then just fell off
the eart[h]"*, and that *"He showed up to my moms house last year fucking out
of his mind"* — the second of which is the January 2016 night above. She replies that Jim had asked them to invest in the same company and that
she had heard, via Instagram, that he was in St. Croix. Neither thread shows
anyone in contact with him.

So when the April 2018 thread opens, Dan is not reconnecting with a stranger.
He is reconnecting with a man he had last written about as a cautionary tale,
and he does not bring it up directly.

## 22 April 2018: the reopening

The first message is Dan's full name, sent at 8:50 p.m. Eastern (corpus
`2018-04-23 00:50:01` UTC) — which reads like a new contact card being filled
in.
Dan leads with the estate: *"okay so the house will be on the market soon.
everybody is super anxious to finish out the estate"*, and his mother is
listing it at $350,000. Jim's first question is *"How's your mum?"* and his
second sentence explains his silence: *"I've been quiet since this whole
thing blew up just so I could knock it out and not get any noise."*

Dan's reply is the most careful sentence in the thread: *"i didn't want to
ask you about it, i figured you were in the middle of some awfulness so i
didn't want to make you go through it all again. soooo glad to hear you're
gonna come out okay though."* Then he changes the subject to someone else's
legal trouble — Steve Kezmarsky's father — and Jim tells his side unprompted
(below).

By midnight the conversation has covered, in order: Fran's death and the
hospital vape alarm ([[wiki/timeline/events/uniontown-hospital-vape-alarm]]),
the new golf course at Nemacolin (Dan: *"the back is the most gimmicky shit
i've ever seen"*), the arrest of [[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis]] and
[[wiki/people/zac-shumar|Zac Shumar]] on Valentine's Day 2017, Annie, the
couple's webcam work, Dan's caddie job (*"6 days a week, i'm a machine from
april to october"*), the estate split, and Donald Trump. It is the busiest day
of the thread by a wide margin: roughly **376 messages** on the Eastern
calendar date.

Three things from that night recur for the rest of the record.

**Jim did not know Fran had died, and minded.** *"I'm sorry I didn't even hit
you up or anything / I didn't know man"*; after Dan sends the video of her
last waking moment — *"she woke up and smiled at us and died"* — *"Wish you
would have hit me up. I would have been there,"* and, twenty minutes later,
*"Yeah man I know you two were super close and I'm sorry for not knowing."*
Dan, who describes himself in the same exchange as *"non spiritual"*, tells him
about trying *"the Houdini experiment"* at her bedside — asking in his head for
a sign — and starting to record seconds before she opened her eyes: *"maybe
it's just a coincidence, that's totally possible. but fuck, i was SHOOK."* See
[[wiki/timeline/events/fran-death-vigil]] for the vigil itself.

**Jim did not know Annie.** He guesses her from her family (*"is she Claire's
sister?"*); Dan gives her age as 24, says they have lived together *"since
august 2017"*, and that she is a cocktail waitress at the casino.

**Money was the frame for everything.** Dan lays out the estate — a third to
his mother, a third to his grandmother, a third split between him and his
sister — and guesses his share at *"200-250ish"*, adding that he still had
*"no access to my accounts via rick"* and had *"never even seen a
statement"*. Jim's response is the first of his several deflations of Dan's
money talk: *"That's some fair coin. Enough to escape Fayette if you aren't
stupid."* Two days later, when Dan jokes about a Pebble Beach trip on the first
distribution check and then says *"50k ain't THAT much,"* Jim: *"You're right. That's gone in a good month …
Only thing you should even consider buying is extra guac at chipotle."* In
July Dan sends him an image captioned *"That money sure does go fast
huh"*, and Jim answers, *"Probably because it's not a lot of money lol."*

## The case, in his words and in the paper's

Jim told the story of his prosecution once, in the first two hours, and did
not dwell on it. His version
([`dat:0981`](../../kb/data/0981-new-jim-shaffer-embezzlement-account.md)):

- He had left the company *"6 months before shit went south but I still had
  235K invested I never collected stock from so once I proved 1) everything I
  did was accounted for 2) it's hard to steal 200 when they owe you 235 then it
  was a wrap."*
- The local paper ran *"total bullshit … Like total smear campaign on their
  part about the amount being listed as twice what it really was in question
  or somehow my mothers house was involved"*; he intended to sue the six
  accusers. (`2018-04-23 01:01:42` UTC.)
- *"They did a terrible prelim investigation. Once I proved my input and they
  saw my financials it was clear that it wouldn't make much sense for me to
  take the money... I didn't need it."* *"Fayette County justice system is a
  fuckin joke."*
- *"I hired best attorney money could buy … Spending 55k on the attorney was
  another way to just prove that I have no reason to steal the money."*
- Three weeks later, while arranging to smoke together: *"221 counts...
  dropped to 0."*
- On **13 June 2018**: a *"$115,800. Payment to Fayette county courthouse so
  they can fuck off forever and clear me of everything... I'm upset I'm losing
  2 months wages."*

The article Dan pasted in March 2017 is the only other account in the record.
As pasted, it gives Jim's age as 28 — which matches Dan's own cohort; Dan turns
30 in this thread on 1 November 2018 — and says he was charged with 37 felonies
after allegedly soliciting six men to invest in a company called Lock Trust
Inc. (formerly Cash Flow Consultants) and keeping part or all of their money in
2014–2015: $5,000 in one case and $10,000 in two others, $100,000 across three
second-round investments with no record of the money reaching the company,
and $50,000 of a $60,000 investment used to pay off a loan. In two of the
transactions he is said to have offered a mortgage on a house owned by his
mother. His quoted explanation to investigators was that he planned to *"invest
the money a little at a time because he feared the company would misuse the
funds."*

The two accounts overlap more than they disagree. Both put the sum near
$200,000; both involve his mother's house; they part on what the house was
doing in the story and on whether the money was his to hold. The record has no
court disposition. The **$115,800** payment is consistent with restitution
under a negotiated resolution, and Jim's *"clear me of everything"* is
consistent with a plea that ends the case without a conviction on the felony
counts — but both are readings, and neither the docket nor any paper after
2017 is held here.

## Golf, which was the friendship

Strip out the first night and December 9, and most of the thread is golf, and
most of the golf is Jim teaching.

He describes himself, fairly consistently, as a scratch player who had lived
at Bay Hill in Orlando and *"played and practiced every day"*; he shoots a 70
at Lakeview without a driver or 3-wood in a money game, a 70 at Uniontown
Country Club *"with a triple on #1"*, and takes $100-a-stroke matches against
men he dislikes carrying *"a 60 degree wedge / And a 7 iron / That's all."* He
knows equipment the way collectors do — shaft profiles, tipping, lofts, which
manufacturers sell tour-grade stock and which don't — and gets most of his
through a friend named Eddie who, he says, has worked Titleist's tour van for
over twenty years. On 20–21 May he spends most of an evening fitting Dan by
video: *"Ok we need to get you shorter putter and over the ball more. Your
eyeline is way inside."* The next night Dan admits he had shown those messages
to another caddie and asked whether any of it meant anything real. Jim: *"At
least you have the balls to ask. Nothing worse than somebody who acts like
they know and doesn't … And I don't try to protect what I know. I'll tell
anyone who wants to learn everything I know."*

Dan's side of the golf is work. Across the thread he caddies at Mystic Rock,
adds Laurel Valley on **22 May 2018** (*"Ya boy is officially now working at
laurel valley gc as a caddie"*, and later: *"lv by like 4x"* in pay), and on
**27 June** announces *"Official pinewood caddie now"* — which, given Jim's
earlier praise of Pikewood, is most likely Pikewood National typed wrong;
the record does not settle it ([[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddie-years]]). In
September Jim asks *"What's this bs about uniontown closing up?"*; by October
Dan is *"playing free golf from 7am until dark at ucc since they closed."*

### The putter

The one transaction in the thread is a putter, and it did not go to plan. On
3–4 June Jim offers a Scotty Cameron mallet, custom length and lie, for $190
through Eddie. They meet in person on the evening of 4 June — Jim afterwards:
*"Your wrists are in good angle when you hold the putter"*; Dan: *"i actually
found you to be a relatively normal version of jim shaffer tonight"*; Jim:
*"normal is not my normal"*. Jim fronts the money. Dan, still at Laurel Valley and
*"limping all day"*, misses the handoff the night before Jim flies to Florida.
From Florida Jim renames him *"Fantasma"* in his phone and reports that the
head had turned out to be a tour-only "Circle T" worth, by his estimate,
around $1,500: *"It will be my 4th Circle T putter. I have a legit $10K putter
collection."* Dan offers to repay him *"for covering me on the flat stick"*.
A second offer follows on 19–20 June (a Cameron at $120 or a TaylorMade Spider
at $140); Jim buys both and tells Dan, *"don't sweat the putter."* Whether Dan
ever received one is not in the record.

## The arrangement, and what Dan's half shows

On the first night Dan told Jim about the arrangement under which he set up
encounters between Annie and other men ([[wiki/mind/psychosexual/arrangement-history]]),
and Jim, who said he had never known anyone to ask about it, asked everything
— and said, within the hour, that he could be persuaded, on one condition:
*"I would need assurance that only 3 people on the planet would ever know
about it."* The prior version of this page recorded the proposition's outcome
as *"not confirmed in the surviving thread"* while holding only Jim's 894
messages ([`dat:0980`](../../kb/data/0980-new-jim-shaffer-thread-dimensions.md)).
Dan's 951 are now read. They show:

- **22 April.** Dan says he has *"briefed her"* and proposes letting Annie
  message Jim from his phone. Jim counters with a slower plan: *"We meet, smoke
  a bowl and just bullshit first time she and I meet. I leave and once I'm gone
  she can tell you if she's down."* Dan agrees that the night *"was much more
  about the fun tonight than anything else."*
- **May–June 2018.** The in-person plans that follow are about weed and golf.
  The one documented meeting (4 June) is about a putter.
- **1 February 2019.** Dan raises it again directly. Jim: *"Sounds great but
  I'm not around at the moment … maybe we can coordinate,"* then *"Travel
  isn't locked in yet. I'll let you know."*
- **After that,** no message returns to it.

**The iMessage record does not show that it happened.** It shows two
invitations and two deferrals, both from Jim. That is a statement about this
channel: the thread refers repeatedly to Snapchat, which is outside every
source held here, so absence here is `never_observed`, not
`known_not_to_occur` (see [`CORPUS_POLICY.md`](../../CORPUS_POLICY.md)).

The same February exchange carries the question the old page quoted — *"So
then you have veto rights over who she fucks?"* — and Dan's answer, which the
old page omitted: *"Even more compartmentalized than that. I contact whoever,
confirm the person with her, set the thing up, and walk the dude to the
door."* By October 2019 Dan is describing the New York version of the
arrangement to Jim, including paid companions, and Jim's reaction is the
three-message burst the old page cited: *"bro. / The hooker thing / I'm
fucking DYING."* That exchange belongs with [[wiki/people/brian]] and
[[wiki/people/jaredtricia]] as a view of the same period from a friend's
side. Jim's own contribution, in October 2018, when Dan asked about finding
escorts: *"I'm definitely a no outlet road on this one."*

## 9 December 2018: Rob

The second-busiest day of the thread (about 229 messages) opens on a Sunday
morning with a memory: *"Do you remember when me, you and rob orange took that
suped up mini van to the Tom Petty concert."* It is the handle's locator in
the corpus and the independent corroboration of
[[wiki/timeline/events/robotussin-s-last-dance]]
([`dat:0360`](../../kb/data/0360-robotussin-s-last-dance-date-and-trip-corroborated.md)).

Here the two retellings differ in a small, telling way from what the old page
said. Jim's is adventure; Dan's is not quite: *"we ALL ate a bottle of
roburussin, each."* Jim: *"All I remember is how hard we would fucking puke."*
Dan: *"We would TRIPLE the speed limit driving to Tom petty concerts which we
had no tickets to, nor would we attend."* So the December retellings name the
drug; what they omit is the dose and the drive-specific detail the 2026
capture adds.

Then the register drops. Dan: *"But really...rob is the only one that I get
sad about."* Jim, asked what year Rob died, first agrees to 2013 and then
corrects himself — *"No. 2014"* — consistent with
[[wiki/people/rob-orange]]'s April 2014 date
([`dat:0984`](../../kb/data/0984-rob-orange-death-account.md)). Dan:
*"Doesn't it feel like...all of this should have been a bigger deal / Half of
our friends died lol."* Jim: *"You and I might be descended from Roaches. We
somehow went the hardest and survived."*

It is Jim who asks about [[wiki/people/steve-kezmarsky|Steve Kezmarsky]] —
*"How did Steve Kezmarski make the cut?"* — and Dan's joke answer produces the
misreading this page once repeated: *"Wait... he's dead?"* Dan,
eight seconds later: *"No no I mean / He's alive but living the least enviable life
EVER / two kids / Sober / Dad going to jail for the rest of his life / Family
in ruins"* ([`dat:1088`](../../kb/data/1088-steve-kezmarsky-alive-correction-2018-12-09.md);
the father's arrest is [`dat:1089`](../../kb/data/1089-elder-kezmarsky-fall-jan-apr-2018.md)).

After a run of photographs of old classmates, Dan poses a trolley problem: *"You're playing god...you can kill everyone in that image to bring
rob back...what do you do."* Jim: *"That's not even a choice. Best deal in
the history of trade deals,"* and then the only sentence in eighteen months
with no joke anywhere near it:

> *"Rob and I would talk on the phone and laugh till we cried for 2 hours at a
> time, 5 days a week. I'd trade everyone in that photo for one more call."*
> (`2018-12-09 17:40:15` UTC)

The same day Jim mentions that he has *"only … to be here for 1 more month
then I'm leaving,"* and is selling a large abstract JFK painting he bought for
$1,500 about six years earlier rather than move it. Dan, the same afternoon,
calls this *"probably the second happiest time in my life"*, and asks, *"Isn't
it great to have money."*

## 2019: New York, and the one piece of advice

The thread goes quiet in January 2019 and resumes on Dan's schedule. On
**26 March** he writes from Manhattan (*"I moved back to nyc. That's an
important part of the story making sense"*) with a story about an escort. Jim's
first line is not about the story: *"I'm always proud of you. Anyone that
endure what we did in high school has my eternal respect."* A few messages
later he offers to write the book — *"We'll start from the convenience story
parking lot leaving Tom Petty with Rob Orange and just go from there."*

On **12 April** Dan, about to take the train back to Pennsylvania to watch
the Masters, tells him he is building *"a solid 5 minutes"* for open mics
([[wiki/interests/stand-up-comedy]]), that he spent $1,500 on an escort the
week before, that *"right now i think the term for what im doing is 'HIGH BURN
RATE'"*, and then, *"no joke and between us if i didnt move here when i did,
rehab would have been in my future,"* adding that the couple were spending half
as much in a $2,500-a-month apartment. Jim's answer is the only unsolicited
advice he ever gives Dan that is not about golf:

> *"You can't outrun yourself. I think it's great you're having fun, just
> don't do anything incredibly stupid."*

Dan's reply is his own rule: *"if you dont drink, and i mean like NEVER EVER
drink, you avoid most problems."* Two days later: *"it'll work out. it always
does. for you and i at least."*

## The last exchange

After April 2019 the thread is four messages in six months. On **19–20
October 2019** Dan sends a video; Jim critiques the person in it; and then Dan,
at 9:13 p.m.:

> *"i miss your life / you are 1 of 3 people that i actually would enjoy
> seeing"*

— the other two, he adds, being his cocaine dealer and a paid companion — and
*"it would bring a single indian tear to my eye if we can link up sometime,"*
and he hasn't played a round all year *"because i've been trapped in
manhattan."* Jim: *"When I take a minute to breathe I wouldn't mind a trip to
NYC."* Dan offers his couch on East 76th Street
([[wiki/places/307-e-76th-st]]) and recommends a Netflix documentary. The
thread ends there, on Dan's message, at 10:35 p.m. Eastern on 20 October 2019.

The record does not show the visit. It does not show any later contact at all
beyond one datum: in the Facebook address-book upload of 16 October 2021, the
contact card is still there, under the name that heads this page.

## What the friendship was

The honest reading from the record is narrower and warmer than the old page's.

It was **asymmetric in money and not in regard.** Jim had the townhouse at
Cheat Lake, the duplex in Hopwood, a condo he was upgrading, the motorcycle
and the $10,000 putter collection; Dan had an inheritance arriving in pieces
and a caddie's income. Jim never lent him money in the record beyond the putter,
and repeatedly told him his windfall was smaller than he thought. Nor does the record show him
condescending to Dan about golf, which was the thing Jim cared about most.

It was **the only thread in which Dan was the one who got told to be careful.**
In most of Dan's adult record he is the caretaker, the planner, the one
giving instructions. Here he gets a scratch golfer's fitting advice, a rich
man's estimate of how fast $50,000 goes, and one line about outrunning
yourself — all from someone who had just spent a year being publicly accused
of fraud.

And it was **held together by the dead.** The two long days in the thread are
the night Dan told him Fran was dead and the day Jim remembered Rob. The book
Jim offered to write starts in a parking lot with Rob in it.

What it was not, on this record, is a sexual partnership or a close adult
friendship in the ordinary sense. They met in person at least once that the thread can see (4 June 2018). Everything else was text.

## Coverage and limits

- **Channel.** One iMessage thread (the 2018 handle), sixteen 2016 rows on an
  older handle attributed to him by the Facebook address book, and third-party
  messages about him in 2016–2017. Snapchat, phone calls and in-person contact
  are referenced in the thread and absent from every source.
- **Identity of the older handle.** Attributed from the address-book card name
  and the golf content. Not otherwise confirmed.
- **The case.** Two accounts — his, and a newspaper article as pasted by Dan
  from a copy his father sent him. No docket, disposition or sentencing record
  is held. What "cleared" means legally is not established.
- **After October 2019.** Nothing. The address-book card shows only that the
  contact was kept.
- **Times.** Quoted rows are identified by their corpus UTC timestamp; dates in
  prose are Eastern (four hours behind UTC in summer, five in winter).
- **Handles** are withheld here per the standing third-party rule
  ([`kb/entities/new-jim-shaffer.md`](../../kb/entities/new-jim-shaffer.md)).
- **Register.** The thread is dense with racial slurs and shock humour from
  both men. That is described rather than reproduced.
