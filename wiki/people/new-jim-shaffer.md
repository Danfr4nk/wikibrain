# "New Jim Shaffer" (Jim Shaffer)

In April 2018 Dan and Jim Shaffer reconnected in the middle of two separate
disasters. Dan's great-grandmother [[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Fran]] had died
eighteen days earlier. Jim was finishing a criminal case in which he had been
accused of taking about $200,000 from a company he had left. Neither had
called the other. Jim said so first: *"I've been quiet since this whole thing
blew up just so I could knock it out and not get any noise."* Dan said the
same thing from his side: *"i didn't want to ask you about it, i figured you
were in the middle of some awfulness so i didn't want to make you go through
it all again"* [JS 2018-04-22 20:53–20:55]. Within two hours Jim was calling
Dan *"my longest standing friend on the planet"* [JS 2018-04-22 22:52].

Over the next eighteen months, until October 2019, the thread holds that
friendship between two men from the same Fayette County high-school cohort.
Jim was wealthy, a scratch golfer, pro-Trump and reflexively crude. Dan was
caddying, living with [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]], spending an
inheritance and then leaving for New York. What they shared was a past both
of them call the best time of their lives, and a list of friends from it who
had died. The thread ends with Dan inviting Jim to Manhattan: *"you are 1 of 3
people that i actually would enjoy seeing"* [JS 2019-10-20 21:14]. The record
does not show the visit happening.

The contact card on Dan's phone reads "new jim shaffer"
(`raw/drive-sweep/20260911/misc/wiki-export-self-timeline-people-mind-work-interests-health-places.md`).
The page title comes from that card. The earlier version of this page
explained the "new" with Jim's own line *"And I'm not the only Jim Shaffer in
your life"* [JS 2018-04-22 22:48]. The line exists. The explanation is an
inference, and the record does not say why the card was labelled that way.

## The record, corrected to both halves

The earlier version of this page counted 894 messages and read the thread as
one-way, Jim to Dan, because its export marked every row Received. The
authoritative corpus holds **1,845 rows: 951 from Dan and 894 from Jim**
([dat:0980](../../kb/data/0980-new-jim-shaffer-thread-dimensions.md)). This
rewrite re-derived that count from the held parts. Of the 1,845 rows, 1,682
are in `raw/imessage/messages-part1-2011-2019.csv` and 163 are in
`raw/imessage/messages-part2-2019-2026.csv`, all under chat_identifier
`+14125139408`. The 894 was the received half exactly. It is one of fourteen
threads where the same error occurred
([int:received-half-count-pattern](../../kb/interpretations/received-half-count-pattern.md)).

That correction settles more than a count. The old page had filed several
questions as unanswerable "in the surviving thread", and the answers were in
Dan's 951 messages, which nobody had read. This version was written from all
1,845 rows. Several of the old page's readings did not survive, and each one
is marked where it comes up.

## April 2018: two cases, one night

**Jim's case, in his words.** On the first night Jim explains the case to Dan
in four messages. He left the company six months before it collapsed. He
still had $235K invested that he never collected, *"so once I proved 1)
everything I did was accounted for 2) it's hard to steal 200 when they owe you
235 then it was a wrap."* He says the Herald-Standard ran *"total bullshit"*,
listing the amount *"as twice what it really was in question"* and implying
his mother's house was involved. *"They did a terrible prelim investigation…
Fayette County justice system is a fuckin joke"* [JS 2018-04-22 20:59–21:02].
He also says: *"I hired best attorney money could buy and went for it.
Spending 55k on the attorney was another way to just prove that I have no
reason to steal the money. I heard Friday that they've closed the suit"* [JS
2018-04-22 21:09].

The dat:0981 extraction printed that sentence as *"money coul[d]"*. The full
row reads *"could buy and went for it."*

Three weeks later he gives a count: *"221 counts... dropped to 0"* [JS
2018-05-14 19:25]. On June 13 he names the price. He will make *"a $115,800.
Payment to Fayette county courthouse so they can fuck off forever and clear me
of everything... I'm upset I'm losing 2 months wages but as soon as that
payment is made in suing the life off everyone involved"* [JS 2018-06-13
17:45]. He plans to sue *"the 6 people who came after me and my ex partner"*
[JS 2018-04-22 21:01].

This is Jim's account of his own exoneration, given to a friend, and nothing
here verifies it independently
([dat:0981](../../kb/data/0981-new-jim-shaffer-embezzlement-account.md)). The
earlier page called the $115,800 a "settlement". Jim calls it a payment to the
courthouse. What kind of payment it was, and how "221 counts dropped to 0"
fits with a six-figure payment, the thread does not say.

**Dan's side, the same night.** Dan's opening messages are about Fran's
estate. The house will be listed *"at 350"*, and he expects the estate to
split *"33% suz / 33% cunt grandma / 33% split me and vanessa"*, with his
share around *"200-250ish"* [JS 2018-04-22 20:52–23:43]. He says he still has
*"no access to my accounts via rick"* and has *"never even seen a
statement"* [JS 2018-04-22 23:22–23:23]. Fran died on 4/4. Dan and Annie were
with her: *"she was comatose for 4 days / she woke up and smiled at us and
died"* [JS 2018-04-22 23:45]. He sends the video and tells Jim about the

video"*. Then he adds, *"maybe it's just a coincidence, that's totally

Jim's response is the most direct thing he says in the thread: *"That video is

2018-04-22 23:45–23:49]. The full vigil is documented at
[[wiki/timeline/events/fran-death-vigil]].

## Golf: the part of the thread that is actually about golf

About a third of Jim's side is equipment talk. He is not exaggerating his
knowledge, and when Dan checks it Jim is proved right. Jim describes himself
as a *"steady scratch player"* [JS 2018-04-22 21:44]. He says he lived at Bay
Hill after moving to Florida: *"When I moved to Florida I live at bay hill and
played and practiced every day"* [JS 2018-05-20 22:34]. Elsewhere he calls it
*"bay hill (my club)"* [JS 2018-06-16 01:39]. He gives his whole bag: M3
driver and 3-wood, Diamana whiteboard and blueboard shafts *"tipped at least
an inch"*, Mizuno blades with DG X100, Callaway MD3 wedges, *"a million
putters"* [JS 2018-05-20 22:36–22:43]. He gets clubs through *"my buddy Eddie
Castleman [who] has worked he titleist tour van for over 20 years"* [JS
2018-05-20 22:52]. Dan shows Jim's shaft messages to another caddie and
reports back that *"they said you (also) are the real deal"* [JS 2018-05-21
23:32].

**The putter.** Jim offers to fit Dan for a putter at cost: *"a Scotty
Cameron mallet for $190… I'd need to meet with you and get some
measurements"* [JS 2018-06-03 23:40]. Over three days Dan misses him. He
sleeps in, then he is still on the bag at Laurel Valley. Jim waits in town
until nearly midnight, then orders it anyway. The head turns out to be a
tour-issue Circle T: *"Pb 1,500 putter… I'll be honest when I say I'm happy
you dicked off and missed out 😂😂😂… It will be my 4th Circle T putter. I
have a legit $10K putter collection"* [JS 2018-06-08 18:12–18:24]. Jim tries
again on June 18 with a Cameron X2 at $120 or a Spider at $140. Dan's timing
fails again, and Jim buys both: *"And don't sweat the putter. I bought them
both because price was good and I like collecting that ish"* [JS 2018-06-20
18:07].

The episode is small, and it shows both men's patterns clearly. Jim keeps his
word and is openly pleased to have been let off it. Dan wants the thing badly
and misses every window to get it.

**Dan's caddie ladder, reported live.** The thread is where Dan announces the
steps in [[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddie-years]]. First: *"Ya boy is officially
now working at laurel valley gc as a caddie"* [JS 2018-05-22 18:27]. Then
*"Official pinewood caddie now / … Pikewood"* [JS 2018-06-27 20:13–20:14].
He says Laurel Valley pays *"by like 4x"* compared with Mystic Rock [JS
2018-05-24 23:02]. In October, after Uniontown Country Club closed, he writes
*"I've been playing free golf from 7am until dark at ucc since they closed"*
[JS 2018-10-19 15:52]. Jim's view of the club's economics: *"it's not a 2018
country club... $25.00 a round, come one/come all"* [JS 2018-09-24 21:28].

## December 9, 2018: Rob

you remember when me, you and rob orange took that suped up mini van to the
Tom Petty concert / … / I miss rob, man... such a goofy kid"* [JS 2018-12-09

What follows is the longest single conversation in the thread's second half:
229 rows that day. It is also the only place where both men speak plainly
about [[wiki/people/rob-orange|Rob Orange]], who died in April 2014
([dat:0984](../../kb/data/0984-rob-orange-death-account.md)). Dan can't
remember the year and guesses 2013. Jim says yes, then corrects himself:
*"No. 2014"* [JS 2018-12-09 11:53–11:55]. Dan: *"But really...rob is the only
one that I get sad about / I honestly don't give a fuck about any of them

2018-12-09 11:54–11:55].

the Kraus family among them), Dan poses a hypothetical about one of the
images: *"You're playing god...you can kill everyone in that image to

12:38–12:40]. "That photo" is Dan's photo, and the trade is Dan's hypothetical.
