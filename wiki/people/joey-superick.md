# Joey Superick

> **Incidental entry** — A one-month 2018 golf friendship whose frame is held-verified and whose portrait rests on an unheld export; complete at this size.

Joey Superick was a golf-and-music friend from June 2018, in Dan's [[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddying|Nemacolin]] caddying period. The held corpus anchors the frame: on 2018-06-04 Dan wrote "I'm supposed to play with Joey Superick tomorrow as long as I don't get a report in the next hour or so" — caddy-shift report-time framing — and named "Joey Superick" again the same day (kb/data/0633). The full name, the June 2018 date, and the golf context all check out against the thread the old page describes.

## The thread, as recorded

The old page, drawn from a message export not held in this repo, describes a 23-message thread (+17245623673, 2018-06-03 to 2018-06-29) whose centerpiece is Dan apparently finding a pair of Joey's lost sunglasses on a gravel road ("No fucking way dude... You're the man!!!"). The two trade emo/post-hardcore music opinions — Brand New, Jesse Lacey, Taking Back Sunday's back catalog, *The Devil and God Are Raging Inside Me* — with genuine enthusiasm, closing on a "talk later fuckboi" register that reads as established, easy friendship rather than a new acquaintance. None of that texture is in the held corpus; it stands as the page's testimony-relay from the unheld dump (kb/data/0633), and the earlier 2016–2017 "Joey" rows in the corpus were excluded as ambiguous rather than folded in.

## Standing
