# Felipe

> **Incidental entry** — An Au Za'atar coworker friendship with a fully counted two-platform record and a confirmed end; complete at this size.

Felipe was a server at [[wiki/work/au-zaatar|Au Za'atar]] Midtown whom Dan called his "unbelievably gay Colombian bff." He announced himself, in Dan's telling, by shattering the old glass door with the metal legs of a table during the two-hour shed build on his first day. Both color details are Dan's testimony relayed through the old wiki; neither appears in the held messages (kb/data/0411).

## The record

The surviving iMessage thread (+13477913706) holds 142 messages — 65 sent by Dan, 77 inbound from Felipe — running 2023-08-11 to 2024-08-22 UTC (August 21, 2024 EDT) (kb/data/0410). The older page figure of 77 was the inbound side only. The acquaintance is older than that thread: a 993-message thread under the name "Andrés Felipe," running July 2021 to July 2024, is identified in the corpus as Dan's Colombian bff (kb/data/1443; kb/data/1446), which makes the iMessage thread the tail of the friendship rather than its span. In [[wiki/people/valeria-iglesias-cid|Valeria Iglesias Cid]]'s thread he survives as workplace cast — judged "a diva" by both Dan and Valeria.

## Standing

Dan confirmed on 2026-07-14 that the contact did not survive past the Au Za'atar job and that he has not spoken to Felipe since; the held record ends in August 2024, consistent with that account. Felipe's surname is not recorded anywhere in the corpus, and his exact tenure dates at the restaurant are unrecorded.
