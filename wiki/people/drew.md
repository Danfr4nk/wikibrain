# Drew

> **Incidental entry** — one eleven-day work collaboration, identified by a first name only; complete at this size because that thread is the whole record.

Drew collaborated with Dan on a green-mapping project at [[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddying|Nemacolin]] over an intense eleven-day stretch in the summer of 2017. The held corpus brackets the thread from June 19 to July 1, 2017 — 102 rows including both directions and attachments — with nothing from this contact before mid-June and nothing after July 1 (dat:0489). The project was almost certainly the caddy yardage books used to help players read the greens.

## The collaboration

The pair trade sketches and design feedback throughout the eleven days: green widths and depths at their widest and deepest points, arrows sized to show slope severity, line-boldness calibration so slopes do not visually read as "cliffs," and whether false-front greens need arrow tips dragged forward to represent the effect. They plan to cover the back nine together, referencing "Hazeltine" — a famous, notoriously difficult green complex — as a confusing comparison case (dat:0489).

The thread is purely collaborative and enthusiastic — "I'm a fan," "outstanding," "should be awesome" — with no personal or transactional content, and it ends abruptly on July 1, 2017 in both exports. No later contact is documented.

Limits: Drew's surname is not carried here — the corpus redacts his email and phone handle, and that convention governs what this page reproduces. How Dan and Drew knew each other beyond the Nemacolin project is undocumented.
