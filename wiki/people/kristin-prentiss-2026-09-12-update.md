## Why this entry exists

The redux matters for five reasons. First, it falsifies the
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/block-unblock-loop|block-unblock-loop]] reading of

reopened, at his initiation, which revises the rule's boundary conditions:

still be reopened by the other side's need.

Second, it confirms the explicit-verbal-commitment architecture as

within hours, unprompted by any crisis, and the declarations were being
issued in banter form ("favorite sex of ever all time forever") before the

Third, her refusal of voice-memo ingestion is the first subject-side "no"
to the "everything goes in" rule, and it is now on the record — with her
reasoning attached ("you're a master at tech and I myself can fabricate
so much"). The veracity ledger tracks Dan's misremembering; it now needs
an entry for the adversarial dimension she named.

Fourth, the Tom material revises two pages at once: Tom cut Dan off ("did
a Kristin prentiss on me") and Kristin cut Tom off ("absolute darkness"),
independently, reported in the same conversation. The introduction vector
of the Kristin thread is severed from both ends.

Fifth, the transcription error and its correction are a clean

material, in real time, on the single most load-bearing line of the
