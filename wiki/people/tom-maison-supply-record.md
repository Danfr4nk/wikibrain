# Tom Maison: The Standing Supply Line

Tom Maison is Dan's primary male friend, and for most of the record that
is the whole sentence that matters. Everything else on this page — the
strips, the mushrooms, the [[wiki/health/cocaine|cocaine]], the klonopin, the owe-and-silence
cycles, the May 2026 no-show that cost Dan a job — is one dimension of
the friendship, not the man. This page exists because the supply dimension
was scattered: fragments on [[wiki/work/bfs-foods|bfs-foods]], fragments in
the [[wiki/mind/synthesis/chemical-architecture|chemical architecture]]'s
node table, the collapse arc on
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/aura-illness-compound-collapse|aura-illness-compound-collapse]].
Consolidated here, with the human story leading and the ledger following.
For the full biography — the arrangement role, the DUI case, the tech
collaboration, the March 31 crisis exchange — see
[[wiki/people/tom|tom.md]], which this entry cites rather than retells.

The one-line thesis: **for sixteen years the friendship was the supply
line and the supply line was the friendship, and neither function
understood the other until May 2026, when the supplier failed and the
friend failed in the same week, for the same reason, and the reason was
that they were the same person.**

## Who Tom is in Dan's life

Start with the register, because it is the friendship's signature and it
never changed. The Tom/[[wiki/people/kristin|Kristin]] dossier's phrase is "irony-poisoned stoic"
**[ATTESTED, wiki/people/tom]**: the man who absorbs Dan's suicide threats
and withdrawals with validation, without judgment, and without ever
pushing toward rehab. In the primary thread this reads as unbothered
absorption rendered as banter. January 3, 2026, Dan sends a long thread
about red-teaming Gemini into what he believes is "a completely honest
conspiracy assessment" — and Tom's entire contribution across the exchange
is: "Tell me." / "Lmao" / "Based." **[ATTESTED, primary thread,
2026-01-03]** — three responses, zero engagement with the substance,
total engagement with the friendship. That is the register: he takes Dan
as found. It is also why the forensic mode stayed off for sixteen years.
You do not audit the one place you are not performing **[ATTESTED,
wiki/people/tom]**.

The friendship's background conditions: roughly 2010 onward, out of the
adolescent [[wiki/places/seven-springs|Seven Springs]] scene **[ATTESTED, wiki/people/tom]**; Tom is
about three weeks older than Dan; Pittsburgh area; Ohiopyle excursions a
recurring shared setting. The documented message weight is 4,160 messages
on the +17249987341 handle plus ~1,603 under phloxenheim@gmail.com —
corrected to ~5,763 after Kristin's handle was found mislabeled under his,
rank #5 in the master dump **[ATTESTED, wiki/people/tom]**. He is the
first-call for major events: the [[wiki/interests/favorites/books/topics/trump|Trump]] shooting, the Biden dropout,
GameStop, the September 2020 [[wiki/work/fastly-fsly|FSLY]] stock tip **[ATTESTED, wiki/people/tom]**.
He is also the recurring third party Dan selected for the 2018–2024
arrangement, chosen specifically because he was a friend the aftermath
could be managed with **[ATTESTED, wiki/people/tom]**, and the
introduction vector for the late-2025 [[wiki/people/kristin|Kristin]]
thread, which entered through his social circle **[ATTESTED,
wiki/people/tom]**.

What made the friendship singular was not the volume but the direction
of the care. Two episodes bracket it. The first is the undated "Max" AI
chat, where Dan calls Tom "my main guy for, well... everything" and
documents Tom's standing gesture toward Suz: she is the only one who can
call him directly — despite Tom being younger than Dan — he "LOVES" her,
and he had bought her a replacement car battery the week before, when hers
died and she couldn't afford one, unprompted, no request made **[ATTESTED,
wiki/people/tom]**. The second is March 31, 2026, six weeks before the
collapse: Tom writing "I'm a fucking mess Dan and I'm gonna make it your
problem" and "considering the rope just so this stops," and Dan talking
him through it with the same dark, deflecting affection that defines the
friendship at its best — "YOU were the one who kept popping it... you
don't get to be fucking emo" **[ATTESTED, wiki/people/tom]**. A week
earlier, on March 24, the phloxenheim thread had already carried Tom's
quieter version of the same ask: "Will you talk to me for a little bit?"
**[ATTESTED, primary thread, 2026-03-24]** — arriving after a stretch of
one-sided sends, the lateral-ally-goes-quiet pattern running with Tom on
the asking side. The care ran both ways. That matters, because the May
collapse is sometimes read as Dan extracting from a passive supplier. It
was not passive, and it was not one-way — not until May.

Tom was also, in the phloxenheim register, the technical peer: by March
2026 he was evangelizing agentic AI to Dan — "Antigravity … the new google
AGENT ai which means it can act like a person," boasting that he had one
AI "interview" his other AI and build him "a custom GROUP CHAT" to talk to
both at once **[ATTESTED, wiki/people/tom]**. The dossier's
"primary peer collaborator" framing is borne out in the thread **[ATTESTED,
wiki/people/tom]**. And in the wall era — August 2025, Dan building a
floor-to-ceiling wall of B&W women's portraits — Tom was the live audience
for the build, the naming, and the purpose: "SHE NEEDS TO SEE THE WALL TOM"
/ "THE WALL IS ALL I HAVE NOW" / "well milo, and the wall" **[ATTESTED,
wiki/people/tom]**; Tom's counsel, August 5: "You should put a picture of
Kristin on your wall" **[ATTESTED, primary thread, 2025-08-05]** — at a
point when Dan didn't yet know her surname ("YOU WON'T EVEN TELL ME HER
LAST NAME"), and Tom supplied the fork: "It's either Kristin Shalene or
Kristin Prentiss" **[ATTESTED, primary thread, 2025-08-05]**. Dan
workshopped what he cared about on Tom in real time. The friendship was
the sounding board, and the sounding board sometimes carried product.

## The supply ledger

Consolidated: the substances that flowed through the friendship were
Suboxone strips, mushrooms, [[wiki/health/cocaine|cocaine]], and klonopin **[ATTESTED,
wiki/mind/synthesis/chemical-architecture]**, with the standing
characterization — quoted across the architecture pages — that Tom
"shows up with substances and announces it" **[ATTESTED,
wiki/mind/synthesis/chemical-architecture]**. The primary thread shows the
announcement pattern verbatim, and it is worth reading the actual rhythm
because it is the rhythm of a friendship doing double duty as logistics:

- August 2, 2025, mid-wall-era: "Do you need some tomorrow?" / "How many?"
  / "Also, probably do mushrooms tomorrow" **[ATTESTED, primary thread]**.
- August 7: "Bought a quarter of mushrooms." August 8: "Eat mushrooms.
  Maybe go to Ohiopyle." **[ATTESTED, primary thread]** — the shared
  setting and the substance in one breath, which is how it worked.
- September 14: "I was gonna offer to bring cocaine" **[ATTESTED, primary
  thread]** — the exact "shows up with substances and announces it" shape,
  unprompted.
- September 22: "What's your sub status?" September 20: "Sub?" September
  10: "Probably just send the sub with her." October 2: "Just come get
  your sub off me today and call it whatever." October 3: "I'm coming to
  Uniontown do you need sub?" **[ATTESTED, primary thread]**.

The klonopin line is the least documented and most human. August 3,
2025, 3:28 AM: "I ate all the k pins I was supposed to sell so now I'm
fucked" **[ATTESTED, primary thread]**. Tom was not just a supplier of
klonopin; he was in it too — consuming his own stock, missing his own
sales, telling Dan about it at 3 AM with the same unbothered register he
used for everything. The supplier's supply failure, as comic miniature,
arriving a year before it would matter at scale.

The strip flow had already absorbed a reversal by June 2025. The
supply-network page corrects the default direction: the June 2025
messages put a prescribing doctor at the head of the Suboxone route and
Tom as the fallback invoked when an out-of-state script could not be
filled — "I am so fucked. I'm gonna have to drive down to see Tom today"
**[ATTESTED, wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]** — which makes the May
2026 withdrawal a second-line failure, not a single point of failure
giving way: the prescriber layer was already jammed by geography, and
Tom was the failover. The failover then failed. The family plumbing ran
underneath: Suz's Cash App transfers facilitated the Tom-era purchases
**[ATTESTED, wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]**, and the [[wiki/timeline/periods/2018-deep-cycle|2018 deep cycle]] ran on irregular transfers of $20 to $3,000 in both directions,
entered as debts and never reconciled — procurement was never fiscally
isolated from the family, a thread the [[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine|estate money spine]] carries
forward **[ATTESTED, wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]**.

The succession context: Tom succeeded Johnny (via the undocumented Tim)
as primary strip source by 2025 **[ATTESTED, wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]**.
The node table puts the whole arc in one row: Tom, ~2010–2026,
Uniontown/Pittsburgh, friend-supplier — strips, mushrooms, cocaine,
klonopin — sole strip source by 2025 **[ATTESTED,
wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]**. What that succession replaced
was depth: the 2018 deep cycle ran Johnny, a second handle, and Teddy in
parallel — a supplier failure was an inconvenience — while the terminal
era ran everything through one friendship-embedded node **[ATTESTED,
wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]**. Redundancy decayed monotonically
into Tom. The record's peak Tom-dependence is 2017–2018 (the poverty
floor, supply during the collapse years), 2025 (the [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] collapse), and
spring 2026 — the friendship's own end **[ATTESTED, wiki/people/tom]**.

## The loss-absorption episode

One undated episode, from the "Max"/Gemini AI chat, is the friendship's
warmest documented supply moment and the one the late-period record
inverts. Someone "jumped [Dan's] place in line" and bought out a
dealer's Suboxone stock right when Dan needed to restock, and Tom came
through anyway — apparently at his own financial loss: "he probably
literally lost money on it too" **[ATTESTED, wiki/people/tom]**. This is
the counter-demonstration to May 2026 that the chemical architecture
names explicitly: "The tether held that time because the human absorbed
the shock. The architecture's redundancy was never institutional. It was
always a person" **[ATTESTED, wiki/mind/synthesis/chemical-architecture]**.
It is also the episode that gives Dan's August 2026 line its specific
sting — "that he would be okay letting me go sick" — because he had
seen, in documented fact, the version of Tom who would not. The
loss-absorption episode is not a softening footnote to the collapse. It
is the control case: the same friendship, the same substance, the same
man, doing the opposite thing. Whatever changed between that episode and
May 2026, it was not the friendship's capacity. It was its willingness
under load.

The architecture page's verdict on the mechanism is exact and worth
importing whole: "the dormancy-not-exit rule (ties suspend, never
disappear) does *not* cover supply ties. The Tom tie did not disappear
— last contact 05-29/30 — but the *supply function* stopped completely.
Tie persistence and function persistence are different things, and the
architecture depends on the second while the social graph only
guarantees the first" **[ATTESTED,
wiki/mind/synthesis/chemical-architecture]**.

## The 2014 precedent: twelve years, one pattern

The spring 2026 collapse is often read as a late-life degradation of an
otherwise-safe bond. The BFS drawer-dispute reconstruction surfaced the
material that forces the revision: a near-identical episode from twelve
years earlier **[ATTESTED, wiki/people/tom]**. From the message record,
August–September 2014:

- **Aug 20, 2014:** "Do you have any fucking clue how shitty I feel? I
  waited 2 weeks already… you need to get me the rest of what you owe me
  TODAY"
- **Sep 1:** "What the fuck man"
- **Sep 10:** "remember how you stopped giving a fuck that you owed me
  when you didn't need me to buy your shit anymore?" followed by "I'm
  done and I'm going to make it my dumb fuck mission in life to blow
  your spot up. I'm not doing this stupid fucking dance with you
  anymore"
- **Sep 15:** "yo looking to purchase sub/tex whatevz. anything around?"
  — the channel reopening the moment Dan needed product again

All quoted **[ATTESTED, wiki/people/tom]**. That is a multi-week
owe-stonewall-escalate-declare-done-reconcile cycle, documented in 2014
and re-run in 2026, with Dan's own 2014 diagnosis naming the mechanism
precisely: **Tom's reliability is transactional — high during mutual
dependence, degrading to indifference once he no longer needs Dan as a
buyer** **[ATTESTED, wiki/people/tom]**. The language rhymes across the
twelve years: 2014's "I'm not doing this stupid fucking dance with you
anymore" and 2026's "this is some real [[wiki/people/kristin|Kristin]]-coded shit" are the same
affective category — *this person is producing outputs functionally
indistinguishable from someone untrustworthy* **[ATTESTED,
wiki/people/tom]**.

The reliability inversion is the 2014 episode stated as a market rule,
and it is worth importing from the supply-network page because it is
the friendship's economics, not a character verdict: **"the more purely
transactional the relationship, the more reliable the supply"**
**[ATTESTED, wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]**. [[wiki/people/menore|Menore]] — a stranger
running a professional delivery service — answered 455 of 458 requests
across 6.5 years at 99.3% availability. Johnny was reliable in-season.
[[wiki/people/teddy|Teddy]] was willing but fragile. And Tom — the friend, the "anchor," the
one node embedded in a real attachment — was the least reliable node in
the record **[ATTESTED, wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]**. "In this
market it is the opposite: distance disciplines, intimacy licenses
failure. A professional cannot afford a missed delivery; a friend can,
because the friendship is expected to absorb it" **[ATTESTED,
wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]**. The mechanism, per the
deferred-audit page: enforcing a standard on a supplier is an audit, and
audits of friends are deferred — Tom sits on that page's two-clocks
table as "model revised only after the mid-May 2026 supply failure...
~18 years" **[ATTESTED, wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]**. Dan
never enforced the standard on Tom because enforcing it would have been
a verdict on the friendship, and on the chooser. That is not a
condemnation of Tom. It is the friendship's protection circuit,
documented.

## May 2026: the break

The record's own full account is on [[wiki/people/tom|tom.md]]; this
entry keeps the ledger's version — what the supply function did, in
order, with the human weight attached. The phloxenheim thread runs
continuously March 24 through May 30, 2026 (the last dated message in
the corpus for either handle), and read in full it documents the
friendship coming apart under its own supply-line economics — not a
single rupture but an accelerating cycle of the same owe-and-silence
pattern, run enough times to burn out the goodwill it had always run on
**[ATTESTED, wiki/people/tom]**.

Late March holds the last real reciprocity: the AI-manipulation tactics
traded for their respective relationships, Dan talking Tom through the
March 31 ideation **[ATTESTED, wiki/people/tom]**. April thins the
transactional layer: sub handoffs continue on roughly weekly cadence,
but delays compound — missed windows, Dan's April 9 "I am going to be
totally out of subs so please figure it out" already reading as a person
managing a supplier, not a friend **[ATTESTED, wiki/people/tom]**. By
late April Tom is declining Dan's calls to be with the new girlfriend
("I can't really talk. I have Brandy here with me"), which Dan needles
as a status reversal — "NOW I AM COOLGUY... SORRY CAN'T TALK COOLGUY HAS
A GIRL IN HIS BED" **[ATTESTED, wiki/people/tom]**.

May breaks it. The "cash app me $30, I'll give you 2" pattern repeats
through early May with growing friction ([[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]]'s ex-boyfriend
commentary, DUI-court scheduling conflicts on Tom's side) **[ATTESTED,
wiki/people/tom]**. May 15–16 is the breaking point: Tom promises subs
after "weed whacking," goes silent for hours while Dan escalates from
"Tom / What the uck" through repeated unanswered pings to "Come on dude
seriously" to "Fuck you dude" — Dan facing a real consequence (calling
off work, risk of termination) from the no-show **[ATTESTED,
wiki/people/tom]**. May 18: Dan apparently blocks Tom and unblocks him
only to set an ultimatum — "I'm unblocking you for the moment — if you
want to let me know what's going on this morning I'll keep it open
otherwise fuck you" — then pivots the same day to a straight financial
demand: "How long will it take you to get the $36 you owe me / I don't
want the tex I want the money back" **[ATTESTED, wiki/people/tom]**.
Eleven days of quiet. May 29: Dan accuses Tom of running "a $30 come up"
on him. May 30, the record's last message: Dan threatening to route the
dispute through Tom's father — "if it really becomes necessary I can
just explain to Phil that I would like the object back that is very
obviously not something that tom got" **[ATTESTED, wiki/people/tom]**.
No reconciliation, no formal severance — the same silence pattern that
used to resolve itself, running out its string.

The pharmacological consequence is what the other pages carry, and it
is load-bearing here too, in one sentence: the May no-show put Dan into
roughly 72+ hours of withdrawal while working a BFS Foods shift; the
withdrawal impaired the shift; the drawer variance was pinned to the
impaired shift; the drawer dispute became the termination **[ATTESTED,
wiki/work/bfs-foods]**. "Single-node supply failure converted directly
into job loss" **[ATTESTED,
wiki/mind/synthesis/chemical-architecture]**. The BFS page's own
upstream-cause section states the counterfactual that is this entry's
central indictment of the friendship's logistics: "had he simply said
'I can't' on Friday morning, Dan would have had time and options to
source elsewhere before the impairment cascade began" **[ATTESTED,
wiki/work/bfs-foods]**. The string-along — "after court," "after a
nap," "after weed whacking" **[ATTESTED, wiki/work/bfs-foods]** —
consumed the optionality Dan needed to source elsewhere while
alternatives still existed. And it was not Tom alone: the same four
days burned Dan three times — Tom stringing along Friday through
Sunday, a second contact taking payment Sunday night and never
delivering, and the desperate workplace play on a customer who took
~$35 and never came back **[ATTESTED, wiki/work/bfs-foods]**. "Three
burns in four days" **[ATTESTED, wiki/work/bfs-foods]**. Tom's was
first, and it set the conditions for the other two.

## What it meant — in Dan's own words

Asked what bothered him most about the 2026 failure, Dan answered in
the 2026-08-10 session — the coinage session where he named the
compound collapse — and his answer is the one piece of this page that
outranks every forensic reconstruction:

> "the thing that bothers me the most is not money but that he would be
> okay letting me go sick" **[OPERATOR, via wiki/people/tom]**.

And in the same session, the friendship's place in the collapse, named
as one of three simultaneous losses alongside the job termination and
the forced housing exit: "i have LITERALLY noone now, even tom isnt
talking to me since he beat me for 35 dollars for a sub he never
brought" **[OPERATOR, via wiki/people/tom]**.

Read these two lines together and the meaning is precise. The money
was never the load — $36 demanded, $35 remembered (the tension is
preserved in the method appendix below, not resolved). What broke was
the load-bearing definition of "safe." The wiki's old framing —
"unconditional safe attachment" — does not survive unrevised, "not
because Tom is bad, but because most of what made him feel safe was
that he was *available during the periods when being available cost
him nothing*" **[ATTESTED, wiki/people/tom]**. That is a real form of
presence. It is not what Dan wanted "safe" to mean — the person who
treats my baseline okayness as non-negotiable **[ATTESTED,
wiki/people/tom]**. The corpus-consistent version, fitting the years
of real connection AND the 2014 episode AND 2026: **lateral ally during
periods of mutual alignment, unreliable to indifference during
asymmetric periods** **[ATTESTED, wiki/people/tom]**.

And the May 2026 collapse is itself a relationship event before it is
a supply event — the chemical architecture's own sequencing: "the
relationship's May 2026 collapse (the 5 sub-sandwich dispute, the
05-15/16 no-shows, last contact 05-29/30) preceded the supply failure
by days; the pharmacological consequence (72+ hours of withdrawal)
followed the relationship consequence" **[ATTESTED,
wiki/mind/synthesis/chemical-architecture]**. The friendship failed
first. The body followed. The job followed the body.

## The October 8 contrast

One primary-record episode, from the number-handle thread, deserves
its own section because it is the sharpest evidence of where the
friendship stood relative to everything else in Dan's life — and it
predates the collapse by seven months. October 8, 2025: [[wiki/people/kristin|Kristin]],
introduced through Tom's own social circle, was supposed to deliver a
sub on Monday; Dan gave her the money; she holds it over his head while
he is getting sick. His message to Tom at 11:16 AM:

> "So Kristin was supposed to get me a sub on fucking Monday. I gave
> her the money. Did the whole thin... If she doesn't bring me a sub
> today I am literally blocking her on everything and will not speak to
> her again" **[ATTESTED, primary thread, 2025-10-08]**.

By 5:19 PM he has recalibrated — "listen I'm not going to bug you
after seeing screenshots of your conversation with her and I have
noone to blame but myself for thinking that she wouldn't immediately
weaponize me needing a sub and land me where I am currently" — and the
ask to Tom follows immediately: "But if there's any way you can help
me out I would really appreciate it" **[ATTESTED, primary thread,
2025-10-08]**. Tom's reply: "Call me when you get a shot"
**[ATTESTED, primary thread, 2025-10-08]**.

The same failure, two different audits. Kristin gets the block
ultimatum — maximum sanction, relationship on the line. Tom gets the
ask — the friendship absorbing the emergency, again. Dan's capacity to
terminate ran freely on the new bond and not at all on the old one,
seven months before the old one would be the one that ended over the
same category of failure. This is the deferred-audit mechanism in the
wild, with the receipts timestamped: the October 8 thread shows the
audit running at full speed on a relationship with no history and
standing down completely on the one with fifteen years of it. It also
shows why the 2014 precedent never converted into prevention. The
pattern was not unseen. It was unaudited.
