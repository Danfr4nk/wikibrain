# Zachariah Harshman

They ran Laurel Highlands together, or believed they did, and twenty years later
they were arguing about PS5 load times.

That is the whole shape of it. Zachariah Harshman is a high-school friend from
Uniontown whose Facebook Messenger thread runs from **October 2011 to October
2021** and passes through three separate men: a fellow burnout trading fond,
slightly alarming memories of a senior year neither of them can fully recall; a
2014–15 weed and wax supplier working out of the
[[wiki/places/155-virginia-ave|155 Virginia Avenue]] household; and — after a
genuine, ugly rupture — a sober, warm, entirely platonic gaming friend who
resurfaces in 2020 with nothing of the earlier register left.

Most ruptures in this corpus are terminal. This one is not, and that is the
single most interesting thing about the file. Dan blew the friendship up at 4:21
in the morning three weeks after leaving [[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis]] for
[[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]]; Zach answered with what the wiki treats as
the only independent third-party account of that switch on record; the thread
went cold, then colder, then dark for two years — and then came back, and was
fine
([`dat:1391`](../../kb/data/1391-zachariah-harshman-rupture.md)).

## The senior year they toast rather than mourn

The earliest substantive exchange in the thread is October 2012, and it is pure
shared nostalgia. Dan writes: *"i seriously don't remember much of 2005/6,"* and
then, *"i don't know if i've blocked it mentally or if it was the amount of
drugs."*

Zach's reply does not do what a reply to that usually does:

> "Dude we killed it tho... N my senior year dude i did u proud lol... We were
> hard as fuck tho we ran LH."

Pride, not concern. This is one of the very few contemporaneous confirmations
anywhere in the corpus that the addiction arc **predates the corpus itself** and
was locally famous while it was happening — not a private struggle later
reconstructed, but a reputation two men were still enjoying six years on
([`dat:1391`](../../kb/data/1391-zachariah-harshman-rupture.md)).

The dates matter. 2005–06 is the school year that contains the November 2005
parental rupture — the disclosure in the car, the rehab, the affair with the
family's dealer — which the wiki treats as the origin point of most of what
follows ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/november-2005-hinge]]). Dan's own description of
those months, given to a friend in 2012 with no analytical frame around it, is
that he cannot remember them and does not know whether the cause is
psychological or chemical. That is a more useful sentence than most of the
retrospective material, because nobody was building anything with it.

There is a second, later flash of the same school-era register. In a separate
thread with [[wiki/people/matthew-palermo|Matthew Palermo]], Dan recalls
disliking a mutual acquaintance named Tyler from third grade through high
school, then reconnecting with him years afterwards **alongside Zach** and being
*"SHOCKED how naturally funny he was"* — a reminder, Dan says, *"that i
shouldn't rely on my perceptions of people that were formed when i was an
absolute shithead"* ([[wiki/people/matthew-palermo]]). Zach is the company Dan
keeps when he revises a twenty-year-old judgement about somebody. It is a small
detail and it is the only one in the file that shows the friendship doing
something other than transacting or exploding.

## January 2014: the man who tried to get him a gig

On **January 28–29, 2014**, a year into the [[wiki/interests/music/aliases/sloppp|SLOPPP]]
alias's run, Zach spends a late night hyping a mix — *"Dude the drop after the
roar samples was perfect... you a fool for this one"* — and then does something
nobody else in the corpus is documented doing this early: he tries to get Dan
booked.

He connects Dan with a Pittsburgh rave-scene contact named **Timmie Sawyers**
(*"she wants to watch u do your thing before she contacts people"*) and pushes
for a slot at a weekly rave night at a Pittsburgh gallery venue. Dan sends over
the SoundCloud link and his email, describes his rig — a Numark NS7 II
controller, Serato DJ, production in Logic Pro X — and the two half-jokingly
plan a studio session: Zach wants beats to rap over, Dan promises *"everything
set up here"*
([`dat:1391`](../../kb/data/1391-zachariah-harshman-rupture.md);
[[wiki/interests/music/aliases/sloppp]]).

The music pages treat this as a dated milestone, and they are right to. SLOPPP
made **three documented attempts** in its life to convert itself into a live
booking: Zach's January 2014 pitch; Dan's own July 6, 2014 cold outreach to
Pittsburgh DJ Phil Lacher; and the March 2015 Frank Swaney near-management deal,
which got as far as discussing terms through a third party who was *"talking to
Dan's parents"* before evaporating
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/dance-music-only-phases]];
[[wiki/interests/music/aliases/sloppp]]). Zach's is the **earliest** of the
three, and the only one where somebody other than Dan did the asking.

Nothing documents whether the Timmie Sawyers booking materialised. What the
exchange does record is a man *"desperate to play"* a first show, eighteen
months before the alias closed, and a friend spending a night trying to make it
happen for him ([[wiki/interests/music/aliases/sloppp]]).

## The other thread, running underneath

From mid-2014 through the end of 2015 the same conversation carries a second,
denser register: Zach is a regular Uniontown supplier. Wax, dabs, "budder," and
flower priced by the cut and the quarter — ***"50/cut 90/q"*** — with occasional
side asks in both directions. Zach floats a one-time wax deal at double asking
price from *"my mom's connect"*; Dan pays him for a ride
([`dat:1391`](../../kb/data/1391-zachariah-harshman-rupture.md)).

The 2014 handoffs reference *"my mom"* and give no fixed address. From January
2015 onward the staging language turns specific and repetitive — *"front door is
open, i'm in the kitchen,"* *"side door to garage,"* instructions to keep
[[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis]] quiet while she is snuck upstairs — and it
matches the January 2015 move to [[wiki/places/155-virginia-ave|155 Virginia
Avenue]] exactly ([[wiki/places/155-virginia-ave]]).

Two structural facts come out of that, and both are load-bearing elsewhere.

**First, Zach is the earliest dated node in the entire supply record.** The
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network|supply network]] page's node table lists him
at **2014–2015, Uniontown, high-school-friend supplier**, three years ahead of
the Johnny/Teddy era the page previously treated as its floor
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]]). The succession that runs from him
through Johnny (2018) to Tom (terminal era) is that page's spine, and
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-graph-vs-chain|the two-layer revision of it]] opens
by naming him as the chain's first link.

**Second, he is the only node in the whole network whose relationship ends in a
friendship rupture rather than a logistics failure.** That is the network page's
own observation, and it is the one that makes him structurally unlike everyone
who came after: *"one that ends in the same friction the later nodes would
repeat: not a supply failure, but a friendship rupture"*
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]]).

He is not isolated in that circle either. [[wiki/people/drew-mcgettigan|Drew
McGettigan]], a tight three-month 2015 buyer and go-between, is independently
tied to it: a July 22, 2015 message notes he is *"coming in to town soon to meet
up with Zach"* the same night as a documented deal
([[wiki/people/drew-mcgettigan]];
[`dat:0488`](../../kb/data/0488-drew-mcgettigan-summer-2015-supply-thread.md)).
[[wiki/people/lukyan-mraz|Lukyan Mraz]] stages his visits around the same
household — *"wanted to see if Zach was there"* — while lending Dan a growing
guide the two of them refer to, half in code, as *"a book... on lap quilting"*
([[wiki/people/lukyan-mraz]]; [[wiki/places/155-virginia-ave]]). By the summer of
2015 the Virginia Avenue house is a small hub, and Zach is the fixed point in it.

## The last week of November 2015

Then the household changed occupants in seventy-two hours, and Zach was standing
inside it while it happened.

The [[wiki/mind/synthesis/bond-switch-2015|bond switch]] runs November 28–
December 1, 2015: Dan meets Annie on the third tee of the Uniontown Country Club
golf course, and by the morning of December 1 Alexis is out of the house and
Annie is in it ([[wiki/timeline/annie-record]]). The hand-read of the two-sided
Annie corpus catches Zach three times inside the first night alone, and the lines
are unambiguous about what he was being used for:

| Time (2015-11-29) | Line | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 01:33 | *"Harshman leaving soon, so I'll have him drop me and say I'm going to his house"* | the cover story |
| 03:56 | *"Just me and Harshman here now"* | physical presence |
| 04:14 | *"Oh hey Zac"* | in the room |

([[wiki/timeline/annie-record]]; [[wiki/timeline/annie-read-notes]].)

He is not a bystander to the switch. He is the **mechanism** by which Dan leaves
the house undetected on the night it starts.

A day later he is doing something harder. During Alexis's move-out a laptop goes
missing; Dan forgets to retrieve it, and by 22:05 on **2015-11-30** the report
comes back through Zach: *"Harshman talked to her and found out that I shouldn't
worry about the laptop / But... it shouldn't be missing in the first place"*
([[wiki/timeline/annie-record]]). He is negotiating with the displaced partner on
Dan's behalf, in the week she is being displaced.

The resolution is unrecorded, and it is worth noting what the record *does* show
about how the laptop actually came back. At 05:04 on **2015-12-01** Dan writes:
*"Got my head bashed against the cabinet and pretended to call the cops. Laptop
retrieved"* ([[wiki/timeline/annie-record]]). Whatever Zach's negotiation
achieved, it was not that.

Two weeks later, Zach's name is still being used as cover — this time by Annie,
to her own family. On **2015-12-13** at 17:43, trapped at her parents' triathlon
Christmas party, she texts: *"HELP / I MADE THE MISTAKE OF SAYING IM GOING TO
SEE ZAC / THEN CLAIRE CALLED ME OUT"* ([[wiki/timeline/annie-record]]). Her
sister [[wiki/people/claire-ulmer|Claire]] catches the lie. The alibi had been in
service for a fortnight by then and it was starting to fail.

## December 23, 2015, 4:21 AM

What Zach did not know, through all of that, was that he was about to get
screamed at for it.

The blow-up is precisely dated. At **4:21 in the morning on December 23, 2015**
Dan sends a long, furious message accusing Zach of *"vanishing into thin air"*
and of making him hear about it *"second hand."* The specific grievance
underneath it is commercial as much as social: Zach had stopped bringing his
supply business to the house
([`dat:1391`](../../kb/data/1391-zachariah-harshman-rupture.md)).

Zach's explanation is the thread's real payload, and it is the reason this page
carries `importance = 5` in the knowledge base. He had become
[[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis]]'s *"best friend other than you for the past
year."* He had been spending regular time with her after the breakup. And he had
pulled back from the house **specifically because of how it would look**:

> "if you're going to all of the sudden hang out 'all the time' with the ex
> girlfriend of your best friend immediately after they break up, you might want
> to make sure the aforementioned friend doesn't feel a complete sense of
> betrayal."

Read that sentence twice, because the thing it is doing is unusual. Zach is not
accusing Dan of anything. He is explaining his own withdrawal by reference to a
norm he assumed Dan shared, and in doing so he describes — from the outside, in
real time, without any analytical apparatus — the exact social mechanics of the
switch. He was inside Alexis's orbit at the moment it happened. His anger is
calibrated to having watched it.

Dan does not deny any of it. His reply is *"That's your decision... Later dude
and seriously. Good luck!"* — and the exchange closes with real hostility on both
sides before trailing into one long, conciliatory paragraph from Zach that goes
unanswered for nearly two months
([`dat:1391`](../../kb/data/1391-zachariah-harshman-rupture.md)).

Somebody wrote a repair attempt and nobody answered it. That is the actual end
of the first friendship.

### The thing neither of them said

Three weeks earlier, Zach had been the man dropping Dan off and telling people he
was at his house.

Neither side of the December 23 exchange mentions it. Dan accuses him of
vanishing; Zach explains his loyalty problem with Alexis; and the fact that Zach
had personally operated the cover story for the switch that produced the loyalty
problem sits in the room unmentioned by both of them
([[wiki/timeline/annie-record]];
[`dat:1391`](../../kb/data/1391-zachariah-harshman-rupture.md)).

It also explains why the fight happened at all. Zach was not a distant friend who
drifted. He was the person who had been closest to both sides of the switch and
who had then had to choose, and he chose by leaving the room rather than by
saying so — which is precisely the behaviour Dan found intolerable and which Zach
was doing *out of consideration for him*.

### Why this rupture is load-bearing

[[wiki/mind/synthesis/bond-switch-2015|The bond-switch synthesis]] reconstructs
the November 28–December 1 pivot almost entirely from Dan's own message volume
and the bootloader's retrospective narrative. Zach's December 23 exchange is the
one piece of that thesis that comes from **outside**:

> "This is not a reconstruction from Dan's own message volume or the bootloader's
> retrospective narrative — it is a third party, inside Alexis's own post-breakup
> orbit, independently placing the switch's visible social fallout in the same
> one-month window the table above establishes from the primary record."

[[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-unbroken-bond|The unbroken bond]] goes further and
makes him half of the only cost on its list that is **paid by somebody outside
the couple**. Both partners were displaced inside the same seventy-two hours, and
both displacements detonated a relationship within the month:
[[wiki/people/emilio|Emilio]] reached into Annie's family on December 2, which
turned the Ulmer confrontation from bad luck into retaliation; Zach's fallout
ended a friendship of Dan's on December 23
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-unbroken-bond]]; [[wiki/people/emilio]];
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/audition-dynamics]]).

The singular-slot mechanism, in other words, has a bill, and Zach is on it.

### A channel fact that looks like a contradiction

Anyone comparing the two records will find that **the Annie corpus contains no
trace of the December 23 rupture.** The hand-read records **zero messages across
2015-12-20 → 12-24** — a five-day window spanning the date exactly
([[wiki/timeline/annie-read-notes]], quantitative markers;
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/bond-switch-2015]]).

That is not evidence against the date. It is a **channel fact**: this thread is
Facebook Messenger and the Annie corpus is iMessage. The switch's social fallout
was transacted in a medium the message corpus does not cover. Stated here because
the silence is exactly the shape `CORPUS_POLICY.md` warns about — in a fragment,
absence of evidence looks identical to evidence of absence.

## The friends who went quiet

Ten days before the rupture, on **2015-12-13**, Annie asks Dan whether his
friends' distance is her fault. His answer is one of the most revealing lines in
the December record:

> "NOOOO / it's shu and Alexis / You are my best friend / And you are all I will
> ever need to be 100% happy" (10:39–10:40)

Three close friends had already asked him about the breakup on November 29. By
December 13 they had gone silent. The same day, at 22:15, Dan notes that someone
*"never returned my call"* ([[wiki/timeline/annie-record]]).

[[wiki/timeline/annie-record|The annie-record page]] reads "shu" as *likely*
Zachariah Harshman. **That reading is now doubtful** and the doubt is worth
flagging on this page rather than buried in an appendix, because it changes who
this section is about. The wiki has since created a page for
[[wiki/people/zac-shumar|Zac Shumar]] — a Uniontown man who dated Dan's sister,
had his own claim on Annie, and argued with Dan about the switch **by text on
December 6, 2015**, seventeen days before the Harshman rupture
([[wiki/people/zac-shumar]]). "Shu" is a far more natural contraction of Shumar
than of Harshman, and Shumar is a man with a documented December 2015 grievance.
The annie-record page was last modified 2026-08-18; the Shumar identification
landed 2026-09-12. The inference predates the better candidate.

This page does not resolve it and does not adopt either reading. What it records
is that the December 13 sentence is currently attributed here on an inference
that the record has since given a competitor, and that nothing in the corpus
names the referent.

What is not in doubt is the shape of the answer. Asked whether she has cost him
his friendships, Dan does not deny that the friendships are gone. He reframes the
loss as proof of the gain: *you are all I will ever need*. He is thirteen days
from the SLOPPP alias's closing release and ten days from the Harshman blow-up,
and he is telling a woman he has known for a fortnight that the emptying of his
social graph is fine.

## February 2016, and a check-in nobody explains

The first contact after the rupture is guarded and short. On **February 18,
2016** — nearly two months of silence after Zach's unanswered conciliatory
paragraph — Zach writes: *"Yo is everything alright with Luke?"*
([`dat:1391`](../../kb/data/1391-zachariah-harshman-rupture.md)).

"Luke" is [[wiki/people/lukyan-mraz|Lukyan Mraz]], named earlier in the thread as
an Xbox-modding console gamer and by then Dan's short-lived cannabis-grow
collaborator. The detail that makes it strange is that Lukyan had sent Dan his
own *"Hey dude you alright?"* on **February 17** — one day earlier
([[wiki/people/lukyan-mraz]]).

Two men in two separate households, on consecutive days, each checking on the
other through Dan. Neither thread says what prompted it. Both pages carry it as
an open gap, and it is the only documented moment where the wider Virginia Avenue
circle visibly worries about itself ([[wiki/people/lukyan-mraz]]).

It is also the first thing Zach says after being told *"Later dude and seriously.
Good luck!"* — and it is not about the fight.

## Thinning, then dark

From 2016 to 2018 the thread is sparse and largely transactional. The last
drug-adjacent ask in the entire record is a **March 2017** *"desperately seeking
oil"* message ([`dat:1391`](../../kb/data/1391-zachariah-harshman-rupture.md)).

Then it stops. From late 2018 to November 2020 the channel goes **fully dormant**
— roughly two years with nothing in it.

Those are Dan's hardest documented years. The 2018 deep cycle, the Fran Coldren
vigil and death in April 2018, the March 2018 eviction notice from 155 Virginia
Avenue served by his own maternal grandmother, the February 2019 move back to New
York ([[wiki/places/155-virginia-ave]]). None of it reaches this thread. Whether
Zach knew about any of it is unrecorded.

## November 2020: the same two men, different men

And then it comes back, and the register has changed completely.

In **November 2020** the thread reopens with two men in their thirties trading
deep, technical, entirely sober opinions about **Skater XL** and the **Tony
Hawk's Pro Skater** remake — replay value, trick mechanics, PS5 load times. The
warmth of the original high-school friendship is intact. None of the
transactional content is
([`dat:1391`](../../kb/data/1391-zachariah-harshman-rupture.md)).

The thread's last message is **October 1, 2021**: Zach circling back to a video
Dan had sent him a week earlier, still engaged.

That is the ending, and it is a good one. Two men who grew up together, who spent
a year and a half moving drugs through a kitchen, who screamed at each other at
four in the morning over a woman neither of them was with, talking about
skateboarding video games five years later like none of it had happened.

[[wiki/mind/synthesis/dormancy-not-exit|Dormancy, not exit]] argues the corpus's
ties do not end; they change job. Its member table sorts its cases three ways in
its own words — *"Franki is deletion, Danielle is reassignment, James is
suspension without reassignment"* — and **Zach is not on that table.** This page
proposes he belongs on it as a fourth kind: **rupture followed by genuine repair,
with the terms of the friendship silently rewritten and never renegotiated out
loud.** That is this page's reading, offered to that page rather than asserted
on its behalf. Nobody ever apologised. The subject simply changed.

## Appendix: forensic notes

Compact and at the bottom on purpose. The story is above; this is what an auditor
needs.

**1. The two-Zachs collision, and why it runs both ways.** A phone-book merge
fused two contemporaneous Uniontown Zachs. Google Contacts holds a bare "Zach"
record (413 + 843) and a "Zach Clingan" record (413 + 724 + 843); Facebook, which
never saw the 843 number, attaches 413 to Clingan alone. Harshman's numbers
(+1412…3533, +1617…4273, (724) 322-1572, SMASHED763@GMAIL.COM) appear in neither
set ([[wiki/people/zach-clingan]]). The error has run in **both** directions:

- **Harshman's surname was given to Clingan's messages.** An earlier page existed
  under the name "Zach Harshman," built on twenty-two received messages and a
  surname it had no evidence for. Corrected 2026-08-08
  ([[wiki/people/zach-clingan]]).
- **Clingan's behaviour was nearly given to Harshman.** A synthesis pass credited
  *this* page with the 2015-12-09 confrontation — *"have you lost your damn
  mind"*, *"keep my eyes open"*, *"he had his ex move out and you were there the
  next day. That makes you look like shit and he's going to treat you like it"*
  — texted to Annie. **That is [[wiki/people/zach-clingan|Zach Clingan]].** Dan
  names him in-thread (*"Zach clingan just texted me"*), and the origin line the
  same afternoon — *"Literally makes me want to puke. THAT is who introduced me
  to drugs"* — is corpus-verified at 2015-12-09 20:20:50 UTC, outbound
  ([`dat:0162`](../../kb/data/0162-zach-clingan-drug-use-origin-disambiguation.md)).
  **Nothing in the Annie corpus has Zachariah Harshman contacting Annie
  directly.** ATTRIBUTION block retained 2026-08-17.
- **A third Zach exists.** The 843 number was withdrawn from Clingan on
  2026-09-12 and re-anchored to an unattributed bare "Zach"
  ([[wiki/people/zach-unidentified-843]];
  [`dat:1213`](../../kb/data/1213-zach-clingan-caddie-yard-supply-inversion.md)).
- **A fourth is "Zac."** [[wiki/people/zac-shumar|Zac Shumar]], identified
  2026-09-12, argued with Dan about the switch on 2015-12-06 — three days before
  Clingan and seventeen before Harshman ([[wiki/people/zac-shumar]]).

**The operating rule:** in this record the Zachs are separated only by first
name. Any pass reading it must check the entity ledger before attributing, and a
bare "Zach" or "Zac" is ambiguous until it is resolved.

**2. A stale row that should not be quoted.** [[wiki/timeline/annie-read-notes]]
still carries, in its cascade table, the line that Harshman was an *"active
threat post-breakup — texts Annie 'I have to talk to you' on Dec 9; tells her she
looks like shit."* That is Clingan, corrected 2026-08-17 in the same document's
own not-applied list (*"Dec 9 texts as Zachariah Harshman's. They are Zach
Clingan's"*). The cascade row was not updated when the correction landed. It is
the highest-risk sentence about Harshman in the wiki and it should not be
propagated.

**3. The 644/~970 discrepancy: four candidate explanations, none confirmed.**
Same endpoints to the day, counts 33% apart. Candidates: (a) the page counted
*message blocks* where the manifest counts *messages* — the Facebook manifest
tracks both and they diverge on other threads (Bobby Cole: 908 messages, 941
blocks; `raw/facebook-threads/MANIFEST.json`); (b) the page's figure was an
estimate carried as a precise one; (c) the two exports differ — the page's
source is the 2026-06-23 `facebook-ihatedanfrank` export, the manifest's is the
2022-09-05 DYI export on Drive; (d) deduplication. **(a) and (c) are the
strongest and are not exclusive.** Resolvable by recounting the source HTML; not
resolvable from metadata.

**4. The Dec 30, 2015 referent is not established.** The read-notes cascade
carries *"he already called Zach to arrange for more"* (2015-12-30, 19:30) and
correctly declines to apply it: *"'He' is ambiguous and the record does not say
which Zach"* ([[wiki/timeline/annie-read-notes]]). The supply inference was
**not** applied to this page and should not be.

**5. The "shu" attribution is inherited, not derived.** Covered in the body above.
Recorded here in auditable form: [[wiki/timeline/annie-record]] (modified
2026-08-18) reads "shu" in the 2015-12-13 10:39 message as *likely* Harshman;
[[wiki/people/zac-shumar]] (2026-09-12) supplies a better-fitting candidate with
a dated December 2015 grievance. Neither is corpus-confirmed. `Shu` also appears
as its own unresolved ledger row in [[wiki/timeline/annie-read-notes]] —
*"rearranged Dan's living room 'more dab friendly'; leaves for Philadelphia Nov
29"*, marked **no page — recurring, likely supply** — which is a third possible
referent and does not obviously match either man. **Three candidates, one
nickname, no resolution.**

**6. The December 26 adjacency is noted, not asserted.** SLOPPP's "Goodbye Demo"
lands three days after the rupture. Nothing in either thread connects the two
closures, and this page does not claim they are connected
([[wiki/interests/music/aliases/sloppp]];
[`dat:1391`](../../kb/data/1391-zachariah-harshman-rupture.md)). The alias's
actual retirement date is later — March 7, 2016, the last public appearance of
the name — and December 26 is the last *release*
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/dance-music-only-phases]]).

**7. What would move this page.** A recount of the source HTML, settling 644 vs
~970 and supplying per-year volumes. Any message resolving "shu." Any contact
after 2021-10-01. An account from Zach of the December 23 exchange, or of the
2020 revival, in his own words. An L2 entity node so the graph stops treating a
fourth-ranked correspondent as a single datum.
