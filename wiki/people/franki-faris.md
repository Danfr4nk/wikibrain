# Franki Faris

Franki Faris was a rebound that collapsed within days, during a brief split
from [[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis Armel]] in the summer of 2013 — a real,
named person, a girl Dan knew from junior high and high school in Uniontown,
but one who appears in the record almost entirely as a retrospective data
point rather than through any surviving direct correspondence. She has no
message corpus of her own; everything known about her comes from Dan's later
AI-assisted self-narration (the Gemini-58 and DANSYNTH chats), from dated
material inside the archive itself, and from two operator captures written
in 2026.

What the episode left behind is larger than the relationship was. One
afternoon in early July 2013 ended the six-year relationship with Alexis in
real time; the July 9–31, 2013 batch of self-typology emails Dan sent
himself around it carries his earliest documented INTP self-identification —
the dated origin of the self-measurement practice [[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-commissioned-self]]
traces forward a decade; and the name outlived the person by thirteen years,
becoming his shorthand for the abrupt-switch pattern he re-ran with Annie
in December 2015. The page that documents her is therefore a strange
object: a person-page in which the person is the least-documented thing,
and the effect is the whole record.

## Before the interlude

Franki was not a stranger found after the split. A first-person operator
capture written 2026-08-02 — the day's only narrative source — says Dan knew
her *"from highschool and junior high."* Her parents' house was
15 Holly Lane in Uniontown; she had her own apartment in Somerset, near Seven
Springs, where she worked as a **ropes-course attendant at the resort**. That
places her inside the Seven Springs orbit that
[[wiki/timeline/events/bald-eagle-cummings]] establishes as the source of most of
Dan's non-school friendships — the same milieu, a few years later.

These biographical details are single-source (the 2026 capture), with no
independent confirmation anywhere in `raw/`. They are carried as attributed
operator testimony, not as settled fact — which is the standing condition
of almost everything on this page.

Dan's later account of her is flatly stated, almost inventory-like:
*"brunette, skinny af, drug-accepting, daddy issues"* — a type, not a
portrait (capture testimony, 2026). What converted the type into an obsession
inside a single afternoon was a credential: she put one song on him that
day — "17" by Youth Lagoon — and his account of it is the wiki's sharpest
real-time case of the cool metric: *"FUCKING NOBODY IN UNIONTOWN WAS
LISTENING TO YOUTH LAGOON,"* and someone he *"immediately bowed down before
to worship."* The song did in minutes what the typology batch would later
try to formalize on paper: it made the opposite real. One credential, one
afternoon, and the reboot was underway.

## The fireworks day (early July 2013)

On an afternoon in early July 2013, Dan took one of his mother's cars, drove
to 15 Holly Lane, and picked Franki up with no destination in mind. Roughly
ten minutes in, a text arrived from Alexis, his partner of six years:
*"I saw your messages. I know you're with Franki."* She had been reading his
messages, prompted by something in his behavior — the discovery was
detection, not coincidence. Rather than turn around, he withdrew $350 from
his mother's ATM card, bought a quarter ounce of cocaine, and stayed out for
more than ten hours: driving between Uniontown and Franki's apartment in
Somerset, passing under a municipal fireworks display on the Rt 119 bypass
near the Main Street exit, picking up hitchhikers near a campground on the
day's own logic, reaching her apartment around midnight. Within days, she
*"got sick of me basically immediately and it went nowhere."*

Dan's own assessment of the day: *"one of the more pivotal days of my young
adult life."* The record supports the weight he gives it — the six-year
relationship ended, the pattern he would later call "Franki Faris 2.0" was
established, and the music-production identity began in the same season.
Full account, sequence table, and dating caveats:
[[wiki/timeline/events/franki-fireworks-day-2013]]. The capture does not name
the date; the "July 4" framing is supported by the fireworks geography but
still not explicitly dated.

## The typology batch (July 9–31, 2013)

In the weeks around the episode, Dan sent himself a batch of self-typology
emails — the corpus's earliest instance of the self-measurement practice.
He typed himself **INTP** (his earliest documented use of the label),
Franki **ESFP / "Performer"** ("magic pixie dream girl"), and Alexis
**INFP/ISFP "Idealist."** In his later framing, the move was an attempt to
"reboot" his identity around Franki as Alexis's opposite — the new woman
typed up as the photographic negative of the old one.

The batch is the origin point of [[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-commissioned-self]]'s
pattern, a full decade before the AI instrumentation that pattern is mostly
built on: the journey's first stop is this page, precisely because the
instrumented self begins here, as an exercise run over a partner in the
middle of a rupture. The typing details themselves come to the page through
Dan's later AI-assisted recounting rather than as independently held primary
artifacts, and are carried accordingly.

The filing history of the batch carries its own lesson. Alexis's typing —
INFP/ISFP "Idealist" — sat **only on this page** until 2026-09-03, which is
what let two independent 2026-08-21 passes conclude the six-year partner had
no recorded type at all. A datum filed on a rebound's page and not on the
partner's page is a datum the partner's page has no way to discover. It now
lives on [[wiki/people/alexis-armel]] as well.

## The collapse and the post-mortem

The clearest primary anchor for the whole episode is an August 14, 2013
email in which Dan performs a point-by-point post-mortem of the previous two
months and admits: "I really didn't have the romantic feelings for franki
that i said i did at the time... I wasn't even able to kiss anyone."

Read plainly, the confession reframes the episode. It was not a rebellion
executed; it was a rebellion attempted and failed — he couldn't perform the
role he'd cast himself in. The "reboot" the typology batch was supposed to
engineer didn't survive contact with the person it was built around, which
is why the interlude collapsed in days while the instrumentation it
produced lasted a decade. The email and the typology batch are the two
contemporaneous dated anchors; both corroborate the summer-2013 window.

## The interim, publicly (July–September 2013)

The public record bounds the rupture from the outside and is worth holding
against the reconstructions. On 2013-08-09 Dan tweeted, in his own words,
"I guess I tweet more when I'm single?" — the relationship was off, on the
record, that August. From 2013-08-21 through the 31st, eleven days of posts
with @katie_efff — Blue Star Point Lookout, "Mountain things," the
Pittsburgh Oddball festival — put [[wiki/people/katie-fletcher|Katie
Fletcher]] publicly inside the gap, the "interim frequency" Dan later named
her. Then, in early September: 2013-09-07 — *"My better 1/2."* /
*"Mystery girl and everything's okay now"* — and 2013-09-08 — *"Alexis
Armel and a doppelgänger mosquito"*; by 2013-09-25, *"If having a bad
bitch was a crime I'd be arrested @alexisarmel."*

So the break runs roughly July to the first week of September: six to eight
weeks, the only documented interval in six years where the relationship is
off, filled immediately, with the reconciliation announced in the language
of relief rather than decision. One gap the public record cannot close:
whether the September 7 "mystery girl" is Katie or Alexis. Both readings fit
— the next day names Alexis — and neither is asserted here.

One tension is left standing: the public naming of Alexis on September 8
precedes the October reconciliation the 2026-08-27 capture asserts, and the
capture's interim sequence (her mother's house in Carmichaels, a new man,
a brief move to New York) fits awkwardly inside six to eight weeks. The
record holds both; neither is smoothed over here.

## The reconciliation (October 2013)

The 2026-08-27 capture supplies the fuller sequence, and it makes the
interim real rather than notional. After her five days displaced to the
Virginia Avenue address, Alexis went to her mother's in Carmichaels, began
dating a man named **Chris Tressler**, and moved with him to New York. One
week later she came home to Pennsylvania to see Dan; he convinced her to
stay; the actual reconciliation landed in **October 2013** — roughly three
months after the fireworks day, not "within weeks."

The return wasn't clean. In the interim Alexis had confronted him — the
source material points to a message along the lines of "I know you're with
Franki" in the early-July window, now located verbatim from the fireworks
day itself — and by the time Dan tried to return, she had already moved on
to another man, described as "fully invested." The six-year relationship
survived its only mid-course rupture by passing through another
relationship first. Full account and the Chris Tressler/Annie timeline this
cascades into: [[wiki/people/alexis-armel]].

## The fallout: the switchboard's first appearance

After the episode, Suz brought Alexis and Danielle Onesi — Dan's first
girlfriend, Alexis's "former enemy" — together to bond over their shared
grievance against him. Dan's account, flat: *"and this caused a huge
thing."* It is the earliest documented instance of the maternal switchboard
operating on the relational graph: the mother convening the current and the
former partner around a common complaint, as an instrument.

Note the shape of it. One afternoon with Franki radiated, within days, into
a multi-party grievance network — the current partner displaced to Suz's
other property, the ex-partner recruited as her ally, the grievance shared
and amplified. Every later switchboard operation the record documents runs
this same pattern: Suz doesn't take sides in the relationship; she
reorganizes the graph around the grievance. The Franki day is where that
instrument first appears in the record.

## Afterlife: the name as shorthand

The Franki incident became Dan's own shorthand for a specific pattern —
leaving a long-term partner abruptly for someone new during a moment of
crisis — and he invoked it, four days into things with Annie, **to Annie
herself**. In the early hours of December 3, 2015 (the evening of December
2 in EST, per the held-corpus timestamps), inside an "I love you" exchange,
he texted: *"I had a Franki Faris 2.0 situation earlier today"* —
*"And then out of nowhere you popped up"* —
*"As in - I left Alexis and had an existential meltdown with another girl.
Annie Ulmer was playing the role of Franki this time."* The "you" who
popped up, and the recipient of the confession, was Annie. (This page
previously said he texted "a friend"; the held exchange corrects it —
see Conflicts.)

The correction matters because it changes what the shorthand was doing.
He wasn't gossiping about the pattern to a third party; he was handing the
casting notice to the woman being cast, in real time, four days in — a
confession and a role assignment in the same breath. Nine days later he
wrote, *"But after the whole thing with Alexis and Franki I stay away"* —
by then the incident was established shorthand between them, a shared
reference with the weight of a warning label.

## A sighting, not a resumption (May 2016)

On 2016-05-09 at 20:07 UTC (16:07 EDT), Dan texted Annie from Suz's place,
during a family blowup: *"I just saw Franki"* — followed immediately by
*"He car broke down going up the mountain lololol"* (sic; "Her car,"
presumably — she was still in the Uniontown/Somerset orbit, three years on).

This is a genuine held-corpus fact, byte-verbatim in the iMessage archive,
and it breaks the claim this page carried for years: "no corpus, no
contact, nothing after 2013." It was flagged as an open gap by the kb datum
that verified it (`kb/data/0357`), and it is recorded here rather than
explained away. What it is not: evidence of contact. A sighting is not a
conversation; no exchange with Franki is recorded; the tie stays dead. The
2018 "Franki" messages to Annie are the pet-nickname usage, distinct from
this 2016 sighting *sent to* Annie, which is not.

The sighting sharpens the control case rather than breaking it. What
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/dormancy-not-exit]]'s tenure floor bounds is the
**tie**, not the geography: the person can remain in the Uniontown orbit,
visible from the road, while the relationship leaves no trace at all.
Presence persisted; occupancy never re-formed.

## The name as vocabulary

By 2018, "Franki" had drifted into a casual pet nickname Dan used for Annie
herself — *"Sorry I thought u would read it when you could safely. Won't
bother u anymore Franki"* (2018-01-02) — the shorthand collapsed all the
way into endearment. Not every "Franki" in the corpus is the person or the
nickname: on 2018-06-09 he wrote *"I'm gonna be around other than going to
Franki's for a bit"* and immediately self-corrected — *"Fuck..Franko's. I've
already spent some time at franki's lolz"* — which reads as a venue
(Franko's), not a person. The lowercase drift is autocorrect, not affection.

The name kept working as raw material long after: *"you are dan franking
this right now"* (2025-09-04) — the surname as verb, the incident as
wordplay. And in March 2026, texting Tom, he surfaced the old corpus under
its incident labels: *"I just found / Chats with Alexis during the Franki
Faris / Katie fletcher incidents."* Thirteen years later, "Franki Faris"
is a filing category — the name of the drawer the summer of 2013 lives in.

## Conflicts in the record

**[2026-07-13] REVISED — the incident dates to summer 2013, not 2014.**
The CATO bootloader's "2014" framing is an artifact of later AI-assisted
reconstruction, superseded by the dated primary emails (the July 9–31
typology batch, the August 14 post-mortem). Node ordering in the DANSYNTH
scrape (Node 34 "Franki Incident" → Node 35 "Katie Fletcher, 2013") is
consistent with the 2013 dating. This resolved a standing gap note on
[[wiki/people/katie-fletcher]].

**[2026-08-02] CONTRADICTION opened — two five-day spans, one week apart.**
A 2026-08-02 capture attached five days to Alexis's stay at the Virginia
Avenue address (*"for 5 days before returning to carmichaels to her
parents"*), while the Gemini-58/DANSYNTH self-narrations attributed a
five-day tenure to Franki. Of Franki's own tenure the capture said only that
she *"got sick of me basically immediately and it went nowhere."* Neither
reading was adopted at the time — the earlier sources were exactly the
class of AI-assisted reconstruction most likely to have conflated the two
spans.

**[2026-08-28] RESOLVED — the five days belong to Alexis, not Franki.**
A second, independent operator capture (2026-08-27), volunteered separately
and unprompted, states again: *"She [Alexis] spent 5 days at Virginia ave
and then went to her moms."* Two independent T0 statements now agree: the
five days are Alexis's displacement; Franki's own tenure is separately and
consistently described as collapsing immediately. The reconstructions did
conflate the two spans, exactly as suspected. This page's title phrase —
"a five-day rebound" — is retired as imprecise: the rebound had no measured
duration; five days is how long it took Alexis to leave the address she was
displaced to. The control case on `dormancy-not-exit` is corrected
accordingly.

**[2026-10-05] CORRECTED — the "Franki Faris 2.0" recipient was Annie, not
a friend.** This page previously said Dan "texted a friend" on December 2,
2015. The held exchange (2015-12-03 02:32–02:35 UTC = December 2, 21:32–21:35
EST) shows the surrounding "I love you" conversation and the line *"And
then out of nowhere you popped up"* — the recipient was Annie herself. The
body is updated; the nine-days-later "I stay away" text stands as before.

**[2026-10-05] CORRECTED — a 2016 sighting breaks "nothing after 2013."**
The held iMessage *"I just saw Franki"* (2016-05-09 20:07 UTC, to Annie,
from Suz's during a family blowup) is a real-person sighting this page's
absolute claim did not account for. The claim now reads: no recorded
*contact*, with one sighting on record. Flagged as an open gap by
`kb/data/0357` and closed here as a dated correction rather than a
quiet edit.

**[2026-10-05] CLARIFIED — the June 2018 "Franko's" is a venue.** The
2018-06-09 *"going to Franki's for a bit"* messages self-correct mid-thread
to "Franko's" — a place, not the person and not the pet-nickname usage seen
in the 2018-01-02 messages to Annie. Carried as a limit note so the two
2018 usages are never conflated.

**[OPEN] The "July 4" framing.** This page carried a standing note from
2026-07-13 that no source anywhere tied a July 4 event to Franki. The
2026-08-02 capture describes a municipal fireworks display at the mall,
visible from the Rt 119 bypass, in early July — but does not name the date.
The framing is now *supported* (a July-4-adjacent event in practical terms)
and still not *dated*. A Uniontown fireworks schedule for 2013 would close
it.

## Assessment

Franki Faris is [[wiki/mind/synthesis/dormancy-not-exit]]'s control case,
and a sharper one now than the page originally stated: an occupancy that
collapsed **immediately** — not the five days once attributed here — left
no message corpus, no resumed contact, and no trace in the message record
after 2013, while leaving the **name** behind as working vocabulary for
thirteen years. The 2016 sighting refines rather than weakens the control:
the floor bounds the tie, not the geography. What survives below the tenure
floor is the label, not the relationship — first as shorthand for the
abrupt-switch pattern, then as a pet nickname for Annie, then as an
incident label in the archive's own filing system.

The page is also a working demonstration of the wiki's correction
machinery: it retired its own title phrase on fresh evidence, re-attributed
its central figure to the right person, and now corrects its own recipient
attribution and its absolute "nothing after 2013" claim — each as a dated,
sourced entry above, none of them deleted for being embarrassing.
