# Jackson Alexander (Alexander Jackson)

Alexander Jackson is a name without a voice. The wiki has carried him since
June 2026 as the owner of **463 Morgantown St** in Uniontown — the house Dan
and his mother [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]] ended up in after
[[wiki/places/337-saratoga-drive|337 Saratoga Drive]] was sold — and every
risk the wiki has assigned to that address has been routed through his title.
In the complete message record he never speaks, is never spoken to, and is
never named. Not once, in 198,000 messages, does the string "Jackson" refer
to him.

What the record does contain is a house. It has a renovation that started
before anyone lived there, a contractor who took the money and slept on the
job, a painter who kept coming back, a son who worked forty-three hours there
for two hundred dollars, a shower that got used more than any other room,
a move done alone and a week late, and — in three short phrases from Dan and
his mother — the only description of the owner that exists anywhere: **a
client of Suz's, and a kid in boot camp.**

This page is about that house, the person the record says owns it, and the
distance between the two.

## What the name rests on

The ownership claim has one provenance chain. The old wiki's page asserted it
from four sources — `operating_manual.md`, `CONTEXT_CORE_EXPANDED.md`,
`BFS_BOOTLOADER_v2.md` and a per-contact export of Suz's thread — none of
which is held in this repository
([`dat:0243`](../../kb/data/0243-alexander-jackson-owner-claim.md),
confidence **low**). Three of those four are AI-secondary syntheses. The page
itself always said so: *"Limited direct corpus on Jackson himself (no
messages/comm details surfaced in reviewed dox)."*

This rewrite searched the whole held record for the name:

The only places the name exists are the old-wiki export and the pages built
from it. The word order is not even settled:
[[wiki/people/john-carney]] records the inversion (*"Oversight by Jackson
Alexander"*) as an open defect. **The name is carried as asserted, not
established.** If a deed, tax record, lease or message surfaces, it can be
promoted; until then everything below is written about "the owner," and the
name is the wiki's label for him.

## What the primary record says about the owner

Three messages, all between members of Dan's household, describe the owner.
None of them names him.

**27 January 2026, 11:52 a.m. — Suz to Dan** (corpus `2026-01-27 16:52:05`
UTC): *"I may need your help. I'm typing a contract for John to send to my
client."* Suz is a working realtor — the listing agent of record on 337
Saratoga ([`kb/entities/morgantown-st-463.md`](../../kb/entities/morgantown-st-463.md))
— and "John," on every later row, is the contractor working the Morgantown
St house. The contract she is typing is between a contractor and **her
client**.

**27 April 2026, 2:27 p.m. — Dan to [[wiki/people/tom|Tom]]** (corpus
`2026-04-27 18:27:01` UTC), explaining why he stopped working for John:

> *"My mom got him a contract to do work on a house shes involved in and the
> owner is a kid currently in boot camp. He got paid the full amount up front,
> did none of the work and is now not speaking to my mom because she
> 'bothered' him about it."*

Three minutes later, in the same thread: *"It looks like we're going to be out
of this place mid-june. the plan right now is to move into that house I was
just telling you about for 3 or 4 months. / And to tie it all together - this
house is literally right across the street from my new job."*

**27 July 2026, 1:47 p.m. — Dan to Annie** (corpus `2026-07-27 17:47:24` UTC),
a month after the 337 sale closed: *"Nothing got thrown away so right now we
have 3 full storage units and then a full garage of the house she is
listing."*

Read together, these give a picture the old page never had: the owner is a
young man away at military basic training; Suz is **his agent** — "a house
she's involved in," "my client"; she procured the contractor for him and
drafted the contract; the Franks planned to occupy the house for a fixed
three-to-four-month window; and by late July it was, on the most economical
reading of "the house she is listing," **on the market with Suz as the
listing agent while her own belongings filled its garage.** That last step is
an inference — "the house she is listing" does not name an address — but no
other house in the record fits: 337 had already sold, and nothing else has a
garage the Franks are using.

"Kid in boot camp" is Dan's phrase and Dan's knowledge. It is not a birth
date, a branch of service or a name, and it is not corroborated by any second
source. It is still the only first-hand description of the owner in
existence, and it is compatible with the name the wiki carries without
confirming it.

## The house before anyone lived in it

The old page dated the move-in to "~early 2026." The record shows something
else: from January to June 2026 the Morgantown St house was a **work site**
that the family visited daily and did not live in. The Franks were still at
337 Saratoga, which Suz was showing to buyers.

- **2 February** — Dan to Annie: *"John is working at the house on morgantown
  st today / And by working I mean he is here, asleep."* Dan is on site.
- **10 February** — Suz, morning: cigarettes *"on my way to Morgantown st"*;
  afternoon: *"Felix showed up but Arnu didn't. He's bringing Elijah over to do
  his homework until basketball practice and Felix is going to paint more. Do
  you want to come over?"* ([`dat:0445`](../../kb/data/0445-felix-painter-463-suz-message-verified.md));
  evening: *"Felix took E to basketball. He's coming back to work for an hour.
  It may benefit you to be here."* This is the only row in the record that
  names [[wiki/people/arnu|Arnu]].
- **15 February** — Dan: Milo *"went to the morgantown st house"*.
- **2 March** — Dan: *"We are going to morgantown street while they show the
  place"* — the family goes to Morgantown St to get out of 337 while it is
  shown.
- **16–18 March** — Dan is working the job himself: *"go work with John and
  Felix"*; and, to Suz, an argument about getting a ride home after *"is John
  done for the day?"*
- **26 March** — Dan to Tom: he was supposed to be paid the previous Friday,
  *"he's supposed to get the check from the client today."*
- **27 March** — Suz: *"I just read the John carney thing."* The dossier
  itself is not held ([[wiki/people/john-carney]]).
- **April–May** — the house as a bathroom and a meeting point. Dan, to Annie, on thirteen separate days between 6 April and 27 May: *"I'm going to
  morgantown street now to shower"*, *"my mom is at morgantown street"*,
  *"meet me at morgantown street in 10 mins"*, *"we could hang out … either in
  morgantown street or here."* On 23 May Suz, worried he is sick: *"If you
  start throwing up we should take you to Morgantown st and take a bed-like
  thing."* The "here" in these messages is 337.

What the rows show, in plain terms: the owner's house was being renovated by
contractors his agent had hired, and her family used it as an annex while
their own house was sold out from under them.

## Dan's forty-three hours

The one labour relationship Dan has with the Morgantown St house is the one
he complains about. In March 2026 he worked for John — the contractor Suz
had put on the job — and on 27 April he told Tom how it went:

> *"First of all it took him 3 weeks extra for him to actually pay me / I
> worked 43 hours … How much money do you think I earned for that 43 hours of
> (admittedly unskilled) work"* — Tom guesses $2,500 — *"Take off the last
> digit and you're still too high … Two. / Hundred. / Dollars."*

That is **$4.65 an hour**, paid three weeks late. In the same message Dan
compares it to his new job: *"I'm only making $11 an hour at BFS but it's
fucking easy and I have guaranteed hours every week"*
([[wiki/work/bfs-foods]]). A month earlier, to the same friend, he had
explained why he never asked what he would be paid: *"I just felt like I was
'lazy hasn't worked In a year guy' esp to John and if I started saying 'HEY
HOW MUCH ARE YOU PAYING ME' and not just trying to do a good job and staying
quiet I would look worse."*

The owner is invisible in this too. The chain of payment Dan describes runs
**client → John → Dan**, and it stalls at John. It is the same chain, and
the same contractor, that took *"the full amount up front"* and *"did none of
the work."* If the owner is who the wiki says, then the first documented loss
at his address is his, not Suz's: a contract paid in full and not performed.

## The move

The plan in April was a mid-June exit from 337 and three or four months at
Morgantown St. What happened, as far as the rows show:

- **15 June** — Suz: *"I'm going to meet Felix and take a shower."*
- **19 June** — storage units: *"I found the key to another lock we can use on
  storage unit."*
- **24 June** — 337 Saratoga sells, $465,000 (MLS, web-corroborated,
  [`kb/entities/morgantown-st-463.md`](../../kb/entities/morgantown-st-463.md);
  the old wiki's 23 June is one day off and unresolved).
- **24 July** — Dan to Annie: *"I swear to god I moved the entire house by
  myself. It took an extra week - the people were so fucking mad"*
  ([[wiki/timeline/events/july-2026-recontact]]).
- **27 July** — three storage units and *"a full garage of the house she is
  listing."*
- **22 August** — Dan to Ally Lubin: *"Do not judge the insane amount of stuff
  outside the house we just moved and lost 3000 square feet and my idiot
  mother will not get rid of literally anything."*

So the move-in is **late June to July 2026**, not "early 2026," and it was a
downsizing by, in Dan's estimate, three thousand square feet into a house
that was — on the reading above — simultaneously for sale.

Then, on **11 August** at 11:04 a.m., from Suz: *"It's time for you to go."*
Five minutes later: *"I'm so tired of you stealing from me. You have no
respect for me and you don't care one single bit about me. I'm so
disappointed."* Whatever the owner's arrangement with Suz was, Dan's tenancy
under it was a tenancy under **her**, and she had just revoked it by text. The
record does not show him leaving: he is at the address on 17 August, on
1 September (*"You have two visitors here"*), and on 10 September (*"Please
clean up the dog pee before Felix gets here"*).

## The address becomes a threat

On **17 August 2026** [[wiki/people/jerel-coles|Jerel Coles]] typed *"Morgantown
st by BFS"* (12:16 p.m.) and then *"463 Morgantown st ?"* with four laughing
emoji (12:26). The second is **the only row in the entire record that attaches the number
463 to the street**; every other mention is "morgantown street" or "the house
on morgantown st." Coles's "by BFS" matches Dan's own "right across
the street from my new job" in April — two independent placements of the same
house.

Dan's replies, in the order the corpus has them: *"Okay enjoy the TRO"*
(12:27), *"let me repeat that you are not welcome on the property I live on.
There are cameras everywhere and I will protect myself if you decide to show
up here"* (12:35), and, after Coles's walk-back at 12:42, *"So why are you
sending me my address? Its an implied threat and any court would read it as
such"* (12:43). The [[wiki/legal/463-morgantown]] page quotes these in a
different order; the timestamps above are the corpus's.

The phrase that matters for this page is *"the property I live on."* Dan does
not say "my house" or "my mom's house." He says the property he lives on. And
the owner — the one person with standing to trespass Coles from it, and the
one person whose property it is — is, on Dan's April account, somewhere in
basic training. See [[wiki/timeline/events/august-2026-morgantown-call]] for
why the audio file from the night before is named for this street.

## What this does to the risk analysis

The old page's thesis was that the owner is *"the structural reason every risk
at this address lands somewhere other than on him"*: liens attach to his title,
while the unwritten tenancy, agency and caretaking arrangements leave Suz
exposed. The primary record changes the shape of that argument without
refuting it.

**Suz's role is agent, not only tenant.** The old page called her
"agent/tenant/caretaker (mixed)." Two of her own rows make the agency concrete:
the contract for "my client," and (on the likeliest reading) the listing.
That is a professional relationship with its own paper — a listing agreement,
at minimum — even if no lease exists. The record contains none of that paper
either.

**The contractor exposure is the owner's loss first.** *"Paid the full amount
up front, did none of the work"* is a claim by the owner against John, not a
claim by John against the owner. A mechanics lien arises when a contractor is
**unpaid**; the one contractor whose payment the record describes was paid in
full. Arnu's lien, "estimated to mature ~2026-07-27," has no row behind it at
all: Arnu appears once, as a no-show on 10 February, and the word "lien" occurs
in the corpus twice, neither time about this house (one is the Chapter 13
"audit" below, the other the French word *lien* in a 2025 screenplay draft). The lien is not disproven;
it is **unevidenced**, as [[wiki/people/arnu]] already says.

**"Unreachable since March" does not survive.** [[wiki/people/john-carney]]
and the old version of this page carried John Carney as ~10–20% complete and
unreachable since March 2026. Dan's April account has John not speaking *to
Suz*; but on **1 September 2026**, asked by Suz who is at the house, Dan
answers *"Carney and Felix"* — and she replies *"Oh lord."* Carney was on the
property in September.

**The Chapter 13 figures have a corpus trail, and it is AI-formatted.** The
$157,000 scheduled liabilities, the October 2024 filing, the 2018–2021 IRS
priority claims and case number 24-22285-GLT reach the message record once:
in a formatted "OFFICIAL DATA AUDIT" Dan sent Suz on **30 March 2026**
(`2026-03-30 17:38:21` UTC), which also describes a $300 monthly plan and an
August 2025 court deadline. It says nothing about Morgantown St or its owner.
The figures are consistent with the wiki's; their origin, like the owner's
name, is a synthesis rather than a docket.

**The open lead is re-aimed.** The old page asked whether the owner knew that
his tenant-agent was a Chapter 13 debtor. If Suz is his realtor, the better
question is what the listing and contracting paperwork between them says — and
whether a young owner in basic training, dealing with a contractor who would
not return his agent's calls, knew who was living in his house.
