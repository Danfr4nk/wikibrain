# Libby Titus

Libby Titus — **Libby Fagen** in the corpus, born Elizabeth Jurist, the
songwriter who co-wrote *"Love Has No Pride"* with Eric Kaz and who was
married to Donald Fagen of Steely Dan from 1993 until her death (public
records give her birth as 1947-07-06, Woodstock, New York) — employed
[[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] as a paid personal assistant in Manhattan
through the first half of **2024**. She was terminally ill for most of it:
on **2024-03-05** Annie reports that Libby told her she had brain cancer,
and the next day that it was also back in her right lung. She died on
**13 October 2024, aged 77**, with an unpaid-wages dispute between them
still open.

Dan never met her. She exists in this corpus entirely through Annie's
running commentary — 116 messages by this page's count, 112 of them between
February and mid-August 2024, plus Dan's replies — and she is the
best-documented thing about Annie's working life anywhere in the archive.
The messages put Annie driving her to appointments, spending half-hours on
the phone with doctors, handling household correspondence, and being
trusted — once — with a spreadsheet of the household bank accounts she
said she felt wrong looking at.

She matters here for three reasons, none of them about her. She is the
**largest documented income source on Annie's side** of the relationship;
the dispute that ended the arrangement is the corpus's clearest instance of
[[wiki/mind/concepts/forensic-method|Dan's forensic register]] pointed
outward on somebody else's behalf — three demand letters he drafted for
her, at $75 an hour, unanswered; and her death removes that income five
months before the next documented financial event on Annie's side.

> **Sensitivity note.** This page concerns a named, identifiable person's
> final illness, her household finances, and a wage dispute with her
> household, recorded from one side of a private conversation she was not
> party to and cannot correct. It is kept because it is load-bearing for
> the biography this wiki is about. **It remains a candidate for the portal
> seal** (`wiki.locks.json`, portal repo); the operator's decision is still

## The arc, February to October 2024

**February — the trial.** It opens on **2024-02-08** with *"Libby asked me
to come again tomorrow lol"*, implying a prior visit the corpus does not
hold. The register never changes: work and money arrive in the same sentence —
Antonuccis food *"hopefully she will give me something"* (02-11), *"I
gotta gotta gotta get up early tomorrow and help Libby. I need money."*
(02-16), with *"tell your boyfriend I said hello"* (02-09) between the
money runs: a casual, cash-first arrangement, not a job with hours.

**March — the illness, and the job turning medical.** Brain cancer (03-05),
*"I think I'm dying.."* (03-05), *"back in her right lung"* (03-06).
Annie drives her to appointments and sits through them (*"we are waiting
to see doctor now"*, 03-11), takes her for blood tests before a scheduled
procedure (03-17), spends *"the last half hour on the phone with doctors"*
(03-19), and receives, on **03-20**, an email of Libby's **cemetery plots**.
Libby tells Annie *"give yourself whatever I don't care"* (03-15) —
money as household cash, not payroll — and on **03-23** Annie skips a day
because *"She wants a relaxing day with her daughter"*: the corpus's only
mention of Libby having a daughter.

**April — the thin month.** The messages nearly stop: check logistics
(03-29, 04-05/04-06 — *"she has to get more checks from her apartment"*)
and Dan's 04-16 note that *"I told him about you working for Libby"*, to
an unidentified man. Then on **05-09**: *"now working for Libby again"* — the wording implies
an April lapse and resumption; the lapse is inferred, not stated.

**May — the peak, and the second job.** On **05-02** Libby fires the other
helper: *"LIBBY FIRED LISA"* — she had been *"using her phone to contact
Libby's travel agent and book stuff for her self."* Annie works two jobs
— *"Now I'll be here 6 days a week and then Libby in mornings…: I
don't want to get worked again and pass out like last time"* (05-08).
On **05-17** the line that will not reconcile: *"I was literally with
Libby for just over 3 hours today.. she paid me 500."* Around it, the
good months: the gaslighting story — *"And Libby screamed 'YEAH ABOUT ME
YOU ASSHOLE!'"* (05-28). The second employer, the six-day-a-week one,
appears nowhere else in this wiki's work pages and is still unidentified.

**June — household turbulence.** The Mark Hotel enters the record: *"Libby
said come to the mark tomorrow"* (06-23); checks are left *"at the front
desk"* (03-29). On **06-22**: *"Both Libby and Donald are threatening
suicide right now."* Two days later: *"Libby called and says take pictures
of everything this is a law suit"* (06-24), and Dan's clearest advice of
the era — *"you need to reaaaaallly be careful… if you're not working for
Libby exclusively it's going to turn her against you quickly lol."*
Between the crises: a Bob Dylan video
for a god-bothering texter — *"that'll show him"* (06-06) — and a photo
captioned *"Libby on left. Carly Simon on the right. James Taylor. Jonie
Mitchell"* (06-14).

**July — the arrears month.** *"Now it'll be more difficult for me to be
paid i dont know"* (07-01); on **07-10**, *"Annie I miss you I need you
please don't be mad at me"* — *"Libby?! I'm never mad at you!"* On
**07-14** Annie is under a hundred; the rent check is written 07-20 with
a week missed; by 07-21 *"she owes me for like a day and a half"* and her
mother is covering rent. On **07-24** the only hard arrears figure the
corpus holds — *"it was just for a total $675"* for the prior week's
hours. On **07-27** the daily refrain — *"let me know what to sign and
when I can get my check"* — plus a long rebuttal of an accusation that
she had misused Libby's information. On **07-28**: *"Looks like Libby
fucking blocked me. Message hasn't delivered."* Dan's note the same day:
*"Libby blocked Annie."*

**August — the letters, and the break.** On **2024-08-01** Annie asks
whether the landlord will hold the rent check while she waits on Libby's,
and Dan drafts the first demand letter. On **08-06**: *"after I had gotten
screamed at by Libby you get mad over Amber taking me for ICECREAM."* On
08-08 the household is a location: *"Aka where Libby
and Donald live lol."* The work stops. Letters two and three go out on
**08-13**; Annie forwards the correspondence and the Fagen-side response
to Libby's address, and on 08-14 adds that *"the hourly rate was set by
Libby Fagen."*

**October–November — the death.** Dan writes ***"Libby died"*** on
**2024-10-16** — in a different thread, not to Annie — and on **2024-11-01**
pastes `https://www.steelydan.com/news/libby-titus-fagen`. Public reporting gives 13 October
2024; Fagen's announcement is quoted in the press:
*"My beautiful wife, Libby Titus Fagen, passed on October 13th, surrounded
by family."*

## The household: Donald, the Mark, and the money

Donald Fagen is never a subject of the messages — he is furniture, until
he isn't: he reminds Annie of Dan, gets shouted down over the gaslighting
claim, emails Annie the household bank-account breakdown, and is the other
half of the 06-22 crisis. The trust extended to Annie is the
unusual part — correspondence cc'd until she is inside the accounts,
keys-level access (*"We had to get a guy to unlock Donald's room. Libby
snuck in"*, 07-17).

The Mark Hotel is the arrangement's infrastructure: checks at its front
desk, Libby summoning Annie there (06-23), and the final cutoff as a
**2:00 AM text** — recounted in the second demand letter — saying only
*"do not come for your check in the morning"* after Annie had been told
she could pick it up there. The money was never formal: *"give yourself
whatever I don't care"* (March); $500 cash for three hours (May); $675
for one week (July); $75 an hour on paper (August). No contract is ever
mentioned. Dan's 2024-07-27 assessment: *"totally off the books and totally
non-contracted"* — but a Department of Labor claim would be *"an easy
win"* given *"her admission that acknowledges you are still owed."*

The rent thread runs underneath it all: the landlord is
[[wiki/people/john-paci|John]], at [[wiki/places/307-e-76th-st]] the
building this wiki already covers — asked on 08-01 to hold the week's
check while Annie waits on Libby's.

## The unpaid wages, and the letters Dan wrote

This is the part the first version of the page missed entirely.

By late July the arrears had a shape: a day and a half's wages (07-21), a
$675 ask (07-24), the daily *"let me know what to sign and when I can get
my check"* (07-27), then the block (07-28). Through August Annie was owed
money, was **asked to sign an NDA**, and heard nothing. **Dan drafted
three escalating demand letters for her**, sent from his handle into the
thread for her to use:

> **2024-08-01** — *"Dear x I'm reaching out to inform you of the unpaid
> wages… The total amount is x (y hours at **$75/hour**)…"*
>
> **2024-08-13, 00:18** — the follow-up: first told she could pick up the
> check *"the following day at the front desk of the Mark Hotel"*, then
> the 2:00 AM *"do not come for your check in the morning"* text, with no
> response from Libby or Donald since.
>
> **2024-08-13, 01:00** — ***"Final Request for Payment of Unpaid Wages"***:
> no further remedy sought from *"Mr or Mrs Fagen"*; she has *"even agreed
> to sign a retroactive non disclosure agreement… in exchange for nothing
> more than the wages I am owed."*

Annie's NDA position is a stated trade — *"in exchange for signing an NDA
I would like to receive the unpaid wages"* (2024-08-01). On 08-13 she
forwards the correspondence and the Fagen-side response (content unknown)
to Libby's address, and on 08-14 adds that *"the hourly rate was set by
Libby Fagen."*

**Nothing in the corpus records a resolution.** The last letter is
13 August; she died on 13 October. On the evidence available the dispute
was open when she died, and this page will not assume otherwise in either
direction. Whether the NDA was ever signed is likewise unrecorded — raised
twice in August, never mentioned again.

## What the record shows about Annie's 2024

She worked **two jobs** through the first half of 2024 — the six-day-a-week
employer this wiki cannot identify, and Libby in the mornings at a
documented rate above anything on record for Dan in the same period — and
when the money stopped she pursued it formally, with dated letters, an
itemised rate, and a stated trade. The Libby money is
earmarked in the messages for phones and phone service, and for rent when
it fails to arrive.

The **$119K–$123K net outflow** at [[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]]
is unaffected — payment-app derived, not employment-derived — but the
inbound side of those exports has still never been swept, and the
single-earner reading of 2024 is not what the record supports.

## The texture, which is still worth keeping

The soured ending does not cancel what came before it: the middle months
show Annie reliable, turning up, trusted with money, medical appointments,
and correspondence by someone who plainly liked her. *"Libby hugged me forever"* (07-14). *"Libby wanted me to send you this"*
(07-14) — Libby sending things to Dan through Annie.

It is also funny. The brooch — *"I did cocaine once, but I didn't like
it"* (05-22). The hijab at the appointment — *"oh with my past I probably
shouldn't"* (03-22). She takes against the replacement helper Janine
(05-10), gives Annie a pair of loafers (05-09), and on 07-03 Annie asks,
*"Did you know Libby was in the movie Big"* — she was, in 1988. On 07-04:
*"I gotta get a birthday card for Libby"*, two days before the 6 July
birthday the public record gives her.

Both things are true and the page holds them together: a woman dying of
brain cancer who liked the person helping her, and an employment
relationship that ended with a screaming match, an NDA, a 2:00 AM text,
and three unanswered letters.

## Conflicts in the record

**[2026-08-23] The identification is closed.** The first version carried
it as *inferred* — Dan's 2024-03-17 Tumblr link (six hours after Annie's
*"Did you know Libby was in a movie with Jack Nicholson"*) and the
household Donald claiming to be *"the first person to ever use the term
'gaslighting' in a song"* (05-28). **Operator testimony of 2026-08-23
confirms it**: she is Libby Titus, married to Donald Fagen,
dead of the cancer the messages record.

**[2026-08-23] "No message names her surname" — false.** On 2024-08-14
Annie writes *"the hourly rate was set by **Libby Fagen**."* The failure
mode is the one `LLM_HANDOFF.md` documents: **a check scoped by the claim
it is testing cannot disconfirm it.** The search was for `Titus`, because
`Titus` was the hypothesis. The corpus says `Fagen`.

**[2026-10-05] The message arc's provenance is repaired.** Notes from
2026-09-09 recorded the 116-message arc as unheld in this repository. It
is held: the Dan↔Annie iMessage thread in the 2026-01-03 takeout carries
the full February–August arc, and the second file carries the *"Libby
died"* notice and the obituary link.

**[2026-10-05] Gap 2 is partially closed: the figure was typed.** The
first version stated the amount owed was never typed — every draft used
placeholders. False for one week: on 2024-07-24 Annie's drafted ask states
*"it was just for a total $675"* for the prior week's hours; the full
balance remains unknown.

**[Open] The rate does not reconcile.** The demand letters state
**$75/hour**, set by Libby. But on 2024-05-17 Annie writes *"I was
literally with Libby for just over 3 hours today.. she paid me 500"* —
roughly $167/hour — and a week later invites Dan to guess the figure for
four hours. Both first-hand, neither retracted. Possible readings: the
rate changed between May and August; the $500 folded in back pay or a
gift; or the $75 figure is the evidenced-in-writing portion of money that
flowed as informal household cash. **Unresolved, and recorded rather than
smoothed.**

**[Open] The standing account of Annie's employment was wrong for 2024.**
The dox-md *"Honest assessment"* states she *"was fired in 2023 and spent
a full year unemployed,"* and the framing propagated. She was working two
jobs through the first half of 2024. Corrected on
[[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]]; recorded here because this page is the
evidence.

**[Open] The later "Libby" loose end.** After 13 October 2024 the corpus
references a Libby twice more: Dan on 2024-12-20 (*"Yeah that's who I told
you to ask Libby about"*) and the Annie handle on 2026-09-06 (*"after
being with Libby from 8am-3:30pm"*). Neither can be Titus; prior research
treats these as a different, later Libby, unresolved.

## Assessment

**The forensic register, pointed outward.** Dated correspondence, an
itemised hours-times-rate total, an explicit statement of what remedy is
*not* being sought, a final-notice escalation on a fixed interval, and a
parallel legal assessment the same week the arrears peaked — *"totally off
the books… you would be able to make a claim for unpaid wages and her
admission that acknowledges you are still owed would make it an easy
win"* (2024-07-27) — the same instrument
[[wiki/mind/concepts/forensic-method]] describes him turning on his own
relationships, and one of very few times in eleven years it is deployed
for a third party's material benefit. Whether it worked is unknown. That
he wrote it is not.

**The engagement record cuts both ways, and the numbers say so.** On the
Libby window (2024-02-01→2024-08-31): **137 documented offers, executed
favors, or action-backed wellbeing check-ins** — roughly one every 1.6
days, concentrated in June (43) and July (48). Against that: exactly two
workday questions in 213 days; "how was your day" zero times; her 97
Libby-topic messages answered within 30 minutes only 42.3% of the time,
against 70.7% for everything else; and the three heaviest disclosures —
the diagnosis, *"I think I'm dying"*, the depressed afternoon at the
doctor's, all March — received no text reply within 30 minutes. The low
day-inquiry rate is dispositional, not a Libby-window departure. The
instrument that drafts the letters is the same one that misses the
heaviest disclosures in the text channel. Calls and in-person responses
are untestable from this data.

**On the 2026 grievance.** Her stated reasons run self-blame from August
2025 through August 2026 before the Libby-era him-blame formulation debuts
on 2026-09-06 — *"ignored," "not ask how my day," "take a toll"* — in a
single ~01:12 outburst retracted within two minutes. The record supports
the narrower claim — extraordinary instrumental engagement, with a
specific, measurable thinness on Libby-topic messages — not the blanket
version. Both halves are in the data; the page keeps both.
