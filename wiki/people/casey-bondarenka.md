# Casey Bondarenka

For about nine weeks in late 2015, Casey Bondarenka was in the room for the
most consequential fortnight of Dan Frank's adult life — and he had been
around for roughly a week when it started.

He arrives in the friend group in the third week of November 2015. On
November 29 he is the person Dan tells first, before anyone in the family,
that a six-year relationship is ending and a new one is starting. On November
30 he is in the house buying beer for the woman being evicted, and driving
Dan on errands while the eviction is being staged around them both. On
December 1 Dan telephones his own sister to keep him out of the house. On
December 2 he is asked, gently and in writing, to stay away. On February 15,
2016 he apologises for whatever it was. Then he checks in occasionally for
two and a half years, and in October 2018 he offers to float Dan some weed
because he has a medical card now, and the record ends.

Fifty messages, 1,060 days, `+17245626199`.

> **CORRECTED [2026-08-17]:** "Overstayed his welcome" implied an established
> friendship. The hand-read of the Annie corpus
> ([[wiki/timeline/annie-record]]) dates his arrival precisely: on 2015-11-30
> Dan complains *"Casey will never stfu here"* and explains, *"He just started*
> *hanging out with us last week"* (13:47–13:48 EST). Casey had been in the
> friend group **roughly one week** when the switch happened, and was asked to
> stay away within a fortnight. The entire documented arc — arrival,
> insertion into the household, expulsion — spans about nine weeks, which
> changes what his behaviour is evidence of: not a long friendship souring,
> but a near-stranger present at close range for the most volatile fortnight
> in the record.

## The week he was in the house

The switch from [[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis]] to
[[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] happened across four days, and Casey is in
all four of them. What follows is the human sequence; the evidence audit sits
at the bottom of the page.

**Sunday, November 29.** Dan meets Annie at the Uniontown Country Club golf
course at roughly two in the morning, in the rain. By 03:14 they have both
said *"I love you."* By 03:28 he has told her, to her face, *"I met someone
that instantly changed my life."* Alexis is asleep in the house he shares
with her.

That evening at **18:33:27 EST** Dan sends the line that is the switch's
earliest primary trace, and by two independent corpus passes he sends it into
Casey's thread:

> *"Things are collapsing with lex. You might be seeing a lot more Annie very
> soon."*

(Four other pages in the wiki send that message to
[[wiki/people/zachariah-harshman|Zachariah Harshman]] instead. The
disagreement is unresolved and is filed in the appendix rather than decided
here.)

A minute later Casey replies with a warning about a woman he has heard about:
*"Dude that's a slippery slope with her man... She's a hoe."* He does not, at
that point, connect the two. The same chat carries Dan
arguing with him about it in real time — *"i'm trying to think of every reason
it's not good but I'm obsessed with this new person Casey. She's fucking
perfect,"* then *"Casey I'm in love,"* then, flatly, *"keep calling my
dreamgirl a hoe Casey."* Three hours later, at **02:41:28 UTC** on the 30th,
the penny drops: *"Wait is that Annie?"*

Eighty-three seconds after the "collapsing" message, Casey had also asked the
question the page has always led with: *"Dude if you're breaking things with
Alexis.. Can I fuck her? Lol."* It is worth reading in the order it was
actually said rather than as a standalone shock. Dan announces a breakup;
Casey asks whether the outgoing partner is available; Casey then spends four
days apologising for having been too much. He is not a strategist in this
record. He is a young man with no filter who has walked into somebody else's
crisis a week after being handed a seat at the table.

**Monday, November 30.** At **00:39** Casey is buying beer for Alexis. The
eviction is already running as a sequence of reversible pressure steps —
her key confiscated on the 28th, the guest bedroom emptied so the police
could plausibly be called, mirrors down, everything packed — and Casey is
inside it, supplying the person being removed. At **13:44** he drives Dan to
get an e-cigarette fixed. At **13:47–13:48** Dan tells Annie he has been in
the group a week and will not shut up. At **23:00** Dan pulls something about
him off text and onto a phone call, with only the assessment left behind:
*"It is bombastic."* Whatever the "Casey saga" was, that sentence is all of it
that survives in writing.

Dan also tells Annie, that day, that the friend group does not understand what
he is doing — *"nobody understands why I'm doing this"* — and that he has not
been able to explain it to them. Three close friends had already asked the
night before. Casey is the one of them who was physically present for it, and
the only one who found out by accident.

**Tuesday, December 1.** Alexis leaves. Dan gets his head bashed against a
cabinet retrieving a laptop at 05:04, pretends to call the police, and by
09:33 the house is under an *"open-door Annie policy."* At **10:34**, in
between, he writes: *"Im calling Vanessa to make sure Casey doesn't come here
anymore."* The removal is not a confrontation either. It is a phone call to
his sister ([[wiki/people/vanessa-frank]]), who confirms Casey is no longer
welcome.

At **10:35–10:36** Dan inventories what Casey has left behind: *"70000 things
here"* — *"6 bottles of e liquid, one complete e cig, strobe light, HIS
WALLET."* A man who has been coming round for a week has left his wallet in
the house. That detail does more for the register of the friendship than any
characterisation: he was not a guest, he was living in the place part-time,
and he was removed before he had collected his things.

**Wednesday, December 2.** The relationship with Annie is one day old. At
roughly **13:33** Dan drafts the message that ends the friendship, and runs
it past her first:

> *"Frank u. Just don't want to hurt his feelings or have him think that I'm
> mad at him. He's just too much to be hanging around."*

Annie, at 13:38: *"I completely agree."* The exit is managed, not fought. The
stated reason is volume — *too much to be hanging around* — not the beer, not
the question about Alexis, not the "bombastic" thing. He is eased out by
consensus of a couple whose relationship is twenty-four hours old.

The same evening, the house's other crisis lands: Annie's mother
[[wiki/people/ellen-ulmer|Ellen]] learns Annie's car was at Dan's that
morning — *"someone told me your car was at Dan Frank's this morning"*
(18:44) — and objects on the grounds of Dan's reputation. Casey is blamed by
both Dan and Annie as the likely informant. **The informant is never named in
any record**, and the attribution stays unproven. It is the last thing he is
accused of and the only one he may not have done.

**February 15, 2016.** Ten weeks after the expulsion, a full apology arrives
— for *"anything I may have done that need apologizing for."* Nothing in the
record says what he thought he had done. Nobody told him. The apology is
generic because the grievance was never stated to him, which is the ordinary
shape of a friendship ended by a message designed not to hurt anyone's
feelings.

## The harvest

There is one more thing Casey is said to have taken from that fortnight, and
it is the only part of his record that Dan volunteered eleven years later
rather than the wiki extracting it.

> **HARVEST CLAIM [2026-09-12, Dan's testimony].** Dan states that during the
> switch window Casey took the harvest of his Virginia Avenue cannabis grow —
> his favourite, which he called **"Dream Queen"** — before it had cured, and
> smoked it all in one night. The taking itself is unattested outside his
> account. The opportunity is documented: he was inside the household
> 2015-11-29 through 2015-12-02, the exact fortnight
> (`dat:casey-uncured-harvest-dream-queen-20260912`).

The grow is real, dated and photographed. Fifteen readable frames of a
sixteen-photo set, sent by Dan on 2026-09-12, document a DIY closet grow at
[[wiki/places/155-virginia-ave|155 Virginia Ave]] from seedling to late
flower: EXIF dates running **2015-08-18 → 2015-11-01**, GPS on fourteen of
the fifteen clustering on the Virginia Avenue fix, shot on an iPad mini
running iOS 8.3 except for the final trichome macro (a Samsung SCH-I545). The
rig is visible in the frames — terracotta pots, Energetic-branded CFLs, a
clamp lamp, a desk lamp — and Dan's own account of it is *"zero growing
experience or knowledge; grown in a closet with clamp lights and incandescent
bulbs"* (`src:virginia-ave-grow-2015`, `dat:virginia-ave-grow-timeline-2015`,
`dat:virginia-ave-grow-flower-2015`).

The **October 4, 2015 frame** embedded at the bottom of this page is the
series' representative image and the one thing here that is not text: the
tallest plant of the run, mid-vegetative, terracotta pot, the clamp-lamp and
desk-lamp rig in shot — a grow being run in a closet by someone who had
looked nothing up. It matters to Casey's page for one reason. **The series
ends on November 1, three and a half weeks before he arrives, and the harvest
itself is never photographed.** Fifteen frames of a plant being grown; no
frame of the thing being cut, dried or jarred. Dan's account explains that
hole, and the hole is the only corroboration his account has. It is weak
corroboration — an absence consistent with a story is not evidence for it —
and it is recorded as weak.

"Dream Queen" is **Dan's name for it**, not a verified strain
identification, and the node filing it says so explicitly. No external
strain-database fact should be imported as his.

What the claim adds, if it holds, is the only thing Casey is alleged to have
actually done rather than said. Everything else on his record is talk —
a crude question, a warning about a woman, an unexplained apology. The
harvest is the one alleged act, and it sits on one man's memory of a night
eleven years earlier, given in passing.

## Later contact, 2017–2018

The thread does not end at the expulsion, and what it becomes is the least
dramatic possible version of itself: *"You working today dude?"* · *"You
duckin me bro or you just busy?"* Low-effort check-ins, apparently
unanswered, spread across two and a half years. The final message, on
**October 24, 2018**, is Casey mentioning that he now has a medical marijuana
card and offering to float Dan.

Read against the harvest claim, that last message is the page's quietest
irony and this page does not overbuild it: the man alleged to have taken the
uncured harvest resurfaces three years later offering to supply. Nothing in
the record connects the two events, and neither party refers to the grow. It
is noted because the dates are what they are.

Two things fall out of the dimensions and neither was on this page before.

**Fifty is a small number that ranks high.** 477 of the 577 held threads carry
fifty messages or fewer, and the median thread carries four. Casey at 50
messages sits **101st of 577** — inside the top 18% — which is a statement
about how thin the long tail of this corpus is rather than about how close
the friendship was. A nine-week acquaintance outranks four fifths of everyone
Dan has ever texted.

**The span is the anomaly, not the volume.** Thirty-seven of 577 threads span
a thousand days or more, and **eighteen of those carry a hundred messages or
fewer.** Casey is one of the eighteen: a channel that stayed technically open
for just under three years while carrying almost nothing. That is the shape
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/dormancy-not-exit]] describes as suspend-not-delete,
visible here in a relationship nobody would have thought worth suspending.
The stay-away message of December 2, 2015 was not a block; the thread simply
went quiet and stayed reachable, and Casey used it four or five times over
three years.

## Appendix — the evidence layer

The forensic material is here rather than in the narrative, because the
narrative is the point and this is the audit.

**The quotes verify.** All three load-bearing quotations were confirmed
verbatim in the held corpus, same thread, by `bin/corpus-query`: the
"collapsing" message at 2015-11-29 23:33:27 UTC, the Alexis question at
23:34:50, and *"Wait is that Annie?"* at 2015-11-30 02:41:28
(`dat:0122`). The 2026-08-17 correction's two quotes also verify verbatim:
*"Casey will never stfu here"* at 2015-11-30 18:47:45 UTC and *"He just
started hanging out with us last week"* 23 seconds later, both from Dan in
the Annie thread (`+17244346811`).

**The Dan's-Law hedge is retired; the attribution itself is not settled.**
This page used to carry the "collapsing" message as a worked instance of
contested attribution, on the grounds that Sent rows in
`MASTER_MESSAGES_DB_DUMP.csv` carry an empty `contact_handle` field, so the
recipient had to be inferred from timing and content. That specific hedge is
gone: in the held corpus the message **sits inside Casey's own thread**, the
recipient handle is present, and the same chat contains Dan writing *"Casey
I'm in love"* and *"keep calling my dreamgirl a hoe Casey"* — handle and first
name in agreement (`dat:0122`, `dat:0712`). The hedge was an artifact of a
deficient export, not of a deficient record, so
[[wiki/mind/concepts/dans-law]] does not get this as an instance and pages
citing it as one should carry the retirement. **What replaces the hedge is a
sharper problem**, recorded immediately below: a different inconsistency about
the same row, between pages rather than inside an export.

**The count is corrected.** The "36 messages" this page carried is the
received half. The held thread is 50 — 14 sent, 36 received — the same
export-side pattern the Bill, Bryan and Bub pages exhibit
([[kb/interpretations/received-half-count-pattern]]). The direction caveat
still applies to the *master dump*; it does not apply to the held corpus,
which distinguishes `is_from_me`.

**The timezone trap, worked.** `dat:0122` notes that this page's "13:47–13:48"
is a local EST rendering of corpus rows stamped 18:47–18:48 UTC. November is
EST, UTC−5. Both renderings are the same two messages. The wiki writes UTC on
some pages and local on others, which is why the log above names the zone
rather than normalising silently.

**The thread identification is an inference, stated as one.** Thread IDs in
`corpus/derived/threads.csv` are hashed, so `97d0ce22a17f9a0e` is not labelled
with Casey's handle. It is identified here because it is the unique row
matching every independently established figure at once — 50 messages, 14
sent, 36 received, first 2015-11-29, last 2018-10-24 — against `dat:0122`'s
direct count on the corpus itself. No other thread in the file matches. The
match is strong but it is a match, not a label.

**What has never been checked.** `dat:0122` lists the items its pass did not
verify, and they are the same items still unverified: the February 15, 2016
apology, the *"slippery slope… She's a hoe"* warning as a distinct row, the
October 2018 medical-card message, and the Ellen informant attribution —
which this page has always left unproven and still does.

## Gaps

- **The "Casey saga."** Whatever Dan called *"bombastic"* on 2015-11-30 moved
  to a phone call at 23:00 and is never restated in text. Look: December 1–3,
  2015 for a retelling to Annie or to a third party.
- **Who told Ellen Ulmer about the car.** Casey is blamed by both parties on
  2015-12-02 and never confirmed. The informant is unnamed in every record.
- **Whether the stay-away message was ever sent.** The December 2 draft is
  recorded, and Annie's approval of it is recorded. Casey's side of that day
  is not, and the February 2016 apology's generic wording is weakly consistent
  with his never having been told a reason.
- **The harvest.** Unattested outside Dan's 2026-09-12 account. The grow's
  photo series ends 2015-11-01 with no harvest frame, which is consistent
  with the account and is not evidence for it.
- **Relationship to Vanessa Frank beyond the removal call.** She is the
  instrument of the expulsion and appears nowhere else in his record. Whether
  they knew each other independently is undocumented.
- **The 2017–2018 check-ins are undated to the day.** Four or five messages
  across roughly two years, known only as a range and a register.
- **No surname corroboration.** "Bondarenka" comes from the wiki's own people
  index; nothing in the quoted message material carries a surname.

## Limits of record

- **This is one side of a thread, mostly.** 36 of 50 messages are his; 14 are
  Dan's. What Casey was told, and when, is largely not in the record —
  including whether he was ever told why he was removed.
- **The corpus is not in this repository.** `corpus/messages.csv` is
  gitignored by policy. Every verbatim quotation above is quoted from the node
  that verified it (`dat:0122`, `dat:0712`); what was computed here from
  committed files is the thread's dimensions and its rank, from
  `corpus/derived/threads.csv`.
- **The master dump understates and misdirects.** Its Sent rows carry no
  recipient handle and its Received flag is unreliable, which is the source of
  both errors this revision corrects (the 36-message count and the
  contested-attribution framing). Any figure on this page sourced to
  `MASTER_MESSAGES_DB_DUMP.csv` should be treated as a floor.
- **Deleted messages are absent.** The corpus is complete with respect to
  `chat.db`, not with respect to what was said. A thread this small is
