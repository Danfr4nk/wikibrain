# Mike Hinkle

Mike Hinkle is a caddy. In Dan's words, he is the realest "looper" he knows — a year-round professional who worked the summer season at Mystic Rock and spent the rest of the year in Tampa, looping at Old Memorial. At one point Hinkle lived with Dan for about a month. He belongs to the Nemacolin caddie-yard world that runs through Dan's 2016–2019 working life, the same yard documented from the inside on [[wiki/people/davey-fitzpatrick|Davey Fitzpatrick's]] page.

That is the identification, and it is worth saying plainly because this wiki spent months not having it. Until 2026-09-17 the corpus carried **two** Hinkle pages — this one, built from an 85-message iMessage thread, and [[wiki/people/michael-hinkle|a second page]] built from three unanswered Facebook messages sent in July 2017 — with an explicit instruction in the evidence layer forbidding anyone from conflating them without proof. Dan supplied the proof directly: Hinkle and Mike Hinkle are one person. The second page is now a pointer to this one, and the contradiction is retired below in a single section instead of three readings and three appendices.

## The working relationship

The record's earliest Hinkle hit is **2016-08-09** — *"invited to golf with hinkle"* — inside Dan's first full caddie season at Nemacolin. The caddie-yard jokes dated to summer 2017 keep him as a recurring, affectionately-mocked fixture: Dan's line to Davey Fitzpatrick, *"I'd pay Hinkle $100 to work our bags lolz,"* with Davey's laugh. A case-insensitive sweep of the held message corpus (`kb/data/0978`) finds golf-circle Hinkle references across 2016–2018, including a **2017-10-01** gratuity note (*"hinkle wrote in our grat for yesterday"*) and several from May 2018. In this record, golf-adjacent is nearly an identity, and Hinkle is firmly inside it.

The living-together month is Dan's testimony, not the corpus's — no lease, no dated message pins it down. It sits in the same window as everything else: the caddie seasons, the iMessage thread, the Facebook messages. A month under the same roof is the kind of fact that explains the casual register of everything that follows.

## The iMessage thread

Eighty-five messages, March 2018 to July 2019 — 64 sent, 21 received. (This page previously reported 21 messages; that was the received half of a two-sided thread presented as a thread total. The recount is documented in `kb/data/0978` and in `kb/interpretations/received-half-count-pattern.md`, which collects fourteen instances of the identical error across the people pages.)

The preserved sample is the one that placed Hinkle in the record's human center: *"What? Are you a pimp now or something? Hope Fran is ok"* — sent during [[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Fran Coldren's]] final decline. He knew Fran by first name and checked on her the week she died (April 4, 2018). That is not a distant contact's message. It is someone who knew the household.

## The three Facebook messages

On **Friday, 14 July 2017**, Dan sent three messages to Michael Hinkle on Facebook Messenger. No reply ever arrived in that thread:

> *"hey dude"*

> *"let me know if you see this"*

> *"annie will be home soon, so i'll call u if not. i found us something"*

Three messages is the exact length of the escalation — greeting, acknowledgement request, then the fallback (*"i'll call u if not"*) with a deadline (*"annie will be home soon"*) and finally the thing he opened the app to say. He did not send a fourth. He said what he had, named a way around the silence, and stopped.

The third message's clock is the most informative clause: it places Dan at home, alone, with [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] out and expected back — in July 2017, that residence is 155 Virginia Ave in Uniontown. The year is the hardest in the corpus ([[wiki/timeline/periods/2017-poverty-floor]]): water shut off, the account overdrawn, the yard jokes and the caddie season running through all of it. What *"i found us something"* referred to is not in the record, and this page declines to guess — though *"us"* implies a standing joint interest of some kind, which now reads naturally as a caddie's *"us."*

The old page treated the unanswered thread as a mystery about a stranger. It was never that. It was a caddy texting a fellow looper.

## One Hinkle: the resolution

For the record, here is what the contradiction was and how it closed.

The corpus held two Hinkle pages that did not obviously describe the same man: a Facebook contact (3 sent, 0 received, one afternoon in July 2017) and an iMessage contact (a 904 number, 85 messages across sixteen months, casual familiarity, Fran condolences). `kb/data/0977` carried an explicit instruction: *"Do not conflate the two Hinkles without evidence."* The michael-hinkle page maintained three live readings — one man, two men with one surname, two unrelated men — and selected none.

Meanwhile both pages were quietly claiming the same man anyway: the Davey Fitzpatrick page linked its caddie-yard Hinkle joke to the *Michael* page, while the golf-circle sweep was filed as narrowing the *Mike* page's open question. Neither page said what it was doing.

Dan's testimony of 2026-09-17 is the discriminator the evidence layer asked for: **one person.** A caddy. Lived with Dan for about a month. Mystic Rock summers, Old Memorial winters. The do-not-conflate instruction is superseded — it served its purpose, which was to prevent exactly the kind of sloppy merge this page now performs with evidence in hand.

One detail stays open rather than smoothed over: the iMessage handle is a 904 number, and the old page read that as Jacksonville, Florida. Dan places Hinkle's winters in Tampa. Area codes are not addresses — people keep numbers when they move — so this page drops the Jacksonville inference and keeps the handle. If a contacts export ever ties the 904 number to a Tampa address, that closes it.

## Gaps and limits

- **The living-together month** is Dan's testimony only — no dated message or document pins it. It is stated as his account, not as a corpus finding.
- **What *"i found us something"* was** remains unknown. No candidate is attached to it by evidence.
- **Whether the July 2017 phone call happened** is not in the record. *"i'll call u if not"* is the last thing either man says in any held source.
- **The thread itself has never been read in this repository.** The three quotations survive through the old-wiki export; the Facebook message file the old page cited (`messages/inbox/michaelhinkle_kybmlrlgtq/message_1.html`) is not held here. The manifest's fingerprint search narrowed it to two candidate files, neither yet pulled.
- **No photograph, no surname verification.** `media/registry.json` holds nothing for this person.
- **Inferred versus documented.** Documented: the caddie identity and living arrangement (Dan, 2026-09-17), the 85-message thread and its dates, the three Facebook messages verbatim, the Fran condolence, the caddie-yard jokes and golf-circle hits. Inferred: nothing structural remains — the one open inference is the 904/Tampa detail above.
