# Mike Zollinger

> **Incidental entry** — A 2018 caddy-season contact with a fully verified thread and one notable crypto exchange; complete at this size.

Mike Zollinger was a fellow [[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddying|Nemacolin]] caddy through Dan's 2018 season. The held thread opens on 2018-04-03 with "How was ur first loop" and runs to 2018-09-15 — 121 rows in the held corpus (kb/data/1164), where the older page figure of 51 appears to have counted a narrower slice. The old page describes the thread's texture as casual shift talk, tip comparisons, weather complaints, and a running joke about buying a house to rent back to him; the caddy framing itself is confirmed by the loop talk at the thread's head.

## The September 2018 crypto exchange

The thread's notable moment is verified verbatim in the held corpus. On 2018-09-12: "Half a bitcoin" (18:32:59 UTC), a Bitcoin address shared at 19:14:47 and again at 19:15:15, the same address as a blockchain.com link at 19:19:33, and "Yeah my ethereum went way down.. thought that way gonna sky rocket" at 19:22:39 (kb/data/1164). It is an early, matter-of-fact crypto thread, two years before the FSLY tip that later defines Dan's [[wiki/timeline/periods/2020-2021-market-era|2020–2021 market era]].

## Standing

The record holds one season: April to September 2018. Nothing in the held corpus extends the contact past that fall.
