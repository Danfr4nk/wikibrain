# The Supply Line

Two things move through this record that look unrelated until they are traced
together: money and drugs. Read separately, one is a family's inheritance
history and the other is an addiction history. Read as one route, they are the
same shape — capital arrives in a lump from a source that held it for decades,
passes through a switchboard, and drains out through a metered flow that never
lets the lump become a balance.

This journey walks that route from end to end, using what the wiki's own pages
already establish, and it prints the two complete logs the route runs on: the
**capital chain** (every dated financial event in the record) and the **node
succession** (every documented supplier, every era). A summary of either sits
above its log; neither summary replaces it.

The route has nine stops. Three of them are additions to this journey's
original six, and each earns its place by answering a question the shorter
version raised and could not close.

## Stop 1 — The source: a lump held for seventy years

[[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Frances "Fran" Whyel Coldren]] — born Jesse Frances
Thomas in Fort Martin, West Virginia on **15 August 1920**, died Uniontown on
**4 April 2018**, aged 97 — is not herself a source of drug money. She is the
source of the pattern's largest lump.

The capital has two stages and they are both hers by marriage. Her second
husband, **Thomas Whyel**, was a coal-and-coke man who died in the 1970s; her
third, **Ira Burdette Coldren Jr.**, was a lawyer she married within months of
that death ([[wiki/people/fran-coldren]]). So the money originates in a Fayette
County coal fortune and is subsequently administered through a legal one, with
**[[wiki/people/fred-adams|Judge Fred Adams]]** — Ira's old law partner,
described by Dan as *"basically the trustee of her accounts"* — as the
institutional valve ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]]).

Fran taught Dan to read before he was school-aged, funded the production
equipment his entire music identity runs on, and for roughly the last six
months of her life employed him at **$15 an hour** to keep her out of a nursing
home — a wage authorised through the estate by Adams himself
([[wiki/people/fran-coldren]];
[`dat:0446`](../../../kb/data/0446-fran-coldren-evidence-table-and-death-moment-retraction.md)).
The photograph of her in the **Sources** section below is the one the wiki uses
for her infobox, and it is worth looking at while reading the number: this is
the woman the largest financial event in Dan's life is named after, and the
relationship the money comes through is the one unambiguously positive
attachment in the entire family record.

The money that reaches Dan after her death is the family's oldest capital,
arriving all at once rather than as income: **$144,069.31**, distributed by
court order signed **21 August 2020**, with the final cheque of **$134,069.31**
released **1 September 2020** and deposited through his mother
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]]).

**Two things the source page establishes that the route depends on.** First,
the anticipation is dated earlier than the arrival: in January 2018, with Fran
alive and declining, Dan writes about what comes *"after gram goes and we have
a chance to get above water with the inheritance"*
([[wiki/timeline/periods/2018-deep-cycle]]). The money is being spent against
two and a half years before it lands. Second, **one asset appears to have left
the estate outside the distribution entirely** — Fran's Florida condominium,
which Dan's account says her daughter [[wiki/people/diane-moore|Diane]] went
down to sell and keep the proceeds of, while not visiting her before she died.
No price, date or instrument exists in the corpus
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]]). If that account is accurate, the
$144,069.31 measures what came through the court, not what the estate held.

## Stop 2 — The route: the money spine

[[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine|The estate/money spine]] traces where
that lump — and every other capital event around it — actually goes. Its
governing finding, already published, is that **capital arrives in lumps and
exits through standing drains**: nothing in the record converts into an asset
that holds.

The single sharpest measurement on that page is the burn rate. $134,069.31
lands on 1 September 2020. By early 2021 — roughly six months — the Au Za'atar
storytime opens with *"the money just ran out."* The market account peaked at
about $25k, so the market cannot have absorbed most of it. **A lump arrival in
this record survives about two quarters.**

The spine also carries a rule the shorter version of this journey did not have,
and it is the rule that makes the route a loop rather than a line: **a lump
arriving anywhere in this family is immediately re-lent inside it, on no paper,
at the moment of arrival.** In August 2018 — four months after Fran's death,
two years before the distribution landed — Dan drew roughly **$20,000** against
the expected estate and transferred about **$14,000** of it to his mother. He
recovered $4,000 before she stopped repaying, and by July 2019 the two of them
were threatening each other with court over the same money, each claiming to be
the creditor ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]];
[[wiki/people/suzanne-frank]]).

## Stop 3 — The switchboard

Dan's mother is not a passive conduit, and the journey's original version
understated her by one full category. Every major flow in the chain passes
through [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]]: the estate cheque was deposited
through her, the deep-cycle borrowing ran through her, the supply-era Cash App
transfers were facilitated by her, the 337 Saratoga sale is her transaction as
both owner and realtor, and the 463 Morgantown landing is structured by her
blended agent/tenant/caretaker role
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]]).

**The spine has no bank in it. It has a mother.** That concentration is the
financial expression of the [[wiki/mind/concepts/contact-gini|contact Gini]]
pattern — a single node carrying flows a diversified system would spread — and
it means her own finances are a single point of failure for the whole chain.
In October 2024 she filed Chapter 13 with roughly **$157,000** scheduled,
mostly IRS priority claims for **2018–2021**: precisely the years this route
treats as the poverty floor and the deep cycle. Over the same decade she
reports earning **$11,000–$14,000 a year against $10,000 a year in property
taxes** ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]]). Both parties were at the
floor at the same time, lending to each other.

**And she is a supply node in her own right.** The 2026-08-18 rewrite of her
page established that she buys from [[wiki/people/jim-vrabel|Jim Vrabel]] in
her own name across a decade, supplied Dan and Annie with cocaine routinely
through 2015–16 — the December 2015 two-sided record has him collecting from
her several times a week — and that the Cash App account the later supply-era
transfers ran through was installed at Dan's request in **August 2018**, the
same month as the $14,000 transfer ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]]).
The network therefore had a **family-internal source available before any named
dealer**, which is part of why a supply failure was survivable for so long.

The photograph filed as `upload-009` in the **Sources** section below is the
single most compressed image of this stop anywhere in the media registry. It is
Fran's living room during the caregiver era. On the table sit a game
controller, two TV remotes, a glass of amber liquid, a purse, a small plastic
bag, a blue-and-white container Dan identifies (2026-09-11) as **Suz's coke
holder, disguised as a cigarette pack**, and a black case he identifies as a
**jewelry scale**. Fran is alive, lucid, and sitting in her own armchair about
five feet away ([[wiki/people/fran-coldren]]). Dan's own point in supplying it
was that the paraphernalia was **out on the table, not hidden**. For this
journey the frame does something else: the source node and the drain are
photographed in the same room, in the same year the estate that funds the drain
is four months from opening.

## Stop 4 — The largest drain

The decade's biggest documented capital flow is not rent, not drugs, and not
the market. It is [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]].

Across Cash App and Venmo, Dan sent on the order of **$139K** and received back
**$16–20K** — a net outflow of roughly **$119K–$123K**, approximately the size
of the entire estate distribution ([[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]];
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]]). The Cash App profile totals
$139,184.96; the account dashboard's all-contacts figure is $138,249.40 sent
against $20,243.37 received across 807 transactions, which means Annie was the
dominant destination of the whole outflow. Venmo adds $99.8K sent against
$15.8K received across 448 transactions
([[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]]).

The relationship's financial history is a three-rotation see-saw — Dan funds
the early NYC years, Dan services the 2023 landlord debt, Annie is sole earner
through 2024 after the involuntary job loss — but the see-saw's net is
six-to-one in her favour ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]]).

Two corrections keep this stop honest. **The middle rotation did not close.**
"Works through" implied the landlord debt got worked off; a 5 March 2025
message from the landlord rounds the balance to a final **$10,000 still owed**
after move-out, and nothing in the recovered record shows any of it paid
afterwards ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]]). **And the inbound side
has never been swept.** Annie held paid work through most of 2024 — personal
assistance and care for [[wiki/people/libby|Libby]], at a documented *"just
over 3 hours today.. she paid me 500"* (2024-05-17) — on top of a second
six-day-a-week job. The single-earner reading the spine invites is not what the
record supports for that year, and the same payment-app exports that establish
the outflow would show any Annie→Dan flow if anyone looked. Nobody has
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]]).

## Stop 5 — The metered drain

On the outbound side, the money becomes drugs. [[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network|The
supply network]] treats fifteen years of procurement not as scattered dealer
contacts but as **a single continuously-operating logistics architecture with
succession, redundancy, failure modes and a signature rupture pattern.** Since
January 2011 Dan has been on a daily Suboxone regimen, described in the primary
sources as a medical baseline rather than an episode — which makes procurement
a permanent institution rather than a habit.

Its governing finding is the mirror of the money spine's: **the architecture
persists because the want is not negotiable, so it cannot be left to one
supplier.** The nodes rotate; the architecture does not.

**What the log measures.** Read as a sequence rather than a list, the roster

model.** Menore, a professional stranger, ran at 99.3%. Johnny was reliable in

attachment — is the **least** reliable node in the record

licenses failure. The mechanism the network page adds is that enforcing a
standard on a supplier is an **audit**, and audits of chosen objects run late

## Stop 6 — The one attempt to cut the line out

There is exactly one documented occasion on which Dan tried to remove the
supply line from the route altogether, and it is not on any of the pages the
original journey visited.

Between **18 August and 1 November 2015**, at [[wiki/places/155-virginia-ave|155
Virginia Ave]], he grew cannabis in a closet. Fifteen EXIF-dated photographs
document it seedling to late flower: vegetative growth in terracotta pots under
Energetic-branded CFLs, a clamp lamp and a desk-lamp rig; early flower with
white pistils on 9 October; developed colas with ambering pistils on 28
October; a trichome macro on 1 November. GPS on thirteen of the fifteen frames
clusters at the Virginia Avenue fix. His own testimony, filed as his: zero
growing experience, grown in a closet with clamp lights and incandescent bulbs.
He named the strain himself — **"Dream Queen"**, his label, not a verified
strain ID ([[wiki/places/155-virginia-ave]]).

The frame reproduced in the **Sources** section below is the 4 October 2015
photograph: the tallest plant, the improvised lighting rig visible behind it. A
home setup, nothing professional, at least two plants.

**He never smoked it.** Per Dan's 2026-09-12 testimony,
[[wiki/people/casey-bondarenka|Casey]] took the uncured harvest and smoked it
all in one night during the Alexis-to-Annie household switch of late November
2015 ([[wiki/places/155-virginia-ave]]). The series of photographs ends in late
flower, four weeks before the switch; the harvest itself was never
photographed, because it never got the chance.

That is the journey's cleanest single irony and the reason this stop was added.
The one time the route was shortened — production moved in-house, the supplier
edge deleted — the product was consumed by somebody else before it reached the
man who grew it. The same address, in the same months, is where
[[wiki/people/zachariah-harshman|Zach Harshman]]'s handoffs happened (*"front
door is open, i'm in the kitchen"*) and where the earliest documented Uniontown
supply node operated. **The house that grew its own supply was also a
handoff location for someone else's.** Vertical integration did not replace
the network; it ran alongside it for three months and then failed in a way that
had nothing to do with horticulture.

## Stop 7 — The route's shape, corrected

The original version of this journey described a **chain**, and the wiki has
since corrected the metaphor.
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-graph-vs-chain|Supply graph vs. chain]] argues the
architecture is a **graph with per-product subgraphs**, not a single line with
a weakest link, and the difference is not academic — it changes what counts as
redundancy.

The Suboxone subgraph is the one with the catastrophic failure mode, because it
is the only subgraph whose interruption produces a physiological cascade. Every
other product can fail and the operator has a bad week; this one fails and he
cannot work a shift. Its historical depth ran Johnny ($15/8mg, $20/12mg),
Teddy, Zaco and Jay Lauer, with a prescriber route somewhere behind them. The
cocaine subgraph is separately edged — Tom, Johnny, the minor layer, Suz, and
finally Dan himself as Annie's supplier — and its demand curve has a governor
the Suboxone curve does not: **the wallet**
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-graph-vs-chain]]).

The measurement that follows is the sharpest thing on the route. **The
2016–2019 cut set is the largest in the record** — four to five parallel
illicit edges, plus the licit prescriber route, plus the NYC professional edge.
The Johnny vacation panics are that era's load tests: primary-edge failures
producing real distress signals (*"I have no other person I can get z from.
Johnny leaving for the beach,"* August 2019) and **no withdrawal**, because
Teddy and the second dealer absorbed it. Five single-layer events, five
survivals ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-graph-vs-chain]]).

By May 2026 there were no such edges left. No licit attempt carried flow across
four days of escalating need. No parallel illicit edge answered. The second
contact and the workplace customer were not edges at all — they were attempts
to *create* edges under load, which is not how edges work. Tom, the supposed
failover, was the failure. **The graph lost roughly two-thirds of its edges in
seven years without the demand changing at all**, and the cut produced
withdrawal, the withdrawal produced an impaired shift, and the impaired shift
produced a termination ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-graph-vs-chain]];
[[wiki/work/bfs-foods]]).

Sixteen years, one cut, one job lost to it.

## Stop 8 — The product

[[wiki/health/cocaine]] names itself directly as *"the primary product moving
through the supply network across its entire documented history"* — from the
2015 family-internal provision through the 2018 deep cycle to the 2026 daily
house calls. The cocaine page carries the demand side; the network page carries
the route; together they close the loop the estate money opened.

The dosage arc is the place the two halves of this journey touch numerically,
and it is the reason the route is one route rather than two:

| ~2006–2016 (baseline) | ~1 g/day | Pre-inheritance constraint |
| 2017–2020 (peak) | **3.5–7 g/day** | Fran's inheritance removed the capital constraint |
| 2020–present | **~0.5–1 g/day** | Inheritance exhausted |

The page's own reading of that curve is blunt: the escalation shows the desired
state is **maximum intensity limited only by supply and money, not by any
internal ceiling**, and the later reduction is *"a resource adjustment, not a
step toward moderation or recovery"* ([[wiki/health/cocaine]]).

**And the record ran the outcome test itself.** Four days after Fran's funeral,
on 2018-04-08, Dan states the hypothesis: *"everything is going to get better
now… we have options for what to do and enough money to have a real chance at
being **middle-class happy**."* The inheritance arrives on schedule against
that claim. Over the same 2018–2020 window his first-person happiness-claim
rate runs **0.87, 0.34, 0.41 per 1,000 sent messages**, against **7.86** in
late 2015 — among the lowest sustained rates anywhere in the corpus, arriving
exactly when the hypothesis predicted the opposite
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]]; [[wiki/health/cocaine]]). The
identical capital that failed to produce the stated outcome is the capital that
drove the dosage from 1 g/day to 3.5–7. **It reached him in full and changed
the wrong variable.**

One late addition keeps the arc honest about its own evidence. Every quantity
in the table above is recollection. On the night of **2026-08-30** the intake
ledger recorded a cocaine unit end to end for the first time: **0.75 g**,
received 17:04, closed 02:35, six intake events, median dose 0.1 g, reconciled
balanced. It lands inside the retrospective band and therefore does not
contradict it — *"and that is the whole of it."* `n = 1`
([[wiki/health/cocaine]]).

## Stop 9 — Where the two roles fuse

[[wiki/people/tom|Tom Maison]]'s own page states his dual function in one
sentence: he functions *"simultaneously as anchor and supply line."* He is
Dan's clearest documented case of safe, lateral, non-paternal attachment — and
the strip source who supplied Suboxone, mushrooms, cocaine and klonopin for
most of the record, becoming the sole strip source by 2025
([[wiki/people/tom]]).

The collapse ran from March to May 2026 and it is a supply failure first and a
relationship event second. Handoff delays compound through April. The pattern
reaches its breaking point on **15–16 May**, when Tom promises to bring subs
after weed-whacking and goes silent for hours while Dan escalates from *"Tom /
What the uck"* to *"Fuck you dude"* — facing a real consequence, calling off
work with termination risk. By **18 May** Dan has blocked him, unblocked him to
set an ultimatum, and pivoted the same day to a straight financial demand:
*"How long will it take you to get the $36 you owe me."* The thread goes quiet
for eleven days, resumes on **29 May** with an accusation of running *"a $30
come up,"* and ends on **30 May** with Dan threatening to route the dispute
through Tom's father ([[wiki/people/tom]]).

**The pattern is twelve years old, not new.** In August–September 2014 Tom ran
the identical sequence, and Dan's own 2014 diagnosis names the mechanism:
*"remember how you stopped giving a fuck that you owed me when you didn't need
me to buy your shit anymore?"* Then, on 15 September 2014, straight back to him
— *"yo looking to purchase sub/tex whatevz"* — the channel reopening the moment
the product was needed again ([[wiki/people/tom]]).

The load-bearing revision is Dan's own, and it is what finally names what
"safe" was carrying. Asked what bothered him most about the 2026 failure, he
answered that it was not the money: *"the thing that bothers me the most is not
money but that he would be okay letting me go sick."* And in August 2026, in a
session mined for the Annie page: *"i have LITERALLY noone now, even tom isnt
talking to me since he beat me for 35 dollars for a sub he never brought"*
([[wiki/people/tom]]).

That is the whole route in one person — which is why this journey ends here
rather than on a page about money or a page about drugs alone. The two roles
that this walk has been tracing separately are, at the end, held by the same
man, and the friendship could not survive the second role failing.

## What would falsify this

If a future ingest found a capital arrival in this record that **did**
accumulate — sat in an account, appreciated, was never drawn down against a
standing drain — that would be a counter-instance the supply-line reading would
have to absorb. None is currently on any page in this route.

The nearest thing to a candidate is the 2020–21 market era, and it does not
qualify: the Robinhood peak of ~$25k was a stake drawn *from* the lump and was
gone by early 2021, with no exit accounting anywhere in the record
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]]). A second candidate would be the
337 Saratoga proceeds, and the spine's own forward claim is that they will
behave like every prior lump — with the added differences that this lump is not
Dan's and most of it is not his mother's either, since the sale was the remedy
in her Chapter 13.

A different and more interesting falsifier: **if the inbound side of the Annie
flow turns out to be materially larger than the screenshots show**, the
six-to-one asymmetry shrinks and the "largest drain" framing weakens. That
check is cheap, has never been run, and is named as a gap on two pages already.

## Coverage limits

**This journey adds no new figure.** Every number on it is carried from a page
another pass wrote, and the additions here are route, arithmetic and
juxtaposition. Where a figure is contested on its home page, it is contested
here.

**The payment-app totals are operator-provided screenshots**, not exports in
`raw/` — flagged as a gap on both [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]] and
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]]. The $119K–$123K figure is the least
independently checkable number in the arithmetic table above, and it is also
the one the route leans on hardest.

**Six months have endpoints and no ledger.** September 2020 to early 2021 — the
window in which $134,069.31 became "the money just ran out" — cannot be
decomposed into rent, supply, Annie and market from current sources. The route
asserts that it drained; it cannot say in what proportions.

**Most of the supply-side dollar figures do not exist.** The network log above
is a roster of names, eras and roles. Apart from a handful of quoted prices
(Johnny's $15/$20 strips, Zaco's $50–$75 for three, Bill's 14 units for ~$95,
the 2026 daily $50–$100) there is no cumulative spend figure for procurement
anywhere in the wiki. **The drain is documented as an architecture, not as a
sum**, which means the comparison between the estate distribution and the
supply line is a comparison between a measured quantity and an unmeasured one.

**Two nodes are unresolved.** "Tim" is named in the succession between Johnny
and Tom with no thread identified and no era confirmed. "Bop" is the current
primary node and his real name, his relationship to the Tom node he appears to
have succeeded, and the terms of the weed-whacking arrangement are all unknown
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]]).

**The Suboxone topology is contested.** The network page treats Tom as the
standing strip route; June 2025 messages describe a **prescriber** at the head
of the route with Tom as failover — *"my doctor said the pharmacy won't fill an
out of state prescription, so i'm going to need your referral service"* — which
would make May 2026 a second-line failure rather than a single point of failure
giving way. Held open rather than resolved
([[wiki/health/the-configured-body]]; [[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]]).

**One claim here is inference, and it is marked as such on its home page.** The
market era opening within weeks of the estate cheque is a temporal join the
corpus supports but never states; the inheritance as the stake behind the FSLY
position is flagged as inference on [[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]]
and is carried at the same strength here.

**What is documented vs. what is inferred, in one line each.** Documented: the
distribution amount and dates, the advance schedule, the Cash App installation
date, the itemised $14,000 statement, the node roster, the dosage arc's
endpoints as self-report, the 2026-08-30 measured unit, the Tom collapse
sequence, the grow's EXIF chronology. Inferred: that the market stake came from
the inheritance, that the Whyel fortune is the money's true origin (the first
husband's identity remains open), that the six-month burn ran through the
drains this page names in the proportions it implies, and that the 2015 grow
was an attempt at supply independence rather than a hobby — Dan's testimony
describes the grow, not the motive.
