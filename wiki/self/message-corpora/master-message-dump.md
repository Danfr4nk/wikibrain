# Master Message Corpora (iMessage Dumps)

The message corpus is the largest single body of evidence in this wiki: the complete text-message record against which every behavioral claim is checked. It holds 192,140 messages across 577 threads and 498 counterparty handles, spanning 2011-03-19 to 2026-09-07 in the authoritative snapshot — 99,360 sent, 92,780 received — with a living master file extending the run from 2010-01-09 at the head to 2026-09-21 at the tail (198,354 messages total as of 2026-09-23).

For most of the wiki's life the message evidence was not one corpus but dozens of fragments: per-contact CSV exports — the master dump, the archaic dump, Annie-specific logs, Kristin-specific exports — each accurate about the thread it covered and silent about everything it left out. The corpus policy of 2026-09-08 retired them as a class: in a fragment, absence of evidence looks exactly like evidence of absence, and a partial export never announces what it left out.

The corpus is where the wiki's measurements come from: burst cadence and the era-by-era growth of message length, contact-Gini concentration, circadian rhythm and reply latency, the attachment sequences that underpin the Annie synthesis. It is also where the wiki's limits are measured: 3,086 messages that cannot be placed in any thread, a 2021–2022 window the database nearly lost, and handles that answer *who* while saying nothing about who was actually typing.

> **Data exhibit** — The master iMessage corpus report: the message dumps and their dimensions, contact distribution, and structural notes. Working material; the conclusions live in the pages that cite it.

## The authoritative corpus

The complete Messages export is the current tier-one evidence: 192,140 rows staged from a full Apple Messages (chat.db) export via a Google Sheet (48,004,305 bytes, sha256 `2c53c54007534dfdd1786c0ebde0cd84c9e49483a8f5d1237ec436eef9d98cbb`), carrying 47 columns — message_id, date_sent, date_read, date_delivered, sender, is_from_me, text, subject, service, chat_identifier, chat_display_name, and the rest of the chat.db schema (`raw/imessage/manifest.json`, recorded 2026-09-08). The corpus spans 2011-03-19 to 2026-09-07: 577 threads, of which 525 are one-to-one and 52 are group threads, across 498 distinct counterparty handles. Direction breaks down as 99,360 sent and 92,780 received; 185,338 messages carry text and 8,120 carry an attachment (`kb/data/0001`, `kb/data/0002`).

Two structural limits are quantified in the record rather than assumed away. 3,086 messages — 1.6% of the corpus — carry no counterparty field of any kind: sent messages with no `chat_identifier` and no `destination_caller_id`. 5.3% of rows arrive with no `chat_identifier` at all; most are recoverable — a received message names its sender, some sent messages carry `destination_caller_id` — but the 1.6% is the irreducible remainder. The corpus deliberately does not assign them by interleaving with surrounding messages: per-thread totals are floors rather than exact counts, which the record treats as a smaller cost than a number that looks exact and isn't (`kb/data/0003`).

The corpus is re-pullable byte-exact from the backing sheet. A 2026-09-09 re-download matched the committed manifest exactly — 48,004,305 bytes, the same sha256, 192,140 rows — with no credentials required: the sheet is shared "anyone with the link", the operator was told, and chose to leave the sharing as it is, recorded as a decision so it is not later "fixed" by someone assuming it was an oversight (`kb/data/0008`, `kb/data/0005`). The consequence is that the message record does not depend on any single machine.

## The 2010 head — the Gmail SMS backfill

A 317-row Gmail SMS archive pushes the corpus head back fourteen months before the iMessage record starts: 2010-01-09 → 2010-08-15, threads labeled "SMS with Dan", 129 messages from Dan and 188 from Suzanne, byte-exact at `raw/gmail-sms-suz-dan-2010/dan-sms-full-transcript.csv` (sha256 recorded in the manifest). The file also holds two non-SMS stragglers kept with the batch rather than split: Thread 4 (AIM, 8 messages) and Thread 6 (anonymous, 1 message).

Per the manifest, no 2010 Suz↔Dan SMS exists in any other corpus: Dan's iMessage export starts 2011-03-19 and Suz's iMessage archive starts 2013-11-18, and verbatim-phrase probes of five distinctive phrases returned zero hits in both — all 317 rows are new material. The backfill sits at the head of the living master file as DAN-SMS-0001 through DAN-SMS-0317, whose totals are 198,354 messages from 2010-01-09 17:05:38 to 2026-09-21 3:39:29 (`raw/imessage/summary.json`, 2026-09-23). The strongest corroborations noted on ingestion: Pro Tools certification dates (Jan 11/18/26 2010), the pill-abstinence 6-month mark dated 2010-08-14 (pinning the start around 2010-02-15, consistent with the Suboxone start of 2010-02-17), and the Jan 31, 2010 vicodin request 17 days before that start.

The largest holds **62,814 messages** (31,635 sent, 31,179 received; 1,686 with attachments) across 2015-11-28 to 2019-07-23 — the PA 724 handle of the Annie decade, the same window the per-contact export `imessage_7244346811_both_all_now.csv` measures at 62,819 rows. The second holds **31,433 messages** (17,936 sent, 13,497 received) from 2023-01-01 to 2026-09-07 — the NYC 212 handle; the two are consistent with context-core's count of 97,768 unique messages across Annie's four handles.

Third: **20,015 messages**, 2025-09-01 to 2025-12-11 — the Kristin thread (the per-contact export counts 20,009). Fourth: **9,469 messages**, 2018-12-26 to 2020-09-13. The largest group thread holds 576 messages (297 sent, 279 received), 2026-06-01 to 2026-08-19. Group threads overall total 882 messages across 52 threads — a structure per-contact exports cannot represent at all, because a group conversation is not any one participant's thread (`kb/data/0002`).

## The fragment era — what the corpus replaced

Everything this page measured before 2026-09-08 was per-counterparty fragments: exports of the form `imessage_export_<handle>_<timestamp>` and `imessage_<handle>_both_all_now`, each accurate about the thread it covered. The inventory includes the master dump at 175,358 rows (2011-03-18 → 2026-03-25 — but no handle column, so it cannot attribute), the archaic dump at 124,177 rows, and `interspersed_messages.csv` at 153,166 rows with direction values of "me"/"them" (all per `wiki/self/message-corpora/source-coverage-index`).

The corpus policy (`CORPUS_POLICY.md`, 2026-09-08) retired the fragments as a class. The failure mode is not falsehood but **silent partiality**: "in a fragment, absence of evidence looks exactly like evidence of absence," and a partial export never announces what it left out. The complete corpus measures what the fragments were missing: 498 distinct counterparties, 52 group threads, and a 15-year span in which the same person appears under several handles — a phone number, an iCloud address, a `@vzwpix.com` gateway — where a single-handle extract shows a relationship stopping dead where it only changed channel. Shelved material may now be used for exactly one thing: establishing what was previously believed, and showing why it was wrong. A shelved extract is evidence *about the reasoning*, never evidence *about the past*.

The source-coverage index (generated 2026-08-14 by `bin/source-index scan`) is the machine-generated ledger of the 52 message sources: 1,786,124 total rows against roughly 187,000 unique messages — the archive is about 9.6x duplicated, deliberate and load-bearing, because every correction the repo has made was found by one export contradicting another. Eighteen sources carry filenames that overstate their coverage (`_all_now` / `_all_time` as a class is unreliable — the 2026-08-11 Rick correction was caused by trusting one). Twenty-two of the 52 indexed sources have no handle column and cannot answer an attribution question. Four indexed sources are header-only and empty while remaining cited (see Conflicts in the record, 2026-08-14).

The ceiling matters more than the row counts. `all_imessages_complete_dump.txt` — 223,070 rows, the one dump whose `direction` field is trustworthy and the default for `bin/mine-messages` — runs 2011-03-18 → 2025-08-10, so a query about a post-August-2025 thread returns zero matches rather than an error. The Kristin page is the worked case: a 22,018-message relationship that returned silence for two months. 41.8% of the dump's records have no row in the 2026-08-13 deep export, which makes it the better source for any window in 2019–2025 for presence and volume — never for authorship.

## What the corpus cannot see

**2021–2022 is the place where absence-of-evidence reasoning is least safe.** The corpus holds 282 messages in 2021 and none in 2022, and nothing in it can distinguish a life that went quiet from a database that lost rows. The Facebook archive answers that directly. Comparing month by month, 403 recovered Facebook threads (15,923 searchable messages of 16,238 in the archive, 2007-01 → 2022-09) hold **1,304 messages in 17 calendar months for which the iMessage corpus holds exactly zero**: 2021-05 through 2022-01, and 2022-03 through 2022-09. June 2021 alone holds 190 against a corpus count of zero; July 2022 holds 500. Four months in the window (2022-02, 2022-10, 2022-11, 2022-12) are empty in both. The distribution is the shape of a real life rather than of a uniform export artifact — two busy months and single digits elsewhere — and the corpus median month is 1,123 messages against a Facebook median of 32, so these are not comparable instruments. The gap is narrowed, not closed: whatever was happening in mid-2022, it was not nothing, and 500 messages in one month is strong evidence against the quiet-life reading (`kb/data/0035`).

**A handle is not a person.** Every row in the coverage index answers *which handle* and *what window*. None of them answers *who was typing* — and on 2026-08-20 that stopped being a theoretical distinction. At least six inbound rows attributed to the NYC 212 handle were produced by Jerel Coles on Annie's phone across three separate episodes: 2026-07-26 05:39–05:57, 2026-08-16 23:42–23:53, and 2026-08-18 21:46–21:50 — including an accusation of sexual exploitation against Dan that a naive read files as Annie's own testimony. All three episodes occur during crises, which is the worst possible distribution: the handle is least reliable exactly where the corpus's highest-stakes claims are drawn from. The standing preflight rule adds a fourth question to the three the index already asks of a source — not just *what window*, *what handles*, *what columns*, but **who else had physical access to the device in this window**. For an iMessage corpus that question has no column and can only be answered from content; the three episodes above were each identifiable from register alone, and whether there are others earlier in the record has not been checked (`wiki/self/message-corpora/source-coverage-index`; full account at `wiki/timeline/events/august-2026-morgantown-call`).

Two further instrument cautions are on the record. The direction-field inversion — the "sic semper lupanis" misattribution — was corrected 2026-08-13. And timezone is recorded from documented convention rather than measured, except for the two 2026-08-13 exports, which were validated against a local-time source on 42,895 text-matched pairs; every other timezone entry in the index is an inherited assumption, and a source silently exported in UTC would not be caught.
## Analytical cuts built on this corpus

The corpus is not just quoted — it is computed on. The circadian/latency synthesis (`wiki/mind/synthesis/message-circadian-latency`) is a primary analytical cut recomputed row-level: circadian rhythm, reply latency, and per-contact volume trajectories, with the direction-field bug sidestepped via the Leviathan sender field. The texting-deviance audit (`wiki/mind/profile/texting-deviance-audit`) is the turn-level structural cut, computed from the only sender-tagged file reaching past 2025 — and the pass that retires this page's original "~8.36 words/message" Voice Patterns line as a 2015–19 baseline against a 2026 figure of 15.03 (see Conflicts in the record, 2026-08-23). Contact Gini is computed off the handle-concentration tables below; the calibrated-confidence campaign's first finding is built from this corpus; the forensic-method and attachment-trauma-bond syntheses cite it as their primary evidence.

The newest verification pass is the Aug–Sep 2026 slice: 5,905 rows (`date_sent` 2026-08-11 00:00:00 → 2026-09-07 21:30:56) of the agent-edition corpus snapshot, filed at `raw/self/message-csv/aug-sep-2026-imessage-export/` so the figures behind the 2026-09-11 Aug-26 block retraction stay archived and re-checkable. ## Voice Patterns (Stylometric from CSVs + operating_manual + core)
- Burst cadence and per-era message length: short phrasal messages, 3-7 discrete bursts per exchange; per-era word counts are measured in the texting-deviance audit — see Conflicts in the record, 2026-08-23, for the correction that retired the original "~8.36 words/message" line as a 2015–19 baseline against a 2026 figure of 15.03.
- Lowercase dominant (80%+); ALL-CAPS for emphasis peaks (9k+ instances noted core).
- Ellipsis `...` as mid-thought breath; no terminal periods often; run-on with commas/ellipses.
- Lexical diversity high: 23k+ unique words.
- From operating_manual and slices: "literally", "like", profanity as intensifier, "lol"/"lmao", informal slang ("idk", "gonna", "ya"); code-switch to fuller sentences with family.
- Specific thread markers: rapid multi-message clusters in emotional (Annie fights, 2025 collapse); tender/affectionate in early/Feb 2025.
- FB cross: Similar informal voice in Facebook messages threads (Annie primary documented).

## Assessment

The message corpus is the wiki's load-bearing evidence layer: the single largest surface of behavioral data, and the instrument every synthesis cites. The 2026-09-08 snapshot converted it from a pile of per-contact fragments into a measured instrument — with the failure modes (direction bugs, silent partiality, handle-vs-person, dark windows) now named and indexed rather than lurking.

What the corpus cannot do is also on the record. SMS is a near-zero-introspection medium for everyone in it, so the spine's claim that all behavioral data defers to this corpus has a jurisdiction, and the psychological layer sits outside it. 1.6% of messages cannot be placed in any thread, and every per-thread total is a floor rather than an exact count. The 2021–2022 window reads as a database artifact, not a life that went quiet. And the handle is least reliable exactly where the stakes are highest — the Coles episodes are the proof that a handle answers *who* but not *who was typing*.
