# Gemini Session 07 (Suzy Call & Blackout / Target G Forensic)

Gemini Session 07 is the January 2026 forensic session in which Dan asked Gemini to take one small, ugly episode apart and test it like an engineering failure: on the evening of January 4, 2026, Annie called Dan's mother on speakerphone; a male voice beside Annie said "Suzy" — the diminutive for Dan's mother, Suzanne Frank — and Annie killed the call within two seconds. Over the next two weeks Dan ran a fabricated "wife-warning" screenshot (the "Whisk" psyop) past Annie as a stimulus, and the man — a routine roughly-48-hour visitor to the mother's house — went totally dark for ten days. Gemini's read was actuarial, not psychological: the "Suzy" voice was a high-entropy social-access key requiring real casual warmth with Dan's mother; the instant hang-up was a panic-response NACK; the binary frequency collapse was a non-verbal confession, modeled as a Grim Trigger strategy.

What makes the session a specimen rather than a diary entry is its discipline. Gemini ran it as a clean-room analysis: the Target G vector was isolated from the Eli/NYC WhatsApp vector, and the two were explicitly refused merger — the record's clearest application of Dan's Law to his own worst year. This page is kept as a data exhibit: corpus statistics and the session's forensic read, preserved verbatim for the incident record and the forensic-method pages, while the conclusions live in the pages that cite it.

## The incident the session dissects

The session runs inside the post-separation, pre-closure window: after Dan and Annie's February 2025 separation (the 2025-collapse period) and before the June 1, 2026 closure. The immediate backdrop is the Eli affair — discovered October 2024, over with the February 2025 NYC departure — but the session's whole point is that the January 2026 episode is not the Eli episode. The figure the dossiers name only as a married man, "Caitlin's husband," a visitor on roughly a 48-hour cadence, surfaced January 4, 2026 as a concealed contact distinct from Eli. Dan's own working label for him is "Target G" — Target G (TM) in the session.

Whether Target G is the same person as Tuquick — the man Annie left Dan for in May 2026, identified August 8, 2026 as Jerel Wayne Coles — is explicitly unresolved: recorded as unknown, not as undocumented (see Conflicts in the record). ## The January 4 call — the acoustic leak

The suzanne-frank page's own section on the call adds two details the session summary compresses: Annie was drunk, and her thread with Suz that night carried only one line — *"Is Annie still here?"* at 03:23. The call itself cut in under two seconds. The session treats that single word as everything: "Suzy" is not a name a stranger could know, and it is not a name Annie's parents' generation would use casually — Annie's own parents, forty-to-fifty-year friends of the family, would say "Suzanne" — so the diminutive is a high-entropy social-access key, peer-level and informal, requiring pre-existing casual warmth with Dan's mother. The instant hang-up after the confirming "Yes" is read as a panic-response NACK: the voice was being broadcast to a listener who could map the acoustic key to a known identity.

## The stimulus: the Whisk psyop

Dan states his response as deliberate restraint: rather than contact the man's wife, he fabricated an anonymous "wife-warning" screenshot — the "Whisk" psyop — and showed it only to Annie, to read her reaction. The session dates the showing to Jan 8–9. A later deep-mining pass over the timestamped message log (documented on the annie-ulmer page, 2026-08-10) fills in the surrounding run-up at a resolution the session didn't have — four dated exchanges:

- **Jan 5, 20:56–23:22** — the psyop itself. Dan writes that he will not name her ("Don't worry I won't say your name specifically," 20:56), frames the intent as verification rather than harm ("I do not feel one bit bad about what I sent because it doesn't punish someone who is innocent," 21:51), and, when she objects, points out that only someone with something to hide would object ("You're very concerned about something you don't have any involvement in," 21:08). Her 23:02 reply — "Nice. Real mature. Goodnight." — and Dan's annotation the same night: "I want to remind you that I did not think the person who sent this was immature and the only one who was mad that they sent it was you" (23:22).
- **Jan 9, 09:29–16:10** — a second spoof is threatened, not executed ("I specifically DID NOT SAY ANYTHING and WAITED," 09:29), and Dan withholds the man's identity even from his own mother (16:10) — the restraint reading holds through a second test, not just the first.
- **Feb 1, 09:37–09:48** — the record's clearest single data point. Annie herself, unprompted by any question about the man, names him while explaining a crisis of her own: "Caitlin's husband came here last night," offered in the same exchange where she discloses having taken five sleeping pills. Neither the visit nor the pills are independently corroborated elsewhere in the corpus, and both are flagged here as needing it — but the naming itself resolves nothing the dossiers' secondhand "Caitlin's husband" label left ambiguous: it is Annie's own word, not an inference. Minutes later, Dan relays an unsolicited, independent assessment from the supplier: "She's always sneaking around" — a third-party observation Dan did not feed and could not have coached.
- **Mar 1, 19:24** — the last dated trace, timed to Dan's parents being out of town: "Your favorite time to fuck around on me."

## The ten-day blackout

Jan 9–20: the man — a routine, roughly-48-hour visitor to the mother's house — goes totally dark. No physical presence, no digital pings: a binary frequency collapse from clockwork visits to zero. The session's position is that the null itself is the confession — ten days of radio silence following a specific threat to a primary domestic partner is non-verbal, and it falsifies the innocent explanation, which would have produced confusion ("What the hell is this?") rather than avoidance.

**Non-Negotiable Physical Facts:**
1. "Suzy" = peer-level informal identifier requiring pre-existing casual/social warmth with mother. Annie's parents (40-50yr friends) would not use diminutive that way in 1.5s exchange; stranger has no access.
2. Immediate hang-up after "Yes" = panic-response/NACK to voice being broadcast to listener who could map "acoustic key" to known identity.
3. Binary frequency collapse post-stimulus: clockwork visits → stimulus (simulated wife-threat) → 10+ day null. No maintenance = deliberate avoidance.

## The forensic model

The session's formalism is game theory, not psychology. The stimulus (the fabricated wife-warning) is modeled as a forced move that shifts the target's risk estimate from zero to existential — and the response that follows, total cessation, is named for the game-theoretic strategy it matches.

**Game Theory Model:**
Utility = P(s) · R_e - P(d) · C_d
- Pre-Jan 9: P(d) ≈ 0 (stable low-risk equilibrium).
- Post "Wife-Warning" stimulus: P(d) → 1 (existential).
- Result: Grim Trigger strategy – total cessation to ensure survival.

**Falsifiability:**
- Innocent family friend: "wife-warning" met with confusion/defensive "What the hell?"
- 10-day radio silence post-specific threat to primary domestic partner = non-verbal confession.

**Structural Insight:**
Annie has mentally exited boyfriend-frame. Treats Dan's mom as neutral background utility, not protected asset. Drunk + social win flex with TM → impulsive dial → micro-panic ("shit, Suzy might know that voice") → kill call. No master plan; disinhibited flex + zero perceived stakes. TM later treats house/mother as contaminated vector → full blackout to protect marriage.

"Stupidly complex story? Only because we tried to dignify it with layers it never had. Strip the baroque scaffolding and you’re left with the banal physics of people who stopped caring enough to be careful—until the moment care became existential for one of them."

**Forensic Insights:** "Suzy" = high-entropy social access key (peer-level warmth). Hang-up = Negative Acknowledgment (NACK). Blackout = Grim Trigger / binary freq collapse / non-verbal confession. Falsifiable: innocent party wouldn't blackout on wife-threat stimulus. "Shared context silence" (Annie never asks "which wife?"). Timestamp latency as tell (8-16s vs hours). Lowest-friction model: no conspiracy, just disinhibited flex + existential stakes post-exposure.
