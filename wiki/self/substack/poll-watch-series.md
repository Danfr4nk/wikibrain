> **Incidental entry** — Dan's two-installment polling roundup on the Substack: aggregates in, conclusions out. Complete at this size.

POLL WATCH was the data arm of *Committee to Re-elect the President*: weekly polling aggregates presented with open contempt for early polling's predictive value. Two installments, then dead.

Key facts:
- #1 (2023-05-26): RCP's 88 general-election polls had Trump +1.4 over Biden; 97 GOP primary polls had Trump 53.9%, +32.6 over DeSantis (21.3%). 538's aggregate: Trump +33.3. PredictIt: Trump 58¢ vs DeSantis 32¢ for the nomination. Quinnipiac had Biden 48 / Trump 46; Marquette had Trump +5.
- #2 (2023-06-03): "Week 2 of this series blesses us with more absolutely useless polling results. But we must beat on — boats against the current, born back ceaselessly into the past." Trump +1.8 over Biden in RCP; DeSantis still −30 back.
- Method on display: RCP + 538 aggregates plus betting markets, with explicit caveats about early-poll uselessness and PredictIt's capital-reallocation mechanics.
- The series ran twice and never returned — the May–June 2023 burst ended and the Substack went quiet until October.

Why incidental: two posts, fully covered here. Promote to major if the series is ever revived or if his polling methodology becomes its own subject.
