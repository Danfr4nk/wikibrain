# Self Overview

Daniel Gillingham Frank — Dan — was born November 1, 1988 in Uniontown,
Fayette County, Pennsylvania, and at 37 he is in the aftermath half of
2026: the childhood home at 337 Saratoga Drive sold ~June 2026, the
current landing occupied but unleased, the decade-long
relationship with [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie Ulmer]] ruptured June 1
and then failed to stay closed. He is a music producer — the aliases run
SLOPPP to MOGZART to GRIPNOTIC, and the MOGZART revival of March 2026 is
the first shipped music of a new era — and a self-taught AI-systems
builder whose multi-agent pipeline work now fills the hours the restaurant
job used to. He is a democratic socialist, an atheist of Jewish paternal
descent, [[wiki/mind/profile/neurodivergence|self-identified autistic]],
sixteen-plus years opioid-stable on daily Suboxone since February 17,
2010, and the subject and author of one of the more complete
self-documentation projects a private person has ever run — of which this
wiki is the current instrument.

The hinge events are few and load-bearing. In November 2005, age 17: his
father entered rehab for cocaine, his mother's affair with the dealer
surfaced, and the marriage ended — the template ("a trusted figure
maintaining a concealed alternate reality") the psychological record
traces through everything after. In 2007 the opiate arc began and ran
five months from first use to addiction; Suboxone day-zero, February 17,
2010, has held without a relapse since. Full Sail's Recording Arts
program (2008–2010) made him a producer before anything else had a chance
to. Annie began Thanksgiving 2015 and ran, through the poverty floor and
the deep cycle and two New York chapters, to the June 1, 2026 group-chat
rupture — and then, in the record's most documented reversal, refused to
close.

This page is the narrative summary of the self domain. The section's directory — every self page, mapped — is [[wiki/self/index]].

## The self-documentation system

The connection this page preserves says the wiki is itself an artifact
of the person it documents — a seventeen-year habit of forensic
over-documentation, industrialized — and the scale backs it out: 217,573
message records in the direction-reliable dump (106,629 sent, 110,944
received, 503 handles), with the source-coverage index counting 52
sources and 1,786,124 rows against roughly 187,000 unique messages
([[wiki/self/message-corpora/source-coverage-index]]). The system runs
in layers: raw corpora; the context-core spine, the curated and
cross-checked read of the raw extraction that other pages defer to; the
profile cluster; the master timeline (3,249 events, 1796–2027, drawn
from 360 pages, generated mechanically); and the annie-record, the
hand-read chronology built by reading the two-sided corpus in order
rather than extracting from it — the method the mechanical timeline
cannot replace.

The honesty layer is structural, not decorative. The spine's own 2026-08-16
staleness audit caught it fourteen days behind its corpus and corrected
seven superseded claims in place rather than silently overwriting them —
including the load-bearing Tom/Kristin mislabel, where a person's corpus
weight had been overstated ~2.9× by absorbing somebody else's thread,
cited by name as an authority elsewhere. The wiki-brain page carries the
defect the audit could not fix: a handle is not a person — at least six
inbound rows on Annie's 212 handle across July–August 2026 were typed by
a third party holding her phone, in three separate episodes, all during
crises, the periods the corpus draws its highest-stakes claims from
([[wiki/self/concepts/wiki-brain]]). Counts are upper bounds. The
instrument-is-subject rule governs the whole stack: the most
authoritative source page is itself a curated, AI-assisted compilation,
so its dated events and counts are admissible on different terms from
its interpretive framings.

Limits are load-bearing for what this system can evidence. The 2021–2022
corpus near-silence remains uncharacterized, and no sustained non-crisis
baseline register exists anywhere in the record — the system measures
him at intensity and is silent about him at rest. That is a property of
the instrument, not of the man, and every claim on this page about
"how he is" should be read with the attention-not-interior boundary
attached: the instruments see what he attends to, not how he is
internally regulated.
