# Bruce Burish

Bruce Burish is a Laurel Highlands friend from high school — not a caddying-era
friend, as this page previously claimed — and a Fayette County contact (724
area code, confirmed via Google Contacts). His footprint in the corpus is one
dense, explicit five-day conversation in October 2018 and then nothing: the
occasion is Dan telling him that he and **Annie** are
starting a webcam show together. The thread is almost entirely Bruce's
enthusiastic, increasingly graphic reaction to that news, plus a throwaway
request for weed next time Dan is in town.

But Bruce's real significance in Dan's life predates the corpus by roughly a
decade. He is the person who got Dan busted for his biggest party — the only
party where Dan's Uniontown friends and his Seven Springs friends collided,
the only party he ever threw at the family condo.

Its value to the wiki is twofold: it is **the camming business described to an
outsider at the moment of launch**, in Dan's own words, to someone with no
stake in it — and it is the sole paper trail of a friendship whose one
documented act of consequence was a teenage party bust with a four-link
cascade back to Rick Frank.

## The Sunridge K2 party

The family condo was Unit K2 in The Villages at Sunridge, Seven Springs — a
ski-in, ski-out unit about 45 minutes from Uniontown (see
**seven springs**). At some point in the high-school years, Dan
threw a party there: roughly 30 people from Laurel Highlands drinking, and the
only time his Uniontown circle and his Seven Springs circle were ever in the
same room. It was also the only party he ever threw at the condo — and the
only one he needed.

Bruce had his mom arranged to come pick him up. He was sloshed. If that wasn't
obvious at first, 45 minutes in the car with him would have made the fact all
too apparent. And Bruce was giving a ride home to Ryan Basescu — whose parents
are friends with Rick.

The cascade Dan remembers, link by link:

1. Bruce gets himself busted — picked up drunk by his mom, forty-five minutes
   of drunken kid in the backseat.
2. The kid he was taking home gets busted too — Ryan Basescu, collateral of
   the same car ride.
3. Basescu's parents are friends with Rick — so the second busted kid's
   parents know Dan's father socially.
4. Basescu's parents tell Rick about the party → Dan busted.

Dan puts it in his own words: *"the only party where my Uniontown friends and
my 7springs friends collided"* — ended by a drunk car ride and a parental
friendship network.

## The webcam conversation

Bruce responds with immediate, sustained interest: how the couple arrived at
the idea, whether Dan's girlfriend had done cam work before — *"Annie did it
before we started dating and it actually works out really well to do it as a
couple"* is Dan's own line, quoted back approvingly — and pushes for a schedule
so he can watch. The register holds for most of two days: solicited detail
about the setup, joking self-comparison (*"I'm Ocho"*), and repeated
propositions to be included in a session in person when he is next in town in
mid-November.

Interspersed is ordinary friend traffic — asking whether Dan is working,
commentary on a book he had just finished, a passing mention of "K-holes" that
Bruce treats as a new and slightly alarming discovery. The thread ends
October 27 with no indication the November visit happened.
