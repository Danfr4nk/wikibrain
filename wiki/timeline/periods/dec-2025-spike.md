# December 2025 Spike

## Date range

2025-12-01 to 2025-12-31

## What the month is

December 2025 is the highest-volume month of the Dan–Annie correspondence
on record, and it happens six months before the relationship ends. That is
the whole point of the page, and it is the reason the month survives as a
period article rather than a line in a table: a relationship at its
communicative maximum in December and over on June 1 is not a story of
gradual mutual disengagement, and the numbers make that unavailable as a
reading.

It is also the month in which a second relationship ends from the other
side, in sixteen minutes, by a block Dan did not set — the only severance
declaration in the corpus that held. And it is the month a seven-week music
re-entry finishes building and immediately archives itself. Three things,
one December, and the old version of this page carried the first at
two-hundred-odd words and the other two not at all.

## The numbers, and the rare fact that they replicate

The headline figures came from `annie_all_time_logs.csv`, a terminal-phase
extract that is **not held** in this repository and is shelved under
`CORPUS_POLICY.md` in any case. They were then re-derived independently
against the held corpus, and they survived.

| Month | Page figure (Dan / Annie / total / ratio) | Held-corpus figure | Delta |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Dec 2025 | 2,391 / 2,266 / **4,657** / 1.06:1 | 2,376 / 2,244 / **4,620** / 1.059:1 | 37 rows, 0.8% |
| Jan 2026 | — / — / — / 1.31:1 | 1,705 / 1,313 / 3,018 / 1.30:1 | 0.01 |
| Feb 2026 | — / — / — / 1.92:1 | 1,542 / 794 / 2,336 / 1.94:1 | 0.02 |

The re-derivation was done by direct scan of `corpus/messages.csv`
(192,140 held records) on `chat_identifier='+12124702449'` — Annie's thread
per `dat:0358`/`dat:0450` — split by `is_from_me`, for the months 2025-12,
2026-01 and 2026-02 (`dat:0591-dec-2025-spike-annie-thread-held-replicated`,
`kb/events/2025-12-message-spike`). The older retired handle carries zero
rows in these months, so the spike is entirely on the current one.

This is the rare period page whose quantitative claims survive independent
re-derivation. The wiki's own README warns that a quoted string is worth
nothing until it has been looked up; here the numbers were looked up and
they held to within a percent.

**The asymmetry is in the held rows, not in the dossier's arithmetic.**
Dan: 2,376 → 1,705 → 1,542. Annie: 2,244 → 1,313 → 794. Her engagement
halves across two months while his holds roughly constant. The near-parity
spike followed by one-sided collapse is held evidence.

**Scope, stated so it is not over-read.** The December peak describes the
**Annie-thread subset**, not the whole corpus. `dat:0542` already filed the
whole-corpus 2025 figure (41,278, replicated at 41,203) and flagged that the
whole-corpus December shape is different. For a relationship-period page the
subset framing is the correct one, but the two are not interchangeable and
this page does not treat them as such.

**What does not replicate.** The interpretive superstructure — "holiday
crisis management," the concealed terminal-phase third-party relationship,
the two written denials (November 2025 and February 2026) — rests on the
LIFE calendar and the DanAnnie dossier, neither held here. The numbers are
confirmed; what the numbers *mean* remains the page's reading, and is
labelled as reading throughout.

## Where the month sits in the terminal phase

The spike is not the start of anything. It is the last high-volume phase of
a correspondence that had already been running hot since the summer. The
run-up is documented at [[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-2025-collapse]]: GPS
sharing removed in August 2025, the DUI stop on October 26, the crate build
beginning October 31, the first written denial of a third party in November.

November is also where the month's shape is set. On **2025-11-29 at
22:31:07** Dan wrote *"goodbye annie. i'm blocking you now indefinitely."*
On **2025-12-01 at 13:12:07**, thirty-eight hours later, the thread resumes
with *"it's over. and you really fucking hurt me…"*
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/severance-declaration-audit]], pair B7). The month
that produces the corpus's peak volume opens with a severance declaration
that had already failed.

That is the pattern the whole December sits inside. The audit's
collapse-boundary table records a second such pair three weeks later:
**2025-12-24 22:37:31** — *"I'm done with this I don't want to argue"* —
followed **24.1 hours later** by **2025-12-25 22:46:05**, *"I'm dead goodbye
goodnight."* Under a 24-hour episode rule those are two episodes; under a
48-hour rule they are one. The wiki has not settled which rule it uses, and
the choice moves the corpus-wide count.

And then January opens at 1.30:1
and February at 1.94:1. The reinvestment did not hold.

## The other severance, and the one that held

December 2025 contains the end of the only other relationship of the Annie
decade. [[wiki/people/kristin|Kristin]] and Dan ran from **August 29** to
**early November 2025** — the decade's one contested-slot episode
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/kristin-channel]], [[wiki/mind/synthesis/vacancy-rule]]).
It had already ended on **November 13** (*"Idk why you just up and
ghosted"* / *"beating my mom for $40"*). What December holds is the
reactivation attempt and its failure.

**December 7–9**: a month of near-silence breaks into a burst — the shortest
documented instance of the dormancy-not-exit shape by an order of
magnitude. On **December 9 at 06:51**, Dan recites the accumulated
grievance: *"you re-neg on us dating, start talking to your ex, put things
on pause, steal my moms $40 and wouldn't hang out ever."*

Twelve hours later it ends, start to finish, in sixteen minutes:

| Time | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| 18:40 | Kristin | *"Get off my shit I'll block you bitch"* |
| 18:42 | Dan | *"guess who has your IP address"* · *"guess who has your WPA2 password"* |
| 18:45 | Kristin | sends `complaint.ic3.gov` (FBI IC3) |
| 18:51 | Kristin | sends the DHS IPR Center referral form |
| 18:52–53 | Dan | *"'I'm CALLING THE POLICE ON YOU!'"* · *"lawyer first question : so did you block him and he used other numbers?"* |
| 18:54 | Kristin | *"I didn't give you my IP address. And trust me I already know the entire process"* |
| 18:55 | Kristin | *"All this is screenshotted. Blocking you now. Don't contact me again or an officer will be reaching out."* |
| 18:56 | Dan | *"mr. officer i live at 337 saratoga drive"* |

On **December 10** there is one outbound message into the block, and then
nothing.

The reason this matters beyond its own thread is what it demonstrates. The
corpus is full of severance declarations that do not hold — the
November 29 / December 1 pair above is one of dozens. Kristin's is the
exception, and [[wiki/mind/synthesis/kristin-channel]] names the mechanism:
*"Instruments work when the person who set them maintains them. She
maintained it."* She needed nothing from the channel, so it stayed shut.

The claim as originally written — *"no contact after December 10, 2025"* —
has since been narrowed, and the narrowing is on the record below.

## The crate, finishing and archiving itself

Inside the same thirty-one days, a seven-week music re-entry completes. The
sequence is dated to the track in
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/taste-as-filter-stack]]: 88 Spotify likes in seven
years, then 1,417 in twelve months, with **November 2025 (628 likes)** as
the singularity. The crate — 232 tracks, four waves, 91.3% pipeline,
DJ-functional — is built **October 31 → December 22, 2025**.

December is the taper: **58 likes** for the month, arriving in two dated
pulses — **December 9 (+16)** and **December 22 (+1)** — with the fourth and
final crate wave closing on the 22nd. Then, on **December 29 at 8:54 PM**,
an **Exportify session** (recovered from Chrome history) exports the crate
mid-build. The re-entry was being archived as data while it was still
happening — which [[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-2025-collapse]] calls the
hinge's archival signature and which is, on its face, the same instinct
that produced this wiki.

The December 9 like session and the December 9 Kristin endgame are the same
day. Nothing in the record connects them and this page does not connect
them either. It records that they share a date.

## Contradictions on the record

**The golf line's speaker.** `dat:0591` and `kb/events/2025-12-message-spike`
both verify the December 13 message as **Dan's**, outbound, single
held-corpus hit, quoted *"and you would just have to walk out your door i
wouldn't make you go out on the golf course like when used to when you
ACTUALLY liked me."* [[wiki/interests/golf]] attributes the same line to
**Annie**, addressed to Dan, with bracketed repair — *"you would just have
to walk out your door, i wouldn't make you go out on the golf course like
when [we] used to, when you ACTUALLY liked me."* One of the two is wrong
about who said it, and the held-corpus row is the stronger evidence. The
line is left on both pages with the conflict stated rather than one side
silently deleted. The wiki's own README names this exact failure mode — four
of six checked quotations sat with the wrong speaker or nowhere at all.

**4,657 versus 4,620.** The page's figure comes from an unheld terminal
extract; the held corpus gives 4,620. A 37-row, 0.8% gap. Both are recorded;
neither is retired, because the extract may hold rows the corpus lost to
deletion before export (`CORPUS_POLICY.md` notes that deleted messages are
absent from `chat.db` and therefore from the corpus).

**372 versus 371.** [[wiki/mind/synthesis/kristin-channel]] counts **372**
messages across December 7–9; [[wiki/people/kristin]]'s own timeline table
counts **371**. One row, two pages, unreconciled.

**"No contact after December 10, 2025."** Filed as the corpus's one clean
inbound severance, then **superseded 2026-09-12**: Kristin attempted contact
on **2026-08-26** (four Messenger messages and a call, into message
requests — a channel with no connected thread), unopened for seventeen days;
Dan re-entered on **2026-09-12**. What survives is the weaker, shorter
claim: *no observed two-way contact December 2025 – August 2026.* The
control-case framing is annotated, not asserted
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/block-unblock-loop]],
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/kristin-channel]]).

**The $40 dating.** The December 9 grievance list names the $40 as a
grievance; the kristin-channel page records that dating the **$40 as the
trigger** to December 9 was disproven — it is a November event, late October
in origin.

**The 24-hour rule.** Pair B1 (Dec 24 → Dec 25, 24.1h) is one of the two
sharpest cases in the audit. Two episodes or one, depending on a rule the
wiki has not fixed. The count of severance episodes corpus-wide moves with
it.

**Whole corpus versus Annie thread.** The 4,657/4,620 December peak is the
Annie-thread subset. The whole-corpus December shape is different, and
`dat:0542` says so. Any page quoting "the corpus peak month" without the
subset qualifier is overstating it.

## Limits of this record

- **The interpretive frame is unheld.** "Holiday crisis management," the
  concealed terminal-phase third-party relationship, and the two written
  denials all rest on the LIFE calendar and the DanAnnie dossier. Neither is
  in this repository. The volume numbers are confirmed; the story about what
  they meant is not.
- **Eighteen of thirty-one days have no record.** December 2–6, 11–12,
  14–20, 26, 28 and 30 carry no dated entry anywhere in the wiki. This is
  the single largest gap on the page and it is not closable from held
  material alone.
- **The PNC incident is dated to the month, not the day**, and its central
  question — whether Dan initiated the transfers — is unresolved by the
  record.
- **The car is a fragment.** *"car totaled Dec 8"* appears in one
  chronology row with no circumstances, no cost, and no second source.
- **The Valeria line sits outside the held exports.** Tagged `[RAW-CSV]`,
  and an exhaustive word-boundaried sweep of 216,101 held iMessage rows
  returned nothing for Valeria, Iglesias or Cid.
- **Single-source claims.** The 938-entry Gemini peak, the Exportify session
  timestamp, the 232-track crate and its 91.3% pipeline figure, the James
  thread's 194-message count, and the *"car totaled Dec 8"* row each rest on
  one source apiece.
- **The corpus's own known limits apply.** Deleted messages are absent;
  1.6% of corpus rows cannot be placed in a thread; timestamps are UTC on
  wiki pages and local in the corpus, a four-hour offset in summer and five
  in winter — which is why the December 13 line reads `2025-12-14 01:39:18`
  in one place and `Dec 13 20:39` in another. Both are the same message.
