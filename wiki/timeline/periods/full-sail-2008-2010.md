# Full Sail (Sep 2008 – Mar 2010)

Three years after the November 2005 family rupture and one year into the
opiate arc ([[wiki/timeline/periods/dark-era-2007-2008]]), Dan left
Uniontown for Winter Park, Florida — Full Sail University, Recording Arts.
This is the hinge period where the recreational/instrumental drug use
stabilizes into permanent chemical maintenance, where the first durable
public voice (Twitter) begins, and where the relationship line pivots
from Danielle to Alexis, the pairing that would eventually lead to [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]].

[![The drive south, 21 September 2008](wiki/media/dan-drive-to-florida-2008-09-21.jpg)](wiki/media/dan-drive-to-florida-2008-09-21.jpg)

*The drive south, 21 September 2008, 6:55am — "Moving to Florida. Leaving PA to live on my own for the first time." (Dan's caption, 2026-09-24.)*

An.S. in Recording Arts, graduated August 2009, top 5% of the class — the
formal credential behind the music-production identity that runs through
[[wiki/interests/music/overview]] (SLOPPP, MOGZART, and eventually
GRIPNOTIC all trace back to skills built here). He stayed in Florida
until **28 February 2010** after graduating, a five-month tail before
returning north. (An earlier version of this page said "through March
2010"; the tweet archive dates the departure to the day — see the
certification section below.)

## Twitter genesis

The @danfrank Twitter voice begins here — Pro Tools labs, hip-hop-producer
aspiration, Orlando nightlife, an in-network that already includes
@alexisarmel and @joshbrannan. One specific early post is preserved as a
marker of the period's ideological drift: an explicit "no longer consider
myself a republican" disavowal, the first documented step away from the
Uniontown household's Republican/talk-radio politics
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/political-psyops]] for where that trajectory ends
up).

## The relationship pivot: Danielle ends, Alexis begins

Dan's relationship with [[wiki/people/danielle-onesi]] ends during this
window in Orlando — by his own account, because he cheated. A later
bootloader session names the specific circumstance: a girl from
Baltimore, connected through Jack from the band All Time Low, with
Dan's own regret over the episode explicitly documented. [[wiki/people/alexis-armel]]
begins the same year (2009) and becomes the operative relationship
through the return to New York and beyond, ending only when Annie enters
in November 2015.

The relationship's beginning is a live reconstruction, and it is
recorded here the way he told it — in layers, with the seams showing.
One of those layers has since been superseded by his own archive, and
the supersession is recorded below with the same seams.

The deepest layer is **September 21, 2008** — not Twitter, not 2009.
On the afternoon he drove to Florida (the drive-to-Florida photo is
EXIF-stamped the same day), **Alexis messaged Dan on Facebook — she
opened, not him.** The notification emails are unambiguous: all three
that day read "Alexis sent you a message," and Facebook notifies only
the recipient, never the sender. Her opener, 13:50 ET: *"Highly doubt
you know who I am, but I randomly read your last note & I had to tell
you how kickass it was. Seriously lol. & the best luck w/ doing your
thing"* — a stranger writing in about the note. (This corrects the
reconstruction as first written, which had Dan opening the exchange —
same words, wrong speaker; `dat:farewell-note-2008-09-comments`.) The
note she called kickass was, in his words, an unhinged farewell blast
he had written naming people from high school — including
**Ryan Basescu** (spelling his; the corpus holds one Basescu, Joni, in
a 2022 Election Day exchange with his mother, placing the family in the
Uniontown scene). Two more messages from her that afternoon read as
replies inside a live back-and-forth on moving day — 15:35 ET: *"to say
the least. i've heard a lot about you, just kinda felt kind of
creeperish lol"*; 17:19 ET: *"I can only begin to imagine what info
you've received on ur end so YES lol. & honestly, nothing that's too
bad, really. Ur so damn lucky to get as far as possible away from this
place, I know that much."* She already knew of him, then: *"you don't
think I know who you are by this point? I mean like we have quite a few
connections, no?"* — *"i've heard a lot about you."* And she knew the
place he was blasting on his way out: *"Ur so damn lucky to get as far
as possible away from this place."* He kept the thread — on February
25, 2010 he forwarded the whole first exchange to himself under the
subject "First ever msg's between D & A <333," which is how Dan's side
of it survives. His memory had compressed fourteen months into "Twitter
months before Thanksgiving"; the corpus wins, by his own rule, and the
real story is better than the remembered one.

Those fourteen months — September 2008 to July 2009 — now have room
for the chapter he did remember. Somewhere in the gap, Alexis got back
with her longtime boyfriend, **Steve Kezmarsky**, the man Dan says got
her on opiates, and the early correspondence ended in an argument over
it. Kezmarsky is not a new name in the corpus — Danielle told Dan in
January 2014 that Alexis "used to steal when she was with Steve
kezmarsky so they cod keep doing their drugs," Dan joked in May 2016
about Alexis accidentally sending him pictures of her with Kezmarsky,
and in February 2018 he warned someone against "the classic Steve
Kezmarsky opportunity move of fucking/stealing my girl." The drug
connection is corroborated; the argument itself is his word alone.

The next layer is the July question, and he left it open the same
night: either the bedroom photo's date is wrong, or they met — hung
out — once in July. What is firm, in his words: "I know did not fuck
her until thanksgiving," and "it is possible we hung out and I didn't
get any play." If the July meeting happened, it was non-sexual —
"didnt fuck her bur she stayed or something." The photo may document
a first meeting that didn't take, or a date stamp that drifts; the
record keeps both possibilities and the one certainty.

What is dated is the July meeting. Dan met Alexis the night of
**2009-07-01** — or re-met her, if the earlier meeting is real; a
bedroom photo of the home-base house (bookshelf, dresser, guitar in
the corner, EXIF 2009-07-02 12:30) is, in his words, "the morning
after I met Alexis for the first time" — and twelve hours after that
shutter fired he was buying her plane ticket to live with him. Her
**first night in Florida** came five months later, **2009-12-02**
(EXIF 23:57, a cuddled selfie captioned in his own words). The ticket
and the move are separate events in his telling — the decision made in
twelve hours, the arrival in December — and both dates are his,
corroborated by the EXIF. He briefly disputed the December date the
same night ("Im positive it happened on thanksgiving"), then talked
himself through the reconstruction above; the EXIF stands per his own
corpus-beats-memory rule, and the dispute is recorded rather than
resolved. He then made a rare explicit exception to that rule for two
points: the move-down date and the ticket timing. His account: he came
home for Thanksgiving weekend, and she moved down about nine days
later — which lands within days of the EXIF-stamped 2009-12-02
first-night photo, close enough that both stand. The twelve-hour
ticket purchase after the July meeting and the December move are both
true in his telling; the ticket was the decision, December was the
arrival.

**A standing claim is superseded here, twice over.** The
[[wiki/people/alexis-armel]] page carries frontmatter claims dating
the meeting to Thanksgiving 2009, sourced to a 2026-08-27 operator
capture — and the reconstruction above as first written dated first
contact to Twitter months before Thanksgiving 2009, sourced to his
word that same night. The September 21, 2008 Facebook exchange,
forwarded by Dan himself on 2010-02-25, contradicts both: first contact
was fourteen months earlier than either claim. The alexis-armel page's
claims carry dated SUPERSEDED annotations (2026-09-24) and the page is
flagged for a full rewrite against this reconstruction.

The relationship's first two months have their own photo record.
**2009-12-24**, Christmas Eve morning, three weeks after her first
night in Florida: "Lexi pays rent" (his caption — pixelated derivative
on file, original never committed). New Year's Eve he was sick — a
hospital gurney photo stamped **2010-01-01 00:29**, sixteen minutes
into the new year, his caption "Dan sick on NYE" — and the sickness
has its own arc in his telling. They drove back up north for
Christmas, stayed too long doing pills, and on the drive back — Xanax
taken for the road, in his words — he got violently sick an hour from
home and stayed that way for two or three weeks. The gurney photo is
the middle of that stretch, not its start. And the answer to
"Lexi pays rent" arrived as a video, his caption "Dan reimburses her
generously" — explicit, so no visual is committed; the original stays
in his media library and the joke is recorded in prose, which is where
it belongs.

February 2010 left two letters in the same Gmail self-forward habit
that preserved the first messages. On **February 19**, two days after
he started Suboxone, Dan wrote Alexis from a week apart: "in less than
24 hours i'll be on my way home to you... Im so proud of you for
staying strong." On **February 26**, his last night in Florida —
"can you believe this is happening? i'm sitting here watching you
pack" — he wrote the relationship's origin story back to her: "i met
you at a very interesting point in my life, I had started down this
'path'... And then I meet you. everything changes immediately." The
letter corroborates the ticket in his own hand: "From the perfect,
warm night that I picked you up at the airport." It ends the way
seventeen-months-in love letters end: "you're the girl I'm going to
marry." The move out came two days later.

A third, previously undocumented relationship falls inside the same
window and complicates the clean two-name sequence: a six-week
long-distance romance with [[wiki/people/stephanie-nalbone|Stephanie
Nalbone]], a Uniontown contact, ran September–November 2009 — a
reconnection right at graduation, a real in-person visit home, and an
abrupt end by early November. Nothing in either thread resolves how it
relates in time to the Danielle ending or the Alexis beginning; it is
recorded as a real, dated event with an unresolved place in the
sequence, not folded into either narrative.

**A 2009 event was restored to this period on 2026-08-15, having spent the
wiki's whole life misfiled six years downstream.** About two weeks after Dan
moved Alexis to Florida to be with him, she cheated on him — per the operator
an **online-only** episode, not in person, which is why nothing in the Winter
Park record marks it. Dan's only surviving account of it is a single sentence
told to Annie on 2015-11-28, as the contrast case for how the *next*
relationship was starting: *"I love this it's the exact opposite way I started
my last relationship... Lex cheated on me 2 weeks in after I moved her to
fla."* Because the sentence was filed under the date it was sent rather than
the date it describes, the wiki carried it for months as a November 2015
betrayal that triggered the switch to Annie — a chain now retracted in full
(see [[wiki/mind/synthesis/bond-switch-2015]] and
[[wiki/people/alexis-armel]]). The episode belongs here, in the relationship's
first month, and it is the earliest dated event in the Alexis record.

Alexis wasn't a long-distance partner during this
window — the Twitter corpus places her physically in Orlando with Dan,
tagged directly in local outings ("springs w @alexisarmel," a day trip to
one of the freshwater springs outside the city) and in the same
closed-network in-jokes as [[wiki/people/josh-brannan|Josh Brannan]] and
other Full Sail-era friends, consistent with her later confirmed presence
through the NYC years documented on [[wiki/people/alexis-armel]].

## The friend group

Full Sail seeds the one social cohort in Dan's record that outlasts
every geographic move that follows: [[wiki/people/jason-bermejo|Jason
Bermejo]], [[wiki/people/eric-jester|Eric Jester]],
[[wiki/people/matt-dunn|Matt Dunn]], and
[[wiki/people/jamie-mohler|Jamie Mohler]], a four-person group whose
members are corroborated across two separate 2025 message threads —
Jason's (which names all four) and Eric's (which independently names
Dunn) — decades after Orlando. Jason supplies the era's
music-taste imprint (Kid Cudi, DIY closet-studio recording, blog-era
hip-hop) that runs forward into Dan's whole production identity; the
group's other three members are lower-volume in the corpus but no less
durable — Eric's check-ins resume every year or two with no repair
needed, and Dunn is the subject of an annual $5 birthday joke Eric has
paid without fail for years. Per the operator, Jamie was in New York
with Dan during the NYC-1 years directly following this period and was
present the day Dan first met [[wiki/people/menore|Menore]] — meaning
the introduction to the dealer who would go on to be the most reliable
node of the entire [[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network|supply
network]] traces back to this same friend group.

The cohort had a physical center. Dan was the only one of the group
who owned his own place in Orlando — literally across the street from
Full Sail — and his house was home base for the four of them: Dan,
Eric Jester, [[wiki/people/matt-dunn|Matt Dunn]] (a live-music student
in Dan's words), and Jason Bermejo (operator testimony, 2026-09-24).
That housing arrangement explains the group's cohesion better than any
shared class schedule: they didn't just study together, they lived out
of the same living room. The 2009 photo series from that house reads
like a documentary of the arrangement — a May 10 arena trip waving an
Orlando Magic rally towel, a late-night August 22 street-party crowd,
an August 30 couch session with a blank canvas and an Arby's cup,
a video chat with Jester against an Earthrise backdrop on January 8,
2009, and the graduation itself, caps and gowns, Full Sail on the
diploma cover — plus a bedroom still-life from July 2009, guitar in
the corner and a bookshelf's worth of books, a photo Dan himself
didn't know existed until it surfaced in his library that night.

## Pro Tools certification (26 January 2010)

The Twitter corpus dates the Pro Tools certification not to a cluster but
to an hour. He sat the "210m" exam and **failed it** on 25 January 2010
("ughhhhhhhhhhh missed my 210m by 3 questions"), retook it the next
morning, and announced the pass at **19:40 UTC on 26 January 2010**. The
process is documented in Dan's own words across three tweets: "one more
test and i'm a certified pro tools operator!", the triumphant announcement
once it landed — "EAT UR HEART OUT BITCH IM PRO TOOLS CERTIFIED" — and a
wry follow-up admitting the tool he'd actually keep using was a
competitor's: "it's sort of ironic how i finish my pro tools certifications
and go directly to Logic. <3 u, anything made by apple." The credential
became his internship pitch on the move north, self-described plainly as
"recent music engineering grad with pro tools certification" rather than
with any of the tweet-era bravado. (Separately, a later AI-generated
retrospective — Gemini-_58.txt, not a primary source, and stylized as a
dramatized "simulation log" — narrativizes this as a **Pro Tools HD 8**
certification specifically, framing it as advanced signal-routing/TDM/
delay-compensation training that only "the 5% tier" of the graduating
class completed. That level of technical specificity isn't corroborated
anywhere in Dan's own contemporary tweets and should be read as retrospective
narrative coloring, not confirmed fact.) The certification is the credential
that completes the Recording Arts program and is the direct trigger for the
move: audio engineering credentials in hand, Dan relocates to
compete in "NYC's density-driven market" rather than stay in Orlando's
post-graduation lull.

**The credential and the decision are eleven minutes apart.** "EAT UR
HEART OUT BITCH IM PRO TOOLS CERTIFIED" is posted at 19:40; "Brooklyn
here we come!!" at 19:51, the same evening. Nothing in the archive
anticipates the move before the exam result — the preceding week is
studying, West Palm complaints and a first tattoo — and the move is
executed within five weeks of it. Whatever else the Recording Arts
program produced, the certification is the thing that moved him: the
period's causal hinge is a test score, and it is dated.

The move itself follows fast and is dated to the day on
[[wiki/timeline/periods/2010s]]: the Williamsburg apartment hunt on 17
February, the U-Haul on the 27th, "peace out, florida" on **28 February
2010**, and `dan@ishlab.com` live on 24 March. The "April 2010" date
this page and context-core's residence table both carried is a month
late.

## Chemical stabilization

The opiate arc that began in 2007 reaches its resolution at the tail end
