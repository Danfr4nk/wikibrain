# The COVID Era (2020)

The pandemic year lands in the middle of the first NYC chapter's later
stretch and directly overlaps the documented
[[wiki/timeline/periods/2020-2021-market-era|2020–2021 market era]]. It is
also the year the [[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Fran]] estate finally pays —
five advances between February and June, a court order on August 21, and a
six-figure deposit on September 1 — and the year the record holds Dan's
own narration of a political conversion, timestamped to the minute.

Those three things happened to the same person in the same twelve months,
and the previous version of this page connected the first two while leaving
the third as a paragraph. It also rested its central claim on a number that
the wiki has since taken away from it. That correction leads, because a
page that keeps a retired premise in its lede is worse than a page with no
premise at all.

## The "micro-radius siege," and the number that no longer supports it

The self-profile frames 2020 as a stress test of the "Metropolitan
Experiment" — Dan's systematic, routine-based approach to inhabiting New
York — describing the pandemic as forcing him *"into a micro-radius
siege."* That is testimony, and it stands as testimony.

What does not stand is the quantification this page used to attach to it.
The earlier text read: *"GPS visit counts collapse to 121 for 2020, a sharp
drop against the surrounding years' baseline, the single steepest
year-over-year decline in the full location history."*

> **CORRECTED [2026-09-17]:** the 121-visit figure is real and the inference
> drawn from it is not. [[wiki/self/location-history]] now annotates the
> 2020 and 2021 rows identically — *"Sharp drop (device-change blackout
> 2020-09-24 → 2021-07-20, not low mobility)"* — and states the conclusion
> in its own text: *"2020-21 low visit counts are the device-change
> blackout... not behavior."* The export simply stops recording places on
> **2020-09-24** and does not resume until **2021-07-20**. A count that
> ends in September cannot measure a year, and it certainly cannot measure
> the spring lockdown, which is entirely inside the period the device was
> still reporting. The siege claim is not disproven — it is **unsupported by
> this instrument**, which is a different and recoverable state.

There is a second instrument, and it points the same direction as the
testimony without depending on the first. The whole-corpus message count for
2020 is **6,311** — labelled "COVID collapse" — against 20,153 in 2019 and
40,514 in 2018 ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/message-circadian-latency]]). That is
a 69% year-over-year drop in communication volume, derived from a different
export, with no device gap in it. It does not measure mobility. It measures
contact, and contact fell off a cliff. Anyone rebuilding the siege argument
should build it on that figure and say so.

## The estate, paid out in instalments

2020 is the year the money that 2018 was spent in advance of actually
arrives, and the sequence is unusually well dated because
[[wiki/people/rod-banks|Rod Banks]] wrote each cheque and said so in
writing.

| Date | Amount | The message |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2020-02-04 | $10,000 | *"Yes, he contacted me yesterday. I will prepare a check for $10,000."* |
| 2020-03-06 | $7,000 | *"Jason contacted me about a check for you. I will prepare a check for $7000."* |
| 2020-03-27 | $3,000 | *"Jason has approved a $3000 check for you."* |
| 2020-04-17 | $7,500 | *"Jason has authorized me to issue another check to you for $7,500."* |
| 2020-06-23 | $10,000 | Dan asks Suz to *"pick up my check from rod this afternoon"* |

Five advances, **$32,500** in total, across five months
([[wiki/people/rod-banks]], [[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]]).
Then the formal distribution: on **August 21, 2020** the court signed the
order distributing **$144,069.31** of the Fran estate to Dan — the
biography's largest documented single capital arrival — and on **September
1** a final cheque of **$134,069.31** landed, deposited via
[[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]].

Two live discrepancies sit inside that table and neither is smoothed here.

**First, the $10,000 may be one advance or two.**
[[wiki/meta/open-questions]] carries it as a live contradiction: the Rod
Banks thread holds five advances totalling $32,500 *none of which is dated
2020-06-23*, with its $10,000 dated **2020-02-04** — while the Suz thread
independently dates a $10,000 pickup to **2020-06-23**. Either there were
two $10,000 advances four months apart, or one of them is misdated. The
corpus does not settle it.

**Second, the $144,069.31 → $134,069.31 gloss does not survive its own
arithmetic.** [[wiki/mind/synthesis/four-financial-inversions]] explains the
landed figure as *"the $144,069.31 order, minus the market account's ~$25k
peak."* The difference between the two figures is exactly **$10,000** — the
size of an advance, not of the ~$25k Robinhood peak. The two numbers are
each independently attested; the explanation joining them is not, and it is
flagged rather than repeated.

What the estate chain does establish cleanly is the burn. $134,069.31 lands
September 1, 2020; by early 2021 the Au Za'atar storytime opens with *"the
money just ran out."* Roughly two quarters. The market account peaked near
$25k, so the market cannot have absorbed it; the rest exited through the
standing drains, and the decomposition — rent versus supply versus Annie
versus market — is not separable from any source held here
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]]).

## The trustee dies before the money moves

[[wiki/people/fred-adams|Judge Fred Adams]] — the man who authorised Dan's
caregiving wage in 2018, mandated the 24-hour rotation at Fran's deathbed,
and held the estate — died in **the third week of May 2020**. The corpus
contains exactly one record of it: Suz's message on **2020-05-24 at 07:24**,
*"I don't know if you will read this or not but Jsyk I found out last night,
quite by coincidence, that judge Adams died several days ago."* Her *"last
night"* is May 23 and *"several days ago"* places the death around **18–21
May**. Nothing else in the corpus mentions it.

Adams sends and receives nothing in the message corpus — he is named in it
at length and never writes in it. His exact date of death, the years he
practised, and who Jason Adams is to him are all unverified here and all
answerable from Fayette County records nobody has pulled. By **August 6**,
Dan is dealing with Jason: *"i talked to jasón adams today and am going on
saturday morning to sign for the inheritance in advance."* The man who held
the money was dead before it moved.

## The conversion, dated to the minute and argued about anyway

On **August 22, 2020 at 22:43 EDT** — recorded in the corpus at
`2020-08-23 02:43:46 UTC`, which is why the pinned date is exact rather
than approximate (`dat:1076`) — Dan narrated his own political turn in a
long unprompted message: *"i took a **HARD turn left recently thanks to
hasan piker, chapo trap house, thoughtslime, etc.**"* Hasan first, Chapo
second, Thoughtslime third ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/2020-left-turn]],
[[wiki/interests/hasan-piker]], [[wiki/interests/chapo-trap-house]]).

The same day he paired the new politics with his own money's two
source-lines: *"it feels super gross knowing that dad fought to keep frank's
auto employees from unionizing and that we have such kushy lives because of
it"* — written the day after a court signed him $144,069.31 of inherited
money ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]]). The juxtaposition is the
record's, not the wiki's.

And the wiki's own pages disagree about what that message dates. The August
narration says *"recently."* But the February 2020 iMessages already have
him evangelising Chapo unprompted — *"do you listen to chapo?"* — six months
before, pre-lockdown and pre-reading-list, and
[[wiki/interests/chapo-trap-house]] reads the February evangelism as
recruiting for a tribe rather than sharing a podcast. One page dates the
hard-left turn to **2019** (Bernie plus Chapo, pre-lockdown) and treats
August 2020 as *"the moment the turn acquired its reading list and its
family-history reckoning"* rather than its beginning. Both readings are on
the record. The narrower statement that survives both: August 22, 2020 is
when the turn was **named**, and the naming is what is timestamped.

Six weeks later it went public. On **3 October 2020**, Twitter carries the
first public political self-identification in seventeen years of that
account — independent corroboration, no AI in the loop, addressed to
strangers ([[wiki/self/twitter/2020]]). Later that month, the ultimatum
form: *"if 2020 hasn't made you a marxist ... you're either frighteningly
uninformed or just a fucking ghoul."*

## Reading and watching during isolation

Slavoj Žižek's *Pandemic!: COVID-19 Shakes the World* appears in the reading
record at four stars, tagged leftist/politics — contemporaneous engagement
with the pandemic as a political-philosophical object rather than only a

of the primary texts (Marx, Kropotkin) is what
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/2020-left-turn]] treats as the conversion's cement.
**Michael Brooks** — Majority Report co-host — died in July 2020, three
weeks before the conversion narration.

## The market channel

Confined to a small radius with reduced routine structure, the same year
produced the [[wiki/timeline/periods/2020-2021-market-era|market era]], and
its opening is dated to the message. On **September 20, 2020**
[[wiki/people/jerad-friedline|Jerad Friedline]] sends the tip: *"FSLY...
busy at the moment but will send my research tomorrow. I made a little over
$200k from April..."* Dan: *"just buy FSLY?"* Jerad: *"Buy FSLY and do not
touch for 2-3 years"* ([[wiki/work/fastly-fsly]]).

He did not hold for two years. By **September 30** he reports *"112 shares
my dude"* and *"fastly killing it"*; by **October 6**, *"dude i made $600
just from FSLY today"*; on **October 7** Jerad notes *"Didn't see it
breaking all time high this quick"* — the same day Fastly announced a Google
Cloud partnership. Roughly $15k of portfolio in, roughly $4k out, before the
January 2021 meme eruption widened the game. The timing was lucky as well as
right, and [[wiki/work/fastly-fsly]] says so.

This page's addition to the market era's own account is the causal
direction: the target was needed specifically *because* the pandemic had
removed the normal environmental inputs. That remains an inference, and the
instrument that was supposed to support it — the GPS collapse — has been
retired above.

## The Train Plan

The largest documented pre-2025 relationship crisis with
[[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] falls squarely inside this period, and the
lockdown context is explicit in the record. The two were staying at Annie's
parents' home during COVID lockdown — the trip itself dated and explained by
an exterminator notice: on **September 10** Dan told Annie *"they are
bombing the apt tomorrow around 11 or so,"* and the couple relocated to
Uniontown for roughly a week ([[wiki/places/307-e-76th-st]]).

Dan discovered she had been telling another man *"terrible, unforgivable,
untrue things"* about him, and that the man had made plans to pick her up
from the train station. His own words, sent **07:13 on the morning after the
discovery, September 20, 2020**, date and frame it precisely: *"this isn't
some accident Annie, you said TERRIBLE unforgivable, untrue shit to some guy
who wanted you and then had him plan to pick you up from the train."* He
took seven Xanax bars, threatened to leave permanently, and sent 200+
messages over multiple days. Annie's own words afterward: *"I know what I
said and I fucking regret it… I have messed shit up. Multiple times."* The
word "cheated" appears in Dan's characterization of the cumulative pattern
and she does not contest it.

The crisis was effectively invisible in earlier passes over the corpus
because 2020 was a near-absent year in some of the message exports — a
textbook instance of the failure mode `CORPUS_POLICY.md` exists to prevent,
where a partial export makes absence of evidence look like evidence of
absence. Seven days later, on **September 27**, Suz warned Dan and Annie in
writing about physical conflict, over Facebook Messenger, after being unable
to reach either of them ([[wiki/people/suzanne-frank]]).

## Supply, in a year when he is mostly the one offering

The 2020 supply record has an inversion worth stating plainly, because it
cuts against the "patient" framing the health pages otherwise carry. On
**October 1**: *"I have a buddy with Suboxone pills."* On **October 20**:
*"Found you six suboxone pills."* Both outbound, both offers. Neither is a
dose point and [[wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]] explicitly declines to
promote them to one ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/chemical-architecture]]).

The buying side is the [[wiki/people/sam|Sam]] thread, and it is the year's
best single piece of street-level lockdown documentation. In **February**,
after Dan left him standing outside the building twice in one day without
answering, Sam **fired him as a customer** — *"Ok I'm leaving don't never hit
this number."* The rupture itself is dated **February 23**: fourteen
escalating messages from the sidewalk (*"I'm in front your building / Should
I leave? / Damn u can't even text me back"*). It did not hold. The
re-approach came from Sam, on **May 14**, deep in the COVID spring: *"You
still in ny,"* followed by a fresh price list. The last exchange,
**September 10**, closes the loop with an unsentimental symmetry — Sam runs
his verification script on Dan as if he were a stranger (*"Send me your
Facebook link for verification"*), then catches himself: *"Don't send / I
remember."*

## Contradictions on the record

**The siege's instrument.** This page's original quantification (121 GPS
visits = steepest decline = the siege, measured) is retired above.
[[wiki/self/location-history]] attributes the 2020–21 collapse to a
device-change blackout running 2020-09-24 → 2021-07-20 and says in its own
text that it is *"not behavior."* The two pages previously said opposite
things about the same number; this one now defers.

**The $10,000 advance, twice.** Filed as a live contradiction in
[[wiki/meta/open-questions]]. The Rod Banks thread dates it 2020-02-04; the
Suz thread dates a $10,000 pickup 2020-06-23; the thread's five advances
total $32,500 with none dated in June. Two advances or one misdating — the
corpus does not say.

**$144,069.31 versus $134,069.31.** Both attested. The published
explanation for the $10,000 gap invokes a ~$25k market peak, which does not
match the arithmetic. Recorded as an open discrepancy, not resolved.

**When the left turn happened.** The August 22 narration says *"recently."*
The February Chapo evangelism is six months earlier and pre-lockdown. One
page dates the turn to 2019; another treats August as its origin. What is
timestamped is the naming, not the turning.

**Fred Adams's death date.** Reconstructed from one message's *"several days
ago."* A range (18–21 May), not a date, and externally checkable from
Fayette County records nobody has pulled.

## Limits of this record

- **The cited `raw/` paths do not resolve in this tree.** Every claim above
  drawn from `Dan Profile.txt`, the dox scans, the message CSVs or the
  Facebook exports is **unverified** in the README's sense — not false,
  unchecked. The figures re-derived against held material are named inline.
- **September 24 onward has no location record at all.** The last quarter of
  the year is spatially blind, and so is all of 2021 until July. Any claim
  about where Dan was between those dates rests on messages, not on GPS.
- **Roughly forty days of the year carry a dated entry.** The rest is
  unrecorded, not quiet. July has two rows. November and December have no
  ISO-dated entry whatsoever — only month-level statements — which is the
  thinnest stretch in the period.
- **The estate's burn is endpoints only.** $134,069.31 in on September 1,
  "ran out" by early 2021. The six months between have no ledger and the
  decomposition is not separable from any source held here.
- **Single-source claims.** Fred Adams's death (one message), the 278 Derrick
  Ave fire (Suz only), the Annie rape disclosure (second-hand, Dan to a third
  party), the "major donors" framing, the five-closings month, and the
  Žižek rating each rest on one source.
- **A May 2020 date that appears nowhere else.** Gemini asserted, from a deed
  lookup it was not asked to show its work on, that Suz held 155 Virginia
  Ave for roughly twelve years and sold it in **May 2020**. That date appears
  nowhere else in the corpus and should be treated as unverified until a
  Fayette County recorder search settles it.
- **A prediction that could not be found.** The claim *"Predicted Roe repeal
  May 2020, two years early"* appears in the interpretation layer; no message
  from Jerad's handle or email containing Roe/abortion/repeal content was
  located during verification. It is carried as unlocated, not as true.
