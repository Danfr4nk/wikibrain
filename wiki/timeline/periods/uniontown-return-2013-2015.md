# Uniontown Return (2013–2015)

The most-corrected stretch in the whole timeline. For roughly two and a half
years — **spring 2013 to Thanksgiving 2015** — Dan was back in Uniontown,
not New York, and nearly everything the old framing misfiled as "later NYC"
actually happened here: the producer aliases took shape, the six-year
[[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis]] relationship ran out its endgame, and
the single-bond switch to [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] was set up.
Context-core is emphatic that this correction matters — "the producer-alias
development, the Alexis→Annie transition, and the poverty/deep-cycle years
all happened in *Uniontown*, not NYC" — because the geography changes what
the period *means*: this is not a metropolitan chapter but a return home,
and the creative and relational reorganization that defines it is a
small-town reorganization.

It is also the period for which this wiki has the **least private**
**evidence** and, until the last four days of it, none at all. That fact
governs everything below and is set out first, because every claim on this
page has to be read against it.

A direct count over the export returns **one** row dated before 12 November
2015 — a single attachment-only message from 2011-03-18 — and **seven** rows
in total before the Annie thread opens on 28 November. Thirty of this
period's thirty-one months are therefore covered by exactly nothing in the
instrument the rest of this wiki reasons from.

`CORPUS_POLICY.md` names the specific danger: *"In a fragment, absence of*
*evidence looks exactly like evidence of absence."* Here the fragment is the
whole period. No claim of the form "he did not talk to X in 2014" or
"nothing was discussed" can be made from the message record about these
years, in either direction, and this page makes none.

What exists instead is three other kinds of record, and they are good: the
**@danfrank archive**, which carries 346 recovered originals in 2013, 171 in
2014 and 62 in 2015 (counted off the year pages' own record sections); the
**Facebook and SoundCloud share trail** behind the production aliases; and
**fifteen EXIF-dated photographs** from the autumn of 2015. The period is
documented; it is just not documented privately.

## The return, and the date nobody has

The departure from Manhattan is the period's opening fact and it is not
dated anywhere. Four values circulate:

| Claim | Source | Standing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 April 2013 | [[wiki/places/90th-st-manhattan]]'s frontmatter | derived from a "thirteen months" arithmetic that the tweet archive has broken; no independent support |
| May 2013 | context-core residence timeline; this page; [[wiki/timeline/periods/2010s]] | canonical, but memory rather than record |
| "substantially in Fayette County from August 2013" | [[wiki/self/twitter/2013]] | dated, but it is presence, not tenancy |
| "i just moved back from NYC" | [[wiki/people/ej-rags]], 12 July 2013 | first-person, dated to the day — and a statement of having moved, not of when |

The public record's shape fits a spring departure and refuses to sharpen it.
New York references fall from 10.2% of 2012 originals to 0.3% in 2013. The
last post placing Dan in the city is the **18 November 2012** internship
pitch (*"living in NYC"*). From **mid-August 2013** the feed is continuously
Western Pennsylvania and stays that way — Carnegie Mellon (14 August),
Sheetz twice (20 August, 3 September), Texas Roadhouse (29 August), the
Oddball Comedy Festival in Pittsburgh (31 August), an argument about Syria
*"with an idiot in Sheetz"*, and on **27 September** *"For those curious*
*what the hooplah in uniontown is about today"* with *"Fucking helicopters*
*and shit"*, filmed in his hometown.

[[wiki/self/location-history]] records that address-level data runs out
after January 2011 and does not resume until the Google export begins in
April 2014, and names that window the corpus's location blind spot. The 2013
feed partially fills it, and the distinction it cannot cross is worth
keeping: **the tweets place him in Fayette County; they do not say he**
**lived there.** The residence is May 2013 on memory, the presence is August
2013 on timestamps, and the two months between them are empty.

He moved back into **337 Saratoga Drive**, the family home, and stayed
through January 2015 ([[wiki/places/337-saratoga-drive]], which carries the
occupancy as "May 2013 – Jan 2015"). The audio-engineering career the
[[wiki/timeline/periods/2010s|NYC-1]] years had attempted did not come back
with him. What came back instead was the ambition in a new form, and it
started within weeks.

## SLOPPP: the recording-arts ambition, migrated — complete release log

The period's most productive throughline is the birth of Dan's **named**
**production identity**. [[wiki/interests/music/aliases/sloppp|SLOPPP]] — a
trap/festival-trap project on SoundCloud — launched in 2013, the same year
as the return, and ran until a track titled *Goodbye Demo* on 26 December
2015, one month into the Annie relationship.

The origin has a cause and a date now, from the operator capture behind
[[wiki/timeline/events/franki-fireworks-day-2013]]: the DJ controller Dan
had acquired while working at ishlab in New York and *"never really*
*touched"* was picked up after the return, and *"by summer, I was*
*dedicating my time to that."* An idle depressive interval after a failed
New York attempt, with a piece of equipment already in the room.

**Three things the complete list shows that a summary would not.**

*The peak is September 2013, not 2014.* Sixteen distinct titles in thirty
days, three of them on the 28th alone, inside the first four months of the
project's life. The prior framing of "2014 output peak" came from a
browser-history hit count, and the release trail does not support it.

*The 2013 count is a floor.* The Twitter archive begins on 17 August 2013
because the source changes there; everything before that date is invisible,
and the earliest trace — 31 July, *"twice now i've had some*
*(unintentionally) played background music while checking latest mix"* — is
already casual about the practice. A person checking a latest mix in July
did not begin in August. **The alias's start date is not datable from any**
**source now held.**

*It was an operation, not a hobby.* Inside the period SLOPPP ran two
recurring series and a monthly wrap-up format, entered Beatport contest
voting and canvassed for votes twice, solicited collaboration in public with
a business address (*"Lets collab! danfr4nk@icloud.com for stems and patch*
*trades"*, 8 May 2014), signed up to Splice, requested stems from other
artists, broadcast live on Ustream, and received named producer support. It
also came within a signature of representation: in **March 2015** a
Pittsburgh-scene promoter, Frank Swaney, heard the *Spring 2015 Demo*, had a
second DJ review it, and pitched full management — 500 demo CDs, 3,000
stickers, a street team, two tentative bookings — with terms to be settled
not with Dan but through a third party *talking to Dan's parents*. Nothing
further survives. Nine months later the alias closed with *Goodbye Demo*.

And he undercut it in public the whole time. On 23 September 2013, at the
exact centre of the most productive month of the project's life, a release
went out hashtagged *"#overcompensating #poseur #fakefuture"*. The output
was real, sustained and prolific, and the self-assessment attached to it was
already a verdict.

Around 2014 the alias handed off to
[[wiki/interests/music/aliases/mogzart|MOGZART]] — a transition that lands
on the **May 2014 account rotation** the
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/totality-themes|migration-grammar]] analysis reads as
an identity-amputation event. The handoff date is bracketed to ~2014 by that
rotation, not pinned by any release; the two aliases overlap rather than
succeed each other cleanly.

## July 2013: the detonation

On an afternoon in early July 2013, Dan took one of his mother's cars, drove
to 15 Holly Lane in Uniontown and picked up
[[wiki/people/franki-faris|Franki Faris]], a girl he knew from junior high.
Within roughly ten minutes a text arrived from Alexis, his partner of six
years: **"I saw your messages. I know you're with Franki."** Rather than
turn around he withdrew $350 from his mother's ATM card, bought a quarter
ounce of cocaine, and stayed out for more than ten hours
([[wiki/timeline/events/franki-fireworks-day-2013]]).

The consequences ran in three directions and all three belong to this
period. Alexis was displaced to **155 Virginia Avenue** — Suz's other
property — for five days, then went to her mother's in Carmichaels, started
seeing a man named Chris Tressler, and moved with him to New York; one week
after that move, in **October 2013**, she came home to Pennsylvania, saw
Dan, and did not go back. That the address later called "the Alexis lair"
was already housing a displaced partner in **July 2013** puts it under Suz's
control eighteen months before the January 2015 lease this wiki dates it
from. The lair predates the tenancy.

The public record bounds the break where the private record cannot: *"I*
*guess I tweet more when I'm single?"* (9 August), eleven days of posts with
a named other person across 21–31 August, and *"Mystery girl and*
*everything's okay now"* / *"Alexis Armel and a doppelgänger mosquito"* on
7–8 September. **Six to eight weeks off, and it is the only interval in**
**the six years where the relationship is not running.** The gap was filled
within two weeks of the earliest single-status post, and the reconciliation
was announced in the vocabulary of relief rather than of decision.

And Suz engineered the aftermath: per the capture she thought it a good idea
to have Alexis and [[wiki/people/danielle-onesi|Danielle Onesi]] — Dan's
first girlfriend, and Alexis's *"former enemy"* — bond over their shared
grievance, *"and this caused a huge thing."* The earliest documented
instance of the maternal switchboard operating on the relational graph
rather than the financial one.

## 2014: the year with no instrument at all

2014 has zero corpus rows, no located tweets bearing on the household, and
one operator capture. What that capture describes is a second eviction the
wiki had not previously distinguished: in **March 2014** Suz threw Alexis
out over a washing-machine incident Dan says he still does not understand,
and for **five months** he snuck her in and out of the house, hidden in a
closet when necessary. The two of them then moved in with a woman named Leah
Tedesco, and by **Christmas 2014** had arrived at 155 Virginia Ave.

This narrows but does not settle an existing contradiction.
[[wiki/places/155-virginia-ave]] and [[wiki/legal/2015-possession-arrest]]
both carry an *earlier* operator account of Alexis "secretly living at 337
Saratoga… hidden for roughly four months," placed immediately before a
January 2015 lease and a February 2015 arrest. Same shape — thrown out,
hidden, months long — dated a full year apart, with an intermediate
residence in one telling and not the other. Both are operator testimony of
different specificity, not primary record against testimony. **Neither is**
**adopted here.** A lease, a dated message, or a primary record of which
eviction is which would settle it, and nothing in `raw/` does.

## The switch, verified line by line

The [[wiki/mind/synthesis/bond-switch-2015|single-bond switch]] analysis
carries the mechanics. Every quotation that page rests on has now been
re-read against the authoritative corpus rather than the shelved master
dump, and all of them verify verbatim. The period-level sequence, with
corpus timestamps in local time:

- **~24 November** — Annie enters, introduced by **Alexis herself**.
  Annie, 1 December 09:37: *"HAPPY ONE WEEK SINCE LEX HANDED YOU TO ME."*
  The corpus holds no rows on the 24th, so the date is hers,
  retrospectively, not an observation.
- **28 November, 18:47** — the thread opens already intimate: *"Fuck my*
  *friends. Fuck birthday dinner. Fucking going drinking. I wanna be with*

  *that you have any part in this. She thinks I'm just creeping you."* **The**
  **bond was running before the in-person meeting**, which the period-level
  narrative has not previously said.
- **28 November, 19:07–19:08** — *"I love this it's the exact opposite way*
  *I started my last relationship"* → *"Like I truly trust you"* → *"Lex*
  *cheated on me 2 weeks in after I moved her to fla."* See the correction

## Transition

The period ends precisely at the switch. 28 November – 1 December 2015
closes the Alexis era and opens
[[wiki/timeline/periods/2015-2016-annie-relationship-start|the Annie
relationship]], which inherits the apartment, the town, and the alias
identity mid-transition, and runs from here to 2026. The closure is physical
as well as relational: Annie moved into 155 Virginia Ave, the same
apartment, making the transfer a **swap** rather than a relocation.

## Limits of this page

- **Thirty of thirty-one months have no private record.** Zero corpus rows
  for 2013 and 2014; seven rows before 28 November 2015. Every statement
  about those months rests on context-core, the public archives, the
  Facebook/SoundCloud share trail, or operator testimony, and none of it can
  be checked against contemporaneous private text.
- **The return date is unresolved** — April 2013, May 2013 and "present in
  Fayette County from August 2013" are three different kinds of claim and
  none of them dates a move.
- **The 2013 tweet volumes are not activity.** The archive is a live
  scrape capped at ten results per month-bounded query before 17 August
  2013; the monthly series (4, 29, 6, 20, 10, 10, 11 through July) has the
  cap baked into it. The SLOPPP counts above are floors.
- **The 155 Virginia lease terms and exact move-in are undocumented**, and
  the January-2015 date sits awkwardly against both the February 2015
  possession arrest and the "Christmas 2014" arrival in the 2026-08-27
  capture.
- **The SLOPPP→MOGZART handoff is bracketed, not pinned**, to ~2014 by an
  account rotation rather than by any release.
- **Inferred vs documented.** Documented: the release log dates, the
  photograph timestamps, every 2015 message quoted above, the corpus census.
  Inferred: that the 2013 Fayette County presence indicates residence; that
  the missing December days are export gaps rather than silence (supported
  by their position between 800- and 900-message days, but still an
  inference); that the July 2013 fireworks show dates the Franki day to the
  4th-adjacent window. Testimony only, with no primary corroboration: the
  2014 eviction, the five months of concealment, Leah Tedesco, and the
  uncured harvest.
