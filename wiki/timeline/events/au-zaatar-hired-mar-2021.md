> **Incidental entry** — Dan's March 2021 hire at Au Za'atar in Midtown Manhattan, the start of his 41-month tenure. Complete at this size; no held document records the hire itself.

In March 2021 Dan was hired at Au Za'atar — the Midtown Manhattan restaurant at 58th & 1st, not the East Village flagship — as a busser, with zero food-service experience. Owner-operator [[wiki/people/tarik-fallous|Tarik Fallous]] hired him on the spot and, per the source page, talked him into the veterans' equal tip split on day one (dat:0452).

The hire began the 41-month tenure (March 2021–August 2024): Dan's longest job, and his most-visited GPS location at 445 visits (kb/events/2021-03-au-zaatar-hired.md).

## The record in order

- **March 2021** — hired. Month only; the day is not recorded in the held material.
- **~April 2021** — per the source page's revised block, Dan got [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] the hostess job at the original East Village location — a separate site from his own employment (dat:0452).

## Evidence and limits

The held Tarik thread (dat:1258, 80 messages, 2023-08-11–2026-04-12) corroborates the warm relationship and the page's "hired March 2021" framing, though the thread opens two years after the hire itself. No held message, application, or document records the hire; the held corpus is nearly silent on the tenure ("zaatar" 10–11 rows, "tarik" 15 across ~192k records, dat:0589). The exact hire date and Tarik's own version of the "hired on the spot" story are not in the held record — the characterization stays page-attributed.
