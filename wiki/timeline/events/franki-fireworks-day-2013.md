# The Franki Fireworks Day (early July 2013)

On an afternoon in early July 2013, Dan borrowed one of his mother's cars,
drove to 15 Holly Lane in Uniontown, and picked up
[[wiki/people/franki-faris|Franki Faris]] — a girl he knew from junior high
and high school — with no destination in mind. He had been back from New
York only weeks. Roughly ten minutes into the drive, a text arrived from
[[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis]], his partner of six years: **"I saw
your messages. I know you're with Franki."** She had been reading his
messages, prompted by something in his behaviour. The discovery was an act
of detection, not a coincidence.

He did not turn around. He withdrew **$350** from his mother's ATM card,
bought a **quarter ounce** of cocaine from the family's supplier, and stayed
out for more than ten hours: driving between Uniontown and Franki's own
apartment in Somerset, near Seven Springs, where she worked as a
ropes-course attendant at the resort; passing under a municipal fireworks
display on the Rt 119 bypass near the Main Street exit, staged at the mall
a few hundred yards off; picking up hitchhikers near a campground on the
day's own logic; reaching her apartment around midnight. Within days, she
*"got sick of me basically immediately and it went nowhere."*

He calls the day **"one of the more pivotal days of my young adult life,"**
and the record supports the weight. The six-year relationship broke that
afternoon and stayed broken six to eight weeks — the only interval in six
years where it is off. His mother housed the displaced partner at an
address the wiki had dated eighteen months later, then convened his current
girlfriend and his first girlfriend to bond over their grievance against
him. And in the same season, the DJ controller he had carried home from
New York untouched became a daily occupation — the dated origin of the
production identity. The day survives as a single memory, told thirteen
years later. Everything on this page is weighed against that.

## The weeks before: a return with nothing to do

The departure from Manhattan is not dated anywhere in the record, and four
competing values circulate for it ([[wiki/timeline/periods/uniontown-return-2013-2015]]).
What is dated, to the day, is the first-person statement of the return:
on **July 12, 2013** — roughly a week after the fireworks day — Dan told
[[wiki/people/ej-rags|Ej Rags]], a Williamsburg vinyl collector, *"i just
moved back from NYC and i'm super output recently."* It is a statement of
having moved, not a date for the move, and it lands the fireworks day
inside the return's opening weeks. The same exchange carries his early
philosophy of destroying his own creative work — *"that was so I can
create something new"* — which is worth keeping because it is the posture
he is in, nine-ish days after the day: already narrating demolition as a
prerequisite for making.

Of the months between the return and the day, the 2026 capture records
almost nothing except two things, and the two things are the interval's
whole character. One: *"a few days of no sleep as i watched the boston
bombing and subsequent manhunt unfold"* — three months after the event,
sleeplessness as a practice, during a period he otherwise describes as
apathetic depression. Two: a disappointing binge of *Arrested Development*
season four. The entropy appetite was the one thing running; everything
else was idle.

The idleness had a piece of equipment in the room. The capture records
that the DJ controller Dan had acquired while working at ishlab in New
York — *"never really touched"* — was picked up after the return, and
that *"by summer, I was dedicating my time to that — it is right as this
began to take hold that I remember this all taking place."*
[[wiki/interests/music/overview]] had carried the origin as *"operating
since approximately 2013."* This is the first source that dates it inside
the year and attaches it to a cause: an idle depressive interval after a
failed New York attempt, with the instrument already in the room. It is
not the day that starts the music. It is the season. But the season is
dated by the day.

The public register, such as it is before the August archive boundary,
supplies one piece of texture from the same week. On **July 10, 2013**
(18:04 UTC — 2:04 PM EDT), Dan tweeted the Kerouac line: *"the only people
for me are the mad ones. mad to to live, mad to talk, desirous of
everything. They burn like a roman candle across the stars."* Three weeks
later, on July 31, he posted *"@alexisarmel stealing my tweets now too?"*
— quoting that same roman-candle line back. The tweet archive does not
settle whether that is hostility, flirtation, or the public residue of a
private rupture being negotiated in public. It establishes only that
within three weeks of the day, the two are in an exchange again — on
record, in public, trading each other's lines. That is faster than the
capture's months-long sequence reads, and the tension is carried, not
resolved, in Conflicts in the record.

## The day

| Time | What happened |
| :--- | :--- |
| Afternoon | Pickup at **15 Holly Lane**, Uniontown — Franki's parents' address, not where she lived |
| ~10 minutes in | Alexis's text: *"I saw your messages. I know you're with Franki."* |
| After | ATM withdrawal of **$350** from Suz's card; a **quarter ounce** of cocaine bought from [[wiki/people/joe-croftcheck|Joe Croftcheck]] |
| Afternoon–evening | Driving between Uniontown and Franki's own apartment in Somerset, near Seven Springs, where she worked as a ropes-course attendant at the resort |
| Dusk | Passing under a municipal **fireworks display** on the Rt 119 bypass near the Main Street exit, staged at the mall a few hundred yards off |
| Somewhere in the mountains | Picking up **hitchhikers** near a campground, on the day's own logic |
| ~Midnight, 10+ hours in | Franki's apartment in Somerset |
| Within days | Franki *"got sick of me basically immediately and it went nowhere"* |

The structure of the decision is what matters, and it is in the
ten-minute mark. Alexis's text is not a discovery he makes; it is a
discovery she announces, about him, to him. The mechanism is newly
specific against the wiki's old standing note — she was reading his
messages, prompted by something in his behaviour — which makes the
rupture an act of detection rather than a drift. He is caught, in the
car, ten minutes in, with the person named in the text beside him. And
then he keeps driving.

What follows is escalation as answer. The $350 comes off his mother's
ATM card — her instrument, not his money — and the quarter ounce comes
from [[wiki/people/joe-croftcheck|Joe Croftcheck]], whom Dan describes as
**"the family plug."** That phrase is what makes the purchase more than
a drug detail: every other node in the supply network the wiki documents
is sought, peer-sourced, or friendship-embedded. Croftcheck is described
as the family's — inherited rather than selected — and this transaction
is the corpus's earliest dated purchase from a named supplier. The
supply chain's first documented link runs through the household, and the
money that funds it is the mother's too. Three of Suz's instruments are
in play before dusk: the car he is driving, the card that funds the
cocaine, and — by the end of the week — a second property she controls,
housing the partner he has just displaced.

The drive itself is the day's content, and the capture gives it as a
string of set pieces rather than a narrative: Uniontown to Somerset and
back, the fireworks under the bypass at dusk, hitchhikers picked up near
a campground "on the day's own logic," Franki's apartment around
midnight. The fireworks are what name the day and what nearly date it —
a municipal show at the mall, visible from Rt 119, in early July, is a
July-4-adjacent event in practical terms — and the dating caveat is
below rather than here, because the day's story does not depend on the
calendar. What it depends on is the shape: a man caught ten minutes into
a joyride who responds by making the day longer, more expensive, and
more public, and who ends it, ten hours later, at the other person's
apartment, having converted a confrontation into a tour.

The taste marker landed inside the same hours. Franki put one song on
him that day: **"17" by Youth Lagoon.** His own account of its effect is
the clearest statement of [[wiki/mind/concepts/the-cool-metric]] in the
corpus applied to a person in real time — *"FUCKING NOBODY IN UNIONTOWN
WAS LISTENING TO YOUTH LAGOON"* — and the consequence he reports is
total: it moved her from a list of ordinary attributes he names flatly
(*"brunette, skinny af, drug-accepting, daddy issues"*) to somebody he
*"immediately bowed down before to worship."* Two things are worth
separating here. The attribute list is a **type**, stated by Dan as
routine and recurring. The song is a **credential**, and it is what
converted the type into an obsession inside a single afternoon. Under
the cool metric, familiarity with an obscure record is not a preference
but evidence about how a person came to know things — sought, not
received — and this is the sharpest documented case of that judgement
being made about a person and acted on immediately.

And then she ended it. *"Got sick of me basically immediately and it went
nowhere."* The August 14, 2013 post-mortem email — the nearest
contemporaneous anchor — supplies the mechanism in his own later words:
*"I really didn't have the romantic feelings for franki that i said i did
at the time... I wasn't even able to kiss anyone."* Read plainly, the
confession reframes the episode: not a rebellion executed, but a
rebellion attempted and failed. He could not perform the role he had
cast himself in. The day detonated the relationship it was aimed at and
collapsed the one it was built around, within days.

## The detonation: five days, a switchboard, and six to eight weeks

The consequences ran in three directions, and all three belong to the
record around this page rather than to the day itself.

**The displacement.** Alexis moved to what Franki called *"suz's other place"* on Virginia Avenue, stayed **five days**, and then returned to her parents in
Carmichaels ([[wiki/places/155-virginia-ave]]). That address dates its
own story from a **January 2015** lease. The five-day stay puts it under
[[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]]'s control **eighteen months earlier**,
and already performing the function the later page calls
lair-continuity: housing a partner displaced from the primary residence.
The lair predates the tenancy. The five-day figure itself was the
subject of the page's sharpest correction — it had been mis-attached to
Franki's own tenure — and the resolution, dated 2026-08-28 with a
second independent capture confirming it unprompted (*"She spent 5 days
at Virginia ave and then went to her moms"*), is set out in Conflicts in
the record.

**The switchboard.** Per the capture — this is Dan's 2026 account of his
mother's conduct, carried as his testimony — Suz thought it a good idea
to have Alexis and [[wiki/people/danielle-onesi|Danielle Onesi]] — Dan's
first girlfriend, and Alexis's *"former enemy"* — spend time together
and bond over their shared grievance, *"and this caused a huge thing."*
That is the maternal switchboard operating on the relational graph
rather than the financial one, and it is the earliest documented
instance of it. Note the shape: one afternoon with Franki radiated,
within days, into a multi-party grievance network — the current partner
displaced to Suz's other property, the ex-partner recruited as her ally,
the grievance shared and amplified. Every later switchboard operation
the record documents runs this same pattern. Suz does not take sides in
the relationship; she reorganizes the graph around the grievance. The
Franki day is where that instrument first appears. On the Danielle
side, the incident is also the first hard evidence that by 2013 her
relationship was with Suzanne, not with Dan
([`dat:0487`](0487-danielle-onesi-first-girlfriend-gabe-james.md)) — the
convening is the mother's instrument, and Danielle is in the room
because of the mother.

**The bounded break.** The public record bounds the rupture where the
private record cannot — the message corpus holds zero rows for 2013 —
and it bounds it tightly:

| Date | Post |
|---|---|
| 2013-08-09 | *"I guess I tweet more when I'm single?"* |
| 2013-08-21 → 08-31 | eleven days of posts with `@katie_efff` — Blue Star Point Lookout, *"Mountain things"*, the Oddball Comedy Festival in Pittsburgh |
| 2013-09-07 | *"My better 1/2."* / *"Mystery girl and everything's okay now"* |
| 2013-09-08 | *"Alexis Armel and a doppelgänger mosquito"* — named again |
| 2013-09-25 | *"If having a bad bitch was a crime I'd be arrested @alexisarmel"* |

So: **six to eight weeks off, from the July detonation to the first week
of September, with another person publicly documented inside it.** That
makes this the only interval in the six years where the relationship is
not running. Two features of it bear on what this page argues. The gap
was **filled almost immediately** — [[wiki/people/katie-fletcher|Katie
Fletcher]] appears within two weeks of the earliest single-status post.
And the reconciliation is announced in the vocabulary of **relief rather
than decision**: *"everything's okay now"*, not a statement that
anything was resolved. The 7 September *"mystery girl"* is not
identified. Both readings fit and the following day names Alexis;
neither is asserted. The twitter-2013 synthesis adds the load-bearing
caveat: the whole reconstruction depends on *"I guess I tweet more when
I'm single?"* being literal rather than a joke — if it is a joke, the
only hard facts left are Katie Fletcher's eleven days and Alexis's
reappearance on 8 September.

The capture's fuller sequence runs longer than the public record's. Per
the 2026-08-27 account, after Carmichaels Alexis began dating a man
named **Chris Tressler**, moved with him to New York, and one week after
that move — in **October 2013** — came home to Pennsylvania, saw Dan, and
did not go back. The six-year relationship survived its only mid-course
rupture by passing through another relationship first. That October
landing sits in tension with the September 8 public naming, and the
tension is held open below rather than smoothed.

## The other things that started that summer

**The production identity has a date now.** The capture records that the
DJ controller Dan had acquired while working at ishlab and *"never
really touched"* was picked up after the return from New York, and that
*"by summer, I was dedicating my time to that — it is right as this
began to take hold that I remember this all taking place."*
[[wiki/interests/music/overview]] has carried the origin as *"operating
since approximately 2013."* This is the first source that dates it
inside the year and attaches it to a cause: an idle depressive interval
after a failed New York attempt, with a piece of equipment already in
the room.

The public trail confirms the season if not the day. The earliest trace
is **31 July 2013** — *"twice now i've had some (unintentionally) played
background music while checking latest mix"* — already casual about the
practice, which means the work predates the record of it. By September,
the project that would be named SLOPPP was releasing sixteen distinct
titles in thirty days. The start date is not datable from any source
now held; the archive's source boundary at 17 August 2013 makes August
look like the beginning only because July is truncated. What the day
gives the music is not a start date but a dated context: the occupation
formed in the same weeks as the rupture, in the same idle room, with the
same equipment that had sat untouched through New York.

**The Boston manhunt is the chaos preference's answered flag.** The
capture describes the months before the day as holding *"a few days of
no sleep as i watched the boston bombing and subsequent manhunt
unfold"* — and, separately, the *Arrested Development* binge. The
detail that carries it is not that he watched, but that he did not
sleep for days doing so, during a period he otherwise describes as
apathetic depression. The entropy appetite was the one thing running.
[[wiki/mind/concepts/chaos-preference]] had named exactly this as its
most serious unsourced claim — but its conflict entry is dated
2026-06-23, six weeks *before* the capture that attests it, and it
types the connection to this page as *contextualizes* rather than
*evidenced-by* until it re-adjudicates. The testimony answers the flag;
the adjudication is still open. Both standings are on the record.

## Conflicts in the record

**[OPEN] The date is not fixed.** "Early July 2013" plus a fireworks
display is as close as the record gets. The page for Franki has carried
a standing note since 2026-07-13 that the "July 4" framing used
elsewhere in the corpus **had no source anywhere in the archive**; this
capture is the first source for it, and it does not name the date. A
municipal fireworks show at the mall, visible from the Rt 119 bypass,
in early July, is a July-4-adjacent event in practical terms — the
framing is now **supported but still not explicitly dated**, which is a
real improvement on unsourced and short of settled. A dated ATM
withdrawal on Suz's account, or a Uniontown fireworks schedule for
2013, would close it.

**[RESOLVED 2026-08-28] The five-day figure belonged to Alexis, not
Franki.** The 2026-08-02 capture attached five days to Alexis's stay at
the Virginia Avenue address, while the earlier Gemini-58/DANSYNTH
self-narrations had attributed a five-day tenure to Franki; of Franki's
own tenure the capture said only that she *"got sick of me basically
immediately and it went nowhere."* A second, independent 2026-08-27
capture then confirmed the five days unprompted — *"She spent 5 days at
Virginia ave and then went to her moms"* — and the figure was
re-assigned. What was claimed: a five-day rebound. What the record
shows: the rebound had no measured duration; five days is how long it
took the displaced partner to leave the address she was moved to.
Current standing: resolved; [[wiki/people/franki-faris]] corrected
accordingly, and the tenure-floor control on
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/dormancy-not-exit]] sharpened rather than
weakened.

**[STANDING] Nothing here is contemporaneous.** The whole page rests on
one 2026 capture — thirteen years after the fact — plus a second
capture covering the aftermath. The July 9–31 2013 self-typology emails
and the August 14 2013 post-mortem already in the corpus are the
nearest dated material and they corroborate the window, not the day.
The hitchhikers, the ropes-course job, and 15 Holly Lane are
single-source details with no independent confirmation anywhere in
`raw/`. The page is marked `knowledge: mixed` accordingly: it is
memory, not residue, and the parts of it other sources can check are
flagged in the body.

**[OPEN] The Boston attestation postdates the flag.** What was claimed
by [[wiki/mind/concepts/chaos-preference]] on 2026-06-23: that the
Boston-manhunt instance was the page's own unsourced construction —
no message or post attested it. What the record shows: the 2026-08-02
capture, six weeks later, attests *"a few days of no sleep as i watched
the boston bombing and subsequent manhunt unfold"* as Dan's own
testimony. Current standing: the testimony answers the flag at the
testimony level; chaos-preference keeps the connection typed as
*contextualizes*, not *evidenced-by*, and has not re-adjudicated since
the capture. Neither page's standing is adopted over the other's here.

**[OPEN] The public reconciliation and the capture's reconciliation do
not land on the same month.** What the public record shows: Alexis named
again on 2013-09-08, *"everything's okay now"* on 2013-09-07. What the
2026-08-27 capture shows: Carmichaels, Chris Tressler, a brief move to
New York, and an actual reconciliation in **October 2013** — roughly
three months after the day, not "within weeks." The July 31
*"@alexisarmel stealing my tweets now too?"* post sits inside the same
tension: a public exchange within three weeks of the day, while the
capture has her gone to Carmichaels and then to another man. Current
standing: the record holds both; neither is smoothed over. The
September 7 *"mystery girl"* is unidentified, and both readings (Katie
or Alexis) still fit.

## Assessment

The day is load-bearing in three directions, and the record supports
all three at different strengths.

As a **relationship event**, it is the cleanest detonation in the
corpus: the rupture is dated to its first hour by the person who
caused it, *"I saw your messages. I know you're with Franki,"* and it
is an act of detection rather than a drift. The six-year relationship's
only mid-course break opens here, runs six to eight weeks on the public
record, and closes in the language of relief. The pattern Dan later
named it by — leaving a long-term partner abruptly for someone new in a
moment of crisis, the "Franki Faris 2.0" he confessed to Annie four
days into things in December 2015 — is established on this afternoon,
whether or not he had the name for it yet.

As a **household event**, it is the day three of Suz's instruments show
up inside a single afternoon — the car, the ATM card, the second
property — and the week the maternal switchboard first appears in the
record, convening the current and former partner around a shared
grievance. The lair predates the tenancy by eighteen months. The
switchboard predates every later instance. Both findings moved
places-page and people-page dates, which is the test of whether an
event page is doing work or holding anecdotes.

As an **origin event**, it is weaker and should be stated that way.
The production identity's dated origin is the season, not the day;
SLOOPP's start is not datable from any source held, and the archive
boundary at 17 August 2013 manufactures a false August beginning. The
cool-metric moment — Youth Lagoon's "17" converting a type into worship
inside an afternoon — is the sharpest real-time case on that page, and
it is dated to the day itself. The Boston detail is testimony, not
residue, and is carried as such until chaos-preference re-adjudicates.

The honest summary is the one the page already carried: one man's
memory, thirteen years on, of an afternoon that broke a six-year
relationship, set a pattern he would re-run, and sat inside the season
his production identity started. The parts the record can check have
been checked; the parts it cannot are labeled.
