> **Incidental entry** — Dan's first documented DUI, offense date February 3, 2019, disposed via ARD. Complete at this size; the charge list rests on a single third-party-lookup table, stated as such.

On February 3, 2019, Dan incurred the first of his two documented DUIs. The record states the charges as: DUI general impairment + BAC .16+ + 4 traffic charges, disposed via ARD (county) (kb/events/2019-02-dui-1.md; dat:0288).

## Source of the record

The offense date, charge list, and disposition come from a single source: the collapsed record table ("The record, 2008–2025") in the old wiki, built from a FOREWARN background check run 2026-08-08 (dat:0288). The wiki deliberately reports the collapsed reading (~9 incident clusters from 64 records) rather than raw counts.

The FOREWARN report itself is not held in this repository, and no PA UJS docket-level verification was performed. FOREWARN aggregates PA court-data sources with heavy duplication across sources and procedural stages, so the table is third-party-lookup testimony, not a primary record. No `raw/dui/` docket file is held in the origin snapshot for this event.

What the record cannot establish: the ARD grant date (not carried in the table as held), docket number, court, stop location, and whether the ARD was completed or the record expunged.
