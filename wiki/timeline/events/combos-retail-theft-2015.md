> **Incidental entry** — A 2015 retail theft of Combos snacks, evidenced by Dan's own contemporaneous December 2015 messages. Complete at this size; store, exact date, charge grading, and disposition are not in the record.

In 2015 Dan took Combos snacks in a retail theft that became a citation. The kb source dates it only approximately — 2015, a few weeks after a separate possession arrest — and that interval rests on the operator's account (dat:0072). The two matters are separate; the theft carries none of the possession case's machinery.

## What the corpus holds

A corpus search found three outbound, contemporaneous December 2015 messages (dat:0030):

- *"To go fix the combos incident"* (2015-12-09)
- *"Fuckin combos"* (2015-12-09)
- *"Because I took combos"* (2015-12-11) — a first-person admission, sent while, in Dan's words, "sitting here waiting AGAIN"

The same cluster includes Dan recounting a barracks trooper's rib — *"DID YOU STEAL MORE COMBOS"* — sent by Dan on 2015-12-11, i.e. Dan reporting what the trooper said, followed by "he was a nice dude" (dat:0030, dat:0072). December 2015 holds 4,088 messages against a corpus median month of 1,123, so the window is well covered.

What the record cannot establish: the store, the exact date of the theft, the value of the goods, the charge grading, and the disposition. None is stated in the sources, and none is supplied here.
