# Group Chat Closure (May 31 – June 1, 2026, and the eleven weeks after)

On the night of May 31, 2026, a man Dan had never met opened a group chat
with him and Annie. His first words were *"So I'm a fagot lmfao"* and *"You're
on heroin buddy"* [GC 05-31 22:24:37–22:24:46]. That man was Tuquick, since
identified as [[wiki/people/jerel-coles|Jerel Coles]], the man Annie was
seeing. For about two hours he read out what he said Annie had told him about
Dan, in front of Annie: that Dan was a heroin addict, a man who could not hold
a job, a man who had her sleep with other men to pay for cocaine, and a man
who had touched her while she was unconscious.

Dan joked through most of it. Then he stopped joking and asked Annie one
question, three times: had he molested her? *"If you so much as THINK or FEEL
like that happened you deserve to say so…so lets please clear that up"* [GC
05-31 23:13:08]. Annie's answer came forty minutes later: *"I have recorded
videos of me obviously passed out still being touched"* [GC 05-31 23:54:37].
It was neither a yes nor a no. Dan wrote *"thank you for clearing that up"*
[GC 05-31 23:55:08]. About ten minutes after his last row in the chat, he sent
Annie *"Goodbye forever… sic semper lupanis"* in their private thread
([dat:0125](../../kb/data/0125-end-fight-stale-attribution.md)).

That goodbye held for 52 days, the longest severance in an eleven-year
relationship. For months the wiki treated it as the end. It was not the end
([[wiki/timeline/events/july-2026-recontact]]). The group chat did not end
either. It stayed open for eleven weeks. Its **576th and last row** is Annie,
at 06:33:29 EDT on August 19, 2026, four and a half hours after she said
goodbye to Dan in the dyad:

> ***"He didn't rape me"*** [GC 08-19 06:33:29]

No page in the wiki recorded that row until now. The
[[wiki/timeline/events/end-fight]] reconstruction counts the chat at 575 rows
ending August 13, and misses it. It is the answer to the question Dan asked on
May 31, given seventy-nine days late, in the same chat where the accusation
was made, with the same witness present.

## Why it was called "closure", and what it closed

This page's original thesis came from the
[[wiki/mind/concepts/attachment-model]]. Dan's attachment had no internal
off-switch, and the Annie record held zero explicit severance signals from
her ([[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]] scopes that finding to the 41,073 received
messages of the dual-handle export). The bond could end only when something
outside it said it had ended, and the group chat was that something. It
confirmed a third party and a defamation campaign at once, in front of a
witness.

Half of that thesis holds. The chat did confirm both things. The third party
was sitting in it, and the campaign was recited in it by him, in Annie's
name. It also produced the longest severance on record: zero outbound from
Dan from June 1 to July 23. That matters against a background of 127 declared
exits and 110 re-engagements inside 48 hours ([[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]]).

The other half, that an external signal would close what the internal system
could not, failed. On July 23 Dan answered an email about the dog, and 624
messages followed in four days ([[wiki/timeline/events/july-2026-recontact]]).
In the rows the "closure" was not something done *to* Dan. It was something
Dan did. Every exit line of the night is his, as are the block declarations
and the sign-off. Annie's last word in the dyad was *"Understood"* (00:10:06),
and her last row in this chat that night was *"Fucking stop?"* [GC 06-01
00:07:21]. The End Fight page states it exactly: she stopped answering; he
ended it.

> **REVISED [2026-07-26]:** "closure" overstates what this event achieved, and
> the wiki inherited the overstatement. June 1 severed contact for fifty-two

Read in order, the fate of row 6 across eleven weeks is the whole case. It
was alleged in Annie's name by a third party. Annie answered ambiguously. The
third party conceded it the same night. He re-asserted it eight weeks later at
1:48 AM. Finally Annie denied it, in writing, in the room where it was made.
The End Fight page calls her May 31 sentence "the doorway sentence", alive
enough to wound and vague enough to disown. The August 19 row closes that
doorway. What is established is narrower than "vindication". In the record,
the person the accusation was made on behalf of withdrew it, in the chat
where it was made. Whether anything else happened on the video Tuquick claims
to hold, and what Annie's "still being touched" referred to, the record does
not show.

## The third party, and "Eli"

The original page had to be corrected once already for naming the wrong
man. The terminal-phase third party is Tuquick, not
[[wiki/people/eli|Eli]] ([[wiki/timeline/events/eli-incident]]; operator
correction 2026-07-13). The chat holds a row that may explain how the two
became confused. Tuquick, at 00:06:59 on June 1: *"Fucking Eli the whole time
🤣"*, followed by *"Dropped her off at your apartment"* [GC 06-01
00:06:59–00:07:11]. He names Eli himself, as a taunt. Whether he meant that
Annie was seeing Eli during the Tuquick period, or was reciting the older
affair as ammunition, the row does not say. It is also one likely route by
which "Eli" entered the forensic record of this night. Dan titled his June
NotebookLM notebook "Tactical Autopsy of the Eli and Annie Incident", and
[dat:1480](../../kb/data/1480-notebooklm-tactical-autopsy-inventory-20260913.md)
resolves that as umbrella naming. Tuquick's row makes the umbrella name
easier to understand. It does not change the correction: the man in the chat
is Tuquick.

Tuquick's own arc runs from total belief to defection. The concession comes
at the end of night one (*"No hard feelings towards you"* [06-01 00:13:51];
*"I don't think you did, that's just what she says"* [00:17:19]; *"Nah not
worth it, there's normal girls in the world"* [00:18:16]). Fourteen days later
he writes *"You can have her back ? She's no good 🤣 trauma bond to the
cuck"* and *"She's a compulsive liar with a drug addiction"* [GC 06-15
13:14:58–13:15:12]. In the same session he also says *"I'd slap your face
off"* [14:03:25]. The threats escalate in July (see the log below). Every
threat in the 576 rows runs one way. Dan makes none. His one statement about
force is conditional and defensive, and it was made in Coles's own thread on
August 17, not here ([[wiki/legal/463-morgantown]]).

Dan posts a Spotify playlist *"inspired by the events of this awesome group

The third artifact is the NotebookLM notebook. About three days after the

failed at capture. The prompts and the inventory are the whole record
([dat:1480](../../kb/data/1480-notebooklm-tactical-autopsy-inventory-20260913.md)).

The June 15 message where Dan names himself is also a fair summary of how he
held the whole episode: *"I may be a junkie cuck loser broke annoying
insufferable autistic. / and YOU may be a psychotic bizarre ragelord rube
violent weirdo stranger asshole / But we both know that Israel sucks"* [GC
06-15 16:40:46]. He had raised Israel in seven rows since May 31, and got one
answer: *"Anti"* [GC 06-15 13:27:54].

## Coverage limits

- **The attachments are not held.** Tuquick's pictures, the video Dan trims
  and sends at 23:59, and the "video" Tuquick says he holds are referenced by
  row only ([CORPUS_POLICY.md](../../CORPUS_POLICY.md)). Nothing on this page
  describes what any of them shows.
- **What Annie told Tuquick** is known only through Tuquick. Every "she said"
  in the ledger is his report of her words. Only rows 6 and 7 carry anything
  from Annie herself.
- **The August 19 row has no context in the chat.** Nothing in the chat
  prompts it, and no one answers it. Why Annie sent it then, whether Dan asked
  for it in another channel, and whether it answers Tuquick's July 26 burst or
  the May 31 question, the record does not show. The dyad around it holds the
  goodbye ritual. The link between the two is adjacency in message IDs and
  time, and it is inferred.
- **Nothing after September 7** is in the frozen parts. The living master file
  runs later, but it was not searched for this chat beyond confirming it adds
  no rows to it.
- **The earlier page's unheld sources** (the dossiers, `THE END FIGHT.csv`,
  the NotebookLM transcript) remain unresolved citations in the frontmatter.
  They are kept as provenance, not as evidence.
