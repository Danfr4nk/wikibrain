> **Incidental entry** — DeSantis's Twitter Spaces campaign launch, May 24, 2023: a technical meltdown Dan read as a character reveal. Complete at this size.

Ron DeSantis announced his 2024 campaign in a Twitter Spaces conversation with Elon Musk. The audio kept crashing. The internet named it #DeSaster within the hour, and Biden's comms team fundraised off the wreckage the same night.

Key facts:
- Dan's read (2023-05-26): "the absolute clusterfuck dumpster fire of a campaign launch" was all the evidence needed that DeSantis lacked the "x factors" — the unknowable campaign environment that rewards "improvisation and gut intuition over wonky nerds who rely on calculated, focus-grouped strategy."

- In Dan's thesis the launch didn't cause the doom — the doom was structural (Trump as variable). The #DeSaster just made it visible early.
