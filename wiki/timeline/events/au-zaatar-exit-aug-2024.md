> **Incidental entry** — The August 2024 end of Dan's 41-month Au Za'atar tenure. Complete at this size; one dated first-person disclosure carries the event.

In August 2024 Dan's 41-month tenure at Au Za'atar ended. Per the source page, the end came with the outdoor-dining shed shutdown: demand for the shed died first once the interior opened, and the city shed tear-down finished it. The source rules the end involuntary, citing hours cut June–August 2024, resumes from July 9, and unemployment filed August 8 (dat:0452).

## The record in order

- **June–August 2024** — hours cut (page claim, via the dossier cited in dat:0452).
- **August 8, 2024** — unemployment filed (page claim, dat:0452).
- **August 11, 2024** — Tarik inbound "grab my check" (held iMessage, dat:1258).
- **August 21, 2024, 9:53–9:54 PM EDT** — Dan's first-person disclosure to [[wiki/people/dimitri|Dimitri]]: *"By the way they officially let me go from AZ"*; *"Tarik was really really nice about it and everything though. We parted on good terms"*; *"And I am already getting unemployment so it didn't cause me too many problems financially"* (dat:0443, held-corpus verified).
- **August 21, 2024 (evening EDT)** — Tarik inbound "CIA agent anymore" joke (dat:1258).

The August 21 disclosure is the event's primary corroboration: a held iMessage, timestamped to the minute. What the record cannot establish from held sources: the shed tear-down date itself and any final-paycheck documentation. The involuntariness ruling and the June–August hours-cut sequence rest on the page/dossier whose underlying sources are unheld (dat:0452).
