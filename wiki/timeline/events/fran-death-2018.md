# Death of Fran Coldren (April 4, 2018)

> **Incidental entry** — The dated death record for Fran Coldren; complete at this size because the full vigil narrative already lives on its own page.

[[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Fran Coldren]] — Jesse Frances Thomas, born August 15, 1920 in Fort Martin, West Virginia, Dan's maternal great-grandmother and the person he names as the biggest influence of his life — died on **April 4, 2018** at Uniontown Hospital. Dan and [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie Ulmer]] were the two people in the room. Dan's contemporaneous verdict was that *"fran was happy when she died SPECIFICALLY because annie and i were there with her"* (kb/events/2018-fran-death.md; dat:0170).

This page is the compact event record: the date, how the date is fixed, and where the fuller accounts sit. The four weeks that led here — the falls, the nursing-home placement, the 24-hour hospital rotation, and the deathbed hours — are carried in full at [[wiki/timeline/events/fran-death-vigil|The Fran Vigil (March–April 2018)]] and, for the fall night itself, at [[wiki/timeline/events/the-fall-of-fran|The Fall of Fran]]. Nothing below duplicates that narrative beyond what the date needs.

## What happened

Fran died in the Uniontown Hospital pediatric wing, where she had been admitted on April 1, 2018 to die, after the March 7–8, 2018 fall ("Down Goes Frazier") removed her from [[wiki/places/117-belmont-circle|117 Belmont Circle]] and a nursing-home placement failed to hold her. A memorial celebration followed at the Uniontown Country Club on May 6, 2018, framed as a happy occasion Fran would have approved of (dat:0170).

## The record in order

- **March 7–8, 2018** — the dated fall night; the next morning's messages document the ambulance ("she was screaming so loud when they were putting her on the gurney it was just awful," March 8) (dat:0169).
- **March 29, 2018** — an eviction notice terminating Dan's residency at 155 Virginia Ave was served mid-vigil (dat:0169; dat:0172).
- **April 1, 2018** — admission to the pediatric wing; a 24-hour rotation was mandated and held by Dan, Annie, Vicki, and Marla (dat:0169; dat:0170).
- **April 3, 2018** — Diane Moore's exclusion letter, setting rules for Dan's presence and demanding the keys, was being read and lawyered that day (dat:0172).
- **April 4, 2018, 07:23 EST** — Marla's *"She made it thru the nite,"* the last message of its kind, verified in the held corpus (dat:0170).
- **April 4, 2018** — Fran died, Dan and Annie present. Davey Fitzpatrick's unprompted *"Sorry for your loss bro"* followed on April 5 (dat:0170).
- **May 6, 2018** — memorial celebration, Uniontown Country Club (dat:0170).

## Conflicts in the record

- **April 1 vs. April 4 (resolved).** An earlier synthesis dated the death to April 1. April 1 was the pediatric-wing admission; April 4 was the death, fixed by Marla's April 4 message and Davey Fitzpatrick's April 5 condolence, both verified in the held corpus (dat:0170).
- **2017 vs. 2018 (resolved).** Two 2026 captures date the fall-and-vigil sequence to March–April 2017. Seven independent records date it to 2018, including the Danny Matthews thread opening February 16, 2018 and the March 29 eviction notice (dat:0494). The 2018 dating stands.
- **The death-moment account and its retraction (both kept).** On April 5, 2018 Dan told the silent-communication story — the request for a sign, the blink, the smile. Three hours later the same night he retracted the supernatural reading after reviewing video showing a monitor alarm for a dropping pulse, adding that it *"def isn't as 'supernatural' knowing that fact"* but did not take away from the moment (dat:0446). Both halves are part of the record; neither is deleted.
- **Fall mechanics (open).** Whether a February 16 fall and a March 7–8 fall were two events or one event fused in retelling cannot be settled from the held corpus (kb/events/2018-fran-death.md). The March 7–8 spine is corpus-verified; the February choreography is attributed testimony.

Limits: the snow outside the hospital, the Lucie Dobbin message text, and the memorial invitation rest on uploads and captures not held in this repository (dat:0170). The $15/hour, six-month caregiving figure is Dan's contemporaneous account; no payroll record was checked (dat:0169).
