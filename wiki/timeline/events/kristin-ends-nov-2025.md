> **Incidental entry** — The end of Dan's relationship with Kristin Prentiss in November–December 2025, closing with her December 9, 2025 block. Complete at this size; the $40 framing is the source page's, carried as testimony.

The Kristin relationship ended in November 2025. The source page frames the cause as a dispute over **$40**, across two message beats on **November 2, 2025 (14:33 ET)** and **November 4, 2025 (21:56–21:57 ET)** (dat:0803). That causal framing is the page's, carried as page-attributed testimony; what the $40 consisted of is not independently corroborated in the held record.

November ran 53 messages — the withdrawal month, measured exactly (dat:0801).

## The record in order

- **November 2, 2025, 14:33 ET** — the first $40 message (19:33 UTC held, dat:0803).
- **November 4, 2025, 21:56–21:57 ET** — the second $40 beat (dat:0803).
- **November 2025** — 53 messages (dat:0801).
- **December 9, 2025** — a 171-row burst ending in the terminal sixteen minutes (18:40–18:56 ET), verified beat for beat: her IC3 complaint link (23:45:24 UTC), the IPR Center referral form (23:51:04 UTC), her block threat — *"All this is screenshotted. Blocking you now. Don't contact me again or an officer will be reaching out. Thank you"* (23:55:49 UTC) — against his *"mr. officer i live at 337 saratoga drive"* (23:56:19 UTC) (dat:0803). No charges are documented on either side.
- **December 10, 2025, 20:29 ET** — the thread's final held row (dat:0801).

A corpus analysis dated 2026-08-16 in the source moved the relationship's end to November 2025; that re-analysis is reported in dat:0304 and is not re-verifiable from the held material cited here. Whether the final December 10 row is a post-block message from Kristin or Dan's outbound tail is an open question in the source.
