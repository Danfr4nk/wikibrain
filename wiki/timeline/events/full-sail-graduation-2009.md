# Full Sail Graduation (September 2009)

> **Incidental entry** — The September 2009 Recording Arts graduation and the audit/certification months after it; complete at this size because four dated artefacts and one correction are the whole event.

Dan graduated from Full Sail University in Winter Park, Florida, with a Recording Arts degree in **September 2009** — top 5% of his class, Pro Tools HD certified on the canonical record — then stayed on through December auditing one class, running labs, and finishing his Pro Tools operator certification (kb/events/2009-09-full-sail-graduation.md). The September date is Dan's own correction to this wiki, made September 9, 2026, when he read the published site and found the month wrong (dat:0058).

The period context — enrolment from September 2008, the Florida years, and the February 2010 departure for New York — is at [[wiki/timeline/periods/full-sail-2008-2010|Full Sail (Sep 2008 – Mar 2010)]]. This page is the graduation itself and why its date is now settled.

## What happened

Dan finished the Recording Arts degree in September 2009. He did not leave Winter Park: for the next three months he audited a class, ran labs, and completed certification work, writing about labs, a practical, and a final exactly as if still enrolled — because from inside, it was just Tuesday (dat:0058).

## The record in order

- **September 2008** — begins Full Sail, Recording Arts, Winter Park, Florida (kb/events/2009-09-full-sail-graduation.md, canonical record).
- **August 31, 2009** — tweet: *"i start my last month of college tomorrow"* — pointing at a September finish. The quotation is the prior wiki's transcription; the tweet archive is not held here (dat:0058).
- **September 26, 2009** — *"I acually just graduated today for music production,"* said the same day to two people in two Facebook messages, read directly from the archive (dat:0045; dat:0058).
- **November 30 – December 19, 2009** — Pro Tools lab, practical, "in class," and an AWS final in the tweets: the post-graduation audit, labs, and certification work, not continued enrolment (dat:0058).
- **January 26, 2010** — Pro Tools certification dated, per the Full Sail period page (kb/events/2009-09-full-sail-graduation.md).
- **February 28, 2010** — leaves Florida for New York City (canonical record).

## Conflicts in the record

- **August vs. September (resolved to September).** The prior wiki carried "graduated August 2009" and scored the testimony *"graduated Full Sail (AS Recording Arts) in August 2009"* as refuted. September is not August, so the refutation stands — but its stated reason does not: it refuted August on the grounds that he was in school until December. The correct grounds are that he finished in September, one month later than remembered, not four months earlier than the record appeared to show (dat:0058).
- **September vs. December "tension" (dissolved).** The published framing of Dan as *"still enrolled in December"* treated coursework as degree enrolment. Auditing a class, running labs, and finishing a certification is the one state of the world in which the August tweet, the September messages, and the November–December tweets are all true at once (dat:0058).
- **Limits.** No transcript, diploma, or certification record is held. Top-5%-of-class is from the longstanding canonical record, not a held primary source. A Full Sail transcript or a dated Avid operator credential would move the date from attributed testimony to first-party record (kb/events/2009-09-full-sail-graduation.md; dat:0058).

What the November–December audit class was, and whether the January 26, 2010 certification is the same certification the December work was toward, remain open (kb/events/2009-09-full-sail-graduation.md).
