# April–May 2026 — The Final Documented Weeks

The March 16, 2026 retraction — Annie's "I think that I had misunderstood the conversation" — was treated for months as the end of the Dan–Annie text record. It was not. A later analysis session, working from the same message corpus, extends the documented timeline seven more weeks, to May 6, 2026: roughly three and a half weeks before the June 1 group-chat closure that finally ended the relationship. This page covers that gap — the last stretch of the relationship's written record before the closing confrontation.

It is the ugliest stretch of the file. The communication did not decline steadily; it oscillated with a worsening floor — one week in late April where Annie out-messaged Dan, the only such week in the entire extended record, bracketed by ratios climbing past four to one against her. May is rated the worst period in the full corpus not by volume but by content, closing in the early hours of May 6 with the most explicit and sustained suicidal language Dan sent in the entire relationship record. And inside these seven weeks sit three of the record's sharpest single rows: Dan annotating the relationship with its own closing date ("25 Nov 2015 – 04 May 2026"), Annie's location being shared with a third party while refused to him, and a man twice calling Suz's number overnight looking for Annie on a night she was unaccounted for. This window is also where the terminal phase's conflict machinery reached a family member directly — the only documented time it did — and where Dan, in the fallout, had an AI draft him a letter to his own mother that he then could not send in his own voice.

## Where this record comes from

This page's underlying evidence is thinner than most timeline pages. Its chronology was built from a single chat-based analysis session rather than a directly re-verified raw CSV extract: the underlying message log for April 1–May 6, 2026 has not been independently located and cross-checked in raw/, and the two analysis-session sources this page originally cited no longer exist in the current corpus. That provenance limit is carried openly through the sections below — dated, quoted messages are flagged where a later pass independently verified them against raw CSVs, and claims resting only on the single session are labeled as the session's readings.

Three rows from this window did get independent verification. The 2026-07-18 primary-count pass located the April 1, May 4, and May 5 rows verbatim in `imessage_2124702449_both_all_now.csv` with timestamps and direction tags, and the moral verdict built on the fallout adopted those rows selectively while explicitly excluding other claims from the same source session. Those verifications are the page's load-bearing rows. Everything around them — the ratio figures, the dream narrative, the Betrayal Catalog, the forensic read of the impersonation campaign — is carried at the confidence its source supports: the session's reading unless the verdict's pass independently anchored it. Where the record cannot establish a fact (the identity of the April caller, the Volvo, the pregnancy mention), this page says so in the relevant section rather than laundering the uncertainty.

## April 1: a man calls Suz twice looking for Annie

The window opens at 12:57 AM on April 1, 2026, with a message from Dan — verified verbatim in the verdict pass as `2026-04-01 00:57:32 | Sent | "Your mom calls my mom at 730 and said you n… (a guy looking for you)"`. A man had called Suz's number twice that night — once at 8:30, once at 10:00 — looking for Annie, on a night Annie was unaccounted for. Dan's framing in the message is accusation and bewilderment at once. Nothing about the caller's identity, his business with Annie, or where she was that night is established anywhere in the corpus.

The thread ran through April 8 — the session records Dan, a week later, still "just refusing to discuss or give me any clarity aout who the fuck was calling my mom" — and then went dormant. It predates and is separate from the confirmed June 1 third party ([[wiki/people/tuquick-17248123683|Tuquick]], later identified as Jerel Wayne Coles). The caller is never identified in the corpus.

## April 2: "No I am not done"

On April 2, in the record's standing direct-answer catalog, Annie answered a pressed question about whether she was done with Dan: *"No I am not done."* The catalog on [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]] treats her late-era "want to be together" statements as answers rather than announcements — every one produced inside a conflict or reconciliation attempt, following an accusation, a goodbye, or a direct demand, none with a behavior attached in the same breath. The April 2 answer is the fifth and latest such statement in the catalog (2025-08-11, 2025-08-23, 2025-08-27, 2026-02-02, 2026-04-02). Each of the earlier answers reversed within days against the next day's behavior. The April 2 answer sits at the head of this page's window, and the seven weeks that follow are the catalog's coda: the answers existed; none survived contact with what came next.

## April 20–26: the last reinvestment window

April 20–26 is the one week in the entire extended record where Annie out-messaged Dan — 206 received against 164 sent, a 0.80 ratio. Everywhere else in the terminal phase the ratio runs the other way, usually past three to one. The week opened with a warm, playful, detailed dream narrative from Annie about their dogs on a golf course, and the session reads the week as producing enough genuine goodwill that it visibly re-engaged Dan for the weeks that followed.

The ratio collapsed back to 3.36:1 the very next week. In the session's reading, the April 20–26 stretch was not a turning point but a reprieve — the final documented inversion before the floor dropped out again. It matters for what it establishes about the mechanism underneath: Annie's responsiveness was the only lever that re-engaged him, and by late April it was available only in isolated weekly bursts. The same week is the last time in the record where her output exceeded his.

## The late-April re-fire: hostile contact, Suz insulted, and the letter Dan couldn't send

The April 1 caller pattern re-fired in roughly late April, documented in an AI-chat session that self-references the April 1–8 thread as "three weeks" prior. This time someone using Annie's number called Suz directly, insulted her personally — the session records the caller "called her a junkie" — and sent Dan a message reading *"you had guys molest me"*. Dan, in the same session, explained that line as a garbled hostile reference to the couple's non-monogamous arrangement, one he maintains was consensual.

Suz's reaction was rage serious enough to threaten arrest-worthy action — the session has her telling Dan "if I call you, call the supplier to come bail me out" — and the fallout produced a live screaming match between Dan and his mother over whose grievance mattered more. The session frames that fight as a real-time instance of the same conflict-engine mechanism documented at scale in the Annie relationship: a resolution-seeking system misreading an emotional bid as a proposition to be litigated, now running between Dan and his own mother, with Suz on record in the March pages as increasingly unsympathetic to Annie ("Karma is a bitch. All I'm saying," on March 20).

The same session built a forensic read arguing the hostile caller was not Annie personally. The argument rests on three signals: the hostile messages consistently misnamed Annie's Suboxone regimen as "heroin/Percocets" — Annie would know the actual medication; a narrative-voice register mismatch; and the total absence of Dan's name from the hostile messages, a signature the analysis treats as corpus-level (Annie's own hostile register, across the full record, always names him). The session explicitly leaves unresolved whether Annie was aware of the impersonation, complicit in it, or an unwitting source of the caller's close household knowledge — the caller knew the water had been shut off — and whether her family even owns the Volvo the impersonator's message referenced. The caller's identity is not established anywhere in the corpus, and this predates the confirmed June 1 third party. Two months later, on July 26, the pattern recurs in recognizable shape: a third party messaging Dan directly from inside Annie's situation, calling her a slut from her own number.

The impersonation campaign's one verified artifact on another person is the AI-drafted letter to Suz. Dan had an AI draft him a letter to his mother, explicitly authorized to include any criticism, and then declined to send it in his own voice. The collaborative-analysis synthesis on the wiki reads that moment as genuinely vulnerable first-person material Dan outsourced — not the memory or the analysis, but the authorship of vulnerability itself — and rates it the clearest instance of that outsourcing in the record. It sits inside this window as the one place where the terminal-phase machinery produced self-recognition rather than another loop.

## May 4–5: the self-periodization, the new job, the GPS row

May 4, at 8:10 PM, Dan sent the bare timestamp — verified verbatim at `2026-05-04 20:08:01 | Sent` in the verdict's raw pass, with the fuller text the pass recovered: *"25 Nov 2015 - 04 May 2026 Now you can be happy…"*. Not an ultimatum, not a goodbye: an annotation. Dan dated the relationship and closed the parenthesis in his own framing, the only instance in the full record of him doing this unprompted. The November 25, 2015 start date is his own periodization of the relationship's beginning, one day off the corpus's earliest held message (2015-11-28) and the same Nov 2015 origin the record consistently carries.

The May 4 evening carried warmth — the session's note for that night reads "I am not okay with that" as Annie's response to a crisis statement — and the next morning the procurement pattern ran exactly on schedule: a cardless-ATM-code request. That is the love-to-request sequence the terminal-phase catalog documents as running unchanged through this window, warmth at night followed by extraction at morning.

On the morning of May 5, in the same message exchange, Annie mentioned starting work at "Marucas" — a new job — and the session's sequencing note matters: she opened the exchange with a drug-supply-chain logistics request, meaning the request preceded the job announcement. The new income source reduced her financial dependency on the Uniontown supply chain in the weeks immediately before the June 1 closure. That same day she arrived without her phone, collected supply, and left — the phoneless pickup the session records as routine by this point.

At 3:09 PM on May 5, Dan wrote the row the verdict later verified verbatim at `2026-05-05 15:09:50 | Sent`: *"stay away from me you liar I can not believe you would give someone else your location after what you did to me."* This is the first documented instance of Annie's location access being actively extended to someone else while still refused to Dan — a materially different finding from the prior "she just turned it off" reading, and it eliminates any residual privacy-as-principle explanation for the GPS removal. Who the third party was is not named in the row; the record establishes the sharing, not the recipient.

## May 6, 3:30–5:57 AM: the worst three hours on record

May is rated the worst period in the full record by content rather than volume, and the three hours of 3:30–5:57 AM on May 6 are the reason. Across that window Dan sent the most explicit and sustained suicidal language in the entire corpus: *"I wish I were fucking dead after today,"* *"I don't think I'll ever be able to feel happy or okay ever again,"* *"I don't want to do this anymore,"* *"this is going to end so fucking badly I'm losing my fucking mind."* Annie's documented responses in that window: *"Can we please just talk tomorrow Dan,"* *"Please Dan,"* *"I am not okay with that,"* *"STOP IT DAN,"* *"wtf Dan"* — the non-substantive pattern the terminal-phase record documents across twelve crisis statements from Dan, now in its 37th week.

The final message of this extract, at 5:57 AM on May 6: *"IF YOU DON'T WANT TO BE WITH ME YOU HAVE TO SAY SOMETHING. I AM NOT GOING TO FUCKING PROCESS ANYTHING UNLESS YOU DO THAT. You don't get it this is going to end so fucking badly I'm losing. My fucking. Mind."* The demand at the center of it — that she say, plainly, whether she wants to be with him — had gone unanswered across the record's entire direct-answer catalog. Every answer she had given ("No I am not done" on April 2 being the latest) had been extracted under pressure and reversed within days; the standing indecision register ("trying to figure out and find myself," "I'm just lost and confused") offered no verdict either. May 6 is Dan asking for the one thing the record shows she would not give him: a plain, durable, behavior-backed decision, in either direction.

Three and a half weeks later, on June 1, that "something" arrived — in a group chat, from third parties rather than from Annie — and closed the relationship ([[wiki/timeline/events/group-chat-closure]]). Four days after that, on June 5, came the only unprompted acknowledgment in the record: *"Daniel, i just want to say that i am extremely sorry."*

## What the terminal-phase mechanisms did in this window

The mechanisms documented for August 2025–March 2026 on [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]] did not relax in the final seven weeks; the session reads them as worsening. The love-to-request procurement pattern (evening warmth, morning extraction), the moral-debt pivot, and the crash-out trap all continued unchanged — the May 4 evening / May 5 morning ATM-code sequence and the May 5 phoneless supply run are the window's exhibits. The ratio, climbing past 4:1 in the most recent full week measured, and the late-night-availability gap — roughly a 6:1 divergence between Dan's and Annie's overnight output — both worsened across the window.

The May 6, 3:30–5:57 AM crisis exchange is the crash-out-trap mechanism at its terminal expression: bursts of explicit suicidal language following Annie's silence, met with the non-substantive response pattern, continuing the dynamic the March pages document (ten-or-more-consecutive-message bursts, all preceded by her silence). What changed across this window was not the mechanism but the floor — the content rating the session gives May has no precedent in the earlier record.

## Conflicts in the record

**The page's two provenance claims do not fully reconcile.** The body states the whole extract comes from one chat-based analysis session, and that the underlying message log for April 1–May 6, 2026 has not been independently located and cross-checked in raw/. But the typed `evidences` edge to [[wiki/mind/synthesis/dan-annie-fallout-verdict]] states the May 5 GPS row and the May 4 self-periodization are [RAW-CSV] verified. The most conservative consistent reading, and the one carried here: specific rows were later verified against raw CSVs (the 2026-07-18 pass located the April 1, May 4, and May 5 rows verbatim in `imessage_2124702449_both_all_now.csv`), while the full seven-week log was never independently re-extracted. The body's weaker claim governs the page as a whole; the three rows are the exceptions. (Datum kb/data/0256-april-may-2026-final-weeks.md, 2026-09-09.)

**The April 1 row's wording differs between the two sources that carry it.** This page's body renders Dan's message as "my mom gets a call from a guy looking for you at 8:30. He calls again at 10:00. What the FUCK could that mean." The verdict's [RAW-CSV] anchor for the same timestamp (`2026-04-01 00:57:32 | Sent`) reads "Your mom calls my mom at 730 and said you n… (a guy looking for you)". Both agree on the substance — a man twice called Suz's number looking for Annie on the night of March 31/April 1 — but the verbatim strings do not match, which is a reminder that the session's quotations and the raw CSV are not the same evidence layer. The verdict's row governs the wording.

**The May 4 row carries more text than this page originally quoted.** The page's body cites the bare timestamp "25 Nov 2015 – 04 May 2026". The [RAW-CSV] anchor shows the full message continued: *"25 Nov 2015 - 04 May 2026 Now you can be happy…"*. The tail does not change the finding (the unprompted self-periodization stands), but the fuller text is the row.

**The source session's excluded claims stay excluded.** The same single session that produced this window also claimed a 14-incident "Betrayal Catalog," a formal BPD-criteria assessment, and — flagged once, not elaborated — a pregnancy (the verdict notes the pregnancy reference appears in two chat-analyses only, with a claimed due date of Nov 18). None are corroborated elsewhere in the wiki's sources; the verdict explicitly excluded the Betrayal Catalog and pregnancy material as uncorroborated and adopted only the two rows above. This page does not adopt them pending verification against primary message data. They are recorded here as the exclusion list, not as facts.

**The exit/relapse arithmetic is re-scoped.** Dan's standing correction (2026-10-04): he and Annie were stable and never split until 22 Feb 2025; all the exits and relapses in the record came after that date. This window (April–May 2026) sits inside that post-Feb-2025 period. The "127 exits" figure the verdict cites and the 37th-week dating of the crisis-response pattern should both be read under that scoping — the terminal window is the post-stable window, not the whole relationship.

**The March 16 "retraction" was the analysis record's end, not the relationship's.** The March page treated March 16's "I misunderstood the conversation" as the most-analyzed close point of the documented record; this page's existence is the correction — the text record demonstrably continued seven more weeks. No claim here supersedes March's analysis; it extends its timeline.

**The "Tuquick" identity on linked pages has been merged.** This page's original text referenced the June 1 third party as Tuquick; that identity was unmasked 2026-08-08 as Jerel Wayne Coles (see kb/events/2026-05-jerel-coles-entanglement.md). The April caller remains a separate, unidentified figure — predating and distinct from Coles.

**The source references in frontmatter are unresolved.** The two dox-md analysis sessions this page was originally sourced from no longer exist in the current corpus. The warnings stay visible above; the page now also cites the surviving provenance datum and the verdict's verbatim anchors.

## Assessment

The record supports a judgment about this window, and the wiki has already made it in pieces. The moral verdict adopted the May 5 GPS-shared-with-a-third-party row and the May 4 self-periodization as its [RAW-CSV]-verified core for the terminal defamation case, while explicitly excluding the same source's uncorroborated remainder — the split adoption is itself the finding: two rows carry weight; the rest of the session's claims do not. May is the worst period in the full corpus by content, not volume, and the 3:30–5:57 AM window on May 6 is the terminal expression of the crash-out-trap mechanism that ran the whole relationship. What this window establishes that no earlier window did: Annie's location access was extended to someone else while refused to Dan (the privacy-as-principle reading is dead), Dan dated the relationship and closed the parenthesis himself, and the conflict machinery reached a family member directly for the only time on record. Three and a half weeks later the group chat closed it — and on June 5 came the record's only unprompted apology.
