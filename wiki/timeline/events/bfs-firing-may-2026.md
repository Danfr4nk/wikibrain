> **Incidental entry** — Dan's termination from BFS Foods in mid-May 2026 over a disputed $50 drawer shortage. Complete at this size; the underlying dispute documents are not held in this repository.

Dan was hired in late April 2026 as a gas-station cashier at BFS Foods in Uniontown — the Bruceton Farm Service convenience store and company-owned Little Caesars in the same building. The stint lasted roughly a month. It ended after an incident shift in mid-May, reconstructed as Sunday, May 17, 2026, and a disputed $50 drawer shortage delivered off-books the following Monday.

## What happened

On the incident shift Dan took over at 3 PM. That night assistant manager Brandon Hill closed the shift, printed the CSR — which read even — then called Dan in back with a demand, as Dan recounts it: *"your drawer was short last night, I paid it out of my own pocket, you owe me… that's money that would go to food for my daughter."* GM Anita was the locus of the shortage claim behind Brandon's demand (dat:0496, dat:0206, dat:0249).

The next day, Monday, at 5 PM Anita told Dan "you work at 8 tonight" — a cut from 36 hours to 7 — and only afterward did Brandon deliver the $50 demand. Brandon later volunteered in a 12:50 AM text that the reduction was because "Anita was in one of her moods" (dat:0496).

Dan's own account of his position is on the record in his words: *"i didn't refuse to pay it and i am not even sure when my hours were cut exactly. i knew it had to be after friday night but before monday."* No deduction was taken. The associated WPCL claim was never escalated past the dispute, and the final payroll disposition — whether the $50 was ever paid or pursued — is not recorded in the held material.

## The record in order

- **Late April 2026** — hired as gas-station cashier.
- **~May 13, 2026** — Nick Mattie sighting, used in the source as a temporal anchor (dat:0496).
- **May 15, 2026** — a delivery promise dated to that Friday, part of the source's dating chain.
- **Sunday, mid-May (reconstructed as May 17)** — the incident shift; Brandon closes and prints an even CSR.
- **Monday, mid-May** — the 36-to-7 hours cut at 5 PM, then the $50 demand.

What the record cannot establish: the exact incident-shift date (May 17 is reconstructed from the May 13, May 15, and May 20 anchors, not primary), and Anita's surname or corporate position. The BFS dispute documents in `raw/legal/bfs-dispute/` are not held in this repository; the quotes above are carried from the prior wiki text as cited in dat:0206 and dat:0249.
