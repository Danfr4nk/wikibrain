> **Incidental entry** — Kristin Prentiss's entry into Dan's life in August 2025, through Tom Maison's social circle. Complete at this size; the introduction itself is page-attributed, and the first held message is dated September 1, 2025.

Kristin Prentiss entered Dan's life in August 2025 through [[wiki/people/tom|Tom Maison]]'s social circle — Tom is the record's named introducer. No held record documents the introduction itself; the month-level dating is the wiki's relationship framing (kb/events/2025-08-kristin-starts.md).

The held iMessage thread opens **2025-09-01 10:07:17 UTC** with its first held row (dat:0801). The thread is the most exhaustively counted in the record: **20,014 rows — 10,102 sent / 9,912 received** (dat:0801).

## The record in order

- **August 2025 (month)** — entry via Tom's circle. Page-attributed testimony, not a dated row.
- **September 1, 2025, 10:07:17 UTC** — first held row of the Kristin thread (dat:0801).
- **September 11, 2025, 08:28:08 UTC / September 13, 2025, 15:49:58 UTC** — Kristin's two self-attestation sends, including *"I just don't know how you expect me, Kristin Prentiss - to believe that you, Dan Frank are sufficiently healed…"* and *"The thing about Kristin Prentiss is I am extremely good at hiding my emotions"* (dat:0802). The surname rests on this first-person self-naming.

The relationship ran roughly ten weeks, front-loaded in volume, with a November withdrawal (53 messages in November 2025, dat:0801) and a December end, covered at [[wiki/timeline/events/kristin-ends-nov-2025|Kristin ends]].

What the record cannot establish: the exact meeting date and circumstances in August 2025 — no dated primary row exists before September 1, 2025.
