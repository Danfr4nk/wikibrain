# March 2026 — The Terminal Phase Peak

The last sixteen days of the documented Dan–[[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] text record (which runs
November 28, 2015 – March 16, 2026) contain the relationship's most
concentrated fortnight: the window where the dynamic the dossiers had
tracked for years ran at full load and, in one forty-eight-hour stretch,
produced the record's sharpest single sequence. March 1 opens with Dan's
explicit crisis statement met, the analysis argues, without care; March 10
compresses the whole bond into a single physical action at a bathroom door;
March 11 strips the re-entry down to a bare request with no buffer left;
and March 12–16 delivers the confession pair — "YES DAN" asked, answered,
walked back in person, and formally retracted in writing — the event the
dossiers rate the single most concentrated gaslighting act in the decade.

The formal closure came later — June 1, 2026, in a group chat
([[wiki/timeline/events/group-chat-closure]]) — and a later analysis pass
extended the text record seven more weeks past March 16
([[wiki/timeline/events/april-may-2026-final-weeks]]), but March is where
the machinery is most legible: extraction, ambiguity, and the systematic
prevention of the one honest conversation that could have ended things
cleanly. What the sixteen days hold together is a pattern visible across
years — the crash-out trap, the block-unblock loop running in both
directions, the procurement rhythm of evening warmth and morning request —
executed here with the fewest buffers the record shows.

This page's sources are layered, and the page carries the layers openly.
The narrative detail comes from the dossier analysis (the DanAnnie docx
set, whose originals are unresolved in the current corpus); the
load-bearing message rows were independently verified against the held
iMessage corpus by a 2026-07-18 primary-count pass
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/dan-annie-fallout-verdict]]) and a 2026-09-09
timestamp-verification datum (kb/data/0359), which resolve the
crisis-statement, laundry-response, gas-station, confession, retraction,
and Karma timestamps to the minute. Aggregate figures — the burst counts,
the silences, the crisis-statement tally — remain the dossiers' attributed
analysis, flagged as such wherever they appear.

## March 1–2: the crisis statement and the laundry response

On the evening of March 1, 2026, at 19:41 EST, Dan sent the exchange this
window is known for — verified verbatim in the raw pass as `2026-03-01
19:41:05 | Sent | I am going to fucking kill myself I can not deal with
you doing this to me`. Annie's documented reply came at 22:17 that night:
`Jesus Christ I can't get my laundry out and put away without getting 7
texts Dan` — nearly three hours later, a complaint about laundry. (This
page previously rendered the complaint as arriving one minute after the
statement; the timestamped rows correct that — see Conflicts in the
record.)

The verdict pass pairs this exchange as the diagnosis-to-behavior gap
running in real time — the dossiers' term for the distance between stated
care and actual response. It is also the exemplar of the crisis-statement
category: the dossiers count twelve documented crisis statements from Dan
across the terminal phase, each met without care — carried here as the
dossiers' attributed figure, not re-computed from raw. The
complete-verdict passage in the dossiers hangs its conclusion on this
category: responding to "I want to die" with the name of a bar requires
having already stopped feeling the weight of the other person's pain.

## March 2–7: the unexamined days

No dated incidents from March 2–7 are carried in this page's sources. The
mechanical exhibits — burst events, silences, the extraction rhythm —
cover the full window in aggregate; the dated narrative the dossiers built
picks back up on March 8. The gap is stated rather than filled: what the
record cannot establish is noted here rather than smoothed over.

## March 8–9: the eulogy and the morning request

The night of March 8 reads in the dossiers' account as a relationship
eulogy — Dan explicitly releasing Annie with the specificity, the analysis
argues, of someone who has stopped hoping. The morning of March 9 opened
with Annie asking for a drug pickup as if the previous night had not
occurred — the love-to-request pivot in its shortest cycle. That evening,
at 20:24, Dan sent the apology the record treats as the pre-closure
window's final outbound note — verified in the raw pass as `2026-03-09
20:24:34 | Sent | i'm very sorry. that last message is not your problem…`
— apologizing, in the dossiers' framing, for having needs.

## March 10: the bathroom incident

March 10 was the convergence of two simultaneous crises: Dan was running
out of his Suboxone prescription (the dealer had refused to source more)
while in acute relational distress — 37 consecutive unanswered messages
between 6:26 and 8:25 PM asking one question: "JUST TELL ME IF SOMETHING
CHANGED." Mid-burst came her only substantive reply — verified in the raw
pass as `2026-03-10 18:56:39 | Received | Dan you know that I am always
completely understanding and try to be as helpful as I can…` — a
character reference for herself in the middle of his panic. Then: Annie
texted "Dan. Calm down. Please. I will be over." (7:37 PM), arrived, told
Dan she wanted to be together, went to the bathroom — and seconds later
attempted to slip out the front door without a word, caught before she
could. The log resumes at 8:01 PM in the same register of panic
("SERIOUSLY STOP MAKING ME CRASH OUT"). The analysis calls it the entire
dynamic in a single physical action — affirmation, extraction attempt,
disappearance, compressed into under five minutes: "The bathroom door is
the most honest thing Annie did in seven months."

## March 11: the goodbye and the gas-station re-entry

At 12:25 AM on March 11, Dan sent the most emotionally contained goodbye
on record: "I am done looking to you for any kind of relationship,
friendship or even just basic decent concern... You've been heard.
Goodbye." Her first contact came seventeen hours later and dropped every
buffer that had cushioned prior re-entries — no "I love you," no "can we
talk" — verified in the raw pass as `2026-03-11 17:25:03 | Received | So
is there anything I can get for you or your mom at the gas station so I
can get a line or two`. No acknowledgment of the goodbye; a drug request
as the re-entry vehicle. In the dossiers' account, the bareness reads as
proof the buffer was no longer necessary: prior exits had all resolved
without cost, so she no longer needed to disguise what the re-entry was
for.

The same day holds a genuine counterweight, and the page carries both
halves. On March 11, after dropping off cigarettes, Annie texted three
times that evening asking whether [[wiki/people/milo|Milo]] was scared or
crying — with no transaction pending and nothing requested. Both things
are true of the same person on the same day: the stripped-bare request in
the afternoon, and the unprompted care for the dog in the evening.

## March 12–16: the confession and the retraction

Asked directly whether "something has changed" in the relationship, Annie
answered "YES DAN" — twice, under sustained pressure, at 21:57 EDT on
March 12 (2026-03-13 01:57 UTC). It is the closest the record comes to an
honest answer in seven months — and the confession half of the pair
remains dossier-transcribed only; the raw verification pass confirmed the
retraction, not the confession, so the two halves carry different
evidence weights on this page. Dan's own reaction deserves equal
scrutiny: even holding the confirmation he had spent months seeking, his
first move was "Ok let me get the keys" — one more delivery run —
followed by "I wish you would have told me. But I appreciate an end." He
could not stop providing even with the evidence in hand that had justified
stopping long before. That night, in person, she walked the confession
back; four days later, on March 16, she formalized the retraction in
writing — verified in the raw pass as `2026-03-16 18:47:51 | Received |
I think that I had misunderstood the conversation when that happened Dan`.
Applied to a two-word exchange repeated twice under direct pressure, the
dossiers rate this retraction "factually incompatible with any ordinary
reading of language" and name it the terminal phase's most concentrated
gaslighting event — the moment the honest conversation was most available
and most actively prevented. This was the record's most-analyzed close
point, but not its actual end — see
[[wiki/timeline/events/april-may-2026-final-weeks]] for the documented
final seven weeks (April 1 – May 6), which run worse than anything above
before the June 1 closure.

## March 16: Dan begins paid work

One line in the chronology lands on March 16 without elaboration: Dan
begins paid work — the first appearance of paid work in the 14-month
record. The page carries the fact as the chronology states it; what the
work was, and its role in the fortnight, is not established in the sources
this page draws on.

## The Crash-Out Trap and the 36.5-hour silences

A "burst event" — ten or more consecutive messages from Dan without a reply
— occurred 94 times in the record. Annie deployed these bursts as evidence
of Dan's instability ("crash-outs"), but the honest framing in the
analysis is sequential — the same [[wiki/mind/concepts/forensic-method|forensic
insistence]] on establishing correct chronology over convenient narrative
that runs throughout the rest of the corpus: every burst was preceded by a
period of her silence; the burst is the end of a waiting period, not the
beginning of a story. The longest documented silence ran 36.5 hours and was re-entered
via a money request. The 94-burst count and the 36.5-hour figure are the
dossiers' attributed analysis, not re-computed here (kb-0359). The mechanism
exploited his documented architecture —
intolerance of open loops — guaranteeing hypervigilance until she chose to
re-enter on her own terms ([[wiki/mind/concepts/conflict-architecture]]).

## The procurement grammar: warmth at night, request at morning

Running underneath the dated incidents is a standing rhythm the record
shows in its shortest cycles this fortnight: evening warmth, morning
extraction. The eulogy night of March 8 is followed by a March 9 morning
drug-pickup request as if the previous night had not occurred; the March
11 goodbye is followed seventeen hours later by the gas-station
request; Dan's "Ok let me get the keys" on March 12 shows his own side
of the grammar — provision deployed reflexively, even with the
confirmation in hand that justified stopping. The companion April–May
page carries the pattern continuing past this window unchanged: the May
4 evening / May 5 morning cardless-ATM-code sequence and the May 5
phoneless supply run are that window's exhibits
([[wiki/timeline/events/april-may-2026-final-weeks]]). It is not a
March-specific invention; it is the terminal phase's standing economy,
visible here with the fewest cushions.

## The bookends: February 21 and March 19–20

Two dated incidents extend the documented pattern on either side of the
March 1–16 window — one a week before it opens, one inside the otherwise
undocumented gap before the April record picks up. The February record
gives these fights their pressure context: February 2026 is the
chronology's peak month for Dan's verbal-abuse escalation (36 instances,
per the full-record count).

**February 21, 2026.** The day starts with unrelated household pressure —
Annie texts that morning that she'd "got screamed at again," that her own
mother called her "a thief," and that she'd "never in my life heard my mom
scream at anyone the way she just did." The afternoon runs ordinary
logistics (a suspected blister, pet food, a dinner-plans back-and-forth)
that curdles by early evening into the familiar interrogation pattern —
Annie pressing Dan for a straight answer on whether he's coming over,
Dan going quiet, Annie reading the silence as evasion. Something happens
around 8:37 PM that neither side names directly in text (Dan: "What did
you do that for") and both sides detonate at once: Annie threatens to
"literally fucking tell your parents what you do," blocks him, unblocks to
send a few final lines, and blocks again. In the extended written rant
that follows, she reframes the fight as exposure risk to her, not him — "I
do not give a FUCK about that. You're living a lie... Once everyone finds
out what you're doing — playing games with peoples families... and your
real experience (remember — you were doing IV drugs before you ever met me
and I was able to get you to stop)" — and, in the same breath, "You think
I'm worried people will find out I do drugs or my mom does and facilitated
you doing them" — an incidental admission, in her own words, that her
mother was a drug source for Dan, not just a household backdrop detail.
When Dan throws a suicide reference back at her ("SHUT THE FUCK UP THAT
YOU WORRY ABOUT ME KILLING MYSELF"), her only softening is a flat "Please.
Dan. Calm down" before the thread returns to mutual "goodbye"s and a
closing threat — "You'll be hearing ABOUT me. Not FROM me. Soon enough" —
the same blame-reversal and threat-of-exposure structure documented
throughout the terminal phase, roughly a week before this page's window
opens. The block-unblock loop page files this fight as the loop's
bidirectional exhibit — the operation as the bond's shared negotiating
grammar, not one party's private defect
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/block-unblock-loop]]).

**March 19–20, 2026** — inside the otherwise-undocumented gap between this
page (ending March 16) and [[wiki/timeline/events/april-may-2026-final-weeks]]
(starting April 1) — records another full-cycle blowup, this one running
in parallel with an ordinary [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]] thread about
pizza and a Spotify link, which sharpens the contrast. March 19 is routine
strain: a $50 shortfall for supply that Annie tries to close by "magically"
getting another fifty from her mother, a black-and-mild candy run, and a
canceled evening ("I cannot stay. My mom asked me to come home... Don't
hate me... Please"). It sours overnight; by March 20 morning Annie opens
with "Hey you know what's really weird? I don't suddenly feel any
different about you cheating on me and shit." By 9 PM she blocks him
("i'm blocking you and now you"), delivering a version of the eulogy
pattern seen elsewhere in the record almost verbatim early — "I knew
everything you were saying was lies... it was that you knew I would care
enough that if you just said 'I love you' I couldn't stop believing you...
I feel 100% sure now that you have been doing this to me and you knew the
whole time you would destroy me" — immediately followed, in the same
minute, by Dan asking Suz "She's gone already?" and Suz answering **"Karma
is a bitch. All I'm saying"** — Dan then sends Suz an unrelated Spotify
link ("Came out today") while Annie is mid-collapse in the adjacent thread.
The 2026-09-09 verification pass independently corroborates the
parallel-thread structure: the Karma row (2026-03-21 01:09 UTC = March 20
21:09 EDT) arrives from a number that is not Annie's — the exchange ran
in the adjacent Suz thread, exactly as the narrative claims (kb-0359).
Suz's words are hers, not his — hearsay, never Dan's voice; the page
treats them as her real-time read of Annie, nothing more. Annie re-opens
roughly twenty minutes later accusing him of lying about his whereabouts
("I would ask where you really are... you're not hiding it anymore") and
spends the rest of the night alternating between apology and accusation
before going quiet at 11:22 PM. Both bookend fights sit outside the March
1–16 day-by-day record above and are consistent with the pattern
documented there; the March 19–20 pair additionally shows Suz's
real-time, unsympathetic read of Annie surfacing well before the "Suzy"
call analysis on [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank]].

## Aftermath

The June 1 group chat confirmed what March had already shown
([[wiki/timeline/events/group-chat-closure]]). On June 5, 2026, Annie sent
a final post-closure message: "Daniel, i just want to say that i am
extremely sorry." It is the only unprompted acknowledgment in the record.

## Conflicts in the record

**The March 1 timing is corrected: the laundry complaint came hours
later, not one minute later.** This page previously rendered Dan's crisis
statement as drawing "What the hell Dan" — "and one minute later, 'Jesus
Christ I can't get my laundry out and put away without getting 7 texts
Dan.'" The timestamped rows resolve it: Dan's statement at `2026-03-01
19:41:05`, Annie's laundry complaint at 22:17 EST — a gap of roughly
two hours and thirty-six minutes (kb-0359; the verdict's 2026-07-18
pass). The finding (the diagnosis-to-behavior gap) is unaffected; the
compression was editorial, not evidential. Corrected 2026-10-07.

**The retraction was quoted in truncated form.** This page carried the
March 16 retraction as "I misunderstood the conversation." The verified
row reads in full: `2026-03-16 18:47:51 | Received | I think that I had
misunderstood the conversation when that happened Dan`. The body above now
carries the full text; the truncation is the minor fidelity note from
the kb-0359 pass.

**The "YES DAN" confession half is dossier-transcribed only; the
retraction is now [RAW-CSV].** The verdict pass (2026-07-18) is explicit:
the retraction row was located verbatim in `imessage_2124702449_both_all_now.csv`
and upgraded [DOSSIER]→[RAW-CSV], but the March 12 "YES DAN" confession
itself remains sourced only from the dossier texts ("report collection.txt"
/ "Attachment and Trust Breakdown.md"). The pair's two halves therefore
carry different evidence weights — stated above, not smoothed over.

**The two overlapping 14-day exports disagree on the final pre-closure
message date.** Whether the last pre-closure message fell on March 9 or
March 16 is not pinned down — both exports end on the same functional
note (Dan's apology for having needs, Annie's "always completely
understanding" self-testimonial), so the substance is unaffected
([[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]], Gaps).

**The aggregate figures are the dossiers' attributed analysis.** The
94-burst count, the 36.5-hour longest silence, and the twelve-crisis-statement
tally are not re-derived in the verification passes; they come from the
unheld dossier analysis and are filed as attributed counts (kb-0359).
They are not independently verified against the held corpus.

**The superlatives are the dossiers' ratings, not raw findings.** "The
most concentrated gaslighting event," "the diagnosis-to-behavior gap,"
"the most honest thing Annie did in seven months," and the March 8 "eulogy"
framing are the analysis layer's judgments. They are carried as the
dossiers' readings because that is what they are; the raw rows underneath
are timestamp-verified, the verdicts on top of them are not.

**March 16 was the analysis record's end, not the relationship's.** This
page long treated March 16's retraction as the most-analyzed close point
of the documented record; the April–May page's existence is the correction —
the text record demonstrably continued seven more weeks, running worse on
content. No claim here is superseded; the timeline is extended
([[wiki/timeline/events/april-may-2026-final-weeks]], Conflicts).

**The exit/relapse arithmetic is re-scoped.** Dan's standing correction
(2026-10-04): he and Annie were stable and never split until 22 Feb 2025;
all the exits and relapses in the record came after that date. The "127
exits" figure the March analysis cites, and the 37th-week dating of the
crisis-response pattern in the April–May page, should both be read under
that scoping — the terminal window is the post-stable window, not the
whole relationship.

## Assessment

Three judgments the record supports, and one it no longer supports.

First, March is the fortnight where the verdict's two halves are visible
in the same window: the March 1 crisis statement met with a laundry
complaint is the diagnosis-to-behavior gap running in real time, and the
March 12 "YES DAN" — walked back that night, formally retracted on March
16 — is the confession pair's retraction half now verified [RAW-CSV]. The
dossiers' rating of the retraction as the most concentrated gaslighting
act in the decade is analysis, not a raw finding — but it is analysis
the timestamps now support on the verified half of the pair.

Second, March 11 is the cleanest exhibit of the re-entry stripped of
buffer: seventeen hours after the most contained goodbye on record, the
first contact is a bare request with no acknowledgment of the goodbye —
and, the same evening, the genuine counterweight of the Milo texts, with
nothing pending and nothing requested. Both halves are on the record for
the same person on the same day, and any assessment has to carry both.

Third, the machinery was running in both directions by March. The
February 21 and March 19–20 fights show Annie deploying the block-unblock
operation the loop page documents as the bond's shared grammar — threats
of exposure, the block, the unblock to send final lines, the re-block —
not a defect unique to either side (see
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/block-unblock-loop]]).

What the record no longer supports is March 16 as the end. A later
analysis pass extended the text record seven more weeks, and the
April–May window runs worse on content — the March dynamics continuing
with a worsening floor. The March page's analysis is not superseded; its
timeline is extended. The ugliest weeks of the record came after the
most-analyzed ones.
