> **Incidental entry** — Dan's six months as paid full-time caregiver for his great-grandmother Fran Coldren, at $15/hour, ending April 4, 2018. Complete at this size; the duration and wage are Dan's own contemporaneous words, verified verbatim.

For the last six months of [[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Fran Coldren]]'s life, Dan was her paid full-time caregiver at $15 an hour. The source is Dan's own contemporaneous summary, sent April 6, 2018, two days after her death:

> *"she was a trip dude but i spent the past 6 months looking after her full time ($15 an hour to play video games with gram is a good deal) and we had a lot of fun. got good closure and finally did something in my life that wasn't completely selfish haha."*

The message is verified verbatim in the held iMessage corpus (message_id 153495, 2018-04-06 21:55:47 UTC = 17:55:47 EDT, outbound). The six-month duration and the $15/hour figure are his words, not the wiki's inference (dat:0446, dat:0708).

## The record in order

- **January 18, 2016** — the handyman arrangement, pre-caregiving: *"I went to grams and made a list of work to do for her tomorrow"* (dat:0446).
- **November 26, 2017** — the nursing-home fear is already live in Dan's messages (dat:0446).
- **~October 2017** — paid full-time caregiving begins. The start is back-computed from the April 6 "past 6 months"; the duration and wage are stated, the start month is inferred.
- **January 23, 2018** — inheritance anticipation appears in Dan's messages while Fran is alive (dat:0446).
- **March 9, 2018** — surgery; nursing home planned for Monday, March 12 (dat:0446).
- **April 1, 2018** — Fran moved to the Uniontown Hospital pediatric wing; a 24-hour rotation held by Dan, [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]], Vicki, and Marla (dat:0169).
- **April 4, 2018** — Fran dies, Dan and Annie in the room.
- **April 6, 2018** — Dan's closing verdict, verified in the held corpus: *"thanks buddy. gram did aaaaaanything i asked her to, so the least i could do was be there for her"* (19:22).

What the record cannot establish: who authorized and funded the $15/hour arrangement, whether Annie drew a separate wage, and the nursing home's name and exact admission date. The canonical record has Dan and Annie as the paid caregivers together; only Dan's wage is stated on record.
