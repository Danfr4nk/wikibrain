# Suboxone Day-Zero: Opioid Maintenance Begins, 17 February 2010

On February 17, 2010, at the tail end of the Full Sail window, Dan began the Suboxone maintenance regimen he has been on every day since [kb/events/2010-02-17-suboxone-start.md]. The KB event node states the run plainly: sixteen unbroken years with zero stated relapses to full opiate use and no recovery-program participation anywhere in the record, framed by Dan as engineered chemical architecture rather than recovery [kb/events/2010-02-17-suboxone-start.md; kb/syntheses/suboxone-16-years.md]. It is the longest-running deliberate intervention in the biography, and the record's most durable fact is the one it talks about least.

The date also closes an arc. The opiate use that began with Roxicet and OxyContin in 2007, five months to addiction at age eighteen to nineteen, relapsed hard at Thanksgiving 2009 through Alexis Armel's return. Stabilization arrived seven weeks after that episode. The day after day-zero, Dan boarded a train to New York [kb/events/2010-02-17-suboxone-start.md].

This page carries the start date, the dated sequence around it, and the provenance problem the KB keeps visible rather than resolving. The dose itself — what was taken, at what amount, across sixteen years — is carried on [[wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]], which inventories the single figure the corpus holds and the twelve bearings around it. This event page links to that inventory and does not duplicate it. What follows is the start, not the curve.

## What happened

Day-zero is February 17, 2010. The event node's confidence is high and its importance is five — the maximum the KB assigns — because the date anchors the maintenance synthesis and terminates the pre-stabilization arc in a single point [kb/events/2010-02-17-suboxone-start.md].

What happened on that date, in the record's own terms, is a beginning that was not recorded as a beginning. No clinical record of the induction exists anywhere on disk. No first prescription, no titration log, no account of the first week's doses appears in any held source [kb/events/2010-02-17-suboxone-start.md; wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]. The corpus names the start date of the run; it never names the start dose. The event is therefore a date with a provenance trail rather than a clinical episode with a chart, and the page holds it at exactly that standing.

The framing Dan gives the regimen — engineered chemical architecture, not recovery — is his, and the KB synthesis records it without endorsing the exemption it implies [kb/syntheses/suboxone-16-years.md]. The framing does work in the record: it exempts the regimen from recovery's evaluative frame (no program, no sponsor, no milestones to miss) while describing, accurately, the only self-intervention in the biography that has never failed. The 2020-2024 relationship hole, the two New York exits, the BFS firing, the August 2026 rupture — every other long system in the record broke or was broken. The maintenance did not [kb/syntheses/suboxone-16-years.md].

## The dated sequence

The sequence below is the event node's table, carried here with its sources attached. Each row names what the record holds for that date and what it does not.

2007 marks the arc's opening: first opiate use (Roxicet, then OxyContin) at age eighteen to nineteen, five months to addiction [kb/events/2010-02-17-suboxone-start.md, citing corpus__3.txt and wiki/health/chemical-architecture]. The KB does not date the addiction's onset more precisely than that window, and this page does not sharpen it.

Thanksgiving, November 26, 2009, is the final pre-stabilization episode, reached via Alexis's return [kb/events/2010-02-17-suboxone-start.md; dat:0244-alexis-armel-dating-and-commitment-speed]. The episode spans roughly twelve weeks from Thanksgiving to day-zero: thirty Roxicet pills, a Winter Park-to-Ocala supply run, a Christmas trip home [wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]. It is the episode the "almost five years" duration claim inside the 2013 dosage exchange contradicts, a tension the dose curve preserves and this page notes without resolving.

January 31, 2010 carries the sequence's first exclusion. In a Facebook message that day Dan writes, outbound, "i'll be back around the 22nd, and moving to NYC the first week in March" [kb/events/2010-02-17-suboxone-start.md; kb/data/0046-facebook-corroborates-two-ledger-adjudications.md]. The move is settled and unremarkable in the message — discussed as good news about catching up — so it cannot be the subject of the stressful-day tweet three weeks later. The ledger's reasoning on that exclusion, reached from the tweet archive alone, is corroborated here against a channel the ledger did not hold, fetched sixteen years later from a different company [kb/data/0046-facebook-corroborates-two-ledger-adjudications.md]. The same day's exchange also fixes a January floor for the pre-stabilization arc: on January 31 Dan asked Suzanne Frank to bring him five Vicodin for his back — pill use live two weeks before day-zero and gone within weeks after [wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve; dat:1879].

February 17, 2010 is day-zero. The same-day tweet reads "this is the most stressful day/decision of my life" — the prior wiki's testimony ledger scores the alignment as confirmed (t014) on the dated coincidence [kb/events/2010-02-17-suboxone-start.md; kb/data/0055-facebook-corroborates-the-2010-maintenance-start.md]. The tweet names nothing. The subject never names the decision, and the ledger states its own limit plainly: this is a dated alignment on an independent artefact, not a clinical record [kb/data/0055-facebook-corroborates-the-2010-maintenance-start.md]. What anchors the alignment is the exclusion of every other candidate — the move settled three weeks earlier — plus the seven-week gap since the final opiate episode [kb/events/2010-02-17-suboxone-start.md].

February 18, 2010 is the next-day corroboration. Rick Frank meets Dan in Pittsburgh; they board an Amtrak to Penn Station. Months of apartment hunting follow, including Rick's two-thousand-dollar-a-month "while you intern" offer, with a lease at 424 Bedford Avenue, Williamsburg, by June [kb/events/2010-02-17-suboxone-start.md, citing wiki/people/rick-frank and Gemini-58]. February 28 is the move to New York in the canonical record.

August 14, 2010, six months into the run, Dan tells Suzanne Frank: "btw tomorrow is 6 months since either of us have done anything in pill form. by far the longest that I've been able to make it last" [wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve; dat:1879]. The back-calculation pins last pill use to roughly February 15 — within days of day-zero. Two independent derivations converge on the same mid-February window: the tweet-bearing day-zero and this six-month anniversary. The scope is abstinence from pills, not the Suboxone regimen itself — the pre-Suboxone pill use the regimen replaced — and the dose content of the bearing is zero. Like day-zero, it dates the regimen's start, not its amount.

July 10, 2013 is the bracket. In a Facebook exchange that day Dan writes outbound: "i've been on maintainence for 3.5 years," "i was on the strips when i had a doctor in nyc" (past tense), "and i'm down to 2mg/day," "yes still doing subs," and, to the same counterparty, "if you're ever unloading or come in contact call me" [kb/events/2010-02-17-suboxone-start.md; kb/data/0055-facebook-corroborates-the-2010-maintenance-start.md]. Counting back three and a half years from July 2013 gives roughly January 2010 — a bracket, not a pinpoint, consistent with February 17 and equally consistent with December 2009 or March 2010, from a channel the ledger never held [kb/data/0055-facebook-corroborates-the-2010-maintenance-start.md]. The 2-milligram figure is the corpus's only dosage figure, stated once, in this exchange, and never again in sixteen years. Its full inventory — what the single point can support, what it cannot, and why the silence exists — is on [[wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]]. This page records only that the bracket exists and where it sits in the sequence: three and a half years after day-zero, the run is already presented as low, arrived-at, and continuing.

## The provenance problem, kept visible

The KB event node insists on this section, and this page keeps it where the node puts it: in the body, not in a footnote.

The day-zero date is not remembered. It is computed. Its origin is raw/self/dox-scan/Gemini-_58.txt: a model, reading email logs, told Dan that "on Thursday, February 18, you and your dad boarded an Amtrak train... bound for Penn Station," and Dan replied: "NO no - if the train left on feb 18 that means that the first day i began my successful suboxone streak was feb 17, 2010" [kb/events/2010-02-17-suboxone-start.md]. The anchor is a model's reconstruction, and the date is the day before it.

The wiki flagged the anchor as misread in a September 3, 2026 contradiction block: the Twitter record shows the train running the other way, breaking the derivation's arithmetic [wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]. What did not move on September 3 is the conclusion. The independently bracketed 2013 Facebook message — three and a half years back to roughly January 2010 — and the February 17 tweet alignment both survive without the train [kb/events/2010-02-17-suboxone-start.md; kb/data/0055-facebook-corroborates-the-2010-maintenance-start.md]. This is the pattern the KB names reasoning sound, provenance unreliable, running at full size: the reasoning that assembles the date is careful, and the date's origin is a model's reading of logs nobody else has re-opened [kb/syntheses/suboxone-16-years.md].

The tweet names nothing, and the page holds that limit without softening it. "Most stressful day/decision" could in principle attach to something else. What anchors it is elimination: the move was settled, celebrated, and discussed as good news three weeks earlier [kb/data/0046-facebook-corroborates-two-ledger-adjudications.md]; the Thanksgiving episode was seven weeks prior; no other candidate in the record fits a same-day superlative about a decision. The ledger's t014 status — confirmed — is the ledger's word for a dated alignment that survived exclusion, not for a clinical record, and the ledger says so [kb/data/0055-facebook-corroborates-the-2010-maintenance-start.md].

No clinical record of the induction exists anywhere on disk. If a pharmacy or clinic record ever surfaces with a different induction date, the tweet alignment collapses into coincidence and the "stressful decision" needs a new referent [kb/events/2010-02-17-suboxone-start.md]. The event page states that falsifier plainly, and this page inherits it. The date's precision — February 17, not "mid-February 2010" — rests on one tweet and one back-calculation. The sixteen years survive a weaker date; the precision might not [kb/syntheses/suboxone-16-years.md].

## What the later record adds

The event node records three additions from the later record, each bounded.

The dual supply topology ran from the start. In the same 2013 exchange that brackets the date, Dan writes "i was on the strips when i had a doctor in nyc" (past tense) to the counterparty he simultaneously solicits as a backup supply: "if you're ever unloading or come in contact call me" [kb/events/2010-02-17-suboxone-start.md; kb/data/0055-facebook-corroborates-the-2010-maintenance-start.md]. The prescription and the informal channel overlap for at least eight years, 2013 to 2021 — fourteen occurrences of suboxone in the Facebook archive, all with one counterparty, including the October 2020 sourcing offers and the March 2021 "3 months at a time" question that presupposes a multi-month prescription whose referent the datum explicitly does not establish [kb/data/0055-facebook-corroborates-the-2010-maintenance-start.md]. The topologies are not successive states. They are parallel, from the beginning of the observable record.

One dosage figure is on record: two milligrams per day, self-reported in 2013 [kb/events/2010-02-17-suboxone-start.md; kb/data/0055-facebook-corroborates-the-2010-maintenance-start.md]. The dose curve page treats that figure as its single point and inventories the twelve bearings around it, including the 2011 appointment with urine screening that proves the run was clinically supervised that year without recording the dose set there [wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]. This page does not repeat the inventory. It records that the event's date and the regimen's only dose figure arrive three years apart, in different channels, and that nothing after 2013 names a dose again.

The prescriber exists as a real arrangement. Recorded exchanges are only about moving the script between pharmacies; by June 2025 the prescription is the default route with the dealer network as failover; the May 2026 supply failure produced more than seventy-two hours of withdrawal mid-shift [kb/events/2010-02-17-suboxone-start.md, citing the agent-D domain summary; wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]. The May 2026 cascade — a standing supplier's no-show, three burns in four days, a thirty-five-dollar handoff worked from inside the withdrawal — is the only point in the sixteen-year interval where the corpus observes the dosing behavior functionally rather than through self-report [wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]. It proves cadence, not quantity: daily dosing was live sixteen years and three months in. That is the far edge of the run this event begins, and it is a frequency proof only.

The prescriber's own history, as the dose curve reconstructs it, shows the same shape at the clinical edge. In August 2011 the regimen involved scheduled appointments at a place with drug screening — monitored treatment, a substantive clinical relationship, with Dan reporting he had been dry since Saturday night before the appointment and had taken an at-home test to check he would pass [wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]. No dose discussed at that appointment survives into the message. By 2019 the doctor is referenced as "my old doctor" in the one logistics quote that verifies verbatim against the authoritative corpus — "try to go get subs from my old doctor," May 31, 2019 [kb/data/0028-prescriber-quotes-partly-unverifiable.md; wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]. The 2025 logistics cluster — a script moved between pharmacies, an out-of-state fill refused, a Walmart fill refused — is quoted from the prior wiki but sits partly in corpus holes: June 8 and June 12, 2025 hold zero messages in the authoritative corpus, and June 2025 holds thirty-five messages total [kb/data/0028-prescriber-quotes-partly-unverifiable.md]. The dose curve's verdict is temporal and deliberately narrow: monitored in 2011, logistics-shaped by 2025, the transition undated, and none of the bearings names an amount [wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]. For this event page the bearing matters only as a boundary on day-zero: the run began under a prescriber whose dose-setting the record never captured, and the capture never improves.

## Conflicts in the record

**The February 17 triple.** February 17 is this event's date, the opening of the 2015 possession-arrest blotter window (2015-02-17), and the date of the 2016-02-17 ARD hearing. No source explains the coincidence. The KB flags it as worth examination before any of the three dates is relied on — genuine docket-scheduling anniversary, or an artefact propagated through earlier passes [kb/events/2010-02-17-suboxone-start.md; dat:0071-arrest-breath-test-and-feb-17-coincidence]. This page carries day-zero as February 17, 2010 on the tweet and bracket evidence above, and carries the triple as an open seam rather than a resolution.

**The train derivation.** The original anchor — a model's reconstruction placing an Amtrak boarding on February 18 — was flagged as misread on September 3, 2026, when the Twitter record showed the train running the other way [wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]. The derivation is broken. The date survives on the two independent supports that do not share the weakness: the 2013 Facebook bracket and the February 17 tweet alignment. A reader who requires the train gets no date; a reader who accepts the bracket gets mid-February with the seventeenth as the ledger's alignment. Both standings are recorded here.

**The "almost five years" arithmetic.** Inside the same July 10, 2013 exchange that supplies the bracket, Dan writes it has been "almost 5 years since i touched an opiate" [wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]. Taken literally, that back-dates last opiate use to late 2008 or early 2009. The dated record holds the Thanksgiving 2009 Alexis episode — thirty Roxicet pills across roughly twelve weeks ending at day-zero — after that window [wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]. Three readings exist, none established: loose rounding in casual text; not counting the maintenance start (which still yields November 2009 to February 2010, not 2008); or compression placing last use with the dark-era window rather than the calendar. The dose curve page flags the arithmetic as a preserved tension, separates the duration claim (disputed at the level of precision) from the dose figure (attested as self-report), and notes that the slippage sets the figure's precision class as approximate rather than measured [wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]. This page inherits the separation: the bracket's year and season are supported; the interval's arithmetic is not.

**The hedonic tension.** The prior wiki carried an unresolved disagreement between two of its own health pages: whether chronic opioid receptor occupancy at a stabilizing maintenance dose blunts hedonic range and baseline access to motivation, so that the dose stabilizing dependency may cap the capacity to regulate a separate anxiety system [kb/syntheses/suboxone-16-years.md; dat:0020-old-wiki-suboxone-hedonic-tension]. The wiki recorded the disagreement on both pages rather than resolving it. No clinical literature, no prescriber, no measurement supports either side, and the KB states the claim should not be promoted without a real source [kb/syntheses/suboxone-16-years.md]. This page records the tension as an open item about the regimen's purpose statement, not as a finding about the regimen.

## Assessment

February 17, 2010 is the biography's most consequential single date and one of its least clinically documented. The event it names — the start of daily Suboxone maintenance — has run for sixteen years without a stated relapse, without program participation, and without a second dose figure ever entering the record. The date itself is computed rather than remembered, anchored originally by a model's reading of email logs, and surviving the breaking of that anchor on two independent supports: a same-day tweet whose referent is established by exclusion, and a 2013 back-calculation from a different channel in a different decade. The KB holds the date at high confidence and holds its provenance at the same visibility, which is the correct posture for a fact the whole maintenance synthesis rests on.

What the event establishes, beyond the date, is a shape. Stabilization arrived seven weeks after the final pre-stabilization episode and one day before a train to New York. The dual supply topology that the later record debates was already running in the first observable exchange. The only dosage figure the corpus will ever hold was stated three years later, in passing, to one person, and the system that produced it never stated another. The full bearing inventory is on [[wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]]. This page's narrower claim is that the run began on a date the record can defend, and that the defense is worth reading because it shows how a sixteen-year fact can rest on a tweet, a bracket, and an absence of any end date. The event's own falsifier sharpens that claim: a clinical record surfacing with a different induction date would retire the February 17 precision without retiring the sixteen years, and the page carries that boundary as part of the date's definition rather than as a caveat after it.
