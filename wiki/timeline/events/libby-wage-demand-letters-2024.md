# Libby Wage Demand Letters (August 2024)

> **Incidental entry** — Three dated demand letters Dan drafted for Annie's unpaid Libby wages; complete at this size because the letters and their contradiction are the whole event.

In August 2024, with [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie Ulmer]]'s employment with [[wiki/people/libby|Libby]] broken down over unpaid wages and an unsigned NDA, Dan drafted three escalating demand letters for her. The drafts were sent from his handle into the thread for her to use (kb/events/2024-08-libby-wage-demand-letters.md). Annie's stated position, recorded August 1, 2024, was: *"in exchange for signing an NDA I would like to receive the unpaid wages"* (dat:0806).

Nothing in the corpus records a resolution. Libby died on October 13, 2024 with the dispute open on the evidence available (kb/events/2024-08-libby-wage-demand-letters.md; dat:0806).

## What happened

The employment had ended in a payment dispute: wages owed, an NDA unsigned, and a proposed trade — signature for payment — on the table. Dan's contribution was drafting, in his dated, itemized correspondence register, a short escalation ladder for Annie to send (kb/events/2024-08-libby-wage-demand-letters.md).

## The record in order

- **August 1, 2024** — first letter: *"Dear x I'm reaching out to inform you of the unpaid wages which I have been unable to receive and request a date that I can be compensated. The total amount is x (y hours at $75/hour)…"* The draft uses placeholders; no total was ever typed (dat:0806).
- **August 13, 2024, 00:18** — follow-up on the unanswered first letter (kb/events/2024-08-libby-wage-demand-letters.md).
- **August 13, 2024, 01:00** — *"Final Request for Payment of Unpaid Wages,"* closing with an offer to resolve without further remedy: *"I would like to resolve this issue without any additional complications and am not interested in seeking further remedy from Mr or Mrs Fagen"* (dat:0806).
- **October 13, 2024** — Libby died; no payment, refusal, or settlement is recorded before or after (dat:0806).

For scale, the surrounding thread record for February–August 2024 shows 5,132 messages from Dan and 7,539 from Annie, with Dan raising Libby unprompted seven times in the window (dat:libby-engagement-metrics). The letters sit inside that traffic as dated, outward-facing advocacy, distinct from the thread's day-to-day reply pattern.

## Conflicts in the record

- **The hourly rate (open).** The letters state **$75/hour**. On May 17, 2024 Annie wrote *"I was literally with Libby for just over 3 hours today.. she paid me 500"* — roughly $167/hour — and a week later invited Dan to guess the figure for four hours. Both figures are first-hand; neither was retracted. Candidate readings (a rate change, back pay or a gift folded into the $500, $75 as the evidenced-in-writing figure) are all speculative, and the record marks the contradiction unresolved rather than smoothed (dat:0806).
- **Provenance limit.** Held-corpus scans for "unpaid wages," "Libby Fagen," and "NDA" in a 2024 context return nothing; the letter texts survive as page-quoted drafts from unheld exports (dat:0806; dat:0807 as cited there). The event is therefore filed at cited knowledge: the drafts are quoted with dates and times, but the underlying exports are not held here.

Whether the balance was ever paid is genuinely unknown, not merely unobserved; one operator sentence could close it (dat:0806).
