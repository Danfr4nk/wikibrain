On February 22, 2026, [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie Ulmer]] sent Dan a late-night message exonerating him of responsibility for the relationship's failure and assigning it entirely to herself. The source carries it as a high-confidence record (kb/events/2026-02-22-annie-self-blame-exoneration.md, citing dat:libby-justification-timeline).

> "You didn't do anything to deserve the way I have treated you... I am the only one to blame for this... I do not deserve the unconditional love you have given me and I know that."

That is the whole event. The kb source presents the message as the clearest statement of a self-blame position Annie held across a longer period, and as the record's own account, in her words, of why the relationship spoiled. No reply, context thread, or follow-up message is carried in the source file, and none is supplied here.

What the record cannot establish from this source alone: the immediate circumstances that prompted the message that night, and whether Dan replied. The message's date — February 22, 2026 — and its wording are what the source verifies.
