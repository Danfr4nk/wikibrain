# Developmental Origins

This page holds the cluster's answer to *where the architecture came from*.
The dossier material names two sources — a hinge event in adolescence and a
five-year cultural imprinting period — and a third, structural account arrives
from the typology layer. All three predate the earliest documented instance in
[[wiki/mind/psychosexual/arrangement-history]] by more than a decade.

Two things have happened since this page was last written on 2026-07-19, and
both are load-bearing. [[wiki/mind/synthesis/november-2005-hinge]] (2026-09-13)
audited the hinge and the axioms attributed to it. [[wiki/mind/profile/enneagram-5w4]]
(2026-09-13) turned the settled typing into a contested one. This page's
central claim — that three independent frames converge, and that the
convergence is the strongest corroboration available — does not survive either
of them intact. What replaces it is stated below, and it is narrower rather
than empty.

And one thing was always missing. This page's own frontmatter has named
[[wiki/timeline/events/bald-eagle-cummings]] as a cause since the typed-edge
pass — *"the first sexual event in the record is a public failure followed by
a year of being renamed for it"* — and the body never mentioned it. The
earliest documented origin was declared in the metadata and absent from the
argument. It goes first now.

## The first event in the record (~2000–2001)

In seventh grade, on a night ski trip to [[wiki/places/seven-springs|Seven
Springs]], Dan's girlfriend attempted to give him oral sex in the woods
between two trails, found he had not yet begun puberty, and stopped. By the
next school day the story was through Laurel Highlands Middle School, and for
**the following full year** he was called **"Bald Eagle"** — sometimes "Bald
Eagle Cummings" — by the older students
([[wiki/timeline/events/bald-eagle-cummings]]).

The compounding facts, all stated in the source capture: he had started school
a year early and was *"always a full year or more younger than his
classmates"*; he was *"genetically a late bloomer"*; he was *"fully and
completely yet to start the process of puberty"*; and sexual gratification
*"was still a concept that had yet to click."* His own summary is that he did
not know what had happened, why it was bad, or what it predicted.

Three features of this event bear directly on everything the cluster
otherwise attributes to 2005.

**Exposure and humiliation arrive in the same instant.** Not sequentially, not
as a consequence — the failure and its publication are one event, and the
publication ran for a year, administered by people older than him. For the
exposure-sensitivity strand of the psychology, this is earlier, independently
captured, and **humiliation-shaped rather than concealment-shaped**, which is
a different mechanism from anything 2005 teaches
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/november-2005-hinge]], "Competitor origins";
[[wiki/health/hyperreflexivity]]).

**The mismatch template is self-named at first occurrence.** The girlfriend
was, in his words, the prettiest girl in the class, and he introduces her by
calling this *"my first experience with something i would go through many
times — somehow getting a girl way above my level to date me."* The adult
record documents that pattern repeatedly
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-serial-monogamist]]); he named it in the seventh
grade, which makes every later instance a repetition of a self-narrated
template rather than an independent finding
([[wiki/mind/concepts/attachment-model]]).

**There is a second injury, learned afterwards.** On the drive home, in Suz's
minivan, the friend in the back seat succeeded in the same hour, in the same
vehicle, at the thing Dan had publicly failed at — and Dan found out later.
The capture reports it as something he *"later found out"*; it is not
corroborated.

The obvious reading — that the first sexual event in the record is a scene Dan
is present for, excluded from, and told about afterwards, and that the
cluster's central adult pattern is authoring scenes he is present for and does
not participate in — is **available and is not made here as a claim.** No
primary source connects them; the resemblance is structural and the interval
is seventeen years. It is recorded as the competitor hypothesis the hinge's
incumbency has kept off the page, and as a testable one: it predicts
exposure-sensitivity findings should date to 2000–01 rather than 2005.

**Status:** single operator capture, written 2026-08-01, roughly twenty-five
years after the fact, `knowledge: mixed`. An exhaustive search of the
2007–2022 Facebook text record on 2026-09-12 (407 message files / 15,923
messages, own posts, photo metadata, albums) returned **zero** hits for "bald
eagle" and variants (`dat:1474`). That exhausts the Facebook channel without
moving confidence in either direction — a 2000–01 middle-school nickname was
never likely to surface in 2007+ Facebook text. The nickname remains
single-source.

## The 2005 axiom, and its audit

The November 2005 parental rupture — [[wiki/people/rick-frank|Rick]]'s
cocaine-rehab disclosure on the drive home from Dan's learner's-permit
appointment, followed by [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]]'s affair with the
shared dealer during his treatment, the marriage ending, and a stage-4 throat
cancer diagnosis that same winter — is the wiki's most-cited event.

The dossier's specific claim for this cluster is narrow: that the event
encoded a foundational axiom — *"intimacy is a precursor to abandonment,
loyalty is conditional, emotional security is an illusion"* — operating as the
assumption underneath the arrangement history rather than merely colouring it.
Read against [[wiki/mind/psychosexual/orchestration-and-voyeurism]], it offers
one account of why control-through-authorship might be more survivable than
being chosen or kept: if closeness reliably precedes loss, a loss
pre-authorised and staged by Dan removes the unpredictability the rupture made
intolerable. **This is the dossier's interpretation and is made nowhere by Dan
himself in the primary record.**

That caveat was already on the page. What is new is that the wiki has now
audited the whole structure, and the audit changes what this section is
allowed to claim.

**The event is not in doubt; its causal product is.**
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/november-2005-hinge]] establishes:

- The corpus's coverage of the rupture is **100% retrospective testimony, 0%
  contemporaneous residue.** The message corpus opens in 2011; the tweet
  archive in 2008. Nothing from either parent, nothing from Vanessa (eleven
  years old, in the house), nothing institutional.
- The four axioms the rupture is said to have installed — *not exceptional =
  worthless; not vigilant = annihilated; love that doesn't cost everything
  isn't real; time = countdown* — were tested lexically against **all 106,629
  outbound messages 2015–2025, with 110,944 inbound from 503 handles as a
  within-medium control.** On every explicit urgency construction but one, Dan
  writes **less** than his controls. The veracity ledger's t011 records the
  outcome as **unfalsifiable**: checked, and the corpus cannot settle it.
- Stated confidence "certain" prices at **0.25** on the ledger's calibration
  ([[wiki/meta/testimony-veracity]]).
- The axioms' canonical formulation has **no located first attestation**.
  Searches of `raw/self/dox-md/`, `dox-scan/`, `context-core/` and `captures/`
  for the canonical strings returned no hits; git archaeology finds the
  formulation arriving fully formed in the 2026-09-10 port commit. Honest
  dating: formulated before 2026-09-10, origin undetermined.

The consequence for this page is precise and limited. The *intimacy-precursor*
axiom is not one of the four — it is this cluster's own, narrower formulation
— so t011 does not test it directly. But it is the same kind of object,
produced in the same layer, by the same process: a model-authored gloss on
Dan's retelling, endorsed by him, cited thereafter as mechanism. The hinge
entry's prescription applies verbatim: **keep it, and label it correctly.**

One further complication the hinge entry supplies and this page should carry,
because it bears on chronology rather than on interpretation. The rupture
cannot be the origin of Dan's relationship to the substance at its centre:
cocaine was a known household fact from 2001 (age thirteen, via the discovery
of the parents' use), and his own first use dates to 2004 with the Seven
Springs ski crew — a year before the hinge ([[wiki/health/cocaine]];
[[wiki/health/chemical-architecture]]). What the rupture can be the origin of
is the *meaning*. The same caution transfers to this page's subject: a
seventeen-year-old who had already had a girlfriend, a public sexual
humiliation and a four-year normalisation of household drug use did not arrive
at November 2005 as a blank.

## The imprinting era (2005–2010)

The dossier names a five-year *"Neuroemotional Imprinting Era"* running from
the rupture through the [[wiki/timeline/periods/full-sail-2008-2010|Full Sail
period]]: *"deep, obsessive identification with emo/pop-punk music"*, read as
seeding *"core relational expectations: intense, tragic, aesthetically rich,
ultimately doomed."* The claim is that the genre's romantic template became
the standard against which real relationships were measured, ahead of and
independent of any actual relationship experience.

**The genre attachment is real, dated, and countable.** This is the one strand
of the page with an evidence base outside the dossier layer
([[wiki/interests/favorites/music]]):

| Evidence | Value |
|---|---|
| 2007 Facebook status layer | *"currently swooning over the new FOB"*, *"in love with the new Say Anything disc"*, Radiohead on loop |
| Current library, emo/pop-punk cluster | My Chemical Romance 9 tracks, New Found Glory 8, Elliott Smith 7, Taking Back Sunday 6, Fall Out Boy 6, Say Anything 6 |
| Provenance of that cluster | **"MUSIC LIST (start-2024)"** — the manually curated ingestion wave, not the Spotify feed |
| Library scale for context | 1,860 tracks, 1,477 unique artists, ~1.26 tracks per artist |
| Cluster share | ~5% of the library — level with the hip-hop and electronic/bass clusters, not dominant |
| Continuity | Elliott Smith liked August 2013; the 2024 manual list re-archives an attachment already eighteen years old |

Two readings follow, and the page holds both. The 2024 manual list is **a
deliberate act of archiving a decade-old attachment**, which is evidence the
attachment persisted and was consciously curated rather than passively
surfaced. And the cluster is one of three roughly equal clusters maintained in
parallel, which means *"deep, obsessive identification"* describes its
persistence better than its share.

**The mechanism, however, has a hole in it.**
[[wiki/mind/concepts/lyric-qualifier]] documents a perceptual
report: sung lyrics do not present themselves to Dan as language to be
understood — they function as texture, vocal grain, structure and low end. The
page's supporting structural finding is strong: across four production aliases
and thirteen years, **there is no original sung songwriting anywhere in the
documented catalogue.**

An imprinting mechanism that transmits a *romantic template* through emo/pop-punk
requires the lyrical content to arrive as content. If it arrives as timbre, the
channel the mechanism runs on is not open. That does not dissolve the
correlation — artists writing about extremity often perform with more textural
extremity, so the thematic and timbral readings are compatible — but it
removes the dossier's stated causal route and leaves the genre attachment
needing a different explanation, or none.

## The convergence claim, audited

This page's strongest sentence has been that three accounts reaching the same
behavioural prediction from three theoretical starting points *"is the
strongest form of corroboration this cluster has to offer."* Convergence is
only corroboration when the converging accounts are independent. They are not.

| Frame | Immediate source | Ultimate source | Independent of Dan's self-report? |
|---|---|---|---|
| The 2005 axiom | `Dan Profile.txt` / CATO dossiers | Dan's retrospective testimony, systematised by a model, endorsed by Dan | No |
| The imprinting era | `Dan Profile.txt` | Same | No — though the *genre attachment* is independently attested in the 2007 Facebook layer and the 2024 library |
| The sx/sp stacking | `FULL PROFILE 2026.md` / commissioned GPT-4.1 sessions (unheld) | Dan's self-report, typed by a model; the one dated self-typing disagrees on the wing | No |

All three frames enter through the **dossier layer** — Dan talking to AI
systems about his life, the systems synthesising, the synthesis re-entering
the wiki as source. [[wiki/mind/synthesis/november-2005-hinge]] names the
structural problem: *"the circuit is not dishonest — every archive is built by
someone — but it is closed, and closed circuits don't test their inputs."*

Three readings produced by one informant through one process are not three
witnesses. They are one witness, cross-examined three ways, which is a real
thing to have and not the thing this page said it had.

**What convergence does still buy.** The three frames are not *identical*:
they name different mechanisms (attachment trauma, cultural imprinting,
instinctual typology) and predict different failure modes. Their agreement on
the behavioural prediction — total, high-control, high-intensity engagement
rather than casual or diffuse intimacy — means the informant's self-model is
**internally consistent across three vocabularies**, which is evidence about
the stability of the self-model. It is not evidence about the world. And the
prediction itself has genuine independent support from the measurement layer:
Gini 0.961 (`dat:0528`), the zero-severance-signal finding across 41,073 of
Annie's messages (`dat:0081`), and the arrangement history's own bursts.

## Gaps and limits of record

- **No primary source documents Dan connecting any of these origins to the
  arrangement history himself.** All three linkages are dossier-authored
  synthesis. This was the page's original closing gap and it is unchanged.
- **The comedy overlay has one sentence of source and a wrong date.** No
  elaboration of "masculine power/comedy/humiliation dynamics" exists anywhere.
- **The intimacy-precursor axiom has never been operationalised.** t011 tested
  urgency constructions, not intimacy-and-abandonment constructions. A test
  against the 503-handle control is designable and unrun — the same gap the
  hinge entry records for the worth axiom.
- **The chat corpus has never been tested.** The single highest-leverage unrun
  check in the whole hinge complex: run the t011 lexical design against the
  Gemini/CATO/dossier chat corpus, the register where Dan introspects at
  length. If the axioms are enacted there, the message-corpus negative is a
  medium artefact.
- **The bald-eagle capture is single-source and undated within its year.**
  Britney Bolen, Victor Fiano, Alan and Jon Allen have no other presence in
  the corpus.
- **The hinge is a single-informant event.** Vanessa (eleven, in the house),
  Danielle Onesi (the contemporaneous first girlfriend, relationship
  beginning ~2005 with the ordering against the rupture undetermined), Rick
  and Suz have all never been asked on the record. The dealer is unnamed
  everywhere.
- **Both commissioned profile sessions are unheld**, as are the `.docx`
  dossier sources; the typology strand cannot be re-derived here.
- **The archive-wide caveat applies.** Of this page's frontmatter sources,
  the `raw/self/...` paths are dangling: the reconstruction preserved the
  pages, not the files they were written against (`README.md`). Those
  citations resolve to nothing, which makes the claims **unverified** rather

- **Provenance tier, restated.** Per [[wiki/mind/psychosexual/index]], this
  cluster's theory layer is single-source AI-authored material and its

  theory layer. The one exception is the genre attachment, which has a
