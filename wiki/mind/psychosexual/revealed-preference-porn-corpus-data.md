## Method

Bottom-up categorization. The analyst derived categories from Dan's search
terms, video titles, and URL slugs — his ontology, not the S/P/A/E/N
diagnostic axes, which were deliberately not forced onto the data. The
analyst never saw his forecast list before categorizing (blind scoring).
Counts are query-hit counts unless marked as visit-hits. "Visit-hits" are
harder signal (actual page loads); query-hits are one step removed (searches,
not confirmed watches).

Known measurement floors: title keywords under-describe acts (a video titled
for its performers says nothing about its acts); cam-rip pages are titled by
model name, not genre, so genre tags systematically undercount cam content;
search-page titles are queries, not confirmed consumption.

Concentration claim ("that pretty much covers the entire list"): **holds on
genre, fails on frame.** His correction — "the cam clips are all lesbian,

lesbian-genre, so the genre misses dissolve into the frame dimension. What

(recorded-not-live, archivist-voyeur, creator-direct, real-world-adjacent)
was the blind spot, and it is the more valuable discovery — it produced
the genre × frame ontology the new scenario bank is built on.

## Decontamination log

Removed before ranking: youtube.com and youtube variants (16,302 "Watched"
rows, non-adult); dictionary/reference sites; "dicks sporting goods" (8 —
the store); "anthony cumia" (7 — the comedian); documentary sites; the
single-letter query "t" (31 — typo). "spank" (72): ~90% are spankbang.com
domain-name hits; real spanking interest ≈ 9, excluded from the category
map. reddit/twitter/youtube rows in the domain table are keyword hits on
page titles, retained in the raw table for auditability, excluded from the
4,258/931 decontaminated counts.

## Open questions

1. **The 2023–2025 cold trail.** Zero adult rows in three years against
   5,253 total rows. Incognito, another browser/account, or a real change —
   unanswered at time of writing, and material to how much weight the
   late-era frame findings carry.
2. **Act-level blindness.** Title-organized corpora can't see what titles
   don't say; the thin act data (category 14) is a floor.
3. **The trans cluster** (16 rows) is exploratory for instrument purposes,
   not load-bearing.
4. **Escort-thread transfer.** The real-world-adjacent frame may not
   transfer to pure fantasy; its scenario tests measure logistics-adjacent
   charge.
5. **Forecast anchoring.** The analyst was blind to the forecast, but Dan
   knew the corpus existed when forecasting — his early-era accuracy
   (twins, sisters) may reflect memory of the same data, not independent
   self-knowledge. The frame blind spot is the cleaner signal: nobody
   memorizes their own consumption *mode*.
