# Scenario-Ratings Profile (2026-09-11)

On 2026-09-11 Dan rated 121 of 122 constructed erotic scenarios on a 1–10 gut
scale (v1 instrument; scenario-rate.html). Mean 8.36, 64% at 9–10, veto never
used. The list was built from his own fantasy space, so the ceiling is the
finding's context, not its substance: what matters is what *didn't* score 10,
and what moved a score when a single detail changed. Each scenario came in a
family — a base plus four or five single-detail variants — and those deltas are
the cleanest evidence in the set.

**Instrument caveat, stated once:** v1's readout is correlational. The v2
instrument (168 single-knob modifiers, each metric perturbed 6–8x, causal
knob-effect deltas) supersedes v1's attribution. Treat everything below as the
profile; v2 as the refinement. Other limits: her attractiveness and taboo charge
were held constant by design (control variables, not findings); some levels rest
on single items (older/younger); the reciprocity metric is a mirage (its
"receive" average is four pegging items plus two 9-rated peripheral items).
Stated preference, one sitting, n=1 — it predicts ratings, not behavior.

## The sole hard boundary

One rejected act in 121 ratings: being pegged while another man watches. Every
variant — filmed, unfilmed, alone together, Dan in charge — scored 3 or 4. The
only family averaging below 5 (4.00), more than two standard deviations under
his mean. He never touched the veto button; the 3s *are* the veto.

The exception inside the exception is the largest positive swing in the dataset:
"the guy joins in on you" lifts the same scenario from 3 to 7. The boundary is
act-specific, not configuration-specific — and even inside the rejected act, the
other man's attention landing on *Dan* is the lever. That lever repeats
everywhere.

## The engine: target clarity

Across every family, the hottest version is the one where it's unambiguous who
the other man wants. "The other guy is into you" — 10. "One of them is only
into you" — 10. In the friend-asks-to-join scenario the base sits at a lukewarm
6; the variant "he is only into her" jumps it to 10. Metric level: "he pursues
you" (8.88) > "mild interest" (8.38) > "he experiments" (7.78), monotonic, all
well-covered. The rule: **who the other man is into matters less than that it
is clear.** Clarity in either direction amplifies; ambiguity moderates.
Replicated across four independent scenario families — the most falsifiable
claim in the profile.

## Proximity is non-negotiable

Every distance variant tanked: "you wait in the bar" 9→5; "you are in the next
room" 8→5; "she calls you right after" instead of during, 8→3. The phone
scenario family ("she is with her ex while you listen on the phone") averages
6.80 — lowest of any family except pegging. The positive image: "shared
blowjob with him" while watching her with her ex is a 10; "she joins in
halfway" while she watches you is a 10. Adjacent is not enough; *in it* is the
requirement.

## The camera multiplies; it doesn't spark

Filmed averages 9.05 against 8.21 unfilmed. "She holds the camera," "you film
your half" — all 10s. Removing the camera from the 2019-shaped scenario drops
it 10→5, one of the steepest falls in the set. But "she films it" on the
pegging scenario stays at 3. The camera is a multiplier on heat that already
exists, never the source.

## She authors, he pursues, Dan is not the focus

The three top families share one role structure: "she watches you get a
blowjob from another guy" (9.80, every variant 9–10), "house party open room"
(9.60), "she sets you up with a guy as a gift" (9.60). She orchestrates or
watches in all three. "She directs" tops authorship (8.48); "he pursues you"
tops pursuit — and "you're the focus" is the *lowest* spotlight level (7.95),
under shared (8.52) and peripheral (8.57). The variant "you are the focus
instead" drops a threesome 9→6. He does not want to be the center. He wants to
be the one things happen to — arranged by her, the other man's attention on
him, everyone's target unambiguous.

## Setting rules

- **Neutral turf wins:** neutral 8.88 > yours 7.87 > hers 7.30. "At his place"
  drops a 10→6; "at their place" 9→5. Not home advantage — *neutral* advantage.
- **Strangers yes, friends no:** stranger 8.43, acquaintance 8.75, friend 7.30;
  "he is a friend of yours" craters a 9→4. Willing 8.53 vs reluctant 6.22.
- **Semi-private, not exposed:** semi-private 9.00 > private 8.27 — the booth,
  the framing. But "word could get out in your circle" drops 6→4. The charge
  is in the *framing* of semi-privacy, never real risk.
- **Talked about beats silent:** explicit talk 9.25; "no talking, unspoken"
  drops 10→6.
- **Intense, ongoing, open-ended:** intense 9.43 > moderate 8.37 > gentle 7.73
  ("slow evening" drops 9→4); ongoing 9.29 > an evening 8.74 > brief 8.10
  ("monthly" is a 10); open-ended 9.25 > includes oral 8.54 > touch only 7.30
  ("soft swap only" drops 10→7).

## Against the behavioral record

Stated preference is not behavior. Read against the documented record, the
profile both converges and diverges — both are informative:

- **Converges — camera architecture.** The 2019 experiment was filmed as a
  matter of structure (security-camera footage, curated selects). The
  multiplier finding matches the behavioral record exactly.
- **Converges — proximity.** Every documented instance has Dan present or
  observing; nothing in the corpus is a "she calls after" configuration.
- **Converges — her authorship.** The 2024 Danny Matthews thread has Annie
  proposing ("We have spoken about you often danny"), Dan aware and involved.
  The April 2019 revision (Annie converting a one-on-one into a threesome
  from outside a locked door) is her authorship in its most literal form.
- **Converges — acquaintance, not friend.** Danny Matthews: "a grade above me
  at school, known as a dork" — acquaintance tier, matching the
  stranger/acquaintance preference and the friend poison.
- **Diverges — neutral turf.** The 2019 encounters ran at 117 Belmont Circle
  (his place) and similar home settings — the opposite of the stated neutral
  preference. Either the preference postdates the practice, or turf was never
  the load-bearing variable he rates it as now.
- **Converges — act-specific boundary.** The same night he rated pegging 3–4,
  Dan corrected "I would let him" to "I would WANT him to" about Danny
  Matthews — appetite for reception stated explicitly. The boundary is the
  *act* (pegging), not reception as such. The profile's own internal finding
  and the night's disclosure agree.

## The prediction

If the profile is right, Dan's perfect 10 is: her directing or watching, the
other man's interest in him unambiguous, filmed, neutral turf, intense and
open-ended, stranger or acquaintance, talked about, part of something ongoing.
Change any load-bearing detail — ambiguous target, no camera, his place,
distance — and it drops three or more points. Cheapest test: run the foursome
family (9.50) "at his place." The model says 7 or below; if it holds at 9+,
the turf rule gets rewritten.
