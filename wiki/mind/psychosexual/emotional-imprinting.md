# Emotional Imprinting

Both dossier sources make the same claim in nearly the same language:
"unemotional sex is a myth" for Dan. Every high-investment sexual or
romantic encounter is described as producing immediate attachment
activation — `Dan Profile.txt` calls it "crush activation on contact" —
rather than staying casual or contained. The report frames this as the
direct mechanism behind two otherwise-separate facts already documented
elsewhere in the wiki: the intensity of Dan's relationship attachments, and
the disproportionate severity of the [[wiki/people/eli|Eli]] betrayal's
psychological impact. In this framing, the Eli incident wasn't processed as
ordinary romantic disappointment — it's described as a "temple
desecration," the violation of something categorically sacred rather than
merely painful.

**State the provenance before the argument, because it governs everything
below.** This claim originates in a single AI-authored dossier that Dan
commissioned about himself. It has never been stated by Dan in his own
words in any held message. Its convergent support is real but sits at the
level of *pattern*, not at the level of the claim's own vocabulary. And it
is now load-bearing for at least one downstream synthesis that cites it as
mechanism rather than as hypothesis. The evidence node built over this page
files it at `confidence = "low"` for exactly that chain — "a relay of an
AI-authored interpretation two layers removed from any primary record"
([`dat:1113`](../../../kb/data/1113-emotional-imprinting-single-source-dossier.md)).

## The mechanism as described

The claim has two parts.

**First, that encounters trigger absorption.** Not just relationships but
individual high-investment sexual encounters produce what the report calls
"emotional imprinting": the other person is "absorbed and mapped into his
internal architecture" essentially on contact, not gradually. The sources
are explicit that the trigger is *high investment*, which includes more
than sex — a point [[wiki/mind/synthesis/no-platonic-channel]] later
converts into its central load-bearing premise.

**Second, that the absorption is what makes disappointment catastrophic.**
"Contamination occurs when reality fails to align with this idealized map,"
producing what the same report elsewhere calls the shift from a partner as
"chaotic mirror" to "dissociated saboteur" — the exact language
independently used to describe the Eli incident's effect on how Dan modeled
[[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] afterward (see
[[wiki/timeline/events/eli-incident]], which reaches the same phrase from
the corrective-source side).

The vocabulary matters and should be named as vocabulary. "Crush activation
on contact," "temple desecration," "absorbed and mapped into his internal
architecture," "contamination" — these are the dossier's metaphors, not
observations. [[wiki/mind/psychosexual/index]] makes the general ruling for
the whole cluster: the vivid metaphor is "interpretive coloring layered
onto real events, not a separate factual claim." This page's mechanism is
made *entirely* of that layer. What lies underneath it — rapid, hard-to-
reverse attachment activation — is a separate claim with separate and
better support, and the distinction is the single most useful thing this
page can hold.

## The source chain, traced

The cluster contains a documented case of two dossiers looking like two
witnesses and turning out to be one.
[[wiki/mind/psychosexual/taboo-and-boundary-testing]] ran that trace on its
own mechanism and found that `DANSYNTH.txt`'s only footnote on the relevant
passage resolves, in its own works-cited list, to **"Pattern Mapping"** —
and `Dan Profile.txt` is the only file in the corpus that discusses pattern
mapping. `DANSYNTH.txt` is downstream of `Dan Profile.txt`, not a second
witness to it.

This page's mechanism comes from **the same "Pattern Mapping" section**,
which is the connection the two pages' shared `mirrors` edge records. So
the "both dossier sources make the same claim in nearly the same language"
sentence at the top of this page — which has stood since 2026-07-14 — is
true and does not mean what it appears to mean. Two documents saying the
same thing in nearly the same language is what one document and one
restatement of it look like. The agreement is a property of the relay, not
evidence about Dan.

**Held status.** Neither `Dan Profile.txt` nor `DANSYNTH.txt` is held in
this repository's `raw/` tree. Neither has been read against the
authoritative corpus, and `dat:1113`'s extraction note records that no
corpus search was run for "crush activation on contact" or "temple
desecration" because the claim's stated origin is the dossier rather than
the messages — a deliberate decision, not an omission.

## Where this converges with independently-documented material

> having none. That contrast is now only partly true: the taboo page's
> orientation-violation claim gained a documented primary-source instance
> (the October 2019 Bryan MMF) on 2026-08-02, though it remains a single
> dated event rather than a body of cases. The distinction that still
> holds is the *kind* of convergence — this page's support comes from an
> independently-built quantitative model (41,073 messages, an
> activation-asymmetry finding reached without reference to this dossier),
> while taboo-and-boundary-testing's support is one confirmed real-world
> event. Both are corroborated; neither is corroborated the same way.

This mechanism has real convergent support, even though it originates in
the same single-source dossier. [[wiki/mind/concepts/attachment-model]] —
built from a much broader evidence base, including the primary message
corpus rather than a single AI report — independently documents an
attachment-activation asymmetry: nothing before Annie is recorded as
triggering full attachment activation, and once triggered, no natural
mechanism existed in Dan's own behavior to close the loop without an
explicit severance signal from the other person. That is a structurally
identical claim to "crush activation on contact" with no off switch,
arrived at from different source material.

### The convergence is at pattern level, and the difference is not cosmetic

`dat:1113` states the limit precisely: the convergence "is at the level of
*pattern* (rapid, irreversible attachment activation), not at the level of
the specific 'crush activation on contact' or 'temple desecration'
language, which remains interpretive framing rather than primary-source
fact."

Concretely, the attachment model supports:

- that an activated bond did not close from inside across eleven years;
- that Dan's own declarations of exit terminated nothing, 129 times;
- that the bond was **not maintained by reward** — the happiness-claim rate
  had collapsed by 2017 and the bond ran at full strength through 2026,
  which is the finding that rules out the ordinary addiction-model reading.

It does **not** support:

- that activation occurs *on contact* rather than over weeks;
- that individual encounters, as opposed to relationships, trigger it;
- that the mechanism is *absorption of a person into an internal map*
  rather than, as the attachment model has it, the weighting of explicit
  verbal statements above behavioral evidence
  ([[wiki/mind/concepts/explicit-verbal-commitment]]).

That third gap is the real one. The two accounts describe the same
observable — a bond that activates fast and will not close — through
different machinery. The dossier says *absorption*; the corpus-built model
says *a stated rule with no delete operation*. These predict differently.
Absorption predicts that any sufficiently intense encounter imprints, full
stop. A rule-based architecture predicts that what imprints is whatever
gets *stated*, and that an equally intense encounter with no verbal
commitment attached to it would not. The corpus has a case bearing on this
and it points at the second account: across seven years living with
[[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis]], Dan reports he "never even thought
about a real future with [her]" — functional companionship without the
attachment system ever fully activating
([[wiki/mind/concepts/attachment-model]]). Seven years of high-investment
proximity with no imprint is a hard result for the absorption model and an
easy one for the rule model.

**The page does not resolve this**, because resolving it would mean
choosing between a dossier and a corpus-built instrument on a question
neither was designed to answer. It records it as the cluster's sharpest
open discrimination and names, below, what would settle it.

## The Fe mechanism, and what it does and doesn't license

The Fe-starvation profile on [[wiki/mind/profile/intp]] (Fe 10% valuing,
46% active — the weak-but-craved feeling function) provides a plausible
mechanism for why rapid over-attachment would be true: a system starved of
the feeling function it needs would be expected to over-attach rapidly to
whatever supplies it, rather than titrate exposure gradually the way a
securely-resourced Fe user might. The same profile page reads the
architecture from the other side — "a system that cannot read the room
builds a room out of sentences instead" — which is the rule-based account,
not the absorption account, stated in the profile's own vocabulary.

The mechanism is plausible and it is not independent. The INTP function
scores are themselves `knowledge: mixed`, derived from instruments Dan
commissioned about himself, and [[wiki/mind/synthesis/instrument-is-subject]]
is the wiki's own standing rule that a self-commissioned instrument
corroborating a self-commissioned dossier is one voice, not two. Citing Fe
10% in support of "crush activation on contact" is closer to restating the
claim in a second register than to evidencing it.

## The Eli case: what the mechanism is being asked to explain

The severity account is this page's most-cited output and it is worth
setting down plainly, without the metaphor doing the work.

A concealed relationship between Annie and her coworker Eli ran from at
least August 2024 until the February 2025 departure from New York. The
documented centre of gravity is not the affair but the months of denial
that defended it: on October 19, 2024, in an hours-long argument, "THERE IS
NO SITUATION," "You're literally fucking insane," and "Grow up. And fucking
trust me," while the affair was active. Discovery came on January 9, 2025,
when six rapid-fire messages arrived on Dan's phone from Eli, from Annie's
phone, beginning at 23:18:49; Dan's own reply at 23:19:42 was "Who are
you?" He had already been trying to reach her for an hour
([[wiki/timeline/events/eli-incident]], primary-confirmed 2026-07-20).

The corrective sources rank the gaslighting, not the affair, as the most
serious failure of the terminal phase, "because it attacked Dan's capacity
to know reality itself." That is a *specific* account of the severity, and
it is not this page's account. The imprinting model says the injury was to
an absorbed internal map. The Eli-incident page says the injury was to
reality-testing — accurate perception systematically reframed as illness.
The attachment model says something third: that the concealment kept the
contradiction flagged as an anomaly rather than closing the file, which is
why the bond survived it.

Three explanations of one injury, from three source bases, none of them
Dan's own stated account. [[wiki/people/eli]] routes the "temple
desecration" phrase here to be unpacked; what unpacking it yields is that
the phrase is the least evidenced of the three readings and the most
quoted.

## The dependency audit — where this claim is now load-bearing

This is new to this pass and it is the finding that most changes how the
page should be cited.

[[wiki/mind/synthesis/no-platonic-channel]] (created 2026-08-28) documents
that every sustained, high-trust tie between Dan and a woman in the corpus
carries a documented romantic or sexual overture inside its own history —
[[wiki/people/ally-lubin|Ally Lubin]] (the $25 paid introduction, the 2019
photograph payments, the ninety-minute-onset arrangement pitch of
2019-08-17), Jenn Lynn (three solicitations across 2019–2020), Lauryn Ashly
(one, declined warmly, friendship intact). Its member table and its
falsifiers are built from primary threads. Its **mechanism**, however, is
this page:

> "[[wiki/mind/psychosexual/emotional-imprinting]] supplies the mechanism
> this pattern needs and states it in almost the same words twice, from two
> independent dossiers."

Two things are wrong with that sentence and neither is the synthesis's
fault. The "two independent dossiers" are one document and its restatement,
per the source-chain trace above. And a `confidence = "low"` relay of an
AI-authored interpretation is not the kind of object that should be
supplying a mechanism to an L5-equivalent synthesis — that is the
conclusion-becoming-premise failure
[`ARCHITECTURE.md`](../../../ARCHITECTURE.md) exists to prevent, arriving
through the wiki's prose layer where the layer validator does not reach.

**The synthesis survives this, and it is worth saying why.** Its rule is a
statement about *instances*, and the instances are primary-sourced. A
pattern does not need a correct mechanism to be a real pattern. What the
dependency means is narrower: if this page's mechanism is wrong, the
no-platonic-channel rule loses its explanation and keeps its evidence — and
[[wiki/mind/profile/enneagram-5w4]]'s sx-dominant/social-absent reading,
which that page cites as the mechanism's second half, is an available
replacement that does not route through this dossier at all.

The other downstream users are lighter. [[wiki/mind/psychosexual/index]]
cites the mechanism in its LLM quick-brief with the provenance caveat
attached. [[wiki/people/eli]] routes a phrase here. Neither rests weight on
it.

## What this does not explain

The emotional-imprinting claim, taken at face value, sits in some tension
with the orchestration/voyeurism pattern documented on
[[wiki/mind/psychosexual/orchestration-and-voyeurism]]: if every
high-investment encounter triggers total imprinting, the mechanism doesn't
obviously predict why Dan would then structure years of his primary
relationship around watching his partner have exactly those
high-investment encounters with other people, rather than avoiding the
exposure. The dossier doesn't resolve this tension explicitly — the closest
it comes is the claim that orchestration lets Dan extract meaning from the
*fact* of the encounter (authorship over the boundary) rather than from
direct sensory/emotional participation, which would in theory route around
the imprinting mechanism rather than triggering it. This is plausible and
unconfirmed by any primary source; it should be read as an open structural
question in the framework, not a resolved one.

**The 2026-09-11 stated-preference run narrows it without closing it.**
[[wiki/mind/psychosexual/scenario-ratings-profile]] is 121 scenarios rated
by Dan on his own gut scale — the first first-person instrument anywhere in
this cluster. Three of its findings bear directly here:

- **Proximity is non-negotiable.** Distance variants collapse: 9→5, 8→5,
  8→3. Whatever the architecture wants, it wants it in the room.
- **The camera multiplies heat but never creates it** (filmed 9.05 vs 8.21
  on the same scenarios).
- **The preferred role is director-*adjacent*, not director-central.** "She
  directs" tops the authorship axis; "you're the focus" is the *lowest*
  spotlight level at 7.95.

The first of those is the one this page needed. An absorption model that
runs on contact predicts a strong presence-condition, and Dan's own ratings
report one. That is the closest thing to first-person support the
imprinting claim has ever had — and it is support for the *presence*
half, not the *absorption* half, and it arrives through a preference scale
rather than a statement about how attachment works. It is also, by the
orchestration page's own reading, evidence that the resolution to the
structural tension is not "authorship routes around the channel" but
something subtler: he does not want the director's chair either, which
makes "authorship over the boundary" a weaker account of the arrangement
than the cluster has been running on.

## What would settle the discrimination

Concrete enough for someone else to go and look for.

1. **A dated high-investment encounter with no verbal commitment attached,
   followed by no attachment activation.** The Alexis seven years are
   suggestive; a single clean dated instance would move the discrimination
   decisively toward the rule-based account and this page's mechanism would
   narrow to a special case of it.
2. **A dated instance of activation occurring inside one encounter** —
   attachment behavior measurable in the days immediately after a first
   high-investment contact, with the corpus's ordinary baseline for
   comparison. That is the absorption model's own prediction and nothing in
   the record has been checked for it.
3. **`Dan Profile.txt` surfaces in `raw/` and its "Pattern Mapping" section
   is read whole.** The claim's exact scope, hedging and internal evidence
   become visible for the first time; every quoted phrase on this page
   becomes checkable.
4. **Dan states the mechanism, or denies it, in his own words.** The corpus
   contains no such statement. `dat:1113` records that no search was run
   for the dossier's phrases because the origin is stated to be the
   dossier; a search for the *substance* — first-person accounts of how
   attachment starts — has not been run either, and is the cheapest test on
   this list.

## Limits of this page

- **The mechanism is single-sourced to an AI-authored dossier that Dan
  commissioned about himself**, and the apparent second source is a
  restatement of the first from the same "Pattern Mapping" section. Neither
  document is held in this repository.
- **Its convergence with [[wiki/mind/concepts/attachment-model]] is real
  and is pattern-level only.** The two accounts describe the same observable
  through incompatible machinery, and the corpus's one bearing case (Alexis)
  points the other way.
- **The convergent instrument's own base is unevenly graded**: two rows
  primary-verified, one row independently recounted testimony, six rows
  dossier arithmetic over an unheld corpus, one row (the 299 affirmations)
  never located at all. The full table is printed above so no one has to
  take the convergence on its headline.
- **This page is cited as mechanism by a synthesis** built on primary
  instances. That dependency is documented above; it has not been reviewed
  by the synthesis's own author.
- **No developmental onset is established.** The earliest sexual event in
  the record is ~2000–2001 and is humiliation-shaped rather than
  attachment-shaped ([[wiki/mind/psychosexual/developmental-origins]],
  [[wiki/timeline/events/bald-eagle-cummings]]); the first *documented full
  activation* is Annie, from November 2015. A mechanism described as
  constitutional has fifteen years between those two points that nothing on
  this page accounts for.
- **"Temple desecration" is the dossier's phrase and appears nowhere in the
  primary record.** It is the most-quoted line this page carries and the
  least evidenced. Where the wiki repeats it, it should be attributed, not
  asserted.
