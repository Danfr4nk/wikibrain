> **Incidental entry** — Dan's May 2023 thesis: with Trump as a variable in the equation, no primary challenger could win. Complete at this size.

The DeSantis Delusion is Dan's flagship prediction: that the media's "DeSantis as Trump-slayer" narrative ignored the one variable that decided everything — Trump himself. On paper DeSantis was the smarter play (more palatable, more competent, fewer skeletons than "the 1980's New York real estate developer"). In reality, none of it mattered.

Key facts:
- Core mechanism, from "The DeSantis Delusion" (2023-05-23): 20–30% of GOP voters were strictly Trump-loyal; a defeated Trump would sabotage the nominee rather than concede, so donors and McConnell were trapped — back Trump or watch him "tear it all down in revenge."
- "This isn't a prediction. This is a precedent" — Trump claimed fraud in every election he didn't win (Iowa 2016 vs. Cruz cited as the template).
- Companion piece "How To Ruin Your Political Career" (2023-05-26): running was "a baffling and wholly unforced error"; DeSantis could have been the 2028 frontrunner with another Florida term, and instead became "little more than a toy for Trump to bat around."
- Outcome: correct. DeSantis never broke 25% nationally, dropped out January 21, 2024 after Iowa, endorsed Trump.

Why incidental: a single thesis, fully stated and scored. Promote to major if it generalizes into a standing theory of Trump-era primary dynamics.
