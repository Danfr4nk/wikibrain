> **Incidental entry** — Dan's June 2023 explainer of Alan Lichtman's fundamentals-based election forecasting system. Complete at this size.

Lichtman's 13 Keys to the White House is the forecasting system Dan took most seriously in 2023: thirteen true/false statements about the incumbent *party* (midterm gains, primary contest, economy, scandal, social unrest, charisma, etc.). Six or more false and the incumbent party loses. No polls, no fundraising, no vibes.

Key facts:
- Dan's post (2023-06-02) frames Lichtman as the anti-grifter: the one 2016 predictor with a system, 10/10 from 1984 to 2016 — including the Al Gore 2000 call, "which, of course, he did" win.
- The post's tension: the keys are "generic and obtuse" versus the temptation of personality-based punditry, "particularly in the age of Trump."
- June 2023 read: the keys favored Biden, but "a lot can happen in a year" and the keys are "unbiased and clinical."
- Postscript (public record): Lichtman officially predicted a Harris win in 2024; Trump won — the system's first public miss.

Why incidental: an explainer stub for a system Dan referenced but never adopted as his own. Promote to major if he ever builds his own fundamentals model.
