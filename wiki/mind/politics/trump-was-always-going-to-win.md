"Trump Was Always Going To Win" (2024-11-12) is Dan's victory lap and his shock: the win he predicted, plus the down-ballot GOP sweep he didn't see coming — "so shocking and terrifying."

Key facts:
- The claim: "Over two years ago, I concluded that a Trump victory in 2024 had become the most likely outcome." The receipts cited: the DeSantis Delusion posts (May 2023).
- The audit: the May–June 2023 posts argue the *nomination* was a fait accompli — airtight. The general-election call was softer ("with every development, my confidence grew"), directionally right but stated with more certainty in retrospect than in real time. Per his own reliability note: testimony, not record.

- What genuinely surprised him: not Trump — the "Republican-controlled Congress filled with loyalists," the popular-vote mandate, and a Democratic Party "devoid of credibility or a foundation for resistance."
- Closes by converting the Substack into a watchtower: "This may become an outlet for tracking what happens next." It never posted again.
