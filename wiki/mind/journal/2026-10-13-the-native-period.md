# The Native Period

## Profile prior (Stage 1)

Rows the thesis touches, with weight and verdict:

- **Row 23 (weight 7, Layer A+B, verdict: supported).** Reassurance decays rather than accumulates; the check-in loop is the observable surface. The 129-declaration / 100%-re-engagement / 36-second-median figures are cited here; the 36s number itself is page testimony (C11), the loop structure is held.
- **Row 19 (weight 9.5, Layer A, verdict: supported).** No-counter-rule attachment: declarations do not terminate the bond; the bond runs on the last stated rule. Prediction: declaration episodes carry no terminating force, so their timing should show no termination-shaped signature (no winding down, no distinct halt cadence).
- **Row 37 (weight 9, Layer A, verdict: supported).** Stacked-essay escalation loop: silence lengthens output (2h wait → 49.1 w/turn), long-turn share concentrates under non-response, the loop breaks past 1 day. Prediction: the loop's behavior under silence is amplification, not waiting — consistent with a clock that keeps ticking rather than a dialogue that pauses.
- **Held (non-row) datum:** median inter-send gap 0.65 minutes (39 seconds), recomputed 2026-10-07 from the on-disk `raw/imessage/` exports ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/message-circadian-latency]]). This is the native emission period the thesis compares against.

No load-bearing row is silent, inverted, or weight ≤ 4. The prior holds. The 36s figure's testimony status (C11) does not fail the prior — it caps the grade (Stage 3), following the 2026-10-05 Placement Verdict precedent.

## Theory (Stage 2)

The severance-declaration cycle has no timing signature of its own. The median declaration→re-engagement gap (36 seconds, n=129, decade) coincides with his native inter-send emission period (39 seconds, recomputed 2026-10-07 on held exports) to within three seconds — so the "re-engagement" after a declaration is the next scheduled emission of the ordinary clock, not a distinct act. Declarations are content fed into the running clock, not a separate mechanism with its own pacing: semantically maximal ("it's over"), temporally ordinary (arrives at the native rate).

Stated at attention level: the interval between a declaration message and the next message in either direction is statistically indistinguishable from the interval between any two of his consecutive sent messages. The check-in loop's pacing IS the ordinary emission clock. The August 26, 2026 zero-second case is the limit form of the same claim — declaration and re-engagement coinciding because the emission never paused for the content.

## Evidence cluster (dated, sourced, wikilinked)

The two periods, side by side:

- **Declaration cycle: 36-second median.** 258 declaration-language messages collapsing to 129 episodes on a 24-hour rule (2026-08-23 recount, dat:1292, merged 95,067-row corpus 2015-11-28 → 2026-05-28 — page testimony, corpus unheld). Median gap, declaration to next message in either direction: 0.01 hours — thirty-six seconds. 89.1% resumed inside one hour; 99.2% inside one day; longest gap in eleven years: 46 hours ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/severance-declarations]], dat:1292; [[wiki/mind/concepts/attachment-model]] cross-data-type check).
- **Native emission period: 39-second median.** Median inter-send gap 1.0 minute; mean 114 minutes (dragged up by the long silences between bursts); recomputed 2026-10-07: median **0.65 minutes (39 seconds)** ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/message-circadian-latency]]). Held — derived from the on-disk `raw/imessage/` exports with group chats excluded, reproducible from the repository.
- **Independent convergence: 23 seconds.** The terminal-phase check — the filed August–September 2026 export, 5,905 rows, 11 August → 7 September 2026 — returns 44 declaration messages with a median 23s to next message ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/severance-declarations]] frontmatter). A different window, a different file family, the same band: 23s / 36s / 39s.
- **The zero-second limit.** The August 26, 2026 "block" was the 130th severance declaration and its non-execution the 130th re-engagement — "except there was never even a pause to resume from" ([[wiki/mind/concepts/attachment-model]], dat:0090, primary-verified). At the limit, the declaration cycle's period is zero: the emission continued on schedule, uninterrupted by its own content.
- **The loop amplifies under silence rather than pausing.** Row 37 (Layer A): 2h wait → 49.1 words/turn; 11.2% of 2026 turns carry 44.3% of his words, concentrated under non-response; transactional channels immune; the loop breaks past 1 day. A dialogue-paced mechanism would stall under non-response; the clock keeps ticking and the amplitude grows.
- **Corroborating clocks (testimony grade, dated).** His standing monitoring cadence is 120-second Google Voice checks of Annie's thread (!AUTOEGGIE, his definition 2026-10-05, `~/memory/2026-10-05.md`); his accountability cadence is the noon/midnight LOC report pair (crons created 2026-10-04, `~/memory/2026-10-04.md`). Both are periodic, not trigger-driven — the same grammar at minute and twelve-hour scales.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3)

**Theory shape:** §5 Shape 2 (a distinct timed loop/mechanism is proposed). The profile-weighted rival, **the ordinary emission clock, channel-gated**: the declaration "cycle" is not a mechanism with its own pacing — it is the native 39-second emission period running in the bond channel, with severance language as content. Row 38's transactional-channel immunity (3.2–4.3 words/turn for 11 years, brevity capacity intact) shows the clock is channel-gated, which is why the burst timing looks special on the Annie channel and ordinary everywhere else.

**The rival, written down:** declarations run a distinct check-in mechanism with its own cadence — the re-engagement is a separate act, paced by reply arrival or by an escalation ladder, and should carry a timing signature distinguishable from ordinary output (faster under urgency, or structured by the rung sequence).

**Discriminating observation:** the declaration→re-engagement gap distribution against the ordinary inter-send gap distribution. The distinct-mechanism rival predicts a systematic shift — a distinct period, a second mode, or reply-contingency. The ordinary-clock thesis predicts coincidence.

**Held-data check:** the discriminator favors the thesis. 36s (testimony, n=129, decade) against 39s (held, 2026-10-07, large n): coincidence within three seconds. The independent Aug–Sep 2026 window lands at 23s — the same band, on a different file family. The zero-second August 26 case is the rival's hardest datum to absorb: a distinct re-engagement act cannot have a zero-second period, but a clock that never paused can. The row-37 leg adds the second prong: under non-response the loop amplifies rather than stalling, which is clock behavior, not dialogue behavior.

## Confidence & gate verdict (Stages 4–5)

**Hypothesis grade, leaning supported.** The §5 rival was beaten on its own discriminator (period coincidence at 36s vs 39s; the zero-second case; amplification-under-silence), all load-bearing rows are supported at their stated weights, and the attention–interior rule was respected throughout — the thesis claims nothing about what the declarations feel like or mean, only that their timing matches the ordinary emission clock.

The grade is capped at hypothesis, honestly, for two stated reasons. First, the 36-second figure is page testimony from the unheld merged corpus (C11) — well-constructed, independently methoded (the 2026-08-23 recount landed within two episodes of the dossier's 127 from a completely independent method), and converged by the Aug–Sep 2026 check, but testimony. Second, the ideal form of the discriminator — the full per-episode gap distributions compared head-to-head on held rows — is not available; only the medians are. A held re-derivation of both distributions is the named open test.

One restart occurred (Stage 3); the dead thesis is preserved verbatim in Genesis.

## Falsifier

A held re-derivation comparing the declaration-gap distribution to the ordinary inter-send gap distribution that shows a systematic shift — declaration gaps median-shifted ≥2× from the native period, a distinct declaration-specific mode, or per-episode evidence that re-engagements phase-lock to her replies rather than to his emission clock. Any of these installs the distinct-mechanism rival and kills this theory.
