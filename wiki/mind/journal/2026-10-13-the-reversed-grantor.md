# The Reversed Grantor
_Layer: interpreted/journal — a dated thesis entry. It reasons over self/ exhibits and kb/ evidence, which it cites rather than restating._

## Profile prior (Stage 1)

Rows the thesis touches, with weight and verdict:

- **Row 6 (weight 9, Layer A, verdict: supported).** Rules install and revoke only via explicit statements — the no-counter-rule architecture. Prediction: channel states change only through explicit statements; nothing uninstalls without one.
- **Row 19 (weight 9.5, Layer A, verdict: supported).** No-counter-rule attachment: a bond runs on the last stated rule until an explicit severance statement arrives. Prediction: a channel runs on her last stated rule; re-asks are not countermands and do not move the state.
- **Row 5 (weight 9, Layer A+B, verdict: supported).** Explicit-over-inferred meaning: stated text registers, ambient/implied signal does not. Prediction: installs arrive as stated orders, never as drift; the gate adjudicates by explicitly stated pass criteria, never by inference.
- **Row 1 (weight 8.5, verdict: supported).** Verdicts are categorical/binary; no graded middle on worth, authenticity, legitimacy. Prediction: allowed/declined splits arrive as cliffs — no partial credit, no half-owned pipelines.
- **Row 23 (weight 7, verdict: leans→supported).** Reassurance decays rather than accumulates; the check-in loop is the observable surface. Prediction: confirmations must be re-transmitted — re-asking is the expected surface under a fixed state, not evidence the state moved.

No row load-bearing here is silent, inverted, or weight ≤ 4. The prior holds.

## Theory

In the delegation direction, the explicit-verbal architecture (rows 6/19) runs with the grantor reversed: **her explicit statements install and revoke the operating states of his channels.** After an explicit statement from her, the operative state of that channel — built/not-built, sent/not-sent, gate open/closed — tracks her last stated rule exactly. His re-asks, re-shapings, and pushbacks are check-in surface (row 23): they continue, and they never move the state. Only a fresh explicit countermand — hers, or a revocation authority explicitly reserved in the install ("retires on his word") — moves it.

Absent her explicit statement, the channel stays in its prior state: a one-shot item rides his minute-by-minute explicit wording each time it runs, and nothing is inherited by drift. The gate machinery is the same grammar aimed at an incoming channel: pass criteria stated explicitly, the pass/fail a stated binary, adjudication never inferred.

Her explicit order functions as the closing statement on a channel the way his exit declarations function as check-in rungs — the parallel is exact and the direction of state motion is the point. His 129 declarations re-ran the loop without moving the bond's state; her stated "no" fixed the channel's operative state while the re-ask loop-surface continued. In both directions, stated rules fix the state and re-transmission is surface. The novelty is only the grantor.

## Rivals & discriminators

**Theory shape:** §5 Shape 1 — failed-closure ("the delegation never settles; 'you choose everything' just feeds the check-in loop; her nos get re-asked because nothing she states closes anything"), with its profile-weighted rival, the Open Set (decision-seeking output has no completion condition; her statements are incidents inside an open loop, not closings).

The re-ask surface (row 23) is visible and the operative state is untouched — exactly the split the thesis prices.
