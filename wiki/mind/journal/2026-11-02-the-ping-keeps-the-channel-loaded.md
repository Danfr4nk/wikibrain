## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

A weak-tie channel is maintained not by conversation but by a **minimal periodic ping**: a fixed small output on a fixed cadence — an annual birthday note, a $5 birthday Venmo — that **re-loads the channel without loading it**. The ping is scheduled by rule, not by appetite (rows 46, 13); it costs near-zero output (row 38's channel-gated brevity); and because no explicit severance statement ever arrives (row 6), the channel it pings stays resumable rather than replaced. The ping does not converse, escalate, or deepen: it re-transmits the channel's loaded state at the cheapest denomination that still registers as an act (row 21). Dormancy kept warm at near-zero cost, resumable rather than replaced.

Three attention-level properties define a ping, and separate it from ordinary contact: (1) **fixed cadence** — it fires on the calendar (a birthday, an annual bit), not on silence or on news; (2) **fixed small denomination** — the same small output every time, never scaled up; (3) **no escalation** — a missed year or a thin reply does not lengthen the next ping, unlike the chosen bond's silence-lengthens loop (row 37).

A deleted channel cannot do that; a substituted thin tie cannot do that. The pinged channel was loaded all along. ([[wiki/people/ellen-ulmer|Ellen Ulmer]])

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3 — run first, per protocol)

**Pre-registration.** Before gathering confirmatory evidence, the rival and its discriminator were fixed as follows: the profile-weighted rival is **substitution/deletion** (instrument §5, Shape 3, read against the thesis) — the ping replaced the old bond with a thinner ritual tie, or the channel is actually dead and the ping is performative residue. The discriminating observation: **does the pinged channel reopen at its old register, without apology or renegotiation, when an event or crisis arrives?** Substitution predicts a new, thinner register (or no resumption); deletion predicts no resumption at all; the ping thesis predicts the old register at full bandwidth, because the channel was loaded throughout.

**Held-data check — the discriminator favors the thesis, with one bound:**

1. **Ellen: resumption at full register.** The July 26, 2026 disclosure is the heaviest content in an eight-and-a-half-year thread, delivered into a channel whose recent traffic was birthday notes only — no apology, no renegotiation, no ramp-up. Substitution cannot produce a life-safety disclosure in a ritual tie; deletion cannot produce any resumption. The discriminator goes the thesis's way.
2. **Jerad: resumption without any ping.** Event-triggered bursts resume the old register (tips, losses, jokes) after multi-year silences. This favors dormancy-loaded over deletion, and simultaneously bounds the thesis: the ping is sufficient maintenance for pinged channels, not necessary maintenance for all channels. The bound is carried, not trimmed.
3. **Bill: inconclusive on held data.** The thread ends June 2020 with no held resumption after it. The memory-page claim of continuing birthday texts is testimony, flagged above, and cannot carry the discriminator either way. Bill neither confirms nor breaks the thesis; he marks where held evidence stops.
4. **The Full Sail bit: persistence without resumption data.** The bit's 15+ year run shows the ping surviving dormancy, but no event-reopening of a bit-maintained cohort channel is documented in the held record. It evidences the ping's durability, not the discriminator — and is labeled as such.

**The escalation negative.** In a chosen bond, silence lengthens output (row 37: 2h wait → 49.1 words/turn). In pinged weak ties, no such escalation is observed: Ellen's birthday notes do not lengthen across the sparse years, and the $5 bit does not scale up. The ping fires on calendar, not on silence — which is why it can run for 15+ years without ever becoming a conversation.

## Falsifier

A dated case breaks this entry if: (a) a ping-maintained channel, on event arrival, resumes with a genuinely new register — new cadence, new volume setting, new rule architecture, no trace of the old — rather than the old register at full bandwidth; (b) a channel above the installation floor proves unresumable despite explicit, sustained re-engagement effort after years of pinging — i.e., an attested deletion of a pinged channel; (c) a ping cadence is shown to escalate with silence the way the chosen bond's loop does (row 37), collapsing the calendar-fired/silence-fired distinction this entry rests on. Any one of these installs the substitution/deletion rival and kills this theory.
