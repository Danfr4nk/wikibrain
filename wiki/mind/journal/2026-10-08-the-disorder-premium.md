- **Row 16** (burst-and-collapse native rhythm, weight 7, leans): enters only as the faced rival, not as support.

## Theory

In disordered work environments, tenure length and forensic-output volume track whether he holds an audit position — informational asymmetry he can work, a stake he can defend, a lateral counterparty who holds the record with him. Where the disorder is un-auditable from his position (no paper trail he can hold, no territory, no ally), tenure collapses and he exits or goes procedural. Where he holds the audit, he stays long (41 and 43 months) and builds rigid structure *inside* the disorder — the tip split, the night-class alibi, the decentralized audit, the filed complaints. "Satisfaction in disorder" (the experiment's term) is priced here at the observable level only: tenure length + forensic-output rate + what got built and kept.

## Evidence cluster

1. **The first pair (§7 experiment #4):** [[wiki/work/au-zaatar-41-months]] (March 2021–August 2024, 41 months; ~690 shifts, 712 GPS-timestamped visits) vs [[wiki/work/bfs-foods]] (late April–mid-May 2026, ~1 month). Same operator, both disordered workplaces, a 41:1 tenure ratio. The difference is the audit position: at Au Za'atar he held the day-one-negotiated equal tip split (his invention; economics confirmed by Dan July 2026: $15/hr plus pool, $800–1,000 per six-day week), ran an 18-month fictitious night-class alibi to hold peak hours, pocketed cash tips as a "decentralized audit" against suspected digital-pool siphoning, and had the Triad (Dimitri — the only boss he ever loved — DJ, the regulars). At BFS the reconciliation was decorative — the CSR printed $0.00 over/under every night for ~4 weeks — and the $50 claim arrived with no paper trail on either side: disorder he could not hold, because there was nothing to hold. No private objective installed; tenure collapsed at the first assigned demand.
2. **The discriminator case:** [[wiki/work/creative-license-exit]] (April 2011–February 2012). A disordered vertical environment (the airfare citation, altered intern contracts, "It's a trust issue. We don't trust you" — February 27, 2012). He held the audit position via his oversight-of-expenses role and did sustained forensic work (IRS and NYS Department of Labor filings, per his own contemporaneous emails; ~35-thread Gmail capture). Crucially — the case that separates this thesis from row 8's payload rule alone — he returned months after exit with the unprompted May 2012 disclosure to Renae Holland about the altered intern contracts, aimed at protecting two interns who weren't him. No payload installed there; advantage alone (the informational position plus the record) was sufficient for engagement. He then conceded the $104 and walked.
3. **The lateral-layer case:** [[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddie-years]] (April 6, 2016–November 2019, 43 months). An informal yard with institutional churn (a caddie manager fired mid-2019). His edge was the peer-visible status ladder — the call-up to the "real loopers" (May 21, 2018, Laurel Valley, 4:30 AM departure), the first two-bag round (May 23, 2018), the Scottsdale transfer pitch — plus the lateral yard (Jack Rusko and the cohort). When friction came from the vertical (Brad Hubeaut, April 20–27, 2018), the lateral layer absorbed it and the tenure continued.
4. **The chosen-structure control (C5):** the 11-year Annie bond shows maximum continuity, never destabilization. Disorder is never imported into chosen structures — the appetite's boundary condition, held in the record.

## Rivals & discriminators

- **Rival 1 — row 14 as originally stated** ("entropy as fundamental truth," pure appetite): predicts indifference to advantage — lingering in BFS's rich disorder, or destabilizing chosen structures for stimulation. Discriminator (C5): (a) restoring normal function in an external system with no personal stake; (b) destabilizing a chosen bond for stimulation. Held data: no no-stake restoration instance located in the territory — that falsifier stays open, not fired; the chosen bond shows defense, never destabilization; BFS — disordered, zero advantage — collapsed to a month. **The discriminator favors the thesis.**
- **Rival 2 — row 16 alone** (burst-and-collapse rhythm; crises suit him, no advantage needed): predicts engagement with BFS's crisis-rich environment. Discriminator: tenure under crisis with vs without advantage. BFS's drawer dispute was crisis-rich and tenure still collapsed to a month. **Favors the thesis.**
- **Rival 3 — Shape 4 acquisition drift:** the "edge" could be misread as accumulating advantage-items. No item-acquisition claim is made; the claim is positional (audit capacity), so Shape 4's discriminator (duplicate-vs-novel, post-acquisition attention) does not apply. Stated and set aside.

## Falsifier

(1) A long tenure in a disordered workplace where he held no audit capacity, no stake, and no lateral counterparty. (2) Exit from a disordered workplace where he held full informational advantage. (3) A documented case of him restoring normal function in an external system with no personal stake (row 14's falsifier #1). (4) Deliberately destabilizing a chosen structure for stimulation (C5's falsifier). A cleaner forward test: a dated record of him naming the advantage calculus before a tenure decision.
