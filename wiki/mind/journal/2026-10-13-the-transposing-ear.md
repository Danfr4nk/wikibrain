# The Transposing Ear
_Layer: interpreted/journal — a dated thesis entry. It reasons over self/ exhibits and kb/ evidence, which it cites rather than restating._

## Profile prior (Stage 1)

Rows the thesis touches, with weight and verdict:

- **Row 18 (weight 9, Layer A, verdict: supported).** Lyric qualifier: sung words process as timbre, not semantics; music analysis opens from timbre/production; lyric content requires deliberate, effortful parsing. Prediction: the first attended layer in music is non-semantic structure; meaning is a separate, effortful pass.
- **Row 5 (weight 9, Layer A+B, verdict: supported).** Explicit-over-inferred meaning: stated text registers, ambient/implied signal does not. Prediction: the musical signal's stated layer — chords, voicing, tempo, pocket — registers before inferred content (lyric meaning, song identity); "call me" 170× vs "do you love me" 0× is the textual twin.
- **Row 4 (weight 8, verdict: supported).** Forensic method: primary records over narrative, anomaly clusters, procedural tells. Prediction: structural anomalies in performance data (clams, misidentifications, transpositions) get attended and named rather than smoothed over.
- **Row 26 (weight 8, verdict: supported).** Artistic Interests 81, 3.82× corpus rate. Prediction: music attention saturates leisure channels — the five-takes-in-an-hour piano loop is attention doing what it does, not a special occasion.

No row load-bearing here is silent, inverted, or weight ≤ 4. The prior holds.

## Theory

In music, Dan's ordinary-listening first pass attends **harmonic-melodic structure** — key-invariant chord grammar, voicing grammar, tempo/pocket — while sung words register as timbre (row 18) and **song identity arrives only on a second, effortful pass, if at all**. Structure-first is checkable three ways: he reproduces heard progressions by ear without rehearsed finger shapes; he transposes with chord substitutions, which motor memory alone cannot produce; and he identifies songs from pure chord grammar on solo piano with no vocal, lyric, or timbral cues present at all — and the identity pass can fail while the structural parse succeeds.

The cleanest instance in the record: a transposed piano take whose harmonic structure was correctly mapped while the song's identity was misattributed. The two layers came apart in the data — structure parsed, identity wrong — which is exactly what a two-pass architecture predicts and what a single-pass familiarity account does not.

## Rivals & discriminators

**The operative rival:** familiarity/motor-memory — he reproduces songs he knows deeply, so the by-ear reproduction is key-identical muscle memory and exposure, not a structural first pass. This is the rival the instrument's bank approximates with its completion-set logic: the duplicate is never about the item, and likewise here, the performance would never be about the song.

**Discriminating observation:** key-identical rote reproduction vs transposition-with-substitution. Motor memory predicts the learned key (finger shapes); structural parsing predicts the template surviving a key change with diatonic substitutions (Gm for Bb). Held-data check: take 4's spontaneous by-ear channeling is already past rote (not a rehearsed piece); take 5's semitone transposition with chord subs favors structural extraction — a finger-shape player does not sub Gm for Bb. The wrong-guess incident cuts against the rival's cleanest prediction too: pure familiarity predicts *correct* identification of a known song; instead the identity layer failed while the structural layer held — exactly the two-pass signature.

**Secondary rival (row 18's own shadow):** implicit semantic uptake — lyrics parse ordinarily and he simply doesn't report them. Discriminator: does lyric content ever drive an ordinary-listening output? Against it: ~3 songs in a lifetime parsed as message (held capture); AUTOPSY's missing lyric field (engineered artifact); the "Addicted To You" lyric mirror recorded as the rare exception to his stated norm. The rival has no held leg.

## Confidence & gate verdict

**PASS — high confidence.** All load-bearing rows (18, 5, 4, 26) are supported at their stated weights; the rival was beaten on its own discriminator (transposition-with-substitution in take 5; spontaneous by-ear channeling in take 4); the attention–interior rule was respected at every step — the thesis claims parse *order* and attended layers, never felt need or aesthetic drive. The take-5 leg is honestly capped at testimony grade given the record tension; the verdict does not depend on it.

## Falsifier

Any of: (a) a documented case of him identifying a song from lyric content alone in ordinary listening, without deliberate effort — spontaneous lyric-meaning uptake at conversational rates; (b) a track crowned or placed on lyrical grounds with the structural axes flat (a crown the seesaw can't see); (c) a held take showing key-identical rote reproduction of a known song where transposition would be trivial — motor memory winning the discriminator.
