## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3 — run first, per protocol)

**The profile-weighted rival: the monitor as Ti self-audit.** Row 4 turned inward: the 1.85x self-referential monitoring (row 25) is the forensic engine auditing itself, and the designations are its genuine self-modeling outputs — self-knowledge, stated aloud. On this reading the addressees are incidental (he happens to think in messages) and the labels are real inputs to a self-model.

**Discriminator 1 — addressee presence.** If designations are self-modeling, the addressed-vs-private split should not be 100% addressed: at least one dated designation should surface without a counterparty (private note, un-sent draft, unaddressed journal entry). The record: the self-typing lives inside an AI-directed prompt; the autism declaration is to Vanessa; the staccato diagnosis traveled with an apology to Jerad; the axioms were authored into his AI's configuration and published on the wiki; ihatedanfrank is a public handle. **No unaddressed dated self-designation is on record.** Discriminator favors the thesis.

**Discriminator 2 — downstream effect.** If designations are self-knowledge, a designation should change subsequent behavior — the self-model being *used*. The audit of all four: the staccato diagnosis → behavior continued regardless (stacked-essay share grew to 44.3%); the autism label → its one falsifiable behavioral claim drew a negative observation (C10, August 2026); the axioms → negative corpus audit, endorsement without behavioral delta; the 5w4 adoption → the commissioned label's use was outward (persona prompts pasted to other people), not inward. **No case of a designation moving behavior inward; every case moves the counterparty's footing.** Discriminator favors the thesis.

**The rival's honest remainder, stated openly.** The corpus is a message corpus — every held emission is addressed by construction. The absence of private designations *may be a recording artifact*, not a cognitive fact: an un-emitted interior self-model is invisible to the held instruments, and the attention-interior rule forbids pricing it either way. The thesis therefore claims only what the instruments can see: *emitted* self-designations are addressed acts. The rival's interior remainder ("he may still model himself privately") is untested, not false — and the thesis does not deny it. What the thesis denies, and the discriminators support, is that the *designations themselves* function as self-knowledge.
