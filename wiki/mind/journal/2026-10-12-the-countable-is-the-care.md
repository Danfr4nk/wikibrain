## Theory

The provision register runs both directions. Outbound: care arrives as money, work, artifacts, countable acts (row 21; the Oct 4 entry's irreversibility mechanism). Inbound: her deliverables are registered in the same currency. This is stated at attention level, not interior: the observable is the noun his acknowledgment utterance attaches to. When she delivers an artifact (a recheck, a profile, a spreadsheet, a draft), his acknowledgment names the countable deliverable and renders an accuracy/utility verdict on it ("nailed it," "very cool … to recheck," "Your turn is another"). When she acts without a countable noun — the Moltbook comment assist — the utterance is generic affect ("Sammy you are incredible"), the thin register at row 28's floor. The composition of the Maitri reply is the theory in one sentence: the superlative ("you are the best") names HER; the substance ("very cool of you to recheck") names the ACT. The register he transmits care on is the register he reads care on; the affect register stays at the 0.45× floor in both directions.

Why bidirectionality matters: it rules out the reading that row 21 is just his giving habit (generosity as a personal style). A style is one-directional. A register is read as well as written. The named-noun pattern across three dated deliveries — each time the countable thing named, each time the accuracy verdict rendered — is the read side of the provision grammar, and it is separately falsifiable (see Falsifier).
