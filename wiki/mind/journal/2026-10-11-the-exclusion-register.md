# The Exclusion Register
_Layer: interpreted/journal — a dated thesis entry. It reasons over self/ exhibits and kb/ evidence, which it cites rather than restating._

**Idea Journal — 2026-10-11, Pass D.**

Dan's stated taste verdicts run one direction only: out. Guitar is barred (2026-10-04), the drum kit is museum-live-only (2026-10-04), Lonelysoul. is exiled (2026-10-04) — each dated, reasoned with a stated bill of particulars, binary, holding. Inclusions never arrive in that form. They arrive as placement events (the ladder, the crate — binding but unstated, per the Oct 5 Placement Verdict) or as provisional affect that gets revised within days (the Oct 2 top-four → Oct 3 refinement). The stated register of his taste is an exclusion register: the only verdicts that get spoken are the bans, and the bans are the ones that hold.

## Profile prior (Stage 1)

Every stated taste exclusion in the record is a legitimacy verdict in exactly this grammar: "lame," "try hard," "scammer," "NOT COOL." No graded middle ever appears — no "guitar is fine sometimes," no demotion tier for Lonelysoul.
- **Row 5 (9, supported) — load-bearing.** Explicit-over-inferred: stated text registers. The verdicts are his words, dated and verified; the analysis reads the statements, not the implied attitude. "Hate" here is a stated verdict token, not a felt-state claim — the instruments see the utterance (§2), not the feeling.
- **Row 4 (8, supported) — load-bearing.** Forensic method: primary records over narrative. The dated verdicts (verified extractions with message IDs), the playlist CSV diffs, and the v3 purge record are the primary record the asymmetry is read from.
- **Row 6 (9, supported) — supporting.** Rules install via explicit statements. The exclusion utterances are rule-shaped ("Sorry, I don't make the rules") and criterion-shaped ("too slow" = the tempo gate, "overproducing" = a provenance gate): the stated form of a gate is its rejection condition. Carried as the function, prospectively testable, not as the load-bearing mechanism.
- **Row 26 (8, supported) — background.** Artistic Interests 81: taste-talk dominates his leisure channels, so the utterance register is dense enough here for an absence claim to mean something.

Not carried: row 17 (cool metric, weight 5) — tested and dropped at Stage 3 (see Genesis).

## Theory

The Ti gate (row 1) renders verdicts on legitimacy, and in this architecture the gate's output is a condemnation. That is why the stated register of taste runs one way: **the only things that get adjudicated aloud are the things being thrown out.** An inclusion is never a verdict — it is attention (plays, keeps, ladder promotion), which the allocation register executes without any statement needing to be made. A stated inclusion would require the gate to render "worth" positively, and the record shows it never does: stated likings arrive as provisional affect ("best vocal layering in this thing" — a within-pool superlative about an already-placed track, immediately measurement-checked) or as revisable picks (Oct 2's top four → Oct 3's refinement).

The exclusion utterance does a job the ladder cannot do: it **installs the rule** (row 6). The stated reasons map exactly onto the intake gates — "too slow" is the tempo architecture, "overproducing / try-hard / scammer" is a provenance/authenticity gate — and the reasons arrive *as* the verdict, not after it. This also explains the direction the Oct 5 entry left unexplained: why the stated layer sometimes ratifies placement after the fact. A gate's stated form is its rejection condition; you never need to state what passes, because the passing is already executing.
