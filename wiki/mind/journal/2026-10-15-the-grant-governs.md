## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

In the dated record, a state installs iff the fixing statement is explicit stated text, addressed to a named state-field, issued by that field's grant-holder, and unrevoked by any later explicit countermand from the grant-holder. The grant is itself an explicit stated rule — installed by stated text (2026-09-28), moved by stated text (2026-10-10 ~21:47), holding until an explicit countermand. The emitter's rank — his standing as the decider — never moves a state on its own. The dated collision adjudicates it: his explicit install-form directive in her granted field ("just get her order manually every day at 10:30," 2026-10-06) met her explicit standing rule ("I'm not running the order book"), and the operative state tracked her statement across four re-ask shapings — zero drift. Stated in observables only: dated orders, dated refusals, dated state outcomes. "Install" is an observable (explicit statement → state change in the dated record), not an interior term; "grant" is an observable (the stated rule naming who may install in a field), not a status claim.

**Discriminating observation (named before evidence gathering):** a collision case — his explicit install-form statement in her granted field against her prior explicit install-form statement.
