At Creative License 2011–2012, a six-week paycheck dispute was audited through IRS and NYS Department of Labor filings into the disclosure of the president's concealed retaliation — Dan's own account used to pressure former employees, altered intern contracts — answered by a false notarized LinkedIn claim ([[wiki/work/creative-license]], [[wiki/people/kevin-mckiernan]], recovered from his own Gmail 2026-08-10). The earliest held fossil already wears the vocabulary: the 2007 Facebook archive's scam-detection groups ("this is some sort of scam. there is NO way i got this wrong") and the "patron saint of liars and fakes" persona ([[wiki/self/facebook]]) — *liars and fakes*, i.e., stated realities with concealed seconds, ~18 months after November 2005.

The cognitive claim, stated at attention level: when the forensic engine (row 4) audits a vertical claim (row 12's scope), its recurring shape is the 2005 disclosure pattern — a stated vertical reality probed for the concealed reality behind it — rather than mere refusal. The 2005 event is the story-form the audits wear; whether it is also their installation point is bracketed as untestable on the held record (see Rivals & discriminators).
