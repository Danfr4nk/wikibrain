## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

Lossless retention has a complement that the forensic-method row never names but the record shows everywhere: when a fact cannot be settled from primary records, or a file must not be published yet, the engine does not emit a best guess and does not go silent. It emits the gap, in a fixed form: a dated label ([UNKNOWN], open question, held-out/pending), the hole's shape (which direction, which composition, which surface is undecided), the source that would settle it (a named thread, an export, his call), and the closure condition (an explicit statement, a settling record). The gap is then filed like any other entry — counted, indexed, carried forward — so that a later explicit statement or record closes it discretely, in one move, rather than the entry being quietly rewritten. Partial closure is itself recorded the same way: what closed and what stayed open are stated side by side, never blended into a smoothed whole.

The claim survived Stage 2 translation intact. It is a claim about the output form of unsettled records — not about what triggers an audit (2026-10-24 the-audit-is-the-governor and 2026-10-28's audit-follows-claim, cited as boundaries, never re-derived), and not about retention of settled records (row 4's home domain). Filing the gap is the move that lets retention stay lossless even where the record is incomplete: nothing is invented, and nothing is dropped.
