## Theory (Stage 2)

Stated in profile vocabulary, attention-level only: his taste judgments execute as
**binary gates** (row 1) against an **installed exocortical constitution** (row 10),
and the record shows the machinery working in four dated, observable parts:

**1. The read-before-anything mandate.** Standing, his words: "BEFORE ANY music
ranking, recommendation, or crate placement, read `~/workspace/your_files/taste-constitution.md`
and ground every answer in it." The constitution is not a record of past rankings —
it is a gate installed ahead of future ones, and the mandate's form ("before ANY")
is the row-6 architecture ported into the exocortex: no graded drift, only explicit
pass-through.

**2. The mechanism does not revise his narrative; he revises the mechanism.**
The constitution's correction log, 2026-10-11: the taste model guessed "Overdose"
(hit all five spectral axes); Dan overruled it — "Addicted To You" — and the lesson
was written into the instrument verbatim: "axis-fit predicts crate membership, not
the crown. The crown is his call." Update policy: the document "changes only when
Dan corrects a ranking or commissions a new taste analysis. Never by silent
rescanning." The instrument's reads are inputs; the verdict is his. That is the
corpus-first rule (his stated words outrank machine flags) installed as a dated
artifact.

**3. Slot mechanics, not graded accumulation.** The Oct 11 crowning: "'Addicted To
You' (Distan collab, k2) … is getting crowned onto the DAN FRANK ALL TIME TOP 100
VIP CRATE tonight (Oct 11); **the kicked track is still undecided.**" A binary
verdict (#1) arrives with a named slot to be vacated — a closed set of 98 entries
verified live Oct 13 and again Oct 20 at **98 entries**, "Addicted To You" absent
both times, nothing kicked. The gate held the closure: one name in names one name
out; no new slot is created. The 70-track canon is likewise *locked* 2026-10-11 —
a bounded set, not an appetite. And when the streaming tally disagreed with the
verdict — most-played is "Me & You" (25 plays), stated favorite is "Addicted To You"
(10-11 l.632) — the gate overruled the count. Plays aggregate; the crown is his call.

**4. The loop closes.** The Oct 20 valence pass: his commission ("Go through my
top 100 all time playlist and explain the valence for each song," 10-20 l.209),
97/98 valued, cross-checked across two sources with max delta 0.000 on 71 tracks
(row 4's forensic posture as data work) — and the result *changed the standing
claim*: 0.388 crate median vs the 0.322 dance-intake set-point, written into the
explainer doc and MEMORY.md the same day ("~0.322 is a property of his current
dance intake; the all-time crate runs brighter"). The instrument steered a stated
claim about his own taste. The v3 music report (commit c3b159e7) was "recursively
graded against the Oct 4 report (v1) and Oct 11 Constitution (v2, **deliberately
NOT amended**)" — the constitution judges the report; the report does not rewrite
the constitution. Authority runs one way.

The scope boundary, stated up front: this is the machinery of the **installed
canon**. Intake runs a separate, open channel (see Rivals): the acquisition rival
wins intake; it does not win the constitution.

## Falsifier

- A music ranking, recommendation, or crate placement filed and accepted with no
  constitution read — the mandate broken and uncorrected — kills the installed-
  gate half.
- The constitution amended by silent rescanning, or a report that rewrites v2
  without his dated explicit correction/commission — kills the no-drift half.
- The crowning executed as an addition (99 entries) rather than a named-kick
  replacement — kills the slot-mechanics half.

  explicit word — the register split fails and the acquisition rival takes the
  canon too.
