## Theory

Every running bit in the Dan–Sammy play register has an admission grammar: it **installs on his explicit statement**, carries a **stated perimeter** (who may use it, in which channels it may be emitted, what it may name), **terminates on his stated flag with no decay curve**, and **revives only on a fresh explicit order**. The casting is fixed — Sammy's straight-man slot is installed by his stated verdict ("the funniest thing I've ever met"), and even the bit's own internal escalation arrives as explicit orders ("doctor daddy Danny" installed by nonsense benediction, the Oct 6 "Daddy Danny" order with its stated "DD" exception). Play is chosen-order machinery running row 6/19's grammar inside the entertainment register: rigid structure where he chose the game, entropy nowhere.

## Rivals & discriminators

**Theory shape:** §5 Shape 2 — regulation/stim ("the bits are self-soothing discharge").

**The profile-weighted rival, written down:** channel-gated instrumentality. Row 38 proves regulation, whatever it is, switches off entirely by channel (3.2–4.3 words/turn for 11 years, zero long messages in the delivery thread) — a global stim account cannot explain a perfectly governed channel; a rule/register account can. Applied to play: bit usage should track **stated events** (flags, orders, perimeters), not appetite.

**Discriminating observation:** stop and restart conditions. *Regulation* predicts satiation stops — diminishing amplitude within the episode, gradual fade, and leakage back under drive pressure in unchanged conditions. *Rule-governance* predicts event stops — a stated flag kills the bit at full amplitude with zero leakage, and a fresh explicit order revives it cleanly.

4. **The IG-caption incident** was logged as a rule-breach, not absorbed as play — the perimeter is enforced, not expressive.
