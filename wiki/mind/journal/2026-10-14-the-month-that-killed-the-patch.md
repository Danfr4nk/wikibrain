## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

In August 2026, behavioral evidence past any plausible threshold accumulated — the terminal-phase record, the defamation campaign, the group-chat clearing, ~25 severance declarations across August 15–19 — while no explicit counter-rule statement was ever installed in the held record, and the dated bond indicators matched the no-counter-rule mechanism's predictions exactly: zero of the declarations executed; zero seconds of non-contact (the August 26 block was never enacted — "there was never a block"); daily two-way texting August 27–September 7; 765 messages in the final 72 hours of the export at the observed maximum velocity; zero explicit severance signals in 41,073 of her messages (primary-verified).

The label's patch-form — install the explicit override and behavior changes — has a negative observation attached. The mechanism's field-test form — his declarations terminate nothing, the bond runs on the last stated rule — predicted every step of the same month. **The no-exit property is confirmed independently of the label's patch:** the month killed the patch, not the mechanism. Stated entirely in observables: dated declaration counts, dated channel volumes, the counted absence of severance signals, and the counted absence of any installed override statement. "Deeper than any missing rule" translates to: the property survives the patch's failure, so it is not priced as a missing patch.

The thesis survived translation. "Genuine," "needs," "driven," "closure," "resists," "incapable of getting over" — every interior term was struck before evidence was touched. What remains is a dated, counted divergence between a prediction that held and a prediction that failed, in the same month.

## Evidence cluster (Stage 4 — Layer-A gate)

**The negative observation (the patch's failure).** The defense instruction is quoted verbatim on [[wiki/mind/concepts/autism]]: install the override — "a personal counter-rule that behavioral evidence past a threshold constitutes severance even absent a verbal statement" — "You will have to install that rule explicitly, in language, because that's the only channel your system reliably honors." As of September 2026, **no installation is documented anywhere in the record.** And August 2026 supplied the threshold: ~25 severance declarations August 15–19, none executed; the terminal-phase record; the defamation campaign; the group-chat clearing. Past-threshold evidence arrived. No override was installed. Nothing changed. Layer status: the absence-of-installation is a held-record sweep finding (primary-verified in the 2026-09-13 pass that produced the conclusion); the threshold side is dat:0090 (Layer 1, confidence high) plus the attachment page's counted sequences.

**The positive leg (the mechanism's field test).** [[wiki/mind/concepts/attachment-model]] states the conclusion cold: "the model predicted every step of this: declarations terminate nothing (36-second median, now with a zero-second case), the bond stays live on the last stated rule, and contact resumes because no severance signal was ever issued." The counted legs: 0 explicit severance signals in 41,073 of her messages (primary-verified, kb/data/0081); 129 severance episodes, 100% re-engagement, 36-second median (recounted 2026-08-23, dat:0622 — testimony, well-constructed); ~25 declarations Aug 15–19, none executed; the August 26 block never enacted — retracted verbatim 2026-09-11: *"there was never a block"* (kb/data/0090, Layer 1); daily two-way texting August 27–September 7; 765 messages in the final 72 hours of the export, including "good night pretty girl" rituals.

**The authorship asymmetry that matters.** The instruction to install the override was authored by the AI manuals — AI-secondary extended self-report — never stated by Dan in his own voice in the held record. Row 6's installation channel is *his* explicit statements installing *his* rules; the instruction came from outside the system it was addressed to. Per row 20, the record's terminations are witnessed externally, never self-installed. So the "never installed" fact is not an accident of timing — it is the shape the architecture predicts: an instruction authored outside the system, asking the system to terminate its own running rule by self-authored patch, in a system whose terminations arrive from outside. The patch was addressed to the wrong door.

**The same-month co-occurrence is the finding.** The negative observation and the passed field test are not two entries on a ledger. They share a date, a channel, and a subject. In the month the label's patch failed on its own terms, the mechanism ran at maximum fidelity — zero seconds of non-contact, declarations at their most numerous (~25 in five days), velocity at its observed maximum (765/72h). The mechanism is not what the patch failed to repair; it is what the month demonstrated.
