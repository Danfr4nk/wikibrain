## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

Place-attention follows the concentration grammar of row 11, but with different slot semantics than the social domain: **the slots that absorb the place-load are home addresses, not chosen venues.** On the held location record, the top three named locations are all residences — 307 E 76th St (1,082 visits), 155 Virginia Ave (849), 337 Saratoga Dr (~415) — together absorbing ~61% of the top-table's named visits while no discretionary venue reaches half the lowest home tier. The single non-home breaker, Au Za'atar (712 on the 2026-09-14 full-dump recount; 445 on the thinner db parse), arrives on a work-shift signature (2–3pm arrivals, 9–10pm departures, median 6.2h, Mondays dark) — imposed-axis presence (row 15), not a chosen slot. Under peak-stress years (2017–2018: 2,827 visits, the poverty-floor/deep-cycle window), visiting did not disperse across new places; it loaded the same home-area slots harder. Across the 2019 base change — the total-loss event for the PA base, physical move dated Mar 3–5, 2019 — the load did not fail over to dispersed visiting; it re-installed whole on the new home (307 E 76th became the corpus's single largest location). **Stress pushes more load into the anchored slot; base loss re-installs the concentration on the new base; discretionary visiting never concentrates.** The spatial domain is more anchored and more concentrated than the social one — places never get chosen-channel treatment.

The claim survived Stage 2 translation intact: every term is an attention-level observable (visit counts, distributions, dated presence, arrival/departure signatures). No interior claim was needed.

Nearest §5 shape: none cleanly — this is a distribution-grammar claim, not one of the six wearing shapes. The profile-weighted rival is written directly:

The rival is not winning. It survives on leg 1 only by agreeing with the narrowed thesis — which is the check working as designed: the rival forced the thesis off its original (chosen-place) formulation onto the anchored-slot formulation that the record actually prices.
