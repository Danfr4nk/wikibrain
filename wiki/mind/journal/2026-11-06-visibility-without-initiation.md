## Theory (Stage 2)

**Thesis, in profile vocabulary:** In a new-person approach, Dan runs two settings at once that a single "low sociability" reading cannot hold together: visibility at maximum, initiation at zero. On the visibility side, output is deliberate, daily, and complete — super-hearting / super-liking *all* of the target's stories, at least once a day, with first position on each story as the explicit aim (row 11's concentration shape: the chosen channel gets the whole load). On the initiation side, output is held at exactly zero by an explicit self-stated rule (row 6): no real reply, no story reply, no DM initiation before the counterparty interacts first. The gate between the two sides is row 5 operating as protocol: only an explicit incoming signal registers — even the counterparty watching his story counts, because that is a discrete, observable incoming act — and only that signal installs the next step. Ambient signal does not unlock anything; an opening being *flagged* (2026-11-05) is recorded as an opening, not acted on as an initiation license.

Stated this way, the 0.73× initiation floor of rows 13/27 is not a deficit the campaign suffers from; it is a parameter the campaign is *designed around*. The design routes around the floor rather than fighting it: the component that runs at high volume is the one that requires no initiation (engagement on material the counterparty has already published), and the component that would require initiation is not merely low — it is forbidden, by name, until a stated external event occurs. That is the difference between a rate and a protocol. A rate describes how often initiation happens to occur; a protocol states in advance the condition under which it will occur at all, and holds at zero in every case short of that condition — which is precisely the observable record here: maximal engagement accumulated over two days, an opening flagged on the second day, and still no initiating move and no posting outcome on record.

**Stage 3 pre-registration (before the evidence verdict, expanded below):** **Rival 1 — §5 Shape 2, regulation/stim:** the daily super-hearting is compulsive checking or self-soothing — a dose that repeats because it regulates something interior. Discriminator (from §5 Shape 2): a regulation account predicts a satiation curve — escalation within the pattern, dose-response, diminishing returns — and stopping tied to the dose landing; an instrumental, rule-gated account predicts stopping on *task-evidence arrival* — here, a hard stop at the rule boundary (no DM, no reply, regardless of how much engagement has accumulated) and a state-change only when the gating evidence arrives (her interaction). The interior half of the rival is additionally capped under §2 from the start: felt soothing is not observable in this record either way, so the rival is tested on its observable shape only. **Rival 2 — ordinary shyness/passivity:** low initiation is simply what low initiation looks like — he does not approach because approaching is not what he does. Discriminator: passivity is a *global* lowering — it predicts low, sporadic, low-effort output on every side of the approach at once. The protocol thesis predicts an *asymmetric* profile: the non-initiating side runs at maximum (daily completeness, a first-engagement race run deliberately against other engagers), while only the initiating side is zero. A passive pattern does not race for first position.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3, run before the evidence verdict)

**Rival 1 — §5 Shape 2: regulation/stim.** On this reading, the daily super-hearting is a self-soothing loop: the engagement repeats because performing it regulates an interior state, and the restraint about DMing is incidental — the loop simply hasn't escalated yet.

- **Discriminator (pre-registered, from §5 Shape 2):** satiation curve vs. task-evidence arrival. Regulation predicts dose-shaped behavior: escalation as tolerance builds, output that decays once the dose lands, and stopping points determined by the dose rather than by external evidence. The protocol thesis predicts the opposite signature: a flat, rule-shaped output (the same completeness, the same cadence), a hard stop at the rule boundary no matter how much engagement has accumulated, and a state-change keyed only to the arrival of the gating evidence — her first interaction.
- **Verdict:** favors the thesis on every observable the record contains. The campaign's stated parameters are completeness and position, not intensity escalation — all stories, first on each, is a *coverage* specification, and coverage does not satiate or escalate the way a dose does; there is no more than "all" to escalate to. The restraint side is not incidental but *stated in advance and by name* (2026-11-04 23:27 EST), with its release condition attached — regulation accounts do not typically arrive with a pre-registered unlocking threshold defined down to a story-view. And the one test the record offers cuts the protocol's way: on 2026-11-05 an opening was flagged — accumulated engagement plus a plausible occasion — and the initiating side did not move. Under the rival, nothing distinguishes "hasn't escalated yet" from "will not escalate"; under the thesis, the 2026-11-05 non-move is the predicted observation.
- **Kept honest:** the record is two days. A satiation curve is a claim about trajectory, and two days of flat output cannot fully exclude a curve that would bend later. This caps the margin, and the cap is declared in Confidence. The rival's interior core (soothing, compulsion as felt states) is separately untestable under §2 and was never the part being beaten — the observable shape was.

**Rival 2 — Ordinary shyness/passivity.** On this reading there is no protocol to explain: initiation is at zero because initiation is generally at zero for Dan (rows 13/27, read flatly), and the story engagement is just the low-effort residue — the part of an approach that costs nothing.

- **Discriminator (pre-registered):** symmetry. Passivity lowers output globally and indiscriminately; it predicts sporadic, incomplete, position-indifferent engagement — liking some stories, some days, whenever. The protocol thesis predicts an asymmetric profile: maximal, deliberate, effortful output on exactly the side that requires no initiation, and zero on exactly the side the rule names.
- **Verdict:** the rival loses on the visibility side's specification. The stated aims are *all* stories, *at least once a day*, *first* engagement — a first-position aim is a race, run against every other engager, on a daily clock. That is effort, scheduling, and competitive positioning aimed at being *seen*, which is the opposite pole from passivity's prediction; a passive pattern does not optimize for prominence. What rows 13/27 actually contribute, read at their stated verdicts, is the design constraint the protocol answers: initiation runs at 0.73× in *unstructured* settings (row 13's own qualifier) — and the campaign's response is to remove the unstructured initiation from the design entirely and replace it with a structured, rule-bound channel where the first move is assigned, by rule, to the counterparty. Shyness explains a man who does little; it does not explain a man who does the maximum of everything except one named move, and names it.
- **Kept honest:** rows 13/27 are carried at leans→supported and leans respectively — the floor is real, and this entry does not explain it away. The claim is about what the floor *is* in this instance: not the cause of the zero, but the parameter the stated rule was built around. If the rule were absent and initiation were merely low rather than forbidden-and-thresholded, the rival would be the better reading.

**Capped claims:** The protocol is claimed only for this campaign (from 2026-11-04, tested once on 2026-11-05) — not as Dan's approach pattern in general, and with no claim about any felt state anywhere. The accessibility observation (component 3) is carried as Dan's stated observation, load-bearing only for the design's stated premises, never as a finding about the target or her audience. The target's location is not claimed.

## Falsifier

Any of: (a) a dated record, before any interaction from the target, of Dan sending a real reply, story reply, or DM in this campaign — the rule observably broken, the zero shown to be a rate rather than a gate; (b) the campaign's engagement decaying on a satiation curve — cadence lapsing or completeness dropping while no gating evidence has arrived and no explicit rule-change is stated — which would move the trajectory evidence toward the regulation rival; (c) a next step taken on an *inferred* signal — Dan treating an ambient cue (a flagged opening of the 2026-11-05 kind, a like on unrelated material) as the unlock, without a stated redefinition of the threshold — which would break the row-5 gate specification at the entry's center; (d) the threshold being redefined *after* a move, retroactively — the stated condition rewritten to cover an initiation that already happened. A stated, explicit rule-change made *before* any move does NOT falsify: rule revision by explicit statement is row 6 operating normally, and the entry predicts the protocol would change that way if it changes at all.
