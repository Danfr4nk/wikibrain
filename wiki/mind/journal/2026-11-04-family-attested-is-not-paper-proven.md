## Theory (Stage 2)

**Thesis, in profile vocabulary:** Dan runs (at least) two non-merging status tracks for a genealogical claim. On the **paper track** (row 4), status is set only by primary written record: the Eddie link — Eddie Cogar is Edward, son of Juanita Elizabeth Thomas by her first marriage to Burdette A. Thomas — stood at SUPPORTED, not proven, because no single written record names Edward Cogar as Juanita's son. On the **attestation track**, status is set by an explicit statement from a named living witness with personal knowledge (row 5): on Nov 2, 2026, Suz's explicit confirmation upgraded the claim. The upgrade is confined to the claim's **sayable** status, and it arrives as a gate-flip into a new labeled category rather than as graded softening (row 1): the packet's tag changed from [supported] to [family-confirmed], its licensed wording changed from "the records strongly support it" to "Eddie Cogar **is** Juanita's son Edward — confirmed by Suz," and the paper status did not move at all. The two tracks are kept non-merging by a mechanical device visible in the artifacts: **the gap sentence travels with the upgraded wording** — every place the family-confirmed wording appears, the sentence stating that no single written record yet names him her son appears in the same breath, with the witness credited in that same breath. The claim is therefore spoken at the party at exactly its priced strength and no stronger: asserted as family fact, credited to its attestation source, and explicitly not claimed as paper-proven.

**Stage 3 pre-registration (recorded before the evidence verdict, expanded below):** The thesis must beat both single-track collapses on their own discriminators. **Rival A — attestation collapses into proof in practice:** once Suz confirmed it, the distinction was bookkeeping and the packet in practice states the link as proven. Discriminator: does the post-Nov 2 packet wording keep the paper-gap sentence attached to the family-confirmed wording, and does its tag stay a distinct category rather than [record]? **Rival B — nothing changes until paper arrives:** attestation changed nothing that matters; the claim stayed supported and unsayable as fact pending Juanita's 2011 obituary or an equivalent record. Discriminator: did the packet's licensed wording actually change on Nov 2 — tag, verb form, and who-is-who line? If the wording did not change, Rival B wins; if it changed and the gap sentence was dropped, Rival A wins; only changed-with-gap-attached favors this thesis.

## Evidence cluster (Stage 4)

All items are from the Fran/Cogar rebuild of Nov 1–2, 2026, and the packet artifacts it produced for one occasion: Eddie & Brenda Cogar's surprise 50th anniversary party, Sat Nov 7, 2026, 3–6pm, The Silo, Granville WV. The packet is, by construction, the document that fixes what may be said there — its status tags and "say it this way" lines are the observable sayability grades. See [[forensic-method]] for the method this entry instantiates.

### 1. The paper track, before Suz: SUPPORTED, with the gap stated as the licensed wording

- The rebuild's Juanita/Cogar run (2026-11-02 ~07:18–07:28 EST, rebuild log) graded the link **"SUPPORTED, NOT PROVEN"**: the 1950 Fairmont census shows Juanita married to Burdette A. Thomas with a one-year-old son, Edward (b. 13 Jun 1948); the 1958 Monongalia County marriage index shows Juanita marrying Okey M. Cogar; public records then pick the boy up as Edward Thomas Cogar of Maidsville. The log's verdict line is explicit: "NO record yet names Edward Cogar as Juanita's son," and its party line: "present the chain as the strong family story it is, NOT as record-proven," with Juanita's 2011 obituary named as a closing record that could settle it.
- The findings notebook (entry added 2026-11-02) carries the same grade in the same words: "almost certainly" is "the honest label," and "no single record yet says 'Edward Cogar, son of Juanita.'"
- The pre-Suz packet (`fran-family-packet-FINAL.md`) defines a three-tag taxonomy — [record] = primary record in hand · [supported] = strongly supported by the records, not proven by a single document · [open] = unanswered — and tags the Cogar seam **[supported — say it this way]**, licensing exactly this sentence: "No single record yet names Edward Cogar as Juanita's son, and Eddie & Brenda's marriage isn't verified in the records searched. The chain fits every record found, but the honest line is 'the records strongly support it,' not 'proven.'" Its who-is-who line is correspondingly hedged: "On the supported reading, Eddie is Fran's nephew — Juanita's son."

### 2. The same rebuild shows the paper track moving — when paper moves it

The supported grade was not paper-track stasis in general; in the same Nov 1–2 rebuild, primary records flipped other items outright, which is what makes the Eddie seam's non-movement informative:

- **Marion Edward Thomas Sr.'s death date:** the GEDCOM said Sep 27, 1953; the WV Deaths Index says Sep 30, 1953 — and the index won. The rebuild log's Batch 5 audit settles it ("MARION DEATH DATE SETTLED: **30 Sep 1953**… The GEDCOM's 27 Sep 1953 is WRONG"), and both packets print Sep 30, 1953 tagged [record].
- **Donald Elmore Thomas:** the old tree's two brothers, Donald and Elmore, were resolved as one person, not two (census households taking turns with his names, SSA Applications & Claims naming "Donald Elmore Thomas") — collapsing the sibling count to nine and matching Fran's obituary line, "the last living of 8 siblings," exactly.

So the paper track has a working upgrade mechanism — a primary record arrives and the grade flips, in writing, the same run. For the Eddie link, no such record arrived on Nov 2. What arrived was a different class of source entirely.

### 3. The attestation event: an explicit statement from a named witness with personal knowledge

- The Suz Q&A addendum (Nov 2, 2026, 19:46–19:50 UTC, relayed by Dan; Question 1, the Cogars) records the event at statement level: Suz knows Eddie and Brenda Cogar personally, from years of family trips to West Virginia with Fran for family gatherings — "a lived relationship, not a paper one" — and, asked directly whether she knows Eddie as Juanita's son Edward, from Juanita's first marriage, she said **yes**.
- The addendum's own statement of the consequence is the two-track device in one paragraph: packet wording "can now move from 'the records strongly support it' to **'family-confirmed by Suz, who knew them personally from years of family trips to WV with Fran'** — crediting her as the source," followed immediately by: "(Still true and worth keeping in the file: no single written record names Eddie as Juanita's son; Juanita's 2011 obituary remains the paper document that could.)"
- The same attestation produced a second item graded wholly on the attestation track: **Alan**, Eddie's brother, deceased, per Suz — with her hedges preserved verbatim in the merged packet (she thinks Alan was the younger brother but is **not sure**), and the merged packet stating plainly that a public-records search on Nov 2 found no obituary or record for him. Alan is sayable as family memory and as nothing more; no paper status was manufactured for him.

### 4. After Nov 2: the wording changed, the category stayed distinct, and the gap sentence traveled

- The merged packet (`fran-family-packet-FINAL-merged-2026-11-02.md`), which supersedes the earlier FINAL plus addendum as the single party document, expands the tag taxonomy to four: it adds **[family-confirmed] = confirmed from personal knowledge by Suz, who knew the people** — a new category, defined separately from [record], not a rename of it.
- Its Cogar seam is tagged **[family-confirmed — say it this way]** and licenses the assertive form: "Eddie Cogar **is** Juanita's son Edward, from her first marriage — confirmed by **Suz, who knows Eddie and Brenda personally from years of family trips to West Virginia with Fran** for family gatherings. That is a lived relationship, not a paper inference." In the **same paragraph**: "Keep the paper gap honest if it comes up: no single written record yet names Edward Cogar as Juanita's son, and Eddie & Brenda's marriage isn't verified in the records searched — Juanita's 2011 obituary is the document that could settle the paper side."
- Its who-is-who line drops the hedge: "Eddie is Fran's nephew — Juanita's son [family-confirmed by Suz]" — where the pre-Suz packet had "On the supported reading, Eddie is…"
- Its Handle-with-care section states the non-merger as an instruction: "**Eddie's parentage:** family-confirmed by Suz — say that, crediting her. Do **not** claim a document proves it; the paper gap is real (Juanita's 2011 obituary is the candidate document)."

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3, run before the evidence verdict)

The closest §5 bank family is the verdict/gate shape (Shape 5's territory, row 1's machinery): a status that moves, if it moves, by adjudicated gate-flip rather than by graded drift. The two rivals below are the two ways a two-track claim can collapse into one track; both were faced on discriminators pre-registered in Theory above, before the packet comparison was scored.

**Rival A — Attestation collapses into proof in practice.** On this reading, [family-confirmed] is proof with a courtesy label: once a witness Dan credits confirmed the link, the packet states it as fact and the paper gap is a footnote that does no work.

- **Discriminator:** post-Nov 2, does the gap sentence stay attached to the upgraded wording, in the seam paragraph itself and in the handling instructions, and does the tag remain a category distinct from [record]?
- **Verdict:** favors the thesis. The merged packet attaches the gap sentence inside the very paragraph that licenses "Eddie Cogar **is** Juanita's son Edward," repeats it in Handle-with-care as a prohibition ("Do **not** claim a document proves it"), and defines [family-confirmed] as its own tag, sourced to a person's personal knowledge, alongside — not instead of — [record]. A collapsed grade would need none of that machinery; the machinery is the non-merger, maintained in the document whose entire purpose is what gets said out loud.

**Rival B — Nothing changes until paper arrives.** On this reading, attestation is inert: without a primary record the claim remains [supported] and unsayable as fact, and Suz's confirmation at most warmed the prose around an unchanged grade.

- **Discriminator:** did the licensed wording change on Nov 2 — tag, verb form, and who-is-who line — in the dated artifacts?
- **Verdict:** favors the thesis. Pre-Suz: [supported], "the records strongly support it," "On the supported reading, Eddie is…" Post-Suz: [family-confirmed], "Eddie Cogar **is** Juanita's son Edward," "Eddie is Fran's nephew — Juanita's son." The change is dated, artifact-to-artifact, and attributable to the attestation event alone — no new written record entered the chain between the two packets; §2 of the Evidence cluster shows what a paper arrival looks like in this rebuild (Marion's date, Donald Elmore), and Nov 2 brought no such arrival for the Eddie seam.

**Capped claims:** This entry covers one claim seam in one rebuild (Nov 1–2, 2026) and the packet artifacts written for one party. It does not claim the two-track grading governs every domain, and it does not grade the Eddie link itself — whether Eddie is in fact Juanita's son is the claim under grading, not a finding of this entry. Suz's confirmation is carried here as what the artifacts carry it as: a named witness's explicit statement, relayed by Dan on Nov 2, 2026 — testimony that the packet credits in-line, not a held primary record, and the thesis needs it only as the dated attestation event whose effect on wording is observable.

## Falsifier

Any of: (a) a packet, cheat sheet, or party document from this rebuild stating the Eddie link as [record], "proven," or document-backed without a new written record having arrived — that would be Rival A winning late, the tracks merging in practice; (b) the family-confirmed wording appearing anywhere in the party materials with the paper-gap sentence and the credit to Suz removed — the coupling device failing is the thesis failing; (c) a pre-Suz artifact already licensing "family-confirmed" or the assertive "Eddie is Juanita's son" wording, which would break the Nov 2 chronology the attestation effect rests on; (d) a case in the same rebuild where a named witness's attestation alone rewrote a paper grade — e.g., Alan's dates or burial printed as record, or a GEDCOM date retained against a primary index on a family member's say-so — which would show source class does not govern what a confirmation may change. Juanita's 2011 obituary surfacing and naming Edward would NOT falsify this entry: it would be the paper track finally moving by its own mechanism, exactly as the graded wording predicts it could.
