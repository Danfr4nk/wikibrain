# Stress Loads the Failing Channel

**Idea Journal — 2026-10-08, pass C (crisis-period timing and message-volume records).**

## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

Under stress on a contested channel, Dan's outbound message volume to that channel rises against its own baseline; the stressed channel absorbs more load, never less. The failure mode is binary — total-commitment flooding or total dormancy — with no graded partial retreat. Expressed only in observables: outbound counts per window rise in dated crisis windows; gap distributions compress (median declaration-to-next-message 36s; 62.7% of inter-send gaps under 2 minutes); channel share stays concentrated (Gini ≥ 0.9 across crisis years); the transactional channel's brevity rate is invariant through the same windows. No claim about felt states; nothing in this theory requires "need," "panic," "anxiety," or "drive" — the burst machinery is the claim.

## Evidence cluster (Stage 4 — Layer-A gate)

**Crisis years are the volume twins.** The OMNI_FORENSIC_DOSSIER cut (175,358 messages, 2015-11-12 → 2026-03-25), published in [[wiki/mind/synthesis/message-circadian-latency]], gives the yearly arc: 2018 (the drug/relationship spiral) **40,514** messages and 2025 (the terminal-phase collapse) **41,278** — within 2% of each other, the corpus's twin peaks. If stress diversified attention across channels, the crisis years would spread; they concentrate. The table is testimony (unheld dossier), but the held monthly log corroborates the direction: 282 messages in 2021, none at all from May 2021 through December 2022, six in January 2023 ([[wiki/timeline/master-timeline]]).

**The Aug 2026 field test floods the contested channel.** ~25 block/severance declarations across August 15–19, zero executed blocks, daily two-way texting August 27–September 7 — **765 messages in the final 72 hours** of the export, including "good night pretty girl" rituals; Dan confirmed verbatim *"there was never a block"* ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/august-26-block-retraction]]). Primary-verified (rows 6/19). The 765/72h figure itself is flagged: it comes from an in-conversation forensic pass and the August 11–September 7 export was not yet filed in raw/ as of the pass — load-bearing as direction, exact figure as counted-in-chat. Under maximum contestation, the contested channel runs at its highest observed velocity, with zero seconds of non-contact.

**The July 2026 recontact floods on reopening.** The June 1, 2026 closure held for 52 days (twenty-seven times the longest resumption gap on record) and collapsed July 23 — then 624 messages across four days ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/severance-declarations]]). Recontact under stress is a volume flood, not a graded return.

**Non-response lengthens output to the same channel.** 2h wait → 49.1 words/turn (row 37); 11.2% of 2026 turns carry 44.3% of his words; Annie 2.40×→3.65× words/turn in a year. The July 26, 2026 reentanglement runs from a rape accusation and "don't contact me again" (05:22) to a joke about the dog catching a rabbit (20:20) with nothing resolved — the reset is unconditional and takes fifteen hours ([[wiki/mind/concepts/attachment-model]]).

**The burst machinery.** Of all gaps between Dan's consecutive sent messages, 62.7% are under 2 minutes; the longest unbroken run with gaps under 10 minutes is 284 messages; median inter-send gap 1.0 minute against a mean of 114 minutes — a bimodal distribution: firing continuously or dark for hours ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/message-circadian-latency]]). The machinery for the total-commitment mode is measured, not inferred.

**Scope: concentration, not universality.** Of Dan's 70,123 sent messages in the latency cut, the top contact alone is 31,635 (45%); the next tier are bounded single-year spikes that vanish from the log — one channel at 45%+ and everything else as transient satellites. And row 38's delivery thread holds 3.2–4.3 words/turn across all eleven years *including* the peak years. Stress loads the failing channel; it does not load every channel.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3 — run first, per protocol)

The thesis is not a Shape-1/failed-closure variant — it is a claim about *where output load goes under stress*, so it faces the task-specified rivals directly.

**(a) Shape 2's channel-gated instrumentality.** Predicts the stress output stops on task-evidence arrival, not on satiation — the crisis volume is logistical (the drug-supply tether, the diurnal shift in the collapse window: "the writing became more diurnal (coordinated, task-driven)"). It has a genuine leg in 2018. But its discriminator is termination: an instrumental sender's output halts when the task-evidence lands. The record shows the opposite: August 2026 ran with *zero seconds of non-contact* — no task, no evidence threshold, no halt, the "block" reported but never enacted. Row 23 prices it: reassurance decays, confirmations must be re-transmitted. And row 37's loop runs the wrong direction for instrumentality: the absence of a reply *extends* output (2h wait → 49.1 w/turn) rather than task-evidence terminating it. Rival (a) fails its own discriminator on the held data.

**(b) Diversification.** Predicts stress spreads load across channels. Killed three ways, all held: the Gini replication 0.961/0.9556 (row 11, weight 10 — the only true replication in the cluster); the 45%-single-contact cut; row 37's "same-counterparty intact" concentration under non-response. The crisis years are the twin peaks, not the twin spreads. Rival (b) fails.

**The mandatory counter-case: the 2021–22 blackout.** 2021: 280 messages; 2022: 4 (OMNI cut) — or held-corpus terms: 282 in 2021, none May 2021–Dec 2022, six in January 2023. Near-total communication silence across two full years. If the thesis said "attention always floods the contested channel," this would kill it. It doesn't, and the integration rests on two independently documented distinctions:

1. **The blackout is the no-stress control, not a stress window.** The era is documented as "low mobility, one anchor institution" — the Au Za'atar 41-month tenure, "the one boss he ever loved" ([[wiki/timeline/master-timeline]]). No contested-channel stress event is documented May 2021–Dec 2022. The thesis is conditional on stress: stressed → the channel absorbs load; dormant → the channel idles at zero. This is precisely row 11's binary failure mode — total-commitment or total-loss, never partial — and the blackout is the total-loss mode observed, matching the 2026-10-05 Dormant Slot claim as background (dormancy, not exit).
2. **The blackout is held-channel silence, not established behavioral silence.** The era's actual stress event — the Valeria entanglement, which "detonated across seventeen days in May and June of 2022" — ran, per her page, a nine-month message gap whose escalation occurred "on a channel the archive doesn't hold, WhatsApp the obvious candidate" ([[wiki/people/valeria-iglesias-cid]], inference flagged). The held instruments cannot observe the channel that was being loaded. So the falsifier is sharpened, not dodged: a dated stress event on a contested channel *inside the held record* whose window shows outbound volume at or below its dormancy baseline would falsify the thesis. The 2021–22 blackout is not that observation.

## Confidence & gate verdict (Stage 5)

Both specified rivals beaten on their discriminators in held data: instrumentality fails termination (zero-second non-contact under maximum stress; non-reply lengthens output), diversification fails distribution (Gini replication, twin peaks, same-counterparty intact). The mandatory counter-case is integrated with two held-data distinctions rather than dodged, and it sharpens the falsifier instead of surviving as a loose end. The attention–interior boundary is respected throughout — every claim is in counts, gaps, latencies, and shares; no interior term is load-bearing.

## Falsifier

A dated stress event on a contested channel inside the held message record, whose stress window shows Dan's outbound volume to that channel at or below its dormancy baseline (i.e., stress with *lower* load than rest, channel-scoped, instrument-verified). Equivalent kill: a re-derivation of the Gini on a fuller corpus landing materially below ~0.9 while crisis-year volume peaks persist — that would break the concentration half of the thesis.
