No row below weight 6, silent, or inverted is load-bearing. Stage 1 passes.

## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

Sung lyric content enters his output (transcription commissions, lyric-content
readings in dated taste judgments, quoted lyric text in the record) **only
where the content instantiates a held anomaly/pattern cluster** — where the
lyrics are legible as an instance of a labeled, deconfounded pattern he
already attends to. Lyric uptake is **pattern-confirmation** (row 4's cluster
mechanism operating through row 18's gate), not semantic processing: what
parses is the pattern wearing words, not the words as words.

Stated at attention level, because the instruments permit nothing else: the
observable is a parse event — he commissions a transcription, or a dated
taste judgment quotes or references lyric *content* rather than vocal
texture — and the parse event's object is always a cluster match, never
novel semantic comprehension. The gate is default-closed (rows 18, 1: timbre,
no graded middle); it opens where a held cluster supplies the register the
lyrics alone cannot supply (rows 4, 5). This extends row 5 to music by
inversion: in ordinary language, stated text registers; in music, stated
(sung) text does not register — *unless* it instantiates a stated/recorded
pattern, in which case the pattern is what registers.
