## Theory (Stage 2)

Stated in profile vocabulary, attention-level only: his graded numbers and stated
verdicts run in **two directed modes**, and in neither mode do they govern his
conduct.

**Outward mode — numbers as audit instruments** (row 4's forensic method applied to
other people's claims): on 2026-10-17 he flatly distrusted Pew's "49% of US adults
use an AI chatbot" and the number was forensically deconstructed against the primary
source, graded caveat by caveat; on 2026-10-19 his own archive's kill-switch
artifact (imessage-toggle.js) was adjudicated to its true function — outbound TCP
5223 kill switch toggled with the spacebar, not the autoresponder — separating two
artifacts from the same era. The numbers go *out* as measurement applied to the
world.

**Inward mode — numbers as intensity markers with no behavioral delta** (rows 3,
9): the 2026-10-11 crowning — his binary true-#1 verdict, "Addicted To You" crowned
onto the DAN FRANK ALL TIME TOP 100 VIP CRATE in his own words — was verified live
on 2026-10-13 and again on 2026-10-20 to be **absent from the Spotify playlist**,
98 entries both times, nothing kicked. The verdict was announced; the behavior was
never executed. A week apart, two live verifications, zero conduct. That is the
row-9 gap in dated form: the analysis was the product; the product changed nothing.

**The fence holds through both modes** (row 1): the inward worth-verdict is binary
(#1, not a percentage), exactly as the fencing predicts — gradation lives in facts,
never in worth — and even in its binary form it steers nothing. His conduct runs
on explicit statements and the re-engagement machinery (rows 5/6/19), never on his
own numerals. The claim is not that his numbers are *wrong*; it is that his
numbers *do not govern him*.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3)

Theory-shapes: none of the six §5 shapes names "numbers as forecasts" directly,
so the rival is constructed from row 3's own falsifier plus a Shape-2-family
check.

**Rival 1 — the forecast rival.** The numbers are predictions about the world;
where checkable, they score. **Discriminating observation:** one dated case where
a measured or stated number changed his own behavior — and, failing that, whether
the numbers aim at scoreable propositions at all.

Faced before confirmatory evidence, per protocol:

- The credences sort, on re-derivation, into interiors (unfalsifiable),
  unwitnessed pasts (no resolution exists), and checkable futures — where there
  are *almost none*. Row 2 predicts exactly this clustering; the forecast rival
  predicts the reverse.
- n=1 resolvable, resolved false (2018-08-08). The rival's one checkable datum
  goes against it.
- The crowning: a stated worth-verdict with two dated live verifications of zero
  behavioral delta (Oct 13, Oct 20). The forecast rival would have to price the
  verdict as a prediction of nothing — it doesn't predict, it *announces* — and
  the conduct it should have governed didn't move.
- The testimony ledger prices "certain" at 0.25. Even as testimony it agrees.

The forecast rival is beaten on its own discriminator. The base rate stands: **no
dated case exists where a stated or measured number changed his conduct.**

**Rival 2 — the mere-rhetoric rival.** His percentages are ordinary emphatic
language, like everyone else's. **Discriminator:** the specificity of the
asymmetry. The record: 24-vs-1 strict re-derivation (~22×), 21-to-0 on the
narrow self-confidence construction (held 13-to-0), against general numeracy at
2.94 vs 2.16 per thousand — 1.36×. Bare numbers barely distinguish him; numbers
pointed at his own mental state distinguish him completely. The rival explains
the 1.36×, not the 21-to-0. Beaten.

**The honest counter-case** (carried from the concept page, not suppressed): the
2024-11-07 self-audit — he treats his own past numbers as auditable against
himself — shows the scoring norm exists inwardly too, so the inward numbers are
not *designed* to evade checking; they structurally do. And the "intensity, not
forecast" reading is the totality's reading, not a measurement. This entry does
not re-litigate the concept page's conclusion; it extends it from "the numbers
aren't forecasts" to "the numbers don't steer."
