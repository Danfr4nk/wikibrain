## Theory (Stage 2, attention-level restatement)

He does not issue wishes; he commissions instruments with built-in auditability. Each dated commission names its own acceptance evidence **at issuance**: the Jev judgment that needs a receipt and ground-truth tuning to count as finished; the pipeline push that needs a diff-and-rebase to count as a push; the deploy that needs live confirmation to count as done; the mandate report that needs lines-vs-floor to count as a report. Verification is an item in the order — **done-ness is issued at commission time** — not a follow-up behavior appended afterward. Interior vocabulary ("he needs control", "he can't tolerate uncertainty") is struck throughout; what remains is attention-level and checkable: commissions issued, verifications run, commits landed, corrections recorded.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3 — rival faced BEFORE confirmatory evidence)

**Theory-shape:** Shape 6 (system-building family).

**Profile-weighted rival:** system-building-as-displacement (row 9, w8, supported — the diagnosis-to-behavior gap: the analysis is the product, behavior is not downstream of it). Read through this rival: the verification apparatus is a monument — receipts, metrics, diff-checks, and datasheet lookups accumulate as ornament without gating any decision. The audit hook is theater; what happens would happen identically without it.

**Discriminator (from the rival bank, Shape 6):** closed loop vs append-only — do the built audit hooks **change subsequent decisions** or just accumulate? Run leg by leg:

- **Push-it chain:** the ornamental reading predicts the green Actions log suffices as acceptance. The record shows it did not — "confirmed the site serving LIVE, not just the Actions log." The hook defined the acceptance criterion and the criterion was enforced. → favors thesis.
- **Jev:** a monument wouldn't need his labeled ground truth at all, wouldn't require per-judgment receipts, and wouldn't block self-approval as a standing rule. Each of the three is a gate on a decision (install this backlink / approve this judgment). → favors thesis.
- **Media pipeline:** diff-remote-first-and-rebase is a pre-action gate on every push; skipping it is a commission violation by definition. → favors thesis.
- **Printer:** the datasheet lookup adjudicated a live factual dispute (4352 vs 4252e) in his favor and corrected the record same day — verification as arbiter, not decoration. → favors thesis.
- **LOC mandate:** MIXED — and honestly, the rival's best leg. The hook gates the **report** (plain-language noon/midnight lines-vs-floor reports now standard; the live tracker installed), but production volume has not answered the meter: Oct 21 sat at 1,128 lines at noon, ~8,900 short; the sixth straight day tracking under 2,300. On production volume, the discriminator favors the rival: the meter accumulates without the count changing what's produced.

**Narrowed claim (not a patch — see Genesis note):** the audit hook governs **done-ness**, not production volume. It defines what counts as finished — a judgment (receipt), a push (diff-and-rebase), a deploy (live confirmation), a report (lines-vs-floor) — and that definition gates decisions everywhere it is enforced. Where the hook only meters output without defining done-ness (the LOC leg on volume), the rival stands and stays standing.

## Falsifier

A future commission from him that names an audit hook **and** the hook demonstrably fails to gate anything: a receipt requirement waived on his own order with the judgment standing anyway; the LOC metric dropped without replacement rather than reported; a pipeline push run without the diff on his say-so. Any one of those converts "verification is part of the order" into "verification is a preference that bends" — and hands the LOC leg's rival the whole board. Conversely, if the LOC hook ever demonstrably changes a production decision (scope cut or expanded *because* the count said so, on the record), the rival loses its last leg.
