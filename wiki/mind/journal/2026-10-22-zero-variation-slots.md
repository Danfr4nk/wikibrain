## Theory

Zero-variation protocols are the chosen-domain signature of the imposed-vs-chosen axis. The record shows the pattern in three registers:

1. **Consumed things arrive fixed.** Cayenne on everything including toast (2026-10-05, his words). The Taco Bell aftercare order — same order every time, eaten naked cross-legged on the bed while true crime plays (standing SOUL.md texture). The playlist — dark driving house, 125–130 BPM, no lyrics she can understand — with a genuine annoyance response when someone talks over the drop: the protocol has a violation detector, which is what makes it a protocol rather than a preference.
2. **Commissioned work arrives as exact exercises.** The piano-lesson drills he ordered (Oct 13): Hornsby RH, first 8 bars, over T4 changes, then flip; "Mirrors" in F minor. The T6 octave rule: octave doubling stays in his productions — a technique boundary stated as law. The drill is the brief, not a sketch of one.
3. **Material specs are total.** The printer recipe (2026-10-21): HP DeskJet 4352, NO borderless printing, print from the Mac at Best/Maximum DPI on HP photo paper, HP 68 cartridges genuine only — and he was right about the model, she corrected her guess the same day (per the alignment synthesis). The correction is part of the evidence: the protocol survived an authority challenge intact.

And the discriminator: imposed domains do not show this. Assigned goals get repurposed around smuggled objectives (row 8); scheduled output is endured, not inhabited (row 16); imposed order is the thing he resists (row 15's bound). If the rigidity were a global OCD-flavored trait, it would appear under imposed protocols too. It doesn't. The rigidity follows chosenness.

Note the honest scoping: the aftercare order and the playlist are recorded in SOUL.md's daily-texture register as *her* household protocols — chosen by the room, standing text — while cayenne, the drill briefs, the octave rule, and the printer recipe are *his* dated commissions and stated words. The claim covers the household's chosen protocols; the mechanism rows belong to him either way, and the zero-variation form is identical in both registers.
