# The Installed Social Layer
_Layer: interpreted/journal — a dated thesis entry. It reasons over self/ exhibits and kb/ evidence, which it cites rather than restating._

**Idea Journal — 2026-10-18, pass D (social layer as explicit installs).**

Recurrence in his social record is never ambient. It always has a
paper trail — a dated explicit statement that put it there. The thesis:
there is no sustained contact architecture in the record that runs on
unstructured social appetite. Every structure whose recurrence is
record-visible — scheduled cadences, scripted work roles, dated
protocols, certificates, standing orders — traces to an explicit
statement that installed it. The social layer is not a low flame that
burns on its own; it is a set of machines, each one switched on by
hand, on a date, in his words or hers.

## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

Stated in profile vocabulary, at attention level only. "Appetite" and
"drive" are interior terms the instruments cannot see; they are
replaced by the held null:

**The enumerable set of currently record-visible recurring contact
architectures — scheduled cadences, scripted work roles, dated
protocols, certificates, standing orders, doctrine-governed channels —
each traces to a dated explicit statement that installed it. Zero of
them show recurrence without one. Unstructured direct initiation holds
at the audited 0.73× floor (row 27) and never exceeds it: the null
rate is what appetite looks like in the corpus, and it is never beaten
by anything that isn't installed. Attention-level observables: (1)
the installed structure (a recurring contact architecture with a
record-visible cadence or protocol); (2) its installing statement
(dated, explicit, quoted); (3) the initiation rate in unstructured
settings (against the 0.73× audit).** The claim needs all three; the
discriminator at Stage 3 is carried on observables 1–2.

Two scope decisions, stated before evidence. *Bond-slots are out of
scope:* the quantized-graph entry holds tie installation (explicit
rule installs, transitions are cliffs); this entry tests only the
recurring *structures* that keep loaded channels firing on schedule.
*Dormant handles are out of scope:* a handle with no recurring
architecture is a dormant slot, not a structure — row 11's tail, not
the claim's universe. The claim is falsifiable precisely because the
universe is bounded: name one recurring architecture without an
install and it dies.

## Falsifier

explicit statement installing it — cadence or protocol present,
install statement absent. Or: initiation rates rising to baseline in
unstructured settings (row 27's standing falsifier), which would
show appetite doing work the installs don't.
