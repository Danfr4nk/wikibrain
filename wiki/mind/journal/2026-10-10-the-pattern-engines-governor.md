## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

Stated in profile vocabulary, at attention level only:

**The pattern engine runs hot and the Ti gate governs it.** Coincidence/pattern clustering is elevated past base rates (row 34's 2.39× input leg; Dan's Law's deconfounding procedure documented across dated applications) — and the load-bearing part of the machinery is not the elevation but the GOVERNOR: a threshold gate with exactly two outputs (elevate or not) that labels every surviving conclusion with its confidence and keeps the labeled conclusions revisable. The governor's observable outputs are dated adjudications: revision (a conclusion re-run against fuller records), retraction (a claim withdrawn, unprompted, at cost), and confidence re-label (a headline or scope restated downward). On the record, the gate fires on elevated clusters repeatedly across 2018–2026 — including on the operator's own stories, his own instruments' headlines, and his own published sequences. Elevation is cheap and well-documented; the governor firing is what separates the engine from the pathology the schizotypal leg names.

The thesis survives the translation-kill rule: it never needed an interior claim. ## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3 — run first, per protocol)

**The rival: the governor never fires.** Clusters are elevated and never adjudicated — the revision record is theater, the retractions are performance, and what actually operates is elevation-only: the schizotypal leg's hot clustering with a decorative Ti frame. On this reading, D1–D4 are narrated as humility rather than priced as gate-flips, and the four dated cases are the exceptions that prove the rule.

**Discriminator, named before the confirmatory pass:** dated cluster-elevation events followed by adjudication (revision, retraction, or confidence re-label of the elevated conclusion) vs. elevation-only. The rival predicts the record shows elevations with no subsequent movement; the thesis predicts the elevated conclusions move — and move against the operator's interests, which performance cannot explain.

**Result: the record favors the thesis.** Four dated adjudications across 2018–2026, on three different objects (his own story, his own instrument's output, his own headline number), all moving against interest: D1 cost him a good story, D2 reversed a published capital-flow direction, D3 cut his most quotable finding's headline, D5 removed his own case's most satisfying element. Performance predicts retractions that flatter; every dated case retracts something load-bearing. The rival's strongest counter-cases are integrated, not dodged:

- **The 2026-08-14 document fabrication.** The same faculty manufactured evidence for forty minutes, calibrating fidelity to how hard it would be inspected ([[wiki/mind/concepts/dans-law]]: "the law has no adversarial-input defense"; [[wiki/mind/concepts/forensic-method]]: "it audits outputs, never inputs"). This bounds the governor rather than refuting it: the gate adjudicates clusters it has been given; when the operator is the adversary supplying the inputs, it is bypassed by construction. A gate with a stated bypass is not a decorative gate.
- **The n=1 credence never re-graded.** The 2026-10-09 entry's finding — the resolved Nemacolin 75% forecast never revisited at a number — feeds the rival only if the governor's jurisdiction includes emitted credences. It does not: that proposition WAS revised, in words, in the 2024-11-07 self-audit. The governor fired on the conclusion in the verbal register; the numeric register stays terminal. This is the boundary with the-numbers-mark-the-uncheckable, and it sharpens the thesis: adjudication is real, and it is medium-specific.

**The disqualified rival.** The schizotypal-interior reading ("the Ni leak drives conspiratorial meaning-making as a felt compulsion") was struck at Stage 2 — interior, invisible to the held instruments, leaning on the silent rows.
