## Theory

The Jev program's ground-truth design is his explicit-statement architecture (rows 1, 5, 6) installed on the machine. In profile vocabulary: his stated verdicts — explicit correction text (the negatives, hand-curated from wiki CORRECTED blocks and RETRACTED.md) and kb datum claims with concrete evidence bodies (the positives) — are the only labels the machine's thresholds tune against (row 6); the machine is never permitted to enumerate candidates from ambient signal, only to judge code-generated claim text (row 5); its output is forced back through binary verdict bands with a refusal trench, never carried as graded partial credit (row 1); and every judgment writes a lossless receipt with model pin, question version, and state digest (rows 4, 10). Stated words install the judge; the machine does not infer meaning, and it does not grade what was never judged. Stage 2 survives translation with the claim intact — the thesis was already an attention-level claim (labels exist, receipts exist, the trench is a documented routing rule), so no interior terms needed striking.

## Rivals & discriminators

**Shape-6 rival (system-building-as-displacement):** the ground-truth design is a monument — receipts accumulate, thresholds are tuned, but nothing ever routes through the machine's judgments; the labels are elaborate bookkeeping.

**Discriminating observation:** whether a dated instrument judgment changed a downstream decision (outsourcing) vs accumulated as an inert receipt (displacement). The discriminator is AVAILABLE. **E1, dated 2026-10-03:** the measured verdict — every edge class below the 0.80 holdout-precision bar — killed the autonomous typed-edge generation path and installed the suggestion-only two-stage pipeline in its place. Pre-instrument base rate would have continued toward typed-edge automation; the instrument's output rerouted the program. Displacement predicts the 5,433 receipts sitting inert; instead they vetoed a deployment. The discriminator favors the thesis.

The secondary rival (vendor-shaped, not profile-shaped): the decision receipt and no-self-approval rules arrived via the field guide. The discriminator — where the design deviates from vendor guidance in the profile direction — also favors the thesis: vendor consensus-label ground truth was explicitly rejected for his labeled data (row 6), the vague phrasing was killed by measurement not by vendor recommendation (F1: "Does this seem right?" erases the low band — row 5's stated-text discipline, enforced), and the claim-gate battery stays undrafted until a stated bar passes (row 1's gates are real gates, not vendor pacing).

**Honesty note on the discriminator's limits:** the strictest form — a Jev verdict itself authoring a wiki link decision — has no dated instance on record; the 2026-10-04 pass and F6 both show judgments stopping at receipts and review queues. That is exactly what the thesis predicts (authority is never self-granted; "no self-approval" is a standing rule), but it means the next rung of the test is still open: the claim-gate battery, currently blocked on F6, is the named open test for the outsourcing reading's next prediction.
