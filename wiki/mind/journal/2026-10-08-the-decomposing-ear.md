# The Decomposing Ear

Perceptual intake in his music domain is production-structural: the ear
decomposes a track into countable production dimensions before any
semantic pass, and the semantic pass on sung words never runs in
ordinary listening.

## Theory (Stage 2)

Stated in profile vocabulary only (rows ≥6, supported/leans); every
interior term translated to attention level or struck:

A track registers in held output as an **assemblage of countable
production dimensions** — drop, sound design, bass weight, groove,
arrangement, mix, vocal-as-texture — decomposed before any semantic
pass. Semantic uptake of sung words is absent from held listening
output (row 18: "I hear the lyric as timbre"; the lyric-sheet detail:
even knowing the words does not ease interpretation — the bottleneck
is processing, not access). Track verdict events in held output arrive
at **structural checkpoints** — his own 2026-09-12 ladder taxonomy:
"Play > skip to 33% in > do nothing OR like OR like + into the current
playlist" — a cutoff delivered at one-third of the object, before any
lyric could unfold as message or any arc could resolve. Instruments
commissioned for him carry **no field for lyrical content at all**:
AUTOPSY's 16-driver WHAT DID IT? grid names "vocal as texture —
voice, not words" as its vocal dimension, and the method notes state
it flatly — *vocals are texture, never words*.

The same phonetic mechanism generalizes past music: track and artist
titles arrive as approximate phonetics ("Eat Me Better" heard as "eat
you better," his own report 2026-09-12; standing practice since:
verify against Spotify), and typed production at speed carries
phonetic spellings ("nopples," "Alexandrian," "rifht," 2026-09-14
specimens). Approximate in, approximate out — the decomposing ear is
not a music quirk but the intake's general handling of verbal sound.

Stage-2 formulation adjustment (permitted at Stage 2, not a restart):
the commissioned starting hypothesis closed with "'liking' a track is
a production verdict, not an affective one." That clause was struck
here — verdict allocation belongs to the Placement Verdict
(2026-10-05); this entry owns uptake. The excised clause is preserved
verbatim in Genesis.

## Falsifier

A documented case of spontaneous lyric-meaning uptake in ordinary
listening at conversational rates — a fourth song parsed as
conceptual message without the deliberate-effort markers of the named
~3 — falsifies the strong form. Narrower: an AUTOPSY driver-lift
ranking in which a semantic-content driver (one would have to be
added — the grid currently has no field for it) out-pulls the
production drivers across a full 240-decision floor would re-open the
affective rival at its strongest point.
