## Theory (Stage 2)

Stated in profile vocabulary only, at attention level:

**In a named catalog, attention goes to the verified era, not to the catalog name; a purchase or attention decision is gated on era verification, not on availability or appetite.**

Concretely: when the catalog name (the performer, the subscription, the archive) spans more than one era, the era marker — dated, primary-sourced era boundaries — becomes the binary gate. Material that passes the gate gets attention; material that fails it, and mixed material that cannot be era-sorted, does not get attention or purchase, even though it is available under the same name. No claim is made here about felt drive, satiation, or interior motive: the observable is which material gets attended to and which available purchase gets held pending verification.

This is distinct from the 2026-10-22 Installed Palate entry, which is cited here and not re-derived: that entry is about taste-constitution/canon mechanics (what gets admitted to a canon and how). This entry is about era-gating of a catalog (what gets attended to or bought within an already-named catalog, on era verification alone).
