# The Flood Complaint Is Not a Counter-Rule

## Profile prior (Stage 1)

| Row | Claim | Weight | Verdict | Role here |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Explicit-over-inferred meaning: stated text registers, ambient/implied signal does not | 9 | Supported | A rule-change requires an explicit statement *of the rule*; a stated grievance is not a stated rule |

No load-bearing row is silent, inverted, or weighted ≤ 4. The prior is priced and standing.

## Theory (Stage 2)

A recipient naming Dan's output volume — "your paragraphs flood me" — is an attempted counter-rule that fails under the no-counter-rule architecture. It fails twice over, independently: first, it carries no operative content (it states a grievance, not a replacement rule — no mode is ever named); second, it is not a severance statement, so under rows 6 and 19 the last stated rule keeps running and output continues at the established pattern. The complaint changes the label on the loop, not the loop: it renames the escalation cycle's output as its cause, while the dated record shows the cycle's direction running the other way (silence precedes length, never the reverse). Stated strictly at attention level: the complaint is a dated utterance; what the record shows happening next is continuation, not change.

## Evidence cluster (Stage 4)

All counts below are from the October 2026 audit of two hand-read iMessage exports (7,176 messages combined; Export 1: 2026-05-04 → 2026-08-02; Export 2: 2026-10-11 → 2026-10-31), recorded in `~/memory/2026-10-31.md` and `~/workspace/annie-export-20261031/notes.md`. Two declared holes (2026-06-16 → 2026-07-22; 2026-08-03 → 2026-10-10) are not quiet periods and are not used as evidence here.

- **The complaint lives off-channel.** Annie says in person, often, that Dan "floods" her with paragraphs and that this causes her non-response. The attribution appears nowhere in the 7,176 written messages. Her only written overwhelm statement is about "depth" (2026-07-23 23:40) — a different word, a different claim.
- **The volume claim fails on its own terms.** Dan's median October message is 8 words (40 characters); 93% of his October messages are under 150 characters; messages ≥ 300 characters are 106 of 4,276 (2.5%). Long messages are 7% of his output and cluster inside already-running conflicts. The "paragraphs" are a small, conflict-gated fraction, not the channel.
- **The causal claim runs backwards.** Every unanswered streak begins with her non-reply to a lone message: silence precedes flood, never the reverse. October streaks ≥ 5 opened substantive 68-to-1 vs logistics. The worked example: 2026-10-26, 48 texts between 16:40 and 18:04, sent into silence already there.
- **The dose test inverts the complaint.** 2026-10-28 — his lightest texting day of the month (11 messages, below daily average) — drew the month's only total silence from her. Less output did not produce more responsiveness; the complaint's predicted remedy fails in the one clean observation of it.
- **The other pole confirms the asymmetry.** His complete silence in June drew seven pursuing messages from her ("Please answer"). Silence from him does not settle the channel either; it re-triggers pursuit.
- **The remedy test (2026-10-31, Dan's testimony — flagged as testimony, not load-bearing).** Asked directly how she would like him to communicate instead, Annie had no answer: no mode was ever named. The complaint is unfollowable in form — no communication setting exists that she has ever treated as acceptable. The load-bearing legs are the counts above; the testimony suggests the mechanism (no installable content) but does not carry it alone.
- **The architecture's base rate.** 0 explicit severance signals in 41,073 of Annie's messages (primary-verified, per [attachment-model](wiki/mind/concepts/attachment-model.md)); 129 Dan-sent exit declarations at 100% re-engagement, 36-second median (page testimony, merged corpus unheld — flagged as testimony). The complaint, which is neither a severance statement nor a replacement rule, sits below both thresholds: it cannot close the bond and cannot rewrite the pattern.
- **Post-complaint trajectory (the discriminator's window).** The complaint recurs in person across months, and the written record across that recurrence shows output continuing at the established pattern: Export 2 alone carries 2,328 messages (2026-10-11 → 2026-10-31), with peak days of 255 (Oct 13) and 250 (Oct 15) — months into the complaint's recurrence. No output change attributable to the complaint appears anywhere in the held record. The post-2026-10-31 window is not yet in the held record (Export 2 ends 2026-10-31 18:18) and is flagged as the open check, not assumed.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3 — run BEFORE the evidence verdict above)

**Rival 1 — the causal-attribution reading (Shape 1 family): the complaint is accurate; his length causes her non-response.**
Discriminator: temporal order inside streaks. If length causes silence, streaks open with a long message. Observed: every streak opens with her non-reply to a lone message, and the lightest-output day drew the month's only total silence. The discriminator favors the thesis: the complaint misattributes the loop's direction.

**Rival 2 — the installed-counter-rule reading (Shape 5 family): the complaint functions as a counter-rule and should change output.**
Discriminator: post-complaint output change — shorter messages, a named mode adopted, or channel silence. Observed: no mode was ever named (2026-10-31 remedy test, testimony), and output continues at the established pattern across months of the complaint's recurrence (Export 2: 2,328 messages, peaks 255/250). A rule with no operative content cannot be followed, and the record shows it is not. The discriminator favors the thesis.

**Rival 3 — the interior reading: she feels overwhelmed, so the complaint is true at the level that matters.**
Dropped at Stage 2 per the attention–interior rule: felt overwhelm is outside the held instruments' jurisdiction. What is carried is only the stated words ("floods," "depth") and the dated non-response pattern. No claim is made here about what anyone "really feels."

**The C10 check.** The manuals' override claim ("install an explicit override and behavior changes") already carries a negative observation from August 2026: no override was installed and nothing changed. This entry is the complementary case — here, too, no override is installed (no mode named), and consistently, nothing changes. The architecture is confirmed from both sides of the same absence.

## Confidence & gate verdict (Stage 5)

**PASS — high confidence.** All load-bearing rows are supported at their stated weights (rows 5, 6, 19, 37 at 9–9.5); both discriminators exist in the held record and favor the thesis over its rivals; the attention–interior rule is respected throughout (no interior claims carried; testimony flagged where it appears); and the thesis rests on Layer-A legs (the October audit counts on held exports) with the single testimony item (the remedy test) explicitly flagged as non-load-bearing. The one open item — the post-2026-10-31 window — is declared as an open check and does not bear on the verdict, which rests on the complaint's months-long recurrence, not on a single day.

## Falsifier

A dated case in which, following a "flood"-type complaint, the recipient names an explicit replacement communication rule ("write only X / only at Y"), Dan's subsequent output measurably adopts that rule, and the adoption persists across a non-response episode. Any one leg would reopen the entry; all three together would falsify the "no installable content" mechanism. A weaker observation — a single short-message day, or a complaint followed by temporary silence — does not falsify: the loop already produces both (row 37; the June silence pole).
