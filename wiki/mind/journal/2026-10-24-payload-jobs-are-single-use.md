## Theory (Stage 2)

Stated in profile vocabulary, attention-level only — no interior terms survive:

A host that carried a payload is a single-use container. Observable form, across the four documented hosts:

- **After a payload-carrying tenure ends, no re-entry into that host is documented** — not as an employee, not as a returning worker — even when the two conditions that would ordinarily predict return are both present: need (months of unemployment) and welcome (an employer whose warmth outlasted the job by years).
- **After a payload-less tenure ends, re-entry into the same host IS documented** — reapplication on instruction, re-engagement bursts — even when welcome is absent (a posted ban) and even when the tenure itself lasted only weeks.
- **Tenure length and re-entry are independent observables.** A job can be re-entered without being sustained (BFS: ~1 month, re-entered; Caviar: 12 months of availability, re-entered in bursts) and sustained without ever being re-entered (Au Za'atar: 41 months, never re-entered; Nemacolin: 43 months, never re-entered). Wage appetite is constant across both directions — the BFS reapplication and the Caviar bursts show income-seeking intact after payload-less tenures — so appetite cannot explain the split. Payload history can.
- **The mechanism, stated as observables:** re-entering a payload job means taking up the bare assignment — the exact role the engine never sustained on its own — with the payload's absence now visible against its former presence. Re-entering a payload-less host means taking up the same thing it always was: a wage source with nothing installed in it. The first is a different object from the one that was left; the second is the same object. That is why one gets re-entered and the other does not, and why the employer's warmth or hostility does not move the outcome in either direction.

## Evidence cluster

**Au Za'atar — the payload job, never re-entered (the need + welcome leg).** [[wiki/work/au-zaatar-41-months|Au Za'atar, 41 Months]]: March 2021 – August 2024, busser → server → de facto manager, ~690 shifts, 712 timestamped GPS visits. The payload stack is documented on the page: the veterans' equal tip split talked into existence on day one, the fictitious 9pm night class held 18+ months, sole custody of the shed's cash tips. The tenure ended when the territory died: the finished interior opened, demand for the sidewalk tables died first, then the city-mandated winter shed teardown finished it — "once the inside opened, they just didn't have enough interest in outdoor dining." After the end: roughly eleven months unemployed before the mid-2025 session noting he had been "working for the first time since june 2024 for the last 2 weeks" — need present, at length. And welcome present: Tarik's warmth is recorded through April 2026, nearly two years past the job's end, via the 80-message direct thread filed 2026-07-20. No re-entry as an employee is documented anywhere in the record after August 2024. **Limitation, stated not hidden:** the Au Za'atar leg carries a geography confound — the restaurant is NYC, and by the 2025–26 period Dan is in the Uniontown area — so non-return there cannot by itself carry the thesis. The thesis does not rest on it; the Nemacolin leg below is the unconfounded non-return, and the BFS/Caviar legs are the positive re-entries.

**Nemacolin — the payload job, never re-entered (the unconfounded leg).** [[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddie-years|The Nemacolin Caddie Years]]: April 6, 2016 – November 2019, 43 months, "Experience Associate – Golf" — the longest sustained commitment in the record before Au Za'atar, and the Uniontown-return era's working life, not a distant-city chapter. The payload is the looper status ladder the employer never set or measured: the call-up to "the real loopers," the first two-bag round (May 23, 2018), the Scottsdale transfer raised directly with the head of that program. The ending is a fade, not a firing: "zero post-2019 golf signal beyond nostalgia, media consumption, and one self-deprecating story about polo shirts." No return to caddying is documented in the six-plus years since. The host still exists, the geography is his home terrain, the payload is gone — and the host is never re-entered.

**BFS Foods — the payload-less job, re-entered despite a ban (the welcome-inverted leg).** [[wiki/work/bfs-foods|BFS Foods (2026)]]: gas-station cashier, roughly one month, late April – mid-May 2026, no documented out installed and no private objective anywhere in the record of the job. The ending was formal and hostile: a posted sign reading "NO HIRE: Daniel Frank" in two locations, BFS and the Little Caesars office. Then, on the morning of **11 August 2026**, Dan told Annie he had his job back: *"Well I got my job at BFS back" · "I guess the same lady who put me on a no-hire list there told the manager today to put in another application like I did before and they're going to try to get me on the schedule for next week."* The re-entry observable is the reapplication itself, on the explicit instruction of the same manager-side chain that posted the ban — row 6's machinery, an explicit statement revoking an explicit statement. The ban did not prevent re-entry; the absence of a payload is what made re-entry costless. Whether scheduling followed is undocumented; the re-entry act is dated and quoted, and that is the leg.

**Caviar — the payload-less assignment, re-entered in bursts (the rhythm leg).** [[wiki/work/caviar-courier|Caviar]]: 290 dispatch messages, April 21, 2019 – April 14, 2020, an app that offers nothing to embed an objective in — no territory, no ladder, no counterparty to negotiate with. The engagement shape is exactly what the thesis predicts for a host that never carried a payload: dense April–July 2019 (100+ assigned orders), near-silent August–November 2019, a scattered handful in March 2020, a final burst April 7–14, 2020. Twelve months of availability, no tenure — and repeated re-engagement without any payload ever being installed. Re-entry without tenure is the payload-less host's normal shape (row 16's burst rhythm), and it is the mirror image of the payload hosts' never-return.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3)

Theory-shape: **straightforward diligence/loyalty** (the task-named rival for this shape), with the wage-appetite variant faced alongside it. Both rivals were stated before the evidence was laid.

**Profile-weighted rival — diligence/loyalty:** he returns where he is loyal and welcome, and stays away where he is unwelcome. Discriminator, stated in advance: if welcome drives re-entry, Au Za'atar (employer warmth recorded through April 2026, nearly two years past the end) must show re-entry, and BFS (a posted NO HIRE sign in two locations) must show none. The record shows the exact inversion on both legs: no Au Za'atar re-entry despite warmth; BFS reapplication despite the ban. The rival also fails the Nemacolin leg (a yard full of friendships — "a manager, an operations hand, a social organizer, a weed middleman, a genuine friend, a marquee NHL regular" — and no return) and the Caviar leg (no loyalty object exists in an app dispatch queue, yet re-engagement recurs). Loyalty/welcome predicts the opposite of the observed pattern at every host.

**Wage-appetite variant:** he returns wherever money is on offer. Discriminator: appetite is constant — the BFS reapplication and the Caviar bursts prove income-seeking intact — so appetite predicts return to BOTH payload and payload-less hosts, and more strongly to Au Za'atar ($800–1,000 for a six-day week, Dan's July 2026 pay confirmation) than to a gas-station cashier job. Observed: return only to the payload-less hosts. Appetite is the constant; payload history is the variable that splits the outcomes.

**Shape 6 (system-building-as-outsourcing) considered and set aside:** its discriminator (closed loop vs append-only — does a built instrument change a subsequent decision) has no purchase here, because no built system mediates any of the four re-entry observables. The re-entry pattern is a property of the hosts, not of an instrument. Not a rival beaten — a rival with no leg in this territory, recorded so the check is visible.

Both live rivals are beaten on held discriminators. The thesis survives Stage 3.

## Falsifier

- A documented re-entry attempt at Au Za'atar or Nemacolin as an employee after the payload tenure ended — kills the single-use claim directly.
- A payload-less host that is never re-entered despite need and explicit invitation — breaks the split's second direction (BFS/Caviar would become the exception, not the rule).
- A payload job re-entered in a different role that re-installs a payload — would narrow the thesis to same-role re-entry and require a restatement, not a patch.
- The next dated employment event in the corpus: if it is a return to a payload host, the thesis fails; if it is a new host or a payload-less re-entry, the thesis holds.
