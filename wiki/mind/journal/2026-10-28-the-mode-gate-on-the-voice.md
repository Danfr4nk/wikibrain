# The Mode Gate on the Voice

**Idea Journal — 2026-10-28, pass C (the vocal channel is gated by mode: sung voice is attended as timbre and texture, while addressed speech is attended as stated text — so the lyric qualifier is a mode gate on the voice, not a global voice-semantics deficit and not a choice).**

## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

In Dan's listening, the vocal channel is gated by mode. A sung voice is attended as timbre — placement, processing, grain, cadence, melodic movement — and its words never cross into the explicit-meaning register, even when the words are supplied as text: the lyric-sheet detail (knowing the words does not make interpreting them easier) shows the bottleneck is not access to the words but the mode they arrive in. An addressed spoken voice, by contrast, is attended as stated text: short functional speech he authors himself (the spoken AI drops and intros on his own records) carries meaning in exactly the addressed, functional mode that row 5 says registers. The qualifier is therefore mode-specific, not voice-general: the same voice, in sung mode, is texture; in addressed-speech mode, it is text.

The claim survived Stage 2 translation intact. Its first formulation did not (see Genesis).

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3 — faced BEFORE confirmatory evidence)

Two rivals were named and their discriminators fixed before the evidence cluster below was assembled:

**Rival 1 — cultivated inattention / genre posture: he chooses to ignore lyrics, or the qualifier is a taste posture.** This is the reading the qualifier page itself rules out in principle, and the discriminator settles it: the lyric-sheet detail. If the qualifier were a choice, supplying the words as text would restore parsing — attention follows the supplied text. It does not: even with the lyric sheet, interpreting is harder yet. A choice account also cannot explain the phonetic series outside music (the 2026-09-12 mishearings, the 2026-09-14 typed-production specimens), where no genre posture is in play. Discriminator favors the thesis: a perceptual mode gate, not a posture.

**Rival 2 — global voice-semantics deficit: he does not process vocal meaning at all.** Discriminator: his own authored addressed speech plus his intact language production. If vocal semantics were globally absent, the spoken drops he writes and places on his own records would be incoherent as meaning-bearing acts — he authors them as text, in the addressed mode, and deploys them as functional speech — and his written output would not run at 1.93× syntactic complexity (row 36). Both legs show language intact outside sung mode. Discriminator favors the thesis: the gate is mode-specific.

A third candidate was considered and set aside rather than faced as a rival: that the lyricist fandoms (JPEGMAFIA, Kanye West, Elliott Smith) break the qualifier. That reading is already derived on the qualifier page (delivery, not bars, rated MED as inference) and the crate segmentation below favors it, so it is background, not a rival to this entry's new claim.

**Leg 3 — AUTOPSY operationalizes the gate (2026-09-14).** The 16-driver WHAT DID IT? taxonomy ([`dat:1708`](../../kb/data/1708-autopsy-driver-grid-16-drivers-cata-method-basis-and-machine.md)) carries "vocal as texture — voice, not words" as a scoring dimension, and the method notes state flatly *"vocals are texture, never words."* The instrument has no field for lyrical content at all: it asks what about a vocal grabbed him — placement, processing, grain — and structurally cannot ask what the words meant. A tool built by and for this listener reproduces the gate in its grid. ([[wiki/work/tech/projects/musictrainer-autopsy]])

**Leg 4 — hook2piano operationalizes the gate as a build spec (2026-09-16).** The TheoryTab→piano-roll rewrite ([`dat:1750`](../../kb/data/1750-hook2piano-pianoroll-rewrite-20260916.md)) treats the qualifier as an engineering constraint: pitch-as-geometry with note names as text, because the user does not parse lyrics as language. ## Confidence & gate verdict

## Falsifier

Any one of these kills or narrows the gate: (a) a sung original surfacing in his documented catalog across any alias; (b) spontaneous lyric-meaning uptake in ordinary listening at conversational rates — him acting on a sung lyric's propositional content without a text-mediated route (row 18's own falsifier, applied to the gate); (c) a case where a lyric sheet *does* restore parsing for a specific song, showing the bottleneck is access after all. The ~3 exceptions, if ever named, test the gate's edge: if all three turn out to have arrived as stated text first (read or discussed as text before ever heard sung), the mode gate strengthens; if any was parsed from sung mode alone in ordinary listening, the gate narrows to a strong tendency.
