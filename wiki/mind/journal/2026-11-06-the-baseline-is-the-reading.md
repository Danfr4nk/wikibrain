## Theory (Stage 2)

**Thesis, in profile vocabulary:** Toward a nonverbal dependent who cannot produce explicit statements, Dan's care runs in the provision register (row 21: countable acts, not condolence) and his readings run on baselines (row 4: primary record over narrative). The two halves meet in one operating shape. On the reading side, a lifelong behavior is priced as baseline and explicitly not re-read as a fresh signal: on the 2026-11-03 walk, the house-peeing is filed under "he's done this his entire life" — the baseline *is* the reading, and no anomaly is declared against it. On the provision side, experiments are single, rule-constrained trials: the 2026-11-04 treat was selected inside a constraint installed years earlier by an explicit statement (row 6 — the rawhide prohibition, still governing present provision from a now-dormant channel), and the trial's verdict was the dependent's observed behavior — frustration, then quitting — recorded without revision. No second trial, no softened retelling, no override of the verdict appears in the supplied record. The shape, stated at attention level only: statements install the constraints, baselines price the behavior, countable acts carry the care, and the dependent's observed response closes the trial.

Scope, stated in advance: this is a claim about Dan's *processing* — what he states, selects, prices, and records — not about the dog's actual states, and not about what any of the behavior medically or behaviorally means. The entry would survive the dog's house-peeing having any cause at all, because the claim is that Dan prices it against the lifelong baseline rather than as a new signal; that pricing is in his own dated words.

**Stage 3 pre-registration (before the evidence verdict, expanded below):** **Rival 1 — §5 Shape 2, regulation/stim (the dog as the owner's self-soothing object):** provision and attention toward the dog are the owner's regulation, and episodes run on the owner's state. Discriminator: whose behavior terminates episodes, and what the readings cite. Regulation predicts provision continuing or escalating on the owner's satiation curve; the thesis predicts provision stopping on the dependent's observed response (quit the bone = trial over) and readings citing the dog's baseline, not the owner's state. **Rival 2 — the novelty-signal reading:** the house-peeing is read as a change/anxiety marker, a fresh signal demanding fresh interpretation. Discriminator: Dan's own dated processing of the behavior — if he prices it as lifelong baseline in his own statement, the rival describes a reading he demonstrably does not run. **Rival 3 — anthropomorphic projection:** the outcome leg is Dan projecting an interior monologue onto the dog, so the "verdict" is his invention, not the dog's behavior. Discriminator: the thesis does not load on the accuracy of Dan's quoted read; it loads on the observable sequence — trial run once, behavior reported (frustration, quitting), no revision and no repeat in the record. Projection, if present, is in the caption, not in the sequence the thesis claims.

- **Discriminator (pre-registered):** Dan's own dated processing. The rival is a claim about how the behavior gets read; if Dan's own statement prices it as lifelong baseline, the rival's reading is not the one running.

## Falsifier

Any of: (a) a dated record of Dan re-reading the lifelong house-peeing as a new signal — treating it as fresh anxiety, a change, or a message — in a context where no new baseline fact has appeared, which would break the claim that the baseline is the reading; (b) a provision trial whose closing behavior Dan overrides — the treat re-offered, replaced, or re-run after a quit until a different verdict is obtained, or the 2026-11-04 verdict revised in a later telling (the frustration or the quitting softened, dropped, or reversed) — which would break the claim that the dependent's observed behavior closes the trial without revision; (c) a provision made in violation of the rawhide prohibition with no explicit revoking statement anywhere in the record — the old rule silently lapsing rather than being revoked in the row-6 form — which would break the claim that the years-old statement still installs the constraint. Ordinary variation does NOT falsify: a new treat tried on a new day under the same constraint is the pattern continuing, not failing; it falsifies only if its verdict is overridden or its constraint silently dropped.
