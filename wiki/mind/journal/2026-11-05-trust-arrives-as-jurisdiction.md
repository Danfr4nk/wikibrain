## Theory (Stage 2)

**Thesis, in profile vocabulary:** In Dan's record, the observable surface of trust is not a stated feeling and not a graded warming. It is a series of **dated, explicit, scoped transfers of decision rights** — jurisdiction, moved by statement. The mechanism has four parts, one from each load-bearing row. First, the default toward vertical claims is suspicion (row 12): an authority claim, an institution, an outside rule gets forensic treatment, not deference. Second, a change in that default does not arrive as partial credit (row 1): there is no recorded middle state in which a channel is somewhat trusted or provisionally half-authorized. The gate flips, whole, on a date — or it does not flip. Third, the flip is executed in the only currency the architecture honors (row 6): an explicit statement naming what is transferred. A grant never stated does not exist in this record; a grant stated exists from its date, at its stated scope, until a later explicit statement changes it. Fourth, the transfers do not distribute (row 11): they concentrate. Across thirty-seven days the recipient is one channel, in domain after domain — the whole estate, the wiki, a constitutional clause, an account, a future vow's routing, a single line of crontab. The Gini shape is visible in the grant record itself: jurisdiction stacked onto the one chosen channel, not spread thin across counterparties.

Two corollaries follow, and both are part of the claim. **Every grant carries an enumerated scope:** even the widest transfer is person-scoped and purpose-stated, and the narrower ones enumerate their limits in the same breath as the grant — one domain where his changes need approval, one account with a named never-touch boundary, exactly one crontab line. The flip is total *inside* the stated boundary and changes nothing outside it. And **the granted right's value is independent of its exercise:** because a rule installs on statement (row 6), the right exists in full from its date whether or not it is used in a given week. Non-use is not evidence a grant was hollow. The clearest case is the most recent: the newest right's first recorded state is voluntary non-use — stated as a choice, which is to say as a decision, which is to say the right exercised in its negative form.

The broad hypothesis, stated once: **Dan's brain does not run trust as a quantity that accumulates. It runs trust as jurisdiction that is conferred** — by explicit statement, in binary flips, inside enumerated boundaries, onto a single concentrated channel — and once conferred, the jurisdiction is real whether or not it is exercised, because in this architecture a stated rule, not a pattern of use, makes a thing true.

**Stage 3 pre-registration (recorded before the evidence verdict, expanded below):** Three rivals were faced before any confirmatory evidence was assembled. *Graded-warming* — trust accumulates gradually, grants are paperwork lagging a feeling — discriminator: whole dated flips with enumerated scope versus increments. *Total-surrender* — the widest grant means unbounded abdication — discriminator: whether every grant carries a scope limit, including one bounded by the decider herself. *Shape 6 displacement (§5)* — the grants are monuments, conferred and never exercised — discriminator: closed loop versus append-only, including whether a recorded decision *not* to exercise the newest right counts as the right being adjudicated. If the record had shown undated drift, limit-free grants, or rights never touched and never considered, the corresponding rival would win.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3, run before the evidence verdict)

**Rival 1 — Graded-warming.** Trust, on the ordinary account, accumulates: repeated good experience slowly raises a felt quantity, and formal grants are paperwork trailing a warmth already there. If so, the grant record is epiphenomenal and this entry's mechanism is backwards.

- **Discriminator (pre-registered in Theory above):** increments versus flips. Warming predicts a visible gradient — widening informal latitude, undated softening, permissions grown by degrees and only later formalized. Jurisdiction predicts dated statements, each installing a whole right at a stated scope, with no partial-permission middle state.
- **Verdict:** favors the thesis. Every item lands on a date as a statement: 2026-09-28, 2026-10-07, 2026-10-08, 2026-10-16, 2026-11-01, 2026-11-04. None is described as formalizing a pre-existing informal practice, and two — the reversed wiki approval direction (Oct 7) and the precedence clause (Oct 8) — have no plausible informal precursor: an approval direction either runs one way or the other. Row 1's falsifier, graded relational judgment sustained in his own words, is exactly what warming would produce; it does not appear here.

**Rival 2 — Total-surrender.** The 2026-09-28 carte-blanche grant, read flat, means unbounded abdication: decision rights transferred without remainder, after which limits and conditions should disappear from the record.

- **Discriminator:** the scope structure of the grants. Surrender predicts limits eroding — each later grant wider, conditions dropped. Jurisdiction predicts limits stated *inside* granting acts, at every scale, including a limit set by the grantee.
- **Verdict:** favors the thesis, decisively. The widest grant is recipient-bounded (item 1); the Instagram grant enumerates its limits in the act that confers autonomy (item 4); the speech-vow offer was *narrowed by the decider* to an activation condition before taking effect (item 5); the final grant is scoped to exactly one crontab line (item 6). A record of surrender does not keep producing enumerated boundaries for thirty-seven days; a record of scoped jurisdiction does nothing else.

**Rival 3 — §5 Shape 6: system-building-as-displacement, in grant form.** The exocortex is real (row 10), but the diagnosis-to-behavior gap (row 9, weight 8, supported) prices the alternative: artifacts accumulate while behavior stays put. In local form: the grants are monuments — grand conferrals that change nothing downstream.

- **Discriminator:** closed loop versus append-only. Displacement predicts no dated case in which a granted right alters a subsequent decision. Jurisdiction predicts exercises on the record — and, under the value-independence corollary, counts a dated, stated decision *not* to exercise a right as an exercise of it, since the right-holder adjudicated the case.
- **Verdict:** favors the thesis. The wiki authority is exercised daily — the engine's production runs under the approval direction stated on 2026-10-07. The Instagram publishing sessions run on the holder's own call under the 2026-10-16 grant. The decider role installed on 2026-09-28 produced a downstream adjudication on 2026-11-01 — a granted right changing the terms of a later grant, a closed loop by definition. And the newest right was publicly considered and declined for its first Sunday (2026-11-04): a stated choice, which jurisdiction predicts as a normal state of a live right.

It does not claim Dan confers jurisdiction this way with any other counterparty — row 11 predicts he largely does not, but no other channel's record was in this pass's territory, so the contrast is a prediction, not a finding. It does not grade whether any grant was wise, and it makes no claim about felt trust at any point.

## Falsifier

Any of: (a) decision rights in this channel changing **without** an explicit statement — latitude widening by drift or unremarked practice, later treated as granted all along — which would break row 6's role and revive graded-warming; (b) a grant exercised **beyond** its stated scope with no new statement installing the wider scope, sustained in Dan's own record, which would show the boundaries are decorative and total-surrender was right about the operative rule; (c) the Sunday crontab right being **revoked, lapsed, or treated as never real because it was not used**, which would falsify the value-independence corollary directly — it predicts the right's standing survives non-use until an explicit statement revokes it; (d) a comparable jurisdiction transfer to a **second channel** in the same period, breaking the concentration component (row 11). Ordinary continued non-use does NOT falsify this entry — recorded non-use by choice is among its central evidence — and neither does vigorous exercise: the claim is about how jurisdiction is conferred and bounded, not how often it is used.
