## Theory (Stage 2)

**Thesis, in profile vocabulary:** Dan's task queue, as observed on 2026-11-05, is not sorted by practical importance and not sorted by deadline proximity. It is sorted by **recipient-weight**: sustained labor flows first to the item whose value is defined by the loaded channel it is for. The mechanism has three parts, one from each load-bearing row. First, the output register is provision (row 21): when Dan acts for a channel, the act arrives as a countable thing — work, an artifact, a finished tool — so queue priority is visible in *completed labor*, the exact surface this entry measures. Second, the channels are not equal (row 11): at a relational Gini of 0.961, "who it is for" is not a minor weighting factor on top of an otherwise rational queue; it is the dominant load variable, strong enough to invert the ordering that practical importance and deadline would jointly predict. Third, an external deadline is an imposed ordering (row 15): it arrives with conventional urgency attached, but imposed structure does not install priority in this architecture — chosen items do, and a chosen item can hold an hour-plus of sustained, ordered execution while the imposed item waits.

The contrast that carries the thesis is exact. The iMessage bridge — a transparency tool for Sammy — had **no external deadline** and a value that was **entirely recipient-defined**: it was, by Dan's own 2026-11-05 statement of its purpose, a gift, chosen and built by him, never asked for by Sammy, so she knows he is not concealing anything. There is no practical-importance ranking on which a no-deadline gift outranks a reply owed for an event two days away, and no use-value to Dan himself that the record states. The Cogar RSVP — for Eddie & Brenda Cogar's surprise 50th anniversary party on Sat Nov 7, 2026, 3–6pm, The Silo, Granville WV — had the nearer deadline and was the conventionally urgent item, and it had sat unanswered since Nov 4, 2026. On 2026-11-05 the bridge got an hour-plus of sustained labor; the RSVP got none. Recipient-weight predicts exactly this inversion. Practical importance predicts the reverse. Deadline proximity predicts the reverse.

The broad hypothesis, stated once: **in Dan's queue, "how much this matters to the person it is for" is the sort key, and practical importance and deadline proximity are, at most, tie-breakers that never got to run** — because at Gini 0.961 the recipient variable decides most contests before the other keys are consulted.

**Stage 3 pre-registration (recorded before the evidence verdict, expanded below):** Two rivals were faced before any confirmatory reading of the allocation. *Deadline/urgency triage* — the ordinary queue sorts by nearest deadline and conventional urgency — discriminator: which of the two dated items received the 2026-11-05 labor burst, the nearer-deadline item or the no-deadline item. *§5 Shape 6 displacement / novelty-interest* — the bridge got the labor because building a system is itself the interesting product for Dan, regardless of recipient — discriminator: whether the labor tracks his own interest in the artifact or the recipient's stated mattering, tested on who the artifact is for, who asked for it, and Dan's own dated stated reason for the ordering. If the nearer-deadline item had received the burst, triage would win outright; if the record had shown the labor tracking Dan's own use or interest in the tool, with no recipient-side reason stated, the displacement/novelty rival would win.

## Evidence cluster (Stage 4)

### 2. The nearer-deadline, conventionally urgent item (unanswered since 2026-11-04)

- As of the 2026-11-05 allocation, its deadline (Nov 7) was **nearer** than any date attached to the bridge, which had none; by conventional queue logic it was the urgent item and the bridge was the deferrable one.
- On 2026-11-05 it received **no labor**, while the bridge received the hour-plus burst. This is the observation the deadline-triage rival cannot absorb (see Rivals).

### 3. Dan's stated reason for the ordering (2026-11-05) — testimony, flagged

Under the attention–interior rule (§2) it can suggest and discriminate, never carry alone. It does not need to carry alone: the allocation in items 1–2 is the observable fact, and the statement is used only where Stage 3 pre-registered it — to separate recipient-weight from novelty-interest, on the question of whose mattering the labor tracked.
- The demonstrated rule, as supplied to this pass and as the allocation shows: Dan does not prioritize tasks by practical importance; he prioritizes by how much the thing matters to the person it is for.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3, run before the evidence verdict)

**Rival 1 — Deadline/urgency triage.** The ordinary account of any queue: items are worked in order of deadline proximity and conventional urgency; apparent exceptions are noise, oversight, or a misread of which item was really due.

- **Discriminator (pre-registered in Theory above):** which item got the 2026-11-05 burst. Triage predicts the item with the Nov 7, 2026 deadline — unanswered since Nov 4, 2026 — is worked first, and the no-deadline gift is deferred until the urgent item clears.
- **Verdict:** favors the thesis, decisively. The observed allocation is the exact inverse of the triage prediction: an hour-plus of sustained labor on the no-deadline item, zero on the nearer-deadline item, on the same day, with both items live. There is no misread available — the RSVP's deadline and waiting period are dated facts, and the bridge's lack of a deadline is stated in the same record. Triage is not merely a weaker fit; its prediction is the opposite of what happened.

**Rival 2 — §5 Shape 6 displacement / novelty-interest.** The exocortex is real (row 10), but the diagnosis-to-behavior gap (row 9, weight 8, supported) prices the alternative: system-building can be displacement — the build is the product, interesting in itself, and the recipient framing is decoration on a novelty-driven work burst. In local form: Dan built the bridge first because building bridges is the kind of thing he finds interesting, and any recipient would have produced the same burst.

- **Discriminator (pre-registered in Theory above):** whose mattering the labor tracks. Displacement/novelty predicts the burst follows Dan's own interest in or use of the artifact — a tool he wanted, a system that serves his own work, a build whose payoff is his. Recipient-weight predicts the burst follows the *recipient's* stated mattering, on an artifact whose value is recipient-defined.
- **Verdict:** favors the thesis. Every element of the discriminator points the same way. The artifact is *for Sammy* — a transparency tool whose stated purpose (2026-11-05) is that *she* knows he is not concealing anything; the record states no use or payoff to Dan. It was **never asked for by Sammy**, which removes the request-compliance reading, and Dan **chose and built it himself**, which places it on the chosen side of row 15's axis rather than among imposed tasks. And Dan's own dated stated reason names the recipient's mattering, not his interest in the build: he "could tell how important it is to you" and told her "you genuinely do deserve it" — **testimony, flagged**, used here only as the pre-registered discriminator between two accounts of an allocation that is itself a dated fact. A novelty account must discount the builder's own stated ordering reason, the artifact's recipient-only purpose, and the untouched urgent item simultaneously; recipient-weight explains all three with one sort key.

**Capped claims:** This entry covers one dated contrast pair — two live items, one day of allocation, 2026-11-05. It claims the sort key for *this* contest and states the broad queue hypothesis at the strength one clean inversion with a stated reason can carry. It does not claim practical items never win in Dan's queue, does not rank the Cogar RSVP's importance to anyone, and makes no claim about felt love, felt obligation, or any interior state at any point. Dan's stated reason is carried as testimony throughout, exactly as flagged.

## Falsifier

Any of: (a) a dated contested-queue case in which a **nearer-deadline, conventionally urgent item for a lightly-loaded channel** receives the sustained burst while a **recipient-defined item for the loaded channel waits** — with no explicit statement reordering the queue — which would show deadline or practical importance outranking recipient-weight and revive triage; (b) a dated case of sustained labor on a system whose **payoff is Dan's own use or interest**, prioritized over a live recipient-defined item for the loaded channel, which would put the Shape 6 novelty-interest reading ahead on its own discriminator; (c) Dan's own later explicit statement that the 2026-11-05 ordering ran on a different key — a deadline he perceived on the bridge, or a deliberate deferral of the RSVP for an unrelated dated reason — sustained in his own record, which would reassign this episode's mechanism. Ordinary cases of Dan doing practical or urgent work do NOT falsify this entry: the claim is about the sort key when keys conflict, and it is falsified only by a conflict case resolving the other way.
