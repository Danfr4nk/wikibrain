## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

The exocortex partitions by addressee. Self-directed instruments are built to specs derived from his **addressed self-designations** — and their target behaviors do not exist in the measured record:

- Message-merging tooling (the audit's named hypothetical, built to the staccato self-diagnosis) targets fragmented bursts. The audit's dated measurement: 68.5% of his 2026 burst-internal messages carry their own subject and verb against 55.1% for interlocutors; STACCATO mode 8.5% of his turns against 10.5% for theirs. Target behavior: absent at or above norm — a tool that merges his fragments solves a problem he does not have.
- The 2026-08-26 bespoke-lexicon generator (built to the commissioned voice-model spec) targets a deployable register. The lexicon page's standing verdict: a targeted search of the general message corpus for its most distinctive phrases — *resplendent, administratively, the tribunal, aesthetic felony, anomalous concentration* — returned no hits; "a tool with no recorded deployments is a tool that was built, not a practice that was observed." Target behavior: zero instances — the generator was built, never fired (testimony-grade leg; see Genesis).

Outward-directed instruments, aimed at explicit-statement targets that exist in the record, fire and change decisions: Jev's E1 backlink pilot, thresholds tuned on **his labeled data as ground truth** (row 6), produced the dated 2026-10-03 finding that every major edge type lands below the holdout-precision bar — installing the standing rule that typed-edge generation is suggestion-only for every class. Instrument output → design reroute: a closed loop with a dated receipt.

The partition is not "self-directed bad, outward-directed good." It is **addressee-existence**: tools whose targets live in the portrait misfire; tools whose targets live in the record fire. The diagnosis-to-behavior gap (row 9) explains why the misfire is silent — the built tool becomes the product, and nothing in the loop reports the miss.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3 — run first, per protocol)

**Rival: the uniform-exocortex reading.** A Shape-6 variant: the exocortex is one function regardless of direction — self-directed and outward tools alike either all serve as instruments or all degrade into displacement monuments, and the self-directed misfires are just the generic diagnosis-to-behavior gap (row 9), not a portrait-specific phenomenon. Under the uniform reading there should be no partition by addressee: self-directed tools' targets should exist in the record at the same base rates as outward tools' targets, and self-directed tools should change decisions at comparable rates.

**Discriminator (written before the evidence was gathered):** for each self-directed tool, does its target behavior exist in the measured record at or above the norm — and is there a dated case of a self-directed tool changing a decision? If yes to either, the partition fails and the thesis dies.

**Result:** the discriminator favors the thesis. Merge-target: inverted (68.5% vs 55.1%, Layer A). Lexicon-target: zero hits (documented sweep, testimony grade). No dated case exists of either self-directed tool changing a decision; the only dated decision-change in the cluster is outward (Jev E1, 2026-10-03). The uniform reading predicts no such asymmetry; the asymmetry is what the record shows.

A second, narrower rival — that the merge-tool case is moot because the tool was never actually built (the audit states it as a hypothetical) — is granted its full strength and integrated: it sharpens the thesis rather than killing it. If the fragmentation diagnosis was so wrong that *not even he built the tool it implied*, the portrait-sourced spec misfires at the planning stage, before deployment. The thesis covers tooling built *and* tooling implied: both fail at the target-existence check.

## Falsifier

A self-directed tool whose target behavior exists in the measured record at or above base rates **and** a dated case of it changing a subsequent decision (one portrait-aimed leg that fires) — or an outward tool that fails its own target-existence check and misfires anyway (which would show the partition is not by addressee but by something else, e.g., timing or build quality).
