## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

The first-person happiness-claim rate in Dan's sent messages spikes within the first five weeks of a bond's formation (7.86 per 1,000, late 2015) and decays to baseline (0.34–0.87/1k) by the third year, while the bond's own indicators — channel volume, severance-declaration cadence, the zero-severance rule — stay at full activation for another decade. The rate is a **formation-dated flare**: a mark of the ignition phase of a loaded channel, not a meter of the bond's state afterward. Expressed only in observables: per-1,000-sent-message lexical rates by dated window against dated bond-intensity indicators (yearly sent-message volume to the channel, declaration cadence, the severance-signal count). No interior term survives: "happiness," "wellbeing," and "reward" are struck and replaced by "first-person happiness-claim rate" — a count of what gets written. The thesis survived translation without losing its claim.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3 — run first, per protocol)

**The nearest rival: the rate marks bond-STATE throughout.** On this reading, 7.86/1k marks high bond intensity and the decay marks waning intensity — the gauge works continuously. **Discriminator:** post-formation windows where bond intensity stayed maximal should show elevated rates. The held record answers: 2018 (0.87/1k) is one of the two highest-volume years in the corpus (40,514 messages); 2019 (0.34/1k) is the lowest rate on record; August 2026 ran at the highest observed velocity (765/72h) with zero seconds of non-contact while the rate series had been floored for years. If the rate metered bond state, the state-meter would read high in exactly these windows. It reads floor. **The rival fails its discriminator on held data.**

**The interior rival (dossier lineage): the decay marks waning *felt* happiness, i.e., the rate gauges interior wellbeing.** This is the reading the old wiki's deviance audit wore ("intensity and forbidden knowledge in place of happiness"). It is disqualified at §2, not at evidence: the held instruments cannot see felt happiness — the same flat series is equally consistent with steady felt happiness and with none. The thesis keeps row 22's attention-level verdict (attended/displayed language) and does not contest the interior reading — it prices it at zero and walks away. This is also why the entry does not re-litigate emotional-imprinting's "not maintained by reward" claim: reward is interior, the flare is lexical; the claims share a series but not a vocabulary.

**Scope caveat the protocol demands:** only ONE formation window has a published sweep (Annie, Nov–Dec 2015). Row 22's own prediction — "rate spikes mark bond-formation phases in any future relationship record" — is the standing forward claim, and it is **prediction, not confirmation**. The Kristin window (Aug–Nov 2025, held in the OMNI cut through 2026-03-25) and the Sammy window (Sept 2026 onward, post-cut, corpus not yet filed) are named prospective tests, not evidence. A directional spot-check of Aug–Dec 2025 during this pass found no concentration of first-person formation language despite the Kristin formation sitting inside it — but the sweep I ran cannot reproduce the original pattern's semantics, so this is recorded as an open question for a proper re-sweep, not a counter-case. The theory's confidence rests on the defeat of the state rival on held data, which is what the gate grades.
