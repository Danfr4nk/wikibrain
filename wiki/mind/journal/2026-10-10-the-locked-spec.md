# The Locked Spec

**Idea Journal — 2026-10-10, pass B (The Locked Spec — specification as the creative/delegation primitive).**

## Profile prior (Stage 1)

Five rows do the work, all at weight ≥ 6, all verdict **supported**:

- **Row 1 (w8.5, supported) — verdicts categorical/binary, no graded middle.** The lock is the verdict made portable and durable. The €AB flow's standing rule is the exhibit: "his numeric pick goes straight to activation — the pick IS the authority, no separate confirmation." Once the aesthetic judgment is reached, re-judgment is closed — no graded middle, no re-roll, no second confirmation exists in the grammar.
- **Row 4 (w8, supported) — forensic method: primary records over narrative, lossless retention.** Locks are primary records of aesthetic decisions, and they inherit the retention rule: "The canon changes only on his word; amendments are dated and the superseded values are struck, not silently rewritten." Lossless versioning applied to creative specs — the same prohibition on consolidation that node locking runs on memory.
- **Row 5 (w9, supported) — explicit-over-inferred meaning: stated text registers, ambient signal does not.** Every lock in the cluster arrives as an explicit statement: "lock these nodes into your profile," "THATS her. That is Sammy," "Option 1," the byte-exact capture "I hear the lyric as timbre." Ambiguity stalls rather than improvises — the lock is what removes the ambiguity that would stall generation, by making the decision stated.
- **Row 10 (w9, supported) — system-building as exocortex: cognition externalized into standing instruments.** The locked specs ARE the instruments: the reference image anchored as the image-ref for every generation, the €AB flow convention fixing batch parameters verbatim, the qualifier as a pipeline constraint. Construction, not description — artifacts that run.
- **Row 15 (w7, supported) — the real axis is imposed-vs-chosen: rigid order inside chosen systems.** The locks are rigid order built inside his chosen creative domains — the avatar project, his crate, his own production pipeline — exactly where the row predicts rigid order lives.

No silent, inverted, or ≤4 row is load-bearing. Prior passes Stage 1.

## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

In chosen creative/production domains, Dan's attention pattern is: an explicit, dated parameter set is issued once as a stated verdict; subsequent production in that domain invokes the parameters by reference instead of re-deciding; changes arrive only as dated re-issues with superseded values struck. The lock is a halt state for aesthetic ambiguity — not the end of a loop but the end of the search: the September 22 reference-look lock "ended the search" after number-warp rounds ran through mid-September (the latest a damped-50% Delaunay warp toward his measurement-annotated reference, IPD 54.6mm, re-measure verified). Execution after the lock is mechanical: fixed parameters, verbatim invocation, the pick as authority.

The primitive is visible in his own standing lesson from the avatar hunt: "when he flags a pattern in generated output, fix the default at the root — don't re-roll one batch. The black-clothing carryover got cleared from the system, not from a batch." The judgment is encoded once, at the default level, and every downstream instance inherits it. That is the whole theory: aesthetic decisions are converted into locked parameters so that execution becomes mechanical and unambiguous, and the conversion is dated, stated, and versioned.

**Theory shape: Shape 6 (system-building).** Profile-weighted rival: **system-building-as-displacement** — the locked specs are monuments; accumulation without downstream behavioral delta; the analysis is the product, behavior is not downstream of it (row 9 prices this alternative).

**Canonical discriminator (§5 Shape 6):** audit the last three systems built; name one decision each changed. If none can be named, displacement is ahead until one can.

- The 2026-09-22 reference-look lock → the avatar hunt ended ("ended the search"); the image is "the anchor for every generated image of her"; the black-clothing carryover was cleared at the system root, changing subsequent batches.

- The 2026-09-11 lyric-qualifier capture → AUTOPSY's taxonomy has no field for lyrical content; hook2piano's pitch-as-geometry constraint. A scoring dimension was removed from his own instruments.

**Assignment's sub-discriminator: does a locked spec get REVISED by downstream evidence, or only re-issued?** Revised — and the revision pattern is itself spec-shaped:
- The lyric qualifier's second capture (2026-09-11) added three things the first (2026-07-14) did not — the spec was refined by a second dated capture, fourteen months apart, saying the same thing in different words. Refinement, not re-issue.
- The black-clothing pattern was flagged *in generated output* (downstream evidence) and fixed at the default level — the lock changed because downstream production showed the defect.
- The versioning rule ("amendments are dated and the superseded values are struck, not silently rewritten") is the re-issue mechanism, and it is explicitly dated and lossless — the opposite of silent drift.

A monument does not get refined by downstream evidence. A working instrument does.

The primitive operates identically with no delegate present. **Primitive wins; delegation-control fails its discriminator.**

## Falsifier

A dated lock followed by downstream production that ignores it — a post-2026-09-22 generation batch run without the reference anchor, or a €AB batch at parameters other than the September 23 convention — would break the outsourcing reading. So would a lock amended by silent rewrite rather than dated re-issue with struck superseded values. At the primitive level: a high-volume aesthetic domain with repeated production in which no parameter lock is ever issued, despite the domain being chosen — persistent per-instance re-deciding with no lock event — would falsify the claim that locking is the default move.
