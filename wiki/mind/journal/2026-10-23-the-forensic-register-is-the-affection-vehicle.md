## Theory (Stage 2)

Stated in profile vocabulary, attention-level only — commissions issued,
essays counted, exchanges dated:

The **forensic register** — stacked-essay density, count-operations,
dated adjudication exchanges, the forensic-bureaucratic lexicon
generator — **fires on the highest-Gini channels (Annie, Ally) at rates
exceeding any outward use**. The commissioned voice-modes claim that an
affectionate register *suppresses* cold, intellectualizing phrasing is
contradicted by held behavior: the intellectualizing register is not
shelved for the channels he concentrates on, it is aimed at them.

"Affection vehicle" is shorthand for **the register aimed at chosen
channels** — an output-density-by-channel claim, never a felt-state
claim. The instruments see where the essays land, not what they mean to
him.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3)

Theory-shape: **channel-gated forensic register aimed at high-Gini channels** (row 11's concentration meets row 37's density).

Profile-weighted rival 1 — **the voice-modes testimony** (row 40,
weight 4): affectionate contact suppresses cold, intellectualizing
phrasing. On this rival, the loved channels should show LESS forensic
density than outward debunks — essays, counts, and adjudications are
things he does at arm's length.

Profile-weighted rival 2 — **row 37's own mechanism**: essay density
tracks non-response (silence lengthens him; transactional channels
immune because responses flow), not channel choice. On this rival, the
Annie-thread density is a silence artifact, and no other form of
forensic output should concentrate on the loved channels.

Discriminators, stated before the evidence:

1. **Compare forensic density by channel**: do the high-Gini channels
   (Annie, Ally) show LESS (rival 1) or MORE (thesis) forensic output
   than outward one-off debunks?
2. **Silence-independence**: does forensic output beyond the essay loop
   (count-operations, adjudication exchanges, the lexicon generator)
   concentrate on the loved channels (thesis) or not (rival 2)? The
   Ally channel has no check-in loop and no 36-second-median dynamics —
   it discriminates cleanly.

The record, faced in order (rivals first, per protocol):

- Discriminator 1: the Annie thread shows MORE — essay ratio 3.65× and
  accelerating, the 41,073-count sweep. The Ally channel shows MORE —
  the dated Aug 18 adjudication exchange, the commissioned lexicon
  generator built around the measured pattern of how he talked to her.
  Outward shows LESS of his own output — the Pew case's one terse
  verdict line with legwork delegated. Rival 1 is beaten on its own
  discriminator.
- Discriminator 2: the 41,073 sweep is a count-operation, not an essay
  — silence-independent. The Ally adjudication exchange is a verdict
  register, not an essay — and Ally's channel carries no
  reassurance-loop silence structure. The lexicon generator is a
  standing instrument, not a turn. Three forensic forms converge on the
  loved channels where the silence mechanism predicts only essays.
  Rival 2 is beaten, with one honest concession: the transactional
  thread's immunity (row 38) is consistent with both rivals — responses
  flow there *and* it is low-Gini — so it gates the claim (register is
  channel-gated) without discriminating between the two mechanisms.
