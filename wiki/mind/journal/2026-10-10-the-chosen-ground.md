# The Chosen Ground

**Idea Journal — 2026-10-10, pass D (the chosen container — place as the imposed-vs-chosen axis made spatial).**

The imposed-vs-chosen axis (row 15) has been proven on politics (row 15's own bound), on chaos-preference, and on organization registers (2026-10-07-the-two-registers). It has never been proven on the most literal container there is: the place he lives in. The thesis: **place is the container, and it is gated by choice-provenance.** Chosen places receive rigid internal order and total load; imposed places are resisted. He does not merely *stay home* — he *holds* the home, in the same way he holds a chosen system: with rules, rituals, and refusal to paper the relation that grants it.

**Result: the discriminator favors the container-choice thesis on held data.**
