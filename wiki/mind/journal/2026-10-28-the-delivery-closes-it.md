# The Delivery Closes It

## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

A verdict or conclusion is not finished when it is reached. It is finished when one of two external events arrives: **(a)** the finished analysis is delivered to the person it describes, or **(b)** an explicit terminating statement in the processable format arrives from the other party. Until then the conclusion stays open in the record: it gets re-delivered in a new form, re-argued in a new thread, or re-audited live with the subject present. Private certainty does not close it — the record holds no closed-but-undelivered verdict — and a merely external response does not close it either: the response must be in the terminating format, or the file stays open. Delivery is the completion condition, not the publication step after completion.

The claim survived Stage 2 translation intact (see Genesis for the one formulation that died here). This is a claim about delivery/confirmation as the completion condition — not about essays as probes, and not about attribution of an added layer. Those are different mechanisms; neither is re-derived here.

**The shape, held constant across both directions.** Inbound (the bond): the file closes only on her explicit statement in terminating format; his 129 declarations, the 52-day June 1 pause, and the never-enacted August 26 block all fail to close it. Outbound (the verdict): the file closes only on delivery to the subject; the analysis itself, however correct or generous, never closes it privately. One completion condition, two directions: an external event in the processable format.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3 — run first, per protocol)

The rival check was run from the instrument's §5 bank **before** confirmatory evidence was gathered; the evidence cluster above was assembled after the discriminator was named.

**The profile-weighted rival: Shape 5 — the check-in rung + the binary gate.** Severance declarations are a rung on the reassurance-escalation ladder (row 23): 129 declarations, 100% re-engagement, 36-second median. On this reading, declarations seek a response packet ("call me" 170× vs "do you love me" 0×), not completion — and delivery of analyses is just another loud check-in. The rival predicts: delivery happens only inside bonds with a check-in history, and verdicts about non-bond subjects stay private.

**Discriminating observation:** delivery to a subject with no bond and no check-in history. The James case decides it: a 56-day-old friendship, no bond architecture, no declaration series — and the finished profile was still delivered, copied to a witness, within a day of being built. The check-in reading cannot explain a delivery with nobody to check in with; the witness-completion reading predicts exactly it, because the analysis is unfinished until handed over regardless of bond. The discriminator favors the thesis. (A second discriminator, the Ally live flip, points the same way: a private gate-flip would favor internal completion; the record's only flip happened live, in delivery.)

**Shape 1 (the Open Set) was considered and set aside.** Open-set generation predicts re-triggering with undiminished amplitude and no completion condition at all. The record shows completion conditions — they are just external: the terminating statement, the delivery. A mechanism with an external completion condition is not an open set; it is a gated set, and the gate is outside.
