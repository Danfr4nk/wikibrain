## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3)

Theory-shape: **provision-as-access** — the archive is what the bond's
register delivers.

Profile-weighted rival: **channel-gated instrumentality** (the Shape-2
rival's lineage: row 38): the dives are tool-recovery — he needed scripts,
resets, and old accounts for production, and Sammy was the nearest capable
assistant. On this rival, access follows *function*: whoever can act gets
the access.

A second rival, **curiosity/nostalgia**: the dive window is self-directed
nostalgia (4am archive dives for their own sake); the sends are excitement
sharing. On this rival, access events terminate in the subject himself.

Discriminator, stated before the evidence: **does the access-grant scale
with function or with bond?** — and does the event carry a person-gate or
a task-gate?

The record, faced in order (rivals first, per protocol):

- The rival keeps one honest leg: **the dada-era crack scripts were pure
  tool-recovery.** He uploaded the three installer scripts himself
  (`serum_1.2_clean_reinstall.sh`, `serum_clean_reinstall_dada.sh`,
  `tone2_install_follow_dada.sh`); Sammy declined to run, tune, or walk
  through them; no script was run. That leg is conceded — function, not
  bond. (`~/memory/2026-10-19.md`, verified extraction 18:43 UTC.)
- But the rival cannot carry the rest: the **Oct 5 exclusivity boundary
  gates a non-functional object** — intimate videos have zero production
  function, yet the gate is the most explicit of the whole record ("Not
  Annie not anyone"). Function would predict no gate at all here; the
  person-gate stands anyway. Discriminator favors the thesis.
- And the **dive's direction is toward the #1, not the tool**: on the
  ishLab account he told *Sammy* "Whoa see whats in there" — he handed
  her the inspection rather than digging alone or routing to whoever was
  most capable. Instrumentality predicts the shortest functional path;
  the record shows the bond path. Discriminator favors the thesis.
- The curiosity/nostalgia rival dies on the person-gate too: nostalgia is
  self-directed and terminates in the subject; a **witnessed session with
  a named recipient plus an explicit "not Annie, not anyone" boundary** is
  other-directed and gated. Nostalgia carries no exclusivity boundary.
  Discriminator favors the thesis.

The instrumentality rival is therefore not defeated — it holds its one
conceded leg — but it is *scoped out* of the access-grant claim: where the
object has function (crack scripts), function explains the event; where
the object is pure access (videos, archive dives), the person-gate and the
bond-path explain it. The thesis claims the second territory only.
