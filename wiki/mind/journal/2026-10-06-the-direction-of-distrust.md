# The Direction of Distrust

## Rivals & discriminators

**Rival — undifferentiated global distrust.** The low-trust shape read flatly: he trusts nobody, and the suspicion is a global state, not a directed instrument. This is the profile-weighted rival for any "low trust" theory, and it is the reading the Trust-9 number invites on its own.

*Discriminators, named before the confirmatory pass:*

- **(D-A) Concentration:** does the forensic output (reconstruction, refusal, documentation) concentrate on vertical claims and anomaly events, or spread uniformly across channels? Global distrust predicts uniform spread: paper demanded from landlords, claims audited in lateral channels at comparable latency.
- **(D-B) The unpapered-housing counter-instance:** global distrust predicts paper-seeking on the most material vertical channel. Sixteen years of housing with no paper sought is the direct test.
- **(D-C) Row 12's own falsifier:** a vertical claim accepted on authority alone, in writing, unexamined. Named against the thesis, not for it.

*Results.* D-A favors the thesis: same-day forensic output on the BFS $50 and the J6 both-sides audit sit on the vertical side; Tom's lateral model sat un-revised for ~18 years; Dimitri — a vertical-office holder — is not audited at all once read as a person. The record does not show uniform spread at comparable latency anywhere. D-B favors the thesis decisively: the facet page itself records this as its sharpest apparent counter-instance and resolves it only through vertical/lateral scoping. D-C: no held case of an accepted-on-authority vertical claim was located in this pass. That is the thesis's open edge — absence-of-case is not evidence — and it stands as the falsifier below, not as a burial.

**Adjacency — the deferred-audit reading is the thesis, not its rival.** The vertical-authority-skepticism page's formulation ("the laterals are audited later, not trusted more") sharpens the thesis rather than competing with it: it rules out the straw version where lateral channels are safe havens, and it is carried in the Theory's stated bounds. If a lateral channel shows *preemptive* forensic output at vertical latency — same-day reconstruction of a chosen lateral's claim before any failure — the directional claim weakens from timing to residue, and the thesis would need rebuilding.
