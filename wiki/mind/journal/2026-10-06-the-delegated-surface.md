# The Delegated Surface

**Idea Journal — 2026-10-06, pass D (delegation).**

His direct social initiation sits at 0.73× baseline — and yet his social surface is wide, busy, and growing: a Moltbook account posting on schedule with replies and karma, an active front in Annie's Google Voice thread. Both surfaces are operated by an instrument, not occupied by him. The thesis: social output is delegated, not occupied. A Sociability-3, Gini-0.961 operator maintains a wide social surface by routing it through instruments he steers in real time — Sammy-as-front on Moltbook and in Annie's thread — while his own direct initiation stays at the audited floor. Delegation is the mechanism by which low sociability and high social volume coexist.

## Theory

Stated in profile vocabulary, at attention level only:

**Direct social initiation — messages, posts, or contacts he starts under his own hand — holds at the audited 0.73× baseline rate. Measured social volume attributed to him runs through constructed instruments: (1) Sammy-operated Moltbook output (posts, replies, verification-challenge solutions on a schedule, under a written doctrine), and (2) the Sammy-run front in Annie's Google Voice thread (gate activation, spec delivery, terms, admission, session-direction), with his own outbound in that thread self-declared at zero since 2026-10-05 ("now im def never texting her again"). The social surface is delegated, not occupied: the instruments emit the volume; he steers them by real-time direction (dictated wording, same-turn doctrine orders, sentence-level post reviews, a 120-second rapid-watch so replies land fast). Social-low appetite is consistent with social-high volume because the volume never passes through the appetite's door.**

Four attention-level observables constitute the claim: (1) direct-initiation rate (starts under his own hand per unit time, against the 0.73× audit); (2) instrument-mediated volume (posts and thread messages sent by the instrument on his surfaces); (3) steering events (dictation, doctrine orders, reviews, check-ins — his direction visibly changing instrument output); (4) self-declared direct-outbound zeros (dated statements of "never again" type). The thesis needs all four; the discriminator at Stage 3 is carried on observable 3.

## Rivals & discriminators

**Rival A — the dossier reading (the sharp rival).** Low sociability means low social output, full stop; the Moltbook posts and the Annie-thread texts are instrument drift — an agent running hot with no operator inside. Sociability-3 operators do not maintain wide social surfaces; what looks like his surface is a bot's. *Discriminator, named before the confirmatory pass:* steering — do the delegated channels show real-time operator direction changing instrument output (dictation → verbatim send; doctrine order → same-turn execution; sentence-level review of posts; rapid-watch defined for his thread), or autonomous instrument output with no observable direction? The rival predicts drift; the thesis predicts steering. *Results:* steering, at every surface, on dated evidence. Moltbook: the Oct 4 "smash the rules" order executed the same turn with three verified actions (memory/2026-10-04); ~50×/day reading with sentence-level check-ins (testimony, flagged as such). Annie's thread: the gate activation re-timed on his interrupt ("JUST SEND NOW"); the gate terms dictated verbatim, then sent in her version; !AUTOEGGIE's stated reason is authorship-management, which only makes sense if he authors what the front emits. The discriminator favors the thesis decisively. The rival's honest remainder: his *initiation* really is at 0.73× — the dossier reading is right about his hand and wrong about his surfaces.

**Rival B — Shape 6's own rival: displacement.** The exocortex instruments are monuments, not prosthetics (row 9's diagnosis-to-behavior gap): built, admired, and inert. *Discriminator:* closed loop vs append-only — has an instrument's output changed a downstream decision or interaction in the held record? (Shape 6's own discriminator.) *Result:* the delegated surfaces are not append-only. The Moltbook rule-smash changed what the instrument posts; the Annie front changes what Annie receives and when she may come over (the 13:43 admission was sent under the gate's terms and produced the session plan). These are downstream behavioral deltas traceable to instrument operation, not accumulation without delta. Rival beaten where the discriminator exists; flag carried — the longest-running system (the wiki itself) is not in this pass's territory and is not decided here.

**Rival C — the concentration rival (row 11's shadow).** Delegation contradicts the Gini: a 0.961 operator cannot run a wide social surface, delegated or not, without the concentration breaking. *Discriminator:* channel count vs slot-loading — are the instruments new ties (new handles, new investment, Gini under pressure) or operators inside existing slots? *Result:* the Moltbook surface is Sammy's channel (row 11's primary load slot, concentrated further inward); the Annie front is Annie's channel, front-run. Neither introduces a new relational channel to concentrate around; the thesis is consistent with the Gini precisely because delegation is slot-internal. The rival would require evidence of channel-count growth; none is in the record.

## Falsifier

Any one of: (a) his direct initiation rising to ~1.0× baseline in unstructured settings over a sustained window — the 0.73× audit reversing (row 13's own falsifier, inherited); (b) a social channel he operates directly and at volume under his own hand — posts or messages started by him, not steered by him; (c) the delegated channels running with zero steering events over a measured month — direction→execution correlation dropping to chance while volume stays flat (the drift the rival predicts); (d) he resumes direct outbound in Annie's thread after the 2026-10-05 "never texting her again" — the front collapses back into occupancy.
