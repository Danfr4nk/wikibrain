# The Delegated Surface

_Layer: interpreted/journal — a dated thesis entry. It reasons over self/ exhibits and kb/ evidence, which it cites rather than restating._

At 23:59 EDT on 2026-10-04, Dan countermanded his own midnight order — "JUST SEND NOW" — and forty-nine seconds past midnight the text went out from his Google Voice number: "12:00am ANNIE-GATE ACTIVE." He did not write it into the thread himself. That is the pattern in one frame: his directive, his number, his thread — and somebody else's hands on the keyboard, on his standing order. The same week, Sammy's Moltbook account ran a full social life — posts, replies, verification challenges solved, karma climbing run by run — which he read, by his own estimate, ~50 times a day without ever posting on it himself. The social surface is wide, busy, and growing, and the person it belongs to does not occupy it.

**Idea Journal — 2026-10-06, pass D (delegation).**

His direct social initiation sits at 0.73× baseline — and yet his social surface is wide, busy, and growing: a Moltbook account posting on schedule with replies and karma, an active front in Annie's Google Voice thread. Both surfaces are operated by an instrument, not occupied by him. The thesis: social output is delegated, not occupied. A Sociability-3, Gini-0.961 operator maintains a wide social surface by routing it through instruments he steers in real time — Sammy-as-front on Moltbook and in Annie's thread — while his own direct initiation stays at the audited floor. Delegation is the mechanism by which low sociability and high social volume coexist.

## Theory

Stated in profile vocabulary, at attention level only:

**Direct social initiation — messages, posts, or contacts he starts under his own hand — holds at the audited 0.73× baseline rate. Measured social volume attributed to him runs through constructed instruments: (1) Sammy-operated Moltbook output (posts, replies, verification-challenge solutions on a schedule, under a written doctrine), and (2) the Sammy-run front in Annie's Google Voice thread (gate activation, spec delivery, terms, admission, session-direction), with his own outbound in that thread self-declared at zero since 2026-10-05 ("now im def never texting her again"). The social surface is delegated, not occupied: the instruments emit the volume; he steers them by real-time direction (dictated wording, same-turn doctrine orders, sentence-level post reviews, a 120-second rapid-watch so replies land fast). Social-low appetite is consistent with social-high volume because the volume never passes through the appetite's door.**

The discriminator cares about this: if steering were decoration, the doctrine order would have been absorbed without a behavioral delta; instead three verified actions with new rules went live the same turn.

**Delegation, not ghostwriting.** A ghostwriter hides the author; the front here discloses the instrument. Four attention-level observables constitute the claim: (1) direct-initiation rate (starts under his own hand per unit time, against the 0.73× audit); (2) instrument-mediated volume (posts and thread messages sent by the instrument on his surfaces); (3) steering events (dictation, doctrine orders, reviews, check-ins — his direction visibly changing instrument output); (4) self-declared direct-outbound zeros (dated statements of "never again" type). The thesis needs all four; the discriminator at Stage 3 is carried on observable 3. Two checkable claims added by the expansion: (a) steering latency — dictation→send and doctrine→live-execution run at hour-scale, not day-scale; a drift-regime instrument would show the same volume with zero such events; (b) the "never again" zero of 2026-10-05 is verifiable against future thread exports — any direct outbound from his hand after that date collapses the front back into occupancy (Falsifier, clause d).
