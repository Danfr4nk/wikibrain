## Theory (Stage 2)

Stated in profile vocabulary, attention-level only: in dated adjudications
where a **mediated verdict** (commissioned instrument, machine detector,
dossier label) contradicted a primary — his stated word or a dated artifact —
the dated record shows the **mediator losing in every case**, and the
direction runs **inward as well as outward**: his own stated word also lost
to primary artifacts in the two inward cases. The resulting ladder, observed
not asserted:

**mediated verdict < stated word < primary artifact.**

"Observed" is the load-bearing word. The thesis makes no interior claim —
no claim about what he *trusts*, *believes*, or *feels* about instruments.
It claims a dated pattern: N dated adjudications, N in which the higher
rung of the ladder won.

Outward legs (mediator vs his word): the C6 headline retracted against the
recomputed counts; the Oct 18–20 machine-detector clearances under the
standing line "his words outrank machine flags"; the C3 keeper — the wiki
retains "autism" because the 2025-09-15 "SHUT UP I'M AUTISTIC" is his word
for the architecture (row 48, dated primary), while the discriminating
test between INTP and autism remains unrun, i.e. no instrument ever voted.

Inward legs (his word vs the artifact): the 2018-04-05 23:26 Fran
death-moment retraction — his same-night stated story corrected ~3 hours
later after he reviewed the video; the Oct 19 kill-switch adjudication —
his message "i think this may be the infamous autoresponder" adjudicated
the same day against the script's actual content (a TCP-5223 kill switch,
not the autoresponder).

## Falsifier

  word or a primary record **and he accepted the mediator** — one clean
  leg drops the ladder from "observed rule" to "tendency with exceptions";
  two dated legs kill it.

  kills the inward direction (stated word < primary artifact).

  running and vindicating the instruments over his word — re-opens the
  naming question and the thesis must be re-run from Stage 1 with the
  new datum.
