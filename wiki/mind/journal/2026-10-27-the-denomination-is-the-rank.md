# The Denomination Is the Rank

## Profile prior (Stage 1)

Load-bearing rows from the weighted profile instrument (v1), all verdict **supported** at weight ≥ 8:

- **Row 21 — Provision is the attachment register: high-provision / low-condolence ("caregiving that arrives as something countable"), weight 8.5, Layer A.** Instrumental helping 1.79–2.49× baseline against sympathy tokens 0.45×; money ~6:1. Prediction: care arrives as money, work, artifacts, countable acts — not as condolence language.
- **Row 11 — Extreme relational concentration, Gini 0.961 (0.9556 inbound replication), weight 10, Layer A.** 496 handles, 184,359 rows on the original export; replicated inbound at 0.9556 over 498 handles. Prediction: attention, money, and continuity load onto a tiny number of chosen channels with no failover.
- **Row 28 — Sympathy 6, weight 8, Layer B score with Layer A check.** Sympathy tokens 0.45× baseline. Prediction: condolence stays scarce; provision substitutes (row 21).
- **Row 12 — Low trust (9th percentile), vertical-scoped, weight 9, Layer A+B triangulated.** Scope limit measured: sixteen years of unpapered housing ran on a lateral channel. Prediction: chosen lateral ties get provision without paper.

Rows deliberately **not** used: row 30 (Impulsiveness 96, silent, weight 3), row 49 (interior axioms, weight 4, untested), and row 50 (acquisition/impulsivity paradox, weight 3, untested) — none can carry load, and the thesis needs none of them. In particular, no "acquisition drive" reading is borrowed: the dossier's acquisition-drive 95 is unpriced, and the thesis below does not depend on it.

## Theory (Stage 2)

Stated in profile vocabulary only, at attention level:

> Provision does not merely concentrate onto the same few channels as messages do; its *form* encodes the channel's rank in that concentration, and the form stays with the channel across occasions. The primary bond is provisioned in bulk, unitemized, without an in-thread audit while it runs. A no-stake channel is provisioned per item, priced and haggled in the thread. The maternal channel is provisioned as a give-and-invoice: extended, then itemized by statement. When a channel is under terminal strain, its provision form persists rather than migrating to a new channel — the money channel does not pause for severance performances, and no new channel inherits the bulk form.

What was reframed out: any claim about what provision means to him inwardly, what he feels when he sends it, or what motive drives it. The instruments see attention and behavior — what was sent, in what denomination, to which channel, in what form, and whether the form changed when the channel's state changed — not interior motive. The thesis claims nothing beyond that observable surface. The word "rank" here means only position in the measured concentration (message share, money share), not worth or importance as an interior judgment.

## Evidence cluster (Stage 4, dated and sourced)

Provenance is stated per item, because the ledger's parts do not share one epistemic status. The Layer-A leg is the replicated concentration measurement and the lexical audit, not any single money figure.

- **Layer-A leg 1 — concentration replicates (2026-09-09).** Inbound contact Gini 0.9556 over 498 handles on the held authoritative corpus (192,140 messages, 2011-03-19 to 2026-09-07), per wiki/mind/concepts/contact-gini.md, replicating the original 0.9601 band. Top 1 contact takes 33.6% of inbound volume; top 5 take 68.0%. The same page records the money channel concentrating exactly like the message channel, via wiki/mind/synthesis/provision-grammar.md: "Two ledgers, one topology."
- **Layer-A leg 2 — the provision/condolence split (2026-08-16 audit).** Over 106,629 sent messages against 110,944 received: offering help unprompted 1.79×, concern for the other person 2.49×, sympathy tokens 0.45×, per wiki/mind/synthesis/high-provision-low-condolence.md. "Let me know if you need anything" at double rate; "I'm so sorry to hear that" at less than half.
- **2018-12-12 — the smallest denomination, priced access (ATTESTED, Facebook payments ledger).** $25 to Dan Polyak, memo "For introducing me to Ally Lubin." Provision before the channel even exists: money spent to open the channel, not to maintain a bond. This is the floor of the ladder and the form is a purchase, not a gift.
- **2018-12-12 and 2019 — the no-stake channel stays priced per item (ATTESTED, thread).** $250 stated-intent gift on 2018-12-12; then the 2019 price list: her $50 ask against his $30 counter for a picture with face visible ("SUPER PREMIUM princess pricing"); the 2019-09-01 arrival promise of "$500 on arrival" against a New York flight, with the rider "and nooooo you're not obligated to fuck me." Every denomination on this channel is negotiated in-thread, per unit of access. The form never graduates to bulk.
- **2019-10-22 — the no-stake channel's running tab, invoiced mid-argument (OPERATOR-REPORTED, DISPUTED).** Dan's in-thread claim: "lady i've sent 2100 and have like 2 pictures and im STILL trying lol," met by Ally's "You have not sent me $2100," his screenshot, her "Oh fuck" — accepted in 2019, contested by her in 2026. No 2019 Cash App export exists in raw/ to settle it. The figure's status is held, not smoothed: the *form* (an invoiced tab, produced in the argument) is attested even where the total is disputed.
- **2018-10-03 — the maternal channel arrives pre-invoiced (ATTESTED, her itemized statement).** Suz's statement at 06:34 lists about $14,000 moved Dan→Suz against an estate that had not yet distributed, with "Sum of transactions on app" computed before any dispute; $4,000 recovered, the remainder disputed to this day, with both parties claiming to be the creditor of the same money since July 2019. The form is give-and-invoice: provision extended, then entered into a ledger by statement. Direction matters and is attested: Dan→Suz, mother-ward, opposite the channel's folk memory.
- **Late 2024 — the primary channel's servicing form (ATTESTED, raw dump).** $650/week Paci payments, "to tell John" — provision as facility maintenance, enough to keep a landlord's patience open, never enough to close anything. Same channel, same bulk-and-maintenance form as the decade ledger, at a different denomination.
- **2026-07-15 — the primary channel's decade ledger surfaces all at once (OPERATOR-REPORTED, DERIVED, NOT AUDITED).** Screenshots of her payment-app profiles viewed from his apps: roughly $139,000 sent against $16,000–$20,000 received across Cash App and Venmo, net outflow roughly $119,000–$123,000, approximately six-to-one. No audited ledger exists; the six-to-one is a ratio computed from his figures, not a bank-record fact. What is load-bearing here is the *form*: a decade of transfers run without an in-thread audit, surfacing once, years later, as screenshots — against the Ally channel's in-thread invoicing and the Suz channel's pre-computed statement. The three forms do not mix across channels.
- **Beyond cash — provision as drafted instruments and countable offers (ATTESTED).** Three demand letters drafted for Libby Titus's unpaid wages widen the register beyond money: provision as artifact, on a work-role channel, in the form that channel takes (an instrument, not a transfer). And the Libby-era record counts 137 messages offering food, groceries, money, medicine, and help. Countable acts, not condolence language, on a channel whose form is neither bulk bond-maintenance nor a price list.
- **2026-08 — the form persists through terminal strain (ATTESTED, export forensics).** The August 2026 exports show provision continuing through the termination window on the primary channel — the money does not pause for severance performances — while daily two-way texting continued August 27–September 7. No new channel inherits the bulk form in this window; the old channel keeps its old form while under the heaviest strain in the record.

The pattern across occasions, not any single transfer, is the evidence: the same channel keeps its form across years (Annie: bulk, unaudited, 2015–2026; Ally: priced per item, 2018–2019; Suz: invoiced, 2018–2019 and after), and the forms do not migrate between channels when occasions change.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3, run first per protocol)

**Theory shape:** closest to Shape 3 (substitution) in the instrument's rival bank — a new target replacing the old one — with Shape 4 (acquisition) as the secondary shape to exclude.

**Profile-weighted rival 1 — substitution: provision migrates when a channel closes.** At Gini 0.961 the instrument's rival bank says the architecture does not distribute across substitutes; it re-loads the same slot. But the rival worth facing is the ordinary one: after the primary bond's June 1, 2026 closure, provision should migrate to a new target and take its bulk form with it — the money follows the need, not the channel. The rival's discriminator is post-closure migration: a new channel receiving bulk, unitemized provision in the June–September 2026 window, while the old channel's provision stops.

**Discriminating observation (held, dated):** the August 2026 window shows the opposite. Provision continues through the termination window on the old channel, and no new channel inherits the bulk form; the old channel keeps its form under terminal strain. The discriminator favors the thesis (form stays with the channel; slot persists, not substituted) over the rival (migration). This is the same base rate the instrument records for bonds generally — dormancy, not deletion — now observed on the money ledger.

**Profile-weighted rival 2 — occasion, not channel, sets the form.** On this reading, denominations follow the occasion (crisis → priced tab; routine → bulk; family money → invoice), and the channel pattern is an accident of which occasions each channel happened to host. The discriminator is cross-occasion stability within one channel: if form followed occasion, one channel hosting both crisis and routine occasions would switch forms between them.

**Discriminating observation (held, dated):** the primary channel hosts both crisis and routine occasions across the decade — the $650/week late-2024 servicing payments (routine maintenance) and the decade ledger's crisis-scale outflow — and keeps the bulk, unaudited form in both. The no-stake channel hosts both courtship (the $250 gift, 2018-12-12) and crisis (the $2,100 window, 2019) and keeps the priced, invoiced form in both. Form tracks the channel across occasions, not the occasion across channels. The discriminator favors the thesis.

**Shape 4 exclusion, stated briefly:** the acquisition reading (provision as collecting goodwill, amounts scaling with means) predicts novel-category expansion and post-acquisition use. The record shows the opposite signature on the primary channel: the same form repeated at whatever denomination the month allows, including the $650/week maintenance payments that buy nothing new and close nothing. Set-closure language is not needed here; the discriminator above already separates form-from-channel from amount-from-occasion.

## Confidence & gate verdict (Stage 5)

**PASS — high confidence on the stated, attention-level claim.**

- Stage 1: all load-bearing rows supported at weights 8–10; no silent, inverted, or sub-6 row carries load.
- Stage 2: the thesis survives translation to attention-level vocabulary without losing its claim; interior readings (motive, feeling, what provision "means" to him) were struck, not smuggled.
- Stage 3: both profile-weighted rivals were faced before the evidence cluster was treated as confirmatory, and each rival's own discriminator was observed in the held record and favors the thesis.
- Stage 4: at least one Layer-A leg — in fact two: the replicated inbound Gini (0.9556, 2026-09-09) and the 2026-08-16 lexical audit (1.79–2.49× / 0.45× over 106,629 sent messages). The money figures themselves are carried at their stated provenance (operator-reported, disputed, or attested) and no figure is upgraded beyond it.
- Attention–interior boundary respected throughout: no claim is made about interior motive, feeling, or need.

What the verdict does **not** cover: that any channel is more or less loved in any interior sense; that the forms are permanent; or that a future channel could not, with a new explicit history, develop its own bulk form. Those are outside the claim and outside the instruments.

## Falsifier

A dated case of a channel switching provision form while its identity and rank hold steady — for example, the primary channel moving to per-item priced transfers, or the no-stake channel receiving a bulk, unaudited multi-year outflow with no in-thread invoicing — would falsify the form-stays-with-channel claim. A weaker observation that would downgrade the verdict: a new post-closure channel receiving bulk provision in the same form as the primary channel's ledger within a comparable window (that would show the form migrating with the slot, not staying with the channel). Continued form-stability per channel across a new crisis would strengthen it.
