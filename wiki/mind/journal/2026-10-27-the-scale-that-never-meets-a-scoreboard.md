# The Scale That Never Meets a Scoreboard

## Profile prior (Stage 1)

Load-bearing rows from the weighted profile instrument
(`~/workspace/goals/wiki-brain-improvement/files/weighted-profile-instrument-v1.md`):

- **Row 2 (weight 9, Layer A, supported):** graded numeric credences on own
  beliefs, private channel, ~22x baseline; direction stable across three
  independent scans; strict re-derivation 24-vs-1.
- **Row 1 (weight 8.5, supported):** verdicts are categorical/binary; no
  graded middle on worth, authenticity, legitimacy; gradation fenced to
  facts.
- **Row 4 (weight 8, supported as practice):** forensic method — primary
  records over narrative, anomaly clusters, procedural tells, lossless
  retention; conclusions labeled with confidence.
- **Row 5 (weight 9, supported):** explicit-over-inferred meaning — stated
  text registers, ambient/implied signal does not.
- **Row 3 (weight 7, leans, small n):** the credences transmit intensity,
  not forecasts; stated "certain" priced at ~0.25 against outcomes on the
  instrument's testimony ledger (dat:0044). This row is carried as
  **testimony, not as a Layer-A leg**: the ledger itself was not located in
  held records during this pass, and the page-level resolvable sample is
  n=1 (see Evidence cluster). Nothing load-bearing in this entry rests on
  the 0.25 figure.

Rows marked silent, inverted, or weight <= 4 were not used. No interior-state
row carries anything here: per the instrument's section 2, every claim below
is stated at attention level — what the output does, where it points, how
often — never at what is felt, needed, or driven internally.

## Theory (Stage 2)

Stated only in profile vocabulary, at attention level:

In Dan's outbound text, graded numeric credences repeat at roughly 22x the
inbound rate and attach almost exclusively to propositions no held record
can score — other people's interiors and unwitnessed past events — while
arriving with cited evidence attached ("I am 95% sure, based on what RT
said"). The number marks the landing-force of a forensic chain at attention
level: it stamps how hard the assembled evidence lands, in the one domain
where grading is permitted (unwitnessed fact), and it is fenced off from
the verdict domains (worth, authenticity, trust, legitimacy) where the
binary gate runs. The unscorability is not an accident of the sample. It is
the selection condition that lets the scale survive: a number aimed where no
scoreboard exists can never be marked wrong. The single instance in eleven
years that did meet a scoreboard resolved false — and the habit never
migrated toward scorable ground afterward. The scale is therefore read as
intensity-stamped forensic closure, not as a calibrated forecasting
instrument. The intensity half of that reading is carried at **leans**
(row 3); the fence and no-migration halves are carried at supported
strength (rows 1, 2, and the Layer-A legs below).

## Evidence cluster (Stage 4)

All counts below are from `wiki/mind/concepts/calibrated-confidence.md`
(re-derived 2026-08-23) and `wiki/mind/synthesis/the-binary-verdict.md`
(2026-08-28), unless noted. Dated, sourced, wikilinked.

- **The strict count (2026-08-23 re-derivation, Layer A).** Under a strict,
  symmetric filter — first-person credences of the form "I am N% sure /
  certain / confident" or "there's an N% chance that [clause]", population
  shares and cited statistics excluded, applied identically to both
  directions — across 98,228 deduped Dan-sent messages vs 91,858 received:
  **24 graded (non-endpoint) credences outbound vs 2 inbound** (24.4 vs 2.2
  per 100,000). One of the two inbound instances is a tapback quoting Dan's
  own message back at him (2025-09-04), leaving **one genuine graded
  credence from 503 other people across eleven years** (2026-02-27: "she
  said that she needs space which I'm 99% sure that I'm fucked"). With the
  tapback removed, the rate ratio is ~22x. The original 43-vs-2 / 15-vs-0
  table did not reproduce and is superseded; the direction survived a
  filter built to break it.
- **The target sort (Layer A).** Reading all 24 strict graded credences,
  they sort into claims about another person's interior ("90% sure those are
  sarcastic apologies", "98.7% sure you don't believe any of your
  catholicism", "99% sure you're a no"), claims about unwitnessed past
  events ("95% sure she quit and didn't tell anyone", "99% sure it was a
  sedative in that", "99% sure it's her"), and forward-looking claims
  about the world — of which there are almost none. **Exactly one instance
  in eleven years is resolvable from the corpus.**
- **The one resolvable instance, resolved false (dated).** On **2018-08-08**
  Dan writes "I am 75% sure this is my last summer at Nemacolin."
  `wiki/work/nemacolin-caddying` dates the job April 2016 – November 2019
  per his own resume: he worked the 2019 season too. A 75% forecast that did
  not happen. n=1 is not a calibration test; it is a negative observation,
  and it is the only one the corpus offers.
- **No migration toward scorable ground after 2018 (Layer A).** The habit
  continues after the failed forecast — graded credences appear in 2019 (5),
  2020 (7), 2023 (1), 2024 (4), 2025 (7) by the original yearly count — and
  the eleven-year total of resolvable instances stays at one. If the scale
  were a forecasting instrument being refined, its targets would drift
  toward checkable ground after a miss. The observed direction is flat:
  same unscorable target classes, same years, no drift.
- **Not one relationship, not one era (Layer A).** The instances spread
  across 12 distinct handles; Annie takes the largest share (13), roughly
  her share of the corpus generally. They appear in every year the dump
  covers (2015–2025, with 2022 and 2026 missing from the dump entirely).
  The habit predates the AI-collaboration period by eight years.
- **Not general numeracy (Layer A control).** Bare percentages of any kind
  run at 2.94 per thousand outbound vs 2.16 inbound — a ratio of only
  1.36x. The divergence is specific to percentages pointed at his own
  mental state, where the ratio is ~22x.
- **The fence, from the binary-verdict side (Layer A + synthesis).** All 24
  strict credences attach to unwitnessed facts, never to a verdict about a
  person's worth, a taste's authenticity, or a relationship's legitimacy.
  The one dated fence exception is logistics, not verdict: on **2015-12-10**
  Dan writes "I like the 90% thing / It doesn't have to be that black and
  white" about shared time inside an arrangement he was actively authoring
  — a graded rule about logistics, not a graded verdict about the
  arrangement or the person. It bounds the fence; it does not breach it.
- **Public-channel corroboration of the scale's object-selection
  (2026-09-04 addition, Layer A on the twitter archive).** In private he
  grades his own mental state ("I am 95% sure"); in public he grades the
  world ("it's 25% at best for Kamala", **2024-07-06**; "There's about a
  100% chance President Brainrot thinks that he's got a real life
  leprechaun on staff", 2024-07-20). The strict pattern returns 0 in 2,718
  public posts where the message rate predicts ~1 — underpowered, not a
  refutation — and the archive has no inbound half, so it can never confirm
  or refute the asymmetry. Two further dated behaviors show the scale
  operating as a norm, not just a habit: he polices calibration in others
  (**2022-03-30**: "That's a level of certainty I'm not sure how you've
  arrived at"; **2022-02-24**, objecting to a suspiciously precise casualty
  figure), and he audits his own past estimates unprompted (**2024-11-07**,
  after the election: he notes his own June estimate was insufficiently
  pessimistic). The audit instinct exists — and the strict private credences
  still never migrate to the auditable ground where it could bite.
- **The 89% tell (Layer A).** 89% is not an intensifier value; nobody reaches
  for 89% to mean "very". The scale also runs downward (0% confident,
  January 2018) and past any warrant as a joke (99.9999%, 99.9999999999999999%)
  — a joke that only works along an axis the teller actually uses.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3, run first)

Stage 3 was executed before Stage 4 evidence assembly: the rivals below
were named and their discriminators checked in held records before
confirmatory evidence was gathered.

**Rival 1 — the intensifier / general-numeracy reading.** The numbers are
verbal emphasis, not estimates; Dan is just more numerate than his
correspondents. *Discriminating observation:* if this were general
numeracy or emphasis, bare percentages would diverge at a comparable ratio
and the values would cluster at emphatic endpoints. *Held-record check:*
bare percentages diverge only 1.36x vs ~22x for credences; the values
include 89% (unusable as emphasis) and a downward 0% run, and the absurd
nines-tail is a joke along a real axis. **The discriminator favors the
thesis; the rival fails.**

**Rival 2 — the untested calibrated forecaster.** He is a good forecaster
whose numbers simply have never been scored; the unscorability is sampling
bad luck. *Discriminating observation:* the distribution of targets. A
forecaster's credences, sampled over eleven years, should land on checkable
futures at something above a 1-in-24 rate, and should drift toward
checkable ground after a miss. *Held-record check:* 23 of 24 targets are
unscorable by construction (interiors, unwitnessed pasts); the single
scorable instance resolved false; post-2018 instances show no drift toward
scorable targets. **The discriminator favors the thesis; the rival fails
on distribution, not on the n=1 outcome alone.**

**Rival 3 (disqualified, not faced) — interior avoidance.** He aims the
scale at unscorable propositions because he fears being proven wrong, or
because some interior need stays unclosed. This is an interior-state claim
(row 30-adjacent vocabulary, silent at weight 3) and is barred by the
attention–interior rule: the instruments cannot see a felt fear or an
unclosed need, so the claim is dropped rather than carried, and the thesis
above is stated without it. The observable — target selection and
no-migration — stands without any interior mechanism.

**Rival 4 — channel artifact.** The habit is iMessage-specific, not a
property of the mind. *Discriminating observation:* the calibrated-confidence
page's own prediction — graded confidence at comparable rates in email,
Messenger, Reddit, and AI sessions, near-absent inbound everywhere.
*Held-record check:* the Messenger/email test remains unrun, and the
twitter check is underpowered (expected ~1, observed 0) with no inbound
half. **This discriminator is unavailable in held data, so the
channel-generality of the thesis is capped at hypothesis grade** and is
labeled as such here: the fence and no-migration claims hold on the message
corpus; whether they hold in every channel is untested, not assumed.

## Confidence & gate verdict (Stage 5)

**Gate verdict: PASS**, with a labeled split in what passes at what strength.

- High confidence: the fence (gradation lives only on unwitnessed fact,
  never on verdict) and the no-migration finding (one resolvable instance in
  eleven years, resolved false, no subsequent drift toward scorable
  ground). Both rest on Layer-A legs (strict re-derivation, target sort,
  dated resolvable case), on rows 1 and 2 at weights 8.5 and 9, on rivals 1
  and 2 beaten on their own discriminators, and on strict attention-level
  statement throughout.
- Leans: the intensity gloss itself — that the number transmits reasoning
  intensity rather than calibrated probability. Row 3 carries this at
  weight 7 on a small-n, partly testimony basis, and the page-level evidence
  (n=1, resolved false) is consistent with it without proving it. The entry
  therefore asserts intensity as the best reading, not as a measured
  calibration result.
- Capped at hypothesis grade: channel-generality (rival 4), pending the
  Messenger/email test the calibrated-confidence page itself specifies.

No restart was required: the thesis passed Stage 3 on its first framing.
(See Genesis note for the one pre-Stage-3 narrowing, which is not a restart.)

## Falsifier

1. A prospective prediction log (the instrument's standing experiment 1,
   rows 2–3) in which Dan's forward-dated, dated credences calibrate well
   against outcomes — Brier scores near his stated values over a scoreable
   set. That would replace the intensity reading with calibration and
   collapse the no-migration claim.
2. A re-run of the strict filter on a fuller corpus (including the missing
   2022 and 2026 message years, or the Messenger/email exports) showing
   graded credences aimed at checkable futures at rates comparable to the
   unscorable classes — i.e., the target distribution, not a single outcome,
   reversing.
3. A documented percentage, fraction, or explicit graded score attached to
   a verdict question (how much he trusts a specific person, how authentic
   he judges a specific taste, how legitimate a specific authority's claim)
   — the binary-verdict page's falsifier 1, which would breach the fence
   this entry relies on.
