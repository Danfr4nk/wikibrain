## Theory (Stage 2)

Stated in profile vocabulary, attention-level only — no interior terms survive:

Initiation is **setting-gated, not person-gated**. Observable form:

- **Where a rule-bound role or script exists, contact initiates and sustains.** The role supplies the next move explicitly: the server script, the tee sheet, the set list, the booth. In those settings the record shows multi-year continuous contact and repeated performance — durations, not dispositions.
- **Where no script exists — rooms, mingling, unstructured people — initiation stalls.** The record shows no sustained unstructured immersion anywhere: the global initiation rate sits at 0.73× baseline, contact concentrates at Gini 0.961, and home-anchoring GPS reads 0.68.
- **The 0.73× rate is a mixture, not a level.** A single global initiation rate averages two regimes that the record keeps apart: role-bound settings running at sustained-contact rates, unstructured settings running near non-initiation. Quoting 0.73× as "how sociable he is" flattens the only distinction the record actually draws — the same flattening row 13's own wording refuses when it writes "avoids *unstructured* people, performs on stages/scripts."
- **His own stated rule is the same split, in his own words.** February 25, 2018: "i'd rather be in front of a crowd of 5,000 people, than in the middle of a room with 500, because at least in front of 5,000 i'd have something to do other than mingle." Crowd size is priced at zero; the presence of something to do — a role, a function — is priced as the whole difference. A 5,000-person stage outranks a 500-person room on exactly one variable: the script.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3)

Theory-shape: the thesis is a **setting-gating** account. The adversarial rival, assigned for this pass, is **flat introversion**: low initiation everywhere, script or not — the 0.73× rate as a uniform trait level, with role-bound contact explained away as endurance rather than initiation.

Discriminating observation, stated before the evidence: **initiation rates in role-bound/scripted settings vs unstructured settings, same person, same era where possible.** Flat introversion predicts the role-bound settings running at the same depressed rate as the unstructured ones — long exposures endured at low initiation, or short exposures abandoned. The setting-gate predicts the split: sustained, repeated initiation inside roles; near-non-initiation outside them.

The discriminator as faced in the held record:

1. **Duration asymmetry (held, dated).** Flat introversion must explain 43 months of caddie-yard contact, 41 months of front-of-house contact, and a year-long stand-up run (23 dated rows) as endurance of a uniformly low-initiation trait. The setting-gate reads them as what they look like: contact initiating freely where the next move is scripted. Three independent role settings, three multi-year/multi-month runs, zero sustained unstructured counterparts. The rival has no corresponding leg: there is no unstructured run of comparable duration to point to.
2. **The stage-lapse natural experiment (held, dated).** The stand-up run ends when the clubs close (March 2020) and does not resume after reopening, though the ambition predates the run by over a year. Flat introversion predicts either no run at all or a run that ends on trait grounds (exhaustion, withdrawal mid-run); the record shows a run that ends on *script* grounds — the venue script lapsed — and stays ended. The setting, not the person, changed.
3. **His own stated rule (dated, his words).** The 2018 tweet prices crowd size at zero and the presence of something to do as the whole difference. Flat introversion predicts crowd size as the operative variable (bigger crowd = harder). The tweet inverts that prediction explicitly: 5,000 with a function beats 500 without one.

**Honest bound on the discriminator.** No setting-split initiation *count* exists in held data — the 0.73× audit was never run split by setting, and the duration evidence above is structural, not a rate. The discriminator is therefore faced at structural rigor (durations + lapse + stated rule), not at count rigor. The thesis passes on the held discriminator, but the count is the named open test: a setting-split initiation count is what would move this from PASS to PASS high, and a flat split would kill it outright (see Falsifier).

## Falsifier

- A setting-split initiation count landing flat — role-bound and unstructured initiation rates statistically indistinguishable — kills the mixture claim and hands the 0.73× rate back to flat introversion.
- A sustained unstructured immersion of comparable duration to the role runs (months of regular mingling with no role, no script, no function) falsifies the unstructured pole directly.
- A stand-up-style run resuming after its script lapses, on trait grounds alone, kills the stage-lapse leg.
- Initiation rates rising to baseline in unstructured settings (row 13's own stated falsifier) kills the thesis wholesale.
