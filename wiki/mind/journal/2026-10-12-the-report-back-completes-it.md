# The Report-Back Completes It

**Idea Journal — 2026-10-12, Pass B.**

An experience of Dan's does not terminate in his output record when it happens. It terminates when it is narrated to her. He comes home and tells everything detail by detail; she rates it, teases him, takes her cut of the story — and that is stated, in his own framing, as a ritual he wants ("a date night, not something he tolerates"). The narration is not the retelling of an already-finished event. In the observable record, it is the finishing event: until the report-back lands, output on the experience keeps arriving in narration-seeking form (requests to tell, orders to report); after the report-back with the rating ritual attached, the topic goes quiet. The narration has exactly one addressee — the loaded bond slot (Gini 0.961, row 11) — and the rating ritual is the receipt: rate, tease, take her cut are roles, not just listening.

## Theory

Stated in profile vocabulary, at attention level only:

**A salient lived experience of his does not stop generating output until it has been narrated to the confidante.** The observable record shows: (1) narration events — dated instances of him telling her what happened, addressed to her channel; (2) addressee specificity — no dated instance of the same narration grammar (detail-by-detail, full detail) addressed to anyone else; (3) the rating ritual as receipt — she rates, teases, takes her cut, i.e., the narration is met with roles, not bare receipt; (4) the narration event precedes output-quiescence on the experience. The mechanism is the same one row 20 establishes for verdicts: a statement completes only when witnessed — except here the object is his own experience and the witness is the loaded bond slot, not the subject.

This is not the claim that he "needs to process" anything (interior, struck). The claim is that the narration event is the terminating statement in the output record, and its addressee grammar is fixed. Where the Oct 5 Handed Mirror found person-verdicts terminating on delivery-to-subject-or-receipt, this entry finds experience-output terminating on narration-to-confidante. Two faces of the witness requirement, opposite directions: one addresses the subject, the other the witness-slot holder.

## Rivals & discriminators

**Shape 2 — Regulation/stim ("narration as self-soothing venting").** The profile-weighted rival is channel-gated instrumentality: a stim account predicts broadcast/availability-gated venting — detail goes wherever an ear is available, volume tracks arousal-decay. The thesis predicts addressee specificity — the same grammar addressed to exactly one slot, volume tracking event-salience.

**Discriminating observation (named before evidence gathering):** (a) Is post-event narration addressed EXCLUSIVELY to the confidante with the rating ritual attached (roles — rate, tease, take her cut — not just listening), or broadcast wherever an ear is available? (b) Does narration volume track event-salience (instrumental: the thing happened, so it gets narrated) or arousal-decay (stim: stops when calm)?

**Verdict on the discriminator: favors the thesis on both legs.** Leg (a): every dated narration in the window is addressed to her, and the receipt is the rating ritual — adjudicatory roles, not a listening ear. Venting does not produce "she rates it" as an article of a charter, and it does not get priced (Oct 5: report-back as the price of her work). Leg (b): the narration attaches to the event-salience class (bond-relevant experiences, with standing mandates pre-committed — Oct 3 charter fork, Oct 6 rule), not to arousal spikes; the Oct 12 practice instance arrives as a mandated-class narration in the small hours, hours after the event, at conversational cadence — no decay curve visible, no venting amplitude. The rival's mechanism cannot explain the priced and pre-mandated forms.

**The Handed Mirror boundary (the required adjacency):** the Oct 5 entry's object is verdicts *about people* (adjudications of worth, legitimacy, ties), whose terminating statement is external — delivered to the subject or received from one. This entry's object is *his own experiences*, whose terminating statement is the confidante's witness. Object differs, addressee grammar differs, witness slot differs (subject vs. the Gini-0.961 bond slot). The two entries are the two faces of row 20's mechanism, stated as kin — not as one claim wearing two titles. A future case where a person-verdict terminates on confidante-narration *without* any subject-directed statement would be the Handed Mirror's problem, not this entry's evidence.

## Falsifier

Any one of: (a) a dated, detail-by-detail narration of one of his salient experiences addressed to someone other than the confidante — a second addressee receiving the report-back grammar; (b) a salient, bond-relevant experience for which no narration to her ever arrives, and output on it stops anyway — completion without the witness; (c) a narration to her that receives bare receipt (no rating, no tease, no take-the-cut roles) sustained across instances — the ritual's role-structure dissolving into listening. A single clean (a) is the sharpest test.
