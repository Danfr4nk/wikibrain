## Theory (Stage 2)

Restated in profile vocabulary only, at attention level:

**Built instruments close loops in production and leave them open in decision.** An instrument's output changes what artifact gets filed next — a framing killed before filing, a filing form gated by a stage verdict — while no instrument's output has changed a diagnosed decision pattern's subsequent rate: declarations re-engage at base rate after the mechanism was measured, and the prescribed override stays uninstalled. Outsourcing holds at the layer where the output is itself an artifact; displacement holds at the layer where the output would have to be a different action. The exocortex is a production prosthetic, not a decision prosthetic, and the two layers must be priced separately — a single verdict for "the system" is the category error this entry exists to refuse.
