## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

Endurance inside imposed roles tracks the smuggled objective (row 8), not the assigned goal. The tenures are Shape-6 objects, and they adjudicate as **monuments, not prosthetics**: what the smuggled objective produced — named, still-running machinery and methods (rows 4, 10) and lateral channels live in the current dated record — persists after the container ends; what the assigned objective produced is absent from the record at attention level (no credential, no portable assigned-task skill, cuisine handled daily and never evaluated). The claim survived Stage 2 translation intact. "Smuggled" carries no motive theory here — it is defined operationally above (self-installed, assigner-unnamed, dated outputs) and used only in that sense.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3 — run first, per protocol)

**Discriminating observation:** did the private objective produce a *named downstream delta* the assigned goal did not — a dated case where the payload's product adjudicated something the container never contained? Prosthetic predicts the deltas die with the host (the skill, the ties, the method go silent after the end). Monument predicts named deltas surviving and operating past the container's end.

**Check — the discriminator favors the monument on three named legs:** (1) the shed audit posture → the vertical-skepticism model contribution → the 2026 drawer dispute's Dan's-Law analysis: the payload's method adjudicating a case two years and one container after its construction; (2) the Menore channel: built across the shed years, still the live operational tie in the 2026-10-04 dated record, 4,413 held messages peaking at the end — the container is gone, the channel isn't; (3) Dimitri, the beloved-boss bond, surviving as a named lateral tie. The prosthetic reading cannot name what the job sustained that outlived it; the monument reading can name three.

**Check — the discriminator favors the thesis, with the rival's partial credit stated:** no capital or credential residue is named anywhere after 84 months of employment (testimony grade — carried as the page's claim, not a held count).
