## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

Under a 10,000-line/day floor, his wiki output stays **burst-shaped**: multi-day runs in the sub-2,300 gross-line band, noon readings far under pace, and evening batches that arrive as bursts rather than as level output. The floor therefore functions as a **gauge that reveals the burst rhythm**, not a governor that changes it. The mandate is **endured and measured against** — the noon/midnight reports keep arriving, the gap keeps being stated in plain numbers — while the bursts are **what is inhabited**: the work that actually lands arrives in batches (journal waves, residual waves, book-mining nights), and no dated reading shows the floor converting a burst regime into a level one.

The claim survived Stage 2 translation intact: "he doesn't care about the floor" would be interior and is struck; "the dated counts stay in the sub-2,300 band while the floor stays at 10,000, and the dated reports keep stating the gap" is attention-level and is the claim. ## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3 — run first, per protocol)

**Rival A — Shape 6, the floor as outsourcing/prosthetic (the strongest rival).** On this account the floor is an exocortex instrument that should change subsequent decisions: a floor reading names a lever, and the next build differs from the pre-instrument base rate. The gauge becomes a governor through the decisions it informs.

**Discriminating observation:** a dated case where a floor reading changed what got built next — instrument output → different action than the pre-instrument base rate — versus readings accumulating with no downstream behavioral delta (displacement: the analysis is the product, row 9).

**Held-data check — mixed, net favoring the thesis:**
1. **For the rival: book mining debuted Oct 21 evening**, after days of floor misses, and ran three nights running. A floor-shaped pressure plausibly sits behind the new source's debut — the record does not state the causal link, and this entry does not claim one, but the sequence is the rival's best dated leg and is recorded here rather than hidden.
2. **Against the rival: the book-mining bursts never leveled anything.** Three burst nights, each priced inside its day's total, and all three days stayed in the sub-2,300 band (1,637; 1,467; 703 gross). If the floor-as-prosthetic were governing, the response it prompted would move the shape; it moved the contents of the bursts, not the shape.
3. **Against the rival: the fresh Jev pass.** Named as a proven-scale lever in the Oct 21, Oct 22, Oct 23, and Oct 24 reports alike — green-lit, repeatedly named, never run through Oct 24. Four consecutive dated readings naming the same lever with zero downstream action is the displacement signature (row 9) in dated form: the reading is the product; the behavior does not follow it.
4. **Against the rival: the reports themselves.** The noon/midnight instrument kept outputting on schedule across the whole run — the gauge never stopped — while the totals never left the band. An instrument that changes decisions should show at least one decision changed; the dated record shows readings accumulating and the shape unmoved.

Net: the discriminator does not cleanly falsify the rival — book mining's debut is a genuine partial counter-leg — but on balance it favors the thesis: the one lever that ran arrived burst-shaped and changed nothing about the shape, and the other named lever never ran at all.

**Rival B — Shape 2, channel-gating (the floor binds only some production channels).** On this account the floor would bind the journal/residual channels while some other channel escapes it and produces level output — the burst shape would be a channel artifact, not a regime.

**Discriminating observation:** any production channel whose dated output escapes the sub-2,300 band or shows level daily output under the floor.

**Held-data check — the discriminator favors the thesis.** No channel escapes: journal waves add hundreds, residual waves add tens, book-mining nights add dozens of notes inside sub-2,300 days, and the Oct 3 launch day (62 articles, the widest channel of all) reached only 3,534 net against a 20,000 directive. Every channel is priced by the same floor, and every channel lands burst-shaped. Channel-gating predicts an exempt channel; the dated record contains none.
