## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

Across the 2026-08-16 Big-30 corpus audit, his output repeats at elevated rates in the registers where the content is a verdict — the aesthetic register at 3.82× baseline (Artistic Interests 81), suspicion-of-motive language at 1.96× (Trust 9) — and holds below baseline in the registers whose content is affective attunement or unstructured initiation — sympathy tokens at 0.45× (Sympathy 6), initiating contact at 0.73× (Sociability 3). The verdict registers are exactly the rows whose content the Ti gate's native output form matches (row 1: categorical verdicts on worth and legitimacy); the starved registers have no verdict content available — condolence language renders no judgment, and an unstructured opening offers nothing to adjudicate, so the register sits idle at 0.73×, consistent with row 13's unstructured-people aversion. The 8.5× spread between the hottest and coldest audited registers (3.82 ÷ 0.45 = 8.49, derived arithmetic) is the allocation signature: volume is not distributed by how much he talks but assigned by what the register's content affords. The translation check: "the gates sit on the affect registers" became "registers whose content is affective attunement hold below baseline," "verdicts are cheap" became "verdicts are the content" — the claim survived translation intact; nothing interior rode along.

## Evidence cluster (Stage 4 — Layer-A gate)

**The floods are verdict-content, both of them.** The aesthetic register at 3.82× is not talk-about-music — it is taste-talk with verdicts in it (placements, exclusions, legitimacy condemnations: the Lonelysoul. exile, the drum-kit ban, all dated 2026-10-04 and holding). The suspicion register at 1.96× is motive-verdicts about people. Both sit under row 1's supported form: categorical judgments on worth and legitimacy, no graded middle. The audit's own summary (big-five-psychometrics.md): "the facets that survive contact with the corpus are the ones about what he attends to — aesthetics (3.82×), patterns (2.39×), other people's motives (1.96×)."

**The starved registers are sub-baseline, not merely lower.** Sympathy tokens run at 0.45× — less than half his interlocutors' rate, in the same medium, against the same within-medium baseline (45 vs 103, 0.42 vs 0.93 per unit). Initiation at 0.73× sits 27% under baseline. These are starvation numbers: the registers are not merely unfavored, they are suppressed relative to the people he is talking to.

**The same 0.45× floor in the within-facet split.** Row 21's audit decomposition of the 1st-percentile Altruism score: instrumental helping 1.79–2.49× vs sympathy tokens 0.45× — the doing-register hot, the feeling-about-feeling register at the identical 0.45× floor. Priced honestly: this is the same datum as row 28's, not a second observation. What it adds is that the floor holds across two framings of the same instrument — the condolence register starves whether the context is others' channels or caregiving.

**The control: brevity capacity is intact.** The delivery thread (texting-deviance-audit.md, the page's own verdict: "the most important row in the table") — 3.2–4.3 words/turn across eleven years, zero 50-word messages ever. Volume is gated, not compulsive. And the floods' vehicle is syntactic: 10.83 vs 5.62 words/sentence in 2026 (1.93×), the 3.05× words/turn premium over interlocutors that same year — the allocation arrives in long sentences, consistent with row 36, deployed by register rather than spread thin.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3 — run first, per protocol)

**The profile-weighted rival: general loquacity / domain enthusiasm ("he just talks a lot about what he likes").** This is a generic talkativeness prior, not one of the §5 shapes — recorded as such rather than forced into a shape. **Discriminator:** loquacity predicts distributed elevation: neutral registers hold at baseline, liked registers rise; global volume should leak across channels. The observed signature breaks all three prongs. (a) The spread is 8.5× between extremes with the cold registers STARVED below baseline (0.45×, 0.73×), not merely lower — a talkative enthusiast fills neutral registers at baseline; these registers are suppressed against his own interlocutors in the same medium. (b) The cut follows content, not liking: both flooded registers are verdict-content (row 1's native output); both starved registers offer no verdict to render. Liking-based allocation has no story for initiation at 0.73× — initiation is structural (opening a channel), not content — while the verdict-content reading predicts exactly that cut. (c) Row 38: eleven years of 3.2–4.3 words/turn with zero long messages in the delivery channel — global loquacity is false on the record; the brevity capacity is intact, so volume is allocated by register and channel, not emitted by talkativeness.

**The contextual-confound rival ("the ratios reflect the rooms, not him").** Faced on the audit's construction: the ratios are computed against within-medium interlocutor baselines — 0.42 vs 0.93 for sympathy tokens, 10.83 vs 5.62 for words/sentence — so the context is controlled by construction. The asymmetry is his.

**The affect-avoidance reading ("he avoids feeling-states").** Dismissed at Stage 2 — an interior claim, invisible to the instruments (§2), leaning on silent rows. The entry makes no claim about felt affect; the floor is a rate (0.45×), not a motive. If a later persistence-capable instrument ever prices the interior side, that reading is welcome to the corpus; it cannot be load-bearing here.

**High-confidence PASS.** Load-bearing rows supported at their stated weights (26@8, 24/12@9, 28@8, 27@8 leans-yes, 1@8.5, 21@8.5, 38@9 — all supported, none silent/inverted/≤4). Labeled caveats: (i) row 27's 0.73× is leans-yes, not proof; (ii) the 8.5× is derived arithmetic from two audited ratios, not itself an audit datum; (iii) row 34's 2.39× was excluded by the weight bar (5.5 < 6) and is unborrowed; (iv) row 25's 1.85× is a sibling pass's territory and was never touched; (v) the row-21 split is the same 0.45× datum as row 28, priced as a parallel, not a replication.

## Falsifier

Three named ways this entry dies. (1) A verdict-content register running sub-baseline in a re-run audit — the allocation cut is the thesis; a verdict register below 1× breaks it. (2) Initiation or sympathy tokens returning to baseline under held conditions — the starvation is the discriminator; baseline kills the rival-beat. (3) The row-38 control failing — a 50+ word message in the delivery thread — which re-opens global-loquacity accounts the entry currently closes. A first full re-audit of the Big-30 ratios with materially different results also retires the 8.5× signature.
