## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3)

Theory-shape: a **general time-deficit** rival — "he just has poor time
management" — which predicts failure across all time domains equally.

**Discriminator:** within the same dated week, do chosen-installed time
instruments fire while imposed time tasks sit pending? A global deficit
predicts both fail. The record shows the split:

- Every chosen install fired: the 06:41 journal runs (4+4 entries), the
  12:00 LOC reports (net +326, +712), the three engine waves, the four
  daily moltbook posts on both days, the Cogar reminders in the index.
- Every imposed tap sat pending: Reason Studios links lapsed unanswered,
  IG login-approval unconfirmed on its third day, Gmail re-sign-in, both
  device/2FA taps, the wiki "push it" — all "waiting on his actions only"
  in the same window.

The split falsifies the deficit shape: a man who cannot keep time does not
have every one of his installed instruments firing on schedule. What varies
is the channel (chosen vs imposed), not the capacity.

**Second rival faced (Shape 6 territory, disclosed and held apart):** system-
building-as-displacement — the crons are monuments, not a clock; they
accumulate without closing any loop. Discriminator: does an instrument
change a subsequent decision of his (closed loop) or accumulate (append-
only)? Conceded leg: several instruments close their loops through Sammy
(she fires the cron, he receives the report). But the loop does close at the
attention level he inhabits: !AUTOEGGIE is HIS install in his own words;
the Oct 30 reminder is installed in his index; the evening IG sessions land
day-4 (Oct 19) and day-5 (Oct 20, 21:10 EDT) posts. The instruments sequence
the days the record shows. The displacement reading is not falsified for
every instrument — it is beaten on the thesis's discriminator, not its own.
