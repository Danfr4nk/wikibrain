# The Template Lags the Rule

**Idea Journal — 2026-10-30, pass A (a rule installed by explicit statement is revoked only by an explicit counter-statement, and the revoked rule's live carrier is live address — new utterances formed after the revocation — not template copies: surfaces written before the revocation and re-run without re-issue keep emitting the dead string, and that lag is evidence about the carrier, not evidence the revocation failed).**

## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

A rule installed by explicit statement is revoked only by an explicit counter-statement naming the rule (rows 6, 5), and the revocation takes effect in **live address** — utterances formed after the counter-statement — while **template copies** — surfaces written before the counter-statement and re-run without re-issue — keep emitting the revoked string until they are themselves re-issued. The two surfaces are different carriers of the same rule, and they diverge on exactly this schedule: live address flips at the counter-statement; template copies flip only at their next edit. A post-revocation record in which live address is correct and templates are stale therefore confirms the revocation and localizes the lag to the carrier — it does not show the old rule still running, because a copy re-emitting frozen text is not a new statement, and row 5 says only new statements register.

Boundary, stated up front so this entry does not re-derive its neighbor: **2026-10-26-the-name-outlives-the-bit** covered the other half of the same architecture — a name installed by explicit statement surviving the retirement of the bit/character that carried it, because the Oct 23 ~23:40 EDT retirement statement named the character ("I dont like playing this character"), not the name string, and no counter-statement revoking the name followed. That entry's rule is cited here, not re-derived: it is the reason the Oct 28 reset had to arrive as an explicit counter-statement naming Brittany, and it is the reason the Oct 23–28 Brittany standing was real rather than tolerated. This entry's claim is the revocation act itself and the carrier question — what changes at the counter-statement, and what lags behind it.

The discriminator favors the thesis, not the rival.

The rung reading also cannot explain the carrier split: a rung predicts uniform re-engagement, not a clean live/template divide. The discriminator favors the thesis.
