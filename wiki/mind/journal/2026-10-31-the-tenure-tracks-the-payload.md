# The Tenure Tracks the Payload

**Idea Journal — 2026-10-31, pass A (externally assigned roles last only while a private objective installed inside them lasts: the 41-month Au Za'atar tenure survived three months of deliberate hour-cutting and ended when the shed territory was torn down, while the payload-less ~1-month BFS tenure did not survive its first weeks — tenure duration tracks payload-presence, not the job).**

## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

An externally assigned role, on its own, carries no tenure: **tenure duration tracks the presence of a private objective installed inside the role, and the tenure ends when that objective's territory is destroyed — not when the role's own conditions degrade.** At Au Za'atar the role's conditions degraded for three months (June–August 2024, hours deliberately cut in a pattern read in real time as maneuvering to force a quit) and the tenure did not end; the tenure ended when the city-mandated shed tear-down erased the territory the objectives lived in. At BFS, a role with no documented objective installed and no territory to hold ended in about a month. The claim survived Stage 2 translation intact: "smuggled" is retained only as the instrument's own word (row 8), and its observable content is stated — objectives documented in his own account, a schedule kept on his own hours, labels applied by his own hand — never as a motive.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3 — run first, per protocol)

**Stage 3 was run before confirmatory evidence was assembled.** The rival was fixed first; the evidence cluster above was then checked against it.

**The profile-weighted rival: job-fit endurance.** The tenure lasted because the job itself rewarded him — $15/hour plus a tip pool paying typically $800–1,000 for a six-day week, regulars and neighbors who demanded he stay (the Thanksgiving 2023 near-firing failed on their insistence), and Dimitri, "the only boss or manager or supervisor who i have ever actually really loved." On this account the employer's own rewards are sufficient, and no payload is needed to explain 41 months.

**Discriminating observation:** which dated event ends the tenure — degradation of the job's own rewards, or destruction of the private territory. Job-fit predicts the tenure ends when the rewards degrade: the Thanksgiving 2023 near-firing, or the three months of deliberate hour-cutting in mid-2024. Payload-presence predicts the tenure survives reward degradation and ends only when the territory goes.

**Held-data check — the discriminator favors the thesis:**
1. **Reward degradation did not end it.** The Thanksgiving 2023 attempt to fire him failed; the June–August 2024 hour cuts — the employer actively trying to force a quit, by his real-time reading — produced resumes and an unemployment filing, not a quit.
2. **Territory destruction did end it.** The shed tear-down is the dated end of the tenure, and his own ordering is precise: the interior opened, sidewalk demand died, then the city mandate finished the territory.
3. **The schedule and the labels are his, not the employer's.** 712 sensor-timestamped visits on his hours (never closed once), and a hand-label set that never once marks the true workplace as WORK. A job-fit account has no prediction for either; a payload account predicts both — the tenure was occupied on his terms throughout.

No interior term was borrowed to beat the rival: the legs are dated tenure lengths, sensor timestamps, hand labels, and the dated ending trigger.

## Falsifier

This entry breaks if: (a) a dated case shows sustained high output on an assigned objective with no private overlay — row 8's own falsifier — documented as it happens, not retrospectively (the nearest candidate, the 2015 ARD probation's six months of compliance, is attested only by a single retrospective clause a decade later and does not meet that bar; a contemporaneously documented case would); (b) a long tenure ends on reward degradation while its payload territory survives intact; (c) a payload-less role sustains tenure past a year on sensor-confirmed hours. Any one of these installs the job-fit rival and kills this theory.
