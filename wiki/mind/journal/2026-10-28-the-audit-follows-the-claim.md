## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

Forensic audit output tracks **claim-form, not speaker-axis**. A proposition that asserts an entitlement ("$50 short, you owe me"), a certainty (an official account, a notarized employment denial), or a verdict (a blame assignment, a drug-source accusation) triggers forensic treatment — primary-record demand, procedural-tell check, published resolution — on the same short clock regardless of who utters it: assistant manager, institution, or the decade bond. Access and provision arriving through an existing channel trigger no audit output before acceptance: no lease demanded from a housing granter, no paper sought behind a family transfer, no audit run on a favour under review. The scope of Trust 9 is therefore **propositional, not personal**: what gets audited is a claim to be settled, and what gets accepted is a grant to be received.

This is not the provision-denomination claim (what form provision takes, or what rank it encodes), and it is not the audit-timing claim (laterals audited later). Timing is part of the evidence against those readings, not the thesis: a lateral-origin claim audited within days breaks any clock that prices laterals as deferred, and a vertical-origin grant accepted without paper would break any axis that prices verticals as always-audited — the second half is the open edge, stated in the Falsifier rather than claimed.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3 — faced before confirmatory evidence)

The rival check was run before the evidence cluster was assembled; the discriminator below was named first, and the cluster was then gathered to test it, not to confirm the thesis.

**Rival 1 — §5 Shape 3's profile-weighted rival: single-channel re-loading (row 11, Gini 0.961).** On this rival, unpapered acceptance is just concentration: with no failover, whatever the chosen channel offers passes, because there is nowhere else to go — and audit output, where it appears, is a property of the channel's failure, not of the input's form. **Discriminating observation (named before evidence-gathering):** a propositional claim arriving *through* the highest-concentration channel itself. Concentration predicts it passes unaudited; claim-form predicts it gets the full apparatus. **Result: the discriminator favors the thesis.** The July 25–26, 2026 Annie-origin drug-source claim — arriving through the decade bond, the record's heaviest channel — got two corpus-built dashboards and delivered URLs within days, the same clock as the BFS manager's claim. A channel theory cannot explain a channel's own claim being audited immediately, while a claim-form theory predicts exactly that.

**Rival 2 — the speaker-axis / deferred-clock reading (verticals audited now, laterals audited later).** **Discriminating observation (named before evidence-gathering):** audit latency by speaker. The axis-clock predicts vertical claims fast and lateral claims slow. **Result: the discriminator favors the thesis.** BFS (vertical) and July 2026 (lateral) both resolve in days; the Tom model revision after the mid-May 2026 supply failure is a retrospective audit of a *failure event*, not of a proposition, and sits on a different clock for a different input-form. Latency tracks what arrived — a claim to settle vs a failure to absorb — not who sent it.

**Why the thesis is not a restatement of row 12.** Row 12 scopes distrust to vertical claims; the thesis prices a finer variable inside that scope and predicts cases row 12 does not: lateral claims audited immediately (July 2026) and grants accepted without audit in any direction. The July dashboards are the observation row 12's axis wording leaves unpredicted.

## Falsifier

1. A propositional claim accepted on authority alone, in writing, unexamined — from any speaker, vertical or lateral — with the acceptance documented rather than the claim tested. (Row 12's own falsifier, extended across the axis.)
2. A grant or provision through an existing channel subjected to forensic audit *before* acceptance — for example, a lease demanded from a housing granter before moving in, or a family transfer audited against records before it is accepted — documented in the record.
3. A lateral-origin propositional claim left unaudited for years while a same-form vertical claim is audited in days, with no difference in testability between the two. Any one of the three breaks the claim-form rule as stated.
