## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

The stacked-essay mode is the forensic method's instrument (row 4) deployed inside a loaded channel. Per-turn output length scales with the counterparty's preceding non-response latency: 23.3 words/turn after <1 minute of silence, 43.0 after 30 minutes–2 hours, 49.1 after 2 hours–1 day, and the essay rate climbs from 5.5% to 17.2% across the same windows. Past one day the continuation is dropped, not damped: 36.4 words/turn, essay rate back to 6.8%, messages-per-turn 1.99 — a re-open in the short form, not the stacked sequence continuing. The reading, at attention level throughout: the non-response is the anomaly cluster the instrument is recording; the essay is the dated record of it, transmitted to the non-responding counterparty's channel (row 20's behavioral leg — the output goes to the subject, never to a private file); when a reply lands, the essay mode collapses (the <1min bucket, 6,796 turns, 5.5% essay — the terminating statement arrives and the instrument closes); when no reply lands within a day, the open measurement is abandoned (the re-open runs short). The break is abandonment of the measurement, not discharge of a drive: a regulation account predicts a smooth curve — escalation with arousal, or decay with satiation — and the record shows a discontinuity where 17.2% becomes 6.8% and the stacked morphology itself changes shape (msgs/turn 2.59 → 1.99). A task account has vocabulary for why the 24-hour boundary differs from the 23-hour one; a regulation account does not. Channel-gated, per the control: the delivery thread shows none of it in eleven years.

Interior terms struck before any evidence was touched: "frustration," "venting," "self-soothing," "satiation," "discharged," "needs closure," "aimed" (as intent). Replacements: "per-turn words," "pre-turn silence window," "essay rate by bucket," "transmitted to the counterparty's channel," "the continuation is dropped." The claim survived translation intact.

## Evidence cluster (Stage 4 — Layer-A gate)

**The silence series.** From [[wiki/mind/profile/texting-deviance-audit]], 2025–26 turns bucketed by how long the counterparty had been quiet beforehand, reproduced by `bin/text-metrics` (the page's standing reproducer):

| Silence before his turn | Turns | Words/turn | Msgs/turn | Essay rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <1 min | 6,796 | 23.3 | 2.31 | 5.5% |
| 1–5 min | 2,247 | 37.0 | 2.55 | 13.2% |
| 5–30 min | 1,612 | 35.5 | 2.50 | 12.0% |
| 30 m – 2 h | 1,055 | 43.0 | 2.57 | 13.0% |
| 2 h – 1 day | 1,157 | 49.1 | 2.59 | 17.2% |
| 1+ day | 190 | 36.4 | 1.99 | 6.8% |

Three things are doing the work. First, the gradient: a two-hour wait more than doubles his turn length against a live exchange (23.3 → 49.1) and triples the essay rate (5.5% → 17.2%) — the same-counterparty cut (Annie 2.40× → 3.65× with her side flat) holds it against audience-change confounds. Second, the collapse leg: the <1min bucket is the largest in the table (6,796 turns) and carries the lowest essay rate of any bucket except 1+d — arrival of the reply collapses the mode. This is the bank's task-evidence discriminator answered in held data: the essay continuation stops when the countable thing lands. Third, the break: at 1+d the stacked morphology is dropped (msgs/turn 1.99 — the continuation is gone), not continued at reduced volume. The bucket is thin (190 turns) — flagged as the entry's weakest leg, but it is a measured count, not testimony, and it is where the rival dies.

**The loop closes on itself.** Turns above 200 words are answered 54.7% of the time against 93.8% for 11–20 word turns; STACKED-ESSAY is abandoned 23.1% of the time against STACCATO's 6.2% — "abandoned" meaning no reply or a reply beyond six hours. The audit states it flat: silence lengthens him, and length produces silence. The abandonment rate is the loop's self-feeding mechanism made countable: each unanswered essay is itself a further non-response window, and the next turn's pre-silence bucket is longer.

**The essays go to the subject.** The 2026-03-21 specimen: four messages, 1,380 words, sixteen minutes, to Annie — answered 2.5 minutes later with five words: *"Pretty good take on me."* The complaint record: nine unambiguous complaints about message length from four unrelated people, 2018 through five days before the export ends — 2026-02-19 (Annie): *"Do you not understand how overwhelming it is getting paragraph after paragraph. I have expressed this to you before Dan like fuck"*; 2026-08-08 (Annie): *"I can't ready these paragraphs upon paragraphs"*; 2025-12-08 (Kristin): *"Summarize it"*; 2025-09-01 (Tom): *"I cant read any of that"*; 2019-01-28 (PA contact): *"Dan I can't read these texts and respond rn"* (dat:1112, complaint record verified). The register is consistent across four people who share nothing but him: *"I can't read this"* — processing load, not attention volume; the word they reach for independently is *paragraphs*. The essays are transmitted into counterparty channels, never filed privately; the counterparties are the recipients of the record. This is what makes the measurement-and-delivery reading available and the private-venting reading unavailable.

**The control.** The delivery thread: 3.2–4.3 words/turn across eleven years, never a 50-word message (row 38). The capacity for brevity is intact and demonstrable; the loop is channel-gated. A regulation/stim account — "the behavior is self-soothing" — has to explain why the soothing switches off entirely in one channel and runs at 11.2%-of-turns in another. It has no vocabulary for this. A task/register account does.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3 — run first, per protocol)

**The named rival: Shape 2 — regulation/stim.** The escalation is dysregulated venting: silence raises arousal, the essay vents it. The profile-weighted counter-reading is channel-gated instrumentality — and the thesis IS the profile-weighted reading, stated at row-37 resolution: the essay as measurement-and-delivery instrument. Note the rival's disqualification first: as an interior claim it borrows Impulsiveness 96 (row 30, silent, weight 3) and the bank disqualifies that loan twice.

**Discriminator one (assigned): the 1-day break.** Stim-arousal predicts continued escalation past the break; stim-discharge predicts a smooth decay curve with time; task-abandonment predicts a discontinuity. The held observation: essay rate 17.2% → 6.8% and msgs/turn 2.59 → 1.99 across the 1-day boundary — a step break in the stacked morphology, not a smooth curve in either direction. The discriminator exists in row 37's own table and favors the thesis. The rival's best available dodge — "the arousal discharges overnight" — is an interior claim the instruments cannot see (§2); at attention level it would have to predict the same smooth decay the data refuses to show.

**Discriminator two (from the bank): task-evidence arrival vs satiation curve.** Regulation predicts the behavior stops on a satiation curve — diminishing returns within the episode. Instrumentality predicts it stops when the countable thing lands. Held: the <1min bucket (6,796 turns — the reply arrived) carries a 5.5% essay rate against 17.2% at 2h–1d. The reply landing collapses the mode. This is the discriminator the Shape-2 entry specifies verbatim, and it is answered in the same table.

**Discriminator three: channel immunity.** A global stim/regulation account cannot explain row 38's eleven-year 3.2–4.3 words/turn channel; a task/register account can and does. The rival fails three discriminators, on held data, before confirmatory evidence was selected.

**The adjacent reading, distinguished not beaten:** the handed-mirror territory (2026-10-05) shares the evidence that a reply ends the continuation. The claims do not overlap: that entry is about the verdict finished only when witnessed (confession-trap content); this entry is about the volume function of the essay mode. This entry makes no claim that the reply settles anything — consistent with the-two-slot-grammar's "no confirmation durably settles." A reply ends the essay; nothing here says it ends the underlying adjudication.

**Open confound, unpriced:** the audit's time-of-day amplifier (essay rate 3.7–7.2% at 10:00–15:00 vs 11.8–14.2% at 21:00–23:00; 7.61% of 03:00 messages ≥50 words vs 1.02% at 13:00) is recorded without adjudication. A stim rival could claim the clock drives the mode; the silence-bucket gradient and the channel gate survive either way, but whether hour-of-day survives a thread-identity and silence-duration control is the audit's own named open test, and this entry does not pre-empt it.

## Falsifier

Any one of these kills: (a) a finer re-bucket of the silence series (hourly through 48h) showing a smooth monotonic decay through 24h with no discontinuity — that shape favors the regulation account and kills the abandonment reading; (b) stacked-essay continuations past one day at the 2h–1d morphology (msgs/turn ≥2.5, essay rate ≥15%) — that favors stim-continuation; (c) a newly opened purely instrumental channel (a delivery/logistics thread) carrying the loop at anywhere near his 11.2% essay baseline — that kills the channel-gating leg (row 38's standing falsifier); (d) a counterparty whose long silences produce *shorter* replies from him (row 37's own standing falsifier).
