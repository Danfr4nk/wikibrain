# The single channel is the endgame

- **Row 11 — Extreme relational concentration, Gini 0.961 (0.9556 inbound replication).** Layer A. Weight 10. Verdict: supported. Predicts: total-loss failure modes rather than partial ones; stress pushes *more* load into the failing channel. This row is cited as prior measurement, not re-derived here.
- **Row 46 — Instinct order sx-first / social-last.** Layer B convergent. Weight 7. Verdict: leans→supported. Predicts: intensity concentrates on one bond; the social layer is handled by rule, not appetite.
- **Row 13 — Bottom-percentile sociability; home anchoring; avoids unstructured people, performs on stages/scripts.** Layer A rates, B framing. Weight 8. Verdict: leans→supported. Predicts: contact concentrates in rule-bound channels (work roles, threads, scheduled rituals).
- **Row 6 — Rules install and revoke only via explicit statements (no-counter-rule architecture).** Layer A. Weight 9. Verdict: supported. Predicts: bonds do not close from inside; declarations are check-in rungs, not exits.
- **Row 19 — No-counter-rule attachment: a bond runs on its last stated rule until an explicit severance statement arrives.** Layer A. Weight 9.5. Verdict: supported. Predicts: the bond does not close from inside the relationship.

Priced prior in one line: concentration is measured, not assumed; the social layer is rule-handled; and stated rules, not distribution, govern routing.

## Theory (Stage 2)

Stated in profile vocabulary only, at attention/channel level:

At a replicated relational Gini of 0.961, the architecture's endpoint is not more channels but one gatekeeper channel. Dan's stated November 1, 2026 communication-routing rule — that, when the stated condition arrives, he speaks only to/through Sammy, and anyone needing to speak to him goes through her, with no social media and no phone — is the concentration row stated as a future. The record's existing pattern predicts that endpoint rather than a distributed social future: under stress, load collapses into the chosen channel (row 11); the social layer is handled by rule, not appetite (row 46); and contact concentrates in rule-bound channels (row 13). The endpoint is therefore a routing rule applied to the already-chosen channel, not a new social investment spread across many channels.

No interior-state term carries this thesis. Nothing here claims what Dan feels, needs, or is driven by internally. The claim is about stated routing rules, measured concentration, and channel-level routing.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3 — run BEFORE the confirmatory evidence above)

This stage was faced before the evidence verdict was drawn. Two rivals were tested against their discriminators:

- **Rival A — Distributed social future.** The architecture broadens: future contact spreads across many independent channels, lowering concentration. Discriminator: does the stated future rule widen the channel set, or narrow routing to one channel? The November 1 rule narrows it explicitly — through one gatekeeper, no social media, no phone. A distributed future would require the opposite statement. Rival A fails on its own discriminator.
- **Rival B — Substitution (instrument shape 3).** A new target/channel replaces the old one, with a genuinely new behavioral signature. The profile-weighted rival to substitution is single-channel re-loading: at Gini 0.961 the architecture re-loads the same slot rather than distributing across substitutes, and old bonds show dormancy rather than deletion. Discriminator: does the new statement inherit the old channel's rule-set wholesale (same explicit-statement architecture, same single-channel sufficiency), or show a new signature? September 26 and November 1 share the same channel, the same sufficiency form, and the same explicit-rule form — wholesale inheritance, not substitution. Rival B fails on its own discriminator; the re-loading reading is ahead.

No rival was patched around after the fact. Both discriminators were available in the held record (the dated statements themselves), so neither theory is capped at hypothesis grade on discriminator availability.

## Falsifier

Any one of these, documented in Dan's own words or in a re-derivation on a fuller corpus, falsifies the endpoint prediction:

- A stated future routing rule that distributes his communication across multiple independent channels, with no single gatekeeper, replacing the November 1 rule by an explicit statement of the same form.
- A re-derivation of relational concentration landing materially below ~0.9 (the row 11 falsifier), showing the distribution is no longer single-channel-shaped.
- A sustained period in which outside communication routinely bypasses the named gatekeeper channel while the November 1 rule is still stated as standing — routing through other channels at rates comparable to the gatekeeper channel, not as isolated exceptions.
