## Theory (Stage 2)

Stated in profile vocabulary, attention-level only — no interior terms survive:

In the selector domain, output returns as a **step, not a ramp, after a dated silence**, and no level-output period is recorded in the held record at all. Observable form:

- **The silence is dated and multi-year.** No titled monthly playlist entries for 2021–2024, against a monthly series running 2011–2020; lifetime alias mention counts of 3 (GRIPNOTIC), 8 (MOGZART), 1 (SLOPPP) on the held corpus.
- **The return is a dated step, not a gradual resumption.** The 2025 MASTER CRATE's first wave alone is 170 of its 232 tracks on 2025-10-31; November 2025 alone carries 628 likes against 88 across the whole of 2017–2023. A ramp would show monthly counts climbing steadily through 2025; the record shows a flat line and then a step.
- **The imposed-schedule block and the burst block are separated on the imposed-vs-chosen axis (row 15).** The silent block coincides with the 41-month imposed shift schedule (March 2021–August 2024, six-day weeks, ~690 shifts); the burst block (October–December 2025, March–April 2026) carries no imposed schedule in the record. The claim is sequence on that axis, not causation — the datum itself warns causation is unproven (see Evidence).
- **The "endured, not inhabited" half of row 16 is unpriced and is not claimed.** The record shows absence of titled curatorial output during the schedule block; it records no verdict from him about that block's output as endured or satisfying. Absence of output is not a verdict about output. The falsifier for that half is named below.

## Rivals & discriminators (Stage 3)

Theory-shape: the live thesis is a **distributional** claim (step-after-silence), so the rivals are the two mechanisms that would produce the same shape for other reasons. Both were faced before confirmatory evidence was laid; the discriminators were stated first.

**Profile-weighted rival (a) — ordinary deadline pressure:** bursts track deadlines, not schedule states. Discriminator, stated before the evidence: a burst whose wave dates carry a named deadline in the held record (rival wins) versus bursts with no deadline named for any wave date (thesis leans). Record: neither the crate datum nor the alias synthesis names a deadline for 2025-10-31, 2025-11-21, 2025-12-09, 2025-12-22, 2026-03-04, or 2026-04-06; the crate waves are described as selector restocking in four waves, not as deadline pile-up. **Verdict on this rival: partially beaten, honestly capped.** Absence of a named deadline in two syntheses is not an affirmative no-deadline record — a deadline outside the held record would revive this rival untouched. This partial facing is the main reason the entry caps at hypothesis grade.

**Profile-weighted rival (b) — procrastination/avoidance misread:** the bursts are displacement of due output, and row 16 misreads avoidance as rhythm. Discriminator, stated before the evidence: due output in the same domain being dodged while the burst fires elsewhere (rival wins) versus a domain showing no due output at all during the silence, with the burst output being the domain's own output (thesis wins). Record: during the silence there was no due curatorial output to dodge — no titled playlists 2021–2024, lifetime alias counts of 3/8/1 — and the burst output IS the domain's output (crate, likes, alias circulation), not displacement into an unrelated domain. **Verdict on this rival: beaten on held discriminators.** The avoidance reading would also predict output clustering just before deadlines; the crate waves span seven weeks with no deadline in record.

## Confidence & gate verdict (Stage 5)

**HYPOTHESIS GRADE, leaning supported.**

- Layer-A legs exist and are cited by datum: the crate build waves and like velocity (`dat-music-2025-crate-filter-stack`), the re-verified alias counts (192,140-row check in `dat:1381`), the dated schedule block ([[wiki/work/au-zaatar-41-months]]).
- The avoidance rival is beaten on held discriminators; the deadline rival is only partially beaten (absence of a named deadline, not an affirmative no-deadline record).
- The central row (16) is **leans, not supported**, and is used at leans weight — the entry does not borrow supported-grade confidence from it.
- Two legs are explicitly unpriced, not hidden: (1) the "endured, not inhabited" half of row 16 — no verdict from him about any level-output period is recorded, so neither "endured" nor "satisfying" is claimed; (2) the public-archive silence leg (2016–2026, 908 originals) is page testimony on an incomplete archive, not Layer A.
- The schedule-collapse causal reading is not carried: the burst follows the August 2024 schedule end by ~14 months (October 2025), which breaks any immediacy the collapse-release mechanism needs. That failed mechanism is preserved in Genesis, not patched into the live thesis.

## Falsifier

- A dated level-output period in the selector domain surfacing — e.g., a complete titled monthly playlist series through 2021–2024 — kills the silence leg.
- A named deadline attached to any crate wave date or alias-push date that accounts for the wave timing — revives the deadline rival and kills the step-return reading.
- A ramp in place of the step — e.g., monthly like-counts rising steadily through 2025 rather than the flat-then-November-spike shape — kills the step claim.
- A verdict in his own words recording a level scheduled output period as satisfying — kills the row-16 "endured" leg's last refuge and forces the row itself to be repriced.
- A complete X export closing the 2016–2026 public gap with regular original music posts — kills the public-silence suggestion (the held-corpus counts would stand, the public leg would fall).
