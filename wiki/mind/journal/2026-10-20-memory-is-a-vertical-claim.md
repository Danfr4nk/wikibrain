## Theory (Stage 2 — profile-vocabulary statement)

The forensic method (row 4: primary records over narrative) does not stop at external vertical claims (row 12's ordinary targets). The dated record shows it running **inward**: his own stated recollection is treated as the untrusted narrator, and the dated artifact is the primary record that settles the conflict. The observable shape is not reminiscence — it is **adjudication**: dated sessions in which he retrieves his own historical artifacts, states a recollection or hypothesis about what they are, and the written record afterward carries the artifact's read, not his stated version. Two dated memory-vs-record conflicts, eight years apart, show the same outcome: the record won, the correction was written down, the latency was short (~3 hours in 2018; same-day in 2026). The Oct 19, 2026 archive-dive window (~00:05–05:43 EDT: Reason Studios resets, ishLab Gmail archaeology, dada-era crack scripts, iMessage kill-switch read) is the method running on himself in the present — and the kill-switch leg is the dive in which his own stated hypothesis was the thing that lost.
