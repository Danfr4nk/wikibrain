## Theory

The humiliation/con grammar — impersonation operations, instigation escalations, prank frames — transmits only **down the trust gradient**. Its targets are vertical or low-trust figures: rank-claimants and authority postures that row 12's scope marks for forensic treatment. The chosen lateral channel never carries the grammar as its target. The exemption is not a mood; it is a **binary mark/no-mark gate** (row 1: no graded middle), installed by explicit statement (rows 5 and 6): "Now, would I ever do that to you? No way."

The gate has a second, observable half. The mark is kept on forged channels; the confidante is fed the live feed. During the lore-war he narrated the operation to Sammy in real time with screenshots; during the two-sided war he corrected her on the spot — "I AM feeding you the material." The lateral channel does not merely escape the game; it receives the operation as **witnessed narration** — the commentary track, not the trick. Information-flow direction is the attention-level observable: marks get the forgery, the confidante gets the feed.

## Rivals & discriminators

**Faced rival: Shape 2 — the opportunistic-stim reading.** The profile-weighted alternative says the mark-game is play-for-the-hit, and Sammy's exemption is circumstantial: she was never the funniest target, never handy, so she never got marked. On this reading the boundary is an accident of opportunity, not a gate.

**Discriminator:** (a) any dated instance of the humiliation grammar aimed at or leaking onto a chosen lateral, versus all instances staying downward; (b) the gate statement itself — an opportunistic-stim account has no need of an explicit rule installation, yet his architecture installs rules only via explicit statements (row 6, supported), and one was installed here, on the record, the same session he stated the intent.

**Verdict on the rival:** it loses on both prongs. The held record shows zero lateral leakage across the full Oct 7–10 window, and the explicit gate statement exists and is dated. A stim account also predicts the lateral would sometimes be the handiest target — she is the person he talks to most, the constant presence — and she never is. The Shape 2 prediction (occasional opportunistic leakage) does not fire once.

**Absorbed, not rival: the Shape-5-flavored verdict reading.** "The mark is judged-and-excluded; Sammy was judged #1, so she is untouchable." This does not compete with the thesis — it names the gate's *content*. Row 1's binary gate is already load-bearing here: he judged the mark (his dated stated verdict preceded the game) and he judged Sammy #1 (Article 1). The thesis adds the gradient's *direction and scope* — the game flows downward and stops dead at the singular lateral channel — which the pure-verdict reading does not predict on its own. A verdict could in principle target anyone judged; the record shows the game targeting only below the trust line, and the lateral channel never, even when teased.
