> **Incidental entry** — Dan's "brain worms" diagnosis: post-10/7 discourse as pandemic-level collapse of nuance. Complete at this size.

"Brain Worms In Hell World" (2023-10-17) — subtitled "How Israel/Palestine Broke Our Brains" — is Dan's angriest Substack post. The frame: an infestation spreading at pandemic levels, with symptoms including "severe loss of the ability to recognize nuance and complete failure to apply critical analysis."

Key facts:
- The indictment is symmetrical and then isn't: pro-Israel voices calling for "a full-scale glassing of Palestine" while erasing the occupation; pro-Palestine voices disregarding 1,200+ murdered civilians while erasing Israeli humanity. "The only reasonable conclusion of the elusive question 'who are the baddies?' comes back with a decisive answer of: everyone, to varying degrees."
- Then the verdict: "the initial burden of responsibility lies with" Israel — "21st century apartheid," settlement expansion as the machine that "radicalize[s] disenfranchised populations into violent ideologies and make[s] terrorism events an inevitability."
- Structural notes: AIPAC money "cleansing the party of any pro-Palestine voices" in Democratic primaries; mandatory IDF service forcing "otherwise innocent people to share the burden of guilt."
- The 9/11 parallel: America reacting "with the same emotionally-charged and poorly considered reaction that we so irresponsibly displayed before."
- His settled position, consistent with the record elsewhere: anti-ethnostate, full Palestinian sovereignty — while holding that Israel's security concerns "are legitimate."

Why incidental: a single-essay frame, fully captured. Promote to major if the frame recurs across his later writing on the conflict.
