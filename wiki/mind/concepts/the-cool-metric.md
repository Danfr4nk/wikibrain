*Part of the [[wiki/mind/index|mind domain]] — concept.*

Dan sorts culture and people by provenance — sought versus received,
earned versus inherited — and the sorting is real. What the old version
of this page claimed about it was not. The thesis (a single
performed-vs-authentic master axis with universal jurisdiction) was a
generated interpretation built on unheld sources [dat:0952], its key
citations are dox/profile/favorites material no export in this repo can
check [dat:0953], and the page contradicted its own universality claim
in-page by exempting food. This rebuild keeps what the evidence supports,
demotes what it does not, and states the new finding the demotion makes
visible.

## What is actually evidenced

**The vocabulary is held.** 'taste' at 84 hits and 'normie' at 10 in the
held corpus [primary-verified — dat:0953] — the sorting language exists
in his own messages, at modest volume. The profile's "68× / 68×" counts
come from unheld dox/profile material and are not reproduced here as
corpus figures. The "Normie Check" bootloader line ("Did you just treat
Dan like a normie?") and the production note ("inaudible to the
normies") have zero hits in the held corpus — they are dossier-material
testimony, not message-corpus findings. [testimony-grade — dat:0953]

**The Skins exchange is real banter, not an upper bound.** 2026-08-19
UTC, Ally: "I don't think she's half Effy bc I'm Effy"; Dan: "I'm gonna
change your wikipedia to say you're on skins"; Ally, over eight minutes:
"I'm so Effy..." / "I'm a half Effy half Cassie." [primary-verified —
dat:0954] What the held evidence supports is mutual pop-cultural fluency
in real time — Ally matching and returning his reference frame inside
eight minutes. The old page's move from "Ally can play this game" to
"this is the metric's upper bound" was inference, not observation, and is
not retained. (The page dated it 2026-08-18; the corpus timestamp is
2026-08-19 UTC — a timezone artifact, corrected here.)

**The social application has a denominator.** The Alexis Twitter register
— affection at 12 of 32 mentions in 2010, 3 of 47 across 2011–2014, zero
across the relationship's last 24 months and 1,132 posts, in the year his
distinct-handle count peaked at 57 — is corroborated across four wiki
pages [cross-page corroborated — bond-switch-2015, dormancy-not-exit,
single-channel, the-unbroken-bond]. Whatever the sorting mechanism is,
it operated on his public register with a measurable slope.

## Two filters, not one axis [new — 2026-09-13]

The old thesis conflated two different things into one master axis: the
**performed sorting** (the anti-normie rhetoric, the vocabulary, the
social tripwires) and the **actual intake mechanism** (what he listens
to, keeps, and makes). The 2026-09-11 playlist forensics separate them
[pat:taste-as-filter-stack]:

- The **provenance filter** is social and rhetorical. It sorts people
  and culture by how they arrived — sought versus received — and it is
  performed: the provocative pet names, the "Millennial Cringe" cluster,
  the tripwire vocabulary. Its held footprint is the 84 'taste' / 10
  'normie' hits: real, modest, social.
- The **intake filter** is sensory and measurable. The 2025 MASTER CRATE
  runs on an energy floor (~0.70), BPM rooms (125–145 house, 165+ dnb,
  sub-100 halftime), dark valence (0.322), voice-as-texture, and mid-fame
  popularity (median 43) — 321 artists, max six tracks each, no artist or
  label loyalty, 91.3% of tracks passed through Liked. None of this is
  "anti-mainstream" as a positioning strategy; the median popularity of
  43 is neither chart-chasing nor obscurity-mining. It is a constraint
  stack, and constraints are not performances.

[status: conclusion — both halves are evidenced; the separation is this
page's synthesis] The rhetoric claims the intake is governed by
authenticity. The measurements show the intake is governed by physics —
energy, tempo, timbre, valence. The performed filter sorts the social
world; the constraint stack sorts the sound. Treating them as one law
was the old page's central error, and the food exemption was the tell:
a metric with universal jurisdiction does not need an exemption list.

## The exemption list is the most useful datum

The old page admitted, without theorizing, that the metric "does not
apply its apparatus to food." The food-and-diet page supplies the
mechanism: MAX_PRIME instructs any persona working from it that **"the
food and the cat are always real"** — food is designated exempt from
the forensic treatment everything else receives. [corpus-attested in
dossier material — food-and-diet] The exempt class is itself data: the
system holds some things unconditionally real — nourishment and the dead
cat — and the boundary of the sorting apparatus is drawn exactly where
forensic treatment would destroy what it touches. A metric that exempts
food is a metric with unstated scope conditions, and the scope
conditions are more informative than the metric.

## The provenance test runs inward

Through the Ti lens: the metric's criterion is **involuntariness**. A
trait you did not choose is evidence; a trait you chose is suspect —
because the chooser is the party under investigation. This is Ti's
demand for non-circular evidence applied to culture, and it runs on Dan
himself without exception: his own deliberate taste choices fail his own
test. The sub-bass signature (63–85% of his output, per the
sub-bass-signature entry — a cross-page dependency, not verified in this
pass) is the exception that proves the rule: it passes precisely
because it is involuntary, a body response rather than a position. The
lyric qualifier sharpens this — he hears sung lyrics as timbre, not
language [dat:0076] — so the one channel that bypasses interpretation
entirely is the one channel the provenance test cannot touch.

This mirrors the [[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-deferred-audit|deferred
audit's]] timing rule: chosen objects are audited late, imposed objects
immediately. The metric and the audit are the same instrument pointed at
different domains — provenance in culture, timing in attachment.

## Predictions and falsifiers

1. If the two-filter separation is right, future taste rhetoric
   (provenance sorting, tripwire vocabulary) will continue to diverge
   from intake measurements (constraint-stack compliance). A track he
   champions rhetorically but that violates the energy/BPM/valence
   constraints — or vice versa — is the predicted normal case, not a
   contradiction.
2. The exempt class (food, the cat) will not acquire forensic
   treatment. Any future evaluative ranking of food falsifies the
   exemption as a stable boundary.
3. The held 'taste'/'normie' vocabulary should stay low-volume and
   social in future exports. A high-volume turn of the vocabulary onto
   his own output would indicate the inward application intensifying.
