# Autism

Dan identifies as autistic. No clinical assessment, no diagnostic date, no
formal evaluation anywhere in the record. This page is the **concept** — what
"autism" names and does inside Dan's cognitive architecture. For where the
claim comes from and how far the evidence actually reaches, see
[[wiki/mind/profile/neurodivergence]], which holds the full sourcing and the
open questions. Nothing on this page upgrades that sourcing, and no
conclusion here should be read as a clinical diagnosis — there is none on
record, and the wiki does not supply one.

## The concept in his own framing

The corpus's interpretive stance is stated as a section header in
`THE_DAN_FRANK_MANUAL.md`: **"Autism as processing layer, not as label."**
The claim is infrastructural, not medical — a description of how input gets
processed, not a diagnosis of what is wrong. `operating_manual.md` gives the
archetype form: **"The 'Autistic Truth Seeker' in the Basement.
Prophet-level insight throttled by dysregulated drive states."**

That framing is what this page holds fixed: autism here is a name for a
processing architecture with observable consequences across attachment,
language, social navigation, and aesthetic perception. Whether it is the
*correct* name for that architecture — as opposed to some other explanation
for the same measured shape — is a question the corpus has no instrument
capable of answering, and this page does not pretend otherwise.

## What the concept explains

### Explicit over inferred meaning

The single most load-bearing trait in the cluster. Dan processes explicit,
rule-like statements; inferred, ambiguous, or socially-implied meaning does
not land. The lyric qualifier is the purest demonstration: he hears sung
lyrics as **timbre, not language** — does not process words as semantic
content even with a lyric sheet in front of him, and has parsed only about
three songs in his life as conceptual message. Song lyrics are
inferential and ambiguous, so they pass through the perceptual system as
sound. The same filter governs relationships: the explicit-verbal-commitment
architecture finds positive verbal expressions functioning as **absolute
rules until an explicit severance signal arrives**, with no counter-rule
admitted from behavior. Measured across 106,629 sent messages: "call me"
170 times versus "do you love me" zero times — he asks for channel, not
compliments, because a channel request is explicit and a compliment is
inference-laden. Trust at the 9th percentile and Self-Consciousness at the
91st mean confirmations decay fast and must be re-stated, not re-inferred.

### Rule-based attachment

Dan states the mechanism in his own voice and attributes it to autism:
*"My brain can't cross out that part of my life that I love unless I know
that you don't feel that way"* — the tie persists because the terminating
statement is never made. The Annie case is the concept's load-bearing
demonstration: 129 of his severance declarations, 100% re-engagement, median
gap 36 seconds; zero severance signals in 41,073 of her messages. The
attachment system does not read behavioral evidence as a rule-change; only a
stated severance counts. See [[wiki/mind/concepts/attachment-model]] for the
full model.

### Language as processing tool

A 23,286-word casual-texting vocabulary and a 2,465-instance "because"
count — language used to **think** rather than to relate. The deviance
audit's hyper-specific linguistic style outlier (97/100) is the same pattern
the corpus's own autism framing describes as reaching for words as a
processing instrument, not a communication instrument. This is the trait
that makes the wiki itself possible: the archive is the processing
externalized.

### Social navigation by explicit rules

Bottom-percentile sociability, extreme social aversion (90/100 deviance
outlier), a stated preference for explicit over inferred meaning — the same
profile the INTP read names Ti-dominance and weak Fe, and the same shape
[[wiki/mind/concepts/social-anxiety]] treats from the felt side. Dan moves
through social space the way he moves through everything else: by rules that
are stated, not by cues that are implied. When the rules are absent or
contradictory, the system stalls rather than improvises.

### The autism-amplifier in moral accounting

The concept does practical work in the wiki's verdicts, in both directions.
The fallout verdict records an **autism-amplifier** factor: Annie knew his
processing architecture — knew that severance without an explicit statement
does not register, knew that denials issued alongside continued contact read
as rules still in force — which raised the moral weight of running a
defamation campaign in parallel with private written denials. In the other
direction, Dan himself deploys the label as explanation: *"someone with
autism who isn't capable of just getting over someone that they loved and
spent a decade with."* Both uses are recorded as claims about the
architecture's consequences, not as diagnostic facts.

## What Dan himself says about it

The clearest primary-source statement is a question, not a description. In
`Breaking the anxiety avoidance cycle.md`, Dan asks whether the label is
doing real work or excusing him from ordinary standards:

> "is it a pathology or is it legitimate to say that the advice and
> perspective most people would need to receive is not applicable to someone
> as edge case autist wei[r]do as I am"

The self-identification is load-bearing enough that he uses it to reason
about which advice applies to him, while simultaneously being uncertain, in
his own words, whether that move is legitimate self-knowledge or a
rationalization. No page in the corpus resolves that tension, and this one
does not either — it is recorded as an open question in his own voice.

His other attributions to the label — the no-exit mechanism, the
perception-framing claims across the Annie record — are recorded throughout
the corpus as **his own framing**, which the wiki preserves without adopting
as diagnosis.

## Convergence is not confirmation

Four instruments converge on the same measured shape without being designed
to test for autism and without citing the autism claim or each other:
[[wiki/mind/profile/intp]]'s Ti-dominant, Fe-weak read; the deviance
audit's 97/100 linguistic-style and 90/100 social-aversion outliers
([[wiki/mind/profile/deviance-mapping]]); the Big Five facet pattern; and
the stylometric profile's vocabulary and "because"-count findings
([[wiki/mind/profile/linguistic-profile]]). That convergence is weak
evidence that the underlying trait is real and measurable. It is **not**
evidence that "autism" specifically, as opposed to some other explanation
for the same shape, is the correct name for it. The corpus has no instrument
capable of distinguishing between those two possibilities, and the wiki
holds that limit open rather than filling it.

## What remains unassessed

No clinical or diagnostic record exists anywhere in `raw/`. There is no date
of self-identification, no account of what prompted it, and no mention of a
formal evaluation ever being sought. No sensory, motor, or
childhood-developmental evidence has been located. No comorbidity discussion
(ADHD is not raised anywhere in the sources). Whether the label carries any
practical consequence — disclosure, accommodation, treatment-seeking — is
undocumented; the corpus frames it purely as an interpretive lens on his own
cognition, never as something that changed a decision. The full gap list
lives at [[wiki/mind/profile/neurodivergence]].
