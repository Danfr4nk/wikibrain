# The Endpoint Requirement

**Status: speculation with a real evidential shape — the 2026-09-13 mind
rewrite's new finding, not a settled concept.** Dan's acquisition drive
does not complete toward wants. It completes toward *endpoints* — defined,
external completion states that exist before the pursuit begins. A want
with an endpoint finishes. A want without one stalls, regardless of how
much determination is behind it.

## The evidence

From [[wiki/mind/concepts/acquisition-drive]]'s full record:

| Completed | Endpoint that existed |
|---|---|
| Hey Monday trip, Dec 2008 | The concert, on a date |
| Procurement runs, all eras | The object, in hand |
| Fran vigil, six months | The funeral — and Dan's own payoff word was *closure* |
| Au Za'atar embedded objectives | Cashable terminals: the tip split paid out, custody of the shed's cash tips |
| 2025 dance re-entry burst | The MASTER CRATE — a bounded, finishable set (standing self-record) |

| Stalled | What was missing |
|---|---|
| MNEME | A specification but no shipped artifact to mark done |
| DANMODEL blind evaluation | No results file; no decision the evaluation would settle |
| AI video essays | Stalled at planning, where "done" was never defined |

Self-origination — the acquisition drive's firing condition — is necessary
and not sufficient. The endpoint is the second half of the requirement the
2026-08-02 falsification left unnamed.

## The profile lens

This is the Ti-dominant architecture's oldest demand stated as a project
rule: **introverted thinking requires stated premises**, and a project
without a defined endpoint is a system without premises — the engine cannot
tell whether it has finished, so it cannot finish. The
[[wiki/mind/concepts/explicit-verbal-commitment|explicit-verbal-commitment]]
architecture is the same requirement in the relational domain: a rule stays
live until an explicit counter-rule arrives, because only the explicit
counts. An endpoint is a counter-rule for a pursuit — the explicit statement
that it is over.

## What would settle it

The test is prospective and cheap: every project he starts from now gets its
endpoint named at inception, in writing. Watch whether nameless projects
stall and named ones complete. One year of that log graduates this page
from speculation or kills it.

**The honest counter-case.** The wiki itself is the drive's largest
sustained output and has no endpoint. The reading that saves the rule: the
wiki is not a pursuit but the drive's *process* — the instrument, not the
target. That exempts it without explaining the exemption away, and the
stalled projects may have stalled for era reasons (the 2025 collapse, the
Annie terminal phase) rather than structural ones. The endpoint correlation
is not yet disentangled from the calendar.

## Gaps

No prospective test has been run. The MASTER CRATE instance rests on the
standing self-record, not a wiki-filed artifact. The "specification but no
shipped artifact" reading of MNEME assumes the specification was the goal
rather than the build being the goal — if the build was the goal, the
stall needs a different explanation.
