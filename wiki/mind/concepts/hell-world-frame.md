> **Incidental entry** — Dan's "hell world" register: American politics as an irredeemable spectacle, reported with gleeful doom. Complete at this size.

The Hell World frame is the house style of Dan's Substack: politics isn't broken, it's *hell* — a batshit-insane horserace unfolding in a world beyond parody, and the only honest posture is to narrate the damnation in real time.

Key facts:
- The publication tagline itself: "Live from Political Hellworld" (May 2023).
- "The Horserace (From Hell)" (2023-06-09): Trump's federal indictment news lands and the verdict is "The 2024 election is going to absolutely, unequivocally batshit insane. It will be unlike anything in American history." Closes: "Strap in."
- "Brain Worms In Hell World" (2023-10-17): the frame turns diagnostic — post-10/7 discourse as pandemic-level neurological collapse, "severe loss of the ability to recognize nuance."
- The register does double duty: it's comedy (irony as analysis) and it's fatalism (nothing can be fixed, so document the fire).

Why incidental: a stylistic frame, fully described at this size. Promote to major if the frame spreads beyond the Substack into his wider writing and becomes load-bearing.
