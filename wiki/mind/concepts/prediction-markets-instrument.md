> **Incidental entry** — prediction markets as Dan's forecasting instrument: prices next to polls, with the mechanics understood. Complete at this size.

Dan reads betting markets the way he reads polls — as another noisy sensor, not an oracle. In POLL WATCH the PredictIt prices sit alongside RCP and 538 aggregates, and he shows his work on how the instrument distorts.

Key facts:
- POLL WATCH #1 (2023-05-26): PredictIt GOP-nomination prices had Trump "yes" at 58¢ vs DeSantis at 32¢ — "a +26 point spread" corroborating the polling gap.
- General-election prices the same week: Biden 45¢ / Trump 32¢ / DeSantis 22¢ — with Dan's own caveat: "due to the way these markets function, it is difficult to gauge general election results at this stage. After a nominee is chosen, capital from shares of inactive candidates will be reallocated to the nominee."
- The throughline: markets as corroboration, never as the argument — the thesis always came from the politics (the DeSantis Delusion), the prices just confirmed the shape.
