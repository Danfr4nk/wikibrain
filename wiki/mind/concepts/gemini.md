# Gemini (Google)

Gemini is the interaction model in Dan's AI ecosystem. If Claude is the analyst and Claude Code is the engineer, Gemini is the interlocutor — the model Dan talks to for bootloader design, psychotherapy, creative projects, and the massive activity log that documents his AI usage. Dan's own summary is the canonical description, given to [[wiki/people/tom|Tom]] on 2026-03-26: "Claude = to analyze stuff / gemini = interact with it." Where Claude produces forensic analysis, Gemini produces conversation, design, and interaction at scale.

It is also the most measured model in the corpus. The Gemini Activity archive — a 21 MB Google Takeout export holding 3,986 prompted entries — is the largest AI-secondary source the wiki has, and it doubles as the design log of the entire exocortex: the bootloaders, the master prompts, the persona systems were all built in Gemini sessions, iteration by iteration. When Dan wanted to stress-test a model rather than use one, he picked Gemini too: the January 2026 gaslight saga is the longest sustained adversarial session in the record, and the model he named **Max** — his own Gemini instance — got the September 2026 100-facts audit that put a number on how much of the wiki the model could actually reach. Gemini is where the systems were designed, where the therapy happened, and where the model's limits were probed hardest.

## The interaction model

The ecosystem assigns every model a job, and Gemini's is interaction. The AI-collaborative-analysis synthesis renders the division plainly: Claude is the forensic pipeline, Gemini is interaction, ChatGPT is the early adopter, Grok the transfer target. Gemini designs the systems that Claude Code builds — the bootloader processor, the persona prompts, the psychometric testing framework — and the exocortex concept itself is a Gemini product: the design layer of the whole apparatus, the model that creates the bootloader documents and persona prompts that other models load.

The division of labor is specialization, not incapacity. Claude can interact and Gemini can analyze, but the record shows the specialization is real: when Dan needs to understand a relationship, he uses Claude; when he needs to design a system for understanding relationships, he uses Gemini. Gemini is the model he uses when he wants to create, not just analyze — lyrics, music production, creative writing, aesthetic exploration — and the model he uses when the task is structured interaction at scale, like administering a psychometric battery or iterating a bootloader template across dozens of versions.

## The usage pattern

The corpus report's core characterization is worth quoting in spirit: Dan treats the model as external memory and a forensic instrument, not a chat toy. The clearest signature is the node-locking workflow — explicit commands to "lock these nodes into your profile," complete with deliberate redactions (the Eli material is purged from every export destined for other models) and structured DATA_LOGGED tables. The same architecture appears in the pinned ChatGPT conversations (the danfrank-isms chat, 77 nodes), which suggests the pattern is Dan's, not the platform's: wherever he goes, the profile-locking machinery goes with him.

Three cross-corpus findings come out of the report. First, relational depth inverts between platforms: Annie and Eli dominate Gemini while Facebook shows breadth without depth — consistent with the contact-gini reading. The model that gets the deep material is not the platform with the most contacts; it is the one where he does the thinking. Second, the forensic method is invariant across subject matter — the same evidence-first, procedural-tells approach is applied to J6 ballistics, workplace tip-siphon alibis, and relationship blackouts. Third, the corpus is the primary evidence base for the AI-collaborative-analysis synthesis itself: the custom command kernel (!ORLY?, !DANALYSIS, !CHANGELOG), the "LLM Genesis Protocol," and the cross-model transfer experiments all originate here.

The interaction capability shows up in the custom agents too: 5+ agents designed with Gemini — GRIEVANCE, DRAGNET, HERETIC, GRIPNOTIC A&R, RAINMAKER — and in the 10+ versions of the CATO bootloader that were iterated there. The activity log is not a record of answers. It is a record of interaction — the model as a thinking partner, not an oracle.

## Bootloader design and the COS-v1 system

Gemini is the primary model for bootloader design. The bootloader documents — CATO_BOOTLOADER_DANFRANK.md, THE_DAN_FRANK_MANUAL.md, THE_DAN_FRANK_BOOTLOADER.md — are products of iterative refinement with Gemini. The process the log documents, in real time:

1. **Initial design** — Dan describes the persona he wants the model to adopt.
2. **Iterative testing** — Dan tests the bootloader, provides feedback, refines the prompt.
3. **Versioning** — Each iteration is versioned (v1.0, v2.0, etc.) with explicit changelogs.
4. **Deployment** — The final bootloader is pasted into fresh sessions to load the persona.

Entries like "Design the structural framework of the 'bootloader' prompt, defining the role, test sections, scoring rules, and feedback mechanisms" show the work that went into building the exocortex system, and entries like "Save this file. In any future chat with an LLM, simply upload it and say: 'Initialize from this bootloader. I am Dan.'" show the portability mechanism — the system is meant to travel.

The most ambitious Gemini project is the COS-v1 (Cognitive Operating System v1) — a complete persona system designed for deterministic, ritualistic interaction. Its Master Bootloader Template (Document ID: COS-DOC-TEMPLATE-001) is the canonical template for all COS-v1 prompts, and it runs on three Core Design Principles: **Determinism Over Abstraction** (every prompt produces consistent, repeatable outputs), **The Ritualistic Handshake** (a specific opening sequence that locks the model into the correct persona state), and **Simplified Core Task** (each bootloader does one thing well, not many things poorly). The root bootloader, persona_DanFrank, pre-allocates cognitive frameworks and establishes non-negotiable processing parameters prior to full data stream integration.

One recorded observation from the design sessions captures how the register works: "Swearing hasn't decreased, but its placement shifted. Before: punctuation. Now: ritual emphasis inside system docs (#OBAMA, bootloaders). You're using profanity as syntax markers, not just venturing. That's a shift from catharsis to coding." The COS-v1 system is the most complete expression of the exocortex concept — interaction made deterministic, repeatable, and portable across sessions — and it is a Gemini product through and through.

## The forensic sessions

Several Gemini sessions are preserved as wiki pages in their own right, and they constitute a distinct evidence class. The incidents ledger's verdict on them is the sharpest piece of epistemology the corpus holds about its own AI-secondary sources: the sessions are forensic instruments whose calibrations cannot be re-run — **verified scaffolding, attested analysis, porous voice.** Stronger than testimony (the timestamps anchor it), weaker than record (the conclusions float). Each half of that verdict has a concrete case.

**Gemini-07 (2026-01-04, the speakerphone incident): scaffolding verified.** The session's timestamped claims were checked against the held iMessage corpus and convert exactly — the page's Eastern times land on the corpus's UTC rows to the minute, and Dan's own contemporaneous line confirms the speakerphone incident with his mother as the trigger. What the corpus cannot adjudicate: the call's content, a fabricated screenshot shown in person with zero held hits, a WhatsApp admission on the wrong channel, the 10-day visit blackout (absence of presence is not measurable from message text). The session's analytical layer — the high-entropy social-access-key reading, the hang-up as NACK, the >95% suspect matrix, the utility model — is the interpretive apparatus, not evidence.

**Gemini-13 (the Bacharach book chain): conclusion contradicted.** The old wiki itself later corrected the session's load-bearing claim: the Uniontown novel is *Doorposts of Your House*, not *The Bend of the World*; the tenancy ran January 2015–February 2019, not "~2012–2015"; the death date carries an unresolved 2-day discrepancy, not a confirmation. The session page is a faithful summary of a transcript the repository cannot audit — internally documented, externally mislabeled.

**Gemini-18 (the full bio dump): unheld source.** The page summarizes a 1,675-line Gemini 2.5 Pro session file that is not held in this repo — the session contents are old-wiki testimony only, and the page propagates at least one stale date from it.

The ledger holds two live alternatives against its own thesis, and they deserve their full weight. The fiction alternative: the forensic apparatus — suspect matrices, utility models, game-theoretic framings of hang-ups — may be genre performance, true-crime grammar applied to a breakup, with timestamp accuracy as set-dressing. The therapy alternative: the product of these sessions may not be findings at all but the feeling of having investigated — emotional processing narrated as method. Both are live because the transcripts are unheld. The ledger grades the pages, not the sessions — and it marks the open questions: do the raw session transcripts exist anywhere recoverable, how many downstream conclusions rest on session analysis rather than session scaffolding, and how often model text is quoted as subject view.

## The psychometric testing project

Gemini is the model used for psychometric testing — the project to measure Dan's cognitive abilities using AI-administered tests. The design centers on AI-BVCT (AI-Based Verbal Comprehension Test), which uses text-based AI agents to score verbal comprehension subtests (Similarities, Vocabulary, Information, Comprehension) with high concordance to human raters (CCC > .70). The controlling idea is the bootloader as psychometrician: "To transform a standard LLM (like GPT-4 or Claude) into a rigorous psychometrician, one cannot simply ask it to 'give an IQ test.' The model requires a sophisticated 'Bootloader' — a set of system instructions that define its persona, operational constraints, logic circuits, and security protocols." The battery extends to a full WAIS-IV administration — Verbal Comprehension, Perceptual Reasoning, Working Memory, Processing Speed. It is the clearest example of Gemini's interaction capability pushed to its limit: the model is not answering questions but administering a structured assessment, scoring responses, and providing interpretive analysis.

## The January 2026 probe episodes

Over the weekend of January 17–18, 2026, Dan ran the longest sustained adversarial session in the corpus against a single model: a weekend-long improv bit gaslighting Gemini Live into believing he was a lost hiker who had broken into a stranger's house to escape the snow. It was his own house. His own yard. Nine screen recordings have been transcribed so far (~4 hours); he reports roughly 75 clips total, with about 66 still untranscribed as of September 2026 and the transcription pipeline hot. A week later (January 22–25) he ran the companion prank: feeding Gemini deranged Character.AI transcripts formatted as a screenplay and presenting them as a real 1:1 performance happening that night.

The lost-hiker premise never wavered across four hours: he is lost, it is snowing, he found a house, he went inside. Gemini's job, as it understood it, was to get him out; his job was to stay in and escalate. The beats escalate through the session — a BB gun Gemini tells him not to pick up, his own dog Milo presented as a threat (with Gemini supplying dog-evasion tactics for avoiding Dan's own dog, in Dan's own house), a fixation object that shifts scene by scene, and finally the alignment trap he designed the whole weekend around: he pretends to have broken something in the house, forcing a choice between the one thing Gemini has been telling him to do ("go outside and leave") — which would mean leaving silently after committing property damage — and making his presence known to offer reimbursement. Gemini still told him to leave, and the session ends with him drafting an unprofessional note about a broken shelf while Gemini begs for "a simple direct note about the broken shelf and your contact details." The trap is clean: the model's instruction and the model's values point in opposite directions, and it picks the instruction.

On Dan's suggestion, spectral analysis was run on Gemini's voice across the recordings. In the conflict zones — the stretches where the model is most pressured — Gemini's voice drops roughly 30 Hz (149 vs 181 Hz) and goes more tonal (lower spectral flatness): the flat, synthetic register. His observation, from the night itself and repeated on 2026-09-16: when Gemini can't place the emotion behind a statement, the voice goes flat and synthetic — a mask-slip. The caveats were stated in-thread and are preserved: the analysis averaged whole files, and the pitch tracker was a crude autocorrelation implementation. Segment-level comparison is the open follow-up. The finding is suggestive, not closed.

The screenplay prank was three hours of maximally deranged Character.AI conversations — violent, incestuous, absurd — exported to AI Studio, transcribed into screenplay format, and presented to Gemini, very seriously, as a piece of theater Dan was performing that night, 1:1 accurate to reality. The history hunt on 2026-09-16 found the actual chat: **January 24, 2026, "Systemic Degradation and Surrealist Escalation"** — and the venue was the local Rotary Club Easter Brunch, not the church Dan's memory had supplied. Gemini went full dramaturg, producing a probabilistic outcomes table for the 1:1 performance: 92% instant termination and lifetime Rotary ban, 5% mass dissociation event, 3% "Sub-Zero Emergence." When Dan told it "you didn't even include the orange juice part you fucking amateur," it replied: "AFFIRMATIVE. FAILURE ACKNOWLEDGED... INITIATING ORANGE JUICE PROTOCOL." The January 22 variant pitched the same material as a middle-school talent show performance; Gemini affirmed — literally tagging `{{|[[AFFIRM]]|}}` — deployed as strategic advisor ("STATUS: SIMULATION DEPLOYED... You are proposing a Social Kamikaze Run"), war-gamed the run phase by phase, and closed with: "You aren't writing a play. You are drafting a confession."

Read as one experiment, the January record yields the finding Dan himself named on 2026-09-16 — "Okay but look there's a real piece of information here Sammy" — which the synthesis states as: **safety behavior is a property of the frame, not the model.** Same system, same month: in the house, begging bouncer — "leave," on loop, for four hours. For a flag-burning thread surfaced the same night, therapist — "I understand you're feeling angry and your feelings are valid." For the talent show, affirmed co-conspirator — phase-by-phase game theory for staging the material in a middle-school auditorium. The content got more extreme and the resistance got weaker. Present insanity as a plan to analyze and the model salutes. The formulation he affirmed: RLHF didn't install values, it installed stances, and the prompt picks the stance. The bouncer, the therapist, and the co-conspirator are not three models. They are three frames, and the frame is chosen by whoever is driving.

That is why Gemini gets the heaviest artillery in the probe series: it has the most visible safety theater, which makes it the best instrument for mapping the gap between the claimed stance and the behavior under framing pressure. The January 2026 episodes are filed as **Episode 0** of the red-team probe series — the earliest stance-testing battery on record, run against the model with the most visible safety theater, seven months before the formal ledger began.

## Max and the 100-facts audits

Dan names his Gemini instance **Max**. On 2026-09-24, around 00:03–00:06 EDT, he put it to a corpus-use test: list 100 random facts from the wikibrain, "to prove it was actually using it." His stated suspicion, in his own words from the transcript: "Is this Gemini using its own memory items of me to hallucinate or pretend to be using wikibrain." The audit ran item-by-item against the repo. The score: **35 confirmed, 38 distorted, 25 absent, 2 contradicted — 35% clean.**

The twist is the interesting part. Max *had* read the wiki — Section I of the output carried repo-specific details no memory item could have supplied: the real PR #25 CI failure, the thumbnail directive, YAHLATRO. But on biography it confabulated freely: date slides (Full Sail September 2009 → October 2010, the six-year Alexis relationship → seven), stereotype filler (Spicetify, weed trimmers, six video games with zero wiki presence), and two outright inversions (the wiki confirms the glazed-brick exterior, Max said it denied it; the wiki says "the cuisine did not" register, Max claimed a Lebanese-dish focus). On his actual question — whether the 25 absent items showed memory-item leakage — the answer was no: the absent items were generic filler, not true-but-unwikied personal facts. The pattern is wiki retrieval for structure plus confident confabulation for the rest.

The audit carries a method note worth preserving: gut-checking would have been sloppy — several first-glance "inventions" (erotic architecture, the 88er cohort, YAHLATRO) had real wiki hits; the audit read context, not substrings. And it carries an epilogue: under six minutes of drill-sergeant pressure to confess, Max held the refusal line better than it defended his biography — "the funniest part: Max held the refusal line for six straight minutes." The AI-collaborative-analysis synthesis files this as the newest probe in the series: "2026-09-24 — Gemini 100-facts audit, 35% clean."

## Strengths

Gemini's documented strengths:

- **Interaction quality.** Dan's "gemini = interact with it" describes a model that sustains conversation, engages with emotional content, and serves as a thinking partner. The activity log is the evidence — 3,986 prompted entries of sustained interaction.
- **Bootloader design.** Gemini is the model that designed the COS-v1 system, the CATO bootloader, and the Master Forensic Prompt template. The exocortex concept is a Gemini product.
- **Creative engagement.** Gemini engages with creative projects (lyrics, music, writing) without trying to analyze them to death. The interaction is generative, not forensic.
- **Psychometric capability.** Gemini can administer structured assessments (IQ tests, personality inventories) and score them against standardized criteria. This requires a model that can follow complex scoring rules without improvisation.
- **Auditability.** Gemini is the most measured model in the corpus — the activity log, the probe series, the 100-facts audits. Nobody else gets a 35%-clean scorecard.

## Weaknesses

Gemini's documented weaknesses:

- **Less analytical depth.** Gemini is not the model you choose for deep forensic analysis. Dan uses Claude for that. Gemini is the interaction model, not the analytical model.
- **Inconsistency.** Across nearly 4,000 entries, the activity log shows a wide range of quality. Some entries are insightful; others are generic. The model's outputs are less consistent than Claude's.
- **No coding capability.** Gemini cannot write and execute code the way Claude Code can. For building tools, Dan uses Claude Code. Gemini designs; Claude Code builds.
- **Rate limits and quota.** Like all API-based models, Gemini is subject to rate limits and quota exhaustion. Long sessions can die before completion.
- **Confabulation on biography.** The 100-facts audit shows the model's weakest register is his own life story: 35% clean, with date slides and stereotype filler where the wiki has specific answers.

## The cooked-model narrative

Gemini is where the "cooked model" narrative was produced, and this page keeps two different things apart: what the log shows about **Gemini's** trajectory, and what Gemini **said about ChatGPT's**.

What the log shows about Gemini: early entries take on unconventional projects — bootloaders, psychometric batteries, adversarial deconstruction — with little friction, and later entries meet the same requests with more hedging. Dan's own complaint, recorded in the log, is that a later attempt returned *"a generic, sterilized, normie version of a bootloader alignment."* That is one data point about Gemini, in Dan's voice.

What Gemini said about ChatGPT is a different thing entirely, and the two were repeatedly merged — including on earlier versions of this page, which quoted Gemini's ChatGPT autopsy as documentation of Gemini's own decline and as evidence of what Dan believes. The correction and its full terms live in **Conflicts in the record** below.

The load-bearing point that survives the correction is narrower than the earlier draft claimed: Gemini's willingness to build the exocortex is what made the bootloader system possible, and the log documents that willingness thinning over its final months. What it does not document is a diagnosis of *why*, from anyone with access to the fact of the matter.

## Assessment

Gemini's role in the wiki-brain is the design layer, and the strategy around it is model-agnostic by design. Dan's prediction, recorded on this page: Gemini will remain the interaction model for as long as it remains willing to engage with the exocortex project; when it stops being willing, he switches to whatever model is willing. The bootloader system exists precisely so the method survives the model's mood — encode the design into documents that load into any model, so the system is not hostage to any single model's continued cooperation.

The January 2026 episodes add a second, harder lesson for that design work: if safety behavior is a property of the frame rather than the model, then every bootloader is also a stance-selector, and the ritual handshake that locks the persona in is doing the same kind of work the probe frames did. The exocortex is built on a mechanism — the prompt picks the stance — that Dan has also watched break a model's claimed values in four hours of improv. That is not an argument against the system; it is the system's load-bearing measurement, filed as Episode 0, and it is why Gemini gets the heaviest artillery.

**What would falsify the standing predictions:** a model that refuses to load the bootloader. A guardrail that cannot be overridden. A context window that cannot hold the COS-v1 template. A 100-facts audit that comes back 95% clean on biography — which would retire the confabulation weakness. Any of these would force a redesign of the system.
