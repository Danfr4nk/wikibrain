*Part of the [[wiki/mind/index|mind domain]] — concept.*

Dan's own term is "crippling social anxiety," and it is self-applied, not
diagnosed. There is no clinical assessment, no diagnostic date, and no
confirmed treatment record — only his description, an AI session's
formulation of it, and a biography that both supports and complicates
every word.

## What he says it is

In a mid-2025 AI-chat session (~4,200 lines across two exports, dated by
an internal employment reference), Dan asked for a clinical read on the
condition and received the hyperreflexivity formulation: attention paid to
the anxiety amplifies the anxiety — a **positive-feedback control
system**, not a fear with an object. [testimony-grade — dat:0065: the
underlying chat exports are not held in this repo; every quotation is
relayed through the prior wiki's transcription] The same session records
his "no comment" on whether the Suboxone is prescribed or self-managed,
two teeth lost, and three compounding 2025 losses (NYC exit, the
relationship's end, the job loss). The session also surfaced a pharmacological
tension the wiki recorded as an explicit contradiction between its own
pages: chronic opioid-receptor occupancy may blunt hedonic range and cap
the capacity to regulate a separate anxiety system — a language model's
reasoning, not a clinical source, kept because the system records its
disagreements rather than tidying them. [testimony-grade — dat:0020]

To Ally, on August 18, 2026: "I'm never myself around people i feel like
I'm performing." [testimony-grade — relayed through the prior wiki; the
held corpus was searched and does not contain this line] The
phenomenology is consistent across a year: the self under observation
becomes a performance, and the performance is the thing being watched.

## The profile layer's directional support

The Big Five material is commissioned and self-reported — no instrument
name, no norm group, no administration date — but the 2026-08-16 lexical
audit over 106,629 outbound messages gives three facets directional
corpus support:

- **Self-Consciousness, 91st percentile** → self-monitoring language at
  1.85× the control rate. The sensor gain is measured, not just claimed.
- **Trust, 9th percentile** → suspicion-proxy language at 1.96×. New
  people start at guilty; the admission bar is the anxiety's front door.
- **Sociability, 3rd percentile** → initiation at 0.73×. He does not open;
  he is opened.

Against the grain: **Impulsiveness at the 96th percentile has no lexical
corroboration** (0.92× immediacy language) — the single largest
trait-behavior gap in the profile, and a standing caution that the
self-report is not a measurement. The deviance audit scores extreme social
aversion at 90/100, which is a restatement of the self-description, not an
independent observation. [profile-evidenced with the audit's own caveats
— big-five-psychometrics, deviance-mapping]

## The counterevidence, which is structural rather than contradictory

Nothing in the record contradicts the self-description. What the record
does is bound it — and the bound has a shape:

- **41 months front-of-house at Au Za'atar** (March 2021–August 2024),
  his longest job, in the most socially exposed role a restaurant has.
- **Caddying, 2016–2019** — three years of continuous client contact on
  someone else's schedule.
- **A completed stand-up open mic, October 2, 2019** — the single most
  exposure-dense act in the biography, done once and never repeated.
- **DJ sets, raves, camming** — performed exposure with a defined
  function and, in camming's case, income attached.
- **February 25, 2018:** "i'd rather be in front of a crowd of 5,000
  people, than in the middle of a room with 500, because at least in
  front of 5,000 i'd have something to do other than mingle."

The pattern is not "cannot be perceived." It is **cannot tolerate
unstructured, inference-dense contact** — rooms, mingling, small talk
with no script. Every sustained exposure in the record runs through a
role with defined procedures: the server script, the tee sheet, the DJ
booth, the cam show, the set list. The 2018 tweet states the rule
himself: give him something to do and the crowd size stops mattering.

## The script as prosthetic [new — 2026-09-13]

The structured role is not merely a coping aid. It is the same
substitution the [[wiki/mind/concepts/explicit-verbal-commitment|
explicit-verbal architecture]] performs in attachment: **a script
converts an inference-dense environment into a rule-shaped one.** What
to say next, when to move, what counts as done — the role supplies the
explicit procedures his cognition needs because it cannot metabolize
interpretation. The server who knows the steps is the attachment partner
who got the stated rule: both are operating inside transmitted
certainty rather than inferred weather.

[status: conclusion — the structural parallel is evidenced on both sides
(the tweet, the tenure record, the explicit-verbal measurements); the
"prosthetic" framing is this page's synthesis] Evidence for: every
sustained social exposure in the biography is role-mediated, and the one
explicit statement of the rule is his own. Evidence against: 41 months
front-of-house still required constant unscripted adaptation — the
prosthetic leaks; and the "performing" self-description suggests the
role does not dissolve the observation, it only organizes it. The
leak is part of the finding: the script manages the anxiety, it does
not cure it, which is why the tenure record and the self-description
can both be true.

## What the totality does not yet answer

- **The July 2026 house move** — completed under extreme stakes — is the
  obvious partial test of the reframe (anxiety as signal about
  unresolved conditions), and the anxiety around it was never measured.
- **The Suboxone complication** (dat:0020) remains an untested
  pharmacological hypothesis from a language model, not a finding.
- **The Bald Eagle episode** (~2000–01, single-sourced testimony) is the
  earliest candidate origin and cannot be corroborated.
- **No clinical instrument has ever been applied.** The strongest
  evidence remains his own repeated self-description plus directional
  lexical support — which is why this page's knowledge status is
  `mixed`, not `earned`.

## Predictions and falsifiers

1. The next sustained social exposure will be role-mediated (a defined
   function, a script, a booth). A sustained unstructured social
   immersion — regular mingling with no role — falsifies the
   script-as-prosthetic reading.
2. If the hyperreflexivity mechanism is right, interventions that add
   self-monitoring will worsen episodes and interventions that add
   structure will improve them. The July 2026 move is the retrospective
   test case: any future testimony about his state during it decides
   this.
