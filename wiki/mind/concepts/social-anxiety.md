# Social Anxiety

Dan's own term is "crippling social anxiety," and it is self-applied, not diagnosed. There is no clinical assessment, no diagnostic date, and no confirmed treatment engagement anywhere in the corpus. What exists instead is a first-person account of unusual precision — a mid-2025 AI-collaborative session in which Dan went looking for a clinical-grade account of his own condition by opening an AI chat and asking it to analyze his own data — plus trait-level instrument scores that converge on the same mechanism, plus a behavioral record of sustained social exposure inside the condition that demands explanation.

The session is the corpus's only direct engagement with a named psychological mechanism, distinct from the addiction and attachment material that dominates the rest of the mind/ domain. It is also, characteristically, an instance of the [[wiki/mind/concepts/exocortex|externalized prefrontal cortex]] pattern: Dan's primary self-knowledge mechanism applied to his own pathology, run through a model instead of a clinician.

## The mechanism, in his own words

He opens the conversation without hedging: *"the more i pay attention, prevent and try to avoid problems... the objectively more i doom myself to them,"* and names the target precisely — *"the anxious, internally probing awareness of my appearance in all senses."* The AI's read, which Dan doesn't contest anywhere in the exchange, names it **hyperreflexivity**: a loop where the monitoring apparatus is itself the problem generator — notice discomfort, audit yourself to prevent it showing, the audit consumes the bandwidth needed to act naturally, perform worse, notice that, audit escalates.

The mechanical explanation for why *prevention makes it worse*: every successfully-managed near-miss gets logged by the nervous system as "the threat was real and I escaped it," never as "the threat wasn't real" — so avoidance behavior trains the anxiety to run hotter, not lower, each cycle. This is [[wiki/health/hyperreflexivity]]'s territory; it is repeated here because the mechanism is the condition's load-bearing structure, and Dan supplied it himself.

## The trait-level signature

[[wiki/mind/profile/big-five-psychometrics]] puts Self-Consciousness at the **91st percentile** and Trust at the **9th**. The corpus's own read on what that pairing does: low trust means a confirmation does not carry forward as a prior, so the estimate decays and must be refreshed — the short half-life of reassurance is the loop's maintenance cycle in the attachment domain. The deviance audit, run independently of the anxiety material, scores **extreme social aversion** at 90/100 (confidence 99%): Sociability 3 read as "near-total withdrawal from conventional human interaction," Dan as "an ethnographer of a species he barely interacts with."

Three instruments, one shape: continuous monitoring of how the self reads from outside, bolted to no mechanism for converting a clean reading into a durable prior.

## The social record

The condition is not only self-reported. On August 18, 2026 at 18:36, [[wiki/people/ally-lubin|Ally]] gives a first-person account of the same night from the other side: *"Pls I called you when I was in NYC once and **you were too scared**."* Both accounts are first-hand, both are about the same night — she was in New York that week, flying out on the 9th — and the contemporaneous record supports Dan's version of the events. What matters for this page is the undisputed residue: in a city where he had lived for years, with a woman he had known for nearly two decades, the operative variable on the night was fear of showing up.

## The earliest source

The hyperreflexivity material carries a causal claim backward: a full year as the middle school's named laughing stock — the Bald Eagle Cummings episode — at an age when Dan was already a year younger than every classmate and pre-pubertal, is the earliest documented source of the social-exposure sensitivity this page treats as a standing condition. The caveats travel with the claim: the date is unfixed (seventh grade for a student who started a year early puts it around the 2000–01 school year, but the capture says only "7th grade" and "one winter day"), and the nickname itself is unattested outside the capture — a year of school-wide use should have left traces in the 2007-era Facebook archive, and nobody has looked.

## The reframe: signal, not noise

A later, separate AI session — explicitly reviewing the same anxiety conversation as retrospective data rather than continuing it — pushes back on treating the loop as a freestanding condition at all. Its read: by the session's second third the conversation had converted almost entirely into an Annie analysis, and that drift is diagnostic rather than incidental — *"the anxiety loop doesn't have much independent existence right now."* It reads as "parasitized" by two concrete, unresolved situations — the Annie relationship and, per internal reference, a "post-June housing void" — being processed as threat by an already-calibrated hyperreflexive system, rather than a clinical entity running on its own.

The reframe's practical implication: "treat the anxiety loop" may target the wrong layer; the loop is functioning as signal about real unresolved conditions, not noise to be corrected independently of them.

> **REVISED [2026-07-26]:** the reframe made a testable prediction — that the loop tracks the two unresolved conditions rather than running independently — and the July 2026 record gives a partial test. One of the two conditions resolved: Dan completed a house move in July, alone, and describes sleeping in a bed for the first time in some while. The other did not, and got worse ([[wiki/timeline/events/july-2026-recontact]]). The corpus does not record what his anxiety did in the same window, so this is a resolved variable rather than a result — worth re-checking against the next export, because a loop that stayed at full amplitude with the housing void closed would narrow the reframe considerably.

## The Suboxone complication

Told that Dan has been on daily Suboxone since 2010 (consistent with the corpus's other dosage evidence — see [[wiki/health/chemical-architecture]]), the AI raises a mechanism the chemical-architecture page doesn't otherwise carry: chronic opioid receptor occupancy may blunt the hedonic range and reward-anticipation Dan would need to break the anxiety loop — the maintenance dose that stabilizes one system may be capping his capacity to regulate the other. This is a genuine, still-unresolved tension between two anchor pages of the health domain, and it is flagged here rather than resolved.

## The Au Za'atar paradox

The session is dated, by internal reference to "nearly a year of not working" since the involuntary August 2024 Au Za'atar job loss, to roughly mid-2025 — the point Dan returned to paid work after an eleven-month gap. Which makes the central paradox of this page unavoidable: the same man who describes his social anxiety as crippling worked **forty-one months** (March 2021–August 2024) in front-of-house restaurant service — the longest job of his life, in the most socially exposed position available to him.

The wiki's read: the anxiety is real and the exposure was sustained, which means a compensatory architecture was doing load-bearing work the whole time. The shape of that architecture is documented across the corpus: explicit rules and scripted roles substituting for the social inference he doesn't trust himself to perform. A server has a script. A busser has a route. The job converts the unstructured terror of "how do I read this room" into a finite set of explicit verbal procedures — which is exactly the interface Dan's cognitive architecture can operate.

## The explicit-verbal mechanism

This is where the anxiety connects to the corpus's central finding about how Dan processes social information at all. Positive verbal expressions function as absolute rules until an explicit severance signal arrives; what is stated explicitly can be trusted, what must be inferred cannot. The iMessage corpus analysis behind the Annie report puts numbers on the asymmetry: "do you love me" appears **0 times** in 106,629 sent messages; "call me" appears **170 times**. He asks for channel, not for reassurance he can't metabolize — the request is for the explicit, rule-shaped thing (call me, a procedure) rather than the inferential thing (do you love me, an interpretation task).

Read against the anxiety, the mechanism is compensatory: social situations are inference-dense environments, and inference is exactly the channel he distrusts. Explicit verbal commitments convert the social world into something rule-shaped enough to navigate. The "not vigilant = annihilated" axiom — one of the wiki's load-bearing psychological claims — is the same posture: vigilance is the audit, and the audit never stands down, because the one time it does is the time the threat is real.

The Fe-inferior framing in the corpus converges here from a fourth direction: Dan craves connection and distrusts his own emotional reads of other people, so he outsources the reading to explicit statements and then treats those statements as load-bearing. The anxiety is what it feels like from inside to run a social life on an interface you know is a prosthetic.

## The abandonment schema

On January 3, 2016 — three days into the documented Annie record — Dan states the standing expectation outright: *"it would be so typical of my entire life / to find something that makes me TRULY happy / and find the most beautiful girl in the world / who for whatever reason loves ME for who i am / and have everything be perfect / until it's just gone."* This is the abandonment schema as worldview, not a reaction to a specific event — a standing expectation that happiness is temporary and loss is inevitable. The anxiety loop and the abandonment schema are the same vigilance architecture aimed at different targets: social exposure in one, attachment in the other.

## What the corpus does not support

- **No clinical diagnosis.** The label "social anxiety" is Dan's self-description; the mechanism "hyperreflexivity" is an AI session's naming, which Dan did not contest. Neither is a clinical finding, and this page must not be cited as one.
- **No treatment engagement.** "No confirmed treatment engagement for anxiety" is the corpus's explicit status.
- **The reframe is untested.** The prediction — loop amplitude tracks the two unresolved conditions — awaits the next export. The July 2026 partial test is a resolved variable, not a result.
- **The Suboxone tension is unresolved.** A raised mechanism, not an established interaction.
- **The autism intersection is unmapped.** The autism self-identification and the social anxiety describe overlapping territory (explicit-over-inferred meaning, bottom-percentile sociability, extreme social aversion) and neither was checked against the other before both were accepted. Whether they are two labels for one architecture or two architectures sharing a channel is an open question — see [[wiki/mind/concepts/autism]].
