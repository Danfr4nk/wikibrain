# Social Anxiety

Dan's own term is "crippling social anxiety." It is self-applied and
self-recognized, but it is not diagnosed: there is no clinical
assessment, no diagnostic date, no confirmed treatment record anywhere
in the corpus. What the record holds instead is a phenomenology he has
stated repeatedly over years, a named mechanism he asked an AI to put
words to in mid-2025, a profile layer that measures the attention side
of it, and a biography that should — on the surface — contradict it.

The texture of the condition is consistent wherever it appears. He is
never himself around people; he feels like he is performing. Being
watched turns the self into an object under surveillance, and the
surveillance is the problem, not any particular audience. The one
sentence he has supplied that states the whole thing — a 2018 line about
preferring a crowd of 5,000 to a room of 500 because a crowd gives him
*something to do other than mingle* — also states the rule that resolves
the apparent contradiction of his biography. Structured exposure with a
defined role is survivable. Unstructured, inference-dense contact — a
room, a mingling, a conversation with no script — is not.

This page is the concept held from the felt side: what the anxiety
reports about itself. The mechanism, the origin material, and the
pharmacological tension live on [[wiki/health/hyperreflexivity]], which
this page treats as mechanism-of. The profile layer's measurement of the
sensor gain lives on [[wiki/mind/profile/big-five-psychometrics]] and
[[wiki/mind/profile/deviance-mapping]]. The autism intersection — raised
by Dan himself, inside the anxiety session — lives on
[[wiki/mind/concepts/autism]] and is treated here as co-occurrence, not
as one label swallowing the other.

## The claim, in plain form

The claim is: social contact runs through a self-monitoring apparatus
set so high that the monitoring becomes the event. He does not fear
what people will do. He monitors how he reads, and the monitoring
consumes the bandwidth that natural behavior would need. This is why
the anxiety's own formulation — delivered to him by a language model in
the mid-2025 session, accepted without contest — is architectural
rather than emotional: attention paid to the anxiety amplifies the
anxiety, a **positive-feedback control system**, not a fear with an
object.

Three corollaries follow from the claim and are all his own:

1. **Prevention makes it worse.** He stated this himself before the AI
   gave it a name: *"the more i pay attention, prevent and try to avoid
   problems... the objectively more i doom myself to them."* [testimony
   — relayed through the prior wiki's transcription of the session;
   the underlying chat exports are not held in this repo, per
   dat:0065] Avoidance is not relief; it is rehearsal. Every
   successfully-managed near-miss gets logged by the nervous system as
   "the threat was real and I escaped it," never as "the threat wasn't
   real" — so avoidance trains the anxiety to run hotter each cycle.
2. **Structure substitutes for courage.** A role with defined
   procedures — server, caddy, DJ, cam performer, open-mic comedian —
   converts an inference-dense environment into a rule-shaped one.
   What to say next, when to move, what counts as done: the role
   supplies the explicit procedures his cognition needs because it
   cannot metabolize interpretation. The 2018 line states the rule in
   his own voice: give him a function and the crowd size stops
   mattering.
3. **The anxiety is not the whole story of the condition.** A later,
   separate AI session — reviewing the same anxiety conversation as
   retrospective data rather than continuing it — argued the loop has
   little independent existence and is instead *parasitized* by two
   concrete, unresolved situations: the Annie relationship and the
   post-closure housing void. On that reading the loop is functioning
   as signal about real unresolved conditions, not noise to be
   corrected independently of them. [[wiki/health/hyperreflexivity]]
   holds the reframe in full; this page treats it as the standing
   rival account of the same territory.

The claim's evidence status is `mixed` on purpose. The self-description
is repeated and stable across years. The mechanism is named in his own
words and accepted by him without contest. The profile layer
corroborates the attention side. But no clinician has ever been in the
loop, the session the mechanism comes from is unheld, and the
strongest rival account says the loop is a symptom of unresolved
situations, not an entity.

## The phenomenology: performing, not being oneself

To [[wiki/people/ally-lubin|Ally Lubin]], on August 18, 2026 — the same
night she became the first outsider to read the wiki: *"I'm never myself
around people i feel like I'm performing."* [testimony-grade — relayed
through the prior wiki; the held corpus was searched and does not
contain this line] The line is kept because it names, in one sentence,
the phenomenology the counterevidence has to answer. A year and a half
earlier, inside the AI session, he had named the target the same way —
*"the anxious, internally probing awareness of my appearance in all
senses"* — with the emphasis on *all senses*: this is not appearance
anxiety about the face. It is awareness of the body as a read object,
in every channel.

The felt sequence, as the session documents it, runs:
notice discomfort → audit yourself to prevent it showing → the audit
consumes the bandwidth needed to act naturally → perform worse →
notice that → the audit escalates. The monitoring apparatus is itself
the problem generator. This is why "relax" is not an intervention that
could work: relaxation is a lowering of vigilance, and the vigilance
is the thing that cannot be stood down.

The body record supplies a physical toll that registers exactly the
way the mechanism predicts — damage scored as exposure. In the same
session, unprompted, he adds: *"maybe the most detrmiental of all is
that i recently lost 2 teeth, and one is like the 'vampire' tooth which
is super visible."* He supplies his own causal chain — *"bulimic as a
high schooler candy as a staple of my diet terrified of dentist (aka
haven't gone) suboxone cigarettes"* — five factors, no weighting, no
medical adjudication. What is first-person and dated is the outcome
and the wound he assigns it: he scores two lost teeth not as a health
event but as an image event — *"the additional self-consious,
humiliating image issue that comes with that,"* landing on top of an
already-collapsed confidence baseline after the Annie relationship's
end. [[wiki/health/hyperreflexivity]] records the chain and its
limits; this page notes the register, because the register is the
mechanism doing its work — damage re-enters the system as a new
surveillance object.

## The profile layer: what the instruments actually measured

The Big Five material is commissioned and self-reported — no
instrument name, no norm group, no administration date — but the
2026-08-16 lexical audit over 106,629 outbound messages (110,944
received as a within-medium control) gives three facets directional
corpus support, per [[wiki/mind/profile/big-five-psychometrics]]:

- **Self-Consciousness, 91st percentile** → self-monitoring language
  at 1.85× the control rate. The sensor gain is measured, not just
  claimed. This is the trait-level signature of hyperreflexivity:
  monitoring pointed outward at the body's legibility, which is
  exactly what the mechanism is.
- **Trust, 9th percentile** → suspicion-proxy language at 1.96×. New
  people start at guilty; the admission bar is the anxiety's front
  door. Confirmations decay fast and must be re-stated, never
  re-inferred — the same explicit-over-inferred filter the autism
  page treats architecturally.
- **Sociability, 3rd percentile** → initiation at 0.73×. He does not
  open; he is opened. The corpus-level behavioral residue of the
  self-description.

Against the grain, and the reason this section carries its own
caveat: **Impulsiveness at the 96th percentile — the highest score in
the entire instrument — has no lexical corroboration** (immediacy
language at 0.92× baseline). That is the single largest
trait-behavior gap in the profile, and a standing caution that the
self-report is not a measurement. The deviance audit's "extreme social
aversion" at 90/100 (confidence 99%, styled by the audit as "an
ethnographer of a species he barely interacts with") is a restatement
of the self-description, not an independent observation —
AI-generated testimony about a self-supplied corpus, with its source
dossiers not held in this repo (dat:0891). It is kept because the
convergence of an independent instrument's ranking with the
self-description is itself data: two self-sourced surfaces reading the
same shape. [profile-evidenced with the audits' own caveats —
big-five-psychometrics, deviance-mapping]

The audit's jurisdiction matters here, because it is the same
boundary the profile-first protocol enforces: the facets that
survive contact with the corpus are the ones about what he attends
to — aesthetics, patterns, other people's motives — and the facets
that vanish are the ones about interior regulation. Self-monitoring
in company is a claim about attention, which is precisely what this
page's mechanism is. The instrument can see the sensor gain. It
cannot see what happens inside it.

## The biography: everything that should contradict it, and why it doesn't

Nothing in the record contradicts the self-description. What the
record does is bound it — and the bound has a shape. Every sustained
exposure in the biography runs through a role with defined
procedures:

- **41 months front-of-house at Au Za'atar** (March 2021–August
  2024), his longest job, in the most socially exposed role a
  restaurant has. The server script — greet, take the order, run the
  food, drop the check — is the prosthetic.
- **Caddying, 2016–2019** — three years of continuous client contact
  on someone else's schedule. The tee sheet is the prosthetic.
- **A completed stand-up open mic, October 2, 2019** — the single
  most exposure-dense act in the biography, done once and never
  repeated. A set list is a script; the one-shot structure matters —
  it was completed, and it was not continued.
- **DJ sets, raves, camming** — performed exposure with a defined
  function and, in camming's case, income attached. The booth, the
  set, the show: all of them are positions from which the crowd is
  an audience rather than a room.

And the rule, in his own words, February 25, 2018: *"i'd rather be
in front of a crowd of 5,000 people, than in the middle of a room
with 500, because at least in front of 5,000 i'd have something to
do other than mingle."* The pattern is not "cannot be perceived." It
is **cannot tolerate unstructured, inference-dense contact** —
rooms, mingling, small talk with no script. The stage converts
inference-dense exposure into rule-bound exposure. Give him
something to do and the crowd size stops mattering.

Two more biographical facts belong here because they bound the
claim from the other direction. First, the deviance audit's
"radical rejection of normative life goals" (92/100) — career,
accumulation, and broad social connection consciously traded for "a
monastic pursuit of knowledge and intensity" — is the same
withdrawal scored as a choice rather than a symptom. The audit's
register ("ethnographer of a species he barely interacts with") is
testimony, but the decision structure it names is observable: the
life is organized around minimizing unstructured contact. Second,
the intimacy record complicates the aversion. He has sustained
intense, years-long bonds — Annie for a decade-plus, the eighteen-year
Ally channel, a marriage — which means the condition is not a
general inability to be close. It is a specific intolerance for the
unstructured middle: the room, the mingle, the interpretive social
space between a script and a bond.

## The mechanism: hyperreflexivity

In the mid-2025 session — dated by an internal employment reference
("*let's establish i've been working for the first time since june
2024 for the last 2 weeks. tomorrow i will hit 40 hours for this
week*") cross-referenced against the Au Za'atar page's dating of
the involuntary job loss (hours cut June–August 2024, unemployment
filed August 8, 2024), so roughly eleven months unemployed before
returning to full-time work — Dan asked an AI for a clinical read
on the condition and received the hyperreflexivity formulation.
[testimony-grade — dat:0065: the underlying chat exports, two of
them, ~4,200 lines combined, are not held in this repo; every
quotation is relayed through the prior wiki's transcription and is
unverified here]

The session's mechanical content has three load-bearing parts, all
held on [[wiki/health/hyperreflexivity]] and summarized here
because they are this page's mechanism:

1. **The loop.** Attention paid to the anxiety amplifies the
   anxiety — a positive-feedback control system. The monitoring
   apparatus is the problem generator.
2. **The avoidance ratchet.** Every successfully-managed near-miss
   gets logged as "the threat was real and I escaped it," never as
   "the threat wasn't real" — so avoidance trains the anxiety to
   run hotter, not lower, each cycle. Avoidance is rehearsal.
3. **The intervention it implies.** The session names ACT and
   exposure-based work — not generic therapy — as the intervention
   class the mechanism responds to. The asymmetry the record holds
   is that the mechanism is named, the effective intervention class
   is named, and **no engagement with either is documented anywhere
   in the corpus.** The mechanism's strongest recommendation has a
   dated result against it: named in mid-2025, not pursued by late
   2026.

The same session surfaced a pharmacological tension the wiki
recorded as an explicit contradiction between its own pages,
kept because the system records its disagreements rather than
tidying them. [testimony-grade — dat:0020: a language model's
reasoning, not a clinical source] Told that Dan has been on
"about 1-2mg daily" of Suboxone since 2011 (consistent with the
corpus's other dosage evidence — a July 2013 exchange gives 2mg
daily at roughly five years opiate-free, and the sixteen-year
unbroken maintenance arc on
[[wiki/health/chemical-architecture]]), the AI raised chronic
opioid-receptor occupancy as potentially blunting the hedonic range
— the highs come down, but so does baseline access to motivation
and reward-anticipation, the exact affective momentum needed to
break out of a slide. The claim is not that Suboxone causes the
anxiety loop; it is that the loop may be running on a floor that
is chemically capped — that the one regimen in the corpus never
interrupted may be stabilizing one system at the cost of limiting
the affective range the other system needs to escape its own
pattern. It has not been tested: the dose has not moved, and no
record ties any change in anxiety amplitude to the regimen.

The venue matters too. This is a self-initiated request for a
clinical read run through an AI rather than a clinician — a direct
instance of the "externalized prefrontal cortex" pattern the
AI-collaborative-analysis page documents across the rest of the
corpus: there is no recorded clinician anywhere in the corpus
doing this work. Asked directly what his current
treatment/support architecture looks like, he does not answer
anywhere in the retained exchange.

## The script as prosthetic

The structured role is not merely a coping aid. It is the same
substitution the [[wiki/mind/concepts/explicit-verbal-commitment|
explicit-verbal architecture]] performs in attachment: **a script
converts an inference-dense environment into a rule-shaped one.**
What to say next, when to move, what counts as done — the role
supplies the explicit procedures his cognition needs because it
cannot metabolize interpretation. The server who knows the steps
is the attachment partner who got the stated rule: both are
operating inside transmitted certainty rather than inferred
weather. The explicit-verbal page's reciprocal edge reads this
side the same way: "the architecture reads as compensatory
prosthetic for an inference-dense social world."

[status: conclusion — the structural parallel is evidenced on both
sides (the 2018 line, the tenure record, the explicit-verbal
measurements); the "prosthetic" framing is this page's synthesis]

Evidence for: every sustained social exposure in the biography is
role-mediated, and the one explicit statement of the rule is his
own. Evidence against: 41 months front-of-house still required
constant unscripted adaptation — the prosthetic leaks; and the
"performing" self-description suggests the role does not dissolve
the observation, it only organizes it. The leak is part of the
finding: **the script manages the anxiety, it does not cure it** —
which is why the tenure record and the self-description can both
be true. He worked a dining room for almost four years and still
says he is never himself around people.

The [[wiki/mind/concepts/institutional-out|institutional-out]]
parallel adds the employment-domain version of the same avoidance.
Dan's own February 2026 statement of the protocol — *"i ALWAYS
create an out when i get a new job. usually it's faking migraines
or something similar but i ALWAYS need a way to miss more work
than anyone else at whatever institution i am being paid by"*
[testimony] — is the mirror image of the script: the structure
that makes the job survivable is also the exit. Read the two pages
together and the shape is complete. The role is the prosthetic
that lets him be in the room; the out is the pre-installed escape
from the same room. The prosthetic and the exit are installed at
the same time — one makes the exposure tolerable, the other makes
the tolerance unnecessary to maintain. The institution is engaged
only through mechanisms it cannot verify or contest.

The July 2026 house move is the obvious partial test of the
reframe (anxiety as signal about unresolved conditions): one of
the two conditions the reframe names resolved — he completed the
move alone and describes sleeping in a bed for the first time in
some while — while the other got worse
([[wiki/timeline/events/july-2026-recontact]]). But the anxiety
around the move was never measured, so the test cannot decide.
The corpus records no state data for the window — this is a
resolved variable, not a result, worth re-checking against the
next export. A loop that stayed at full amplitude with the housing
void closed would narrow the reframe considerably.

## The autism intersection

Dan himself placed this condition inside the autism framing. In
the anxiety session — *Breaking the anxiety avoidance cycle.md* —
he asks whether "the advice and perspective most people would
need to receive is not applicable to someone as edge case autist
weirdo as I am" — the label used to challenge whether ordinary
anxiety advice reaches an edge-case architecture at all, while
simultaneously being unsure, in his own words, whether that move
is legitimate self-knowledge or a rationalization. The two pages
describe the same territory from opposite sides: the
[[wiki/mind/concepts/autism|autism]] page treats the
explicit-over-inferred processing architecture (the stages-vs-rooms
distinction is its behavioral instance), and this page treats the
felt experience of moving through rooms anyway.

The architecture the autism page documents makes the script
reading nearly mechanical. "EXPLICIT-STATEMENT WEIGHTING," from
`THE_DAN_FRANK_MANUAL.md`: he weights explicit symbolic
statements far above ambient behavioral signal, and the system
requires an explicit counter-rule to revise an established rule.
A room of 500 people with no script is a machine running on
inferred meaning — compliments, hints, register, the entire
ambient weather his system does not metabolize. A stage with a
set list is explicit-symbolic input. The anxiety is not a separate
condition sitting beside the processing architecture; on this
reading it is what the architecture feels like from inside when
the environment refuses to be rule-shaped.

> The *Breaking the anxiety avoidance cycle.md* session is dated to
> mid-2025 (June–August) via its internal employment reference
> (dat:0065); the standalone "SHUT UP I'M AUTISTIC" declaration is
> 2025-09-15 21:49:05 UTC (dat:0939). So the autism framing emerged
> first inside anxiety-help-seeking — deployed to question whether
> standard anxiety advice reaches an edge-case architecture — and
> only later appeared as a standalone identity declaration. Note
> the register caveat on the September message (work-0078,
> dat:1554: revised toward banter, recipient his sister).

Whether they are two labels for one system or two systems sharing
a channel remains open. No clinical diagnosis exists on either
page (dat:0940), and nothing on either page upgrades the other's
sourcing. The one falsifiable claim the autism label makes — its
defense instruction, predicting that an installed explicit
override would let the next bond close on behavioral evidence —
has a negative observation against it from August 2026: ~25
severance declarations across August 15–19, none executed, a
reported block that was never enacted, daily two-way texting
straight through. [[wiki/mind/concepts/autism]] holds both
surviving readings. This page notes the implication for the
anxiety: if the explicit-verbal patch does not install even when
written in the only channel the system honors, the no-exit
property — and the vigilance that comes with it — may be deeper
than a missing rule.

**THE SUBOXONE COMPLICATION — untested hypothesis, kept as such

## Assessment

The record supports treating this as a real, self-recognized
standing condition rather than a passing self-description. The
self-label is stable across years ("crippling social anxiety" is
the term he reaches for, not a label anyone put on him); the
mechanism is named in his own words and accepted without contest;
the profile layer measures the attention side at 1.85×; the body
record supplies a physical toll scored in the mechanism's own
register. What the record does *not* support is treating the loop
as a freestanding clinical entity. The follow-up reframe's
"parasitized" reading — the loop as signal about two unresolved
situations rather than noise — has the testable prediction and
the cleaner fit, and the July 2026 partial test is still open
because the anxiety in the window was never measured.

The unresolved hinge is treatment: the intervention class (ACT,
exposure) is documented, the engagement is not. Until something
in the record moves — a measured anxiety state during a resolved
window, an engagement with the named intervention, or the reframe
decided by data — the page's knowledge status stays `mixed`: a
condition described from the inside with unusual precision, a
mechanism named and unpursued, a biography that survives the
self-description by wearing a script.
