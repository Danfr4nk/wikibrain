# Two Phases of Dance-Only Listening

Dan's listening history contains two periods in which he listened to dance music exclusively — and the periodization is his own **[OPERATOR]**, commissioned 2026-09-11: the 2013 SLOPPP trap phase, and the 2025–current re-entry. The 2026-09-11 playlist forensics **[DERIVED]** corroborate both the switch and the shape of each phase, and they date the second one to the track. What the forensics add is the mechanism of the switch: in 2013 production led and listening followed; in 2025 listening led and production followed months later. The decade between them is the fallow one — and it is, almost exactly, the Annie decade.

## Phase 1: 2013–2016, the SLOPPP trap phase

The first dance-only phase is production-led. SLOPPP is Dan's earliest confirmed production alias, dated 2013-01-01 to 2016-03-07 on its page, the signature project of the Uniontown-return period — the recording-arts ambition surviving the NYC-career failure by migrating to a Uniontown SoundCloud in 2013. The operating record is unusually complete for an early alias: 2014 tweets showing release cadence, a Beatport entry, a public collab address, live streams, named peer support; Zachariah Harshman's January 2014 pitch of Dan to a Pittsburgh rave-scene contact, the earliest documented instance of SLOPPP seeking a live booking; and a December 2015 "Goodbye Demo" close. (The alias page's date range ends 2016-03-07 while its own connections say December 26, 2015 — a small source tension, preserved as a gap below rather than resolved.)

The listening correlate of phase 1 survives in the 5,321-track historical dump. Its top-artist roster reads as a trap/festival-era signature: `trap sounds` 162, Diplo 39, Yellow Claw 34, Major Lazer 29, Tropkillaz 23, Hucci 22, Lil Uzi Vert 21, Jack Ü 20, Keys N Krates 20, RL Grime 20, Skrillex 19, Troyboi 17 **[DERIVED — HIGH on the counts; the "2014–2017 trap/festival era" reading is MED]**. The dump is the archive of everything, not a phase-1 playlist — but its center of gravity is the era Dan was producing trap in, which is consistent with his framing that the listening of those years was dance-only. Production and listening were aligned on the same genre, under the same alias, in the same Uniontown rooms.

Phase 1 predates Annie (November 2015). The trap phase belongs to the pre-relationship life.

## The fallow decade: 2015–2025

The Annie decade (November 2015–August 2026) is, near-exactly, the fallow decade for dance-world participation. This is not the claim that he stopped making music — basement GRIPNOTIC development ran 2015–19 — but that the development was *solitary*: production without scene participation, the inverse of what the 2025 re-entry would do. The alias churn of the period (SLOPPP → MOGZART → Mogged Up → GRIPNOTIC) tracks experimentation and consolidation, but it happened in a basement, not a scene.

The curatorial record shows the same shape. Monthly Spotify playlists run 2011–2020, then jump to 2025 — the 2021–24 gap is the Au Za'atar employment block (March 2021–August 2024, 41 months), the longest job of his life swallowing the curatorial habit whole. The liked-songs timeline is starker: roughly 88 likes across the entire seven years 2017–2023 **[DERIVED — HIGH]**. The hand that now likes 600 tracks in a month was, for most of the Annie decade, not reaching for the button at all.

## The switch: 2025, dated to the track

The re-entry is listening-led, and the forensics date its mechanism precisely. Two bulk-like sessions in autumn 2025 — **September 29 (191 tracks) and November 8 (286 tracks)** — followed by a purge: 498 URIs present in the oldest Liked export are absent from both June 2026 exports **[DERIVED — HIGH on counts and dates]**. The purged content is the old canon: Elliott Smith (8), Paramore (6), All Time Low (5), Lana Del Rey (4), Bright Eyes (4), R.A. The Rugged Man (4), Tyler, the Creator, Pixies, Hey Monday, Atmosphere — emo, pop-punk, indie, alt-rap, the pre-dance taste. The September 29 session coincides with a `LIKED SONGS 29SEP25` Spotify playlist, a snapshot taken the same day. The reading — bulk nostalgia-like, then deliberate purge — is **MED-HIGH**: the timestamps and the disappearance both point there, and under the lyric qualifier it is suggestive that the one batch containing his stated lyricist fandoms was the batch removed from the working library while the bass canon stayed. The fandom lives in memory; the working library is timbre-first.

Then the build. The 2025 MASTER CRATE (232 tracks, full Spotify audio features) was assembled in four dated waves: **October 31 (170) → November 21 (+45) → December 9 (+16) → December 22 (+1)** **[DERIVED — HIGH]**. The liked timeline around it: 151 likes in May 2025, 122 in July, 270 in September, 153 in October, **628 in November**, then 58 in December — roughly 1,417 likes in 2025 against ~88 in the preceding seven years combined. The November spike is the crate-build month: of the crate tracks that appear in his liked set, 172 of 209 were liked in November 2025. The hear → Like → promote pipeline measures at **91.3%** liked-to-crate (209/229) on direct URI matching — the crate is a near-total selection *from* his listening, filtered for dancefloor function. The 20 crate tracks never liked are almost all post-November-11 additions: the workflow changed mid-build from selection-from-liked to direct-to-crate. Two removals between the November snapshot and the final crate: HOOLIGANS (version swap, the Ghastly-featuring version kept) and ELECTRIC LOVE (AVELLO — cut outright, the only melodic-bass removal; the genre barely exists in the crate otherwise).

The late-64 additions shifted the crate's profile measurably: energy 0.808 vs 0.849 for the first 170, tempo 130 vs 133, valence 0.30 vs 0.331, and the genre lean moved housier (bass house 17, tech house 12, stutter house 10 — the Fred again.. cluster) against the first 170's dubstep dominance (54). The crate's tail is where the stutter-house wing lives. The nine VIP crates (FINDERS_FEE, MaxOut, ULTRA_DEMi, GOODBYE_BPD_GIRLZ, FALL_2025_MEGAMIX, ALPHABET_BOYS_JAN26, flat_circle_mix, what_huh, FATSO_FLATSO) plus the 489-row MASTER_COLLECTED_CRATES super-crate extend the same three rooms into 2026 intake — two of them (GOODBYE_BPD_GIRLZ, FATSO_FLATSO) are dedicated liquid-dnb boxes. An Exportify session on December 29, 2025, 8:54 PM (via Chrome history) exported the crate mid-build: the re-entry was already being archived as data while it was still happening.

The re-entry lands exactly on the 2025 collapse — February–April 2025: the NYC exit, Annie's move to her parents — per Dan's own timeline confirmation, 2026-09-11 **[OPERATOR]**. The dance-only switch of phase 2 is not a hobby resuming; it is a life reorganizing, with the record box as the first rebuilt structure. And then production followed: the MOGZART DnB jump-up revival on March 4, 2026 ("Odd Mob – Never Alone (feat. Lizzy Land) [MOGZART DnB JUMP UP REMIX]" on SoundCloud) and the "oof" set in April 2026 — the first shipped output of the post-closure era. Listening rebuilt first; the alias came back for the second act.

## What the re-entry taste is

The filter stack, from the forensics **[DERIVED — HIGH on all counts; glosses MED]**:

- **Energy is the load-bearing filter.** Median 0.836, Q1 0.704 — three-quarters of the crate above 0.70, genre sprawling across 30+ tags. Remove the energy floor and the genre coherence dissolves.
- **Three BPM rooms, not one playlist.** 146 of 232 tracks (63%) in the 125–145 house corridor, a 19-track 165+ dnb wing, a 15-track sub-100 halftime basement. Three DJ sets.
- **The voice is an instrument.** 96.1% of the crate carries no foregrounded vocal performance; the largest segment (47.4%) is voice-as-texture — chops, samples, one-liners. Under the lyric qualifier this is not a paradox but a prediction: a man who hears lyrics as timbre and names JPEGMAFIA, Kanye West, and Elliott Smith as his gods is worshipping delivery, flow, cadence — vocal instruments — and the crate is the same appetite in the DJ room. His love of lyricists was never about bars; the crate proves it by containing almost none.
- **Dark, mid-fame, disloyal.** Median valence 0.322 (heavy, not happy); median Spotify popularity 43 (neither obscurity-mining nor chart-chasing); 321 artists and 162 labels with none above 2.6% — he follows the scene, not names or imprints. Acousticness median 0.019: fully electronic, no organic instruments.

The crate is **ammunition, not canon** (MED): 59.5% 2025 releases, 70.7% 2024–2025, zero exact track overlap with the 5,321-track historical dump, only 16 liked∩dump. The selection function is *this season's* box. Against that tightness, the two strangest entries are load-bearing precisely because they are unexplained: Shostakovich's "Jazz Suite No. 2: VI. Waltz 2" (energy 0.184, acousticness 0.984 — a classical waltz in a bass-house crate) and Frank Ocean's "Thinkin Bout You" (energy 0.329 — slow, sung, sincere, three things the box otherwise never does). A crate this tightly filtered doesn't admit accidents; whatever function those two serve is part of the taste, not noise. Dan has not yet said what it is.

## The two phases, held together

Phase 1 (2013–2016): production-led, trap, Uniontown rooms, pre-Annie — listening and making aligned under one alias. Phase 2 (2025–current): listening-led, bass-house/dnb, built in seven weeks on the floor of the 2025 collapse — listening rebuilt first, the alias revived for production a season later. Between them, the fallow decade: solitary basement production, the Au Za'atar curatorial gap, 88 likes in seven years. The sub-bass signature (63–85%) runs under all of it — the production constant across the listening rupture. The dance-only phases bookend the relationship: one before it, one after its effective end.

## Gaps

- The **three exceptions** to the lyric qualifier — the ~3 songs in his life whose lyrics he parsed as conceptual message — are still unnamed. When named, the analysis wants what broke the timbre-lock, not what the lyrics meant.
- The **Shostakovich waltz and "Thinkin Bout You"**: set-closer joke, genuine left turn, private function — Dan confirms or kills.
- **SLOPPP close-date tension**: the alias page dates the range end 2016-03-07 but its own connections say the December 26, 2015 "Goodbye Demo" was the close. Unresolved in the sources.
- **Pre-2013 listening**: what the first dance-only switch displaced. The emo/pop-punk canon (MCR, NFG, TBS, FOB, Say Anything — all present in the liked log, all purged November 2025) and the 2005–2008 tour-following years are the likely answer; the displacement itself is undated.
- **Phase-1 exclusivity**: "only listening to dance music" in 2013–2016 is Dan's framing **[OPERATOR]**; the dump corroborates trap/festival dominance but cannot prove exclusivity. The claim stands as his periodization, not a measured fact.
