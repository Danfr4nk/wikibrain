# The May–August 2025 Bridge

Between the April 27, 2025 clearing — the [[wiki/self/concepts/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] "impartial arbiter"
session — and the August 29 first contact with [[wiki/people/kristin|Kristin Prentiss]], there are
four months the wiki has never narrated. The hinge entry ends at the
clearing. The Kristin entry begins at first contact. In between: the
Saratoga landing, the basement, the return to work, a friendship started
and incinerated in twelve minutes, the wall of portraits, the heaviest AI
usage of his life, and the terminal turn of the [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] channel. The numbers
exist. The narrative doesn't. This entry reconstructs what the corpus
supports, and marks what it doesn't.

The thesis, stated so it can be checked: **the corridor is where the
collapse stops being an event and becomes a routine.** February through
April was a hinge — staged eviction, exit, clearing. May through August is
no longer a hinge; it is what life looked like with the hinge's
consequences already in place: a listed childhood home, a basement studio
beneath it, a pair-bond conducted over distance, a body maintained on a
prescription the pharmacy system kept failing to honor across state lines,
and an empty social calendar slowly being replaced by conversations with
machines. August is the month the routine breaks — cheating accusation,
burning ritual, the wall, the terminal turn — and September is the
construction. But May, June, and July are the floor the construction stood
on. That is why the bridge matters out of proportion to its event count:
every structure built after it was built on its floor.

## The thin record, stated as method

Before any month-by-month account, the honesty constraint. The message
corpus is thinnest exactly where this entry most needs it. The facts:

- The direction-reliable dump holds **zero of the 20,009 Kristin-thread
  messages** that begin August 29 — a coverage ceiling the Kristin page
  already documents — and the broader census is worse where it counts.
- A 2026-09-09 census of the authoritative corpus (192,140 rows, sha256
  re-pulled and manifest-verified) returns **35 messages in all of June
  2025**, against 21,290 outbound across the year **[DERIVED — HIGH;
  dat:0028]**.
- Two of the three mid-year prescriber quotes the prior wiki reported for
  June 8 and June 12 land on days where the corpus holds **zero messages
  at all**. The census node (dat:0028) rules explicitly: absence here is
  `never_observed`, not `known_not_to_occur` **[ATTESTED]**.
- The 2025 Annie volume is real in aggregate — **12,255 messages** in
  `annie_all_time_logs.csv` (December alone 4,657) — but the month-level
  split for May–August is not resolved in the record I can cite
  **[DERIVED — HIGH on the aggregate; UNKNOWN on the monthly split]**.

The consequence is structural: this entry runs on adjacent instruments —
AI chat exports (which peak in the corridor), the life-events calendar
(259 events for 2025, August 30 / September 97 / October 58), dated
quotations carried in other pages, and the two or three primary records
that survive with timestamps. Where the message record is absent, the
entry says so. The hole is the finding, not the excuse for one.

One inversion worth stating up front because it shapes everything after:
the corridor is the one stretch of the biography where the AI exports are
*thicker* than the human correspondence. The archived ChatGPT export
(375 conversations, 2022-12-10 → 2025-07-01) shows **May 2025 as the
heaviest month on record** — 142 conversations in the March–July window
alone **[DERIVED — HIGH, via wiki/self/concepts/chatgpt]**. As the human
channels thinned, the machine channels thickened. The bridge is, among
other things, the months he started talking to the models the way he used
to talk to people — and on 2025-07-10 he named it: *"i've been using that
as free therapy"* **[OPERATOR]**.

## May 2025: the landing, the listing, the wall of machines

The hinge entry's framing correction holds through this corridor and
sharpens it: the February 22 return was not a forced exit, and the landing
it produced was not temporary in his mind. By May he had been at 337
Saratoga Drive for two and a half months. What May's record shows is what
that landing was *inside of* — because in May 2025 the house was listed
for sale at **$615,000**, the first visible move of the Chapter 13
liquidation that would eventually close at $465,000 in June 2026
**[ATTESTED — [[wiki/places/337-saratoga-drive|the Saratoga page]] and [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|the Suz page]] agree on the ask and the
month]**. The "safe place to crash land" was on the market before he had
finished landing. The asking price would run down through a $500–550k
range; a June contract would terminate over inspection and pest reports;
the court's "drop dead" provision would attach in August. But in May, the
material fact of the month is the simplest one: the childhood home had a
price tag on it, and he was living under it.

The rest of May is scattered primary records — each small, each dated,
none of them addable to a day's shape, but together they set the month's
register:

  fall out boy albums and the ghost of gore vidal"* **[ATTESTED — the Fall
  Out Boy page]**. The deadpan register is intact; the content is his
  actual canon (first-three-albums cutoff, Vidal as in-joke groomsman).
  That same month he finished Michael Wolff's *All or Nothing* — all five

  summary]**.

What May does *not* contain is any record of production work, any record

month. That is the corridor's texture at its opening: the machines are
where the day goes.
## June 2025: eleven months, then two weeks

June is the corridor's hinge *inside* the corridor — the month the
unemployment ends and the house's sale attempt fails. Both events are
dated; both arrive through instruments other than the message record,
because the message record barely exists for the month.

**The return to work.** The single most material fact of the corridor
arrives unprompted, mid-conversation, in the AI session the wiki files
under hyperreflexivity: *"let's establish i've been working for the first
time since june 2024 for the last 2 weeks. tomorrow i will hit 40 hours
for this week"* **[OPERATOR — quoted in
wiki/health/hyperreflexivity]**. Cross-referenced against the Au Za'atar
page's own dating (hours cut through June–August 2024, unemployment filed
August 8, 2024), the session lands at roughly mid-2025 — **eleven months
unemployed, then a return to full-time work** **[DERIVED — HIGH; the page
itself does the dating]**. The employer is unnamed in every record that
mentions the job **[UNKNOWN — the corpus names no 2025 employer]**. The
wiki's standing constraint applies hard here: this is *not* BFS Foods.
BFS is April–May 2026, offered-then-unconfirmed rehire August 2026. The
2025 job is a different, unnamed job, and importing the 2026 one backward
is exactly the error this entry exists to prevent.

The AI's response in that session is worth preserving because it sets the
corridor's economic register: two weeks of functional output at full-week
volume says the problem is "more about access conditions than capability
ceiling," and Dan pushes back that it isn't a recovery arc because he
"can't and won't get" one "until there's a turnaround with improving life
circumstances and material conditions" **[OPERATOR — the session as
quoted]**. The conversation then pivots — in the same breath — into the
three compounding 2025 losses he names together: **Annie pulling away,
the sale of his childhood home, and the identity structure built around
the relationship going with it** **[OPERATOR — [[wiki/health/hyperreflexivity|the hyperreflexivity page]]'s
paraphrase]**. That triple is the corridor's emotional inventory, stated
by the only witness who was in the room: himself, talking to a machine.

**The failed contract.** The Saratoga listing's June chapter: a contract
terminated over inspection and pest reports **[ATTESTED — the Saratoga
page]**. The first sale attempt of the Chapter 13 liquidation fails in
the same month he goes back to work. The house keeps him and keeps not
selling — through the $500–550k range, toward the eventual $465k, toward
the August "drop dead" provision. Nothing in the record shows Dan
involved in any of it **[INFERRED — no messages, no documents, no
mentions; the transaction is Suz's and the court's]**.

**The prescriber episode [DISPUTED — held open].** The prior wiki quotes
a June 8–13 sequence: *"my doctor said the pharmacy won't fill an out of
state prescription"* (June 8), *"my doctor tried to send a prescription
to walmart and they won't fill it either so i'm completely out of options
now"* (June 12), *"I am so fucked. I'm gonna have to drive down to see Tom
today"* (June 13) **[OPERATOR per the prior wiki — the supply-network
page carries the same quotes]**. Against that, dat:0028: the authoritative
corpus holds **zero messages on June 8 and zero on June 12**, and none of
the three 2025 quotes is verbatim present anywhere in the 192,140-row
re-pull **[ATTESTED — the census node]**. The standing rule for this
entry: the episode is `never_observed`, not `known_not_to_occur`. What
*is* attested by a separate instrument is the state the quotes describe:
by June 2025 the prescription is the default Suboxone route with the
dealer network as failover **[ATTESTED — the agent-D health timeline]**,
and Tom Maison is the named failover — the "drive down to see Tom"
ending, whether or not the quoted messages survive verification, is
consistent with everything the supply record establishes about Tom's
standing role **[INFERRED — MED]**.

June's texture, then: a new job with no name, a house that won't sell, a
prescription the pharmacy system won't honor across state lines, and a
corpus that holds 35 messages for the whole month. The three compounding
losses, named to a machine in the same session the job is announced. This
is the corridor's floor — the most ordinary month of the four, and the
one the record can say least about.

## July 2025: the dossier, the gig's last ember, the machines continue

July has one hard event, one datable cultural footnote, and a background
hum of AI sessions. The hard event is the corridor's most consequential
hour.

**July 10–11: the James Analysis PDF.** The sequence, from the forensic-
method page's table: on **July 10**, Dan sends James a ChatGPT profile of
*himself* — *"i've been using that as free therapy"*, evangelism, accepted,
opens the channel. On **July 11**, he hands James the analysis of *James*
— a two-person message log run through a model into a dossier, handed to
its subject, "a favor, curated to be kind." **The channel closed in twelve
minutes.** Afterward he commissioned a third analysis — of himself, de-
armoring by example — and no record shows it was ever sent
**[ATTESTED — [[wiki/timeline/events/james-analysis-pdf|the james-analysis-pdf event page]] and the forensic-method
table agree on the dates and the outcomes]**.

The wiki's 2026-08-18 correction is the reason this belongs in the
corridor's center rather than as a footnote: the July 2026 Leviathan
dashboards were *not* the method's first outward deployment. The first was
here, July 11, 2025 — a year and two weeks earlier — and its social cost
was immediate and total: twelve minutes from delivery to severance
**[ATTESTED — the correction notice]**. The James thread itself runs May
27 to December 23, 2025 (194 iMessages, 54 sent / ~140 received), but the
event that defines it took twelve minutes in July. The lesson Dan
evidently did not take — the only variable that ever changed across
deployments was intent, generosity in 2025 and leverage in 2026, and the
reception was hostile both times — is filed under the forensic-method
page, not here **[INFERRED — MED; no July-2025 record shows him drawing
the lesson]**.

July's other dated traces:

  show i went to is the first and ONLY time that [Take This To Your Grave]
  was played in its entirety"* **[ATTESTED — texted, per the Fall Out Boy
  page]**. The forensic-archival reflex applied to his own adolescence:
  a 2007 Buffalo secret show, run through ChatGPT for verification. The

- **July 25** — the last located Paci correspondence: two messages, neither
  answered, and nothing in Paci's own voice past this date
  **[ATTESTED — [[wiki/people/john-paci|the john-paci page]]]**. The $10,000 landlord debt keeps

- **July (undated in the month)** — Annie's reaction to a Paci text,
  *"Fucking John Paci just texted me"* **[ATTESTED — the annie-ulmer
  page]**, months after Dan left New York — consistent with the debt

- The ChatGPT export runs through **2025-07-01** (375 conversations);

  covers — the instrument goes dark as a record exactly when the corridor

What July does not contain: any production record, any social outing, any

began May 27 is over by the 11th. What remains of the month is the
machines and the debt — both humming, neither resolving.
## August 2025: the terminal turn

**The burning ritual.** *"One more load to burn..."* — the ritual

**August 3: the silence and the reply.** One of the twelve documented
suicidal-intent statements: a 12-hour-11-minute silence, then *"Are you
alright Dan"* **[ATTESTED — [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|the annie-ulmer page]]]**. The fallout verdict's
audit found the pattern had no exception: all 94 of Dan's message-burst
events were preceded by her going quiet, and in not one of the 94 did she
acknowledge the silence as the cause on re-engaging **[ATTESTED — the
verdict's numbers]**. August 3 is that pattern's early-form instance —

hours late and engaging with nothing. The verbal-abuse monthly count for

zero is the anomaly, not August's nine.

**The GPS removal.** She removed location sharing in August 2025, after a
night at her ex-partner's residence — ending a ten-year mutual norm

architecture of the relationship being dismantled from her side while the

channel load-bearing. August is where the wiki's "dual reading" of the
removal lives: surveillance or self-protection, both readings cited, the
record not settling it **[ATTESTED as contested — the page carries both]**.

**August 1–2: the wall.** In the first days of August, Dan printed
dozens of black-and-white portraits of women and put them up on his

**[OPERATOR — [[wiki/mind/synthesis/kristin-channel|the kristin-channel page]]]**. The stated purpose: *"we need
to show kristin my wall of despair"* — an audience of one, named nearly
four weeks before first contact. The portraits are also the raw material
of the attraction-guide workstream before they were a courtship prop
**[ATTESTED — [[wiki/mind/synthesis/kristin-channel|the kristin-channel page]]]**. Tom's comment on it is what
gave the wiki the "Wall of Despair" name as a life-anchor

built to impress a woman he had never spoken to. Whatever the Kristin
channel became — 22,018 messages, ten weeks, forty dollars — it began

**August 24: the verdict on the machines.** At 11:23 PM EST, a Gemini

him using ChatGPT as free therapy and closes with him declaring it
cooked — to Gemini, at night, in five words. The migration of the
exocortex is complete inside the window.
