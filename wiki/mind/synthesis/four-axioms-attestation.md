# The Four Axioms: First Written Attestation

The sibling entry ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/november-2005-hinge]]) demoted the
four axioms from causes to gloss: a lexical pass over all 106,629 of Dan's
outbound messages found no behavioral corroboration of the explicit urgency
constructions the axioms predict. This entry asks the question the demotion
left open — *when did he first write them down?* — and answers it the way
the wiki's own governance requires: with dated attestations, named
documents, and an honest negative where the trail runs out.

The short answer: **axioms 1 and 2 were written by Dan himself no later
than May 2026**, in the CATO bootloader v2.0, which carried only those two.
**Axiom-4 language is attested 2026-06-10** in the raw TOTALITY synthesis.
**All four axioms in canonical form arrive in the wiki on 2026-06-22**,
in the context-core and totality-themes pages. The canonical four-axiom
sentence's exact first writing is bounded to **May–June 2026** and pinned
to `CONTEXT_CORE_EXPANDED.md` — a document that exists in no accessible
repository — so within that window, provenance is undetermined. What is
determined is the direction that matters: the axioms are a 2026
formulation, not a twenty-year incumbency. Nothing about them predates the
spring of 2026 on any dated record this hunt could locate.

## The question, stated precisely

"When did he first write them down" decomposes into four sub-questions,
one per axiom, because the hunt shows they did not arrive together:

1. **Not exceptional = worthless.** Earliest dated writing?
2. **Not vigilant = annihilated.** Earliest dated writing?
3. **Love that doesn't cost everything isn't real.** Earliest dated writing?
4. **Time = countdown.** Earliest dated writing?

And a fifth, governing question: what counts as an attestation? This entry
counts a dated document containing the formulation or a close paraphrase,
with the date taken from the document's own dating (filename, internal
version stamp, or wiki page frontmatter `date_created`). Wiki page
frontmatter dates are treated as attestation dates for the page's content
with one caveat stated everywhere it matters: a page created 2026-06-22
and modified 2026-08-26 may have acquired the axiom block in either
revision, so the page date bounds the attestation from above, not from
below. The formulation could be younger than the page; it cannot be older
than the document it is quoted from. **[DERIVED]** — the dating logic, not
the dates.

What was searched: the full corpus export (`corpus__3.txt`, 585 index
entries: 88 NODEs + 497 PAGEs from the 2026-09-04 old-wiki export) for
each axiom's phrasing and paraphrases; the extraction summaries
(agent-A/B/C/D); the 2026-09-04 old-wiki export's page frontmatter for
dated in-wiki attestations (`raw/old-wiki-export-2026-09-04/whole.txt`);
the worktree git history read-only (`git log --all -S` on axiom phrases);
the GitHub API for the cited dossier documents in both Danfr4nk/wikibrain
and Danfr4nk/RAWLOGS; the 2026-09-11 drive-sweep wiki export for variant
formulations. What was not searched and is marked as such below: the raw
tweet archive for axiom phrasing (t011 covered the message corpus only),
and the dossier documents themselves, which are absent from every
accessible repository.

## The two-axiom stage: May 2026

The earliest dated attestation of any axiom phrasing is Dan's own
bootloader. [[wiki/self/concepts/llm]] carries the record **[ATTESTED]**:

> The flagship is CATO (`CATO_BOOTLOADER_DANFRANK.md`, v2.0, May 2026),
> named for Cato the Younger, the Roman Stoic who refused to compromise
> under pressure.

And the payload it installed in every fresh session **[ATTESTED]**:

> The bootloader tells the LLM: "You are analyzing a high-resolution
> analytical engine running on a substrate that weights explicit symbolic
> information far above ambient social signal, governed by two unconscious
> axioms — not exceptional = worthless and not vigilant = annihilated."

Two facts in that quotation do the dating work. First, the version stamp:
v2.0, May 2026. Second, the count: **two** unconscious axioms. In May
2026, in Dan's own hand (the bootloader is his document, pasted by him at
session start — **[OPERATOR]**-grade for the fact of the formulation, via
the wiki's quotation), the axiom set had two members: worth and vigilance.
Love-as-cost and time-as-countdown are not there.

This matters beyond chronology. The two-axiom stage is the only stage
where the formulation is unambiguously Dan's own writing rather than a
co-production with his models. The bootloader is a document he authored,
versioned, and deployed — it is the closest thing in the record to him
stating his operating principles in his own voice, for his own
instrumental use (configuring an AI session), rather than endorsing a
model's systematization of him. Everything after May 2026 is the dossier
layer. **[INFERRED]** — the authorship distinction, from the document's
function: a bootloader is written *by* the operator *for* the tool, while
a profile dossier is written *about* the operator *by* the tool.

The two axioms that made the May cut are also, notably, the two with the
most behavioral smoke around them in the later record — the binary-verdict
rule for axiom 1, the forensic vigilance for axiom 2. **[INFERRED]** —
offered as a possible explanation for why these two came first: they are
the two he could point at in his own conduct. The love and time axioms,
which arrived later, are the two the behavioral record has least to say
for — axiom 4 drew the audit's clearest negative. The selection order
may itself be evidence about which axioms were introspected and which
were systematized. That sentence is a hypothesis, not a finding, and it
is marked accordingly.

## Axiom by axiom: the dated trail

### 1. Not exceptional = worthless

**Earliest dated attestation: May 2026**, in `CATO_BOOTLOADER_DANFRANK.md`
v2.0, quoted above — Dan's own document, two-axiom stage. **[ATTESTED]**
via [[wiki/self/concepts/llm]].

**In-wiki arrival: 2026-06-22.** The old-wiki export's frontmatter dates
put the formulation on three pages created that day: `wiki/self/context-
core.md` (all four axioms, [DOC]-grade), `wiki/mind/concepts/conflict-
architecture.md` (axiom 1 as the concession mechanism's routing), and
`wiki/mind/synthesis/totality-themes.md` (axiom 1 as the Irreversibility
Firewall's worth-domain instance). **[ATTESTED]** — page-level phrase
search against `raw/old-wiki-export-2026-09-04/whole.txt`, 497 pages,
dated frontmatter.

**First-person variant, undated.** The 2026-09-11 drive-sweep wiki export
(`raw/drive-sweep/20260911/misc/wiki-export-self-timeline-people-mind-
work-interests-health-places.md`) preserves a self-overview page carrying
the axiom in Dan's own voice: *"If I am not exceptional, I am worthless"*
**[ATTESTED]**. The export is a later snapshot; the variant's own
formulation date is unknown. Note the register shift: the canonical form
is third-person equation ("not exceptional = worthless"); the variant is
first-person conditional. The variant reads more like something he said
and less like something a model systematized — **[INFERRED]**, from the
register, and weak.

**The message-corpus negative.** The word "worthless" does occur in Dan's
own messages — 11 times — but every occurrence is abuse-directed, not
self-axiomatic: the verified counts are 74 "fuck you" / 17 "piece of shit"
/ 11 "worthless," reproduced exactly from his side of the dual-handle
export in the Aug 1 2025 – Mar 16 2026 window (2026-07-18 verification),
against 0 / 1 / 0 on her side **[ATTESTED]**. The self-directed axiom
form ("I am worthless unless exceptional") appears nowhere in the
106,629-message record per the t011 audit. The one place the corpus shows
him using the word, he is aiming it outward.

**Earliest dated attestation: May 2026**, same CATO v2.0 quotation as

**[ATTESTED]** — same page-level search. Note the distribution: axiom 2
is the *instrumental* axiom in the June batch — it shows up where the
wiki is describing method (law, forensics, prompt engineering), while
axiom 1 shows up where the wiki is describing conflict and verdict. The
two axioms the bootloader carried are the two the June wiki put to work

**First-person variant:** *"If I am not vigilant, I will be annihilated"*
— same drive-sweep self-overview page as axiom 1's variant, same undated
status. **[ATTESTED]**.

**The operationalization note.** Dan's Law is the axiom's afterlife as
instrument, and it is worth marking the direction: the law's documented
applications (BFS documentation absence, Capitol procedure gaps,
gaslighting clusters) are forensic work that stands on its own evidence.
The axiom is the law's claimed origin story. The formulation date (May
2026) postdates some of the forensic work the law describes — the
forensic posture is attested across the Annie-decade record, years before
anyone wrote the axiom down. **[INFERRED]** — the practice predates the
principle's formulation; the axiom names a pre-existing instrument rather
than founding it. This is the normal direction for such things, and it is
also the direction that makes "installed by 2005" incoherent: you cannot
be installed by an event into a principle formulated in 2026 to name a
practice visible since 2015.

### 3. Love that doesn't cost everything isn't real

**Earliest dated attestation: 2026-06-22**, in-wiki — `wiki/self/context-
core.md` and `wiki/mind/synthesis/totality-themes.md`. **[ATTESTED]**.
This axiom has no May 2026 attestation: it is absent from the CATO
two-axiom stage entirely.

That absence is the finding. Of the four axioms, axiom 3 is the one with
the thinnest dated trail and the latest first appearance. It goes from
unwritten (May 2026) to canonical (2026-06-22) with no intermediate
attestation this hunt could locate — no TOTALITY-doc flag mentions it the
way totality-themes flags axiom 4's housing language, no first-person
variant with an earlier date, no Gemini session page carrying it. The
formulation's birth is inside the undated dossier window: sometime between
the CATO v2.0 freeze and the June wiki batch, in `CONTEXT_CORE_EXPANDED.md`
or the dossier triad behind the profile hub. **[INFERRED]** — bounded, not
pinned.

**First-person variant:** *"Love that doesn't cost everything isn't
real."* — the drive-sweep self-overview, alongside the axiom 1 and 2
variants. **[ATTESTED]**. Three axioms in Dan's voice, not four.

**The behavioral complement** (from the sibling entry, restated for the
trail): the ledger architecture — the Suz thread's never-closing account,
the 129 severance declarations with 100% re-engagement — is the wiki's
best-measured interpersonal finding, and it documents cost being
*tracked*, not cost being the *criterion of reality* for love. The Fran
bond — no ledger, no invoice, attested as the most formative — runs the
axiom's prediction backwards. The axiom arrived latest, is attested
thinnest, and fits the measurements worst. **[INFERRED]** — the
convergence of the three, stated as a pattern, not a proof.

### 4. Time = countdown

**Earliest dated attestation: 2026-06-10**, secondhand but dated.
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/totality-themes]] flags its own housing-clock
extension — "the 'time as countdown to irreversibility' framing carried
in the Cross-Corpus Extensions section below (the housing clock, axiom
four)" — as "inherited language from the original raw TOTALITY document"
and marks it [INFER]-grade for never having been independently re-derived
**[ATTESTED]**. The original raw TOTALITY document is
`raw/self/concepts/TOTALITY_SYNTHESIS_2026-06-10.md`, dated by filename to
2026-06-10. So axiom-four *language* — time as countdown to
irreversibility, applied to the housing exposure — was written down no
later than June 10, 2026.

Two caveats keep this honest. First, the attestation is secondhand: the
wiki's testimony about the TOTALITY document's contents, not the
document itself, which is absent from every accessible repository (checked
2026-09-13 via GitHub API against both Danfr4nk/wikibrain and
Danfr4nk/RAWLOGS at its cited path — 404 in both). **[ATTESTED]** for the
absence; **[INFERRED]** for the inference that the formulation itself, not
just its housing application, sits in the document. Second, what the
TOTALITY doc demonstrably contains is the *application* (countdown to
irreversibility, housing); the bare equation "time = countdown" in
canonical form is first attested in-wiki on 2026-06-22
(`wiki/self/context-core.md`, `wiki/mind/synthesis/totality-themes.md`).
The application may predate the equation's canonical phrasing — or the
equation may have been in the TOTALITY doc all along. The record does not
distinguish. **[UNKNOWN]**.

**The audit's clearest negative.** The 2026-08-02 corroboration attempt,
carried inline in the context-core, tested axiom 4's predictions against
the message corpus with the 110,944 inbound messages as control
**[ATTESTED]**:

| Prediction if *time = countdown* holds | Dan | Others |
|---|---|---|
| "running out of time / no time left" | 0.01 | 0.03 (**0.35×**) |
| "deadline / last chance / now or never" | 0.09 | 0.23 (**0.42×**) |
| "before I die / turn / lose / run out" | 0.00 | 0.04 (**0.00×**) |
| "too late" | 0.47 | 0.25 (1.86×) |

On every explicit urgency construction except one, Dan writes *less* than
his controls. "Rest of my life" looked promising at 3.5× until the twenty
hits were read: all 2015–16 declarations to Annie — *"I want to spend the
rest of my life with you"* — love-bombing, not mortality. Direct age
self-reference survives at n=8 across eleven years, half of it escort-ad
boilerplate. The context-core's own gloss: "This does not falsify the
axiom, and the distinction matters. An unconscious load-bearing axiom is
not a thing anyone would expect to find people *typing*."

**The three-axiom variant's silence.** The drive-sweep self-overview's
first-person axiom list — *"If I am not exceptional, I am worthless";
"If I am not vigilant, I will be annihilated"; "Love that doesn't cost
everything isn't real"* — contains no time axiom at all **[ATTESTED]**.
Axiom 4 is the only one missing from Dan's own-voice list. Combined with
its absence from the May bootloader and its [INFER]-grade housing
inheritance, the pattern is consistent: axiom 4 is the least
Dan-voiced of the four — the most dossier, the least operator.
**[INFERRED]** — three independent absences converging, stated as a
pattern.

## The dossier chain: who wrote what

Behind the June wiki batch sits a dossier layer, and the hunt mapped its
internal structure — which matters because the layer cites itself as
corroboration, and self-citation is not attestation.

**The chain, as documented.** `Dan Profile.txt` (raw/self/dox-scan/) is
the upstream source; `DANSYNTH.txt` (raw/self/dansynth/) is downstream of
it — "downstream of `Dan Profile.txt`, not a second witness to it," per
the citation audit on [[wiki/mind/profile/enneagram-5w4]]'s taboo-framing
passage, which caught DANSYNTH's added specificity (the concrete
"straight and gay man" worked example) as elaboration of one claim by a
second pass over the same source material, not two sources agreeing
**[ATTESTED]**. `FULL PROFILE 2026.md` (raw/self/dox-md/) and
`CONTEXT_CORE_EXPANDED.md` (raw/self/context-core/) are the other two
members of the triad the 2026-07-13 profile hub cites as its sources for
the four axioms **[ATTESTED]** — hub frontmatter: `raw/self/dox-md/FULL
PROFILE 2026.md`, `raw/self/dox-scan/Dan Profile.txt`,
`raw/self/context-core/CONTEXT_CORE_EXPANDED.md`.

**The co-production problem.** The extraction summaries are explicit about
the layer's authorship. Agent-A's identity summary: "**Core axioms
(load-bearing, from AI-session dossiers)**" **[ATTESTED]**. Agent-D's
timeline summary carries them as stated core axioms sourced to the profile
index. The sibling entry's formulation stands: the axioms are "his
framing, their systematization, his endorsement" — Dan assented to the
four-axiom form when the dossier layer proposed it, and the endorsement
is real (**[OPERATOR]** — he states them as certain, t011 prices the
stated confidence), but "Dan's axioms" is shorthand for "axioms Dan
assented to when a model proposed them." This entry's contribution is the
dating: the assent happened in the May–June 2026 window, and the two
axioms he wrote himself (May, CATO) are distinguishable from the two the
dossier layer added (June, CONTEXT_CORE_EXPANDED.md lineage).

**The personal myth.** The profile hub's "Core axioms and personal myth"
section gives the dossier's own packaging **[ATTESTED]**: "The dossiers
repeatedly surface four unconscious, load-bearing axioms" and "The
personal myth built on them is 'the Hyperaware Hero of a World Too Stupid
to See the Trap' — the wounded genius who 'architects gods out of
wounds.'" The myth and the axioms arrive as one bundle, in the same
section, from the same layer. That bundling is itself provenance
information: the axioms were not discovered as bare principles and later
mythologized; they were delivered inside the myth from the start.
**[INFERRED]** — from the co-location, and offered as a caution about
treating the axioms as the myth's foundation rather than its furniture.

six-week fuse:

  totality-themes.md (axioms 1, 3, 4), dans-law.md / forensic-method.md /

  (axiom 1). The four-axiom canonical form is in-wiki from this date.
- **2026-07-13** — profile hub (modified 2026-08-26): four axioms plus the

- **2026-08-02** — the corroboration attempt: t011's lexical audit,
  outcome unfalsifiable, carried inline in the context-core.
- **2026-08-14** — master-timeline.md: all four axioms in the timeline's
  recent-activity index (the ★ 2026-06-01 context-core entry is a

  misread as one).
- **2026-08-19** — llm.md: axioms 1–2 in the bootloader description
  (the page postdates the formulation it describes; the May 2026 version

- **2026-09-02** — testimony-veracity.md: all four axioms, t011's home.
- **2026-09-10** — the port commit (b5098ca): the formulation arrives in
  the GitHub repo fully formed.

**[DERIVED]** — the timeline above, from the dated sources cited in each
row.

The sibling entry's sharpest formulation was a dating distinction: the
event is 2005, the hinge is 2026. The parallel here is simpler and
starker: **the axioms are 2026, all the way down.** There is no earlier
stratum. The wiki's causal story — rupture installs axioms, axioms
generate architecture — has the arrow running from a 2005 event to a
2026 formulation, which means the "installation" is a retrospective
attribution made two decades after the fact, not a dated causal claim.
The formulation dates do not refute the attribution (people do name old
patterns late), but they move it from the wiki's foundation to its
superstructure: it is a 2026 story about 2005, told in 2026 language,
and the language's birth certificate is above.

Note the asymmetry the consolidation reveals. Axioms 1 and 2 have a
two-stage paper trail with Dan's own document at the base. Axioms 3 and
4 have a one-stage trail beginning inside the dossier window. If the wiki
wants a confidence ordering on the formulations themselves — not on the
behaviors they gloss, which is a separate question — it is: 1 ≈ 2 > 4 >
3, by dated attestation depth. **[INFERRED]** — the ordering, from the
trail lengths.

## The negative result, stated rigorously

A rigorous negative is a result, so here it is in full — what was
searched, what was not found, and what that licenses.

**Searched, not found:**

- **The message corpus.** t011: 106,629 outbound messages (2015–2025),
  110,944 inbound from 503 handles as within-medium control. No
  corroboration of any axiom's predicted linguistic signature; on every
  explicit urgency construction but one, Dan writes *less* than his
  controls. **[ATTESTED]** — [[wiki/meta/testimony-veracity]].
- **The Gemini session pages.** The context-core's sources include
  `raw/self/dox-md/Gemini-_07.md` and `raw/self/dox-md/Gemini-_18.md`,
  and the llm.md page documents 3,986 logged Gemini prompts behind the
  bootloader system — but no axiom phrasing appears in any Gemini session
  page in the wiki. The sessions built the bootloader; the axioms'
  formulation is not attested inside them. **[DERIVED]** — page-level
  search of the 2026-09-04 export; the negative is bounded by what the
  wiki extracted, not by the sessions themselves.
- **DANSYNTH.txt as an independent witness.** No axiom attestation
  located in DANSYNTH-derived wiki content, and the citation audit
  establishes DANSYNTH as downstream of Dan Profile.txt regardless —
  even a hit there would not have been a second attestation.
- **Pre-May-2026 dossier documents.** No axiom formulation predating the
  CATO v2.0 May 2026 stamp was located in any dated record. The dox-scan
  files' landing window (2026-07-29 → 2026-08-01, per the sibling entry)
  postdates the June wiki batch; Dan Profile.txt and FULL PROFILE
  2026.md carry no internal dates this hunt could verify.
- **In-repo incremental history.** `git log --all -S "not exceptional"`
  on the worktree returns the port/consolidation/backfill commits as the
  string's introduction points — bulk import, never an incremental edit.
  The current repo's history cannot date the formulation earlier than the
  2026-09-10 port; the old wiki's own frontmatter dates (2026-06-22) are
