# Music Taste Report — Bing Bong Update, Oct 20 – 23, 2026
_Layer: interpreted/synthesis — connects two or more concepts. It names the self/ exhibits it reasons over and cites them rather than restating them._

Three days after the Bing Bong deviation was filed, Dan delivered the updated export with one word: "updates." The update is small — thirteen tracks — and it is exactly the kind of small that a recursive profile exists to catch, because almost everything version 3 said the next version should watch for is either answered or sharpened by it. Nothing was purged. The UK garage monoculture that ended the prior week added not one more track. The overnight binge pattern that built half the list vanished entirely — all thirteen new tracks arrived in the daytime, in shrinking sessions of five, four, two, and two. In their place: a new artist bloc inhaled in album pairs (NXSTY, four tracks, the JONCASS pattern repeating with a new name), a quiet return of canon personnel through the side door (ero808, rSUN, bystander, kenu — four of the thirteen adds touch the 'This Shit Is Personal' canon), and the brightest intake slice yet measured, whose median valence of 0.393 nonetheless moves the full list's median only from 0.322 to 0.325. The deviation did not continue in the direction it was pointing. It forked.

## Grading version 3's predictions

Version 3 closed with four predictions made to be graded, not admired. The grades:

**1. Purge asymmetry (dubstep/riddim purges faster than UK garage) — UNTESTED.** Zero tracks were removed from the list in the window. UK garage / bassline-tagged tracks: 20 of 20 retained. Dubstep / riddim / deathstep-tagged tracks: 14 of 14 retained. A purge prediction cannot be graded on a window with no purge; it carries forward unchanged. The honest secondary fact is that 100% retention at day 3–8 is itself information: whatever the overnight UKG session was, it has not been regretted yet.

**2. The JONCASS test — NO RECURRENCE.** Version 3 predicted the Mary Droppinz / SVDKO / JONCASS bloc (5 tracks, one Oct 18 movement) would either recur in the next intake window or appear nowhere, with no middle outcome. It appears nowhere: zero of the 13 adds credit JONCASS, SVDKO, or Mary Droppinz. By the prediction's own construction, this classifies the bloc as a single-release inhalation rather than an adoption — with the standing caveat that the window is three days, and all five tracks remain on the list, unpurged. Not adopted; not regretted. Filed.

**3. Tempo reversion or ratification — RATIFICATION LEG.** Version 3 staked the test on the next export's median: back within 128–133 would reclassify the deviation as tourism; at or above 136 would ratify the re-centering. The added slice medians at **140.0** and the full 69-track list at **139.0** — both clear the ratification line. Caveat recorded with the grade: the added slice is bimodal, not centered — 4 of 13 sit in the 125–131 home room (the highest home-room share of any slice yet, 30.8%) and 6 of 13 sit above 145, including three detonation-class tracks at 172–180. The median ratifies; the shape says the 140 shelf and the detonation slot are now co-primary, with the home room returning as a real third pole rather than a fringe.

**4. The doorway track outlasts its session — RETAINED, UNGRADED ON ROTATION.** "Love Is Gonna Save Us" (ARTBAT; Benny Benassi) remains on the list. Version 3's claim was about active rotation, and this series still has no play-count data for the list — intake and retention are all an export can show. The track survives; the salience-law bet it carried remains open, and the measurement debt that blocks it (no rotation data) is restated below rather than papered over.

## What the thirteen actually did

**The session shape inverted.** Version 3's week was built overnight: a recount from the export's own timestamps gives 28 of the prior 56 adds between 00:00 and 06:00 ET (version 3's prose said 29; its own enumerated overnight list contains 28 — the recount is adopted here). The update window: **0 of 13 overnight.** The five-track Oct 20 session ran 16:26–16:34 ET — beginning roughly an hour after the first export was handed over, as though the digging simply continued in daylight. Intake rate fell from ~10 tracks/day to ~3.5/day, and session sizes decayed 5, 4, 2, 2. Whether this is a schedule artifact or a genuine shift from binge-digging to grazing is a next-version question (prediction 3 below); what is not in question is that it happened.

**UK garage stopped; bass house and scatter took the slot.** Of the 13 adds, zero carry a UK garage tag — the genre that supplied 15 tracks in 35 minutes to close the prior week contributed nothing at all. Bass house is the update's top tag (4 hits, all four NXSTY tracks). Around it, the Oct 20 afternoon session reprises the prior week's Oct 15 scatter in miniature: crunk, jersey club, EDM trap, untagged rap — and the hip-hop presence that version 3 noted "arrived in the first seeding day and did not recur" has now recurred, three days later, in the same seeding-day role: first session of a new window, then gone again.

**The NXSTY inhalation.** Four tracks, one artist, two sources, two days: on Oct 21 evening, two tracks from ero808 & NXSTY's *MIXTAPE ONE* (2024) — a back-catalog dig, the update's only pre-2026 material; on Oct 22 afternoon, two tracks from NXSTY's *Break The Body EP*, released Oct 16, added 91 seconds apart. This is structurally identical to the JONCASS event version 3 flagged — whole-release inhalation in a single sitting — at 4 tracks against JONCASS's 5. Version 3's framework now has a second instance to test against, which is why prediction 1 below mirrors the JONCASS test exactly.

**The canon walked back in through its 140 shelf.** Four of the thirteen adds credit canon personnel — ero808 twice (with rSUN on "EMERALD 2.0"), bystander, kenu. As in version 3, the bridges are not the K3YN0T3 summit (still zero tracks by K3YN0T3, Distan, T3CHN0T3, or J3RY in the full 69) but its force-and-texture wing: ero808 is canon #57 ("POUR IT OUT"), rSUN canon #48, bystander a co-credit on canon #31, kenu canon #17. And the freshest possible intake sits inside this group: kenu's "places" was released Oct 23 and added Oct 23 at popularity 0 — caught on release day, before any chart existed to catch it from. The "devouring new stuff" finding from version 3 (45 of the full 69 are 2026 releases; 11 of the 13 adds) holds and, in this one case, reaches its limit form.

**Brightness: the slice moved, the set-point didn't (yet).** The added slice is the brightest measured in the series: median valence 0.393, mean 0.395, and a new list maximum (Zaug, 0.807 — above the prior max, "Bones" at 0.715). Yet the full list's median moves only 0.322 → 0.325 (the exact middle track of 69 is "Mind Games" at 0.325), because the slice also carries six sub-0.3 tracks, down to 0.110. Read plainly: his newest intake reaches brighter than ever and just as dark as ever, in the same thirteen tracks. The standing valence coordinate survives as a property of the accumulated list; whether it still describes his current intake is now a live question, staked as prediction 4 below. Energy did not move at all: added median 0.823 against the prior 0.803 — the force function remains the most stable number in the profile.

## Assessment

Version 3's summary sentence was: he changed what he listens to while changing nothing about what he wants it to do to him. The update adds a second, quieter move underneath the first. In the update window he also changed *how* the listening arrives — daylight instead of overnight, grazing sessions instead of binges, album pairs instead of genre shelves — while the intake's felt coordinates (energy, the dark floor under a brighter ceiling, release-day newness) held again. Two of version 3's four predictions are graded (JONCASS: no recurrence; tempo: ratification leg), one is untestable on a zero-purge window, and one is blocked by the same missing instrument as before. That is the recursive series working as designed: half of what it predicted, it can already score.

**Debts carried forward.**
1. *Harmony.* Still no chroma pass on the Bing Bong corpus (now 69 tracks). The I–vi question remains the profile's largest open measurement.
2. *Rotation.* Still no play counts. Retention is measured; preference is not.
3. *Spectral comparability.* Still unmeasured for this corpus; unchanged from version 3.

**Predictions** (made 2026-10-23, to be graded by the next version).
1. **The NXSTY test.** Mirroring the JONCASS test exactly: NXSTY either recurs in the next intake window beyond the two inhaled releases (*MIXTAPE ONE*, *Break The Body EP*) — adoption — or appears nowhere — a second inhalation event. No middle outcome is predicted. *Confidence: high that the test is decisive, by construction.*
2. **First-purge target.** When the first removal from the 69 finally occurs, it will come from the Oct 20 afternoon scatter session (the crunk/trap detour) before it comes from the UKG bloc or the NXSTY pairs. *Confidence: low-moderate.*
3. **The daylight test.** The next window's adds will again land majority outside 00:00–06:00 ET if the session-habit shift is real; a return to majority-overnight reclassifies Oct 21–23 as schedule noise. *Confidence: moderate that the test discriminates.*
4. **The brightness test.** The next added slice's median valence will fall back under 0.35 if 0.322 is an intake set-point and the 0.393 slice was a bright excursion, or hold at/above 0.38 if the set-point itself is moving bright. A slice median between 0.35 and 0.38 is the genuinely surprising outcome. *Confidence: moderate.*
