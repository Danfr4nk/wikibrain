# The August-26 Block Never Happened: Retraction

On August 26, 2026, Dan reported that he had kept Annie blocked for several days — described at the time, including by the wiki, as the first held boundary in the entire eleven-year record. On September 11, 2026, that claim was retracted. CSV forensics found roughly 25 block or severance declarations across August 15–19, none of them executed, and daily two-way texting continuing August 27 through September 7. Dan confirmed it himself, verbatim: *"there was never a block."* This page is the wiki's public correction: the original claim, the disproof, what the retraction costs, and what survives it.

## The claim, as recorded

The August 26 record exists in five dated pages, all written from Dan's operator report of that day:

- [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer-2026-08-26-update]] — "Annie is blocked"; the block as the notable fact, analyzed as potentially more than an emotional event.
- [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer-2026-08-26-visible-update]] — "Dan reports that Annie has remained blocked for several days, and the block is not currently being used as a dramatic event"; the tentative conclusion that the August rupture may have produced "a stable behavioral boundary that does not require emotional warfare to maintain."
- [[wiki/timeline/events/2026-08-26-dan-consistency-test]] — the two-channel test: blocked Annie plus ordinary Ally friendship, with "Annie remains blocked" as the test's fixed premise.
- [[wiki/timeline/events/2026-08-26-visible-change]] — "Dan appears to be maintaining a boundary with Annie while maintaining a comparatively ordinary, non-punitive friendship with Ally."
- [[wiki/timeline/events/2026-08-26-visible-change-2]] — boundary persistence as the first scored item: "Annie remains blocked rather than the block becoming a dramatic announcement."

The claim then propagated. [[wiki/mind/synthesis/high-provision-low-condolence]] named "the 120-day severance horizon to December 19, 2026" as the live test window of its prediction. [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]] carried the horizon as the money record's live test. The kb entity and datum layers recorded "Aug 26 block held (first held boundary)."

Provenance at the time: **[OPERATOR]**. Dan said he had blocked her. The wiki recorded his report. The pages themselves show the correct caution in places — the August 16–19 record "even contains a declared block that was not executed," the 08-26 update notes, flagging that declarations had failed before. The caution was pointed at the wrong risk: the worry was that the block would not *hold*. The actual failure was that it was never *enacted*.

## The disproof

September 11, 2026, forensic review of the message exports **[DERIVED]**:

- Roughly 25 block or severance declarations across August 15–19 — and **zero executed blocks**.
- Daily two-way texting continued August 27 through September 7 — 765 messages in the last 72 hours of the export, including "good night pretty girl" rituals. (Figure counted in a forensic pass that post-dates the Agent Edition snapshot; the export covering August 11–September 7 is not yet filed in raw/ — re-export and file it.)
- Dan confirmed verbatim: *"there was never a block."*

Chronology, kept explicit because the wiki's correction protocol requires it: Dan reported that he had blocked her. Later forensic review showed that he had not. The sixteen days between were not a held boundary decaying; they were a reported boundary that never existed.

## What the retraction costs

- **The "first held boundary" conclusion is withdrawn.** It appears in the five August 26 pages, the kb entity and datum layers, and anywhere downstream that cites them. Each carries a dated SUPERSEDED annotation; none has been silently rewritten. The pages stand as the historical record of what was believed on August 26.
- **The 120-day severance-horizon test to December 19, 2026 is void.** There was no severance to measure. [[wiki/mind/synthesis/high-provision-low-condolence]]'s Prediction section named the horizon as its live window; that window no longer exists. The prediction itself — provision preceding consolation in the next crisis — survives; its test does not.
- **The August 26 bullet in the severance sequence is struck.** The sequence the wiki records — June 1 "Goodbye forever" (his line, misattributed to her until August 2026), August 19 group-chat clearing (first public discrimination under her name), August 26 the block held — now ends at August 19. What was the sequence's third and most hopeful item was the performance of a boundary, not a boundary.

## What survives

- **The conversation with Ally happened as reported.** Dan told Ally he had Annie blocked for several days and described it as "a hill he needed to get over"; Ally replied he was "giving another person too much power over his life"; Dan agreed; the exchange moved on. Only the block the conversation described did not happen. The correction-acceptance, the ordinariness of the channel, the skepticism Ally maintained — all of that stands, and it stands *without* the boundary it was supposedly tested against, which makes the Ally half of the record more interesting, not less.
- **The block-unblock-loop analysis is reinforced, not damaged.** The loop's whole thesis is that in this system the declarations are the performance and the re-engagement is the fact — 129 episodes, 100% resumed, median gap 36 seconds. The August 26 block is the thesis's purest instance: a declaration so complete it fooled the wiki's own write-back for sixteen days, attached to zero seconds of non-contact. The page's rule now reads as having predicted exactly this failure mode.
- **The August 16–19 declared-but-unexecuted block**, which the 08-26 pages themselves noted in passing, was the truer tell all along. The wiki had the counterexample inside the same pages as the error and read it as the exception rather than the pattern.

## Why the false claim survived sixteen days

The mechanism is worth naming because it is a standing wiki failure mode, not a one-off. The August 26 report arrived as operator testimony about Dan's own behavior — the category of evidence the wiki is most inclined to accept at face value, since Dan is the one witness who cannot be wrong about whether he tapped "block." But "I blocked her" turned out to be a statement about a boundary the way "goodbye forever" was a statement about an ending: a declarative performance in the severance register, not an enacted fact. The wiki recorded the report as observed and built analysis on it. The correction protocol — dated SUPERSEDED annotations, no silent rewrites, the retraction as its own entry — is the same protocol that caught the June 1 misattribution, the 187:4 deflation, and the "sic semper lupanis" reversal. This is that protocol working in public, on the wiki's own error, sixteen days later. That is the point of keeping corrections visible: the next false "first" will meet a reader who knows this one happened.

- Whether any of the ~25 August 15–19 declarations produced even a partial or hours-long block (a block enacted and lifted between exports) is unresolvable from daily exports alone; the claim as stated — "never a block" — is Dan's own verbatim retraction, and the export record is consistent with it.
