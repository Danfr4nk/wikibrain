# Steady-State Invisibility: What a Message Corpus Structurally Cannot Show

[[wiki/mind/index|mind]]

A message corpus is a record of **deltas**. It captures what changed, what
broke, what was being arranged, and what had to be explained. It does not
capture what held. The things a person does identically every day for years are
the least narratable facts in a life, and a 192,140-message archive of that life
will be dense on everything that moved and silent on everything that did not
**[ATTESTED as the corpus's size, `corpus/derived/summary.json`]**.

This is not a gap in the record. It is the record's shape. And it has a
direction that makes it dangerous: **the facts most likely to be invisible are
the facts most likely to be load-bearing.** A sixteen-year daily pharmacological
regimen, an eighteen-year nicotine habit, a rent arrangement, a sleep schedule, a
dose — these are the structures the rest of the life runs on, and they are
precisely the ones that generate no message traffic until they fail.

The [[wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve|dose curve]] filed this as the third of
four reasons a sixteen-year run holds one dosage figure, in one paragraph, and
then had to get back to its inventory **[ATTESTED,
wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]**. It is a cross-register rule, it has a control
case, it has falsifiers, and it changes how every silence in this wiki should be
read. That is a page.

## 1. The claim, stated precisely

> **Narratability varies with variance.** A fact's probability of appearing in a
> message corpus is a function of how recently it changed, not of how important
> it is. A constant approaches zero probability of being typed. A constant that
> breaks approaches certainty.

Three consequences follow immediately, and they are not the same consequence
said three ways:

**(a) Silence about a stable thing is not weak evidence of its absence. It is no
evidence at all.** The expected message count for a true steady state and for a
thing that never existed are both approximately zero. The corpus cannot
distinguish them.

**(b) The corpus systematically over-represents failure.** Everything it holds
about a steady state is drawn from the moments that state was in trouble. A
regimen appears in the record as a supply crisis, an appointment, a refusal at a
pharmacy counter. Reading those back as the regimen's character is reading a
system entirely through its exceptions.

**(c) A steady state's *existence* can often be proven from a failure, while its
*magnitude* cannot.** A withdrawal proves dosing was live. It says nothing about
the dose. This asymmetry — cadence recoverable, quantity not — recurs across
every row tested below.

## 2. The type specimen: sixteen years, one number

The [[wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve|dose curve]]'s finding is the cleanest
instance in the archive. Daily Suboxone from 2010-02-17, and the corpus holds
exactly one dosage figure: **2mg/day, self-reported 2013-07-10**, in a one-off
overnight Facebook exchange with a fellow recovering addict, never repeated
**[ATTESTED, `kb/data/0055-facebook-corroborates-the-2010-maintenance-start.md`]**.

The inventory around it is the evidence for this page's claim, because of what
kind of bearings survived:

| Bearing | What it is | Why it exists |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2010-02-17 | run starts | a decision — a change |
| 2011-08-04 | appointment + urine screen | an appointment — a scheduled event |
| 2013-07-10 | **2mg/day** | an unprompted confession to a stranger |
| 2019-05-31 | "subs from my old doctor" | a sourcing problem |
| 2020-10 (×2) | offers of pills | someone else's inventory |
| 2021-03-31 | "3 months at a time… subs or subutex?" | a question about someone else |
| 2025 (×5) | script moved, refused, refused, unfillable, failover named | routing failures |
| 2026-05 | ~72h withdrawal | the supply breaking |

**[ATTESTED, the bearing table on wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve, sourced to
dat:0025, dat:0028, dat:0033, dat:0055 and wiki/work/bfs-foods.]**

Twelve bearings across sixteen years, and **eleven of the twelve are events**:
a start, an appointment, offers, refusals, a failure. The twelfth — the only one
carrying a number — is an anomaly in the channel's own terms, produced by a
context with no audience and no status incentive. The dose enters the record
once, sideways, and never again.

The mechanism is visible in what the record does hold. The corpus is *dense* on
Suboxone: 190 lines in one working file contain the term, fourteen occurrences in
the Facebook archive, two independent term censuses **[ATTESTED,
wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve; `kb/data/0055`]**. This is not a subject nobody
wrote about. It is a subject written about constantly, in which the steady-state
parameter appears once — because every other mention was occasioned by a change
and the parameter was not the change.

## 3. The control case: a relapse that exists only in arithmetic

The nicotine row is where the mechanism can be *watched*, because a public feed
with a counter accidentally instrumented a steady state.

Complete log, as assembled from the Twitter archive
**[ATTESTED, wiki/health/chemical-architecture, from
[[wiki/self/twitter/2008]], [[wiki/self/twitter/2010]], [[wiki/self/twitter/2011]],
[[wiki/self/twitter/2017]], [[wiki/self/twitter/2019]]]**:

| Date | What was posted | Delivery system |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2007 (stated 2017-12-28) | first pack, at a promotional Fall Out Boy show | cigarettes — onset |
| 2008-10-16 | *"I think I'm gonna stop smoking cigarettes. Another problem solved."* | quit #1 announced |
| 2008-12-22 | *"coffee and cigarettes- the cure for a ROUGH night."* | quit #1 over, 67 days |
| 2010-12-13 | *"3 days after moving to an e cig, i'm (un) hooked. today is day 1 with no tobacco."* | e-cigarette |
| 2010-12-14 | *"25 hours tobacco free. i should have done this years ago :("* | day 2 |
| **2011-01-10** | ***"1 week smoke free!"*** | **the counter has restarted** |
| 2015-12-13 | *"Vape shops all closed on Sunday. Gross Juiceman until tomorrow"* | vape, and a supply problem |
| 2019-10-09 | *"…i could replace the constant taste of burnt tobacco with strawberry"* | vape, reasons stated |
| 2019-10-10 | *"today i bought a can of skoal…"* | + smokeless |
| 2020-02-08 | vape juice as a budget line item | vape |
| 2022-03-31 | disposable vape, complained about by flavour | disposable |

**The 2011 row is the whole argument.** Day one was 2010-12-13. One week from
day one is 2010-12-20. On 2011-01-10 he posts *"1 week smoke free!"*, which dates
a **fresh** day one to roughly 2011-01-03. The December attempt broke somewhere in
between, and **the relapse is nowhere in the archive**: it is visible only because
a counter restarted **[ATTESTED, wiki/health/chemical-architecture]**.

Read that as an experiment. A steady state broke; the person was posting daily to
a public feed the whole time; the break produced **zero** direct statements. It
survived because a number happened to be attached to the state, and numbers
carry their own history. Remove the counter and the relapse is gone from the
record entirely — not hidden, not denied, simply never the sort of thing that
gets typed.

Now apply the control to the specimen. The Suboxone row has **no counter, no
public feed, and no arithmetic to do** **[ATTESTED, wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]**.
If the dose moved in 2015, 2019 or 2023 there is no mechanism in the record that
would show it. The nicotine case establishes that apparent continuity in an
archive is fully compatible with unrecorded discontinuity in the behavior — and
the dose-curve page draws the uncomfortable corollary: the Suboxone row has *less*
evidence of continuity than the nicotine row and is cited with *more* confidence
**[ATTESTED, that page]**.

## 4. The instrument built to defeat this, and its residue

[[wiki/health/intake-ledger|The Intake Ledger]] exists for exactly this reason: a
dated first-party log of an act too routine to narrate, on the premise that
*prose recollection of consumption is the least reliable testimony a person gives
about themselves* **[ATTESTED, wiki/health/intake-ledger]**. It is the right
response to this page's mechanism — you cannot mine a steady state out of a
message corpus, so you build a channel that records states rather than deltas.

Its limits section states the residue with unusual honesty: **an unlogged night
is indistinguishable here from a night with nothing in it**, and the ledger's
silence before its first unit is *"the absence of an instrument, not the absence
of use"* **[ATTESTED, that page]**. A purpose-built instrument does not abolish
steady-state invisibility; it relocates it from *what the medium records* to
*whether the log was kept*. That is a real improvement — a compliance question is
tractable where a medium-shape question is not — but it is a smaller version of
the same problem, not its solution.

**The limit that matters most for this tree, stated plainly:** `intake/events.jsonl`
is not in this repository **[ATTESTED, `README.md`]**, and the ledger page
accordingly renders with zero units, zero events and zero corrections
**[ATTESTED, wiki/health/intake-ledger]**. So the two rows the ledger was built
to measure — cocaine and cannabis — are, *in this tree*, in the same evidentiary
position as the Suboxone row: described, not measured. The dose curve's contrast
between rows with ledger nights and rows without does not currently hold here.

## 5. Four corollaries

**Corollary 1 — the absence asymmetry.** Presence is cheap to prove and absence
is expensive. One message establishes that something happened; establishing that
something did not requires a complete instrument over a stated window. The wiki
has repeatedly spent its evidentiary budget in the wrong direction
**[ATTESTED as the doctrine, wiki/self/message-corpora/message-request-blind-spot]**.
Steady-state invisibility is why: the cheap claims are about deltas and the
expensive ones are about states.

**Corollary 2 — the stability illusion.** Thirteen years of silence about a dose
looks like thirteen years of stability and is not. *"A maintenance dose held for
thirteen years and a maintenance dose adjusted thirteen times with no record look
identical in this corpus"* **[ATTESTED, wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]**. The
illusion is strong because it is the *pleasant* reading, and because nothing in
the record objects to it.

**Corollary 3 — instrument-created visibility.** A steady state becomes visible
only when something attaches a number to it that carries history: a counter, a
prescription duration, a ledger, a bank statement, a lease. These are not
narrations; they are artifacts with internal arithmetic. **Wherever the wiki can
see a steady state, an artifact of this kind is why** — and where no such artifact
exists, the wiki's confidence should drop regardless of how much the archive
talks about the topic.

**Corollary 4 — the dense-channel trap.** Volume is not coverage of a parameter.
The Suboxone case is the proof: hundreds of lines on the topic, one number. A
researcher who checks *is this well-attested?* by counting mentions will conclude
the record is rich, and the record is rich about everything except the thing
being asked.

The pattern across the column is the finding: **every `maximum` is a magnitude

rather than asserted, across five independent rows.

## 7. What this page does not license

A mechanism that explains a silence is not a licence to fill it. Four refusals,
stated so they can be quoted against future drafts:

1. **It does not make a silence into evidence of stability.** Explaining why a
   record is quiet says nothing about what it was quiet *about*. Non-observation
   remains non-observation.
2. **It does not upgrade a self-report.** The 2013 figure is approximate and
   unmeasured — *"nobody weighed anything that night"* **[ATTESTED,
   wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]**. Explaining its rarity does not improve its
   precision.
3. **It does not compete with coverage.** Where a channel was not running, the
   behavioral explanation is unnecessary and asserting it is a guess dressed as a
   mechanism. Rule out the mechanical cause first, against
   [[wiki/self/corpus/channel-coverage-gaps]], and only then reach for this page.
4. **It does not generalise to things people do narrate.** Plenty of daily
   constants *are* typed — greetings, sign-offs, complaints. The claim is about
   parameters, not about routines. A nightly *"goodnight"* is a ritual with social
   content; a dose is a number with none.

## 8. Falsifiers

1. **A second dosage figure from an ordinary, non-confessional context.** If the
   number turns out to appear in the sort of message this page predicts it cannot
   appear in, the narratability rule is wrong or badly scoped.
2. **A dated stretch where nicotine is absent rather than re-housed.** The
   continuity claim's stated falsifier, and the longest untested gap is 2011–2015
   **[ATTESTED, wiki/health/chemical-architecture]**. If continuity breaks
   silently there too, the counter case becomes typical rather than exceptional —
   which strengthens this page and weakens every continuity claim built on it.
3. **A steady state visible in the corpus with no attached artifact.** Corollary 3
   predicts none exists. One instance refutes it.
4. **A parameter recovered by volume alone.** If a well-attested magnitude turns
   up in a topic's message density rather than in an artifact, corollary 4 fails.
5. **A ledger period with dense message traffic about the same nights.** If the
   `intake/events.jsonl` record is recovered and its nights *also* generated
   proportional message discussion, the narratability rule is measuring the wrong
   thing.

## 9. Two scoreable predictions

**Prediction A.** Any future dosage figure that surfaces for this regimen will
come from an artifact with internal arithmetic — a prescription duration, a
pharmacy record, a pill count — and not from a message in which he states the
number to someone. Scoreable on the next dose figure of any provenance.

**Prediction B.** The June 2025 window, now known to hold 4,898 messages in the
dump against 35 in the export **[ATTESTED,
`kb/data/1502-corpus-coverage-hole-2025.md`]**, will contain prescriber and
supply traffic when read and will still contain **no dose figure**. This is the
sharpest available test, because it separates the two explanations cleanly: a
coverage hole predicts the dose might be there, and this page predicts it is not.

## 10. Why this archive is worse than the general case

Steady-state invisibility is a property of message corpora. Two features of
*this* one make it sharper than the general case, and both are checkable.

**The cognition that produced the corpus narrates anomalies.** The profile
carried on [[wiki/mind/profile/intp]] and restated in the context core is a
Ti-dominant forensic analyst who *"treats every domain — relationships, politics,
work, AI — through the same anomaly-detection lens"*, whose named failure mode is
the diagnosis-to-behavior gap rather than ignorance **[ATTESTED,
wiki/self/context-core]**. A diagnosis is a delta by construction: it is the
statement of what deviated. An archive generated by that cognition is not merely
delta-weighted the way all message archives are — it is delta-weighted *twice*,
once by the medium and once by the author's default mode of attention. The
things that held are not just unposted; they were not the sort of thing that
drew his attention long enough to be posted.

**The archive over-collects and under-audits.** `README.md` records the pattern
found by the 2026-09-09 census: discipline tracked *recency of work*, not subject
matter, and half the page corpus was never opened in the final work window and
carries 9% of its self-corrections — `interests/` runs 0.11 correction marks per
page against 0.46 in its touched quarter **[ATTESTED, `README.md`]**. The
operative warning there is the same one this page arrives at from the other
direction: *a low correction count in an unworked area means nobody got there,
not that nothing was wrong.* Invisibility compounds — a parameter that the medium
does not record, in an area nobody re-read, reaches the reader as settled
background.

**The practical consequence.** Where a claim in this wiki reads as background —
unfootnoted, uncontested, repeated across pages — that is the profile of a
steady-state assertion, not the profile of a well-evidenced one. The 217,573
message figure is the worked example from the other page: carried on at least
three pages as *the corpus*, inherited rather than checked, and describing an
artifact that stops in August 2025 **[ATTESTED,
`kb/data/1502-corpus-coverage-hole-2025.md`; carried on
[[wiki/self/concepts/wiki-brain]], [[wiki/self/context-core]],
[[wiki/self/concepts/llm]]]**. Nothing about a number repeated three times looks
like a steady-state claim, and that is exactly what it was.

## 11. Gaps

1. **The rule is asserted, not measured.** No count exists in this tree of how
   often any steady-state parameter appears per thousand messages. The inventory
   in §6 is a qualitative scoring, and it should be a measurement.
2. **The nicotine case is the only worked control.** One instrument-preserved
   break is enough to establish the mechanism can be caught and not enough to say
   how often it is missed.
3. **`intake/events.jsonl` is not held**, so the ledger contrast in §4 is argued
   from the ledger's design and its own limits section rather than from its data
   **[ATTESTED, `README.md`]**.
4. **The Facebook archive's later years are the obvious place to look for a second
   dose figure**, and its message coverage ends in the 2022 window **[ATTESTED,
   [[wiki/self/facebook/messages]]]** — so the most likely home for a falsifier is
   in a channel that stops four years before the interval ends.
5. **No cross-person test.** Every row inventoried is Dan's. Whether the same
   silence shape holds for counterparties' steady states in this corpus is
   unexamined, and it is the cheapest available check on whether this is a
   property of message corpora or of this person's use of them.

## 12. Limits of record

- **Observed:** the twelve-bearing Suboxone inventory; the eleven-row nicotine
  tweet log; the 2010-12-13 / 2011-01-10 counter dates; the ledger's zero-unit
  state in this tree; the 2025 monthly corpus-vs-dump counts.
- **Reported (self-report, uncorroborated):** every quoted tweet and message in
  §2 and §3 is the subject's own account of his own behavior; the 2mg figure, the
  "3.5 years", the two 2019 stated reasons for vaping.
- **Calculated:** the 2011 relapse window (day one 2010-12-13, restated day one
  ≈2011-01-03, giving a break between 2010-12-20 and 2011-01-03); the
  eleven-of-twelve bearing split.
- **Inferred:** the narratability rule itself; the §6 exposure column; corollary
  3's claim that artifact-with-arithmetic is the *only* route to steady-state
  visibility, which is a generalisation from two cases.
- **Unknown:** the frequency of parameter mentions per unit of message volume;
  whether the 2011–2015 nicotine gap contains a break; whether any counterparty's
  steady states are visible in this corpus.
- **A stated dependency.** This page's argument only applies where the channel
  was actually running. Its scope is therefore bounded by
  [[wiki/self/corpus/channel-coverage-gaps]], and where that page marks a year
  `○`, nothing here is applicable.
- **No media.** No photograph in the archive carries a steady-state parameter,
  which is itself an instance of the claim: a photograph records a moment, and a
  moment is a delta. The Sources list below carries no thumbnails.
