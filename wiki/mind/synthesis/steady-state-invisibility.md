# Steady-State Invisibility: What a Message Corpus Structurally Cannot Show

[[wiki/mind/index|mind]]

On July 10, 2013, in a one-off overnight Facebook exchange with a fellow recovering addict, Dan states his Suboxone dose: **2 mg a day**. He had been taking it daily since February 17, 2010. He would still be taking it in 2026. Across those sixteen years, across a corpus of 192,140 messages dense on everything that moved, the number appears **once** — sideways, in a context with no audience and no status incentive, and never again **[ATTESTED, `kb/data/0055-facebook-corroborates-the-2010-maintenance-start.md`; corpus size `corpus/derived/summary.json`]**. The regimen is not a subject nobody wrote about: 190 lines in one working file contain the term, fourteen occurrences sit in the Facebook archive, two independent term censuses exist **[ATTESTED, wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]**. It is a subject written about constantly, in which the steady-state parameter surfaces a single time — because every other mention was occasioned by a change, and the parameter was not the change.

A year and a half earlier, the same archive accidentally caught a steady state breaking. On December 13, 2010 Dan posts that today is day one with no tobacco; on January 10, 2011 he posts "1 week smoke free!" — which dates a fresh day one to roughly January 3. The December attempt failed somewhere in between, and the relapse is stated nowhere: it exists only because a counter restarted **[ATTESTED, wiki/health/chemical-architecture]**. Remove the counter and the break is gone from the record entirely — not hidden, not denied, simply never the sort of thing that gets typed.

A message corpus is a record of deltas. It captures what changed, what broke, what was being arranged, and what had to be explained; it does not capture what held. The things a person does identically every day for years are the least narratable facts in a life, and an archive of that life will be dense on everything that moved and silent on everything that did not. This is not a gap in the record. It is the record's shape, and it has a direction that makes it dangerous: the facts most likely to be invisible are the facts most likely to be load-bearing. A daily pharmacological regimen, an eighteen-year nicotine habit, a rent arrangement, a sleep schedule, a dose — these are the structures the rest of the life runs on, and they are precisely the ones that generate no message traffic until they fail. The [[wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve|dose curve]] filed this as the third of four reasons a sixteen-year run holds one dosage figure, in one paragraph, and then had to get back to its inventory **[ATTESTED, wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]**. It is a cross-register rule, it has a control case, it has falsifiers, and it changes how every silence in this wiki should be read. That is a page.

## The claim, stated precisely

> **Narratability varies with variance.** A fact's probability of appearing in a message corpus is a function of how recently it changed, not of how important it is. A constant approaches zero probability of being typed. A constant that breaks approaches certainty.

Three consequences follow immediately, and they are not the same consequence said three ways:

**(a) Silence about a stable thing is not weak evidence of its absence. It is no evidence at all.** The expected message count for a true steady state and for a thing that never existed are both approximately zero. The corpus cannot distinguish them.

**(b) The corpus systematically over-represents failure.** Everything it holds about a steady state is drawn from the moments that state was in trouble. A regimen appears in the record as a supply crisis, an appointment, a refusal at a pharmacy counter. Reading those back as the regimen's character is reading a system entirely through its exceptions.

**(c) A steady state's *existence* can often be proven from a failure, while its *magnitude* cannot.** A withdrawal proves dosing was live. It says nothing about the dose. This asymmetry — cadence recoverable, quantity not — recurs across every row tested below.

## The type specimen: sixteen years, one number

The dose curve's finding is the cleanest instance in the archive. Daily Suboxone from 2010-02-17, and the corpus holds exactly one dosage figure: **2mg/day, self-reported 2013-07-10**, in the overnight Facebook exchange above, never repeated **[ATTESTED, `kb/data/0055-facebook-corroborates-the-2010-maintenance-start.md`]**.

The inventory around it is the evidence, because of what kind of bearings survived:

| Bearing | What it is | Why it exists |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2010-02-17 | run starts | a decision — a change |
| 2011-08-04 | appointment + urine screen | an appointment — a scheduled event |
| 2013-07-10 | **2mg/day** | an unprompted confession to a stranger |
| 2019-05-31 | "subs from my old doctor" | a sourcing problem |
| 2020-10 (×2) | offers of pills | someone else's inventory |
| 2021-03-31 | "3 months at a time… subs or subutex?" | a question about someone else |
| 2025 (×5) | script moved, refused, refused, unfillable, failover named | routing failures |
| 2026-05 | ~72h withdrawal | the supply breaking |

**[ATTESTED, the bearing table on wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve, sourced to dat:0025, dat:0028, dat:0033, dat:0055 and wiki/work/bfs-foods.]**

Twelve bearings across sixteen years, and eleven of the twelve are events: a start, an appointment, offers, refusals, a failure. The twelfth — the only one carrying a number — is an anomaly in the channel's own terms.

## The control case: a relapse that exists only in arithmetic

The nicotine row is where the mechanism can be *watched*, because a public feed with a counter accidentally instrumented a steady state.

Complete log, as assembled from the Twitter archive **[ATTESTED, wiki/health/chemical-architecture, from [[wiki/self/twitter/2008]], [[wiki/self/twitter/2010]], [[wiki/self/twitter/2011]], [[wiki/self/twitter/2017]], [[wiki/self/twitter/2019]]]**:

| Date | What was posted | Delivery system |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2007 (stated 2017-12-28) | first pack, at a promotional Fall Out Boy show | cigarettes — onset |
| 2008-10-16 | *"I think I'm gonna stop smoking cigarettes. Another problem solved."* | quit #1 announced |
| 2008-12-22 | *"coffee and cigarettes- the cure for a ROUGH night."* | quit #1 over, 67 days |
| 2010-12-13 | *"3 days after moving to an e cig, i'm (un) hooked. today is day 1 with no tobacco."* | e-cigarette |
| 2010-12-14 | *"25 hours tobacco free. i should have done this years ago :("* | day 2 |
| **2011-01-10** | ***"1 week smoke free!"*** | **the counter has restarted** |
| 2015-12-13 | *"Vape shops all closed on Sunday. Gross Juiceman until tomorrow"* | vape, and a supply problem |
| 2019-10-09 | *"…i could replace the constant taste of burnt tobacco with strawberry"* | vape, reasons stated |
| 2019-10-10 | *"today i bought a can of skoal…"* | + smokeless |
| 2020-02-08 | vape juice as a budget line item | vape |
| 2022-03-31 | disposable vape, complained about by flavour | disposable |

**The 2011 row is the whole argument.** Day one was 2010-12-13. One week from day one is 2010-12-20. On 2011-01-10 he posts *"1 week smoke free!"*, which dates a **fresh** day one to roughly 2011-01-03. The December attempt broke somewhere in between, and **the relapse is nowhere in the archive**: it is visible only because a counter restarted **[ATTESTED, wiki/health/chemical-architecture]**.

Read that as an experiment. A steady state broke; the person was posting daily to a public feed the whole time; the break produced **zero** direct statements. It survived because a number happened to be attached to the state, and numbers carry their own history.

Now apply the control to the specimen. The Suboxone row has **no counter, no public feed, and no arithmetic to do** **[ATTESTED, wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]**. If the dose moved in 2015, 2019 or 2023 there is no mechanism in the record that would show it. The nicotine case establishes that apparent continuity in an archive is fully compatible with unrecorded discontinuity in the behavior — and the dose-curve page draws the uncomfortable corollary: the Suboxone row has *less* evidence of continuity than the nicotine row and is cited with *more* confidence **[ATTESTED, that page]**.

## The instrument built to defeat this, and its residue

[[wiki/health/intake-ledger|The Intake Ledger]] exists for exactly this reason: a dated first-party log of an act too routine to narrate, on the premise that *prose recollection of consumption is the least reliable testimony a person gives about themselves* **[ATTESTED, wiki/health/intake-ledger]**. It is the right response to this page's mechanism — you cannot mine a steady state out of a message corpus, so you build a channel that records states rather than deltas.

Its residue is stated with unusual honesty on that page: **an unlogged night is indistinguishable here from a night with nothing in it**, and the ledger's silence before its first unit is *"the absence of an instrument, not the absence of use"* **[ATTESTED, that page]**. A purpose-built instrument does not abolish steady-state invisibility; it relocates it from *what the medium records* to *whether the log was kept*. That is a real improvement — a compliance question is tractable where a medium-shape question is not — but it is a smaller version of the same problem, not its solution.

One dated development belongs here rather than in the conflict register: the ledger now exists. Its page, built 2026-08-31 and updated 2026-09-24, renders 4 units opened (all closed) and 9 intake events with quantities, first unit received 2026-08-30, most recent activity 2026-08-31 **[ATTESTED, wiki/health/intake-ledger summary table]**. The two rows the ledger was built to measure — cocaine and cannabis — therefore now have the beginnings of an instrument channel where this page's earlier draft found none; the channel is two days deep, which is to say it measures almost nothing yet, but it has crossed from design to data. See Conflicts for what that does to this page's older statement of the ledger's state.

## The cross-register check: intake under a rotating subject

[[wiki/mind/synthesis/intake-constancy|Intake-Constancy]] is this page's mechanism photographed from the other side. That entry documents a fixed rate of intake running underneath a rotating cast of subjects: what rotates gets narrated, in volume; what holds does not get narrated at all; and a reader of the narration mistakes the rotation for the whole of the behavior. The two pages make one claim in two registers. Constancy predicts both the chemical rows above (a dose held for years generates one figure) and the intake rows (a rate held under changing subjects generates a literature about the subjects and silence about the rate). Where the earlier pages scored exposure row by row, the shared rule is what a reader should carry between them: in every register this wiki tracks, the constant is the term least likely to appear in its own record, and the changing term is the one that absorbs the attention — including the attention of whoever later reads the archive as if its proportions were the life's proportions.

## Four corollaries

**Corollary 1 — the absence asymmetry.** Presence is cheap to prove and absence is expensive. One message establishes that something happened; establishing that something did not requires a complete instrument over a stated window. The wiki has repeatedly spent its evidentiary budget in the wrong direction **[ATTESTED as the doctrine, wiki/self/message-corpora/message-request-blind-spot]**. Steady-state invisibility is why: the cheap claims are about deltas and the expensive ones are about states.

**Corollary 2 — the stability illusion.** Thirteen years of silence about a dose looks like thirteen years of stability and is not. *"A maintenance dose held for thirteen years and a maintenance dose adjusted thirteen times with no record look identical in this corpus"* **[ATTESTED, wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]**. The illusion is strong because it is the *pleasant* reading, and because nothing in the record objects to it.

**Corollary 3 — instrument-created visibility.** A steady state becomes visible only when something attaches a number to it that carries history: a counter, a prescription duration, a ledger, a bank statement, a lease. These are not narrations; they are artifacts with internal arithmetic. **Wherever the wiki can see a steady state, an artifact of this kind is why** — and where no such artifact exists, the wiki's confidence should drop regardless of how much the archive talks about the topic.

**Corollary 4 — the dense-channel trap.** Volume is not coverage of a parameter. The Suboxone case is the proof: hundreds of lines on the topic, one number. A researcher who checks *is this well-attested?* by counting mentions will conclude the record is rich, and the record is rich about everything except the thing being asked.

## What this page does not license

A mechanism that explains a silence is not a licence to fill it. Four refusals, stated so they can be quoted against future drafts:

1. **It does not make a silence into evidence of stability.** Explaining why a record is quiet says nothing about what it was quiet *about*. Non-observation remains non-observation.
2. **It does not upgrade a self-report.** The 2013 figure is approximate and unmeasured — *"nobody weighed anything that night"* **[ATTESTED, wiki/health/suboxone-dose-curve]**. Explaining its rarity does not improve its precision.
3. **It does not compete with coverage.** Where a channel was not running, the behavioral explanation is unnecessary and asserting it is a guess dressed as a mechanism. Rule out the mechanical cause first, against [[wiki/self/corpus/channel-coverage-gaps]], and only then reach for this page.
4. **It does not generalise to things people do narrate.** Plenty of daily constants *are* typed — greetings, sign-offs, complaints. The claim is about parameters, not about routines. A nightly *"goodnight"* is a ritual with social content; a dose is a number with none.

What is not known, stated in place rather than gathered into a separate register: the rule is asserted, not measured — no count exists in this tree of how often any steady-state parameter appears per thousand messages, and the inventory above is a qualitative scoring until one does. The nicotine case is the only worked control; one instrument-preserved break establishes that the mechanism can be caught, not how often it is missed. The Facebook archive's message coverage ends in the 2022 window **[ATTESTED, [[wiki/self/facebook/messages]]]**, so the most likely home for a second dose figure is in a channel that stops four years before the interval ends. And no cross-person test exists: every row inventoried is Dan's, so whether the same silence shape holds for counterparties' steady states in this corpus — the cheapest available check on whether this is a property of message corpora or of this person's use of them — remains unrun.

## Falsifiers and predictions

Five dated tests, kept together because they score the same claim:

1. **A second dosage figure from an ordinary, non-confessional context.** If the number turns out to appear in the sort of message this page predicts it cannot appear in, the narratability rule is wrong or badly scoped.
2. **A dated stretch where nicotine is absent rather than re-housed.** The continuity claim's stated falsifier, and the longest untested gap is 2011–2015 **[ATTESTED, wiki/health/chemical-architecture]**. If continuity breaks silently there too, the counter case becomes typical rather than exceptional — which strengthens this page and weakens every continuity claim built on it.
3. **A steady state visible in the corpus with no attached artifact.** Corollary 3 predicts none exists. One instance refutes it.
4. **A parameter recovered by volume alone.** If a well-attested magnitude turns up in a topic's message density rather than in an artifact, corollary 4 fails.
5. **A ledger period with dense message traffic about the same nights.** If the `intake/events.jsonl` record held dense nights *also* generating proportional message discussion, the narratability rule is measuring the wrong thing.

Two predictions are scoreable against future finds:

**Prediction A.** Any future dosage figure that surfaces for this regimen will come from an artifact with internal arithmetic — a prescription duration, a pharmacy record, a pill count — and not from a message in which he states the number to someone. Scoreable on the next dose figure of any provenance.

**Prediction B.** The June 2025 window, now known to hold 4,898 messages in the dump against 35 in the export **[ATTESTED, `kb/data/1502-corpus-coverage-hole-2025.md`]**, will contain prescriber and supply traffic when read and will still contain **no dose figure**. This is the sharpest available test, because it separates the two explanations cleanly: a coverage hole predicts the dose might be there, and this page predicts it is not.

## Why this archive is worse than the general case

Steady-state invisibility is a property of message corpora. Two features of *this* one make it sharper than the general case, and both are checkable.

**The cognition that produced the corpus attends to anomalies, and the record shows it.** Stated at attention level only — what the corpus shows its author doing, not what he experiences: anomaly detection is the default output mode across domains (relationships, politics, work, AI are all processed through the same forensic lens in the record), and the standing failure mode is the diagnosis-to-behavior gap rather than ignorance **[ATTESTED, wiki/self/context-core; wiki/mind/profile/intp]**. A diagnosis is a delta by construction: it is the statement of what deviated. An archive generated under that attention pattern is not merely delta-weighted the way all message archives are — it is delta-weighted *twice*, once by the medium and once by what its author habitually notices long enough to type. The things that held were not suppressed; they did not compete for the typing. That framing is deliberately weaker than an interior account, and it is sufficient: the mechanism needs only the observed typing pattern, not a motive behind it.

**The archive over-collects and under-audits.** `README.md` records the pattern found by the 2026-09-09 census: discipline tracked *recency of work*, not subject matter, and half the page corpus was never opened in the final work window and carries 9% of its self-corrections — `interests/` runs 0.11 correction marks per page against 0.46 in its touched quarter **[ATTESTED, `README.md`]**. The operative warning there is the same one this page arrives at from the other direction: *a low correction count in an unworked area means nobody got there, not that nothing was wrong.* Invisibility compounds — a parameter that the medium does not record, in an area nobody re-read, reaches the reader as settled background.

## Conflicts in the record

**The ledger's state in this tree (this page, superseded 2026-08-31).** This page's earlier draft, dated 2026-09-17, states that `intake/events.jsonl` is not in the repository and that the ledger page "renders with zero units, zero events and zero corrections," and scores the cocaine/cannabis row accordingly. The ledger page itself, created 2026-08-31 and modified 2026-09-24, renders 4 units (4 closed), 9 intake events, first unit 2026-08-30, most recent activity 2026-08-31 **[ATTESTED, wiki/health/intake-ledger]**. Claimed (2026-09-17): zero units in this tree. Record shows (2026-08-31 onward): four units and nine events across two days. Current standing: the earlier statement is superseded; the corrected position is carried in the instrument section and the inventory table above. Two days of logging do not retire the row's exposure — the ledger's own residue (an unlogged night indistinguishable from an empty one) now does the work the absence argument used to do.

**The 217,573 figure (repeated as background, corrected 2026).** At least three pages have carried **217,573** as *the corpus* — a steady-state-style background fact: unfootnoted, uncontested, repeated until it reads as settled **[ATTESTED, `kb/data/1502-corpus-coverage-hole-2025.md`; carried on [[wiki/self/concepts/wiki-brain]], [[wiki/self/context-core]], [[wiki/self/concepts/llm]]]**. The figure describes an artifact that stops in August 2025; the June 2025 denominator test (4,898 dump rows against 35 export rows in a single month) shows the underlying coverage moving underneath the repeated number. Claimed (multiple pages, into 2026): 217,573 messages is the corpus. Record shows (dat:1502): the figure is one artifact's boundary, already stale at the moment of repetition. Current standing: this page cites the dated, per-instrument denominators throughout and carries 217,573 only here, as the worked example of corollary 2 applied to the wiki's own bookkeeping — nothing about a number repeated three times looks like a steady-state claim, and that is exactly what it was.

**Two causal accounts of the same silences (unreconciled by design).** This page's mechanism (the channel was running; the fact was not typed) and [[wiki/self/corpus/channel-coverage-gaps|channel-coverage-gaps]]' mechanism (the channel was not running that year) both predict quiet rows, and both are live on overlapping territory — the dose row's 2015–2023 silence sits under a behavioral explanation here and under partial coverage there. Claimed: each page states its own cause first. Record shows: both causes attested in this archive, on different rows. Current standing: unresolved, and deliberately — the refusal in "What this page does not license" (3) sequences them (coverage ruled out first, behavior second) without merging them, and any row scored before the coverage check is flagged as exposure INFERRED rather than observed.

## Assessment

Read as a reading rule rather than as a claim about Dan, the page's usable output is a single instruction with a dated evidence base behind it: where a wiki claim reads as background — unfootnoted, uncontested, repeated across pages — that is the profile of a steady-state assertion, not the profile of a well-evidenced one, and the claim should be re-anchored to its artifact before it is built on. The mechanism itself is supported at the level this record can support anything of its kind: one control case caught by arithmetic (2011), one specimen scanned across twelve bearings (2013's single figure), one instrument built against it and now logging (2026), and two scoreable predictions still open. The profile-level framing adds only this: the bias is doubled in this archive, once by the medium and once by an observed attention pattern that types diagnoses — and diagnoses are deltas. Nothing here grades what any constant *was*. It grades how much of it a message corpus could, in principle, have shown.
