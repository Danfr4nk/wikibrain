# Provision Grammar

The money in Dan's attachments is not a pile of facts about three relationships.

It is one grammar spoken in three registers, at denominations separated by orders of magnitude, with an identical function: **money sent when words are untrusted.**

[[wiki/mind/synthesis/four-financial-inversions]] holds the decade's rotations inside the Annie channel — four dated inversions of the provider direction — and its thesis is that every grievance built on a single rotation is locally true and historically incomplete.

[[wiki/mind/synthesis/high-provision-low-condolence]] names the measured mechanism — instrumental generosity at 1.79–2.49x against sympathy tokens at 0.45x — and its rule: provision precedes or substitutes for verbal consolation, and significant transfers are later invoked or itemized.

What neither page does is state the grammar itself: what a transfer *says*, how the denominations ladder from a $25 introduction purchase to a six-figure decade outflow without changing function, why provision substitutes for the verbal commitments he demonstrably never makes ("do you love me" **0 times** in 106,629 sent messages against "call me" **170** — [[wiki/mind/concepts/explicit-verbal-commitment]]), and what fuels the grievance machine that each channel's ledger runs.

This entry is the cross-channel grammar of money-as-attachment.

Measured per channel, never synthesized as one grammar, until now.

## The figures, held to the numbers-discipline line

Before the grammar, the provenance — because a grammar built on soft figures is vibes with a ledger.

**Annie channel.** ~$139K sent against ~$16–20K received across Cash App and Venmo, net outflow roughly **$119K–$123K**, approximately **six-to-one** in her favor **[OPERATOR-REPORTED and DERIVED]**. The provenance, stated exactly as [[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]] carries it: these are operator-provided screenshots of her profiles viewed from his apps, taken **2026-07-15**, not in `raw/`, not an audited ledger. The six-to-one is a ratio computed from his figures, not a measured fact of a bank record. Say it plainly: **there is no audited ledger.** The screenshots are the closest thing the family system ever produced to one — a system in which $134,069.31 of estate capital arrived and vanished in six months with both endpoints documented and the middle a blank is not a system that keeps ledgers. The payment-app totals exist because an app kept them, not because anyone reconciled them. Any sentence in this entry that treats $119K–$123K or six-to-one as audited fact is a sentence that has left the record.

**Ally channel.** **$2,100** claimed across the August–October 2019 crisis window — Dan's figure, stated in-thread on **2019-10-22**: *"lady i've sent 2100 and have like 2 pictures and im STILL trying lol"* **[OPERATOR]**, met by her *"You have not sent me $2100"*, his screenshot, her *"Oh fuck"* **[ATTESTED — thread, via wiki/people/ally-lubin]**. Accepted by her in 2019; contested by her in 2026 — *"Also there's no proof you ever sent me $2100 except your [screenshot]"* **[ATTESTED]**. No 2019 Cash App export exists in `raw/` to settle it **[UNKNOWN]**. The figure is his number, her acceptance, her later contestation, and no independent corroboration. Hold all four.

**Suz channel.** **~$14,000** transferred Dan→Suz in August–October 2018, drawn against an estate that had not yet distributed, with nothing in writing; $4,000 recovered; the remainder disputed to this day **[ATTESTED — her own itemized statement 2018-10-03 and his contemporaneous messages; the recovery is his account]**. Direction: **Dan→Suz** — mother-ward, opposite the channel's folk memory. Both parties have claimed to be the creditor since July 2019; neither claim was ever adjudicated or withdrawn **[ATTESTED — suzanne-frank.md "The ledger that never closes"]**.

**Scale reference.** The estate distribution order: **$144,069.31**, court-signed 2020-08-21 **[ATTESTED]**. The Annie net outflow (~$119K–$123K) is roughly the size of the entire estate distribution — the spine's standing observation, repeated here only to hold the scale.

The denominations differ by **four orders of magnitude**: tens of dollars to six figures. The function, this entry's claim, is identical. That is the finding the denominations ladder makes visible, and it is why the ladder needs to be climbed in order rather than asserted.

## Provision as utterance: what each transfer says

A transfer is a sentence in a language with very few words.

The vocabulary is the amount.

The grammar is the channel.

Read the three channels' transfers as utterances, and each one says something the verbal channel of the same relationship demonstrably cannot.

### The Annie register: the sentence is "I am still here, and here is the proof"

On the Annie channel, transfers are the bond's operating register for a decade. The ~$139K sent against ~$16–20K received is not a collection of gifts; it is the sentence *"I am the node you can count on,"* repeated at whatever denomination the month allows, through all four of [[wiki/mind/synthesis/four-financial-inversions]]'s rotations — including inversion 3, the rotation where she was the sole earner and he sent less, and inversion 4, where the currency changed from cash to procurement.

The terminal-phase sentence is the one he says out loud. **2025-07-28**: *"even the worst thing I've done — getting you drugs — didn't really benefit me nearly as much as it was about that being the only way you would see me"* **[ATTESTED]**. Provision as retention mechanism, stated by the provider against his own position in the argument. The supply handoff is not a different architecture from the transfers; it is the grammar switching currency. In inversion 1 he transmitted his mother's capital. In inversion 4 he transmits the network's product. The sentence never changed: *this is the channel through which the necessity flows, and the channel is me.*

Two properties of the Annie register matter for the grammar.

**First: the provision is never itemized to her while it runs.** No in-thread audit like the Ally channel's "i've sent 2100 and have like 2 pictures." The decade's ledger emerges years later as screenshots taken in July 2026 — the system keeps no books, and then, once, it does, and the number is $119K–$123K. This is the high-provision page's ledger half running in slow motion: significant transfers are later invoked or itemized, but the "later" here is *years*. The provision half ran continuously; the ledger half arrived all at once, when someone opened the app and looked.

**Second: her side of the channel contains zero severance signals in 41,073 of her messages** [DERIVED — via wiki/mind/concepts/attachment-model] **and 129 of his own severance declarations, 100% re-engaged, median gap thirty-six seconds** [DERIVED — via wiki/mind/synthesis/severance-declarations]. The verbal register on this channel is a system of performances — declarations that never enact. The provision register is the system of facts underneath the performances. Money is the one thing on the Annie channel that actually moves. Everything else is stated.

### The Ally register: priced per item, invoiced in real time

The Ally channel speaks the grammar at the opposite surface. Here provision is not the invisible substrate — it is the conversation. The 2019 spike's price list, verbatim:

- *"lol help me here - how do i try to sell you on letting me pay for your noodz while you're in bummer island? i can solve these financial problems! also the self esteem/self image ones too."* (2019-08-17) **[OPERATOR]**
- *"More for a video but $50 for a tit pic"* (her counter) / *"i'd do $30 for a tit pic if your face is visible… that is like SUPER PREMIUM princess pricing"* (his counter) **[ATTESTED — thread]**
- *"public service announcement: i just got a nice check in, so if you are drunk or just want money...i'll GLADLY overpay for a peek."* (2019-09-25) **[OPERATOR]**
- *"let's work out some kind of sugar baby/daddy situation"* (2019-09-07) **[OPERATOR]**
- *"promise 1: $500 on arrival"* against an NYC flight, with the rider *"and nooooo you're not obligated to fuck me"* (2019-09-01) **[OPERATOR]**

The denominations are small — $25 to $500, the crisis window totaling his claimed $2,100 — and that is the point the grammar is making. On the Ally channel, **the price list IS the utterance.** Where the Annie channel's transfers say *"I am the node,"* the Ally channel's priced offers say *"here is what access costs, and I am the buyer."* The amounts are denominated in items because the attachment's operating condition is inaccessibility — [[wiki/people/ally-lubin-cognitive-profile]]'s finding, and the [[wiki/mind/synthesis/annie-ally-contrast]] entry's Protocol B. You do not provision a no-stake channel the way you provision a primary bond. You price it. The price list is the grammar adapting to a channel where the goal is discharge, not retention — *"so i can get the poison out"* (2019-10-22) **[OPERATOR]**, the load-bearing sentence of the whole channel.

And the ledger half of the rule runs in real time here, not in slow motion: the October 22 audit is his — *"lady i've sent 2100 and have like 2 pictures and im STILL trying lol"* — delivered mid-argument, invoiced while the provision is still warm. Same rule as the Annie channel's. Opposite tempo.

The origin purchase belongs in the ladder too, because it is the grammar's first utterance on the channel: **$25 to Dan Polyak on 2018-12-12, memo "For introducing me to Ally Lubin"** **[ATTESTED — Facebook payments ledger]**. Access purchased, not a bond transferred — [[wiki/mind/synthesis/annie-ally-contrast]]'s line, and the denominational floor of the whole grammar. The smallest sentence in the language: *I will pay to open the channel.*

### The Suz register: give-and-invoice

The Suz channel is the grammar's third register, and it is the one where provision and invoicing are the same act. The artifact that proves it is the message Suz sent at **6:34 on the morning of 2018-10-03** — not prose, an account statement:

> Dan to Suzanne
> $1000
> $14,000+
> Sum of transactions on app
>
> Suzanne to Dan (past week)
> $450
> If you can give me $1450, that erases the above and adds $1000 to what I borrowed from you. That way I can pay the other bills I owe and have a hundred or three to live on.

**[ATTESTED — suzanne-frank.md "The ledger that never closes"]**

Read it as grammar, not as bookkeeping. On the Annie channel, provision runs and the ledger surfaces years later. On the Ally channel, provision is priced per item and audited in the same argument. On the Suz channel, **the transfer arrives pre-invoiced** — her itemization is the channel's native speech. The sentence each transfer says here is not *"I am the node"* (Annie) or *"here is what access costs"* (Ally). It is *"this is what is owed, and here is the running total."* Provision as utterance, invoice as grammar — the family system's version of the rule.

The $14,000's direction is the register's load-bearing fact: it moved **Dan→Suz**, mother-ward, drawn against an estate that had not distributed, 70% of a $20,000 bridging advance re-lent within weeks, nothing in writing **[ATTESTED — estate-money-spine]**. The spine's rule — *"a lump arriving anywhere in this family is immediately re-lent inside it, on no paper, at the moment of arrival"* — is the Suz register stated as law. And the register's characteristic move is the one both parties have performed since July 2019: each claiming to be the creditor of the same money. Dan: *"I LENT YOU 14,000 … which i'm supposed to be paid back when the inheritance comes right"* (2019-06-20) **[OPERATOR]**. Her: *"I have the PayPal receipts from the $14,000 you stole from me"* (2019-07-03) **[ATTESTED]**. Three hours later, her standing frame: *"$14,000 is a drop in the bucket compared to what you have cost me since 2004."* **[ATTESTED]**

That last line is the grammar's third utterance type in miniature: **provision as grievance-denominator.** On the Suz channel, a transfer does not settle anything — it becomes the unit in which the next grievance is denominated. The $14,000 is not an amount that was lent and might be repaid. It is a measuring stick that shrinks everything measured against it ("a drop in the bucket") and a crime that grows everything accused under it ("stole"). Same number, two utterances, neither of them about the money.

The ladder's claim is falsifiable in the way good claims are: find a channel where he provisions heavily and the provision does *not* substitute for a verbal commitment he cannot make — a channel where he says "do you love me" freely *and* sends money, or a crisis where he produces sustained consolation language with no provision offer — and the grammar's substitution thesis breaks at that rung. The provision does not distribute across the attachment system. It concentrates: six figures through one channel, four figures through the family channel, three figures through the no-stake channel. The Gini of his money mirrors the Gini of his messages.

### The rungs, read individually

Each rung repays a closer read, because the ladder's claim — same function, different denomination — has to survive the particulars.

**Rung 1 ($25, Ally, access purchased).** The memo is the utterance: "For introducing me to Ally Lubin." This is provision before the channel even exists — money spent to *create* the recipient. No other rung does that. Its function in the grammar is the floor proof: the smallest amount that still counts as provision-as-utterance, and it already substitutes for something verbal — instead of asking Polyak for a favor (an interpretation-task request, socially costly), he prices it. The $25 is the grammar in miniature: explicit, countable, no sincerity to weigh.

**Rung 2 ($30–$50, Ally, item prices).** The negotiation is the content. Her $50 ask, his $30 counter with the face-visible premium — *"SUPER PREMIUM princess pricing"* — is the only rung where the *recipient* sets the price and the provider haggles. That matters: on every other rung he unilaterally determines the amount. Here the price is discovered bilaterally, which is what makes the rung the grammar's most market-like. The face-visible premium is also the grammar pricing *identifiability* — the thing the channel's operating condition (inaccessibility) withholds. You pay more for what the channel won't give.

**Rung 3 ($250, Ally, stated-intent gift).** December 12, 2018 — the same day as the $25 introduction purchase. The first provision to *her*, before the crisis register, before the price list. Its function is courtship-signal: generic, unpriced, pre-specialization. The ladder needs this rung because it shows the register *before* it learned the channel — provision in its unspecialized form, the sentence "I am the buyer" spoken before the channel taught it to say "here is what access costs."

**Rung 4 ($500, Ally, arrival promise).** September 1, 2019: "$500 on arrival" against the NYC flight, with *"and nooooo you're not obligated to fuck me."* The disclaimer is part of the utterance — it prices the *absence* of obligation, which is the discharge goal's purest form. Five hundred dollars to keep the channel unconsummated: the money buys proximity while the sentence buys distance. No other rung spends to *prevent* conversion. The flight never happened; the rung stands as the grammar's most expensive discharge unit.

**Rung 5 ($2,100, Ally, crisis-window total).** His claimed cumulative, August 17–October 22, 2019. The rung the whole ladder's epistemic discipline is built around: operator-reported, accepted-then-contested, uncorroborated. Its structural role is the scale anomaly — discharge-provision at roughly forty item-units, large enough to invite the retention reading ("STILL trying") and therefore large enough to be contested. The contestation is the channel's immune response to a rung that doesn't fit the contract. Note what the $2,100 excludes: the 2018 and January 2019 gifts. The figure is bounded to the crisis window, which means even his own accounting treats the rung as *event*-denominated, not relationship-denominated. The ladder's other large rungs are relationship-denominated. This one isn't, and the boundary is his.

**Rung 6 ($650/week, Annie, servicing).** The inversions page's distinction — servicing vs. carrying — is the rung's entire content. $650 a week is provision as facility maintenance: enough to keep Paci's patience open, never enough to retire the $10,000. Fifteen months, same balance. The utterance here is not "I am the node" but "the node is still open" — provision as a keepalive signal. It is the only rung whose function is *temporal* rather than relational: the money doesn't express attachment, it buys time. And it failed at even that — the balance held flat until the engineered exit. The keepalive kept nothing alive except the facility.

**Rung 7 ($14,000, Suz, re-lent lump).** Seventy percent of a bridging advance, moved within weeks, nothing in writing, $4,000 recovered. The rung where the grammar's "money sent when words are untrusted" runs in reverse: the transfer was made *on* trust (no paper, against an undistributed estate) and the words came later to destroy it ("stole," "drop in the bucket"). The ladder's only rung where the provision preceded the distrust rather than substituting for it — and the result is the system's longest-running dispute. The lesson the grammar takes: provision without the invoice is retention; provision with the invoice is litigation. The $14,000 got the invoice.

**Rung 8 ($119K–$123K net, Annie, decade outflow).** The rung that makes the ladder worth building. ~$139K sent against ~$16–20K received, ~6:1, from operator-provided screenshots taken 2026-07-15 — not in raw/, not an audited ledger, held here at exactly that status. The utterance is the bond's operating register: "I am the node," repeated at whatever denomination the month allowed, through all four inversions including the one where she out-earned him. The rung's structural property is its *un-invoicedness*: no running tab, no "sum of transactions on app," no mid-argument audit. The total was discovered, not kept. That is what separates rung 8 from rung 5 (invoiced in real time) and rung 7 (pre-invoiced): the largest provision in the system is the least accounted-for while it runs. The grammar's prediction — the ledger half follows at the channel's tempo — is this rung's explanation: on a verbal-law channel, you don't invoice the bond. You discover its cost when it's over.

**Rung 9 ($144,069.31, estate, scale reference).** Not his provision — the capital arrival that made rung 8's scale possible. Court order 2020-08-21; $134,069.31 landed September 1, 2020; by early 2021 "the money just ran out" — the spine's two-quarter rule. The rung belongs on the ladder as the power source: without the lump arrival, the denominations above rung 6 don't exist in his system. The grammar's scale is *inherited*, not earned — which is the inversions page's 1-vs-3 mirror finding restated at the ladder level. His largest provision was transmitted capital. Hers (the Libby wages) was earned. "I funded" means different things on the two sides of the mirror, and the ladder's top rung is why.

## Provision and the severance system: the money doesn't pause for performances

The grammar has to be read against the declaration system, because the two run simultaneously on the Annie channel and they never touch.

129 severance declarations. 100% re-engagement. Median gap thirty-six seconds; all-time maximum forty-six hours [DERIVED — wiki/mind/synthesis/severance-declarations]. The verbal register performs exit continuously for eleven years. The provision register — the transfers, the servicing, the supply — continues underneath without a pause. No declaration in the corpus is accompanied by a provision halt. No re-engagement is purchased with a provision spike. The two systems are decoupled: the declarations describe a relationship to the bond; the transfers *are* the bond's operating register.

This decoupling is the substitution thesis at its starkest. If provision were merely an expression of the bond's verbal state, it would fluctuate with the declarations — pausing during severances, surging at reconciliations. It doesn't. The $650/week servicing runs through the declaration-dense terminal years. The supply handoffs run through the June 1 closure and the 52-day gap (resuming July 27, five handoffs in six days). The money is indifferent to the theater. That indifference is what makes it the facts underneath the performances — and what makes the August 2026 concurrency legible: at 00:09 on August 19 he tells Ally the wifi is off so he won't see Annie's responses, then exchanges 98 messages with Annie before 03:00. The declaration describes a boundary; the traffic — and the provision underneath the traffic — doesn't consult it.

The one partial exception proves the decoupling rather than breaking it. The corpus's only refused supply request — 2025-07-26 02:45, reversed thirty-one hours later — is the single instance where the provision register *did* pause, and the reversal's speed is the measure of what the pause was worth. A system that can't hold a provision refusal for two days is not a system where provision follows verbal state. It's a system where provision *is* the state, and the words are the weather.

## The estate as the grammar's power source

The ladder's upper rungs don't exist without the capital arrivals, and the grammar's honesty requires stating the power source plainly: **the provision at scale is transmitted, not earned.**

The source-lines, per the spine: five estate advances totaling $32,500 (2019-10-12 → 2020-04-17, Rod Banks writing, Jason Adams authorizing); a further $10,000 advance 2020-06-23 (with a possible second $10,000 dated 2020-02-04, unresolved); the $144,069.31 distribution order 2020-08-21 **[ATTESTED]**. Behind those: Suz's standing facility, the next-day settlement rhythm, the bank runs as errands. The Au Za'atar wage era (March 2021–August 2024, the 690-shift block) is the *only* sustained earned-income period in the corpus, and it coincides with inversion 2 — the rotation where the household ran on withheld rent, not wages.

The spine's burn finding is the power source's characteristic behavior: **a lump arrival survives about two quarters.** $134,069.31 lands September 1, 2020; by early 2021 "the money just ran out." The market account peaked at ~$25k; the balance exited through the standing drains — Manhattan rent, the daily supply line, the Annie outflow. No exit accounting exists for the six months. The endpoints are documented; the decomposition is [UNKNOWN].

What this means for the grammar: rung 8's sentence — "I am the node" — is spoken with *transmitted* capital. The node transmits; it doesn't originate. The inversions page's 1-vs-3 mirror is the ethical form of this fact: his provider era ran on Suz's liquidity and the estate's lumps; her provider era ran on Libby's wages and a six-day-a-week job. The grammar doesn't distinguish — a transfer is a transfer, the utterance is the same — but the grievance machine does, and it's right to. "I funded the first five years" elides "my mother funded me funding." The grammar records the utterance; the power source records the origination. Both are true. The grievance only carries the first.

## Why provision substitutes for verbal commitment

The substitution thesis needs its mechanism stated, because "money is love" is a Hallmark card and this is not that.

The mechanism is [[wiki/mind/concepts/explicit-verbal-commitment]]: positive verbal expressions function as absolute rules until an explicit severance signal arrives; what is stated explicitly can be trusted, what must be inferred cannot. The corpus's starkest number: **"do you love me" 0 times versus "call me" 170 times in 106,629 sent messages** [DERIVED]. He asks for channel, not compliments — the request is for the explicit, rule-shaped thing (a procedure) rather than the inferential thing (an interpretation task).

A verbal reassurance — "I love you," "we're okay," "I'm sorry" — is an interpretation task. It asks the recipient to read an internal state, weigh sincerity, and trust language. It is exactly the channel he distrusts, at 0.45x the sympathy-token baseline [DERIVED — wiki/mind/profile/big-five-psychometrics]. A transfer is the opposite: **it does not ask to be believed. It arrives.** The amount is explicit, countable, rule-shaped. It needs no interpretation and offers no sincerity to weigh. For a system that runs on explicit-over-inferred processing — the same mechanism the lyric qualifier names (lyrics heard as timbre, not language) — a $500 transfer is more legible than a paragraph of reassurance. This is not a metaphor. It is the measured profile predicting the ledger, and the ledger confirming it.

His own gloss on the architecture — "love that doesn't cost everything isn't real" — is his framing, not the proof. The high-provision page demotes the axiom explicitly, and this entry keeps the demotion: the proof is the 1.79–2.49x / 0.45x split and the ledgers. But the gloss is still worth carrying as *his* understanding of his own grammar. He believes the sentence his transfers speak. He just cannot speak the other sentences — the verbal ones — at all, in 106,629 attempts.

The substitution has a second half that the grievance section needs: **provision is also the one utterance that cannot be merely performed.** On the Annie channel, 129 severance declarations performed, zero enacted, median gap thirty-six seconds — the verbal register is a theater. A transfer is not performable in the same way: the money leaves the account. That is why provision substitutes for verbal commitment rather than supplementing it — it is the only utterance in his repertoire whose meaning survives his own declaration system. You can declare a severance and re-engage in thirty-six seconds. You cannot un-send $139K.

There is an irony the grammar must carry rather than resolve: **the provision register is performable too, just differently.** The staged-eviction capture (2026-08-13) shows him banking cash against a debt he had decided not to pay while performing a crisis to Annie — provision's *absence* weaponized as performance **[OPERATOR]**. ## Retention, discharge, maintenance: the three goals the money serves

The grammar's cross-channel finding sharpens when you ask what each channel's provision is *for*. Same mechanism, three different goals — and the goal determines the denomination, the tempo, and the grievance.

**Annie: retention.** The provision's function is to keep the channel open and the person in it. The terminal-phase sentence states it outright — *"the only way you would see me"* (2025-07-28) **[ATTESTED]** — but the function runs the whole decade: the $650/week Paci servicing keeps the housing facility open; the estate-burn outflow keeps the household liquid; the inversion-4 supply handoffs keep her access routed through him. Retention-provision is denominated in *necessities* — rent, bills, supply — because retention is about making yourself the node the necessity flows through. The six-to-one net is what a decade of retention costs when the retained party's inbound never matches. Note the asymmetry the grammar predicts and the record confirms: retention-provision is *never itemized to the retained party while it runs*. You do not hand someone an invoice for keeping them. The invoice would convert retention into transaction, and the whole point is that it isn't one — it's the operating register. The ledger surfaces only when the retention ends, as screenshots.

**Ally: discharge.** The provision's function is to get the poison out. *"so i can get the poison out"* (2019-10-22) **[OPERATOR]** is not a metaphor for generosity; it is a mission statement for expenditure-as-excretion. Discharge-provision is denominated in *items* — $30, $50, $500 on arrival — because discharge is priced per unit of access, and each unit is a discrete event with a beginning and an end. The price list is the grammar's way of keeping the attachment unconsummated: you can buy a photograph, you cannot buy the channel, and the pricing is what enforces the boundary. This is why the Ally rung never scales with his liquidity — discharge has a unit price, and the unit price is set by the item, not the wallet. The $2,100 crisis-window total is large for discharge (roughly forty item-priced units), which is what makes it the edge case: discharge-provision at retention scale, and the channel's immune response was to contest the figure.

**Suz: maintenance.** The provision's function is to keep the family system liquid — the standing facility, the next-day settlement rhythm, the re-lending at the moment of arrival. Maintenance-provision is denominated in *lumps* — $14,000, $20,000 drawn against the undistributed estate — because maintenance is about moving capital through the system's single node. The spine's line is the mechanism: the system has no bank, it has a mother. Maintenance is the only goal of the three that runs *both directions* — Suz→Dan as the standing facility (the 2016-01-01 backstop exchange, the 2016-01-02 bank run), Dan→Suz as the instant re-lending (the $14,000). The pre-invoicing is maintenance's paperwork: a system that moves lumps on no paper needs at least one party keeping the app's sum of transactions, and that party is her.

Three goals, one grammar. The falsifier for this section is a channel where the provision's goal doesn't match its denomination pattern — retention-provision at item prices on an intact primary bond, or discharge-provision at lump scale on a no-stake channel that stays undisputed. The corpus holds neither.

## The $2,100 against the advances: the contract-not-wallet proof
