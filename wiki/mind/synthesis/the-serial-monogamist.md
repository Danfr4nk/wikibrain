# The Serial Monogamist

Dan has been inside a long-term romantic relationship for roughly
seventeen consecutive years — since November 2009, through two bonds, with
the longest unattached interval the record can measure lasting fifty-two
days ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-unbroken-bond]]). In that time he has
produced almost nothing in the way of a lived account of single life, and
exactly one sentence stating his own theory of his dating pattern: *"i'm a
serial monogamist so i've only been with a few girls, and they all fit a
very specific type"* (Dan, 2019-08-17, 22:26). That sentence is the only
instance of the phrase anywhere in 217,573 messages, and it was offered to a
third party with nothing riding on the answer.

What the record does contain, in unusual density, is the mechanics of how
one bond ends and the next begins. The 2015 handoff from
[[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis]] to [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]]
completed inside a single calendar month, the replacement sourced before
the vacancy occurred ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/bond-switch-2015]]); the June 1,
2026 severance held for fifty-two days and then reentangled with the same
partner rather than resolving into a period of dating around. Both are
transfers or aborted transfers — never an occupied stretch of single life.

This page tests the one self-theory and the long pattern against the same
record, and asks what kind of claim "a very specific type" is when the
archive that should confirm it keeps handing back specification sheets
instead of felt preferences. The corpus is a record of attention and
behavior, not interior state; nothing here is a claim about what Dan feels
or needs, only about what the dated record shows him doing, saying, and
writing down.

## The claim in plain form

> Whenever the corpus can check a claim Dan makes about his own dating
> pattern or what draws him to a woman against the documented record, the
> claim resolves to either an engineered specification or an allocation
> policy over an arrangement he authored — never to an organic account of a
> felt type arrived at by trial. And whenever the corpus can check whether
> "single" is a state Dan has actually occupied, the record shows a
> transfer completed before the vacancy could be examined, not an interval
> he lived inside.

This is stated as a rule so it can be broken: it is a prediction about
every checkable instance, and the falsifiers below name what would break
it. The rest of this page is the evidence against which the rule is run.

## The occupied slot, at the resolution of days

[[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-unbroken-bond]] establishes the header fact, and
its chronology is worth carrying over whole because the claim rests on the
two bonds being contiguous, not merely long:

| Period | Bond | Duration | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ~2007–2009 | *(not established)* | — | Post-high-school window is undocumented in `raw/` |
| Nov 2009 – Nov 2015 | [[wiki/people/alexis-armel]] | ~6 years | Includes a Jul–Oct 2013 separation; the bond resumed |
| Nov 2015 – present | [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]] | ~10.5 years | Severance 2026-06-01, issued by Dan; contact resumed 2026-07-23 after 52 days |

The 2015 handoff, at the resolution of days
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/bond-switch-2015]]): **~2015-11-24** — Annie enters,
introduced by Alexis herself (*"HAPPY ONE WEEK SINCE LEX HANDED YOU TO
ME"* — Annie, Dec 1). **2015-11-29** — Dan meets Annie in person; the same
night, outbound: *"Things are collapsing with lex. You might be seeing a
lot more Annie very soon."* **2015-12-01** — Alexis moves out (*"Helping
lex leave"*). **2015-12-01 to 12-09** — breakup (*"I'm in love with Annie
Ulmer"*), first dates, panic attacks, move. **2015-12-10** — **728 messages
sent in a single day.** Then 682, then 363. The slot was vacant for
something on the order of a week, and a 2026-08-02 correction to
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-unbroken-bond]] sharpened the agency: it was
**Dan** who left Alexis within twenty-four hours of meeting Annie, not the
reverse. This is not rebound behavior in the ordinary sense — a rebound
implies a gap being coped with. There was no gap to cope with. **The
replacement was sourced before the vacancy occurred.**

The 2026 interval is the only other documented vacancy, and it is measured
rather than assumed. The June 1 closing line — *"Goodbye forever. This was
not how it should have ended but. sic semper lupanis."* — was corrected on
2026-08-13 to Dan's own outbound (`2026-06-01 00:27:49 America/New_York`,
sent, the last of an unbroken seven-message outbound run) rather than
Annie's. Across all five Annie identifiers, dyadic and group threads
together, the fifty-two days to `2026-07-23 14:54:01` contain **zero
outbound from Dan** and **seven inbound from Annie in four separate
approaches**, none answered. Contact resumed July 23, running **624
messages across four days**. Fifty-two days is the longest vacancy the
record can measure — and it sits well under the roughly-six-month bar this
page's first falsifier requires. There is no week in the documented adult
record where Dan is dating, single, or between relationships in the
ordinary sense of the phrase. The slot is occupied, or it is in the process
of being re-occupied by someone already identified.

The one real hole: the **2007–2009 window is undocumented**, the same gap
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-unbroken-bond]] already flags. Whether Dan
experienced any period of being single between high school and the Alexis
relationship is unknown, and it is the single biggest hole in this page's
central claim.

## The transfer, not the exit

[[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-rescue-premise]] is the member most directly
relevant to a "perspective on single life," because it tests the specific
premise that being extracted from a relationship — landing, if only
briefly, in the unattached state this page is about — would help. The
corpus supplies a controlled instance to test it against: the corpus's
**only completed exit from a long relationship** is the one this page has
already named, and it did not produce single life even briefly. It
produced a transfer, completed inside a week, to a bond that then ran for
the next decade. That page's own framing is the sharpest available
statement of what this means for anyone hoping a rescue would work: *"somebody
comes and gets me out of this"* is *"the single most instantiated move in
Dan's romantic record,"* with *"a sample size of one completed instance,"*
and that instance's cost was the entire following decade. A system built
this way does not distribute its load into a period of being single when
the primary bond fails; per [[wiki/mind/synthesis/single-channel]]'s
finding, it relocates the whole of the load onto whichever channel is
nearest. Single life, on this evidence, is not a state Dan's architecture
is built to pass through. It is the one outcome every documented transition
in the record has been engineered to avoid.

The 2026 case narrows the claim rather than refuting it, and the narrowing
is on the record. The 2026-08-13 correction on
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-unbroken-bond]] withdrew that page's "exit is
structurally unavailable" clause, because Dan did leave — unilaterally, by
his own sentence, and sourced no successor: the corpus contains no
replacement bond in June or July 2026, and the slot sat empty for
fifty-two days while he refused four direct approaches from its previous
occupant. What replaces the strong clause is the better one: *the
constraint was never on the act of leaving; it is on **staying gone**.*
The slot tolerates vacancy, and tolerates it for weeks; what it does not
tolerate is vacancy becoming permanent. Two cases now exist and they
disagree about method — 2015 filled the slot before vacating it, 2026
vacated it empty — which makes successor-sourcing **situational**, not
structural.

Two further entries from [[wiki/mind/synthesis/vacancy-rule]] keep the
picture honest. First, a successor bid was live within twenty hours of the
June 1 severance anyway — the Ally burst at 20:06:05, misaddressed, **19
hours 38 minutes** after the closing line — so the fifty-two-day hold is
not the story of a man resting in vacancy; it is the story of a bid
misfiring and nothing taking hold. Second, the August 18–19 dual-load
(**408 messages to Annie, 552 to Ally in forty-eight hours**) is the
rule's one bounded contradiction — two-channel load inside seventeen
years, carried as the exception that sets the singular-slot claim's
tolerance rather than argued away. And the decade's only rival bid for an
occupied primary slot — Kristin, **2025-08-29 to early November, 22,018
messages in ten weeks, exclusivity demands**, extinguished by early
November, failed December reactivation — is the contested-slot case: the
one time another bond bid for the position, the slot resolved to one.
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/no-platonic-channel]] generalizes the mechanism
behind the sourcing side: sustained ties carrying overtures are sourced
while the incumbent bond still runs, so the pipeline exists before the
transfer is ever called a transfer.

## The self-theory, checked against the record

The corpus contains exactly one first-person statement of Dan's own theory
of his romantic pattern, offered to a third party with nothing riding on
the answer: *"i'm a serial monogamist so i've only been with a few girls,
and they all fit a very specific type"* (2019-08-17, 22:26). Two things
about the timing matter. It is the only instance of the phrase anywhere in
217,573 messages, and it lands six weeks before the
[[wiki/people/kelly-johansson|Kelly Johansson]] run (Sep–Oct 2019, a
five-week run of paid sessions) and two months before the filmed MMF with
[[wiki/people/bryan-5088682461|Bryan]] (Oct 20, 2019) — the most sexually
open documented window of his adult life.
[[wiki/mind/psychosexual/arrangement-history]] runs the corpus-wide check
this deserves: `monogam` appears 7 times and `faithful` 3 times across the
entire message record, against 304 camming-signal tokens and 107
non-monogamy tokens from the same corpus. **The vocabulary of commitment
is nearly absent from a record in which the vocabulary of arrangement is
abundant.** That page's own conclusion is the one this page adopts:
*"openness was never the requirement here — authorship was."* Applied to
the self-theory, "serial monogamist" is best read not as a description Dan
checked against his behavior, but as the label the continuous-occupancy
pattern earns almost by definition — one partner at a time, no matter how
open the arrangement inside that partnership becomes. It describes the
slot, not the sexual practice, and the corpus shows Dan using the word at
the exact moment the practice was furthest from what the word ordinarily
means. A standing limit: the self-theory quote is n=1, and whether Dan
stated it again in different words elsewhere in the eleven-year corpus has
not been checked systematically — a `bin/mine-messages` pass for
self-description language around relationships and dating would either
strengthen or complicate this page's reading of the one instance found.

The "very specific type" half of the sentence has its own test, and it
also does not survive contact with the record as a claim about organic
preference. [[wiki/mind/concepts/erotic-architecture]] documents a literal
specification sheet — roughly twenty physical attributes, each scored on a
1–10 scale, hair color given as a hex range, a five-item aesthetic profile
scoring named archetypes by intensity (*Effortlessly Sexy* 9.5,
*Post-Soviet Waif* 8), and a rejected trait (*Tomboy Femininity*, scored 2)
named explicitly rather than simply omitted. Whether or not the document
names a specific person is unresolved — that page treats it as generic by
construction and draws no line from it to anyone in the corpus, and if it
does encode a real person, this page's "engineered rather than organic"
reading would need checking against whether the specification was built to
match someone already met rather than to search for someone not yet found.
But its existence answers what kind of claim "a very specific type" is:
not a felt preference discovered through experience, but a quantified
target constructed with the same precision the corpus applies to message
counts and chemical dosing. [[wiki/people/ally-lubin-cognitive-profile]]
supplies the closest thing the record has to Dan stating an organic
attraction in his own words — the 2026 love letter's line about her
*"ability to spot trends"* and *"spotting slang and memes that I won't
find out about for like 3 more months"* — and even that sentence, on the
page's own reading, describes a quality he **prices**, not a feeling he
simply has. Both documents belong to the same person and the same habit:
nothing in the record of what attracts Dan is left to run on unexamined
default.

## The mechanism

The record carries several overlapping mechanisms for the pattern, and
they agree at the attention level even where they come from different
instruments. [[wiki/mind/profile/enneagram-5w4]] records an **sx-dominant
instinctual stack**, and [[wiki/mind/synthesis/bond-switch-2015]] states the
consequence directly: sexual-dominant *"organises life around **one**
relationship at maximum voltage, not many."* The weighted profile
instrument's one replicated measurement sits on the same finding from a
different direction: relational attention Gini **0.961 outbound, replicated
at 0.9556 inbound** — attention, money, and continuity load onto a tiny
number of chosen channels with no failover. The prediction that
single-and-diffuse would leave the organizing structure of life missing,
not merely leave a man unattached, is consistent with a record that shows
the position being refilled at the first opportunity every time it has come
open, and it is the reading this page adopts rather than treating the
seventeen years as evidence of a stated preference for monogamy examined
and chosen.

Layer on the second mechanism from
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-unbroken-bond]]: the deep attachment system was
arguably locked down since the 2005 paternal rupture, the six-year Alexis
bond ran with that system suppressed — the control case — and Annie was
its first activation. This resolves what would otherwise be a puzzle: how
a bond can last six years and still be described as shallow. The slot's
*occupancy* and the attachment system's *activation* are separate
variables. For six years the slot was full and the system was off. That is
the strongest form of the finding: **the requirement is that the position
be filled, and it is prior to, and independent of, whether the
relationship is emotionally live.**

The contradiction with [[wiki/mind/concepts/chaos-preference]] is worth
carrying explicitly: a declared appetite for entropy has kept one
attachment slot continuously occupied for seventeen years. The one domain
where absolute order is demanded is the one he chose himself — the
objection was never to order, it was to imposed order. He defends the bond
against exactly the disorder he wants everywhere else, because it is his.

One asymmetry in the corpus sharpens the picture of what continuity runs
alongside. `cheat` returns 141 uses across 217,573 records, and every era
names Dan as the party cheated on, never the reverse — *"Lex cheated on me
2 weeks in after I moved her to fla"* (2015-11-28), *"I got cheated on
while I was working before and it's a super helpless feeling"*
(2016-02-18), *"I've always been super worried about being cheated on"*
(2024-08-12). The rate more than quadruples in 2025 — 62 hits, 4.29 per
1,000 sent messages against 0.24–1.22 in every prior year — almost all of
it directed at Annie. Seventeen years of continuous occupancy therefore run
alongside a decades-long, directed fear of infidelity, and two documented
instances of it actually happening. Whether the continuity causes the fear
or the fear causes the continuity is not settled by the corpus and is
stated here as open rather than assumed either way.

A load-bearing limit, kept with the mechanisms it constrains: no primary
source states, in Dan's own words, why "single" is avoided rather than
merely never occurring. Every mechanism this section cites — the
sx-dominant stack, the occupancy/activation split, the no-failover
concentration — is an outside reading of the pattern, not his stated
account of it. The corpus is a record of attention and behavior; it is
silent on interior state, and an interior claim the instruments cannot see
is untested, not false.

## The pattern is older than any adult relationship

[[wiki/timeline/events/bald-eagle-cummings]] supplies the earliest data
point in the corpus and it changes how everything above should be read.
Describing his seventh-grade girlfriend — by his own account the prettiest
girl in the class — Dan names the shape of the story himself: *"my first
experience with something i would go through many times — somehow getting
a girl way above my level to date me."* That sentence is dated to
roughly 2000–2001, a full eight years before the earliest bond
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-unbroken-bond]] can document. **Dan had a named
theory of his own dating pattern before he had a documented adult dating
history to build one from.** Every later instance — the "very specific
type" claim, the engineered ideal-face document, the mismatch pattern
visible across the corpus — is therefore better read as a repetition of a
self-narrated template laid down at twelve than as an independent
conclusion each adult relationship separately arrived at.

## Falsifiers and predictions

1. **A documented period of Dan being genuinely single** — dating multiple
   people without a primary bond, or simply unattached and reporting on the
   experience — lasting longer than roughly six months, at any point since
   2009. None has been found in this pass; the longest measured vacancy is
   the fifty-two days of June–July 2026.
2. **A first-person account of attraction to a specific person that does
   not resolve to a priced, specified, or engineered quality** when checked
   against the surrounding record — a stated preference that survives being
   tested rather than one this page's members already show collapsing under
   inspection.
3. **A documented instance of Dan expressing relief, contentment, or
   positive self-report about being unattached**, comparable in register to
   the [[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Fran]]-vigil "got good closure" sentence
   ([[wiki/mind/concepts/acquisition-drive]]) but about single life rather
   than about a finished project. The corpus has not been searched
   specifically for this.

1. If a future export ever documents Dan genuinely single for a sustained
   period, the corpus should show him treating it as an anomaly requiring
   resolution — actively working to end it — rather than as a neutral or
   preferred state, consistent with the sx-dominant mechanism this page
   reasons from.
2. Any future first-person statement of "what I look for" will be
   checkable against a document, a habit, or a specification, the same way
   the ideal-face file checks the "very specific type" claim, rather than
   standing as an unexamined preference.
3. The next documented transition out of a long bond, if one occurs, will
   show a successor identified before or immediately upon the vacancy,
   consistent with both documented transitions to date (2015, 2026).

> and it checks in the way predicted: specified, priced, engineered

> the profile's structural appetites — ongoing over one-off, open-ended over
> bounded, "it happens again monthly" a 10 — sit inside the serial-monogamy
> identity without contradiction: he wants continuity *of arrangement*, not
> of exclusivity. Second, the profile postdates the Annie decade's end by a
> month; whether these are stable traits or post-severance reconstruction
> is untestable until the v2 causal instrument runs.

## Conflicts in the record

- **2026-08-13 — the June 1 severance is Dan's own outbound, not Annie's.**
  [[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-unbroken-bond]] had read the June 1 closing
  line as terminal and implicitly as Annie's; the deep export showed it
  sent by Dan at 00:27:49, the last of an unbroken seven-message outbound
  run. That page's "exit is structurally unavailable" and "leaving
  requires a successor" clauses were withdrawn: Dan left unilaterally and
  sourced no successor, and the slot sat empty for fifty-two days. The
  replacement claim is weaker and better: the constraint is on *staying
  gone*, not on leaving. This page's account of the 2026 interval follows
  the corrected version.
- **2026-08-15 — the 2015 "cheating revelation" did not happen that week.**
  The bullet on [[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-unbroken-bond]] naming a
  2015-11-28 cheating revelation was removed: the message it rested on is
  Dan describing the **2009** start of the Alexis relationship. The
  sequence this page inherits is sharper for the removal — the removed
  bullet was the only item supplying a *reason* for the ending, and it
  leaves a six-year bond closing in seventy-two hours with nothing
  prompting it but the arrival of a replacement.
- **2026-08-02 — the agency of the 2015 switch is Dan's.** The claim that
  Annie left her existing relationship within twenty-four hours of the
  November 2015 introduction was corrected: it was Dan who left Alexis
  within twenty-four hours of meeting Annie. This page's
  transfer-not-exit reading gained its sharpest instance — his own act,
  not his partner's — and needed no revision to absorb it.

## Assessment

The record licenses the strong reading and refuses the interior one. The
strong reading: the pair-bond slot has been continuously occupied since
November 2009, the two documented transitions are a transfer and an aborted
transfer, the longest measured vacancy is fifty-two days, and every
first-person statement of what draws Dan to a woman resolves, on checking,
to an engineered specification or an authored arrangement rather than an
organic type. The refused reading: why the vacancy is avoided. No primary
source states it, and every mechanism on this page — the sx-dominant stack,
the occupancy/activation split, the no-failover concentration of attention
— is an outside reading of the pattern rather than Dan's own account of
it. Held to attention level, the finding is that the corpus shows the
position being refilled at the first opportunity every time it has come
open, and shows nothing that functions as single life in any ordinary
sense. What the record cannot establish — why, and whether the 2007–2009
window holds an exception — stays stated as open.
