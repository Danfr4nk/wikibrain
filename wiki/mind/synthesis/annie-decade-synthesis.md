# The Annie Decade: Formation, Tenure, and the 2026 Unwinding

The relationship with [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie Ulmer]] is the only attachment in Dan's record large enough to be counted rather than described. The held corpus holds 97,768 unique messages dated 2015-11-28 to 2026-06-05 across four handles [kb/entities/annie-ulmer.md; kb/syntheses/annie-decade.md], and the systematic read over that corpus — the Annie Read — is the biography's one instrumented experiment: a near-decade of two-sided traffic, an origin week that can be dated to the minute, and an exit idiom that can be measured because it happened often enough to form a distribution.

That scale is what makes the decade matter to the wiki and what limits what any single synthesis can claim about it. The decade contains a four-year hole with no two-sided message data, a period where the record resumes already in collapse, and a terminal year where the counts are unusually precise precisely because the system that produced them was counting as it went. This entry holds the decade at the scale its source synthesis intends — formation, tenure, and the 2026 unwinding — and links to the event and people pages that carry the dated detail. It does not re-derive the biography. Where the KB synthesis and the entity pages disagree, both are carried and the disagreement is left visible.

The decade's central measurement is the one its source synthesis puts first: in 129 distinct severance episodes in which Dan-sent severance language appears, every episode that had a following message resumed contact. One hundred percent. Median gap thirty-six seconds. All-time maximum forty-six hours [kb/events/2026-08-23-attachment-model-resolved.md; kb/syntheses/annie-decade.md]. A declared exit in this relationship is not an exit. Measured against the full corpus, it is a rung on the escalation ladder — the form a check-in takes when it is answered quickly. The decade is worth a synthesis page because only a decade produces a base rate, and only a base rate can make a later severance legible as an event rather than a story.

## Formation: the bond switch, November 2015

The corpus opens already inside the relationship. The first held rows are dated 2015-11-28 — Annie's birthday — with Dan already calling her "the love of my life" that evening and Annie answering "I've known that since day one" minutes later [kb/events/2015-11-annie-relationship-starts.md; dat:0165-annie-record-origin-findings-nov-2015]. The phrase "day one" is already past tense on the corpus's first day. Back-dating from Annie's December 1 message — "HAPPY ONE WEEK SINCE LEX HANDED YOU TO ME" — puts an introduction near November 24, 2015, with Alexis Armel herself making the introduction [kb/events/2015-11-annie-relationship-starts.md]. The November 29 Uniontown Country Club meeting is a first in-person meeting, not a first contact.

Escalation is measurable in hours. "YOU ARE MY EVERYTHING" is timestamped 2015-11-29 08:24:14 UTC, 03:24 EST, roughly ninety minutes after the first in-person meeting [kb/events/2015-11-annie-relationship-starts.md]. The dossier baseline that placed escalation on days two and three does not survive the held timestamps. What the minute-level corroboration establishes is tempo: the merger idiom is present within the first evening, not after a courtship interval.

Two corrections sit inside the origin and this page carries them rather than smoothing them. The 24-hour switch from Alexis was Dan's move, previously misattributed to Annie; Alexandra Lubin's correction on 2026-08-02 reversed the subject — Dan left his relationship within twenty-four hours, and the point is mutual [kb/entities/annie-ulmer.md]. Suzanne Frank brokered Dan's side of the switch, including the November 30, 2015 provision message verified verbatim in the held corpus [kb/entities/suzanne-frank.md; dat:0294]. The eviction of Alexis and the start with Annie land within the same seventy-two-hour window [kb/events/2015-11-alexis-evicted.md]. The full dated sequence is on [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]] and [[wiki/timeline/events/group-chat-closure|the relationship-start event]]; this synthesis records only the scale and the tempo, because the tempo is what the later exit distribution is measured against: a bond that formed in ninety minutes produced exits that, for eleven years, reversed in thirty-six seconds at the median.

## The control case: Alexis Armel

[[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis Armel]] is the decade's control case in the KB's own framing: six years, Thanksgiving 2009 to November 2015, that "occupied the attachment slot without ever activating it" [kb/syntheses/annie-decade.md; kb/entities/alexis-armel.md]. The phrase is the KB's interpretation, and this page carries it as a reading rather than a fact about the earlier relationship. What the held record supports without interpretation is narrower and still useful: the two relationships are adjacent to the day, the eviction and the bond switch overlap, and no comparable exit-episode idiom was derived from the Alexis thread in the work the KB cites. The comparison is load-bearing for the synthesis because it bounds the decade's claim. If the exit distribution were a property of any long relationship in this corpus, the control case would show it. In the KB's accounting, it does not.

The Alexis thread also anchors the decade's prehistory. The Thanksgiving 2009 episode — the final active-use episode before the February 17, 2010 Suboxone day-zero — is dated through the Alexis relationship [kb/entities/alexis-armel.md; dat:0060], and the November 2018 arrangement reunion, including the verbatim line "Initially I offered her $3000 to be a plaything for Annie," is confirmed against the held corpus [dat:0124; kb/entities/alexis-armel.md]. Those details belong on the Alexis page. Their function here is structural: the control case is not a footnote to the decade; it is the adjacent dataset that lets the decade's distribution read as specific rather than generic.

## The comparison case: Kristin Prentiss

[[wiki/people/kristin-prentiss|Kristin Prentiss]] is the decade's comparison case, and the comparison runs the other direction. Ten weeks, August to November 2025, 20,014 held messages, ended over $40 [kb/entities/kristin-prentiss.md; dat:0801]. The block imposed December 9, 2025 has been held since. She is the record's one clean severance: the most exhaustively counted thread in the corpus, with the terminal sixteen minutes verifying message-for-message [kb/entities/kristin-prentiss.md; dat:0803].

Against a base rate of 100% resumption across 129 episodes, one ten-week relationship completed the operation the eleven-year relationship performed 129 times and never finished. The KB synthesis makes the comparison explicitly: the Kristin block is the proof that the instrument can hold a block; the Annie block is the experiment that tests whether it can hold one in the hard case [kb/syntheses/annie-decade.md; kb/syntheses/severance-2026.md]. The denomination carries the same structural point the ladder in [[wiki/mind/synthesis/provision-grammar]] carries for money: a three-month bond ends over a three-month denomination, and the grievance structure is proportional to the bond rather than to the figure.

## Tenure: the arrangement era and the record hole

The decade's tenure, insofar as the held messages describe it, runs through cohabitation in Uniontown and New York, the 307 East 76th Street lease signing pinned to February 25, 2019 by contemporaneous photographs [kb/entities/annie-ulmer.md], and an authored non-monogamous arrangement with paid and unpaid third parties. The summer-2019 escort window under the alias "Hazel" is carried as old-wiki testimony with no held-corpus corroboration yet in the KB [kb/entities/annie-ulmer.md; kb/syntheses/annie-decade.md]. The November 2018 Alexis arrangement is the tenure's one fully verbatim-confirmed arrangement episode. The tenure pages — [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]] for the biography, the arrangement material for its own pages — hold the detail. This synthesis records the era's shape only: a relationship that, in the years the corpus can see, ran an explicit arrangement while the exit idiom ran underneath it, unbroken.

Then the record stops. From November 2020 to December 2024 the Annie corpus holds five messages in four years [kb/entities/annie-ulmer.md; dat:0160; dat:0164]. The KB is explicit that this is a hole in the record, not a quiet period in the relationship, and the warning is load-bearing: "a zero is data only when the system could have observed a one," and here the system could not [dat:0164]. Every claim about those years is constrained to non-message evidence. The 2024 Libby Titus employment — Annie's work as paid personal assistant and informal carer for songwriter Libby Titus from February to early August 2024, ending August 6, 2024 and confirmed three ways against her October 13, 2024 death — is the one tenure event in the hole the KB dates from outside the message record [kb/entities/annie-ulmer.md; dat:0116]. What the hole means for the decade's interpretation is stated in the Conflicts section below; here it is enough to mark that the tenure's middle is unobserved, and the synthesis does not fill it.

Per-year direction counts are the tenure's one continuous measurement. Annie out-messaged Dan in every held year except 2015 and 2025–2026, with the 2016 hinge depending on whether the parallel AOL channel counts as the same conversation; merged, the prior wiki's claim is exactly correct [kb/entities/annie-ulmer.md; dat:0115]. The direction reverses in the terminal phase, which is where volume becomes a finding rather than a background statistic.

## The corpus as instrument

The decade is the corpus's largest single relationship record, and the KB is disciplined about what that does and does not establish. The count 97,768 unique messages, 2015-11-28 to 2026-06-05, is the operator's corrected figure, validated to within 0.2% against the held corpus; the prior wiki's 126,683 is roughly 29% overstated and may not be quoted [kb/entities/annie-ulmer.md; dat:0113]. The Annie Read — a hand-read chronology producing two outputs per window, a timeline and working notes — had processed 13.9% of the corpus through 2016-01-24 as of the August 18, 2026 synthesis pass, with roughly 370 events recorded [dat:0160; dat:0164]. Those progress figures matter because they bound how much of the decade any synthesis has actually read. The distribution claims — the 129 episodes, the 36-second median — are machine counts over the merged corpus; the narrative claims are a partial read over less than a fifth of it. This page keeps that distinction visible: the exit idiom is measured, the tenure narrative is read, and the two have different confidence classes.

The same discipline applies to two claims the record cannot carry, which the entity page flags and this synthesis inherits. "I'm in love with Annie Ulmer," often quoted, is in the held corpus Annie's coaching script for Dan to deliver to a third party, dated 2015-12-02, not Dan's declaration [kb/entities/annie-ulmer.md; dat:0541]. Dan's account that Annie is a rape survivor whose assailant received forty-five years exists exactly twice in the held corpus, both times in Dan's own words, with no Annie-side, legal, or third-party corroboration of the underlying fact; it is evidence about Dan, not about Annie's history [kb/entities/annie-ulmer.md; dat:0158]. Neither claim is used as a premise in this synthesis.

## The terminal phase: spike, closure, and the failure of the closure

The terminal phase begins where the record resumes. December 2025 holds the relationship's volume peak: 4,657 messages in the page's count, replicating within one percent in the held thread at 4,620, with Dan at 2,376 to Annie's 2,244 [kb/events/2025-12-message-spike.md; dat:0591]. The ratios that follow are the finding. In January 2026 Dan sends 1,705 to Annie's 1,313 (1.30:1); in February, 1,542 to 794 (1.94:1). Her engagement halves while his holds roughly constant. The spike is not a reconciliation; it is a near-parity peak followed immediately by one-sided collapse, visible in the held rows themselves rather than in dossier arithmetic.

June 1, 2026 is the first exit that crossed the wall. The group-chat closure is an unbroken Dan outbound run ending 00:27:49 EDT — "Goodbye forever. This was not how it should have ended but. sic semper lupanis." — with 666 messages across May 31 to June 1 (601 Dan, 65 Annie) [kb/events/2026-06-01-annie-closure.md; dat:1343]. The sign-off is Dan's, misattributed to Annie on the prior wiki page until August 13, 2026; her only recorded contribution afterward is the June 5 apology [kb/events/2026-06-01-annie-closure.md]. The closure then failed at fifty-two days. That figure — June 1 to July 23 — is "by a wide margin the most durable severance in a relationship with 127 declared exits," in the page's pre-resolution numbers [kb/events/2026-06-01-annie-closure.md]; against the resolved distribution (129 episodes, maximum forty-six hours), fifty-two days is not a longer tail. It is a different regime, the only episode that ever crossed forty-six hours until the August sequence began. The closure's full sequence is on [[wiki/timeline/events/group-chat-closure]]; this page records the arithmetic and the attribution correction because both bear on what the decade's unwinding is an unwinding of.

The May-June Coles entanglement is the closure's third-party context. In the May 31, 2026 group chat Annie left Dan for Jerel Wayne Coles — "Tuquick" — with the announcement existing as page-attributed testimony and no held export of that chat [kb/events/2026-05-jerel-coles-entanglement.md]. June 1's chat is three-party in the held corpus, including Coles's handle. The corpus carried Coles as two people — "Tuquick" and "the unnamed man" — until the August 9, 2026 correction merged them [kb/events/2026-05-jerel-coles-entanglement.md]. The identity work is on [[wiki/people/kristin-prentiss|the people pages]]; this synthesis notes only that the terminal phase's third party was, for two months, two handles in the record and one person outside it.

August 16 to 19, 2026 is the terminal rupture: 1,199 messages across four days (811 Dan, 388 inbound), opening with a fifteen-minute-twenty-seven-second call Dan recorded without placing it, in which Coles confesses to searching Annie's phone while she slept, holds the phone for the duration, and interrogates her on tape while Annie asks for her phone five times and says she wants to leave from 04:16 [kb/events/2026-08-16-annie-terminal-end.md; dat:0257]. The event page carries the campaign that followed and the corrections printed on it. This synthesis records the window's dates, the call's existence and its validation against an independent transcription pass, and the fact that the recording was aimed at Annie's mother Ellen as leverage from inception at 13:30 while the call was still running [kb/events/2026-08-16-annie-terminal-end.md]. The detailed reading is at [[wiki/timeline/events/august-2026-morgantown-call]].

The August grievance verdict, dated August 20, 2026, adjudicates ten stated complaints against dated evidence: seven fully supported, two partly supported, one false [kb/events/2026-08-19-august-grievance-verdict.md; dat:0975]. The false one is the verdict's most useful output. Grievance six — "You won't even say what you want" — scores false: the held corpus shows Annie stating a want at 2026-08-17 20:12 EDT, "I want to be alone. I want to get help," repeated August 19 at 15:00 and 15:06. A binary "him or me" demand could not register a third-option answer as an answer. The verdict's structural finding is capacity: documented coercive control by a third party plus a disclosed suicide attempt relocate culpability without reducing the wrongs [kb/events/2026-08-19-august-grievance-verdict.md]. This page cites the verdict's method — ten complaints, dated evidence, one false — because the method is the decade's own discipline applied to its ending.

## The designed severance and its base rate

The 2026 severance is the only breakup in the record designed before it was executed, and [[wiki/mind/synthesis/severance-2026]] carries that design. The KB synthesis states the experiment plainly: on a base rate of 129 episodes at 100% resumption with a forty-six-hour maximum, Dan set a 120-day horizon — December 19, 2026 — and the check is the thing a future pass can actually run [kb/syntheses/severance-2026.md; kb/syntheses/annie-decade.md]. The 120-day figure is roughly sixty-two times the longest observed resumption gap. A test without a base rate is a wish; this one has a base rate, a dated falsifier, and named threats to validity: the Milo channel, through which every prior recontact ran and which Dan pre-emptively closed August 19, 2026 at 14:53:25, and handle attribution during Coles-present episodes, where at least six inbound messages from Annie's handle were typed by Coles [kb/syntheses/severance-2026.md].

This synthesis does not adjudicate the experiment's outcome. As of the KB record dated September 9, 2026 the block is reported held [kb/syntheses/annie-decade.md; kb/syntheses/severance-2026.md], and the entity pages carry a later retraction that the Conflicts section below records without reconciling. What this page adds to the severance entry is only the decade framing: the experiment exists in the space between the attachment model's number and the calendar. Neither node contains it alone, and that is why the decade needs a synthesis distinct from its event pages.

## Conflicts in the record

**Stale exit counts.** The closure page and the synthesis pages carried 127 episodes and 110 re-engagements inside forty-eight hours. The August 23, 2026 recount — Dan-sent severance language collapsed to episodes within twenty-four hours over the 95,067-row merged corpus (2015-11-28 to 2026-05-28) — returns 129 episodes, 128 with a following message, all 128 resumed [kb/events/2026-08-23-attachment-model-resolved.md; dat:0622]. The dossiers' 127/110 pair is withdrawn. The block-unblock-loop page still carries the stale figures unreconciled alongside its own final recount. Neither figure may be quoted without the other [dat:1292]. This page uses the resolved figures throughout.

**June 1 sign-off attribution.** The "sic semper lupanis" exit line was carried as Annie's on the main Annie page until August 13, 2026. The held corpus confirms it row by row as Dan's unbroken outbound run, rows 223934 to 223939, ending 00:27:49 EDT [kb/events/2026-06-01-annie-closure.md; dat:1343]. The end-fight page still carries the stale attribution — a propagation failure [dat:0125]. Any reading of the closure's agency that treats the line as hers reasons from a superseded attribution. This page attributes it to Dan.

**Fifty-two-day arithmetic.** The closure is described as failing within fifty-two days, June 1 to July 23. The fireworks email that ended the severance is dated July 4 — thirty-three days after June 1, not fifty-two. One of the two figures is off, and the corpus's own resolution stands unresolved [kb/syntheses/annie-decade.md; kb/entities/annie-ulmer.md]. The "most durable severance" claim — the bar the 120-day test is trying to beat — rests partly on arithmetic the corpus has not settled. If the true figure is thirty-three days, the bar is lower than stated. This page carries fifty-two days as the page's figure and July 4 as the dated artifact, and does not choose between them.

**The "first held boundary."** On August 26, 2026 the operator-observed addendum recorded Dan keeping Annie blocked, "the block is still being honored," flagged as the first held boundary in the record [dat:0250]. Within forty-eight hours the page's own falsifier fired: August 27 to 31 resumption at 16, 39, 108, 84, and 65 messages per day, two-sided, in the crisis register [dat:0112]. As of the September 9, 2026 record the block is again reported held — the sequence is honored, violated, re-held [kb/syntheses/severance-2026.md]. On September 11, 2026 the entity record retracts further: Dan confirmed verbatim "there was never a block," with roughly twenty-five block and severance declarations August 15 to 19, none executed, and daily two-way texting continuing August 27 to September 7 [kb/entities/annie-ulmer.md; dat:0090-block-retraction-2026-09-11]. The KB syntheses dated September 9 do not contain this retraction. The people page dated September 21 records near-daily contact through that date. This page carries both the syntheses' held-block report and the entity's retraction, and does not reconcile them. The 120-day horizon test named in the syntheses measures the re-held state; the retraction voids the premise that a severance existed to measure. Which description governs the December 19 check is an open item the record has not settled.

**Handle attribution.** At least six inbound messages from Annie's handle were typed by Coles [kb/events/2026-08-16-annie-terminal-end.md]. During Coles-present episodes the speaker is not the handle. No contact claim in the terminal phase scores without sender verification. This caveat applies to the August 27 to 31 resumption counts and to any future "contact" that would fail the December 19 test.

**The 2020-2024 hole.** The synthesis's open question — what happened November 2020 to December 2024, the largest single unobserved span in the decade — is not a conflict between sources. It is a structural absence that constrains every causal claim about the terminal rupture. If the hole ever fills with evidence that the relationship ended years before the corpus resumes, the rupture's causes move from August 2026 into an unobservable window [kb/syntheses/annie-decade.md]. This page treats the hole as a limit on the decade, not a period in it.

**The 126,683 figure.** The prior wiki's corpus-size figure is roughly 29% overstated against the held corpus and may not be quoted [kb/entities/annie-ulmer.md; dat:0113]. The corrected 97,768 is used here. The prior figure survives in older pages; any count reasoning from it inherits the overstatement.

## Assessment

At decade scale, the Annie relationship is the record's best-measured attachment and its least completely read one. The exit distribution — 129 episodes, 100% resumption, thirty-six-second median — is derived from the full merged corpus by a stated method and survives an independent recount by a different method. That makes it the strongest quantitative claim the attachment cluster holds. The tenure narrative underneath it is a partial hand read over less than a fifth of the corpus, with a four-year hole in the middle that no synthesis can fill from messages. The terminal phase is unusually well dated for a collapse: a volume peak that replicates within one percent, a closure whose sign-off attribution is confirmed row by row, a rupture whose central call is validated across two transcription passes, and a grievance verdict whose method — ten complaints, dated evidence — produces one false finding that is more informative than the seven supported ones.

The decade's unwinding, as the record stands in September 2026, is not a single event. The June 1 closure failed at fifty-two (or thirty-three) days; the August rupture produced a designed 120-day test whose premise the September 11 retraction voids; the September 21 people page records contact continuing. A synthesis that chose one of those as "the ending" would be selecting among records that disagree. This page leaves the unwinding as the record leaves it: measured at the base rate, dated at the events, and open at the boundary the record has not yet settled.
