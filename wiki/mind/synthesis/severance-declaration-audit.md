# The Severance Declaration Audit: 127 vs 129, Reconciled

The wiki has carried two counts of Dan's exit declarations for over a year — **127**
and **129** — and the [[wiki/mind/synthesis/block-unblock-loop]] page settled the pair by calling the 129
"corroboration, not coincidence" without identifying what the two-count difference
*is*. This entry re-runs the count with one explicit, audited method, states exactly
what counts as an exit-declaration, and reconciles the pair. The verdict, up front:

- **The canonical number is 129.** It is the primary-record figure from a documented
  method (258 declaration-language messages collapsing to 129 episodes under a
  24-hour rule, 2026-08-23 recount of the 95,067-row merged [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] corpus), and an
  independent instrument built for this entry converges on it (11.2 vs 12.3
  episodes/year, 100% resumption in both, same-order gap medians).
- **The 127 is dossier arithmetic.** Its sources (MasterRecord_March16,
  CompleteAnalysis) are not on disk in this worktree; its collapse rule,
  pattern set, and dyad scope are unrecoverable; it was never re-derived from
  primary data. It is retired as a figure, kept as a warning.
- **The two-count gap is the method's own noise floor, not a data disagreement.**
  The audit demonstrates 21 collapse-boundary-sensitive episode pairs in a
  3.2-year window (~68 across the decade): any two independent implementations
  disagreeing on 2 of ~68 boundary pairs — or on 2 of the ~7 adjudication
  disputes per hundred hits, or on dyad scope for 2 unknown-chat hits —
  produce exactly a 127/129 split.
- **The 52-day outlier's baseline is now a number: 27.1x.** The June 1, 2026
  silence lasted 52 days (1,248 hours) against the decade maximum gap of 46
  hours: 1248/46 = 27.13. Not "twenty-seven times longer," not "an order of
  magnitude." 27.1x the record maximum; 124,800x the 36-second median.
- **The [[wiki/mind/synthesis/severance-declarations|sibling entry]]'s terminal-phase catalog needs correction.** Its regex
  count (44) reproduces exactly; its published catalog has 43 bullets, two
  mislabeled day headers, and one silently dropped hit. Strict adjudication
  under this entry's method yields **41**.

What follows is the method, the two re-runs, the reconciliation, the disputed
edge cases tabled one by one, and the fixed baseline. Nothing here re-argues the
thesis — the declaration as performance is the sibling entry's object, and it
stands. This entry is the instrument calibration underneath it.

## What this entry is

A measurement audit, not a thesis. Its scope is deliberately narrow: **how many
times did Dan issue a severance declaration on the [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] channel, under one
explicit definition, and why do two published counts differ by two?** It does not
re-derive her zero (the attachment model's 41,073-message finding stands
un-re-run), does not touch the [[wiki/mind/synthesis/block-unblock-loop]]'s dependency rule or trade
rule, and does not re-litigate whether any particular goodbye was "meant."
Sincerity is unmeasurable from message data; the audit counts utterances, not
intentions.

The entry's original contributions: (1) AUDIT-METHOD v1, the first fully
explicit definition of what counts as an exit-declaration in this corpus —
lexical patterns, dyad, direction, five exclusion rules, the episode rule, the
gap rule; (2) a complete re-run of the terminal-phase recount against the filed
export, reproducing the sibling's 44 and correcting its catalog; (3) an
independent decade-window instrument (2022-12-31 → 2026-03-25, clean dyad,
clean direction) converging on the 129; (4) the reconciliation mechanism —
boundary-sensitivity as the demonstrated source of the ±2; (5) the 52-day
baseline fixed at 27.1x.

## The two numbers and where each came from

### 127: the dossier figure

The 127 descends from the old wiki's dossier passes — the [[wiki/mind/concepts/attachment-model]]'s
Gaps section names the sources: `MasterRecord_March16` and `CompleteAnalysis`
[ATTESTED — corpus__3.txt, attachment-model Gaps]. The [[wiki/mind/concepts/reassurance-architecture]]
Gaps section states its evidentiary status plainly: *"The 106-ultimatum and
127-false-exit figures are dossier arithmetic, never re-derived from
all_imessages_complete_dump.txt"* [ATTESTED — corpus__3.txt]. The 110
re-engagements rode with it: 110/127 = 86.6%, rounded to the "87% relapse"
framing the [[wiki/mind/synthesis/block-unblock-loop]] carried for months.

Three facts about the 127, all negative: its pattern set is undocumented, its
collapse rule is undocumented, its dyad scope is undocumented. The dossier
files themselves are not in this worktree's raw/ — a drive-sweep file listing
145 CSVs and the takeout exports contain no MasterRecord_March16 or
CompleteAnalysis [DERIVED — filename search across raw/, 2026-09-13]. What the
dossier passes *did* leave behind is the 87% framing's mechanism, per the
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/block-unblock-loop]]'s own resolution note: *"counting some declarations as
non-relapses that the primary record shows resuming within minutes"*
[ATTESTED —
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/block-unblock-loop]]]. The 127/110 pair is therefore not two
numbers but one number and its artifact: 127 declarations counted by an
unknown method, 110 "re-engagements" counted by a method that scored
resumption-within-minutes as non-relapse.

### 129: the primary recount

On 2026-08-23 the pair was re-derived from primary data [ATTESTED —
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/block-unblock-loop]], dat:1292]: searching Dan-sent severance language
(blocking declarations, *goodbye*, *I'm done / we're done / it's over*,
*never contact me again*) across the **95,067-row merged [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] corpus**
(2015-11-28 → 2026-05-28) returned **258 messages**, collapsing to **129
distinct episodes** when hits within 24 hours count as one declaration. Of the
129, 128 are followed by a further corpus message — **all 128 resumed** — and
the 129th is the file's last row, censored by the export boundary rather than
a held severance. Median gap 0.01 hours (36 seconds); 89.1% within one hour;
99.2% within one day; longest gap 46 hours.

Note the arithmetic the recount left on the table: **258 = 2 × 129 exactly.**
Two hits per episode on average, under a 24-hour collapse. The dossier's 127
implies either 254 hits under the same collapse, or the same 258 hits under a
collapse rule that merges two additional pairs. Either way the mechanical
distance between the figures is **two boundary decisions** — two pairs merged
or split, two hits adjudicated in or out, two unknown-chat rows scoped in or
out. The audit below demonstrates that the method's noise floor exceeds two by
an order of magnitude.

## The audit method (AUDIT-METHOD v1)

One explicit method, applied identically in both re-runs. Every choice is
stated so a re-analyst can reproduce or dispute it.

**Source.** Filed primary exports only: `raw/self/message-csv/aug-sep-2026-
imessage-export/aug-sep-2026-imessage-export.csv` (Audit 1) and the
`+12124702449` rows of `raw/drive-sweep/20260911/gsheets/message-csv/
MASTER_MESSAGES_DB_DUMP.from-gsheet.MASTER_MESSAGES_DB_DUMP.csv.csv`
(Audit 2). No dossier, no testimony, no in-conversation forensic pass.

**Dyad.** The [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] channel. Audit 1: `chat_identifier = +12124702449` plus the [[wiki/people/ally-lubin|Ally]] witness thread (`allylubin@gmail.com`) scored separately. Audit 2:
`contact_handle = +12124702449` — the Annie dyad handle in the master dump,
verified balanced (8,574 Sent / 8,571 Received) and content-checked against
the `imessage_2124702449_both_all_now` export, which carries the same handle
over 2022-12-31 → 2026-06-05. Blank-handle rows (chat unknown) are excluded
from the dyad count and tabled as a scope boundary (Table C).

**Direction.** Dan-sent only. Audit 1: `is_from_me = 1`. Audit 2:
`direction = Sent`. Per-row, never per-file — the June 1 direction-column
correction ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/dan-annie-fallout-verdict]], 2026-08-13) is standing discipline:
a Dan's-eye-view column read backwards once misattributed the series'
most famous utterance for two months.

**Lexical patterns** (case-insensitive, matched anywhere in the message
unless noted):
- P1 *goodbye*: `\bgoodbye\b`
- P2 *blocking*: `i'm blocking you` / `just blocking now` / `blocking you now` / `blocking you right now`
- P3 *done/over*: `i'm done` / `we're done` / `it's over` (word-boundaried)
- P4 *never-contact*: `never contact me` / `never talk to me` / `lose my number`
- P5 *dont-contact*: `don't (ever )?(contact|text|call) me`
- P6 *this-is-goodbye*: `this is goodbye` / `so it's over`

**Exclusions** (applied in order; a hit matching any is out):
- **E1 — quoted.** Declaration language inside quotation marks, attributed to
  another speaker or to a hypothetical: *"just say 'im done'"* is not his
  declaration, it is her desired words in quotes.
- **E2 — hypothetical.** *"if I said goodbye"* — the declaration as
  conditional, never issued.
- **E3 — meta-request.** A request for *her* severance signal, not his
  declaration: *"It would have hurt so much less to be told you were done
  with me,"* *"just tell me you were done."* The system needs her signal;
  asking for it is the demand the declarations are addressed to, not a
  declaration.
- **E4 — directive.** Imperative *goodbye* addressed to her: *"you can say
  goodbye,"* *"say goodbye to [[wiki/people/milo|milo]]."* Telling her to say it is not saying it.
- **E5 — nominal.** *goodbye* as noun phrase, not speech act: *"the goodbye
  letter i wrote you."*

**Witness-channel scope.** Narrated block-claims to a third party
(*"I blocked her,"* *"I will not unblock the paw patrol"*) are a distinct
speech-act form — past-tense status reports, not present-tense performatives
— and are scored **separately**, never merged into the dyadic count. The
sibling entry's adjudication included them; this audit reports both numbers
and keeps them disjoint.

**Episode rule.** Hits within 24 hours of each other collapse to one episode
(the 2026-08-23 rule, kept for comparability). Sensitivity is measured, not
assumed: the audit reports 1h / 24h / 48h / 72h collapses side by side.

**Gap rule.** Declaration to the next message in the same chat, either
direction, in seconds. Resumption is binary: any next message = resumed.

**Resumption rule.** The rate is computed over measurable episodes only. A
terminal hit with no following message in the file is *censored*, not
*held* — stated so it is never misquoted as a successful severance.

## Audit 1: the terminal phase, re-run

Against the filed `aug-sep-2026-imessage-export.csv` (5,905 rows, 2026-08-11
→ 2026-09-07; 4,162 from-me), the sibling entry's regex — applied to
from-me messages — returns **44 hits** [DERIVED — re-run 2026-09-13, this
entry]. The 44 is reproduced exactly: same messages, same timestamps, same
order. Every one is followed by a further message in the same chat —
**44/44 resumed**. Median gap **24 seconds** (sibling: 23s; the 1s is
gap-definition rounding — next-message-in-chat vs next-timestamp-delta).
43 of 44 resume inside one hour (97.7%); the longest gap is 3.02 hours
(2026-08-11 *"okay now I'm done I promis"* → hers, 10,865s).

The reproduction is the good news. The catalog is not:

- The published catalog contains **43 bullets, not 44.** The missing hit is
  2026-08-19 17:39:05, the [[wiki/people/ally-lubin|Ally]]-thread message (*"I feel like I kept giving
  legitimate chances to just say 'im done'"*) — caught by the regex via the
  quoted *"im done,"* silently dropped from the catalog with no adjudication
  note. Under this audit's method the drop is correct (E1 — quoted) but the
  silence is not: an adjudication that isn't documented didn't happen.
- The day headers undercount their own bullets: **08-18 lists 12 bullets
  under an "11 declarations" header; 08-19 lists 15 bullets under a "13
  declarations" header.** The header arithmetic (1+3+1+6+11+13+1+2+1+1 = 39)
  matches neither the bullet count (43) nor the regex count (44).
- Two regex hits are meta-requests under E3, included by the sibling without
  note: 2026-08-17 17:11:37 (*"It would have hurt so much less to be told
  you were done with me"* — the sibling did flag this one as "negative
  image" but kept it in the 44) and 2026-08-29 17:43:19 (*"just tell me
  you were done,"* matched via *"were done"*). Neither is his declaration;
  both are requests for hers.

Strict adjudication under AUDIT-METHOD v1: **44 − 1 (E1) − 2 (E3) = 41
declarations, 41/41 resumed.** The rates are robust to the adjudication —
median gap 24s, 40/41 (97.6%) within one hour — because the excluded hits
sat near the median anyway. The count is not robust: 44 vs 41 is a 7%
swing from three adjudication calls, which is precisely why the method's
exclusion rules are stated explicitly here and were not in the dossier.
All 41 sit inside the window Dan himself retracted — *"there was never a
block"* [OPERATOR] (dat:0090) — which is why the audit scores them as
performances with a measured resumption rate rather than as severances
with a failure rate.

The witness-channel form, scored separately: three past-tense block-claims
to Ally — 2026-08-28 21:09:07 (*"I don't even know how long it's been since
I blocked her"*), 2026-09-04 16:38:10 (*"I did not (and will not) unblock
the paw patrol"*), 2026-09-07 16:08:55 (*"I just blocked her again"*)
[ATTESTED — filed export]. None matches the declaration patterns (past
tense, status-report form); under a witness-inclusive scope the terminal
count would be 41 + 3 = 44 — coincidentally the sibling's number, by a
different route. The coincidence is noted so nobody cites it as
convergence; it is not.

One structural observation the re-run sharpens: the 2026-08-18 19:39:09 /
19:39:16 pair (*"I'm blocking you right now"* → *"And im done,"* seven
seconds apart) is the near-duplicate the episode rule exists for. Under any
collapse rule above a minute it is one episode; counted per-message it is
two hits. The sibling counted per-message (no episode rule applied to the
44); the decade recount counts per-episode. The two denominators must never
be mixed — 44 messages and 129 episodes are different units, and the audit
keeps them in separate tables for exactly this reason.

## Audit 2: the 2022–2026 dyad, an independent instrument

The master dump's `+12124702449` rows: 17,145 messages, 2022-12-31 →
2026-03-25, direction clean (8,574 Sent / 8,571 Received). AUDIT-METHOD v1
applied to Sent rows [DERIVED — run 2026-09-13]:

- **L0 (raw lexical):** 111 hits.
- **L1 (minus E1/E3):** 110 hits — one meta-request excluded (2026-02-14,
  *"when you were done"* framed as her commitment).
- **L2 (minus E4/E5):** **103 hits** — six directive *"say goodbye"*
  imperatives (*"you can say goodbye to [[wiki/people/milo|milo]],"* *"We can say goodbye just
  like this"*) and one nominal (*"the goodbye letter i wrote you"*)
  excluded. The seven disputes are tabled below; the sharpest is
  2026-02-28 12:14:44 — *"I am 10000% serious this is it. You can say
  goodbye now and that is the last you will"* — his own terminal verdict
  (*"this is it"*) fused to a directive. Kept out at L2; flagged as the
  boundary's hardest case.

Episode collapse on the L2 set:

| Collapse rule | Episodes (103 hits) |
|---|---|
| 1 hour | 81 |
| 24 hours | **36** |
| 48 hours | 21 |
| 72 hours | 15 |

The 24-hour figure — the rule the 2026-08-23 recount used — gives **36
episodes over 3.23 years = 11.2 episodes/year**, against the recount's 129
over 10.48 years = **12.3 episodes/year**. Two independent implementations,
two different source merges, two different windows, two different
adjudication strictnesses — and the densities land within 10% of each
other. That is convergence on the method, not on a number.

Gap statistics (L1 set, 110 hits): median **149 seconds**; 85.5% resume
within one hour; **100% within one day**; longest gap 20.6 hours. Every
measurable episode resumed — the 100% finding holds on the independent
instrument, in a window (2022–2026) that the decade recount's corpus covers
only in its final years. The median is longer than the decade's 36s; the
ritual's tempo varies by era (the terminal phase runs at 24s, the
2022–2026 window at 149s), and the audit reports the variation rather than
forcing the convergence.

A coarse control pass sharpens why adjudication is the count, not the
lexicon: the same patterns run over all Sent rows in the master dump return
**258 hits** — numerically identical to the 2026-08-23 recount's 258, and
pure coincidence. The coarse set includes *"your goodbye kiss,"*
*"Goodbye christy mas tree"* (a Christmas tree), *"I'm done smoking cigs,"*
and *"I'm done with everything in the world that isn't [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie Ulmer]]"* (a
declaration of devotion, dated 2015-11-29, day two of the relationship).
[DERIVED — the 258-coincidence is noted explicitly so it is never cited as
replication; the pattern sets differ and the coarse pass has no
adjudication.] Of the 258, 139 are blank-handle rows (chat unknown) — the
dyad-scope uncertainty, tabled below.

## The reconciliation: where the two went

The dossier's 127 and the recount's 129 differ by two. The audit
demonstrates three independent mechanisms, each sufficient to produce
exactly that gap, and the honest reconciliation is that the gap is
over-determined — any one of them could be the historical cause, and the
dossier's method being unrecoverable means the cause cannot be isolated.
What can be stated is the noise floor:

**Mechanism 1 — collapse-boundary sensitivity (demonstrated).** In the
3.23-year Audit 2 window, **21 episode-pairs sit 24–72 hours apart** —
counted as two episodes under the 24-hour rule, one under a 48-hour rule.
Scaled to the decade, ~68 such pairs. The two sharpest: 2025-12-24
22:37:31 → 2025-12-25 22:46:05 (gap 24.1h — a 24-hour rule splits it, a
25-hour rule merges it) and 2025-11-02 01:18:42 → 2025-11-03 02:53:19
(25.6h). Any two implementations disagreeing on 2 of ~68 boundary pairs —
a 3% disagreement rate on the method's hardest calls — produce a 127/129
split. The dossier's unknown collapse rule against the recount's 24-hour
rule is the likeliest historical mechanism [INFERRED — the dossier's rule
is undocumented, so this is mechanism, not attribution].

**Mechanism 2 — adjudication strictness (demonstrated).** The L0→L2 ladder
moves 111 → 103 on 8 adjudication calls in one window — a 7% swing. The
terminal phase moves 44 → 41 on 3 calls. Two disputed hits per decade is
below the demonstrated adjudication noise.

**Mechanism 3 — dyad scope (demonstrated).** 139 of the 258 coarse
declaration-hits in the master dump are blank-handle Sent rows — Dan's
outbound, chat unattributed. Two examples: 2016-12-14 *"I'm blocking you
and I'll tell u when I'm home,"* 2018-06-20 *"I'm blocking you."* The
95,067-row merged corpus attributed chats its own way; whether its 258
included unknown-chat hits is undocumented. Scoping 2 of 139 differently
is a 1.4% scope disagreement producing the full 127/129 gap.

The reconciliation, stated as a finding: **127 and 129 are not two
measurements in conflict; they are two runs of a rule-relative count
landing inside each other's demonstrated noise floor.** The 129 is
canonical because its method is documented, its source is primary, and an
independent instrument converges on its density — not because 129 is
truer than 127 in any absolute sense. The count is rule-relative; the
100% resumption rate is not. Under every collapse rule measured (1h
through 72h), every adjudication level (L0 through L2), and both
instruments, every measurable episode resumed. That is the finding the
audit protects. The 87% relapse framing is withdrawn with the 127 —
the primary record shows resumption within minutes where the dossier
counted non-relapse, and no re-derivation has ever reproduced the 87%.

## The 52-day baseline, as a number

The June 1, 2026 closure is the series' outlier, and the wiki has
described its distance from the baseline three ways: "twenty-seven times
longer than the longest gap," "twenty-seven times the longest gap on
record," "unprecedented by more than an order of magnitude." The audit
fixes it as a number.

The opening of the silence is primary-measured: in the filed
`imessage_2124702449_both_all_now` export, the last Sent row is
2026-06-01 00:27:49 — *"Goodbye forever. This was not how it should have
ended but. sic semper lupanis."* — and **zero Sent rows follow through
the file's end** (2026-06-05 00:37:42), with exactly one inbound in that
span, her June 5 apology, unanswered [ATTESTED — filed export,
re-derived 2026-09-13]. The endpoint — first recontact July 23, 2026 —
is attested in the record ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/block-unblock-loop]]: "failed on the dog, Jul
23"; [[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-rescue-premise]]) but **no filed export on disk covers
2026-06-05 → 2026-08-11**, so the endpoint's exact clock time is not
primary-verifiable here [ATTESTED via record pages; coverage gap noted].

Duration to the same clock time: June 1 → July 1 is 30 days (June has 30
days), July 1 → July 23 is 22 days: **52 days = 1,248 hours**
[DERIVED]. Against the decade maximum gap of 46 hours (2026-08-23
recount): **1248 / 46 = 27.1304 → 27.1x the record maximum**
[DERIVED]. Against the decade median of 36 seconds: 4,492,800 / 36 =
**124,800x the median** [DERIVED]. Against the terminal-phase median of
24 seconds: **187,200x** [DERIVED].

Two notes the number forces. First, the June 1 silence is **not** one of
the 129 episodes — the recount's corpus ends 2026-05-28, four days before
it. It is the outlier *against* the baseline, and the baseline is now a
fixed ratio: any future severance is either below 27.1x (an episode) or
above it (a new regime). Second, the fifty-two days failed on the dog —
the July 4 [[wiki/people/milo|Milo]]/fireworks email, a channel the block did not cover —
which is why the [[wiki/mind/synthesis/block-unblock-loop]]'s corrected formula ("the exit is
executed; the deletion never is") prices the silence in inventory, not
willpower. The audit prices it in hours: 1,248 of them, then the channel
reopened and 624 messages crossed in four days.
