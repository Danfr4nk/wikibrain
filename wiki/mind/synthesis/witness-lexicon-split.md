# Witness-Lexicon Split

> **Incidental entry** — One witness channel is measured in full and no second channel has been mined; the entry is complete as a single-case finding with its test stated.

When Dan describes [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] to [[wiki/people/ally-lubin|Ally]] in August–September 2026, he uses words that appear nowhere in the eleven-year thread with Annie herself. When he describes Annie to Annie in the same weeks, he uses the opposite register. [[wiki/mind/synthesis/witness-channel-declarations|Witness-Channel Declarations]] documents the declarations themselves — six block claims in eleven days, all to the witness, none enacted. This entry isolates the smaller fact underneath them: the witness channel holds its own vocabulary for the severed party, and the direct channel never imports it. The question the entry leaves scored, not answered, is whether every witness channel in the corpus does this, or only the one that has been read closely.

The measurement stays at the level of word counts and timestamps. It describes which strings appear in which channel at which dates; it does not grade what anyone meant by them.

## The one measured split

The witness-channel forms are two nicknames, both Dan-origin, both confined to allylubin@gmail.com: "paw patrol" (2026-09-04, "I did not (and will not) unblock the paw patrol"; 2026-09-07, "So I unblocked paw patrol to see if I would get anything from her") and "trashfire" (2026-09-04, "I am done with that trashfire and her lunatic new boyf"). The direct channel's contemporaneous forms run the other way: "good night pretty girl" (2026-09-07 01:44, the export's last ritual instance), "My favorite girl" in the same terminal weeks, and the nightly sign-off shape that persists through the declaration-dense nights of August 15–19 (dat:0090). The 111,864-message Annie thread contains no instance of either witness nickname; the Ally channel contains no instance of the direct channel's endearments addressed about Annie. The split is clean in both directions across the August 11–September 7 export window (5,905 rows).

The reverse naming shows the same channel-keeping in miniature. Dan names Ally to Annie 8 times in 111,864 texts, all Dan-origin, Annie-origin zero ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/annie-thread-ally-sweep|the sweep]]); the first (2019-01-01) is minimized and deferred ("this chick named ally that I know from Florida"), and the 2026 cluster deploys the name inside grievances about other subjects. Dan names Annie to Ally 29-plus times across the two Ally handles, with 7 Ally-origin namings of Annie beginning in 2019 ("I like Annie," 2019-08-18). Each channel, in other words, has a settled way of referring to the third party, and the ways do not cross: minimized prospect in the direct channel, detailed character in the witness channel, endearment in the direct channel, nickname in the witness channel. The severance-language atlas's corpus-wide result — declaration vocabulary on 25 threads, the performance concentrated in two dyads — is the background; this entry's claim is narrower, that the witness channel's vocabulary for the severed party is an additional, channel-private layer on top of the shared grammar.

## A neighboring name-split

One neighboring case shows channel-private vocabulary for a shared object without the loyalty structure, and it is included to keep the claim honest about its boundaries. The dog [[wiki/people/milo|Milo]] is "Mimi" in Annie's register — 67 uses, 2018–2025, resolved to this dog on the Milo page — and "Milo" in Dan's, including the August 19, 2026 pre-closure that names the welfare-question route in his vocabulary ("when something happens to Milo"). The July 2026 re-engagement itself arrived in her register's form, the recurring welfare question ("Is Mimi ok," "How is Mimi"). Two names for one animal, each native to one side of the channel, with the reopening traveling under hers: a name-split can route around a closure stated in the other name. That is a routing fact about shared objects, not a loyalty lexicon for a severed party — nobody is performing allegiance by writing "Mimi" — and the distinction is why the dog case supports the plausibility of channel-private vocabularies without counting as a second instance of this entry's claim.

## What would count as a second case

The corpus holds other witness configurations — severance talk about Annie addressed to Tom, to Suz, inside the Dan–Coles–Annie group chat, and to the wiki itself as archive — and none has been mined for third-party vocabulary. A second case, scored strictly, would require: a witness channel with enough volume to hold a vocabulary (the atlas's thin-handle warning applies — below roughly 200 messages, absence is uninformative), at least one severed-party referring expression that recurs in the witness channel and never appears in the direct channel, and dates overlapping direct-channel traffic so the registers are contemporaneous rather than successive. The Ally case meets all three. Whether the Tom or Suz channels meet them is unknown, not negative: the mining pass has not been run, and this entry records the single measured split, the neighboring routing case, and the test, without generalizing the title's question into an answer.
