# The Frank Family System

The Frank family, as the record holds it, is organised around two parents whose observable contact patterns point in opposite directions. [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suzanne Frank]] is present at high volume and at close range: the second-largest correspondence in the corpus, a financial pattern the entity page calls "give-and-invoice" running through the thread, and, in early 2026, cohabitation with Dan at 463 Morgantown St. [[wiki/people/rick-frank|Richard Frank]] is present at low frequency and at a distance the record can date: repair episodes attested through 2020 and again in 2023–24, then a silence in 2025 that the held corpus shows Dan, not Rick, maintaining, and a single scheduling reply on 2026-09-01 that re-opens the channel without yet repairing it.

A third person made that two-person pattern a triangle while she lived. [[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Frances Coldren]] (1920-08-15 – 2018-04-04), Dan's maternal great-grandmother, raised him alongside Suz, is named by Dan as the biggest influence in his life, and was the person Dan cared for full-time in the last six months of hers. Her daughter, [[wiki/people/diane-moore|Diane Moore]], is the one member of the maternal line whose documented posture toward Dan is not distance but exclusion, set down in a letter in April 2018. Dan's younger sister, [[wiki/people/vanessa-frank|Vanessa Frank]], is the quietest tie in the system: steady, low-conflict and minimal in contact across the same years. [[wiki/people/lisa-frank|Lisa Frank]], Rick's partner since at least December 2015, is present in the record for a decade and written into it, in any detail, only in August 2026.

Money moves through this system in ways the record can partly measure, and the direction of the largest measured movement was corrected in 2026. In August–October 2018 Dan drew down roughly $20,000 against the expected estate and transferred about $14,000 of it to Suz, recovering $4,000 before she stopped repaying; by July 2019 the two were threatening each other with court over the same money, each claiming to be the creditor (kb/data/1293-estate-spine-direction-reversal-dan-to-suz.md). That correction matters here because a family reading built on money flowing the other way would describe a different system.

This entry stays at the level the instruments can see: who writes, how often, who answers, who does not, who lives where, who was in the room, and what money moved. It does not assign interior motives to any of those patterns, because the held corpus — messages, counts, timing and transfers — records attention and behaviour, not what any member of the family felt or needed. Where the older synthesis language reaches for a mechanism, this entry reports the observable pattern underneath it and leaves the mechanism named as a reading, not a fact.

## November 2005: the hinge the record cannot corroborate

In November 2005, when Dan was seventeen, Rick disclosed his cocaine addiction and entered rehabilitation, and Suz was revealed to have been having an affair with the family's own cocaine dealer during his treatment. The marriage ended. Dan names this event the hinge of his life (kb/events/2005-11-parental-rupture.md). Rick and Suz had married on 1985-08-31; the divorce dates to about 2005 on the Rick entity page.

The substance is common to both halves of the event as Dan tells it: the father's addiction and the mother's affair with the man who supplied it. The event file also records what complicates a clean origin story. Cocaine was a known household concept from 2001, when Dan was thirteen and discovered his parents' own use, and his own first use dates to the Seven Springs ski-crew context of 2004–2006 — overlapping the rupture rather than following it. The opiate arc that follows (Roxicet/Oxy onset in 2007–2008, five months to addiction on the event file's account) therefore does not begin with the divorce in any simple sequence the dates will support.

What the record does not hold is equally specific. No contemporaneous record of November 2005 exists; the held message corpus begins years later. Rick and Suz have no on-record account of the event anywhere in the corpus, and the dealer is not named in any held source. The month's consistency across retellings — the old wiki, the Developmental Origins dossier and Dan's own summaries — is consistency of a single witness's testimony over two decades, and the event file says so plainly. The hinge is the most load-bearing date in the family system and the least corroborated, and both halves of that sentence stay on the page.

## Fran: the third point, in observable terms

Fran Coldren, née Jesse Frances Thomas, born 1920-08-15 in Fort Martin, WV, raised Dan alongside Suz and is named by both Dan and Suz, on the synthesis's account, as a guiding force in their lives. The observable record of the last phase of that relationship is unusually concrete. For the last six months of her life Dan was her paid full-time caregiver at $15 an hour — his own contemporaneous summary, sent 2018-04-06, two days after her death: "she was a trip dude but i spent the past 6 months looking after her full time ($15 an hour to play video games with gram is a good deal) and we had a lot of fun. got good closure and finally did something in my life that wasn't completely selfish haha" (kb/events/2017-2018-fran-caregiving.md). The message is verified verbatim in the held corpus; the six-month duration and the wage are his words, which is why they survive as the correction against an older multi-year caregiving inference. [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie Ulmer]] was a co-caregiver in the canonical record; only Dan's wage is stated on record.

The sequence around the end is dated at message resolution. A handyman arrangement is visible on 2016-01-18 ("I went to grams and made a list of work to do for her tomorrow"). By 2017-11-26 the family-threat reading is already in Dan's messages: "Especially and most specifically not people directly connected to my grandparents who are looking for any excuse to throw her in a nursing home." Inheritance anticipation appears while Fran is still alive, on 2018-01-23: "after gram goes and we have a chance to get above water with the inheritance...i want". Surgery follows on 2018-03-09, with a nursing home expected on March 12. On 2018-04-01 Fran was moved to the Uniontown Hospital pediatric wing to die, with a 24-hour rotation mandated by Judge Fred Adams and held by Dan, Annie, Vicki and Marla (kb/events/2018-fran-death.md).

Fran died on 2018-04-04, with Dan and Annie in the room. The same event file holds the record's most-quoted pair: Dan's 2018-04-05 account, to a friend, of silently asking for a sign, Annie shaking him awake to report a blink, a smile, and a slow passing — and his retraction three hours later, after reviewing video showed a monitor had alerted them that her pulse was dropping: "def isn't as 'supernatural' knowing that fact. but doesn't take away from the cool moment we had with gram." Both halves are verified to the minute, and the entry that carries them keeps both. The first inheritance cheque is recorded on 2018-04-27 ("i got my inheritance check from grandma today"), and a memorial celebration followed on 2018-05-06 at the Uniontown Country Club.

Two objects tie this phase to the rest of the biography without any interpretive step. The Numark NS7 Fran gifted Dan is carried on the GRIPNOTIC alias page as one of his life anchors — a fact about an object and its provenance, linking [[wiki/work/gripnotic-revival|the music record]] to the caregiving record. And the money that begins moving after April 2018 connects Fran's death directly to the estate sequence below: anticipation in January, first cheque in April, drawdown by August.

## Diane: the documented hostile setting

Rebecca Diane Van Voorhis Moore, born 1939, is Fran's only child and Dan's maternal grandmother. The record of her posture toward Dan is not inferred from volume or latency; it is a document. An eviction notice terminating Dan's residency at 155 Virginia Ave and demanding the keys was served on 2018-03-29, mid-vigil, by "Dian V. Moore" — identified on 2026-08-18 as Diane herself. An exclusion letter followed, read, forwarded and lawyered on 2018-04-03, the day before Fran died, setting rules for Dan's presence in Fran's house and closing with "any violation of the rules will be considered trespassing" (kb/entities/diane-moore.md). Dan's read that night was "i think dave wrote it," naming Dave Moore, Diane's second husband, who had been speaking on her behalf to the hospital's social workers. The letter was addressed to Dan alone; Annie received no letter, a fact Dan noted contemporaneously.

The operative effect is also documented: the two people holding the house together were given no shifts in the mandated rotation, attended anyway off-roster, and were the two in the room at the end. After April 2018 the tail is nearly empty — one accidental contact on 2018-08-01 (Annie: "Wait wtf. Your mom ran into Dian and Dave today?!"), one hostility outburst on 2020-03-28, and no reconciliation on record. Two 2017 letters from Diane to Fran are now held as document photos: on 2017-08-25 she presses Fran to cooperate in selling the Palm City vacation house and to authorise a realtor; on 2017-11-01, with shoulder replacement scheduled for November 13, she requests a $20,000 loan against her inheritance for surgery and in-home nursing, copying Fred Adams. Those letters show the same formal, documented mode a year before the vigil; they do not explain it.

Why Diane's posture was hostile is not in the record. No held source states a motive, and this entry does not supply one. The exclusion is the datum; the cause is an open question the family pages carry as open.

## Suz: availability as a measurable pattern

Suzanne Frank, born 1962-09-15, is a licensed Pennsylvania realtor and, in the synthesis's phrase carried from the entity page, the record's constant pole. At attention level that means three observable things. First, volume: she is the second-largest correspondence in the corpus, with a headline figure of 33,698 messages carried as old-wiki testimony from a fuller source the held slice does not contain; the held slice itself holds her 2023-onward thread (3,500 rows, 2023-01-01 to 2026-09-07). Second, proximity: in early 2026 she and Dan cohabited at 463 Morgantown St, owned by a third party, with no signed lease, and housing still unsettled as of August 2026 — a fact carried in kb/syntheses/family-system.md and the entity summary it synthesises. Third, continuity through conflict: the thread that contains the 2018–2019 creditor dispute (below) is the same thread that remains, in 2026, the family's largest living channel.

The annual volume table — Dan ahead in 2015–2016, near-tie in 2017, Suz permanently ahead from 2018 — cannot be reproduced from the held slice, which contains no Suz-thread rows for 2015–2022. It is page-attributed testimony, neither verified nor falsifiable from held data, and this entry carries its shape only as testimony. A Chapter 13 filing in October 2024 is also entity-page testimony; the month rests on the operator's record, not on a held docket.

## Money between the poles

The estate sequence is the part of the family system with numbers attached, and the numbers were corrected. The older spine described deep-cycle money as running toward Dan, at a generalised rate of $750 a week. The correction (kb/data/1293-estate-spine-direction-reversal-dan-to-suz.md) records that the $750 figure was a single accusation from 2018-12-13 that a manual generalised into a rate, with zero held "$750" messages on that day, and that the largest single movement ran the other way: roughly $20,000 drawn down against the expected estate in August–October 2018, about $14,000 of it transferred to Suz, $4,000 recovered before repayment stopped, and mutual court threats by July 2019, each side claiming to be the creditor. The itemised statement the correction cites ("Dan to Suzanne / $1000 / $14,000+ / Sum of transactions on app," dated 2018-10-03 on the page) is itself unheld testimony — a search of the held corpus for "Dan to Suzanne" returns zero rows — so the direction correction rests on the wiki's re-read of its own pages, stated here at exactly that strength.

What that establishes, without any motive attached, is sequence: Fran dies in April 2018; the first cheque lands that month; by October a five-figure sum has moved from son to mother; by the following July the same sum is the subject of reciprocal legal threats. The relationship channel that carries all of this is also the channel that, seven years later, involves cohabitation. The record shows both movements in the same thread and does not reconcile them; neither does this entry.

In August 2026 the house itself became a target from outside the family: the synthesis records an exchange on 2026-08-17 in which Jerel Coles typed the Morgantown address unprompted and threatened Dan's mother (kb/syntheses/family-system.md). That is included here for one reason only: the family system is not background to the 2026 events but one of the things those events were aimed at, and the address at issue is the one Suz and Dan share.

## Rick: withdrawal, in dated messages

Richard Harrison Frank, born 1959, runs Frank's Auto Supermarket in Uniontown. Married Suz on 1985-08-31; divorced around 2005. With Lisa Frank since at least December 2015.

The observable pattern after the rupture is not continuous silence. The entity page records real repair episodes through 2020 and again in 2023–24, as page testimony from the fuller unheld dump. What the held corpus can date begins in February 2025: on 2025-02-25 Dan sent his last message to Rick's number — proposing a get-together, which Rick accepted on February 26 — and then did not write again for over eighteen months (kb/entities/rick-frank.md, kb/events/2026-09-01-rick-silence-breaks.md).

Rick's side of that interval is preserved nearly verbatim: a thirteen-message unanswered tail from August 2025 to 2026-01-01, including "What's going on?", two uncaptioned photo attachments, "When is a good time to get together? You can fill me in on what you've been up to," a Halloween check-in, two birthday messages on November 1, "Is there some reason that I'm in timeout?" on November 10, "Have you been a good boy this year? Santa's watching!" on December 10, a ski-boots message on December 22, and a New York taxation question on January 2. The held slice for the whole thread is 17 rows (2025-08-14 to 2026-09-02), one sent and sixteen received.

On 2026-08-31 at 00:55 Rick wrote, "Can you do dinner Tuesday?" On 2026-09-01 at 00:33 Dan replied: "Hey I just got my schedule for the week and I'm going to be at work until 11 tomorrow night. Are you busy towards the end of the week?" Rick answered the same night ("We'll figure something out for next week. Thx for letting me know") and again on September 2 ("Our plans changed. Not going away this weekend. So whatever works for you"). Corpus coverage ends 2026-09-07 with the dinner being arranged, not completed. The "work until 11" line names no employer and is not read here as employment confirmation.

The shape, stated at behaviour level, is the one the event file names: this is the first held-corpus sighting of Dan voluntarily re-opening a channel he let die. The channel died from his side, after his own proposal; the re-opening is one scheduling message. Whether it becomes a repair or remains a blip is not observable in the record as held.

## Vanessa and Lisa: the quiet edges

Vanessa C. Frank, born 1994-01-16 in Greensburg, PA, is Dan's younger sister. The master dump, unheld here, is said to hold 709 messages from 2015-12-01 to 2026-03-01; the tweet archive reaches back further, to eleven tweets across two handles in 2011–2013 (kb/entities/vanessa-frank.md). She works ski school in Vail; an extraction also records her living in New York City, and the two records are not reconciled. She is described in the entity body as a steady, low-conflict, minimal-contact thread defined by shared left politics rather than shared history. The sender correction on her page is the entry's cautionary example in miniature: a Bernie Sanders 2020 canvassing trip once attributed to Vanessa was, on re-reading the Sent rows, Dan's own message in Dan's own voice; who it was sent to is plausible, not confirmed. No rupture is documented in this thread across 2015–2026, and the page's own verdict — if evidence stays this thin, the node should stay short — is adopted here.

Lisa Frank has been Rick's partner since at least December 2015. A December 2 held message ("We could go eat with Rick and Lisa") predates by 25 days the page's "present since 2015-12-27" reading, which was measured against a different export. She is described as a teacher living in Rick's household, with a possible September 2023 wedding; all three claims rest on unheld sources, marriage is unconfirmed (the surname is from household use), and she was written into the record in any detail only in August 2026, a decade after she entered it. The dinner now being arranged through Rick's thread is, if it happens, the household's first documented movement toward Dan since February 2025.

## Conflicts in the record

- November 2005 as the hinge. Dan's testimony is consistent across two decades and every canonical source agrees on the month. No contemporaneous record exists, neither parent has an on-record account, and the dealer's identity is unnamed (kb/events/2005-11-parental-rupture.md). Current standing: carried as single-witness testimony, load-bearing in Dan's own account, uncorroborated in the held corpus.
- The estate-money direction. The older spine read the deep-cycle capital as running toward Dan at $750 a week; the 2026-08-18 correction reverses the direction and retracts the rate (kb/data/1293-estate-spine-direction-reversal-dan-to-suz.md). The cited itemised statement is unheld. Current standing: direction corrected as stated above; the correction itself rests on the wiki's re-read, not on a held transfer record.
- The Suz volume table. Dan ahead 2015–2016, near-tie 2017, Suz permanently ahead from 2018 — page-attributed testimony with no Suz-thread rows in the held slice for 2015–2022. Current standing: the shape is reported as testimony; no number from it is used as a measurement here.
- The authorship of Diane's letter. Dan's 2018-04-03 read was "i think dave wrote it"; the eviction notice was served by "Dian V. Moore," identified as Diane herself; the hospital social-worker account rests on the unheld dump (kb/entities/diane-moore.md). Current standing: one letter, served late, recipient Dan alone — settled; who drafted it is Dan's inference, not established.
- Vanessa's residence. Vail (ski school) against New York City (extraction), unreconciled in kb/entities/vanessa-frank.md. Current standing: both records stand; neither is used.
- Lisa Frank's occupation, household and possible 2023 wedding. All rest on unheld sources; the held corpus supports only presence from December 2015 onward. Current standing: presence is documented; the portrait is not.

## Assessment

Read only through what can be counted — message volume, unanswered tails, cohabitation, who was in the room, which way money moved — the system has a durable asymmetry. One parent is continuously reachable and, in 2026, sharing a house; the other is reachable in episodes separated by silences that the held corpus shows Dan initiating as often as enduring, with the most recent episode still unresolved at corpus end. The person who sat outside that pair, Fran, is also the one whose final six months produced the record's most precisely verified first-person account of care, and her death in April 2018 is immediately followed, in the same record, by the estate money moving and then becoming a dispute. A sister and a father's partner remain present at low volume and low documentation throughout. That is the system the dates support. Any stronger claim — about why the patterns repeat, or what anyone in the family is like inside — would have to be carried by instruments this corpus does not contain, and it is not carried here.
