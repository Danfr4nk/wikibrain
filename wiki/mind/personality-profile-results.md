# Personality Profile Results

The registry of MBTI determinations produced by the stringent instrument —
one row per person, with the result, the confidence, and the evidence basis.
Each person's own entry carries the full per-letter audit in a section
titled "MBTI — stringent instrument run (2026-11-07)"; this page is the
index of those sections. Dan's own profile is not part of this run — it
lives at [[wiki/mind/profile/intp]].

**Run date: 2026-11-07**, on the operator's order, under the
full-source-verification protocol: for each person the main article, its
satellites (share, assessment, cognitive-profile and update pages), the
`kb/` evidence nodes, and the cited raw sources were checked before any
letter was called.

## The instrument

1. **Own-voice / behavioural corpus evidence only.** No role stereotypes —
   being a mother is not evidence for F; being a girlfriend is not evidence
   for anything.
2. **A letter is called only on two or more independent classes of
   evidence**, or on a tested result that the behaviour independently
   corroborates.
3. **Conflicting prior instruments are reconciled or the letter is
   omitted** — rendered X. An omission is a result, not a failure.
4. **A source flagged ⚠ unresolved blocks the claim it carries.** Claims
   resting on unheld sources are stated as relayed, with the flag attached.
5. **Confidence is stated per person**, and single-context corpora (one
   relationship, one crisis window) are flagged as such.

## Omissions and conflicts — the notes that matter

- **Suzanne Frank is the registry's open conflict.** The wiki's forensic
  assessment (33,698-message corpus) determines ISTJ; the profile generated
  from her own machine over 153,706 of her sent messages determines ESFJ.
  The two agree on S and J and split on E/I and T/F. Neither is suppressed;
  both are recorded on her entry, and the contested letters are omitted
  until a Fe-vs-Fi / Te-vs-Ti adjudication pass — or a self-administered
  test — exists.
- **Four people are not callable at all**, and the reasons differ:
  Danielle Onesi's own messages do not survive in readable form; Joe
  Oshnack's thread bodies are unheld (only Dan's side is quotable); Sena
  LeDonne's held thread is real but her side of it is a handful of short
  moves; Claire Ulmer has no direct thread and exists in the corpus only
  through her sister's narration. In each case the entry says so, in place
  of a type.
- **Alexis Armel's N/S letter is left exactly where Dan left it in 2013** —
  his own typing was INFP/ISFP, undecided; the archive leans concrete but
  cannot carry the letter under this instrument.
- **Jerel Coles's row describes conflict conduct, not a settled
  preference**: every primary communication held for him dates from the
  August 2026 crisis window.
- **Fran Coldren's row is behavioural inference only** — no message she
  wrote is held — and is not the same species of result as the others.
- **Danny Matthews**: the determination rests solely on the held direct
  thread. The separate record under the same name on Annie's phone was not
  used, because the identity of the two men is uncorroborated.
