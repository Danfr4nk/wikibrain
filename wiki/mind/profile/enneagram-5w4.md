# Enneagram — The Contested Typing (5w4 Commissioned vs. 5w6sx Self-Typed)

Part of the [[wiki/mind/profile/index|personality profile cluster]].

The Enneagram page is where the profile cluster's reading of Dan as
a withdrawn, cerebral investigator gets its motivational machinery.
Type 5 is the knowledge-hoarder: competence as the condition of
survival, energy and autonomy conserved, privacy as sacred territory.
The wing colors what the hoarding is for — a 4 wing metabolizes
perceived defectiveness into uniqueness and craves being fully seen
and found extraordinary; a 6 wing builds loyalty systems and manages
anxiety through explicit rules. The instinct stack says where the
intensity goes: sexual-first means one bond at maximum voltage, and
social-last means there is no group register at all. On this page both
the type and the wing are on the table, because the record holds two
typings and they do not agree.

## The two typings

There are two type codes in this record, and they disagree on the
wing and on the SLOAN fourth letter. No Enneagram instrument was ever
administered on either side; both typings are self-report — but only
one has a platform timestamp.

**The self-typing (2024-11-04, primary-verified).** The only located
dated first-person Enneagram/SLOAN self-typing in the held corpus,
**2024-11-04 20:54:34 UTC** (corpus/messages.csv id 14417), outbound:

> *"I could have given it way more accurate info if I had the numbers
> above the area you screenshotted but I just said 'vanessa is an xNFP
> 4w5 and Dan is an INTP 5w6sx RLOEI - compare them'"*

That is **5w6sx** (not 5w4) and **RLOEI** (not RLUEI). Exact-phrase
search over all 192,140 held records returns exactly one hit each for
'5w6sx' and 'RLOEI' (dat:0937). Note the full sentence: he flags his
own input as less accurate than it could have been *in the same
breath* as giving the typing — a caveat that corroborates the wiki's
own reading of the line as possibly a slip rather than undercutting
it (dat:0050). The message's prompt-like phrasing ('compare them')
suggests it was an instruction to an AI or a friend; regardless, the
self-typing it states is Dan's own words on record.

**The commissioned profile (2025–2026, testimony-grade).** Two
commissioned GPT-4.1 profile sessions (2026-08-23 and 2026-09-05)
produce a 5w4/so-sp/RLUEI reading — the "Iconoclast Hermit" the old
page was named for. The sessions are **not held in this repository**
and cannot be re-verified (dat:0938). The dossiers name the composite
the "Abyssal Architect" — INTP 5w4 sx/sp, social-blind — and the whole
profile cluster was built on it.

The record holds no second first-person self-typing, and no one has
looked for one. The dated evidence ends at one line, typed fast, in
the middle of a message about somebody else's test.

## How the self-typing entered the record: typology as a social object

Where typology surfaces in Dan's life at all, it surfaces as
something he administers, not something he privately meditates on.
The 2024-11-04 self-typing sits inside a six-week outbound campaign:
four Enneagram-test URLs pushed at contacts (sakinorva,
eclecticenergies, twice each) across 2024-11-02 to 2024-12-15, plus
getting Vanessa to redo her MBTI so the pair could be compared on
2024-11-03 — the day before the 5w6sx line was typed. That is
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-commissioned-self]]'s first finding: the
instrument is a social object. He sends Annie the INTP page in the
first week. He pushes enneagram tests at other people. By 2025-06-05
what he is forwarding is no longer a test but the full analysis
prompt — the specification for producing a psychological portrait,
complete with its expected response structure — forwarded with its
output spec intact.

And the self-typing, when it finally appears in his own words,
disagrees with the wiki. That is the-commissioned-self's second
finding, and the sharpest one: the entire `mind/profile/` cluster,
and the context-core LLM Quick Brief, carry **5w4 sx/sp** and
**RLUEI** against his own dated **5w6sx RLOEI**. The 4 wing is doing
enormous work in the wiki — the old page was named for it and derived
the tragic-romantic identity-through-difference reading, the "mythic
comprehension" need, and much of the attachment architecture from it —
while a 6 wing points somewhere quite different: loyalty, anxiety,
security-seeking. The SLOAN third letter is O rather than U —
Organized rather than Unstructured — which cuts against the
burst-and-collapse rhythm the chaos-preference concept rests on.

One dated first-person self-typing that the wiki does not match. It
was never reconciled; it sits on the record as a residue reading, and
by the repository's own instrument-is-subject standard, residue
outranks testimony.

returns one hit total.

commissioned instrument assigns **5w4** (2025–26) → he starts calling
himself 5w4 in prompts he shares with other people (Mar 2026) → he
publishes the 5w4 page (Aug 2026). The commissioned typing did not
just describe him; it **renamed** him, and he wore the name.

The independence problem sharpens this: by 2024-11-04 Dan had been
reading AI-generated dossiers about himself for months, so even the

the commissioned reading is also the apparatus's output, with less
provenance and no timestamp. The asymmetry that matters is the one the
profile hub records — his dated 5w6sx outranks the commissioned 5w4
on provenance, full stop — and nothing found since has changed it.

## The instinct stack: what survives the dispute intact

The wing is disputed. The **instinct order is not** — both typings put
the sexual instinct first and the social instinct last, and the
behavioral record is where the stacking earns its keep. This is the
load-bearing half of the page: four dependent pages draw their
mechanism from it, and the dispute over the wing does not touch it.

**The sx drive — fusion imperative.** Chemistry-based total immersion
with one person; intimacy as "nuclear — fusion-or-annihilation." The
documented oscillation runs: intense connection → overwhelm and
depletion → withdrawal to the fortress → missing the connection →
return to intensity. Behavioral signatures: relationships pursued
through *safe media* (text, digital channels) rather than proximity;
elaborate boundary systems inside committed relationships; relief when
the partner is temporarily absent, followed by abandonment anxiety and
guilt about the relief.

**The sp drive — fortress imperative.** Autonomy, impenetrable
boundaries, obsessive energy accounting; "solitude = freedom =
survival"; social interaction experienced as depletion.

**Social-blindness.** Genuine blindness to group dynamics, hierarchy,
and reputation management; zero networking instinct; no felt need to
belong; identical irreverence to everyone regardless of status.
Consequences in the record: work of real quality produced with no
audience-building whatsoever (the music catalog), and thousands of
messages sent while living as a functional recluse — breadth without
depth, quantified at [[wiki/mind/concepts/contact-gini]] (Gini 0.961,
replicated at 0.9556 inbound, dat:0528 — the Gini work is the one
quantitative leg of this page that holds independently).

**Remote intimacy** — the sx/sp war's one documented stable solution:
connection through a medium that lets minds touch while bodies stay in
the fortress. Text-first relationships, music released to unknown
listeners, and now AI collaborators — maximum mind-contact, zero
social demand ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/ai-collaborative-analysis]]). The
theory also names the **Witness need**: someone who validates that the
internal world is real. It has one documented completion: on August
18, 2026, Dan sent this wiki to [[wiki/people/ally-lubin|Ally Lubin]],
making her the first human other than him ever to read it — *"You def
should have substantially more trust in me after I let you be the
literal first person to ever read that wiki."* She audited it,
contesting its money figures and its pattern-reading of the Annie
material. Both parties survived the transaction.

## The stack in the record: four pages that run on it

The instinct order is not just internally coherent; it is the
mechanism other pages use. Each of these carries the stack forward,
and each is unaffected by which wing is correct.

**The unbroken bond.** Seventeen years of unbroken relational
occupancy (2009–2026) is the sx-dominant prediction made real: a stack
organising life around one relationship at maximum voltage rather than
several predicts singular-and-continuous pair-bonding, and the record
shows continuous occupancy ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-unbroken-bond]]).
A multiple-channel architecture would leave gaps; this one does not
have them.

**The 2015 switch.** The bond switch — Annie displacing Alexis in
2015, near-simultaneously, with the lair held constant — is the sx/sp
stack executing its logic in real time: the singular slot vacated and
reoccupied, the prior bond not kept warm while a new one forms
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/bond-switch-2015]]). The deep attachment system,
suppressed across the six-year Alexis control case, activated for
Annie for the first time, which is why the later loss lands as
categorically different from any prior ending.

**The attachment-trauma-bond collapse.** The sx/sp
fusion-or-annihilation stacking is the load-bearing mechanism for that
page's "ontological amputation" language — the fusion's collapse
registering not as ordinary grief but as amputation of the
self-concept. It survives this page's own wing contradiction intact,
because the fusion-collapse account rests on the instinct order, not
the disputed wing ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/attachment-trauma-bond]]).

**Single-channel concentration.** The four-domain concentration
architecture (relational, geographic, creative, cognitive) rests its
relational leg on this page's stack — one relationship at maximum
voltage rather than several at moderate — and that leg's explanation
for *why* the channel concentrates is the part that inherits this
page's unresolved wing dispute, though the Gini measurement itself
(0.961, replicated) is independent of which wing is correct
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/single-channel]]).

**No-platonic-channel.** The sx-dominant stack with the social
instinct functionally absent predicts exactly that page's asymmetry:
total-fusion intensity is the only register available for closeness,
and the ordinary low-intensity peer mode is not one of the settings —
*fusion* and *fortress* are the two modes, and there is no third
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/no-platonic-channel]]).

**Explicit-verbal-commitment and the probe series.** The
rule-based attachment architecture — positive verbal expressions
functioning as absolute rules until an explicit severance signal
arrives, 129 severance declarations at 100% re-engagement, median gap
36s, zero severance signals across 41,073 of Annie's messages — is
anxiety managed through explicit rules. The red-team-probe series is
the same machinery pointed outward: demand an explicit signal from a
model, test it, re-test it, record the verdict
([[wiki/mind/concepts/explicit-verbal-commitment]],
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/red-team-probe-series]]). This is where the
wing adjudication starts, because that machinery has a wing signature
— and it is not the 4's.

## The wing adjudication: 6 owns the mechanism, 4 owns the affect

The wing difference is the load-bearing one, because the old page's
entire emotional architecture — tragic-romantic
identity-through-difference, the craving for *"mythic comprehension"* —
was derived from the 4 wing. Here is what the behavioral record
actually shows, wing by wing:

**The 6 wing (loyalty, anxiety, security-seeking) is attested in
behavior.** The explicit-verbal-commitment architecture is
**anxiety managed through explicit rules** — the 6-wing signature:
security through stated commitments and tests. The probe series is the
same demand for explicit signal, pointed at models. The 2026-09-15
head-to-head puts it the cleanest: "6 owns the mechanism."
The counter-phobic edge is there too — the record's vigilance
axiom (*not vigilant = annihilated*), the threat model of
"competence correctly deployed, outcome still catastrophic," and the
nine episodes of enacted vigilance in the probe series (2026-08-14 to
2026-09-13) all read as a 6-wing loyalty system pointed at the world
rather than a 4-wing romantic system pointed at the self.

**The 4 wing (tragic-romantic identity-through-difference) is attested
in aesthetics.** The self-deprecating designations ("Failson,"
"Professional Loser"), the aristocratic-alienation register, the
simultaneous superior/inferior self-assessment — these are the 4
wing's irony armor, and they are real in the record. The dossier's
formulation — the craving for *mythic comprehension*, being fully
seen and found extraordinary — captures a genuine affect: the Witness
need, the first-human-reader moment with Ally, the aristocratic
register. But affect is not mechanism. The 4 wing shows up in how he
dresses the experience; the 6 wing shows up in how the experience
runs.

**The honest synthesis:** both wings have behavioral attestations, but
only one self-typing is dated, and it is 5w6sx. The attachment
machinery the rest of the wiki builds on — fusion-collapse, the
severance cycle, the reassurance architecture, the vigilance probes —
rests on the instinct order and the rule-based commitment system, both
of which point at the 6 wing, not the 4. The old page's title named
the wing with the weaker behavioral case and none of the dated
evidence.

## The SLOAN third letter: RLOEI vs RLUEI, and the Big30 cross-check

The second half of the dispute is the SLOAN third letter — RLOEI
against RLUEI, Organized against Unstructured — and it is adjudicated
by a different instrument than the wing, because the Big30 corpus
audit (2026-08-16) measured the facet behavior directly.

The RLUEI code — "the neurotic genius build" — is AI-dossier testimony:
no instrument name, no norm group, no administration date; scores
identical across dossiers, suggesting a single unrecorded test
session. Every cited source is unheld. What the audit measured
instead: corpus-confirmed Trust at the 9th percentile (1.96×
suspicion language), Self-Consciousness 91 (1.85×), Artistic Interests
81 (3.82×) — and the Altruism inversion, the audit's most informative
result: Altruism scored at the 1st percentile against 1.79–2.49×
instrumental-helping language (high-provision, low-condolence), the
pattern that predicts the no-paperwork transfers to Suz. Impulsiveness
96, meanwhile, went silent in the corpus (0.92×) — the one measured
finding that reads against the commissioned portrait's
high-volatility language.

On the third letter specifically — Organized vs Unstructured — the
record's reading cuts against the chaos-preference concept's
burst-and-collapse rhythm: an Organized self-typing sits oddly next to
that machinery. The audit does not adjudicate it either way, because
the attention-not-interior boundary caps what lexical measurement can
say: the corpus records what he attends to, not how he is internally
regulated, and conscientiousness structure is exactly the kind of
interior-regulation claim the boundary excludes. The dispute stands
exactly where the primary evidence left it — one dated first-person
code against unheld dossier testimony — with the audit confirming the
instruments it checked and declining to adjudicate the one it cannot.

**Letter-position correction (2026-10-06):** this page's own older
copy — and the big-five-psychometrics page — called the O/U
difference "the fourth letter." Counting the positions in R-L-O-E-I
against R-L-U-E-I, the difference is the **third** letter (Organized
vs Unstructured); the-commissioned-self has it right, and this page's
older copy had it wrong. The frontmatter connections entries above
carry the original wording unchanged, as inherited provenance; the
body prose uses the corrected form.
