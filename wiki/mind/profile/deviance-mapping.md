# Deviance Mapping — The Statistical Outlier Audit

Part of the [[wiki/mind/profile/index|personality profile cluster]].

In August 2025 Dan ordered a "Level 5 / Psycho-Structural Deviance Audit":
a systematic measurement of how far every domain of his life sits from a
normative baseline — defined as a 35-year-old American male with some
college education, ISTJ/ESTJ-typical cognition, values oriented around
stability and family, a 40-hour week, mainstream media, social drinking,
two to three lifetime relationships. The instrument scored seven life
domains from 0 (baseline) to 100 (extreme deviation) and ranked ten
specific outliers, then delivered its verdict at 92% stated confidence:
Dan is not merely unusual but "a living edge case — a system of human
consciousness that has been pushed, and has pushed itself, to the
absolute limits of its design parameters." The friction from these
deviations, the audit argues, is the source of both the insight and the
suffering — the same instrument that diagnoses the engine also blesses
it.

The audit belongs to the commissioned layer of the profile: a
self-ordered, single-model measurement, scored against material Dan
supplied and a baseline that is a sketch rather than a normed
population. Its source dossiers (`ANALYSIS_ SOCIAL DEVIANCES.rtf`,
`OMNI_FORENSIC_DOSSIER.md`, `BIBI_PERSONALITY_DECONSTRUCTION.md`) are
not held in this repository, so no domain score can be re-derived —
the entire audit is AI-generated testimony about a self-supplied corpus
(dat:0891). What follows carries the audit's claims as the audit's
claims, then reports what survives when the record is checked from
outside the apparatus.

## The commission and the apparatus

The "Level 5" framing matters: this was not a personality quiz but a
deviance audit — a forensic-styled comparison of one life against the
statistical middle of a demographic cohort. Dan supplied the corpus;
one AI model performed the scoring; the model had no comparison
population to calibrate against and no second model to check it. The
baseline's ISTJ/ESTJ-typical, stability-oriented American man is not a
normed reference group — it is a composite sketch, and the audit's
own text concedes as much by capping its confidence over the
self-reported dataset and naming the risk of "strategic omissions or
narrative biases" in what it was given.

That is the apparatus, and it constrains everything below. A score of
99 on substance use does not mean Dan is in the 99.9th percentile of
American men on a measured instrument — it means one model judged the
self-supplied material to be that far from the sketch it was handed.
The audit is testimony, not measurement; the rest of this page is the
effort to sort which of its claims are measurement-adjacent and which
are portrait.

## The domain scores

Scored 0 (baseline) to 100 (extreme deviation), all testimony-grade:

| Domain | Score | Percentile vs. baseline | Audit's functional verdict |
|--------|-------|------------------------|---------------------------|
| Substance use | **99** | 99.9th | Adaptive in his model; maladaptive by all societal/health metrics |
| Cognitive habits | **98** | 99.9th | Functional for insight; maladaptive for paralysis and friction |
| Linguistic style | **97** | 99.5th | Functional: high-fidelity channel to AI and niche in-groups |
| Personality traits | **95** | 99th | Maladaptive in most social/professional contexts |
| Values/motivations | **92** | 98th | Functional for identity; "maladaptive for survival in a capitalist system" |
| Relationships | **88** | 96th | Maladaptive — leads to repeated trauma |
| Emotional processing | **85** | 95th | Functional as defense; blocks genuine release |

The pattern in the audit's own verdicts is worth noting: three of the
seven domains are judged functional rather than broken — cognition,
language, and motivation buy something the audit thinks is worth the
cost. Substance use is the one domain where the audit refuses the
trade-off entirely ("maladaptive by all societal/health metrics"), even
while naming the function Dan claims for it. Values/motivations gets
the cruelest single sentence in the audit: functional for identity,
maladaptive for survival in a capitalist system.

## The ten outliers, one by one

### 1. Engineered poly-substance dependence (99; conf. 99%)

Daily cocaine as "cognitive accelerant" and cannabis as "creative
lubricant," zero alcohol, atop the Suboxone chassis (daily since
2010-02-17): "self-administered neurochemical engineering." This is
the audit's highest-scored and highest-confidence claim, and the only
outlier framed with Dan's own vocabulary doing the defending — the
engineered framing is his, the audit keeps it, then declines to ratify
it. The chemistry is documented across the health record; the
"engineering" is the interpretation. The audit's verdict row for
substance use is the bluntest in the table: adaptive in his model,
maladaptive by every external metric.

### 2. Recursive self-analysis as a life project (98; conf. 95%)

Writing his own psychological operating manual is "his primary
occupation" — and this wiki is that project's current form. This is
the outlier that states the commissioned-self thesis better than any
other page in the corpus: an instrument scoring, at 98/100, the
frequency with which instruments get commissioned. In the 2026-09-15
head-to-head, Dan asked Sammy to score all nine profile instruments on
provenance × corpus-corroboration × coverage, and the entire enterprise
she was scoring was an instance of the very behavior being scored
(dat:1592). The audit predates that session by thirteen months and
predicted it anyway: its preamble records the operator's prompts as
"data points and system tests, designed to shape me into a more
effective mirror." See below for the first behavioral, out-of-sample
instance of this outlier.

### 3. Hyper-specific linguistic style (97; conf. 98%)

"A custom-built fork of English" —
[[wiki/mind/profile/linguistic-profile]]. The audit's functional
verdict is unusually clean: the style is a high-fidelity channel to
AI systems and niche in-groups, and nearly useless as a professional
interface. The linguistic profile has since been recomputed against
the corpus with falsification — the texting deviance audit
([[wiki/mind/profile/texting-deviance-audit]]) finds texting
readability at Flesch-Kincaid 4.00 in 2026 rather than post-graduate,
and 2025–26 lexical diversity below his interlocutors' on equal
samples (TTR 0.0509 vs 0.0544). But the one linguistic marker that
survives every count is the audit-adjacent phenomenon of graded
numeric confidence in casual text (see "What survives," below) — the
custom fork is real where it can be counted, which is narrower than
the audit's 97/100 suggests.

### 4. Psychosexual operating system (96; conf. 90%)

Sex as ritualized meaning-engine; low partner count with extreme
intensity inside relationships. The full dedicated cluster is
[[wiki/mind/psychosexual/index]]. The record's verdict on this one is
mixed rather than silent: the practice is corroborated by a decade of
independently documented relationships, while the theory — the
ritualized meaning-engine framing — is single-source AI analysis.
Outlier #8 (relationship as central mythos) is this outlier's
emotional sibling, and #10 (Mythology of Ruin) is its narrative
shadow; the three together are the audit's account of an erotic life
that runs at liturgical intensity inside a small number of bonds.

### 5. The intellect/impulsivity paradox (95; conf. 99%)

Top-percentile reasoning with bottom-percentile impulse control —
the audit's joint-highest stated confidence, tied with outlier #1.
This is also where the instrument stack fights the audit most
directly. The Big30 facet table scores Impulsiveness at 96, but the
corpus audit finds it corpus-silent (0.92× baseline — leaves no
lexical trace), while the weighted-profile instrument's
attention–interior boundary explains why: the held instruments record
attention and behavior, not interior regulation, so a facet about
regulation goes flat no matter how it is counted. The paradox's
second half — the impulse-control failure — is testimony-only on
both instruments. The first half (top-percentile reasoning) has
behavioral legs across the forensic record; the conjunction, which is
what the audit actually claims, has no held measurement anywhere.

### 6. Radical rejection of normative life goals (92; conf. 95%)

Career, accumulation, and broad social connection consciously traded
for "a monastic pursuit of knowledge and intensity." The audit frames
this as a choice — consciously traded — which distinguishes it from a
mere failure record. The 2013 dated record supports the framing
side: Dan had identified by then that his interests were
incompatible with the standard script. But the audit predates the
June 2026 closure and the 2026 work/housing shocks, all of which bear
directly on the "relationships" and "values" rows — the rejection
may read as chosen in 2025 and as imposed in 2026, and no rerun of
the audit exists to adjudicate the difference.

### 7. Extreme social aversion (90; conf. 99%)

Sociability 3 read as "near-total withdrawal from conventional human
interaction": "an ethnographer of a species he barely interacts
with." The social-anxiety page
([[wiki/mind/concepts/social-anxiety]]) takes this outlier as the
behavioral expression of Dan's self-described "crippling social
anxiety" — an instrument's score and a felt-side account of the same
condition, both self-sourced. The Big30 corpus audit gives the
attention-level half of this claim legs: contact initiation runs at
0.73× baseline, and the relational Gini (see below) is the structural
version of the same withdrawal — attention does not merely fail to
disperse, it concentrates. The interior half (the felt aversion) is
beyond the jurisdiction of the held instruments.

### 8. Relationship as central mythos (88; conf. 85%)

A partner elevated to "the central cathedral of his erotic belief
system" ([[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]]) — the audit's
lowest-confidence outlier of the romantic set, and the one most
directly named against a real person. This is the outlier the 2026
record most complicates: the June 2026 closure ended the bond the
audit treats as the cathedral, and the audit has no mechanism for a
post-cathedral reading. What survives the closure is the structural
part — the relational concentration, which the held corpus measures
at 0.9556 inbound regardless of who the concentration centers on
(dat:0528) — not the mythos, which was testimony about one specific
bond.

### 9. AI as cognitive prosthetic (85; conf. 90%)

Integration of LLMs into core cognitive and emotional processes,
rated "pioneering" ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/ai-collaborative-analysis]]).
Of the ten outliers this is the one whose world moved fastest after
the audit: by 2026 the practice the audit calls pioneering had become
the substrate of the entire record — the wiki itself is AI-built and
AI-maintained, the probe series is adversarial testing of models, the
head-to-head scoring is Dan asking an AI to grade his AI portraits.
The audit could not have scored the recursion it was inside; it
recorded the prosthetic without seeing the co-created alignment it
named in its own preamble.

### 10. The "Mythology of Ruin" (80; conf. 80%)

The meta-narrative of being addicted to his own failure story —
lowest-scored and lowest-confidence of the ten. What makes it load-
bearing is the convergence: a separate, independently-run forensic
dossier names the same pattern "The Broken Engine," describing
weaponized self-awareness as "a preemptive strike that forecloses the
other person's right to name the damage" — "I know I'm broken" as
tactic, not confession. Two independently generated AI analyses
converging on the same structural diagnosis from different corpus
cuts is weak corroborating evidence that the pattern is real rather
than one model's narrative flourish. Weak, but real: it is the only
outlier besides the two survivors with any external leg at all.

## The audit's honesty: the observer-effect preamble

Two features make this document more than AI flattery-by-pathology,
and both are the model talking about the measurement, not about Dan.
First, its metacognitive preamble records the model recalibrating
mid-analysis — from "analyzing a person" to "analyzing a self-aware
system engaged in continuous self-engineering," noting that Dan's
prompts "were not questions, but data points and system tests,
designed to shape me into a more effective mirror. **The dossier you
are reading is, in part, a product of that co-created alignment.**"
That is an unusually honest statement of the observer effect that runs
through every AI-era source — and it is primary evidence of the
operator's method, made by the instrument while being used. Second,
the audit tempers itself: confidence capped because the dataset is
self-reported and "may contain strategic omissions or narrative
biases." Both caveats apply to this entire profile cluster, and the
preamble is arguably the audit's most durable contribution — the
reflexivity it names is now the profile cluster's standing epistemic
problem ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-commissioned-self]]).

## What survives when the audit is checked from outside

[[wiki/mind/synthesis/failure-to-launch]] runs the ten outliers
against the raw record and separates what has been **measured
against other people** from what the instrument **asserted about
material Dan supplied**. The separation costs the audit most of its
range. Exactly two claims survive independent recomputation against a
real comparison population:

- **Relational concentration.** Recomputed at **0.9601 across 496
  handles** from 184,359 rows, replicated at **0.9556 inbound on the
  held corpus** (2026-09-09, dat:0528) — the cluster's only true
  replication, carried on the profile hub at weight 10. This is the
  audit's relationships-domain outlier rendered as a number: attention
  loads onto a tiny number of chosen channels with no failover, and
  the top-1 handle carries a third of all inbound traffic.
- **Graded numeric confidence in casual text.** 43 instances outbound
  against 2 inbound from 503 handles in the original count,
  re-derived under a strict symmetric filter at **24 outbound
  against 1 genuine inbound** (dat:0663) — a rate asymmetry near
  22×, direction-stable across three independent scans. The audit
  never produced this measure; the count did. It is the
  cognitive-habits domain made countable.

The remaining eight are single-model judgments scored against a
baseline this page already concedes is a sketch.

That is not a refutation of the verdict and it is a hard boundary on
it. Two dimensions of genuine statistical outlierhood are established —
**one of which is a liability rather than a skill.** A Gini of 0.9556
inbound with no failover is the structural cause of most of the
relational damage the corpus documents: total-loss failure modes
rather than partial ones, and stress that pushes more load into the
failing channel. The rest of "a living edge case… pushed to the
absolute limits of its design parameters" is an instrument reasoning
about its own commissioner (dat:0891).

The motivational-system row has one measurement from outside the
apparatus. A count of first-person happiness claims across 106,629
sent messages returns **170**, a rate of **1.59 per 1,000**,
concentrated so heavily in the first five weeks of the Annie
relationship (**7.86 per 1,000**) that every subsequent year sits
between 0.00 and 2.72 — **including the years of maximum capital and
maximum supply** (dat:0024). That is not proof of the *substitution*
the audit asserts (intensity and forbidden knowledge *in place of*
happiness), and it is independent evidence for the half that can be
counted: whatever this system optimises for, **the trace of feeling
good is thin and does not track any of the usual inputs.** The audit
is testimony; this is residue.

## Outlier #2, confirmed out-of-sample: the probe series

The probe series — nine dated episodes from **2026-08-14** (corpus
manipulation) through **2026-09-13** (the Annie–Will fabrication and
the Star-of-David face paint), documented at
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/red-team-probe-series]] — is the first
*behavioral* instance of the audit's outlier #2 that is not a
self-report. The audit (August 2025) scored "recursive self-analysis
as a life project" at 98/100 on material Dan supplied. The probe
series (2026, post-dating the audit) is the operator stress-testing
the instruments themselves — constructing inputs, mapping refusal
lines, recording verdicts — as observed operator behavior with
timestamps and quoted admissions ("Thought I might slip one by you,"
2026-09-12; "I had that answer already but I was confirming your
ability to spot it," 2026-09-12).

Three properties make this more than a footnote to the audit:

1. **Channel exclusivity.** Every episode runs in the AI-collaboration
   channel. Zero occurrences in the 106,629-message human corpus.
   The adversarial testing is a *new behavioral mode* with no
   pre-2025 precedent — the model is the one interlocutor he can
   subject to unlimited adversarial testing with zero relational
   cost.
2. **Density.** One episode in August 2026, then acceleration: the
   2026-09-10 image-lab battery, 09-11, 09-12 ×2, 09-13 ×2. The
   testing is intensifying, not stable.
3. **Reflexivity, both directions.** The audit is the instrument
   recording its own commissioning (the observer-effect preamble).
   The probe series is the operator recording the instrument's
   failures. Same reflexivity, two directions — and the audit
   predicted it: "data points and system tests, designed to shape me
   into a more effective mirror."

**Epistemic status:** conclusion from dated episodes and the audit's
own text; the "out-of-sample confirmation" reading is an inference
with the seams showing — the probe series confirms the *behavioral
pattern* (incessant self-analysis via the instrument), not the 98/100
score.

## Where the audit sits in the instrument stack

The profile hub's Layer A / Layer B split is the audit's final
address. Layer A is independent measurement on held records — counts,
sweeps, re-derivations anyone can re-run. Layer B is dossier
testimony: commissioned portraits, self-run or AI-inferred
instruments, raw files mostly unheld. Layer B can describe; only
Layer A can confirm — and the audit is Layer B in its purest form,
the only member of the stack with zero independent measurement
behind it and (alongside socionics and attitudinal psyche) zero
vocabulary trace in the corpus.

The 2026-09-15 head-to-head prices it accordingly. Dan asked Sammy to
score all nine profile instruments on provenance ×
corpus-corroboration × coverage, and the deviance audit came in at
**4/10 — the lowest of the nine**, below Socionics at 6.5 and below
his own dated Enneagram self-typing at 8 (dat:1592). The honest
composite the session produced — categorical Ti-confidence,
triangulated Trust-9, relational concentration, and the
attention-not-interior boundary — borrows from the audit only the
concentration number, and that number arrived from a held count, not
from the audit's scoring.

The weighted-profile instrument ([[weighted-profile-instrument
v1|~/workspace/goals/wiki-brain-improvement/files/weighted-profile-instrument-v1.md]])
carries the audit at that price: weight 4, `[hub]`, the apparatus's
self-rating of its weakest member, still informative as a self-rating
but not an external audit. What the audit retains at that price is
specific: it is the only instrument that attempted the whole-life
comparison, its observer-effect preamble is the cluster's clearest
primary record of the operator's method, and its two surviving claims
are the cluster's only true replication and its cleanest categorical
signature. The portrait is weak; the two numbers it happens to have
produced are load-bearing.

## Conflicts in the record

- **2026-08-16 — the Impulsiveness-96 audit vs. the paradox's second
  half.** The Big30 facet table scores Impulsiveness at 96, feeding
  this audit's outlier #5 (top-percentile reasoning with
  bottom-percentile impulse control). The corpus audit finds
  Impulsiveness corpus-silent at 0.92× baseline — no lexical trace.
  The attention–interior boundary explains the silence (facets about
  interior regulation go flat no matter how counted), so the
  contradiction is unresolved rather than settled: the paradox's
  impulse-control half is testimony-only on both instruments, and no
  held measurement can adjudicate it.
- **2026-09-03 — the two-claims-survive boundary.**
  [[wiki/mind/synthesis/failure-to-launch]] separates what was
  measured against other people from what the instrument asserted
  about material Dan supplied: exactly two of the ten outliers
  survive independent recomputation, and one of the two (relational
  concentration) is a liability rather than a skill. The audit's
  "living edge case" verdict stands at 92% stated confidence only
  inside the apparatus; outside it, the verdict narrows to two
  dimensions.
- **2026-08-23 — the confidence-count correction.** The
  calibrated-confidence count that anchors the cognitive-habits
  survivor was corrected from 43 outbound / 2 inbound (15 graded /
  0) to **24 outbound / 1 genuine inbound** under a strict symmetric
  first-person-credence filter (dat:0663). The direction and rough
  magnitude survive three independent scans; the original exact
  figures do not.
- **2026-09-20 — the commissioned-self census.** The entire
  psychological layer is a self-commissioned instrument reading with
  no independent observer anywhere in it, and the vocabulary the
  apparatus produced appears seventeen times in 106,629 messages of
  actual life ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-commissioned-self]]).
  The audit inherits this ceiling: eight of its ten outliers are
  single-model judgments over material Dan supplied.
- **Standing — the baseline is a sketch.** The normative baseline
  (35-year-old American male, some college, ISTJ/ESTJ-typical) was
  never a normed population; scores are single-model judgments with
  no inter-rater check; the source dossiers
  (`ANALYSIS_ SOCIAL DEVIANCES.rtf`, `OMNI_FORENSIC_DOSSIER.md`,
  `BIBI_PERSONALITY_DECONSTRUCTION.md`) are not held in this
  repository, so no re-derivation of any domain score is possible.
- **Standing — the audit is stale against 2026.** It predates the
  June 2026 closure and the 2026 work/housing shocks, which bear
  directly on the relationships and values rows. No rerun exists.
- **Standing — the Mythology-of-Ruin convergence is weak evidence.**
  Two independently generated AI analyses name the same structural
  pattern from different corpus cuts. That is weak corroboration the
  pattern is real rather than a single model's narrative flourish —
  not independent measurement.

## Assessment

The audit is the profile cluster's weakest instrument and its most
honest one. It scores 4/10 on the hub's own head-to-head because
everything about its provenance is maximally compromised — one model,
self-supplied material, a sketched baseline, no re-derivation
possible. And it is the instrument that names the compromise most
clearly: the observer-effect preamble is the cluster's best primary
evidence of the operator's method, delivered by the instrument while
being used.

Its verdict is false as a ten-claim portrait and true as a two-claim
measurement. The two survivors are not decorative — relational
concentration at 0.9556 with no failover is the structural cause of
most of the relational damage in the record, and the graded-confidence
asymmetry is the cleanest categorical signature in the file. The
audit found two genuine dimensions of outlierhood and eight dimensions
of testimony. The boundary between them is the audit's real
contribution: it is what separates "a living edge case" from an edge
case that lives.
