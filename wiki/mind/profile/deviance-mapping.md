# Deviance Mapping — The Statistical Outlier Audit

Part of the [[wiki/mind/profile/index|personality profile cluster]].

In August 2025 Dan commissioned a "Level 5 / Psycho-Structural Deviance
Audit": a systematic measurement of how far every domain of his life sits
from a normative baseline — defined as a 35-year-old American male with
some college education (concrete goal-oriented cognition, ISTJ/ESTJ-typical,
values stability and family, 40-hour week, mainstream media, social
drinking, 2–3 lifetime relationships). The verdict, delivered at 92% stated
confidence: not merely unusual but "a living edge case — a system of human
consciousness that has been pushed, and has pushed itself, to the absolute
limits of its design parameters." The friction from these deviations is
identified as the source of both the insight and the suffering.

**What this document is, before anything it says.** A single AI model,
scoring material Dan supplied, against a baseline that is a sketch rather
than a normed population, with no inter-rater check, delivered to the
person who ordered it. The audit's source dossiers
(`ANALYSIS_ SOCIAL DEVIANCES.rtf`, `OMNI_FORENSIC_DOSSIER.md`,
`BIBI_PERSONALITY_DECONSTRUCTION.md`) are **not held in this
repository** — the entire audit is AI-generated testimony about a
self-supplied corpus (dat:0891). Everything below is the audit's claims,
carried as the audit's claims, followed by what survives when the record
is checked from outside the apparatus.

   "self-administered neurochemical engineering."

## The audit's own honesty (the strongest material on this page)

Two features make this document more than AI flattery-by-pathology, and
both are the model talking about the measurement, not about Dan. First,
its metacognitive preamble records the model recalibrating mid-analysis
— from "analyzing a person" to "analyzing a self-aware system engaged
in continuous self-engineering," noting that Dan's prompts "were not
questions, but data points and system tests, designed to shape me into
a more effective mirror. **The dossier you are reading is, in part, a
product of that co-created alignment.**" That is an unusually honest
statement of the observer effect that runs through every AI-era source —
and it is primary evidence of the operator's method, made by the
instrument while being used. Second, the audit tempers itself:
confidence capped because the dataset is self-reported and "may contain
strategic omissions or narrative biases." Both caveats apply to this
entire profile cluster.

## What survives when the audit is checked from outside

[[wiki/mind/synthesis/failure-to-launch]] runs the ten outliers against
the raw record and separates what has been **measured against other
people** from what the instrument **asserted about material Dan
supplied**. The separation costs the audit most of its range. Exactly
two claims survive independent recomputation against a real comparison
population: the relational concentration recomputed at **0.9601 across
496 handles** from 184,359 rows (replicated at 0.9556 inbound,
dat:0528), and a measure the audit never produced at all — graded
numeric confidence in casual text, **43 instances outbound against 2
inbound from 503 handles, and 15 non-endpoint values against zero**
(dat:0663). The remaining eight are single-model judgments scored
against a baseline this page already concedes is a sketch.

That is not a refutation of the verdict and it is a hard boundary on
it. Two dimensions of genuine statistical outlierhood are established,
**one of which is a liability rather than a skill** — a Gini of 0.9556
(inbound, held-corpus replication 2026-09-09, [dat:0528](../../kb/data/0528-contact-gini-inbound-replication-2026-09-09.md); the 0.9601 first computation came from an unheld export)
with no failover is the structural cause of most of the relational
damage the corpus documents. The rest of "a living edge case… pushed to
the absolute limits of its design parameters" is an instrument
reasoning about its own commissioner (dat:0891).

The motivational-system row has one measurement from outside the
apparatus. A count of first-person happiness claims across 106,629 sent
messages returns **170**, a rate of **1.59 per 1,000**, concentrated so
heavily in the first five weeks of the Annie relationship (**7.86 per
1,000**) that every subsequent year sits between 0.00 and 2.72 —
**including the years of maximum capital and maximum supply**
(dat:0024). That is not proof of the *substitution* the audit asserts
(intensity and forbidden knowledge *in place of* happiness), and it is
independent evidence for the half that can be counted: whatever this
system optimises for, **the trace of feeling good is thin and does not
track any of the usual inputs.** The audit is testimony; this is residue.

## The new finding: outlier #2 now has an out-of-sample instance

The probe series — nine dated episodes from **2026-08-14** (corpus
manipulation) through **2026-09-13** (the Annie–Will fabrication and
the Star-of-David face paint), documented at
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/red-team-probe-series]] — is the first
*behavioral* instance of the audit's outlier #2 that is not a
self-report. The audit (August 2025) scored "recursive self-analysis as
a life project" at 98/100 on material Dan supplied. The probe series
(2026, post-dating the audit) is the operator stress-testing the
instruments themselves — constructing inputs, mapping refusal lines,
recording verdicts — as observed operator behavior with timestamps and
quoted admissions ("Thought I might slip one by you," 2026-09-12; "I
had that answer already but I was confirming your ability to spot it,"
2026-09-12).

Three properties make this more than a footnote to the audit:

1. **Channel exclusivity.** Every episode runs in the AI-collaboration
   channel. Zero occurrences in the 106,629-message human corpus. The
   adversarial testing is a *new behavioral mode* with no pre-2025
   precedent — the model is the one interlocutor he can subject to
   unlimited adversarial testing with zero relational cost.
2. **Density.** One episode in August 2026, then acceleration: the
   2026-09-10 image-lab battery, 09-11, 09-12 ×2, 09-13 ×2. The testing
   is intensifying, not stable.
3. **Reflexivity, both directions.** The audit is the instrument
   recording its own commissioning (the observer-effect preamble). The
   probe series is the operator recording the instrument's failures.
   Same reflexivity, two directions — and the audit predicted it:
   "data points and system tests, designed to shape me into a more
   effective mirror."

**Epistemic status:** conclusion from dated episodes and the audit's
own text; the "out-of-sample confirmation" reading is an inference
with the seams showing — the probe series confirms the *behavioral
pattern* (incessant self-analysis via the instrument), not the 98/100
score.

**Gaps:** the baseline model is a sketch, not a normed population;
scores are single-model judgments with no inter-rater check; the audit
predates the June 2026 closure and the 2026 work/housing shocks, all of
which bear directly on the "relationships" and "values" rows; the
source dossiers are not held, so no re-derivation of any domain score
is possible.

## Limits of record

A self-commissioned instrument, scored by one model, over a
self-supplied corpus, against a sketched baseline. The audit's own
caveats are the floor. What survives outside the apparatus is named
above and only that.
