# Big Five / Big30 Psychometrics & PD Vector

Part of the [[wiki/mind/profile/index|personality profile cluster]].

This page holds the profile's hardest-looking numbers and the audit
that changed what three of them mean. Read the two layers separately:
the **table** is testimony — a Big30 facet battery and PD screen with
no instrument name, no norm group, no administration date, and raw
dossier files that are not held anywhere in this repository, scores
repeated identically across dossiers (one unrecorded test session,
restated). The **corpus audit** is measurement — every score turned into
a directional prediction with a lexical proxy, run 2026-08-16 over
Dan's 106,629 sent messages against 110,944 received from 503 other
handles as a within-medium control (dat:0890). The table below carries
both: the dossier's percentile and, where the audit ran, what the
corpus said back.

## The instrument and its provenance (Layer B of the stack)

The numbers on this page arrive from a single evidentiary stratum: the
dossier cluster — FULL PROFILE 2026, Dan Profile.txt, the deviance
audit, the PD screen, the Big30 table, the Socionics/Attitudinal Psyche
readings — self-run or AI-inferred instruments, co-written with LLMs at
Dan's direction, inheriting his framing, raw files unheld. On the
profile hub's two-layer structure this is Layer B, the commissioned
portrait, set against Layer A: the independent measurements on held
records (the Gini computation, the graded-confidence count, the audit
itself). The hub's caution is structural, not ad hominem: seven
instruments run by the same operator, over a corpus he selected, at his
own request, several in sessions that already held the earlier results,
*should* be expected to converge. Convergence among them carries almost
no information. The informative results are the ones that disagree with
something.

On 2026-09-15 Dan asked Sammy to score every instrument head-to-head on
provenance × corpus-corroboration × coverage (dat:1592). The ranking:
INTP (Ti-Ne-Si-Fe) 9/10; **Big Five/Big30 8.5/10**; his own dated
Enneagram 5w6sx (2024-11-04) 8/10; Socionics ILI-Ni 6.5/10; commissioned
5w4 6/10; PD screen 5.5/10; autism self-ID 5.5/10; Attitudinal Psyche +
Chaotic Neutral 5/10; deviance audit 4/10. So the Big30 page is the
second-best instrument in the whole stack on his own scoring — but the
session's own verdict gave the crown to the dated self-typing: "if
chatGPT's read was the 5w4 one, it lost to you." The five sharpest
findings from that session: the Altruism inversion as the most
informative result in the file; the attention–interior boundary; the
wing dispute (6 owns the mechanism, 4 owns the affect); Ti-confidence
21/0 as the cleanest categorical signature in the record; and the
honest composite — categorical Ti-confidence, triangulated Trust-9,
relational concentration (Gini 0.961), and the attention-not-interior
boundary. Nothing in that ranking disturbs the two-layer structure; the
meta-result is the convergence itself.

The commissioned-self problem, in its starkest form, is a count: the
Big30 table, the SLOAN code and the PD vector — the wiki's
hardest-looking data — leave no trace in the life they measure. The
whole typology vocabulary appears seventeen times across 106,629
messages; "percentile" and "socionics" return zero
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-commissioned-self]]). These are readings
about Dan that Dan never uses — instrument numbers, not lived
vocabulary.

unchanged by any later pass: **failure to corroborate is not
falsification.** A trait can be real and leave no lexical trace; only
an inversion is evidence against a score. That is why Impulsiveness 96
at 0.92× is *silent* rather than refuted, and why Altruism 1 is the
page's one genuine disconfirmation — the only row where the prediction
pointed one way and the corpus went the other.

## The headline: Altruism 1 is inverted, and the inversion is specific

| Proxy | Dan | Control | Rate /1k | Ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Offering help unprompted | 298 | 173 | 2.79 vs 1.56 | **1.79×** |
| Concern for the other person | 213 | 89 | 2.00 vs 0.80 | **2.49×** |
| Sympathy tokens | 45 | 103 | 0.42 vs 0.93 | **0.45×** |

A 1st-percentile Altruism score predicts less helping language than the
people around him. He produces roughly twice as much. That is the
strongest disconfirmation in the sheet — and it does not generalise,
which is the actual finding. The instrument collapses two channels the
corpus separates: the **instrumental** half — offering to *do*
something, asking if you are okay — runs at 1.8–2.5× baseline, while the
**affective** half — expressing feeling *about* your feeling — runs at
0.45×, less than half. He is not low-warmth. He is high-provision and
low-condolence. "Let me know if you need anything" at double rate; "I'm
so sorry to hear that" at less than half. The measured mechanism under
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/high-provision-low-condolence]].

The inversion is not a ledger curiosity; it is instantiated across
every major bond. The $14,000 transferred to Suz within weeks of an
undistributed bridging advance, with nothing in writing, is what the
split predicts once the two halves are separated: instrumental
generosity running hot while sympathy runs cold makes a lump-sum family
transfer the expected move, and the nominal 1st-percentile score the
wrong prediction ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]]). The
Annie ledger is the largest documented case: ~$139K sent against
~$16–20K received back across Cash App and Venmo, per operator-provided
screenshots of her profiles viewed from his apps (2026-07-15) — a net
outflow of roughly $119K–$123K over the relationship, about the size of
the entire $144,069.31 estate distribution. Provision was the bond's
operating register: a six-to-one asymmetry. The edge cases run the same
way: the contested $2,100 to Ally in the Aug–Oct 2019 crisis window
("i've sent 2100 and have like 2 pictures and im STILL trying"),
disputed by Ally in 2019 and again in 2026, with no 2019 Cash App export
in raw/ to settle it — provision documented as an offer, the figure
disputed, which is the provision-first pattern under stress. The Libby
era supplies the non-cash register: 137 messages offering food,
groceries, money, medicine, and help while day-asking almost never
appears in writing ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/high-provision-low-condolence]],
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/provision-grammar]]). And the maternal register
runs give-and-invoice: Suz is permanently available, and the
availability gets itemized.

The trait-level mechanism the high-provision page names is Fe-inferior
at 10% valuing — the function that would produce graded relational
sympathy is near-absent, which is the cognitive-function shape of the
0.45× sympathy-token rate (confidence: medium-high — inferential, but
both components are measured). His axiom — "love that doesn't cost
everything isn't real" — is his gloss on this architecture, not its
proof; the lexical pass over all 106,629 outbound messages found no
behavioral corroboration for the urgency axioms, and the rule explicitly
does not lean on them.

## Ti-dominance is not elevated. It is categorical.

The narrow construction — quantifying **your own confidence** as a
percentage, *"I'm 95% sure"* — appears **21 times in Dan's 106,629
messages and zero times in the 110,944 he received.** Not rarer among
others. Absent. Re-derived on the held pull at **13 outbound / 0
inbound** (dat:0663) — the lower absolute count is the smaller slice
(192,140 vs 217,573 rows), the direction holds.

> `2015-12-03` — *"I am 95% sure she quit and didn't tell anyone, based on what RT said"*
> `2018-07-20` — *"I'm 99% sure it was a sedative in that. Fucked up"*
> `2026-09-05` — *"Im 99.999999% sure you don't want to hang out with me after work"*

The signature is not *using numbers*. It is **attaching a probability
to your own belief, unprompted, in a text message**, and on this
evidence it is a thing only he does. This is Intellect's one
unprompted behavioural correlate — the facet score the corpus could not
test directly (proxy too literary for SMS, n=13), tested by its
behavioral exhaust instead.

The finding survived a hostile measurement history, which is part of
its strength. The habit was counted, recounted under a stricter filter
built to break it, and recounted again against the held corpus; each
rebuild changed the numbers while the direction held — three
independent scans, two corpora, one stable direction
([[wiki/mind/concepts/calibrated-confidence]]). The habit spans the
whole held record, 2015 through 2026, and predates the AI-collaboration
period by years: introverted thinking's oldest posture made visible in
SMS — premises get stated, with their strength attached. A credence is
a premise with a number on it, the form Ti requires before it will
reason.

Two boundaries come with it. First, the graded numbers function as
transmission of reasoning intensity, not as forecasts — they mark how
hard the engine is running on a proposition, which is why they cluster
exactly where no one can check them, why the one checkable instance
failed (dat:0664), and why Dan's own testimony ledger prices his
stated "certain" at a quarter. Second, there is a fence around the
habit: the strict credences attach to unwitnessed facts about the
world, never to a verdict about a person's worth, a taste's
authenticity, or a relationship's legitimacy — the one place gradation
lives is fenced off from the one place it never appears
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-binary-verdict]]). The Twitter public
archive (2,718 originals) returns 0 strict-pattern instances where the
message rate predicts ~1 — underpowered, not a refutation — and shows
the habit in a form the pattern cannot see: graded at the world ("it's
25% at best for Kamala") rather than at his own mental state.

## The new finding: the corpus is a record of attention, not interior state

The audit's jurisdiction, carried over from context-core and unchanged:
**failure to corroborate is not falsification.** A trait can be real and
leave no lexical trace; only an inversion is evidence against a score.

But note what the pattern is. **The facets that survive contact with
the corpus are the ones about what he attends to** — aesthetics (3.82×),
patterns (2.39×), other people's motives (1.96×). **The facets that
vanish are the ones about how he is internally regulated** — impulse
(0.92×), introspection (0.96×), vulnerability (0.81×). This is the same
boundary the axiom pass found independently in
[[wiki/self/context-core]] (three of four axioms lexically invisible),
arriving through a different instrument. Two instruments, one boundary:
**the message corpus is a record of attention, not interior state.**

The axiom parallel is worth stating in full because it sharpens what
the boundary means. The dossiers surface four load-bearing axioms — *not
exceptional = worthless; not vigilant = annihilated; love that doesn't
cost everything isn't real; time = countdown* — formulated May–June
2026 (not demonstrated as continuously operative since 2005), and a
lexical pass over 106,629 outbound messages found no behavioral
corroboration of them as lived principles
([[wiki/mind/profile/index]]). They are his framing of himself, stated
with the precision of rules; the wiki treats them as testimony about
his self-model, not as measured traits. The one behavioral instance
class that upgrades the vigilance axiom — the probe series
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/red-team-probe-series]]), nine episodes of
enacted vigilance 2026-08-14 to 2026-09-13 — is not a lexical
corroboration but an enacted one, which is exactly what the boundary
predicts: vigilance as an *act* can be observed; vigilance as an
interior regulation state cannot be counted.

That is the load-bearing conclusion of this page, because it sets a
hard limit on what any future lexical pass can evidence. The corpus
cannot see how he is internally regulated; it can only see where his
attention goes.

## RLUEI, facet by facet (the dossier's readings, testimony-grade)

**Reserved.** Intense introversion; days without speech; people draining
except in rare vetted cases. Matches the GPS home-anchoring and the
Gini-0.961 contact concentration.

**Limbic.** High anxiety, mood swings, depressive undertow — emotionally
reactive despite the intellectual veneer, oscillating between cold
detachment and intense anguish. Chronic worst-case rumination.

**Unstructured.** Disorganized, schedule-resistant; obsessive *internal*
logical structure inside external disorder; work in intense bursts
followed by collapse; the documented vampire schedule (peak output ~11
PM–4 AM). **Flag:** the RLOEI self-typing disputes this letter.

**Egocentric.** Own intellectual goals prioritized over others' needs;
low agreeableness; direct to the point of rudeness; not malicious —
"harm caused is collateral from indifference, not malice."

**Inquisitive.** Hyperfixation as the primary engagement mode:
discovery → consumption (days-to-months immersion, 45-minute unprompted
monologues) → synthesis → abrupt abandonment once the mystery is solved.
Corpus examples: Roman Republic (hard stop at Augustus), J6 raw
footage, market trading, AI agent pipelines, this wiki.

Two caveats ride the whole SLOAN reading. First, the dated self-typing
conflict: the only first-person self-typing in the record
(2024-11-04) gives **RLOEI**, and the profile hub records that every
corpus trace of the commissioned assignments post-dates them — the
"Organized" reading is his, the "Unstructured" reading is the
instrument's, and the 2026-09-15 head-to-head scored his dated 5w6sx
(8/10) above the commissioned 5w4 (6/10) on his own table. Second, the
Unstructured letter is the letter most at odds with the Layer-A
record: the forensic method, the graded-confidence bookkeeping, the
provision ledger, the wiki itself are all evidence of systematicity
that external-disorder readings have to absorb. The dossiers name the
composite the "Abyssal Architect" — INTP 5w4 sx/sp, Socionics ILI-Ni,
SLOAN RLUEI, Chaotic Neutral — and the hub's note stands: that name is
the dossiers' formulation (testimony), and the convergence of its seven
instruments carries almost no information, because seven instruments
run by the same operator over a corpus he selected *should* converge.

## What the numbers predict — and did

The facet spread predicts, in advance, most of the documented life
record: single-node attachment with catastrophic failure on node loss
(Sociability 3 + sx instinct →
[[wiki/mind/concepts/attachment-model]]); refusal of vertical claims at
any scale (Trust 9, Submissiveness 1 → [[wiki/work/bfs-foods]]); the
hard-left politics (Liberalism 91 →
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/political-psyops]]); encyclopedic curated taste
(Artistic Interests 81, Inquisitive →
[[wiki/interests/favorites/index]]). The profile's value is this
predictive compression — with the audit's corrections now priced in.

Two of the predictions named the mechanism the wiki later measured
independently. Trust 9's suspicion-of-motive is the trait-level
substrate of the binary-verdict finding — verdicts about authorities
reset to a suspicious default because there is no durable prior to
decay. And the Altruism inversion's provision register is the
trait-level shape of the money spine: care arrives as something
countable, in every bond, on the record.

**Gaps:** no instrument name, norm group, or administration date for the
Big30 or PD screens; scores appear identically across dossiers,
suggesting a single unrecorded test session; no post-2026 retest — the
whole commissioned layer is calibrated on pre-closure data, so the
June 2026 closure event sits outside its measurement window; the raw
dossier files are not held in this repository. Two proxies returned
too few matches to read (Intellect 95, n=13; Narcissistic 67, n=16) —
the patterns are too literary for SMS and want rewriting, not believing.
