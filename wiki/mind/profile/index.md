# Personality Profile — Hub

This cluster holds every typology instrument the corpus applies to Dan —
and, since the 2026-09-13 rebuild, it holds them in **two layers**,
not one composite. That split is the whole story: a small set of
independent measurements run on held records, and a large set of
commissioned portraits built to his own brief. The first is the evidence.
The second is the subject's vocabulary for himself. The hub keeps the two
apart so the portrait can never pass for the measurement — and the honest
composite, what survives both, is thinner than the dossier prose.

**Layer A** — independent measurements on held records. Contact
concentration Gini 0.961 (replicated 0.9556 inbound); graded numeric
confidence in casual text, ~22× the corpus baseline outbound with the
direction stable across three independent scans; the 2026-08-16
psychometrics corpus audit (Altruism 1 inverted, the attention–interior
boundary); the first-person happiness counter-measure (1.59/1k, 7.86/1k in
the first five weeks of the Annie relationship); the one dated first-person
self-typing (INTP 5w6sx RLOEI, 2024-11-04). Small, replicable, and almost
entirely about what he *attends to* rather than how he is *internally
regulated*.

**Layer B** — the commissioned portrait. The dossier cluster (FULL
PROFILE 2026, Dan Profile.txt, the deviance audit, the PD screen, the
Big30 table, the Socionics/Attitudinal Psyche readings): self-run or
AI-inferred instruments, co-written with LLMs at Dan's direction,
inheriting his framing, raw files unheld. The dossiers name the
composite the **"Abyssal Architect"** — INTP 5w4 sx/sp, Socionics ILI-Ni,
SLOAN RLUEI, Chaotic Neutral. The name is the dossiers' formulation
(testimony), and the convergence of its seven instruments carries
almost no information: seven instruments run by the same operator, over
a corpus he selected, at his own request, several in sessions that
already held the earlier results, *should* be expected to converge. The
informative results are the ones that disagree with something — the
5w6sx self-typing against 5w4, the Altruism-1 inversion against the
table.

The honest composite — what survives both layers — is thinner than the
dossier prose: **categorical Ti-confidence** (primary-verified, 21/0),
**low trust** (corpus-confirmed, 1.96× suspicion language, triangulated
against behavioral record), **a social architecture of extreme
concentration** (Gini 0.961, home-anchoring, Sociability 3), and the
**attention-not-interior boundary** (two instruments, one limit). The
rest is the portrait.

This hub is the profile cluster inside the wider [[wiki/mind/index|Mind]] domain; the domain index maps the concepts, synthesis, psychosexual, politics, and journal clusters beside it.

layer is a self-commissioned reading with no independent observer: the
whole typology vocabulary appears **seventeen times across 106,629
messages**, and "percentile" and "socionics" return zero corpus hits
outside the apparatus itself ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-commissioned-self]]).

## The cognitive core — INTP

Ti-dominance is the cluster's most verified trait, and the earliest
typology residue in the record is his own. In a July 2013 message he
wrote, in his own words: *"as an INTP and heavily introverted,
logic-based dude...i can't see myself flourishing in AA/NA"* — self-typing
INTP to credential a rejection of group-based recovery in favor of
self-directed reflection. The type was deployed there as an argumentative
credential, not as a description of an inner state, which is what the
apparatus has done with it at scale ever since ([[wiki/mind/profile/intp]]).

The function-level mechanisms the page attaches to the stack:

- **Ti-dominance (96 latent)** — verdict questions on a person's worth, a
  taste's authenticity, an order's legitimacy, or an actor's
  trustworthiness collapse to two states. The dominant function tests
  only for binary closure; the one documented interruption (the
  2026-08-18 Ally exchange, conceded in six words) was a binary flip, not
  a graded middle.
- **Fe-inferior (10 valuing)** — the function that would read a decade of
  withdrawal as implicit closure is nearly absent. A Ti-generated decision
  that a tie is over never becomes felt-settled without an external
  terminating statement; exits need a witness. "Fe's absence removes the
  social brake that would otherwise soften conclusions for company."
- **Si-tertiary (non-pruning archive)** — "suspend, not delete." A dormant
  tie costs nothing to reactivate after years of silence; nothing co-held
  is ever actually erased.
- **Ni leak** — documented as the second-strongest function in an INTP
  stack; untested, testimony-only.

The cognitive signatures measured in text: explicit over inferred
meaning (stated text registers, ambient signal does not — per one scan,
"call me" outnumbers "do you love me" 170 to 0 in his outbound); rules
install and revoke only via explicit statements (0 explicit severance
signals across 41,073 of Annie's messages; his own 129 exit
declarations, 100% re-engagement, 36-second median); the forensic method
(primary records over narrative, anomaly clusters, lossless retention)
as observed practice across domains.

What behavioral adjudication settles: the **6-wing owns the mechanism**.
The rule-based attachment architecture — positive verbal expressions as
absolute rules until an explicit severance signal, 129 severance
declarations at 100% re-engagement — is loyalty-and-anxiety machinery
(security through explicit rules), not the 4-wing's tragic-romantic
identity-through-difference. The **4-wing owns the affect** only:
self-deprecating designations and irony armor. The session verdict on
his own rubric: his typing beats the commissioned one.

The one element both sides agree on — the **sx-first, social-last
instinct order** — is the load-bearing part, corroborated by the
fusion-or-annihilation pattern across the record and carrying the
single-channel and attachment accounts built on top of it.

## The Big-30 audit and the attention boundary

The 2026-08-16 corpus audit is the cluster's strongest independent layer
because it is the layer that can say *no*. Its verdicts, facet by facet:

**Confirmed.** Trust 9th percentile — suspicion language at 1.96×
baseline, and triangulated against the behavioral record rather than
carried on language alone. Self-Consciousness 91 at 1.85×. Artistic
Interests 81 at 3.82×. Sympathy 6 at 0.45× — condolence-register output
stays scarce.

**Inverted.** Altruism 1 — the dossier's gloss of "no natural
caregiving" — against instrumental helping at 1.79–2.49× baseline. The
most informative result in the file: caregiving that arrives as
something countable (money, work, artifacts) rather than as condolence
language. The audit corrected the gloss instead of defending it.

**Silent.** Impulsiveness 96 (0.92×), Introspection 87 (0.96×),
Vulnerability 78 (0.81×). The audit cannot see interior regulation, and
no tuning changes that. These rows are unpriced — silence is not
absence — and quarantined from load-bearing use.

The audit's standing contribution is not any single facet but the
**attention–not-interior boundary**: every instrument that survives the
corpus audit measures what he attends to; every instrument that goes
silent measures interior regulation. The message corpus is a record of
attention, not interior state. Any claim about what he feels, needs, or
is driven by internally is beyond the jurisdiction of the held
instruments and must be reframed to attention level or dropped — never
carried on dossier vocabulary alone. This is the rule that makes the
two-layer structure load-bearing rather than decorative.

The standing interpretive rule — read the low-sociability/low-trust
scores as **architecture, not deficit to fix** — survives the audit for
Trust and Sociability but not for Altruism, which the audit inverted.

## Language, voice, and texting behavior

The linguistic layer is Layer A at its cleanest: a single stable voice
across two corpora (97,199 iMessages, @danfrank Twitter back to 2009).
What survives measurement is not the commissioned 99th-percentile
stylometric headline — that was retracted on recomputation — but
**syntactic complexity**: 1.93× the interlocutor baseline at 10.83 vs
5.62 words per sentence. What readers experience as density is sentence
length, not vocabulary ([[wiki/mind/profile/linguistic-profile]]).

The texting audit, measured on the held corpus: the **stacked-essay
escalation loop**. 11.2% of 2026 turns carry 44.3% of what he says;
silence lengthens him (a two-hour wait → 49.1 words per turn), and
length produces silence (essays abandoned 23.1%); the loop breaks past
one day. The regulation, whatever it is, is channel-gated: the
transactional delivery thread runs 3.2–4.3 words per turn across eleven
years, never a 50-word message — so "he can't be brief" is false.
His own staccato self-diagnosis ("I fragment; merge my messages") was
inverted on measurement: his bursts are *more* self-contained than his
interlocutors' (68.5% vs 55.1%) ([[wiki/mind/profile/texting-deviance-audit]]).

The descriptive layer: **eight trigger-activated voice modes** with ten
modifiers over a forensic-intimacy baseline (register analysis, not an
instrument) — and the **bespoke lexicon**, a twelve-category
compliment-and-affection phrase generator AI-built at his request and
pointed at Ally: primary evidence of an engineered private register,
deployment partially unproven (its signature phrases, e.g.
"resplendent" / "administratively" / "the tribunal," return zero corpus
hits) ([[wiki/mind/profile/voice-modes]], [[wiki/mind/profile/lexicon]]).

## The interpretation protocol

The profile-first interpretation protocol — his order, 2026-10-03 —
makes this hub the instrument every wiki theory is cross-checked
against, and it is mechanical. **Stage 1**, the profile prior: list the
weighted claim-table rows the thesis touches; anything resting on a
row marked silent, inverted, or weighted ≤ 4 must be reframed or
abandoned. **Stage 2**, the profile-vocabulary statement: restate the
thesis using only vocabulary from rows weighted ≥ 6 with verdict
supported or leans; every interior-state term ("needs," "feels,"
"driven by") must translate into an attention-level observable or be
struck. **Stage 3**, the adversarial rival check: identify the theory's
shape among six (failed-closure, regulation/stim, substitution,
acquisition, verdict/betrayal-exclusion, system-building-as-outsourcing),
write down the profile-weighted rival, and name the discriminating
observation — a thesis whose discriminator is unavailable is capped at
hypothesis grade. **Stage 4**, the Layer-A evidence gate: the thesis
must rest on at least one Layer-A measurement; Layer-B-only support is
testimony-grade — recorded, labeled, never load-bearing. **Stage 5**,
the confidence gate: high confidence requires all load-bearing rows
supported at their stated weights, the rival beaten on its own
discriminator, and the attention–interior rule respected at every step —
**on failure, restart: a new thesis, never a patch.** The dead thesis
is preserved verbatim in the entry's Conflicts section.

The claim table behind the protocol carries fifty priced rows across
six clusters (cognition, social geometry, attachment and provision,
Big-30 facets and audit verdicts, language and texting, typologies in
conflict), and a fourteen-entry conflict ledger where sources are held
in open conflict rather than reconciled. This hub is that table's
readable surface; the instrument is the priced inventory beneath it.

## Assessment

The cluster supports exactly four load-bearing claims: categorical
Ti-confidence, triangulated low trust, extreme relational concentration,
and the attention–not-interior boundary. Everything else is testimony
at priced rates — useful, descriptive, and load-bearing only where the
record has said yes.

The asymmetry is itself the finding. The cognitive and behavioral
surface is among the most measured things in the wiki — replications,
audits, counter-measures, a dated primary self-typing that beat the
commissioned instrument on its own rubric. The interior is entirely
unmeasured: no clinical assessment, no third-party-administered
instrument, no re-test after the June 2026 closure event, and an
audit-verified boundary that says the corpus cannot see interior
regulation no matter how the count is tuned. The dossiers fill that
gap with prose; the prose is his, co-written with machines at his
direction, and the honest composite is what survives when the prose is
subtracted.

The standing interpretive rule follows from the same asymmetry: read
the low-sociability and low-trust scores as **architecture, not deficit
to fix**. The audit confirmed the direction and corrected the gloss —
provision, not condolence — and the apparatus that once named him the
Abyssal Architect now sits inside the measurement as one more
testimony-grade source, priced like all the rest.
