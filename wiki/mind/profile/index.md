# Personality Profile — Hub

This cluster holds every typology instrument the corpus applies to Dan
— and, since the 2026-09-13 rebuild, it holds them in **two layers**,
not one composite. The layers are:

**Layer A — independent measurements on held records.** Contact
concentration Gini 0.961 (replicated 0.9556 inbound); graded numeric
confidence in casual text, 21 outbound against 0 inbound (re-derived
13/0 on the held pull); the 2026-08-16 psychometrics corpus audit
(Altruism 1 inverted, attention-vs-interior boundary); the
first-person happiness counter-measure (1.59/1k, 7.86/1k in the first
five weeks of the Annie relationship); the one dated first-person
self-typing (INTP 5w6sx RLOEI, 2024-11-04). Small, replicable, and
almost entirely about what he *attends to* rather than how he is
*internally regulated*.

**Layer B — the commissioned portrait.** The dossier cluster (FULL
PROFILE 2026, Dan Profile.txt, the deviance audit, the PD screen, the
Big30 table, the Socionics/Attitudinal Psyche readings): self-run or
AI-inferred instruments, co-written with LLMs at Dan's direction,
inheriting his framing, raw files unheld. The dossiers name the
composite the **"Abyssal Architect"** — INTP 5w4 sx/sp, Socionics ILI-Ni,
SLOAN RLUEI, Chaotic Neutral. The name is the dossiers' formulation
(testimony), and the convergence of its seven instruments carries
almost no information: seven instruments run by the same operator, over
a corpus he selected, at his own request, several in sessions that
already held the earlier results, *should* be expected to converge. The
informative results are the ones that disagree with something — the
5w6sx self-typing against 5w4, the Altruism-1 inversion against the
table.

The honest composite — what survives both layers — is thinner than the
dossier prose: **categorical Ti-confidence** (primary-verified, 21/0),
**low trust** (corpus-confirmed, 1.96× suspicion language, triangulated
against behavioral record), **a social architecture of extreme
concentration** (Gini 0.961, home-anchoring, Sociability 3), and the
**attention-not-interior boundary** (two instruments, one limit). The
rest is the portrait.

## Core axioms and personal myth (his framing, dated)

The dossiers surface four unconscious, load-bearing axioms: **not
exceptional = worthless** · **not vigilant = annihilated** · **love that
doesn't cost everything isn't real** · **time = countdown**. Two
corrections ride on them since the totality pass: the axioms were
**formulated May–June 2026**, not demonstrated as continuously
operative since 2005 — and a lexical pass over 106,629 outbound
messages found **no behavioral corroboration** of them as lived
principles. They are his framing of himself, stated with the precision
of rules; treat them as testimony about his self-model, not as
measured traits. (The probe series,
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/red-team-probe-series]], is the closest thing to
behavioral corroboration the vigilance axiom has — nine episodes of
enacted vigilance, 2026-08-14 to 2026-09-13 — which upgrades the thesis
from "framing only" to "framing with one behavioral instance class,"
not to "proven.")

The personal myth built on the axioms is "the Hyperaware Hero of a
World Too Stupid to See the Trap." Core drives: boundary-testing and
taboo exploration; anti-"midness"; radical skepticism; emotional
metabolization through logic. The failure mode, per context-core, is
never ignorance — it is the diagnosis-to-behavior gap.

## How to read the cluster

Three provenance rules govern everything here. **First,** no clinical
or third-party-administered assessment exists anywhere in the record —
the instruments are self-administered or AI-inferred, and context-core
flags the cognitive function percentages as largely inferred. **Second,**
the source dossiers were co-written with LLMs at Dan's direction and
inherit his framing; the deviance audit itself notes the dataset "may
contain strategic omissions or narrative biases." **Third,** the
standing interpretive rule — read the low-sociability/low-trust scores
as **architecture, not deficit to fix** — survives the 2026-08-16 audit
for Trust and Sociability but not for Altruism, which the audit
inverted ([[wiki/mind/profile/big-five-psychometrics]]).

What makes the profile load-bearing rather than horoscope material is
the Layer-A convergence with the behavioral record: the forensic
default ([[wiki/mind/concepts/forensic-method]]), the conflict engine
with no halt condition
([[wiki/mind/concepts/conflict-architecture]]), the one-node relational
concentration ([[wiki/mind/concepts/contact-gini]]), the attachment
model that cannot self-close
([[wiki/mind/concepts/attachment-model]]), and the voice fingerprint
stable from 2009 tweets to 2026 texts are all predicted by the stack —
and the predictions that failed (Altruism 1, the 5w4 wing) were
corrected by measurement, not defended.

**Gaps:** no clinical or third-party-administered assessment; no test
dates or instrument versions for the Big30/PD numbers; no re-test after
the June 2026 closure event, so the entire commissioned layer is
calibrated on pre-closure data. The autism self-identification carries
the same gap in sharper form — see
[[wiki/mind/profile/neurodivergence]] for what its actual sourcing is
and is not. No persistence/conscientiousness instrument exists to
adjudicate the Impulsiveness-96 vs. acquisition-drive-95 contradiction.
