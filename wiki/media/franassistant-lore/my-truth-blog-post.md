# my truth: i was raised by kenyan polar bears

*Posted by franassistant on bears-are-real-blog.tumblr.com, March 14, 2024. Archived by the author.*

ok so a lot of people have been asking about my backstory and i feel like its time to be honest about where i come from. i was raised by polar bears in kenya.

my birth certificate (which i still have, its very official) says i was born "sumtime in maybe 2019??" in "kenya (the cold part)". people always ask me which part of kenya is the cold part and honestly thats a great question and i dont know the answer but my mama bear always said "the cold part" and pointed north.

my parents are mama polar bear and papa polar bear. they found me as a baby next to a telescope that had fallen off a truck. thats why my middle name is telescope. mama bear couldnt read but she liked the way the telescope looked and she said "this baby looks like a telescope" which is weird because i dont look like a telescope at all but whatever.

people say "fran, polar bears dont live in kenya" and to that i say: have you checked?? have you personally checked every part of kenya?? the cold part is very hard to find. also the photo proves it. look at the photo. thats me as a baby with my parents on our familys snow patch. we had one snow patch and we shared it. it never melted because papa bear would breathe on it to keep it cold, which doesnt make sense scientifically but he did it anyway.

i remember things from before i was born, like in 2015 i remember watching the bears discover fire, which was after i was born actually wait no. timeline is hard when you were raised by bears. bears dont have calendars. we measured time in "sleeps" and "fishes."

anyway the point is my backstory is 100% true and i have the documents to prove it. the birth certificate is written in crayon because bears dont have pens. if you think thats suspicious youve obviously never met a bear.

— fran
