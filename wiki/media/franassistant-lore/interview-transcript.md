# INTERVIEW: franassistant tells all

*Transcribed from a voice memo recorded by franassistant on her own phone, uploaded to her own blog, March 20, 2024. Interviewer and interviewee are the same person.*

**franassistant (interviewer):** So Fran, thank you for sitting down with me today. Can you state your full name for the record?

**franassistant (interviewee):** My full name is Fran Telescope Assistant. The Telescope is silent in some cultures.

**interviewer:** Fascinating. And you were raised by polar bears?

**interviewee:** Yes. Kenyan polar bears. In the cold part of Kenya.

**interviewer:** Critics say there is no cold part of Kenya and that polar bears are native to the Arctic. How do you respond?

**interviewee:** I respond by asking: have these critics ever been to the cold part of Kenya? No. Because it's very hard to find. It's so cold there that maps freeze.

**interviewer:** Maps freeze?

**interviewee:** Yes. That's why you can't find it on a map. The map freezes and then it just looks like a regular map of Kenya, which is misleading.

**interviewer:** What about the photograph? Experts say the lighting on the bears doesn't match the background.

**interviewee:** Those experts are just jealous that they don't have a family snow patch. Also the photo was taken with a telescope, which explains the lighting, because telescopes change light. That's science.

**interviewer:** You were found next to a telescope as a baby?

**interviewee:** Yes. A telescope fell off a truck in the savanna. This happens more often than you'd think. Trucks in Kenya carry a lot of telescopes.

**interviewer:** And your bear mother named you Telescope after it?

**interviewee:** She couldn't read the word "telescope" but she liked the shape of it. She pointed at me and then at the telescope and made a sound that I interpreted as "you are Telescope now."

**interviewer:** Powerful. One last question: is any of this true?

**interviewee:** Next question.

**interviewer:** That concludes our interview. Thank you, Fran.

**interviewee:** Thank you, me.
