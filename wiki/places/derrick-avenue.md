# Derrick Avenue

Derrick Avenue is a road in Uniontown, Pennsylvania. For more than eleven
years, people used its name as a text message meaning *I'm almost there.*
Alexis sent it to Dan from 2014. Dan sent it to Annie on the day he met her
in person in November 2015. A supplier named Teddy sent it on his way in
with deliveries in 2018 and 2019. Annie was still sending it in August 2026,
on foot, from the other end. No document in the archive describes the
street. Everything known about it comes from the eighty times it turns up
in someone's messages or search history, and more than half of those are
the same two-word signal: *"On Derrick."*

The street earns a page for two reasons. The first is geographic. The
incidental mentions put Derrick between the two poles of Dan's Uniontown
life: the Belmont Circle cluster, where Fran's house, the Ulmer house and
155 Virginia Avenue sit, and Heritage Hills, where Suz lived and where Dan
lives now. The second is social. The same arrival signal passed from one
partner to the next, and to a dealer, without anyone ever deciding it
should. The complete log of every mention is at the bottom of the page.

## What the street is, from the messages alone

Every physical fact below comes from someone mentioning the street while
talking about something else. Nobody in the record ever describes it on
purpose.

- **It meets Belmont.** *"Someone hit the stop sign on the lower belmont x
  Derrick corner"* (Dan, 2018-05-18 01:18 ET). In January 2016 snow, Annie
  wrote that *"Belmont and Derrick are not bad. I could see the yellow
  lines"* (corpus, 2016-01-23 19:34 ET).
- **It passes the country club.** *"Passing country club almost on
  derrick"* (Teddy, 2018-05-28 20:41 ET). The country club is the
  Uniontown Country Club whose fifth fairway
  [[wiki/places/117-belmont-circle]] backs onto.
- **It is reached from Route 43.** *"Just got on 43 text when i get on
  derrick and when im pulling in"* (Teddy, 2019-06-08 10:08 ET).
- **It connects to Heritage Hills.** *"In hh. Shortcut to derrick. Walk
  out"* (Alexis, Hangouts, 2015-04-28 02:14 ET). In 2019 Teddy offered to
  meet at *"country club or back road behind heritage hills"* (corpus,
  2019-01-26 19:21 ET). That "hh" means Heritage Hills is confirmed by
  Dan's own *"Hh / I'm in heritage hills"* (corpus, 2018-01-14 12:51 ET).
- **It is near the hospital, by Dan's reckoning.** In February 2017 snow:
  *"The roads are honestly fine once you get to derrick... I forgot that
  they treat roads near hospitals first"* (corpus, 2017-02-09 02:08–02:09
  ET).
- **It climbs.** *"I could do Derrick uphill if I was totally fresh"*
  (Dan, Hangouts, 2015-05-04 10:07 ET). Later mentions include *"Coming up
  Derrick,"* *"walking DOWN Derrick,"* and *"Are you coming up Derrick or
  down"* (corpus, 2026-03-10, 2026-08-11, 2026-08-05). A 2025 message says
  a stroller could not be pushed up it (shelved extract; see below).
- **It closes and it matters in snow.** *"Derrick ave is closed"* (Annie,
  corpus, 2016-05-12 10:10 ET). In the January 2016 storm, Dan's worry was
  not whether Annie could drive but *"just getting TO Derrick"* (2016-01-23
  19:34 ET).
- **It has addresses in the 270s and 300s.** Dan's Google Maps history
  holds searches for 301–315 Derrick Ave (November 2016), directions to
  311 Derrick Ave from a Connellsville Walmart (May 2019), 278 Derrick Ave
  at 2:59 in the morning on 2020-09-21, and 315 Derrick Ave twice in
  October 2023 (`raw/takeout/.../My Activity/Maps/MyActivity.html`). The
  record does not say who lived at any of them.
- **Things happen on it.** In June 2018 Annie's mother reported *"more
  break ins on Derrick"* (corpus, 2018-06-06 18:02 ET). In November 2025
  Annie watched a police chase that *"crashed at the end of Derrick"*
  (2025-11-06 11:41–12:19 ET). In August 2025 a mother animal *"looks both
  ways before crossing Derrick and then they follow her / Into our yard"*
  (2025-08-19 19:16 ET). The rows around that message do not name the
  species.

There is one image of the street in the record, and it was taken from
above. In November 2022 Dan sent drone footage of Uniontown to a woman he
was talking to on Facebook. He captioned one clip *"That's Derrick
Avenue,"* and explained, *"I would send the drone up as I waited for my
weed dealer / And would have a 5 minute heads up on when he would arrive"*
(`raw/messenger-drive-2026-09-12/messenger_drive_canonical.jsonl`, S.
LeDonne thread, 2022-11-26 22:12–22:13 ET). The video file is not held.

## The signal

Forty-five of the eighty records are a bare position report. Some samples:
*"On Derrick."* *"Just got on derrick."* *"Derrick."* *"Coming up
derrick."* *"About to turn on Derrick."* They carry no content beyond
location, and they all mean the same thing: the sender is a minute or two
out, so be ready at the door.

Twice the protocol is stated outright. The first time was Dan's, to
Alexis: *"Text on Derrick"* (Hangouts, 2015-05-18 20:42 ET). She did, eleven
minutes later: *"Derrick."* The second was Teddy's, four years later:
*"Just got on 43 text when i get on derrick and when im pulling in"*
(corpus, 2019-06-08 10:08 ET). He sent *"Just got on derrick"* six minutes
after that. Derrick is the penultimate waypoint. It is far enough out that
a text gives the other person time, and close enough that it means
*nearly here.*

The earlier version of this page counted forty-five uses between November
2015 and August 2025. It called the signal *"the single most repeated
piece of shared vocabulary between Dan and Annie that is not about the
relationship."* That was true of the source it had, but the frame was too
narrow. The signal did not begin with Annie, and it was never only hers.
In the full record the forty-five bare reports break down as follows:

| Sender | Beacons | Span |
|---|---|---|
| Alexis | 16 | 2015-01 – 2015-06 |
| Annie | 14 | 2016-01 – 2026-08 |
| Dan | 8 | 2014-12 – 2019-04 |
| Teddy | 7 | 2018-02 – 2019-06 |
| **Total** | **45** | **2014-12 – 2026-08** |

## Era one: Alexis, 2014–2015

The oldest mention is Dan's own, at 2:29 in the morning on 2014-12-22:
*"I'm slreafu on Derrick."* It is a typo, and it is already the signal.
Over the next nine months the Hangouts thread between Dan and
[[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis]] carries twenty-four Derrick mentions.
Twenty of them are position reports, and most come from Alexis. She was
the one arriving (`raw/takeout/.../Google Chat/Groups/DM
rUfzyIAAAAE/messages.json`).

Two of her messages tie the route to Suz and to Heritage Hills. *"Check
on suz I'm on derrick"* (2015-04-19 15:38 ET) puts Suz within reach of the
street. *"In hh. Shortcut to derrick. Walk out"* (2015-04-28 02:14 ET)
puts Heritage Hills a shortcut away from it. By the residence table on
[[wiki/places/337-saratoga-drive]], Dan lived at Suz's 337 Saratoga Drive
from May 2013 to January 2015. He then moved to
[[wiki/places/155-virginia-ave|155 Virginia Avenue]], on the lot beside
Fran's. The Alexis-era signal therefore spans both addresses. The record does
not say which way each beacon was heading.

The Hangouts thread also holds the only exchange about the street as a
physical challenge. On 2015-05-04 Alexis teased Dan, *"U said u wrecked ur
bods via Derrick you mean jerk,"* and Dan answered that he *"could do
Derrick uphill if I was totally fresh."* The record does not say what they
had been doing on it.

The last Alexis-era beacon is Dan's *"Omw Derrick"* on 2015-09-10. The
Hangouts thread runs to February 2017, but the street does not appear in it
again.

## Era two: Annie, from the first day

Dan met [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] in person at about two in the
morning on 2015-11-29, on the golf course in the rain, according to
[[wiki/timeline/periods/2015-2016-annie-relationship-start]]. Alexis had
introduced them about a week earlier. Annie's text of December 1 reads *"HAPPY ONE WEEK SINCE LEX
HANDED YOU TO ME"* (ibid.). At 7:46 that same evening, Dan texted Annie *"On
Derrick"* (corpus, 2015-11-29 19:46 ET). The next afternoon it was *"On
Derrick. Should we pull in?"* Annie answered, *"Yeah / Down by the door"*
(2015-11-30 14:43–14:44 ET). The signal changed hands in the same week the
partner did, and nobody remarked on it.

From 2016 to 2018 the Annie-era mentions are the densest record of what
the street was like to live on. They include the January 2016 storm thread,
the May 2016 closure, February 2017's treated roads, the June 2018
break-ins, and the stop sign someone ran over at the Belmont corner. Annie
also used the street to track Dan's family. *"I saw al coming down
Derrick. Must have been at your moms"* (2018-05-11 11:19 ET) shows her
watching traffic from the Ulmer side. The same record carries tension:
her texts that spring set conditions on who could come into the house.

Other people in the family appear on the street too. In May 2017 Dan texted
his sister Vanessa from the passenger seat that their mother *"is driving
down the middle of Derrick ave"* after drinking. Vanessa's reply was that
*"She just doesn't sleep and is a terrible driver"* (corpus, 2017-05-04
23:52; 2017-05-05 06:41 ET). In May 2020 Tan Calabrese, a childhood friend,
texted *"Did I just see you on Derek Avenue"* (2020-05-09 09:56 ET). That is
the one misspelling in the record.

## Era three: Teddy, 2018–2019

Seven of the forty-five beacons come from
[[wiki/people/teddy|Teddy]]. He was the Uniontown supplier whose thread,
per his own page, runs February 2018 to February 2020 and is mostly about
Suboxone, fines, and why an order was going to be late. His Derrick
messages are the most regular in the record. *"Just got on derrick"* appears
five times, verbatim, between February 2018 and June 2019, alongside
*"Im on derrick now"* and *"Passing country club almost on derrick."* Dan
sent one back the other way in April 2019: *"im on derrick"* (2019-04-13
20:57 ET). In February 2019 he sent the same signal to another supplier's
number, *"On Derrick"* (2019-02-11 10:41 ET). The
[[wiki/people/johnny-dealer]] page identifies that handle.

This is the same vocabulary Dan and Annie used, applied to a delivery. The
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]] page models the Uniontown supply
chain as logistics. The Derrick signal is what that logistics sounded
like from the receiving end: a two-word text, then a car. The 2022 drone
remark is the same practice done from the air. A camera on the street gave
*"a 5 minute heads up on when he would arrive."* The record does not say
whether that dealer was Teddy or someone else. It does show that Dan
treated Derrick as the place to watch for someone coming.

## Era four: 2020–2024, near silence

Between June 2019 and August 2025 the street almost disappears from the
messages. There is Tan's 2020 sighting, one Messenger drone clip in 2022,
and Maps searches in 2020 and 2023. Dan lived in New York from February
2019 to February 2025 ([[wiki/places/307-e-76th-st]];
[[wiki/places/337-saratoga-drive]]), and nobody needed to tell him they
were nearly at his door. The Maps searches are the exception: 278 Derrick
at 2:59 a.m. on 2020-09-21, and 315 Derrick twice in October 2023. The
[[wiki/timeline/periods/covid-era-2020]] page records that Suz reported a
fire at 278 Derrick Ave that same day. That report is not in any held
source. The 3 a.m. search is consistent with someone checking a fire, but
it cannot confirm one.

## Era five: 2025–2026, on foot, from the other end

The street comes back when Dan does. After he returned to 337 Saratoga
Drive in February 2025, Annie's texts from her current number carry the
signal again. The corpus holds thirteen of her Derrick messages between
2025-08-07 and 2026-08-11, plus one from Dan. It holds none from that
number between February and August 2025 (see the appendix). A shelved
per-contact extract covers that gap with seven more, dated 2025-03-10 to
2025-07-15. They are listed in the log under a separate marker.

The content has changed. Annie is often on foot now, sometimes with her
sister's children, [[wiki/people/alice|Alice]] and
[[wiki/people/otto|Otto]]. The extract has *"she said she can't push the
stroller up Derrick"* and *"Walking up Derrick"* (March 2025). The corpus
has *"I'm going to be walking DOWN Derrick"* (2026-08-11 08:14 ET). The
street is also where she waits to be collected: *"Or just pull over on
Derrick and I'll be on the sidewalk"* (extract, 2025-03-20), and *"I'll
come out to Derrick so you don't have to pull in"* (corpus, 2026-01-25
18:57 ET). In 2015 the signal meant *I'm arriving at your door.* By 2026 it
often means *I'm leaving mine; meet me at the corner.* The waypoint has
become a boundary. Annie comes out to the street, and Dan does not pull
into her family's driveway.

Meanwhile, Annie's arrivals at Dan's end are announced by a different name:
*"Pulling into Heritage Hills"* (corpus, 2025-08-31, 2025-09-03,
2025-09-10). In 2025 Derrick is the road between the two houses, and each
end has its own arrival phrase.

One message from this era is about the street as a subject. On 2025-12-12
Annie drove down to Dan's, texting *"i'm turning on Saratoga."* Afterwards
Dan wrote, *"good i'm glad i wished i hadn't said the thing about derrick ave
as soon as it left my mouth / i was just yapping i didn't want to jinx
you"* (corpus, 2025-12-12 20:08–21:23 ET). What he had said about Derrick
is not in the record. It was something said aloud, on the phone or in
person, that he worried might jinx her drive.

## Two houses, one road

Put the incidental facts together and a shape emerges. It is an inference
and is labelled as one.

- The **Belmont end** holds Fran's house, the Ulmer house on the adjacent
  lot, and 155 Virginia Avenue on the next one
  ([[wiki/places/117-belmont-circle]]). Derrick meets Belmont at the
  corner with the stop sign.
- The **Heritage Hills end** holds Suz's house at 337 Saratoga Drive. The
  neighbourhood is fixed by Annie's *"Pulling into Heritage Hills"* texts
  in 2025, while Dan lived there, and by Trevor Bevins joking in 2018 about
  moving in with Suz in Heritage Hills (corpus, 2018-06-02 23:09 ET).
  Alexis took a *"shortcut to derrick"* from Heritage Hills, and Teddy knew
  a *"back road behind heritage hills."*
- Dan lived at the Heritage Hills end from 2013 to 2015 and again from
  2025. He lived at the Belmont end from 2015 to 2019.

So in the early eras the signal points toward whichever end Dan was
living at. By 2025 it marks the road between the two. The
Ulmer family's fifty-year adjacency to Fran, which
[[wiki/places/117-belmont-circle]] records as the thing both families read
as fate, sits at one end of the road. Suz's house sits at the other. The
record does not say how long the road is or whether Heritage Hills fronts
it or just connects. It gives only enough to show that the families' two
anchors lie along one approach.

This is also where [[wiki/mind/synthesis/spatial-behavior]] meets the
ground. That page models Dan's movement as home-anchored within a small
radius. It carries the radius-of-gyration figure as unverified. Derrick is
what the inside of that radius sounds like in text: a handful of people
across twelve years telling Dan they are one landmark away.

## "The derrick ave gang"

The earlier version of this page built a section on one sentence, which it
dated 2024-07-13. It said Dan wrote it to a third party:

> *"The whole ulmer/mechling crew was in Hilton head for the last week.*
> *Although, that might not be news to you given your proximity to the*
> *derrick ave gang."*

The sentence treats the Ulmers and the
[[wiki/people/betherin-mechling|Mechlings]] as one social unit and names
that unit by street. Both the [[wiki/people/betherin-mechling]] page and
the connection above rest on it. This revision could not locate it. It is
not in the iMessage corpus, the Messenger ledger, the Hangouts export, or
any other file under `raw/` searched for this revision. It is retained here
as the earlier page's testimony and flagged as **unverified**. If it is
genuine, it is the only place anyone names the street as an identity rather
than a waypoint.

## What the record shows and does not show

**Documented.** Eighty dated records of the street name, from five sources:
twenty-four Hangouts messages (2014–2015), forty iMessage corpus rows
(2015–2026), one Messenger message (2022), and eight Google Maps activity
entries (2016–2023). A shelved extract adds seven rows for a window the
corpus does not hold. Forty-five of the eighty are bare position reports.

**Not in the record.** The street's length and route. Whether it runs
through Heritage Hills or past it. Which house is the Ulmers', and whether
any of the Maps addresses (278, 301–315, 311, 315) belong to anyone in the
story. What Dan said about Derrick on 2025-12-12. Who the 2022 weed dealer
was. Whether 278 Derrick Ave burned. No photograph of the street is held,
and the 2022 drone video is referenced but not stored.

**Unverified claims inherited from the earlier page.** These cannot be
found in any held source: *"snowmobiling up Derrick ave"* (2016-01-23);
*"They pulled us over on Derrick"* (2017-11-21); *"I went the back way out
of hh and then down Bailey so I didn't have to drive Derrick"*
(2017-11-22); construction delays in August 2018; the 278 fire *"reported
by Suz"* (2020-09-21); *"the derrick ave gang"* (2024-07-13). The corpus
does hold a nearby Bailey Street message. On 2017-11-20 Dan told Annie he
would *"just drive up and down bailey"* to find her walking home (23:33 ET).
That places Bailey in the local route network. It does not confirm the
quoted message.

**Inferred.** The two-ends reading above. That the 2025 walking messages
involve Alice and Otto. The extract's stroller line says "she," and the
children's pages establish that Annie cared for them in these years. That
"our yard" in the August 2025 animal message is the Ulmer family's.

## Appendix: forensic notes

1. **The count.** The earlier page said *"forty-five times... between
   November 2015 and August 2025,"* from per-contact extracts that no
   longer exist. That figure cannot be reproduced.
   `kb/data/1303-derrick-avenue-mention-count-held` measured forty rows in
   the held corpus, and this revision confirms forty street mentions there.
   Of the earlier page's quotations, seven survive only in the shelved
   extract and six are not found anywhere. **CORRECTED [2026-09-27]:** The
   street's documented span begins on **2014-12-22**, in the Dan–Alexis
   Hangouts, not in November 2015, and runs to **2026-08-11**. The
   frontmatter `date_range_start` and `date_range_end` still carry the
   earlier values. They are left unchanged pending a frontmatter pass.

2. **The corpus gap.** The authoritative corpus holds five rows from
   Annie's current number before 2025-08-07, all from 2023. It holds none
   from February to early August 2025. A shelved per-contact extract of
   the same number covers that window. Under `CORPUS_POLICY.md`, a fact
   that appears only in a fragment is a bug to investigate, not a fact to
   keep. The seven rows are therefore printed with a marker and not used
   as the sole support for any conclusion above. The gap itself should be
   opened as a corpus-completeness item.

3. **The anniversary coincidence.** Dan's first Derrick text to Annie is
   dated the day they met in person, and his 2018 note to Trevor Bevins
   calls 2018-11-30 their *"third anniversary"* (corpus, Trevor thread,
   2018-11-30 16:28 ET). The page reports the coincidence and does not
   read meaning into it.

4. **Unplaced row.** Row 42, the stop sign, is one of the corpus's sent
   messages with no counterparty field. It cannot be assigned to a thread.

5. **Handles.** The earlier page printed one sender's phone number. This
   revision names senders instead, using [[wiki/people/teddy]],
   [[wiki/people/tan-calabrese]], [[wiki/people/vanessa-frank]], and
   [[wiki/people/johnny-dealer]]. The numbers are in the corpus and
   gain nothing by being repeated on a public page.
