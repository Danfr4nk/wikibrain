# 155 Virginia Ave

155 Virginia Ave, Uniontown PA was Dan's residence from January 2015 to February 2019 — the address where the two defining relationships of his adult life handed off, and where the hardest documented years of his life happened. Context-core calls it "the Alexis lair": leased during the end of the Alexis relationship, and then — in a move the record flags as self-preservation lair-continuity — Annie moved into the same apartment when the new relationship began after Thanksgiving 2015. The container held while the occupant changed.

It is the most location-proved address of the Uniontown era. The 2026-09-14 location teardown — 121,733 Google Takeout pings, April 2014 to May 2024 — makes it the dominant home of 2016–19: 849 visits, primary residence every month of 2018 by overnight pings. It sits on the lot adjacent to Fran's house, so the four-year residence and the caregiving job that defined the era were a property line apart. The MOGZART era, the "smashonista" couples-camming period, the 2016 politicization, the 2017 poverty floor (water shut off, DJing for free, cam income, drug-fronts as credit — [[wiki/timeline/periods/2017-poverty-floor]]), and the 2018 "deep cycle" (peak chaos and output, multiple dealers, ~40k messages/year — [[wiki/timeline/periods/2018-deep-cycle]]) all played out at this address.

But the address predates the tenancy. Eighteen months before the January 2015 lease, it was already under Suz's control and already doing the job the lair-continuity finding would later name: after the Franki Fireworks Day of July 2013, Alexis was displaced to "suz's other place at 155 virginia avenue," stayed five days, and went back to her parents in Carmichaels. The lair was performing its displaced-partner function before Dan ever signed for it.

## Before the lease: Suz's other place (July 2013)

The five-day figure was resolved 2026-08-28, when a second, independent operator capture confirmed it unprompted — *"She spent 5 days at Virginia ave and then went to her moms"* — and retired the franki-faris page's mis-attachment of the five days to Franki's own tenure, which both captures describe only as collapsing immediately. Two independent operator captures thirteen years after the fact is the one doubly-sourced item in this chain, and it moved a places-page date by eighteen months: the address was under Suz's control — "suz's other place" — and already the displaced partner's shelter in July 2013.

What followed at the address that summer matters for what it became. Suz then brought Alexis and Danielle Onesi — Dan's first girlfriend, Alexis's "former enemy" — together to bond over their shared grievance, *"and this caused a huge thing"*: the record's earliest documented instance of the maternal switchboard operating on the relational graph. And the same summer put Dan's production identity in motion: the DJ controller he had acquired at ishlab and "never really touched" was picked up after the NYC return and became a daily occupation by summer 2013 — the first sourced dating of the production identity inside that year.

The five days echo forward. On 2015-12-03 — two days after Alexis moved out of the apartment for the last time — Dan texted the line the wiki keeps as its shorthand for the switch: *"I had a Franki Faris 2.0 situation earlier today"* / *"As in - I left Alexis and had an existential meltdown with another girl. Annie Ulmer was playing the role of Franki this time"* (2015-12-03 02:32–02:35 UTC, held-corpus verified, byte-verbatim). Nine days later: *"But after the whole thing with Alexis and Franki I stay away."* The 2013 displacement at this address became the template the 2015 handoff was named after.

## The lease and the lair (January 2015 – November 2015)

The page dates the residence from January 2015: leased during the end of the Alexis relationship, "the Alexis lair." The held corpus corroborates the address's presence in Dan's life without establishing tenancy dates — three Dan-sent address-form mentions: "155 Virginia ace" 2015-12-30, "I really want to get out of Virginia ave" 2016-05-17, "155 Virginia ave" 2016-12-24. No lease, no rent figure, no signatory, no deposit appears anywhere in the record for this address — the unpapered-address synthesis counts it among seven residences and sixteen years held entirely by relationship, terminable by the person, with no instrument. The one housing instrument that does touch this address arrives at the end, and it is served by family.

The house was also the earliest documented Uniontown supply node. From January 2015 — the same month the page dates the lease — the address is [[wiki/people/zachariah-harshman|Zachariah Harshman]]'s consistent handoff location: *"front door is open, i'm in the kitchen,"* *"side door to garage."* Nearly every documented handoff in the 2014–15 supply relationship happens here, three years earlier than the network's other documented nodes, and inside the same month the Alexis-to-Annie transfer Zach would react to in December takes place. [[wiki/people/lukyan-mraz|Lukyan Mraz]]'s visits are staged around the same household (*"wanted to see if Zach was there"*), and a lent lap-quilting book plus "the room" with "equipment" point to a small grow attempt run out of the same Uniontown social circle — the circle that was about to make the room real.

**The grow.** The lair grew its own supply. Dan sent fifteen EXIF-dated photographs (2015-08-18 through 2015-11-01) documenting an indoor cannabis grow at this address, seedling to late flower: vegetative growth in terracotta pots under CFL lighting — Energetic-branded CFLs, a clamp lamp, a desk-lamp rig — then early flower with white pistils (Oct 9), developed colas with ambering pistils (Oct 28), and a trichome macro (Nov 1). A home setup, nothing professional; one frame shows two pots, so at least two plants. GPS on thirteen of fifteen frames clusters at the address's fix (the Nov 1 macro came from a Samsung Galaxy S4, no GPS). Dan's testimony, filed as his: zero growing experience or knowledge, grown in a closet with clamp lights and incandescent bulbs. The strain was his favorite, and he gave it his own name: **"Dream Queen"** — his label, not a verified strain ID (`dat:virginia-ave-grow-timeline-2015`, `dat:virginia-ave-grow-flower-2015`). The series ends in late flower, four weeks before the switch; the harvest itself was never photographed — because per Dan's 2026-09-12 testimony it never got the chance: [[wiki/people/casey-bondarenka|Casey Bondarenka]] took it before it had cured and smoked it all in one night during the Alexis-to-Annie switch (`dat:casey-uncured-harvest-dream-queen-20260912`). The taking rests on his testimony alone, but the window fits the one independently documented fact about Casey: he was inside this household 2015-11-29 through 2015-12-02, the exact fortnight of the handoff. The irony is load-bearing, not decorative: the container that held constant across the attachment transfer was, in the months before the transfer, literally cultivating.

The February 2015 possession arrest and the ARD process that followed fall in the address's first months — the legal thread of the Uniontown era opening at the same time as the supply node was forming. Its sequencing against the January 2015 start date is held open below.

## The switch (November 25 – December 2015)

The **danfranksgiving** party — Nov 25, 2015, the day before Thanksgiving, funded by Zachariah Harshman, per Dan's 2026-09-12 testimony — is the origin week's house party: Annie attended, and Dan snuck out at 2am to meet her on the golf course they both border. That 2am sneak-out (early Nov 26) is resolved as distinct from the corpus-anchored Nov 29 6:40-7:30 golf meetup (*"meet you on the golf,"* *"I'm on 3 tee,"* *"I would sleep in the wet grass with you"*): the origin week holds at least two golf-course meetings, one testified, one textually anchored. The coinage survived — *"happy franksgiving"* appears 2017-11-23, his word two years on (`dat:danfranksgiving-2015-11-25`).

Over roughly 72 hours in late November, the six-year Alexis relationship ended and Alexis was evicted from this address — the terminal one of at least two Suz-driven evictions of Alexis, and the one with dated messages (`evt:2015-11-alexis-evicted`). The corpus dates every beat: **Nov 28**, key confiscated, Dan telling Annie the Alexis origin story while she is still living with him in Uniontown; **Nov 29 ~02:00**, Dan meets Annie at the Uniontown Country Club in the rain and tells Zachariah *"Things are collapsing with lex"*; **Nov 29 14:54**, Dan naming the tie holding her in the house — *"Ahhhh I know why she isn't leaving / Or like HASNT left / …she doesn't have another drug source"*; **Nov 30 00:35**, her property removed from the guest bedroom — *"so she knows I could actually call the cops"*; **Nov 30 13:42**, parents summoned as a pressure step; **Dec 1**, *"Helping lex leave"* — Alexis moves out of the address; **Dec 2**, *"Listen I broke up with lex, I'm in love with Annie Ulmer"* / *"Alexis only left yesterday."*

The eviction ran as reversible pressure steps, not a confrontation, and it was brokered and paid for by family. Per the read pass's quotation of the Annie corpus — a source not held in this repository — on Nov 30 Suz brought Dan cocaine and promised a car "this week" if he got Alexis out. The Nov 29 phone call in which Suz told Dan he should date Annie — the name fits the family: *"Anne Dan Suzanne Fran Diane Van"* — is verified verbatim in the held corpus (2015-11-30 18:49 UTC). The wiki's biggest open correction of the origin story stands: the switch was **mutual** — Annie had her own partner and ended it the same week — and **brokered and paid for** by the family. Seduction became transaction, dated to the first 72 hours.

Zach was the operational cover for the switch itself: *"Harshman leaving soon, so I'll have him drop me and say I'm going to his house"* (2015-11-29). Dec 1: *"Zach has to take lex to blue moon"* — Zach ferrying Alexis while covering for Dan. Dec 2: *"I told you that I told Zach outright about having a crush on you."* Then **Dec 23, 2015**, the rupture: Dan's 4:21 AM accusation of "vanishing," answered by Zach's explanation that he had become Alexis's *"best friend other than you for the past year"* and pulled back because *"if you're going to all of the sudden hang out 'all the time' with the ex girlfriend of your best friend immediately after they break up, you might want to make sure the aforementioned friend doesn't feel a complete sense of betrayal"* (`dat:1391-zachariah-harshman-rupture`). Independent, real-time, third-party corroboration of the switch's speed, dated within a month of the Nov 28-29 pivot — the bond-switch page's load-bearing evidence, and it lives at this address. Three days later SLOPPP closed with the Dec 26 "Goodbye Demo."

The address's camera record of the switch: on **2015-11-26 at 14:33 EST** — Thanksgiving Day — Annie sent Dan what he states is the first nude she ever sent him (EXIF-stamped, iPhone 6s front camera; her GPS fix ~470m from the address, her position not his). What the frame no longer establishes: that no physical contact preceded it — that reading was withdrawn 2026-09-12 after Dan's origin account placed them having sex around ~Nov 19 (`dat:annie-first-nude-milestone-20260912`).

And on **2015-12-11** — two months into the Annie relationship, two years before Dan and Annie became Fran's paid caregivers — Fran Coldren, then 95, posed delightedly with Dan's glass bong in three iPhone frames. Per Dan's 2026-09-12 statement the frames were taken at this address, and the EXIF GPS fix (39.887639,-79.721992) lands 29.6m from the geocoded address point versus 83.4m from Fran's house (`dat:bong-photos-gps-virginia-ave-20260912`): Fran at the lair, two months into the Annie relationship — she was a guest in their space, not the other way around.

## The eras of the address (2016 – March 2018)

MOGZART — the 2014–16 SoundCloud alias, ~40 tracks and 100 mixes per the page — spent its productive 2015–16 run here; then the "smashonista" couples-camming period; the 2016 politicization; the 2017 poverty floor (water shut off, DJing for free, cam income, drug-fronts as credit); and the 2018 "deep cycle" (peak chaos and output, multiple dealers, ~40k messages/year). Four eras in four years, all under one roof, one relationship.

The DANSYNTH ingest added the address's deepest layer: it sits **adjacent to [[wiki/places/117-belmont-circle|117 Belmont Circle]]** — Fran's house on the Uniontown Country Club fairway — on a boundary the Frank/Whyel and Ulmer families shared for fifty-plus years. Living there put Dan and Annie next door to the caregiving job that defined the era: they were Fran's caregivers until her March 2018 fall — paid full-time for roughly the last six months of it — and Dan's contemporaneous account has him paid $15/hour for the preceding six months, a figure no payroll record has been checked against. The location teardown confirms the adjacency thesis from the sensor side: the two lots are ~80m apart and assignment bleeds between them, but the primary/secondary pattern survives it — this address as the primary residence every month of 2018 (84–487 overnights/mo), Fran's house strong Jan–May (95–322) then fading as secondary, the family home at Saratoga marginal throughout. 849 visits total: the dominant home of the era, with the caregiving house running as its satellite (`wiki/self/location-history.md`).

The vigil sequence landed at this address. The second fall, "Down Goes Frazier," came the night of March 7–8, 2018 — Dan and Annie took the overnight, and the next morning's messages document the ambulance; Dan's gurney line to Vicki is verified in the held iMessage corpus (2018-03-08 18:29:31 UTC / 13:29 EST, outbound): *"she was screaming so loud when they were putting her on the gurney it was just awful."* From the hospital Fran was mandated to a nursing home, and within ~two weeks her state failed completely; on April 1 she was moved to the Uniontown Hospital pediatric wing to die, where Judge Fred Adams — the estate's trustee — mandated a 24-hour rotation held by Dan, Annie, Vicki, and Marla (`dat:0169-fran-vigil-fall-and-hospital-sequence`).

On **March 29, 2018** — mid-collapse — Dan was served an eviction notice terminating his residency and demanding the keys, served by "Dian V. Moore," identified 2026-08-18 as his own maternal grandmother (`dat:0039-old-wiki-eviction-notice-march-2018`). It is one of only two housing instruments in sixteen years, and the estate moving on the house while he was holding the 24/7 line is the unpapered-address mechanism made literal: tenancy by relationship, terminable by the person — here, terminated by the family itself.

## The paper ending and the lived ending (March 2018 – March 2019)

A Fayette County summary summons filed **April 5, 2018** — seven days after the notice, docket MJ-14101-NT-0000082-2018 — gives the defendant's address as the family home at Saratoga rather than this address. Whatever else the notice did or did not accomplish, the address the Commonwealth held for Dan one week later was his mother's house, not this one. The page's narrow reading stands: an address on court paper is an address **of record**, which can lag or lead a place of sleep — and a third house was in use across the same spring. The residence timeline's "until February 2019" cannot stand unqualified: the correction it needs is that the tenancy's **paper life ended in March 2018 while its lived life may not have**.

He left for Manhattan in early March 2019 — the location teardown pins the physical move to Mar 3–5: last Pennsylvania overnight Mar 2, first NYC overnight Mar 6, and the *"Au revoir Pennsylvania. Adiós Trump country."* tweet (Mar 3) lands on departure day. Movement record beating testimony, this time confirming it (`dat:1534-location-teardown-20260914`).

On 2026-09-13, asked which address holds "a bunch of really weird and odd coincidences," Dan answered this one and clarified: *"I was referring to the series of Jacob Bacharach coincidences that center around the house"* (`dat:1478-bacharach-virginia-ave-centering-20260913`). The chain: novelist [[wiki/people/jacob-bacharach|Jacob Bacharach]] used this house's exterior only — never set foot inside it — as a fictional rental setting, picked for the glazed brick; the interior details are invented. Bacharach's own February 2021 message names *The Doorposts of Your House and on Your Gates* (Liveright, 2017) as the Uniontown novel — five years of the wiki calling it *The Bend of the World* (2014) is superseded (see Conflicts). Either way the chronology survives: Dan moved into the setting after the book existed, January 2015 against a 2014 or a 2017 publication. The false-contradiction episode — an earlier pass had conflated the house with the Bacharach family's actual former home down the street from the Franks — is resolved as two separate facts. In Dan's current telling the Virginia house, not the Saratoga thread, is the center of gravity of the series.

## Conflicts in the record

A Fayette County summary summons filed **April 5, 2018** — seven days after the notice — gives the defendant's address as **337 Saratoga Dr, Uniontown PA 15401** (docket MJ-14101-NT-0000082-2018). That is a narrow finding and worth keeping narrow: an address on court paper is an address **of record**, which can lag or lead a place of sleep, and [[wiki/places/117-belmont-circle]] documents him using a third house across the same spring. The residence timeline's "155 Virginia until February 2019" cannot stand unqualified — but the correction it needs is that the tenancy's paper life ended in March 2018 while its lived life may not have, which is the same shape this page already argues about the instrument itself. The reading that survives both facts is the one in the typed edge below: the notice *is* one of only two housing instruments in sixteen years, and it did change something after all — the paperwork. Whether it changed where he slept is a separate question the corpus still cannot answer.

**Which Bacharach novel — SUPERSEDED 2026-09-15.** The body above names *The Doorposts of Your House and on Your Gates* (Liveright, 2017). The page previously named *The Bend of the World* (2014) in every retelling — including the REVISED [2026-07-13] block (novelist used this house's exterior only, never set foot inside; interior details invented; unrelated to the Bacharach family's actual former home down the street from the Franks — two separate facts an earlier pass had conflated) and the REVISED [2026-08-08] block (which added the two corrections: Bacharach's own February 2021 message identifies a different book as his Uniontown novel, and the chronology of writing-the-house-while-Dan-lived-in-it does not survive the January 2015 tenancy start). ## Assessment

The address is the wiki's strongest spatial thesis, and the record keeps confirming it. The lair-continuity finding — the apartment leased for Alexis becoming the apartment Annie moves into — is the dormancy-not-exit rule applied to real estate: nothing leaves the graph; the container is retained while the occupant is swapped, and the July 2013 five-day stay shows the container was already performing the displaced-partner function eighteen months before the lease. The unpapered-address reading holds end to end: no lease, no rent, no signatory, no deposit across the whole tenancy, and the one housing instrument in sixteen years arrives served by the family that held the tenancy — the mechanism made literal.

Two things the page got right early and the record has only strengthened: the paper-versus-occupancy distinction (an address on court paper can lag or lead a place of sleep) and the adjacency thesis (the caregiving job and the residence a property line apart, the Frank/Whyel–Ulmer boundary shared for fifty-plus years, confirmed from the sensor side by the location teardown). And one irony the 2026 photo drops made load-bearing: the container that held constant across the attachment transfer was, in the months before the transfer, literally cultivating — seedling to late flower, August to November 2015 — with the harvest taken before it cured, undocumented except by the taking.
