# 337 Saratoga Drive

337 Saratoga Drive, Uniontown, Pennsylvania, is the Frank family house —
built in 1996 with [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]] "very involved in the
design and construction," per her own seller's disclosure — and Dan's
childhood home across three separate eras of his life. It is the house
where the November 2005 parental rupture happened inside its walls; the
house whose basement carried the GRIPNOTIC production identity across two
eras; and the house that anchored the location record's home measurements
for a decade. In June 2026 it was sold for $465,000 — not by choice, but
liquidated inside Suz's Chapter 13 bankruptcy, the court's August 2025
"drop dead" provision having made a binding sale the only alternative to
conversion or dismissal. Dan moved out on July 8, 2026.

The sale is the estate money spine's first capital event where Dan is
bystander rather than beneficiary: the family-built asset liquidating
around him while it also takes his housing. The owner-realtor who
executed the sale is also the person he lives with, which collapses the
family's largest asset liquidation and the loss of his housing into a
single transaction run by one party.

## Building and childhood, 1996–2008

The family moved to Saratoga Drive in 1996 from 12 Bryer Ave, and the
location-history page's address sequence runs the house 1996–2019
([`dat:1264`](kb/data/1264-location-history.md) — page testimony; the
tenancy table below carries the era-by-era version). Suz's seller's
property disclosure, signed March 30, 2026, fixes the hard facts: acquired
**May 30, 1996** — twenty-nine years of ownership — and her note that she
was "very involved in the design and construction of the home"
([`dat:1674`](kb/data/1674-saratoga-seller-disclosure-facts.md); the
disclosure is Suz-authored, so it is hearsay rather than Dan's voice, but
it is the closest thing to a primary document the house has).

These are the ski-identity years, the childhood and adolescence Dan left
behind when he went to Full Sail in September 2008. A dog and several
cats lived in the house across the twenty-nine years, per the same
disclosure.

## The rupture inside these walls

November 2005 is the hinge of the record, and this house is where it
happened. Richard Harrison Frank and Suzanne Frank had married August 31,
1985; twenty years of marriage, then November 2005. Per Dan's account, the
rupture's disclosure was heard in the car — and the boy who heard it
slept that night, and the nights after, in the bedroom of the house that
would be sold twenty-one years later to settle his mother's bankruptcy.
The event page is explicit: "The house is not incidental set dressing. It
is the rupture's location, and its eventual sale closes the property arc
the rupture opened."

Dan was seventeen. His sister Vanessa was eleven — the rupture's only
other in-house narrator, and she has not narrated it anywhere in the
corpus. That absence is load-bearing: the entire event, as the wiki holds
it, is one testimony carried by the same man at thirty-eight, with no
second witness from inside the house. The page's treatment of the rupture
as settled fact is therefore a treatment of one testimony as settled, and
the house's walls are the boundary of what any witness could know.

For most of the decade between the two Uniontown eras, Dan's paper address

incident — Fayette County summary non-traffic docket
**MJ-14101-NT-0000082-2018**, filed April 5, 2018 (the day after Fran

([`dat:0495`](kb/data/0495-uniontown-hospital-vape-alarm.md)). The fuller

hospital properties." The summons was mailed to Saratoga and would have
arrived during the week of the funeral; the two April-17 photographs of

Its disposition is unresolved — both plea lines blank and the stub
undetached in the April 17 photographs; no court date, fine, or docket

summons names would settle it and nobody has run it. And the positions in

What the address finding means for this page is narrow but real: filed a

## The sale

**The house was not sold by choice. It was liquidated in a bankruptcy.**
[[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]] filed Chapter 13 in October 2024 (case
24-22285-GLT, ~$157,000 scheduled liabilities, most of it IRS priority
claims for tax years 2018–2021), on stated income of $11,000–$14,000/year.
337 Saratoga was owned free and clear and was the only asset capable of
satisfying the plan; in August 2025 the court attached a "drop dead"
provision under which failure to secure a binding sales agreement would
convert the case to Chapter 7 or dismiss it. The reason the price fell
$150,000 in thirteen months is that the property needed work she had no
money to do while tax claims accrued interest daily. A bankruptcy sale
optimises for certainty of closing rather than price, because the
alternative is a trustee taking fees out of the equity first.

**Provenance note, read once.** Everything in this section's paper trail
is Suz-authored — the sale documents, the bankruptcy dossier, the
disclosure — pulled from her computer 2026-09-17. Per the standing
authorship rule (2026-09-18), that material is hearsay: useful for facts,
never Dan's voice, never attributed to him. Dan's own entries in the
chain are the 2026-03-30 pasted audit message and his 2026-07-14 closing
confirmation, both labeled below.

**The price path.** The asking price ran **$615,000 in May 2025** down
through $544,900 and $525,000 to a $500,000 cut on 2026-01-01, pending
2026-04-03, to the eventual $465,000 — Compass MLS #1721561, listing
agent Suzanne Frank of Park Place Realty Group
([`dat:1262`](kb/data/1262-337-saratoga-drive.md);
[kb/entities/suzanne-frank](kb/entities/suzanne-frank.md)). A June 2025
contract was terminated over inspection and pest reports. The $615k
figure first reached the record secondhand — an AI-generated case audit
Dan pasted into the Suz thread on 30 March 2026, a 2,968-character
message verified verbatim in the held corpus at 2026-03-30 17:38:21 UTC,
AI-secondary and evidently built from what she had told him. An outside
audit later flagged it as the softest claim in the sale narrative
(everything else checkable held: the price path, the pending date, the
closing). It no longer rests on one telling. Suz's own bankruptcy
dossier, generated 2026-04-28 and pulled from her computer, states the
$615k original list and the failed-inspection contract independently — a
second source, in her own hand, a month after the audit. The claim is now
the first settled entry in the testimony ledger (t002, confirmed). On
2026-09-18, Suz's Claude audited the wiki's Saratoga claims and held up
on everything checkable — the price path, the pending date, the closing.

**The mechanics.** There were two offers on the table in early April 2026
— one from SWC Realty for Jennifer J. DelVerme, one from Howard Hanna
for Doug and Heather Wolfe — and Suz countered both on April 1 before
signing the DelVerme agreement (dated March 31)
([`dat:1673`](kb/data/1673-saratoga-two-offer-sale-mechanics.md);
[`dat:1734`](kb/data/1734-saratoga-615k-confirmed-20260918.md)). Her email
chain with bankruptcy counsel Corey Sacca shows every offer was subject
to bankruptcy court approval and Chapter 7 trustee acceptance, and that
she asked about a motion for reconsideration (counsel: possible, but the
judge was unlikely to change his decision). She was not taking a
commission on her own listing — "the job I have already done" — and told
Sacca her year-to-date earnings were **$2,759**. The DelVerme agreement
then wobbled on its own buyer line: Scott P. DelVerme was removed from
the agreement on April 28, the $5,000 earnest money moved into Attorney
Robert Sloan's escrow on May 7, and Scott was added back as a buyer on
May 8 (Suz countersigned May 26). The buyers' names are page testimony,
relayed but not independently verified. It closed anyway — June 23 per
Dan directly (2026-07-14), June 24 per the MLS record, a one-day
discrepancy that is noted, not arbitrated — at $465k, contingent on the
sale of the buyers' own property (19 Jeffries, ≤$229.9k) and the court
approval above, with a **7-day post-close vacate clause**. The planned
out-date of July 1 (consistent with the 7-day clause) slipped a week to
an **actual move-out of July 8**.

**Consequence.** The sale put Dan and Suz at 463 Morgantown St with no
signed lease and no post-close financial plan
([`dat:1262`](kb/data/1262-337-saratoga-drive.md);
[event](kb/events/2026-06-saratoga-sold.md)). The successor housing has
its own unresolved legal tangle
([[wiki/legal/463-morgantown]]). Context-core carried the situation as
the acute open variable of mid-2026: "no confirmed post-close plan."

## The house itself

Suz's seller's disclosure, signed March 30, 2026, fixes the hard facts
([`dat:1674`](kb/data/1674-saratoga-seller-disclosure-facts.md) — her

**May 30, 1996**; last occupied **September 16, 2025, by two people**

master bedroom more than once since); attic moisture was remediated in
2019 by opening the vents. A recent pest inspection came back negative
for termites. The oil, gas, and mineral rights were "conveyed long ago"
and are not part of the property. Outstanding at sale: kitchen sink
plumbing needing repair, a laundry-room sprayer not working well, the
right garage door inoperable on a broken cable, an original hard-wired
alarm system due for updating, and an upstairs window unit that failed
after a caulking job and let water into the bar area below. A dog and
several cats lived there across the twenty-nine years.

([`dat:1478`](kb/data/1478-bacharach-virginia-ave-centering-20260913.md)).

## Assessment

No other location carries comparable weight in the record, and the
record now shows why in three independent registers. First, the house is
the control in the unpapered-address rule: the one residence in the
corpus decided by real recorded paper — deed, Chapter 13 docket, court
drop-dead provision — was decided by somebody else's paper, which proves
