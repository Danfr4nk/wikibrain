# 307 E 76th St (Manhattan)

307 E 76th St, Upper East Side, was home for the entire second New York
chapter — February 2019 to February 2025 — shared with
[[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]], and the stage for the relationship's
last whole years and its rupture. The six years at this address contain the
2019 hard-left politicization (Bernie + Chapo), the pandemic bunker years,
the 2020–21 market era ([[wiki/timeline/periods/2020-2021-market-era]]),
the 41-month Au Za'atar job a downtown train ride away
([[wiki/work/au-zaatar]]), the 2024 political-obsession peak, and — in its
final autumn — the concealed [[wiki/people/eli|Eli]] affair whose January 2025 discovery ended
the household ([[wiki/timeline/events/eli-incident]]): Annie was found
unconscious with the coworker in a building lobby ~80 minutes after her
shift, location sharing off. Dan left the address, and New York, on
February 22, 2025.

## The cast

**[[wiki/people/john-paci|John Paci]]** owned the building and ran it
personally for the entire tenancy — leasing it directly ("he's the one who
stuck his neck out for us and held the apartment when we found it," per
Annie, September 2020), scheduling exterminators himself by text, and
collecting rent and debt payments by name into at least mid-2025, four
months after Dan had left. He is the addressee and signatory on the
building's final word on the tenancy: a March 5, 2025 message rounding
the couple's outstanding balance to an even $10,000 and asking Dan to
begin paying it down (see "The debt," below). Full account at
[[wiki/people/john-paci]].

**[[wiki/people/jim-blanchard|Jim Blanchard]]** was the building's
unofficial super and rental broker — his own description, given to Suz
on 2025-02-08: "I was the quiet 'unofficial super' at my building and
still help tenants whenever I can. I was the rental broker for many
years." The operator's 2026-08-10 capture named him "world's worst
building super/real estate agent"; the role words check out against 259
dated messages across two dyads (54 rows with Dan, 2019–2023; 205 with
Suz, 2020–2025), but the superlative is uncorroborated-to-contradicted
by the contemporaneous record, which is warm throughout. He met Dan and
Annie on move-in day, sent building-wide tenant notices under his own
name, coordinated Paci's access, scheduled water shutoffs and
exterminator visits, and referred to Paci as "your landlord" in the
third person. Full account at [[wiki/people/jim-blanchard]].

## The roach bombing and the September 2020 trip to PA

On September 10, 2020, Dan told Annie the apartment was scheduled for
extermination: "sorry to keep bugging you just wanted to give you an
update. they are bombing the apt tomorrow around 11 or so." The couple
relocated to Annie's parents' home in Uniontown for what became roughly a
week — Dan references "the past week, especially the first 4 days of the
trip" in a message sent nine days later. That nine-to-ten-day stay is the
same trip during which the relationship's [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Train
Plan crisis]] happened: on the night of September 19, mid-argument, Dan
is simultaneously coordinating a **second** exterminator visit directly
with Paci — "i'm on the line with john paci tomorrow… nobody is to enter
that apartment, other than me and the exterminators" — while the
relationship blows up around him. By the next morning both Dan and Annie
are texting about packing, animals, and driving back, and Dan separately
tells a PA-area contact "it's dan, suzanne's son" the following day,
suggesting the household relocated in full, dog and cats included ("i got
all my stuff packed and the dog and cats"). The roach problem was not a
one-time event: a second, unrelated infestation shows up in the corpus
in December 2020 and February 2021 ("I just found a fucking roach!!!!
I'm furious"), and exterminator visits recur as late as December 2024
("Exterminator will be at 307 on Monday morning. John PACI").

The operator's own memory of the sequence — that the bombing happened
"right after the train incident" — has the order reversed from what the
corpus shows: the bombing (Sept 10–11) precedes the Train Plan discovery
(Sept 19–20) by about nine days, and the two exterminator visits (the
original bombing and the one Dan is arranging on the night of the
blowup) are easy to conflate in memory into one event. Both readings
converge on the same underlying fact, which is the one worth keeping:
**the trip to PA that hosted the Train Plan crisis had a mundane,
previously undocumented cause.** Nobody had connected the two before this
pass.

## The debt

By 2023, Dan and Annie had accumulated debt to Paci substantial enough
that they were making scheduled payments against it — $650 sent in
November 2024, for instance, explicitly earmarked "to tell John." That
debt was not retired. On March 5, 2025 — two weeks after Dan's
departure — Paci wrote: "Dan, after paying to have the remainder of the
stuff you left removed, and deducting the security deposit. I rounded the
balance down to an even $10,000.00. Let me know when you can begin to pay
it down. John." Annie's own reaction to a Paci text four months later
("Fucking John Paci just texted me," July 2025) is consistent with the
debt remaining an active, unpleasant subject well after the household
dissolved. Whether any of the $10,000 was ever paid is not in the
recovered record.

(per Sam's delivery notes); the lease's original signatory, the monthly

## The rent, and the terms it ran on

> **GAP CLOSED [2026-09-24]:** the Gaps note above also records the
> original signatory and monthly rent as not recovered. The February 2019
> Gmail leasing thread recovers the rent: the listing headline is
> *"E 76th 1st/2nd 1 bdrm W/D $2300"* — $2,300/month — and the "Check
> amounts" thread (2019-02-27/28) fixes the move-in money exactly:
> **$13,800 security deposit** (six months) and **$11,500** (six months'
> rent minus the $2,300 check Dan had already brought Jim), both as
> cashier's checks payable to **"John Paci Jr"**, with rent running
> **every other month starting 5/1/19** — plus a $2,300 agency fee to
> James Blanchard (*"I don't charge the usual 15% fee"*). The structure
> matches the February 13–14 broker scripts Dan wrote for Annie to send
> ("no adequate credit scores... willing to take unusually extreme
> measures"): no-credit tenants buying their way in with twelve months'
> cash up front. The original signatory remains unrecovered.

What the same thread establishes is that the tenancy never ran on a right. Dan's
own account of securing it, February 2019: *"the guy was willing to take us on,
and said that as long as we sustain jobs the lease would be open for us on a
second year."* Seven months later, on managing the renewal: *"we've made damn
sure that there's not a single issue with any of our rent checks so our landlord
won't have to deliberate about signing next years lease."* Annie, in September
2020, on the same man: *"he's the one who stuck his neck out for us and held the
apartment when we found it."*

Willing to take us on; as long as we sustain jobs; won't have to deliberate;
stuck his neck out. Six years of housing described, accurately, as a favour
under continuous review — which is the structure every other address in the
record also has ([[wiki/places/the-unpapered-address]]).

## October 27, 2024 — the lease as a threat

Four months before the physical separation, inside a rupture that opens with
*"We aren't together anymore,"* Dan writes: *"Tomorrow I'm calling John"* /
*"And telling him that I will no longer be in the lease."* Annie answers within
the minute: *"Please don't do this Dan. Please."* He does not call. The lease
exit is never mentioned again, and the tenancy ends in **February 2025** because
Annie ends the cohabitation.

This is the corpus's only instance of Dan reaching for a housing instrument on
his own behalf, and it is not a property act — it is a severance declared inside
an argument, with the lease as the instrument of the declaration and the same
execution rate as every other declared exit
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/block-unblock-loop]]).
