# Caviar Courier (Gig Work)

> **Incidental entry** — Caviar courier gig work, 2019–2020: 290 automated dispatches, counted exactly. Complete at this size.

The iMessage corpus contains 290 messages from April 21, 2019 to April 14, 2020, all of them inbound from Caviar's automated dispatch number, and every one an order-assignment or expiration notification: "New Caviar order assigned," "Order #XXXXX is ready for pickup," assignment-expired warnings. The count and the span have been re-measured against the held corpus and match the record exactly, to the row — first message April 21, 2019, 20:48 UTC; last April 14, 2020, 22:02 UTC (`dat:0497`). The dispatch number is withheld here under the corpus redaction convention. What the thread documents is Dan working as a Caviar food-delivery courier during the NYC-II era — and nothing else, because the thread contains nothing else. Across a year of courier work there is not one human message, not one note about a restaurant, a customer, or what was in the bag.

The activity shape is the whole story, and it is burst-shaped rather than steady. The dense run is April–July 2019: over 100 assigned orders, some sessions showing back-to-back assignments minutes apart, consistent with active shift-based courier work. Then a long near-silence from August through November 2019, a scattered handful of orders in March 2020, and a final short burst from April 7–14, 2020 — the first weeks of COVID-19 lockdown in New York, consistent with gig delivery picked back up as a stopgap income source at the start of the pandemic. The corpus gives no earnings totals and no delivery counts beyond the assignment notifications themselves; the notification text carries no dollar amounts, and no summary export exists to supply them. That absence bounds the page: hours, income, and any reason for the 2019 silence are unmeasured and unstated.

Read against the longer employment record, the shape is diagnostic. An app that dispatches orders to a phone offers no territory to hold, no ladder to climb, no counterparty to negotiate with, and no oversight to evade — nothing, in the terms of [[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-embedded-objective|the embedded-objective synthesis]], in which to install a private objective. What results is twelve months of availability with no tenure: an appetite for income that was constant across the period, attached to nothing, producing bursts rather than a job. The contrast cases are the corpus's two long tenures — forty-one months at [[wiki/work/au-zaatar|Au Za'atar]] held by objectives installed inside the role, and the BFS month that ended at its first assigned demand. Caviar is the negative control between them: the same worker, the same income pressure, an employer that is only an algorithm, and a record that starts and stops without ever becoming a job. It is also, as the food record goes, the purest instance of food as freight — hundreds of meals passing through one person's working days without a single one being evaluated.
