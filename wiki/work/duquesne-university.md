## What the silence suggests

Quarantined inference — these are readings of the gap, not evidence. Kept separate from fact above.

- **The unremarkable-years reading.** Duquesne may simply have been ordinary: enrolled, attended, left, with nothing in it dramatic enough to narrate. A man who narrates everything narrates drama; the absence of narration may mean the absence of drama.
- **The short-stint reading.** The implied window (high school → September 2008) is narrow. If attendance was a single year or less, there may have been little to document and less to remember fondly.
- **The lost-media reading.** His pre-September-2008 archive is thin across the board — the dark-era page exists precisely because 2007–2008 is under-documented. Duquesne's silence may be a property of the era's archive, not of the school.
- **The post-rupture reading.** Duquesne was his first institution after the November 2005 hinge. The silence of those years sits next to the most documented rupture of his life. Whether the two are connected is unknowable from here — but the adjacency is the one thing the record does establish, and it is why the gap matters.

None of these can be promoted to fact on current evidence. They are listed so future evidence has something to test against.
