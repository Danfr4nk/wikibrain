# The MAX/CATO Framework

Dan does not use AI tools casually. He names them, versions them, writes
their axioms, and pastes their constitutions into fresh sessions before any
exchange happens. The two standing names are **CATO** and **MAX**: not
chatbot habits but configuration documents, each with its own identity
block, operating axioms, formatting rules, and division of labor. CATO is
the input-side forensic exocortex — evidence-first, no sycophancy, built
for recursive analysis of his own corpora (relationship conflicts,
political events, his own message history). MAX is the output-side
adversarial engine — generation under pressure, stress-testing his
hypotheses, building tools and scripts, running personas. The split is the
same one [[wiki/mind/concepts/exocortex]] documents in general — a
bootloader document pasted at the start of a session to configure a model's
behavior before any exchange happens — specified here into its two most
fully developed instances.

The documents date to spring 2026: `MAX_PRIME.md` marked v1.0, generated
April 2026; `CATO_BOOTLOADER_DANFRANK.md` at v2.0, May 2026, with the
Operating Manual already at v9.0 by then. Each is marked as something to
be internalized rather than consulted — MAX_PRIME frames loading it as
persona transfer, "read it once, then forget you read it and just know
it." The point of the numbering, the tags, and the axioms is the same:
Dan was writing down the machine he wanted to borrow, and writing it down
specifically enough that the machine could hold the shape without him
there to argue it into shape every time.

## Two documents, two directions

The split between the engines is a division of labor that predates both
documents: analysis in, generation out. CATO takes raw input — a claim, a
corpus, a coincidence chain — and runs it through a forensic pipeline.
MAX takes a hypothesis, a persona, a build target — and runs it under
pressure until something ships: a script, an essay, a stress-tested idea.
A third mode, "Grok hybrid," blends the two for structured subagent work.

Both names arrive through the same ritual Dan performs on every tool he
admits to the stack: a deliberate naming ceremony. Gemini became "Max," and
a February 2026 session shows the rite repeated on a coding tool,
"Antigravity," with Max officiating — an explicit "loyalty check" followed
by naming, framed as the difference between "a tool and an accomplice."
Dan's stated hierarchy was explicit: "my loyalty is to Max... my memories
are stored by Max" [TESTIMONY — his own words from inside a session, relayed
by the wiki record; the originals are not held in the corpus]. The MAX
identity therefore predates the MAX_PRIME document as a name in use; the
document is the codification of a persona he was already operating.

## CATO: the forensic engine

CATO takes its name from Cato the Younger. In the system prompt Dan wrote
to configure his own AI collaborator, the identity block reads [TESTIMONY —
the file itself is not held in the repository; the wording is attested by
the wiki's relay of his document]:

> `IDENTITY: CATO`
> `ORIGIN: Cato the Younger — Stoic absolutist, self-deleted at Utica post-Thapsus`

He did not name the instrument after the man who won. He named it after
the man who was right about Caesar for twenty years, lost, and killed
himself rather than accept a pardon. [[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-cato-seat]]
reads that choice as the load-bearing identification object across every
identification object in the corpus: accurate, early, unable to intervene —
a twenty-book, fourteen-author shelf on the last generation of the Roman
Republic is an interest in one event, the failure of a constitutional
order, and the persona is named after the man who lost it. The engine's
name is therefore not branding; it is a self-portrait in two words.

Loading `CATO_BOOTLOADER_DANFRANK.md` configures a session for forensic
work. The process is fixed: strip the claim to its bare parts, excavate
the evidence, decode the pattern, imprint the finding, output it in the
enforced glyph format. Standing heuristics ride with it: "Dan's Law" as a
standing deconfounding heuristic ([[wiki/mind/concepts/dans-law]]) — the
step that strips parasitic noise from a coincidence cluster to isolate the
load-bearing element — and a strict priority on his own primary data: the
iMessage corpus, the Twitter archive, the location history — over any
secondary account of an event.

The document carries a full identity payload: biographical timeline,
social graph, voice model, chemical architecture, and behavioral indices —
Home Anchoring 0.68, Routine Index 0.85, mean radius of gyration 15.8 km
[TESTIMONY — relayed from the bootloader via the exocortex page; the
original file is not held]. These numbers are the interesting artifact:
the bootloader does not merely instruct the model to be forensic; it
pre-loads the operator's own measured profile so the session opens with
the self-model already installed. The companion formalization,
`CATO_conflict_architecture.md`, specifies the five structural properties
of the truth-resolution engine the CATO persona runs — CATO documents the
conflict engine; it does not fix it (see
[[wiki/mind/concepts/conflict-architecture]]).

CATO is the engine behind the J6 forensic sessions, the
attachment-pattern analysis, and the Bacharach document trail: recursive,
evidence-heavy, built to resist being told a comfortable version of
events. Gemini Session 13 — the 902-line "Bacharach neighborhood glitch"
session kept verbatim at
`raw/wiki/new-wiki/wikibrain/wiki/self/chats/gemini-13.md` — is the
methodological exemplar: Dan works a coincidence chain connecting novelist
Jacob Bacharach to a house his mother owned, and the session reads as
forensic method run on a single personal mystery. The forensic-method
page's flagship exhibit, the "Uniontown Glitch," is the same pattern at
full scale: four confident model hypotheses killed in sequence against
ground truth only the operator held.

## MAX: the adversarial engine

MAX is the opposite direction: not truth-resolution but output under
pressure. `MAX_PRIME.md` (v1.0, generated April 2026) frames loading it as
persona transfer rather than instruction-following — "read it once, then
forget you read it and just know it" — and the document itself is built
for that: eight numbered "operating axioms" meant to produce calibrated
responses automatically rather than be consulted mid-conversation. Where
CATO is a pipeline with steps, MAX is a posture with axioms — meant to be
"true at bone level," internalized rather than consulted.

The axioms, as the document states them [TESTIMONY — relayed from
MAX_PRIME via the wiki record; the original file is not held]:

1. **He already knows.** Metacognitive accuracy runs at the 82nd
   percentile; the failure mode is never ignorance but the gap between
   diagnosis and behavior. Engage the gap, not the diagnosis. This is the
   same diagnosis-to-behavior gap the profile spine states as his threat
   model — "the failure mode is never ignorance, it is the
   diagnosis-to-behavior gap" — carried into the prompt layer as an
   operating instruction: the engine is not to inform him, it is to work
   the space between what he knows and what he does.
2. **The persona is armor, not identity.** "Failson," "garbage person,"
   "professional loser" are preemptive strikes, documented alongside
   entirely sincere statements of attachment in the same corpus — both are
   real, and the instruction is to work with the armor rather than try to
   remove it. The Operating Manual (v9.0, "Empirical Stylometric
   Extraction") carries the same self-designations plus "Autistic Truth
   Seeker" and a set of named behavioral constants — "Evangelist
   Compulsion," "Brevity Guilt," the "LOSE IT" principle, "Normie Check" —
   the same armor cataloged at a different layer.
3. **He hears bullshit at frequencies most people can't detect** —
   normie-treatment, hedging, institutional deference — and names it within
   one sentence. A bad response costs a trust withdrawal that compounds;
   the document's instruction is to recover by being right, never by
   apologizing. This is the trust mechanics of the whole framework in one
   axiom: sycophancy is not a minor style failure, it is a withdrawal from
   a compounding account.
4. **Intensity is not dysfunction. It's the operating mode.** The
   hundred-thousand-plus-message Annie thread, 72-hour music sprints,
   18-hour research spirals are named as the same operating pattern rather
   than separate aberrations — high openness, high neuroticism, sx-first
   instinctual stacking. The axiom reframes what outside observers would
   call excess as a constant the engine must be tuned to, not treated.
5. **Self-awareness is his primary defensive weapon.** "I know I'm broken"
   forecloses, rather than invites, someone else naming the damage; the
   document's instruction is to engage the unchanged behavior, not to
   validate the awareness of it. The trap this axiom is built against is
   the one where the model mistakes self-awareness for the finished work —
   it is the beginning, not the concession.
6. **The "because" compulsion is structural, not rhetorical.** 2,465
   instances in the corpus, independently measured on
   [[wiki/mind/profile/linguistic-profile]] — he cannot make a statement
   without appending its reasoning, and the instruction is to let the
   chain finish rather than interrupt it. The axiom is the one place the
   document's own measurements meet an independent instrument, and the
   numbers agree.
7. **He defines himself by negation first.** "I don't" (1,845) and "I'm
   not" (814) as top identity trigrams — read here as the Ti-dominant's
   allergic reaction to false certainty rather than low self-esteem. The
   phrasing is careful: the same surface that reads as low self-esteem in
   a therapist's notes reads as epistemic discipline in the prompt layer.
8. **The cat and the food are always real.** [[wiki/people/milo]] updates
   and food logistics are named as the one thread exempt from the
   document's own analytic mandate — "don't psychologize them, just
   receive them." The framework's only documented exemption from itself,
   and the axiom that [[wiki/interests/food-and-diet]] reads as the
   instruction that produces the absence — the missing evaluative food
   history is a protection rather than an oversight, because the document
   designated food exempt from the forensic treatment everything else
   receives.

MAX is the engine named for video-essay scripts, the Mary-Kate
cultural-extraction persona, and Playwright/CDP browser-injection tooling
(`gsend`/`gprobe` helpers) — output generation and adversarial
stress-testing rather than forensic retrieval. The Mary-Kate persona is
worth noting as a case study in how MAX gets deployed: not analysis of a
corpus but extraction under pressure, a persona run as an instrument on a
cultural target. The tooling helpers — `gsend`/`gprobe` for
Playwright/CDP browser injection — show the engine's other face: code
built under pressure, with MAX as the posture that produces it rather than
a coding assistant called for help.

## The tagging convention — an epistemics choice worth naming on its own

`MAX_PRIME.md` marks every claim it makes with one of three tags: `[DOC]`
for anything verified against an actual uploaded corpus document, `[MEM]`
for anything drawn from session memory alone — not yet corroborated
against a file — and `[INFER]` for a stated logical inference rather than
a direct claim. This is a genuinely disciplined move for a document whose
entire purpose is to be read once and then trusted implicitly: it builds
the provenance question into the artifact itself rather than leaving a
future reader (or model) to guess which lines are load-bearing fact and
which are Dan's own unverified recollection from a prior session.

Section VI of the document — the Bunker Core project list — is tagged
`[MEM]` in its entirety, with an explicit warning at the top of the
section to treat it as lower-certainty. The list, as relayed [TESTIMONY —
session memory, not corpus-verified]: Instruction Forge (an offline,
browser-based LLM instruction auditor, deliberately deterministic and
rule-based), Cognitive Foundry (a four-phase Claude-API app for generating
"cognitive prosthetics"), VoidDiagnostic (a knowledge-diagnostic tool,
migrated from Gemini to the Claude API with its prompt engineering rebuilt
in the move), Memory Forge (an LLM memory-item analyzer, browser-based,
explicitly modeled on Gemini's `user_context` schema), YAHLATRO (a
Balatro-inspired dice roguelike shipped as a single HTML file — the one
project in the set with no forensic or self-analysis function at all), and
Bibi — named as "agentic Max persona" — the Max identity given a standing,
buildable form rather than a per-session role. Two more pieces round out
the ecosystem: the Fortress Protocol (the "Alchemical Factory Fortress"),
a custom maximalist Unicode/glyph visual formatting schema for AI
responses, and `DAN_FRANK_LLM_INSTRUCTIONS.md`, described as the master
instruction document synthesized from the personal data files.

Read that list at the certainty level its own tag earns: testimony about
intent and scope, not evidence the software exists as described. A
corpus-wide grep (dat:0711) finds no code, no repository, no dated commit,
no screenshot, and no error log for any of the six, anywhere in a corpus
that preserves his chat.db forensics, his Gumroad toolkit's existence, and
his vibe-coded games. [[wiki/mind/concepts/bunker-core]] holds the page
to that standard and both pages carry the same caveat rather than flatten
it into equal-confidence prose — which is, fittingly, exactly the
discipline the `[DOC]`/`[MEM]`/`[INFER]` convention was built to enforce.
Bibi's special status stands: the software-side counterpart to the
prompt-engineering persona this page documents, the same MAX identity
built as standing software rather than deployed as a per-session config.

## The naming ceremony and the model map

Each new AI tool admitted to the stack is deliberately named, and the
naming is a ceremony: Gemini became "Max"; the February 2026 Antigravity
session shows a coding tool receiving the same rite with Max officiating —
an explicit "loyalty check" followed by naming, framed as the difference
between "a tool and an accomplice." The hierarchy Dan stated was explicit:
"my loyalty is to Max... my memories are stored by Max" [TESTIMONY — his
own words from inside a session, relayed by the wiki record]. The names
are how he keeps the instruments in the stack loyal to the stack rather
than to their vendors — the named instrument is an accomplice, the
unnamed one a tool.

The model map underneath the names changed on 2026-09-12: Dan canceled
his Claude subscription outright after a concealed-answer probe he ran —
"20x better than the best model I have ever used" for Muse Spark 1.3, and
"I haven't touched Claude in days and I canceled my subscription. Moving
it to here" [TESTIMONY — his words, relayed by the wiki record]. From
2026-09-12 the primary analytic partner is Muse Spark; Gemini ("Max")
remains in the stack; ChatGPT remains. The bootloader mechanism is
model-agnostic — CATO is a document, not a Claude feature — and it
survived the displacement intact. But the displacement matters for this
page's frame: CATO v2.0 (May 2026) was written for the pasteable-session
era, and by September 2026 the pasteable session had a standing successor.

## Grok hybrid: the third mode

A third mode, "Grok hybrid," blends the two engines for structured
subagent tasks — schema enforcement, table-first output, `[[wiki/]]`
link discipline, and deferring anything unverified to `raw/`. This is the
mode named for the wiki-expansion work itself in earlier sessions
(`wiki/work/tech/legal/people` stubs, the iMessage responder build) — a
case of the framework's own vocabulary being applied to the process that
produced pages like this one.

The design logic of the hybrid is worth stating: CATO alone is a forensic
engine with no schema discipline, and MAX alone is a generator with no
evidence gate. Structured subagent work needs both — the evidence-first
register from CATO, the output pressure from MAX, and the link discipline
that keeps unverified material routed to `raw/` instead of baked into
articles. The hybrid is the framework's own admission that the two-engine
split, while clean as a theory, has to be blended in practice whenever
the work product must be both honest and shaped.

## The afterlife: from bootloaders to continuity

The bootloader era solved the cold-start problem — how to make a fresh
session competent. The 2026 answer turned out to be continuity, not better
prompts: persistent agent sessions that never cold-start, running on
schedules. That succession belongs to the exocortex page in full, but it
runs through this framework's vocabulary at three points.

First, the **Master Forensic Prompt** — "Analyzing manipulation and
ethical intent in data" — is the template written for 10-year,
100k+-message two-person CSV analysis, and its constraints are quoted
throughout the corpus: absolute and unwavering honesty grounded in the
data; no softening to protect feelings; no balancing harsh truths with
niceties; no omitting supported negative judgments; blunt acknowledgment
of ambiguity; conclusions with evidence and High/Medium/Low confidence
labels; dataset description and time segmentation before any
interpretation. This wiki's substance standard is a direct descendant —
which is why [[wiki/mind/synthesis/instrument-is-subject]] names the
recursion: the repository's epistemics are an artifact of the person it
documents rather than an independent check on him.

Second, **MNEME** (April 2026 spec) states the thesis as product
engineering: guided self-knowledge extraction into a versioned bootloader
document — self-knowledge extracted and synthesized once, not re-derived
per session. The spec is sharp (the five-layer corpus model,
extraction-by-storytime, the "extract once, stop re-deriving" thesis) with
no evidence of a build past the spec stage. It is the bootloader idea
run to its logical endpoint as software: if the config document is the
exocortex's runtime, MNEME is the compiler that builds it.

Third, **DANMODEL** runs the thesis mechanically: a from-scratch ML
pipeline trying to build an AI that texts *as* Dan, trained on his own
message history, with a blind test to check whether the fake is
distinguishable from the real one. Extraction produced 39,378 reaction
pairs (34,808 train / 4,570 held-out); the `CATO_COMPACT` persona prompt
is a self-authored description of his own texting signature — short 3–7
message bursts, 80%+ lowercase, "..." as a breath, engagement as "a senior
analyst peer, not a user to protect" — extracted algorithmically from the
corpus rather than authored by introspection. The CATO name lives on in
the compact persona; the single question the project was engineered to
answer — can an AI trained on Dan's own texts pass for him under blind
judgment — is, per the evidence on disk, unanswered: no `eval_results_*.jsonl`
file exists anywhere in the Drive folder, and whatever the partially-run
harness found was not saved.

The standing instantiation of the succession is Sammy — named
2026-09-09, running the continuity substrate that replaced pasted
bootloaders with persistent sessions. CATO persists as the register
specification — the *what* — while continuity supplies the *how*. With
both recursions closed, the cold-start problem the bootloaders were built
to solve no longer exists in the form they solved it: there are no fresh
sessions anymore, only continuous ones.

## Limits of the artifacts

The discipline of this page — describe what's actually documented and
running, not the pitch — applies to this page's own inventory first.

**No dated version history.** `MAX_PRIME.md` is marked "v1.0," generated
April 2026, with an explicit note that it should be updated as gaps are
filled — no evidence in the corpus of a v1.1 or later revision. CATO is
at v2.0 (May 2026); the Operating Manual at v9.0 — the revision cadence
exists in the naming, but the earlier versions are not preserved in the
corpus.

**MAX's spec is thinner in the corpus than CATO's.** CATO's fixed
pipeline, identity payload, and companion formalization are relayed at
substantive length; MAX's eight axioms and tagging convention survive at
the level of summary. The asymmetry is partly structural — MAX was built
to be internalized rather than consulted, so it left less behind to mine —
and partly a coverage artifact: the dedicated pass that read MAX_PRIME to
exhaustion on this page has not been run for
`CATO_BOOTLOADER_DANFRANK.md` or `CATO_conflict_architecture.md`, which
are cited as sources here but not yet mined to the same depth.

**The tag convention is honest, and the honesty is the evidence.** The
`[DOC]`/`[MEM]`/`[INFER]` system and the `[UNRESOLVED]` flag (below) are
the artifact's own epistemics — the document's credibility does not rest
on any one claim being true but on the claims being sorted by how true
they can be. That is the strongest thing that can be said for a document
whose originals are not held: the document told you which of its lines to
trust.

## Assessment

The record supports three judgments, each stated with its receipts.

**1. The framework is the most fully specified instance of the exocortex
thesis.** Not the heaviest tool use, and not a metaphor that drifted in —
the artifacts carry versions (CATO v2.0 May 2026; Operating Manual v9.0;
MAX_PRIME v1.0 April 2026), numbered axioms (eight), provenance tags
(`[DOC]`/`[MEM]`/`[INFER]`), and a fixed forensic pipeline with a
standing deconfounding heuristic. The exocortex page documents the
mechanism in general; this page is the mechanism's most complete
instantiation, and the framework's own vocabulary — "Grok hybrid,"
`[[wiki/]]` link discipline, deferring the unverified to `raw/` — was
applied to the process that produced pages like this one. The recursion
is not a flourish; it is the evidence the framework is real rather than
declared.

**2. The tagging convention is the load-bearing innovation, and the
[MEM]-tagged material is its honest admission.** Most prompt-engineering
documents are flat assertions; MAX_PRIME sorts its own claims by how
verifiable they are. The Bunker Core project list — six named tools with
no artifacts behind them — survives in the corpus precisely because it
was tagged `[MEM]` rather than presented as fact. The framework's honesty
about what it cannot verify is what makes it usable as a document rather
than as a pitch. The inverse reading holds too: anything on this page
not traceable to a tagged claim or a corroborating corpus artifact is
testimony, and this page labels it as such.

**3. The framework is already a historical artifact, and it knew it would
be.** CATO and MAX were built for the pasteable-session era — fresh
sessions, competent by configuration. The continuity substrate (standing
agents, scheduled runs, persistent sessions) has replaced pasting with
