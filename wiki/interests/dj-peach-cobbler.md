# DJ Peach Cobbler

## The name is a misnomer

DJ Peach Cobbler is not a DJ and has nothing to do with Dan's dance-music listening. He is an American YouTuber and animator making long-form satirical video essays: analytical deep-dives on video games (Dark Souls 3's bosses, Cyberpunk 2077's redemption, Far Cry's failures), a multi-year Ancient Rome series ("What did the Romans think of the Greeks / the Jews / barbarians & Carthage?", the two-part "When Rome Met Persia"), and semi-regular news-parody installments ("Cobbler Crackdown") touching finance and politics. The channel launched in September 2013 and sits at roughly 528,000 subscribers across 118 videos and 55.6 million views as of mid-2026, with a slow upload cadence (about two videos every three months) and a ~24-minute average runtime. All of the above is web-sourced public fact, not corpus-attested.

The entry matters because the obvious assumption — a dance-music DJ/producer — was wrong, and the record is unambiguous about it: the name appears zero times in his Spotify history and zero times in the Exportify CSVs (the only "peach" hits are Hobo Johnson's "Peach Scone," unrelated). The relationship is entirely a viewing one. Note for future cross-linking: the forthcoming dance-music-only-phases synthesis covers his two eras of exclusive dance-music listening; this figure belongs nowhere near it.

## What the corpus actually attests

Two facts, both documented:

**1. The tweet.** On December 7, 2024, at 16:57 UTC, Dan tweeted: "DJ Peach Cobbler is our only hope." 13 likes, 0 replies, 0 reposts. The surrounding tweets that day are about the Luigi Mangione / UnitedHealthcare CEO-killer news cycle ("It's gonna be so sad when they ID the CEO killer and his online footprint will ruin the nonpartisan consensus"). In context it reads as an ironic anointment — the essayist as the only trustworthy voice in a disinformation cycle — in Dan's standard deadpan register, not a declaration of fandom.

**2. The watch history.** The July 2026 YouTube export logs 17 real watches on the channel across August 2025 – July 2026. It sits in the long-tail commentary tier of that year's diet: well below the spine (Breaking Points 112, LastWeekTonight 95, Majority Report 93, Tor's Cabinet of Curiosities 91) and alongside the other new-to-the-corpus names the export flags — We're In Hell, JREG, STRANGE ÆONS, exurb1a. A steady trickle, roughly one watch every three weeks, sustained across the full year window.

## Placement

The channel occupies the essayist-commentary tier of the 2025–26 media diet: too long and too analytical for the outrage-feed, too satirical for the straight news shows. The Rome-history content is a plausible affinity vector with Dan's Roman Republic interest — stated here as inference only, since the watch record is channel-level counts and no individual video titles are attested for him.

## What we don't know

Which videos he watched. Whether he subscribes. Whether the December 2024 tweet referenced a specific video. Any off-platform engagement. The 17-watch count comes from a single-account export and, per the youtube-watch-history page's own unresolved caveat, multiple YouTube accounts share the Google login — the figure is a lower bound, not a total.
