# Stand-Up Comedy

For about a year, from March 2019 to March 2020, Dan Frank did stand-up in New York. By his own account he was often bad at it and knew it. The documented sets end when the clubs closed in March 2020. After they reopened he still talked about performing, but the record does not show him doing it again. Before that he had spent most of a decade listening to the comedy that trained him, and most of 2018 promising himself he would try it. After it he kept going to shows. The record holds his first open mic, his first material, a class he signed up for, the bit that died on the word "escort", the friend who was the only person he knew who had seen him perform, the night Jim Gaffigan dropped in unannounced, and two photographs from the audience at *Legion of Skanks*.

Most of that record is his own messages, not anyone's summary. This revision reads it directly: the held iMessage corpus (`raw/imessage/messages-part1-2011-2019.csv`, `raw/imessage/messages-part2-2019-2026.csv`, cited below as **[IM]**) and the Facebook Messenger export (`raw/messenger-drive-2026-09-12/messenger_drive_canonical.jsonl`, cited as **[FB]**). Both were on disk and unread by every earlier pass on this page. A complete dated log is printed at the bottom.

> **CORRECTED [2026-09-27] — four claims the primary record overturns.**
>
> 1. **"The corpus's only direct evidence of a completed open-mic performance"** (the October 2, 2019 Bobby Cole message) is wrong, as [`dat:1340`](../../kb/data/1340-standup-only-openmic-evidence-claim-fails.md) had already shown from one 2020 message. The first open mic is dated to early March 2019: *"I just did my first open mic the other day"* (to Bobby Cole, 2019-03-16, [FB]). The complete log below has 23 dated rows about performing, classes or material, from March 2019 to June 2020.
> 2. **The October 2 message itself is verified.** `dat:1340` held it as *unchecked*, since the Bobby Cole thread was unheld. It is in [FB], word for word, split across several messages (2019-10-02, thread "Bobby Cole").
> 3. **The Chip Chipperson taping was the night of Friday, December 7, 2018**, not December 8. Dan forwarded Annie her ticket on December 7 at 14:21 ET ([IM]), and his account to Bobby, in the past tense, carries the export stamp 2018-12-08 04:51 ([FB]).
> 4. **The live run did not start on 2019-04-29.** Dan saw Ron Bennington at the Comedy Cellar on March 15, 2019 and bought tickets for The Stand for March 23 ([FB], [IM]). He had been going to New York comedy clubs since at least May 2012 ([FB], to Nathan King). The 2019 itinerary table from `Dan Profile.txt` ([`dat:1341`](../../kb/data/1341-standup-comedy-page-remainder.md)) is a subset of his attendance, not the whole of it.

## Upstream: a decade in the radio

Before he saw it live, he heard it. The largest single thing in Dan's YouTube history is the *Opie & Anthony* shock-radio archive: about 450 watches across three fan-archive channels, with the account's all-time peak in 2012–2013 ([[wiki/interests/opie-and-anthony]]; [[wiki/self/youtube-watch-history]]). On 18 November 2012 he asked two O&A staffers how to apply for an internship and said the same day that he had sent the application ([[wiki/people/bobby-cole]], citing [[wiki/mind/synthesis/twitter-2012]]). The comics who later appear on his live list (Rich Vos, Vic Henley, Jim Norton, Ron Bennington, Dave Attell, Big Jay Oakerson, Bobby Kelly) are the O&A and *Ron & Fez* cast and their circle ([[wiki/interests/opie-and-anthony]]). The friendship with [[wiki/people/bobby-cole|Bobby Cole]], second-largest thread in the Facebook archive, was built on trading clips from that radio world for nine years ([[wiki/people/bobby-cole]]).

The early live record is thin but real. In January 2011 a friend, Adam Lucidi, invited Dan and Alexis to see him at the New York Comedy Club on East 24th Street ([FB], 2011-01-20). On 3 May 2012 (export stamp 00:24), writing to Nathan King: *"hey dude i've gotta roll, i'm going to a show at the comedy cellar"* ([FB]; [[wiki/people/nathan-king]]). On 30 August 2013 he was at the Oddball Comedy & Curiosity Festival in Burgettstown, PA (Chappelle, Mulaney, Hannibal Buress), which the concert log records as its only stand-up entry ([[wiki/interests/concert-record/festivals/oddball-comedy-curiosity-festival]]). His Facebook likes put Gotham Comedy Club, the Comedy Cellar and The Stand in a 2017–2020 "comedy/skeptic" cluster alongside Bill Burr, Jim Norton, George Carlin and *Comedy Bang! Bang!* ([[wiki/self/facebook/interests]]). He was rating his own jokes by that last show by December 2015: *"My jokes are comedy bang bang level bad"* (to Annie, 2015-12-30, [IM]).

## The promise, 2017–2019

The intent is dated long before the act. At 13:39 ET on 28 December 2017, to Annie: *"One thing I'm going to force myself to do in 2018 is try stand up at least once."* A minute later: *"I'm gonna ask Vanessa where the open mic was she went to and try to get a couple minutes of material together"* ([IM]; verified in [`dat:1339`](../../kb/data/1339-standup-ambition-messages-in-held-corpus.md)). On 9 March 2018: *"well...until i start doing stand up"* ([IM]). On 10 August 2018, to [[wiki/people/joe-oshnack|Joe Oshnack]]: *"My self promise is that I try standup before 30"* ([FB]). On 26 October 2018: *"I'd like to start trying comedy"* (the row has no counterparty field; it is one of the corpus's 3,086 unattributable sent messages, [`CORPUS_POLICY.md`](../../CORPUS_POLICY.md)). On 2 November 2018, to [[wiki/people/davey-fitzpatrick|Davey Fitzpatrick]]: *"I'm gonna do whatever for a day job and try my hand at comedy"* ([IM]).

In 2018 none of that happened. He was in Uniontown, the caddie season was ending, and his grandmother had died that April ([[wiki/timeline/periods/2018-deep-cycle]]). What he did do in 2018 was drive to watch it. On 1 December he asked Bobby Cole *"You wanna go?"* about a Chip Chipperson show in Philadelphia and added *"Nana cumia will be there"* ([FB]). He forwarded Annie a ticket on December 3 and again on December 7 ([IM]; the ticket rows are in [`dat:1339`](../../kb/data/1339-standup-ambition-messages-in-held-corpus.md)). After the show he told Bobby the truth, which he said he would admit to no one else:

> *"...pretty bad"* / *"Like I'm not admitting that to anyone, myself included, but I didn't laugh once. I did crack a smile a few times."* / *"I got to philly 1 minute before the show started and we left 5 minutes before it was over and went straight back. 9+ hours in the car for 65 minutes of horrible anti-humor"* ([FB], 2018-12-08)

A day later he said *"I'm glad I went! I really am"*, and *"It was just fucking cool to watch them do 'radio' after 10 years of listening to them all day everyday."* The Chipperson trip also brought Annie into the radio world. On December 13 (export stamp): *"since we went to that chip so my girlfriend has been into the show in a big way and it's the best to be able to car crash her and she gets it"* ([FB]). Sixteen months later he wrote that *"both girlfriends that i have exposed to this world of comedy"* preferred Jim Norton's stage act to his riffing (to Bobby, 2020-04-19, [FB]).

The move to New York was, in his own words, for this. To [[wiki/people/tom|Tom Maison]] on 15 February 2019, ten days before the lease signing at [[wiki/places/307-e-76th-st|307 E 76th St]]: *"The whole point of moving to nyc is to try standup"* ([FB]). On February 19, [[wiki/people/rj-ritchey|RJ Ritchey]] answered Dan's *"Tragedy + time = comedy"* with *"Yesterday I literally had the thought of you doing stand up and using that story in a bit I swear I did"* ([IM], handle `+1724…4280` = RJ per [[wiki/people/rj-ritchey]]). That is the "friend" of earlier versions of this page, now identified. [`dat:1339`](../../kb/data/1339-standup-ambition-messages-in-held-corpus.md) listed the line as not found. It is in the held corpus at 2019-02-19 15:28 UTC.

## The stage, March 2019 – March 2020

**The first set.** On 16 March 2019 Dan wrote to Bobby that he had seen Ron Bennington the night before (*"He's doing 2 sets a week at the cellar"*) and had *"met vos for a second"*, then: *"I just did my first open mic the other day."* Bobby asked how it went. Dan's answer is the first description of his material anywhere in the record:

> *"It was okay. Most of the people in attendance at those things are the other comics that are waiting to go up so it's kinda hard to get a real feel for how good the material is"* / *"But it's def less intimidating than I expected. I did a couple minutes about how millennials are awful and going to ruin the world and then a couple minutes about what happens to your stuff after you die (how little nostalgic value it has as it gets passed down from generation to generation)"* / *"Honestly anyone who has absorbed as much comedy need radio as we have could do it as long as you aren't super adverse to being on stage"* ([FB], 2019-03-16)

**The five minutes.** On 2 April, RJ handed him a stage name: *"Dude I came up with your comedy name for when u do standup/YouTube. It's 'Nad Knarf'"*, which is "Dan Frank" reversed ([IM]). On 12 April, to his childhood friend [[wiki/people/new-jim-shaffer|Jim Shaffer]]: *"heres my comedy set so far"* and then *"im just trying to develop a solid 5 minutes because thats usually what you get at an open mic"* ([IM]; verified in [`dat:1339`](../../kb/data/1339-standup-ambition-messages-in-held-corpus.md)). On 18 April, to [[wiki/people/ally-lubin|Ally Lubin]]: *"okay so this is part of the material i'm writing to try doing stand up but how fucking dangerous is that technology"* ([FB]). On 20 May, to [[wiki/people/shelbie-breakiron|Shelbie Breakiron]], in the middle of a hard stretch with Annie: *"i've been writing new material like crazy though and had some better shows so at least my comedy is benefiting from my recently. tumultuous life"* ([IM]).

**Most nights a week.** By summer he described it as routine. On 3 July, to [[wiki/people/mike-hinkle|Mike Hinkle]]: *"surviving here and trying to get some work doing standup comedy"* ([IM]). On 18 July, to [[wiki/people/seth-ledonne|Seth LeDonne]]: *"also if you're ever looking for something terribly unfunny to do, i'm doing stand up most nights a week"* ([FB]). On 20 July he turned the Trinity St. Clair booking into a bit and tried it on Annie in text ([[wiki/people/trinity-st-clair]]).

**The class.** At 04:01 ET on 4 August 2019, to Annie: *"i just signed up for a class. every monday for the next six weeks from 7-10 at gotham comedy club"*. Six hours later he was passing on stagecraft (*"when you get on stage you're supposed to take the mic out of the stand and then set the stand behind you"*) and a link to `manhattancomedyschool.com` ([IM]). On August 7, to Ally: *"great because i'm finally doing open mic's and taking some standup classes and that's coming along"*. On August 18: *"i'm in a panic _ i have comedy class tomorrow night and no material written yet and i'm going to bomb again"*. On September 1: *"i'm stressed...i'm supposed to do an open mic tomorrow and i know i'm going to eat shit"* ([IM]; the September 1 row is also in [`dat:1339`](../../kb/data/1339-standup-ambition-messages-in-held-corpus.md)). The record does not show whether he finished the six weeks.

**The bit that died.** The October 2 exchange with Bobby is the fullest first-person account of a set going wrong, and it is now verified in [FB]. It begins with Dan telling Bobby he had walked into the SiriusXM "fishbowl" to apply for a job ([[wiki/people/bobby-cole]] covers that half). Then:

> *"btw comedy is SO much harder than i thought"* / *"the only thing that has kept me going to open mics is one thought:"* / *"'rich vos AND vic henley can do this'"*

Bobby said he had done an open mic in Pittsburgh himself, *"10 min on psycho ex girlfriends"*. Dan: *"the writing seems to be the easy part"* / *"it's turning that into a performance that i can't figure out yet"* / *"also all of my material is just me saying a bunch of ways 'i am a drug addict and a failure in most endeavors'"* / *"which, i guess, is not funny in and of itself."* Then the specifics:

> *"it's such a weird time to start a career in comedy...RIGHT at the moment where everything is being scrutinized for content"* / *"i have a bit about a hooker using my credit card to cut a line of coke and not realizing she was going to remember the digits on the card to buy herself stuff online"* / *"and i barely finished the word 'escort' before i heard groans"*

He finished by encouraging Bobby (*"'the first 1000 times on stage don't matter'"*) and with a kind word about Vic Henley, who he said was *"really the only notable comic i've gotten to interact with on multiple occasions"* and *"like the sweetest dude in the world"* ([FB], 2019-10-02). On August 14 he had told Annie *"i just saw vic henley."* ([IM]); whether that meant in person is not stated. Bobby told him on 6 April 2020 that Henley had died ([FB]).

**What the material was about.** Beyond the escort bit and the first-night material, the record names five more subjects, all Dan's own life. The first is a friend: *"i have a joke in my act about you"*, told to [[wiki/people/tan-calabrese|Tan Calabrese]] on October 12, who replied *"Super disrespectful... but awesome you're doing standup!"* ([IM]). The second is his first sexual experience with a man: *"i'm already talking about my first experience with another guy in my set"*, to [[wiki/people/dan-polyak|Dan Polyak]] on October 25 ([FB]; [[wiki/people/dan-polyak]]). The third is Kobe Bryant, the day he died: *"i'm busy trying to come up with kobe jokes for an open mic tonight"*, to Trevor on 26 January 2020 ([IM]; also in [`dat:1339`](../../kb/data/1339-standup-ambition-messages-in-held-corpus.md)). The fourth is a five-dollar charity donation: *"i have a bit about donating $5 to someone's cancer fundraiser and then how much time i spent thinking about it and kinda wishing i hadn't"*, to [[wiki/people/bekah-fullem|Bekah Fullem]] on 8 February 2020 ([FB]). The fifth is a story involving a contact named Bryan ([[wiki/people/bryan-5088682461]]). The pattern is the one he named to Bobby: confession as the premise, himself as the target.

**One witness.** On 4 December 2019, [[wiki/people/trevor|Trevor Bevins]], the travelling caddie from the Nemacolin yard, said *"I caddie for comedians"*. Dan replied *"so far RJ is the only one to have seen me do standup"*, and then *"comedy is a way for me to justify every awful thing i do in life"* and *"at least an open mic you can just leave if you bomb"* ([IM]; [[wiki/people/trevor]], 2019-12 row). RJ had asked on September 30 to be told when Dan was doing an open mic (*"I want to be there"*), and on October 6 wrote *"there's no reason why you couldn't be a good stand up comedian"* ([IM]). The record does not show which night RJ was there.

**The last sets.** Around the New Year's Eve show (the [FB] stamp is 2020-01-01), Dan wrote to Bobby: *"i need to stop going to shows and going to open mics instead 🙄"*. Bobby answered: *"make this the year of the open mic"* ([FB]). On February 10, to Bekah's iMessage handle: *"i did my set last night and then left at 6am for nashua"* ([IM]). That puts a set on the night of February 9. On February 13, to [[wiki/people/matthew-palermo|Matthew Palermo]]: *"(while i eat shit constantly doing open mics)"* ([FB]). On March 28, to a correspondent on `+1724…8111` who was in drama school, he gave his most complete self-assessment:

> *"just open mic"* / *"yeah i've had a few really good sets but it's hard to judge from an open mic because most of the crowd are the other comics"* / *"but i'm staying off social media with it entirely until i get a little bit more developed. i'm not good enough to adopt the term 'comedian' yet"* / *"i can't wait to see what it's like to do comedy in a post-corona world"* ([IM], 2020-03-28)

([`dat:1340`](../../kb/data/1340-standup-only-openmic-evidence-claim-fails.md) found the "few really good sets" line and dated it 2020-03-29 UTC, which is the evening of March 28 in ET.) The same month he told [[wiki/people/jerad-friedline|Jerad Friedline]] *"if i don't make it doing comedy i'm fucked lol"* (March 23) and *"this kind of shit happens to me all the time and it's always so ridiculous that i has no choice other than to pursue comedy"* (March 25) ([IM]; handle `+1916…3615`, attributed to Jerad on [[wiki/people/shane-brannan]]).

**Studying it while the clubs were closed.** He kept reading. On 23 April 2020 he sent Bobby Greg Dean's *Step by Step to Stand-Up Comedy* as a PDF and walked through a Louis C.K. joke to show how its misdirection works. He also recommended *Zen and the Art of Stand-Up Comedy* ([FB]). The same week, to the `…8111` correspondent: *"ive read a lot of comedy books. this one is seriously a game changer"* ([IM]). The receipts are on [[wiki/self/two-year-synthesis]]: *The Hidden Tools of Comedy*, *Comedy Bang! Bang!* seasons and "the stand-up textbook", bought in 2019. On June 2, 2020 he named where his delivery came from: *"i modeled my cadence doing standup from Movies with Mikey"* ([IM]). His hopes for reopening shrank as the year went on. On April 24 he wrote *"i'm excited to get to try stuff out now....in 2025 when comedy clubs reopen"* (to Bobby, [FB]). On August 22 it was *"every comedy stage is still closed"*, and on September 30 *"so since comedy is dead forever"* ([IM]). On April 24–25, trading jokes with Trevor over *Call of Duty*, he asked *"am i a comedian yet"*. Trevor: *"Almost. You're just not that funny yet"* ([IM]).

## The room, as audience

Dan's own performing stopped with the pandemic. Going to shows did not. The venue that recurs is **The Stand** on East 16th Street. On 4 November 2019 Dan told a contact he had to *"make it down to 16th street to do comedy by 9"*. That same night was the *Legion of Skanks* taping he had told Annie *"we'll be at the stand for"* ([IM], 2019-10-29 and 2019-11-04).

**Autumn 2019, day by day.** On Monday, October 28, he didn't get into *Skanks*. On Tuesday the 29th he saw Colin Quinn (*"i'm going to see CQ tonight to make up for not getting into skanks"*, [FB]). On Wednesday the 30th came the itinerary's six-comic night, and Dan's own report: *"i was at a comedy show tonight at jim gaffigan did an unannounced drop in set"* / *"i went to see shane gillis lol"* ([IM]; [`dat:1341`](../../kb/data/1341-standup-comedy-page-remainder.md)). Then *Skanks* on Monday, November 4, with three tickets, and The Stand again on Tuesday the 5th (Tim Dillon, Ian Fidance), per the itinerary and his messages to Annie and Bobby ([IM], [FB], [`dat:1341`](../../kb/data/1341-standup-comedy-page-remainder.md)). On November 14, to RJ: *"we were there the night that big jay oakerson got fucked by a gun, did you happen to catch any of that"* ([IM]). That is a contemporaneous record of the "gun as a dildo" taping which Dan's 2026-09-11 testimony places him at, and which [`int:legion-of-skanks-dildo-episode-attendance-testimony`](../../kb/interpretations/legion-of-skanks-dildo-episode-attendance-testimony.md) had held as testimony only. Its date is not fixed. The two candidates in the record are the November 4 *Skanks* night and the itinerary's November 7 entry (Bobby Kelly, Big Jay Oakerson).

**Skanksmas.** Tickets arrived on December 9 and 22 ([IM], ticketing handle). At 21:40 on 23 December 2019 Dan took the first photograph below, and at 23:08 ET he texted RJ *"I'm at legion of skanks"* ([IM]). To Bobby, the same night: *"Legion of skanks Christmas show"*, *"watch it when it comes out tomorrow and you'll see me"* ([FB]). The photograph is taken from a seat behind a man in a grey hoodie. A projection screen reads **MERRY SKANKSMAS** with crossed candy canes, a man in an elf hat stands at the left of the frame, and a Santa hat is visible at the far right. It is a full room at small round tables ([`src:legion-of-skanks-tapings-photos-2019-12-23-2020-08-25`](../../kb/sources/legion-of-skanks-tapings-photos-2019-12-23-2020-08-25.md); EXIF 2019-12-23 21:40:36 local, GPS 40.735603, −73.988411). The GPS fix is on the East 16th Street block. [`dat:1408`](../../kb/data/1408-legion-of-skanks-tapings-2019-12-23-2020-08-25.md) calls The Stand a "proximity inference". The "16th street" message of November 4 is independent support for it.

**New Year's Eve.** The Stand confirmed him for its New Year's Eve show on December 30. On December 31 he passed on a rumour that Louis C.K. would drop in. After midnight: *"the guy sitting at my table literally yelled something like 'i bet u wish that SNL didn't fire you!' to shane gillis"*, *"which is one reason why i said 'good set' to all of the comics i saw on my walk out"*, and *"vos opened and tim dillon closed"* ([IM], 2019-12-30 – 2020-01-01).

**The election show.** On 24 August 2020 he told RJ *"guess who is going to be at the legion of skanks presidential election show tomorrow"* and sent Bobby the guest list: *"ari shaffir, shane gillis, dan soder, ron bennington, anthony cumia, zac amico, robert kelly, tim dillon, andrew schulz , and a 'special guest'"* ([FB]). The second photograph was taken at 20:13 on August 25. It shows an outdoor patio at night under a lit marquee, with American-flag foil balloons, potted palms, and a masked man standing in the doorway. On the table in the foreground are a drink, a folded face mask, and a hand holding a cup of lemonade. Part of a *Jack Daniel's* neon sign shows in the window ([`src:…`](../../kb/sources/legion-of-skanks-tapings-photos-2019-12-23-2020-08-25.md); EXIF 2020-08-25 20:13:13 local, GPS 40.735631, −73.988183). It was a COVID-era show held outside, the same summer he wrote that every stage was closed. Afterward he reported to RJ that *"ari was one of the four people running for president of the skanks"* ([FB]).

**After.** On 3 June 2021, to Bobby: *"Btw comedy is back on in nyc. I'm going to see ari, big Jay and Attell on Sunday"* ([FB]). On 3 August 2021, to a friend: *"Like I'm still waiting tables and trying to do comedy living in a studio apartment in nyc. That's the kind of thing you should have outgrown by 25 lol"* ([FB], thread "CJ Lindsay"). That is the last first-person statement of intent to perform in the record. No set after March 2020 is documented. On 1 June 2022: *"I go to a lot of standup shows lol"* ([FB], [[wiki/people/valeria-iglesias-cid]]). The later record has comedy as listening, not doing: Tim Dillon's podcast in March 2026, *Comedy Bang! Bang!* again in August 2026 ([IM]).

## What it amounted to

Dan kept a movie about a delusional aspiring stand-up, *The King of Comedy*, in his eleven-film canon. The corpus holds no discussion of it at all ([[wiki/interests/film-canon]], "The King of Comedy" row: 0 hits). The record of his own attempt reads nothing like Rupert Pupkin's. Dan's self-reports are accurate and deflationary: *"It was okay"*, *"I'm going to bomb again"*, *"i'm not good enough to adopt the term 'comedian' yet"*. He could name the syllable where a room turned. He stayed off social media on purpose until he was better. What drove him was a working comic's floor, *"rich vos AND vic henley can do this"*, not the dream of the big stage.

Four existing pages depend on this one and now need correcting:

- [[wiki/mind/concepts/social-anxiety]] lists *"A completed stand-up open mic, October 2, 2019 — the single most exposure-dense act in the biography, done once and never repeated."* The record contradicts "once" and "never repeated". It shows a first set in early March 2019, a report of "most nights a week" by July, and a set on 9 February 2020. The page's argument about performed versus unstructured exposure is, if anything, strengthened: he did it repeatedly.
- [[wiki/mind/psychosexual/developmental-origins]] dates the comedy "overlay" to 2019 and re-dates it to the 2012–13 radio binge. The May 2012 Comedy Cellar message and the 2011 club invitation support the re-dating.
- [[wiki/mind/synthesis/interests-as-era-markers]] and [[wiki/mind/synthesis/intake-constancy]] treat 2019 stand-up as the New York era's marker. That holds, with its edges now dated: first set March 2019, last documented set February 2020, clubs closed March 2020.
- [[wiki/people/bobby-cole]] calls the October 2 passage "the only detailed first-person account in the entire wiki of Dan actually standing on a stage and bombing." It is the most detailed account of bombing. It is not the only account of performing.

The grammar the stage asked for (the five-minute unit, the confession as premise, the misdirection he studied in Greg Dean) is the grammar [[wiki/mind/profile/linguistic-profile]] finds in how he writes to everyone.

## Coverage limits

- **No set is witnessed by anyone but Dan, in the record.** Every performance above is Dan's own report. RJ Ritchey is named as the one person who saw him, and his account of it is not in the record. There is no video, no set list, no venue name for any open mic, and no dated count of how many he did.
- **"Most nights a week"** (July 2019) is Dan's own claim to a new acquaintance. The dated P rows are much sparser, but they are messages *about* sets, not a log of them.
- **The club itinerary** (the [DP] rows) comes from an unheld dossier. Its October 29–30 and November 4–5 rows are corroborated by independent messages. Its April 29, November 7 and November 21 rows are not.
- **Not found in any held source**: the 2018-12-03 *"We're going to Philadelphia for a taping of a standup special"* message to Suzanne, the 2019-04-15 *"i already got a third ticket for you"* message, and the *"$7 a piece"* reply, all quoted by earlier versions of this page ([`dat:1339`](../../kb/data/1339-standup-ambition-messages-in-held-corpus.md); rechecked 2026-09-27 against [IM] and [FB]). They are unverified, not disproven. The Chipperson trip itself is corroborated by the tickets and the Bobby thread.
- **The gun taping's date** is not fixed (see "The room").
- **Messenger timestamps.** The time zone of the [FB] export is inferred from one anchor, and [FB] dates are given as the export stamps them. If the export is in fact UTC, rows stamped between 00:00 and 05:00 belong to the previous evening in ET. The rows affected here are 2012-05-03, 2018-12-08, 2018-12-13, 2019-12-21 and 2020-01-01, and none of them changes a conclusion on this page.
- **Instagram and TikTok DMs** in the same Drive archive ([`dat:1443`](../../kb/data/1443-messenger-drive-correspondents.md)) were searched only in the combined canonical file. Nothing there bears on performing after 2021.
