# Film Canon

The movies favorites list is tiny — 11 titles, unrated, no metadata — so
[[wiki/interests/favorites/art-and-movies]] correctly filed it as a thin
category. But the message record shows film is not a thin interest at all:
it is something Dan *evangelizes*, uses as a compatibility test on
partners, and returns to as a self-mythologizing mirror. The eleven titles
are a canon, not a watch-log.

That was this page's original thesis and it survives. What has changed
since it was written is the evidence underneath it. A direct scan of the
authoritative corpus on 2026-09-10
([`dat:1245`](../../kb/data/1245-film-canon-quote-verification.md)) put
each of the page's seven quoted exemplars to the test: **five verified
verbatim and to the day; two are absent from 192,140 messages.** The two
absences are the two the obligation argument leaned on hardest. That result
is printed in full below rather than summarised away, because it is the
most useful thing this page now contains.

## The list

Parasite; The Prestige; Pulp Fiction; The Witch; The Shining; The King of
Comedy; Taxi Driver; There Will Be Blood; The Graduate; Eyes Wide Shut;
Kill Bill.

Eleven titles, unrated, no creators, no tags, no watch dates. The source is
`raw/self/favorites/FAVS MASTERLIST.csv`, which **is not held in this
repository**. The list is nonetheless better attested than most page
content here: two independent retrievals — the 2026-09-04 corpus page and
the old wiki's own copy — agree on every entry and on the count
([`dat:0196`](../../kb/data/0196-art-and-movies-favorites.md)), and
[[wiki/interests/favorites/art-and-movies]] reproduces the same eleven in a
table. So the list is well-relayed testimony rather than primary evidence,
and the distinction has teeth, as the next section shows.

Three authorial gravity wells account for most of it: **Kubrick** (The
Shining, Eyes Wide Shut, plus 2001 named in messages),
**Scorsese/De Niro** (Taxi Driver, The King of Comedy), and **Tarantino**
(Pulp Fiction, Kill Bill). The remainder — Parasite, The Prestige, The
Witch, There Will Be Blood, The Graduate — are precision, dread, and
unmasking pictures. The tag cluster on his art favorites (mask, wound,
dread, observer, recursive) is the same grammar as this film list
([[wiki/interests/favorites/art-and-movies]]).

### CONTRADICTION [2026-09-21] — two kb nodes print two different canons

The two evidence nodes built over this material do not agree on what the
list contains, and neither noticed.

- [`dat:0196`](../../kb/data/0196-art-and-movies-favorites.md) prints
  eleven titles matching this page exactly, and states that two independent
  retrievals agree "on every count and on the full movie list."
- [`dat:1245`](../../kb/data/1245-film-canon-quote-verification.md), in the
  course of a corpus-verification run, restates the canon in passing as
  **twelve** titles: *2001: A Space Odyssey, Eyes Wide Shut, Taxi Driver,
  King of Comedy, The Godfather, Goodfellas, Pulp Fiction, The Social
  Network, The Dark Knight, Parasite, The Graduate, There Will Be Blood.*

The overlap is six. `dat:1245` adds 2001, The Godfather, Goodfellas, The
Social Network and The Dark Knight; it drops The Prestige, The Witch, The
Shining and Kill Bill. Both nodes cite the same unheld masterlist through
the same relay.

**Not resolved here**, per the layer law's refusal to settle contradictions
automatically. The most economical reading is that `dat:1245`'s list is a
reconstruction from memory or from a different section of the masterlist
rather than a second retrieval, since its own extraction note describes a
Python scan for *quotes*, not a re-read of the list — and the dropped
titles include The Shining, which its own verified quote
("Same director as the shining") depends on. But that is a reading, and
`dat:0196`'s two-retrieval agreement is not proof either, since both
retrievals pass through the same prior wiki. The honest state: **the
canon's membership is testimony, one of the two nodes built over it
disagrees with the other, and the underlying CSV would settle it in one
query if it ever surfaces.**

## Film as proselytizing and compatibility test

Dan does not just watch these; he assigns them. The verified exemplars show
him pushing the canon onto partners and treating an unseen classic as a gap
that has to be closed:

- **2015-12-16:** "But you HAVE to see pulp fiction. I can't believe you've
  made it this far without catching it," in a thread where he's reciting
  Tarantino's filmography ("Directed Pulp Fiction, Kill Bill, inglorious
  basterds, django unchained"). *Corpus-verified.*
- **2016-03-23:** identifying a film as "Same director as the shining" —
  Kubrick as a reference axis. *Corpus-verified.*
- **2017-03-30:** "Did u see the witch" — checking whether someone caught
  the A24 folk-horror pick. *Corpus-verified.*
- **2017-03-29:** naming his Kubrick completism directly — "theres only one
  kubrick movie i haven't seen … and eyes wide shut." *Corpus-verified.*
- **2016-04-10:** "Which means you should have seen citizen Kane, 2001 a
  space odyssey, and the prestige" — a canonical homework list handed to a
  partner. **Not in the corpus.** See below.

### The argument's best evidence is its least evidenced

The homework list is the cleanest possible exhibit for canon-as-obligation:
an explicit curriculum, three named titles, assigned to another person with
a modal verb. It does not reproduce.
[`int:film-canon-obligation`](../../kb/interpretations/film-canon-obligation.md)
states the resulting thesis flatly — "the two quotes that most cleanly
evidence the obligation reading are the two quotes the held corpus does not
contain" — and files the same shape next door, on
[`dat:0618`](../../kb/data/0618-folie-a-deux-cutoff-quotes-two-verified-three-absent.md),
where a music-cutoff claim's two 2019 quotes verify and its three 2025
quotes showing the cutoff *persisting* do not. The generalisation the
ingest reached: **verified soft core, testified hard shell** — the softer,
earlier, mood-flagged material survives verification and the interpretively
load-bearing hardening does not.

Both absences are `never_observed` in years (2016, 2023) with no coverage
gap on paper. That is what makes them load-bearing rather than merely
missing — though `CORPUS_POLICY.md`'s own limits section is the standing
caution against over-reading it: deleted messages are absent from the
source database, 3,086 rows cannot be placed in a thread, and
`never_observed` is not `never_sent`.

The interpretation node offers three alternatives and this page does not
choose between them:

1. **The five verified quotes carry the argument alone.** "You HAVE to see
   pulp fiction" is prescription language; the Kubrick completion target is
   obligation language pointed inward. On this reading the absent quotes
   were load-bearing only in the page's *telling*.
2. **The obligation is self-directed.** The canon is a curriculum Dan sets
   himself, and the messages to others are him thinking aloud. The
   direction of the obligation inverts, and "assigned to others" becomes
   the wiki misreading enthusiasm as pedagogy.
3. **The canon is affinity, not obligation at all**, and the forensic frame
   is this wiki projecting its own seriousness onto ordinary fandom.

Alternative 2 is the one with independent support, and it comes from
outside this page: [[wiki/mind/synthesis/closing-the-set]] measures the
same completism across 2,016 curated entries and finds the unit of
attention is a bounded set with a findable edge, closed by the collector
for his own reasons. On that account the Kubrick line is the primary
datum and the prescriptions to partners are secondary.

## Eyes Wide Shut — the load-bearing one

Of the eleven, Eyes Wide Shut recurs with the most personal weight, and its
meaning shifts over time. This is the page's contribution to
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/interests-as-era-markers]], which files the film
canon as its **control case**: the contents don't rotate, but the readings
do, so even a fixed list dates its own use.

- **2017-03-29** — it is the *last* Kubrick he hasn't seen: a completion
  target, and the sentence [[wiki/mind/synthesis/closing-the-set]] uses to
  prove that where the boundary is a dead director's filmography rather
  than an event, the completism attaches to the director. *Verified.*
- **2019-07-18** — he reaches for it as a lens on his own life during the
  arrangement period: "i feel like i'm living some eyes wide shut shit
  vicariously through her." *Verified.* This is the single most cited line
  on this page across the rest of the wiki:
  [[wiki/mind/psychosexual/orchestration-and-voyeurism]] treats it as the
  clearest primary self-description of the spectator frame — Dan naming the
  structure in his own words rather than a dossier naming it for him. The
  chronology matters: the arrangement had been running since February 2018
  ([[wiki/mind/psychosexual/arrangement-history]]), so the film arrives as
  a *retrospective* frame on something already seventeen months old, not as
  a blueprint for it.
- **2023-12-24** — a household in-joke and ritual: "In a little bit we're
  going to watch my favorite Christmas movie…eyes wide shut lol."
  **Absent from the corpus.** The 2025-10-28 line — "i make the same joke
  about it being my favorite christmas movie" — attests the joke as habit
  without attesting this instance of it.

The film is set at Christmas; naming Kubrick's masked-orgy picture as the
"favorite Christmas movie" is a deadpan register the corpus documents
elsewhere. The three-stage arc — target, self-lens, ritual — is intact in
shape and now carries one verified anchor, one verified anchor, and one
displaced substitute.

## The eleven against the Cato seat — the pass that had not been run

[[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-cato-seat]] argues that every cultural object Dan
has curated for identification occupies one position: *accurate, early, and
unable to intervene.* It lists as its own third gap: "**Nobody has read the
eleven films against the rule.** The canon list carries no tags and no
ratings, and four titles — Pulp Fiction, Kill Bill, The Graduate, Parasite
— are placed here on a reading rather than on evidence."

Here is that pass, with each title marked for what actually attests it.

| Title | Position of its protagonist | Fits the seat? | Attested by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Taxi Driver** | Isolated hyper-observer, correct about the rot, acts catastrophically | **Yes, canonically** | The film; **0 corpus discussion** |
| **The King of Comedy** | Delusional accuracy about his own talent; the act that works is a kidnapping | **Yes, canonically** | The film; **0 corpus discussion** |
| **The Shining** | The seer is a child; the adult who could act is the danger | Partial — the seeing is real, the intervention fails | Verified corpus mention (2016-03-23) |
| **Eyes Wide Shut** | A man shown the machine, warned off, and returned to his life unchanged | **Yes** — the purest "seeing arrives too late to help" in the list | Verified ×3 |
| **The Prestige** | Two men whose total understanding of the trick destroys them both | **Yes** | Named only in an **absent** quote |
| **The Witch** | The family's reading of its own crisis is correct and useless | **Yes** | Verified corpus mention (2017-03-30) |
| **There Will Be Blood** | Accurate, early, and *wins* — then is destroyed by what winning made him | **Contested.** Closest thing in the list to a counterexample | **0 corpus discussion** |
| **Parasite** | A plan executed competently; catastrophe arrives from outside it | Partial — "competence correctly deployed, outcome still catastrophic" | **0 corpus discussion** |
| **The Graduate** | Sees through the whole arrangement; the act is a disruption with no plan after it | **Yes** | **0 corpus discussion** |
| **Pulp Fiction** | No observer seat; the one character who reads the sign leaves the film | **No** | Verified corpus mention (2015-12-16) |
| **Kill Bill** | Revenge succeeds; the plan works | **No** | Named in the 2015-12-16 recital |

**The result is genuinely mixed, and that is the finding.** Eight of eleven
fit the seat, two do not (Pulp Fiction, Kill Bill), and one is a real
edge — There Will Be Blood's Daniel Plainview is accurate, early, and
triumphant, which is exactly the Cato-seat page's own falsifier #1: "a
curated figure who was right and won and kept it." He does not keep it, so
the falsifier is not cleanly fired. But the fact that the two clear misses
are the two Tarantino titles is worth stating: **the seat rule holds across
the Kubrick and Scorsese wells and fails across the Tarantino one.** That
is a pattern with a plausible non-psychological explanation — Tarantino
entered the canon through a 2015 filmography recital to a partner, the
oldest verified film exemplar in the record, and may simply be an earlier
stratum of taste than the rule was built on.

**The severe caveat on the whole table.** Six of the eleven have zero
corpus discussion, and the two the Cato-seat page treats as its flagship
self-portraits — Taxi Driver and The King of Comedy — are two of the six.
Their placement rests on the films' own content plus the psychological
profile at [[wiki/mind/profile/intp]], not on anything Dan is recorded as
saying about them. The pass above is therefore a reading of a list, not a
reading of a record, and it should be labelled that way wherever it is
cited.

## The King of Comedy / Taxi Driver — the self-portrait pair

Two De Niro/Scorsese pictures sit in the canon and rhyme with the
[[wiki/interests/stand-up-comedy]] thread: **The King of Comedy** (Rupert
Pupkin, the delusional aspiring stand-up who wants the stage at any cost)
and **Taxi Driver** (Travis Bickle, the isolated hyper-observer narrating a
rotten city). Both are portraits of a socially-isolated man building a
private mission the world hasn't asked for — the "hyperaware hero of a
world too stupid to see the trap" self-myth named in his profile, which
[[wiki/mind/profile/intp]] scores as Ti at 96% latent against Fe at 10%
valuing.

That he keeps a movie about a wannabe comedian in his top eleven *while*
writing his own five-minute open-mic set is not a coincidence worth
flattening. The stand-up thread is dated: the resolution is
**2017-12-28** ("One thing I'm going to force myself to do in 2018 is try
stand up at least once"), the craft goal is
**2019-04-12** ("im just
trying to develop a solid 5 minutes because thats usually what you get at
an open mic"), and the only evidence of a completed set is
**2019-10-02**, where the bit — "a hooker using my credit card to cut a
line of coke" — drew groans before he finished the word "escort," and his
own read on his material was "all of my material is just me saying a bunch
of ways 'i am a drug addict and a failure in most endeavors'"
([[wiki/interests/stand-up-comedy]]).

Pupkin's whole film is a man who cannot tell the difference between having
material and having a career. The rhyme is exact, and the record does not
show Dan making it. He never mentions The King of Comedy in any held
message.

## What the canon does not contain

Negative shape is cheap to overlook and this list has a clear one. No
comedies (The King of Comedy is a film about comedy, not a comedy). No
science fiction on the list itself — 2001 is named in messages, never
listed. No documentary. No foreign-language title except Parasite. Nothing
from before 1967 (The Graduate), against a reading shelf whose densest
cluster is the last generation of the Roman Republic
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/closing-the-set]]). Nothing made after 2019
(Parasite), against a music record where 47% of 1,860 entries were released
in 2024 or later.

That last contrast is the sharpest. The same person, curating two
collections with no external constraint, produces a music record that is
overwhelmingly *current* and a film record that stops dead five years
before the export. [[wiki/mind/synthesis/closing-the-set]] names the
mechanism: music is the one domain where Dan is a producer rather than an
observer, so intake there is scouting, which has no completion condition.
Film is observed, so it closes. An eleven-title canon that has not moved
since 2019 is what a closed set looks like from outside.

## Why it matters

The film list looked like the wiki's most disposable favorites category.
Read against the messages it is the opposite: a curated canon used to test
intimacy, a Kubrick completion project, and — in Eyes Wide Shut and The
King of Comedy — two films he explicitly reads himself *through*. Read
against the *corpus*, it is also the wiki's cleanest worked example of its
own standing rule: **take the argument, verify the quote**
([`README.md`](../../README.md)). The argument survived the check. Two of
the seven quotes did not.

## Limits of this page

- **The masterlist is unheld.** The eleven titles are relayed through the
  prior wiki, and the two kb nodes built over that relay disagree about the
  membership (see the CONTRADICTION block above).
- **Six of eleven titles have zero corpus discussion.** Parasite, The
  Graduate, There Will Be Blood, The Prestige, Taxi Driver and The King of
  Comedy are asserted by the list only. The last two are the ones the
  wiki's psychological synthesis leans on hardest.
- **Two of seven quoted exemplars are absent from 192,140 messages**, and
  they are the two on which the obligation reading depends. The page does
  not withdraw the reading; it labels which parts of it are verified.
- **No watch dates exist for any title.** The movies CSV carries none. The
  three-stage Eyes Wide Shut arc is reconstructed from *mentions*, not from
  viewings, and the corpus cannot distinguish a film discussed from a film
  watched.
- **No ratings or ranking exist.** No Letterboxd export, no Goodreads
  analogue, nothing that would order the eleven against each other. The
  three-gravity-wells reading is a structural observation about the list,
  not a weighting.
- **The Cato-seat pass above is a reading of films, not of evidence** — see
  its own caveat.
- **Nothing here establishes when the canon was assembled**, so the "no
  title after 2019" observation could equally mean the list was written in
  2019 and never revised.

## Falsifiers

1. **`FAVS MASTERLIST.csv` surfaces and prints a third list.** The
   membership question closes in one query, and whichever kb node is wrong
   gets withdrawn.
2. **The two absent quotes turn up in a dated record** — a fuller export,
   the Twitter archive, a shelved extract — with the page's dates intact.
   The obligation argument regains its best evidence and
   `int:film-canon-obligation` is withdrawn.
3. **A dated message shows Dan discussing Taxi Driver or The King of
   Comedy.** The self-portrait pair converts from a reading of two films
   into a documented identification, and the Cato-seat pass above stops
   being a list-reading.
4. **A twelfth title is added after 2019.** The closed-set reading of the
   canon's 2019 stop date breaks, and film joins music as an open,
   scouted domain.
