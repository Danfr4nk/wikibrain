# The Type Machine

Dan Frank has been running instruments over himself since at least July 2013
— a full decade before any AI system existed to do it for him. This journey
follows that practice from its first dated instance to its current, largest
form: this wiki itself.

It is a journey rather than an article, which under
[[wiki/meta/journeys/index]] means it states no new fact or figure: every
claim below is already published on one of its stops. What it supplies is the
**sequence**, which none of the stops states end to end, and — since the
2026-08-26 version — three corrections, because two of the stops have moved
under it and one of its citations pointed at the wrong page.

## Where it starts: July 2013, a household gets typed

[[wiki/people/franki-faris|Franki Faris]] is a rebound from the summer of
2013 that left almost no independent trace — no message corpus, no contact
after 2013, nothing. It collapsed, in the operator's own phrasing across two
separate captures, essentially on contact: she *"got sick of me basically
immediately and it went nowhere."*

What it did leave is a batch of self-typology emails Dan sent himself between
**July 9 and July 31, 2013**, and the batch is the artifact this journey is
about. In a fortnight he typed three people:

| Subject | Type assigned | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Franki Faris | ESFP | "Performer" — the "magic pixie dream girl" |
| [[wiki/people/alexis-armel\|Alexis Armel]] | INFP/ISFP | "Idealist" |
| Himself | INTP | — |

The framing is his own, from a later account: an attempt to "reboot" his
identity around Alexis's opposite. Note what kind of operation that is. It is
not curiosity about a personality system. It is a typology *run over a
household*, mid-crisis, to produce a statement about what kind of system he
was and what kind he should be attached to next.

Two properties of the batch make it the prototype rather than an early
curiosity.

**It is self-sent.** The emails go from Dan to Dan. There is no
correspondent, no test administrator, no audience. The apparatus's first
documented venue is a channel with one participant, which is exactly the shape
the AI sessions take a decade later.

**It is dated, and it is primary.** [[wiki/people/franki-faris]] weighs the
episode's dating against two competing accounts and the emails win:
`CATO_BOOTLOADER_DANFRANK.md` gives "2014," but that is a later AI-assisted
reconstruction and is contradicted by contemporaneous dated emails. The
DANSYNTH node ordering (Node 34 "Franki Incident" → Node 35 "Katie Fletcher,
2013") agrees with 2013. The batch is therefore one of the few points in the
whole apparatus where a dated primary record beats a commissioned one.

There is a boundary worth stating here because the rest of the journey rests
on it. The batch lives in the **email** corpus.
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-commissioned-self]]'s census — the measurement the
middle of this journey turns on — runs over the **iMessage** dump. So the
earliest and widest instance of the apparatus sits outside the count that
measures it, and the count knows this: the page lists it as a gap.

## The credential: July 10–11, 2013

Inside the same fortnight sits the one instance of the whole apparatus that
nobody commissioned, and it is on a stop this journey previously skipped.

[[wiki/people/elizabeth-eleanor]] is a fellow recovering addict whose entire
relationship with Dan is a single overnight Facebook exchange — July 10–11,
2013, months after his return to Uniontown from New York. She is several
years into 12-step recovery and pushes him toward NA/AA and the Big Book. He
declines, specifically and articulately, and the reason he gives is his type:

> *"i think my brain works a little differently than most and as an INTP and
> heavily introverted, logic-based dude...i can't see myself flourishing in
> AA/NA"*

That is the earliest dated primary-source instance of Dan self-typing INTP,
three years before the personality material that dominates the later profile
pages and a decade before the models.

**And it is the pattern's origin rather than an exception to it.** The type is
not being used introspectively in that sentence. It is a *credential in an
argument* — a claim about what kind of system he is, deployed to justify
refusing a specific external structure. The commissioned-self synthesis makes
this the hinge of its whole reading: twelve years later the same operation
runs at industrial scale, producing a specification detailed enough that a
composite voice model can generate his prose from parameters. The instrument
was always for producing statements about himself that other people would have
to take as findings. What changed is only who runs it and how much it outputs.

The exchange is also, incidentally, the source of the corpus's only concrete
Suboxone dosage figure — *"i'm still on 2mg of suboxone a day"* — which is a
useful reminder of what these early records are: ordinary conversations that
happen to contain instrument-grade data, rather than instrument sessions.

One verification note belongs here, because it is the only external check the
2013 claim has ever received. `dat:0491` ran the held message corpus for
`intp`: **nine rows in 192,140 messages**, the earliest **2015-12-03** — two
and a half years *after* the Facebook exchange. The Facebook thread itself is
not held, so this is consistency rather than independent confirmation. Nothing
in the authoritative record contradicts the 2013 dating, and nothing in it
supports the dating either.

## The type that stuck

[[wiki/mind/profile/intp]] is where the four letters became a measured
structure. The function stack is extreme enough that the page says the label
matters less than the numbers:

| Function | Score | Plain reading |
|----------|-------|---------------|
| Ti (dominant) | 96% latent / 95% aptitude | Lead logic; builds frameworks from first principles |
| Ne (auxiliary) | 84% latent / 80% valuing | Exploratory; scans for patterns and edge cases |
| Ni (leak) | 84% latent | The profile's biggest irregularity; pattern-overfitting |
| Si (tertiary) | 57% latent | High-fidelity memory; also carries the trauma material |
| Fe (inferior) | 10% valuing / 46% active | Weak but craved; used tactically |

Two of those rows are load-bearing for this journey specifically.

**Ti at 96 is the faculty doing the typing.** A dominant-Ti reading of the
self is Ti turned inward, producing a system description of a person with the
same move it makes on a message corpus. That is why the apparatus's own
self-theory falsifies well when it falsifies at all: it gets stated precisely
enough to be wrong.

**The Ni leak at 84 is why the apparatus needs a countermeasure.** An INTP is
not supposed to run this much introverted intuition, and the page names the
consequence: mythic overfitting, cosmic meaning ascribed to coincidence,
events woven into a fated narrative. The corpus treats
[[wiki/mind/concepts/dans-law]] as the deliberately installed counterweight —
a formal procedure for stripping mythic gravity off a genuine anomaly cluster.
So the instrumentation habit is not only a habit of measuring; it is a habit
of building measuring *procedures* to discipline a faculty the same
instruments identified as unruly. The machine diagnoses itself and then
installs a governor.

The page's own caveat is the one that should ride with every number above: the
function scores are **AI-inferred from corpus behaviour, not from a proctored
instrument**, the Ni measurement has no independent confirmation, and every
source predates June 2026.

A second reading of the same shape arrived in September 2026 and is worth one
line here because it bears on what the apparatus is for.
[[wiki/mind/concepts/autism]] describes the same measured traits — Ti
dominance and weak Fe, systematizing over relating, explicit over inferred
meaning — in a clinical vocabulary. The INTP page keeps both, notes that the
discriminating evidence (sensory and childhood-developmental material) has
never been located, and says plainly that "autism" stays on the other page
because it is Dan's word for the architecture, not because the instruments
voted for it. Two vocabularies, one profile, neither checked against the other
before both were adopted.

## The control case, corrected

[[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis]] was typed in the same July 2013 batch, and
the story this journey originally told about it was inverted.

Her page carries the typing — **INFP/ISFP, "Idealist"** — and carries it
under a heading that explains why it took so long to get there: *"The datum
has lived on Franki's page rather than here, which is backwards: a typing of
the corpus's six-year partner sitting on the page of a five-day rebound is why
two independent 2026-08-21 passes concluded she had no type at all."*

The two passes were not a finding. They were a **filing failure**, and a
particularly instructive one: the wiki looked for a type on the page of the
person who had one, did not find it, and concluded there wasn't one — because
the datum was sitting on the page of somebody who mattered far less to the
record. The corrective act was not analysis. It was moving a sentence to the
right page.

Alexis remains a control case here, twice over, and both are worth keeping:

**Control one, for attachment.** She is the corpus's six-years-without-full-
activation case — occupancy of the attachment slot without the deep attachment
system this wiki otherwise documents in catastrophic detail ever fully
switching on.

**Control two, for the apparatus.** She is the only person in the record
typed *early*, by Dan, outside any commissioned session, whose typing then
sat unread on a stranger's page for thirteen years. That is a measurement of
the apparatus's **retrieval** rather than its output: the instrument produced a
reading, the reading survived, and the system that stored it could not find
it. An apparatus that cannot index its own results is not obviously better
than one that produces none.

The provenance caveat her page attaches is the right one and applies to the
whole batch: *"It is Dan's read of her, not an assessment of her."* No
instrument was administered to Alexis. A man typed his partner, in an email to
himself, during the week he left her for somebody else.

## The apparatus, industrialized

[[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-commissioned-self]] picks the practice up a decade
later and names what it had quietly become: not an unusual interest in
personality tests, but **"an unusual amount of instrumentation."** Its
governing line: *"Dan knows himself the way he knows a stranger: by running an
instrument over the evidence and reading the output."*

### The census, and what it measured

The measurement that converts this from an observation into a rule is a
**negative** one, and it had never been run before 2026-08-19. Across
**106,629 outbound messages**, the entire apparatus appears **seventeen
times**: `INTP` four, `MBTI` seven, `enneagram` five, `Big Five` once. The
four figures sum to exactly seventeen, which is the only internal check the
count carries and it passes. `socionics`, `5w4`, `percentile`, `personality
type` and `attachment style` return **zero**.

The vocabulary that carries the entire `mind/profile/` cluster does not exist
in the record of his actual life. It exists in commissioned sessions, and it
stays there.

The page rules out the obvious objection: this is not a coverage artifact,
because the same dump returns `food` 968 times. Typology simply is not
something Dan says.

**Most of them are him typing somebody else.** The instrument is a social
object, not a private one. He sends Annie the INTP page in the first week of
the relationship; he gets Vanessa to redo her MBTI so the pair can be
compared; he pushes enneagram tests four times across six weeks in late 2024.
By June 2025 what he forwards is no longer a test but the **prompt** — the
specification for producing a psychological portrait, complete with its
expected response structure.

That trajectory is the journey's spine restated in miniature. 2013: type three
people in a self-sent email. 2015: hand a partner the test. 2024: retype a
sibling and feed both results to a model. 2025: hand somebody the apparatus
itself. 2026: publish the whole thing where other instruments will read it
back.

### The line the wiki does not match

> **CONTRADICTION [carried from the-commissioned-self, 2026-08-19] — the one**
> **first-person type code in the primary record is not the one the profile**
> **cluster is built on.** On 2024-11-04, quoting back a prompt he had typed
> himself, Dan writes: *"vanessa is an xNFP 4w5 and **Dan is an INTP 5w6sx
> RLOEI**."* The entire `mind/profile/` cluster carries **5w4 sx/sp** and
> **RLUEI**. Two codes differ. The wing is 6, not 4 — and the 4 wing does
> enormous work in this wiki, since [[wiki/mind/profile/enneagram-5w4]] is
> named for it. The SLOAN third letter is O rather than U, which cuts against
> the burst-and-collapse rhythm [[wiki/mind/concepts/chaos-preference]] rests
> on.
>
> A partial adjudication landed 2026-09-13: the 5w4 page's rebuild from
> primary material found the **6 wing's** loyalty/anxiety signature attested
> in behaviour — the explicit-verbal-commitment architecture, 129 severance
> declarations with 100% re-engagement, is security-through-explicit-rules
> machinery — while the 4 wing's attestation is aesthetic rather than
> architectural. Both wings have traces; **only 5w6sx has a date.**

For this journey the contradiction is the single most informative data point
in the whole apparatus, and for a structural reason. Seven frameworks agreeing
about a person carries almost no information when all seven were run by the
same operator, over the same self-selected corpus, at the same person's
request, several of them in sessions that had the earlier results in context.
Agreement is the expected output of that arrangement. **The informative
results are the ones that disagree** — the Socionics ILI-Ni mismatch against
the MBTI's LII prediction, and this one line typed fast inside a message about
somebody else's test.

## The first instrument to fail

[[wiki/mind/profile/linguistic-profile]] is where the apparatus met a control
group for the first time, and did not survive it.

The stylometric analysis had reported Dan at the **99th percentile for lexical
diversity** and at post-graduate (16th grade+) readability, with the phrase
"a custom-built fork of English" attached. On **2026-08-23** both were
recomputed against the sender-tagged corpus and both failed:

| Measure | Reported | Recomputed |
|---|---|---|
| Flesch-Kincaid readability | 16th grade+ | **2.08** (2015–19) to **4.00** (2026) |
| Type-token ratio, 2025–26 | 99th percentile | **0.0509** against his interlocutors' **0.0544**, equal 200,000-token samples |

He is marginally *less* lexically diverse than the people answering him.

The mechanism is precisely the one this journey has been describing: **no
percentile was ever computed against a comparison group.** The analysis ranked
Dan against an implied population that was never assembled, at Dan's request,
over Dan's corpus. The moment somebody built the obvious control — the other
side of his own conversations, already sitting in the same file — the finding
inverted.

The prediction that follows is cheap and specific, and it is still open: the
remaining percentile-reporting instruments — Big Five at "Impulsiveness 96,
Altruism 1", the deviance audit at "80–99/100 across seven domains" — were
produced the same way and have never met a control group either. The deviance
audit has since been partly checked and the result is consistent with the
prediction: of its ten outlier claims, **exactly two survive independent
recomputation against a real comparison population**, and the remaining eight
are single-model judgments over material Dan supplied.

The audit's own framing is worth quoting because it shows what an
uncontrolled instrument sounds like when it is confident: a verdict delivered
at 92% stated confidence that Dan is *"a living edge case — a system of human
consciousness that has been pushed, and has pushed itself, to the absolute
limits of its design parameters,"* scored against a baseline that is a sketch
of a 35-year-old American man rather than a normed population, with no
inter-rater check, delivered to the person who ordered it.

There is one finding in the whole psychological layer that did not have to ask
Dan anything, and it is the counter-example that makes the rest legible.
[[wiki/mind/concepts/calibrated-confidence]] found a behavioural signature by
**counting**: graded numeric confidence appearing at 22× the rate of 503 other
people, present in every year from 2015. Its own description of itself — *"the
same architecture caught with nobody watching"* — is the standard the
instruments do not meet.

## The apparatus pointed outward

[[wiki/mind/concepts/the-handed-mirror]] adds the stage the census only
gestures at: the analysis does not stop at being produced. It gets written
down, delivered to its subject, and copied to a witness.

The July 11, 2025 sequence is timed to the second. At 21:18:49 Dan says
*"okay i have something for you."* At 21:23:00 he sends the attachment — a
profile built by pasting their text log into a model. At 21:27:11, four
minutes later: *"i sent that to danielle too."* At 21:30:54 and 21:30:57,
three seconds apart, the recipient's two Questioned tapbacks land. At 21:35:11:
*"OK, you can quit MASS texting me."* **Twelve minutes and eleven seconds from
delivery to rupture.**

Twenty hours earlier, in-corpus, the generosity framing: *"because i've been
using that as free therapy and it's insane how good it is."*

The same act runs hostile a year later — three forensic dashboards built from
his and Annie's own message logs, delivered into a live dispute on July 25–26,
2026. Same corpus-derived form, generosity replaced by leverage.

For this journey the handed mirror closes a loop the 2013 batch opened. The
typology emails typed a household and went nowhere, because there was nobody
to send them to; the apparatus had no delivery mechanism yet. By 2025 it has
one, and the delivery is the step that ruptures. The instrument was never only
for knowing. It was for producing a document about a person that the person
would then have to answer.

## Where the apparatus points now

[[wiki/self/concepts/wiki-brain|The wiki-brain]] — this repository — closes
the journey by naming itself as a member of its own subject matter. Its
frontmatter carries a `caused-by` edge into the-commissioned-self stating
plainly: **"This repository is the current and largest instance of a
self-measurement apparatus that already ran four generations deep — and the
first whose readings are published where other instruments will read them
back."** That claim survived the page's 2026-09-19 regeneration
byte-identical, and a 2026-09-20 re-check confirms it.

The wiki is also, as of 2026-09-16, the first instrument in the sequence to be
governed by written standards rather than by a prompt. The **article
minimums** are Dan's, and they are the apparatus specifying its own output
format: M1 length (≥3,000 words of body text), M2 totality, M3 complete log,
M4 evidence with layer discipline, M5 stated limits, M6 human story first on
people pages. The sibling complete-log doctrine is his too — *"More is better
than less every time"* — and it is why the tables above are printed in full
rather than summarised. This page is written under those rules, which makes
it a mildly recursive object: a journey through a measurement practice,
conforming to a specification that practice wrote.

Two recursions are named on the wiki-brain page and both belong here as the
journey's terminus. The first, from
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/instrument-is-subject]]: the wiki's honesty standard is
a prompt Dan wrote, so the repository's epistemics are an artifact of the
person it documents rather than an independent check on him. The second is
structural: the exocortex maintains the wiki on a schedule, and reads the wiki
back as its persistent memory layer. Cognition flows his prompts → agents →
wiki → agents read wiki → agents act → wiki updates. The page states the cost
of that in one sentence: **the exocortex is a memory system with no
independent verification layer.** Honest by design and uncheckable by
construction, both true at once.

A 2013 email batch typing a rebound, her predecessor and himself, and a
595-page second brain reasoning across typed connections, are — on this
reading — the same practice at two scales, thirteen years apart.

## What would falsify this

The 2026-08-26 version of this page offered one falsifier. Four are available,
three of them from the stops:

1. **A typology exercise run on somebody other than himself or a partner, for
   a reason unconnected to explaining or managing his own behaviour.** That
   would make the instrumentation habit general curiosity rather than
   self-directed apparatus-building. None of the stops supplies one — though
   the 2018-12-20 *"So did you ever determine your mbti type?"* and the 2024
   Vanessa retype are the nearest misses, and the second is explicitly in
   service of a comparison with himself.
2. **Typology in ordinary speech.** A stretch of corpus where Dan explains
   himself to another person in the apparatus's vocabulary, unprompted, with
   no instrument in the exchange. A handful of instances would break the
   "stays in the laboratory" clause.
3. **An independent assessment.** Any clinical evaluation, employer instrument
   or third-party psychological account anywhere in `raw/`. One would convert
   the whole cluster from self-commissioned to partly corroborated.
4. **A framework he did not commission**, applied to him by somebody else, for
   their own reasons, whose result he then adopted.

The prediction that most directly tests this journey's thesis is the
commissioned-self page's fourth: *this page's own reading will be commissioned
before it is tested.* The corpus's standing habit is to resolve a question
about Dan by asking a model about Dan. If the 5w6 contradiction gets settled
by putting it to a model rather than by counting something, that is the
apparatus instantiating itself, and it should be recorded as such — including
if it happens here.

## Gaps and limits

**This journey states no new date, figure or typology result.** Every claim
above already lives on one of its eight stops. What it adds is the sequence,
the three corrections in the block at the top, and the two complete logs.

**The census measures one channel.** Seventeen appearances across 106,629
outbound messages is a count over the iMessage dump. The 2013 batch is in
email; the Elizabeth Eleanor line is on Facebook; the AI sessions are in chat
exports. None is in the census. Running the same terms across
`raw/self/facebook/` and `raw/self/chats/` would establish whether the
vocabulary is absent from Dan's speech or only from his SMS, and nobody has.

**The census's term list was fixed.** Adjacent vocabulary — *"my type," "I'm a
five," "introverted thinking"* — was not counted, and the dump covers
2015–2025 with **2022 and 2026 missing**. A wider pass could raise seventeen
substantially, and "stays in the laboratory" is the clause that would move.

**The counts disagree with the authoritative corpus.** The census reports
106,629 outbound messages; `corpus/derived/summary.json` records **99,360**
sent across 192,140 total. The two are different artifacts — the census ran
over the superseded `all_imessages_complete_dump.txt`, which
[`CORPUS_POLICY.md`](../../../CORPUS_POLICY.md) shelves — and no claim above
turns on the difference, but the seventeen-appearance figure has never been
re-derived against the held corpus and should not be quoted as though it had.

**The order the instruments were run in is not established.** If the Socionics
and Attitudinal Psyche readings were produced in sessions holding the MBTI
result, their agreement is worth nothing; if independently, it is worth
something. The session exports could settle this and have not been read for it.

**Nobody has collected what other people say Dan is like.** The corpus holds
110,944 inbound messages from 503 handles and no sustained characterisation of
him by anyone who is not either him or an instrument he commissioned. That
absence is what makes the cluster unfalsifiable from outside, and it is the
cheapest high-value operation any of these pages identifies.

**The three corrections at the top were available for weeks before this pass.**
The Franki resolution landed 2026-08-28 and the Alexis inversion was readable
on her page from 2026-08-21; this journey carried both errors until 2026-09-21.
That is the staleness failure the ladder's own immune response —
`bin/wiki-climb check`, which reports articles reasoning from premises modified
more recently than the article itself — exists to catch, and it is recorded
here rather than quietly patched.
