# Conspiracy Theory Verdicts

The complete log of Dan's conspiracy-theory positions as given on
September 22, 2026, in the 𝖢𝖮𝖭𝖲𝖯𝖨𝖱𝖠𝖢𝖸 side chat — 32 live verdicts
plus entry #33 (January 6th) added at his request from the wiki corpus,
where it was already his most documented position. The synthesis of what
these verdicts reveal about how he thinks is at
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/conspiracy-worldview]].

His confidence labels are kept verbatim — **believe**, **reject**,
**no position** (doesn't know enough), and the **gut-vs-official**
splits where he explicitly separates the two. The split is a
first-class datum, not a hedge: the gut gets logged, the official
verdict stays blank.

Each entry also carries a **corpus status** from the 2026-09-22
cross-check: *settled* (a prior on-record position matched or extended),
*evolution* (a prior position that moved), *engaged without verdict*
(prior corpus engagement, no stated position — today's verdict is
new-on-record), or *new-on-record* (no prior corpus statements found).

These are positions, not findings. The wiki records what he believes
and how confidently; nothing here is endorsed as fact about the world.

## Reading notes: the hybrid recurrence

One structural observation belongs on the log itself, because it is
visible only when all 33 entries sit side by side. The hybrid template
— real event plus an operation layered on top, the blur between the
layers doing the work — appears independently in four verdicts: 9/11
(real hijackers, rigged buildings, Mossad driver), J6 (real crowd,
inserted lethal core), Boston (real bombing, FBI-asset nexus), and the
2020 election (real votes, engineered rule changes). Four separate
cases, one shape, no cross-contamination between the verdicts — they
were given in the same session but the shape predates it (J6 alone is
~18 months settled). That recurrence is what makes the template a
cognitive primitive rather than a per-case conclusion, and it is the
strongest single finding in this log: the signature of his judgment is
not *what* he believes but *the shape the beliefs take*.
