# The Acquisition Drive

Once Dan converts a want into a decided outcome, the pursuit runs to
completion more or less independently of cost, risk, proportion or
circumstance — and it cannot be aimed by anyone else. Both halves of that
sentence are his, from a 2026-08-02 capture: "there is no hyperbole in the
claim that this resilience for the goal is truly 95+ percentile," and "there
is little control over what desires are organically felt, as these are the
only ones which can truly take advantage of this determination." The first
number is **testimony, not measurement** (dat:0707) — the figure appears
nowhere else in the corpus, no psychometric instrument tests persistence
directly, and the population baseline is unspecified. The engine is
enormous and there is no steering column.

This page is built from the operator's own theory of himself, and the
2026-09-13 rebuild keeps that framing because the theory has now been
falsified twice *by the record it pointed at* — which makes it the rare
self-account in this wiki that got stronger by losing. See
[[wiki/mind/index]] for the concept cluster this belongs to.

## What it looks like at full load

The domain where it runs most often is narcotics procurement. The threshold
event is a decision, not a craving — the operator describes it as the moment
"the determination is made that 'we/I are getting something'," after which
"that thing will be obtained at all costs and challenges." The word doing the
work is *determination*: a state change after which the outcome is treated as
already owed. [[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]] is the physical
consequence of that state recurring for a decade — a standing multi-node
supply architecture whose individual dealers rotate constantly while the
architecture never lapses, because a want that must be satisfied at all costs
cannot be left to a single supplier.

Four documented episodes show the drive at very different stakes and the
same indifference to context. Two of them fall within weeks of each other in
2015, which is the point: the drive was not producing one anomalous night, it
was producing a rate.

| Episode | The objective | What it ran through |
|---|---|---|
| [[wiki/legal/2015-retail-theft-arrest]] (2015) | Combos snacks | A retail-theft citation |
| [[wiki/legal/2015-possession-arrest]] (2015, weeks earlier) | A 10% bump saved for later | A state police arrest, a lawyer, several thousand dollars and the ARD program |
| [[wiki/timeline/events/the-fall-of-fran]] (2018-03-07/08) | The arrangement's opening encounter | A 97-year-old's broken hip, in the house, mid-event |
| [[wiki/timeline/events/uniontown-hospital-vape-alarm]] (April 2018) | One drag of nicotine | A hospital-wide fire alarm, four fire trucks, and an escort off the property |

The Fran row is the expensive one and the vape alarm is the diagnostic one. The
vape alarm cost Dan more than it could possibly have gained under any
accounting, which is exactly why it is the clean case: with nothing at stake,
the pursuit still completed, and the deliberation that preceded it was not
deliberation about *whether* — it was a physics argument constructed to
authorise the act. That is the intellect/impulsivity paradox
([[wiki/mind/profile/big-five-psychometrics]]) caught doing the thing it
actually does. Top-percentile reasoning is not fighting the impulse and
losing. It is working for the impulse.

## The young form, and the class buffer

The operator distinguishes an earlier and a later configuration of the same
trait. In the earlier one the drive ran "in tandem with the kind of
stupidity, impulsivity and short-sighted thinking that led to several
situations which could have landed him in legal trouble." The illustration he
picks is his sister's bank card: while [[wiki/people/vanessa-frank|Vanessa]]
was away at college her ATM card was, for some reason, at
[[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz's]] house, and Dan used it "for a little
while" under a pretext he flags in his own text as a pretext — "'accidentally'
… 'got them mixed up'." The scare quotes are his. Note what the story is an
example *of*: not theft as such, but a resource appearing within reach while a
pursuit was live, and being consumed because it was there. **Epistemic status:
testimony only.** The episode has no date, no amount, no duration and no
corroboration anywhere in the message corpus — Vanessa's own thread (709
messages, December 2015 onward) never mentions it.

He also supplies the structural explanation himself, which is rarer than it
sounds: "a lot of observable behavior here … results from the sheltered, safe
upper-middle class upbringing he enjoyed where consequences were less likely
than those whose parents couldn't afford the same capital toolbelt." That is
a first-person account of class as a risk-tolerance subsidy, and it is
corroborated by the record rather than merely asserted — the 2015 arrest
produced a hired lawyer named Jack and an ARD diversion with expungement, and
the family's capital is documented across
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]]. The drive did not become safer
over time so much as it stopped being underwritten.

The later configuration is described as "honed into something more useful and
semi-productive." The corpus supports this without needing to be asked: the
same engine, pointed at something that happened to be constructive, produces
the two-sided Gini measurement on [[wiki/mind/concepts/contact-gini]], the
DANMODEL extraction, the dashboards of [[wiki/mind/concepts/forensic-method]],
and this wiki. None of those were assigned to him.

### A dated instance of the young form, from the trivial end

The public archive supplies an instance with a date, a named object, and a
named venue — everything the ATM story lacks. December 2008. Dan is twenty,
three months into Full Sail, and flies home to Pennsylvania for Christmas.
**The return is booked around a concert before he has left:**

| Eastern time | Post |
|---|---|
| 2008-12-19, 09:56 | *"I don't want to go to cold, lame PA. Be back on the 28th for hey Monday :)"* |
| 2008-12-19, 12:34 | *"at the airport. i fucking hate going home."* |
| 2008-12-27, 19:40 | *"Flying to Orlando for hey monday tomorrow. Wooo"* |
| 2008-12-28, 10:38 | *"Hate airport days."* |

The four tweets verify verbatim in the twitter-2008 archive at timezone-correct
Eastern stamps (dat:0709) — platform-timestamped primary records, the
strongest tier this corpus has. He cut a family Christmas short and flew
Pennsylvania to Florida for one show, in a small room, by a band that had
existed for under a year ([[wiki/interests/favorites/music/artists/hey-monday]]).

**Why the triviality is the value.** Nothing was at stake here — nobody was
defrauded, no risk was run. What the episode isolates is the mechanism on its
own: a want became a decided outcome — *"Be back on the 28th"*, stated as
settled, nine days out — and the calendar rearranged around it. And it dates
the trait to 2008, in a first-party contemporaneous record, earlier and firmer
than anything else this page has. One instance is not a rate, and a concert is
not the class of pursuit the rest of this page is about — it is evidence that
the mechanism predates the record, not evidence about its amplitude.

## The consequence nobody had drawn: altruism was never the variable

[[wiki/people/fran-coldren]] carries a standing claim that the caregiving
period is "the strongest documented evidence against reading Dan's
low-altruism scores as incapacity." Read against this page, that claim needs
correcting rather than defending. The vigil is not evidence against Altruism
at the 1st percentile. It is evidence that altruism was not the operative
variable in either direction.

Fran's care was a goal Dan generated himself, held privately, and was never
assigned; it therefore had access to the full engine, and it got months of
unglamorous, costly, sustained execution — the same faculty that obtains
narcotics at all costs, pointed at an old woman's wish to stay in her house.
The prediction that followed was testable, and the page tested it:

> **PREDICTION FALSIFIED [2026-08-02] — and the rule is better for it.** The
> employment record was audited, as this paragraph invited. It contains two
> assigned commitments that dwarf the Fran vigil on both axes: **41 months at**
> **[[wiki/work/au-zaatar]]** and **43 months at**
> **[[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddying]]**, against six months of caregiving. On
> duration and on cost the prediction fails outright, and it fails on the
> first place it told anyone to look.
>
> What survives is the limitation, not the inference drawn from it. The engine
> still cannot be aimed by anybody else — but an *assignment* turns out to be
> a container rather than a competing category, and Dan sustains a long one by
> installing a private objective inside it. At Au Za'atar those were a
> negotiated tip split, an eighteen-month fabricated night class, and sole
> custody of the shed's cash tips; the job ended when the third was destroyed
> — the finished interior killed sidewalk demand, then the city ordered the
> sheds torn down — and not during the three months the employer spent cutting
> his hours to force a quit. The diagnostic property is that the embedded
> objective is separable from the role and usually adversarial to it. Full
> argument, with [[wiki/work/bfs-foods]] and
> [[wiki/work/caviar-courier]] as controls, at
> [[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-embedded-objective]].
>
> **A second correction, which costs this page more.** The strong reading
> above — that a self-set pursuit runs to completion "more or less
> independently of cost" — does not survive the project record either. MNEME
> was never built past specification, the [[wiki/work/tech/danmodel|DANMODEL]]
> blind evaluation has no results file anywhere and may never have been run,
> and [[wiki/work/tech/ai-video-essays]] stalled at planning. All three were
> entirely self-set and none completed. **Self-origination is necessary for**
> **the engine to fire and is not sufficient for it to finish.** The 95th-
> percentile claim should be read as being about what the drive does when it
> engages, not about how reliably it engages.

> **CONTRADICTION [2026-08-02]:**
> [[wiki/mind/profile/big-five-psychometrics]] measures Impulsiveness at the
> 96th percentile and reads the corpus's pattern of "brilliant analysis
> coexisting with self-sabotaging execution" as an impulse-control deficit —
> the diagnosis-to-behaviour gap. This page holds that a second dimension,
> a self-estimated completion drive, is present in the same behaviour and is
> not on the instrument's facet table at all. The two readings are not
> compatible: one describes a brake that fails, the other an engine that
> works. The evidence for the second is currently one first-person operator
> claim plus the event record above; the evidence for the first is a scored

## The payoff condition: closure, not completion

The Fran vigil has a verdict from Dan himself. Four days after the funeral,
unprompted, to a third party — and the message verifies byte-for-byte in the
held corpus (dat:0708: message_id 153495, 2018-04-06 21:55:47 UTC = 17:55:47
EDT, exactly the page's 17:55 once the corpus's UTC convention is converted):

> **DAN (2018-04-06 17:55):** *"she was a trip dude but i spent the past 6*
> *months looking after her full time ($15 an hour to play video games with*
> *gram is a good deal) and we had a lot of fun. got good closure and finally*
> *did something in my life that wasn't completely selfish haha"*

The six-month duration and the $15/hour are Dan's own contemporaneous figures
— which is why the page's correction of the old wiki's multi-year caregiving
inference lands. A `bin/mine-messages` sweep of all 106,629 sent messages
finds no second instance of Dan reporting a good outcome from something he
*did* — every other positive self-report in the corpus is attributed to a
person or thing he *had*. (That sweep is the page's assertion; the generated
negative was not re-run for this pass.)

This narrows the claim rather than restoring it. "Runs to completion more or
less independently of cost" is not what the vigil sentence describes — it
describes an ending that felt resolved. That is a narrower and more testable
condition than completion, and it is consistent with the second correction
above: MNEME, the DANMODEL evaluation and the video essays were all self-set
and none of them stalled for lack of determination — they stalled because
none of them had a defined endpoint to close against.
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/closing-the-set]] takes this as its starting premise.

## NEW — the endpoint requirement [SPECULATION, evidence for and against]

The totality of the completions and the stalls forces a further conclusion
the old page never drew, and it is this pass's new finding for this concept:

**The drive does not complete toward wants. It completes toward endpoints.**
Every completed pursuit on this page has a defined, external completion
state: the concert happened on a date; the supply run ends with the object
in hand; the vigil ended at a funeral; the embedded job objectives each had
a cashable, terminal form (a tip split paid out, custody of the cash tips).
Every stalled self-set project lacks one: MNEME has a specification but no
shipped artifact to mark done; the DANMODEL blind evaluation has no results
file and no decision it would settle; the video essays stalled at planning,
where "done" was never defined.

**Evidence for.** The Fran vigil's own payoff word is *closure*, not
completion — the drive's success condition, in the only documented instance
of it, is the arrival of an ending, and endings have to exist before they
can arrive. The 2025 dance re-entry is the cleanest music-data instance: the
MASTER CRATE is by definition a bounded, finishable set, and the burst
ran October 31–December 22 as a closable project (standing self-record), not
as an open-ended practice. His digging workflow is endpoint-shaped the same
way — "Play > skip to 33% > like OR like + into the current playlist" is a
binary triage with a terminal state per track.

**Evidence against.** The wiki itself is the drive's largest sustained output
and has no endpoint; if the requirement were strict, the wiki would be the
counter-example. The honest reading is that the wiki is not a pursuit but
the drive's *process* — the instrument, not the target — which exempts it
without explaining the exemption away. And the stalled projects may have
stalled for era reasons (the 2025 collapse, the Annie terminal phase)
rather than structural ones; the endpoint correlation is not yet
disentangled from the calendar.

**Status:** speculation with a real evidential shape — checkable against
every future project he starts (name the endpoint at inception; watch
whether nameless ones stall). Written up as its own concept page at
[[wiki/mind/concepts/the-endpoint-requirement]] because the finding is
portable: it predicts which of his projects will finish.

## The profile lens

Read through the Ti-dominant forensic-analyst profile
([[wiki/mind/profile/intp]]), this page is the operator theorizing his own
engine — Ti turned inward, producing a system description of the self with
the same move he makes on message corpora. That is why the theory falsified
well: it was stated precisely enough to be wrong, which is the Ti signature
(the 2026-08-02 falsifications are the profile's method applied to its own
author). The drive itself is what Ti looks like with no steering column
available: the reasoning faculty cannot choose the objective, so it does
what a subordinate intellect does — it *serves* the objective, building
physics arguments for vape alarms and procurement architectures for wants.
"Top-percentile reasoning working for the impulse" is not a paradox in this
lens; it is the expected division of labor.

The axiom lens sharpens one edge: "love that doesn't cost everything isn't
real" is this drive stated as a value — cost-independence as proof of
seriousness. The drive is the mechanism; the axiom is the drive's own press
release about itself.

## Cross-data-type check

The concept was built from testimony and event narrative; the 2026-09-13
pass checked three non-obvious data types for manifestations.

- **Music data (intake and crates).** The digging workflow is the drive at
  low stakes and high rate: a want converts ("like + into the current
  playlist") or it doesn't, with no maybe-state — the same binary
  determination threshold as the procurement domain, run dozens of times a
  week. The 2025 dance re-entry burst is the drive completing toward a
  bounded artifact (the MASTER CRATE), per the endpoint finding above.
- **Financial data (provision and burn).** The estate spine ($144,069.31
  distributed per the 2020-08-21 court order) funded the 2020–21 meme-stock
  era, peak ~$25k, exhausted early 2021 — a want converted to a decided
  outcome and run at all costs until the capital, not the determination,
  gave out. The supply network's standing cost is the same shape in the
  narcotics domain: the drive does not budget; it procures until an
  external limit stops it.
- **Timeline placement.** The earliest dated instance (Hey Monday, December
  2008, age twenty) and the falsification-relevant durations (41 and 43
  months of employment) both come from dated records, not testimony — the
  mechanism predates the self-theory by eighteen years, which is why the
  operator's 2026 account reads as recognition rather than invention.

## Gaps

The ATM-card episode has no date, no amount, no duration and no corroboration
anywhere in the message corpus — it exists only in the operator's own
telling. The 95th-percentile figure is a self-estimate offered against a
population baseline that is not specified; it is recorded as testimony, not
as a measurement, and nothing in the psychometric battery tests persistence
directly (dat:0707). The mechanism by which a want becomes a "determination"
is undescribed — the operator reports the threshold without reporting what
crosses it, which is the single most useful thing a future capture could
supply. The claim that the drive was "honed" into a productive form is
asserted rather than dated: the corpus can show productive outputs after
roughly 2020, but not that anything about the trait itself changed rather
than its targets. The endpoint-requirement speculation above needs a
prospective test — named endpoints at project inception — before it
graduates from speculation.

## Limits of record

The central number (95th percentile) is one man's August 2026 estimate of
his own engine. The episode table is dossier-grade narrative for the 2015
arrests and the 2018 Fran events; only the Hey Monday tweets and the Fran
vigil message are platform-timestamped primaries. The 95,067-row merged
corpora and the dox-scan `.docx` sources behind several cited figures are
not held in this repository. The corpus snapshot underlying the quantitative
sweeps is 2026-09-04; the August 11–September 7, 2026 export had not been
filed in raw/ as of this pass.
