# Mind — Beliefs, Ideology, Psychology, Values

The domain holds the psychology: profile instruments, concepts, syntheses,
the psychosexual cluster, and politics. Every child entry is listed below
with its current epistemic status. Status words are the pages' own
frontmatter (`active`/`stable`, `earned`/`mixed`/`derived`); the qualifiers
in parentheses are this index's read of what the evidence actually
supports as of 2026-09-13.

## How to read this domain: measurement status [new — 2026-09-13]

The domain's entries fall into four evidentiary tiers, and the tier
matters more than the topic. **Tier 1 — primary-measured mechanisms:**
claims cut directly from the message corpus or other primary records
(reassurance-architecture's zeros and character ratios,
explicit-verbal-commitment's 0-in-41,073, severance-declarations'
129-episode recount, taste-as-filter-stack's playlist forensics,
contact-gini, message-circadian-latency). **Tier 2 — commissioned
interpretations:** instruments Dan asked to be run on him
(big-five-psychometrics and its lexical audit, deviance-mapping,
the-commissioned-self, the dossier reports behind erotic-architecture
and ancestral-dialectic) — real measurements with the commissioner's
fingerprints on the frame. **Tier 3 — testimony-only concepts:** his
framing, relayed through the prior wiki or his own documents, without
corpus corroboration (the four axioms' formulation, the hyperreflexivity
mechanism, the cool metric's old thesis, phenomenology-lens — which at
least labels itself an overlay). **Tier 4 — contested syntheses:** pages
that argue with each other or with the record (the deferred audit's
timing rule against provision-grammar's substitution thesis —
complementary, not contradictory; the grievance verdicts; the binary
verdict). A Tier 3 concept cited as if it were Tier 1 is the domain's
characteristic failure mode; this index marks the tier so the reader
does not have to excavate it.
