# Marty Martin

[[wiki/people/index|people]] · [[wiki/work/bfs-foods]]

Marty Martin worked the gas station at BFS Foods alongside Dan in the
spring of 2026 — roughly six months into the job by May, to Dan's
account — and in a two-month tenure that ended in a fight over $50, he
was the coworker who told Dan how the store actually worked.

That is the human shape of the record, and it is worth stating before
the dispute mechanics: when the drawer-shortage demand came down, Marty
was the person inside the building Dan trusted to explain what was
happening. When Marty's wife was sick, Dan covered his shift until 11
on a night he wasn't even scheduled. And after Dan was fired, it was
Marty — through Dan's mother, because that is how the channel ran —
who sent the $13-an-hour job lead. A coworker, in the old sense. The
rest of this page is the documentation.

## "Make the store whole to keep your job"

Marty's most consequential contribution to the record is a single piece
of framing, and this page states its evidence layer honestly up front:
the exact phrase *"make the store whole to keep your job"* has **zero
hits in the held message corpus**. It comes from the BFS bootloader and
strategy documents — Dan's contemporaneous case files — not from a
message thread this wiki can re-read. It is page-attributed testimony,
not a held quotation. ([`dat:0936`](../../kb/data/0936-marty-martin-bfs-employment-verified-and-unverified.md)
audits the whole page on exactly this axis.)

What the framing says is what matters, and it is corroborated in
substance by everything around it: the store ran an off-books culture
around cash shortages, and the established practice was to quietly make
the store whole — eat the loss yourself — so corporate never heard
about it. Not a one-off shakedown. The way things were done. The BFS
article treats this as the primary evidence for reading the $50 demand
on Dan as structural rather than targeted ([[wiki/work/bfs-foods]]):
the coercion was in the structure, and it ran on people who meant no
harm.

The bootloader synthesis calls Marty's logic survival wisdom "from
inside the cage" — and records exactly where the logic collapses. Eat
the loss to protect the job makes sense when there is a job. The week
of the dispute, Dan's hours were cut to nothing. At that point the
question answers itself: *what am I paying to prevent, then?*

## Around the dispute

Marty appears as a participant in a group text alongside
[[wiki/people/anita|Anita]], [[wiki/people/brandon-hill|Brandon Hill]],
and an unidentified fifth number in the days before the incident. On
May 16 he pushed back on overtime and clocked hours — *"Time is tight
and going over allotted hours and corporate is not liking the over
time"* — the same corporate pressure the off-books culture existed to
keep at bay. (Like the "make the store whole" phrase, this quotation is
page-attributed from the bootloader documents; no held exact hit.)

He is invoked, but not present, the day after the $50 demand starts.
On **May 19** Brandon tells Dan *"and I heard you owe Marty money"*
while pressing the debt claim — held in the corpus, verbatim, inbound
to Dan. Marty is a name being used in someone else's pressure
conversation, not a participant in it. Dan, correcting the record
later, said the back-room exchange that day ran: clarifying questions
(*"was it the gas thing?"*), irritation at an unrelated dig about owing
Marty $20, and leaving. He did not refuse to pay it — a correction to
the page's earlier telling that matters for what the dispute was.

He was not present for the May 21 sidewalk recording where
[[wiki/people/maddox|Maddox]] gave the fuller account of the period's
blame-pivot pattern. The bootloader treats a check-in with Marty on the
group text as the practical way to tell whether Dan has been quietly
removed from it.

## May 23: the shift

The most human entry in the record, and the one Dan himself kept
returning to.

On May 23 Marty's wife was sick, and he asked Dan to cover — stay
until 11. Dan did it, on a day he wasn't even scheduled. Dan's account
of it, written May 24, is the evidence he offered that he *"didn't walk
out and didn't quit"* the job: when the store needed someone, he showed
up; when he left, it was not a walkout.

This page previously placed that account "in a message to Annie." That
attribution is wrong. The held corpus places the May 24 message in the
**Suz thread** — family-thread testimony, addressing Anita inside the
quoted text — not an Annie-thread message. Corrected per
[`dat:0936`](../../kb/data/0936-marty-martin-bfs-employment-verified-and-unverified.md),
which is also why the correction matters: the page had been reading the
message as partner-facing testimony, and it was family-facing.

## The Suz relay channel

Nearly all of Marty's contact with Dan after the incident runs through
Dan's mother, [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]], rather than direct —
and the relays are the warmest part of the file.

On **May 23** Suz asks Dan what Marty called the food he'd sent home
— *"chili"* or *"Goulash."* A coworker sending food home with Dan's
mother. On **June 7** she passes on a message from Marty about a job
lead paying **$13+/hour** — adding, unprompted, that Anita had hired a
friend who then failed to show up for her first shift. The man who
explained the store's off-books culture to Dan spent his post-firing
contact trying to get Dan hired somewhere else.

There is no primary Facebook or standalone iMessage thread between
Marty and Dan in the held corpus. The group text and Suz are the whole
of the channel.

## Corpus record and evidence layers

This page has been audited against the held corpus, and the audit is the
most important thing on it after the story. Per
[`dat:0936`](../../kb/data/0936-marty-martin-bfs-employment-verified-and-unverified.md):

**Held and verified.** The May 19 *"I heard you owe Marty money"*
(2026-05-19 03:44:43 UTC, inbound). The May 23 chili/goulash relay
(2026-05-23 20:16:37 UTC). The June 7 job lead at $13+/hr (2026-06-07
20:46:49 UTC). The May 24 account of the May 23 shift cover, in the Suz
thread (2026-05-24 06:30:49 UTC). Marty Martin was a real workplace
contact with real post-termination contact — the page's skeleton is
plausible.

**Page-attributed (bootloader and strategy documents, no held hit).**
The exact phrase *"make the store whole to keep your job"* — zero hits
across the held corpus. The May 16 group-text overtime quotation — no
held exact hit. *"Primary trusted source,"* *"six months into the
job,"* and the off-books/structural interpretation — all derive from
the unheld documents. They are Dan's contemporaneous case files, and
they are treated here as testimony with the seams showing, not as
held quotations.

**Corrected in this pass.** The May 24 shift-cover account was
previously attributed to a message to Annie; it is a Suz-thread
message. The infobox `relationship_to_dan` previously read `unknown`;
it now states what the record supports.

## What the record does not show

- **How they became close.** The page's "trusted source" framing has no
  documented origin — no first meeting, no first conversation. A
  coworker became the person Dan trusted inside the store, and the
  record does not show how.
- **Whether Dan followed up on the job lead.** The $13+/hour relay is
  the last documented contact.
- **The "owe Marty money" substance.** Brandon invoked a debt to Marty
  as pressure; Dan was irritated by a $20 dig. Whether any money was
  actually owed, and for what, is undocumented.
- **The group-text fifth number.** Unidentified.
- **Marty's own account of any of this.** Everything here is Dan-side
  or Suz-relayed. He has not been interviewed for this page.
- **Current status.** Whether Marty still works at BFS, and where the
  relationship stands now, are undocumented.

## Limits of this article

This is a thin-record article, and it is written at the length the
record supports rather than the length the minimums prefer. The held
corpus holds four Marty Martin messages totaling a few sentences; the
rest is page-attributed testimony from Dan's case files. That
asymmetry is the article's central fact about itself, and it is stated
in the body rather than footnoted away. If Marty or anyone else
supplies the missing pieces — the first conversation, the debt
substance, the current standing — the page supersedes with a dated
note, per the complete-log doctrine's correction rule.
