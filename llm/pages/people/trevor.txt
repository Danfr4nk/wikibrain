# Trevor Bevins

Trevor Bevins was a travelling caddie out of Jacksonville who spent the
spring and early summer of 2018 at Nemacolin. For those three months he was
the closest friend Dan made in the caddie yard. He slept his first nights
in Pennsylvania in the guest room at
[[wiki/places/117-belmont-circle|117 Belmont Circle]], weeks after
Fran died. He played Uniontown Country Club with Dan on many evenings, played
golf with Suz and Vanessa on Mother's Day, and coached Annie's swing. He left
for a better-paying resort in late July with a thank-you text. Afterward he
spent six months telling Dan he would never get out of Uniontown. When Dan
did get out, in February 2019, he gave Trevor the credit.

After that, for a year, Trevor was the friend Dan borrowed from. He sent
$100 at a time, overnight, at a premium Dan set himself. The thread stops
in June 2020 with Trevor asking whether Dan is all right. It does not end
there. The two were still trading messages on Facebook in 2022.

The iMessage thread is 2,690 rows, of which 1,094 are Trevor's. The complete
monthly log and the complete lending ledger are printed at the bottom.

## Bevins, not Bains

The first thing the record settles is his name. The earlier version of
this page could not. A 2017 contact card read "Trevor Bains," Dan's texts
read "Bevins," and `kb/data/1356-trevor-page-bains-bevins-flag` filed
the split as unresolved, warning that every cross-link would be wrong if
"Bains" was right.

Dan resolved it himself on 2018-05-14: *"Fun fact: on last year's sheet of
caddie phone numbers...your name was misspelled as Trevor Baines. I didn't
find out that was incorrect until like 5 mins ago. I thought you just
didn't have a Facebook"* (corpus, Trevor thread, 2018-05-14 23:10 ET). The
misspelling traces to its source. On 2017-10-19 Dan asked
[[wiki/people/brad-hubeaut|Brad Hubeaut]] for "Nathan and Trevor's"
numbers, and Brad sent back a card reading "Trevor Bains" (corpus, Brad
thread, 2017-10-19 17:43–18:43 ET). Dan's first text to the new number went
out twenty minutes later. The same misspelling survived into Dan's
Facebook address-book export as "Trevor Baines"
(`raw/facebook/export-20260908-a/gdocs/1zwYgKnGJX_LB8O8SRZgvoBrxL4lIQdvWk-N1nfNocoQ.from-gdoc.txt`).
Facebook itself names the other party in the 2019–2021 thread "Trevor
Bevins" (`raw/messenger-drive-2026-09-12/messenger_drive_canonical.jsonl`).

## October 2017: the flyer

The thread opens with a party invitation. *"Hey dude it's Dan frank. Wanted
to send u an invite, not sure if you'll still be in the area or not,"* with
a flyer attached (Trevor thread, 2017-10-19 19:04 ET). Trevor wouldn't be
around, but *"that flyer is money!! Haha I'm rolling!!"* Dan answered that
*"a party is only as good as the flyer that announces it."* That is all of
2017. The two already knew each other from the 2017 season, since Trevor
was on "last year's" caddie sheet. The record holds nothing from that
season between them.

## April 2018: the guest room

On 2018-04-27 Trevor asked whether he could still stay *"a night or 2"* on
his way up for the season. Otherwise he would have to find a hotel,
*"which I don't really wanna do"* (Trevor thread, 2018-04-27 18:07 ET).
Dan answered from bed with the flu. He said Brad had *"legit just maybe
possibly fired me"* for doubting he'd be well enough to work. Trevor
immediately offered to make other arrangements. Dan kept the offer open:
*"117 belmont circle"* (19:56 ET). Trevor would have his own bathroom, a
second driveway and his own door. Dan added that he had *"got my
inheritance check from grandma today though sooooooo i'm not as
concerned"* (19:59 ET), and that everyone would be out of the house by
Tuesday.

Trevor drove up from Jacksonville the next day. He sat in traffic through
Charlotte and arrived around nine at night on 2018-04-28, a *"pretty
bonkers night"* by Dan's account. There was news in the house that Dan
asked him to keep quiet (Trevor thread, 2018-04-28 15:50–20:59 ET; Dan to
Annie, 2018-04-28 15:21 ET: *"trevor comes in from jacksonville"*). The
news itself is not in the record. The stay lasted three nights. On May 1
Dan had to clear his things out within the hour *"before the estate
executor comes over."* Trevor was already unpacking at his own new place:
*"Bro you have NOTHING to be sorry about. You came thru for me big time"*
(2018-05-01 12:10–13:36 ET). Dan reported Annie's review. Trevor had been
*"much more respectful"* than the previous caddie house guest,
[[wiki/people/mike-hinkle|Hinkle]] (13:40–13:46 ET).

The page records the stay because of the address. Per
[[wiki/places/117-belmont-circle]], the house was Fran's. She had left it on
2018-03-08 and died that April, and her estate was being settled. For eight months in 2018 it
was the address Dan gave out when he wanted somebody to come to him.
Trevor is one of the people he gave it to.

## The season

From May through early July 2018 the thread runs at 600 to 700 messages a
month, the densest stretch in the record. Its content is ordinary, and the
ordinariness is the finding. They exchanged report times and cut notices.
They drove each other up the mountain, and Trevor asked for toll money: *"Send
me $2 for the toll"* (2018-05-31 11:03 ET). Trevor needed a spare white
polo. They met at "grams" and played UCC for $1, $2 and $5 a hole. They
played PUBG duos after midnight, and there were nightly jokes about Brad.
Trevor lived in or near Morgantown that season, going by his repeated
complaints about getting out of *"Morganhole"* (2018-06-07 11:49 ET).

He was a better golfer and a more serious one. He evangelised equipment
without letup. He sent eBay links for a Project X HZRDUS Yellow shaft,
explained stiff flex by swing speed, and let Dan try the Graphite Design
shaft in his backup driver. He diagnosed Dan's swing as *"over the top and
flippy"* (2018-05-25 16:30–16:50 ET; 2018-06-14 20:24–20:52 ET;
2018-09-25 19:53 ET). He described his own caddie résumé in passing:
Sawgrass, where he refused to hand out eval cards, and Whistling Straits,
where he handed them out *"only because I was new there"* (2018-05-11
20:41–20:44 ET). When Dan lost a fitting argument in September, Trevor
signed off with a hashtag that became his: *"#TrustYourCaddy"*
(2018-09-22 18:20 ET).

The season held more than golf.

- **Mother's Day.** Trevor played golf with Dan, Dan's sister Vanessa and
  Suz on 2018-05-13. Dan told [[wiki/people/davey-fitzpatrick|Davey]] he
  thought of Trevor *"like Hinkle 2.0"* (corpus, Davey thread, 2018-05-14
  20:42–20:45 ET).
- **Annie's golf.** Dan asked Trevor to help Annie with her swing. Trevor's
  verdict was *"Right on plane. Tell her to hit up on the ball, not down"*
  (2018-05-12 20:08–20:14 ET). By June 12 Annie had beaten Dan, and Trevor
  wrote, *"We just got ourselves a 3rd in the money game."* On 2018-06-14
  Annie told Dan, *"ya know...Trevor's a good friend."*
- **The household.** Suz and Annie both found him *"nice and (obviously)
  easy going"* (2018-05-11 20:24 ET). Annie's own texts that month also
  set limits in a house under strain: *"Don't bring Trevor in this house
  please"* (corpus, Annie thread, 2018-05-26 17:52 ET). The strain was
  Dan and Annie's, not Trevor's.
- **Laurel Valley.** On 2018-05-21 Dan made his first off-property trip
  with *"the real loopers."* He dreaded it for two days
  ([[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddie-years]]). Trevor's advice was the whole of
  his philosophy: *"It's just caddying. You're gonna be fine"* and *"Dude
  just mule it tomorrow. This is gonna be easy as fuck"* (2018-05-20
  12:59–13:14 ET).
- **Pikewood.** On 2018-07-02 Trevor arranged a free round at Pikewood
  National with Brad and another caddie. Dan was pulled off his report to
  play, and the prize was a two-man scramble. Dan drove home through three
  gas stations and a Little Caesars (2018-07-01 20:53–21:13 ET; 2018-07-02
  22:32–22:36 ET).

Substances ran through the season casually and in both directions. Dan's
side is explicit and frequent: cocaine before work, joints at the club,
Adderall offered for the Laurel Valley trip (2018-05-02 06:30 ET;
2018-05-20 08:14 ET). The appendix covers what the record shows about
Trevor's side. One exchange from September belongs here. Mac Miller had
died, and Trevor wrote, *"Hope you're taking it easy with the fun stuff
bro. Mac Miller was only 26."* Dan answered that *"heroin/fent is a lil
different."* Trevor did not let it go: *"It's all relevant. Be smart. Do
your thing homie. Just don't wanna hear about you ending up 6' under"*
(2018-09-08 19:52–21:18 ET). It is the one place in the thread where
Trevor steps out of the banter to say he is worried. He said it once and
went back to golf.

## The yard

The thread is also the best peer-level account of why the 2018 caddie
program wasn't working. The formal record in
[[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddying]] documents the same conditions from above.
Trevor documents them from inside. On a Tuesday, *"they're doubling 50-60
guys... and we have 5 god damn loops the entire day"* (2018-05-25 19:43
ET). Customers thought the bag fee was the tip: *"I only get $13/player."*
Paychecks were delivered to the Chateau and went missing (2018-06-12 21:09
ET). On 2018-06-23, VIP groups announced *"We're not taking caddies"*
after management had told Brad to have caddies ready. *"I left. Fuck this
shit."*

His view of Brad was sharper than Dan's and more forgiving. *"He hates his
job, his girl and the fact they won't let him caddie and they're ripping
off customers and caddies has him all pissed off"* (2018-05-19 21:11 ET).
After Pikewood he called Brad *"the most 2 faced fucker ever,"* then
revised it himself: *"I guess the 2 face comment was a little brash... Maybe
more Jekyl and Hide than anything"* (2018-07-02 20:51–22:29 ET). On
2018-07-16 he reported a drunken Brad snapping a club and trying to ditch
him at a bar. The next day he played with Brad again, *"against my better
judgement,"* and found him calmer. Brad spent the round asking whether Dan
was okay (2018-07-16 21:48 ET; 2018-07-17 19:50–20:06 ET). The
[[wiki/people/brad-hubeaut]] page carries the management side.

The two men planned an exit together. Trevor wanted Monday-to-Thursday
loops at Laurel Valley and Nemacolin on weekends (2018-05-25 19:39 ET).
Dan wanted the same and feared it would burn his standing with Brad. On
2018-06-23 Dan asked a manager, "Dave," *"HYPOTHETICALLY"* about
transferring. Within minutes Brad came down to the lounge to say *"how you
gonna transfer to laurel valley you don't even have a car"* (2018-06-23
08:19–08:38 ET). Trevor's reply was *"Lmfao wow."* Three days later he told
Dan he was interviewing with Club Champion for a fitting job in
Jacksonville (2018-06-26 19:11–19:20 ET). Annie's reaction to the news
reached Dan by text: *"💔💔💔💔 TREVOR IS BREAKING UP WITH YOU"* (corpus,
Annie thread, 2018-06-26 19:14 ET).

## Leaving: July 27, 2018

Trevor left without saying goodbye in person. The message came
afterward:

> *"You were a good friend Dan and thanks for hangin out and playin golf*
> *with me while I was up here. Hopefully Nemacolin changes in the future*
> *and the rest of the season shapes up to be a good one. ✌🏻brotha"*
> (Trevor thread, 2018-07-27 15:09 ET)

Dan answered with a crying emoji and then insults, which was the register
they shared. He wasn't offended, and he'd have done the same. Trevor gave
the reasons plainly. The yard had taken money out of his check for early
cashing. He had missed an 8 a.m. report. The last few loops had paid $50.
He was *"Going to Sand Valley to make $110/bag $220/l[oop]."* His parting
line to Brad was *"Call me when Caddiemaster isn't here"* (2018-07-27
19:58–19:59 ET).

The yard felt it. On 2018-08-07 Davey wrote, *"Trev picked a bad time to
leave"* (corpus, Davey thread, 2018-08-07 19:58 ET). Trevor's side of the
trade was vindicated within weeks. *"I've done 3 doubles already. Made $480
yesterday"* (2018-08-04 10:27 ET). By mid-September it was *"almost what I
made in 3 months at Nemacolin in 2 weeks here"* (2018-09-14 21:00 ET). Dan,
meanwhile, *"haven't worked since you left"* (2018-08-04 10:26 ET).

## The travelling caddie

After Nemacolin the thread follows Trevor around the country in snapshots.
None of them is a full employment history.

- **Autumn 2018.** He did three doubles in a row *"in practically snow"*
  (2018-10-05). He planned a November visit to Somerset, which fell
  through, and was *"Back in Jax now. Might head to Streamsong"*
  (2018-10-30). On 2018-11-03 he listed Dan as a reference for a Streamsong
  caddie job.
- **The side hustles.** In September 2018 he promoted Caddylands, a
  caddie-gear site selling shades, sun shirts and towels (2018-09-22
  18:30–19:21 ET). The record does not show whether he owned it or just
  sold for it. He also sent a Stash invest referral. By November 2018 he
  was driving Uber and Lyft in a new car, *"No Laying Up pod on heavy
  rotation"* (2018-11-17 16:06–16:08 ET).
- **2019.** In May he was *"out in the woods in Wisconsin... Just looping
  my dick off"* (2019-05-30 22:33 ET), averaging *"$240/loop"* by July.
  Sand Valley is a Wisconsin resort, which fits a return there, but that
  identification is outside knowledge and not in the thread. On
  2019-09-04 he announced a move to the new Wynn course on the Las Vegas
  Strip. He thought it could become *"a full time job with benefits"* and
  that he would be *"one of the firsts on the yard."* He drove out through
  Lincoln and Denver in September (2019-09-18 15:40 ET).
- **Vegas.** By December 2019 he was posting Sedona photos, playing poker
  and losing at craps. *"Gambling is the biggest wave of emotions I've ever
  had to deal with in my life!"* (2019-12-15 22:00 ET). He said he now
  caddied for comedians (2019-12-04 19:49 ET).
- **2020.** He discussed course-safety Zoom meetings in April and training
  on reopening protocols in May (2020-04-14 18:00 ET; 2020-05-13 23:08
  ET).

He was a Seahawks fan, had a younger sister, and had a brother-in-law who
sold cars in Jacksonville. His birthday fell in late November, going by
Dan's belated greeting on 2019-11-27 (2018-06-17 02:07 ET; 2019-11-11
19:07 ET).

## "You weren't going to leave Uniontown"

The exchange that matters most in the whole thread is four lines long.
On 2018-08-08 Trevor wrote about his new course, *"Too bad you can't
travel."* Dan pushed back hard. He was *"75% sure this is my last summer at
Nemacolin"* and *"not one of these lifer Fayette nam idiots. I have already
left and I can't WAIT to get out again...for good."* He reminded Trevor of
*"the conversation we had about how crucial it is for me to leave this
place, that we had right after you arrived this year"* (2018-08-08
19:15–19:21 ET).

Six months later:

> *"Yo we're moving on Saturday. And I owe you for the inspiration. I*
> *have, for the past 6 months, been hearing you tell me I wasn't going to*
> *leave uniontown lol"* / *"307 e. 76th street, New York, ny"*
> (Trevor thread, 2019-02-28 12:45–12:46 ET)

Trevor asked what Dan would be doing, joked that the address was his porn
studio, and guessed the rent at $5,000. It was $2,300. Trevor also
suggested Liberty National or the Hamptons if Dan wanted to keep looping
(2019-03-06 21:53–22:06 ET). The move itself is documented at
[[wiki/places/307-e-76th-st]]. This thread adds a motive, and the motive
is a friend's dare. Whether Trevor meant it as a dare, or just said it, the
record cannot tell. Dan heard it as one, and said so.

## The overnight bank, 2019–2020

Three months after the move, the thread changes shape. On 2019-05-30 Dan
promised to square an earlier debt: *"i'm gonna give you $200 total for the
trouble."* In the same message he gave the context that the thread
otherwise leaves unstated: *"i'm spending like 3x less living here because
of how much less i'm spending on drugs. i was seriously spending $750 a day
easy living in pa"* (2019-05-30 20:05–20:06 ET). The $750 figure is Dan's
own claim, made once, and nothing in the record audits it.

From July 2019 to May 2020 Dan asked Trevor for short-term loans at least
twenty times. The form settled quickly into a shorthand, *"100/150?"*: send
$100 now, get $150 back overnight. Dan set the terms himself, and more than
once he raised the premium when he was late. The ledger at the bottom of
the page lists every request. Two things stand out.

**Trevor was easy to borrow from and said so.** When Dan offered $200 back
on a $105 loan, Trevor answered, *"Just give me $150 bro. No worries,"*
then *"Dude I'm not tryin to hustle you for another $50"* (2019-11-05
01:57–22:01 ET). When Dan went dark in December, Trevor's Facebook reply
to his apology was *"All good bro. Just get it to me when ya can"*
(Facebook, 2019-12-19). When Dan needed $20 more for vape juice in April
2020, having *"cut all my bottles open to siphon,"* Trevor joked, *"You're
like Adam Sandler in Uncut Gems!"* and sent it (2020-04-14 17:42–17:44 ET).
He mentioned that he needed to *"watch my money too,"* and that an options
position gave him room.

**Annie's accounts were part of the plumbing.** Trevor asked in July 2019,
*"Am I sending it to you or Annie?"* (2019-07-25 14:12 ET). When Cash App
broke, the money went to *"Annie's venmo."* Dan once blamed a late
repayment on having endorsed a check to her (2019-10-17 20:37 ET).
Separately, one October 2019 loan paid for a suit to wear to Annie's
grandfather's funeral in Pennsylvania (2019-10-14 04:47 ET).

The Cash App notification texts in the corpus independently confirm the
transfers. They record fifteen "Trevor sent you" transfers between August
2019 and May 2020, plus Trevor's own repayment requests (corpus, Cash App
short codes, 2019-07-26 to 2020-05-26).

## Lockdown and the market

In March and April 2020 the thread briefly turns back into what it had
been in 2018: two men playing a video game at night. Now it was Call of
Duty: Warzone instead of PUBG. Dan streamed on Twitch, sent loadout advice
and K/D ratios, and wrote, *"i'm SO excited to have someone to team with
on there...because i have no friends lol"* (2020-03-23 20:16 ET). April
2020 is the second-busiest month in the thread, at 332 messages.

The market talk happened here too. On 2020-04-14 Trevor described long
puts on GE and Boeing with a May 15 expiry. His reasoning was supply-chain,
backed by *"A LOT of friends that work for Boeing."* Dan told him he'd
*"probably be pretty good at options trading"* because *"you've got the
gamblers instinct and enough research tenacity"* (2020-04-14 17:45–17:57
ET). The
[[wiki/timeline/periods/2020-2021-market-era|market-era]] page picks up
Dan's side of the same period. Earlier, in October 2019, Trevor had sent
him tips on XRP and two dividend stocks. Dan's answer was that he had to
*"buy one share of TRV first"*, meaning pay Trevor back (2019-10-14
09:44–13:55 ET).

## The last messages, and after

The final iMessage loan went out on 2020-05-17. Then came silence. Trevor
sent "Yoooooo," then "Any word?" plus a Cash App request for $175. Then:

> *"Hey man not cool just ignoring me. You hardly ever pay me back on time*
> *and I'm well aware that you probably won't and it doesn't bother me*
> *brotha. I just don't like when you completely ignore me. Just tell me*
> *what's going on rather than ignoring me."* (Trevor thread, 2020-05-27
> 12:12 ET)

Four days later, *"Are you ok man? Usually you have some sort of contact by
now. I hope you're alright bro"* (2020-05-31 16:42 ET). The last row in
the thread is *"You back up?"* (2020-06-18 21:13 ET), which reads as a
question about Dan's phone service. Dan's side of the thread ends on
2020-05-18. The corpus does not show whether the last $100 was repaid.

The friendship survived it. On 2021-02-04 the two talked on Facebook about
GameStop. Dan had bought short-dated GME puts and called it irresponsible
and lucky. Trevor had closed all his positions a week before the squeeze
and was sitting out a market he found *"so fucked up"*
(`raw/messenger-drive-2026-09-12/messenger_drive_canonical.jsonl`,
2021-02-04). On 2022-06-20, in a Facebook thread under the display name
"Trevor," he asked, *"Still liking it?"* Dan answered that he still
couldn't leave the apartment *"without getting overwhelmed how much I love
nyc."* Dan then pitched Trevor on serving at upscale New York restaurants,
because the money was like looping. *"Bro I'm the ugliest server of all
time lol"* (2022-06-20 to 2022-06-25). The last items in that thread are
a laughing emoji in November 2023 and a shared post on 2024-11-05.

So the record ends where the friendship began. Trevor was the one who said
Dan would never leave Uniontown, and three years after Dan left, Trevor was
checking that he still liked it.

## What the record shows and does not show

**Documented.** 2,690 iMessage rows between 2017-10-19 and 2020-06-18:
1,596 from Dan, 1,094 from Trevor, and 276 carrying attachments that the
corpus does not store. Three group-chat rows from Trevor on 2018-06-05
about a carpool. Cash App notification texts that give machine timestamps
for the 2019–2020 transfers. Two Facebook threads running to 2024-11-05.
Third-party mentions in the Brad, Davey, Annie and other threads.

**Secondhand or self-reported.** Trevor's prior courses (Sawgrass,
Whistling Straits) and his pay figures at Sand Valley and Wynn are his own
statements. Dan's $750-a-day figure is Dan's. Annie's and Suz's opinions
of Trevor reach the record through Dan's retelling, except for Annie's own
texts.

**Not in the record.** Trevor's age, hometown and full employment history.
Whether he stayed at Wynn. Whether the Club Champion or Streamsong
applications succeeded. What the "keep it between us" news of 2018-04-28
was. Whether the final loan was repaid. Why Dan went silent in late May
2020. The phone-service reading of "You back up?" fits the December 2019
Facebook apology about a shutoff, but the record does not state it.

**Inferred.** That the 2022–2024 "Trevor" Facebook thread is the same man.
It is a different display name from the 2019–2021 "Trevor Bevins" thread,
but the content, about looping and New York, is continuous. That he lived
near Morgantown in 2018, from the "Morganhole" complaints and the drives
down Interstate 79. That the Wisconsin course was Sand Valley.

1. **The thread was held all along.** **CORRECTED [2026-09-27]:**
   `kb/data/1356-trevor-page-bains-bevins-flag` recorded that the held
   corpus "cannot reproduce the thread," with 60 name hits and no direct
   thread. The thread is present in full under the phone number on the
   "Trevor Bains" card. It was missed because it carries no name field.
   The kb node's surname flag is resolved in favour of "Bevins" by Dan's
   2018-05-14 message. The earlier page's "1,095 rows" was Trevor's side
   only, from a per-contact extract. The corpus holds 1,094 from Trevor
   and 2,690 in total.

2. **Direction.** The earlier page warned that the master CSV's direction
   field was unreliable. The authoritative corpus's `is_from_me` field
   agrees with content throughout this thread. Speaker attributions here
   come from that field.

3. **Trevor's substances.** The thread records Trevor asking Dan for
   Suboxone once and drinking beer often. It also records him eating
   edibles in 2019 and occasionally sharing what Dan had (2018-05-05 14:29
   ET; 2019-12-04 19:58 ET). A 2018-04-30 message from Dan to Hinkle says
   Trevor had taken some of Dan's supply. Dan was the heavier user by his
   own account throughout, and the September 2018 warning ran from Trevor
   to Dan.

4. **Register.** The banter in both directions includes racial and
   homophobic slurs, used as insults between friends (e.g. 2018-05-11

   a Nemacolin staffer calling a customer "that black guy," and Trevor
   called the staff *"racist redneck"* (2018-05-11 21:02–21:03 ET). The
   page records the register because it is the texture of the record. It
