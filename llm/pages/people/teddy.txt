# Teddy

For two years a man called Teddy answered his phone when Dan Frank needed
Suboxone, and for two years he explained why it was hard. He had fines. He
had borrowed from his father to cover the order. He had an ARD term to
finish, a DUI class, community service, a doctor's appointment, a car that
kept breaking, and a roof of his father's he was supposed to help with. He
said all of this to a customer, unprompted, in a text thread that otherwise
consisted of amounts and meeting times.

That is the whole shape of him in the record. He is not a close friend, not a
narrator of Dan's life, and not a figure anyone in the corpus reflects on
after he goes. He is a local supply node who could not stop telling the buyer
how thin his own margins were — which turns out to be the single most useful
thing about him, because it is the only place in a fifteen-year procurement
history where the record looks at the chain from the supplier's side. The
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network|supply network]] page's finding about
him is exactly that: *"Teddy is the 2018–20 Uniontown parallel node whose own
fines, probation, and borrowing show the network's suppliers were as
financially fragile as its buyer — precarity ran the whole chain."*

His thread opens **20 February 2018** and closes **11 February 2020**, and
the master export carries **1,077 rows** across that span
([`dat:1389`](../../kb/data/1389-teddy-supply-node.md)). He opens one day
after [[wiki/people/johnny-dealer|Johnny]]'s thread goes live
([[wiki/timeline/periods/2018-deep-cycle]]), and he outlives every other node
of that year by eight months. He has no surname in the record and no page in
anyone's life but this one.

## What the thread actually was

The working pattern is repetitive and legible: Teddy asks what Dan needs,
confirms the amount and the price, proposes a time and a place, and reports
on stock — what he has left, when the upstream source will have more, whether
a delay is his or someone else's. The vocabulary is abbreviated throughout —
*"sbs," "strips," "adds," "bars," "zs,"* and occasionally *"blue"* or
*"pink"* tablets — and the abbreviation is a real limit on what can be said
about the inventory, not a stylistic note
([`dat:1389`](../../kb/data/1389-teddy-supply-node.md)). The thread supports
recurring access to Suboxone strips and to pills identified by colour or
brand, with quantities and prices negotiated directly. It does not support a
clean pharmacological itemisation, and this page does not attempt one.

Two features separate him from a one-off dealer. The first is that he
**held stock and extended credit** — he would reserve product against a
stated intention and absorb the float himself. The second is that he
sometimes operated as the contact for a broader group, announcing what he
had left and asking for advance commitments before he went to buy more
([`dat:1389`](../../kb/data/1389-teddy-supply-node.md)). Both behaviours make
him a piece of infrastructure rather than a transaction, and both are why his
own cash position keeps surfacing in the thread: a man who fronts stock to
other people with borrowed money has to talk about money.

The accommodation ran the other way too, and it had limits he stated out
loud. He offered to make deliveries before a shift or a class. He also
repeatedly asked whether Dan was certain before committing his own money to
an order, and warned that he could not keep carrying uncertainty while
another buyer wanted the same stock. That is a supplier managing working
capital in plain language, and it is the clearest documented case in the
network of a node enforcing terms on Dan rather than the reverse — the only
comparable instance is [[wiki/people/sam|Sam]] firing Dan as a customer in
February 2020 ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]]).

### Where the exchanges happened, and one thing that is not settled

The logistics geography is local and domestic: the country club, his father's
house, **Fairchance**, an Arby's/Sunoco meeting point, and vehicle or mailbox
handoffs ([`dat:1389`](../../kb/data/1389-teddy-supply-node.md)). Messages
place him passing the country club and working around Fairchance, and timing
exchanges against Dan's caddying reports and work schedule.

**Which country club is not established, and the wiki has quietly assumed
the wrong one.** [[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]] reads this as
*"At the country club, Teddy timed handoffs around caddying reports"* —
placing the handoffs at Dan's workplace. But Dan's caddying job in this
window was at **Nemacolin Woodlands Resort** in Farmington, a resort rather
than a country club, and his off-property day trips went to Laurel Valley and
Pikewood National ([[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddying]]). The country club Dan
actually lived on is the **Uniontown Country Club**: Fran's house at
[[wiki/places/117-belmont-circle|117 Belmont Circle]] sits on its fifth
fairway, and through 2018 that house was the address Dan gave out when he
wanted somebody to come to him — including, on 5 September 2018, for a
pharmaceutical handoff with [[wiki/people/bill|Bill]]
([[wiki/places/117-belmont-circle]]). On the geography alone, "the country
club" in Teddy's thread is far more likely to be the UCC — that is, Dan's
own street — than the resort forty minutes south. **Flagged as a reading, not
a finding.** The underlying messages are not held in this repository, and
until they are, both readings stand and the supply-network page's version
should not be treated as settled.

## Teddy's own year, in his own account

The financial and legal disclosures cluster in **May and June 2018** and
recur through the thread. In that window he said he needed small outstanding
amounts because of fines, that he had borrowed money from his father to cover
an order, and that he was trying to complete an ARD/probation extension while
paying it down. He described a doctor's appointment, a DUI class, community
service, temporary work, car problems, and the obligation to help on his
father's roof
([`dat:1389`](../../kb/data/1389-teddy-supply-node.md); the same May–June
2018 entry is carried in [[wiki/timeline/master-timeline]]).

**These are his statements, not verified legal history.** Nothing in this
repository corroborates a docket, a charge, a sentence or an ARD admission for
Teddy, and no surname exists here to look one up with. What the statements do
establish — reliably, because they are contemporaneous and against his own
commercial interest — is the operating condition of the business: exact
payment mattered, advance notice mattered, and cash or product left unattended
was an unacceptable risk. Every one of those insistences is the behaviour of
somebody with no reserve.

The same fragility shows up on the supply side rather than the personal one.
At different points Teddy said a source needed money that night, that
insurance might not cover a prescription, that someone had a hacked bank
account, and that a buyer had robbed him. In **February 2019** he described
beating a friend after saying that person had taken money from him
([`dat:1389`](../../kb/data/1389-teddy-supply-node.md);
[[wiki/timeline/master-timeline]]). That last item is an uncorroborated
account in a transactional text thread and is recorded here as such — it is
evidence that he said it, not evidence that it happened, and the wiki's own
testimony rule (`ARCHITECTURE.md` §Testimony) is the reason the distinction
is kept rather than smoothed.

The durable conclusion is narrower than any individual anecdote and more
useful: **Teddy operated inside a chain of personal debts and short-notice
acquisition, not a predictable retail system.** His stock depended on an
upstream source's cash position on a given night. His cash position depended
on whether the last buyer paid. His legal exposure depended on a payment plan
he was servicing out of the same revenue. That is the supplier-side view the
rest of the network's pages cannot supply, because no other node in the
record narrated his own balance sheet to the buyer.

## Dan's place in the thread

Teddy treated Dan as a repeat customer he expected to pay and to communicate
with — and also as someone worth accommodating. He held products. He offered
to deliver before work or a class. And after **Fran Coldren died on 4 April
2018** ([[wiki/people/fran-coldren]]), he told Dan he could call for a ride or
a favour ([`dat:1389`](../../kb/data/1389-teddy-supply-node.md)).

That last item is small and worth pausing on, because it is out of register
for everything around it. The death fell four days into the
[[wiki/timeline/periods/2018-deep-cycle|deep cycle]]'s busiest quarter, in the
same month Dan's first inheritance cheque arrived (27 April 2018,
[[wiki/people/fran-coldren]]) and the same month the house he had been living
in became a contested estate asset ([[wiki/places/117-belmont-circle]]). The
condolence comes from a supplier, three months into the relationship, and it
is one of the few moments in the thread where the transaction is not the
subject.

### July 2019: the vouch

The single most consequential thing Teddy does in the record has nothing to do
with drugs. In **July 2019**, [[wiki/people/rj-ritchey|RJ Ritchey]] — a
Fayette County golf-and-caddie acquaintance Dan had invited to New York — got
anxious about the trip and the ticket. Teddy vouched for Dan: told RJ that Dan
would not strand him with a one-way ticket and that Dan had the money to
follow through ([[wiki/people/rj-ritchey]];
[`dat:1389`](../../kb/data/1389-teddy-supply-node.md)).

RJ's own page calls this exactly what it is: *"the one place the supply
network and the friendship network visibly touch"* — and it establishes
something about how Teddy read his customer. He knew Dan as a man who had left
Uniontown and could sponsor a trip, not only as a buyer. The visit went ahead:
RJ boarded from Pittsburgh on **5 July 2019**, sent live updates as the bus
approached Manhattan, and left for Fayette County on **8 July**
([[wiki/people/rj-ritchey]]).

### August 2019: ranked against an alternative

On **2 August 2019** Dan told a friend he *"might be getting those subs from
emaly tomorrow rather than teddy"* — [[wiki/people/emaly-minerd|Emaly
Minerd]] and Teddy being the two people he could source Suboxone from that
day ([[wiki/people/emaly-minerd]]; [[wiki/timeline/master-timeline]]).

It is the only dated moment in the record where Teddy is weighed against a
named competitor, and it is a clean measurement of what redundancy meant in
that year. The same week — 1 August 2019 — the Annie thread carries the deep
cycle's characteristic distress signal about the *primary* node: *"I have no
other person I can get z from. Johnny leaving for the beach"*
([[wiki/people/johnny-dealer]]; [[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]]). Both
statements are true in the same seven days. The panic is about one product
line; the Emaly/Teddy choice is about another. A market that produces a
fallback for strips and none for "z" is not a redundant market — it is a
market with redundancy in one place and a single point of failure in another,
which is what [[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-graph-vs-chain]] means when it
insists the network is a graph with per-product subgraphs rather than a single
chain.

## The last message, and the silence after it

The final exchange is dated **11 February 2020**, and it is administrative:
Teddy cleaning up his contacts, asking whether both Dan's and Annie's numbers
were still current ([`dat:1389`](../../kb/data/1389-teddy-supply-node.md);
[[wiki/timeline/periods/covid-era-2020]] logs it as *"The 2018 supply layer's
last trace"*). The corpus does not show why the thread ends there.

Two things follow from it. The first is about [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]]:
a supplier's routine contact audit checks **both** their numbers, which places
her inside the thread's operating routine rather than outside it — consistent
with her documented facilitation role in the Johnny era ("drops, pickups, *I'm
at Johnny's*") and with the wiki's standing correction that she was a logistics
participant in the 2018–19 chain, not a bystander to it
([[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]]; [[wiki/people/johnny-dealer]]).

The second is that the closure is *not* observed by the person it most
affected. In **May 2020**, three months later, RJ worried that Teddy's absence
would leave him without access for a planned trip
([[wiki/people/rj-ritchey]]). RJ's worry is downstream of a channel that had
already closed, and he did not know it. Teddy's page-level epitaph is RJ's
page's line, which is better than anything this page would write on its own:
*"A supply node doing social work at one end of the friendship and missed as
infrastructure at the other."*

## Where he sits in the network

Read against [[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]] in full, Teddy carries
three loads that no other node carries in the same combination.

**He is the depth of the 2018 graph, in one name.** The deep cycle ran four
overlapping nodes — Johnny and Teddy from February, [[wiki/people/zaco|Zaco]]
from March, [[wiki/people/al|Al]] from May,
[[wiki/people/jajuan-davis|Jajuan Davis]] from late November
([[wiki/timeline/periods/2018-deep-cycle]]) — inside a market whose earlier
layer reaches back through [[wiki/people/zachariah-harshman|Zach Harshman]]
(2014–15) to [[wiki/people/joe-croftcheck|Joe Croftcheck]], the "family plug"
who is the only node in the record described as inherited rather than sought
([[wiki/people/joe-croftcheck]]). [[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-graph-vs-chain]]
converts that into the measurement that matters: the 2016–2019 cut set is
*"the largest in the record — four to five parallel illicit edges plus the
licit route plus the NYC professional edge,"* and the Johnny vacation panics
are its load tests, producing distress but never withdrawal, *"because the
parallel nodes (Teddy, the second dealer) absorbed it."* Teddy is not a
footnote to that finding; he is one of the edges the finding is counting.

**He is the supplier-side proof of the reliability inversion.** The network's
nodes sort by reliability in a direction that falsifies the intuitive model:
the more purely transactional the relationship, the more reliable the supply.
[[wiki/people/menore|Menore]], a professional stranger, answered 455 of 458
requests across 6.5 years. Johnny was reliable in season and produced panics
out of it. **Teddy was semi-social — willing but chronically fragile.** And
[[wiki/people/tom|Tom]], the friend, is the least reliable node in the entire
record ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]]). Teddy occupies the interesting
middle of that ranking, and he explains it: his unreliability is *material*
rather than relational. He fails because he has no money, not because the
friendship is expected to absorb the failure. That distinction is what keeps
the inversion honest — without a node like Teddy, "distance disciplines,
intimacy licenses failure" could be a story about character. With him, it is
visibly a story about cost structure.

**He is the record's only view from under the chain.** Every other node is
documented through what it delivered or failed to deliver. Teddy is documented
through his own arithmetic — the fines, the father's loan, the buyer who
robbed him, the source who needed cash that night. The
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine|money spine]] traces capital arriving
in lumps and exiting through metered drains on Dan's side of the transaction;
Teddy is the first evidence in the wiki that the metered drain is somebody
else's precarious income, servicing somebody else's payment plan. The chain
was broke at both ends at once.

## Record limits

Stated at length, because this page is one of the wiki's weaker evidentiary
positions and the weakness is not obvious from the confidence of the prose
around it.

**1. The thread is not in the authoritative corpus.** Under
`CORPUS_POLICY.md`, a message-derived claim either traces to
`corpus/messages.csv` or it is unsupported. The Teddy thread does not: the
held-slice check of 2026-09-09 found **no chat identifier or display name
containing "teddy"** anywhere in the 192,140-row corpus, and only **50 message
texts** that mention the name
([`dat:1389`](../../kb/data/1389-teddy-supply-node.md)). Every substantive
claim on this page therefore traces to `MASTER_MESSAGES_DB_DUMP.csv` by way of
the prior wiki's own page, and is carried as **attributed testimony** —
`attributed_to = "src:old-wiki-export-2026-09-04"`, confidence *moderate*. The
1,077-row count and the date range are in that category. They are not
re-verified figures.

**2. Speaker attribution is reconstructed, not read.** The master CSV marks
its rows `Received` almost uniformly, and that field is known to be
unreliable. Attribution here was reconstructed from message content and
remains uncertain for any message lacking conversational context. This matters
most for the disclosures: "Teddy said he had fines" is a reconstruction of who
was speaking, not a field read off the export.

**3. Nothing about him is externally corroborated.** No surname, no docket, no
address, no current status, no other party's account of him. The ARD, the
probation, the DUI class and the community service are his statements about
himself. The robbery and the February 2019 assault are his statements about
third parties, and they are recorded as statements. This page is not a
criminal record, a diagnosis of his substance use, or proof of anything he
said about anyone else.

**4. The product list is a vocabulary list.** *"sbs," "strips," "adds,"
"bars," "zs," "blue," "pink"* are the terms in the thread; the mapping from
those terms to substances is partial at best. "Strips" and "sbs" are
consistent with Suboxone and are treated as such because the surrounding
context supports it. "Adds" is consistent with Adderall — the neighbouring
[[wiki/people/bill|Bill]] thread three miles and six months away uses
*"addies"* explicitly ([[wiki/people/bill]]) — but that is an inference from
an adjacent thread, not a reading of this one. "Bars," "zs," "blue" and
"pink" are left uninterpreted here on purpose.

**5. The "country club" is ambiguous**, per the section above. Dan's employer
was a resort; the country club he lived on was the UCC. The wiki currently
asserts the resort reading. This page does not resolve it.

**6. Why the thread ended is unknown.** The 2020-02-11 contact audit is not a
farewell and reads like routine housekeeping. There is no rupture, no unpaid
debt, no argument, and no later mention of him by Dan anywhere in the corpus.
What the record shows is a thread that stops. RJ's May 2020 worry is the only
documented consequence, and it is a third party's.

**7. One gap worth opening rather than closing.** Fifty held rows mention
"teddy" in text. Nobody has read them against this page. If any of them place
him after February 2020, or name him in a context this page does not have, the
"final exchange" framing is wrong. That is a cheap check and it has not been
run.

## Appendix — forensic notes

*Kept compact and at the bottom, per the standing rule that a person's page
leads with the person.*

- **The held-slice absence is not evidence he is fictional.** 156 of 498
  counterparty handles in the corpus resolve to a name; the rest are bare
  handles (`CORPUS_POLICY.md`). A supplier saved under a nickname or a bare
  number would not surface in a name search, and 3,086 messages (1.6%) cannot
  be placed in a thread at all. Absence from a name-keyed search is weak
  evidence of anything.
- **The 1,077 figure should be treated as a floor, not a count.** The same
  master CSV is documented to undercut [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]]'s
  thread fourteen-fold, and [[wiki/people/johnny-dealer|Johnny]]'s 3,462 is
  explicitly flagged as a floor against 5,089 records in
  `all_imessages_complete_dump.txt` ([[wiki/people/johnny-dealer]]). No
  equivalent recount has been run for Teddy.
- **Timestamps, if anyone goes to the corpus for the 50 text hits.** The held
  corpus stores `date_sent` in **UTC** and the wiki's page times are local —
  four hours apart in summer, five in winter
  ([`dat:1802`](../../kb/data/1802-held-corpus-timestamps-are-utc-not-local.md)).
  Add the offset going from a quoted local time to a corpus row; subtract it
  coming back. The hour field is also unpadded in 44% of rows, so the string
  is not text-comparable
  ([`dat:0056`](../../kb/data/0056-corpus-timestamps-are-not-zero-padded.md)).
- **This page inherits a corrected neighbour.** [[wiki/people/zaco]]'s
  inbound-marketing ratio was corrected on 2026-09-19 (58:48 across 106 held
  messages, not 58:7 across 65) after a retired figure was re-published
  ([`dat:1357`](../../kb/data/1357-zaco-thread-recount.md);
  [[wiki/timeline/periods/2018-deep-cycle]]). Teddy's figures come from the
  same class of unheld export and have not been through that process. Assume
  they will move.
