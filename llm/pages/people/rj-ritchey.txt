# RJ Ritchey

[[wiki/people/index|people]] · [[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddying]] · [[wiki/interests/golf]]

RJ Ritchey is a golfer from Fayette County, Pennsylvania — a caddie, a
country-club regular, a guy who films his swing in slow motion and shows his
dad. He is Dan's friend, and Dan's own word for him, stated as a one-word
shorthand on 2026-09-09, is *best friend*.

That word needs to sit next to the archive's word, because the archive
disagrees on the shape and the wiki does not sand down disagreements. By
message volume RJ is a peripheral-tier correspondent: 1,103 iMessages across
fifteen months, 426 Facebook messages across a few years — hundreds, not the
tens of thousands that mark Dan's core attachments. Both facts are true at
once. The corpus measures traffic; Dan named the relationship. This page holds
both and does not resolve them into one.

What follows is the friendship as the record shows it: a man who said no to
something and stayed friends anyway, who rode a bus to Manhattan with no money
and total trust, who argued politics with Dan for years without either of them
converting the other — and to whom Dan once said the most sincere political
sentence in fifteen years of messages.

## The man in the messages

Before the chronology, the person, because the thread is unusually good at
showing him.

RJ worked at **the Maywood** with reduced hours while waiting to hear back
from **Nemacolin HR** about caddying work. He talked about his father. He took
footage of country-club rounds — *"I just showed my dad this,"* *"Lmao I found
all of our slow motion footage"* — the register of someone whose golf game is
a thing he shares with his family, not a status he performs. He followed the
caddie-yard news closely enough to report that a Nemacolin manager had been
fired amid a narcotics rumour.

He had no money and said so plainly. Planning the New York trip, he asked Dan
to **book** the bus ticket rather than reimburse him for it — a request that
only makes sense from someone who could not float the cost, and who would
rather say that than pretend otherwise. He worried the ticket was one-way, got
combative about it, and apologised afterwards. He is, in the messages, direct
about what he can't do and quick to own it when he's out of line.

He is also risk-averse in a way the people around him were not. He reacted to
Dan and Annie's paid-companion plans with concern about the cost and the
pressure of them — interested, sometimes teasing, consistently the most
cautious person in the conversation. The register throughout is someone
watching a life he is partly attracted to and partly alarmed by, and saying
both things out loud.

## February 2019: the boundary

The iMessage thread opens **2019-02-12**, and it opens in the middle of
something. Dan and Annie's non-monogamy arrangement had been an active
practice since February 2018, and RJ was being invited into it.

What the record shows is a man saying yes in the abstract and no in the
particular. He said repeatedly that he did not want to betray Dan, that he did
not want to offend either of them, and that he had discovered he was not
comfortable with the situation once it stopped being hypothetical. He was
particularly alarmed by the possibility that he had been recorded, and asked
Dan not to put any video online. The messages do not establish a complete
account of the encounter. They establish that he was curious, that he turned
up, and that he then withdrew rather than perform an enthusiasm he did not
have.

On **2019-02-19** the arrangement was formally closed. Two *"It's over"*
messages sit on this thread, both outbound from Dan's handle — and both were
**typed by Annie from Dan's phone**. The exchange opens *"Hey it's Annie"*,
and RJ asks, reasonably, *"why are you texting me from Dan's phone."* The
fuller of the two reads: *"It's over, I wouldn't have even brought it up if I
weren't concerned that you were mad or thought I was mad."*

That is somebody ending something so that nobody would have to be angry. It
is a reassurance, not a rejection — and it came from Annie, not from RJ, and
not from Dan. The severance atlas files this thread as its clearest
*handle-is-not-a-person* case ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/severance-language-atlas]]):
a declaration counted in Dan's outbound column that Dan did not write.

For the friendship, the point is simpler. He was the third party in an
arrangement between two people. He said he wasn't comfortable. He got a text
from one of them on the other's phone, and he asked why. And the friendship
did not end. That is the shape of this relationship and it is unusual enough
in this archive to state first: most of the ruptures documented here resume in
under a minute or never resolve at all. This one produced a real boundary, was
absorbed, and ran on for another fifteen months.

## The months between: talk, distance, and the plan to visit

The withdrawal did not end the friendship, and the record of the following
months is mostly a man describing the couple's life from outside it.

He talked about coming to the city. He asked for pictures. He talked about
golf and about work. The trip took real logistical work, and the thread is
unusually clear about it because RJ had no money and said so — reduced hours
at the Maywood, waiting on Nemacolin HR, a ride to coordinate to Pittsburgh,
and the ask that Dan book the ticket rather than reimburse it.

[[wiki/people/teddy|Teddy]] became a point of contact around the travel. In
July 2019 Teddy vouched for Dan to an anxious RJ — telling him Dan would not
strand him with a one-way ticket and that Dan had the money to follow through.
It is the one place the supply network and the friendship network visibly
touch: Teddy knew Dan as someone who had left Uniontown and could sponsor a
trip, not only as a buyer.

The held corpus preserves the moment the anxiety turned into a plan. On
**2019-06-28**, inbound:

> *"Lol good shit dude, I can't imagine what that might be. I'm going to look into bus tickets/figure out how to get up there and I trust you have full co[verage]"*

*I trust you* is the operative clause. A week later he got on the bus.

## July 2019: the visit

RJ boarded from Pittsburgh on **2019-07-05** and sent live updates as the bus
came into Manhattan. He described seeing the skyline through clouds. He was
confused by the bus route. He wanted video from a night of skating to show his
mother. He left for Fayette County on **2019-07-08**.

Three days, and the record of them is almost entirely about first sight. This
was not a symbolic reference to New York — it was a man who had never been
there, being shown it by someone who had moved there four months earlier. Dan's
own overnight-ping record puts the physical move at **3–5 March 2019**, and
the apartment at [[wiki/places/307-e-76th-st|307 E 76th Street]] had been
leased on 25 February. RJ arrived four months into a six-year tenancy, at the
point where the new life was new enough to be worth demonstrating.

His messages frame Dan as the person who could show him a place he had never
seen. They frame Dan and Annie as a couple whose plans and resources he had to
trust from a distance — which he said, in those words, a week before he
travelled.

## Golf, the Maywood, and the caddie world

RJ's baseline world was Fayette County golf and contingent work, and it is the
subject the relationship keeps returning to across both platforms and all
fifteen months.

This is the same world [[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddying]] documents from Dan's
side: a resort caddie operation with a visible status ladder, off-property
day trips to prestige courses as the marker of having made it, and a cohort of
loopers who each have a thread in the corpus. Dan worked there April 2016 –
November 2019; his first off-property call-up was **21 May 2018** to Laurel
Valley Golf Club, leaving Uniontown at 4:30 AM. RJ's later ask — to be got in
the door at Laurel Valley — is a request to enter the ladder at the rung Dan
had already reached.

The Facebook thread carries the relationship past the point where iMessage
stops, into **2021–22**: the country-club talk, the slow-motion footage, and
the ask itself — *"I'm thinking about coming back for a few months and caddying for
the summer. Would you ask your dad if he could get me in the door at Laurel
Valley?"* Whether the ask went anywhere is not recorded. Note that it is
addressed to *Dan's* father, which is the one place the record shows RJ
treating the friendship as a route to something.

*(Evidence note: Facebook thread bodies are gitignored and not held in this
repository. Every FB quotation on this page is **page-attributed** — the prior
wiki's transcription of an export whose contents cannot be re-read here.)*

The golf strand also places RJ in a documented cohort rather than as an
isolated contact. [[wiki/people/marc-charles]] occupies the same slot from the
same years — an 85-message Facebook thread, golf equipment and scores, a drone
flown over the country club — and [[wiki/people/jack-rusko]] the caddie-yard
version of it, 202 messages across the 2017–18 season. Read together they
describe a real but low-volume periphery: people Dan liked, saw at a course,
and messaged in the hundreds rather than the tens of thousands. RJ is the one
Dan called a best friend anyway.

## February 2020: the politics, and the one sincere sentence

This is the part of the relationship the page previously reduced to a clause,
and it is the part with the best evidence on it — and the part that says the
most about what Dan thought of RJ.

The old version said RJ *"expressed conspiratorial and socially conservative
views that often diverged from Dan's"* and that *"the conversation survived the
disagreement because both treated media skepticism and speculative discussion
as a shared pastime."* Both halves are consistent with the record. What the
record adds is that **Dan was not merely disagreeing. He was recruiting.**

Over 7–8 February 2020, on this thread, verified row-by-row against the held
corpus:

| Timestamp | Message | Service |
|---|---|---|
| 2020-02-07 21:55:19 | *"there's a podcast called 'chapo trap house'"* | SMS, sent |
| 2020-02-08 01:28:22 | r/ChapoTrapHouse shared, followed by *"that will keep you up to speed"* | SMS, sent |
| 2020-02-08 10:17:21 | *"i just spent 7 hours listening to the chapo trap house audiobook"*, followed by *"we should do a podcast."* | SMS, sent |

The audiobook is *The Chapo Guide to Revolution*. The same thread carries
Buttigieg jokes and *"did you know he is a cia asset?"*

And on the same two days, at **2020-02-08 02:17:46 UTC — 2020-02-07 21:17
EST** — on this thread, sender *Me*:

> *"the doctor thing is a really big thing for me...like, there's no reason that 50,000 people die a year because they don't have insurance"*

That sentence is **the one place in the political record where Dan names a
grievance as his own rather than as analysis** — and the substring matches
exactly one row in the entire 192,140-message corpus.

Three things follow.

**First, the chronology.** February 2020 is **six months before** the
2020-08-22 conversion narration in which Dan names *"hasan piker, chapo trap
house, thoughtslime, etc."* as the causes of his turn. He was already far
enough inside to recruit before he had the story about how he got inside. The
evangelism predates the conversion narrative.

**Second, the target selection.** Three distinct recruitment targets are
documented across the two February pitches and a March follow-up, and RJ is
one of them. He was not a bystander to Dan's politics. He was a mark, chosen
from a periphery of several hundred handles — and chosen specifically because
he disagreed.

**Third, and this is the human part.** The conspiratorial, socially
conservative friend from the golf circle is the person to whom Dan made his
only first-person political statement in fifteen years of messages. Not to
Annie. Not to the primary male ally. To the guy from Fayette County who
thought Buttigieg jokes were funny and mainstream politics was a con. Whatever
else the relationship was, it was a channel Dan used when he wanted to be
*believed* rather than agreed with.

The record does not show whether RJ listened to the podcast.

## The long tail: 2021–22, and eight unread messages

The relationship did not end with the iMessage record, which is exactly the
failure mode a single-channel extract produces — a friendship stopping dead
where it only changed platform.

**Facebook Messenger: 426 messages**, the sixth-largest Facebook correspondent
in the archive, ahead of Alexis Armel (317) and Suzanne Frank (290). For
comparison, Annie has 133 Facebook messages against 97,768 iMessages — the
platform split is decisive, not a gap.

The FB thread is alive in 2021–22, more than a year after iMessage stops: the
country-club talk, the slow-motion footage, the Laurel Valley ask, a *"Didn't
know you were doing TikTok"* and a missed call in April 2021, and the political
exchange in which RJ writes *"I feel like I'm far from falling for that or any
other mainstream political bullshit... I'd rather know the truth and find a
higher meaning in life than to be ignorant and a willing pawn"* and Dan
answers *"yeah dude i just think it's such an important time to make sure
people don't fall for this right wing bullshit."*

Read against the February 2020 SMS thread, that exchange is the same
conversation continuing on a different app, two years later, with both
positions intact. Neither converted the other. Neither stopped talking.

**Messenger, 2022–23: eight unread.** A screen recording of Dan's Messenger
thread list made on 2026-09-12 shows roughly 38 threads spanning October 2022
to the present, and among the 2022–23 entries: **"RJ Ritchey 8 new msgs."**
Eight messages sent and not opened. The content is not held — Messenger
history after 2022-09 is an uncovered gap requiring a fresh Facebook export —
so the date, the count and the unread state are the whole of what can be said.

It is nonetheless the last thing the record holds about this friendship, and
it is one-directional.

## May 2020: the last months of the iMessage thread

The thread's final stretch runs back through the material it began with —
videos, bodybuilding, gaming, comedy, conspiracy material, the 2020 election.

RJ asked Dan for dealer contacts and spoke openly about wanting pills or other
"party supplies" for the New York trip. In **May 2020** he worried that
[[wiki/people/teddy|Teddy's]] absence would leave him without access. These
are contextual messages; they do not establish that any named substance was
obtained or used.

Teddy is the thread's quiet structural fact — the person who vouched for Dan
to RJ in July 2019 and the person whose absence worries RJ in May 2020. A
supply node doing social work at one end of the friendship and missed as
infrastructure at the other. Teddy's own thread runs 2018-02-20 to 2020-02-11
and ends with him cleaning up his contacts, asking whether both Dan's and
Annie's numbers were still current. RJ's worry three months later is
downstream of a channel that had already closed.

**The iMessage thread ends 2020-05-19.** The corpus does not show why.

## What the record does not show

- **The "best friend" against the numbers.** Dan's shorthand (2026-09-09) and
  the corpus volume (peripheral-tier by every count) are different kinds of
  facts. The wiki records both and does not adjudicate between them — closeness
  is not message count, and message count is not closeness.
- **Who he is now.** Current location, employment and status are undocumented.
  The last dated trace is a 2022–23 Messenger thread nobody opened.
- **Whether contact continued past 2022.** Facebook Messenger coverage ends
  2022-09-04 and Instagram DM coverage ends 2025-08-09; the window from 2022-09
  to the present is an acknowledged gap requiring a fresh Facebook export.
  Eight unread messages are evidence that *he* kept writing, and nothing about
  what happened next.
- **The full name.** The Facebook friends-list sample carries both *"RJ
  Ritchey"* and *"Robert Ritchey."* "RJ" is a conventional shortening of
  Robert, and the two entries are plausibly one person listed twice — but no
  reconciliation has been done, and this page does not assert it.
- **How they met.** The golf/caddie circle is the setting; the introduction is
  undated and unrecorded.
- **The February 2019 encounter itself.** The messages establish approach,
  discomfort, withdrawal and a recording worry. They do not establish what
  happened.
- **Whether he listened to the podcast.** The February 2020 pitch is fully
  documented on Dan's side. No response is recorded in any held source.
- **Whether the Laurel Valley ask went anywhere.** Dan's own transfer request
  to Laurel Valley, made to caddie master Carl in May 2018, also never
  happened.
- **The Facebook thread body.** Every FB quotation here is page-attributed;
  thread contents are gitignored and cannot be re-read in this repository.
- **Inferred vs documented.** Documented: all dated rows in the log above,
  both thread totals, and the February 2020 timestamps. Inferred: that RJ was
  *selected* as a recruitment target rather than merely being the person
  available that evening — three targets is a small sample. Inferred: that the
  2021–22 FB politics exchange is the February 2020 conversation continuing.
  Not in the record: anything about his side of the political exchange after
  2022, and anything about his life independent of Dan's archive.

## Appendix: the count, and how it was wrong

*Kept short and kept at the bottom, because it is a fact about the wiki rather
than a fact about RJ.*

This page originally described a **"518-row iMessage thread"** and carried a
caveat that the master export's `direction` field was unreliable. Both halves
of that were true. The conclusion drawn from them was not.

The held corpus holds the thread at **1,103 messages — 585 sent, 518
received** — across exactly the date range this page already stated. **The 518
figure is byte-equal to the received count.** The page had read precisely one
side of the conversation and reported it as the whole of it.

This is not an isolated slip. It is the **fourth instance filed in a single
September 2026 batch** and one of **fourteen** corrections with an identical
shape (kb int:received-half-count-pattern). The mechanism is the same every
time: the MASTER-dump exports marked nearly everything *Received*, pages
noticed the field looked wrong, annotated it *"unreliable — read as two-way"*,
and then kept the received-only count anyway.

The interpretation node states the cost precisely, and it applies here:

> *"The page's number is almost always right as the received half — that is what makes it dangerous. The count verifies; the shape claim smuggles… A count error changes a table; a shape error changes what questions are considered answered."*

**What that means for this page specifically.** Every reading on it before
2026-09-09 was built from RJ's 518 messages without Dan's 585. The February
2019 section is the most affected: it characterised a boundary-setting episode
from one participant's side, and it missed that the closing declarations came
from Annie on Dan's handle — which is in the other half. That correction is
now in the body, where it belongs, rather than here.
