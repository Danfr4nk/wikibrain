# Zach (843, unattributed)

In the last weeks of the 2017 caddie season at [[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddying|Nemacolin Woodlands]], a new caddie named Zach asked Dan Frank whether he could get some weed. Over the next three weeks they traded forty messages: one wasted Sunday evening of changing plans, a late-night offer of edibles, and an invitation to the end-of-season party. Six months later, nine days after Dan's grandmother died, Zach wrote once more to ask whether Dan had started looping yet. Nobody answered. The thread ends there. It is the whole of what the record holds about him.

The counterpart sits on the `+1843…3264` handle: **41 iMessages, September 27, 2017 – April 13, 2018**, 22 received and 19 sent (`corpus/derived/threads.csv`, thread `bf1852f0bf909d2d`; `raw/imessage/messages-part1-2011-2019.csv`). For eleven days in 2026 the wiki filed it under [[wiki/people/zach-clingan|Zach Clingan]]. That attribution is withdrawn. As of this revision, Dan's own words from the night itself back up the withdrawal.

> **ATTRIBUTION WITHDRAWN [2026-09-12]:** This thread lived on Clingan's page on the strength of a contact-export inference: Google Contacts holds two records for the person — one bare "Zach" carrying 413 and 843, one "Zach Clingan" carrying 413, 724 and 843 — joined via the shared 413 number that Facebook independently attaches to Clingan, with IG synced contacts corroborating bare "Zach" = 8439903264. No message in the thread names the counterpart; the counterpart's only name usage is addressing Dan ("Hey Dan", April 13, 2018). On 2026-09-12 Dan testified that his Clingan ended on December 9, 2015 — "never heard from him again" — and that the thread's friendly tone cannot be his Clingan. The attribution is withdrawn. The analysis below is preserved in substance and re-anchored to the unattributed entity.

> **ADDED [2026-09-27] — identity gap narrowed:** Dan named this man to two other people on the evening of October 1, 2017. He called him **"that new zach dude"** at 19:29 ET, writing to [[wiki/people/davey-fitzpatrick|Davey Fitzpatrick]] (`+1724…9933`). He called him **"that new kid zach"** at 20:10 ET, writing to a golf-course coworker on `+1412…9265` (`raw/imessage/messages-part1-2011-2019.csv`, 2017-10-01 23:29:18 and 2017-10-02 00:10:47 UTC). So in autumn 2017 the counterpart was a new arrival and not a long-standing acquaintance, which is exactly how Dan's 2026-09-12 testimony separates him from Clingan. It also closes the old question of whether Dan "knew him before the yard": he did not. The surname is still unknown.

## Who he was, as far as the record goes

He was new. That is Dan's word for him, used twice in forty minutes to two different coworkers (cited above). He was young enough for "kid". He drove a lot. On a single Sunday he placed himself leaving work early, heading to southern West Virginia, "heading down from IUP", on I-79, near Dan's house, and finally at home with "a grill charcoal n food" (thread, 2017-10-01; complete log below). Dan's summary of that evening to Davey was *"first he told me he was at IUP, then he said he was in morgantown, then he was at my house, but just gave up and said he was at home and staying in....all in 15 mins"* (`raw/imessage/messages-part1-2011-2019.csv`, 2017-10-01 23:30:28 UTC). Morgantown never appears in the thread, so Dan must have heard that part some other way. Zach's own "You hung up dick ??" (19:20 ET) points to a phone call that the message record cannot show.

His phone carried a South Carolina area code. His register was a yard register: *"Bro"*, *"FUACK no I'm good"*, *"Aight"*, *"✌️"*. He was friendly throughout. None of the forty-one messages is hostile, and the last one is a courtesy. That tone was the ground on which Dan rejected the Clingan attribution on 2026-09-12 ([[wiki/people/zach-clingan]]), and it holds up on a full read.

He is not otherwise identifiable from anything held. The record has no occupation outside the yard, no last name, no social-media handle and no photograph.

## The season in the thread

### September 27: the ask

The thread opens with Zach and not with Dan. At 17:08 ET on Wednesday, September 27, 2017, he wrote *"Bro can I grab some smoke 💨?? You work today ??"* (complete log, row 1). Dan's reply, if there was one, is not in the export. The next message is four days later.

### October 1: one Sunday, twenty-eight messages

Twenty-eight of the forty-one messages fall on Sunday, October 1, 2017, between 11:28 and 19:32 ET (complete log, rows 2–29). The day has two halves.

**Late morning, the yard.** Dan was out on a loop. Separately, a coworker on `+1412…9265` had been texting him since 11:16 ET for a fresh cart or a GPS reset, and Dan answered *"i'm already on 4 you goon"* (`raw/imessage/messages-part1-2011-2019.csv`, 2017-10-01 15:16:48–15:28:31 UTC). At 11:28 he wrote to Zach, *"u wanna blaze it after loop"*. Zach had already gone home: *"Just left bro"*, *"Nope"*, *"Cut"*. Being cut means being dropped from the day's roster, and on that yard it meant losing the day's pay. Dan's answer was *"sheeeeeeeit were u trying to get cut? i would have given u my 2 ball"*, then *"hahah come on man that's a cool $40"*. A two-ball is the double-bag round, and Dan was offering to hand his own over. On a yard where the private status ladder is the reward ([[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddying]] sets out the ladder, the "real loopers" and the first two-bag round), that is not a small offer. Zach turned it down. Dan then made the second offer: *"but if you're trying to get funky fresh later i've still got some chocolate kush and picking up something new after work...if you want to come rip the bong let me know"* (row 10; the same row is independently verified in [`dat:1213`](../../kb/data/1213-zach-clingan-caddie-yard-supply-inversion.md)). Zach answered *"Aight heading to so wv .."* and the thread went quiet for seven hours.

**Evening, the plan that kept moving.** At 19:04 ET Zach came back: *"You still trying to smoke ? In the area heading down from IUP ,, ??"*. Dan was about to buy (*"yeah dude i'm about to go pick up a g"*) and sent an attachment. What followed over twenty-eight minutes was a plan that never settled. *"Be there in 20"*. *"Skiing ??"*. Dan wrote *"drive safe u sound shitcanned haha"* and, when asked, *"does the pope wear a funny hat?"*. Zach wrote *"I'm aight .. intuitive"*, *"Just getting gas honestly just wanna drive n burn one"*, *"You hung up dick ?? What's up ?? I'm right by the crib you trying to burn or no ?"*, *"We're burning fuck u"*, *"Jk I'm staying home bro 😂😂"*, *"I have a grill charcoal n food .. bout to be home but will def stop n smoke n chill .."*, *"On 79"*, and finally *"I'm chilling man .. ✌️"* (rows 12–29).

Dan's side of the evening was recorded somewhere else. Three minutes before Zach's last message, Dan sent Davey *"omg dude i offere to smoke that new zach dude and this is what i get for trying to be nice"* along with an attachment. Forty minutes later he sent the `+1412…9265` coworker *"also this. i was being nice and told that new kid zach i'd blaze one with him. he's drunk and being retarded"*, also with an attachment (`raw/imessage/messages-part1-2011-2019.csv`, 2017-10-01 23:29:18–23:29:27 and 2017-10-02 00:10:47–00:10:55 UTC). The attachments are referenced and not stored (see [`CORPUS_POLICY.md`](../../CORPUS_POLICY.md), "Attachments are referenced, not stored"). Whether they were screenshots of this thread is inferred from timing only.

Those two messages settle something about Zach, and they also show what else was going on in the yard that night. The rest of Dan's evening with those two coworkers was gossip about the caddie program. A manager, "hink", had told him the program would be taken over by an outside operator ("two words: CaddyCentral"). A "brad" was said to be "100% out when they change over". Hinkle had written the caddies' gratuity in for the day before: *"we almost all got fucked out of our master grat"* ([[wiki/people/mike-hinkle]]; `raw/imessage/messages-part1-2011-2019.csv`, 2017-10-01 23:33–23:44 UTC). In the same exchange Dan told Davey *"i'm just conflicted because i like the job and culture and community of caddying so much, first job i've found that i don't end up dreading work everyday"* (2017-10-02 00:07:50 UTC). Zach turns up in the middle of that as the new kid who could not settle on a plan. He was never a subject of the evening. He was an interruption to it.

### October 2: the late offer

At 22:47 ET on Monday, October 2, Dan made a different offer: *"edibles if ya want"*, *"watermelon gummies for sure"*, *"and supposed to be cereal bars but they might be gone already"*, *"i'm 11 report tomorrow. i got a lil leaf"* (rows 30–33). An "11 report" is an eleven o'clock start at the yard. Zach did not answer in the record.

The previous version of this page dated the gummies to October 3. The rows are stamped 02:47–02:50 UTC on October 3, which is 22:47–22:50 ET on October 2 (per [`CORPUS_POLICY.md`](../../CORPUS_POLICY.md), "Timestamps"). This page uses Eastern time throughout.

### October 18: the invitation

On Wednesday, October 18 at 17:20 ET, Dan asked *"U still loopin?"*. Zach answered *"Ya just got back n town, be there tomorrow 900"*. Dan sent an image, then *"Need to end the year on good vibes"* and *"Just a bonfire and hangs, I'm sure there will be plenty of getting fucked up going on"*. Zach's reply was *"Nice"* and then *"Cool cool"* (rows 34–40).

The image went out as `IMG_0103-1.JPG`. It was the flyer for Dan's end-of-season Halloween party on October 31. The same `IMG_0103` file left Dan's phone four times in about twenty-seven hours:

That puts Zach on the yard's invitation list, thirteen days out. The party was planned in parallel with [[wiki/people/christo-coan|Christo Coan]] on Facebook as an open invite "for everyone who works in golf up there", meaning "literally every caddie, staging, and pro shop employee" ([`dat:0485`](../../kb/data/0485-christo-coan-party-thread-dui-corrected.md)). In that planning thread Christo wrote *"Brad and Zach to both endorse it"*. That "Zach" is **not** established as this man. The same week Dan asked Zach Clabaugh by iMessage to spread the invite through the pro shop (table above), and he is the likelier referent. The record does not settle it. It does not show whether this Zach came to the party, or whether the party happened as planned ([[wiki/people/christo-coan]], Gaps).

Where the party was held: Dan told Christo *"I live on the golf course... my next door neighbors are my grandparents and my girlfriend's grandparents"* ([`dat:0485`](../../kb/data/0485-christo-coan-party-thread-dui-corrected.md)). The earlier version of this page named [[wiki/places/155-virginia-ave|155 Virginia Avenue]]. That page records the house as sitting on the lot next to Fran's at [[wiki/places/117-belmont-circle|117 Belmont Circle]], which fits Dan's description, but neither page states the party's street address outright.

Then the thread goes silent for 177 days.

### April 13, 2018: the ping

The last message in the thread is Zach's, at 14:39 ET on Friday, April 13, 2018: *"Hey Dan , have you started looping yet ??"* (row 41).

He could not have known what those six months had been. From roughly October 2017, Dan had been paid full-time to care for his grandmother, [[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Fran Coldren]] ([[wiki/places/117-belmont-circle]]; [[wiki/people/fran-coldren]]). Nemacolin's 2018 season opened on April 1. Dan worked two loops in the cold that day and then got the call that Fran needed to go to the hospital ([[wiki/work/nemacolin-caddying]]). Fran died on April 4 ([[wiki/timeline/periods/2018-deep-cycle]], 2018-04-04). Zach's question came nine days later. On the same day, Dan's mother wrote *"So I need $642 by the 23rd or my gas will be shut off"* ([[wiki/timeline/periods/2018-deep-cycle]], 2018-04-13). The record holds no reply to Zach. The thread ends with a question about looping, sent to a man who had spent the off-season watching his grandmother die.

Two weeks earlier Dan and another yard contact had traded the same kind of season-opening messages. Dan wrote first (*"i'll be at mystic for day 1 tomorrow"*), and on March 30–31, 2018, [[wiki/people/christo-coan|Christo Coan]] replied *"I told em i dont want the brutal schedules i had last year. Zach was brutal at times maybe this year will be nice!"* (`raw/imessage/messages-part1-2011-2019.csv`, 2018-03-31 01:19:01 UTC; handle `+1510…3276` = Christo per [[wiki/people/christo-coan]]). A Zach who set brutal schedules was someone in authority over them, not a new caddie. This is almost certainly not the man on this page. It is logged here so that no later pass attaches it to him (see the appendix).

## What the thread shows: the seller's side of the network

On [[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]] Dan is nearly always the buyer. This thread runs the other way, and it is the clearest instance in the record. Zach asked, on September 27 and again on October 1. Dan held the stock: chocolate kush, "something new after work", a gram he was about to pick up, watermelon gummies, cereal bars that "might be gone already", and "a lil leaf" (rows 1, 10, 13, 30–33). The direction is corpus-verified on the kush row ([`dat:1213`](../../kb/data/1213-zach-clingan-caddie-yard-supply-inversion.md)).

The thread does not show money changing hands, a price, or a completed exchange. The October 1 meet-up dissolved. The October 2 offer went unanswered. The October 18 exchange was an invitation, not a sale. "Seller" here means the person holding stock and offering it. Whether any of it was sold and not shared is not in the record, and the wording (*"if you want to come rip the bong"*, *"blaze one with him"*) reads more like offering to smoke together than like dealing. The network page's inversion claim holds for the direction of supply. It should not be read as evidence of commerce.

## Coverage limits

- **Identity.** The record has no surname and no message in which anyone names him alongside the 843 number. What it has are Dan's two "new zach" descriptions (a first name and a status, both contemporaneous) and the contact exports' bare "Zach" record. If the Google and IG contact cards were held here, they would be the next place to look. They are cited on [[wiki/people/zach-clingan]] to unheld Facebook and Google export paths.
- **What was said by phone.** Row 22 implies a call. Dan's IUP/Morgantown summary to Davey includes a place (Morgantown) that is not in the texts. Calls are not in the corpus.
- **Dan's unsent or deleted messages.** He may have replied to row 1 or row 41 in a message later deleted. The corpus is complete with respect to `chat.db`, not with respect to history ([`CORPUS_POLICY.md`](../../CORPUS_POLICY.md), "Deleted messages are absent").
- **The party.** Whether Zach came, and whether the October 31 party took place as planned, is not documented.
- **After April 2018.** Nothing. The record does not show whether he worked the 2018 season, or whether Dan ever saw him again.
- **Inference versus documentation.** Documented: the 41 messages, the four flyer sends, and Dan's two descriptions of him. Inferred: that he was a caddie (from the vocabulary of loops, getting cut and report times, not from a job title), that the October 1 attachments to Davey and the coworker were screenshots, and that the `IMG_0103` image was the party flyer (Trevor calls it "that flyer" on 2017-10-19; the image itself is not stored).

## Appendix: forensic notes

Compact on purpose. The story is above.

**1. The attribution chain, and why it failed.** Google Contacts held a bare "Zach" record (413 + 843) and a "Zach Clingan" record (413 + 724 + 843). Facebook attached 413 to Clingan alone. The two records were joined on the shared 413 number, and that join gave the 843 thread a surname ([[wiki/people/zach-clingan]], CORRECTED 2026-08-08). The join is the signature of a phone-book merge, and a merge can fuse two people as easily as it can confirm one. Dan's testimony of 2026-09-12 broke the chain. The October 1, 2017 "new zach" messages now break it independently: Clingan was a fixed figure in Dan's life from 2009 onward ([[wiki/people/zach-clingan]], "2009: the faun"), and Dan would not have called him new in 2017. On 2018-01-19, three and a half months after the thread, Dan listed *"Zach Clingan/Steve Kezmarsky"* among the people he would not be "chill with" (`raw/imessage/messages-part1-2011-2019.csv`, 2018-01-19 05:55:01 UTC, to Annie). That fits Clingan still being an adversary and does not fit him being a yard friend.
