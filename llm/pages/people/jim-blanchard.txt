# James "Jim" Blanchard

James "Jim" Blanchard was the unofficial super of [[wiki/places/307-e-76th-st|307
E 76th St]] and a rental broker — his own description, given to
[[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suzanne Frank]] on 2025-02-08: *"I was the
quiet 'unofficial super' at my building and still help tenants whenever I
can. I was the rental broker for many years."* For the full six years Dan
and [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] lived at the address, Jim was the
building's day-to-day man: he met them on move-in day, sent building-wide
tenant notices under his own name, coordinated the landlord's access to
apartments, scheduled water shutoffs, chased leaks, ran the exterminator
visits, and had basement access. He referred to the actual owner,
[[wiki/people/john-paci|John Paci]], as "your landlord" in the third
person — he was never the landlord, though Dan briefly believed he was in
the week before the lease was signed ("No jim is the landlord," Dan to
Annie, 2019-02-25 10:05 ET; Annie's gloss the same minute: "James 'Jim' is
the guy we have been talking to"). The confusion is itself evidence of
his function: Jim was the person you talked to. Paci was the person you
paid.

## The move-in, March 2019

The Dan↔Jim thread opens four days before the lease began, and it reads
like a man doing his job well. 2019-03-01 18:20 ET, Dan: "Hey jim it's
Dan Frank. Just wanted to confirm that we're still set for around noon
tomorrow. We'll do our best to get there earlier than that." Jim, two
minutes later: "Yes Dan, absolutely. That would be great at that time.
There is snow coming here, maybe leave a bit early if possible. Thanks
for checking on Dan. Safe travels" — then he forwards a Notify NYC
travel advisory for the incoming snow, and: "Safe travels guys. Thanks
for switching to text, much easier for me." Move-in day, 2019-03-02,
runs on logistics: "How are you guys doing for time? Hit the bad
weather?" (09:30), "text me when you are parked. Meet you in front"
(09:49), and at 11:59, the sentence that fixes the building's org chart
for the whole tenancy: "No rush. We figured you would be delayed with the
weather. **John is painting another apt in the bldg** so he will be busy
for a while." Jim meets the tenants; John paints. "I'll need 15 min to
get to the building" — he didn't live on-site, or at least not
then. Later that afternoon: "We are working the window." By 15:44 he's
forwarding the next storm watch. This is the register of the entire
relationship: practical, warm, slightly old-school.

Jim was the broker who placed them — stated outright, not inferred. The
paper trail runs a full week before the first text message. **2019-02-23
11:41 ET**, Jim emails Dan the listing: *"Video: E 76th 1st/2nd 1 bdrm
W/D $2300"* with a YouTube walkthrough — pre-war, pet friendly, four
flights up, south-facing, exposed brick, washer/dryer in the unit,
*"Landlord will accept 1 yr up front. $100 application fee each."*
Annie is CC'd from the first message (alulmer28@gmail.com). Dan's reply
that afternoon is the introduction — *"Dan Frank (or Annie's boyfriend
to most people)"* — laying out the pitch: years in NYC 2010–2014, 424
Bedford in Williamsburg, a year on 90th and Lex, no credit built yet,
but investment money available now to fund the move. **2019-02-24**, Jim
forwards two scanned application PDFs from the Bond New York office
scanner (postman@bondnewyork.com) and Dan returns the packaged
application the same day, thanking Jim for *"sticking your neck out to
keep the apartment available for us over the weekend"* — Jim had held
the unit for them. By that evening: *"All set guys."* **2019-02-27/28**,
the "Check amounts" thread fixes the money: $13,800 security deposit
and $11,500 (six months' rent minus the check Dan had already brought
Jim) payable to **John Paci Jr.**, rent every other month starting
5/1/19 — plus a $2,300 cashier's check to **James Blanchard** for the
agency fee: *"I don't charge the usual 15% fee."* Dan's thanks name the
mechanism plainly: Jim *"not only saved us by both keeping it available
for us but also by vouching for us with John."* Move-in is set for
Saturday 2019-03-02 at noon; Jim notes John will be at the building
around 9:30 (*"He lives about an hour away"*), and closes the thread
with *"Let's use text from now on. Much easier for older people like
me."* — which is why the iMessage dyad opens March 1. A 2019-03-16
"Thank You Again" email closes the arc. The earlier "high-confidence
inference" framing is retired: the Gmail thread states the placement
outright. "I only rent to really nice people these days. I turn 80% of
my potential clients away," he told Suz on 2020-09-17 — the sentence of
a man describing his own tenant selection, now corroborated by the
paper he left behind.

## The building's day-to-day man

Across the Dan thread, Jim's function is visible in the artifacts of
building management:

- **Building-wide notices under his own name.** "Tenants of E. 307 E:
  The exterminator, whose name is Joe, will be at the building tomorrow
  9-10am & will be knocking on everyone's door to see if you would like
  your apartment treated. Thank you" (2023-09-10 19:19 ET). Variants in
  August and November 2023. In August he adds the editorial aside that is
  the most Jim sentence in the corpus: "Out of respect & honor I try to
  help the tenants with things like this. **Your landlord has no
  appreciation for this, obviously.**"
- **Access coordination.** "Hey Dan, Are you guys home? John needs to
  get into the apt." (2020-05-30 13:57 ET) — a leak check; "There is
  water coming in downstairs so we need to check for possible leaks in
  the bathroom… Sometimes it's as simple as caulking around the tub"
  (18:10 the same day). "When can I get in to take a look?"
- **Infrastructure.** "Tomorrow, Thursday December 28, the water will be
  off between 11am-12pm" (2023-12-27 20:45 ET). A 2023-12-24 photo: "In
  front of basement door. My anti theft measures."
- **Rent-collection logistics for the landlord.** "just a friendly
  reminder that John Paci will be at the building tomorrow morning. He
  will be working to help our new neighbor for apt 16 move in and also
  collecting the rent for October" (2023-09-30 18:35 ET) — Paci collects
  in person; Jim announces it.

The COVID-era thread (April–May 2020) is meme and photo exchanges — a
deli rant, a Blue Angels rooftop video from E 76th/2nd, dog pictures —
the texture of a friendly tenant relationship, not a transactional one.
The last exchange in the Dan thread, 2023-12-12, is Dan reporting a
false alarm ("False alarm! It's shut now") and Jim answering: "Ok, that
was very nice of you Dan. You & Annie are really good people. Oh and
your mom is very cool and very cute/attractive as well." Zero Jim rows
in Dan's corpus after 2023-12-28. The relationship didn't end; the
record of it just moves to Suz's phone.

## September 2020: the roach bombing and the long exchange

The building's worst month is documented only in Suz's archive, and it
is the single richest window on who Jim was. It opens 2020-09-07 12:35
ET with his self-signed introduction: "Hey Suzanne, I'm trying to reach
Dan and Anne. Would you mind sending me his temporary Google number. He
texted me with it last week when he was stuck on the roof but I can't
find it. Thank you. **- Jim Blanchard -307 E 76th**" — the signature
that verifies the handle, and a cross-corroboration: Paci's own Google
Voice records pin Dan's roof lockout to 2020-09-01 (see
[[wiki/people/john-paci]]), "last week" exactly.

On 2020-09-16: "We are Fogging the apt… A few treatments are needed.
It's overwhelming." The next day, the business: "Hey Suzanne,
everything is fine at the apt. Problem solved and just have Dan call
John Paci the landlord force quick chat. I'm in this insane business
for 28 yrs and I have a very, very eclectic resume' and life
experiences as a civilian. **John is one of the most outstanding
people/ landlords I have come across in all these years.** I love Dan
and Anne, they are a great young couple. I only rent to really nice
people these days. I turn 80% of my potential clients away. Thank you"
(2020-09-17 16:19 ET). "force" is his speech-to-text for "for a." The
endorsement matters: it is the only third-party character reference for
Paci in the corpus, and it comes from the man who worked the building
under him.

The 2020-09-21 exchange is the longest in either thread and the most
human. Jim had been cleaning the apartment himself — "I will be
vacuuming tomorrow for the third time" — and he asked the question a
building man asks when he's seen something he can't unsee: "Let me ask
please is there a drug problem I should know about? I've never seen
anything like this and I really like them but this is something
different. I've been around a long time. I get it." Suz: "Not heroin,
pretty sure. He conquered opiates years ago." What follows is an hour
of old-school tough love — "Jimmys Boot Camp is about to be in
session," "I will put a flame thrower from WW2 under their asses when
they get here" — mixed with genuine offers: crash space in the spare
room of his downstairs 1BR, and "ad a cell phone to my Verizon account
for them for 6 months." He is blunt to the point of rudeness about Annie
("Anne is super nice but a cute little anchor… Cut the anchor loose"),
openly flirtatious with Suz ("Your hot for 58, good for you… I don't
kiss ass… Just a standard observation"), and insistent throughout that
he is on their side: "I'm on their side all the way." Suz's verdict at
the end of the night: "You're a good person."

Five days later, 2020-09-26, a fight spilled into the hallway and Jim
reported it to Suz the way a super does: "Call Dan, big fight that
spilled into the hallway. Did not sound good at all… I invited one of
them to shut the fuck up and come and crash in my other vacant room in
my 1 bedroom downstairs and drink a few beers. And let things cool off.
I'll keep an eye on them for sure." Then, unguarded: "At 54 yrs old I
never had children but I came close… my ex girlfriends kids still ask
about me." The night ends with "And this is between us as friends" —
and it was; the thread stays warm for four more years.

## The one friction point

2021-06-10 is the only confrontation in 259 rows. Jim had been pressing
about rent arrears, and Suz drew the line: "The two of them are both at
work. They will get in touch with you and/or John tomorrow. **You do not
have the right to harass them while at work.**" Jim: "I'm worried as an
older school guy. That's all… All John wants is for son to call him,
man up an work it out. That is all." He was acting as Paci's
intermediary and enforcer on rent — the one documented instance of Jim
as the landlord's pressure arm, and the plausible real-world seed of
any "worst" feeling. It is one exchange, it ends warmly ("I trust Dan.
Good nite and thanks"), and the thread resumes its meme-broadcast
register within the year.

## The operator's "worlds worst" — assessment

The 2026-08-10 capture that commissioned this page calls him "JIM
BLANCHARD (worlds worst building super/real estate agent)," in a
passage whose own context is the September 2020 roach infestation: "we
had so many roaches and didn't do anything about it tthat we had to
leave for a week so they could bomb the apartment." Note the grammatical
subject — *Dan and Annie* didn't do anything about the roaches. The
messages show Jim doing the remediation: fogging, vacuuming three
times, coordinating the exterminator, offering crash space.

Verdict, claim by claim:

- **"Super / real estate agent":** confirmed, with the nuance
  *unofficial* — Jim's own words, plus four years of building-wide
  notices, access coordination, and infrastructure scheduling.
- **"Worlds worst":** uncorroborated-to-contradicted. The entire
  contemporaneous record — 259 rows, both dyads — is warm; Dan and Suz
  both speak of him warmly to his face; the one friction point
  (2021-06-10) is a single rent-pressure exchange that ends warmly. The
  phrase reads as retrospective color from August 2026, eighteen months
  after the staged-eviction saga and the $10,000 debt to *Paci* ("not
  only did we stiff him 10k" refers to Paci, not Jim), not as a
  documented incident of Jim's malfeasance.

Per capture-note handling the phrase is preserved as attributed
testimony — [OPERATOR] — but the article is required to report that the
primary record does not support the negative characterization and in
several places cuts against it.

hand-posted ("Your Mailbox is effing Full"): "I was the quiet
'unofficial super' at my building and still help tenants whenever I can.
I was the rental broker for many years." The past tense is unexplained —
did he leave 307 E 76th St, lose the super arrangement, or is this just
loose speech? He says in the same breath that he still helps tenants.
Two weeks later, 2025-02-21: "[photo] Here's a classic; after leaving
landlord/tenant court last week I was getting on the subway…" — an
L&T court appearance, his own case or the building's or someone else's,
entirely unexplained. That afternoon: "It was nice to see you today
Suzanne" — she was in New York for the move-out. The thread runs meme
broadcasts to 2025-03-30 17:49 ET and then stops. No falling-out on
record; just silence. Nothing in either corpus after 2025-03-30.
