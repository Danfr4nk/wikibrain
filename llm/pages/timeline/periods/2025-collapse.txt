# 2025 Collapse

**Date range:** 2025-01-01 → 2025-12-31 (the period's causes reach back to
August 2024 and its consequences run to June 2026; both boundaries are named
below rather than absorbed).

2025 is the year the old life's structures came apart and the first structure
of the new one got built. Three things happened in it that a year-level
summary tends to blur into one, and keeping them apart is the point of this
page:

- **The era ended in February.** Six years at
  [[wiki/places/307-e-76th-st|307 E 76th St]], eleven years of cohabitation,
  and the whole metropolitan cost structure ended on **2025-02-22**.
- **The bond spiked in December.** December 2025 is the highest-volume month
  of the Dan–[[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] correspondence on record —
  4,620 held messages at near-parity — six months before the relationship
  ended.
- **The disengagement started in January 2026.** The asymmetry opens after
  the spike, not before it: 1.059:1 → 1.30:1 → 1.94:1 across December,
  January and February.

Three dates, three different events, one year that the aggregate blurred.
Nothing in the older version of this page said "gradual mutual
disengagement" outright, but the year-aggregate grain invited it, and the
December numbers make that reading unavailable
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-2025-collapse]]).

**What this page is for, and what it is not.** The cross-corpus structural
read of the period — the hinge thesis, the housing clock, the precarity
ledger, the output-port recursion — lives at
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-2025-collapse]] and
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/totality-themes]]. The week-level chronology of
February–April is [[wiki/timeline/periods/feb-apr-2025-return-and-rupture]];
the four months nobody had narrated are
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/may-august-2025-bridge]]; the day-by-day of the move is
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/2025-move-chronology]]; December at day resolution is
[[wiki/timeline/periods/dec-2025-spike]]. **This page holds the year's event
record at month resolution and the complete logs of its temporal metrics.**
Where a sibling page owns a window, this page links rather than restates.

> The older version of this page, and the return-and-rupture page's lede,

> The primary record corroborates the arrangement at four independent points

> **2025-02-04 09:53 ET** line *"I guess if I don't hear from you I will have
> to start the eviction"* — a landlord announcing an eviction he has been
> asked to announce — and, on **2025-02-01**, Dan thanking his own evictor
> *for help* twenty-eight minutes after an unanswered question. Nobody thanks
> a landlord for help mid-eviction.
>
> The rescope changes the period's agency, not its consequences.
> [[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-2025-collapse]] flagged this page's stale framing
> as a candidate for a dated correction pass; this is that pass. **One

> **2025-02-03 20:39 ET** he wrote asking Dan to call, and roughly $10,000 in

## AUDITED [2026-09-21] — the 259-event figure, and what the generated index counts

The figure this page has carried since 2026-06 is **259 events (high)** for
2025, with a stated distribution of **August 30 / September 97 / October 58**.
Its source is `raw/self/dox-md/LIFE_EVENTS_CALENDAR.md` — an auto-extraction
over 175,358 iMessages, 1,104 event entries, 633 unique dates, range
2015-11-28 → 2026-03-23 ([[wiki/timeline/events/timeline]]). **That file is
not held in this repository** (`dat:0542`), so the 259 cannot be re-derived
here, and it is kept as the calendar's figure rather than as a measurement.

What *can* be counted here is the wiki's own generated index.
[[wiki/timeline/master-timeline]] is built mechanically by `bin/wiki-timeline`
from the structural positions where pages record events, and it holds **5,608
events across 80 years drawn from 437 pages**, tiered ★ / ◆ / · . Extracting
every entry whose date lead falls in 2025 returns:

| Measure | Count |
|---|---|
| Total dated 2025 entries in the generated index | **689** |
| ★ Tier 1 (births, deaths, arrests, moves, jobs, relationships, hospitalizations, legal, financial) | **140** |
| ◆ Tier 2 (concerts, trips, releases, purchases, celebrations) | **66** |
| · Tier 3 (everything else dated) | **483** |
| The LIFE calendar's figure, for comparison | 259 |

**The two numbers are not the same measurement and neither is wrong.** The
calendar counts life events extracted from message text; the index counts
dated statements standing in structural positions across 437 wiki pages, so a
single event discussed on six pages contributes six entries. The index is
therefore a measure of **the wiki's attention**, not of the year's density —
the same distinction [[wiki/timeline/periods/dec-2025-spike]] draws when it
notes that eighteen of December's thirty-one days carry no dated entry against
a held average of 149 messages a day.

The distributions also disagree, and the disagreement is worth recording
rather than smoothing. The calendar gives Aug 30 / Sep 97 / Oct 58. The
generated index, counting day-dated and month-dated entries together, gives
**Aug 73 / Sep 71 / Oct 37**. Both agree that August–October is the year's
densest stretch; they disagree about which month is the peak. Until the
calendar file is recovered, the calendar's shape stays the calendar's.

## The complete log — 2025, month by month

Every month of 2025 in order, with what the wiki dates inside it and the page
that carries the evidence. Months are named even where the record is thin —
the quiet-stretch collapse at month resolution, per
[[wiki/meta/complete-log-doctrine]]. Day resolution for December lives on
[[wiki/timeline/periods/dec-2025-spike]] and is not restated here.

**Running through the whole year, undated at month resolution:** 41,203 held
messages across all threads; the relationship conducted at distance from
February; Suboxone maintained by prescription with the dealer network as
failover; 337 Saratoga on the market from May; the terminal phase of the
Annie channel running from August.

## Limits of record

- **This page's own headline keyword counts do not reconcile, and are kept as
  the calendar's.** The `annie_all_time_logs.csv` figures — *"eli"* 388,
  *"love you"* 541, *"goodbye"* 110, *"lying"* 150, and the 12,255-message
  2025 Annie total — come from a **shelved** extract that `CORPUS_POLICY.md`
  bars as support for any claim about the past. Worse, two of them are
  arithmetically impossible as subset counts: *"goodbye"* at 110 and
  *"lying"* at 150 **exceed** the whole-corpus 2025 counts for those tokens
  (44 and 81) under case-insensitive substring matching. A subset cannot
  exceed its whole under identical rules, so the extract's matching rules
  differ — token versus message counts, different normalisation, or a
  different export (`dat:0542`). They are filed as the extract's figures, not
  reconciled, and nothing on this page rests on them.
- **The corpus is not in this repository.** Every held-corpus figure here is
  quoted from the node that computed it (`dat:0542`, `dat:0591`). What was
  computed for this revision from committed files is the per-year histogram
  (`corpus/derived/summary.json`) and the 2025 entry counts from
  `wiki/timeline/master-timeline.md`.
- **UTC versus local.** The wiki writes UTC on some pages and local on
  others; the corpus stores `date_sent` in UTC. A four-hour offset in summer
  and five in winter is why the December 13 line reads `2025-12-14 01:39:18`
  in one place and `Dec 13 20:39` in another. Both are the same message.
  Times on this page are given as their source gives them, with the zone
  named where the source names it.
- **The generated index is a measure of attention.** 689 entries is what 437
  wiki pages say about 2025, not what happened in 2025. One event discussed
  on six pages contributes six entries, and the deduplication in this page's
  audit was by text similarity, not by event identity — so 689 is an upper
  bound on distinct statements and not a count of distinct events.
- **The interpretive frame of the terminal phase is unheld.** The concealed
  third-party relationship and the two written denials (November 2025,
  February 2026) rest on the LIFE calendar and the DanAnnie dossier. The
  volume numbers are confirmed; the story about what they meant is not.
- **Deleted messages are absent** from `chat.db` and therefore from the
  corpus; 3,086 rows (1.6%) cannot be placed in a thread; 156 of 498
  counterparty handles resolve to a name. The corpus is complete with respect
  to the database, which is not the same as complete with respect to history.
- **Dan's date memory displaces**, and the veracity ledger documents the
  pattern. Every date on this page is carried by a timestamp or a dated
  record, not by recall; where only recall exists, the entry says so.
