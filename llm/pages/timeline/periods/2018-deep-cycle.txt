# 2018 Deep Cycle

**Date range:** 2018-01-01 to 2018-12-31

2018 is the loudest year in the record and, on three independent
instruments, the biggest. The whole-corpus message count is **40,514** —
the highest of any year until 2025 matched it, and the two are within 2% of
each other ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/message-circadian-latency]]). The GPS
semantic database records **1,716 place visits**, the highest year in the
full location history, with **258 in August alone**
([[wiki/self/location-history]]). The LIFE calendar counts **254 events**,
also the highest year it covers ([[wiki/timeline/events/timeline]]). Three
different measuring systems, built from three different exports, all name
the same twelve months.

What they are measuring is not one thing. Inside those twelve months the
record holds: a woman's death at 97 and the four-week hospital vigil that
preceded it; the opening and consummation of a non-monogamy arrangement
that had been discussed for a year; an eviction notice served mid-vigil by
the dead woman's only child; an estate that begins paying out in April and
does not finish until 2020; a multi-dealer supply configuration running at
least four concurrent nodes; roughly $14,000 moving from Dan to his mother
across three months against money that would not arrive for two more years;
two tattoos, a third commissioned; and, in the last twenty-five days of the
year, the opening of the longest-running attachment in the corpus.

The old version of this page called that "peak chaos/output" and left it
there. The word is not wrong. It is just not a record. This rewrite carries
the year's complete dated log on the page, per
[[wiki/meta/complete-log-doctrine]] — every dated entry the wiki holds for
2018, in order, with the quiet stretches collapsed to a line rather than
dropped.

## What led in

The year before is documented at
[[wiki/timeline/periods/2017-poverty-floor]]. Two things carry across the
boundary and both matter for reading 2018.

The first is the caregiving job. Dan and Annie were
[[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Fran Coldren's]] caregivers, and for roughly the
last six months of her life Dan was paid full-time at **$15 an hour** — his
own figure, given two days after she died and verified verbatim in the held
iMessage corpus at `2018-04-06 21:55:47 UTC` (`kb/events/2017-2018-fran-caregiving`,
citing `dat:0446`). The wage was authorised through the estate by its
trustee, Judge Fred Adams; Dan's own March reasoning — *"fred would have put
the kaibosh on me being paid to watch gram if there was an ongoing issue"* —
puts Adams directly in the payroll chain ([[wiki/people/fran-coldren]]).
That is the household's income floor for the first quarter of 2018, and it
ends when she does.

The second is that the money everyone was waiting on was not the money
anyone had. In January 2018, with Fran alive and declining, Dan writes to
Annie about what comes *"after gram goes and we have a chance to get above
water with the inheritance... i want to seriously get out of here"*
([[wiki/people/fran-coldren]]). The first cheque lands April 27. The
court-ordered distribution of **$144,069.31** does not land until 2020
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]]). Everything in between is run
on expectation, and the year's financial behaviour — the Cash App churn, the
fronts, the $14,000 outflow — is what expectation looks like when it is
spent in advance.

## The supply layer, as a configuration rather than a habit

The prior page said "multiple dealers" and named one. The corpus supports a
sharper claim: 2018 is the year the supply relationship stops being a
single line and becomes a **redundant network**, and the redundancy is
legible in the dates.

**[[wiki/people/johnny-dealer|Johnny]]** (`+17243223678`) is the primary.
His thread carries **3,462 messages** — the seventh-largest in the master
dump, which puts a dealer inside Dan's top ten correspondents for the
period. Direct thread activity opens **February 19** and the grammar is
established immediately: *"Whats up buddy"*, *"Grams right?"*, *"On way"*,
*"Here"*, *"Come to door"*, *"Pullin in"*, and pricing stated openly — *"I
can do five for four or buck for 8"*. The logistics are domestic rather
than furtive-in-the-abstract: garage meets, *"Kill you lights when pullin
in"*, and the standing reference point being Fran's house.

**[[wiki/people/teddy|Teddy]]** opens **February 20**, one day after Johnny's
thread goes live. He is the accommodating secondary — holds stock, extends
credit, coordinates around the country club and Fairchance — and his thread
runs to February 2020. His own disclosures of fines, probation and cash
shortages are the record's evidence that the suppliers were as financially
fragile as the buyer.

**[[wiki/people/zaco|Zaco]]** (`+1724***3381`) opens **March 12** with
*"Yo it's zaco"* and runs to a single-night coda in November. The held
thread is **106 messages — 58 from him, 48 from Dan** — across 35 active
days, and he opens 21 of them; 14 of those openers announce stock without
being asked: *"I got strips,"* *"3 strip for 50,"* *"I'm in town I got 3
subs for 70 lmk if u want em."* The unprompted advertising is real and is
uncommon in this corpus. The inversion this page drew from it is not: Dan
opens the other 14 days, initiates the thread's trade ten days in, and
drives its two largest transactions. [[wiki/people/zaco]] carries the
complete log.

**[[wiki/people/al|Al]]** — saved in contacts under the joke label
"Socialist" — runs May 13 to November 1, 53 messages, explicitly cocaine.
**[[wiki/people/jajuan-davis|Jajuan Davis]]** (`+17243233522`) opens
**November 29** and runs 157 messages into February 2019, covering the
winter. Read as a sequence, the year's shape is four overlapping nodes
rather than one dealer: Johnny and Teddy from February, Zaco from March, Al
from May, Jajuan from late November. The redundancy is the finding. It is
also the first year the record documents single-node supply panics — when
Johnny's phone died or he went on vacation, the thread shows the system
failing ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]]).

And the flow is not one-directional. On **October 26** a Cash App
notification records *"Emaly Minerd just accepted the $50 payment you sent
today"*, and [[wiki/people/emaly-minerd]] documents Dan on the selling side
— Adderall, Xanax, kratom, cocaine — which is the part of the deep cycle
the phrase "multiple dealers" obscures entirely.

## The money, and a correction the page had to swallow

The old text said Dan borrowed *"$750/wk"* from his mother. That was
corrected on 2026-08-18 and the correction is preserved below in full,
because the shape of the error is instructive: an AI-secondary source
(`operating_manual.md`) generalised a single accusation made in a
**December 13, 2018** argument into a standing weekly rate, and inverted its
direction.

> **CORRECTED [2026-08-18]:** the clause above read *"mom borrow $750/wk."* The
> rate does not exist and its direction is inverted. The corpus holds one $750
> statement — an accusation about a single week, made in a 13 December 2018
> argument — which `operating_manual.md` (AI-secondary) generalised into a
> standing rate. The largest documented 2018 movement between them runs the
> other way: ~$14,000 from Dan to Suz in August–October, drawn against an
> estate that would not distribute until September 2020, $4,000 recovered.
> See [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank]] and
> [[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]]. What survives is the churn
> itself — irregular transfers of $20 to $3,000 running both ways, entered as
> debts and never reconciled — which is what "financial velocity" should have
> meant here all along.

The single best artifact of that churn is not prose. At **06:34 on the
morning of October 3, 2018**, [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]] sent Dan an
account statement — *"Dan to Suzanne / $1000 / $14,000+ / Sum of
transactions on app"* — computed before the argument it belonged to
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/provision-grammar]]). The infrastructure keeping its
own books, at dawn, in a text message. Against that sit her own messages
from the same year: **February 15**, *"I think I'm going to move my car into
the garage because I'm afraid it's going to get repossessed"*; **April 13**,
*"So I need $642 by the 23rd or my gas will be shut off"*; **September 27**,
*"I need money for gas, milk, shampoo, toothpaste more than anything."* Both
things are true at once, and the page should not pretend one cancels the
other.

The rail itself was Dan's idea. **August 31, 2018**: *"Do me a favor,
download 'cash app' from the App Store"* ([[wiki/people/suzanne-frank]]).

## Fran, and what her death did to the rest of the year

The night of **March 7–8** has its own page
([[wiki/timeline/events/the-fall-of-fran]]) and the four weeks after it have
another ([[wiki/timeline/events/fran-death-vigil]]). What belongs here is
the structural fact: the fall and the death sit at the year's hinge, and
almost every other thread in 2018 either feeds into them or runs off them.

The arrangement's opening night and the fall are the same night. The
[[wiki/people/danny-matthews|Danny Matthews]] thread opens **February 16**
and contains the sentence that fixes the chronology — *"you guys would be
the first couple we were with"* — verified in the held corpus at
`2018-02-16 23:32:34 UTC`, with the camming disclosure (*"we make like $150
an hour"*) seven minutes later (`kb/events/2018-fran-death`). Three weeks
later the encounter goes ahead in a house where a 97-year-old woman has just
fallen and broken her hip. Dan's defence is her own standing directive —
*keep me at home no matter what* — and he has never retracted it.

The vigil then generates the year's institutional conflict. On **March 29**,
mid-vigil, an eviction notice for [[wiki/places/155-virginia-ave|155
Virginia Ave]] is served by "Dian V. Moore," identified on 2026-08-18 as
[[wiki/people/diane-moore|Diane Moore]] herself — Fran's only child. On
**April 1** Fran is moved to a private room in the Uniontown Hospital
pediatric wing to die, a room she had because she and Ira were major donors
to the hospital, and [[wiki/people/fred-adams|Judge Fred Adams]] mandates a
twenty-four-hour rotation so she would not be alone. On **April 3** Diane's
exclusion letter is read, forwarded and lawyered — rules for Dan's presence
in the house, closing *"any violation of the rules will be considered
trespassing."* On **April 4** Fran dies with Dan and Annie in the room.

The same April supplies the year's two most-quoted messages and the
retraction of one of them, three hours apart and both verified to the
minute. At `2018-04-06 00:10:05 UTC` (= April 5, 20:10 EDT) Dan tells the
death-moment story — the silent request for a sign, the blink *"for the
first time in a week,"* the smile. At `2018-04-06 03:26:52 UTC` (= 23:26
EDT) he takes it back: *"upon further review of the video, it looks like a
monitor went off alerting us that her pulse was dropping... def isn't as
'supernatural' knowing that fact"* (`dat:0446`). He filmed it, watched the
footage, and corrected himself inside a day. That is the single strongest
piece of evidence in the year for how his self-reporting actually behaves
under check — and it is his own behaviour, not the wiki's.

And the money entered the mourning immediately. **April 4, 16:19**, hours
after the death: *"also.....,.inheritance will help with the mourning
proce$$"* ([[wiki/people/fran-coldren]], `dat:0446`). **April 27, 19:59**:
*"i got my inheritance check from grandma today."* The page records both
without softening either.

## The arrangement, from negotiation to November

2018 is the year the arrangement becomes a practice rather than a
conversation, and the dates now sit eight months earlier than the wiki once
had them. [[wiki/mind/psychosexual/arrangement-history]] previously treated
the November Alexis reunion as the earliest well-documented instance. The
February 16 thread moves it: the arrangement was negotiated, disclosed and
venue-scouted in a single twenty-minute exchange, and consummated three
weeks later.

What follows across the year is a ledger rather than a romance. **April
10**: *"give me a break after arranging a hookup for you and then having you
flake because you wanted to sleep."* **April 22**: the arrangement is
disclosed to [[wiki/people/new-jim-shaffer|Jim Shaffer]], who responds with
enthusiastic curiosity and his own red room. **May 21**: *"Nicole and Annie
can do the pool party thing"* — the women had a separate social thread
inside it. **June 27**: *"I said seriously that I would spend money to get
you an escort."* **October 18**: *"Find two escorts that you would be into
and I'll set it up."* **October 25**: outreach to a NYC provider, *"we'll pay
above rate"*, signed **Annie**. In every documented instance the money runs
from Dan outward ([[wiki/mind/psychosexual/arrangement-history]]).

Then November. [[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis Armel]] — Dan's ex, the
woman he left for Annie — is released after a year at SCI Muncy, and on the
night of **November 3** she and Annie hook up at Dan and Annie's house with
Dan present. He reports it to his supplier the next evening, flatly: *"She
just got out of jail. Locked up for a year. Last night she came over and
hooked up with my new girl"* (Nov 4, 7:50 PM). **November 5** is Dan's
birthday and becomes the reunion's culmination; the full sequence is at
[[wiki/timeline/events/annie-alexis-reunion-november-2018]]. On **November
28** he gives [[wiki/people/emaly-minerd|Emaly Minerd]] the fullest
retrospective account in the corpus, including the detail that he had
*"initially offered her $3000 to be a plaything for Annie"* — and Annie's
own framing that what actually happened was *"a different connection."*
Both sides of that are on the record and they do not agree.

## The channel that opens in the last month

The year's final twenty-five days open the longest-running attachment in
the corpus, and the record of its beginning is unusually precise about
money and unusually blank about the conversation itself.

**December 6**: Dan messages Dan Polyak — *"Dude guess what?"* — and the
first real conversation with [[wiki/people/ally-lubin|Ally Lubin]] happens.
It is **in no message corpus held here**; the iMessage record starts eleven
days later. A Sephora gift card is sent the same day. **December 11**: the
first Cash App notification naming "Alexandra Lubin," $42. **December 12**:
$25 to Polyak, memo *"For introducing me to Ally Lubin"*, preserved in the
Facebook payments ledger; $101 the same day; and a $250 Cash App gift.
**December 14**: $50. **December 17, 20:59:53**: Ally's first inbound
message in the corpus.

That is a man paying a finder's fee for an introduction to someone he had
already been reading for a decade — *"i've spent 10 years idolizing this
girl i've never met as the epitome of whatever 'cool' is"* (December 7).
The same week supplies the register failure that
[[wiki/mind/concepts/explicit-verbal-commitment]] builds on: **December
17**, Dan jokes *"I promise not to show up in North Carolina"* while asking
for her address to buy a Christmas gift, and she reads it literally —
*"You've literally said you'd show up to my house!!!"*, *"I'm scared to
hahah"*. The re-label failed in real time.

## Contradictions on the record

**The $750 rate.** Corrected 2026-08-18, preserved above. A December 13
accusation about one week became a standing weekly rate in an AI-secondary
source, with the direction of the money reversed. The corrected reading —
irregular two-way transfers of $20 to $3,000, never reconciled — is what
"financial velocity" should have meant.

**The November 7 clip.** Dan's testimony dates the annotated Annie/Alexis
cut to November 7, 2018. The camera overlay on the footage reads
**2018-06-06**. Neither has been retired
(`kb/events/2018-11-07-annie-alexis-annotated-clip`, confidence
`moderate`). The reunion's documented spine — November 3 first hookup,
November 4 report, November 5 birthday repeat — rests on messages, not on
the clip, and is unaffected either way.

**February 16 versus March 7–8 as the fall night.** The held-corpus spine is
March 7–8: the gurney quote is timestamped to the morning of March 8, and
the March 9 surgery message reads as a hospital course beginning rather than
continuing. A 2026 capture-based reconstruction, however, places a screaming
fall, a 911 call and a broken hip on **February 16** as well — sharing
enough detail with the March account that fusion in the 2026 retelling
cannot be ruled out. The night is the arrangement's one corpus-verified fact
(the Danny thread); the falls' choreography is attributed testimony. Filed,
not resolved (`kb/events/2018-fran-death`).

**2017 versus 2018 for the whole sequence.** Both 2026 captures behind
[[wiki/timeline/events/the-fall-of-fran]] date the fall-and-vigil to
March–April **2017**. Seven independent records date it to 2018. The page's
inference for the misdating — that April 2017 and April 2018 shared the
caddie-season-opening tag, and April 2017 held a second death Dan processed
that week ([[wiki/people/jay-lauer|Jay Lauer]], overdose, April 11, 2017) —
is reasoning, not testimony, and is labelled as such on that page.

**The July 13 entry.** The generated master timeline files it at 2018-07-13
with a message stamped 2018-10-11. That is a `bin/wiki-timeline` extraction
artifact. It is recorded above rather than quietly removed, because a
generator's errors are part of what this wiki knows about itself.

**The death-moment story and its own retraction.** Not a contradiction
between sources — a contradiction Dan resolved himself, on video, within
three hours, and the wiki keeps both halves.

## Limits of this record

- **The cited `raw/` paths do not resolve in this tree.** This page's
  `sources` name message CSVs, dox scans and Facebook exports that are not
  present in this repository; see the README's standing note. Claims resting
  on them are **unverified**, not false. Where a claim has been
  independently re-derived against the held corpus, that is stated inline
  (the February 16 timestamp, the April 5–6 story and retraction, the April
  6 caregiving wage, the Ally Cash App traces).
- **Ninety-four of 365 days carry a dated entry.** The remaining 271 days
  are not quiet; they are unrecorded. Whole stretches — most of July, the
  back half of June, the last eleven days of December — have no entry at
  all, and the year's own volume figure (40,514 messages) proves the silence
  is the wiki's, not the life's.
- **Single-source claims.** The 254-event LIFE-calendar count, the $15/hour
  wage, the caddie-trip payment figures, the memorial's framing, and the
  "major donors to the hospital" explanation for the pediatric room each
  rest on one source. The 1,716 GPS visits and the 40,514 messages each come
  from one export apiece, though from different exports than each other.
- **The estate arithmetic is not reconstructable here.** The first cheque
  (April 27) has no amount in the record, and the gap between it and the
  2020 distribution is not itemised.
- **No payroll record was ever checked** for the caregiving wage. It is
  Dan's contemporaneous account of his own pay.
- **Danny Matthews's side is empty.** His thread continues after March 7–8
  with no reference to the night, so what he knew is unestablished and only
  he could settle it.
