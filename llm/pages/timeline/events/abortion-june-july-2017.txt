# The abortion (June–July 2017)

**Date correction 2026-09-18:** the story-time pilot session surfaced a
two-year displacement in Dan's memory — this is a **2017** event, not 2018.
Everything pins to a June 24 → July 8, 2017 spine: photo timestamps, message
timestamps, the sonogram. The full dated record with the complete message log
is at [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer#junejuly-2017-the-abortion|June–July 2017:
the abortion]]; the evidence nodes are dat:1754–dat:1779; in-session
testimony t003–t006. Dan reviewed the complete record in-session and offered
**no corrections and no additions** — the record stands as told.

Annie discovered she was pregnant in the third week of June 2017, at
nineteen weeks' gestation against Pennsylvania's twenty-four-week cutoff.
Two weeks of scrambling — money, an appointment, telling the people who had
to be told — then three days of total silence across every thread in the
corpus (July 5–7), then a single text on the night of July 8 that says it
happened and that she had a five-year Nexplanon implant put in at the
procedure.

## The people in the room, June 2017

**Annie** — twenty-six, living the caddy-season Uniontown life, a year and
seven months into the relationship. When the test came back positive she told
Dan she was relieved at "knowing that I am able to conceive" — the fertility
anxiety was real and predated the crisis. Her heaviest grief statement came
six weeks later (July 23): "It's just really hard for me to see girls posting
pictures saying they are due around the time I would have been... It's like
what I would have given to have known when I first got pregnant... to know
that all those weeks I was sick and not feeling like myself to know there was
a baby. That I could have fixed this. Or have more time."

**Dan** — twenty-eight, Nemacolin caddy, daily drug use, broke. His first-day
line to her: "just remember that we're making sure that when we're really
ready and prepared that we can do this and have tears of joy, not sadness...
I'm gonna get $1000. It's simple and easier than you think." He told Suz the
evening of June 24, told Rick two days later while lying that Suz didn't
know, and told a friend the dark jokes both days. He did not look at the
ultrasound on June 27 — "Probably better."

**Suz** — fifty-four, the only steady adult in the story for two weeks. She
gave the stakes speech, the only hard information anyone had about what came
next (she had a D&C herself), the money planning, and — the one piece the
record keeps coming back to — the only on-record emotional processing of the
event, sent July 10 to a friend: "I had a life changing event last week. It
was very sad, but we all got through it." See
[[wiki/people/suzanne-frank#junejuly-2017-the-abortion|her page]] for the full
account.

**Rick** — fifty-eight, the emergency valve. Dan's plan was to call him for
the money; Rick "barely said anything," worried Dan would "say something to
bill and Ellen," and funded the $1,000 anyway — confirmed by Dan's later
"Thank you for another bailout." Rick's side survives only through Dan's
report to Suz. See
[[wiki/people/rick-frank|his page]].

**The periphery, in the order they appear:** Brad, Dan's fellow caddy, who —
per Dan's 1:55pm June 24 text — "was there a few weeks ago" at the same
provider; Annie was upset Dan had told him ("I want to cry"). A friend at
+17243668799 who got the June 26 "I was composed and put together...and then
I got pregnant" dark joke. Claire, who "knew as soon as I walked in / And
started crying because she's happy" when Annie told her family on July 2.
+17243236307, the long-term friend of Suz's who received the July 10
confiding — **not Dan**; the number was disproven as Dan's on 2026-09-18.
David Keller, who got the August 8 "100%" joke. The western-Massachusetts
friend who got the April 2018 retelling. Tom Maison, who got the February
2019 "late term abortion doctor who saved my youth" line.

## The telling order

Suz got it first — the evening of June 24, the same day as the test photo:
"Didn't mean to just drop that on you so blindly. I just didn't know when or
if she would need someone to talk to, and I wanted you to be able to process
it a little before she reaches out." Rick got it second, the morning of June
26, with the lie attached: Dan told him he hadn't told Suz yet. Annie's
family got it third — July 2, 1:04am: "Claire knew as soon as I walked in."
Her parents' insurance was the constraint nobody could solve in time: "if I
use my parents insurance, they will find out it was used at Magee" (June 28).
Dan told friends throughout — Brad on day one, +17243668799 on day three,
David Keller in August, the western-Mass friend in April 2018, Tom Maison in
2019. The telling order is the map of who Dan trusted with what: Suz got the
stakes and the inflation plan; Rick got the lie and the ask; friends got the
jokes; her family got it last, by her, in person.

## The money

The financing is the decade's money pattern in miniature — Rick as the
emergency valve, Dan inflating the ask to cover side debts, Suz as the ledger
he confesses the inflation to.

- **June 24:** $45 for the First Response plus groceries — Dan's first
  out-of-pocket, same morning.
- **June 25:** the only known real cost — $120 for the ultrasound. Dan's
  plan, confessed to Suz: "I should probably inflate it and get some more so
  I can pay you and get things straightened out a bit. I don't think it's
  gonna be worse to ask for 1500 than it would be for..." — $1,500 against an
  unknown total, partly to pay back what Dan owed her. Rick "may need a few
  days to get money."
- **June 26:** the Rick call. Per Dan's report to Suz, Rick "barely said
  anything" — "did exactly what I said he would 'why do you only call me
  about emergency stuff'." Suz: "Did he say he would get money for you?"
  Dan: "He didn't say and he's not answering now." The same day, the dark
  jokes to a friend: "Let's go buy a PS VR with this money / I wish I would
  have thought to use this as a money scam before / Hey I knocked a girl up,
  can I have lots of moneys please."
- **June 27:** nothing about money in the appointment-day thread — the
  appointment was the day, not the money.
- **June 29:** the Rick thread resumes — "Will know when she goes in to
  McGee by this afternoon... They said it usually takes a week" — and closes:
  "I'm glad / Life is complicated / BTW- I'm not glad. I relieved."
- **Sometime after:** "Thank you for another bailout." Rick funded the
  $1,000. The real total cost is unrecorded.

## The spine

**June 24** — Annie photographs a positive test (~3:46am); Dan buys the First
Response; his 1:37pm line: "just remember that we're making sure that when
we're really ready and prepared that we can do this and have tears of joy,
not sadness... I'm gonna get $1000." Annie's fertility-anxiety relief:
"knowing that I am able to conceive." Brad had "been there a few weeks ago"
(per Dan); Annie: "I want to cry." That evening Dan tells Suz — the
disclosure, the dark jokes, and her stakes speech exist **only in Suz's
archive**; Dan's own export has none of this thread.

**June 25** — "I want to go jump off a cliff right now" (1:53am, outside
Annie's parents' house). Money planning with Suz: the $120 ultrasound, the
inflate-to-$1,500 plan.

**June 26** — The Rick conversation, morning: Rick "barely said anything,"
and Dan lies that Suz doesn't know. Dan's later "Thank you for another
bailout" indicates Rick funded the $1,000. 12:21am, the dark joke to a friend:
"I was composed and put together...and then I got pregnant." 6:23pm, Annie:
"I feel like your mom hates me. She hasn't spoken to me since you told her."

**June 27 — appointment day**, recorded live in the Suz thread: "We're
passing century 3 / Going / 130 appt"; protestors warned about; 2:39pm —
"Sorry. They couldn't do it here we have to make an appointment." The window
math: "24 weeks is the cut off in PA" / "19 weeks" / "A month later and it
would have been too late." Ultrasound done; Dan didn't look — "Probably
better"; Annie had wanted to but a coworker advised her not to. Waiting on a
callback "from the people at McGee." And Suz's one piece of hard information
— **she had a D&C before**: "She to have a DNC which I've had before / It
isn't bad physically really but she may have to have a day or two of rest."

**June 28** — The sonogram: photo timestamped 1:36pm; 6:01pm measurements —
"BPD: 4.30 cm / FEM: 2.68cm 18w/d." "Magee didn't answer again"; "the other
place" — 412-578-1018. Insurance worry: "if I use my parents insurance, they
will find out it was used at Magee."

**June 29 – July 2** — The McGee wait ("They said it usually takes a week");
"I've been doing drugs every Day" as the stated urgency (July 1); Dan's
11:45pm July 1 panic attack ("I'm like having a panic attack right now");
Annie July 2, 1:04am: "Claire knew as soon as I walked in / And started
crying because she's happy."

**July 3, 4:36pm (Suz thread)** — "Are you finished?" Ambiguous referent.
If it meant the procedure, the event window pushes earlier than the July
5–7 reading.

**July 5–7** — Zero messages anywhere, every thread. Dan's hedged in-session
reconstruction ("Im not quite sure"): the procedure plus a day or two
recovering together, no texting because they were together. The tense shift
brackets it: future tense before the gap, past tense after. Suz's June 27
"day or two of rest" matches. The gap is the event horizon.

**July 8, 11:36pm** — "I'm sorry you had to go through that. I want you to
know that you won't need all 5 years of that implant." The procedure
happened around July 6–8, and Annie got a **5-year Nexplanon implant at the
procedure**. The same day, the Suz thread: grams, casino machines, "Are you
going to be able to get her when she's ready" — normal life resumes
mid-sentence.

## Aftermath

**July 10** — implant cramps ("remember they said that the implant would
make you cramp for a couple weeks") and Suz's confiding to her friend —
"I had a life changing event last week... It was horrible / But the only
responsible choice and thankfully there is still a choice" — her only
on-record processing of the event, sent sideways to a friend rather than
home to her son.

**July 18** — the intimacy exchange. Annie: "(Like no wonder I was never in
the mood and when we did fuck it was like you were hitting something, there
was a baby in there lol)." Dan: "since you've been preggo was the first
extended period of time you weren't lying about talking (etc) to other guys
behind my back / and i actually trust you again now lol." Annie: "I haven't
been even since before I had gotten pregnant." — her denial recorded as made,
unreconciled with his claim.

**July 23** — her heaviest grief statement, verbatim: "It's like I knew we
did what was best in the situation... I'll feel like I'm still a bad person
for not going through with the first pregnancy even though we did the best
thing... It's just really hard for me to see girls posting pictures saying
they are due around the time I would have been... It's like what I would have
given to have known when I first got pregnant... to know that all those weeks
I was sick and not feeling like myself to know there was a baby. That I could
have fixed this. Or have more time."

**October 12** — "This past year, I was pregnant and didn't even know. And
finding that out answered a lot of questions about my moodiness and sex and
ect. My mental state is honestly not back to where it was before getting
pregnant... And there has not been a single day that it doesn't cross my
mind." The same morning, Dan in an intimacy fight: "but as we both know, it's
an auto win to bring up the abortion...so i will stop before i dig myself a
hole. i mean it's not like you've come to me even once to talk about it or
anything." Two griefs in rooms that never connected.

**October 26** — "since I had that procedure it's like my lady part doesn't
work the same? I dont know if it's in my head or what." — her own hedge is
the honest frame; the record can't settle it.

**April 6, 2018** — his retelling to a western-Mass friend: "......well not
since the abortion..... LOL / fucking every night has its risks / and my
girlfriend waited about 5 months too late to recognize them / was no bueno.
however, the world knows i am not ready for parenthood. i did society a
service." "Five months too late" is his retrospective rounding of the
19-week sonogram — his memory ran long even at nine months.

**The later references, all of them:** August 8, 2017, to David Keller —
"the number of abortions I've had to pay for has gone up 100% since my gf
and I started haha" — **a joke; Dan confirmed in-session there was exactly
one abortion.** February 15, 2019, to Tom Maison — "the late term abortion
doctor who saved my youth." May 31, 2019, to Annie — "the abortion, gram,
moving to nyc, etc." — the line this wiki previously cited as its only
evidence the abortion happened, circa 2019, "without further detail." It is
now the tail of a complete record, not the head of a mystery. May 27, 2020 —
"someday i'll look back and tell my children's children about the exciting
and illuminating days of going through a legal late term abortion."

## Open questions

- **Magee vs. "the other place" (412-578-1018).** June 27: waiting on a Magee

## Why it matters

The money pattern that defined the next decade is fully formed here — Rick
as the emergency valve, Dan inflating the ask to cover side debts, Suz as
the ledger he confesses the inflation to. The pregnancy is, by Dan's own
July 18 account, the hinge of the relationship's early trust economy: the
first extended stretch he believed she was faithful, her denial standing
beside it unreconciled. And the grief never synchronized — hers daily and
private, his the absence of a conversation he wanted and never got. Dan's
own read in the story-time session — the Citizen Kane GIF, the archive never
getting the private part, the reporter chasing the record while Rosebud
stays his alone — is the honest limit on everything above: the messages are
the complete public record, and the private part stayed private.
