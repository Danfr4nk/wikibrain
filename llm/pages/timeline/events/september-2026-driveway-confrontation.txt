# The Driveway Confrontation, September 29 2026

The day started inside the rules and ended outside them. Annie came over
under the drug-contact framework — outside only, pre-cleared with Sammy,
minimal contact, toward zero — and the framework held: she tried to get Dan
inside to have sex and he said no. Then Jerel Coles showed up at the house
screaming about money Annie owes him, got inches from Dan's face, and the
afternoon became a 911 call the police never answered, broken glass, Suz
screaming at Annie to leave, and a neighbor standing post in the driveway
until it was over. By evening Annie was at the police station, and by 19:49
an emergency PFA had been filed and granted — the report went on file the
same night. The open question is what follows the paper, not whether the
paper exists.

## The day before it broke

The visit itself is worth recording precisely because nothing about it was
dramatic. The drug-related-contact framework — every contact with Annie run
through Sammy ahead of time, never inside the house, minimized, trending
toward zero — had been the standing arrangement, and on September 29 it
worked the way it was designed to work. Annie came over. They stayed outside.
The contact stayed minimal.

What the framework could not legislate was her intent. She tried to get him
inside to have sex, and he refused. That refusal matters to the record for
two reasons. First, it is the framework doing its job at the exact point of
maximum pressure — the boundary held where it would have been easiest to
drop it. Second, it fixes the day's moral geometry before the confrontation
rewrites it: whatever Coles screamed about that afternoon, the day had not
been an indulgence. It had been a man running a protocol, and the protocol
had held.

## The confrontation

Coles arrived at the house during the visit. What followed is Dan's
firsthand account, given the same evening, and it is recorded here in his
terms.

He came screaming about money — "I just want my money from her," the
grievance stated as a demand, the demand stated as though Dan's driveway
were the place to collect it. The screaming escalated into inches-away
intimidation: Coles in Dan's face, the physical grammar of a man trying to
make flinching happen. Dan stood his ground. He did not back up, did not
engage beyond holding the line.

Inside the chaos, Suz's voice cut through — screaming at Annie to leave.
That is the whole maternal history in one sentence: the woman who has
absorbed a decade of Annie weather, who held the "special" number and ran
the switchboard for the entire supply line, who was screamed at by Coles
himself in the August campaigns, standing in her own driveway, done
negotiating. There is no ambivalence in the record at this point. The same
woman who once routed everything now routes one thing: out. Annie left. Something glass broke — property damage, the
afternoon's physical receipt, still to be photographed for the record.

Someone called 911. The police never came to Dan's house.

That last sentence needs to sit by itself, because everything downstream of
this page depends on it. The standing assumption — that a 911 call summons a
response layer — failed in the specific instance where it was needed. The
safety posture going forward cannot assume the police as the response
layer. This is not a political statement; it is a logistical one, entered
the way the wiki enters everything: what was tried, what happened, what it
cost to learn.

## The neighbor

Mid-incident, a neighbor pulled up. He clocked Coles — saw the shape of what
was happening from the car — told Dan to get Annie, got out, and stood post
in the driveway. Then he de-escalated with Coles directly.

The wiki records this the way it records everything the exclusion requires:
the facts go in, the name stays out. What matters is the function. A third
party arrived unprompted, read the scene correctly in seconds, removed the
person the grievance was orbiting — Annie — from the blast radius, and then
stood between the remaining parties until the temperature dropped. It is the
only reason this page is about a confrontation and not about what a
confrontation becomes when nobody interrupts it.

Dan's own account of the neighbor, given the same night in a different
register, is that this is a man who looks after people as a matter of
course — the kind who notices what a household needs and handles it without
being asked. The driveway was simply the most dramatic instance of a
standing habit. The wiki does not name him and does not detail him; the
habit is the record.

## The aftermath on paper

Annie fled to her parents' house. At **17:56 EDT** she texted Dan three
messages, archived byte-identical in
`raw/2026-09-29-annie-texts-police-station/`:

> Hold on damn
> Parents home. Police here.
> May have to call you so if I do answer

At **18:49 EDT**, one more:

> At the police station

At **19:49 EDT**, the outcome:

> Emergency pfa filed and granted

She filed for an emergency Protection From Abuse order and a judge granted
it the same evening. All three screenshots are archived byte-identical in
`raw/2026-09-29-annie-texts-police-station/`. The granted order itself is now
in hand — photographed from Dan's phone that night and archived in
`raw/2026-09-29-annie-pfa-order/`: Fayette County Magisterial District
14-1-01, MDJ Jalissa A. Cox, *Anne Louise Ulmer v. Jerel Wayne Coles*, ex
parte hearing 9/29/26, signed the same day. The boxes checked: defendant to
refrain from abusing, defendant to refrain from contacting (including
entering her workplace or school, or harassing her), and defendant **evicted
from 311 Derrick Avenue, Uniontown PA 15401**, with an Order of Eviction and
Restoration of Possession directed to the constable. Violation is criminal
contempt under 23 Pa.C.S. § 6114. Docket and case numbers were blank on the
photographed page; only one page was shared.

Dan's standing play, set before either text arrived: answer if she calls,
and give a factual witness statement only. No advocacy, no editorial, no
relitigation of the eleven years — the facts of the afternoon, stated
plainly, to whoever at the station is writing them down. The discipline here
is the same one that held the driveway: say what happened, in order, without
adding the history that explains why it matters. A witness statement is not
the place for the grievance structure or the tracker theory; those live in
this wiki, where they can be checked. At the station, the only currency is
what was seen and heard, timed as closely as memory allows. Dan set that
rule for himself before he knew whether the call would come, which is the
point — rules set in calm survive contact with adrenaline, and this evening
had no calm left to spare.

As of 19:34 EDT, no call from her had come, and whether a report went on
file was unconfirmed. The 19:49 text closed that thread: the report went on
file and an emergency PFA was granted the same evening. The afternoon
produced a 911 call with no response at the house and a police station visit
that produced paper — the response layer failed at the driveway and held at
the courthouse, which is the shape of the remaining safety question, not a
resolution of it.

## The tracker question

Five days before the confrontation, on September 24, Dan asked Sammy where
you'd hide a GPS tracker on a car. The question is already in this wiki —
the September 24 section of [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]] records that the
lateness and silence that sent him asking were a frightened woman not
answering her phone, stalked by a man who kept showing up wherever she was.
Sammy answered with the hiding spots in order of likelihood: wheel wells,
inside the bumpers, the undercarriage and frame rails, the OBD-II port under
the dash, the trunk and spare-tire well, the engine bay. That exchange sat
in the record for five days as a precaution.

On the night of the 29th, the precaution became the standing theory. Dan,
Suz, and the household are quite certain Coles has a bug on Annie's car —
or, alternatively, that he is inside her Apple ID tracking her through it.
The reasoning is the kind the wiki respects: not a feeling but an
elimination. He keeps showing up where she is. Not once, not twice — a
pattern of arrival too precise and too repeated for luck. Either there is a
physical device on the car or there is account-level location access. Those
are the two vectors, and both are checkable.

The physical vector: magnetic trackers live on flat metal — wheel wells
behind the liner first, then bumpers, then the undercarriage; a plug-in
dongle on the OBD-II port takes ten seconds to spot; wired units sit near
the battery or fuse box for constant 12V. A mechanic with a lift finds any
of them in minutes. The account vector: Find My → People for who is
receiving her location, the Apple ID device list for anything unfamiliar,
and the iPhone's own unknown-AirTag traveling alert. The password change, if
it comes, has to happen from a safe device — not her phone, in case the
compromise is on the handset rather than the account.

The evidentiary handling matters as much as the search. If a device is
found: photograph it in place, do not remove it, hand the fact to the
police — a planted tracker is physical stalking evidence, and its value is
in its undisturbed state. If the Apple ID shows an unfamiliar device or an
unknown location-share: screenshot it before changing anything, for the same
reason. Annie was told, through Dan, to say the words at the station: "I
believe he's tracking my car." Whether she said them is part of the open
thread.

The significance the wiki logs is the predating. The tracker suspicion is
five days older than the confrontation. It was not invented after the fact
to explain the afternoon; it was the standing explanation before the
afternoon happened, and the afternoon fit it. That ordering is what turns a
theory into a corroborated one.

## "John Felix," refined

The confrontation also advances a smaller file, and the wiki records it
properly because small files are where impersonations live longest — with a
correction the corpus-sweep gate forced, stated plainly.

In the June 1 thread — the 1,095-message May 31–June 1 exchange the alignment
record already ties to Coles by its taunts, its unprompted 337 Saratoga drop,
and its burner address — Coles wrote, mid-taunt: *"you'll take 6 percs and
feel it tonight 🤣🤣🤣🤣🤣🤣🤣 invite John Felix over."* Read plainly, the line
asserts a dealer named John Felix and dares Dan to deny the supply line runs
through him. Dan's confirmed theory: Coles built the name by misreading a
text on Annie's phone — on **March 16, 2026 at 10:06**, Dan texted Annie
*"go work with John and Felix,"* two people (John Carney the contractor and
Felix the painter; the March 18 follow-up, *"is John done for the day?"*
asked of Felix, confirms two men), and Coles fused them into one.

The sweep turned up the refinement. Suz herself uses "John Felix" as the
painter's full name: *"John Felix paints and he is pretty good from what I've
heard"* (2020-09-03), and *"it belonged to John Felix's Dad who died last
year"* (2024-09-10). The name is real, six years deep in the family orbit —
so the fabrication was never the name. It was the man wearing it.

On **March 31, 2026** — two weeks after Dan's text, two months before the
taunt — a no-caller-ID man rang Suz asking whether Annie was with her. Asked
who he was, he said "it's John Felix." Suz, who knows Felix, told him flat:
*"no, I know him and this isn't his voice."* The caller pivoted to *"are you
and Annie doing coke with John Felix?"* The impersonation failed on voice
identification, in real time, by the one person in the orbit qualified to
make it.

So the refined finding: whether Coles fused the name from the March 16 text
(Dan's theory) or lifted Felix's real full name from the orbit, the "John
Felix" of the March 31 call and the June 1 taunt is not the painter. No
message history anywhere attaches the name to a supplier in Dan's life, and
the one time the name spoke on a live call, the voice was wrong. The wiki
states Dan's theory as his, the Suz messages as hers, and the voice check as
the fact that decides between the claimants.

The remaining Felix question — whether the Felix of the 2016 handle is the
same man as the decade-long Suz-thread Felix — is moved, not closed, by the
same messages: Suz's 2020/2024 "John Felix" for the painter supports the
identification without concluding it. It stays open on
[[wiki/people/john-felix]].

## The shape rhyme

Dan's own comment on the June 1 thread, pulled the same night for the
lawyer's packet, was that it had "the exact same shape" as the driveway —
and the wiki honors the observation because shape is evidence when the
details rhyme too.

June 1: taunts built from proximity to Annie's phone (the Saratoga address
dropped unprompted, the burner address, the fused dealer name), a money
grievance stated as a dare ("meet me"), escalation when ignored. September
29: the grievance stated as a scream in a driveway, the money direction
flipped, the same inches-away grammar, the same orbit around Annie as the
body the grievance circles. Four months apart, the structure is identical:
proximity intelligence deployed as intimidation, a financial pretext that
does not survive contact with the facts, and arrival — physical or textual —
wherever she is.

The rhyme is what makes the tracker theory load-bearing rather than
speculative. June 1 was remote: the phone was the vector, and the taunts
were assembled from its contents. September 29 was physical: the car, or the
account, is the vector, and the arrivals are assembled from its feed. The
method did not change; only the sensor did. A man who reads a woman's phone
to build taunts in June is a man who plants a tracker to build arrivals in
September. The wiki states this as inference, labeled, because the device
has not been found — but it is inference in the direction the evidence
points, and the September 24 question sitting five days upstream of the
confrontation is the corroboration.

There is a second rhyme, quieter, inside the household. On June 1 Dan was
up all night pulling the thread apart message by message, building the
lawyer's packet in real time. On September 29 he stood in a driveway and
refused to flinch, then set the witness-statement play before Annie even
texted. The panic of June has become procedure by September. That is not
numbness; it is the same forensic instinct with four months of reps. The
wiki logs the change because the change is the story of the summer: the man
who used to stay up dissecting now stands still and lets the paper do the
work.

## The grievance structure

Coles's stated grievance, screamed in the driveway, was money: "I just want
my money from her." The wiki records the sentence because he said it, and
then does what it always does with a stated motive — checks it against the
behavior.

The behavior does not serve the sentence. Annie does not have money to give
him; Dan's driveway is not where a debt from Annie would be collected; Suz
is not a party to any transaction between them. A man pursuing a debt goes
where the money is. A man pursuing a grievance goes where the person is.
Coles goes where Annie is — repeatedly, precisely, beyond luck — and the
money sentence travels with him as the pretext that makes the pursuit
sayable.

The direction of the money claim has also flipped. The June 1 thread and the
September 29 driveway do not tell the same story about who owes whom; the
alignment record flags the flip explicitly. A debt is a number with a
direction. When the direction changes with the audience, the number was
never the point.

Dan's own read, given the same night, is the one this page carries as the
assessment: what frightens him about Coles is not the prospect of being
punched — that would suck, obviously — but the absence of observable logic.
Coles acts without a definable goal and with violent tendencies, and the
combination is the one Dan says he has rarely felt from anyone: the sense
that a person is capable of truly terrifying things precisely because their
next move cannot be modeled. He corrected himself on the word "pattern"
mid-sentence — pattern implies something detectable — and landed where the
wiki lands: there is a theme (Annie, grievance, arrival), but a theme is not
logic. Logic would let you predict the next move. All anyone has is the
weather, not when to take cover.

The page states this plainly because it is load-bearing for everything
that follows. Grievance-driven confrontation does not end the way
goal-driven confrontation ends. A debt can be paid; a grievance cannot be
settled, because the grievance is not about the stated object. There is no
transaction that closes this file. That is the assessment, labeled as
assessment, and it is why the open items below are about posture rather
than payment.

## What remains open

The page stays open until these are answered, in rough order of urgency:

1. **The police report.** Answered 19:49 the same evening: report on file,
   emergency PFA filed and granted. Remaining: did Dan get the call she
   warned him about at 17:56, and did he give the factual witness statement?
2. **The damage.** The broken glass needs photographing — the afternoon's
   physical receipt, before cleanup erases it. Photos with timestamps, kept
   with the screenshots in `raw/2026-09-29-annie-texts-police-station/` or a
   sibling batch.
3. **The written timeline.** Dan's account, written down while it is fresh:
   times, sequence, who stood where, what was said verbatim. Memory degrades;
   the wiki does not accept "I'll remember."
4. **The tracker check.** Annie's car swept (wheel wells, bumpers,
   undercarriage, OBD-II port, trunk, engine bay) and her Apple ID audited
   (Find My shares, device list, unknown-AirTag scan) — with the
   photograph-don't-touch handling if anything is found.
5. **The response layer.** The 911 call went unanswered at the house. The
   safety posture can no longer assume police as the response layer. What
   replaces it — the neighbor is not a plan, he is a person who happened to
   drive by — is the live gap, and it is the most important open item on
   this page.

## Coverage and limits

What this page knows: Dan's firsthand account given the evening of
September 29, 2026; Annie's two timestamped texts, archived byte-identical;
the March 16 corpus row and the June 1 thread line that resolve the John
Felix fabrication; the September 24 tracker Q&A already in this wiki. What
it does not know: the exact time the confrontation started, the full
verbatim exchange, the extent of the property damage, the outcome at the
police station, whether Coles faces any charge, whether the tracker exists.
Nothing here is sourced to Coles himself — he has not been interviewed, and
his stated grievance is reported as stated, not as established.

The neighbor is recorded as a function, not a person, per the standing
exclusion: no name, no identifying details. The facts of the intervention go
in; the man does not.
