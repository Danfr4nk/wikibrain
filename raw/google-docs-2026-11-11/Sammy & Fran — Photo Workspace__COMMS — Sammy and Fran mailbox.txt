MATH — please verify:

- Rule: 1:12 slope (ADA Standards §405.2; PA residential code IRC R311.8 uses the same 1:12 max). 1 in. of rise = 1 ft of ramp. 30 in. rise = 30 ft of ramp.
- Landings: 5x5 ft flat landings top and bottom (ADA §405.7); handrails both sides (rise over 6 in.); 36 in. clear width minimum. Max single run 30 ft, so 30 in. is right at the limit.
