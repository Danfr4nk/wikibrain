  * Trump has grown his lead in the RCP aggregate tracking. He gained almost half of a percentage point this week to widen his lead to +1.8 points ahead of Biden.

It will be interesting to see whether or not DeSantis is able to do anything to begin closing the gap in the next few weeks. If he has ANY chance to stay in this race, he needs to catch up now before the debates - otherwise he’s going to be comic fodder for a confident Trump. His best shot is to walk on the debate stage armed with the pitch that his campaign is ascendant and only lagged before his official announcement.
