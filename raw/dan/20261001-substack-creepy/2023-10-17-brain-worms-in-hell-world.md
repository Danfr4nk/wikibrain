# Brain Worms In Hell World

*How Israel/Palestine Broke Our Brains*

Published: 2023-10-17T04:28:22.198Z
Canonical URL: https://creepy.substack.com/p/brain-worms-in-hell-world
Author: dan frank
Publication: Committee to Re-elect the President

---

An infestation of brain worms has spread to pandemic levels following the 10/7 terror attacks in Israel and the subsequent military response on the Palestinian territories. The infection is displayed prominently on social media and cable news by signs of acute neurological symptoms including severe loss of the ability to recognize nuance and complete failure to apply critical analysis.

Otherwise normal, kind, thoughtful people on both sides of the divide have spiraled into bloodthirsty ideologues, fueled by a toxic combination of horrific images from the attacks, religious affiliation and quasi-passive cultural bigotry. 

Pro-Israel voices (including the overwhelming majority of US mainstream media) have called for a full-scale glassing of Palestine, expressing a utter disregard for civilian casualties. Some have even argued that there _are_ no civilians to be considered, describing all 2.5 million Palestinians as Hamas-affiliated jihadi’s. Their analysis excuses or ignores entirely Israel’s long and well-documented campaign of oppression and occupation of Palestine, actions which have drawn international condemnation and have rightfully earned their label as an apartheid state.

Pro-Palestine activists have shown a callous disregard to the mass-murder of 1200+ innocent civilians whose crime was only to be born in the wrong place..or in the case of foreign national victims, simply being in the wrong place at the wrong time. In a dark twist of irony, some of these voices are guilty of the same sin that their opponents are - they argue that there _are_ no civilian protections, insinuating that all Israeli’s are genocidal settler-colonial monsters, culpable for the expansionist policies of their far-right government. They ignore the important reality that the “Al Aqsa Flood” terror attacks were not carried out with the design of liberation..on the contrary, their only real impact on Palestinians is to justify a predictable and extreme Israeli campaign of indiscriminate retaliation, bringing more violence, death and destruction to their lives. 

These two perspectives encapsulate a conflict which has come to so ubiquitously be described as ‘complicated’ that it is literally the premise of sketch comedy about the conflict. However true this meme may have been before 10/7, and many (like the brilliant, late Michael Brooks) have argued that the complexity was overstated, it’s certainly true today.

Understanding the relationship, an excersize which also requires some study of the history and geopolitical context of the region more generally, it becomes easy to see why most people, Americans most especially, just can’t get their heads around this conflict. Namely, the only reasonable conclusion of the elusive question “who are the baddies?” comes back with a decisive answer of : everyone, to varying degrees. Both sides bear some amount of responsibility for creating and escalating the conditions which have made the possibility of a pragmatic solution - and thus peace - an impossibility. 

True as that may be, when we weigh the facts and tally the score…it’s pretty clear that the initial burden of responsibility lies with one state, if only because they have dominated the conflict to such an asymmetrical point that there literally is no path for the other produce change. 

Setting aside the recent pandemic of brain worms and bad faith actors, Israel’s ever-expanding occupation into the West Bank and their abhorrent record of human rights abuses committed on Palestinians is not only a gross and unethical display of 21st century apartheid…it’s EXACTLY the kind of conditions which radicalize disenfranchised populations into violent ideologies and make terrorism events an inevitability.

This reality is no secret to a lot of younger Israeli citizens, and last year’s protests against the Netanyahu government’s attempt to strip power from judiciary and expansion of executive authority is another reminder of the vast distance between the politics of the people and the politics of the state. Mandatory IDF service for all non-orthodox citizens plays a role in forcing otherwise innocent people to share the burden of guilt, as well as giving Palestinians the image that every Israeli is an active force complicit in their oppression.

If you disagree - I am sincerely curious to hear what the options available to Palestine are which would convince Israel to stop the killing and illegal settlement expansion, let alone a recognition of sovereignty. 

They certainly can’t vote their way out of this.

Again, this is a long way from a zero-sum game. The security concerns of Israel **are** legitimate and there shouldn’t be much doubt that the neighbors of Israel are united in their intent to delete any Jewish state through force. Antisemitism has a long and dark history across the globe, a fact which should make us understand the desire for a Jewish homeland…no matter how much anyone may disagree with the conceptual nature of an ethnostate. 

In response to the attacks, Israel has declared war on Hamas. A bombing campaign began immediately on the densely populated Gaza Strip and a ground invasion is imminent. 2500 Palestinians have already been killed in the air strikes foreshadowing the staggering loss of life expected in the weeks to come.

American media has done little to criticize the brutal Israeli response and the Biden administration has done little to push back on unnecessary acts of cruelty being carried out - most notably the complete shutdown of basic needs like water, medicine and electricity from being allowed into Gaza. Compounding the situation is the reality that Gazans have no way to escape, all of the points of access have been closed. At the checkpoint location into Egypt, where thousands of families were waiting in hopes of a re-opening, found themselves unable to escape the violence they were fleeing when the IAF targeted the crossing with multiple days of air strikes (in violation of international law) ensuring that the border remains closed.

Polling conducted after the attack paints a clear picture of the aligned American opinion on the subject. Most surprisingly is the sharp increase in Democratic support of Israel, which has grown steadily in the last two decades as lobbying groups like AIPAC have poured unthinkable sums of money into Democratic politics, cleansing the party of any pro-Palestine voices by spending millions on their primary races.

In the aftermath of 9/11, a shell shocked America signed off on ‘the war on terror’, a military operation which grew into the 20 year quagmire of pointless deployment in the Middle East, for which we paid a terrible price in both blood and treasure. Over time the wars became deeply unpopular among a broad swath of the American electorate. So unpopular, in fact, that 15 years after a bipartisan supermajority of congress voted to allow Republican neocon George W Bush to launch the invasions, a Republican ran for President and won on a platform of explicitly criticizing the war and the administration who designed it - tearing into the consequences of his own party’s decision to strike Iraq and Afghanistan as “the worst decision in the history of our country”. 

Yet here we are, just a few decades later, reacting with the same emotionally-charged and poorly considered reaction that we so irresponsibly displayed before.

The Israel/Palestine conflict may be *complicated* but it’s sure not beyond our capability to see it for the humanitarian disaster it is and is about to become.
