# The DeSantis Delusion

*Why the DeSantis 2024 Campaign Was Doomed To Fail*

Published: 2023-05-23T02:56:08.999Z
Canonical URL: https://creepy.substack.com/p/the-desantis-delusion
Author: dan frank
Publication: Committee to Re-elect the President

---

In the last few months, most observers have accepted the reality that Donald Trump is the clear 2024 GOP front-runner and unlikely to face a substantial challenge on his way to winning the nomination. This consensus represents a shift from the (largely media driven) narrative of Ron DeSantis as a formidable and serious primary contender who had the potential to consolidate support from the far right base and key demographics like suburban women who represent the party’s moderate wing. The case theory for this is built on an analysis of DeSantis which compares his ideological similarities with Trump’s, while highlighting the contrast between their abilities (cognitive and legislative), tactics and most importantly - personalities. Donald Trump and Ron DeSantis may have shared political objectives, but as the theory goes - only the latter has the temperament, vision and establishment support necessary to accomplish them. 

On paper, this works. Remove all of the context and political nuance and a compelling case can be made that nominating DeSantis is clearly a smarter strategic decision for a party that has lost the popular vote in 4 of the last 5 elections. He IS, as the theory asserts, a more palatable and capable version of Trump - and would undoubtedly outperform Trump in a general election with the critical demographic segments of suburban moderates and women whose support has peeled away slowly over his political career. Further, while he hasn’t yet faced the full force of presidential election oppo research initiatives, there’s absolutely no way that the skeletons in his closet outnumber those of the 1980’s New York real estate developer (with billions of assets in international grey money, cash deals) /a-list tabloid celebrity. 

In a political vacuum - DeSantis should be considered AT LEAST a competitive threat to challenge a disgraced and legally imperiled ex-President.

But, here’s the thing: none of this matters when you factor in Donald Trump as a variable in this equation.

People say Trump is ‘unpredictable’…but that’s absolutely wrong. To be sure, he’s outrageous, unbelievable, bizarre and unconventional - but he is this way in an incredibly predictable manner. Subsequently, his predictability can be connected to one factor of concern: short-term egocentric survival or advantage. He will say or do anything, regardless of any other consideration, which lets him win ‘the moment’.

This plays out in a number of repetitive scenarios depending on the situation, and one of these which we have the advantage of historical example of is electoral contests. 

Fun fact: Donald Trump has claimed fraud in every election that he did not win. When Ted Cruz won the 2016 Iowa caucus, Trump took to Twitter and TV to protest the results with the same scattershot fraud allegations he used in 2020, eventually settling on the narrative that a Cruz campaign robocall had misinformed voters as the source of his grievance. And we’re all familiar with the events which followed his 2020 loss to Joe Biden.

So let’s imagine a 2024 primary season where DeSantis leads in the delegate count as he heads to the convention. What does Donald Trump do? Does he graciously accept defeat and, in an act of ideological virtue, support and campaign for a GOP victory without his name atop the ticket? Or does he attack? Does he mobilize his loyalists to boycott the general election, or worse - vote for Joe Biden? As outrageous as this sounds, the sting of a fresh loss against DeSantis makes this a more likely outcome than a gracious endorsement of his own party’s nominee. 

And what impact would this have on the GOP general election viability? While MAGA support has dwindled since he left office, Trump still controls a loud and important constituency within the Republican Party. Even the most conservative estimates still show that 20-30% of Republican voters remain strictly loyal in their support of Trump - if only half of THOSE people were deactivated for the 2024 election, it would remove any path to victory for DeSantis. 

This is the most glaring of several impossible obstacles that Trump presents to any primary challenger. 

If you run against him, he will attack you with full force. His attack will include the mobilization of his base against you to propagate opposition narratives with the same or more intensity as those reserved for cross-party general election attacks. 

This isn’t a prediction. This is a precedent and we already see it playing out as Trump again begins distributing nicknames to his opponents. We know, for a fact, that he will sabotage any Republican nominee that bests him. 

You know who else knows this? Mitch McConnell and the Republican mega-donors. Ronna McDaniel knows this. Everyone with a financial or ideological stake in seeing a 2024 GOP victory knows that a defeated Trump will lead to a Biden victory. 

So as much as they might long for the calm, presentable fascism of President DeSantis…they’re stuck. 

By appeasing and propping up Trump through the most inappropriate and unethical moments of his presidency, they’ve cemented his support base enough that the only escape from this fait accompli is to back Trump..or watch as he tears it all down in revenge.
