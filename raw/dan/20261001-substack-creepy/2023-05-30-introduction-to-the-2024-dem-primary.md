# Introduction to the 2024 Dem Primary

Published: 2023-05-30T02:51:25.448Z
Canonical URL: https://creepy.substack.com/p/introduction-to-the-2024-dem-primary
Author: dan frank
Publication: Committee to Re-elect the President

---

Though you may not know it if your media diet is restricted to exclusively mainstream/legacy outlets, but Joe Biden does face some primary challengers in his re-election bid for the 2024 Democratic nomination.

Let’s be clear - Biden will have no problem in cruising to an inevitable nomination _if not_ for any dramatic, unforeseen development large enough to fundamentally shift the current political calculus. Despite the current President’s problematic situation - long underwater in national approval polling, an economy mired by inflation, few policy victories to claim and (perhaps most dangerously) his age the subsequent reflection it has on cognitive ability - there’s not much resistance to renominating Biden, even from the more contrarian progressive wing where Bernie Sanders has already offered his endorsement.

But there are almost 15 months available until Election Day for an unexpected event to upset the race…so let’s look at the Democratic primary field as it exists today.

**Robert F Kennedy Jr -** being a Kennedy means a few things: sailing trips in Hyannis Port, serial marital infidelity and a big boost in support during your inevitable political endeavors. Bobby Kennedy’s son Robert Kennedy Jr is doing his best to carry on this historic legacy and as such, currently occupies the 2nd place slot in the early polling despite an ideological platform that can most generously be described as…unusual.

RFK Jr. has been a vocal advocate of the long debunked theory connecting vaccines with autism. Long before the current (post-COVID) generation of vaccine skepticism, Kennedy championed the scientifically rejected narrative popularized in the 1990’s. His nonprofit org Children’s Health Defense saw a donation surge during the fallout from COVID vaccine controversy and further solidified Kennedy as the marquee advocate in vaccine skeptic communities. While he is not shy about using this issue in stump speeches, his current website makes no reference to any related policy proposals.

While the vaccine stuff is bad in both strategy and impact - Kennedy does have some interesting tactical ideas that have a Trump-like appeal. The central issue to his campaign (according to his website and comms releases) is a government reform effort targeting a wide range of legitimate concerns across the federal government. Here, Kennedy is looking to reign in the same intelligence agencies and Foggy Bottom dwelling permanent-class civil servants that Trump ham handedly (or disingenuously) targeted, known in right wing parlance as the “deep state.” Per his campaign website 

> We are going to remake public institutions to serve the public. We will roll back the secrecy and make government transparent. We will protect whistleblowers and prosecute officials who abuse the public trust. We will rein in the lobbyists and slam shut the revolving door that shunts people from government agencies to lucrative positions in the companies they were supposed to regulate, and back again. We will get money out of politics. We will open our institutions to real citizen involvement. We will restore integrity to government.
> 
> [Link](https://www.kennedy24.com/honest)

RFK Jr has experience in this realm. For years, he has broken with family precedent by speaking openly about CIA involvement in the JFK assassination, and appeared prominently in Oliver Stone’s recent documentary on the event. 

Other platform issues include a national reconciliation. His website states 

> America is more polarized and divided now than at any time in living memory. Both sides seem to agree that the basic problem is the horrible people on the other side. Both sides are wrong. The basic problem is the division itself.
> 
> [Link](https://www.kennedy24.com/heal)

Interestingly, he proceeds to lay out a few reasonable policy measures that would invariably be denounced as “woke” CRT by almost every sitting GOP representative

In the case of race relations, reconciliation includes repairing the damage caused by centuries of bigotry. Our administration will take racial healing seriously through a program of Targeted Community Repair.

It perfectly exemplifies how delusional his claim of moral equivalency on both sides is. It’s an unusual strategy to say the least and only serves as an indicator of the demographics which the campaign is targeting - independents. Specifically, what the former President would call “Never Trumper’s.” 

I find the whole thing to be very fucking weird. There’s a not-too-subtle appeal to the conspiracy minded, even without the vaccine stuff finding it’s way into the official platform. At the same time, the campaign is doing its best to make a ‘serious’ go at things and makes an effort to paper over anything that could open up the perception that Kennedy is a liberal Alex Jones. The result is a campaign that lacks cohesion in policy and rhetoric. He’s hoping that the stars align - Biden dropping out late, his name ID giving him the clout to raise enough cash to stay in the race and the DNC failing to properly prepare a substitute candidate who can coalesce both wings of the party. He’s going all in on the hopes that he pulls an inside straight.

**Marianne Williamson** running in the Bernie Sanders aisle is Houston’s most famous and controversial crystal mommy. 

Williamson is a polarizing figure to say the least, especially among progressives who remain divided about the strategic implications of supporting a primary opponent to run against an incumbent of their own party. On paper, she is a much better ideological match for progressives - an ardent supporter of M4A, climate action, student loan forgiveness and even slavery reparations. But politics is a game of strategy and even most of the justice dems see the prospect of backing a long shot candidate as too large of a risk, potentially costing committee positions or oppose for a role in the Biden administration.

Marianne, while facing astronomically low odds of winning nomination, will not be the subject of scrutiny from me..as I believe that healthy electoral competition is a good thing and I personally agree with the progressive policy agenda she is running on. 

Will she win? Uh, no.

But I like her and I’m always happy to see the party move left.
