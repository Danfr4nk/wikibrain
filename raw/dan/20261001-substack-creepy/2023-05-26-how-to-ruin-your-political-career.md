# How To Ruin Your Political Career

*A helpful guide to self-sabotage*

Let’s say you’re a well known neofascist politician with a promising future ahead of you. After a short but noteworthy congressional stint, you advance to become governor of a top 5 population state - easily winning a second term. You’ve become a household name. You are one of the most influential and powerful voices in the Republican Party, and you’ve carved a niche within the party as the most prominent opponent of culture war issues with your (at times novelty) policy portfolio. For politics, you’re young…not yet 45. 

Your future is so bright, you need shades..and as a Floridian you probably already have some ready to go.

Whether it’s impatience or delusion or pure stupidity, whatever impulse has inspired Ron DeSantis’s decision to run against Donald Trump for the 2024 GOP nomination is a baffling and wholly unforced error. 

In my last post, I laid out the folly of his run from the perspective of the ‘Trump effect’ on general election strategy…but there’s several other layers of painful consequence involved here and no one will feel this more than DeSantis himself. 

After months of posturing and speculation, DeSantis has now filed the FEC paperwork and officially entered the race. From the perspective of Trump loyalists, he has opened Pandora’s box - a Republican who threatens Trump has committed an act of unforgivable treachery. 

While the Trump/DeSantis fued has slowly escalated during the run up to his assumed campaign launch, the point of no return hadn’t been crossed. In fact, I personally believe that had DeSantis hit the brakes on his own run and went all-in on a Trump endorsement..he would have been able to fully salvage his reputation in MAGA world. He would have been a shoe in as the 2028 front runner, with another full term as Florida governor available to continue his initiative of successfully passing a string of megalomaniacal authoritarian laws combatting the “woke mind virus.”

This was not to be - and now DeSantis will learn the lesson that 17 other Republican presidential hopefuls received a few years ago.

To paint the picture of where this is all going, we need to go back 6 years - the 2016 Election and Donald Trump’s rise to political power. Primary politics are the ideal environment for Trump’s smash mouth style of insult theater. Unlike the general election, where courting independents requires at least a modicum of restraint and reverence, the first phase of an election is an appeal to the base - and no one is more skilled in throwing red meat to this crowd than Donald Trump. His unique advantage is the decades of performative self-branding and a surprisingly accurate gauge of an audience who have as little in common with his life experience as they do with his economic interests.

Just look at the career destruction that Donald Trump single-handedly orchestrated as he fought for party control. Top tier names in the party like Marco Rubio and Ted Cruz were irreparably spoiled, reduced to trading blind loyalty in their voting power in exchange for keeping a fraction of their former political influence. 

Those who refused to fully surrender were met with total annihilation, forever ostracized from conservative politics. Ironically, the best case study of this is another Florida governor - Jeb Bush. Once cast as the future of a more diverse and inclusive GOP, the damage of taking on Donald Trump was fatal and irrevocable.

The fury from Trump and his flunkies has now begun in earnest and it’s an effort which will prove as easy as it will be enjoyable for veterans of the conservative meme wars. DeSantis is notoriously an unskilled operator in his appearances, somehow (bewilderingly) apparently still lacking proper media training - often losing composure when confronted. 

He will also be haunted by his general vibe of (what can best be described as) inhuman tendencies. This will come as no surprise to him, having already dealt with an incident where he was claimed to have eaten a pudding cup by using two fingers instead of a spoon.

And finally, the “x factors” of a campaign…the unknowable and random environment which highlights improvisation and gut intuition over wonky nerds who rely on calculated, focus-grouped strategy to display their executive abilities. 

The absolute clusterfuck dumpster fire of a campaign launch that befell DeSantis yesterday should be all the evidence necessary to understand just how disadvantaged he truly is in this regard. Even Joe Biden’s comms team found the opportunity in the Twitter space disaster to fundraise by clowning on what became known as the #DeSaster.

It just doesn’t make any sense to me. Why someone, who would benefit so much from playing the long game would make such a predictably bad career decision. Instead of a terrifyingly dangerous prospect for democracy as the likely 2028 front runner, Ron DeSantis has chosen to make himself little more than a toy for Trump to bat around for a few months.
