# LLMs in Dan's life: importance and position vs. age cohort

## The claim, stated plainly

For Dan, LLMs are infrastructure, not oracle. He does not ask models questions; he builds systems that think: prompts that yield cognitive partners, pipelines that yield persistent analytical capability, bootloaders (CATO/MAX) that turn any fresh session into a pre-configured forensic analyst. The wiki-brain is not a product of asking LLMs about his life. It is a product of building LLMs that analyze his life systematically, then iterating until they work.

## Importance: the daily organ and the builds

- **Daily cognitive organ.** Heavy multi-year collaborator: long Gemini sessions (77-"node" lore sessions; 3,986 logged prompts across which the bootloader system was designed iteration by iteration), forensic corpus mining over ~217k records, persistent context injection ("LLM Quick Brief" blocks on pages), lossless extraction demands ("keep ALL of the information in").
- **Role-assigned, model-agnostic stack.** Claude = analytical workhorse ("Master Forensic Prompt"); Claude Code = tool-builder (shell access; a model that can run `bin/wiki-lint` is held to a mechanical check no conversational model is); Gemini = interaction model and confessional layer (the Fran death story was told to the AI first, with full awareness of how that reads from outside); ChatGPT = early bootloader, retired on a one-line verdict. Grok = agentic/subagent builds.
- **What got built:** MAX/CATO dual-engine persona system; MNEME (Apr 2026 spec — personal-memory platform solving LLM cold-start via five-layer corpus ontology); DANMODEL (voice-clone pipeline, pure Python stdlib + numpy, 39,378 stimulus-response pairs, Jaccard vs. TF-IDF-RAG baselines, blind distinguishability test); iMessage Analysis Toolkit (Gumroad, Feb 2026); Bunker Core agentic frameworks; AI video essays (planned).

## Philosophy: external processor, adversarial by default

AI is external processor, not companion: memory, structuring engine, steelman partner, honesty-enforcement tool. Operating demands: no sycophancy, no softening; primary evidence over narrative; lossless detail retention; labeled confidence; ambiguity stated bluntly; hypotheses challenged adversarially; positions updated only on evidence. Best AI value is adversarial, not generative — reverse the weak hypothesis, supply the contradictory evidence, force re-analysis. Delivery optimum measured at 11-20 words, dense, evidence-first.

## Position vs. the 30-49 cohort

Dan is 37 (DOB 1988-11-01; as of 2026-09-09), so the 30-49 bracket is the baseline. Population figures are survey records, not testimony:

- **Adoption:** 61% of 30-49 ever use AI chatbots; the bracket is the most intensive, 34% daily (Pew 2026). 55% ever used ChatGPT as of Feb 2026 (Pew 2026). NORC (Apr 2025, 30-44): 16% daily, 14% weekly, 39% never for personal activities.
- **Use shape:** 42% use chatbots as search replacement; 25% entertainment; 24% image/video editing (Pew 2026). 51% of LLM users say main purpose is personal/informal learning vs. 24% work (Elon/Imagining the Digital Future Center). Only 9% have ever tried Claude.
- **The gap:** the cohort consumes answers; Dan builds the machine that analyzes him. Consumer (oracle queries, shallow habit, single-model default: 72% ChatGPT) vs. infrastructure-builder (bootloaders, pipelines, exocortex, multi-model role assignment). Outlier on intensity, depth, and intent — top fraction of a percent on data richness brought to the models, by his own 2026-09-09 self-assessment.

## The day-one daily-driver rule

No model gets anointed daily driver on day one. A day-one verdict is a certainty claim, and the testimony-veracity ledger prices stated certainty at ~0.25 actual — hedged claims outperform confident ones. The bootloader architecture exists precisely so no single model is load-bearing; naming a driver on first contact collapses the system it took months to build. The gauntlet before any verdict: Master Forensic Prompt on a live question, a corpus-mining task with ground truth, a bootloader load into a fresh session, and a documented failure-mode map. n=1 day has no falsifiability. (Recorded 2026-09-09, at Dan's request for an honest assessment.)
