## The one-paragraph compact form

Dan's conspiracy worldview: power is exercised through real institutions — intelligence services, party
machines, corporate lobbies — using mundane mechanisms (structural incentives, legal-but-engineered rule
changes, managed belief). The exotic, mystical versions of conspiracy theories are often themselves
operations: limited hangouts and controlled opposition designed to discredit the boring-but-true ones.
His decision rule is mechanism + motive + historical precedent, not vibe. He leaves questions open rather
than filling them, and he treats the official story as a hypothesis to test — not a default, not an enemy.

## Part I — Non-obvious threads

**1. The mundanity principle.** The best predictor of his belief across 33 verdicts: how boring the
mechanism is. Believed: mail-in ballot rule changes, insular elite power structures, covert-war funding
streams, FBI informant handling. Rejected: lizard people, chemtrails, 5G sickness, vaccine-autism.
Exoticism is a *demerit* in his system — the opposite of the typical truther, for whom it's the appeal.

**2. The hybrid template — his signature move.** 9/11: real hijackers + rigged buildings + Mossad driver,
CIA as the open question. J6: real rally/march + inserted lethal core, the conflation IS the mechanism.
Boston: real bombing + FBI asset nexus. 2020: real votes + engineered rule changes, "conspiracy in plain
sight." He almost never claims total fabrication. He claims a real event with an operation layered on
top, and the blur between the layers doing the work. This is the rarest conspiracist structure — and
it's the same template three times independently (9/11, J6, Boston), which suggests it's a cognitive
primitive for him, not a per-case conclusion.

**3. Standing suspect class, but plural.** CIA, FBI, Mossad, DNC forces, LAPD, Ted Turner. No single
"they." The JFK verdict alone names three distinct parties (CIA rogues, mob, Israeli intel) in coalition.
He builds coalitions, not monoliths — and assigns each actor a distinct motive.

**4. Cui bono + precedent, not pattern-matching.** His motive lists are historically literate: Bay of
Pigs + detente + RFK's crime war (JFK); post-Iran-Contra funding continuity (CIA-crack); on-ramp logic
(Pearl Harbor). He cites documented precedents (COINTELPRO for J6) as the license for inference. This is
why he's not default-suspicious about celebrity deaths: no motive-precedent fit, no verdict. Cold, not
reflexive.

**5. Lone-actor rejection calibrated by victim significance.** He rejects lone-shooter stories for
JFK/RFK/MLK — system-level threats to entrenched power — but accepts the official story on Sandy Hook
and stays agnostic on most celebrity deaths. The discriminator: does the removal serve an institutional
interest? The 60s victims share a through-line — anti-war, anti-corruption, left — they are his
political ancestors. His conspiracy beliefs track his politics: the people he believes were killed are
the people he would have marched for.

**6. The meta-layer: theories about theory-management.** Pizzagate = real phenomenon laundered through a
limited hangout; QAnon = controlled opposition built to farce-associate and discredit the real thing. He
models *belief itself* as a managed battlespace. This connects directly to the wiki project: whoever
controls the record controls what's believable. The conspiracy theorist is usually the *object* of these
operations; Dan theorizes them as instruments — one level up from the genre.

**7. Emergency-opportunity theory.** The 2020 verdict and the COVID verdict share a structure: a crisis
expands the action space, and the actors who benefit are the ones already positioned to exploit it. Not
"they released it to steal the election" — subtler: the crisis is the medium, the engineering happens
inside it, legally. This is the "conspiracy in plain sight" frame generalized.

**8. The agnosticism gradient as data.** 9 of 33 with no position; 4 with explicit gut-vs-official
splits (Fed, fluoride, Cobain, Seth Rich). The corpus cross-check found *zero* cases where he was
previously vocal about something he's agnostic on today. His agnosticism is genuine, not a retreat. This
is the veracity-ledger pattern in the wild: stated certainty is cheap, hedged certainty is expensive.
For the wiki writeback, the gut/official split is itself a first-class datum.

**9. New-on-record vs settled.** Only the 2020 election position was settled and documented before today
(Gemini pinned session, Node 8 — near-verbatim match, today extends it). ~20 verdicts are new-on-record.
Boston is a genuine 13-year evolution: 2013 tweets *mocking* Boston trutherism → today: FBI-asset theory
(likely via later Waltham-murders and FBI-handling reporting — worth pinning down in dissection). J6 is
the most documented: three AI platforms, ~18 months, unmoved. So the worldview has a long-stable core
and a large periphery being articulated for the first time *today*.

**10. The Israel thread — newly on-record.** Mossad as a driver in JFK (possible) and 9/11 (deep).
Sits inside his settled anti-ethnostate / full-Palestinian-sovereignty position. Notably: he keeps it at
the agency-and-motive level (regime change in Iraq) — never the mystical register. The corpus has no
prior statements on this thread; today is the first articulation. The dissection needs to hold his
framing, not drift.

**11. Economic reductionism.** Guidestones = "Ted Turner" (a name, no mechanism — the sparsest verdict in
the set). Big Pharma = "the only realistic scenario." Illuminati = wealth preservation through insular
structures. When in doubt he reduces to money and institutional self-preservation.

## Part II — vs. the typical conspiracist worldview

**Similarities (real, don't deny them):** the official story is a hypothesis, not the default;
intelligence agencies are the standing suspect class; cui bono is treated as evidence; pattern-seeking
across events (Pearl Harbor's on-ramp logic → 9/11); a taste for the hidden variable.

**Differences (the load-bearing ones):**

1. *Mundane > exotic.* The typical conspiracist believes the hidden truth is wild. Dan believes the truth
is usually boring and visible, and the wild versions are often the cover (QAnon as farce-association).
A genuine inversion of the genre.

2. *He accepts official stories.* Moon landing real, Sandy Hook real, vaccines don't cause autism,
chemtrails are condensation. ~15% of his verdicts are outright conspiracy-rejections. The typical
conspiracist worldview has no "yes, that one happened as reported" category this large.

3. *Agnosticism instead of gap-filling.* The typical conspiracist fills every gap with certainty. Dan
leaves 9 of 33 blank and labels gut feelings as gut feelings. His epistemic posture is closer to an
intelligence analyst's confidence ratings than a truther's.

4. *No monolithic "they."* Typical: one cabal behind everything. Dan: distinct actors, distinct motives,
sometimes in coalition, sometimes opposed. He never collapses them.

5. *Real-event + layer, not fabrication.* Typical: crisis actors, no planes, staged. Dan: the event was
real; the operation rode on top. Harder to dismiss, harder to prove — he seems to understand both.

6. *The meta-move.* Typical conspiracists are the objects of limited hangouts; Dan theorizes them as
instruments. One level up.

7. *Left-political valence.* The typical American conspiracist worldview is right-coded. Dan's is
left-coded: the victims he believes in are anti-war/anti-corruption left figures; the villains are intel
agencies, DNC forces, Big Pharma, the banking order. His J6 position is the tell — it fits neither the
left's (pure fascist coup) nor the right's (total fed setup / total innocent), and he treats that
epistemic loneliness as corroboration.
