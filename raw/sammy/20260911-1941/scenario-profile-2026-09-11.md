## 4. The cross-cutting rules

These are the regularities that survive across groups — stated as falsifiable claims:

1. **Target-clarity beats ambiguity.** The hottest configuration in every triangle is the one where the other man's target is unambiguous: "The other guy is into you" (10), "One of them is only into you" (10), "He is only into her" (+4), "The guy joins in on you" (+4). The coldest pursuit level is "he experiments" (7.78). Ambiguity about who wants whom is the moderator; clarity in either direction is the amplifier.
2. **Proximity is load-bearing.** Every distance/exclusion variant tanks: "You wait in the bar" −4, "You are in the next room" −3, phone-ex group mean 6.80 (lowest non-pegging group), "She calls you right after" −5. Involvement variants do the opposite: "You are involved: shared blowjob" 10, "She joins in halfway" 10.
3. **Filming amplifies heat, doesn't create it.** "Not filmed this time" −5 on the 2019 shape; "She holds the camera" / "You film your half" / "You are filming" all 10s. But "She films it" on pegging stays 3. Camera is a multiplier on a hot scenario, not a rescue device.
4. **She orchestrates, he is pursued, he is not the center.** Top groups: "she watches you get a blowjob from another guy" (9.80), "she sets you up with a guy as a gift" (9.60), "filmed scene: she directs from behind the camera" (9.25). She-directs 8.48 tops authorship; he-pursues-you tops pursuit; you're-the-focus is the *lowest* spotlight level.
5. **Neutral turf, known-quantity men, no friends.** Stranger/acquaintance fine, friend poison (−5); neutral ground beats both his place and theirs (−4s); older male partner −6 (single-item, see caveats).
6. **Talked-about beats silent; intense beats slow; ongoing beats one-off.** Explicit talk 9.25, "No talking" −4; intense 9.43, "Slow, no rush" −5; ongoing 9.29, "It happens again monthly" 10.

## 7. Caveats (read before quoting any number)

- **Ceiling effect:** the instrument samples his existing fantasy space (mean 8.36, 64% at 9–10). Metric-level means near the ceiling are compressed; the deltas in §2–3 carry more information than the level means in §5.
- **Single-item levels** (⚠): M_age younger/older, novel routine, film ambiguous, N_x "you cross alone", E_env open-ended (n=8), inten intense (n=7), expl explicit talk (n=4) — directionally suggestive, not established.
- **Confounded levels:** recip "you receive" mixes the pegging act with peripheral-threesome receiving; the 5.17 mean is meaningless as a direction-preference — the act-level data (§1) is the real finding.
- **Non-diagnostic by design:** F_attr and N_x were held near-constant across the instrument (her highly attractive, mutual taboo-crossing throughout). Their flatness is a design property, not a finding about his preferences.
- **F_part "partial" level** has zero items; M_enth has no "eager" level in the data (only reluctant/willing appear).
- The veto was never used — the scale absorbed it. If a v2 is built, the pegging cluster suggests the veto needs a lower anchor than the current item set provides.
- All of this is stated preference on imagined scenarios, n=1, one sitting. It predicts what he *rates*, not what he'd do.
