Here are 30 content prompts to help you come up with your next batch of content:

# Stories

Stories are one of the most powerful ways to let your audience get to know you on a deeper level and build authority around your expertise.

- <i>How did you get started doing what you do?</i>
- <i>What was a time when you really struggled with something?</i>
- <i>When was the last time you cried?</i>
- <i>What’s a fun fact about you that most people don’t know?</i>
- <i>Tell your audience about a client you worked with that was successful</i>

# Current Events

Give your opinions or takes on things that are happening right now in the world or in your industry.

- <i>What’s something big that’s happening in your industry?</i>
- <i>What lesson did you learn recently from an article you read?</i>
- <i>What big macro trends do you see coming?</i>
- <i>What are your thoughts on something that’s trending on X?</i>
- <i>Who just launched something in your industry that you can share a lesson from?</i>

# Polarizing Opinions

Sharing your polarizing opinions on your industry is a great way for your audience to align with you on your beliefs, and it creates more unique content that is different from what everybody else is saying.

- <i>What’s a polarizing opinion you have about your industry?</i>
- <i>What is one thing that most people do wrong?</i>
- <i>What is something that people teach in your industry that is harmful?</i>
- <i>What is something that everyone avoids doing that you think they should do more?</i>
- <i>What is one thing that you do differently than most people?</i>

# Advice/ Tutorials

Teach a tip or trick that your audience would find useful.

- <i>What are 5 software products you can’t live without?</i>
- <i>What are 3 things your audience can do right now to get a result?</i>
- <i>What’s the #1 thing you would do first if you were in your audience’s shoes?</i>
- <i>What’s the biggest secret to success that no one thinks about?</i>
- <i>What are 5–10 lessons you learned from your last successful project?</i>

# Business

Use these prompts to gain more attention for your company or business without it feeling like you’re constantly promoting what you do.

- <i>What does a day in the life at work look like for you?</i>
- <i>Who was a client of yours that got insane results recently?</i>
- <i>How did you come up with the name of your business?</i>
- <i>What lesson did you learn from your business in the last week?</i>
- <i>How did you overcome a past challenge in your business?</i>

# Lifestyle

- <i>What’s something you did recently for fun?</i>
- <i>What was your most recent vacation?</i>
- <i>What’s a weird hobby you have that most people wouldn’t know?</i>
- <i>What do you do in your spare time when you’re not working?</i>
- <i>What lesson can you share from your last grocery store trip?</i>

---

I highly recommend pulling from all of these different categories so you get a lot of variety in your content.
