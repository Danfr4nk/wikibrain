## <b>How to Prompt</b>

### What is a Prompt?

<p>
A <b>prompt</b> is a set of instructions or a description you provide to an AI to help it generate content. Think of it like giving directions to a creative assistant: you tell the assistant what elements you want in a project and how you envision the final outcome. 
</p>

Prompts are essential because they guide the AI in creating content that matches your vision. The clearer and more detailed your prompt, the better the AI can understand what you want and deliver a result that meets your expectations.

### How to Prompt

1. <b>Be Specific:</b> Clearly describe the main subject, setting, and any key elements you want in the video.
   - Example: "A serene beach at sunset with waves gently crashing on the shore and seagulls flying in the sky."
2. <b>Include Details:</b> Mention any particular details that are important for the video.
   - Example: "The beach has golden sand and a few palm trees. The sky is painted with hues of pink and orange."
3. <b>Focus on Emotions or Atmosphere:</b> Describe the mood or atmosphere you want to convey.
   - Example: "The video should feel peaceful and calming.”
4. <b>Use Simple Language:</b> Avoid complex terms and jargon. Simple, straightforward language is most effective.
   - Example: "A cozy living room with a crackling fireplace and a cat sleeping on the rug."

By following these steps, you can create effective prompts that help the AI generate high-quality videos that align with your vision.

### What is ‘Enhance Prompt’?

<p>
If you are not entirely sure how to write a prompt, use the ‘enhance prompt’ feature to let the AI turn your simple concepts into detailed prompts. 
</p>

It’s highly recommended to additionally use “enhance prompt” even on <b>image-only inputs</b> as well for best results.

# 🥳 Video Generation

## Creating Your First Video: Image-to-Video

You can use real photos, including selfies, to create stunning videos. To get the best results, make sure your images are in landscape (16:9) format. This format performs better on Luma due to the model's training.

### Step-by-Step Guide

1. <b>Choose Your Image:</b>
   - <b>Using AI Generators (Midjourney, DALL·E, Leonardo):</b>
     - Enter your preferred prompt with a 16:9 aspect ratio.
     - For Midjourney: Use the <code>-ar 16:9</code> parameter.
     - For DALL·E: Request a 16:9 aspect ratio in your prompt.
     - Download the generated image.
   - <b>Using Your Own Photos:</b>
     - Ensure your image is in landscape (16:9) format for optimal results, though portrait mode also gets great results.
2. <b>Go to Luma Labs:</b>
   - Visit [lumalabs.ai](https://lumalabs.ai/dream-machine).
     - Tap ‘Try Now’ to sign in if you aren't already.
3. <b>Upload Your Image:</b>
   - Click the photos icon and select your image.
   - Ensure the ‘enhance prompt’ box is checked (a black checkmark will indicate it is enabled).
4. <b>Generate Your Video:</b>
   - Leave the text box blank or type your desired direction.
   - Press the far right arrow to create your video.

That's it! Follow these steps to transform your images into amazing videos with Luma’s Dream Machine.

## Image to Video Tips

<ul>
<li>
Upload a JPG, JPEG or PNG image by clicking the image icon on the generation page, you can also choose to provide a prompt to describe the scene.
</li>
<li>
With “Enhance Prompt” enabled, you just need to describe what you want to happen in the scene. For example: <code><image of a car> + “</code><i><code>a red car driving on a road</code></i><code>”</code> 

- It’s especially helpful to describe the types of motions that you want to happen in the video
</li>
<li>
If you cannot get the desired output, you can try without “Enhance prompt”. This requires describing the image + what you want to happen in the scene. 

- You might see no or low motion with unenhanced prompts. Try to include motion cues in your prompt if necessary.
</li>
<li>
In either case, it helps to describe the important details in the image as much as possible, what types of actions are being performed, and how the different parts of the scene should move. E.g., <code>A woman dancing ballet, her arms gracefully extending upward as she twirls around, the dancers behind her bowing low to the ground</code>
</li>
<li>
In general, you may get better results by being more specific about:

- Camera motion: <i><code>“A dramatic zoom in”, “An FPV drone shot”</code></i>
- Actions and motion: <i><code>“A teddy bear swimming with its arms and feet as the turbulent water splashes all around”</code></i>
- Object features: <i><code>“A white teddy bear wearing sunglasses with soft fur texture”</code></i>
- Setting and background: <code>“A beautiful cloudy sunset near a Caribbean beach”</code>
</li>
</ul>

## Creating Your First Video: Text-to-Video

Text-to-video prompting allows you to create dynamic videos from simple text descriptions. By entering a text prompt, you can generate a video that visually represents the content of your text. This is perfect for creating engaging content without needing extensive video editing skills.

### Step-by-Step Guide

1. <b>Go to Luma Labs:</b>
   - Visit [lumalabs.ai](https://lumalabs.ai/dream-machine).
2. <b>Enable Enhance Prompt:</b>
   - Check the ‘Enhance prompt’ box if it is not already checked.
3. <b>Enter Your Text Prompt:</b>
   - Type your desired text prompt into the box.
4. <b>Generate Your Video:</b>
   - Click the far right upwards-facing white arrow.
5. <b>Wait for Completion:</b>
   - Allow time for your creation to finish processing.
6. <b>Download or Extend:</b>
   - Download your result or extend it as needed, and you’re all set!

By following these steps, you can easily create a stunning video from text using Luma Labs.

<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
<b>Tip:  Always check the ‘Enhance prompt’ box for best results. If your text prompt results in an error with ‘Enhance prompt’ enabled, simply uncheck the box and re-run your prompt.</b>
</callout>

## Text to Video Tips

- A great way to start to get good results is to use “Enhanced Prompt”. By enabling this you can use short prompts like <i>“a teddy bear swimming in the ocean” </i>and the Dream Machine will automatically generate additional descriptions to help create a more detailed generation.
- If you are not able to get the expected results, try without “Enhance Prompt”. <b>Unenhanced prompts need to describe the contents of the scene and the desired action (3-4 sentences)</b>. Ex: <code>“</code><i><code>In a somber, nostalgic style, a young man sits on a tree stump in a forest, the warm autumn leaves surrounding him. He wears a brown jacket, dark shirt, and blue jeans, his fingers deftly moving along the fretboard of an acoustic guitar.</code></i><code>”</code>
- In general, you may get better results by being more specific about:
  - Camera motion: <i><code>“A dramatic zoom in”, “An FPV drone shot”</code></i>
  - Actions and motion: <i><code>“A teddy bear swimming with its arms and feet as the turbulent water splashes all around”</code></i>
  - Object features: <i><code>“A white teddy bear wearing sunglasses with soft fur texture”</code></i>
  - Setting and background: <code>“A beautiful cloudy sunset near a Caribbean beach”</code>

## How To Extend Videos

You can extend the length of your AI-generated video clips to maintain character consistency and scene flow by 5 seconds each time you click ‘Extend’. Here's how.

1. <b>Locate the Extend Button:</b> On your generations page, find the video you wish to extend.
2. <b>Click the Extend Button:</b> Press the button labeled 'Extend' below your video.
3. <b>Automatic Extension:</b> The AI will automatically extend the video by another 5 seconds, starting from the last frame of your original video. This ensures a seamless continuation, keeping characters and scenes consistent. Extends cost 1 generation.
4. <b>Refine with Prompts:</b> You can type additional details or adjustments into the prompt box to specify what you wish to see in the extended part of the video.
   <image source="notion-file-block://247198c3-27ba-81ec-97c0-eff5f7f26e44/30906f25-2073-422e-ac2a-0a1e14797a50?space_id=94e198c3-27ba-81da-aaa3-0003f4fa72e3&name=Screenshot_2024-06-23_200816.png">
   Extending your video will cost 1 generation. Type your desired prompt into the text box to direct your scene’s extension.
   </image>
5. <b>New Generation:</b> After typing your prompt, click the right arrow button to submit your clip. The extended video will appear as a new generation on your videos page, alongside the original video you extended. It doesn't replace the old video; it creates a new one.

By extending videos, you can maintain continuity and ensure smooth transitions, enhancing the overall quality and coherence of your video projects.

# 🪄 Keyframes in Dream Machine

Dream Machine is rolling out its latest feature - Keyframes! This guide will walk you through what keyframes are, how to use them, and how you can create smooth transitions by setting beginning and ending frames, along with optional text instructions. Additionally, we will explain how you can extend your video beyond the last keyframe for even longer animations.

### What are Keyframes?

Keyframes are specific points in a video timeline where you define the starting and ending frames for an animation or transition. In Dream Machine, keyframes enable you to specify the beginning and ending frames, allowing the system to interpolate, or transition, between them.

### What is Interpolation?

Interpolation is the process of calculating intermediate frames between two images to create a smooth transition. When you set a start and end frame, Dream Machine generates the in-between frames based on the provided images and any text instructions. This ensures a seamless visual flow from the start image to the end image.

## Using Keyframes in Dream Machine

### Step 1: Upload Your Initial Image

<ol>
<li>
<b>Open Dream Machine</b>: navigate over to [https://lumalabs.ai/dream-machine](https://lumalabs.ai/dream-machine).
</li>
<li>
<b>Upload Image</b>: Upload your initial image as you normally would for image-to-video. 
</li>
</ol>

### Step 2: Setting the End Frame

1. <b>Look for the New Button</b>: Once you've uploaded your initial image, a new button will appear to the right of your uploaded photo. This button has a "+" button and text reading "End Frame (optional)".
2. <b>Upload End Frame</b>: Click on the button to upload a second image that will serve as your end frame.
   <image source="notion-file-block://247198c3-27ba-8129-8d45-ffa823028a84/bf8a8d3e-dbc0-4782-a569-4febcbfa00cc?space_id=94e198c3-27ba-81da-aaa3-0003f4fa72e3&name=Screenshot_2024-06-27_001748.png">
   Once you’ve uploaded your initial image, the ‘End Frame’ box will pop up, allowing for you to upload a second image which serves as your end frame.
   </image>

### Step 3: Adding Text Instructions (Optional)

1. <b>Text Prompt Box</b>: Beneath the two image frames, there is a text prompt input box.
2. <b>Leave Blank or Fill In</b>: You can either leave the text box blank, or fill in with instructions on what you want the transition to do. These instructions can include details about movement, transformation, color changes, and other effects. Press the white arrow button to submit your generation.

### Step 4: Interpolating Between Frames

- <b>Automatic Interpolation</b>: Dream Machine will automatically interpolate between the start and end frames you have uploaded, creating a smooth transition based on the provided images and any text instructions. Once your video has finished processing in the queue, you will be able to view the final result!

### Step 5: Extending Your Generations (Optional)

<b>Extend Beyond the Last Frame</b>: After setting your keyframes and creating the initial generation, you can further extend your video beyond the last keyframe.

1. <b>Continuous Extension</b>: Dream Machine allows you to extend your generation by clicking the ‘Extend’ button. This will cost 1 generation.
2. <b>Text Instructions for New Frames</b>: As before, you can add text instructions beneath each extension to guide the process, building on your initial keyframe experiment!

### Tips for Effective Use

- <b>Experiment with Different Images</b>: Try using different images for your start and end frames to see how Dream Machine interpolates between them.
- <b>Use Text Instructions</b>: Provide specific instructions in the text prompt box to guide the interpolation and achieve the desired effect.
- <b>Extend Your Generations:</b> Continuously extend your results to stretch beyond the last frame.
- <b>Plan Your Extension</b>: When extending your video, try to plan or envision the sequence of events and text-based transitions you want to use to maintain a coherent and engaging animation.
- <b>Try Many Different Instructions: </b>Feel free to experiment.<b> </b>This will allow for you to create and explore completely different transitions!

By following these steps and tips, you'll be able to effectively use the Keyframes feature in Dream Machine to create stunning animations and transitions. Enjoy exploring and creating with this powerful new tool!
