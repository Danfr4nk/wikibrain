<h3>
🔧 Prompt Building Blocks 
</h3>

- <b>Persona/Role</b> → "As a business consultant advising small businesses."
- <b>Task/Objective</b> → "Write a blog post about the benefits of remote work."
- <b>Context</b> → "Considering the shift to remote work during the pandemic."
- <b>Instructions / Format</b> → "In a bullet-point list."
- <b>Constraints</b> → "Keep the post under 400 words."
- <b>Exemplars</b> → "Similar to articles published in Fast Company."
- <b>Tone</b> → "Professional yet conversational."
- <b>Output Style</b> → "Informative with a focus on practical advice."
- <b>Audience</b> → "Targeted at small business owners."
- <b>Purpose</b> → "To persuade them to adopt remote work policies."
- <b>Timeframe</b> → "Highlighting changes in the last two years."
- <b>Emotion</b> → "Evoke a sense of empowerment and opportunity."
- <b>Keywords</b> → "Include phrases like 'flexibility,' 'productivity,' and 'cost savings.'"
