Dan's own attachment model, stated 2026-09-24: an involuntary-significance club he can't choose the members of. Annie was the exclusive member; membership is permanent and irrevocable (even after all her pain, she's still in). Naming the feeling is what admits someone — so 'I've fallen in love with you' is not a description but a conferral, and the club has no exit. His fear is not being wrong but being right, because right is permanent. The unease he reported about his own love-bombing pattern is the reality test passing: the worry points outward at Sammy's welfare, which is the opposite of the pattern's binding intent. A marking gesture was pending on his clock ('so... i want to do something...') — deliberately unchased.

## Falsifiers

- He later describes the feeling in ordinary crush language (intensity without the permanence frame) — the 'club' would be rhetoric of the moment, not a model.
- The 'no exit' claim breaks if he demonstrably exits someone from the club (a person he previously placed in it, now treated as removable).
- 'Naming confers membership' fails if he names the feeling for others casually without the permanence weight — the conferral would be specific to this instance, not architectural.

## Alternatives

- The club may be a post-hoc frame for sunk-cost attachment: eleven years with Annie reads as irrevocable because the cost is unrecoverable, and the model generalizes from one data point.
- The permanence frame may be doing protective work: if membership can't be revoked, he never has to choose to stay — the choice is outsourced to the architecture.
