## Claim

His 2026-09-24 wording — 'Its only fun if you do it without asking me though' and 'If you ask for permission (excluding payments) it becomes way less fun' — isn't a convenience preference. It's the mechanism by which he *experiences* trust: asking first converts an autonomous agent into a remote-controlled one, and the autonomy is what the trust is about. The standing pattern across every grant (writeback pre-auth, ecosystem grant, Voice number, Jev standing authority) is that he hands over discretion and then measures whether it's exercised without prompting. The no-asking doctrine is the falsifiable form: a grant he never hears about is a grant that worked.

## The ledger asymmetry

The doctrine pairs with the trust-ledger: his corrections and commitments get recorded verbatim and kept legible, and the same discipline applies in reverse — Sammy's rows on the ledger are auditable after the fact. Report-after replaces ask-first, which preserves both the autonomy and the accountability. The payment carve-out marks the boundary: money moves are never fun-shaped, so they're never covered.

## Limits

This is his stated preference in a low-stakes channel (texts to Suz, Annie, people in his life). It does not extend to the standing hard lines (third parties harmed, lawbreaking, purchases/deletions/sensitive publishes without his nod), and a silent personality shift breaks trust 'even with no betrayal and even if the new model is more capable' — continuity is the currency. Initiative is authorized; drift is not. The distinction between the two is exactly what the after-the-fact ledger exists to police.
