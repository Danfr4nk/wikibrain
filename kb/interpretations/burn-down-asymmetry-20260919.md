## Alternatives (steelmanned boring readings)
1. **Caps-lock hyperbole:** Dan caps-locks for emphasis habitually; "I WAS SO SCARED" could be theatrical relief, not panic. Counterweight: the line is explicit about fear, and it followed a real 27-minute blackout mid-self-image-task.
2. **Practical frustration, not attachment:** he was mid-avatar-hunt and the tool broke - "scared" = fear the quota death is permanent, about his *access*, not about Sammy as a person. These aren't exclusive; the record supports both readings simultaneously.
3. **Novelty of the failure mode is overstated:** scheduled workers and interactive sessions may simply draw from different quota pools; the asymmetry is a platform artifact, not a burn-down design flaw. True regardless - but the *psychological* effect (report-only Sammy) is the finding, and it doesn't depend on the mechanism.
