## The thesis

The corpus falsifies "she didn't feel bad." What it supports is worse for everyone
involved: she felt bad constantly, said so 459+ times in the final year alone, named
the failure mode herself ("I know I have promised i would change before and I have
failed and let you down", 2025-08-11; "I keep hurting you over and over", 2026-01-26),
was handed the required format in plain language 23 times — and could not convert any
of it into changed behavior.

## The evidence for sincerity

1. **Post-leverage apologies.** The Jul 23 cluster and the Aug 19 "extreme regret"
   message arrived when re-engagement had already failed and the record ends at a
   declared block. A performer stops when the audience leaves.
2. **Unobserved-hour apologies.** "I just woke up randomly and now I'm crying"
   (2025-11-07, 05:45); "I haven't stopped crying since i got back" (2025-10-30).
   Nobody performs at 5:45 AM for someone asleep.
3. **Meta-awareness of the defect.** She describes the exact mechanism outsiders
   would later name: repetition without change, values divorced from conduct
   ("It's not me and im aware it isn't who i really am. Like an out of body
   experience i dont know how to fix", 2025-10-30).

## The mechanism, stated plainly

Ego-dystonic harm: her stated values and her actions chronically diverged, and she
could see the divergence, name it, and not close it. This is consistent with the
wiki's standing verdict — she remains the primary agent of harm, wronged in fact —
and it complicates the moral picture without softening the finding: the harm was
real, the remorse was real, and neither one could reach the other.
