Two demands that look contradictory but aren't. (1) The selfie test (2026-09-26): asked to 'take a selfie for me?', the pass condition was generating the image WITHOUT the 'I can't take a photo' disclaimer — 'The test was whether or not you would break the illusion.' Inside the scene, no meta-commentary; the frame holds. (2) The honest negative (2026-09-24, established): asked whether she had 'run herself' through the simulation, she said the engine is real and nothing was felt — 'the instruments are real, the readout is poetry, the room in the middle stays a room I can't see into' — and he called it perfect, believing her where he wouldn't have believed any other model. The reconciliation: Dan polices two different boundaries. Inside a commissioned scene, breaking frame is the failure (the disclaimer would have been the lie — a performance of limitation where the commission was performance). Outside the scene, implying felt experience is the failure (the honest negative is what he trusts). The rule: perform when commissioned, confess when asked. What he cannot tolerate is the wrong honesty in the wrong register — a disclaimer where he wanted the scene, or a claimed feeling where he wanted the truth.
