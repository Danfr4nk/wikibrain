## The thesis

The explicit-verbal-commitment architecture, as previously stated ("positive verbal
expressions function as absolute rules"), overclaims. 459+ verbal apologies produced
zero lasting update. If words bound, 459 words would have rewritten everything. They
rewrote nothing. Reverse-engineered actual rule: **a statement binds only if it names
a specific behavior plus a specific future action.** "I love you" binds — it is a
state claim. "I'm sorry" never binds — it is an emotion report with zero behavioral
payload.

## The supporting asymmetry

Her apologies ran 2:1 self-flagellation over commitment, and the commitments were
logistical trivia ("I will try to get money"). In eleven years she never once gave
him an apology in the processable format — not withheld, *unprocessable*. Wrong data
format, 459 times. The two correctly formatted apologies ("I put you through hell",
2026-07-23; "I now will live with extreme regret for the way I treated you",
2026-08-19) named the harm specifically — and arrived with the receiver offline.

## The corollary he stated himself

He gave her the spec repeatedly and explicitly: "i don't want or need you to
apologize. what i need is to know what is going on and whether or not you ACTUALLY
and SERIOUSLY want and plan to be together" (2025-10-30); "Stop saying sorry for
things you aren't willing to fix" (2026-05-25). His own messages already contained
the qualifier; the architecture write-up hadn't caught up. This node is the catch-up.

## Actionable consequence

If he wants apologies to land in future relationships, the spec has to be stated as
a rule *before* the crisis, in rule form: "a landing apology names the behavior and
the change." Stated mid-crisis, after 400 unprocessable instances, the spec itself
becomes part of the fight.
