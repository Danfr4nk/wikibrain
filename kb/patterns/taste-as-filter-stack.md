## The filter stack

The 2025 MASTER CRATE analysis reveals selection by constraint, not by category:

- **Energy floor ~0.70** (Q1 0.704, median 0.836) — the load-bearing filter; remove it and genre coherence dissolves.
- **Three BPM rooms** — 125-145 house corridor (146/232), 165+ dnb wing (19), sub-100 halftime basement (15). Three DJ sets, not one playlist.
- **Dark register** — median valence 0.322; 75% at or below neutral. Heavy, not happy.
- **Voice-as-texture** — 96.1% no foregrounded vocal; 47.4% voice-as-chop. Under the lyric qualifier this is one appetite: voice-and-rhythm as physical material.
- **Mid-fame** — median popularity 43; neither obscurity-mining nor chart-chasing.

## Anti-loyalty

- 321 artists, max 6 tracks each (2.6%). 162 labels, max 6 each. No imprint loyalty, no name loyalty — he follows the *scene*.
- Zero track overlap with his 5,321-track historical dump. The crate is ammunition, not canon: 59.5% 2025 releases, built in four waves Oct 31-Dec 22, 2025.

## Pipeline

Hear → Like → promote to crate: 91.3% of crate tracks passed through Liked. The re-entry is visible in the Like button itself (1,417 likes in 2025 vs 88 in 2017-2023). The 498 bulk-liked-then-purged URIs (Sep/Nov 2025) are the old canon removed from the working library.

## Falsifiers

- A crate era with genre concentration AND energy variance would kill the energy-floor claim.
- Sustained artist/label concentration in a future crate would kill the anti-loyalty claim.
- The Shostakovich waltz and Frank Ocean 'Thinkin Bout You' outliers remain unexplained — a tightly filtered crate admitting two slow sung sincere records; function unknown (set-closer? joke? genuine left turn?).
