## The shape

Two numbers describe the relationship's operating system. His: 129 severance
declarations, 0 severance signals from her (documented in
[[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]]; median re-engagement gap 36s). Hers: ~1 in 20 of all
messages an apology, rising to ~1 in 17 in the final year, carrying no behavioral
commitment. Threat → apology → reset → repeat, for a decade. The apology's
behavioral payload is measured at zero: 23 explicit specifications of the required
format from him (Oct 2025–May 2026) produced no format change (bare-"sorry" share
still rose to 67% in 2026-Q2).

## Why it was stable

Both parties kept ledgers of the other's unfelt remorse. Hers: "You throw a fit over
a fucking sandwich and I never even got an apology" (2024-07-19). His: 23
meta-apology messages itemizing what a real apology would contain. The asymmetry is
in enforcement, not in grievance: his feeling had a weapon (severance threats), hers
had only repetition. Each side's move reliably produced the other's countermove and
nothing else. It wasn't a relationship failing to repair; it was a system
successfully doing the only thing it knew how to do.

## The terminal break

The loop broke in the terminal rupture not because the apologies stopped but because
the receiver went offline: the two spec-perfect apologies ("I put you through hell",
2026-07-23; "I now will live with extreme regret for the way I treated you",
2026-08-19) arrived when re-engagement had already failed and the record ends at a
declared block.
