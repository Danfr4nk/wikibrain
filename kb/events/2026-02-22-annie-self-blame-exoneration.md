## What happened

In a late-night message, Annie explicitly exonerated Dan of responsibility for
the relationship's failure and assigned it entirely to herself:

> "You didn't do anything to deserve the way I have treated you... I am the
> only one to blame for this... I do not deserve the unconditional love you
> have given me and I know that."

## Why it matters

This is the clearest statement of her eighteen-month self-blame position (Aug
2025 to Aug 2026) and the position her 2026-09-06 Libby-era disengagement claim
reverses. It is also directly contradicted by that later outburst, which is
why the two events carry a contradicted edge between them. The verdict page
wiki/mind/synthesis/libby-era-engagement-verdict.md treats this message as the
record's own account of why the relationship spoiled, against which the late
him-blame claim fails as a cited reason.
