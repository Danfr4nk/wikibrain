## What happened

During an overnight fight, Annie accused Dan of having been an absent partner
during her Libby-employment era (Feb–mid-Aug 2024): coming home from Libby
8am–3:30pm then straight to the restaurant until 10/11pm to find him ignoring
her, never asking how her day was, asking about his own clean clothes while
dishes stacked up, while she worked 6–7 days a week. "I felt so fucking just
alone."

## Provenance facts (the point of this node)

- **The disengagement formulation has exactly one surviving written instance:**
  "ignored" / "not ask how my day" / "take a toll" appear together only in the
  2026-09-06 outburst. (The word "Libby" itself appears throughout her 2024
  messages — 97 Libby-topic messages Feb–Aug 2024 — so this is a claim about
  the formulation, not the name.)
- **Two years after the era described.** Never raised contemporaneously in this
  form; never raised in the 13 months of heavy texting after the era
  (Aug 2025 → Sep 5 2026).
- **Argument shut down in the same sitting:** "I'm Sorry" / "Forget it" 49
  seconds after the burst ended, then "I took Benadryl for my stuffy nose."
  This reads as ending the fight, not a factual retraction of the underlying
  claim; the claim is never repeated, corrected, or raised again.
- **Reverses her own position:** on 2026-02-22 she wrote "You didn't do anything
  to deserve the way I have treated you… I am the only one to blame for this";
  on 2025-08-13 she assigned disengagement to herself ("physically, mentally,
  emotionally").
- **Dan's reply conceded facts, objected on procedure:** "I am not saying
  you're wrong" / "I can and have taken accountability" / "I don't know how you
  can do what you did without even having one conversation where you kinda let
  me know that i was being so egregious that it would cost me the relationship."

## Assessment

The outburst is first-person testimony of a feeling ("I felt so fucking just
alone") — unfalsifiable and taken at face value — attached to a causal story
(his disengagement during the Libby era as the reason the relationship spoiled)
that the record does not demonstrate as the master cause: the idle-at-home
mechanism is date-displaced (he was employed at Au Za'atar through Aug 2024;
the at-home-all-day period is post-Aug 2024), the household-labor specifics
have one contemporaneous adjacent request (2024-05-12 "please help me out") but
the outburst's clean-clothes/stacked-dishes specifics appear nowhere else, and
the offers ledger documents ~137 offer/favor messages across the era. Full
analysis: wiki/mind/synthesis/libby-era-engagement-verdict.md.
