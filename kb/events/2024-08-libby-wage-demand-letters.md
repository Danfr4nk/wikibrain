## What happened

With Annie's Libby employment broken down over unpaid wages and an unsigned
NDA, Dan drafted three escalating demand letters for her, sent from his handle
into the thread for her to use:

- **2024-08-01** — "Dear x I'm reaching out to inform you of the unpaid wages
  which I have been unable to receive and request a date that I can be
  compensated. The total amount is x (y hours at $75/hour)…"
- **2024-08-13, 00:18** — follow-up on the unanswered first letter.
- **2024-08-13, 01:00** — "Final Request for Payment of Unpaid Wages," with "I
  would like to resolve this issue without any additional complications and am
  not interested in seeking further remedy from Mr or Mrs Fagen."

Annie's stated position: "in exchange for signing an NDA I would like to
receive the unpaid wages" (2024-08-01). Nothing in the corpus records a
resolution; Libby died 2024-10-13 with the dispute open on the evidence
available.

## Why it matters

This is the corpus's clearest instance of Dan's forensic register — dated
correspondence, itemized hours-times-rate, explicit statement of what remedy is
*not* sought, fixed-interval escalation — turned outward on somebody else's
behalf, for a third party's material benefit. It is contemporaneous, documented
engagement with exactly the Libby situation that Annie's 2026-09-06 accusation
frames him as absent from, and it is the single strongest piece of
contemporaneous evidence against the disengagement framing of the Libby era.
