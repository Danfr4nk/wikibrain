Russ Baker's investigation of the Bush family as the center of a durable nexus of oil money, finance and intelligence work, with George H. W. Bush ('Poppy') as the key figure: an intelligence-adjacent paper trail in 1963, the concealed de Mohrenschildt relationship, the Townhouse Operation money, the Bath/bin Laden trusteeship and Saudi money into George W. Bush's ventures, Team B, and the Nayirah testimony.

Phase-1 reliability finding: a serious researcher with an exceptional, checkable document haul (NARA and Mary Ferrell document IDs, court files, sworn depositions, named on-record interviews). The structural weakness is register laundering: documents, single-source interviews, hostile-source claims and pure inference are narrated in the same assured voice, so the connective tissue should be discounted roughly one confidence tier everywhere. Trust the paper he reproduces; the Lowman abortion story and the Yamashita-gold architecture do not hold on his own shown evidence.
