# Provenance of the Seth LeDonne material

## Direct answer: where "Rejected Gazette" was learned

From Dan's own message. On 2020-04-17 at 21:36:07 UTC, in his Facebook
Messenger thread with Seth LeDonne, Dan Frank wrote:

> "just another overdue moment of deserved praise...growing up in
> \"the scene\" and being exposed to things like **rejected gazette**
> and the entire vibe of things.....it really had such a lasting
> impact on me and I'm certain that i wouldn't have the appreciation i
> do for counterculture if not for you, andy, paul, Nathan, kessler"

That message is held in this repository at
`raw/messenger-drive-2026-09-12/messenger_drive_canonical.jsonl`
(thread key `dan frank|seth ledonne`). The wiki page's "Rejected
Gazette," capitalized as a titled publication, is an editorial
elevation of Dan's lowercase "rejected gazette." Whether the
publication's formal name is Rejected Gazette cannot be established
from anything held; the name is attested only in Dan's phrase. Later
in the same thread (2020-06-07) Seth offers to mail Dan "my zine";
the record does not name that zine, and identifying it with the
Gazette is plausible but unattested.

## The chain, link by link

1. **The primary thread exists and is held.** The full Seth LeDonne
   thread — 47 messages, 2020-03-10 to 2020-06-23 — survives in the
   canonical Messenger Drive JSONL, drawn from a Facebook export
   retrieved from Google Drive (`gdrive-fb-20220905`) and
   canonicalized into the repo on 2026-09-12.
2. **The wiki page did not come from that record.** The Seth entry was
   carried verbatim from the old wiki export (2026-09-04,
   `raw/old-wiki-export-2026-09-04/whole.txt`), which rendered the
   thread from the ihatedanfrank HTML export — a file
   (`raw/self/facebook/facebook-ihatedanfrank/messages/inbox/sethledonne_ydkapzhbla/message_1.html`)
   that is not itself held. `raw/facebook-threads/` holds only
   MANIFEST.json metadata (396 threads, 15,923 messages, no bodies).
3. **The 2026-09-10 audit (kb dat:1166) concluded the page's claims
   were "unresolvable from held sources."** That finding was correct
   when written — and it is now stale. The Drive JSONL landed
   2026-09-12, two days later, carrying the very thread the audit
   could not find. dat:1166 and its sibling dat:1202 (Batteries Not
   Included) remain accurate about the old wiki's unheld HTML source;
   their "zero rows / neither confirmable nor falsifiable" verdict is
   superseded for this thread as of 2026-09-12. (kb dat:1202's
   corpus-wide greps for "Rejected Gazette" returning zero rows
   predate the same landing.)
4. **Repo-wide greps, 2026-10-04:** "Rejected Gazette" appears in the
   old-wiki page copies, the current wiki entries, and kb nodes
   citing them — and in exactly two primary containers: the Messenger
   Drive JSONL above, and two Google-Doc conversions inside the
   ihatedanfrank export (`raw/facebook/export-20260908-a/...gdocs/…from-gdoc.txt`,
   duplicated in export-b), which carry the same tribute text and are
   consistent with a document copy of the message rather than an
   independent attestation.

## What the held record corrects in the wiki page

The old wiki's rendering, carried into the current entry, diverges
from the held thread in three ways — the first is material:

- **The protest question was not deflected by Dan.** 2020-06-07
  22:04:49 UTC, Seth asks: "Have you attended protests?" Thirteen
  seconds later Seth defers *his own* question: "Actually let's talk
  about this later I WILL CALL." The "class obligation" is also
  Seth's ("I just need to get this class done by 12," 22:05:09).
  The page attributed Seth's deferral and Seth's excuse to Dan.
  Whether Dan attended the 2020 protests remains genuinely open —
  the question was asked and withdrawn by its asker; it was never
  answered.
- **Quotes are stitched compressions, not verbatim.** The page's
  tribute quote omits held text ("deserved praise," the "lasting
  impact" clause, "unfortunately you all turned me into a completely
  cynical snob"), normalizes capitalization, and presents three
  separate messages (21:36, 21:39, 21:47 UTC) as one continuous
  passage.
- **"beruit," not "beirut."** Dan's held spelling (2020-06-07
  22:00:10 UTC) is "it's been like living in beruit." The page
  silently corrected it.
- A smaller point: the thread's ending is not an "unreturned call"
  by Dan. The final record (2020-06-23 10:26:54 UTC) is a missed-call
  notice — Seth called, Dan missed it. The call Seth promised on
  June 7 was, on the evidence, attempted from Seth's side.

## Standing of the material after this audit

Substantively confirmed: the counterculture-debt tribute and its five
names (Seth, Andy, Paul, Nathan, Kessler), the 311 tie-dye mockery,
the April 14 Biden holdout ("i do not think i can vote biden in
november... i feel so gross giving my vote to the corporate DNC
forces"), and the June 7 curfew dispatch all exist in Dan's held
words, timestamped, in the thread the page describes. Formally
corrected: the page's quotes and its one inverted attribution. Still
unverifiable: the nature of Seth's current circumstances, and
whether the zine Seth mailed Dan (address exchanged in-thread; the
address itself is not reproduced here) was the Rejected Gazette.

The wiki entry `wiki/people/seth-ledonne` was corrected against this
record on 2026-10-04; its Conflicts in the record section carries the
dated corrections.

## Addendum — 2026-10-04 (Dan, testimony)

Dan confirmed tonight: **the Rejected Gazette was Seth LeDonne's own zine in high school.** This is Dan's testimony, not a corpus find — nothing in the held record names the zine's author or era. It is consistent with the thread: on 2020-06-07 Seth offered to mail Dan "my zine" and Dan supplied his mailing address in reply. Whether the zine Seth mailed *was* the Rejected Gazette is unconfirmed. Recorded here so this document's evidence basis stays honest: zine authorship = Dan's word (2026-10-04); everything else in this document = held record.
