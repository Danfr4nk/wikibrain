A content-farm omnibus assembled to capture "dark psychology" search demand on Amazon/KDP circa 2020: five short, separately sellable pop titles stapled into a value bundle under a cover promise ("Persuasion, Analyze Body Language, Hypnosis Techniques, NLP Secrets & Mind Control") no responsible textbook would make. The byline is seven names — Caroline Empathy, Power Laws, Jack Mind, Robert Dale Goleman, Daniel Brandon Bradberry, Travis Greene Carnegie, Gino Gerrard — several transparently constructed by riffing on famous self-help brands (Carnegie, Goleman, Bradberry, Robert Greene's *Power Laws*), the rest keyword labels ("Empathy", "Mind") rather than person-shaped identities. No credentials, no usable citations, no editorial apparatus; large stretches recycle the same chapters across the five books or read as paraphrase/stitching of outside pop sources.

Reliability: **low / folk — this node is the reliability warning.** The omnibus is a primary source for one thing only: the folk model of manipulation that circulates on YouTube, TikTok, Reddit relationship forums and a thousand identical KDP titles — the vocabulary millions of people now use to describe real relationships. It is zero-weight as a secondary source for any fact about psychology, personality, or any individual person. Anyone citing it to support a claim about a real human being has, by that citation alone, weakened their claim.

Phase-1 reliability finding, in brief. What touches something real: ordinary influence tactics (framing, decoys/anchoring, reciprocity, mirroring, timing, first-impression management) have real but modest, context-dependent effects in the social-psychology literature; the gaslighting pattern of Book 3 — persistent reality-denial that induces self-doubt and dependence — is a documented abuse pattern, and the book correctly cites the term's origin in Patrick Hamilton's *Gas Light* (1938) and the 1944 film; the Dark Triad (narcissism, Machiavellianism, psychopathy — Paulhus & Williams, 2002) is a legitimate *dimensional, subclinical* research construct. What does not hold: "dark psychology" as a field (no such recognized subfield); mind control via NLP, embedded/subliminal commands or covert hypnosis; body-language lie detection and the "60–65% of communication is nonverbal" figure; brainwashing at scale through media/education/religion as a coordinated system; the invented 99.99%/0.01% predation statistic; pop-EQ maximalism; the Enneagram as personality measurement; and the book's load-bearing move — turning dimensional traits into a remorseless predator *type* (narcissist / sociopath / psychopath / "empath's" counterpart) and explaining abuse by that type, conflating psychopathy with psychosis and sociopathy with antisocial personality disorder along the way. The folk model's signature error is rescaling: influence → control, traits → types, deception → reality-warping.
