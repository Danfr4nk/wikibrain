METHOD. Each photo was sampled in CIELAB (D50) at multiple skin regions (face, neck, midriff/arms where visible) with a white or gray in-frame reference used to estimate the illuminant cast; warm-cast frames were discounted, not trusted at face value. Undertone band: warm skin reads b* +18..+28; neutral-cool +9..+14 (Dan's own Deep Winter calibration the same day).

READINGS, photo by photo.
(1) Indoor set, photo 1: face b* 11-13 (foundation/flush suspected, judged not undertone); shoulder/upper-arm body b* 18-19.
(2) Photo 2, different lighting: cheek b* 18.0, midriff b* 22.6, arms/shoulders b* 18-24 -- warm at the face this time, killing the photo-1 face/body split as a lighting/makeup artifact.
(3) Daylight outdoor, gold standard: white top b* -1.1 (zero cast); midriff b* 22.3, face 19.4, neck 21.9.
(4-6) Three further daylight/mixed photos (6-photo unanimous set): body b* 18-31 across all frames.
(7) Flash photo, neutral white wall b* +1.8: barest face read, b* 12-15 (neutral, never cool).
(8) Daylight outdoor face: b* 17-20 warm.
(9) Warm-indoor face: b* 27-31 (cast-inflated; direction still warm).
(10) Makeup close-up: face b* 18-21, uniform warm even through foundation. Eyes observed hazel -- brown-amber with green -- classic autumn marker.
(11) Dim warm-indoor face: b* 17-19.
(12) Outdoor garden face: b* 14-18.
(13-16) Brick-wall / warm-indoor set: b* 25-35, heavily warm-cast (white wall in one frame reads b* 17.1, confirming the cast; skin still reads warmer than the wall). Weakest absolute numbers in the set; direction identical.

AGGREGATE. Faces run neutral-to-warm by lighting and makeup, never cool; body warm in all 16. No photo in the set has ever read cool. Warm-leaning undertone + dark warm-brown hair + medium-deep soft contrast = Deep Autumn, high confidence.

PALETTE (from the verdict). Best: burnt orange, rust, terracotta, mustard, olive, forest green, espresso, camel, cream, brick; gold over silver. Her navy set works (depth suits her); ideal darks run warm -- espresso, deep forest, dark olive. Avoid: bright white, true black, cobalt, fuchsia, icy pastels.
