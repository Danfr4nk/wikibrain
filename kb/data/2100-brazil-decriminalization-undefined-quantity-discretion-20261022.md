## Personal bearing

Dan's documented supply world is the population this mechanism falls on: a three-man Dominican crew — Menore on phone/coordination, his cousin Weatherman and his little brother Curly as crew — running a cocaine delivery line for years (November 2018 – February 2025, roughly 280 deliveries). Dan's own legal exposure in that record runs through them. Hart's Portugal/Brazil contrast bears here only at that point: a Portuguese-style panel reform protects the buyer at the panel; it does nothing for the Menores, and a Brazil-style undefined reform would not even reliably protect the buyer, because the user/trafficker line stays a street-level discretion call.

## Evidence

Hart's Brazil account comes through Julita Lemgruber over lunch in Ch. 9 — 'if you're white, you're a user; if you're black, you're a trafficker' — set against Portugal (Ch. 2), where decriminalization was paired with defined procedures and a treatment build-out. The phase-1 report rated the Brazilian post-2006 rise in trafficking arrests med-high confidence on the sources Hart cites.
