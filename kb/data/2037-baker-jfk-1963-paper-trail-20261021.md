## Evidence

The Hoover memo surfaced through researcher Joseph McBride (Nation, July 16, 1988); the substitute Bush's refutation is his own sworn deposition; a named corroborating figure (Capt. William Edwards, 1991) adds weight; the Tyler call rests on the FBI's Parrott memo, declassified under the JFK Records Review Board process. None of this requires trusting Baker's narration - it requires reading the documents he reproduces.

## Limits

This establishes an undisclosed intelligence-adjacent paper trail in 1963 and a fabricated official denial. It does NOT establish that George H. W. Bush was a CIA operative (as opposed to an asset, contact or informal collaborator), and it does NOT establish any operational role in the assassination itself. Baker's reading of garbled names in the Parrott memo as deliberate obfuscation was adjudicated med-high doesn't-hold (hurried phone-note transcription explains the artifact).
