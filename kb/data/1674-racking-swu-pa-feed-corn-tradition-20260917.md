## The definition (his words, 2026-09-17 ~01:15–01:21Z)

> "racking is throwing hard corn kernels at peoples houses at night"
> "It has nothing to do wirh stealing. You buy a huge bag of feed corn and
> fill up a backpack and throw corn at the houses of people you know it will
> most annoy. This is a very 12 year old boy thing."

Three corrections to the naive reading, all his:

1. **The throwing IS the act.** Racking is not procurement, casing, or a
   prelude — it is the throwing itself.
2. **Not theft-adjacent.** The corn is bought (feed corn, huge bag), not
   stolen; the crime isn't theft, it's targeted psychological warfare against
   whoever annoys easiest.
3. **Target selection is social.** Houses "of people you know it will most
   annoy" — the mischief is aimed, not random.
