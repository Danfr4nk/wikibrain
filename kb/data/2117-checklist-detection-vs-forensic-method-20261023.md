## Personal bearing

This node's bearing on Dan is protective, and it is a contrast, not a match. Dan's documented method ([[wiki/mind/concepts/forensic-method]]) earns its conclusions the expensive way: construct the condition under which a claim breaks, hold it, record the failure modes, and quantify — the stance-testing battery against Gemini, the quantified probing verdict (dat:1617), the corpus counts behind the wiki's relationship pages. The manipulation-manual genre sells the cheap substitute: confidence that the world of hidden intent becomes legible if you memorize enough signs. That substitute is the single most likely future citation error in a wiki like this one — a genre checklist quoted as if it were a forensic instrument, lending a suspicion the costume of a measurement. This node records, in advance and next to the method it protects, why that citation would be an error: the detection claim itself fails in the research literature, independently of who cites it.

## Evidence

Phase 1 adjudicated the book's detection apparatus directly (§iii.5, high confidence: doesn't hold): crossed arms, gaze, lip-biting, posture — singly or as checklists — do not reliably diagnose deception, intent, or character; deception detection in controlled studies is poor; the nonverbal-percentage figure is a genre descendant of Mehrabian's narrow experiments on inconsistent feelings-messages, inflated into a general law. The Dan-side anchor is the forensic-method page's own documented practice, including its failure-mode discipline — his instruments are audited against their break conditions, which is precisely the step a sign-list skips.

## Limits

The contrast is epistemological, not a claim that cues carry zero information or that Dan never reads people informally — ordinary social reading is not what the book sells or what this node rejects. What is rejected is the strong form: a memorized list functioning as diagnosis. No book claim here is presented as Dan's belief; the node's Dan-side claim is only that his documented method is the opposite of the genre's, which his record shows.
