## Personal bearing

Dan's standing decision to keep the Wiki Brain fully public - raw material and personal numbers included, at real cost, against repeated pressure to privatize - fits this mechanism exactly: the display is costly, hard to fake, and publicly inspectable, and its value depends on being seen. The mechanism predicts, correctly per the record, that he would resist privatizing it even when privatizing would be cheaper and safer. This does not displace the wiki's instrument function (kb/interpretations/wiki-brain-as-instrument.md); it names a second function the same artifact performs at the same time.

## Evidence

Sexton & Sexton's Prius premium rests on real vehicle-registration data and is listed in the phase-1 audit among the book's surviving evidence. The wiki-side facts are held record: the fully-public standing policy, numbers included, and the refusal to re-erect exclusions.

## Limits

Limits: The analogy limit is explicit: a car purchase is a market transaction with a priced premium; the wiki's signal value has no equivalent price readout, and the mechanism is offered as the best available account of the publicity decision's shape, not as proof of Dan's motive for it. No book claim here is Dan's stated belief.
