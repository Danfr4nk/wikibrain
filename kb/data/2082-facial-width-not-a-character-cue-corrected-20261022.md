## Personal bearing

Dan runs face-based tooling (the face-tag system) and has run facial-preference mapping and an audited face library; those systems are built, at their best, on his own evidence discipline - the face library's audit famously rejected most of its own inputs. What he should not do is import the book's width cue into judgments of actual people in his circles: it would put a debunked accuracy claim inside systems he otherwise runs on measurement. The stereotype is usable only as data about perceivers, never as data about the perceived.

## Evidence

The phase-1 audit places facial width-to-height ratio among the book's decorative evolutionary material that has largely collapsed under later large-sample and preregistered work; deleting it changes no theme in the book, which is the tell that it was garnish. The perception-versus-accuracy distinction is the standard resolution in that later literature.

## Limits

Limits: The book's version named as the fail: width as a valid trust / aggression cue (Ch. 4). The corrected claim does not deny that face perception influences Dan's own preference mapping - his attraction-guide work measures his perceptions, which is legitimate - it denies that the width cue measures the other person's character.
