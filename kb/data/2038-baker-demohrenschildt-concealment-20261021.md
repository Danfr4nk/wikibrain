## Evidence

The contact reports are declassified CIA paper; Baker adds a 2008 on-record confirmation from CIA veteran Gale Allen; the 1976 letter exchange is archival and quoted verbatim; the 1975 memo was found in December 2006 by researcher Jerry Shinley via the Mary Ferrell Foundation, and Devine, on record to Baker in 2007, refused to discuss it on Bush's instruction.

## Limits

The concealment is documented; the relationship's purpose is not. The inference chain from Devine's meetings to Bush himself is adjudicated separately and remains an inference. De Mohrenschildt's 1977 death on the day an HSCA investigator was seeking him is documented as to timing; manner and meaning are unresolved.
