Scratching a sub-gap doesn't kill the parent, which is why reassurance misfires ('he scratches the question she asked, not the one that's itching'). Deceit = counterfeit scratch — a gap-closer that doesn't close the gap; betrayal totalizes, so the whole past starts itching at once. Self-imposed vs unbidden itches differ in start, not in run."
