## Personal bearing

Dan's Taste Constitution documents an unusually legible taste identity - the K3YN0T3 / Distan / T3CHN0T3 orbit, the I-vi seesaw across the canon, the 125-130 BPM home room - and his crates and playlists circulate publicly. On this evidence those artifacts are self-presentation in effect whether or not they are aimed as such: anyone who can read the code receives a personality-and-tribe transmission from them. This is a claim about what the book's cited research says taste does socially; it is not a claim that Dan curates in order to signal.

## Evidence

The Rentfrow programme (music-preference dimensions and personality inference from taste) is the cited basis; the phase-1 audit counts the taste-as-communication theme among the parts of the book carried by real behavioral and sociological literature rather than by the book's neuroscience. The wiki already holds the intake side of Dan's taste as measurement (kb/patterns/taste-as-filter-stack.md; dat:0077, dat:0078); this node adds the reception side the book documents.

## Limits

Limits: Consensus about what a taste profile connotes is not accuracy about the person, and connotation varies by audience - only listeners inside or near the code read it finely. The claim covers music taste specifically; it does not license reading every preference Dan holds as a deliberate message.
