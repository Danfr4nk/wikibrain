## Personal bearing

This is the textbook frame for the calibration habit Dan already practices and documents. In the stylometry work he self-labeled a first line on September 11, 2026, and logged a self-declared cocaine-heavy day on September 21, 2026 through onset, peak, redose and come-down — that is dose-and-phase tracking, an attempt to build a personal baseline in which dose and timing are the variables under observation. Hart supplies the vocabulary and the pharmacology for a practice already in Dan's record; he does not supply the practice. No book claim here is presented as Dan's belief.

## Evidence

Hart states the dose / route / set / setting triad most cleanly in Ch. 3 and uses it throughout: the same heroin that is a domestic ritual in his own housed, employed life is a death in a Belfast alley, and the difference he identifies is housing, tolerance, knowledge, company and whether anyone present has naloxone — not the powder. The phase-1 report rated this high confidence as basic pharmacology and as a description of what Hart's own laboratory designs can show.

## Limits

Limits: the triad is a framework, not a measurement of any individual episode. It does not license inferring Dan's set, setting or subjective state on any documented occasion from the fact of use alone, and it does not make a logged dose safe — it names the variables a calibration practice has to track, including the ones (setting, company, supply identity) that a timing log does not capture.
