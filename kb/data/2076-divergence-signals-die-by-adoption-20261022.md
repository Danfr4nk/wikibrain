## Personal bearing

For Dan's crates and orbit, this names a structural event in advance: when a sound or artist in his orbit is adopted by an audience he does not identify with, its value to him predictably falls through signal dilution. That drop is a predictable crate event to anticipate, not a betrayal to relitigate each time it happens. His documented variant-keeping (regular plus sped-up versions of the same tracks) reads, in this frame, as signal maintenance - holding a distinguishable version when the standard one congests.

## Evidence

Berger & Heath's experiments (including the bracelet-adoption studies the book retails) are among the findings the phase-1 audit lists as surviving: divergence behavior is experimentally demonstrated, and the book's own narrative shows niches re-congesting immediately after adoption, which is the mechanism operating in the wild.

## Limits

Limits: The experiments measure abandonment of identity goods under laboratory adoption manipulations; the size of the effect in a working producer's crate decisions is an application, not a measurement. Divergence also relocates competition into fresh niches rather than abolishing it - the book's happier gloss (a solution to the Status Dilemma) is not part of this claim.
