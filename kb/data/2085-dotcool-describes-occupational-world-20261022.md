## Personal bearing

This is the economy Dan works in as an AI / agent-pipeline builder, and its edge for him is specific: in that economy the pitch never turns off, so his public artifacts - the wiki, the crates, the profiles - are continuously read as competence signals by exactly the people whose partner-choice decisions matter to his work. That is a reason the artifacts' quality is load-bearing, not vanity. Note for the wiki's own vocabulary: DotCool's cool (friendly, competent, creative) is a different construct from Dan's cool / lame sorting metric (wiki/mind/concepts/the-cool-metric.md) - same word, different mechanism, and the two should not be merged.
