## Evidence

Baker quotes contemporaneous and retrospective coverage, including Newsweek's 2003 observation that the Team B report faulted CIA analysis for being based on too much 'hard data'; the 1976 Team A/Team B experiment, its membership and its inflated conclusions are established in the mainstream historical record.

## Limits

Phase 1 did not separately adjudicate Team B in its Adds up list; this node is graded moderate rather than high for that reason. The claim that Team B was created in order to reach a predetermined conclusion is Baker's characterization of motive; the documented facts are the authorization, the composition and the divergence of the estimates.
