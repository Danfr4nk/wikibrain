## Personal bearing

Of everything in the book, this is the observation that maps onto Dan's documented life with the least forcing — because the wiki itself is the evidence. The profile cluster exists because the appetite Wiest describes was acted on, repeatedly and expensively: instruments commissioned, typings disputed, a dated first-person self-typing (5w6sx, 2024-11-04) eventually beating the commissioned 5w4 the composite was built on. Wiest's essay contributes exactly one thing the cluster does not say about itself: from outside, the stack reads as a hunger for self-awareness first and a set of measurements second. That framing is worth holding beside the cluster's own adjudications — it predicts the demand side (why new instruments keep being commissioned) that the per-instrument pages, focused on provenance, do not address.

## Evidence

Phase 1 verified Essay 76's list (Myers-Briggs, Big Five, Enneagram, astrology, presented together as hunger signs) against the profile cluster's documented contents — the same first three instruments appear in both, with astrology the one list item the cluster does not run. The cluster's provenance disputes (5w4 vs. 5w6sx; instrument-defective Attitudinal Psyche results quarantined) are held on wiki/mind/profile/index.md itself.

## Limits

Wiest names an appetite; she adjudicates nothing. Her list deliberately flattens instruments of very different standing (the Big Five's psychometric literature against astrology) into one cultural gesture, and this node does not import that flattening — on the wiki, each instrument's products stand or fall on their own provenance, per the profile protocol. The mapping is also one-directional: Essay 76 explains why the stack exists, not whether any instrument in it measures anything.
