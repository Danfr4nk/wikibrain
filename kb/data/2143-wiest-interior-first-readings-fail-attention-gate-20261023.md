## Personal bearing

The attention-not-interior boundary is binding on this wiki: it is stated in the profile hub and in the standing profile-first protocol, and it disciplines every theory built from Dan's instruments — a thesis that cannot be reframed to what the instruments actually see (attention) does not get filed as knowledge about his interior. Wiest's entire method runs on the other side of that line: an essay tells the reader what their feeling means about their inner state, with the feeling treated as a transparent readout and the interior as directly re-choosable. This node is the standing caution that follows: if Wiest-derived readings of Dan were ever offered as profile evidence — "his anxiety signals X about his interior" in her register — they would fail the gate the wiki's own theories have to pass, and should be dropped or reframed to attention level like everything else.

## Evidence

Phase 1 documented the uniformity of Wiest's interior-first treatment across the essays (the emotional-intelligence essays 3, 17, 18, 42, 56, 80 all define health as correctly reading what feelings signal about the interior) against the protocol text held on wiki/mind/profile/index.md and in the weighted profile instrument the protocol names.

## Limits

The gate governs wiki evidence, not reading: nothing here says Wiest's interior language is useless as private vocabulary or that Dan may not find an aphorism resonant. What it cannot do is enter the record as a measurement of his interior regulation — the same restriction the wiki applies to its own commissioned instruments, applied here to a far weaker source. Confidence is medium because the mapping is methodological (a whole book's stance against a protocol), not a single checkable claim.
