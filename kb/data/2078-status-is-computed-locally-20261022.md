## Personal bearing

Dan's standing registers where he actually lives it: the production scene, the wiki's readership, his circles - not on any global socioeconomic ladder. Two consequences follow for how he should price effort and injury. First, legibility and rank inside those groups buy more than generic status display aimed at strangers who hold no local ladder he is on. Second, in-group slights will sting out of proportion to their objective size, because the meter that registers them is local - that disproportion is the mechanism working as designed, not oversensitivity.

## Evidence

The local-ladder theme is carried by the local-status research literature the book synthesizes, which the phase-1 audit counts among the book's surviving load-bearing material (as distinct from its neuroscience). It coheres with the book's divergence material: niches are precisely local ladders with their own currencies.

## Limits

Limits: Local computation does not mean global hierarchies are unreal - money and institutional rank still constrain options across groups. The claim is about where status is felt and evaluated, not about where power sits.
