## Personal bearing

Sense-making is not a habit Dan happens to have; on the wiki's own pages it is his method and his infrastructure. The forensic-method page documents the practice end to end — primary evidence over narrative, dated corrections published when the instrument catches its own error, conclusions carrying confidence labels. The exocortex page documents the machinery built to support it: lossless, unconsolidated retention ("never summarize away detail") so that sense can be made later, from the record, rather than from memory's edits. Wiest's Essays 37 and 39 prescribe abandoning exactly this: the attempt to make sense of one's trajectory is named as the obstacle itself. Filed as a tension because the prescription is aimed, in the book, at a deflection — sense-making as a way to avoid living — and nothing in Dan's documented record shows his analysis functioning that way; the same activity that is avoidance in her frame is, in his, the thing that has repeatedly caught errors every other channel missed.

## Evidence

Phase 1 located the anti-sense-making instruction at Essay 37 (which also, characteristically, instructs the reader to trace the genesis of their feelings and make sense of their thinking in the same essay) and the overthinking-as-avoidance framing at Essay 39. The wiki side is held: the forensic-method page's operating rules and self-falsification record, and the exocortex page's node-locking and retention protocol.

## Limits

The book's target is rumination — sense-making that substitutes for action — and this node does not claim Dan is immune to that failure mode; it claims the wiki documents no instance of it needing this prescription, and that a blanket instruction to stop making sense cannot distinguish rumination from the method that produced this wiki. The tension is recorded, not resolved in the method's favour by fiat: the pages' own self-falsification records are where any future evidence on that question would land.
