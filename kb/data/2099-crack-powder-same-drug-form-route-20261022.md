## Personal bearing

Two narrower bearings, both documented. First, route: this is the cocaine-specific instance of Flag 1's dose/route principle underneath Dan's calibration practice — route is a variable in the effect, which is part of why a dose log that ignores route and form is incomplete. Second, law: the crack/powder distinction Hart dismantles pharmacologically is the distinction that built the sentencing-disparity regime (in Hart's figures, more than 90 percent of those sentenced under the crack laws were black and 85 percent of federal crack defendants were black) — the legal risk environment in which every cocaine transaction in Dan's documented record, his suppliers' most of all, has taken place. Nothing in this node documents or implies crack use by Dan; none is in the anchors for this mine.

## Evidence

Hart treats crack as 'the epidemic that wasn't' (Ch. 9): the pharmacology is cocaine's, the faster onset and shorter duration of the smoked form explain the intensity difference, and the catastrophic social outcomes assigned to a unique molecule track poverty, enforcement and setting instead. The phase-1 report rated the same-drug claim high confidence.

## Limits

Limits: same molecule does not mean same risk profile in practice — route changes onset, duration, compulsion dynamics and pulmonary harm, and Hart's own dose/route/set/setting frame (dat:2095) requires keeping those differences in view. The sentencing figures are Hart's, presented for the disparity argument; this node does not independently re-derive them.
