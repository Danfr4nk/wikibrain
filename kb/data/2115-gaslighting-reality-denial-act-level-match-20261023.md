## Personal bearing

This is the one place the omnibus's folk model touches Dan's documented life at act level — and this node is written so the touch can never be mistaken for the book explaining Dan's life. The wiki's fallout verdict already rates months of gaslighting above the affair itself in ending the Annie relationship ([[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]], [[wiki/mind/synthesis/dan-annie-fallout-verdict]]), and [[wiki/mind/concepts/attachment-model]] carries the "gaslighting architecture (compact)": written private denials running concurrent with a defamation campaign. Those positions rest on Dan's record — dated messages, written denials, the sources the verdict cites — not on any book. What this folk specimen adds is only a vocabulary check: the pattern-name a reader will meet everywhere online ("gaslighting") does correspond, in its core form, to the acts the wiki documents. The wiki's evidence is the acts; the book is a witness to the word, not to the events.

## Evidence

Book 3's core list — persistent denial, countering memory, trivializing, staging doubt until the target apologizes compulsively, excuses the abuser, and loses the ability to decide independently — matches the pattern as described in abuse literature, and its etymology is right: Hamilton's *Gas Light* (1938), the 1944 film. Phase 1 rated the origin and the pattern-as-described high confidence; any given checklist item diagnosing a given case, medium. On the Dan side, the anchors are pre-existing wiki positions built from the primary record, cited above; the attachment-model page itself flags its two "written denial" instances as unevenly sourced (November 2025 confirmed; February 2026 unlocated), and this node inherits that caveat rather than smoothing it.

## Limits

The book explains the pattern by a predator *type* — the remorseless narcissist who fakes empathy and plans cruelty. That explanation does not hold (phase-1 §iii.10, high confidence): it conflates distinct and non-diagnostic categories, reverses base rates, and substitutes type for documented acts. It is rejected here in full. Nothing in this node diagnoses any person, attributes any book claim to Dan as his belief, or adds the book as evidence for any act in his record. Where the folk vocabulary is used on wiki pages, it names acts the record documents — denial, memory-countering, trivializing — and nothing more.
