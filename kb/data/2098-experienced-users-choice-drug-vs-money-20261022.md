## Personal bearing

The laboratory analogue of Dan's documented control-through-procurement style: planned, budgeted, premium-priced purchasing from a benchmark supplier over six-plus years (the Menore operation, November 2018 – February 2025), with 'Menore's bags' retained as a quality standard even after the service ended. In Dan's record the control sits at the procurement layer — supplier choice, price accepted without negotiation for reliability and tested product, a benchmark kept after exit — and Hart supplies experimental evidence that experienced users, in a controlled setting, are likewise not compelled takers of whatever is offered. The bearing is that analogue, at that layer; see Limits for where it stops.

## Evidence

The drug-vs-money result is Hart's own published work and has not been overturned; the phase-1 report rated the laboratory finding itself med-high confidence. It sits in the book beside his related double-blind finding that experienced methamphetamine users could not distinguish methamphetamine from d-amphetamine (Ch. 5).

## Limits

Limits: small samples of screened, experienced users, acute doses, a laboratory setting — med confidence as a general theory of use. It does not transfer cleanly to in-the-moment dose refusal in Dan's own record: the wiki's dosage arc (dat:0062) documents quantity moving with finance (roughly 1 g/day, to 3.5–7 g/day when an inheritance removed the capital constraint, to 0.5–1 g/day after), and reads the contraction as a resource adjustment rather than moderation. Procurement-level control and dose-level titration are different claims; this node supports the first as an analogue and does not claim the second for Dan.
